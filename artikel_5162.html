WhiteBIT Relaunches OTC Desk for Large-Volume Crypto Transactions

WhiteBIT has relaunched its over-the-counter (OTC) platform and enhanced its institutional offering with a large-volume trading section for digital assets and fiat currencies. It is tailored for institutional and corporate clients, investment funds, family offices, and other participants in the market that seek a special approach when having high-value deals.

Unlike normal exchange trading, where big orders may impact market prices and execution expenses, OTC trades are transactions carried out outside the public order book, straight between a client and the trading desk. This can reduce the impact on the market, give more flexibility in terms of the execution period, and give more customization for institutional trading.

“The institutional engagement with cryptocurrencies is growing at the level of more than just access to cryptocurrencies. Equipped with a reliable compliance framework, a dedicated team of support, and processes that are predictable in their operations, funds, corporate treasuries, and professional investors require execution models that allow them to operate in the same way they do in traditional financial markets. By relaunching our OTC desk, we’re strengthening the infrastructure that enables these participants to execute large transactions more efficiently and with greater flexibility,” said Volodymyr Nosov, Founder and President of W Group, of which WhiteBIT is a part.

The relaunched desk supports crypto-to-fiat and fiat-to-crypto transactions in major fiat currencies, including the US dollar (USD), euro (EUR), British pound (GBP), and Swiss franc (CHF), as well as the stablecoins USDT and USDC. Also, the top 10 crypto assets by market capitalization are now available for trading on the OTC desk against USDT and USDC. Other crypto assets outside the top 10 and additional fiat currencies are available upon individual request.

WhiteBIT does not impose a fixed upper limit on OTC transaction sizes. Instead, the maximum amount is determined by the source of funds and the results of the required compliance checks, so capacity is set around each client’s actual activity.

Each client is assigned to a dedicated OTC manager, who provides them with support in negotiating the transaction parameters, agreeing the terms of execution, and support throughout the transaction. The service protects client data and transactional information and follows Know Your Customer (KYC), Know Your Business (KYB), and all applicable compliance regulations.

WhiteBIT is introducing a zero-fee offer for new OTC clients, covering their first transaction of up to $2 million. It is a one-time offer, valid for three months from release, after which it can be extended; transactions with the top 10 crypto assets are not covered by the offer. Deals with a value of more than 2 million are processed as a single tranche with an indicative price derived from the OTC pricing strategy established in advance with the client’s dedicated OTC manager, allowing institutional and corporate clients to plan bigger deals from the beginning.

The relaunch is a part of WhiteBIT’s institutional and professional market business growth strategy. With the addition of a dedicated OTC execution channel, WhiteBIT will aim to provide crypto liquidity and allow more flexibility in handling large-scale digital asset transactions for businesses and investors.

WhiteBIT is a leading global crypto exchange by user traffic, offering over 1020 trading pairs, 370+ assets, and supporting 8 fiat currencies. With a strong focus on regulatory compliance, WhiteBIT serves 10 million users across six continents and more than 150 countries. Founded in 2018, the platform is part of W Group, which has more than 40 million customers globally. WhiteBIT collaborates with Visa, FACEIT, FC Barcelona, Juventus FC, and the Ukrainian national football team. The company is dedicated to driving the widespread adoption of blockchain technology worldwide.
