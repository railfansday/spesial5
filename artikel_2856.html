Robert Saleh: We've put more on Cam Ward's plate and he's proving he can handle it

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Titans lost their third straight game on Sunday, but head coach Robert Saleh continued to like what he saw from quarterback Cam Ward during Jacksonville's 40-23 win.

Ward was 28-of-39 for 231 yards, two touchdowns and an interception while running three times for 39 yards. That marked a season-high in completion percentage for Ward and he has completed at least 64.5 percent of his passes in all three of the losses.

That run coincides with what Saleh called a decision to "shift our philosophy with how we wanted to approach offense and put more in his plate with regard to being a general at the line of scrimmage." Saleh said that Ward has done "a really nice job taking command" and has shown the Titans what they were hoping to see in the process.

"To be fair to Cam, we've said from the beginning we were going to give him every opportunity to prove that he is a franchise quarterback of the Tennessee Titans," Saleh said at a Monday press conference. "So, philosophically, the change was: How are we going to evaluate whether or not is he capable of being our franchise quarterback if we don't give him the opportunity to prove it? And I think over the last three weeks he has done that. Or, at least is doing it."

The Titans scored 84 points in their first six games and they've put up 83 in the three games since the tweak in their offensive approach. That step in the right direction has not resulted in wins, but it is reason to hope that will change if Ward is able to sustain the improvement that the Titans have seen over the last few weeks.
