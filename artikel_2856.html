UH quarterback Conner Weigman listed as questionable against West Virginia with undisclosed injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

MORGANTOWN, W.Va. - University of Houston quarterback Conner Weigman is listed as questionable for Saturday's game against West Virginia, according to the Big 12 availability report released Friday night.

Weigman is dealing with an undisclosed injury he suffered in last week's loss at Colorado. There was no other immediate word on his status for the game.

A final availability report will be released 90 minutes prior to kickoff.

Weigman has thrown for 2,552 yards and 25 touchdowns and has a team-high seven rushing touchdowns for the No. 20 Cougars, who are 7-3 entering the final two weeks of the regular season.
