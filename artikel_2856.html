Ravens doomed by 4 turnovers vs. Panthers, including fumble through end zone in back-and-forth thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Justice Hill thought he had a touchdown Sunday against the Carolina Panthers.

Instead, the Baltimore Ravens running back fumbled through the end zone to spark a wild sequence of events that ultimately resulted in a score for the Panthers.

The sequence proved critical as the Panthers went on to a 35-33 win that was sealed by a go-ahead Panthers field goal followed up by a Jacoby Brissett interception.

With the win, the Panthers improved to 5-5 to increase their lead in the NFC South to two games over the rest of the division. The Bucs, Falcons and Saints are all 3-7.

With 12:17 remaining in the second quarter and Baltimore holding a 12-7 lead, Hill took a handoff from the Carolina 37-yard line and broke free through the secondary toward the end zone. But just before he reached the end zone, Carolina cornerback Will Lee punched the ball out of of Hill's grasp.

The ball then bounced through the end zone to trigger perhaps the most controversial rule in football. Even though the Panthers didn't recover the loose ball, they took over possession via a touchback.

Just like that, a would-be Ravens score turned into a turnover at the last moment thanks to a heads-up play from Lee.

The Panthers didn't initially do much with their good fortune. They punted after a 3-and-out on their ensuing possession. But another Ravens miscue set them up with another opportunity.

This time, LaJohntay Wester muffed the punt return, allowing the Panthers to recover deep in Baltimore territory.

This time, the Panthers capitalized. Two plays later, Chuba Hubbard ran 18 yards around the left edge for a touchdown and a 14-12 Carolina lead.

All was not lost for the Ravens in the wild sequence.

The very next touch for the Ravens resulted in a score. Devontez Walker returned the ensuing kickoff 86 yards for a touchdown to put the Ravens back on top with a 19-14 lead.

If you're counting, that's three consecutive Ravens touches that resulted in a fumble through the end zone, a muffed punt that led to a Carolina touchdown and a kickoff return for a Baltimore touchdown.

But Baltimore's lead would not hold. The lead changed hands five more times from there in a back-and-forth battle that gave Panthers fans in Charlotte their money's worth.

The teams exchanged two more touchdowns before halftime, and the Ravens went into the break with a 26-22 advantage. Carolina then opened the second half with a touchdown for a 29-26 lead that that the Ravens took back on a 15-yard Derrick Henry touchdown run with 6:32 remaining.

That left Carolina with the ball and a 33-32 deficit.

Bryce Young led a 12-play, 55-yard drive from there to set up Ryan Fitzgerald for a 37-yard field goal and a 35-33 Panthers lead with 1:44 remaining. From there, Tyrel Dodson iced the win with an interception of Brissett.

Brissett evaded pressure on the first-and-10 play from the Baltimore 32. But he threw an ill-advised pass off his back foot that resulted in a batted ball in the secondary that Dodson caught.

The late interception encapsulated a day in which the Panthers secured a 4-1 advantage in takeaways. Henry lost a fumble in addition to the turnovers by Brissett, Hill and Wester. The Ravens out-gained the Panthers in total offensive yardage, 421 to 378, but their miscues ultimately cost them the game.

The Ravens also lost Henry to injury. He left the field after his fourth-quarter touchdown for concussion evaluation. His status wasn't immediately clear postgame.

The loss drops a disappointing Ravens team to 4-7, well out of the AFC playoff picture.
