Cardinals add Carson Beck to injury report as a limited participant

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cardinals made an unwelcome addition to their injury report on Thursday.

Quarterback Carson Beck was a limited participant due to a back injury. Beck dealt with back issues earlier in the season as well and an update on his condition heading into Sunday’s game against the Chiefs will come on Friday’s final injury report of the week.

Josh Johnson and Joey Aguilar are the backups. Both quarterbacks have been on the team for less than two weeks.

Guard Jon Gaines (ankle) was the only player out of practice for Arizona. Defensive lineman Roy Lopez (toe, ankle) joined Beck as a limited participant.

Safety Budda Baker (rest), defensive lineman Walter Nolen (illness), guard Isaac Seumalo (rest), linebacker Josh Sweat (rest, shoulder), linebacker Mack Wilson (rest), center Hjalte Froholdt (concussion), cornerback Max Melton (knee, hand), left tackle Jayden Williams (knee), defensive lineman Zach Carter (knee), and defensive lineman Darius Robinson (wrist) were full participants.
