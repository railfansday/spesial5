Browns-Texans fantasy and prop bets: Bet on Cleveland’s passing game

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Cleveland Browns are at home against the Houston Texans in Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season this Sunday, November 15, at 1:00 pm ET. The Browns are 4.5-point underdogs against the Texans, according to FanDuel Sportsbook.

Last week was the second time this season that we didn't hit on any of our picks. RB Quinshon Judkins didn't get it going in the running game like I expected, TE Harold Fannin came up two yards short of what he needed, and Isaiah Bond didn't even have an offensive snap, let alone a receiving touchdown. Let's do better this week.

Here are my favorite player prop bets from the Browns' perspective in Week 10.

This bet has worked (or would have worked) three weeks in a row, so why not keep riding the gravy train? The bonus here is that the Browns often utilize Dylan Sampson, and Deshaun Watson isn't wanting to force balls, so Sampson will always get some looks that way. And then, in two-minute situations, or if the team is behind later, Sampson is also the guy in the game to catch passes. The Jaguars' pass defense is susceptible, so take Sampson again here.

After somewhat of a three-week lull in receiving yards, Denzel Boston had a season-high 98 yards receiving last week. Houston's defense has been prone to giving up the big passing plays, so this could be another week if Boston catching a long ball and getting all of this yardage in one moment. I'm expecting that Cleveland won't be able to run the ball effectively, so they should have a gameplan to come out aggressive in the passing game.

If you believe the theme that I'm saying — Cleveland won't be able to run the ball, but they'll get the passing yards against this Texans defense — then Deshaun Watson throwing for 209+ yards seems feasible. For some perspective, the Browns are allowing 201 yards passing (6th in the NFL) per game, while the Texans are allowing 236 yards passing (27th in NFL). I don't have a parlay listed here, but if you're going to attempt a parlay, all three of the picks in this article — along with a Browns win (might as well go big with a parlay) — would be something you could try.

Browns fans, let us know which player prop bets you're looking at this week against the Texans.
