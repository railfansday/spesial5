Reacts Results: Win and you’re in their hearts

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

All it took is one skin-of-their-teeth win over the New Orleans Saints to end a three-game losing skid, seize first place in the NFC South, and restore some faith from fans for the 4-5 Carolina Panthers. It helps, of course, that they are facing, at home, a 4-6 Baltimore Ravens team who is on their own five-game losing streak and is 1-5 in their last six games since Lamar Jackson was injured the first time for the season. This week they are giving newly acquired Jacoby Brissett his second start at quarterback for the Ravens and we're all going to see what happens after that.

Confidence rising 26% after a win in which the team arguably played a less complete game than they did in their loss to the Denver Broncos the week before has me wondering if this survey is just a lagging indicator of wins. They are the division leader with the worst record, with the NFC East leading New York Giants coming in as a close second at 5-3-1. Third place is a four way tie of 7-2 teams, including the Detroit Lions who currently sit atop a three-way 7-2 tie in the NFC North. There is no wonder we are seeing a lot of contemplation about changes to the playoff format when the Panthers are on track for a home playoff game (or whoever 'wins' the NFC South) and the NFC North is likely to have one or more teams with winning records miss the playoffs entirely.

As for this week, most fans are unsurprisingly expecting a win. The Ravens have suffered injuries both natural and self-inflicted—looking at you, Marlon Humphrey—and are struggling to find a quarterback solution to keep their own playoff hopes on life support. The path to a wildcard berth is more open in the AFC, but extremely unlikely all the same.

Have the Ravens given up as a team? Are they simply hopeless without Jackson? Are the Carolina Panthers capable of resurrecting any team's lost pride and dignity with a surprisingly competitive game? A disciplined Panthers squad that is getting healthier are catching the ailing Ravens at the perfect time. Whether or not that is what we see on Sunday is anybody's guess.
