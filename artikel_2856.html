Roman Wilson full participant, Nick Herbig DNP in Steelers’ latest injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Steelers had a mix of good and bad news on their Wednesday injury report ahead of a Week 11 clash with the Eagles in Philadelphia.

Wednesday was headlined by the Steelers opening the 21-day return windows for wide receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson as well as rookie guard Gennings Dunker. All three participated in practice although still not activated to the 53-man roster. Wilson (hand) and Dunker (knee) practiced in full, while Pittman (foot) was limited.

New names on the injury report included linebacker Patrick Queen (limited; hand) and outside linebacker Nick Herbig, who was a non-participant with a hip/ribs designation. He was seen leaving the field in pain in the Steelers' loss to the Bengals in Week 10. Safety DeSon Elliott (knee) was limited Wednesday after being ruled out just before the Steelers' Bengals matchup.

Veteran defensive back Jalen Ramsey did not participate Wednesday with a non-injury related/personal designation, while tight end Darnell Washington, outside linebacker T.J. Watt, and defensive lineman Cameron Heyward were also non-participants with rest days.

Starting left guard Mason McCormick, who suffered an ankle injury against the Bengals, was not featured on the practice report after being placed on injured reserve earlier in the day.

The Steelers' Week 11 opponent, the Eagles, have yet to release their Wednesday injury report at the time of this writing.
