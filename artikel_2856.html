Eagles injury update: Zack Baun misses 2nd straight practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles injury update: Zack Baun misses 2nd straight practice originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Eagles linebacker Zack Baun (back) missed his second straight practice and his availability against the Steelers in Week 11 is now in question.

Baun and fellow linebacker Smael Mondon Jr. (ankle) were the only Eagles players not on the field at the start of Thursday's practice.

DNP: LB Zack Baun (back), LB Smael Mondon (ankle)

LIMITED: DB Michael Carter (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee), CB Jonathan Jones (hamstring), RB Will Shipley (foot), OLB Nolan Smith (illness), WR Dontayvion Wicks (ankle)

FULL: RB Saquon Barkley (hamstring), LB Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), OLB Jonathan Greenard (ankle), OLB Jalyx Hunt (ankle), QB Tanner McKee (rib), S Drew Mukuba (knee), WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring)

If Baun can't play on Sunday, the Eagles would start Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr., who had been rotating at the spot next to Baun. Campbell is coming off a couple injuries himself but has practiced the first two days of this week.

DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) each missed the three games leading into the Eagles' Week 10 bye, but are on track to play against the Steelers. That should be a big boost for Joe Milton, who will make his second-career start.

Will Shipley (foot) continues to practice this week after getting his practice window opened coming back from the bye. Shipley, who was limited to begin the week, has been on IR since Oct. 16.

Every other limited player from Wednesday — DB Michael Carter II (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee), CB Jonathan Jones (hamstring), OLB Nolan Smith (illness) — was practicing again on Thursday.
