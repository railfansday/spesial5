'I could care less about those guys': Kirk Ferentz dismisses College Football Playoff rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The College Football Playoff selection committee has been the most unpopular group in sports since the reveal of this season's first ranking on Nov. 3. After ESPN's CFP rankings show this week, Joel Klatt compared it to "an Orwell novel," and Danny Kanell called it all a "ridiculous sham."

Iowa Hawkeyes head coach Kirk Ferentz took a different approach: Playing hard to get.

On Saturday, the No. 15 Hawkeyes (9-2) defeated Illinois (6-5) by a dominant score of 34-17, notching their fifth consecutive win. Afterward, Ferentz took the opportunity to dunk on the College Football Playoff selection committee.

First, Big Ten Network sideline reporter Brooke Fletcher asked Ferentz what he hoped the Hawkeyes' statement win "shows the College Football Playoff committee."

Ferentz chuckled, shook his head, and said, "I swear to God, I could care less about those guys. They're gonna do what they want to do, and I really don't care. Our goal is to come in here and win. It's not easy winning in college football."

A little while later, Ferentz infused his opening remarks at his postgame press conference with a heavy dose of sarcasm.

"Just really proud of our team," Ferentz said. "Anytime you go on the road in this conference, it's not easy. We all knew that coming in here. Illinois played really hard and played well, and we were having a hard time matching their tempo there at the start.

"Pleased with the way our guys responded. That second 30 minutes was a lot better. Last thing, just real quick: Hopefully, we get a little points here. You know, we got asked about style points and things like that. So we called for the pick-six there at the end. Trying to get a little zip on that thing. Maybe get noticed by the committee. But we'll see happens."

Meanwhile, the likes of ESPN's Adam Rittenberg and College Football Happy Hour co-host Lucy Rohden, also a die-hard Iowa fan, were more direct in advocating for Iowa to reach the College Football Playoff.

"Iowa wins its fourth straight and has outscored those opponents 116-34," Rittenberg wrote on X. "CFP committee can't keep leaving this team in place."

Rohden reposted that and separately tweeted, "PUT IOWA IN THE PLAYOFF YOU COWARDS."

Theoretically, since there doesn't seem to be a method to the CFP committee's madness, Iowa should jump up in next week's rankings, given the chaotic results across the Big Ten on Saturday.

No. 4 UCLA (10-1) lost to Michigan in triple overtime, No. 8 Indiana (9-2) lost to Washington in overtime, and No. 14 Ohio State (7-4) fell to Nebraska, while No. 13 Oregon and No. 18 USC handled Michigan State and Maryland, respectively, with ease.

The Hawkeyes will host Nebraska (7-4) in their regular-season finale next Friday, Nov. 27.
