Steelers-Bengals Week 10 Game Will Snap Five-Decade Streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Steelers-Bengals game on Sunday Night Football in Week 10 will mark the first non-September primetime AFC North matchup between two teams with .750+ win percentages since 1975, according to Pro Grid Sports. The last time it happened was in 1975, between the Steelers and the Houston Oilers in the old AFC Central.

While the Steelers had a bye in Week 9, the Bengals beat the Atlanta Falcons, 34-17, on Sunday in Madrid. Cincinnati's offense has been red-hot lately. Quarterback Joe Burrow, who has a 23-6 touchdown-interception ratio this season, has thrown three or more touchdowns in each of the last four games.

The Steelers' defense has been playing lights out lately, too, however. The defense has allowed just 16 points in the last two games.

The Bengals' win over the Falcons moves them to 6-2 overall and in a first-place tie with the Steelers, who hold the tiebreaker thanks to their head-to-head win over the Bengals in Week 3.

Defensive tackle Derrick Harmon strip-sacked Burrow late in the game to seal a 30-27 win. Sebastian Joseph-Day came up with the recovery.

The Bengals felt like they let one slip away, as they had two critical special teams errors in addition to the Burrow fumble.

Ke'Shawn Williams, who used to be with the Steelers, made a massive mistake against his former team, and it cost him his job.

The Bengals released Williams on Tuesday, Sept. 29, after a crucial muffed punt. Williams' mishap in the fourth quarter allowed the Steelers to tie the game at 27-27 before Pittsburgh kicked another field goal to win, 30-27.

Bengals defensive end Cedric Johnson also had a roughing the punter penalty on Cam Johnston that extended a Steelers' drive in the third quarter. The Steelers ended up scoring a touchdown on the possession.

The Steelers will be playing for first place for the second game in a row. Before their Week 9 bye, the 5-2 Steelers played the 5-2 Cleveland Browns at Acrisure Stadium in Week 8, with the Steelers' 27-6 win putting them alone at the top of the division.

The Steelers and Bengals have split their last two season series, with the Steelers earning the last sweep in the rivalry in 2023. The Steelers last won in Cincinnati in 2024.
