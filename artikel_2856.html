Ravens snap counts vs. Chargers: Breakdown, analysis from 23-20 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jacoby Brissett threw for 340 yards in his Ravens debut, but Baltimore lost 23-20 Monday night to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers, falling to 4-6. Zay Flowers' fumble after a 15-yard reception ended Baltimore's final possession with 2:03 remaining. Tarheeb Still forced the turnover, and Elijah Molden recovered before Los Angeles ran out the clock. Brissett completed 33 of 49 passes with one interception. Mark Andrews caught eight passes for 82 yards, both season highs, and Mike Green recorded two sacks. But Baltimore managed just 16 rushing yards while allowing 160. Derrick Henry scored both Ravens touchdowns but finished with 10 yards on 10 carries. His fourth-quarter score brought Baltimore within three before Flowers' turnover extinguished the comeback.

Defensive lineman Nnamdi Madubuike was ruled out with a hamstring injury near the end of the third quarter; his availability remains unresolved. He entered the game having played 73% of Baltimore's defensive snaps.

Baltimore visits Carolina on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET. Brissett's production offered encouragement, but the Ravens head into the short week needing to restore their running game and run defense to stop their first five-game skid since 2021.

Brissett handled every offensive snap in his Baltimore debut, completing 33 of 49 passes. His arrival produced a more productive passing game, but the Ravens remained short on balance, finishing with just 16 rushing yards.

Henry played only five more snaps than Hill as Baltimore leaned heavily on its passing game. Henry scored twice but managed 10 yards on 10 carries. Both backs caught at least five passes, giving Brissett underneath options when the running game stalled.

Bateman and Flowers dominated playing time, with Lane clearly occupying the third receiver role. Lane's opportunities extended beyond his snap count: He led Baltimore with 11 targets and finished with three catches for 61 yards.

Moore, Walker and Wester combined for 10 offensive snaps, leaving little work outside the top three receivers.

Andrews led the position in snaps and delivered eight receptions for 82 yards, catching every pass thrown his way. Vannett played 14 snaps after being elevated from the practice squad.

The biggest change came at right guard. Ioane exited with a shoulder injury, and Edoga replaced him. Coach Jesse Minter did not have an immediate postgame update.

Simpson, Rosengarten, Jurgens and Stanley never left the field. Vinson's nine snaps added another lineman to the offensive personnel mix.

Green led the edge rotation, playing four more snaps than Hendrickson. Young and Burke supplied the remaining rotational work.

Jones carried the heaviest workload up front. Madubuike's night ended with a hamstring injury, another concern for a defense that allowed 160 rushing yards.

Smith was Baltimore's only defender to play every snap. Simpson remained the primary second inside linebacker, while Buchanan had a limited defensive role.

Hamilton, Starks and Wiggins rarely came off the field. Martin and Awuzie handled the largest reserve workloads in the secondary.

The immediate personnel questions are on the lines. Ioane's and Madubuike's health could affect Baltimore's preparation for Carolina, while Lane's workload establishes him as a significant part of the passing attack with Brissett at quarterback.
