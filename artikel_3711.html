How Xbox’s Streaming Deal For GTA 6 Could Expand Its Marketshare By 30%

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Microsoft and Take-Two recently announced a deal to allow people to stream Rockstar's GTA 6, and that significantly increases the game's addressable market on Xbox and means people can play the game for as little as $92.

This is according to a newly issued report from Ampere Analysis, which said the streaming deal could reach the estimated 10 million "active" Xbox One consoles out there and expand the total addressable market for GTA 6 on Xbox by 30%.

For anyone just catching up, Microsoft worked out a deal with Take-Two to allow GTA 6 to be streamed to an Xbox console--that could be an Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, or even an Xbox One.

There are some key things to know, including how the game streams from an Xbox Series S version of the game and your streaming experience will depend on the strength and consistency of your connection. A report from Digital Foundry said the Xbox Series S edition of GTA 6 struggles with its frame rate at some points, but that might not be an issue for everyone.

No such arrangement is in place for PlayStation, so the only way to stream GTA 6, at least right now, is on Xbox. Ampere said there are about 25 million PS4 consoles in the market that are still active, while Sony is the market leader for current-generation consoles with the PS5.

Why do a deal with Microsoft and not Sony? Ampere said Take-Two was likely interested in the potential expanded reach with Xbox to help sell more units, but there might have also been a "direct payment" or a revenue-share agreement on Xbox that was more preferential to Take-Two. Ampere said Take-Two wants the PS4 audience as well via streaming, but that might not happen until 2027 at the soonest, if such a deal ever materializes at all.

Beyond this, Ampere said there could be "technology-based reasons" why Sony didn't get a deal done for GTA 6 streaming. The research firm pointed out how Microsoft is a "hyperscaler" when it comes to streaming via its vast Azure network, which "puts it in a stronger position to serve a large influx of users streaming a brand-new game like GTA 6 compared to Sony." 

"The favorable distribution costs to Microsoft as the service provider and owner of the cloud infrastructure also gives it a commercial advantage," Ampere said. "While PS5 users can generally stream their owned games as long as they have an active PS Plus Premium subscription, PS4 users don’t have that functionality. Essentially, Sony is not set up to offer its PS4 users the same streaming experience."

Beyond all this, Ampere highlighted how PS5 users ($731) are spending more than PS4 users ($580) over the same lifecycle stage of each console. "Converting users to PS5 is more important to Sony than adding some sales of GTA 6 or PS4 users," Ampere said.

Under $100 to play GTA 6 on Xbox

Xbox's streaming deal for GTA 6 also makes Xbox the most affordable place to play GTA 6. Ampere said the total cost to play on Xbox could be $92, assuming the player already has an Xbox One, a copy of GTA 6, a controller, and a screen.

Ampere did the math by estimating the cost to be 0.24 cents per hour to stream the game, for a total of $92 via streaming, assuming a 50-hour runtime for the game.

This rate leaves little margin for Xbox, Ampere said, but the fact that people have to buy streaming time up front means there will no doubt be "a significant amount of playtime languishing in accounts that is never used."

As announced previously, people can stream GTA 6 to their Xbox console via a Game Pass membership or by purchasing time without a Game Pass membership. The pricing is below.

Cloud playtime packs pricing:

  • 10 hours for $4
  • 25 hours for $8
  • 50 hours for $12

Coinciding with GTA 6's release and the new streaming deal, Microsoft just instituted limits on how many hours members can stream before being asked to pay more. These are:

  • Game Pass Ultimate: 15 hours
  • Game Pass Premium: 10 hours
  • Game Pass Essential: 5 hours

Even beyond the streaming deal, Xbox is the most affordable place to play GTA 6 considering that a new Xbox Series S console costs $500, compared to $600 for the cheapest new PS5.
