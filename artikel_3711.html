NEOWIZ signs publishing partnership with Winterkeep Interactive for fantasy 4X strategy game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

NEOWIZ has signed a publishing partnership with Sweden-based studio Winterkeep Interactive for a new fantasy 4X strategy game described as a “new premium intellectual property,” the companies announced.

Winterkeep Interactive is led by Mattias Wiking, Petter Nallo, Karl Emdin, Frida Eriksson and Ola Enberg, and comprised of Paradox Interactive veterans whose previous credits include titles such as Stellaris, Crusader Kings II, Europa Universalis IV, Victoria 3, and other games.

“As we explore different gameplay experiences across PC and console, our partnership with Winterkeep Interactive is a pivotal investment as we widen our publishing portfolio,” said NEOWIZ co-CEO Jason Park in a press release. “With the team’s history of developing some of the most beloved and best-in-class strategy games and the genre’s very dedicated fan base, we feel very confident in the team’s ability to craft the next great strategy game.”

Winterkeep Interactive CEO Mattias Wiking added, “We chose NEOWIZ because we know how strongly they support their development partners in fulfilling the vision of their games. Our studio is focused on building a strategy experience that offers deep gameplay with a ton of replayability, and we are grateful to have a publishing partner that shares the same values we do in building ambitious new games for global audiences. We look forward to sharing more about the game in the near future.”
