In an effort to better understand how human neurons behave in situ, researchers are increasingly turning to samples of brain cells taken from people undergoing surgery.

Neuroscientists around the world are working with surgeons to access the tissue and keep it alive in their labs, allowing them to investigate the intrinsic electrical properties of cells and how they communicate with each other during both health and disease.
