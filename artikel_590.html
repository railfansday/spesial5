Melissa Gilbert Expresses “Undying Gratitude” After Timothy Busfield’s Child Sex Abuse Case Dismissed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

"This Thanksgiving I’m incredibly grateful for all those who came running toward us this year," she wrote in a lengthy Instagram post.

Melissa Gilbert took to her Instagram on Thanksgiving to express her “undying gratitude” after husband Timothy Busfield’s child sex abuse case was dismissed.

“This Thanksgiving I’m incredibly grateful for all those who came running toward us this year,” she wrote.“To those who checked in, took care of our home and animals when we were away, came to hearings, fed us, housed us, spoke up, testified, wrote letters, listened, comforted us, held our hands and checked in regularly, we love you.”

“Our undying gratitude also, to our extraordinary team who bravely fought and continue to fight for so hard for what is right and true. On this day, as we all acknowledge the blessings in our lives, we send our love and eternal gratitude to each and every one of you,” Gilbert added, who has been married to Busfield since 2013. Last month, a judge overseeing the proceedings tossed out Busfield’s case in Albuquerque, New Mexico, accusing prosecutors of failing to “act in a fair and impartial manner” and of violating grand jury statutes that are “designed to protect the structural integrity of our grand jury system.”

He had been accused of child sex abuse on the set of the former Fox/Warner Bros. Television series The Cleaning Lady, episodes of which he directed between 2022 and 2024.

Throughout the proceedings, Gilbert defended Busfield, who denied the allegations. She told ABC’s George Stephanopoulos in April 2026, “For Tim, it’s done. He’s canceled. Even if he’s exonerated, he will always be that guy, the last person in the world who would hurt a child. And believe me, if I thought for a second that Tim Busfield hurt a child, he’d have a lot more to worry about than prison.”
