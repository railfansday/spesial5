Every year has a Prime Day—some years even have two Prime Days!—where we celebrate the life and achievements of the greatest leader the Autobots have ever known: the inimitable Optimus Prime. But just as every dawn is twinned by a dusk that steals its light, so too did Optimus Prime’s bright spark fade, extinguished by the foul hand of Megatron during the Great Decepticon Incursion of 2005.

It is a day that lives in infamy forever, and we all know the tale—how the evil Megatron (cursed be the flow of his energon!) commandeered an Autobot shuttle, cruelly murdered its crew, and launched a cowardly surprise attack against Autobot City—a plan utterly bereft of honor, as was so typical of Megatron’s schemes. Even as individual Autobots strove to secure Autobot City—heroes, all of them!—hope began to fade under an onslaught that included an attack by the fearsome Devastator, against which there appeared to be no defense.

And then, at the precipice of disaster, Optimus Prime made the fateful declaration whose words still ring out to this day: “Megatron must be stopped, no matter the cost.” The fearless Autobot transformed, rolled out, and got the job done—even though that cost was high. Many brave Autobots besides Optimus Prime lost their lives during the fighting. It was truly a dark day, a black day—and so, every year on the Friday after Thanksgiving, we pause to remember the noble sacrifices made by Prime and by so many others. This is why today is known as Black Friday.

And how do we remember those Autobots who joined the Allspark so that we could live in freedom? Why, we celebrate Black Friday through commerce and buying things—just like Optimus Prime would have wanted! Below is a list of the finest deals we could locate, in a broad range of categories. If the darkness of the Black Friday story threatens to overwhelm your soul, fear not. Optimus Prime was succeeded by Rodimus Prime, who defeated the darkness and brought hope back to all Cybertron. (We don’t talk about the brief Ultra Magnus-led Prime Interregnal Period.) Even Optimus Prime himself wouldn’t stay dead for very long—his own glorious and perhaps narratively inevitable resurrection would occur not that long after. Indeed, we will be celebrating those grand events in just a couple of days, on Cybertron Monday (or just “Cyber Monday” for short), with more shopping!
