Four UK men convicted of sexual assault and rape of woman at invitation of husband

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former Tory councillor advertised ex-wife on swinging websites and drugged and bound her for sex

Four men have been convicted of sexually assaulting and raping a woman at the invitation of her husband who had drugged and bound her.

Philip Young, 50, advertised his then wife, Joanne Young, on dating and swinging websites, presenting it as a “kink” for both of them, Winchester crown court heard. In fact, he had been sedating her, plunging her into an “inanimate state” where anything could be done to her by whoever answered the adverts, jurors were told.

The former Conservative councillor pleaded guilty earlier in the year to dozens of charges of abuse against his former wife, who has waived her right to anonymity.

Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32; Richard Wilkins, 62; Norman Macksoni, 48; and Mohammed Hassan, 38, were convicted of various offences against Joanne Young after a three-week trial.

Sanderson-Doyle, a Ministry of Defence contractor from Swindon, was convicted of sexual assault and assault by penetration; Wilkins, a taxi driver from Swindon, was found guilty of assault by penetration and rape; Macksoni, a former DJ and waste management worker from Bedfordshire, was convicted of rape; and Hassan, also from Swindon, was found guilty of sexual assault.

A further defendant, Dean Hamilton, 47, from Somerset, has admitted one charge of assault by penetration, two of sexual assault and one of rape.

The case has drawn comparisons to that of Gisèle Pelicot, who was drugged by her husband and raped by dozens of strangers in France. It can now be revealed that Wiltshire police received advice from the Pelicot investigators on how to approach their inquiries.

The men, including Young, will be sentenced together at a later date.
