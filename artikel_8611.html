Get the Platinum Credit Card if you’re a Navy Federal member who carries a balance or wants to move one. It’s the credit union’s lowest-rate card, with a variable rate of 10.49% to 18.00% and no transfer fee. If you pay in full every month, pick a Navy Federal cash back card, since this one earns no rewards.

Only Navy Federal members can apply, and membership is open to service members, veterans, Defense Department civilians, and their families. A $5,000 transfer at the 0.99% introductory rate, repaid in 12 equal monthly payments, costs about $27 in interest, against the $150 a 3% transfer fee would cost on a 0% balance transfer card.

Members who carry a balance get Navy Federal’s lowest card rates here, and those moving one pay no transfer fee. Members who pay in full every month never pay interest, and for them the no-fee cashRewards card pays 1.5% or 2% cash back, depending on the credit limit Navy Federal sets.
