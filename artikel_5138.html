Aave (AAVE), a leading decentralized lending protocol, is currently trading within a defined range as both network fundamentals and price structure move into a stabilization phase. As November goes on, the answer may depend on whether Aave can translate stable usage into renewed capital inflows and reclaim higher levels. Read on as we break down Aave’s price prediction for November and the broader 2026 outlook.

Aave is approaching December with a bearish technical structure after falling from its October peak near $175 to approximately $95. The token has broken below the important $120–$125 support zone, which now acts as immediate resistance. AAVE is also trading below its major moving averages, indicating that sellers remain in control. The next significant demand area sits around $70–$75. If buyers defend this region, a recovery toward $110–$125 could develop. However, a sustained move above $125 would be needed to improve the short-term outlook, with stronger resistance near $170–$180.

Fundamentally, Aave continues to show substantial lending activity. Its V4 deposits reportedly reached $1.75 billion in November, while V3 deposits remained around $31 billion. However, weaker third-quarter revenue highlights the challenge of translating protocol growth into stronger financial performance. December’s outlook therefore remains cautious, with $70–$125 as the key trading range to watch.

Looking ahead, Aave’s 2026 trajectory will be closely tied to the broader evolution of DeFi lending and liquidity conditions across the market.

From a macro standpoint, AAVE appears to be in a reaccumulation phase, with price consolidating despite steady protocol usage. This divergence suggests that while fundamentals remain intact, capital inflows have yet to fully return.

In a bullish scenario, where DeFi activity accelerates and borrowing demand increases, AAVE could move toward the $120 to $150 range, supported by renewed liquidity and ecosystem expansion. A base-case outlook assumes a gradual recovery in DeFi participation, positioning AAVE within the $150 to $200 range over the year. In a more conservative scenario, where market conditions remain subdued, AAVE may continue trading below $100, delaying a breakout despite stable fundamentals.
