XRP's weekly momentum remains weak, with $0.84 emerging as critical trendline support; reclaiming $1.12 could strengthen the recovery.

XRP Ledger active users surge, while futures open interest drops, highlighting a growing disconnect between network activity and market participation.

The broader crypto market is still struggling to find its footing after the recent sell-off, leaving investors cautious about what comes next. Bitcoin’s attempts to recover have yet to bring much confidence back into the altcoin market, and XRP is no exception. The token has managed to climb back above $1 after slipping below $0.90, offering some relief to buyers. However, the recovery is still fragile, particularly as the broader market continues to face selling pressure.

Interestingly, XRP’s price isn’t the only thing worth watching right now. Activity on the XRP Ledger has been picking up even as the token struggles to build a sustained recovery. This contrast adds another dimension to the current market setup. Is XRP simply caught in the broader market weakness, or are there signs of a shift beneath the surface? Its price action over the coming sessions could offer some clues.

XRP’s weekly chart continues to favour the bears, with the token struggling to recover after its sharp decline from the $3.50 peak in 2025. The recent selling has pushed the price back towards $1, and buyers are yet to show enough strength to turn the trend around. Although XRP has managed to stay close to this psychological level, the broader chart suggests that the recovery remains uncertain.

The weekly RSI is at 36.05, sitting below its moving average of 48.43, while the CMF remains negative at -0.12. Both readings point to continued weakness, with buying pressure yet to make a meaningful comeback. The RSI is approaching oversold territory, but it hasn’t dropped below 30, leaving room for further weakness.

One level that could become particularly important is the ascending trendline near $0.84. It has supported XRP through several major corrections since 2022, making it an important level for buyers to defend. If the price holds above this trendline, it could give bulls some breathing room to attempt a recovery.

However, a weekly close below it would weaken the longer-term structure and bring the $0.55 region back into focus. For now, reclaiming $1 is the immediate challenge, followed by the $1.10 to $1.20 resistance zone. Until XRP clears these levels, any recovery attempt could face seller resistance.

XRP is showing two very different trends beneath the surface. While participation across the derivatives market has dropped considerably, activity on the XRP Ledger has been moving higher. This contrast is particularly interesting given that XRP’s price continues to struggle near $1.

Data from CoinGlass shows that XRP futures open interest fell from about $3.4 billion on October 8 to around $1.9 billion by November 18. That’s a contraction of roughly 44% in just over a month. The decline suggests that traders have been reducing their exposure, with a significant amount of leveraged positioning being unwound during the recent price weakness. The picture looks quite different on the network side.

According to Token Terminal, daily active users on the XRP Ledger have climbed to 48,400 as of November 18. The metric largely fluctuated between 15,000 and 20,000 during August and September before picking up through October and accelerating further in November. This suggests that engagement across the network has increased even as XRP’s market performance has weakened.

The divergence raises an interesting question about what is driving the increase in network activity and whether it can eventually translate into stronger demand for XRP. For now, the two metrics point to a market where network engagement is improving, but speculative interest remains subdued.

XRP’s recovery is still facing an important test. The token needs to hold above key support levels and attract stronger buying interest if it is to move out of its current downtrend. For now, the $0.84 trendline remains an important level to watch, while a move above 1.10–1.20 could give buyers some confidence that the recovery is gaining strength.

There are also some interesting developments beneath the surface. While futures traders have been pulling back, activity on the XRP Ledger has picked up considerably. Whether this growing network engagement will eventually translate into stronger demand for XRP remains to be seen. Until buyers return in greater numbers and the price starts reclaiming important resistance levels, a sustained recovery may remain difficult. For the XRP price, the next move will depend on whether improving network activity can finally find support in the market.
