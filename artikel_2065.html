25-year-old must come straight into the Derby County XI amid positive injury update: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Derby County have had a tough season to date, but they entered the international break on a high by beating Bolton Wanderers 3-1 at Pride Park. It gives DCFC fans some renewed hope, and might have saved John Eustace his job as well.

The Rams have been way under par, but injuries have torn through the squad and played a big part in their struggles. Eustace’s defence has been heavily impacted especially, and among those sidelined has been Eiran Cashin.

But, in a welcome boost, the Brighton & Hove Albion loanee has been back in training over the first week of the international break. Cashin may need a little more time before he gets back to full fitness, but once deemed ready, he should come straight back into their starting lineup.

Cashin was a popular signing in the summer transfer window. The 25-year-old is a product of the Derby County academy and left to join Brighton in January 2025, but has struggled to make the grade at the Premier League club and spent spells on loan back in the Championship as a result.

He didn’t impress as hoped at Birmingham City, but offered a timely reminder of how good he can be at the level during his stay at Blackburn Rovers. Cashin helped the Lancashire outfit steer clear of relegation last season before reuniting with the Rams for the 2026/27 campaign.

The Irishman has only played twice for DCFC since his return due to this calf injury, but he will be backed to land a key role when fit and available. He’s a dependable and busy defender at this level, with his 3.26 tackles per 90 last season more than any other Championship defender.

Cashin also managed 12.96 defensive contributions, 9.33 duels won, and 14.94 line-breaking passes per 90, ranking among the league’s best in these metrics too (FotMob). He’ll offer Eustace an option at left-back and centre-back, and give the boss the option to switch to a back three as well.

He will bring more balance to the backline as a left-footer and has the love for defending that has been coached out of some modern-day centre-backs.

The Bolton Wanderers win was only one victory, but it will hopefully mark a big step in the right direction. It lifts a weight off the shoulders of Eustace and his Derby County players coming into the break.

This paired with the improving injury picture should give DCFC hope that they can start to find some form. Not only is Cashin back in training, but Patrick Agyemang and Matt Clarke featured in the behind-closed-doors friendly at Everton on Thursday as well.

With defensive depth and key players returning, the Rams will be targeting some valuable points to push up the Championship table and away from the relegation zone.
