The 3 summer targets West Ham should pursue again in January, including a new Arne Engels midfield partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

West Ham currently sit 9th in the Championship table following the sacking of Nuno Espirito Santo and hiring of Scott Parker. WHUFC will hope the three-time promotion-winning boss can deliver the same success after his spells at Burnley, Bournemouth and Fulham.

However, the squad also needs refreshing when the January transfer window comes around. The defence has been heavily criticised and is sure to be an area targeted for reinforcements by the Hammers.

New targets will be on the radar, but West Ham identified some smart options in the summer who could be available this winter. Here, we put forward three summer transfer targets who should be back on their radar ahead of January…

For all of West Ham’s attacking success this season, their defence has been a concern. Konstantinos Mavropanos has chipped in with five goals as a set-piece threat, but defensively, he and Max Kilman have left a lot to be desired.

Mavropanos is the subject of persisting interest from the likes of Besiktas. A new centre-back may be needed regardless of what comes of the Greek defender’s situation, and Axel Disasi should be on the radar.

Disasi has endured a tough start to life at Crystal Palace and rumours are circulating about an early departure from the Premier League. Should he do so, a return to WHUFC could benefit all parties.

West Ham have endured uncertainty in midfield for a while, with the futures of Edson Alvarez and Soungoutou Magassa up in the air. Arne Engels has been the only regular in the midfield, but he needs a partner who can offer a powerful presence alongside him.

Nelson Deossa was targeted by WHUFC in the summer, and he can be the answer to their midfield problems. The Colombian has played sixteen matches so far for Real Betis this season and is under contract until 2030.

Tomas Soucek has served as a key physical asset in midfield, but a long-term heir for his role is needed. Deossa would bring strength, power and work rate, as well as a well-rounded game in possession with regard to his passing and ball carrying.

A January deal could be tough given Deossa’s calibre and the Hammers’ status in the Championship, but it would be an ambitious and exciting move for the club to make.

One of West ham’s defensive issues this season has been at left-back. Nuno favoured Morato in that position while Ollie Scarles has had opportunities and Ezra Mayers is breaking through, but no one has really made that spot their own.

One man who could be an immediate fix is Matt Targett, who starred in the Championship with Middlesbrough last season. The Englishman’s move to Hull City has not worked out and a winter change of scenery could be just what he needs.

WHUFC could land a quick fix at left-back with Targett while youngsters Mayers and Scarles find opportunities as backup and competition.
