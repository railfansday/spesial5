Celtic boss Martin O'Neill responds to Frank McAvennie's Auston Trusty remarks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Celtic manager Martin O'Neill has criticised comments made by former Parkhead forward Frank McAvennie about Auston Trusty following the sad passing of his father.

McAvennie questioned the amount of time the American had taken off to celebrate the life of his dad, before apologising via a statement on X.

Speaking ahead of Celtic's Europa League clash with Besiktas, O'Neill responded to McAvennie's initial remarks.

The 74-year-old explained that Trusty had requested leave and that he is with the defender 'the whole way'.

"That's really poor taste," O'Neill said of McAvennie's comments. "I know I've said some things and ended up backtracking. But that's a family bereavement.

"And that's making someone's mind up about how long they should be grieving and stuff like this here. That's not on.

"Trusty asked me for some time [off]. Even if it's a celebration [of his late father’s life], it doesn't matter. The father's been in poor health for quite some considerable time.

"And I know there was a stage where he thought that he wasn't going to see it through. But I'm with Trusty the whole way there. Because I wouldn't want anybody to tell me that I had to grieve over a brother or sister dying. And I've had that.

"He's 28 years of age. And he's taken it badly. Honestly. I've afforded him all the time he wants.

"At some stage, obviously, he'll be coming back. But at the end of the day, I've given that time because I know that we might be talking about different eras or whatever the case may be. That's nothing to do with it. This is a family thing. And that's not right.

"So, Trusty has got the time and he's got the backing of myself and he's got the backing of the club."
