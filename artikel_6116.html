Airbnb Opens Its API to Business Travel, Starting With Perk: Exclusive

Airbnb has courted business travelers for a decade, but corporate bookings mostly happened off to the side, through referral deals and workarounds. Letting Perk tap its API puts live Airbnb listings inside a corporate booking tool for what Perk says is the first time. Rival travel management companies are undoubtedly on the way.

Perk, a business travel and spend management platform, will announce Tuesday it’s the first travel management company to integrate Airbnb’s API for business travel.

Employees of Perk clients can now search Airbnb listings with live pricing and availability, book them within company travel policy, and send them for approval alongside flights, cars and rail, without leaving Perk.

Until now, Perk customers who wanted an Airbnb had to go through Perk’s concierge service, pasting a listing link and dates, and then waiting for staff to book it. Even with that manual process, Perk generated Airbnb bookings in the “double-digit millions” last year, Kristina Carter, Perk’s vice president of global partnerships, told Skift. This year it has already surpassed that total, she said.

Founded in 2015, Perk, formerly known as TravelPerk, has headquarters in London and Boston.

The integration follows Airbnb's exclusive B2B short-term rental deal with American Express Travel, and comes after CEO Brian Chesky told investors on the third-quarter earnings call that partnerships are one of Airbnb's biggest untapped growth opportunities. Perk's data shows Airbnb filling a different need than hotels: Airbnb nights booked through Perk rose 94% over two years, stays average nine nights, and 78% of bookings since January 2024 have been for entire homes or apartments.
