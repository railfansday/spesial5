Airbnb disclosed in its quarterly SEC filing that it sold intellectual property to a private AI lab founded and controlled by CEO Brian Chesky, with CTO Ahmad Al-Dahle holding a stake and both executives working for the entity. The November transaction brought Airbnb $7.1 million in cash covering the IP and expense reimbursements combined, and a special committee of independent, disinterested directors approved it under the company's related-party transaction policy. In return, Airbnb received a license to certain lab IP for internal use and the right to invest in future equity rounds, though the filing does not identify the IP, explain its valuation, separate the purchase price from expenses, or disclose the license economics. Airbnb said the executives' roles at the lab will not change their responsibilities to the company. The arrangement lands as Airbnb prepares a proprietary AI travel agent for the first half of 2027, built to assemble maps, photos, comparisons, calendars, and itineraries around guest queries. Neither the filing nor the shareholder update says whether that agent draws on the lab's technology, or whether the lab could license the same IP to competitors such as Booking Holdings or Expedia.
