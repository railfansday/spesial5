Contour Adds New Short-Haul Route

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Contour Airlines is expanding its presence in the Southeast.

Starting March 1, 2027, the independent regional carrier will connect Macon, Georgia, and Nashville, Tennessee. Contour serves Macon via Middle Georgia Regional Airport.

Contour officials said there is strong demand for Nashville service from Macon.

“Listening to the communities we serve is an important part of what we do, and this new route is another way we can respond to what local travelers want and need,” Contour President Ben Munson said in a news release. “We look forward to providing more convenient travel options and connecting Macon to even more destinations.”

Contour currently connects Macon to Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and Baltimore, and Nashville to Tupelo, Mississippi, Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, and Quincy, Illinois.
