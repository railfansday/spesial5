Lufthansa Ditches Huge 509-Seat Airbus A380: Final 4 Routes Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lufthansa is one of the few airlines that continue to operate the Airbus A380. According to ch-aviation, the German flag carrier currently has a fleet of eight A380-800s and plans to keep the aircraft in service well into the 2030s.

The airline operates the Airbus double-deckers in two configurations, offering either 509 or 499 seats. However, as reported by AeroRoutes, it is planning to end operations with its 509-seat A380s next month.

Per aeroLOPA, Lufthansa's 509-seat A380 features eight first-class suites, 78 business-class seats arranged in a 2-2-2 configuration and based on the Collins Diamond Parallel platform, 53 premium economy seats and 371 economy seats. The Star Alliance member will operate its final flight using this configuration on December 17, on its daily service between Munich Airport (MUC) and Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

According to scheduling data from aviation analytics company Cirium, the German airline has deployed its 509-seat A380s on several routes this year, including services from Munich to Bangkok, Mumbai, Delhi, Denver, Washington Dulles, Boston and San Francisco. However, only four routes are scheduled to see this configuration during its remaining weeks of operation, as detailed in the table above.

This is because Lufthansa is gradually reconfiguring its 509-seat A380s to the 499-seat layout as part of a business-class cabin retrofit. The aircraft's existing business class product is more than a decade old and, given its 2-2-2 layout, only around 67% of passengers have direct aisle access.

The German flag carrier is replacing these seats with Thompson Aero Seating's Vantage XL product, an off-the-shelf business class seat arranged in a staggered 1-2-1 configuration. Notably, Lufthansa also uses the Vantage XL product on its former Philippine Airlines Airbus A350s.

However, these aircraft are scheduled to be transferred to Discover Airlines in 2027, leaving the A380 as the only aircraft type in the airline’s mainline fleet to feature these seats in the longer term. The new product will bring Lufthansa's A380 business class cabin up to date and provide every passenger with direct aisle access.

However, the retrofit will also reduce business class capacity by ten seats, from 78 to 68, bringing the aircraft's total capacity down to 499 seats. According to aeroLOPA, four of Lufthansa's eight A380s have already received the new business class product. The airline expects to complete the retrofit program across its entire A380 fleet by mid-2027.

The upgrades are carried out as part of a wider maintenance and modernization program.

Nevertheless, the A380 remains one of Lufthansa's main long-haul workhorses, and the airline continues to deploy the double-decker on several routes from Munich. Between December 2026 and October 2027, the Star Alliance member has scheduled more than 3,400 flights using the type.

For the remainder of the winter season, in addition to the routes mentioned above, it will deploy the double-decker on its service to Mumbai as well. The airline is set to bring back the quadjets on the route from January 11, 2027, operating five times a week until February 27. When the summer schedule begins on March 28, Lufthansa will increase this frequency to daily.

Lufthansa A380 deployments for summer 2027*

Overall, for summer 2027, the airline plans to operate the Airbus double-decker quadjets on seven routes, although deployment will vary depending on the destination. For instance, Lufthansa will bring the superjumbo back on its Munich–Denver route in May, replacing the Airbus A350-900 currently scheduled on the service.

Similarly, the A380 will return to San Francisco in August, although it will operate just three times a week. Lufthansa will continue to offer daily flights to the West Coast destination, but the remaining services will be operated by the A350-900.
