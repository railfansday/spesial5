Fuentesaúco Pharmacy / David Hernandez Arquitectura

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Read the original version in Spanish here.

Text description provided by the architects. The Fuentesaúco Pharmacy offers a reflection on the role of architecture in the recovery and modernization of essential services in rural areas. In a town of approximately 1,500 inhabitants, the project goes beyond renovating a commercial establishment to reaffirm the importance of maintaining nearby, accessible healthcare facilities that are connected to the everyday life of the community. In a context marked by population decline, architecture also becomes a tool for preserving quality of life and strengthening the community's identity.
