The 25 Sexiest Movies on Prime Video Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Including the best erotic thrillers, raunchy comedies, and all the mess in between.

Even in just the past decade, sexy cinema has come a long way from Fifty Shades of Grey. No judgement here—the entire trilogy is available to stream on Prime Video if you haven’t seen the Dakota Johnson and Jamie Dornan-starring franchise. But if you’re looking to expand your range a bit, we have a lot to recommend.

Sexy movies are having a comeback in 2026, thanks to Wuthering Heights, I Want Your Sex, and—another Olivia Wilde film—The Invite. While Emerald Fennell adapted Emily Bronte’s novel into a tortured and depraved sexual experience, Wilde’s I Want Your Sex and The Invite used sex more as a vehicle for dark comedy. But it’s not just new films on this list. We can also point you to Weekend, Ghost, Before Sunrise, and just any kind of sexy movie you’re looking for on screen.

Below, you’ll find our pick of the 25 sexiest movies you can stream on Prime Video right now.

Olivia Wilde and Cooper Hoffman star in Gregg Araki’s raunchy workplace romantic comedy about a high profile artist (Wilde) who ropes one of her new assistants (Hoffman) into a dominant/submissive relationship. At first, he enjoys the attention, but the rollercoaster of their sexual power dynamic eventually drives them both insane.

Sydney Sweeney does Rear Window meets Peeping Tom in this sexy crime drama about a couple who becomes obsessed with the sex lives of the people they can see from their apartment window across the street. The film also stars Justice Smith and Ahsoka’s Natasha Liu Bordizzo.

No Hard Feelings follows a down-on-her-luck Uber driver (Jennifer Lawrence) who is hired by a very wealthy couple to take their inexperienced nineteen-year-old son (Andrew Barth Feldman) on a date to boost his confidence before he leaves for college. The film treads dangerous waters, but Lawrence won over the audience and earned herself a Golden Globe nomination for the sexy comedy.

Love her or hate her, Emerald Fennell can make a steamy movie. Wuthering Heights is a little more of a reimagining than an adaptation, taking quite a few liberties with the text, but it puts the romantic and sexual dynamics right at the forefront of the story. And there’s no beating Margot Robbie and Jacob Elordi as the leads.

The Fault in Our Stars director Josh Boone teams up with It Ends With Us novelist Colleen Hoover for a romance drama about a teen pregnancy that alters the course of two lovers’ lives. That is, until later in life, when the couple (Allison Wiliams and Dave Franco) reunite to explore what could have been.

Emerald Fennell’s steamy The Talented Mr. Ripley–esque thriller, Saltburn, follows a young student at Oxford University who becomes infatuated (read: dangerously obsessed) with one of his aristocratic classmates, who invites him to his family’s grand estate. It’s a darkly funny and wildly intense ride.

The phenom that saved dark romance novels: Fifty Shades of Grey and the entire Fifty Shades trilogy is available to stream on Prime Video. The story, for those who still don’t know, follows a recent college graduate (Dakota Johnson) who signs a contract to take part in a sexual bondage relationship with billionaire Christian Gray (Jamie Dornan).

L’inconnu du lac (known as Stranger by the Lake) is one of the most acclaimed erotic thrillers of the 21st century, picking up Best Director and the Queer Palm awards in the year it premiered at the Cannes Film Festival. In Stranger by the Lake, Franck, a man in his 30s, meets Michel by a lake where gay men often bathe and immediately falls for him. What he doesn’t know, however, is that Michel is a psychopathic murderer. Stranger by the Lake is as smart as it is sexy, and it will truly keep you on your toes.

Who said rom-coms can’t be sexy too? Secretary charts the dominant-submissive relationship that blossoms between a lawyer and his secretary. It’s certainly on the crazier side and may not be for everyone, but Secretary is a brilliantly acted film that feels quite far ahead of its time for such a mainstream movie in its original take on a romantic BDSM relationship.

Carol does something a little different: It’s a slow-burning romance with sensuality bubbling beneath the surface. It follows a department-store worker and aspiring photographer whose life takes an unexpected turn when she falls in love with a mysterious, beautiful older woman going through a difficult divorce. Todd Haynes’s Oscar-nominated period romance is a masterpiece of modern queer cinema, with career-defining performances from Rooney Mara and Cate Blanchett. Hugely romantic and remarkably executed, Carol is a special film that’ll certainly stick with you.

Alongside Four Weddings and a Funeral, Sirens was one of the films that brought generational British heartthrob Hugh Grant to American audiences. Grant plays a progressive reverend who, with his wife, pays a visit to an eccentric artist whose work is viewed as blasphemous. His wife soon finds herself drawn to the three models working with the painter, and what ensues is a clever breakdown of 1930s sexual repression.

Everyone loves a meet-cute, and Weekend’s is as brilliant as they come. Russell thinks he’s signed up for a one-night stand with Glen, a man he meets in a club, but over the course of a single weekend, a deeper relationship quickly grows between the two. Touching, oddly realistic, and distinctly British in its sense of humor, Weekend is sexy, funny, and grounded in a way that few romantic comedy-dramas manage.

In Eternity, Elizabeth Olsen plays a woman who, upon reaching the afterlife, is forced to choose between two people to spend eternity with: her first husband, who died young, and her second husband of 65 years. Eternity is infectiously sweet, a little sexy, and an essential modern comedy.

It might be a stretch to call this one “sexy,” per se, but you won’t find a romantic drama quite like this one—and you definitely won’t find one that’s more intense. Luca Guadagnino’s Bones and All is a road-trip drama that follows a young woman who develops feelings for a disillusioned young drifter on a 3,000-mile journey across America. Oh, and they’re both cannibals.

Wong Kar-wai’s romantic masterpiece proves that a film can be truly romantic, sensual, and intense while still holding solidly on to a PG rating. In the Mood for Love follows a man and a woman who discover that their spouses are having an affair with one another, but as they bond, they begin to develop their own connection. This is one for the yearners.

Sexy films are by no means a new phenomenon, and Caligula is one of the originals. Though the original was pretty poorly received, the film recently received a restoration and rerelease that reconstructed the film entirely from footage that didn’t make the final cut.

Love triangles don’t get much more complicated than this, and sports dramas don’t get much sexier. Challengers follows two friends who fall in love with the same young tennis prodigy and the complicated chaos that ensues when their relationships get messy. Tennis has never looked so thrilling

Set in the dreamy Italian countryside, Call Me by Your Name remains the film that introduced Timothée Chalamet to the masses as a future leading man (and got him his first Oscar nomination). Here he plays a teenager who falls in love with his father’s assistant and comes to terms with his sexuality. The film is sad, symbolic, and deeply romantic.

The legendary leading duo of Patrick Swayze and Demi Moore speaks for itself. Ghost is maybe a little more on the sentimental side than some of the other “sexy films” in Prime Video’s lineup, but it’s a deeply moving one worth your time.

Portrait of a Lady on Fire was an immediate hit with critics and audiences when it was released in 2019. The film follows an artist commissioned to paint an aristocratic heiress, but things become more complicated when the two fall in love. Romantic films don’t get more French than this one, which is also loosely based on the timeless tale of Orpheus and Eurydice.

Nicole Kidman and Harris Dickinson are an absolutely electrifying onscreen pair in this erotic thriller that sees a CEO begin an affair with her power-hungry intern, putting the stability of her family life and career on the line. Kidman picked up the Best Actress award at the Venice Film Festival for her role, and it’s been called one of her most ambitious performances to date.

Can anything capture your heart like a meet-cute romance? American traveler Jesse and French student Céline unceremoniously meet on a train from Budapest to Vienna, deciding on a whim to spend his last hours on the continent together. It’s the first in Richard Linklater’s acclaimed Before trilogy, and it sets you up for a brilliant slow burn.

If a Skarsgård signed up for it, I’m interested. He’s about to play a love interest made out of wicker, but last year Alexander Skarsgård played the mysteriously sexy biker-gang leader Ray in Pillion. The romantic black comedy tells the story of a shy man who begins a dominant-submissive relationship with the aforementioned biker, and it traces his personal growth as he navigates their dynamic. Pillion is sexy, crazy, and downright hilarious. It’s British black comedy at its finest.

Starring the king of crazy, surprisingly sexy indie movies, James Spader himself, Sex, Lies and Videotape is a true cult classic. The film revolves around a married couple with intimacy problems whose lives are changed when a mysterious drifter with a peculiar interest comes to stay with them.

David Cronenberg’s notorious erotic thriller is perhaps the most transgressive entry on this list, following a group of people who become sexually fixated on car crashes. Adapted from J.G. Ballard’s novel and starring James Spader, Holly Hunter, and Deborah Kara Unger, Crash is disturbing, provocative, and about as daring as erotic cinema gets.
