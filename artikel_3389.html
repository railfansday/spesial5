Paramount's 13-Episode Post-Apocalyptic Fantasy Series Is So Good, It's Already Renewed For 3 More Seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Paramount+'s 13-episode post-apocalyptic fantasy series is so good, it's already been renewed for three more seasons — and the next installment arrives in just a few months. These days, there's an abundance of fantasy storytelling on the small screen, from epic, high fantasy sagas like The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power and George R.R. Martin's House of the Dragon to family-friendly hits like Disney's returning Percy Jackson and the Olympians series and HBO's upcoming Harry Potter TV remake. Between live-action and animated fantasy, there's never been more choice.

Just last month, Paramount revisited one of TV's most beloved animated fantasy masterpieces. Avatar: Seven Havens, a sequel to Nickelodeon's original Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra, returns to a world filled with benders — those who can manipulate, or "bend," one of the four primary elements: fire, air, water, and earth. The most powerful bender is the Avatar, the only one capable of harnessing all four elements. Though each can be watched on its own, both The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra influence Seven Havens.

Avatar: The Last Airbender is a wartime story following Avatar Aang and his friends as they save the world from the tyrannical, colonizing might of the Fire Nation. The Legend of Korra explores the Avatar's connection to the Spirit World. Meanwhile, Avatar: Seven Havens is a post-apocalyptic survival story, after a devastating global disaster known as the Cataclysm forced humanity to retreat into the seven titular havens. Though season 1 only finished on October 30, season 2 premieres in May 2027. Two more seasons of Seven Havens are confirmed, with the third also slated to arrive next year.

Set 225 years after The Legend of Korra, Seven Havens is far enough removed from its predecessors for the show and its leading Avatars, twins Pavi and Nisha, to cement their own legacy. It essentially functions as a soft reboot for the franchise. Though Korra's history is intertwined with the legend of the Cataclysm and directly affects Nisha and Pavi's reputation, the show's post-apocalyptic framework has wiped out most of the technological and spiritual progress made in The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra. This gives Seven Havens more freedom to play with the franchise's already established worldbuilding, and it does so with real emotional depth.

Pavi and Nisha's home, Ellora, faces a crisis. Fewer benders are born each year. Young children with abilities are conscripted into the bending brigade, often against their will. They're running out of resources. The city is largely cut off from the outside world; barren, dangerous wastelands and massive storms separate it from the other havens. There's a lot to handle, but the show balances everything beautifully, and it never feels like it's relying too heavily on what came before.

Amid all this drama and political intrigue, the show also explores the mystery of Pavi and Nisha's missing parents, what really happened to Avatar Korra, the cause of the Cataclysm, and why, for the very first time in history, there are two Avatars instead of one. The extent of the Avatar's spiritual powers is similarly intriguing, as a revelation from Nisha's past changes everything we thought we knew about their connection to the Spirit World. Avatar: Seven Havens is a meaningful story about overcoming fear in the face of overwhelming adversity. It's an expansion and a reinvention.

For reasons that won't be spoiled here, Pavi and Nisha go their separate ways in Avatar: Seven Havens' season 1 finale. Having unlocked her Root Chakra after facing her (and her sister's) greatest fear and revealing herself as the Avatar, Pavi has more work to do. The rest of Pavi's story will involve traveling to the remaining six havens to unlock her remaining six chakras. Nisha will stay in Ellora (for now), though the twins will surely be reunited again in the future.

Set centuries after The Legend of Korra, Avatar: Seven Havens introduces an interesting, powerful new group of benders — and two fledgling Avatars.

As such, it seems the rest of the show will combine familiar narrative elements from both Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra. Pavi will follow in Aang's footsteps. Whereas Aang traveled between the Earth Kingdom, the Water Tribes, and the Fire Nation to learn more about each bending style, Pavi will travel between the havens to learn more about her chakras. Nisha, on the other hand, will follow in Korra's footsteps, staying in one settlement — Ellora in place of Republic City — to train further with Captain Karthik. How they find their way back to each other remains to be seen. After all, Avatar: Seven Havens is far from over.

Avatar: Seven Havens season 2 premieres with three episodes on Friday, May 21, 2027, only on Paramount+.
