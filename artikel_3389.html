Tommy Replaced Himself In Landman But It Won’t Work

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tommy Norris (Billy Bob Thornton) started his own oil company, but replacing himself as President ultimately won't work in Landman season 3. Taylor Sheridan and Christian Wallace's hit Paramount+ boomtown oil drama closed out season 2 with Tommy taking his future into his own hands, founding the oddly named CTT Oil Exploration & Cattle company.

Landman's series premiere quickly established Tommy Norris as the most knowledgeable and competent landman in West Texas. When he worked for M-TEX Oil, Tommy spent nearly every waking hour of every day driving hundreds of miles across the Patch and beyond, troubleshooting every conceivable crisis that can befall an oil company. This even resulted in Norris getting kidnapped and tortured by a Mexican drug cartel.

Tommy was promoted to M-TEX's President when his best friend and M-TEX's chairman, Monty Miller (Jon Hamm) died. However, Monty's widow, Cami (Demi Moore), found a business partner she liked better in Danny Morrell (Andy Garcia), who is also a drug kingpin named Gallino. Cami ultimately fired Tommy, which led Norris to start his own oil outfit to control his own future. That need for control will become an issue in Landman season 3.

When Tommy formed CTT Oil Exploration & Cattle in Landman season 2's finale, he recruited almost all of his friends and family members, giving them positions in its corporate structure. Tellingly, Tommy didn't name himself President; instead, he took the Vice President role and gave the President's job to his son, Cooper Norris (Jacob Lofland).

Landman season 3 is filming now, and social media footage from production shows a slow burn storyline kicks into full gear in the new season.

Tommy decided to become Vice President because it gives him the freedom to continue as a landman. Even when he was M-TEX's President, Norris refused to go corporate and spend his time behind a desk. Giving Cooper that role means Tommy can do what he does best: drive all over the Patch, cut deals, solve problems, and run and gun. However, it won't be quite so easy.

Cooper likely won't want to be desk-bound either, but Tommy's son really has no business being President of an oil company, even a startup, and Tommy is smart enough to know that. Cooper has proven instincts in the field, but he's not a CEO. This means Tommy will not only clean up any messes Cooper creates, but he's likely to eventually lose patience and take on the President's role himself.

Or worse, anything Cooper doesn't know how to do (which is plenty), Tommy will pass off to Nate (Colm Feore), his treasurer, or, most likely, Rebecca Falcone (Kayla Wallace), the COO and Chief Counsel. This will infuriate Rebecca, who has lingering issues with Cooper from Landman season 1, and she surely doesn't think he is fit to be President either.

No matter what, Tommy will run CTT himself and everyone will treat him as the guy really in charge, which he is. Cooper will be a de facto President, and that's another powder keg in Landman season 3.

Cooper as CTT's President is a liability for Tommy in another way: Norris has too many enemies who can strike at him through his son. From Mexican drug lords to his frenemy Gallino to oil magnate William Crockett (Michael Kelly) joining Landman season 3, and maybe even Cami Miller, there are plenty of people who won't want Tommy's new oil company to succeed.

​​​​​​​Earnest and inexperienced Cooper will have an even bigger target on his back as CTT's President.

When Tommy found out Cooper struck a business deal with Gallino, not knowing who "Danny Morrell" was, he immediately tried to shield his son. Earnest and inexperienced Cooper will have an even bigger target on his back as CTT's President, and Ariana Medina (Paulina Chavez), CTT's new secretary, is also vulnerable because she dates Cooper.

The corporate structure Tommy built for CTT Oil Exploration & Cattle is wonderful in terms of including friends and members of the Norris family, but it's a house of cards bound to collapse. The problem starts at the top with Cooper as President, and Tommy knows that. Norris' problem is he should be President, but he would rather be a landman.

The truth is, no one in Landman can replace Tommy, but there's also only one of him. Cooper should have been the new landman and Tommy should be President. In CTT Oil Exploration & Cattle's current structure, Tommy will be stretched too thin trying to do everything himself and solve problems Cooper would be better off tackling as he learns to be a landman.
