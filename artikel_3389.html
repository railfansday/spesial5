PlayStation Plus December 2026 Free Games Lineup Officially Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Players can download their free PlayStation games beginning Tuesday, Dec. 4. Here's everything PlayStation fans can expect as part of PS Plus next month.

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 and 4 is a remake of two early 2000's Pro Skater games.

Before Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 launches next year, check out its predecessor. Xenoverse 2 is an oldie-but-a-goodie 3D fighting game that goes heavy on the PRG elements. Create your Dragon Ball character and travel through time, fighting iconic battles from the series with new twists, like duking it out alongside Super Saiyan Goku on Namek against both Frieza and Cooler.

From the makers of Soma, Amnesia: The Bunker is a first-person survival horror game.

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 and 4, Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, and Amnesia: The Bunker will be available until Monday, Jan. 4, 2027. You can still grab November's free PlayStation Plus essential games — Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider 4-6 Remastered, and Monster Train 2 — through Nov. 30.

​PlayStation Plus Essential is the lowest tier of Sony’s subscription game service. It offers online multiplayer access, a monthly assortment of free PS4 and PS5 games, cloud storage, and discounts on PlayStation Store purchases. PlayStation Plus Essential costs $79.99 annually, $27.99 for a three-month subscription, or $10.99 monthly, following a price hike across all tiers of PS Plus in May.
