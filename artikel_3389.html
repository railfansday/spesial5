Billy Joel Motorcycles Sell for Roughly 5 Times Their Estimate

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Billy Joel is known for smashing the record for most shows performed at Madison Square Garden ... but this weekend, he also pulled off another rare feat -- selling his motorcycles for five times their estimate.

Gooding Christie’s auctioned off a total of 69 of his motorcycles and raised more than $2 million -- roughly quintupling their pre-auction estimate of $405,000.

Of course, this was all for a good cause ... with the proceeds benefiting the Joel Foundation.

Each and every lot sold exceptionally well ... often 5 to 10 times their estimated values! Talk about a Big Shot!

The standout sale, however, was his 1978 Triumph Bonneville T140 'Madison Square Garden' Custom.

Given to BJ as a gift for his 150th sold-out MSG performance, it fetched $577,000 ... more than 50 times the $10K-$15K projected value.
