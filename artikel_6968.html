Everyone Wants to Know if the MacBook Neo Will Go on Sale for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The cheapest MacBook might get even cheaper.

I am not the first person to point out that MacBooks are expensive. I will not be the last. If you monitor this stuff like we do, you’ll have seen the irony that Apple released the budget-conscious MacBook Neo earlier this year—only for the global computing shortage to almost immediately raise the price from $599 to $689.

But, the Neo is still cheap. You can buy it full-price and get your money’s worth. That said, we’d take the bet that it will go on sale. A handful of Prime Days have come and gone without any discounts to the newest MacBook, but with the new flagship released, we suspect that it Black Friday might give us a Christmas miracle.

Designed as Apple’s answer to the lower budget laptops running Windows, the Neo’s target audience is technically students—hence the good branding and cute colors. That being said, if you need a personal computer for watching Netflix and doing your taxes, but don’t want a full desktop, it’s a perfect choice.

The Neo runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip—the same one that powers the iPhone 16 Pro and Pro Max. If the idea of buying a laptop that runs on a phone chip gives you pause, your stress is better channeled elsewhere. While this isn’t the right device for someone who is planning to run heavy-duty software, it can handle standard internet browsing, video calls, and the like with ease. Just keep in mind that it only has 8GB of RAM, so it’s best-suited to basic workflows.

Of course, this computer has the added benefit of four jazzy color options: blush and citrus for the bold, indigo for those more subdued, and silver for that classic MacBook aesthetic. Wrapped in an aluminum body with a 13-inch Liquid Retina display, the Neo weighs in at just 2.7 pounds (same as the MacBook Air) and is 0.5 inches thick. (For reference, the iPhone 17 Pro Max is 0.35 inches thick.)

Despite our best efforts, we don’t quite know how Apple determines its deals for big sale events—which means if this laptop does indeed get discounted, there’s no telling exactly how deep the price cut might be or when it’ll hit. Maybe it’ll shave $100 off to get back to its pre-increase price, or maybe we could be looking at a sub-$500 MacBook for the first time ever.

Whatever the discount may be, the Neo is undoubtedly a smart buy for anyone who wants a laptop that can handle all of the basics. Even at full price, it’s a fraction of the cost of a discounted MacBook Pro or Air, which reliably go on sale for Black Friday.

If you’re not willing to wait for a potential Neo deal, a handful of other MacBooks are already on sale.
