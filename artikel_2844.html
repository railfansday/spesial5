Report: Eagles to release veteran OL Fred Johnson

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Eagles are releasing veteran offensive tackle Fred Johnson, Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports.

The move comes shortly after veteran receiver Hollywood Brown requested his release, and the sides reached a non-injury settlement.

The Eagles have seen an overhaul of their offensive line this season.

Johnson was serving as a backup to rookie Markel Bell at right tackle since the in-season retirement of Lane Johnson. He saw action in seven games this season, playing 24 offensive snaps and 22 on special teams.

Johnson, 29, returned on a one-year contract this offseason.

He originally joined the Eagles in 2023, playing 58 games with 14 starts.
