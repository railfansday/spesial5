Imaging showed no fracture in Justin Herbert's finger

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert underwent imaging on his injured left middle finger, and coach Jim Harbaugh said the tests came back negative for a fracture.

“So he should be good to go,” Harbaugh said Monday, via the Associated Press.

Herbert injured his non-throwing hand on a sack by Logan Hall when the quarterback tried to stiff-arm the defensive lineman with 14:12 remaining in the fourth quarter. Herbert, who was sacked eight times in the 44-22 loss, threw a touchdown pass two plays later.

Herbert had his left hand surgically repaired during the 2025 season after breaking a bone in the back of the hand during a Week 13 game. He played through the injury and expects to play through his current injury, he said postgame.

The Chargers, who play the Ravens on Monday Night Football in Week 10, have lost 11 consecutive games.
