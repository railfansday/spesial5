Zelenskyy praises ‘exemplary’ drone operation that sank Russian warship in a Libyan port

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on Monday lauded an "exemplary" military operation that sank one of Russia's newest ships at a Libyan port, after Kyiv's forces struck their target with several domestically made naval drones.

The incident followed a suspected Ukrainian drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Baltic Sea on Saturday and occurred less than a week after damage was inflicted on Russian President Vladimir Putin's prized Crimea bridge.

In a social media post, Zelenskyy said Ukrainian forces deployed seven sea drones to attack the Merkury, a Russian navy corvette, at Benghazi's port in the early hours of Sunday morning. Ukraine's president posted dramatic video footage showing Ukraine's naval drones triggering a massive explosion on the Russian ship.

"Despite all the enemy's efforts to counter the attack, the Russian corvette Merkury was sunk. It's one of their newest ships, commissioned in 2023, during the full-scale war," Zelenskyy said. "We continue our operations in designated waters," he added.

Satellite imagery taken on Sunday showed the Merkury mostly underwater, having capsized

The Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which Zelenskyy said Moscow used to transport weapons, was hit by Ukrainian forces. A Ka-27 military helicopter on the Merkury was also destroyed.

Asked about reports of attacks on Russian ships off the coast of Libya, Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said information on this would be provided via the Defense Ministry.

The Security Service of Ukraine, the SBU, said Monday that the attacks "once again demonstrated that the Russian military fleet and its logistics have no safe routes even far beyond the Black Sea," according to a Google translation.

"Such unique special operations destroy the enemy's logistical capabilities and force Russia to pay for aggression even at a strategic distance from Ukraine," the SBU said via Telegram.

The United Nations Support Mission in Libya, or UNSMIL, expressed "serious concern" over the drone attacks in a statement saying the country "cannot afford, and must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts."

"UNSMIL calls for an immediate, independent and transparent investigation to establish the facts and hold those responsible to account," it added.

The incident is not the first time Libya has seen attacks linked to the Russia-Ukraine war near its territory.

In March, Moscow's Transport Ministry accused Ukraine of striking the Arctic Metagaz, a liquefied natural gas tanker, as part of a suspected sea drone attack in the Mediterranean between Libya and Malta.
