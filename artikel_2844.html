Badgers handle business, bounce back in major way vs. Purdue

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Wisconsin Badgers needed a strong bounce-back performance after their embarrassing loss to the Maryland Terrapins, and they got one, beating the Purdue Boilermakers 42-10 on the road on Saturday afternoon.

Wisconsin pounded the ball on the ground, going for 381 rushing yards and five rushing touchdowns, as they improved to 7-4 on the season. The loss was Purdue's second-biggest of the season behind their 39-point defeat against Notre Dame, and was a big statement from Luke Fickell's squad going into the final week of the season.

The Badgers started the game with the ball and instantly controlled the pace, going 14 plays for 75 yards and a touchdown on a drive lasting nearly seven minutes to open things up. Wisconsin converted three third downs and a fourth down on the drive, capping things off with a 24-yard Eugene Hilton rushing touchdown.

They quickly forced a three-and-out and were back on the offensive, once again slowly moving the ball down the field before Colton Joseph broke off for a 32-yard rushing touchdown, his first of a few in the game.

Purdue looked like it was heading to another three-and-out, but converted a fourth down, and a Wisconsin penalty was enough for them to get into field goal range. They stalled out on the next set of plays and kicked a 53-yard field goal.

That score was short-lived as the Badgers went two plays and 75 yards for their third touchdown of the game. Jacob Harris had a long 26-yard catch-and-run to start the drive before Darrion Dupree went 49 yards to the house for a 21-3 lead.

That's when Purdue was finally able to respond with some life offensively. The Boilermakers went pass-heavy and slowly moved the ball through the air before Ryan Browne hit Cooper McCutchan for a 24-yard catch, getting Purdue inside the five-yard line, where they punched it in three plays later on a direct snap to Fame Ijeboi.

Wisconsin's offense had its first punt of the day with a three-and-out, and Purdue was once again able to slowly move the ball, getting two first downs, including one from a Jai'mier Scott hold. But three straight incompletions forced Purdue to punt it back to the Badgers with 2:43 left in the first half.

And Wisconsin looked to make the most of the opportunity, as Colton Joseph went for 19 yards on the first play of the drive, getting the Badgers out from deep inside their own territory. The Badgers picked up another first down and a personal foul set them up in striking range at the Purdue 34-yard line. But a major negative play on second down, a loss of 12, killed the drive for the Badgers, taking them out of field goal range and costing them a final scoring chance before the half ended.

The Boilermakers got the ball to start the second half, but quickly gave it up with a three-and-out after a holding penalty on first down. And the Badgers got to work on the ground. This time, the Badgers went 12 plays for 74 yards and a touchdown, going with nine runs and three passes for a Darrion Dupree one-yard touchdown.

However, a couple of key plays on the drive came through the air. On 3rd & 7, Colton Joseph hit Chris Brooks for a first-down conversion, extending the drive. Joseph had another 11-yard hit to Jaylon Domingeaux and a key 20-yard completion to Domingeaux on 3rd & 2 to set Wisconsin up at the two-yard line.

Suddenly, it was 28-10 Badgers with Wisconsin in full control.

Wisconsin's defense continued its superb day, forcing another punt after one first down, with another holding penalty killing the Boilermakers. Then, the offensive onslaught on the ground continued, with the Badgers rushing 11 times and throwing just twice on their following drive, resulting in an 11-play, 85-yard touchdown drive.

Colton Joseph had a carry for 23, Darrion Dupree had an 18-yard run, and Joseph had another 22-yard run to keep Wisconsin in scoring position before Eugene Hilton hit Joseph for a 13-yard touchdown pass on a trick play to send the Badgers up 35-10.

Purdue, struggling for explosives, caught a break with a pass interference call to start its next drive, and they were able to get two first downs while slowly moving the ball. But, once again failing to make a big play, the Boilermakers were stuck with a 2nd & 5 on Wisconsin's 34 and had three straight incompletions to force a turnover on downs.

Colton Joseph called game from there, taking the first play 66 yards to the house to seal the deal for the Badgers with a 42-10 blowout, and the game ended that way, marking a huge win for Luke Fickell's squad after a poor outing last weekend.

Wisconsin's defense held Purdue to under 200 yards of offense, while the offense went for over 500 in the big win.

Next up, the Badgers cap their season off against the Minnesota Gophers, looking to reclaim the Axe for the first time in three years.
