Bright Side Wonders, Vol 8: Is a trade incoming?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Phoenix Suns are starting to get their act together after a 1-5 start. They're now 5-7 after a 2-2 week, but noise about one of their key players' future has gotten louder. Prominent NBA Insider said that Jalen Green has been put on the trade block amid the team's search for a point guard, and Dejounte Murray's trade request out of New Orleans and the Suns' reported interest in acquiring him has added fuel to the fire.

So with trade rumors flying and some improved play, here are the questions we want your answers on in this week's edition of Bright Side Wonders:

Reports are going back-and-forth about the Suns' interest in Dejounte Murray and their willingness to part with Jalen Green, but what remains true is that conversation about the Suns' need for a point guard has heightened. The starting lineup continued to struggle on offense during Sunday's loss against the Lakers, with Devin Booker and Green shooting a combined 32% from the field, and on the season, Green is averaging career-lows in scoring and from the field.

It's become a common question around the Suns world but we have to ask it again, what do you think the Suns should do with their point guard situation and are you willing to give up Green to improve the position? Would Murray be an upgrade? What would you be willing to give up for him?

After rough starts to the season, two key members of the Suns' surprising success from a season ago Jordan Goodwin and Collin Gillespie have started to get going on offense. Gillespie is averaging 17 points with Goodwin at nearly 13 a game in that stretch. Their strong performances helped the Suns stay in the game against the Lakers on Sunday and Goodwin helps on the boards and Gillespie with playmaking.

If the two continue to contribute similar to the way they did a season ago, how will your confidence change in the team's second unit?

Grayson Allen and Royce O'Neale played their first games against the Suns since being traded to the Hornets for Miles Bridges during the offseason on Thursday. The two played major roles in helping Phoenix turn the chemistry around a season ago as veteran presences and were beloved figures in the community. Both have taken mostly a bench role to start their time in Charlotte.

While it's too early to know who won the trade, what did their returns to Phoenix mean to you?

Jalen Green is struggling on the offensive end. His four games under 10 points this season when starting are already more than he had last season under double digits when he didn't leave a game early because of injury.
