Broncos vs. Steelers: Week 12 Live Fan Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Update: Broncos get absolutely dominated in road loss to Steelers on Black Friday.

The Denver Broncos have a chance to put a game up on the Kansas City Chiefs with a road win over the Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday. It's been reported ahead of the game that the Steelers players feel completely unprepared and the team is in disarray after the fallout and subsequent release of quarterback Aaron Rodgers on Tuesday. This is a situation that Denver should be able to take advantage of, so we'll see how it goes.

Kickoff is set for 1:00 p.m. Mile High time on Friday, November 27, 2026 at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. You can watch on Amazon Prime. The game will be called by Al Michaels (play-by-play), Kirk Herbstreit (analyst), and Kaylee Hartung (sideline). You can also check local Broncos radio network affiliate stations.
