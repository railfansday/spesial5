Panthers take 14-13 lead on Bryce Young's second TD pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Panthers lead the Broncos 14-13 after the first drive of the second half.

With an early 10-0 lead, it appeared Denver was on its way to a blowout. The Panthers, though, have scored 14 of the past 17 points.

Darren Waller caught a 15-yard touchdown pass from Bryce Young on third-and-9 with 11:30 left in the third quarter.

The Panthers took the second half kickoff 65 yards in six plays.

Waller had a 21-yard reception in the drive, and Broncos defenders Ja’Quan McMillian and Dondrea Tillman had 5-yard penalties for defensive holding and illegal use of hands, respectively.

The Broncos will play the second half without cornerback Riley Moss and wide receiver Jaylen Waddle, who are in concussion protocol.
