Opinion: It's now looking very likely that a world-class player is coming back for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Andoni Iraola has an exciting player to look forward to using at Liverpool very soon.

These are extraordinary times in the Premier League. There's an ongoing investigation into Manchester City and their 114 charges to which they claim innocence.

If the guilty verdict decided upon by the Premier League is ratified it will have huge ramifications on the rest of the league.

To the extent that we must ponder what the future of English football looks like. Liverpool will be at the forefront of these conversations after all, they will have been directly impacted by what Manchester City had done.

Iraola will be the man tasked with navigating the waters of this murky terrain. All the while there are changes afoot at Anfield as well.

1892 Holdings have bought a significant minority stake in the club. There are talks that this could lead to a majority stake.

How will Liverpool's future look like in the next five years?Going forward the ambition from the new investors will be to build a global empire. Liverpool's existing reach is already far and wide being one of the most popular clubs in the world. However, there are avenues to grow the club both commercially and on the pitch as well. Recruitment could improve. Liverpool have already started changing their strategy to previous years. There have been big money signings and heavy recruitment at youth level as well not just domestically but globally, too. Ultimately, Liverpool want to be at the pinnacle of the footballing world, but not only that, they want to be at the pinnacle of the sporting world. That will be the ambition going forward.

One area where Liverpool's future vision is already clear is in the centre-back department. Ifeanyi Ndukwe has looked class in pre-season and is blowing spectators away with his performances at Levante. Jeremy Jacquet has been world-class since arriving at the club.

We saw merely a glimpse of him before his devastating injury last year. But that glimpse was so exciting.

In those brief moments he spent on the pitch, he looked utterly world-class. Now, it looks like he is on his way back.

The club has confirmed that he is training with the team, and he looks like he is in great shape from the videos Liverpool shared.

Just look at this recovery tackle he makes on Jeremie Frimpong, who is known for his speed. This is a world-class defender.
