Options traders are bracing for Nvidia’s market value to swing by roughly $335 billion after the chipmaker reports third-quarter results, Reuters reported, citing Susquehanna data. Contracts imply an 8.5% move in either direction on Wednesday, more than double the stock’s average post-earnings reaction over the past eight quarters and close to the 8.74% jump that followed its previous report. That implied swing is larger than the market value of about 90% of S&P 500 companies. Nvidia’s valuation has dropped to about $3.9 trillion from roughly $5.8 trillion in early October, according to LSEG data, with the slide deepening after Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last week.
