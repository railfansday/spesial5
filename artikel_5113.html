Bitcoin’s $41K Bottom Is Back in Focus as Four-Year Cycle and Cup-and-Handle Pattern Flash More Downside

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bitcoin might not be out of the woods yet, despite recently staging a relief rally toward $60,000 after renewed market optimism over Nvidia’s announcement that it is reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO and president.

Market analyst Crypto with Sarmad believes Bitcoin’s next four-year cycle bottom could form around $41,000.

According to the analyst, if BTC continues following its historical cycle pattern, another notable decline could be ahead before the market establishes a formidable bottom.

Adding to the concerns, on-chain analytics provider Tranquility Crypto has identified a bearish cup-and-handle formation on Bitcoin’s weekly chart.

The pattern’s neckline sits around $60,000, making it a critical level for determining BTC’s next direction, since a confirmed breakdown could expose Bitcoin to a deeper correction toward $36,000.

Meanwhile, analyst SeffafNet recently outlined another potential downside target of $49,000, identifying several support levels that could come into play if selling pressure accelerates.

The analyst also anticipated the possibility of one final capitulation event before bulls return in full gear and potentially trigger a broader recovery.

According to CoinCodex data, BTC is trading at $60,404, placing it close to the critical $60,000 neckline identified by Tranquility Crypto.

Why is Bitcoin’s short-term price action important? Holding above $60,000 could help Bitcoin avoid an immediate bearish breakdown, while losing this psychological level could deepen its woes and set up another leg lower.
