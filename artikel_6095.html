KLM Axes All Flights To This UK Airport: See Map Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The United Kingdom is one of KLM's largest markets. The Dutch flag carrier serves nearly 20 destinations across the market and offers around 70 daily flights. In April this year, the airline expanded its network to include a rather unusual destination, Jersey, on a seasonal basis.

The island is technically a self-governing British Crown Dependency rather than part of the UK, but is included in the UK market figures for this analysis. It appears that the airline will not return next summer. According to scheduled data from aviation analytics company Cirium, there are no KLM flights to Jersey Airport (JER) in 2027.

KLM initially offered one weekly flight between Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS) and Jersey and increased frequencies to up to daily during July and August. The flights were operated by its regional subsidiary KLM Cityhopper using its Embraer E175 aircraft, although the Embraer E190 was also deployed on the route.

The SkyTeam member entered the market after easyJet withdrew from the route. The British low-cost carrier briefly served the island from Amsterdam during the summer 2023 season, when it offered twice-weekly flights. However, according to Ports of Jersey, while the service proved popular among Jersey residents, it failed to attract enough visitors from Amsterdam despite local marketing efforts.

Similar challenges may have influenced KLM's decision. Simple Flying has reached out to the airline to establish whether it has permanently withdrawn from the market and, if so, what prompted the decision. However, as of November 26, no flights are scheduled on the route for the upcoming summer.

Elsewhere in the UK market, KLM has made no other destination cuts and is set to return to all 18 remaining destinations for the summer 2027 season. Cirium data shows that the SkyTeam member has scheduled more than 14,300 flights to the UK, offering over 1.8 million seats in one direction.

*Based on the current 2027 schedule, which is subject to change

London Heathrow Airport (LHR) will remain the carrier's busiest UK destination. KLM and its regional subsidiary will collectively offer seven to eight daily flights from Amsterdam. London City Airport (LCY) ranks second, served between three and seven times daily, followed by Manchester Airport (MAN), which will receive six daily flights.

Looking more broadly at KLM's European network, the airline has announced three additions for the upcoming summer season. All three routes from its Amsterdam hub will launch on March 28, 2027, and be operated by KLM Cityhopper. These include a daily service to Shannon Airport (SNN), which will mark the carrier's entry into the west of Ireland and complement its existing services to Dublin and Cork.

It will also introduce daily, year-round flights to Sofia Airport (SOF) in Bulgaria. The third destination is not new to KLM's network but rather a returning one. The carrier discontinued flights to Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP) in summer 2025 to consolidate its operations at Milan Linate Airport (LIN), which is closer to the city center.

Twice weekly (Saturdays and Sundays) from March 28 to July 2;

Daily from July 3 to August 29; twice weekly (Saturdays and Sundays) from August 30 to October 30

Now, the SkyTeam member will serve Malpensa on a seasonal basis using E175 aircraft. It will offer twice-weekly flights on Saturdays and Sundays, increasing to daily from July 3 to August 29. The seasonal service could allow the airline to tap into peak summer demand for popular holiday destinations such as Lake Como and Lake Maggiore.
