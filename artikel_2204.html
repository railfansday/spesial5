Sheffield United rival Portsmouth in race for star who can produce 'absolutely crazy numbers'

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Sheffield United are already looking ahead to the January transfer window, as Chris Wilder prepares to strengthen areas of weakness throughout the Blades squad, though they may well face some strong competition from division rivals Portsmouth.

Sheffield United are looking forward to the January transfer window and have set their sights on Belgian youth international Renzo Tytens to reinforce their defensive options.

However, Championship rivals Portsmouth are also heavy admirers of the current Lyngby left-back, as this saga is set to heat up as the winter window draws ever closer.

According to Rudy Galetti's Substack, the Belgian journalist has reported that Portsmouth are the current frontrunners for the signature of Tytens, though they face a threat from Sheffield United and French side Toulouse.

Tytens, 21, came through the youth ranks of Dutch outfit PSV Eindhoven before spending a brief period with Club Brugge's youth team between the summer of 2024 and January 2026, when he moved to Denmark with Lyngby.

Since joining Lyngby, he has helped his side achieve promotion back to the top flight and locked down the left-back spot as his own, with his performances in the Superligaen attracting interest across Europe.

Speaking to Danish publication Tipsbladet, former Brentford defender and current Lyngby coach, Andreas Bjelland, was full of praise for Tytens, as he said: "Our scouting department and sports director have done a fantastic job finding Renzo, who we think is a good fit. He's not Gustav Mortensen, but he's a bit like that.

"He can produce some absolutely crazy numbers and has a good foot and has proven to be really, really important for us already."

Sheffield United are also reportedly considering Motherwell's Emmanuel Longelo, though no formal discussions have taken place at this stage for either player.

Sheffield United's interest in Tytens and Longelo will surely come as a surprise to many, with each of Harrison Burrows, Sam McCallum, and Rhys Norrington-Davies already battling for a starting berth at left-back at Bramall Lane.

However, Wilder may well be looking to the future, and with Norrington-Davies out of contract next summer, a gap in the Blades' squad may open up for Tytens, though surely one of Burrows or McCallum must leave also.

Portsmouth, meanwhile, have Jayden Meghoma and Connor Ogilvie battling for a place in the Pompey side under John Mousinho, though with Meghoma only on loan from Brentford and Ogilvie now 30, it's hardly a shock the South Coast outfit may be looking to the future with Tytens.

Portsmouth are the favourites for his signature as things stand, and having spent time in two prestigious academies and gaining experience in a top-flight European league in three different countries already, Tytens could well be making England his next destination sooner rather than later.

Now into the midst of a two-week international break, clubs across the Championship will take a step back to assess their current squads and begin making preparations for the January window, as Sheffield United and Portsmouth will be no different.

Both sit just outside of the play-off positions as things stand, and both Wilder and Mousinho will feel as though there is no reason their sides can't go on and claim one of those top eight spots.

The winter window will provide a chance for both to strengthen areas of weakness, as a move for Tytens appears to be on the cards for both.
