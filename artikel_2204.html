West Ham backed to strike double transfer deal with Man United & Blackburn Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

West Ham United have been urged to consider two major transfer moves in the upcoming January window, the first of the Scott Parker era at the London Stadium.

The Hammers entered their first Championship season in 14 years as the overriding title favourites, but in some ways, have flattered to deceive across the first 15 games of the campaign.

By no means has it been a total disaster for the East London club as they sit ninth, just six points behind leaders Birmingham City with plenty of encounters left to make up ground, but it was enough for the London Stadium hierarchy to install former midfielder, Parker, in place of Nuno Espírito Santo earlier this month.

He will have found out a lot about his new chargers in away encounters against Cardiff City and Blackburn Rovers before the international break, and it has since emerged that Parker has laid down the law in terms of potential transfer plans.

West Ham were tipped to win the division at a canter by many due to the vast experience and individual quality which runs through the squad in E20.

It recently emerged that Argentine forward, Taty Castellanos, who has scored just once this season, is attracting interest from River Plate, with a fresh transfer stance from Parker subsequently coming to the fore.

Alan Nixon reports that the Hammers boss is looking to recruit a specialist left-back and a new striker with a proven record at Championship level.

It remains to be seen who will feature on the club's eventual shortlist, but it has caused plenty of debate online as to who should front them in particular.

West Ham's current predicament at left-back is certainly intriguing, with Oliver Scarles featuring eight times so far this season ahead of Athletic Bilbao loanee, Adama Boiro, whilst 19-year-old, Ezra Mayers, was utilised on the flank in the 2-1 loss at Ewood Park, last time out.

As such, Football League World's West Ham United fan pundit, Ryan Horn, has urged the club to target Manchester United's Harry Amass, who is reportedly expected to leave Old Trafford in January after a lack of first-team minutes.

"My left-back option would be Harry Amass, the youngster from Manchester United. Obviously, he could be leaving for free next year," Horn told FLW.

"I feel like, if we could offer him a deal with first team football, him and Scarles going out for left-back, that'd be great.

"He's got a lot of raw talent. I think he'll be brilliant, and going for that younger approach, where we can have a player in a position for a few years to come with some resale value if a big club comes in for him.

"Because of the fact he's not being picked over Luke Shaw, I honestly feel for him. He should be playing more, and I think he'd be brilliant at West Ham under Parker," he added.

Horn also expressed his desire to see Blackburn's Andri Gudjohnsen in claret and blue, with the Icelandic striker one of just three Championship players already into double-figures this season.

"My striker pick would be Gudjohnsen from Blackburn.

"Again, young, scoring goals for fun. He works hard, has that sort of 'building for the future' feel, that spine where he could grow into his role a bit more."

Amass previously excelled in the Championship on loan at Sheffield Wednesday, before an injury in January significantly derailed his second stint in the division with Norwich City under Philippe Clement.

The 19-year-old's contract situation and desire for a fresh challenge means there is certainly scope for West Ham to secure a cut-price deal, but that would be far from the case with Gudjohnsen.

Blackburn tied the 24-year-old to a three-year contract last summer, and they could extend those terms until 2028, especially if a bidding war ensues over his services.

Gudjohnsen's work-rate, ability to bring fellow attackers into play and his own poacher's instinct would make him a desirable option for the Hammers, especially with the speed of Jarrod Bowen and Edvin Austbo on either flank.

It was recently claimed by EFL pundit, Tom Garratt, that the striker is worth £15m in the current market, but with the East London side breaking the Championship transfer record in August after Arne Engels' £22m move from Celtic, they are a club capable of fronting up sums of cash which would be hard for their divisional rivals to turn down.
