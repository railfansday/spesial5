Sheffield United man answers question over long-term future after West Ham, QPR summer interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Sheffield United saw Harrison Burrows endure a tougher 2025/26 season after a fantastic debut campaign in 2024/25. Rival Championship clubs were lurking in the summer in case SUFC opted to cash in on the 24-year-old, with West Ham and QPR credited with interest while Burnley saw a late bid rejected.

However, Burrows has been back in the side and back in form in recent weeks. While speaking to The Star, the former Peterborough United man was asked about his long-term future and whether he sees it with the Blades, to which he responded: “Yeah, 100%. I’m really enjoying my football here. I really enjoy being with the lads, I really enjoy the club and I really enjoy the city. So, yeah, I’m very happy.”

Burrows is currently under contract at Bramall Lane until 2028. Sheffield United are looking to tie down a number of players to new deals, and the left-back is one of them alongside Sydie Peck, Femi Seriki and Michael Cooper.

As touched on before, Burrows endured a fantastic debut year at Sheffield United. After signing from Peterborough United, he stepped up to the Championship brilliantly as he recorded six goals and six assists in 48 games for a Blades side that made the play-off final.

Form proved harder to come by last term, and Sam McCallum emerged as the first choice at left-back before injury hit. He locked down his spot in Chris Wilder’s side early on this season too, performing well while Burrows was sidelined himself.

Harrison Burrows’ Sheffield United stats (all competitions)

However, injury has cast McCallum to the treatment room once again, and Burrows has made a fine return to the team. He has started the last five Championship matches, with only Saturday’s meeting at Charlton Athletic ending in defeat.

Burrows’ form has served as a reminder of what he offers on the left, with his technical ability making him a creative asset for SUFC. There are questions over the defensive side of his game at times, but the quality he offers with the ball can see him play a highly influential role when he’s in good form.

Aged 24, Burrows is already approaching 300 career games. He played 185 times for former club Peterborough United, and Saturday’s start against Charlton Athletic marked his 101st appearance for Sheffield United.

It means he has both an excellent amount of experience and plenty of room to keep developing. Burrows spent his youth in midfield and broke into first-team football in this position too, so he still has progress to make defensively.

With improvements in this area, SUFC will have a well-rounded left-back with strong experience and leadership abilities – as shown by his past captaincy at Peterborough United.

A challenging season and summer interest brought questions over his Bramall Lane future, but Burrows can be a valuable asset in the now and future if a new long-term deal is agreed.
