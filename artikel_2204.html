Three goals from 1.27 xG: Sydie Peck defies the numbers to break seven-year Sheffield United duck

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sheffield United continued their fine form on Tuesday night as they defeated Preston North End 3-2 away from home. SUFC are now four games without defeat, and they’ve won four of their last six Championship matches.

It was a strong team performance from the Blades, but the man-of-the-match display from hat-trick hero Sydie Peck can not be overlooked. The 22-year-old midfielder scored three in the first half, becoming the first United player to score a competitive first-team hat-trick since Billy Sharp against Aston Villa in February 2019.

Peck did so from an expected goals (xG) of just 1.27 too. First, his cross bounced all the way through and beyond Daniel Iversen (0.02 xG) before he kept the Preston North End ‘keeper at bay and floated beyond a despairing defence for his second (0.21 xG). Finally, the Blades star slid in at the back post to convert from Romelle Donovan’s cross (0.81 xG) to seal his hat-trick.

Peck registered a total of 1.27 xG across his six shots – the most by anyone in the game. His attacking threat was emphasised by the fact his six touches in the opposition box was more than any other player too.

The Sheffield United man saw to his usual duties. He created two chances in total and completed 52 passes, also winning six out of seven aerial duels – a tally bettered by only Mark McGuinness in the SUFC side (Fotmob).

It was the complete midfield performance from Peck, who has grown in influence this season. His importance creatively is displayed by 21 chances created and 76 line-breaking passes, and 779 touches shows how regularly he is the man to get on the ball when the Blades have possession.

Peck has made 54 defensive contributions too though, winning 52 duels in total. This shows his willingness to get stuck in and do the dirty work, while 50 recoveries highlights his work rate as well (Fotmob).

Now on four goals this season, if Peck can start to score on a more consistent basis, he will really be a complete midfielder for Chris Wilder.

Peck has been with Sheffield United for over five years now, joining the academy after his exit from Arsenal. He broke into the team in the 2024/25 campaign and would grow in the 2025/26 season with five goals and five assists in 41 games across all competitions.

The London-born midfielder drew interest from West Ham in the summer, but he expressed a desire to pay back all those who have shown faith in him at Bramall Lane – including manager Wilder.

Peck is happy with SUFC and his continued improvements this season show his development isn’t being impacted by sticking around either. However, if he continues in his current form, interest is bound to grow, and exit opportunities will become more tempting.

Sheffield United are keen to renew Peck’s contract as his current deal runs out in 2028. It would be wise to strike an agreement sooner rather than later with clubs sure to be alerted by his impressive displays of late.
