From Dark Chocolate to Milky Mocha, These Brown Nail Polishes Are So Good for Any Fall Manicure

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

These make a rich manicure so easy to DIY.

To me, November means a few things: Friendsgiving feasts, Black Friday shopping, and most importantly for us manicure devotees, brown nail polish season. It’s the time of year when I start reaching for richer hues, and brown is one of my favorite shades to wear this time of year.

So, whether you’re on your DIY manicure journey or want to bring your own bottle to your next nail appointment, I rounded up the best brown nail polish shades to try this fall and winter.

If you're after a true deep espresso shade, Essie's Odd Squad is the perfect brown polish. It's a dark chocolate brown that’s equal parts moody and rich, and it looks universally flattering on any skin tone.

For a slightly lighter and creamier brown hue, this OPI shade is more like a milky coffee or a warm cup of hot cocoa. It's a softer take on brown that's still plenty cozy for fall.

If you’re someone who doesn’t have the patience or time to sit around waiting for your nails to dry (same), a quick-drying polish is the way to go. This formula helps cut down on drying time, so you can get back to your day sooner and spend less time worrying about accidentally smudging your fresh manicure.

A gel polish is a great way to get more mileage out of your favorite brown shade. This OPI formula cures under a UV or LED lamp, leaving you with a high-shine finish and salon-quality longevity right at home. And the entire bottle is cheaper than the price of one salon manicure!

If you're going for a darker manicure, a long-wearing formula is especially ideal since chips tend to be more noticeable against deeper shades. Sally Hansen's Xtreme Wear is a great option, with a durable finish designed to help your manicure last longer. Plus, it’s less than $3 a bottle!

Cat eye and shimmery polishes are trending for winter, and this formula from Zoya in the shade Thatcher gives you the iridescent finish—no magnets or curing necessary.
