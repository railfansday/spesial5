From “Magnetic Merlot” to “Soft Licorice,” 10 Dark, Minimalist Manicure Ideas to Try This Winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Bolder hues are getting the starring role as winter sets in

While soap manicures, subtle petal nails, and longevity techniques once dominated the summer months, a darker color palette is quickly taking over now that the clocks have gone back and evenings are early. Yet if you're hesitant to venture into "too bold" territory or fear a pop of color on your fingertips, a dark manicure doesn't have to feel overly attention-grabbing.

In fact, as professional nail technician Samantha Rudge shares, dark manicures with a more minimalist finish are proving to be winter's most popular manicure trend. "For this season, I think we'll be seeing darker classic manicures with minimalist touches," she shares, adding that she has already seen an uptick in shade requests for Bio Sculpture's rich, chocolate-toned Decadent Cocoa and the ever-popular Garnet Noir, a moody red-black hue. "Nail designs like chrome, cat eye, micro French tips, and tortoiseshell will also dominate this season," she adds.

"Short nails are also very popular right now, no matter the nail art or shades," she adds. Regardless of shape or color, Rudge stresses the importance of prioritizing nail health at this time of year. To protect nail longevity and prevent chipping, Rudge recommends assessing your natural nail condition, choosing the right base (BIAB or gel), and using a nourishing cuticle oil to repair and strengthen brittle nails.

Ahead, discover 10 new dark manicures for those who love a more minimalist finish, from “Garnet Noir” nails to subtle lines.
