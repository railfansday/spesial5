Report: Real Madrid could now decide Liverpool star's future

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool's squad has undergone several massive changes since winning the Premier League in 2025.

The team that Arne Slot inherited from Jurgen Klopp has now been dismantled, and a series of big money additions have formed the core of Andoni Iraola's current team.

Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola became the first trio to all cost over £100 million to be fielded at the same time this season, and more big changes are on the horizon at Anfield.

A number of the Reds' established stars are being linked with exits as Iraola continues to find his feet, and an update has now been given on one contract standoff.

Alexis Mac Allister made his disappointment at not being offered a new deal this summer very clear.

The World Cup winner went public with his feelings early in the season and has delivered some very impressive performances on the pitch since.

Uncertainty around the midfielder's future has led to an increase in links to a move away, with Real Madrid one of the teams most interested in Mac Allister.

A new report from Football Insider has revealed that the Argentine is using the interest from Los Blancos to leverage his position when negotiating a new deal with the Reds.

After Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch were recently rewarded with new deals, Mac Allister is believed to be sticking strictly to his demands, and Real Madrid's position could decide his future.

If the Spanish giants are legitimately interested in bringing in the Argentine midfielder, then Liverpool may decide to cash in rather than paying out to sign the 27-year-old to an extended deal.

With a contract running through to 2028, the Reds are under no immediate pressure to sell Mac Allister but, if no new deal looks likely in the summer, then a sale would make a lot of sense.

Real Madrid's interest in Mac Allister looks likely to decide his future, and the midfielder may be playing in white rather than red sooner rather than later.
