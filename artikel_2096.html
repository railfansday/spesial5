Wolves should challenge Birmingham City and Middlesbrough for perfect Andre partner: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Wolves need to be keeping their options open for the January window despite a decent start to the season. Cesar Peixoto’s side currently sit 4th in the Championship and a good window will put them in a very strong position to push for automatic promotion.

Wolves should be keeping a close eye on Arthur Avom ahead of the January transfer window, with the Lorient midfielder offering an opportunity to strengthen their promotion push while potentially laying the foundations for a Premier League midfield.

The 21-year-old has emerged as an attractive target for several English clubs, with Birmingham City and Middlesbrough reportedly tracking his situation. However, Wolves should be looking to get ahead of their Championship rivals, particularly given the opportunity to negotiate a relatively affordable deal.

Avom has already established himself in French football, making 31 Ligue 1 appearances last season after helping Lorient win promotion from the second tier in 2024/25. He has continued to feature regularly this term and has also earned senior international recognition with Cameroon.

His contract situation makes the opportunity even more interesting. Lorient’s agreement with Avom currently runs until June 2027, potentially leaving the French club with limited leverage next summer.

The biggest attraction is how well Avom could complement Andre. Wolves already have an established midfield anchor in the Brazilian, who committed his future to the club until 2030 following relegation from the Premier League.

Andre provides defensive discipline, composure and the ability to dictate possession, whereas Avom offers greater mobility, energy and forward-running ability. The Cameroonian is comfortable winning possession, carrying the ball through midfield and contributing defensively, making him a natural candidate to operate alongside Andre in a double pivot.

That partnership could be particularly valuable across a demanding 46-game Championship campaign, allowing Andre to concentrate on controlling games while Avom supplies the athleticism and intensity needed to compete in both boxes.

There is also a longer-term argument for the move. Much of Wolves’ existing midfield group is already established, with Marshall Munetsi approaching 30 and Tommy Doyle and Boubacar Traore in their mid-twenties.

Avom would introduce another young option alongside Jordan James, helping reduce the need for an extensive midfield rebuild if promotion is secured. His experience in Ligue 1 and international football also means he would not necessarily need several seasons to adapt to a higher level.

Wolves cannot afford to overlook the immediate competition, either. Birmingham and Middlesbrough are both capable of offering attractive projects, while previous Premier League interest demonstrates Avom’s growing reputation.

If Wolves are serious about building a team capable of returning to the top flight and staying there, Avom is exactly the type of recruitment they should be considering. A January move could strengthen their promotion ambitions while giving them a potentially long-term partner for Andre.
