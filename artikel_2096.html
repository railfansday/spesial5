Sean Steur was called up in the September international break for the Netherlands Under 21s, a very first time, thanks to the impressive early season Newcastle United form.

The 18-year-old then made his first ever appearance at that level for his country as the Netherlands played out a goalless draw at home to Norway on Friday 2 October, the teenager coming off the bench in the 83rd minute. Then Sean Steur started his first ever Netherlands Under 21s match, playing the full match and impressing as the Dutch U21s beat their Slovenia counterparts 4-2.

Another call up in November and this time a two-legged European Championship qualifying play-off against the Denmark Under 21s.

In the first leg on Friday, the 18-year-old played very well as he helped the young Dutch side to a commanding 3-0 win, playing the opening 78 minutes and as well as an outstanding all action display, also provided an assist for the third goal.

The Netherlands U21s had one foot in next summer’s Under 21 European Championships finals after that 3-0 win in Denmark.

The return leg on Tuesday looking pretty much a formality but the young Danes had them sweating.

By the 52nd minute it was 3-1 to Denmark on the night and the Dutch only leading 4-3 on aggregate. However, a 71st minute goal dispelled any nerves and whilst the Netherlands lost 3-2 at home, it was a comfortable 5-3 victory on aggregate.

Sean Steur having another very good game and played the full match as he helped guide the Netherlands Under 21s into next summer’s Euros.

Also earlier on Tuesday, Yoane Wissa played the first 73 minutes of Sierra Leone 1 DR Congo 1.

With four of the six AFCON group qualifying games played, DR Congo are top of their group on 10 points, three clear of the second placed team.

Meanwhile it ended Gambia 2 Ivory Coast 2, with Newcastle’s Bazoumana Toure an unused sub.

Ivory Coast are also  top of their group on 10 points, three clear of the second placed team.

Amar Dedic started at left-back for Bosnia & Herzegovina v Romania.

Anthony Elanga is on the bench for Poland v Sweden.
