Southampton release pre-Middlesbrough video and it's sparking a reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The first meeting between Southampton and Middlesbrough since last season's play-off semi-finals is just hours away, and the anticipation is building nicely.

Back in May, these two sides were set to contest two legs to decide who would head to Wembley and go within 90 minutes of the Premier League, but a Boro official discovering a Saints analyst illegally spying on their training session was about to add plenty more layers to the meeting.

The games still went ahead, but under plenty of intrigue as to what punishment, if any, Southampton and Tonda Eckert would be handed. In the end, they'd be expelled from the play-off final after they prevailed over Middlesbrough in extra time, with Boro being reinstated to take on Hull City.

It's a decision which has fuelled this newly brooding rivalry, as, despite admitting to the breaches, those associated with Southampton would have felt the punishment too severe, and that Middlesbrough shouldn't have been allowed to progress in their place after losing the semi-finals.

That's the focus of a pre-match hype package that Southampton released ahead of the game with Middlesbrough this evening at St Mary's, anyway, that Boro were allowed "to have another go" at promotion, while they weren't.

It's a good way to build anticipation ahead of what is already a hotly anticipated matchup on the South Coast under the lights.

While Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert have done their best to play down the impact that last season's Spygate drama will have on this year's meetings, the two sets of fans will be battling tooth and nail both in the stands and online. This video has done exactly what was intended: rile up fans ahead of the game.

Boro fans were quick to mock the video on X. One commented, "Boro really have become your Cup Final 🏆🤣 Spend the whole of the video talking about last season, but then decide your only focused on this season - make your mind up 🤣🤣🤣"

"Tragic this. Cheated, caught, punished, move on. Enjoy the trophy lift and open-top bus parade if you get a result tonight 👍🏼," another added, while another questioned whether Saints fans would be "embarrassed" by this kind of promo.

"Can’t believe you’ve made this video 😭," and "Comfortably the worst thing I’ve ever seen," were also among the comments.

Granted, it has to be said that a lot of the reaction to the video is from Middlesbrough supporters. One Southampton fan concluded that they must "have our club's notifications on."

"Can admin please play “stop crying your heart out “ by oasis after we beat boro tonight please. 😇😎," was also among the Southampton reaction to the tweet.

Another fan made sure to praise the reaction that they've got from Middlesbrough fans on the tweet, which, ultimately, is the sole goal of the video. It helps build this new rivalry and adds intrigue to what was already a must-see matchup this evening at St Mary's.

Fanbases often like to play down the importance of big games, such as this one, but deep down, everyone associated with either side will want to win this one a little bit more than a "regular" game.

This video shows it. The build-up from Southampton indicates that they view this one as a big game, while the majority reaction from Middlesbrough fans does show the same. Clearly, if the roles were reversed, it would be Saints fans in the Boro comments reacting in tune.

As neutrals, fans get to sit back and watch how it all plays out from a rivalry standpoint, but also as two teams battling for the Championship title, coached by two of the brightest young head coaches in the game today.

Middlesbrough have been excellent this season, while Southampton have been unstoppable at St Mary's. It's a clash between two of this year's second-tier titans, and whichever side you sit on, whether this is a rivalry or not, you can't deny how important the game tonight is.
