The Mag Podcast Episode 18: Newcastle United 2020-21 featuring Steve Bruce, Joe Willock and the Road to takeover

Published: 18/11/2026 | Duration: 55 mins | Hosts: Jamie Smith, Simon Cahill & David Hutchinson

In Episode 18 of The Mag Pod, we find Jamie, Simon, and Hutch reflecting back on the time of Covid and a pre-Newcastle United takeover era that featured Steve Bruce, Mike Ashley and Joe Willock. A strange time indeed to be a United fan.

Listen to the latest episode of The Mag Pod! Join Jamie, Simon, and Hutch for unfiltered football debate.

Newcastle United’s difficult 2020-21 season under Steve Bruce, Joe Willock’s remarkable loan spell and the build-up to the Saudi-backed takeover are the focus as Jamie Smith, Simon Cahill and David Hutchinson return for the final instalment of their international break specials.

The trio revisit football behind closed doors, compare the pre-takeover squad with today’s Newcastle United and look ahead to the return of Bruno Guimarães with Arsenal.

The 2020-21 campaign was a strange time to be a Newcastle supporter. With St James’ Park largely empty due to Covid restrictions, Mike Ashley still in charge and Steve Bruce overseeing some desperately uninspiring football, the connection between supporters and their club was arguably at its lowest point.

Simon takes us back through the season, from the arrival of Callum Wilson and Ryan Fraser to the struggles of a side relying heavily on Allan Saint-Maximin for attacking inspiration. Despite spending much of the campaign uncomfortably close to the relegation places, Newcastle eventually finished 12th, thanks in no small part to Joe Willock’s extraordinary impact after joining on loan from Arsenal.

There’s also a moving personal story from Simon about his family’s experiences during lockdown, providing a reminder that football was only one part of an extraordinary period in everyone’s lives.

The discussion then turns to the present.

Has Newcastle’s squad improved as much as it should have since the takeover? Are there players from Bruce’s team who would strengthen today’s starting eleven? And how much patience should supporters have with the club’s latest recruits?

With the international break finally ending, there’s a round up of Newcastle players representing their countries before attention switches to Arsenal’s visit and the potentially awkward reception awaiting former Magpies favourite Bruno Guimarães.

Plenty of nostalgia, disagreement and the usual irreverent humour in another edition of The Mag Pod.
