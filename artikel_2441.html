Bayern star leaves France camp following Italy defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bayern Munich centre-back Dayot Upamecano has departed the French national team camp.

The French Football Federation confirmed on Friday that the 28-year-old has left the camp early for personal reasons.

Upamecano played the full game as France suffered a 3-1 defeat to Italy in the UEFA Nations League on Thursday, despite taking an early lead through Michael Olise.

The Bayern defender conceded the penalty that allowed Pio Esposito to put Italy 3-1 ahead in the 74th minute.

France coach Zinedine Zidane has initially decided against calling up a replacement for Upamecano.

Les Bleus face Turkey in their final Group A1 fixture on Sunday.
