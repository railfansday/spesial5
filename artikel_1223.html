Ben Jacobs Expects Man Utd To Move On From Lisandro Martinez

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United are leaning towards allowing Lisandro Martinez to leave the club next summer despite already lacking depth at the centre-back position. United are in troubled times, currently sat in 19th position and desperately hoping new boss Mauricio Pochettino can turn things around quickly.

Liverpool thrashed United 4-0 in Pochettino's first game, although the Red Devils were not helped by a red card awarded to Leny Yoro that was later removed on appeal. But United did not play well in any case, and they certainly didn't defend well amid ongoing issues at the back.

Matthijs de Ligt lacks match fitness and sharpness, while Yoro is still learning and Martinez has been in poor form this season. Harry Maguire, meanwhile, cannot get a look in.

United have some interesting contract decisions to make in the summer window, and one of the biggest is Martinez, who initially arrived at the club on a £56.7million deal. Two long-term ACL injuries have followed, and it's fair to say the Argentine hasn't lived up to expectations. He didn't help himself when he spoke out against the club earlier this summer amid struggles under Michael Carrick.

Martinez is out of contract in the summer, but United do have an option to extend the deal by one year, albeit they are only likely to activate that if both they and the player want to continue their relationship.

And according to GIVEMESPORT's Ben Jacobs, United are not likely to extend the deal as things stand. He said on the Market Madness podcast: “I think the Lisandro Martinez direction of travel is a departure, and there is the option to extend by one year. But my gut says that Manchester United, even if they can’t get defenders in the winter, will add further in the summer because they don’t just need one defender; they need a centre-back, they need a left-back, you could say they need a right-back.

"And in getting these full-backs, they might even get a full-back that’s a centre-back. So you might have centre-back, a wing-back that can play centre-back, and you’ve obviously still got young players like Ayden Heaven and Leny Yoro.

So this feels to me like Martinez, on form and on age, and even to a degree on wage, although he’s not like top five at Manchester United, the beginning of the end.

"And even if Man United extend the contract, that might just be a protection so they can still sell him.”

There have been reports that Pochettino wants to keep Martinez, with the defender certainly having the ability with the ball at his feet that the new United boss likes in his centre-backs, and there is an obvious Argentine connection. But there is also a business reality to the contract situation, and that will mean club higher ups like Jacon Wilcox will get a big say.
