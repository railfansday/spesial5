UCL | PSG 2-2 Roma: Giallorossi hold reigning Champions

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Roma earned a more than creditable 2-2 draw away to reigning Champions League holders PSG, as Mario Hermoso and Donyell Malen’s penalty cancelled out Illia Zabarnyi and Ousmane Dembélé.

The Giallorossi had twice thrown away two-goal leads for defeat to Real Madrid and Manchester United, but at least the injury crisis was calming down, with only Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini still out.

Surprisingly, Donyell Malen, Paulo Dybala and Bryan Cristante were initially benched for Niccolò Pisilli and Santiago Castro. Former Roma coach Luis Enrique lost Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Désiré Doué to torn anterior cruciate ligaments in the space of a few weeks.

It was Roma who took the lead after just five minutes when Mario Hermoso’s high press saw him rob Maghnes Akliouche, then Matias Soulé dribbled down the left and just about kept it in play to pull back from the by-line, where Hermoso was ready to fire in.

Achraf Hakimi needed a well-timed header to take the Soulé cross off the head of Castro at the back post, while Nuno Mendes failed to trouble Mile Svilar.

The biggest problem for Gian Piero Gasperini’s side this season has been corner kicks, and they were punished yet again by an Illia Zabarnyi free header at the near post with very little marking on the Senny Mayulu set play.

Evan N’Dicka also threatened from a corner with a header flashing inches wide, then after the restart Safonov flapped a Wesley cross into the path of Hermoso to volley just over the bar.

Roma had a huge opportunity to restore their lead on 51 minutes when Pisilli ran from midfield and combined with Castro to force a desperate one-handed Safonov save.

Joao Neves was perhaps offside anyway when his diving header went wide on the Nuno Mendes cross, but Wesley was caught out by the pace of ex-Inter full-back Achraf Hakimi, pulling him back by the shoulder for a penalty. PSG also wanted a red card, but the referee opted for the yellow. Ousmane Dembele just came on and converted the penalty.

Malen and Dybala came off the bench, and it was La Joya’s lay-off for Soulé that saw the shot charged down in the six-yard box by the raised arm of Vitinha, eventually spotted by VAR for a penalty, converted by Malen.

It remained wide open to the end, as first Hermoso turned over from a very promising position when off balance, then Fabian Ruiz had two attempts charged down, as Hakimi drilling inches wide in added time.

Dembele forced a difficult Svilar save with the boot at the near post, but it was a point apiece in Paris.
