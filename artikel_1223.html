Amorim: ‘Milan can defend better, were too soft in Europa League’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ruben Amorim insists that despite a very attacking Milan line-up against Olympiacos, they can ‘defend better’ than in previous ‘soft’ Europa League performances.

It kicks off at the Stadio Georgios Karaisakis in Piraeus at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game, Juventus-Omonia Nicosia and Borac-Atalanta on the Liveblog.

The Rossoneri absolutely must get points in Greece this evening, because they lost three of their four Europa League games so far this season, winning 4-0 away to RB Salzburg.

They make the trip without Luka Modric, Francesco Camarda, Davide Bartesaghi, Strahinja Pavlovic, Samuel Chukwueze, Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers and Pietro Terracciano.

It is an all-attack formation, with Diego Moreira, Alphadjo Cisse, Omari Hutchinson and Goncalo Ramos together.

“In football, you need to defend and attack. Aside from the characteristics of the players, we need to attack with 11 and defend with 11,” Amorim told Sky Sport Italia.

“The players know the positions that we want on the pitch, we are ready to play and must be more compact compared to the matches against Benfica and Ferencvaros. Even with these players, who are more attacking in their characteristics, we can defend better.”

Milan have never won away to a Greek side in Europe, managing just four draws and two defeats, including a 3-1 loss to Olympiacos in December 2018.

It is notable that they have been unable to be consistent with two games in a week, so does Amorim see the right attitude today?

“I think they understood, we watched the videos, we are improving as a team. We were a little soft against Ferencvaros because we were not compact, we need to really focus on the details, when the position makes all the difference two metres one way or another.

“We did it several times in Serie A, now we need to do it in the Europa League too.”

Olympiacos can boast several former Serie A players, as Lorenzo Pirola was an Inter youth product, Remo Freuler spent time with Bologna and Atalanta, while Marcus Pedersen played for Torino.

“We need to be a good team, and to do that, we must be able to win in Serie A and in Europe. We have that responsibility, as we always say, the present is important, but we are in a long-term project. We need to know if we are ready and today is another important test for us to win,” concluded Amorim.
