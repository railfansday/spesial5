All of the Best Looks From the 2026 LACMA Art + Film Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Last night, the 2026 LACMA Art + Film Gala took place at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art in California. The night, co-chaired by actor Leonardo DiCaprio and trustee Eva Chow, brought together influential figures from the worlds of art, film, fashion, and entertainment to support the museum’s mission to make film more central to its future programming. Artist Vija Celmins and filmmaker Denis Villeneuve also received special honors.

Before the ceremony began, the red carpet saw a legion of Hollywood A-listers sport their most grand styles. Every year, the LACMA Gala—which is supported by Gucci—produces memorable (and extremely glamorous) formal attire moments, and this evening was sure no different.

Last year saw some of newly-installed creative director Demna’s first big red carpet moments, like stars Elle Fanning and Doja Cat appearing in their boldest looks by the Italian label. This year saw things amp up even more: Kim Kardashian played with her enduring love of the cut-out in a Bordeaux-toned sequin dress, while Parker Posey looked radiant in a sparkling white kaftan. Sienna Miller made the case for the LSD (the Little Silver Dress) while Rose Huntington-Whiteley went to glamorous extremes with some serious skin on show—all were care of Gucci.

And while Gucci was well represented across the evening, striking looks were also served courtesy of Valentino, Saint Laurent, and Chanel.
