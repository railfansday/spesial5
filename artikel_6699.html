Catherine Zeta-Jones’s Daughter Carys Borrows Her Mom’s Lacy Gucci Top From the ’90s

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A gem from the beloved movie star’s closet

When your mom is Catherine Zeta-Jones, there is hardly a ’90s or Y2K gem you can’t get your hands on. From turn-of-the-century Fendi baguettes to red-carpet gowns (that crimson Versace dress from the 2004 Oscars particularly comes to mind), the Academy Award-winning actor embraced all the best of the decades’ trends. And now, her daughter gets to as well.

Yesterday, Carys Douglas, the 23-year-old first-born child of Zeta-Jones and husband Michael Douglas, went through her mother’s archives for a night-out look perfectly suitable for a Gucci x Moda Operandi cocktail event in New York City. Her choice was a sheer long-sleeve lace top from the Italian fashion house, with an elongated, deep U neckline secured solely by a single clasp. The piece, with its raw hems, was a familiar pull—last seen on the Chicago star at the 1996 premiere of The Long Kiss Goodnight in Los Angeles.

At the ’90s event, Zeta-Jones layered the top over a little black cami and paired it with slim trousers in the same sheer lace. She added a large cross necklace, chunky rings, and a pair of open-toe heels, and slung a large bag over her shoulder.

Three decades later, her daughter put her own spin on the piece, wearing it with a little black miniskirt and semi-sheer black tights. Douglas took a cue from her movie-star mom’s glam, wearing her hair down in slept-in waves and focusing her makeup on the lips, adding a pop of color. She grabbed a quilted Gucci bag to finish things off.

This was not the first time Douglas borrowed something from her mom’s closet. In 2024, she celebrated her 21st birthday in a pink Emanuel Ungaro slip previously seen on Zeta-Jones at the 1999 MTV Movie Awards. After posting the look on Instagram, her famous mom lovingly commented: “You deserve it sweetheart.”
