8 Fresh Perfumes Everyone Will love (Yes, Even Maximalists)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

These dreamy scents smell like soap, fresh laundry, blooming greenery, and more.

Even though I love writing about fragrance, I don’t necessarily want to smell like perfume. Most of the time I wish the scent of my body wash lasted for hours or that my clothes eternally smelled like I just pulled them out of the dryer. But, as we all know, a majority of those wonderful clean scents disappear in an instant. That’s why I made it my mission to find the dreamiest fresh perfumes.

Fresh perfumes typically feature notes like ambroxan and aldehydes (two synthetic compounds that smell, you guessed it, clean and fresh), as well as citrus and musk. They can be aquatic, green, warm, or even floral, but of course, there’s an overarching subtlety about them that fits in this specific family. There's no better smell in the world (to me) than soap, fresh laundry, or simply skin, but "clean" means something different to everyone. That’s why we found an array of fresh perfumes to choose from. Just take a look at some of our faves...

If you want to smell shower-fresh at all times (who doesn’t?), keep reading to discover team Cosmo’s favorite fresh perfumes.

One Cosmo editor says: “Nothing compares to the scent of a mineralic sea breeze, and Jo Malone bottled the essence perfectly. If I close my eyes when I spritz Sea Salt & Bergamot, I’m transported back to the rocky Cornish coast. This perfume is simple in the best way possible. Even though it has just three notes—bergamot, sea salt, and driftwood—it’s completely unforgettable. I’ve gone through a full bottle since it launched this summer.”—MARY HONKUS, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “Alto Astral is simply fresh. (Fitting that it won the Best Fresh Perfume category in our Holy Grail Beauty Awards, right?) It’s clean and light thanks to aldehydes and coconut water, yet it becomes deep and complex with hints of sandalwood and salted amber. If you like to keep things simple, wear it alone. Don’t be afraid to layer with it though! It works with every scent family.”—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: "Sometimes I want to smell exactly like a bubble. And Replica has, ahem, replicated that fragrance. It's the epitome of squeaky clean. Somehow, this reminds me of being in the tub as a kid and using fluffy soap bubbles to turn myself into Santa Claus. That may not sound super appealing as a fragrance, but trust me, with the additions of creamy coconut and lavender, it's amazing."—JAYNE LEMIRE, EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT

One Cosmo editor says: “Just like a tall glass of water on a hot summer’s day, this perfume feels inexplicably refreshing. Light florals—including hyacinth, lotus blossom, and jasmine—are enshrouded with a unique water accord to make the notes smell dewy and wispy. Then this settles in an ambery musk for a soft yet warm finish.”—MH

One Cosmo editor says: “A nod to fresh laundry, Rinse Cycle is a fresh, clean fragrance with a bright, citrus opening. After it came out last year, I traveled everywhere with a mini size—I’m talking from Coachella to Paris to Miami to NYC. No matter where I went, this scent worked with every destination. It's also entirely season-less, making it a wonderful choice for hot summer days as well as cool winter nights.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “I fell in love with this perfume at first spritz. Created in collaboration with Morgan Riddle, it's reminiscent of fresh tennis whites, cut grass, and breezy air. I adore how it makes my clothes smell, most of all. It’s like I used the best laundry detergent in the world. It smells incredible on skin, too!”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “You know that feeling when you’re lounging in nature and a sliver of light perfectly heats up your skin? I don’t know how, but 5Sens turned that moment into a perfume. This isn’t your average skin scent: It combines fuzzy apricot with a delicate bouquet of peonies and freesia that’s gently warmed with woods and skin musk. It’s the ideal understated perfume that helps me shine a little bit brighter.”—MH

One Cosmo editor says: "There's always been something so supremely comforting about the smell of fresh, warm laundry straight out of the dryer. The only downside is that the wonderful detergent aroma fades quickly. Thankfully, Clean Reserve's Warm Cotton has miraculously managed to bottle it. If you've ever walked around NYC, you'll know there are a million different scents at any given time (many of them, truthfully, not amazing). However, one of my favorite city smells is walking past an apartment building and smelling clean laundry through one of the many vents along the sidewalk. I'll always pause and take in a deep breath; it reminds me of home. Warm Cotton is that moment."—JL

Mary Honkus is a beauty contributor for Cosmopolitan with over eight years of experience researching, writing, and editing beauty stories. She is an authority in all beauty categories, but has a sweet spot for fragrance with a collection of over 600 scents. While researching for this story, she tested dozens of fresh to bring you an array of calming options.
