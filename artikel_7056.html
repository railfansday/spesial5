Eco Plus Solutions AB: German-Speaking Full-Stack Developer– Healthcare / MedTech (Remote)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Location: 100% Remote (within Germany) | Headquarters in Kiel, Germany

Compensation: €60,000 – €75,000 gross per year (based on your experience and qualifications)

Experience: Minimum 4 years in professional software development

Tech Stack: Angular, TypeScript, Java, Quarkus, Jakarta EE

We are a long-standing, forward-thinking HealthTech organization based in Kiel, Germany. For over three decades—since 1991—we have pursued a single, dedicated purpose: enabling remedial therapy and healthcare professionals to thrive. Because remedial therapy makes a meaningful difference in patients' everyday lives, we build integrated digital solutions—spanning practice management software, advisory services, training, and operational tools—that relieve practitioners of heavy administrative workloads. Our vision is to keep healthcare practices running smoothly and reliably, freeing up therapists to dedicate their energy to what truly counts: caring for their patients.

Our engineering culture and day-to-day teamwork are guided by three foundational principles:

Clarity & Transparency: We foster open, straightforward, and unambiguous communication across every level of the organization.

Action- & Goal-Oriented Execution: We prioritize practical, results-driven solutions that create genuine value for our end users.

Reflective & Respectful Collaboration: We engage with one another constructively, openly, and thoughtfully—growing and refining our craft together as a cohesive team.

In the role of Mid/Senior Full-Stack Developer (m/f/d), you will become part of an autonomous, cross-functional Scrum team responsible for the flagship software powering thousands of therapy practices nationwide. You will connect resilient backend architectures with clean, intuitive user interfaces while playing an active part in steering the technical direction of our platform.

End-to-End Feature Delivery: Own the entire lifecycle—from concept and technical design to coding and deployment—of new full-stack capabilities built specifically around the nuanced daily operations of healthcare practices.

Backend Engineering & Optimization: Build, maintain, and systematically enhance high-performance, scalable backend services leveraging modern Java ecosystems.

Frontend Architecture & User Experience: Craft clean, modular, and intuitive web applications in Angular and TypeScript that streamline complex administrative workflows for non-technical medical and therapy staff.

Architectural & Technical Co-Design: Contribute directly to evolving our system architecture, assessing emerging technologies, and driving long-term technical decisions alongside your engineering peers.

Security, Compliance & Software Quality: Uphold rigorous standards for data protection, system reliability, and application security—embedding security-by-design practices and comprehensive automated testing throughout the development lifecycle, essential within the regulated healthcare space.

Agile Teamwork: Collaborate daily with Product Owners, UX specialists, and fellow developers in a self-organized Scrum environment, actively participating in peer code reviews, technical knowledge exchange, and ongoing workflow improvements.

Healthcare / MedTech Domain Experience (Mandatory): You possess verified, hands-on experience building software solutions in the Healthcare, HealthTech, or MedTech industry, bringing practical insight into sector-specific workflows, sensitive patient data handling, and regulatory standards.

Proven Software Engineering Experience: A minimum of 4 years of professional software development experience, ideally spanning both frontend and backend engineering.

Backend Specialization: Extensive, practical command of Java backend development, with focused experience in Jakarta EE and/or Quarkus.

Frontend Expertise: Deep, hands-on proficiency in developing modern single-page web applications using TypeScript and Angular.

Clean Code & Engineering Craft: A strong dedication to Clean Code practices, maintainable modular design, automated testing, and modern software engineering standards.

Formal or Practical Education: A university degree in Computer Science, completed IT vocational training (Fachinformatiker/in or equivalent Ausbildung), or comparable real-world engineering credentials.

Language Proficiency: Business-fluent German at a minimum C1 level (written and spoken) to communicate effortlessly within German-speaking teams and navigate specialized healthcare terminology.

Complete Remote Autonomy: Work 100% remotely from anywhere inside Germany, paired with flexible working hours (Gleitzeit) so you can structure your schedule around your life.

Generous Leave & Social Time Off: Receive 30 days of paid annual vacation, plus an extra paid week off every year reserved for social or charitable volunteering.

Health, Retirement & Income Protection: We support your long-term security through employer-funded supplementary health insurance (Krankenzusatzversicherung), alongside company pension plans (betriebliche Altersvorsorge) and occupational disability coverage (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung) subsidized by up to 40%.

Continuous Learning & Upskilling: Benefit from a dedicated annual budget for conferences, courses, and professional training, in addition to complimentary access to Babbel for language development.

Home Office & Lifestyle Benefits: Enjoy a monthly stipend for phone and internet expenses, exclusive employee discounts through our corporate benefits platform, and favorable terms on company bicycle leasing (Firmenrad-Leasing).

Purpose-Driven Work: Build technology that delivers real, measurable social value—empowering healthcare practitioners who help people heal and improve their quality of life every single day.
