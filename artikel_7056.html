A Brutally Honest Editor’s Guide to Amazon’s Black Friday iPad Deals

As a shopping editor who tracks prices on Apple products all year, I have high standards. Truth be told, Apple tech deals have had some highs and lows this year: some items reached their lowest prices ever during major sale moments like Prime Day, while others (I’m looking at you, Labor Day deals!) have been less than impressive. Now that Amazon’s 12-day Black Friday sale is here, I’m creating an official guide to the iPad deals you should shop now...and which are worth waiting a little longer for.

I’m forecasting steeper deals during the day of Black Friday (November 27), but that doesn’t mean you’re doomed to wait. Ahead, you’ll find an updated guide to all the iPad models on sale right now:

So far, the best Black Friday iPad deal up for grabs is $100 off the iPad Mini. This small but mighty tablet is powered by the speedy A17 chip, with a 12MP camera that can capture 4K videos. It’s compact at 7.7 inches tall and weighs less than a pound (0.66 pounds, to be exact), which makes it the perfect size to toss in your purse for on-the-go streaming and note-taking.

Though this is the steepest iPad deal thus far, it still pales in comparison to last year’s discount. This exact model was a record-low $349 during last year’s Black Friday sale, so there’s a good chance it’ll get even cheaper in the days to come.

You can also score $100 off the latest iPad Air model. It’s powered by the M4 chip, which boasts advanced graphics and a powerful processing system that multitasks with ease—whether you’re editing videos, jotting down notes, or creating presentation slides. When paired with the Magic Keyboard, it can function more like a laptop than a typical tablet. It’s available in 11-inch and 13-inch sizes, with storage ranging from 512GB to 1TB.

Price-wise, this isn’t the best discount I’ve ever seen. It’s been on sale for as low as $519, a whopping $130 cheaper than its current sale price. Even though it’s the steepest deal available right now, there’s a strong chance that it drops in price on Black Friday.

Another iPad deal I’m keeping an eye on? The 11th-generation iPad, which was released in 2025. Although its current $50 discount isn’t particularly impressive, I saw it drop below $300 during last year’s Black Friday sale. I recommend holding out just a little longer to get a better deal.

Compared to the iPad Air, this model sacrifices some processing power for a more affordable price point. The A16 chip is better suited for tasks like scrolling your TikTok feed, flipping through your e-book library, and streaming the Love Is Blind finale, so it’s a great option if you prefer using your iPad in recreational settings. It comes in three storage sizes—128, 256, and 512GB—with the best discount on the 128GB model.

All in all, the iPad deals available right now aren’t worth writing home about—but don’t despair! These deals can literally change in an instant, and I’m predicting that prices will become comparable to last year’s Black Friday sale if you’re willing to wait until next week.
