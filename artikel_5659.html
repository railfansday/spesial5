Zoox Partnering with Uber to Bring Autonomous Mobility to More Riders

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Now live in Las Vegas, with Los Angeles to follow

In March 2026, we announced a multi-year partnership between Zoox and Uber to bring our robotaxi experience to Uber’s growing autonomy platform in select cities.

Now, beginning today, Uber riders in Las Vegas will have the opportunity to be matched with a Zoox robotaxi on eligible trips. Zoox will also continue to offer rides through the Zoox app. In addition, Zoox and Uber are the official robotaxi and rideshare partners of MGM Resorts’ Las Vegas properties. Together, they’re working with MGM Resorts to make pickups and drop-offs easier and more intuitive, with improved on-property wayfinding, in-app guidance, and infrastructure improvements designed to support smoother traffic flow.

The Zoox purpose-built robotaxi gives riders the agency to create a space all their own, with design elements for enhanced socialization and safety not possible in a traditional car. Bringing this experience to Uber will help introduce the benefits of purpose-built autonomy to new, curious riders.

Within the Zoox and Uber apps, riders can take a Zoox to popular resorts and venues on and around the Strip, as well as rides in the Downtown & Arts District. More destinations will be added soon.

We will continue to offer our service through the Zoox app, in addition to rides being available through Uber in those cities.

Zoox was founded in 2014 on a bold belief: if you’re going to reinvent mobility, you have to start from scratch. Instead of retrofitting cars with autonomous technology, we designed the world’s first purpose-built robotaxi: fully autonomous, all-electric, bidirectional, and intentionally engineered around the rider experience. Since the very beginning, we’ve been focused on our vision of delivering safe and enjoyable mobility.

Like Zoox, Uber was founded in San Francisco with a vision to improve urban transportation. Uber started in 2010 as a ridehail app connecting riders with cars, but quickly evolved. Over the past decade, it expanded into shared rides, food delivery, freight logistics, micromobility, and is now building an autonomous vehicle network, consistently redefining how people and goods move through the world. By leveraging smartphone technology, dynamic pricing, and real-time data, Uber helped catalyze the on-demand economy and reshape how people get from A to B on a global scale. This partnership is a testament to Uber’s dedication to advancing transportation within the ridehail space.

Zoox has always taken a measured, step-by-step approach by starting small, learning quickly, and scaling responsibly. This launch with Uber mirrors that approach. We’re beginning with a small fleet with the potential to expand as we refine our operations, technology, and customer experience.

Uber and Zoox will launch in Los Angeles next year, after Zoox opens service on the Zoox app.
