How to watch Seahawks vs. Raiders, NFL Week 10: TV network, live stream, kickoff time

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Seattle Seahawks (5-4) have won three of their four games against AFC opposition, and their final one is against a longtime AFC West rival from yesteryear. Klint Kubiak left the Seattle Seahawks after winning the Super Bowl as offensive coordinator, and so far he's got the Las Vegas Raiders (4-5) reasonably in the playoff hunt. Just a year ago under Pete Carroll and Geno Smith, the Raiders were 3-14, so this is already an upgrade in performance.

Of course, the big story of the week is not just Kubiak facing his old team, but now it's Kubiak facing his old team with his No. 1 overall pick, Fernando Mendoza, making his first career NFL start. The Raiders traded Kirk Cousins back to the Minnesota Vikings, which paved the way for the former Indiana star to get promoted to QB1 midseason. While Mendoza will have the awesome Brock Bowers to throw to, he won't have the luxury of an effective run game or a top-flight defense to help him in his development this season. Seattle has opportunities to exploit the Raiders' worst-ranked rushing offense and worst-ranked scoring defense, and it will have some of its key guys back. Drew Lock once again starts for the injured Sam Darnold, but he'll have Rashid Shaheed back in the lineup. On defense, Uchenna Nwosu returns after missing two games with an ankle injury. The likes of Leonard Williams, Charles Cross, and Quenton Nelson won't be seen until after the bye.

It's a must-win for Seattle to stay afloat in the playoff hunt. Here's all the info you need on how to watch Seahawks vs. Raiders, including TV schedule, kickoff time, live stream info, and game announcers.
