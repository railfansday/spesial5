Jaguars vs. Titans odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Jacksonville Jaguars will look to get back on track in Week 12 against the Tennessee Titans.

The Jaguars (7-3) are coming off a gutting 23-18 loss at MetLife Stadium. I guess the special teams unit was okay, and the defense probably didn't play as poorly as we all remember, but it was definitely far from the best day for the offense. And do not get me started on the refs. Maybe we'll look back on this game the same way we look back at the Week 10 collapse against the Houston Texans last season.

The Titans (1-9) are coming off a horrific 51-30 loss in Jerry's World. Cam Ward's outrageous touchdown pass with 30 seconds remaining was the equivalent of "Chris Paul hits a huge three to cut the lead down to 42." 30 points is nothing to brag about when it's against the league's worst defense and in a game where your own defense got burgered. This week, I'm thankful that the Titans still stink.

According to FanDuel Sportsbook, the Jaguars are 14.5-point favorites against the Titans in Week 12. The total is set at 47.5 points.
