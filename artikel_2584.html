There figured to be some growing pains in Miami early this season after it traded nearly half its roster to Milwaukee this summer to get Giannis. The Heat started 3-6 for their worst nine-game start to a season since beginning the 2016-17 campaign at 2-7. But they seem to be figuring things out, winning four straight (2-2 ATS) to get over .500 for the first time this season – but still only 4-9 ATS.

The Greek Freak was terrific in Saturday's 113-117 upset home win vs. Detroit with 34 points on 13-for-17 shooting, with 14 rebounds and three blocks. It was his third straight game with at least 30 points. Only three weeks ago, the Heat lost by 17 at home to these same Pistons.

"As a team, I think we're just clicking better," said Antetokounmpo, who was dealt to the Heat in a blockbuster trade this past offseason. "We know each other's spots better. And the most important thing out of all, we're competing. I think our effort is way better the last nine games."

In the past nine, Miami has the NBA's fifth-ranked offensive rating. Giannis is averaging 29.1 points on 61.3% shooting, along with 13.8 rebounds and 5.2 assists, in that span. He's set O/U 28.5 points. Fellow All-Star Bam Adebayo is averaging 22.9 points on 58.2% from the field, 9.1 rebounds and 2.1 steals in that span.

When Miami started 0-4 and those two were getting used to one another, the team was outscored by 31.6 points per 100 possessions in the 71 minutes they shared the court. They have each pulled down at least eight rebounds in each of the past nine games, marking the longest such streak by a pair of teammates in Heat history.

Dating to last season, Miami is 17-0 when outrebounding its opponent by double figures. But the Rockets are a better rebounding team overall with a plus-3.6 differential on the season compared to the Heat's minus-2.3.

One of Miami's other big offseason acquisitions was signing Tim Hardaway Jr. to a one-year deal to serve as scoring punch off the bench, but he appears to have fallen out of Erik Spoelstra's lineup, as Hardaway didn't play at all Saturday and saw seven minutes or fewer and didn't score in each of the three prior to that. Hardaway was still a solid player last year with Denver in putting up 13.5 PPG off the bench.

It's the first meeting of the season between the clubs with the rematch on Feb. 28 in Houston. They split last season, each winning at home – the Heat have won seven of the past eight at home in the series. They are 1-2 ATS with the rest advantage this season, while the Rockets are 2-0 ATS with the rest disadvantage, winning both in the in the second of a B2B. So that's why I jacked up the spread a bit. It's hard to imagine anyone but Giannis leading this game in scoring, short of foul trouble or injury.
