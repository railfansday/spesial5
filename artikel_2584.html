Dan Quinn: Scars from our late-game losses run deep, glad Jayden Daniels won it for us

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Commanders have repeatedly come up short this season in the final seconds of close games. Commanders coach Dan Quinn acknowledged that was motivating him to get it right in the final seconds of Monday night’s win over the Bengals.

“Those scars run deep,” Quinn said. “There’s no question, failures in some. And I think those scars help.”

Quinn said he trusted Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels to have the ball in his hands at the end, and wanted to manage the clock in such a way that Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow would not be the quarterback with the chance to win it at the end.

“I didn’t want [Daniels] to not touch the ball at the end of the game,” Quinn said. “This is not a quarterback on the other side that you want to have holding it at the end of the game.”

Daniels has been harshly criticized this season for mismanaging late-game situations, but Daniels made two tremendous throws on the final drive to get the Commanders into range for their game-winning field goal.

“I couldn’t have been more proud of Jayden at the end of the game,” Quinn said. “To see those guys come through at the end, I thought that was one of our best team wins. I didn’t think anything could top last week’s but tonight did.”

The Commanders are now 4-5-1 and still alive in the NFC East race. And they have some confidence that if they need their quarterback to deliver in the clutch, he’ll deliver.
