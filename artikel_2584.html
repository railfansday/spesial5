Aaron Rodgers requested Mike McCarthy be removed from relaying plays

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pieces of the puzzle are coming together around the Steelers' shocking decision to bench Aaron Rodgers.

The Steelers released a statement, through their team spokesman Burt Lauten, that Mason Rudolph will start against the Denver Broncos on Black Friday. Rodgers won't even dress and is already announced as inactive. NFL insider Mike Garafolo said on NFL Network that the Steelers were in the process of changing the delivery of their play calling duties to quarterbacks coach Tom Arth.

"There was going to be a change in the process this week," Garafolo said. "Tom Arth is the quarterbacks coach. He was going to be the one to radio the plays in to Rodgers. That change was that this was going to be how things were going to go for the entire game," Garafolo said. "They worked through that at practice yesterday. They were also having meetings as late as 5:30- 6 o'clock to figure this stuff out."

"Rodgers was the one that wanted this change to be made and that's why it was happening as early as the walkthrough yesterday," Garafolo said.

Rodgers took first-team reps on Tuesday and met with the media like any typical week. What happened between Rodgers talking and 11:10 p.m. Tuesday night?

"Nobody was talking about Rodgers being benched so I do believe that it was late last night when Mike McCarthy finally made the decision to bench Aaron Rodgers," Garafolo said. "The question is, why?"

Arth was the Steelers quarterbacks coach in 2025 when Rodgers joined the Steelers and was the only assistant coach to stay on staff when Mike McCarthy took over. Arth and Rodgers have had a longstanding relationship.
