Espí’s late winner seals ninth straight win as Real Madrid draw level with Barcelona

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Substitute Carlos Espí struck late as Real Madrid came from behind to beat Celta Vigo 2-1 at the Santiago Bernabéu. The result keeps Madrid firmly in the title race with Barcelona.

The victory over Celta on Saturday 21 November 2026 was Madrid’s ninth in a row. It also kept their 100% home record this season intact.

The winner began with a Vinícius Júnior cross. Espí headed it, Celta goalkeeper Ionuț Radu saved, and the striker scored from the rebound in the six-yard box. He had been on the pitch for only a few minutes.

Espí had played only 44 minutes of LaLiga football this season before kick-off, according to ESPN. All three of his league goals have now won games, against Espanyol, Elche and Celta. He also scored in the 4-3 Champions League comeback win at Roma. The goal was his fourth of the season, the club said.

Espí called his first goal in front of the Bernabéu crowd his most special for the club. José Mourinho described him as a pure penalty-box striker. The coach added that football history has many players like him who decide games without playing many minutes.

Pablo Durán gave Celta a sixth-minute lead from a Williot Swedberg pass. Kylian Mbappé then missed a penalty in the 39th minute, and the visitors led 1-0 at the break.

Mourinho had started Vinícius and Federico Valverde on the bench. He sent them on with Marc Cucurella in the second half to chase the game. Thibaut Courtois kept Madrid in it with a strong low save from a long-range Durán shot in the 65th minute. A minute later, Jude Bellingham equalised from an Mbappé assist.

The figures show how one-sided the contest was. Madrid had 24 shots to Celta’s 8 and an expected goals total of 3.8 against 1.52. Radu made five saves, and the hosts held 64% of possession.

Celta coach Claudio Giráldez said Madrid’s substitutes had decided the game. He pointed out that he had started six academy graduates. Many of his players were appearing at the Bernabéu for the first time. Giráldez replaced Hugo Álvarez with Ferran Jutglà at half-time and sent on Iago Aspas for Durán in the 76th minute. Celta are 13th on 13 points.

Barcelona still top the table on goal difference, 35 to 22. They can move clear again when they host Villarreal on Sunday 22 November.

In the LaLiga scoring chart, Mbappé is second with 13 goals. Barcelona’s Raphinha leads on 15.

Earlier on Saturday, Rafael Nadal was elected to Real Madrid’s board of directors at an Extraordinary General Assembly at Ciudad Real Madrid. Later, he watched the game from the stands and was visibly emotional.

Celta host Bournemouth in the Europa League at Balaídos on Thursday 26 November, with 3 points from four games. Madrid face PSV in the Champions League at the Bernabéu on Tuesday 24 November at 20:00 UTC. They have won all four of their league-phase games and sit second on 12 points, while PSV are 24th on 4 points.
