Bitcoin Faces Two Red Years For the First Time Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bitcoin has never posted back-to-back losing years, but 2026 could change that.

Bitcoin has never posted back-to-back losing years, but 2026 could change that. BMTV’s Chart of the Day looks at Bitcoin’s yearly candles: 2025 closed down about 6%, the first red year directly after a halving, and 2026 is down about 16% with seven weeks to go. To finish green, Bitcoin has to get back above $87,500, about an 18% rally from here. Sean Hagan says the timeline is full of panic hands treating two red candles as proof Bitcoin is struggling, and he wanted to look at the actual data. The numbers, he argues, may tell a different story.
