The People Against the Arch

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Protesters in DC roast the Arc de Trump, plus the Whitney Museum announces the next curators of its blockbuster biennial.

Rise ’n shine! No sooner had the last Whitney Biennial closed than we got news about the next one — or its curators, at least. Staff Writer Anna Lee has the details on the appointment of Meg Onli, co-curator of the 2024 edition, and Zack Winokur, opera director and producer, for the blockbuster American art exhibition’s 2028 iteration.

Speaking of museums of American art, we caught up with pathbreaking Kiowa and Tongan curator Jordan Poorman Cocker, who just organized a major reinstallation of contemporary Indigenous art at Crystal Bridges. She talks with Petala Ironcloud about approaching Indigeneity as a global framework and telling a “more honest story of American art” through her community-driven work.

Protesters in DC rally against Trump’s ill-fated triumphal arch, a new book chronicles the tragic lives of the Jewish sisters in Renoir’s beloved painting “Pink and Blue," and more must-reads below.

—Lakshmi Rivera Amin, associate editor, books
