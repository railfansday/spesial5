SpaceX and Tesla merger talks are advancing toward a December deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Tesla's annual shareholder meeting is set for Dec. 3, and the companies have discussed an announcement slipping into early 2027

SpaceX and Tesla $TSLA are making headway in their merger talks, and a deal could be unveiled as early as December, according to Bloomberg. Such a deal would be the biggest ever, Bloomberg said.

The companies have also considered a possible delay that would push the announcement into early 2027, according to Bloomberg. Their boards and advisers are currently negotiating the deal terms, along with how the merged company would be governed, Bloomberg said.

Tesla stock climbed by as much as 1.9% to hit an intraday high on Thursday, and SpaceX shares were up 0.5% at 3:20 p.m. ET. Following its June initial public offering, SpaceX is valued at more than $1.9 trillion. Tesla is worth about $1.5 trillion.

Obstacles remain, among them the question of how to wall off Tesla's China business, according to Bloomberg. The number of SpaceX shares offered to Tesla investors is among the factors under discussion, Bloomberg said. The companies have also aimed to close a merger before the end of President Donald Trump's term.

Democrats, who will control Congress starting in January after their midterm wins, have vowed to scrutinize the business and political activities of CEO Elon Musk, according to Bloomberg.

Tesla is scheduled to convene shareholders for its yearly meeting on Dec. 3, according to Bloomberg. Musk holds about 20% of Tesla, but super-voting shares gave him roughly 85% of the voting power at SpaceX, according to Electrek, citing SpaceX's S-1 filing.

The boards of SpaceX and Tesla formed special committees of independent directors to examine a combination, Musk disclosed in October on SpaceX's earnings call. "Because I'm a conflicted party, I can't actually be in the discussions of the special committees," Musk said.

Musk said a merger would be a good idea but that shareholders have to support it. A deal would need the backing of 50% of all Tesla shareholders, not just a majority of votes cast, he said.

The two companies are already linked commercially, among other things through a shared project to construct a Texas chip factory known as Terafab, according to Bloomberg.
