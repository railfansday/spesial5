Peloton is launching its first junk bond in a refinancing of about $600 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Peloton will use the proceeds and cash on hand to repay all outstanding term loans with senior secured notes

Peloton Interactive is launching its first junk bond offering, seeking roughly $600 million to refinance its term loan, according to Bloomberg.

The company announced the offering on Monday. It said it will use the net proceeds, together with cash on hand, to repay all outstanding term loans. The loans were drawn from a term loan arrangement already in place, worth $1 billion over five years, according to Bloomberg.

The notes will be senior secured obligations of Peloton, with interest payable semiannually, the company said. The interest rate and other terms will be set when the offering prices.

The bonds mature in more than five years, according to Bloomberg. The deal is being marketed with a yield in the low-to-mid 9% range, and it is due to be sold this week. Goldman Sachs $GS Group is leading the offering, and a lender call is scheduled for 11 a.m. ET on Monday.

Peloton turned to leveraged loan investors in 2024 for $1 billion, at a rate roughly 6 percentage points over the benchmark, according to Bloomberg. That deal formed one piece of a larger financing package, which also featured a convertible bond.

Peloton has been reducing its debt. After its fiscal first quarter closed on Sept. 30, the company paid down $280 million of its term loan. Gross principal debt outstanding is $1.048 billion, down 32% from $1.534 billion on Dec. 31, 2025.

In that quarter, net income rose to $96 million from $14 million a year earlier, and revenue rose 2% to $559 million.
