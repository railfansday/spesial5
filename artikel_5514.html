2026 BCDA Merit: Agrihood

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Shaped by years of input from Santa Clara, California, leaders and residents, Agrihood’s urban farm and open space gathers people of all ages, backgrounds, and incomes to live side by side while honoring the city’s agricultural heritage.

Developed by The Core Cos., the project’s style and materiality complement the neighborhood’s existing architecture, while landscaping incorporates native and productive plants that suggest local growth and green design.

In 2023, the 1.7 acres of open space supporting physical and mental wellbeing, including Santa Clara’s first ever urban farm, opened in conjunction with 165 LEED-Silver certified affordable homes for seniors and veterans. Created both as an amenity and a shared civic asset, the farm provides fresh produce to low-income seniors.

Award: MeritCategory: Community AmenityArchitect: Steinberg HartBuilder: CORE BuildersDeveloper: The Core Cos.Location: Santa Clara, California

Through thoughtful site design, productive landscape planning, and regenerative agriculture, the development reflects a holistic approach to sustainability with an on-site water tower that supplies reclaimed water to the farm and green roofs and vertical gardens.

Ensuring the farm serves as a source of nourishment and connection, local partners oversee cultivation, harvesting, and educational programming. The project also fosters everyday connections through weekly volunteer opportunities on the farm, a locally owned coffee shop on site, and seasonal live music events
