Netlist Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Net sales rose 149% year over year to $105.1 million and included initial license fees from Samsung

Strengthened balance sheet with cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash rising to $327.7 million at quarter-end

IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Netlist, Inc. (OTCQB: NLST), a leading innovator in advanced memory and storage solutions, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 26, 2026.

"We are pleased to report our third consecutive quarter of significant year-over-year revenue growth and improved profitability led by both product sales and the recognition of license revenue under our Samsung agreement," said Chief Executive Officer, C.K. Hong.  "Year-over-year product revenue growth was driven by strong demand from customers in AI and data center applications."

"Gross margins improved and was driven by our focus on higher margin opportunities," said Gail Sasaki, Chief Financial Officer. "During the quarter, we substantially strengthened our balance sheet with the initial proceeds from our Samsung agreement and generated operating cash flow of $245 million."

C.K. Hong, Chief Executive Officer, and Gail Sasaki, Chief Financial Officer, will host an investor conference call today, November 10, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time to review Netlist's results for the third quarter ended September 26, 2026. The live webcast and archived replay of the call can be accessed for 90 days in the Investors section of Netlist's website at www.netlist.com.

Netlist is a leading innovator in advanced memory and storage solutions. With a rich portfolio of patented technologies, Netlist's inventions are foundational to the advancement of AI computing. To learn more about Netlist, please visit www.netlist.com.
