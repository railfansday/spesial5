Rolls-Royce is announcing a $2 billion stock buyback as demand grows

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The £1.5 billion interim repurchase is due to finish by Feb. 25, 2027 and is part of a £7 billion-£9 billion plan through 2028

Rolls-Royce announced an interim stock buyback of £1.5 billion ($1.99 billion) on Thursday, according to The Wall Street Journal. The company said the buyback will be completed by its full-year results on Feb. 25, 2027.

The interim buyback is part of an existing £7 billion to £9 billion buyback program across 2026 to 2028, the company said. It added that it has completed most of its previously announced £2.5 billion buyback for 2026. Rolls-Royce said the move signals its belief that its cash generation will keep expanding on a durable basis.

The company said its backlog of orders for large engines has swelled to roughly 2,500 units. Orders in the second half include 70 Trent 7000 engines for Avolon and 22 Trent 7000 engines for the Cathay Group. Air China selected 15 Trent XWB-84 engines, and Philippine Airlines selected 18 Trent XWB-97 engines.

The company said large engine flying hours were up 4% from a year earlier over the first 10 months of 2026, at 114% of the 2019 level.

In Power Systems, order intake in the first nine months of the year rose 62% from a year earlier. The order backlog stood at a record £8.6 billion at the end of September, up from £8.0 billion at the half-year mark. Rolls-Royce said it is finalizing a multi-year deal to provide a hyperscaler with backup engines, together with a framework agreement that runs through 2031.

Rolls-Royce also held to its full-year targets, forecasting free cash flow of £3.8 billion to £4.0 billion and underlying operating profit of £4.7 billion to £4.9 billion. It plans to reassess its medium-term goals alongside its annual results due in February, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Rolls-Royce stock rose about 2.5% in early trading in London. The stock has gained 20% so far this year.

The company raised its full-year guidance in July as demand grew across its civil aerospace, defense and power systems divisions. First-half underlying operating profit was £2.5 billion, up 46% from a year earlier.
