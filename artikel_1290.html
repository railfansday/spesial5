Three clubs interested in January transfer for Juventus winger Zhegrova

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Opportunities are still proving hard to come by for Edon Zhegrova at Juventus, and reports in Italy claim that three different clubs are interested in offering him a way out ahead of the January transfer window.

Zhegrova joined Juventus in a deal worth €15m plus add-ons at the end of the summer transfer window in 2025, but almost 15 months later, he has still not appeared in the Bianconeri starting line-up.

Fitness issues limited him to just 19 league appearances last term, all off the bench and his nine appearances in 2026-27 have all come as a substitute as well. The 82 minutes that he played during Kosovo’s 0-0 draw with Israel on Saturday was the longest run-out that Zhegrova has managed in one game in well over two years.

The Kosovo international could be among the players that Juventus decide to sacrifice in order to make room for new additions in January, and reports from TMW claim that the player himself is interested in leaving in search of more consistent playing time.

When all fit and available, Zhegrova faces stiff competition for game time from the likes of Kenan Yildiz, Francisco Conceicao, Nico Gonzalez, Kerim Alajbegovic, Jeremie Boga and the versatile Weston McKennie.

According to Sunday’s report from TMW, at least three different clubs are interested in offering Zhegrova a way out of Juventus. Saudi Pro League side Al-Ittihad had reportedly made an enquiry at the end of the summer transfer window and remain interested following an injury to young winger Roger Fernandes.

There are options in Europe as well, as both Monaco in Ligue 1 and Besiktas in the Turkish Super Lig are also said to be interested.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
