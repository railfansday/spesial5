Buffalo Bills elevate a pair of veterans from practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Grappling with injuries on defense, the Buffalo Bills have elevated linebacker Shaq Thompson and cornerback Tre'Davious White from their practice squad ahead of Sunday's matchup with the Miami Dolphins.

Thompson, 32, played in 12 regular-season games for Buffalo last season, starting 6. He totaled 56 tackles, 1 sack, 6 tackles for loss, 2 pass breakups, and 1 fumble recovery. In Buffalo's two playoff games, Thompson was excellent, notching 13 total tackles, a team-high 10 solo tackles, 2 tackles for loss, and an interception. The veteran is a former first-round pick of the Carolina Panthers, having been the No. 25 overall selection in the 2015 NFL Draft.

Thompson will have a role this week in place of Dorian Williams, who is out with a calf injury that he suffered late in last week's win over the New York Jets. Whether Thompson plays a significant role on defense or not is yet to be determined, but at worst, he provides strong depth for the Bills. Rookie Kaleb Elarms-Orr is the other likely option to start next to Terrel Bernard.

White, 31, also spent last season with the Bills. He played a significant part in the team's defense, starting 16 games in the regular season across from Christian Benford. It's thanks to Benford's injured groin that White finds himself elevated this week, as he'll either start across from rookie Davison Igbinosun or serve as depth behind the second-year man that he started over last season, Maxwell Hairston.

During the 2025 season, White totaled 40 tackles, 10 pass breakups, and an interception. In the postseason, White started both games, totaling six tackles and three pass breakups. White originally entered the NFL in 2017 as Buffalo's first-round draft choice that year, and after spending the 2024 season with the Baltimore Ravens and Los Angeles Rams, he returned home and played well last season. White allowed just 51% of the pass thrown when he was the closest man in coverage to be completed, holding opposing quarterbacks to a 71.1 quarterback rating when targeting him.

The Bills and Dolphins are a 1 p.m. EDT start on Sunday. Each team's official inactive lists will be released 90 minutes prior to kickoff.
