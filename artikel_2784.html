Puka Nacua suspended three games, will serve suspension while on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Puka Nacua was already set to miss the Rams’ next three games while on injured reserve.

The NFL announced on Thursday that Nacua has been suspended for three games for violating the personal conduct policy.

But Nacua can serve that suspension while on injured reserve, so he won’t need to miss any more time than was already scheduled. The difference is now he will not be paid as he recovers from sprains to two ligaments in his knee and turf toe.

The suspension will cost Nacua roughly $950,000 from his salary.

Multiple reporters also note the league told Nacua that any further violation of the personal conduct policy — including failure to satisfy conditions of his diversion program — could lead to additional discipline, up to and including banishment.

Nacua has caught 70 passes for 715 yards with four touchdowns in seven games this season. He’s also taken seven carries for 41 yards with one touchdown.

Nacua is eligible to be reinstated after Los Angeles’ Week 14 matchup against the 49ers. It’s currently unclear if he’ll even be healthy at that point. If he’s not activated off of IR, he’ll be able to receive his game checks from Week 15 and beyond.
