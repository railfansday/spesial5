Cowboys injuries: Dak Prescott (hand) full participant Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Food, family, and of course, football. The Dallas Cowboys play on Thanksgiving Day this year 59th time in their history, and for just third time will play the Philadelphia Eagles on the holiday.

This game carries extra intrigue as Joe Milton is coming back to Dallas to face his former team. Milton led the Eagles to a stunning win over the Pittsburgh Steelers yesterday and could spoil everyone's turkey if he manages to pull off the win over Dallas.

However, he'll have to do it without some key players available. After Monday's report, here's the status of both teams.

For Dallas, Jaishawn Barham (Hip), Tyler Booker (Ankle), Dee Winters (Knee), and Jake Ferguson (Knee) were all listed as DNP. Coach Schottenheimer is optimistic that Winters can play after sustaining a knee contusion, but it'll be something to monitor this week.

Jalen Thompson (Hamstring), Joey Porter Jr. (Foot), and Dak Prescott (Hand) were listed as full participants. Thompson has a strong chance return Thursday after a lengthy stint on injured reserve.

Meanwhile, P.J. Locke (Concussion) and Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) were limited.

As for Philadelphia, Smael Mondon Jr. (Ankle) and Quinyon Mitchell (Hamstring) were listed as DNP. Saquon Barkley (Arm) was limited to day and Jalen Carter was a full participant with a quad injury.
