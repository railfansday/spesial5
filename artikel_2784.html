The Packers have one of the hardest NFL schedules to end 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

According to ESPN's Football Power Index (FPI), the Green Bay Packers have played an average-ish NFL schedule this season, as they're ranked 16th in strength of schedule league-wide. Coming out of the bye, though, their slate will get a lot tougher leading up to the postseason.

Per FPI, the Packers have the fifth-hardest schedule in the league moving forward, only behind the Los Angeles Rams, Seattle Seahawks, Miami Dolphins and Kansas City Chiefs. For perspective, the other NFC North teams rank sixth (Chicago Bears), 16th (Detroit Lions) and 22nd (Minnesota Vikings).

At the moment, Green Bay sits in the seventh spot in the NFC playoff race with a 6-4 record, a three-way tie in which the Packers hold an advantage over the Seahawks and the Rams. Currently, Green Bay has a 37 percent playoff chance, according to The Athletic's playoff simulator, but a lot will change based on their upcoming game in Los Angeles.

If the Packers win that game, their playoff chances jump up to 59 percent. If they lose, they will drop to 24 percent. That's a huge amount of playoff leverage, because Green Bay is fighting to hold onto that seventh seed against a team with the same record.

Yes, the Rams game means more than the two divisional games left on their schedule, mostly because the simulations only give the Packers a one percent chance to win the division at this point. Being 6-4 in mid-November in one of the toughest divisions in NFL history will do that.

Against non-division opponents, the NFC North is 21-4 this season, an 84 percent win percentage. The best mark for a division in NFL history was the 1984 AFC West, which posted a 78 percent mark. For what it's worth, the best all-time full-season division in NFC history was the 2024 NFC North, when they collectively won 75 percent of their non-division games. Even compared to 2024, the 2026 North is a step ahead.

The Packers still control their own destiny, but this upcoming Rams game is a cornerstone to that path. If they end up losing to Los Angeles, they're going to need some help to get into the postseason — even as a wild-card team. On top of that, they'll need to make up that distance while playing one of the toughest schedules in the league.

Things are shaping up for a very competitive race for a playoff spot between the Rams, Seahawks and Packers, who all have top-five schedules coming out of their Week 11 byes.
