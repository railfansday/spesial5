Strength in numbers carries Warriors to 88-87 victory over Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Golden State Warriors took advantage of a rest advantage in their schedule to end a five-game losing streak and inch back above .500. The Dubs defeated the Boston Celtics 88-87, improving to 9-8 on the season. Golden State was back home with a couple days off after an arduous five-game road trip. The Celtics, on the other hand, were playing their fifth game in seven days, and lost 97-96 to the Kings on Saturday night in Sacramento.

Draymond Green, perhaps playing with a little extra motivation after some recent internet skirmishes with Eddie Johnson, came out particularly aggressive offensively. Green was 3-for-4 from three in the first quarter and helped the Warriors build a 27-20 lead at the end of the first quarter.

After some atrocious defensive showings on the road trip, the Warriors were consistent on that end of the floor all night. A wore out and shorthanded Celtics (who rested Paul George on the second half of a back-to-back) seemed a step slow. The lone exception, Jayson Tatum, who rested in last night's game.

Tatum was clearly the best player on the floor all night. He was dominant in the first half scoring 23 points, and single-handedly kept the Celtics from a double-digit deficit. Despite the improved defensive performance, the Warriors offense still looked in the way fans have come to expect with Steph Curry out. The Dubs missed several open looks and a handful of G-League worthy turnovers.

The Celtics took the lead early in the third quarter, but Golden State was able to keep things from getting out of hand. Tatum cooled off after his scorching hot start and the Warriors capitalized on some success grabbing offensive rebounds to keep within striking distance.

The Dubs trailed 75-69 heading into the final quarter, but also had an opportunity to get back in the game with Tatum on the bench. Gui Santos sparked a mini run for the Warriors with back-to-back threes before back-to-back layups by Yaxel Lendeborg gave the team an 81-79 lead. Tatum returned with 6:23 left in regulation, and the Celtics trailing 83-81.

The lead bounced between both teams over the next few mintues and the Warriors led 88-86 at the start of the final two minutes. The Dubs got two offensive rebounds to get a few shots at the apple to try and extend the team's lead. Coming out of the chaos, Brandin Podziemski knocked down a corner-three that seemed to put the game out of reach. However, Podz flopped on the shot trying to get a four-point play. The refs originally obliged his attempt, but the Celtics challenged and overturned the call. The shot was erased and the Celtics got the ball back.

Tatum drew a shooting foul on the other end, and likely had an and-one dunk if not for an excellent uncredited block by Santos. Tatum split the free throws, leaving the Warriors ahead by one. Still, the Dubs offense could not put the game away, and ultimately committed a shot clock violation without getting a shot up.

The Celtics in-bounded the ball out of a timeout and took advantage of a miscommunication between Green and Lendeborg that left Tatum open. However, he missed the three after Green recovered in time to break his rhythm.

The Celtics trapped Podziemski and rather than hoping for a foul call, he used the Warriors final timeout. Head coach Steve Kerr drew up an in-bound play, but an unexpected flash from White threw off Green and led to a turnover. White turned down an open look and the Celtics called a timeout to reset the possession with 8.2 seconds remaining. The Dubs would need one more stop against the best player in the game.

Tatum went right at Green, but Draymond's defense is the reason he's heading to the Hall of Fame. Tatum settled for a contested free-throw line fadeaway and missed.

Both teams had surprisingly egalitarian offensive statlines, with 11 players on both teams scoring at least two points. Tatum finished with a game-high 26 points alongside 10 rebounds, 6 assists, 4 turnovers, and 1 steal, but did not make a field goal in the second half.

Podziemski led the Warriors with 18 points and added 7 rebounds, but also committed 6 turnovers. Lendeborg had 12 points, 8 rebounds, and 3 assists in 34 minutes. Charles Bassey recorded 13 points, 9 rebounds, 3 assists, and 3 blocks. Santos was also incredibly valuable off the bench, and closing, finishing with 13 points, 5 rebounds, 3 assists, and 1 steal on 5-for-8 shooting.
