49ers beat Raiders 38-17, improve to 7-1 in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The 49ers may have stumbled just before their bye. But they rolled to victory after their week off.

San Francisco beat Las Vegas 38-17 on Sunday with some strong defensive play.

The 49ers picked up five takeaways off the Raiders — intercepting Kirk Cousins three times and recovering a pair of fumbles from Brock Bowers and Michael Mayer.

San Francisco dominated the first half with three touchdown drives of at least 12 plays, building a 21-10 advantage at the half.

That lead got extended on the first play of the second half, with Kirk Cousins throwing a pick-six to linebacker Jaden Dugger to make the score 28-10.

The Raiders got back on the board with Brock Bowers’ 21-yard touchdown catch. But the fumbles from Mayer and Bowers kept the team off the board in the third quarter.

Las Vegas ended up with a giveaway on each of their last four possessions and five of the team’s last six in the contest.

Brock Purdy finished 17-of-25 for 232 yards with two touchdowns and an interception. He also rushed for 40 yards on seven attempts before Mac Jones came in for mop-up duty late in the fourth quarter.

Cousins finished 22-of-32 for 216 yards with one touchdown and three interceptions. Bowers finished with seven receptions for 111 yards with a TD.

But Las Vegas’ run game continues to be a non-factor, with Ashton Jeanty tallying just 25 yards on six carries.

With the win, the 49ers are now 7-1. They will be on the road to face the Cowboys in Week 10.

The Raiders, who have lost five of their last six, will be back at home to face the Seahawks next Sunday.
