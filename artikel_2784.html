Ja'Marr Chase explodes for 142 receiving yards in first 25 minutes of play in Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Bengals wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase is having an eye-popping first half today in Madrid.

Chase caught eight passes for 142 yards and a touchdown in the first 25 minutes of play against the Falcons, helping the Bengals take a 21-10 lead over the Falcons.

It’s the most receiving yards that Chase has ever had in the first half of a game in his career.

The rest of the Bengals’ offense hasn’t done anything special, with a total of 91 yards on any play that isn’t a pass to Chase, but they haven’t needed to. Chase is taking over the game.

It’s going to be a long flight home from Spain for the Falcons’ defense if they can’t find a way to stop Chase from running free through their secondary.
