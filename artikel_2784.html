Report: Titans' Carnell Tate expected to play against Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One of the biggest questions for the Tennessee Titans ahead of their Week 11 matchup is whether or not rookie wide receiver Carnell Tate will return after missing the past two games with a shoulder injury, and per at least one NFL insider it looks like that will be the case.

On Sunday morning, NFL Insider Ian Rapoport took to X to report that Tate is expected to suit up against the Dallas Cowboys.

This is a big boost for the Titans' offense, who will need all hands on deck against a struggling Cowboys' defense in what could be a high-scoring affair.

Despite missing two games, the NFL Offensive Rookie of the Month for October is the Titans' leading active wideout, with Wan'Dale Robinson now on injured reserve. Tate has recorded 34 receptions for 413 receiving yards (12.1 yards per catch) and one touchdown across seven games and has the potential for another breakout against the Cowboys.
