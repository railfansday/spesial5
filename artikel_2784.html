Georgia 34, South Carolina 6: You Gotta Asphyxiate Your Clunkers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kirby Smart's Georgia Bulldogs did not have their best stuff this evening in Columbia.

In the beginning that looked like a problem. In the end, that fact was a little comforting. Why?

Because even though they missed some tackles, didn't take control of the line of scrimmage until late, and squandered some plum scoring opportunities, they were still four touchdowns better than a top 25 South Carolina team, pulling away with 31 unanswered points for a 34-6 victory.

Things started off with that queasy feeling Bulldog fans have felt before in Williams-Bruce. The Gamecocks matriculated the ball steadily down the field to score on their opening drive. Georgia managed a field goal to tie it, then gave up another agonizing drive punctuated by a pair of LaNorris Sellers third down runs that ended in three more points. The Gamecocks matriculated fans were loud and proud. The Bulldog offensive line was catching more than driving. And Sellers was sharp enough that it was hard to imagine he'd been unable to play a week before.

But as so often happens in football games coached by Kirby Smart, things turned in the middle eight minutes.That's when the patient pressure of the UGA offensive line began to pay dividends. Georgia engineered a 10 play, 88 yard touchdown drive to take a 10-6 lead. The Red and Black then punched in another Frazier touchdown on their first possession of the second half to run the advantage to 17-6. While it wasn't quite over at that point, the Bulldogs were never not in control from that point forward.

The Garnet and Black churned out 123 yards of total offense on their two first half scoring drives. They scrounged out only 95 yards of offense in the remainder of the game. The Athenians on the other hand poured it on, getting better as the evening wore on. The Dawgs finished with 421 yards of total offense, led by a sterling effort from Gunner Stockton. The Tough Guy from Tiger turned in a 19 of 23 passing performance for 281 yards and a touchdown. Critically he avoided any turnovers, in the process avoiding the one thing that could have given the 'Cocks the momentum they needed.

While Stockton didn't find any Gamecocks with the ball, he found a whole lot of Bulldogs, completing passes to nine different receivers. Jaden Reddell exploded for 88 yards on 3 receptions, including a 66 yarder. Talyn "Talons" Taylor made another highlight reel catch of 37 yards. Georgia ran right at the Gamecocks then rope-a-doped the Gamecocks with deep strikes, and once those started hitting the result became a fait accompli.

LaNorris Sellers appeared to be the Gamecocks' only hope. In the end, he was a non-factor. Sellers was an anemic 10 of 18 passing for 87 yards and a red zone interception that killed any remaining hope of a comeback.He added 24 yards on 9 rushing attempts, but had only a single rush of over 10 yards. I think it's safe to say the Dawgs have turned one of the crippling deficiencies that allowed them to lose to Auburn, stopping running quarterbacks, into a true strength.

Georgia now heads into Clean, Old-Fashioned Hate having locked up their sixth straight SEC Championship Game appearance. Before heading into the College Football Playoff the Dawgs have in order the game Bulldogs fans hate more than any other to lose, then the one their coach perhaps wants more than just about any other to win. The Classic City Canines will have to play better than they did this evening to win it all.

But tonight was a powerful reminder that this team's middling effort is head and shoulders above even a decent football team. A well-worn college football cliche holds that you ""have to win your clunkers." That's not entirely accurate. You can also just smother them slowly but surely with a display of unrelenting brute force, as the Red and Black did this evening. The Dawgs remain the only team capable of beating the Dawgs. Until later…
