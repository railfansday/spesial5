Game Preview #20: Wolves vs Grizzlies Game Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Happy Thanksgiving, everyone. As your holiday winds down, I'm pleased to bring you a brief preview of the next Minnesota Timberwolves game, which is against the Memphis Grizzlies.

Who: Minnesota Timberwolves (14-5) at Memphis Grizzlies (14-5)

This matchup will be the final game of the group stage for the NBA Cup, and the winner will get an automatic place in the cup playoffs. The Wolves have yet to reach that stage of the tournament, so putting together a win to make it there would be yet another great chapter within the storybook of this resurgent season.

A loss would not be ideal, though it would not be the end of the world as far as the Cup is concerned. Minnesota is in a pretty good position to make it in as a wildcard team over the Denver Nuggets based upon point differential. Obviously winning any game is the best-case scenario, though the Wolves have multiple paths towards competing for the increasingly-coveted NBA Cup title.

Regardless of Cup implications, this should be yet another good matchup between two teams that have been consistently strong in the Western Conference. Minnesota took the last matchup at home 104-95 which featured strong performances from Jaden McDaniels, Ayo Dosunmu, Jonathan Kuminga, and Terrence Shannon Jr. The Wolves will need to keep up their positive momentum from all of those guys if they look to clinch the group and pass the Grizzlies in the Western Conference standings.

That said, it may still be tough sledding for an already-injured team that may be dealing with another one for the foreseeable future. Keeping that in mind, here are a couple things I'm looking for in the game tomorrow:

Watching LaMelo Ball gingerly limp off of the court against the Oklahoma City Thunder was not a welcome sight. We got the win, but everyone is hoping that his turned ankle does not turn out (pun intended) to be anything serious.

It would not be out of the realm of possibility for Ball to miss this game. However, the team that used to boast significant guard depth is looking considerably weaker if we have the quadrio of Ball, Anthony Edwards, Donte DiVincenzo, and Jaylen Clark all sidelined.

As it were, this means that Bones Hyland should get a sizable helping of playing time. I would not be shocked to see G-League stud Zyon Pullin also get a fair bit of run to ease what would otherwise be a hefty serving of minutes for Hyland and Dosunmu. We've learned this season just how important adequate point guard play is with the addition of Ball, and I do not think Chris Finch would overwork the two guards left that are capable of stuffing that position.

On the other side of the scorer's table, the Grizzlies also have their share of injuries to deal with. Rookie forward Cameron Boozer is still out with injury, and Zach Edey is a game time decision after picking up a knock in their last game against the Los Angeles Clippers. With Jerami Grant, GG Jackson, and Scottie Pippen Jr. all still on the injured list as well, Memphis has had plenty of guys step up and give the team quality minutes in their absences. They may need more help if they are looking to defeat the Wolves on Friday.

What can't Jaden McDaniels do? Nothing, apparently. The guy has been a machine as of late, averaging 26 points per game while shooting the ball well over 67% from the floor and 69% from deep across his last five games. Those are some nice numbers.

On top of that, Jaden is continuing to put the clamps on opposing teams' best players. He locked up Shai Gilgeous-Alexander at the end of the game last evening, and McDaniels never appears to tire across the course of the game. He's doing it all for the Timberwolves, and that production has been necessary in the wake of the Edwards injury.

There is a legitimate argument to be made regarding Jaden as an All Star candidate this season. That argument will be bolstered if he can continue to control games with Ball sidelined. It's not McDaniels's fault that he is in this position to carry the team on his back, though his admirable performances are earning attention league-wide.

Next man up is always the mentality, and Jaden has been doing his part in keeping this injury-addled team afloat. Adding another injury to that mix certainly changes the dynamic of the team, though McDaniels has been playing strong enough that he should be able to overcome the absence of Ball.

I've mentioned in past articles that while Ball hasn't had the most statistical significance across the last couple weeks, he is unquestionably controlling the game and making the offense easier for everyone else. With him out, someone else will have to step up to make some shots and draw the defense in their direction. That person should be Terrence Shannon Jr.

He's had a few up-and-down performances lately, and his last game against the Thunder saw him score only eight points on 2/10 shooting while turning the ball over three times. Minnesota won the game, but they will need more production out of TJ with Ball being out against a tough opponent in Memphis.

A brief positive for TJ could be conceptualized with the news of Ball's absence, as the ball will likely be in the hands of Shannon more during his minutes. Last year gave the film buffs tons of evidence about how effective TJ can be as a scorer if he is initiating the offense. If he has more opportunity to control the lineups when he is deployed, he should be able to find a rhythm quickly.

Efficient scoring performances from TJ are always welcome sights. He has already been an integral part of this team with the current injury situation, and that importance will be inflated with yet another injury to a key player that plays heavy minutes. Expect Finch to lean on Shannon early and often in this contest.

This one is going to be a nail-biter, but I can't bet against the Wolves no matter who we play against. Expect TSJ to have a strong scoring night and, ideally, a grand re-opening of the Bone Zone. I think the Wolves take this one, but not by much.
