Tarik Skubal return to Detroit as a Met: When will it happen?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Are Detroit Tigers fans ready to see Tarik Skubal in another team's uniform in the Motor City (again)?

With the left-hander agreeing to a record $450 million free-agent deal (over nine seasons) with the New York Mets, we can start counting the days till the ex-Tiger's second return to Detroit as an opponent.

Skubal made a start at Comerica Park last season after he was dealt from the Tigers to the Los Angeles Dodgers at the trade deadline. On Aug. 28, Skubal allowed one run while striking out seven with no walks over six innings in a 2-1 Dodgers win.

The crowd at Comerica Park had mixed reactions, with a pregame ovation followed by mid-game boos.

Of course, that was back when Skubal was merely the two-time reigning AL Cy Young winner and fans had hopes for a reunion in free agency. (It was also before we learned Skubal reclaimed the espresso machine he gifted the Tigers clubhouse.)

So when will fans at Fifth Third Park – remember, the Tigers' home has a new moniker for 2027 – get to let Skubal know how they feel about his new deal, which makes him the highest paid pitcher in MLB history?

A quick glance at the Mets' schedule shows them set to visit Detroit early in the season, with a three-game series set for Fifth Third Park on April 9-11.

And assuming Skubal is the Mets' Opening Day starter, and that they stick with a five-man rotation, and that they don't skip any starts … Skubal looks likely to get the start in the series finale, slated for 1:40 p.m., Sunday, April 11. (He could even face two of the players acquired in the deal that sent him to L.A., as right-hander River Ryan is a candidate for the Opening Day rotation and outfielder Zyhir Hope will have a chance to claim a roster spot in spring training.)

Of course, all this is assuming that the impending lockout by MLB owners – made even more impending now that the Mets have stepped on MLB's salary scale even further – is all cleared up with a new collective bargaining agreement in time to start the season on time, on March 26.

That may be the biggest assumption of all.
