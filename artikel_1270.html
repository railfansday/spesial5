Man Utd In Talks Over January Deal for Calvin Bassey as Pochettino's First Signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United are in talks over a deal to sign Fulham defender Calvin Bassey in January as Mauricio Pochettino's first signing, according to The Athletic.

The Red Devils have had a horrific start to their season so far with just one win in their opening 11 Premier League games, leaving them 19th in the table, while new manager Mauricio Pochettino is yet to win a game since taking over from Michael Carrick earlier this month.

United have got the worst defensive record in Europe's top five leagues this season, having conceded 36 goals in all competitions this season, and that has seen the club prioritise bringing in new defensive options for the second half of the campaign.

According to a report by The Athletic's Laurie Whitwell, the club have now opened talks over a deal to bring in Nigeria international during the January window.

Bassey has become a key player for Fulham since arriving at the club, and was handed the captain's armband by new manager Alvaro Arbeloa in the summer, proving how highly they rate him. His current contract at Craven Cottage is due to expire in 2028, and the London side are keen to give him a new deal to reflect his new status.

But Man Utd are holding talks with the relevant people about getting a deal over the line to bring him to Old Trafford. The 26-year-old is seen as an excellent one-on-one defender who can drive into midfield with the ball, and the belief is that he could fit in well with how Pochettino wants to play.

Fulham currently have no intention of allowing him to leave mid-season, but if United push hard enough then it's possible that there is a deal to be done, with Bassey likely to be open to making the switch.

Bassey has previously played in the Champions League and Europa League with Rangers and Ajax, while he has earned 49 caps for the Super Eagles.

Bassey has featured heavily for Fulham this season, and played a major role in their goal when they played against Man Utd earlier this season, as they drew 1-1.

Earlier this year, United forward Bryan Mbeumo also heavily praised the Nigerian defender, admitting that he was one of the toughest opponents in his career.

“Calvin Bassey, damn. I think he really stood out in games. Even the two previous games against them, he was solid,” Mbeumo said.

“Yeah, Calvin Bassey is a beast, the guy is a beast. He’s too strong, it’s crazy, and on top of that he’s fast too. It’s not cool to play against him.”

With major question marks about the futures of Lisandro Martinez, Harry Maguire, Matthijs De Ligt, Ayden Heaven, and Luke Shaw, Bassey would be a signing that could plug a lot of gaps and bring some fresh blood into a defence that is desperate for upgrades.
