Conte: ‘I would love to join an organised club, rather than always adding value’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Antonio Conte insists he would love ‘before ending my career to take over a club that is already organised’ after years of ‘building value’ from troubled situations like Juventus, Chelsea, Inter and Napoli.

The coach is currently between jobs after walking away from Napoli in two years where he won the Serie A title and the Supercoppa Italiana.

There were reports he could come to Manchester United or even return to his old side Juve, but he is in no rush for his next role.

Speaking to the Cronache di Spogliatoio podcast, Conte did outline his greatest strength, and why he perhaps eager to cast it aside and do something different next time.

“There is one thing I’d love to do before ending my career as a coach, which is to take over a club that is already organised,” said Conte.

“It would be like stepping into a Formula 1 car in pole position. It never happened in my career, but evidently that is just the way it goes for me. I always arrived at complicated situations and was fortunate, but also deserve credit, for taking a team back to victory.

“Often, it involved returning them to their history, a dimension that perhaps they had forgotten.”

It all sounds like the scenario at Manchester United, who have not won the Premier League since 2013, following in the footsteps of other clubs he went through.

“It happened at Juventus, who got back to winning after seven years, Inter hadn’t won the Scudetto for 10, and Chelsea were coming off a disastrous season when I arrived, finishing in 10th place, but then won the Premier League with me.

“The same was true at Napoli, where they had finished 10th, then I arrived and took them to victory.”

That also means Conte brings with him certain expectations, something he is quick to point out in press conferences from the very start.

“It is inevitable that when you arrive with a curriculum like this, you are accompanied by big expectations. People and the media expect you to win. I still remember when I arrived at Napoli, a team that finished 10th, a beautiful city, their objective was to qualify for the Champions League.

“I thought I could just enjoy my work and the city for a while, but the moment I signed, the media completely changed the message. I don’t know how many other coaches would be treated that way.”

In fairness, Napoli had won the Scudetto the previous season under Luciano Spalletti, then limped to 10th place after going through a series of coaches, but retained much the same team.

When it comes to Inter, Conte also argues he laid the foundations for all those who followed him.

“I arrived and the Appiano Gentile ground was disastrous, the pitches had big holes in, there was always an alibi from the staff. Inter invested in revamping the pitch, and a month later it was ruined again,” said the coach.

“The club spent €120,000 and in those two years, I also asked for them to set up somewhere to sleep. If you look at the Inter training ground today, it is a gem.

“There are many clubs where there is a before me, and an after me, and those who come later find this.”

Conte also discussed the player sales that sides like Inter made thanks to his development of certain players.

“I become an investment for clubs that pays off. We signed Romelu Lukaku and Achraf Hakimi, then sold them on for €120m to Chelsea and €70m to PSG. I added value to the squad.”
