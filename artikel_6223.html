Avianca Adds New U.S. Route

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Colombian flag carrier Avianca is adding a new connection to the U.S. Northeast.

Starting Feb. 2, 2027, the airline will offer service between Barranquilla and New York-JFK.

Flights will operate four times weekly using Airbus A320-family aircraft, each with up to 180 seats.

“The launch of this nonstop route to New York reaffirms our commitment to [Barranquilla] and the entire Colombian Caribbean region,” Avianca President Gabriel Oliva said in a statement. “We want to continue connecting regions with major international markets, making travel easier, supporting tourism, and contributing to Colombia’s economic development.”

Avianca currently serves JFK from Bogota, Cartagena, Medellín, Pereira, Colombia, Guatemala City, San Salvador, El Salvador, Quito, and Guayaquil. From Barranquilla, it flies to Miami, Bogota, Medellín, and Cali.
