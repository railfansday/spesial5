World of Hyatt Card Limited Time Bonus Offer: Earn Four Free Night Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

We’ve just seen a new limited time welcome offer rolled out on the World of Hyatt Credit Card, making it the ideal time to apply. I’d consider this to be one of the most lucrative hotel credit cards out there — it’s a product you don’t get just for the bonus, but instead, for the long term value that it offers.

Let’s take a look at the details of the card bonus and eligibility requirements, as well as the long term value.

Link: Learn more about the World of Hyatt Credit Card bonus offer

The World of Hyatt Credit Card is currently offering a limited time welcome bonus that consists of four Category 1-4 free night awards after spending $5,000 within the first three months.

World of Hyatt Category 4 properties retail for up to 25,000 points per night, so the absolute maximum value of this offer is 100,000 points. However, I of course wouldn’t value it that highly. That’s because these free night awards expire 12 months after they’re issued, and you also can’t top off these awards to redeem at more expensive properties. So actually maxing out the value of these isn’t easy.

One thing I should mention is that while I don’t love the lack of flexibility of this welcome offer, it’s significantly better than the standard bonus, which tends to be lackluster. Obviously Chase and Hyatt know that this is a card people will get for the long term value, rather than just for the bonus.

For what it’s worth, based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider World of Hyatt points to be worth 1.4 cents each.

If you’re interested in applying for the World of Hyatt Credit Card, the eligibility rules for the bonus are pretty straightforward. This product is available to you if you don’t have this exact card, and haven’t received a new cardmember bonus on this card in the past 24 months.

So that means you could potentially earn the bonus if you’ve had the card in the past. Furthermore, eligibility is unrelated to whether you have the World of Hyatt Business Credit Card (learn more).

On top of that, Chase’s general application rules apply, including that you can typically be approved for at most two Chase cards every 30 days, and the Chase 5/24 rule is also worth considering (you may not be approved if you’ve opened five or more new card accounts in the past 24 months).

There’s not a consistent credit score you need to be approved for this card, though I’d hope to have a score in the “good” to “excellent” range, ideally 700+ points. However, people with lower scores have been approved, and people with higher scores have been rejected.

If you’re looking for a Hyatt co-branded credit card but want a business card instead, then the World of Hyatt Business Credit Card is for you. The card is currently offering a bonus of 70,000 points after spending $7,000 within the first three months.

At my valuation of 1.4 cents per point, that’s roughly $980 in value, which more than offsets the $199 annual fee in year one.

Eligibility is straightforward, as you can’t currently hold this specific card, and you can’t have received a welcome bonus on it within the past 24 months. Having the personal World of Hyatt Credit Card doesn’t affect your eligibility, as you can hold both cards and earn bonuses on each. Standard Chase application rules apply.

However, the bonus isn’t the main reason you get this card. Instead, it’s about the long term value proposition, including elite nights toward status, an anniversary free night award, etc. Furthermore, it’s a card that’s worth spending $15,000 on per year, if you can swing it, so you can earn a second free night award and six more elite nights toward status.
