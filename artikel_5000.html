Next Technology (NXTT) Sells 500 Bitcoin to Fund AI Push

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Next Technology Holding (Nasdaq: NXTT) sold about 500 bitcoin last week and said it will put the money toward artificial intelligence projects, the latest public company to trim its bitcoin treasury as prices slide.

The Japan-based software firm disclosed in a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Tuesday that it sold the coins on Nov. 21 for about $29.22 million. That works out to an average price of $58,468.11 per bitcoin, net of fees and expenses.

The sale leaves NXTT with 5,333 BTC. The company was paid in tether (USDT), the dollar-pegged stablecoin, rather than in dollars.

NXTT said it plans to use the proceeds for research and development of its AI-related initiatives, including its AI software products and services, as well as for general corporate purposes.

The sale is the first under a program the company’s board approved on Oct. 30. That program allows NXTT to sell or pledge its bitcoin “from time to time in one or more tranches,” through exchange sales, block trades or loans that use the coins as collateral, with the stated aim of “reducing risk exposure and enhancing liquidity.”

The filing also repeats a line from the company’s latest quarterly report that marks a clear break from its earlier approach. NXTT “does not currently expect to purchase additional Bitcoin,” the company said, adding that it has adjusted its strategy to focus on managing the coins it already holds.

The program has no expiration date and doesn’t require the company to sell any set amount. The board can change, pause or end it at any time. As of Nov. 24, NXTT said it had not pledged any of its bitcoin as collateral.

NXTT, which is incorporated in Wyoming but based in Saitama Prefecture, Japan, describes itself as running two strategies: AI-enabled software development and holding bitcoin. It keeps substantially all of its coins with Japan-based institutional custodians, according to its quarterly filing.

The company held 833 BTC at the end of 2024 and added 5,000 BTC in 2025, bringing its total to 5,833 BTC. Its filing puts the cost basis of those coins at about $183.1 million, or roughly $31,400 per bitcoin, meaning last week’s sale still came in well above what the company paid on average.

Falling prices have still hit the books. NXTT reported a net loss of $32.2 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with net income of $295.1 million a year earlier. The company said the swing came mainly from a drop in the fair value of its digital assets.

The company has also been reshaping its leadership around AI. Earlier this month it named Chan Phang Chuen, a former platform architect focused on computing infrastructure and AI inference, as chief technical officer after Hongliang Liu resigned from the role.

NXTT joins a growing list of public companies selling bitcoin to fund other businesses. Ionic Digital sold 737 bitcoin this month to pay for a data center buildout, and Rumble sold two-thirds of its bitcoin as its AI cloud business delivered its first profitable quarter.

Miners have been selling too. Bitdeer sold all 281 bitcoin it mined last week and now holds none. Not every treasury company is stepping back, though. Strategy added 457 bitcoin for $29.6 million during the same week NXTT was selling.

Bitcoin has struggled to hold $60,000 this week amid heavy outflows from U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs, and NXTT sold its coins near those levels.

The company said the proceeds from any future sales or pledges will depend on market conditions and the timing of execution.
