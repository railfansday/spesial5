Your Horoscope for the New Moon in Scorpio

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

This lunation is bringing to the light everything that was done in the dark.

New Moons are a time for rejuvenation and manifestation. If you have a lack in your life, then this is the time the collective will work to fill it, whether through a new experience, person, or item. We must make space for beginnings and foolish behavior during new moons. Mind you, we’re all fools when beginning something new, so don’t be scared to get experimental.

For newbies, a New Moon is when the moon conjuncts the sun to the same degree. New moons are for setting intentions and goals to manifest. Take the theme of each new moon (manifestation period) as inspiration to bring forth the best version of yourself! The New Moon in Scorpio arrives Monday, November 9, 2026, at 2:02 a.m. ET, at 17 degrees Scorpio. I'm actually such a big fan of this new moon, because we're going to see a lot emerging from it—a lot more clarity. Things that were done in the dark are going to be exposed now. But don't expect everything to come out all at once, because new moons are dark for a reason. Look at the sky: it is not bright, it is a dark moon because the Moon sits between Earth and the Sun, meaning the side facing Earth receives no direct sunlight. In astrological terms, that means our emotions are too close to our sense of purpose, that we are not able to recognize how our feelings and ego are influencing life on Earth. We are going to see things unfolding over the next two weeks.This new moon is making a square to Mars, the planet of conflict and drive. A square is an astrological connection that represents friction—think about the phrase “squaring up.” You might be trying to get to the same goal as someone, but you can't exactly agree on how it's going to be done. We may be wanting something that is loud and bold and perhaps even radical. However, this new moon is testing our patience and basically saying “actually, wait.” We need to investigate. We need to understand what's going on before we can make big, dramatic, theatrical changes. We need to do the work of unearthing. Mars is also making a sextile with Venus retrograde in Libra, the last Venus retrograde that we're going to see like this in a while. When Mars sextiles Venus, what these two planets are doing is making a harmonious agreement: Mars is agreeing to expose if Venus is able to give justice, and Venus is willing to get harmony if Mars is willing to put things under a spotlight. This is going to be a very spicy new moon. If you expected boring, please know that boring is not going to be the theme of this week's moon.

Do not expect a downpour of information. We are not going to get a release of what is hidden immediately. Expect for things to take time to adjust, and then eventually information will start to unravel. If situations unfold fast then be careful, accelerated speed may indicate a potential crash out.

Retrogrades are about returns, reflections, re-evaluation. This isn't a time to make any huge, drastic changes or start something completely new. This is a time to look at what was done, how it is affecting us, so that we can begin to plan moving forward. And it's very important that we follow these steps.

Try to zoom out of yourself as the individual and think about what collective or communal justice would be. Perhaps you as an individual felt betrayed or overlooked or disappointed, but chances are, if it's happening in your neighborhood, then it's happening to somebody else on the block. So don't treat things as if they're occurring in isolation. In fact, wisdom comes from learning that very rarely are things happening to just one person.

Make sure that you are not acting out for the sake of acting out. This moon is calling for a sense of decorum, and considering that Pluto is the modern ruler of Scorpio this new moon’s theme is transformation. Currently, Pluto is direct in Aquarius, which means Pluto is governing change happening to the larger community. No change happens in isolation. We may not need huge masses of people to experience a breakthrough, but we do have to be strategic.
