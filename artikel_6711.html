The ‘Victorian Psycho’ Popcorn Bucket Looks Like A Dead Baby Doll—And Fans Think It’s ‘Tactless’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Wake up, babe, another unhinged popcorn bucket just dropped.

With every movie premiere, it feels like we stray further from the definition of a popcorn bucket. Need proof? Take a look at the one tied to the upcoming film Victorian Psycho.

The gothic horror movie depicts mysterious events at a 19th-century English estate after a strange new governess arrives.

To celebrate the theatrical release on November 13, the film's souvenir popcorn bucket has officially arrived—and it’s a bloody Victorian baby doll.

The movie studio describes it as “the most devilishly macabre popcorn bucket of its kind.” That’s not an understatement. The doll has mottled skin, a terrifying face, and a bloody open hole in its torso to hold your popcorn.

True to the time period, the doll comes with a Victorian-era bonnet and baby clothes. The promotional photos also feature an ornate wooden cradle, with a lid that pushes it further into coffin territory.

Author and podcast host Rose Dommu got a firsthand look at the new popcorn bucket at an advance screening, and it somehow looks even freakier in real life.

Social media users, understandably, have thoughts. “The coffin/crib is cool, but the actual bucket is the dead baby part?? That’s…odd,” one X user wrote. “I get horror. But this is just vulgar and tactless,” another added.

Most commenters so far don’t seem to be huge fans of the doll, but a few appreciate how campy it is. “Yes girl let’s eat some dead baby popcorn,” one social media user joked.

This isn’t the only wacky popcorn bucket we’ve seen this year. Just last week, AMC debuted the Dune 3 souvenir bucket that contains not one, but two sandworms. And last month, social media went wild for the new Godzilla Minus Zero popcorn bucket, if you can even call it that: a nearly two-foot-tall Godzilla figurine.

Details about the Victorian Psycho popcorn bucket—including the price and where to buy it—have yet to be revealed. But it’s already clear that this might be the most unhinged popcorn bucket we’ve seen yet.
