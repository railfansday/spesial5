Your Virgo Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Deep conversations bring relief and positive developments once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4. You’re exploring vulnerability in new ways, either sharing personal feelings with someone near and dear, or perhaps someone is confiding in you. You might also learn news that feels transformative or potentially jarring that requires you to turn inward. You have some extra luck on your side when it comes to self-expression, so put yourself out there and ask for what you desire.

You’re in a more sentimental mindset once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6. Mercury retrograde might be over, but you find yourself more and more thinking about the past. There might be certain moments that can offer perspective, healing, and wisdom into your current situation. It’s also possible you’re spending more time at home or with your chosen family, and feeling in a more private mood.

A New Moon in Sagittarius helps you re-establish a healthier foundation on December 8. You could experience a shift in a personal relationship or have a realization about a family pattern or dynamic that feels freeing and affirming. You might also make an important decision about where you live—deciding to move, finding a new roommate, or reorganizing your space in some way. Relationships with family members are under the spotlight and subject to change, too.

It’s time to face the music and pay off some debt once Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. If you’ve been spending a bit haphazardly, it might finally catch up with you. This can help you come up with a plan to get your finances in order to help you create a more solid foundation for your future. You’re also more aware of the give and take in all your commitments—you might need to ask for a raise at work or more effort from someone important in your life.

You’re in a forgiving mood when Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. If you’ve dealt with feelings of jealousy, envy, or obsessing over someone or something else, this can bring a healing reality check. You’re able to understand what you’ve been moving through with more clarity and a new perspective. This can help you become more self-aware, accountable, and evolve as a result. Your intuition is also very active today, so pay attention to all of your urges and instincts, big and small.

You might feel like a guardian angel is looking out for you when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You could find yourself making your way out of a difficult situation relatively unscathed. This experience might feel profound and have you feeling a deeper connection with, or leaving you with more questions about, the Universe. The signs and symbols that show up in your life are direct messages from your Loved Ones and Ancestors who have crossed over. Listen to your intuition above all else. The energy can also be healing, helping you express grief that resides in your subconscious, which might feel challenging in the moment, but it can be healing later on.

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, helping you reconnect with your zest for life. You’re ready to have more fun and leave your worries by the wayside. There’s more romance and creativity in the air, which can put you in a positive mood and help you attract what you desire and deserve into your life.

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 helps you reach a milestone and feel worthy of celebration along the way. You’re in a social mood and this can be a fun time to gather with friends or attend a fun event that lights you up. This can also bring helpful people into your life or developments regarding some of your goals and dreams for the future. Your job today is to live in the present moment and create more space for gratitude and the people in your life who give you a true sense of belonging.

The month wraps up with some positive news when Mercury enters Capricorn on December 25. You might also feel a shift in your own mindset, finding kinder ways to speak to yourself and feeling relief and more gratitude as a result. You’re also craving more fun, and the next few weeks give you the confidence and commitments necessary to pursue your heart’s truest desires. Romance is also in the air, and you might receive more than a few flirty texts, too.
