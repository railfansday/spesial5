Taylor Swift Is at the 2026 CMAs Looking Stunning in a Neon Yellow Gown

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

We’re so back. Taylor Swift just arrived on the 2026 CMAs red carpet in anticipation of her first time performing at the annual award show in 13 (naturally!) years. Tay showed up wearing a neon yellow strapless gown with a gathered front:

The singer is nominated for “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5, and rumor has it she’ll be performing the song tonight (though it’s also possible she could surprise everyone with “Patient Zero” or something else off The Life of a Showgirl). Tay’s also nominated for Single of the Year, which she reacted to on Instagram with “LOOKS LIKE I HAVEN’T YEE'D MY LAST HAW.”

While “I Knew It, I Knew You” was technically written for the Toy Story 5 soundtrack, fans have been speculating that the song marks a return to her country roots.

Back in August, she chatted about her country origins in Nashville at the Grammy Museum, saying “I love my country roots more and more as I go further in this path. I want to honor them more and more, the artist who shaped who I am, and that all happened in Nashville.”

Stay tuned, because some fans are convinced tonight’s performance from Taylor is coming with an announcement (but we’ve clowned before and been wrong, so).
