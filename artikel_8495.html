Get the Costco Anywhere Visa® Business Card by Citi if you’re a Costco member whose business buys plenty of gas and shops at Costco. Gas earns 5% at Costco and 4% at other eligible stations, on up to $7,000 a year. Skip it if you want monthly rewards, since they come once a year as a certificate redeemable at Costco.

The Costco Anywhere Visa Business Card by Citi earns the same cash back as the personal Costco Anywhere Visa, but for a business and its employees. Citi sets no minimum business size, so side businesses can apply, though you and the business are both liable for the balance.

Costco members whose business buys a lot of gas come out ahead, since $500 a month of it at other eligible stations earns $240 a year on this card, against $120 on a flat 2% card. Owners who rarely shop at Costco, spend mostly outside this card’s categories, or want rewards monthly should get American Express’s no-fee Blue Business Cash Card, which pays 2% on the first $50,000 a year as a statement credit.
