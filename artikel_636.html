Patrick Stewart Was ‘Struggling With His Health’ on Last Day of Filming ‘Avengers: Doomsday’; Marvel Plans to Add Tribute to Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige and co-directors Anthony and Joe Russo are getting candid about filming “Avengers: Doomsday” with the late star Patrick Stewart, whose role became his final on-screen appearance after his death in October.

In “Avengers: Doomsday,” Stewart reprises his “X-Men” character Charles Xavier for the ninth and final time; even before his passing, EW notes that Stewart was offered a role in the film’s sequel, “Avengers: Secret Wars,” due out in Dec. 2027, but declined. “It was in the back of all of our heads, we may never see him on set again,” Anthony said, describing the atmosphere as “very emotional.”

Anthony continued: “It was clear that he was struggling with his health, and he worked so hard to deliver a performance and a presence — for us as directors, for his other actors, for the crew. You could see it wasn’t just the effort of a regular actor showing up and delivering that kind of presence and performance and experience for others, collaboration. It was someone who was really being challenged in a very deep way on a health level, showing up and doing that. For him to have muscled through and delivered the performance that he did in that movie, and the experience that he delivered for all of us as collaborators, it made it intensely emotional.”

Stewart died after a brief battle with Alzheimer’s and Lewy Body Dementia on Oct. 29, and both Feige and the Russo brothers are currently planning how best to pay tribute to the actor in the forthcoming film. “We’re long done filming that movie, and he did an amazing job for us,” he said. “We’re looking now at how we can put something together that honors and respects our friend.”

“In a lot of ways, Xavier’s the conscience in the movie,” said Joe. “He has a lovely scene with Ian McKellen, who’s his best friend, in the film. It’s a very emotional scene about forgiveness, and I think very resonant in the wake of his passing.”

“Avengers: Doomsday” hits theaters on Dec. 18.
