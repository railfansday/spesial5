Jennifer Lopez Teases ‘Monster-in-Law’ Sequel With Jane Fonda: ‘We’re Talking About Maybe Doing Another One’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jennifer Lopez and Jane Fonda might reunite for a “Monster-in-Law” follow-up, the singer and actress teased recently.

In a clip from Lopez’s upcoming appearance on “The Drew Barrymore Show,” she reminisced with the host about past projects, including the 2005 rom-com she starred in with Fonda.

The rom-com starred Fonda as Viola Fields, a mother-in-law-to-be determined to stop her son (Vartan) from marrying Charlotte “Charlie” Cantilini (Lopez). Monet Mazur, Wanda Sykes, Adam Scott, Annie Parisse and Will Arnett also starred.

Lopez said working with Fonda was “amazing.” “I mean, loved her.” She added, “We’re talking about maybe doing another one.”

Lopez and Fonda have remained close since filming “Monster-in-Law.” In 2013, Fonda spoke at Lopez’s Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony, and the two reunited at a 2025 pre-Oscars party.

Barrymore also showed the audience shots from Lopez’s Fonda-inspired Halloween costume from this year. “The Halloween Barbarella nod was as hot as it gets,” Barrymore said.

“I don’t know what came over me,” Lopez said. “I was like, ‘Maybe I’ll get dressed up,’ and then I thought of Barbarella. It was a little tip to some of the music I’m making.”

Lopez’s appearance is in promotion of her new concert film, “The JLo Show,” created from Lopez’s final performance during her Las Vegas residency. The film premieres Nov. 26 on CBS and will stream on Paramount+. On Friday, the star made headlines for donning a 186-carat diamond necklace to the premiere.
