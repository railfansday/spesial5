Southern Charm’s Austen Kroll and Allie Newman’s Relationship: What to Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Southern Charm’s newest cast member Allie Newman turned heads during her reality TV debut — in part because of her romantic connection to star Austen Kroll.

During the November 2026 season 12 premiere, fans learned that Allie and Austen briefly dated before filming the latest season of this hit Bravo franchise. The details on their relationship, however, were a bit fuzzy.

When Shep Rose asked Allie whether she and Austen “went on two or three dates” before he allegedly called off the romance when she said she didn’t want to get physical without being exclusive, she quickly set him straight.

“Shep, no. We went on a few dates and he was calling me when he was in New York [in February 2026],” she claimed during the premiere. “He was like, ‘I don’t know why I’m treating you like a girlfriend.’ I was like, yeah, ‘I don’t know but it’s fine.’”

The model recalled having “fun” with Austen, but explained, “I wasn’t ready to get intimate with him because there was a lot between him and Salley [Carson].”

When Allie confronted Austen about his alleged retelling of their breakup story, he claimed, “I never said that” to Shep, but then wavered on what he did tell his pal.

So what’s the true story? Scroll down for everything Us knows about Allie and Austen’s short-lived relationship:

“Allie and I were talking over a one-month period. I wasn’t trying to hide. I was going out with Allie very much in the open,” Austen told the cameras during the season 12 premiere, showing a photo of him and Allie together dated December 2025. “I did like her.”

While Austen put their relationship timeline around the end of 2025, Allie told Shep that the beermaker was calling her in February 2026 while the cast was in New York City to film the season 11 reunion. It appears that the romance fizzled out shortly after Austen got back to Charleston that month.

According to Allie, she ended her romance with Austen amid his flirtation with close friend Salley — and because he was fresh off his relationship with Audrey Pratt. (Austen and Audrey split in 2025 after dating more than one year.)

“[Shep] told me that I asked you to be exclusive [and] you were the one who ended it and you didn’t want anything to do with me because I wouldn’t sleep with you,” Allie revealed during episode 2 of the Bravo series.

After Austen denied ever telling Shep anything of the sort, Allie reminded the By the Way coowner what allegedly transpired between them.

“I told you straight up I didn’t feel comfortable inserting myself into your dynamic with Salley, so I shut it down,” Allie alleged.

Austen replied, “I said that you told me that you don’t sleep with men,” to which Allie clapped back, “Not that I don’t sleep with men, that I’m picky.”

Austen didn’t argue any further, telling Allie, “But I did like you.”

Allie, meanwhile, told the girls later that episode that she “ghosted” Austen.

Allie shed some light on her connection with Austen during the season premiere, telling the cameras, “The whole time we were hanging out, Salley was blowing up his phone. I was hearing, ‘They’re in love with each other, and that they’ve been kissing and they’ve been seeing each other.’ I can’t put myself in the middle of that.”

The model noted in episode two that she thought she “could get serious” with Austen but once “reality hit” she realized he wasn’t ready.

“I think he’s very open about not being sure about what he wants in his life. For me, I need a plan. I want to be able to follow along with somebody and their plans too,” she confessed.

“I think that Allie and I were going down a path of like, ‘OK, are we dating now?’” Austen recalled during episode 2, detailing the end of his and Allie’s relationship. “She provided me with an offramp and I quickly took it because I just knew I wasn’t in a place to fully date and commit to somebody.”

Salley and Austen have had a flirty relationship since they became friends and it heated up at the end of season 11 after Austen broke up with Audrey. After Austen claimed at the season 11 reunion that he and Salley hadn’t had sex — despite having sleepovers — the pair hit a bump in their friendship.

When season 12 premiered, Salley and Austen were back to hanging out weekly and talking almost daily. Salley, for her part, asked to have “boundaries” with Austen during the premiere when she learned that Austen had dated Allie but refused to take her out on a date.

During episode 3, Salley revealed she was “very confused” by Allie after Austen allegedly told her that she was “talking negatively” about her to him.

“I confront[ed] her on it, and I [said], ‘Hey, Allie, point blank, did you say the most attractive thing about Austen was that he hadn’t f***ed me yet?’” Salley told Austen during a group Easter brunch.

Salley noted that Allie denied ever saying that and called the remark “disgusting.” Austen, meanwhile, tried to backpeddle, telling Salley, “Honestly, I don’t remember telling you that.”

“Salley, you know that you and I have been close for a long time. When I was seeing Allie, you would call me, and it was important for her to know if you and I had slept together yet,” Austen explained. “Did she say the most unattractive thing about you? Not sure if that was it. But it was pretty goddamn similar to that. Of like, ‘Phew, thank God that you hadn’t.’”

Salley was visibly shocked by Austen’s omission, especially after Madison LeCroy pointed out there was a “huge difference” between what he allegedly told Salley and what Allie seemingly said.

Southern Charm airs on Bravo Thursdays at 9 p.m. ET.
