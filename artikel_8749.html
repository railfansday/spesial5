As we know, protein is a crucial part of healthy eating and building muscle. Without adequate daily protein, the chances of packing on all the muscle you want are slim.

While the ideal amount of protein will vary from individual to individual, it’s a good idea to spread out your intake of this crucial macro throughout the day to make it easier to get all that you need. Trying to pack 100 grams of protein into a single meal is never a wise move. Who ever said that getting your fill of protein needs to stop at dinner? The protein spread can (and should) include dessert. With a little effort and know-how, you can give your ab-friendly sweat treats a protein boost without making them taste all healthy and what-not. Using the right ingredients, it’s certainly possible to make a dessert that gives you 10 grams or more of protein, which is significant in your daily pursuit to get enough. And, sure, you could just blend together a post-dinner protein shake, but where’s the fun in that?

Higher-protein desserts, be it for an end-of-day treat or part of a game day spread, can help you reach your goals for this clutch macronutrient. Here’s how to make some treats that do much more than satisfy a sweet tooth. Everything in moderation, right?

Certain ingredients can give your treats a protein boost. So if you’re looking for a dessert that’ll help you build muscle, include any of these protein heroes.

Protein Powder: Good for much more than your post-gym recovery shakes, versatile protein powder can be used in a huge variety of dessert recipes, both cooked and uncooked. For baking, it can replace some of the flour in a recipe. But it’s best to use plant-based protein powders in baking recipes like muffins and cakes as they give a much better texture than powders made from whey.

Peanut Butter Powder: This is the best thing to add nutty taste to dessert that you probably aren’t using. Peanut butter powder is made by pressing roasted peanuts to remove most of their natural oils, then grinding the defatted peanuts into a fine powder. This process significantly reduces the fat and calorie content compared to peanut butter, and results in a more protein-dense product. A good buy is Naked Nutrition Peanut Butter Powder.

Greek Yogurt: Enhance the protein content of your desserts by using Greek yogurt or Skyr instead of regular yogurt in recipes. It’s an easy substitution that packs an extra protein punch with minimal change to the flavor or texture.

Chia Seeds: Chia seeds offer four grams of protein in just two tablespoons —and they help create a creamy pudding that will be a delicious high-protein dessert (or breakfast).
