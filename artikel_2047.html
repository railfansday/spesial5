‘It was a matter of time’: Norwich City vs QPR predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Norwich City come into their midweek clash with QPR as one of the Championship’s form sides. The Canaries have now won five league matches on the trot, leaving their poor start to the season in the past and surging up the table.

Philippe Clement’s men rose to 11th in the league with a 2-1 victory over a dangerous Cardiff City side on Saturday. Paris Maghoma scored his fifth goal in five consecutive games, and Mathias Kvistgaarden continued his solid sophomore season with his fifth of the campaign as well.

QPR make the trip to Carrow Road in a confident mood. They were victorious themselves on Saturday, with returning Danish midfielder Nicolas Madsen scoring and starring in a 1-0 away win over 10-man Millwall.

The Hoops have strengthened their grip on a Championship play-off spot as a result, moving into 7th. It was their first away win in five league matches, so Julien Stephan will hope his side can find some consistent form on the road heading into the hectic winter schedule.

“Norwich City’s winning run has seen them surge back into the play-off conversation. It did feel like it was a matter of time before they found form, but I certainly didn’t anticipate them reeling off five consecutive Championship wins.

“Philippe Clement has had to deal with some injury problems but he’s got the squad to get results. Paris Maghoma is a real maverick in attacking midfield, and Mathias Kvistgaarden coming good in his second season has massively softened the blow of losing Mohamed Toure.

“QPR are going to be a really tough opponent for the Canaries though. The Hoops are prone to big losses on the road – as shown by 3-0 and 4-1 defeats to Birmingham City and Southampton. They’re a team brimming with talent from back to front, and I think they can be a great match for the hosts here.

“But, given the visitors’ away form and Norwich City’s recent success, I am leaning towards a home win. This should be a good game, and don’t write off QPR.”

“Norwich City are on a roll and will fancy their chances of a sixth win on the bounce. NCFC’s players will be full of confidence and they look like genuine promotion contenders.

“The Canaries took a while to get going earlier this season but they appear to have fully clicked now under Philippe Clement. They have won their last two matches at Carrow Road on the spin and are starting to make their home ground a fortress.

“QPR are no pushovers though and will be tough opponents for the hosts here. They are in the top eight for a reason and Julien Stephan has done a brilliant job with the London outfit.

“The Hoops have lost only one of their last five and that was to Southampton. I think they will be good for a useful point in East Anglia on Tuesday night.”
