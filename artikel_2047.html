Arsenal have no intention of selling centre-back Piero Hincapie in the January window, sources have told Football Insider.

The Ecuador international is attracting significant interest from Barcelona of late, but the Gunners have no intention of letting him out the door.

Mikel Arteta is determined to retain the depth in his squad, and is keen to ensure he has two top-quality players in every position.

Sources say that the only way he would consider selling Hincapie next summer is if he could secure a replacement to come in immediately.

The 24-year-old is yet to start a single game in the Premier League so far this term, but remains valued as part of Arteta's squad.

He is expected to get further opportunities as the campaign wears on, rather than being shown towards the exit door.

Hincapie impressed during his first season with Arsenal last term, but that hasn't translated into a starting spot for him in 2026-27.

He has been used in a bit-part role in league action, having played just 67 minutes in the Premier League so far this term.

The former Bayer Leverkusen man is locked in a battle for the left-back spot with Riccardo Calafiori, and has been unable to dislodge his teammate so far.

However, Hincapie's versatility remains valued by Arteta, who knows that he can also trust him to play at centre-back when called upon.

The boss is having to juggle the league campaign with a Champions League push once again, and knows that he will need a deep squad in order to have success on both fronts.

Football Insider revealed last week that Arsenal are chasing a move to sign young Rangers sensation Kai Urban.

The athletic right-back is widely regarded as one of the most exciting young prospects to emerge at Ibrox in recent years.

Urban represents Scotland at U15 level, and is already tipped to have a huge future ahead of him with Rangers.

However, with Arsenal now lurking for a day, his future with the Glasgow giants has been forced into serious doubt.
