CM: Milan searching for left-back with Estupinan heading for exit – the names on the list

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

AC Milan are not just looking for a new source of goals in January. They are also searching for ways to help create them and keep them out.

As Calciomercato.com report, Gerry Cardinale is determined to refine Ruben Amorim’s squad, which needs more quality and depth. Endrick remains the headline name that Milan are pursuing, but the idea of signing a left-back is gaining momentum quickly.

While waiting for the arrival of Markus Krösche to be made official, work is already underway to examine the possibility of signing a new defender. The very likely departure of Pervis Estupinan – who has suitors in Spain – could help facilitate a winter swoop for a left-sided player.

Noussair Mazraoui is one option, whose contract with Manchester United expires in 2028. It’s too early to know whether he’ll be a valuable asset to new manager Mauricio Pochettino, but Amorim is an admirer of his and the Moroccan could be a versatile and experienced asset.

Another player whose contract expires in a year and a half is Alejandro Balde, whom Barcelona are looking to sell in January after unsuccessful attempts over the summer. His agent Jorge Mendes has offered him to several clubs, including Milan.

The directors are keeping him on their list. This year, he’s made just five appearances for a total of 125 minutes across LaLiga and the Champions League. So, he might have to leave the Blaugrana to play more regularly.

There are a couple of domestic options too. Gabriele Calvani is shining for Frosinone (though he struggled on Sunday in his San Siro audition). The 22-year-old centre-back can play full-back, and while he is owned by Genoa, the two clubs are in talks over Christian Comotto which could aid things.

Juan Miranda is the other name, even though he is negotiating a contract renewal with Bologna given his deal expires in 2028. The Spaniard is an old favourite having been linked before, and he would be expected to cost €15m or more.
