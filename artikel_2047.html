Celtic have an uncomfortable Jota truth, 0% and 17% stats vs Kilmarnock show it

Jota is a fan favourite at Celtic, stemming from his original spell with the Glasgow side.

The Portuguese winger returned in the winter of 2025, but was then hit by an ACL injury in April of the 2024-25 campaign.

Jota would then be ruled out for 18 months, missing the entirety of last season, with Celtic going on to lift the title.

Now, in the last month, the 27-year-old has returned to action and was handed his first start since 2025 against Kilmarnock.

While Celtic picked up the 1-0 win, there is an unfortunate truth emerging about Jota.

He may not be ready to be a starter for the Glasgow outfit.

There is no doubt that Jota possesses the quality to make a significant difference for Celtic when he is fully fit and firing.

However, his performance against Kilmarnock showed just how much work remains before he can be trusted to deliver consistently at the level expected of him.

As per FotMob, Jota completed zero of his three attempted dribbles against Kilmarnock.

Additionally, he was weak in his duels, winning just one of seven throughout his 45-minute display - a 17 per cent success.

The left-winger also had just two touches in the opposition box, while he was beaten down the wing twice by being dribbled past.

Whether or not the same player Celtic fans have loved is still there remains to be seen.

But that showing against Kilmarnock proved that he may still be a long way off returning to that.

O'Neill cannot afford to hand out sentimental starts, even if supporters want to see him on the pitch.

He has spent a long time on the sidelines, and he is clearly not ready to have an impact to that degree yet.

With the international break now set to get underway, Jota must use this as a chance to improve himself, and potentially get himself ready for a starring role in the future.

Right now, that chance should go begging, as he clearly is not at the right confidence level to perform.

It is normal for a player to need time to get back up to speed after a long lay-off.

Celtic must be patient with Jota, allowing him to build his match fitness and confidence through substitute appearances, rather than from the first whistle.
