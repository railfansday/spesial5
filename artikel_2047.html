Interesting to read through these BBC Sport ‘neutrals’ comments, following on from England defeating Spain.

The hosts going 3-0 down inside an hour as the visitors blew them away.

England goals coming from Kane (pen), Konsa and White, though it could have been five or six up if Thomas Tuchel’s side had turned their superiority into the goals it deserved.

Spain had not lost a competitive game since March 2023, some 44 matches ago.

A daft red card for Elliot Anderson and goals gifted by Quansah and Gordon caught in possession, Spain threatened to keep that unbeaten record going.

However, the final scoreline read Spain 2 England 3 and Tuchel and his players absolutely deserving of the victory against the World Cup winners.

Always interesting to hear the ‘neutral’ perspectives on the England players.

The BBC Sport readers comments from neutrals…

‘Quansah costing Anderson (who got punished for trying to save a teammate) and nearly ruining what turned out to be a memorable night. But overall, brilliant England and that included Quansah.

The player that makes me smile, did a job on Yamal (has an unpleasant side), is Lewis Hall. Something about his determination and composure. Really like him.’

‘Lewis Hall could become something special’

‘Weren’t you predicting an easy win for Spain? 6-0 I think you said.’

And another great performance by Lewis Hall.

‘Agreed, Hall and Trafford both played well; and White deserves a mention too. Scored the winning goal and made an important block near the end.

‘England in cruise control until Quansah did what Quansah does. Again.’

‘Huh?? It was Anderson that was sent off.’

‘Because of Quansah’s lack of concentration.’

‘It was Quansah who got caught in possession, even if it was a foul.

I said beforehand that he is a disaster that has already happened.’

‘Why is everyone calling out Quansah and not Gordon?’

‘Not only him, why did Gordon after a perfect performance try showboating in the middle? He had passes on and declined them to look good. He’s a brilliant player, no need.’

‘Yamal’s reputation is the most closely protected commercial asset in football.’

‘Hopefully one day when five nil down someone will put him in the stand.’

‘Thinks he is the greatest player on the planet. But he isn’t.’

‘He’s been bigged up on the strength of a handful of early performances.

Overrated. And goes down in ‘agony’ if an opposition player gets closer than a yard to him.’

‘Lewis Hall played well against him – so much belief.’

‘What a game against the world champions and arguably the best England performance in many years.

Hall and White were immense and Anderson was probably MOTM until his sending off. Hopefully they can keep building on this before the Euros!’

‘…and well done Tuchel. The team is evolving tactically.’

‘They look like a club side now – he’s built something.’
