TikTok releases its 2026 Music Impact Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The study found that 94% of songs on the Billboard Global 200 in 2025 had a viral moment on TikTok before climbing the chart.

TikTok has published its 2026 Music Impact Report, which looks at the role that the platform plays in broader music and artist discovery.

The report is based on a study conducted by Luminate, in partnership with TikTok, which incorporates responses from more than 4,000 U.S. TikTok users. The response data underlines just how influential TikTok has become as a music and artist discovery tool, with 45% of U.S. TikTok users saying they discover new music in the app. That, according to the report, is higher than on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook.

The most significant claim of the report, however, is that 94% of songs that entered the Billboard Global 200 in 2025 went viral on TikTok before climbing the chart.

That’s a significant correlation, which is up from 84% 2024, per TikTok’s 2025 Music Impact report.

The data underlines TikTok’s value as a music discovery platform, which makes it a critically important consideration for musicians and publishers when it comes to mapping out their promotional approach.

The data underlines the platform’s growing role as a music discovery tool. In some ways, TikTok has replaced the MTV of old as a key link between music trends and audiences.

That makes it a more valuable consideration for music-related marketers, while the insights also highlight the broader role that the platform plays in informing broader cultural shifts.

Which could be an important consideration for brands seeking to align their campaigns with what’s trending.
