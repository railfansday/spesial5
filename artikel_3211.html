Danny Burstein & Caroline Portu Among Cast Added To Ari Frenkel’s ‘See You On The Other Side’ As Production Wraps

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

EXCLUSIVE: Tony winner and record nine-time nominee Danny Burstein (Cupertino), Caroline Portu (Tony), Camrus Johnson (One Piece), Jetta Juriansz (Mistakes You Make Before You Die), Ori Laizer (Shababnikim), Liz Larsen (The Baltimorons) have joined the cast of indie feature See You on the Other Side from writer, director and actor Ari Frenkel (I’m A Virgo).

They join previously announced Ella Stiller, Judy Gold, and Richard Masur. Production took place over the course of the last year to reflect the passage of time, as well as navigating delays in response to a family bereveament. Inspired by true events, the story “follows Noah (Frenkel), a neurotic musician stuck at home with his mother (Gold) and sister (Stiller) as they mourn the death of their father (Masur). Set against the backdrop of suburban New Jersey, Noah is sent on a journey to find his father’s high school sweetheart and deliver the bad news, with the help of his own past love, Maya (Portu). He is begrudgingly guided by his rabbi David (Burstein) and close friends Brad (Johnson) and Liv (Juriansz).” Watch on Deadline

Laizer and Larsen play undisclosed roles. Rounding out the cast are Elaine Bromka (Eleanor the Great), Tamir (Fleishman is in Trouble), Stephen Brower (Hacks), Bruce Faulk (Gossip Girl), Jennifer C. Johnson (And Just Like That), Sharina Martin (Elsbeth), Joy Hermalyn (Caroline or Change), and Matt Walton (The Housemaid).

Producers include Amy DePaola, Adam Gold, Golan Ramraz, Josh Sathre, Judy Gold and Frenkel. Executive producers are K180’s Becky Korman, Lily Korman, and Shai Korman, Awesome Daughters’ Rob Kolson and Scott Prisand, Ariel Lindenbaum Sebag, Meir Goldberg, Kenlyn Mirbach, Chaya Amor, and Tzvi Amor. Sherise Dorf serves as co-producer.

Cailin Yatsko is DoP, casting is by Daryl Eisenberg, production design by Jordan Janota (Theater Camp) and costume design by Alexandria Hoffman (Maya and Her Lover).

The film is backed by Entertainment 2 Affect Change, and produced by Frenkel’s Lamalo Pictures in association with DePaola’s Pink Among Men Productions.

Burstein is repped by BRS/Gage Talent. Johnson is with Untitled Entertainment and Gersh. Juriansz is repped by Artists First and Prototype Talent Agency. Laizer is with Wright Entertainment, Inc. and Yitzug 1. Larsen is repped by 44 West Talent and CESD.
