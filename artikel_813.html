Chelsea fined £150,000 for discriminatory chanting

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chelsea have been fined Â£150,000 by the Football Association as part of a punishment for anti-Catholic and anti-Irish chants during an FA Cup third-round win at Charlton Athletic in January.

The club have also been warned about their future conduct and had an action plan imposed on them in relation to the offence.

"Chelsea FC failed to ensure its spectators and/or supporters (and anyone purporting to be supporters or followers) did not behave in an improper, offensive, abusive or insulting way with either an express or implied reference to religion or belief," said an FA statement., external

Chelsea admitted the charge but had an appeal against the fine issued by an independent regulatory commission rejected.

The FA's written reasons stated that although the discriminatory chanting "lasted approximately one minute and was not repeated, the scale of participation was a significant aggravating feature".

"We unequivocally condemn this behaviour," said a Chelsea statement., external "Sectarian abuse - chanting that creates division targeted towards a particular religious or political group - and discriminatory language of any kind has no place in our football club.

"Chelsea takes a zero-tolerance approach towards discriminatory behaviour in whatever form it takes. We will always take steps to identify those responsible and take appropriate action where necessary."

The FA itself was fined by Uefa for a similar incident of chanting, from England's away support during their Nations League victory in Spain on Sunday.
