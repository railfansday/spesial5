Anthropic shares began trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT, opening at $111.50, about 34% above the $83 IPO price. The opening briefly valued the Claude developer at around $2 trillion, ahead of SpaceX’s record debut in June, Reuters reported. The early rally faded as investors took profits and major US indexes slipped, with the stock trading near $99 later in the session. Anthropic priced the offering above its earlier $70 to $78 range and raised the number of shares to 980 million, lifting the IPO size to about $81.3 billion. Of those, 956.35 million shares were sold by the company and 23.65 million by existing shareholders. Including a $26 billion private placement with chip and cloud partners, total proceeds reach about $107.3 billion. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led the deal and hold a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares. The Wealth Consulting Group’s Talley Leger warned that retail investors buying hot tech IPOs on day one have historically trailed the broader market over three years.
