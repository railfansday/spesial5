EGLD is approaching a potentially important turning point, but the market still has something to prove. MultiversX is attempting a rebound from the lower boundary of a descending channel on the daily chart. Meanwhile, development activity continues. That’s an interesting disconnect, prices remain under pressure, yet developers aren’t standing still.

The daily chart shows EGLD trading around the $2.93 area, close to the lower boundary of its latest descending channel. The highlighted region around $2.85-$2.45 represents an important support zone.

A successful bounce could change the immediate technical picture. However, buyers first need to reclaim $3.45, the nearest major upside level marked on the chart.

Beyond that, the next resistance levels sit at $4.85 and $5.80. A stronger recovery could eventually bring $7.55 into focus, while $11 represents the larger upside target.

Of course, drawing an arrow toward $11 doesn’t make it happen. EGLD still needs confirmation from price action.

Here’s why the story gets more interesting. MultiversX’s latest development updates highlight several improvements delivered within a single week.

VEIL verification is reportedly 40% faster. VM opcodes v2 have been implemented, validator rewards have been introduced, and the TCS gateway has been shipped for local testing.

These aren’t price catalysts by themselves. Still, they show continued development while EGLD’s chart remains under pressure.

That’s the question investors should be watching. Technical recovery and ecosystem progress are two different things, and neither automatically guarantees the other.

For EGLD, reclaiming $3.45 would provide an initial bullish signal, followed by resistance at $4.85 and $5.80. The $7.55 and $11 targets remain further milestones.

MultiversX developers are shipping upgrades, but EGLD still needs buyers. Until the chart confirms a reversal, the $11 projection remains a possibility rather than a promise.
