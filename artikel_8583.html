Mailchimp SMS Marketing Review – One List for Texts & Emails, but Credits Expire Monthly

Add Mailchimp SMS Marketing if you already email customers from Mailchimp and want texts in the same audience and automation flows. It starts at $20 a month for 1,000 U.S. credits, and unused credits expire every month, so a store that sells mostly by text should choose a service built for texting.

Mailchimp started in Atlanta in 2001 as an email service for small businesses, and Intuit bought it in 2021. Its texting add-on sends to the U.S., Canada, Australia, and more than 30 European countries, and every business has to apply and be approved before its first text goes out.

Texting needs a paid Mailchimp email plan, Essentials or higher, and several features below need Standard or Premium.

A text written with plain letters, numbers, and common symbols holds 160 characters, and one with an emoji or another special character holds 70. A longer text splits into parts of 153 or 67 characters, and each part uses its own credits. Your company name opens every text by default, since some carriers require it, and it counts toward the limit along with spaces, emojis, and opt-out instructions.

On the Standard and Premium plans, programs in the U.S. and Canada can also send picture messages, called MMS. Each carries one JPEG, PNG, or GIF and up to 1,600 characters, and costs 3 credits in the U.S. and 5 in Canada. Those plans also get branded short links, which shorten your links under a subdomain you choose. On any plan, the builder can shorten and track links, add merge tags that personalize each text, and insert a discount code from a Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, or Lightspeed store.

You can add a Send SMS step to the same automation flows that send your emails. Mailchimp’s prebuilt flows include one for abandoned carts, which texts a shopper once per cart left behind. A text a flow triggers during quiet hours, the late-night and early-morning window when marketing texts are held, waits until they end. On the Essentials plan, a flow holds no more than four steps.

Each country needs its own registered sending number, and texts reach only contacts in the countries you’ve registered. A text uses 1 credit in the U.S., 3 in Canada, 5 in the U.K., and 12 in Germany. U.S. businesses on Standard or higher can also apply for a short code, a 5- or 6-digit number built for large audiences. It costs extra, and carriers take 8 to 12 weeks to review the application.

Replies land in Mailchimp’s Inbox, and every answer you send uses credits. You can reply to a contact three times in 7 days, and the 7-day window restarts each time they text you again. That covers a question about an order, not ongoing customer service by text. On a two-way number, replies of STOP or HELP get automatic answers that use no credits.

Credits come in monthly blocks. On Mailchimp’s U.S. pricing page, 1,000 credits cost $20 a month, 2,500 cost $25, 5,000 cost $45, and 10,000 cost $90, with blocks up to 450,000 for $2,500. By our math, a plain U.S. text costs 2 cents at the smallest block and 0.9 cents at 5,000 or 10,000. Mailchimp’s 14-day free trial of its email plans doesn’t cover texting, and the Free plan can’t add it.

Your block renews with your email plan each month, and unused credits expire when the next billing cycle starts. A refill bought mid-month costs full price, while a change to your monthly block takes effect at the next cycle. You pay nothing until your application is approved, the first month is prorated, and refunds come only for a system malfunction or an account closed without cause.

Email consent doesn’t cover texts, so each contact has to opt in to SMS through Mailchimp’s signup landing page or an SMS phone field added to your other signup forms. Mailchimp’s own SMS signup forms record the date and time each contact was asked and agreed, and importing a list means confirming that every contact gave explicit SMS consent.

Carriers bar texts about sex, hate, firearms, tobacco, cannabis, gambling, and political campaigns, and they allow alcohol only in programs that verify age. Among financial services, high-interest loans and debt collection are barred too, and breaking the rules can get an account suspended. In the U.S., carriers cap how fast texts go out by a score they give each business, from 75 a minute to AT&T customers at the entry tier to 4,500 at the top. Texts over the cap wait in a queue. Every U.S. account is registered with carriers automatically, and an upgrade called standard vetting may speed up sending. It comes with a block of 5,000 or more credits or with the Premium plan.

Mailchimp SMS Marketing works best as an extension of email you already send from Mailchimp. Texts reach the same audience through the same segments and flows, the compliance work runs in the background, and a small U.S. program costs $20 to $25 a month.
