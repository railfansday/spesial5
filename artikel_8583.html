Over a year, that fee comes to 12% of the credit limit you pick. By our math, the fee costs less than interest only when you owe more than 57% of the limit, at the 21.19% average rate the Federal Reserve reported for bank credit cards in August 2026.

Readers in TD’s states who’ll owe more than 57% of the limit month after month pay less in fees on TD Clear than they would in interest on a typical card.
