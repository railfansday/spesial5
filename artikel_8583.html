Choose USAA Bank for checking if your military family moves often. Its Classic Checking has no monthly fee, refunds up to $10 a month in other banks’ ATM fees, and can deliver military pay up to two days early. Keep your savings at a higher-yield bank, since USAA Savings pays just 0.01% a year.

USAA Bank is the banking side of USAA, which also sells insurance, investing accounts, loans, and credit cards to the military community. Its free checking runs on an app and ATMs rather than branches, which suits a family that moves every few years, while its savings pay little.

USAA Bank is worth it for service members and veterans who move often and want free checking without a nearby branch. Keep savings you want to grow in a high-yield savings account, and for in-person banking, look at Navy Federal Credit Union, which serves military families from more than 360 branches.
