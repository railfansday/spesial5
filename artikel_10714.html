Chipotle is giving away free food to anyone with a tattoo for one hour only

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Chipotle is bringing back its popular tattoo-themed buy-one-get-one-free promotion for Friday the 13th, this time with a special collaboration featuring rapper Wiz Khalifa.

The restaurant chain announced on November 9 that its “Tatted Like a Chipotle Bag” promotion will return on November 13, giving customers with tattoos a chance to score a free entree with their purchase.

The deal follows a record-breaking promotion earlier this year that helped Chipotle achieve its highest single-day sales in company history. This time, the company is also releasing limited-edition bags inspired by Wiz Khalifa’s tattoos.

Chipotle’s BOGO promotion will run from 3 p.m. to 4 p.m. local time on November 13 at participating restaurants across the US and Canada.

Customers simply need to show up with a tattoo to qualify, and they don’t need to have permanent ink to participate.

Real tattoos, temporary tattoos, and even designs drawn onto the skin will count toward the promotion, meaning customers can participate without getting an actual tattoo.

The deal is available exclusively for in-store purchases and cannot be redeemed through Chipotle’s app, website, or delivery services.

Customers can receive up to five free entrées per check, with each free item requiring the purchase of another entrée of equal or greater value. Each free entree must be collected by a customer wearing a qualifying tattoo.

The promotion originally launched in June 2025, inspired by the viral “tatted like a Chipotle bag” meme, which compares heavily tattooed people to the artwork printed on the restaurant’s takeout bags.

When Chipotle brought the deal back in March 2026, it helped generate approximately 10% more sales than the company’s previous single-day record, set on National Burrito Day in 2024.

Alongside the returning promotion, Chipotle has partnered with Wiz Khalifa to release limited-edition takeout bags featuring designs inspired by the rapper’s own tattoos.

The bags also feature Khalifa’s signature and will be available exclusively at Chipotle’s Oakland neighborhood restaurant at 3510 Forbes Avenue in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

Customers who make an in-store purchase at that location between 3 p.m. and 4 p.m. ET on November 13 can receive one of the collectible bags while supplies last. Purchases are limited to one bag per person.

Fans who cannot visit Pittsburgh will also have an opportunity to win one of 13 bags through the Chipotle Rewards Exchange.

From November 12 through November 14, US Chipotle Rewards members can exchange 10 points for a sweepstakes entry, with a maximum of 20 entries per person.

“I’ve been a big Chipotle fan for years, so seeing my tattoos take over the bags is wild,” Khalifa said.

“Usually people are trying to get tatted like a Chipotle bag—now the Chipotle bags are tatted like me. Getting to do it in Pittsburgh makes it even better.”

The collaboration will also introduce Khalifa’s personal Chipotle order to the chain’s digital menu beginning November 13.

His usual order consists of a double chicken bowl with white rice, roasted chili-corn salsa, romaine lettuce, and a side of chips and guacamole.

The promotion coincides with the November 13 release of Khalifa’s new album, “COOL,” which arrives on the same day as Chipotle’s returning Friday the 13th deal.
