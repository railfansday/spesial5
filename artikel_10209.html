This $199 Amazon TV is an easy choice for your guest bedroom

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This Amazon Ember TV Black Friday deal takes up to 40% off the company’s own TV brand, making it ideal for a secondary set.

Amazon Fire TVs (now known as Amazon Ember TVs) are typically quite affordable and, of course, Black Friday week is making those prices even better than usual. Case in point, the company’s most affordable TV is usually $329, but that same model is down to an incredible $199, making it ridiculously easy to recommend no matter how picky you might be about your TVs. And yes, this is the 2026 model, so you’re not buying something ancient.

I have no idea why Amazon is so obsessed with fire-related names (Kindle, Fire TV, Ember, etc.), but it’s hard to ignore the company’s leanings toward value-heavy products that cost very little to acquire. The Amazon Ember TV lineup sports 4K resolution, support for HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG, Dolby Audio, an ambient artwork screensaver, and lots of free streaming content built-in. It’s even got Amazon Luna built in, which gives you games like Star Wars Galactic Racer for free.

All three models ship with the latest Fire TV experience and Alexa+, which is a significantly smarter, LLM-powered version of Alexa (Alexa GPT, basically). It has apps for all the major streaming networks and even YouTube, but these don’t have the Google Play Store or a Google TV app, so keep that in mind before buying.

Of course, a $199 TV will not have the same image quality as a $600-plus TV, but that’s probably not an issue if this is just a secondary TV for a spare bedroom (or a backup TV).
