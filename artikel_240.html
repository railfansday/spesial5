President Donald Trump promised to put a “tremendous tax” on companies that build data centers outside of the US, even as states and cities around the country are placing restrictions on new applications to build them.

“We’re not going to allow our companies to build data centers outside of our country,” Trump said at a press conference Friday following his summit with China President Xi Jinping and Russia President Vladimir Putin. “They allowed that to happen with chips and everything else, and it was very destructive to our country. It’s where we started going behind.” Should the US allow data centers to be built outside of the country, Trump warned, “China will take them all.”

Trump didn’t share many details behind the plan, and apparently was floating the proposal publicly for the first time. “I don’t know if that’s a breaking story. But I haven’t even said that to my people,” Trump said, flanked by Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio. “But I’m telling them now. They’re here. Many of them are here.”

Trump’s promise could be difficult to fulfill with a new Democratic majority Congress being sworn in within a couple of months. Even members of his own party recognize that the data center buildout has become an unpopular issue across the board. With states installing moratoriums and new restrictions on data center proposals, companies may be forced to look outside of the country, but Trump’s threat is an attempt to make that a less attractive alternative.

Here’s a transcript of the key part of Trump’s remarks:

Whoever wins SI wins. And you ought to take advantage of the data centers. Make them pay you. Make them pay your community. But take advantage. Don’t shut them off. And there’s always somebody else in this country. But if it’s not this country, there’s somebody else outside.

We’re not going to allow our companies to build data centers outside of our country. We’re not going to allow that to happen. You know, they allowed that to happen with chips and everything else, and it was very destructive to our country. It’s where we started going behind.

So I just, I don’t know if that’s a breaking story. But I haven’t even said that to my people, but I’m telling them now. They’re here. Many of them are here. And we’re not going to allow companies to build data centers in other countries. If they do that, they can, but we’re going to put a tremendous tax on them.
