Best Amazon Device Black Friday Deals I’ve Found (2026): Kindles, Echo Speakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

My house has been powered by Alexa and Amazon devices to some degree for the past decade. There's always an Alexa somewhere in my house, ready for a quick command, and it's an ecosystem I find myself coming back to again and again—especially since Amazon always has something new coming out to catch my eye.

While not everything is on sale from Amazon's various ecosystems (sorry if you've got an eye on one of the newest Kindles; those have yet to be on sale), there are several great Amazon devices you can get for a better price than ever during Black Friday. Here are all the best deals I've found on Amazon devices.

Looking for more discounts? Don't miss our guide to the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals. Hunting for Kindles and e-reader deals specifically? I've got a story for that, too.

This is my new bedside companion. The Echo Time feels more like a second-generation Echo Spot than the actual Echo Spot that came out two years ago; I'm curious why they didn't just make this a third-gen Spot. The Echo Time brings back the larger screen of the original Echo Spot and has a fun range of customizable clock faces you can use. It has sleep sensing to wake you up in the half-hour window before your official alarm time. You'll have to place it perfectly for that feature to work, but I've liked it a lot more than I expected. It also has no camera, so you can put it in your bedroom without any worries. It's expensive for what it is, so now is a great time to pick one up for yourself.

If you want an Amazon speaker that's small with plenty of sound power, the Echo Dot Max takes the small form of the long-popular Echo Dot and adds better sound and a smart-home hub. It even acts as a Wi-Fi extender if you're an Eero user. The price is great, too, for all you'll get.

Looking for something small but more affordable? The fifth-generation Echo Dot has improved sound over the older Dot models (though this one's getting up there in years, too) and has one of the cheapest price tags you'll find for a good Alexa-powered speaker.

If you want an Amazon-powered speaker with the best sound, the Amazon Echo Studio is always the answer. The original Echo Studio was my pick for sound quality, and the second generation still is. It also has a built-in smart home hub with Omnisense technology, spatial audio, and Dolby Atmos, and it can double as an Eero Wi-Fi extender.

The best of Amazon's latest generation of smart displays is the Echo Show 11. The Echo Show 8 is usually my go-to, but I think the Show 11 is worth the extra money for its bigger screen and better sound. It also has a built-in smart home hub, but it won't double as an Eero extender like the Studio or Dot Max.

As an avid note-taker, I love the Kindle Scribe. It's my go-to device to bring on a plane in case I get the itch to write a list or brainstorm an idea, while also knowing I could switch over to read my latest library book all in one device. The newer Kindle Scribes have been hard for me to recommend with the high price tag, but right now the third-gen Scribe is actually cheaper than the older second-gen model. I also really like the homepage design on the newer Scribes.

If you're looking to upgrade your e-reader experience, try a color Kindle. The first-generation Kindle Colorsoft is on sale, letting you add color highlights and see your book covers in full color (though a little muted since it's an e-paper screen). It's been hard to recommend because of the price and the lack of other features besides the color screen, but this deal price is pretty great, since it's actually a little cheaper than the older model for once.

You can also get a better price on an even bigger color screen with the Kindle Scribe Colorsoft. It's on sale for a massive discount during Black Friday—$180 off—and adds a color screen to all the features you'd already get on the newest Kindle Scribe. It's a great device, though it does have a shorter battery life due to the color screen (Amazon rates it for up to eight weeks of reading or two weeks of writing). It’s only $50 more than buying the older, no-color second-gen model I've long loved.

If you're looking for the classic best Kindle to buy for a lower price, it's the Kindle Paperwhite. The newer version isn't on sale, but the 12th-gen version from 2024 is still great at an unbeatable price. You could spend a little less by buying the 2024 basic Kindle on sale, but I think the larger screen, a warm-toned front light, and longer battery life are well worth the extra cost. You can also get the Signature version on sale, but I wouldn't splurge on the older Signature, as the newer Signature will be compatible with Amazon's newest Kindle accessories, like the page-turn case.

If you want to control more things with your Echo speaker, these compact smart plugs from Amazon let you turn various devices in your home into voice-controlled ones. The plugs are a little expensive normally, but $15 per plug is a solid deal, so you can pick up a few of these for things like lamps.

These smart bulbs aren't Amazon-branded, but they are compatible with Amazon's smart home ecosystem. I love Cync's bulbs best out of all the ones I've tested, and you can get a two-pack for less than $30 during Black Friday sales.

One of my all-time favorite smart devices is a smart lock. If you want one that looks good without costing an arm and a leg, the Aqara Smart Lock U50 is the one I recommend. You will need a hub to let it have Wi-Fi and work with Amazon's smart home ecosystem, though.

If you want a TV powered by Amazon, our latest favorite from Amazon's lineup is actually its art TV. The Ember Artline is our favorite TV in Amazon’s lineup. Samsung’s The Frame feels more premium, but our team found the Artline plenty capable for displaying artwork, and it costs much less. It's a great option to get an art TV on a budget, especially during Black Friday.

If you already have a TV you love but wish it was controlled by Amazon, grabbing a Fire TV Stick will fix that. Our favorite of Amazon's streaming sticks is the Fire TV Stick 4K Max. You can get picture-in-picture from your security cameras that are connected to your Amazon ecosystem, and while it's designed with Prime Video in mind, you can install other streaming apps on here, too. You'll also get 16 GB of storage and Wi-Fi 6E support, plus Alexa voice control.

If you want a mesh Wi-Fi system, there's a lot to choose from these days. Eero has been around for a while and has a handful of different models, but the best one we've tried is the Eero Pro 7. It's our favorite for a simple system, and it also supports Alexa if you're already an Alexa user. Plus, newer speakers from Amazon can double as Eero extenders.
