Nvidia reinstates former CEO Jensen Huang despite China espionage scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring back the disgraced CEO, who resigned this month amid news of his affairs. He's suspected of having had a relationship with a Chinese intelligence asset.

US tech company Nvidia said on Monday that it had reinstated Jensen Huang as its CEO "effective immediately."

This follows just a matter of days after his resignation after admitting to extramarital affairs, reportedly including a dalliance with a woman who was a Chinese intelligence asset.

The board unanimously decided that his return was in the "best interests of the company," Nvidia said, adding that it was satisfied that Huang had demonstrated his "candor and ability to lead."

Meanwhile, the 63-year-old said in a statement that he was "honored" to return to the company and that he would take on the renewed responsibility "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment."

He thanked interim CEO Colette Kress, who is set to return to her role as chief financial officer.

"I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support," Huang said. "Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can't wait to get back to work."

US President Donald Trump rapidly commented on the development online, saying it was the "correct and proper decision" and referring to Huang by his first name as a "Great Patriot and National Treasure."

"The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen's return to Nvidia poses any risk to National Security," Trump wrote, also saying that the US had no evidence of him engaging in unlawful conduct.

Huang liked to flaunt his personal relationship with Trump. He was reportedly influential in convincing the US president to allow the export of higher-quality Nvidia chips to China that are useful in AI development than other US officials and entrepreneurs endorsed.

The decision was announced as trading started for the new week in New York.

The stock price of what investors deem the world's most valuable company has nosedived amid the scandal, with Nvidia's stock price falling from almost $220 (roughly €200) per share in late October to around $125 per share at the end of last week.

The announcement prompted a rapid early-morning rally on Wall Street, including Nvidia stocks rising back into a region between $160 and $170.

Huang quit earlier this month after admitting to extramarital affairs in a note to employees.

This concession came amid widespread reporting in the US linking him romantically to one woman in particular, who was running a major Nvidia client called Megaspeed based in Singapore and who was said to be an asset of Chinese intelligence services.

Her company is also suspected of diverting chips not approved for export to China to the Chinese market, circumventing US sanctions.

While these allegations have not been proven, they appeared to be based on the assessments of US and other western intelligency agencies, and to be information that was known for around a year. One possible indication of this was Singapore refusing the Chinese national entry into the country earlier in 2026.
