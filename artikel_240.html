This 50% off AMC+ Premium subscription is sneakily one of the best Black Friday streaming deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While many of the Black Friday streaming deals this year offer steep discounts, the majority of those discounts are only available on ad-supported tiers. If you're the kind of person who would rather watch paint dry than sit through ads, one of the best (and only) ad-free streaming discounts this year is on AMC+ Premium.

AMC+ Premium is highly underrated as a streaming service. It's where you can watch the entire Shudder library, as well as Sundance Now, BBC America, and IFC Films. So it's a must-have for horror freaks, British TV lovers, and indie movie fans. It's also the home of The Walking Dead universe, classic AMC and IFC shows like Mad Men and Portlandia, AMC+ original series like The Audacity and The Vampire Lestat, and some cult favorite films of the last few years, including Forbidden Fruits and Good Boy.

In order to get the annual deal, select the AMC+ Premium plan without ads, then you'll have the option to choose between the monthly and annual plans. Just remember that once the promotional period is over for either option, you'll be charged full price through your Prime Video account unless you cancel.
