Vindictus: Defying Fate confirmed for PS5, ‘Cinematic’ trailer teaser

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Medieval fantasy action RPG Vindictus: Defying Fate will be available for PlayStation 5 alongside PC via Steam when it launches in 2027, NEXON announced.

Here is a brief overview of the game, via NEXON:

Vindictus: Defying Fate is a fast-paced medieval fantasy adventure that combines adrenaline-fueled combat with the depth and customization of RPGs. Play with a large roster of distinct characters, each boasting unique abilities and combos, including series favorites Lann, Fiona, Karok, and Delia. Face fearsome Fomorian creatures based on classic Celtic mythology and brace for cunning monsters that tower over your character, poised to challenge your prowess. Embrace the challenge, embark on your journey, and be part of the saga that defies fate!

A teaser of an upcoming cinematic trailer was also released. The full trailer will premiere on November 13.
