Amorim calls for Milan consistency on all fronts ahead of Olympiacos test

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Ruben Amorim urged AC Milan to find consistency across all competitions as they look to turn around their dire Europa League form.

Milan travel to Olympiacos for their fifth league-phase game in Europe, with Amorim's side sitting 28th in the 36-team table, but just one point off the play-offs.

That is in contrast to their returns in Serie A, where Milan are fourth after three straight wins, including a 5-1 victory over Frosinone last time out.

Milan have won just one of their four Europa League games so far, though (L3), losing each of the last two to Bournemouth (3-0) and Ferencvaros (2-0).

They have never lost three major European games in a row without finding the net before, and Amorim says Milan cannot be classed as a good team unless they show it on all fronts.

"To be a good team, you have to do well both in the league and in Europe," insisted Amorim.

"That's the only way to be a good team. We have to be, especially at Milan. I'm always waiting for things to go badly, because it doesn't take much to change the situation.

"That's what I'm trying to make my players understand, because it won't always be this way. We're at the beginning of a process, and I think we need everyone.

"We lost [Aleksandar] Pavlovic, [Luka] Modric, [Alexis] Saelemaekers, [Samuel] Chukwueze, and everyone has to be ready, because there are no excuses at big clubs. We have to be ready for this. I can't believe we lost all these games in Europe because we rotated.

"We have to always be ready and approach the games like against Inter, Frosinone, and Genoa. We're building so many things. [It] is another test for us."

Milan have never won a major European away match in Greece in six attempts (D4 L2), failing to score in three of those matches.

They have netted four goals in the Europa League this term, but all of those came away to Salzburg in a single match.

"It's a fact that we need to improve in the final third," Amorim added.

"The only thing we can do is improve our principles. We need to improve on these little things, and that's the only way I can help my players.

"Against Olympiacos, it will be a different match than the one against Frosinone, just as it will be different than the one against Cagliari.

"We need to improve, even in our defending. We've almost always conceded a goal, so that's another area we need to improve."
