Arsenal legend Alan Smith names two stars who could be sold to fund January spree

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal great Alan Smith believes the Gunners will be tempted to cash in on Viktor Gyokeres and Martin Zubimendi when the January transfer opens.

The Premier League champions spent close to £200million bolstering their already formidable squad during the summer transfer window and are six points clear at the summit as they bid to hold onto their crown.

Arsenal signed Christos Tzolis from Club Brugge in the summer but failed to make any further additions in attack despite interest in Vinicius Jr and Julian Alvarez. While looking fearsome in all other departments, Arsenal’s forward line has still looked short on outstanding quality, not helped by Bukayo Saka’s absence with the winger expected to be sidelined until Christmas.

Gyokeres netted a wonderful strike in the Champions League against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday but is firmly second choice behind Kai Havertz in the league where he has started just one game this season.

The Sweden international recently played down talk of a mid-season move but former Gunners star Smith believes a sale could be considered at the Emirates if Arsenal find themselves needing to sell before they can buy in January.

Arsenal are interested in RB Leipzig star Antonio Nusa, with club legend Ian Wright telling the Gunners to go out and spend big on the Norway international. Bournemouth star Junior Kroupi is also firmly on their radar.

If it means funds for another recruit, Smith believes Arsenal will listen to offers for their striker.

‘Well, if it meant they could get somebody else in, I think you would,’ Smith told Metro when asked if Arsenal will listen to offers in January, speaking on behalf of Best Betting Sites.

‘He’s still a very sellable striker, I think. Other clubs would look at him and think he would fit into our style of play in a way that perhaps he doesn’t with Arsenal. And he showed with that goal last night that he’s still got something to offer. But on a consistent basis, it’s not been working for Arsenal.

‘So it’d be a good negotiating tool, I think, if you could do a swap, get somebody in and Gyokeres would go the other way.’

Zubimendi meanwhile was an almost ever-present figure in midfield last season following his £60million arrival from Real Sociedad but a draining season took its toll, ending the campaign on the bench with Myles Lewis-Skelly taking his place.

The Spain international is currently nursing a hamstring problem but has struggled for game time this season, starting just one match in the Premier League and Champions League.

Chelsea have been credited with interest in the 27-year-old with Smith believing he is likely be frustrated with his current role.

Asked if the Spaniard could be another who moves on in January, Smith said: ‘He might be. Zubimendi is probably getting a bit frustrated. He played so much when he first came, and maybe he was overplayed and began to look tired.

So it’s a bit of a conundrum, that one, whether Arteta’s decided he’s actually not the kind of midfield player he wants in there. He’s a world champion, he’s got great experience, you wouldn’t let him go lightly, but as things stand he’s on the fringes.’
