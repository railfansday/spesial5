Kevin Porter Jr. reportedly finalizing 2-year deal with Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Kevin Porter Jr. is finally on a team for the 2026-27 NBA season. A month after being waived by the Milwaukee Bucks, Porter will reportedly sign a two-year deal with the Orlando Magic, per ESPN.

Porter, 26, underwent knee surgery in April and was waived by the Bucks just days before the first game of the regular season.

Porter — whose 2025-26 season was delayed due to a knee injury — was out to a great start with the Bucks last season, averaging 17.4 points and 7.4 assists. But he was limited to just 38 games after sustaining another knee injury, this one forcing him to undergo surgery in April.

Porter picked up a player option to remain with the Bucks and was able to take part in Milwaukee's training camp. He appeared in the team's preseason game against the Indiana Pacers just prior to his release, scoring 11 points in 15 minutes.

Despite proving he was healthy, Porter remained on the free-agent market for over a month before reportedly joining the Magic. He also met with the Denver Nuggets during the season.

Once he signs, Porter will join his fifth NBA team since being drafted in the first round of the 2019 NBA Draft.

Porter has had some off-the-court issues that have contributed to his vagabond NBA journey, most notably pleading guilty to misdemeanor assault and a harassment violation in New York in 2024 in a case involving former girlfriend and ex-WNBA player Kysre Gondrezick.
