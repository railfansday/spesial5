Colts revealed to have turned down great trade offer from Ravens for Daniel Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Colts revealed to have turned down great trade offer from Ravens for Daniel Jones originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Indianapolis Colts managed to ship off a lot of players at the NFL trade deadline, but they notably didn't make a few other roster moves with Daniel Jones and Jonathan Taylor.

Of the two, their star running back would've brought back the best return. But, since he's under contract beyond 2026, the Colts didn't have to move him. Jones, on the other hand, was released after Week 11, and clearly should've been traded.

But, what could they have even got for Jones? According to ESPN's Adam Schefter, the Colts had a great trade offer on the table for Jones that they turned down in what's now very clearly a huge missed opportunity for the Colts.

"The Ravens offered the Colts a fourth-round draft pick for Daniel Jones and a fifth-round pick, with the condition that fifth-rounder would be returned to Indianapolis if Baltimore advanced to the playoffs," Schefter reports. "Minnesota also had interest in Jones. Indy held him, then released him."

This is a brutal report from Schefter for the Colts. They had a chance to land a strong return for Jones that, at the very least, would've been a nice upgrade from the fifth to the fourth round.

While that alone might not be worth trading a team's starting quarterback, in the Colts case, Jones only remained the starter for two games after that trade was declined by Indianapolis.

There was a scenario where the Colts accept that trade, and the Ravens make the playoffs, and the Colts walk away with a fourth- and fifth-round pick for Jones, whom they would've released.

Instead, the Colts didn't get anything for Jones, as they cut him one day after Thursday Night Football, and over one week after the NFL trade deadline had passed.

It's about as clear of a missed trade opportunity as it can get. Combined with not trading Grover Stewart and Taylor despite solid offers being on the table, the Colts arguably made three big mistakes at this year's trade deadline.
