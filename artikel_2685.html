Five takeaways from USC's 35-21 victory over Maryland

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On Saturday evening, the USC football team defeated Maryland 35-21 at the Coliseum. With the victory, the Trojans improved to 8-3 on the season and 5-3 in Big Ten play.

What did we learn from the game? Here are a few key takeaways:

By no means did USC play a particularly good game. The Trojans found themselves trailing a bad Maryland team 14-13 late in the third quarter.

Eventually, however, the Trojans stepped up and finally did what they were supposed to do. USC scored 21 unanswered points in a span of just over five minutes, with two Maryland interceptions helping them out.

Last week against Indiana, ball security was a major issue for USC. Against Maryland, however, the Trojans did not turn the ball over, and recorded three takeaways on defense.

Special teams were also a big problem for USC last week. This week was much better, with the Trojans converting both of their field goal attempts and avoiding any dumb special teams penalties. Instead, it was Maryland who did just that, with the Terrapins committing a late roughing the punter penalty that gave the Trojans a first down and effectively ended the game.

Wide receiver Trent Mosley continued to show why he is one of the best freshmen in college football. Mosley caught eight passes for 78 yards and two touchdowns in the victory.

USC will wrap up the regular season with the Crosstown Showdown rivalry game against UCLA at the Rose Bowl next Saturday. Kickoff is set for 12:30 p.m. Pacific time on CBS.
