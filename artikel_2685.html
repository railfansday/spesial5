Demarcus Robinson totals four targets in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Demarcus Robinson posted three receptions for 22 yards on four targets in Week 9 against the Las Vegas Raiders.

Why it Matters: Robinson has been seeing a handful of targets with the receiving corps in San Francisco banged up. Once Mike Evans is back, or Christian Kirk can make his debut, Robinson's fantasy appeal will dry up. As it stands, he is only worth a look as a WR5 in most fantasy leagues.
