Jahmyr Gibbs puts Lions up 10-0, Colston Loveland out with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Lions are up two scores in the second quarter in Detroit.

Running back Jahmyr Gibbs punched the ball in from the 1-yard-line with 12:51 to play in the first half and the Lions are now up 10-0 on the Bears.

The Bears’ comeback efforts will be hindered by the loss of tight end Colston Loveland. Loveland was carted off in the first quarter and the Lions announced that he is out for the rest of the game with a knee injury.

Gibbs is now up to 16 touchdowns on the season and he’s picked up 55 yards from scrimmage thus far. Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown has three catches for 69 yards, including a 23-yarder on the touchdown drive.

Loveland lost a fumble on the Bears’ first possession and the Lions forced a three-and-out on the second, so the offense needs to get in gear for Chicago.
