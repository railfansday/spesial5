Giannis Antetokounmpo isn't losing sleep over Heat's struggles: 'Hell no'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Yet the numbers don't lie, with Miami now ranked 19th in net rating, 19th in offensive rating and just 25th in defensive rating. It's that last data point that boggles the mind, with the Heat led by a reputed defensive coach in Spoelstra and boasting elite defenders like Antetokounmpo, Bam Adebayo and Davion Mitchell on their roster (not to mention others like Andrew Wiggins and Klay Thompson who were deemed as such once upon a time). For Antetokounmpo's part, he swears he's taking it in stride. "My level of belief is 100 percent," Antetokounmpo told The Athletic. "It never wavers. No matter what anybody says. That's how I operate." So … no sleepless nights? "Hell no," he fired back as he boarded the team bus. "Losing sleep? Hell no."The Athletic
