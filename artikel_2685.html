Aaron Rodgers’ brother Jordan sounds off on sudden benching

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers' brother Jordan sounds off on sudden benching appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

On Tuesday, the Pittsburgh Steelers announced they were benching Aaron Rodgers for their Friday game against the Denver Broncos. Instead, Mason Rudolph will start at quarterback.

On Wednesday, Rodgers' brother Jordan put them on blast for their decision-making, per ESPN. He pointed out that a practice was scheduled for Tuesday—a practice where Rodgers was presumably the starting QB. Then Jordan gave head coach Mike McCarthy the benefit of the doubt. McCarthy coached Rodgers with the Green Bay Packers. Instead, Jordan put it on general manager Omar Khan.

"Him and Mike have been together so long, the conversations have spanned of what you could with a head coach and a quarterback," he said. "So I don't think that's the issue. To me this has to be Omar Khan."

Then Jordan criticized Khan and the roster construction in part for their recent struggles, which include two straight losses to the Cincinatti Bengals and Philadelphia Eagles.

"This season and the lack of success to me is more of an indictment of Omar Khan and how he's constructed this roster because they are in the middle of the last dance and rebuild," Rodgers said. "It's a bad combination to be in that spot."

Rodgers is in his final season as the Steelers rebuild, with younger QBs like Will Howard and Drew Allar waiting in the wings. Meanwhile, Rudolph is an eight-year veteran.

In response, Rodgers didn't show up at the team facility Wednesday morning, seemingly upset about being benched. The Steelers and Broncos will kick off on Friday at 2:00 pm CST/3:00 pm EST.
