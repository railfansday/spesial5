KENS 5 snubbed from high school football state championships broadcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This was meant to be a monumental year for fans of Texas high school football. More specifically, it was meant to be a great year for high school football fans who enjoy watching the state championships on TV. Well, turns out folks in San Antonio are going to have a problem come mid-December.

The UIL football state championships run from December 16 through December 19, as 12 champions will be crowned at AT&T Stadium. Fans unable to attend in person have had to rely on a carousel of TV stations broadcasting the games, and this year Nexstar and its network of stations are set to play all 12 games for free, according to the UIL's broadcast information. However, don't expect to catch the games on KENS 5.

Nexstar may have acquired KENS 5 through its $6.2 billion acquisition of TEGNA and its numerous stations, but following the acquisition, Nexstar received a "Hold Separate Order" in April from the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California. That means Nexstar may own TEGNA stations, but all TEGNA stations operate independently of Nexstar's broadcast plans.

"TEGNA has no involvement in distribution arrangements Nexstar may have made for Nexstar-operated stations," TEGNA Content Coordinator Danielle Ingram said to MySA.

But fear not, San Antonio. If you have a smart TV, you can watch the state championships on one of Nexstar's many free streaming apps, such as KXAN+ (Austin), CW39+ (Houston) and CW33+ (Dallas). The apps let viewers create free accounts and stream without a subscription.
