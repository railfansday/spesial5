What Record Will the Cleveland Browns Have at the End of the Season? Reacts Survey

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Browns fans and fans across the country.

The Cleveland Browns have seven games remaining this season, including three straight home games after their bye against the Las Vegas Raiders, Cincinnati Bengals, and Atlanta Falcons. Then, they finish off at the New York Giants, at the Baltimore Ravens, back home against the Indianapolis Colts, and then at the Bengals.

When you think of the quarterbacks who will be playing in those games, five of the seven games seem quite enticing for the Browns' defense. And they sometimes have Joe Burrow's number too, although I hesitate to mention that this year because Burrow is on quite the tear.

Nonetheless, this week's Reacts question asks what record you think the Browns will finish with. They are 6-4 with seven games to go, so the best they can be is 13-4, and the worst they can be is 6-11. And then below that, we have our weekly confidence question, which I expect to get a healthy boost after the team's comeback win over the Texans, the bye this week, and being promoted to Sunday Night Football at the end of November.
