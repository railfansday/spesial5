Midterm election traffic spiked on Twitch, but Hasan Piker can’t hold a candle to Steve Kornacki

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The 2026 U.S. midterm elections resulted in a historic victory for Democrats, but liberals weren't the only big winners on the night of November 3. Streamers across YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Rumble picked up more viewership than they've ever received during midterm election coverage.

According to Streams Charts, election-related broadcasts on those four platforms combined to haul in 20.87 hours of watch time, collectively reaching 3.4 million concurrent viewers. Across 3,400 channels, election night traffic was more than four times higher than it was during the previous midterms in 2022. (Streams during the presidential election year of 2024, however, did deliver more traffic.)
