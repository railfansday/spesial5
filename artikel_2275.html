Raul Jimenez reveals major four-word update that Wolves fans will love

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Raul Jimenez has shared a fresh update on his ongoing recovery from an MCL injury, which will provide a serious boost to Wolves fans as the Mexican striker's return to action slowly but surely edges closer.

The good times are rolling right now at Wolves, who have ascended to second in the Championship behind league leaders Middlesbrough ahead of Saturday lunchtime's crunch trip to Wrexham following three successive victories over Watford, Cardiff and Bristol City.

It has, of course, relieved some much-needed pressure and scrutiny upon the shoulders of Cesar Peixoto, though the Portuguese boss is still not completely out of the woods and remains searching for the backing of some of the Old Gold faithful since his arrival from Gil Vicente to replace Rob Edwards over the summer.

Nonetheless, Wolves are beginning to truly find their rhythm and that's only made all the more impressive by the continued absence of Jimenez, who begun the campaign in strong form upon his emotional homecoming to the Black Country but has not kicked a ball in anger since September.

Jimenez has, however, revealed a new progression update relating to his recovery update, and the words used will provide delight to supporters, teammates and Peixoto alike as all at Molineux eagerly anticipate the veteran frontman's return.

The 36-year-old has been out of action since before the three-week international back in September after suffering a serious MCL injury 36 minutes into Wolves' 1-0 EFL Cup third-round defeat at Everton, in which many believed he shouldn't have been risked in the first place.

Peixoto, then, was left to rue his selection decision when the 130-cap Mexican international's injury turned out to be more severe than expected, forcing the Championship promotion hopefuls to initiate ligament surgery instead.

Surgery would see Jimenez ruled out of proceedings until January, representing a significant blow for Wolves in the wake of a lightning-fast reintroduction to life at Molineux after lighting up the World Cup with co-hosts Mexico by scoring three goals from four matches upon leaving Premier League outfit Fulham.

Wolves' patchy form following the international window amid defeats to both Middlesbrough and Bolton Wanderers ensured that his absence was sorely-felt, although others have stepped up to the mantle in recent weeks to allow the ex-Benfica and Fulham striker to focus entirely on his recovery process instead of rushing to get back sooner rather than later.

His latest update is, however, an extremely positive one, and it may leave the Old Gold faithful optimistic of an even earlier return to the pitch.

Jimenez has kept supporters in the loop with many social media posts and updates throughout his time on the treatment table, which will only be appreciated by the club's fans as they await his return.

He's been stepping up his recovery process in recent weeks, moving on to more intensive physical work as his condition continues to improve.

Jimenez has generally appeared to be in good spirits, too, and that's evidenced by his latest update.

Speaking via his Instagram story on Thursday, Jimenez wrote: "Making really good process 🔥💪🐺."

Just four words were required for an admission that will leave Wolves supporters delighted, with the caption accompanying a video of the goal-getting ace undertaking leg exercises in a bid to regain his strength and mobility.

It was either side of a series of stories posted to show Jimenez going through his paces with some arduous gym work, and he's certainly not prepared to let up or allow this injury to get the better of him as he continues to work harder and harder ahead of a return early in the new year.

Jimenez's fellow frontmen at Wolves have begun to find the back of the net with increasing regularity in recent times, and he'll only be hoping that trend continues until he's back on the pitch himself.

One of the main worries when Jimenez suffered his injury was about exactly who was going to come to the fore and provide the cutting-edge in the final third to compensate for his long-term absence after kicking off his second stint at Wolves with three goals from six Championship fixtures.

Thankfully, both Adam Armstrong and Sean Neave are finding form to ensure that Jimenez's absence doesn't end up derailing Wolves' campaign.

Armstrong, who has largely struggled for form since arriving from Southampton in February, looks to have rediscovered his goalscoring boots - which are esteemed at this level, of course - after recording his first strikes since August in back-to-back wins against Watford and Cardiff.

He wasn't on the scoresheet during Wolves' most recent midweek victory at Bristol City, where Newcastle United loanee Neave instead stepped up to the billing by appearing from the bench to score his first professional goals in style with a late brace in the final 10 minutes to seal a dramatic 2-1 away victory for the white-hot visitors.
