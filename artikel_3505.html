Elementary School Teacher Arrested for Allegedly Dealing Meth Out of Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A New Jersey teacher and another person were arrested after they were accused of dealing meth out of a home.

The Phillipsburg Police Department announced in a press release on Friday, November 20, that John Schorb and Jennifer Paulus were arrested earlier that day as part of a joint narcotics investigation with the New Jersey State Police.

Officers executed a search warrant at a home in Phillipsburg on Friday around 6 a.m., according to an affidavit of probable cause obtained by NJ.com.

During the search, investigators found pills of suspected alprazolam on the living room coffee table and what appeared to be meth and other pills hidden throughout the home, including in the bedroom and kitchen, per the affidavit.

Authorities reported finding other drugs and drug paraphernalia on the dining room table and in other areas. Additionally, white boxes labeled methylprednisolone that stored over two dozen white oval pills were found on the main floor of the house.

Police said that once the search was completed, Schorb, 44, and Paulus, 45, were detained on multiple drug-related charges that included possession of meth, possession of a controlled dangerous substance, possession of narcotic paraphernalia and distributing drugs within 1,000 feet of a school and within 500 feet of a public park.

Paulus worked as a fifth-grade teacher in the Phillipsburg School District at the time of her arrest, according to WFMZ.

She claimed to authorities that she did not know there were drugs in the home when she was arrested, per the affidavit.

The Warren County Prosecutor’s Office issued a statement following the arrests, stating that the “alleged criminal activity” did not appear to have taken place “on school property or involved students,” per TAPinto Phillipsburg.

PSD Superintendent Dr. Greg Troxell told the outlet that Paulus has been placed on “administrative leave and is prohibited from entering District property” following her arrest. Troxell added that the school district is fully cooperating with local authorities amid the investigation.

Troxell said that the district was “taking the appropriate steps within our schools to maintain a safe and secure learning environment for all students and staff.”

“We recognize that our community has entrusted us with its children, and we take that responsibility seriously,” Troxell added in a note to parents, obtained by the outlet.

It’s not currently clear whether Schorb or Paulus have entered pleas or retained legal counsel following their arrests. The Phillipsburg Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
