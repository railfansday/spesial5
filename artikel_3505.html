Jason Loves Cats In GTA 6, But Not All Of Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 co-protagonist Jason is, like many Americans, a fan of cats. Naturally, being an open-world game with seemingly endless possibilities, GTA 6 does indeed let Jason express that love by petting cats he sees. However, not every cat is down to be pals with Jason.

As shared on social media, if you're walking around Leonida and spot a friendly-looking cat, you can go give it a pet. Jason even talks to the cat, because you'd have to be a monster not to, and the action even gives him a little focus boost.

But not all cats are the same. Another clip shared online shows Jason carjacking someone, as one does in GTA. After driving away, loud meowing can be heard, and the camera cuts to inside the car as a cat viciously attacks Jason. A quicktime event is necessary to get the cat to stop, at which point Jason is able to stop the car and let the cat out.

We can only imagine what would happen if that were a Rottweiler in the back seat of that car and not a sort-of-large cat. There are plenty of other animals to kill you while you're roaming Vice City and the rest of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto 6, and most of those would be less embarrassing to mention in your obituary than "cat."

Grand Theft Auto 6 is one of the most expansive and detailed games ever made, and if you're looking for some help, you can check out our GTA 6 guides hub as well as our guide on what to do first.

In GameSpot's GTA 6 review, Tamoor Hussain said, "This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I'd wager won't be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7," ultimately awarding it a 10/10 score.
