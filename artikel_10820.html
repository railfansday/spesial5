Pastor goes viral after giving sermon in $4,000 massage chair

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A pastor has gone viral after delivering part of his Sunday sermon from a $4,000 massage chair, reclining and enjoying a massage while preaching to his congregation.

Michael Todd, the lead pastor of Transformation Church in Tulsa, Oklahoma, is known for his unconventional approach to sermons, often using unusual props to illustrate his messages.

But his latest service on November 1 has attracted attention online after he wheeled out a luxury massage chair and spent several minutes relaxing in it.

During his sermon, Todd climbed into an Osaki Champ II massage chair, which sells for around $3,999. But it wasn’t just for his own comfort, it was used to make a point.

“I would tell you that righteousness is right standing with God. But most believers are in what I call right sitting with God,” he told the congregation as he sat down. “You are living for Christ in a La-Z-Boy.”

He then reclined and activated the chair’s massage functions before continuing to preach about those who are only faithful when it’s easy.

“I’m only doing it if it’s comfortable,” he said. “I’m only doing it if it does not require me to move out of my spot.”

He then used the chair to explain how prioritizing comfort could prevent people from helping others.

“How good could I fight from this position?” he asked. “How good could I protect myself? How good could I actually help somebody who was in need right there?”

“Right standing with God means that I have to stop letting my life of righteousness be reclined.”

The pastor eventually left the chair, using the demonstration to encourage his congregation to become more active in their faith. Clips of the unusual sermon have since circulated on social media, attracting millions of views.

This isn’t the first time a pastor has thought outside the box. Earlier this year, another went viral for promising to make someone a “multi-millionaire” if they donate $10,000.
