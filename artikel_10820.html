KFC is replacing GTA 6’s missing physical disc with fried chicken

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

KFC Spain is celebrating the launch of GTA 6 with an unusual giveaway, offering fans a fried chicken disc to replace the missing physical disc in their game cases.

The promotion, called “The Replacement Disc,” comes as Rockstar’s highly anticipated game launched on November 19, 2026, following more than a decade of anticipation.

Unlike previous entries in the franchise, the physical edition includes a digital download code rather than a traditional game disc.

To mark the occasion, KFC has created a limited-edition fried chicken disc that fans can claim for free at select restaurants across Spain. The giveaway also includes special packaging designed to resemble a traditional video game case.

The Replacement Disc promotion runs on November 19 and 20 at ten participating KFC restaurants across Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Zaragoza, and Málaga.

Fans looking to claim one must bring their original GTA 6 game case to a participating location between 4 PM and 8 PM local time. Those who present their case will receive a free fried chicken disc while supplies last.

KFC has also designed exclusive packaging featuring Colonel Sanders on the cover, making it resemble a video game of its own.

The promotion is intended to recreate the experience of opening a physical game case and finding a disc inside, something that has become less common as publishers increasingly move toward digital distribution.

According to KFC Spain, the initiative was created in response to the ongoing shift away from physical media, particularly as collectors and players continue to debate the future of boxed video games.

Rather than offering a traditional replacement, the fast-food chain is giving fans an edible version that fits the theme of the launch.
