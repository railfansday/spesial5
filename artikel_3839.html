Book Nook launches December 1 for PC, later for Switch

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cozy diorama crafting game Book Nook will launch for PC via Steam on December 1, followed by Switch at a later date, publisher WINGS and developer Malapata Studio announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Build tactile, satisfying miniature diorama scenes piece by piece in Book Nook. Complete beautiful book nooks as you place furniture, decorations, and tiny details. Relax and enjoy the peaceful reward of every detail falling into place, as you slowly unfold memories with each diorama you build.

Learn what it means to build a life together, in this short, restful, cozy experience.
