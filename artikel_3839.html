How to get Sweet Dreams Sprite in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Treasure Hunters mini-season in Fortnite comes with its own array of unique Sprites you can find and collect around the island. This season is shorter than usual, so you’ll have to act fast to find them all. Perhaps the trickiest one to get is the Sweet Dreams Sprite.

Sprite hunting is generally a pretty tough process, especially for Mythic variants like this one. If you’re having trouble tracking it down, here’s how to get the Sweet Dreams Sprite in Fortnite.

The Sweet Dreams Sprite is a random loot drop item, which means you need to go looting around the island and hope you find it. For the most part, how easy or difficult it is to collect comes down to luck and random chance, although you can focus heavily on Sprite hunting to increase your odds.

Sprites can sometimes be found wandering around the island, but rare ones like this one are very uncommon to spot this way. Instead, you’ll want to focus on using Sprite Wanted Posters to dig up and open Sprite Chests and opening Vaults to find Sprite Chests. These two methods are the overall best ways to add Sprites to your collection.

If you’re determined to add the Sweet Dreams Sprite to your collection, there is one guaranteed method you can use to get it. This method takes a lot of patience, hard work, and dedication, but it does ensure you end up with this elusive Sprite in your collection.

Here’s a complete breakdown of how to guarantee you get the Sweet Dreams Sprite.

There is no guaranteed spawn location for the Sweet Dreams Sprite in Fortnite. Instead, you must loot around and hope you encounter it randomly, or work hard to use the method mentioned above to ensure you find one.

The Sweet Dreams Sprite grants you a random item each time it reaches a new level. Once it’s fully maxed out at level five, it grants you an array of Mythic loot. The value of the loot it provides increases with each level.
