Sheep grazing in solar fields offers farmers economic, ecological gains

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Cornell researchers found that solar grazing can boost beneficial insects at lower stocking rates while helping sheep farmers improve profitability. They also developed a decision-support tool to help farmers optimize flock size, costs and revenues for solar grazing operations.
