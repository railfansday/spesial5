PubMed Pauses Major Journal; CDC Alert Blocked; DOGE Chief's Spouse Hired at HHS

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

— A weekly roundup of how public health policy is shaping practice

The National Library of Medicine has stopped indexing articles from Scientific Reports in Medline while the journal is under review. Articles from the publication will not appear in PubMed during the pause. (Retraction Watch)

Some of the top takeaways for healthcare from the midterm elections: physicians won big, signaling renewed trust in science; the Make America Healthy Again movement is on life support; and making healthcare affordable is critical to voters. (STAT)

CDC scientists wanted to publish an advisory for clinicians on the risks of measles during pregnancy, but it wasn't approved. (Washington Post)

Richard J. Hodes, MD, the long-time director of the National Institute on Aging, will retire in December, the NIH announced.

Federal regulators upheld the right to unionize for early-career NIH researchers after a challenge from the Trump administration. (Science)

The New York Times offered a big-picture take on how the Trump administration is dismantling science and research in the U.S.

Rep. Frank Pallone Jr. (D-N.J.), the ranking member of the Energy and Commerce Committee, asked the Government Accountability Office to review TrumpRx to determine if it's actually saved Americans money on their medications.

The State Department plans to shift up to $125 million in foreign aid to cover skyrocketing energy costs, including some $49 million that Congress appropriated for contraception in low-income countries. (New York Times)

Just weeks after massive layoffs, the husband of Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) chief Amy Gleason landed a job at HHS's Office of the Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation. (Bloomberg)

A top lawyer at HHS was reassigned after reporting that he was trading stocks of at least one major HHS contractor. (Washington Sun)

HHS announced a proposed rule to update infant formula nutrient requirements.
