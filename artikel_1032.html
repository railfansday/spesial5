Jurgen Klopp began this international window knowing Germany would probably need to win both games to reach the Nations League quarter-finals, and they completed the first part of their objective with a comfortable 3-1 victory over nine-man Serbia on Friday.

However, the tougher of the two tests comes in Berlin tonight, as Xavi's unbeaten Netherlands side head to the German capital fresh from their 7-0 trouncing of Greece. A draw will only be enough for Klopp's men to reach the last eight if the Greeks lose at home to rock-bottom Serbia, so they almost certainly need three points.

Germany have no new injury concerns, but they remain without Bayern Munich forwards Jamal Musiala and Serge Gnabry.

The Netherlands snatched a 1-1 draw in the return fixture courtesy of Cody Gakpo's last-gasp volley, and a repeat result would do just fine for Xavi and Co.

The visitors are in the driving seat in Group A2 thanks to their rout of Greece, in which former Manchester City midfielder Tijjani Reijnders scored a 21-minute hat-trick in the first half.

It was not quite the perfect evening for the Netherlands, though, after Barcelona midfielder Frenkie De Jong was forced off with a back injury that has seen him leave the camp. Juventus' Teun Koopmeiners could replace him in the starting XI in Berlin.

This has all the makings of a classic, so we are predicting a high-scoring draw.
