Opetaia out of Wilder bout on Fury-AJ undercard

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Australian star Jai Opetaia was due to step up to heavyweight to box Deontay Wilder on the undercard of Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on December 11; Opetaia has had to withdraw from the contest after aggravating a hand injury

Jai Opetaia has withdrawn from his bout with Deontay Wilder.

The star Australian cruiserweight was due to step up to heavyweight for the first time and box Wilder on the undercard of Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua on December 11.

But an injury to his hand exacerbated in training has forced him out.

Opetaia's co-promoter Mick Francis said: "On behalf of Jai Opetaia and his team, I can confirm that Jai has unfortunately been forced to withdraw from this scheduled December fight with Deontay Wilder.

"Jai sustained a hand injury during his September bout against Noel Mikaelian and has since aggravated it during training."

Francis added: "This is a disappointing setback for Jai, who was looking forward to what promised to be a cracking fight. His focus now is on making a full recovery and continuing his quest to become the undisputed cruiserweight champion of the world."

Opetaia said: "Apologies to everyone and especially 'Bronze Bomber' and his team for withdrawing from the bout. Fighter to fighter I know the feeling and it's frustrating. I've always prided myself on fighting anyone, any place... It is what it is.

"The goal has never changed and still like I've always said remains the same. Undisputed! They all said I couldn't after signing with Zuffa Boxing, everything always against us, stripped and vacated multiple times ... but you watch.

Wilder however won't have a shortage of potential opponents offering their services as a replacement on the Principality Stadium bill.

Derek Chisora, who fought Wilder in an exciting rumble earlier this year, has publicly declared his interest in fighting on the card.

British heavyweight champion Richard Riakporhe posted on social media to tell Wilder: "I will fight you on less than four weeks' notice. I am ready for 12 full rounds. None of this eight round business, let's go."

Another UK heavyweight, Johnny Fisher also posted: "Wilder in Cardiff? Oh, go on then. Bosh."

Frazer Clarke has called for a battle of Olympic bronze medallists with Wilder too.
