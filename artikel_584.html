Gayle King’s Son-in-Law Virgil Miller Dies at 45

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

King to take a leave of absence from 'CBS Mornings'

Gayle King‘s son-in-law Virgil Miller has died at 45 following a battle with cancer.

“CBS Mornings” executive producer Jon Tower delivered the sad news to the morning show’s staff on Friday in a memo obtained by Variety. In the note, Tower shared that Miller died “over the Thanksgiving holiday” following a “courageous battle with cancer.”

Further details about Miller’s illness were not disclosed. King will take a week off from “CBS Mornings” to “help with family matters,” Tower wrote, as private arrangements are being made. The TV anchor is expected to return the week of Dec. 7. In December 2020, Miller married King’s daughter, Kirby Bumpus, in a private ceremony at King’s best friend Oprah Winfrey’s Santa Barbara home. Miller and Bumpus shared two children, son Luca, 5, and daughter Grayson, 2. King is the mother of two children, Kirby Bumpus and son William Bumpus Jr., from her marriage to ex-husband William Bumpus.

Miller was a Florida A&M University alumnus. There, he earned a bachelor’s degree in biology and a master’s degree in public health, while becoming a prominent student leader and drum major of FAMU’s famed Marching 100. Miller served for nearly 13 years in the U.S. House of Representatives, most recently as the chief of staff for Rep. Cedric L. Richmond (of Louisiana). In 2019, Miller joined the Washington, D.C. office of Akin as a senior policy and strategy adviser, later becoming a senior adviser focused on lobbying and public policy.

King regularly shares glimpses of her family life on social media, often featuring her children, their spouses and her grandchildren as they celebrate birthdays and special occasions. On Father’s Day, King posted a cute candid shot of Virgil Miller, Kirby Bumpus, William Jr., and his wife, Elise, at a World Cup game in Los Angeles after she gifted them tickets for the holiday. King regularly referred to Miller as her “fav son-in-law” in those heartfelt posts.
