Daily Show Desi Lydic reacts to Nvidia CEOs affair with alleged Chinese spy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It's been a few days now since Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted to having an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, and Daily Show host Desi Lydic is here to unpack the entire debacle.

"All right, look, I don't want to be sexist, but this is why I always say men shouldn't be CEOs. I'm sorry but they get all hormonal and horny when it's that time of the month — usually the first through the 30th," says Lydic in the monologue above. The host then recaps the many photos of the pair together at social functions, adding, "You guys were really flaunting it. Even the Coldplay couple ducked from the camera."

Lastly, Lydic shows a clip of Donald Trump defending Huang, with the president saying the CEO is "definitely suffering."

"He had an affair, not a terminal illness," says Lydic. "Why is this the one thing you can muster up empathy for?"
