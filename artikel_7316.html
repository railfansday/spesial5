Martech Entrepreneur Adam Robinson Among Nine Dead in Florida Plane Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Adam Robinson, an entrepreneur who founded and led multiple martech companies, is reportedly among those dead after a Learjet 60 en route to the Bahamas crashed in Palm Beach County, Florida on Sunday.

The founder, age 44 at the time of his death, according to TMZ, was traveling with his wife Helen Sharp, two young children, Emma and Charlie, their nanny, and two friends.

The private aircraft departed Austin-Bergstrom International Airport on Sunday morning and was airborne for more than an hour before plummeting to the ground in a residential area of Palm Beach County, resulting in the deaths of all nine people aboard, including two pilots.

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
