Huddersfield Town duo shortlisted for League One awards alongside Bradford City man

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Drury is in the running for the manager's accolade, while Sanches Fernandes is competing for the players’ gong.

Also among the four-strong player nominations list alongside Fernandes is Bradford City goalkeeper Jon McCracken.

The duo, currently on international duty with Cape Verde and Scotland respectively, enjoyed eye-catching months.

Fernandes either scored or assisted in each of Town's six league games, scoring an impressive five goals and notching two assists.

He netted in the club's keynote wins at Bradford and Plymouth Argyle ahead of earning his maiden international call-up.

Meanwhile, McCracken registered four successive clean sheets in five league games last month.

The duo are competing against MK Dons striker Aaron Collins, who scored eight goals in October, including a four-goal blast against Doncaster Rovers and Stockport County frontman Kyle Wootton, who struck four times last month.

Drury is among the nominees for the managerial gong following an excellent October.

Town collected 16 points from six unbeaten games last month, scoring 16 goals along the way to move up to the third-tier summit.

Oxford United chief Aaron Ramsey, former Rotherham United boss Paul Warne, now in charge at MK Dons, and Luton Town head coach Jack Wilshere have also been short-listed.

Award winners will be named on Friday morning.
