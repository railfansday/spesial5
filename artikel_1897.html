Sources: Man United set sights on Nathan Collins amid Mauricio Pochettino January reveal

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Manchester United have set their sights on a deal to sign Brentford star Nathan Collins, sources have told Football Insider.

The 25-year-old is enjoying an outstanding season for the Bees as he continues to turn in strong and consistent performances, placing a number of top clubs on alert.

Mauricio Pochettino is expected to strengthen his defensive options in the January transfer window, and work is being done to identify leading targets across the back line.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Collins ticks a number of boxes for Man United and is likely to be targeted in the near future.

The Republic of Ireland international's leadership qualities and reliability would be a major improvement on some of their other options amid a weakness at centre-back.

Pochettino may have his own targets in mind shortly after his arrival at Old Trafford, but Collins has been on the club's list for a while, and could soon be prised away from Brentford.

Football Insider revealed during the summer transfer window that Brentford were bracing for interest in Collins as Liverpool and Tottenham were among his admirers.

Since then, Man United have been keeping a close eye on the centre-back's performances, and are now prepared to join their Premier League rivals in the race for his signature.

Collins ticks a number of boxes for Man United, and could be seen as the ideal signing at centre-back as they continue to assess their options ahead of the transfer window.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I can understand why Man United want Collins, he's Premier League proven, he's got great experience even though he's still only 25.

"He's captain of Brentford, captain of the Republic of Ireland as well so he's got leadership qualities in that fact as well. Obviously Brentford won't want to lose him.

"Man United are obviously going to be carrying out a major defensive overhaul in the next couple of transfer windows.

"Nathan Collins would tick a lot of boxes, but as I said, Brentford won't want to lose him. It just shows maybe that United are looking within the Premier League for reinforcements."

Collins has emerged as a leading target for Man United as they step up plans to improve their squad, having failed to strengthen the back line during the summer.

This comes after former chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 12 November that Man United want to add a "big, physical" centre-back to their heart of their defence.

Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven finished the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa as the centre-back partnership, but lack the physicality provided by the more experienced Harry Maguire.

There are concerns that, when Maguire is not in the side, United lack fight and authority at the back, and they are now putting plans in place to resolve those issues.

Collins would tick a number of boxes in that respect, with his physicality a major asset, while his leadership skills would add some much-needed guidance at the back.
