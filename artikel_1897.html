Manchester United injury update: Latest news and return dates for first Mauricio Pochettino game

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Manchester United will, reportedly, emerge from the international break with a new managerial appointment.

A 4-4 home draw to Aston Villa last weekend left Michael Carrick on the brink of the sack, with the club languishing in the Premier League relegation zone.

It has since been widely reported that Carrick will be relieved of his duties as an agreement is in place with Mauricio Pochettino to become the next Man United manager.

The Argentine is no stranger to the Premier League, having previously managed Tottenham and Chelsea, though it will be a tough baptism of fire if he does take over at Old Trafford with his first game set to be against Liverpool at Anfield.

The task of getting a positive result on Merseyside will ultimately depend on the players available for selection as United went into the international break with fresh injury concerns.

Harry Maguire lasted just 45 minutes on Sunday after succumbing to a suspected back problem to add to club woes.

Here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

Maguire shook off a knock to start against Aston Villa, but had to be withdrawn at half-time with a back problem.

The centre-back appeared to be limping after being substituted late in Tuesday's 3-2 Champions League victory over Roma, but started at Old Trafford five days later.

Speaking after the game, Carrick told MUTV: "Harry had a bad back. A really bad back spasm that he had with maybe 10-15 minutes to go in the first half.

"I don't know how bad it is but, going into the internationals, there should be plenty of time for him to recover."

“Definitely not this week and not over the international break, it will be a little bit longer yet.”

Marcus Rashford continues to miss out with a persistent hip injury, which has kept him out since the 2-0 defeat by Atletico Madrid last month.

It had been hoped that Rashford could return to face Villa, but will now spend the international break building up his fitness.

Speaking recently about Rashford’s injury, Carrick said: “Marcus, we will just have to see, day by day, how he keeps improving.”

Luke Shaw has been sidelined with the muscular problem since the 1-1 draw with Tottenham in Man United’s first game after the autumn international break.

The left-back has not yet returned to first-team training.

Asked for an update last week, Carrick said: “Luke definitely won’t be around this week and fit at that point.”

Recent reports suggest Shaw is targeting a return to action against Liverpool, thus ensuring Pochettino - if appointed - can have Man United’s first-choice left-back available for selection.
