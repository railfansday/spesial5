Huddersfield Town chief Martin Drury highlights where Sean Roughan has transformed his Terriers' fortunes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While the on-pitch transformation in fortunes for the striker—one of the form players in League One so far this season—has been a standout feature of the Terriers' blossoming campaign, there have been other tales to savour for the league leaders.

Among them is the rise to prominence of defender Sean Roughan, who is starting to show the form which alerted Town to his potential during his yeoman service at Lincoln City.

The Irishman has impressed after casting aside an untimely thigh injury sustained in the club's opening game of the season in early August, the Carabao Cup tie against Preston North End.

Roughan, 23, has not looked back since being restored to the starting line-up for the game at Plymouth Argyle on October 17.

The Dubliner was excellent in the 2-0 victory at a high-flying competitor at the top end of the third tier, after stepping in for the injured Murray Wallace, and has started the club's last five league matches.

That spell, in which Town have been unbeaten, has seen Martin Drury's side pick up an eye-catching 13 points from a possible 15 ahead of Saturday's mouth-watering test against another form side in Leicester City, who visit the Accu Stadium.

Along the way, Roughan also laid a ghost to rest.

After a very tough appointment at Bradford City last autumn - when Town were humbled 3-1—he played an instrumental part in the visitors turning things around at Valley Parade this season in a 3-0 victory on Hallowe'en.

Head coach Drury, whose side are three-quarters of the way through a run of four successive home games in all competitions, said: "I've really been impressed by Sean's mentality this season.

"He's been out injured and he's missed some time. He's come back into the team. I think he's showing more leadership and he's more vocal and he's competing.

"It was a tough game at Bradford because they play long balls and diagonal balls and you're constantly fighting and scrapping and it's not a clean game. I felt his mentality was strong throughout that game and it has been in other games.

"So a lot of credit needs to go to Sean and the medical and performance staff for the way they brought him back."
