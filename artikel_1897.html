Sources: The real reason Crystal Palace want to appoint Thomas Frank revealed

Crystal Palace are closing in on an agreement for Thomas Frank to become their next manager, sources have told Football Insider.

Pierre Sage's dismissal was not in the plans for the South London club, who had been keen to stick with the Frenchman, but they have now been forced into a change.

The former Tottenham and Brentford boss has quickly emerged as the front-runner for the position at Selhurst Park, with talks having taken place throughout this week.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Frank's Premier League experience and remarkable work with Brentford have made him the top choice.

The boss is also viewed as a good motivator, which is something Palace are searching for to improve their morale having dropped to the bottom of the Premier League table.

Crystal Palace hope Frank's reign will reflect the work he did with Brentford rather than with Tottenham, where he struggled, and believe he is the right man for the job.

Football Insider revealed on 13 November that Crystal Palace are locked in talks with Frank to become their next manager and are making progress in their discussions.

A number of other candidates have been considered, including the likes of Sean Dyche and Vitor Pereira, but the former Brentford boss has always been the first choice.

Palace had initially hoped to have his appointment wrapped up quickly, but despite the drawn-out timeframe, they still believe he will be the man to deliver positive results.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Discussions are happening between Crystal Palace and Thomas Frank, they have made him their number one target to replace Pierre Sage.

"Obviously the key thing for Thomas Frank is that he's got the Premier League experience that Palace probably need right now to get them out of their current predicament.

"They want to make sure they're not involved in a relegation battle as the season goes on, and think Frank is the man to get them out of that.

"His CV is quite good. He's a good motivator as well. He did a great job at Brentford and I'm sure Palace will be hoping that he could repeat his success that he had at Brentford at Crystal Palace.

"The squad is good at Palace as well, I think it's just about getting the best out of them, and I'm sure that Palace feel that Thomas Frank is the right man to turn things around at Selhurst Park."

As previously mentioned, Crystal Palace had hoped to have the appointment of their next manager wrapped up closely after Sage's exit earlier this week.

However, one week after the 3-1 defeat against Liverpool which put the writing on the wall for Sage, they have still not reached an agreement with their top target.

Football Insider revealed on 13 November that Frank's appointment has hit a new stumbling block regarding the backroom team who would join him at Selhurst Park.

The Eagles are currently working to negotiate deals with Frank's rather sizable team, as they aim to back him with his trusted allies after doing the same for Sage previously.

While the deal is not expected to face any major issues as a result, the ongoing negotiations explain the reason for the delays surrounding Frank's official appointment.
