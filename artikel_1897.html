Premier League panel confirm ruling on Kai Havertz incident in Hull City’s defeat to Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Arsenal should have been awarded a penalty in their 1-0 win over Hull City before the international break, a Premier League panel have ruled.

Martin Odegaard struck the decisive goal for the Gunners, who played host to the Tigers on November 7.

Hull were widely lauded for their defensive grit in the game, although the division’s key match incidents panel has claimed they should have been penalised.

Tigers defender Nobel Mendy held on to Gunners forward Kai Havertz yet even after a VAR review, a spot-kick was not awarded.

The panel said: “Although Harvertz is clearly in an offside position, there is a clear and impactful holding action by Mendy before any potential offside offence by Havertz.

“Given the chronology of events, a penalty should have been awarded.

“The panel consdered the VAR intervention was correct; however, the outcome after the on-field review was considered to be incorrect as a penalty should have been awarded.”

Arsenal will not feel too aggrieved given they secured three points, but it would most likely have been a different story had they not.

Reflecting on the game, Tigers defender John Egan told the club’s media team: “I feel like we put a really good performance in, put a lot into the game. You know, what we worked on all week, we put on the pitch. Maybe [it was] a little bit of a missed opportunity the way we played.

“I think we deserved a result. We made it hard for them. [I’m] proud of the lads. I really thought we played really well, so mixed feelings. Disappointed not to get a result, but also a really tough place to come. We played really well.

“When you play a game like this, you know, you do everything you can to get a result. We did that. You know, on another day the ball falls to us. Today, it fell to them.
