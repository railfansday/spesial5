US Moves to Recover $1.1 Billion From Binance Hack, Saylor's Strategy Buys 457 BTC, XRP Briefly Reclaims $1 — U.Today Crypto Digest

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Monday brought a sharp rebound across crypto markets after Bitcoin's weekend crash toward $55,000.

XRP briefly reclaimed $1 as a short squeeze followed a major Nvidia-related rally, while Strategy continued adding Bitcoin to its treasury despite the market downturn.

Meanwhile, U.S. authorities took action to recover more than $1.1 billion in cryptocurrency linked to the October Binance hack.

The U.S. Department of Justice filed a civil forfeiture action on Nov. 23 seeking to recover more than $1.1 billion in USDC allegedly stolen from Binance by North Korean hackers.

The assets had previously been frozen under a restraining order obtained by the government. The latest filing represents another step toward recovering funds following the Oct. 29 attack, which reportedly resulted in more than $6 billion in stolen cryptocurrency.

The DOJ claimed that its recent civil forfeiture action focuses on over $1.1 billion worth of USD Coin (USDC), a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the U.S. dollar and issued by Circle.

U.S. authorities said they had helped freeze, seize or return more than $2.9 billion in cryptocurrency since the theft. The case highlights the role of cooperation between law enforcement agencies and crypto companies in tracking and recovering stolen digital assets.

Strategy purchased another 457 BTC for approximately $29.6 million, according to a Nov. 23 report based on the company's SEC filing.

The latest acquisition was made at an average price of $64,898 per Bitcoin, bringing Strategy's holdings to approximately 851,305 BTC. The company has spent around $64.23 billion accumulating Bitcoin at an average purchase price of $75,446.

The firm's recent purchase comes as the leading cryptocurrency has been struggling to rebound from the heavy sell-off that recently dragged it to a two-plus-year low.

XRP briefly climbed above $1 on Monday after Nvidia's Jensen Huang returned to his roles as CEO and president, triggering a sharp rally in U.S. technology stocks and broader risk assets.

On Binance's XRP/USDT perpetual contract, the token moved from approximately $0.94 to $1.02 within minutes after 14:00 UTC, before retreating to around $0.984.

CoinGlass data cited by U.Today showed that XRP short liquidations exceeded long liquidations by 578%.

Across the crypto market, 110,663 traders were liquidated for $660.89 million over the past 24 hours, while shorts accounted for $515.94 million of that amount.

The move showed how quickly market sentiment can shift during a volatile rebound, particularly when leveraged traders are positioned against a sudden price increase.
