Anthropic plans to sell 825 million shares in its Nasdaq IPO at $70 to $78 each, according to an updated SEC filing on Tuesday. At the top of the range, the Claude developer would raise up to $64.35 billion and be valued at as much as $1.405 trillion on a fully diluted basis, up from $965 billion in its May funding round but below the $2 trillion some backers once hoped for. Seven chip and cloud partners, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC, have agreed to buy $26 billion of stock in a private placement at the IPO price. Those shares will not face lock-up restrictions. Anthropic reported $33.24 billion in revenue for the first nine months of 2026, up about 1,200%, and said its run-rate revenue hit roughly $81 billion in October. It booked a $104 billion net loss, driven mainly by accounting changes on convertible notes. The stock will trade as ANT.
