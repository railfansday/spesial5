Luis Enrique: ‘Roma can win the Scudetto, I wouldn’t want to face them again’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Luis Enrique was full of praise for his former club, Roma, after their 2-2 draw away against Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League on Wednesday evening, claiming that the Giallorossi have what it takes to go on and win the Serie A title and to make a deep run in Europe this season.

Roma impressed as they held the back-to-back Champions League winners to a 2-2 draw at the Parc des Princes on Wednesday night thanks to goals from Mario Hermoso and Donyell Malen, becoming the first side to take any points off PSG in the Champions League this season.

Roma have also started their Serie A season in impressive fashion: They are one of two sides that remain unbeaten after the first 12 league games of the season and are currently in second place on 30 points, two shy of league leaders and city rivals Lazio.

Enrique, whose first job in charge of a senior side came with Roma in 2011, claims that the Giallorossi could go all the way and win the Serie A title under Gian Piero Gasperini this season.

“Gasperini’s Roma is unique, they have a lot of personality and are very aggressive, we saw that from the first few minutes,” Enrique told Prime Video after full-time on Wednesday night (via Mediaset).

“They scored from their first opportunity. It was tough, a really complicated game,” he continued.

“Congratulations to Roma for the way they played, I’m happy with a point and we could have lost.

“Roma have a wonderful future ahead of them. They can go and win the Scudetto, I have no doubt that they can finish among the top 24 [in the Champions League], and I wouldn’t want to face them again. Maybe in the final.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
