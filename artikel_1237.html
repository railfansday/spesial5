Augmenting reality with integrated photonics

Nature volume 659, pages 915–926 (2026) Cite this article

Augmented-reality (AR) glasses promise to transform how we interact with the world by seamlessly blending digital content with our physical surroundings. Yet making them viable for comfortable, all-day use remains deeply challenging, constrained by hard trade-offs of size, power and display performance. This calls for fundamentally new ways to generate and control light with high precision in an ultracompact form factor. Here we examine how integrated photonics can help bridge the technology gap in AR hardware. We review the basics of AR displays and recent advances in integrated photonics for new AR display components and architectures, then turn to key challenges and future directions, from component development to system innovation and scalable manufacturing, and highlight broader opportunities for synergy with fields such as quantum computing, bioimaging and optical sensing.
