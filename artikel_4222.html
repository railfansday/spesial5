Everything In The GTA 6 Selfridges Collection Is Available Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

You have almost certainly seen the stunning new GTA 6 display in London’s Selfridges, filled with new merch, some of which can’t be found anywhere else. There is good news for those of you who won’t be popping to the UK anytime soon, though - everything in the new collection is available to buy online.

There are 62 items in the GTA 6 x Selfridges collection, some of which are also available through the Rockstar website, others that aren’t - and may never be - available elsewhere. The GTA 6 varsity jacket, the most expensive item in the collection, falls into that latter category.

Priced at $3,025, the jacket is black and white with some rather colorful Vice City-themed embroidery on its back and up and down its sleeves. The good news and bad news for those of you tempted by it is that the jacket appears to have already sold out. Bad news because you can’t have one, good because you just saved yourself $3,000.

T-shirts, hats, bags, and more have been added to what is now a far more full GTA 6 merch store.

There is a second, all-black, considerably cheaper varsity jacket with Vice City across the chest. That one costs a measly $2,115. For those of you not interested in spending multiple current-gen consoles worth of money on a single piece of GTA 6 merch, most of the other items available won’t require you to not pay your mortgage for a couple of months.

A lot of those cheaper items are the same ones you can buy through Rockstar. T-shirts and caps with Rockstar and GTA 6 logos, for the most part, and I would advise buying them through Rockstar if you can. The T-shirts, for example, are $35 when bought directly from Rockstar, but $67 at Selfridges for what I’m almost certain are the same items.

You might catch out some casuals in-store with those inflated prices, but you won’t be catching us, or most others shopping for GTA 6 merch online.

There is a lot of cool stuff at Selfridges that you can’t find anywhere else, for any price. A GTA 6 basketball, a dartboard, and some beach towels sporting the VI from the GTA 6 logo. There’s even merch themed after some of the game’s fictional sports teams and brands, which might be the best of this collection.

There’s a ton of merch dedicated to the Vice City Manatees, including a football and a plush of the team’s manatee mascot. The Vice City Narcos, GTA 6’s basketball team, also has lots of merch available. Plus lots of items that have been designed to look like you bought them from Burger Shot, a chain of fast food restaurants you’ll be able to visit in GTA 6.

That includes a $115 pair of slacks that are part of the Burger Shot uniform.

The collection also includes a selection of “I Love VC” items that are designed to look like the I Love NYC stuff you can buy on every street corner in New York City. You can also pick up clothes adorned with GTA postcards, enamel pins, magnets, silk scarves, keychains, the first ashtray I’ve seen on sale for about 20 years, a members-only jacket, a luggage tag, and a money clip.

You know what, I lied. There aren’t 62 items in the Selfridges GTA 6 collection; there are 63. The $3,025 jacket isn’t the most expensive thing on sale either. No, it’s actually the Vice City Grotti Cheetah in the store window. That’ll cost you a cool £1 million, or roughly $1.34 million. I'm assuming there's just one of those, but at the time of typing this, it is still available.

Just like Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 6 is getting a collector's bundle instead of an edition featuring the game.
