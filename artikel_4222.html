Researchers trace how gut microbes transform dietary plant sterols into circulating sulfated metabolites

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Phytosterols are plant-derived compounds found in vegetable oils, nuts and seeds. They are studied for their ability to reduce cholesterol absorption, yet only about 5% enter the circulation and most are excreted. How gut bacteria influence their metabolism and tissue distribution has remained unclear.
