Governors Awards 2026 Red Carpet Photos: Zendaya, Inde Navarrette, Timothée Chalamet and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The red carpet for the Academy’s 2026 Governors Awards is now open.

Inde Navarrette, Jaafar Jackson, Andrew Garfield, Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder are just a few of the Oscar contenders who have arrived for the 17th gala at the Ray Dolby Ballroom in Hollywood.

This year’s program will present honorary Oscars to Glenn Close, Ridley Scott and Disney’s first Black animator Floyd Norman. Scott previously announced that he is unable to make it to Los Angeles to accept the award in person.

Killer Films co-founders Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler will receive the Irving G. Thalberg, making the first female producing team and first independent producers to earn the honor.

The Governors Awards are considered the first for the year’s Oscar contenders, allowing nominee hopefuls to shake hands and schmooze with hundreds of voting members of the Academy.

The evening is produced by Jennifer Todd and presented in partnership with Rolex.
