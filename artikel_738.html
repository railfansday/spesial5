Williams handed F1 penalties after minor breach of cost cap

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

watch the Qatar GP on Sky Sports F1 this weekend, with qualifying at 6pm on Saturday and the race at 4pm on Sunday

Williams have reached an "Accepted Breach Agreement" with the FIA in terms of F1's cost cap, receiving financial and wind tunnel time penalties.

"The FIA has determined that the team committed a minor overspend breach and an accompanying procedural breach, unintentionally exceeding the cap by £627,000 - 0.48 per cent over the 2025 cost cap," a Williams statement read.

"The agreed outcome is a financial penalty and a 1.5 per cent reduction in wind tunnel and CFD testing over the next 12 months.

"The breach relates to administrative costs we incurred making changes to our company structure. We believed these costs fell outside of the cap. Following discussions with the FIA, we have agreed to resolve the matter in line with their interpretation that the costs should have been in cap.

"The FIA has noted that Williams did not gain any sporting or financial advantage, and nor did we ever seek to. It has also noted that we acted cooperatively and in good faith throughout the review process.

"Integrity is central to what Williams stands for and we can now close this case and remain focused on our future.

"We have always been and will continue to be strong supporters of the cost cap, which naturally requires accurate policing to be effective."
