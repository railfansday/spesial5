Billie Eilish & FINNEAS Follow Lucy Dickins to CAA for Global Touring, WME to Share in North America

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kirk Sommer will share North American representation for Eilish in North America and continue booking FINNEAS in the territory.

Billie Eilish and FINNEAS have followed Lucy Dickins to CAA for international booking, while Dickins will share representation of Eilish in North America with WME’s Kirk Sommer, Billboard can confirm. Sommer will continue booking FINNEAS in North America, and the siblings will continue to be represented by WME’s Max Maulitz for film and TV. The news was first reported by ROSTR.

The news arrives several months after Dickins joined CAA after stepping away from WME, where she led the agency’s contemporary music division. On Monday (Nov. 16), CAA announced a strategic partnership with Korean company HYBE, a deal in which Dickins also played a part.

Eilish’s 2019 song “Bad Guy hit No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, and she’s had two songs land at No. 2, including “Birds Of A Feather” and “Therefore I Am.” Two of her albums have spent three weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, including 2019’s When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? and 2021’s Happier Than Ever. Her most recent release, 2024’s Hit Me Hard And Soft, peaked at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and has spent 130 weeks on the chart.

Eilish’s brother, FINNEAS, serves as her producer and has released three solo albums, including 2021’s Optimist, 2024’s For Cryin’ Out Loud! and 2025’s The Dream.

In 2024, Eilish and FINNEAS moved to WME for representation in touring and live events after having previously been booked by Wasserman Music (now THE•TEAM). WME has represented Eilish and FINNEAS for film and TV since January 2023.

CAA and representatives for Eilish and FINNEAS did not respond to Billboard‘s request for comment at press time.
