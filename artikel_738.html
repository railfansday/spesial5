‘SmartLess’ Trio to Back ‘Small Town Narcos’ Podcast (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The partnership with New Metric Media will produce a Chris Kelly-hosted true-crime comedy podcast about Canada's largest drug sting.

SmartLess hosts and creators Jason Bateman, Will Arnett and Sean Hayes are partnering with Canadian indie studio New Metric Media and the CBC, the country’s public broadcaster, on a true-crime comedy podcast series, Small Town Narcos, to be hosted by Chris Kelly.

The six-part Canadian series, to premiere globally in Feb. 2027, will center on a 1989 drug sting in the province of New Brunswick, and combine a real-time investigation, interviews with retired Mounties and cartel lawyers, archival materials and dramatic recreations. The podcast will recount two Colombian cartel pilots crash-landing a plane half-filled with Pablo Escobar’s cocaine into the New Brunswick woods, only to be arrested by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) after a tip-off and in place to make the country’s largest-ever drug bust.

The drama to follow included a foiled jailbreak plot, a daylight assassination, a Supreme Court case argued in secret and an RCMP informant who had infiltrated the cartel. Small Town Narcos, to launch at On Air Fest in Brooklyn, follows on a stable of comedic podcasts from Smartless Media that includes the mega-hit SmartLess, Just Jack & Will, Bad Dates and Owned.

Kelly, while hosting and executive producing the Canadian podcast, is also vp of podcasts at New Metric Media, the producer of Letterkenny and the spinoff Shoresy that has also branched out into podcasts, gaming and other digital media ventures.

“I grew up in a corner of Canada where I was sure nothing exciting had ever happened. Then I found out that for one stretch of 1989, it was the most exciting place on earth. It’s like learning your boring parents were secretly spies!” Kelly said in a statement. “As we launch New Metric Podcasts, we’re looking to create exactly these kinds of stories – original ideas, distinctive creative voices and unexpected worlds that can connect with audiences in a big way. Small Town Narcos is a great example of what we want to build, and we’re excited to bring it to life with our incredible partners at CBC and SmartLess Media.”

Small Town Narcos, written by Kelly and Eunice Kim, is produced by SmartLess Media and New Metric Media for the CBC. The executive producer credits are shared by Arnett, Bateman, Hayes, Richard Korson, Bernie Kaminski, Mark Montefiore, Kelly, Chris Okea and Cesil Fernandes, with Kim serving as senior producer.
