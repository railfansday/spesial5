Hulu Series ‘The Spot’ Starring Claire Danes & Ewan McGregor Gets Release Date, First-Look Photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Spot, Hulu‘s upcoming psychological thriller starring Claire Danes and Ewan McGregor, will premiere on Jan. 13. Along with the release date announcement, the streamer has released first-look images from the A24 drama series.

Created and written by Ed Soloman, who serves as showrunner, The Spot follows a successful surgeon (Danes) and her schoolteacher husband (McGregor) who begin to suspect she might be responsible for a child’s hit-and-run death. Their quest for truth spirals into a web of mounting suspicion and dark secrets, testing their resolve and their relationship as they confront the possibility of hidden guilt and betrayal.

Daveed Diggs, Michael Patrick Thornton and Macon Blair also star. Danes succeeded Kate Winslet who had been originally attached to the project. Watch on Deadline

Soloman executive produces alongside A24, Robin Sweet and Ariel Kleiman, who directed. A24 serves as lead studio, co-producing with 20th Television.
