Michael Jackson Lawyer Sued by Siblings Who Accuse Him of Silencing Abuse Claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The five adult Cascio siblings who allege Michael Jackson groomed and sexually abused them when they were minors have sued prominent entertainment attorney Bryan Freedman for legal malpractice, alleging he duped them into signing an “unconscionable agreement” that silenced them and shielded the late pop star’s estate.

In a new lawsuit filed Wednesday and first reported by Rolling Stone, Frank Cascio and his siblings Dominic, Marie-Nicole, Edward, and Aldo allege that Freedman “exploited the family’s vulnerability and trust” to persuade them to sign a 2019 agreement with Jackson’s estate. Freedman was already representing Edward and Marie-Nicole’s husband in separate legal matters at the time, according to the complaint.

The siblings allege Jackson’s estate was seeking to contain fallout from the 2019 documentary Leaving Neverland, which featured allegations of sexual abuse against the late pop star, when it purportedly enlisted Freedman to “silence further victims from coming forward.” They say Freedman reached out and ultimately managed to “deceive [them] into signing a hush-money agreement.” According to the lawsuit, Freedman assured the siblings he was acting in their best interests. They say he discouraged them from hiring their own lawyers while concealing his alleged conflict of interest.

The siblings say the agreement was presented as a deal to acquire the rights to their life stories, but that Freedman failed to disclose provisions barring them from publicly discussing Jackson’s alleged abuse and releasing the estate from any liability for allegedly facilitating or concealing it. Under the agreement, the estate was to make five annual payments of $690,000 to each sibling, and any disputes would be resolved through mandatory arbitration, according to the lawsuit.

Attempts to reach Freedman were not immediately successful. A lawyer for the estate did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

According to the new lawsuit, Freedman returned to the family in 2024, after they signed the initial agreement, and allegedly said the estate wanted to make further payments to the siblings because it had not paid them enough. Freedman purportedly called the original agreement “toilet paper” and claimed he “had enough on the estate” to convince it to “do right” by the Cascios.

The siblings said they recognized a conflict of interest at this point and hired a different lawyer to represent them and let the estate know that they intended to challenge the agreement as “void and unconscionable.” They subsequently filed a child sex trafficking lawsuit in February that was ordered into arbitration in August.

The new malpractice lawsuit against Freedman, filed in Los Angeles County Superior Court by prominent attorney Howard King, also includes claims for breach of fiduciary duty, fraud, and negligent misrepresentation.

In the new complaint and prior court filings, the siblings allege Jackson used his wealth and fame to groom them after meeting them through their father, who worked at a luxury hotel the pop star frequented. They say Jackson gained the family’s trust with gifts and affection before isolating the children from trusted adults, giving them drugs and alcohol, exposing them to pornography, and sexually abusing them individually. A lawyer for the estate previously called the siblings’ allegations “a desperate money grab.”

“The [Cascio] family staunchly defended Michael Jackson for more than 25 years, attesting to his innocence of inappropriate conduct,” Martin Singer, a lawyer for Jackson’s estate, said in a prior statement to Rolling Stone. He claimed the siblings were attempting to obtain “hundreds of millions of dollars from Michael’s estate and companies.”
