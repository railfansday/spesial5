Madonna & Slayyyter Lead 2027 Dance/Electronic Grammy Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Grammy Awards are set to take place Feb. 7 in Los Angeles.

Madonna, Slayyyter, Tame Impala and Tinashe lead the nominees in the dance/electronic categories at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

Nominees were announced early on Monday (Nov. 16), setting up the race for the awards ceremony happening Feb. 7 in Los Angeles.

As widely predicted, Madonna leads the pack, with nominations in all four of the dance electronic categories. Her July album Confessions II generated nods for best dance/electronic album, best dance/electronic recording, best dance pop recording and best remixed recording.

Meanwhile, Slayyyter comes in second on the list, with her March album Wor$t Girl In America getting nods in the best dance/electronic album category along with best dance/electronic recording and best dance pop recording.
