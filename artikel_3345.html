Atlanticus (ATLC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Two divestitures and record receivables shape a strong quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Atlanticus Holdings Corporation (ATLC -5.41%) reported third quarter results characterized by record managed receivables of $7.1 billion and a 60% increase in total operating revenue to $776 million. Management completed two significant strategic divestitures during the period, selling the CAR Auto Finance operations for $71.2 million and the Mercury intellectual property for $27.5 million to concentrate capital on core consumer credit opportunities. The company reported adjusted diluted EPS of $3.00 and an adjusted return on equity of 30%, which management attributed to sustained organic growth and the ongoing integration of the Mercury portfolio. CFO Bill McCamey noted that while expenses rose to $162 million, the operating expense ratio improved as revenue growth outpaced the expansion of the platform.

Second, we completed the sale of the MERCURY word mark and related intellectual property for $27.5 million in cash. The transaction did not include any credit card receivables associated with the Mercury-branded program. Under the agreement, we have the right to use the brand for marketing purposes for 12 months and for servicing for an additional 5 years, giving us ample time for the necessary rebranding. We view these transactions as a continued refinement of our strategic focus, and the proceeds will be invested in our higher return, higher growth lines of business.
