PS5 And Xbox Series X|S Console Sales Surged Just Before GTA 6 Came Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and to play the game natively, you need a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S. Unsurprisingly, new data shows that PlayStation and Xbox console sales surged in the week before release.

According to Circana's Mat Piscatella, there was a "significant surge" in hardware sales in the US for the week that ended November 14. PS5 sales were >2.4x the same week last year, while Xbox Series X|S sales increased by >2.3x.

GTA 6 is not available on Switch 2, but that console saw a 40% surge in sales compared to the same week in 2025.

Before launch, Piscatella said GTA 6 should help boost PS5 and Xbox Series X|S sales, but maybe not so dramatically due to the astronomical cost of a new console.

To help save money, you could buy an Xbox One and stream GTA 6. Alternatively, people have discovered that you can stream the game to a PlayStation Portal device.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 and had a gigantic debut, selling 32.5 million copies by launch and generating around $4 billion on day one.
