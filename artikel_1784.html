Tim Sherwood urges £64m Arsenal star to leave: ‘Mikel Arteta doesn’t want him’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Tim Sherwood has urged Viktor Gyokeres to leave Arsenal and believes Mikel Arteta has made it clear that he does not want the striker in his squad.

Gyokeres was signed in a £64 million deal from Sporting CP last year and he scored 21 goals in his first season to help Arsenal win the Premier League and reach the Champions League final.

However, the Sweden international has started just one game in the Premier League this season with Arteta preferring Kai Havertz to lead Arsenal’s attack.

Gyokeres scored an excellent goal to open the scoring for Arsenal in their 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday evening, but Sherwood feels the 28-year-old should push to leave in order to secure more playing time.

‘They don’t want Gyokeres, Arsenal don’t want Gyokeres,’ Sherwood said on The Overlap Daily.
