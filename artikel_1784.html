Brighton injury latest: Fabian Hurzeler issues Kaoru Mitoma update ahead of Newcastle

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Brighton will again be without Kaoru Mitoma for Sunday's Premier League meeting with Newcastle United, but head coach Fabian Hürzeler is increasingly optimistic about the winger's return.

The Japan international is recovering from a hamstring injury which required surgery and has kept him out since May.

Mitoma, 29, last featured for the Seagulls in the 3-0 Premier League victory over Wolves on May 9, when he was forced off after 58 minutes.

He has since missed Brighton's final two league matches of last season and all 11 Premier League fixtures so far this campaign.

Hürzeler confirmed the winger would not be available against Newcastle but suggested he was approaching an important milestone in his rehabilitation.

"Kaoru won't be available. That's what I can say. But I'm quite positive that all the other players will be available," said the Brighton boss.

"Like I said a few weeks ago, we have to be patient with him. He's getting really close, so I think in the next one or two weeks he'll be back training with the team. That's very positive."

Mitoma does though face a battle to get back in the first team. Maxim De Cuyper has impressed during his absence, while Femi Azeez, Ibrahin Osman, Diego Gomez and Yankuba Minteh can all operate on the left flank.

Ibraham is pushing for more Premier League minutes after his two goal blast against Craiova in the Conference League last night.

“Yeah, I think he [Osman] showed a really good performance [against Craiova], he scored the first goal, he created plenty of other moments.

“We know that his biggest quality is one against one, especially against teams who are sitting in a low block, this is very helpful to have these one against one players.

“He showed a very impressive style today, besides that I'm really happy that he understands more and more how important it is to keep working hard for having the clean sheet and also he's responsible for that

“As an offensive player because that's always the foundation of our game.”
