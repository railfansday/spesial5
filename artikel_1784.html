Wednesday Wisdom: Identify the missing Milan player from 2014-15

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Welcome to this week’s missing player quiz! We are starting off light with what we believe should be an easy one, given the iconic game, but it might still need some thinking.

We have been wanting to do quizzes for quite some time, but it wasn’t until a few weeks ago that the right idea popped into our heads. Having covered many Milan games, we will put that game knowledge to use by quizzing you on a missing player each week.

The first edition is from the iconic 5-4 win away at Parma, when Jeremy Menez scored that backheel goal. Obviously, he’s not the player we are looking for (would be a bad quiz!). Instead, it’s at the other end of the pitch, as you can see on the pitch below.

We don’t really want to give much away in terms of clues, but we will say this: he was actually the captain in this game. That might help some figure out who it is, but to be honest, we wouldn’t have made the association just based on that.

Let us know in the comments if you got the answer right! Also, since this is a new thing, please do give us feedback on what we can improve/add. We think we’re going to have a lot of fun with these quizzes as the weeks go by.
