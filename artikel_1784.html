Is Bradford City v Barnsley FC on TV? Channel and streaming details for Yorkshire derby in League One

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bradford City and Barnsley are set to do battle in a Yorkshire derby under the lights.

Both were involved in derbies over the weekend, with Bradford drawing 2-2 with Doncaster Rovers and Barnsley losing 2-1 to Sheffield Wednesday.

Michael Duff’s Bantams will be looking to climb into a stronger play-off spot with a victory on their home turf, while Barnsley are going to be desperate to escape the foot of the League One table.

Previewing the game against his former club, Duff said: “Barnsley didn't look like a wounded animal on Friday, but a football team with loads of energy and good players.

"I don't think we're at a point now where we can assume that, well, ‘we're just better than them'. Everything that's got us, this football club, the team, the squad, to where it has and the success it's had, is because it's had that underdog mentality. What we can't have is we slip into 'oh, we're better than them'. Because that's when trouble starts, isn't it?”

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Bradford and Barnsley prepare to lock horns.

Kick-off at Valley Parade is scheduled for 7:45pm tonight (Tuesday, November 24).

Yes - those without a ticket can watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions are available online.

Supporters can stream via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Leeds and BBC Radio Sheffield.
