All the Photos of Princess Kate and Prince William on Their Overseas Tour of Mumbai

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The royal pair will spend three days in India for the 2026 Earthshot Prize Awards

On Monday, November 16, Princess Kate and Prince William landed in Mumbai, India, for their first overseas royal tour in four years. The last time the pair made an international state visit was when they traveled to the Caribbean in the spring of 2022 to celebrate the late Queen Elizabeth II’s Platinum Jubilee.

Now, the Prince and Princess of Wales have hit the road again for the 2026 Earthshot Prize Awards, which are being hosted in Asia for the first time ever. The annual prize, founded by Prince William with an inaugural ceremony in 2021 and set to last until 2030, celebrates innovators who are breaking ground in the environmentalism space.

While it was announced back in February that William would be traveling for the ceremony, it was confirmed only this past weekend that Kate would be joining him. This marks her first major trip with William since her 2024 cancer diagnosis and eventual remission (she also took a solo trip to Italy this May).

The 2026 Earthshot ceremony takes place on November 17, but Kate and William are spending a total of three days in India. They began their trip with a visit to Ballard Estate, the business and shopping district of Mumbai. There, the Prince and Princess of Wales played a bit of gully cricket, took a ride on the metro, and visited local vendors and performers. Then, on day two, they stopped by the Bandra Worli Sea Link promenade and attended the Children’s Earthshot ceremony before dressing up for the official event.

For the rest of their time abroad, the royals will be “celebrating innovation, sustainability, youth leadership, and the enduring connections between the United Kingdom and India,” per their office at Kensington Palace.
