Dakota Johnson Doubles Down on Animal Print for a Night Out With MGK

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The two even coordinated in studded accessories

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly’s Hollywood couple style is getting more fun by the day. The pair was seen leaving a recording studio in Los Angeles early this morning (presumably after spending the night there, recording), and their coordinating looks were so actress-dates-rockstar-coded.

For her base, Johnson wore a classic white T-shirt, baggy blue jeans, and black platform boots. But the layers were what really made the look. To stay warm, the Materialists star wrapped herself up in a long tiger-print coat with a black shearling collar. The sharp outerwear is the “Nina coat” by Nour Hammour and retails for $4,120.

But one animal print is not always enough. To double down on the pops of wild pattern, Johnson cinched her jeans with a tan-and-black belt that looked like a blend between tiger print and leopard print. Either way, it went perfectly with her coat. Some rings decorated Johnson’s fingers, while from her arm hung a little black leather bag covered in silver studs.

MGK—born Colson Baker—popped on his own studded accessory: a black leather belt with silver hardware. He also chose the same base as his girlfriend: a white tee and slouchy blue jeans. The “Bloody Valentine” singer completed his fit with big red boots, a bright red beanie, and layers of silver and diamond jewelry, including a luxe choker.

Photos show MGK stretching his hand out for Johnson to help her down some stairs, and the actress flashing a flirty smile.

Couple or no couple, this duo’s fashion is certainly keeping us entertained.
