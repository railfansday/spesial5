I would recommend HNW investors keep 7 to 10 years living expenses in fixed - either in tips or treasuries or CDs and keep the rest in equities...obviously you have to sell equities and replenish your fixed every year or 2, and/or make adjustments if you have sudden expenses or situations that require you to spend more fixed than planned.
That's more or less my plan, increase equity allocations when the portfolio is increasing or income (SS) increases, hold AA if markets are down.
