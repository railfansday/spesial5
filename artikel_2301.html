Man City injury, suspension list and return dates for Nottingham Forest clash: Antoine Semenyo latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

By Oliver Thomas | 05 Nov 2026 07:00, Last updated: 05 Nov 2026 20:54

Manchester City return to Premier League action on Saturday evening when they travel to the City Ground to face Nottingham Forest.

The Citizens head into this fixture after beating RB Leipzig 3-2 in a thrilling Champions League contest held in Germany on Wednesday night.

Enzo Maresca’s men had previously suffered a 1-0 home defeat to Brighton & Hove Albion in the Premier League, allowing champions Arsenal to climb above them to the top of the table.

City are now seeking to win their final match before November’s international break, and here, Sports Mole rounds up their latest injury and suspension news ahead of their upcoming clash with Oliver Glasner’s men.

Possible return date: December 19 (vs. Hull City)

Antonine Semenyo is facing around eight weeks on the sidelines with a knee injury picked up against Ipswich Town on October 17.

Man City have no suspended players for this match.
