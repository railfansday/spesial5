Flight canceled? Capital One will now let some travelers switch airlines for free

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Flight delays and cancellations can be a headache for anyone, but for business travelers, a disrupted flight can also mean missed meetings and potentially missed deals.

Capital One is introducing a new perk designed to make those disruptions a little easier to manage. Starting Wednesday, select Capital One Business customers can receive complimentary flight disruption rebooking on flights purchased through Capital One Business Travel.

When your original flight is canceled or significantly delayed, the limited-time benefit allows eligible travelers to book a replacement flight on any airline at no additional cost, up to $5,000 per traveler. For instance, if your First Class flight on United gets canceled due to a maintenance issue, you can rebook a First Class ticket on Delta for no additional cost.

“Business owners and professionals often operate on tight schedules, and travel disruptions can have a significant impact on productivity,” said Natalie Conners, senior business director of Capital One Business Travel. “While delays and cancellations are often unavoidable, the stress and uncertainty they create don’t have to be.”

How Capital One’s free flight rebooking works

The complimentary benefit is automatically added at checkout when eligible customers book flights through Capital One Business Travel.

Capital One will start monitoring those flights 24 hours before departure. If a flight is canceled or delayed by at least three hours on the day of travel, travelers receive a notification giving them access to a self-service rebooking tool.

Flights can be rebooked for the same or following day, and must be in the same or a lower fare class and arrive at the original destination or a nearby airport. The benefit covers disruptions caused by weather, airport operations, and air traffic control issues, among other circumstances.

Travelers can rebook digitally without speaking to an agent, although Capital One also offers 24/7 live travel support.

Who qualifies for complimentary rebooking?

The promotion is available to select Capital One Business customers booking through Capital One Business Travel through the end of January 2027, while supplies last.

Cardholders can check their eligibility by logging into the Capital One Business Travel platform through their business account. Customers who don’t qualify for the complimentary offer can still purchase flight disruption assistance through Capital One Business Travel or Capital One Travel.

The new perk builds on Capital One’s existing business travel platform, which allows cardholders and their employees to book flights, hotels, and rental cars, earn loyalty rewards, and manage company travel expenses.

For eligible business travelers heading into the busy holiday travel season, the biggest advantage may be having more options when a flight goes wrong.
