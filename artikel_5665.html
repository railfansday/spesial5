Planning to Join the ANT IPO? Binance Opens $500M Tokenized Anthropic Share Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Planning to join Anthropic’s ANT IPO? Binance is giving crypto investors another way to gain exposure to one of the biggest technology listings of 2026. Binance Wallet has opened an eight-hour subscription campaign for Anthropic tokenized stocks through its xStocks partnership. The offering carries a $500 million USDC subscription cap, with indicative prices ranging from $70 to $78 per token.

The campaign opened on November 18 at 06:00 UTC and closes at 14:00 UTC. Eligible participants can apply through Binance Wallet using USDC, with a minimum subscription of 100 USDC. Successful applicants will receive tokenized Anthropic exposure following the allocation process.

The ANT IPO campaign uses an allocation system based on Binance Alpha Points. Users with more than zero Alpha Points receive a subscription quota of $20,000. Higher balances unlock substantially larger limits. Users holding more than 40 points qualify for $100,000, while those exceeding 100 points receive $200,000.

The highest tier requires more than 200 Alpha Points and unlocks a $500,000 subscription quota. Binance also offers an additional $50,000 quota to eligible users holding its Trenchers Badge.

These figures are the maximum subscription limits, not guaranteed allocations. The campaign uses an oversubscription model, meaning successful applicants could receive full or partial allocations.

Participants must commit USDC through a non-custodial smart contract. Unused funds will return to their Binance Wallet after allocation calculations finish. The subscription also carries a 5% partner service fee charged by xStocks. Binance Wallet does not charge an additional participation fee.

Binance has set an indicative subscription price between $70 and $78 per token. This matches Anthropic’s proposed IPO pricing range in its registration filing. Anthropic plans to offer 825 million Class A shares under the proposed listing. The company has applied to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

At the upper end of its proposed price range, the offering implies a valuation of approximately $1.41 trillion. At $70 per share, the implied valuation falls to $1.26 trillion.

The final offering price will depend on demand, valuation conditions, and arrangements between the issuer and underwriters.

The distribution process begins after the application window closes. Successful participants will receive their tokenized exposure after the underlying issuance is completed.

Investors who missed the Anthropic IPO window still have another crypto launch approaching. Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER) is heading toward its November 26 launch, with its presale already attracting more than $33.2 million.

The token currently sells for just $0.0136, putting it just below its indicated $0.0137 initial listing price. Its staged presale structure increases prices as allocations advance, giving earlier participants access before subsequent price steps. There is also no individual purchase maximum listed, leaving room for larger allocations.

Staking adds another incentive, with rewards offered at 35% APY. That gives early holders an opportunity to accumulate additional HYPER while the project prepares its token launch.

Bitcoin Hyper is building a Bitcoin Layer 2 using Solana Virtual Machine technology. The goal is to combine Bitcoin’s liquidity with Solana-style transaction speed, opening the door to faster BTC transfers, decentralized applications, and on-chain finance.

The timing could prove interesting. Anthropic’s blockbuster IPO is bringing fresh attention to alternative investment opportunities, while Bitcoin Hyper approaches its November 26 milestone. If crypto sentiment recovers alongside renewed institutional interest, HYPER enters the launch window with a $33 million fundraising milestone, an established presale community, and staking incentives already in place.

With the November 26 launch approaching, the remaining presale window is becoming increasingly important for investors watching the next wave of Bitcoin ecosystem projects.
