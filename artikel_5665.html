WASHINGTON — In the first days following their sweeping midterms victory, Democrats are asking themselves: How far can we go?

That question has grown particularly interesting in health care — a policy area that many Democrats argue was critical to their win and remains a top priority for early action.

There has been broad agreement among congressional Democrats that they should roll back the historic Medicaid cuts championed by President Trump, and restore the additional Affordable Care Act health insurance tax credits that, upon expiring, put coverage out of reach for many.
