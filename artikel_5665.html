2026 BCDA Merit: Marabel Duplexes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Agnieszka Jakubowicz & R. Brad Knipstein Photography

With the character and functionality of detached homes, the Marabel Duplexes in Newark, California, reimagine small-lot living. Designed by Robert Hidey Architects for Robson Homes, the alley-loaded, three-story residences pair two distinct four-bedroom plans, each designed to stand independently or work in tandem.

Every home is an end unit, allowing natural light to reach all three levels through generous window placements. Ground-floor bedroom suites create a welcoming arrival, while interior level changes respond to site grading and give each residence a custom feel.

Agnieszka Jakubowicz & R. Brad Knipstein Photography

Award: MeritCategory: Townhomes and DuplexesArchitect: Robert Hidey ArchitectsBuilder: Robson HomesSize: 2,255 square feetLocation: Newark, California

Inspired by Spain’s white villages, the architecture pairs a restrained monochromatic palette with layered textures and materials, including brick, wood siding, tile, and iron detailing.

Together, the residences create a cohesive architectural statement, while variations in elevation across the broader neighborhood bring visual interest to the mix of attached and detached homes.
