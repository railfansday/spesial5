Sonys retro MDR-3R headphones are back in stock after selling out twice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With the holiday season fast approaching, headphones worth hundreds of dollars are at their record-low prices of the season, but it's a pair of $59 Sony headphones that have sold out twice since their release in early November.

That's simply the retro appeal of the Sony MDR-3R headphones, Sony's modernized version of the classic Walkman style. They don't come with noise cancellation or a Bluetooth connection, but they do come with a striking look. And two different colored pairs of ear cushions (blue and orange) for added customization. As of Nov. 25, they're back in stock at Sony's website and Best Buy, though they remain completely sold out at Amazon. Based on the amount of compliments on the design I received while testing them, I'm not surprised these have been popular enough to sell out. They're undeniably cool, even when plugged into an iPhone. Plus, they make listening to classic '80s tracks feel just that much better. Soundscape-wise, this on-ear design isn't going to give you the best performance of what's available, but it's surprisingly clear (if a little on the flatter side). The USB-C connection makes them simple to connect to modern devices, and the in-line remote and built-in mic are both great for handling playback and calls.

At $59.99, they're certainly not the fanciest headphones, but they're some of the coolest. While I can't say whether or not they'll go on sale for Black Friday, what I can say is if you want to guarantee having a pair in hand for Christmas, grabbing them while they're in stock now isn't a bad idea.

Bethany Allard is a Los Angeles-based shopping reporter at Mashable covering beauty tech, dating, sex and relationships, and headphones. That basically means she puts her hair through a lot, scrolls through a lot of dating apps, and rotates through a lot of different headphones. In addition to testing out and rounding up the best products, she also covers deals for Mashable, paying an especially obsessive amount of attention to Apple deals and prices. That knowledge comes in handy when she's covering shopping holidays like Prime Day and Black Friday, which she's now done for five years at Mashable.
