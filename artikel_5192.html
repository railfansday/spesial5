Shibarium's official X account is a vital communication channel for updates on the Shiba Inu Layer 2 network, ecosystem developments, and community announcements. The account's inactivity has sparked questions around who is running the account, why updates have stopped coming in regularly, and whether the community can expect to see a return to consistent communication.

Until any further clarifications are provided, the Shiba Inu community is expected to continue asking questions about who is running its official Shibarium account and why it has been silent for so long.

The Layer-2 blockchain network associated with Shiba Inu, Shibarium, continues to experience relatively flat activity, indicating subdued on-chain momentum amid dull market sentiment, with the Shiba Inu price trading in the red.

At the time of writing, SHIB was down 1.15% in the last 24 hours to $0.00000445.
