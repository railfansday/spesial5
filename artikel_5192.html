Many of the large-cap crypto assets were deep in the red amid intense selling pressure in the past 24 hours. Chainlink [LINK] was one of the assets with the most bearish sentiment in the speculative markets.

At the time of writing, the Open Interest (OI) fell 11.6% in 24 hours alongside a 7.3% price slump as the The Funding Rates also retreated toward zero but were still positive.

The past month’s liquidation heatmap revealed a notable magnetic zone at $12 and another just under $13. These clusters of liquidity could attract LINK prices higher in the short term before a move lower.

In related news, Chainlink expanded access to Asian stock markets. This tokenization push has not bred new demand for the altcoin.

The weekly chart revealed a swing level at $15 that the altcoin swept in September before falling lower. Another swing point at $10.87 was on the verge of being broken.

A weekly session close below this level would suggest a move toward $7.88, and $7 could be the next multi-week price leg.

As the liquidation heatmap showed, there was a smattering of liquidity around $10.30, which had a confluence with a recent weekly low of $10.27. Hence, a bullish short-term scenario would be a price sweep of $10.27 followed by a bounce past $11.80.

For weeks now, Chainlink has seen a steady bearish trend sustained on the 4-hour timeframe. In particular, the month-long trendline resistance (orange) was a formidable opponent for buyers.

Additionally, it could be decision time for LINK. The $10.61 swing low from mid-September was under increasing pressure from the sellers, evidenced by the lower lows in recent weeks. Moreover, something has to give, and it is likely to be the buyers’ resolve.
