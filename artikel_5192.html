Upbit lists HYPE – Can Hyperliquid narrow its 45x Spot trading gap?

Hyperliquid has received another major boost, but the impact could be bigger than it initially appears.

Upbit has officially listed HYPE on their South Korean exchange. This is significant, as it means the largest crypto spot exchange in South Korea now holds HYPE. This gives traders in South Korea a massive new pool of liquidity. However, it’s Upbit’s dominance in South Korea that makes the listing even more significant.

According to FP Research, Upbit controlled a significant 68.6% of trading volume across the country’s top five major crypto exchanges in April 2026, much higher than Bithumb’s 19.5%. This gives HYPE access to South Korea’s largest crypto trading platform and a much wider pool of local investors.

Notably, though, Hyperliquid’s [HYPE] expansion into South Korea could be just the beginning.

Recently, Bitso, one of Latin America’s largest crypto exchanges, also added HYPE to its perpetual market. This is interesting since Upbit has a spot listing for HYPE. Put simply, Bitso increases HYPE’s exposure in derivatives, while Upbit provides increased exposure for HYPE to South Korean spot traders.

Together, these listings expand HYPE’s exposure into several new markets and trading segments. However, could the Upbit listing offer more value to Hyperliquid? On-chain activity shows that exposure to South Korea’s large spot market may attract some new buying interest and create increased spot liquidity to help reinvigorate the HYPE ecosystem.

Hyperliquid continues to lead the perp trading market, with competitors still struggling to close the gap.

According to DeFiLlama data, Hyperliquid saw more than $65 billion in perp volume this week alone, compared to Aster’s $11 billion. This shows how much of a commanding lead Hyperliquid has in derivatives. Meanwhile, Q4 perp volume has already reached nearly 50% of Q3’s $620 billion total, suggesting that trading activity on Hyperliquid remains strong despite broader market weakness.

Despite this, there is still plenty of growth left to come. Looking at the chart below, Hyperliquid has been averaging around $15 billion each quarter in spot trading volume this year. However, in Q3 2025 it hit $54 billion.

Looking at the gap between this and the derivatives volume in Q3 2025, which hit around $630 billion in perps, it is safe to say that Hyperliquid’s derivatives volume is almost 45x larger than Spot volume. In essence, Hyperliquid still depends heavily on derivatives activity to drive its on-chain momentum.

Against this backdrop, Upbit’s listing could put HYPE in a stronger position to close that gap. The opportunity to access South Korea’s big spot market opens up new buying opportunities, increasing spot liquidity, and helps diversify Hyperliquid’s trading activity beyond perpetual futures.

If this momentum continues, spot growth could become an important driver of the next phase of Hyperliquid’s ecosystem expansion.

Upbit’s 68.6% market share gives HYPE access to South Korea’s largest spot trading market.

With perp volume nearly 45 times larger than spot volume, Upbit’s listing could bring new buyers, boost spot activity and support HYPE’s growth.
