David Storch ‘delighted’ but urges ‘patience’ as Sheffield Wednesday appoint Dave Challinor

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sheffield Wednesday have named Dave Challinor as the permanent successor to Henrik Pedersen after the Dane’s departure in October. The 51-year-old takes charge of SWFC on a contract until 2030 with Clint Hill and Anthony Sweeney joining as assistant coaches.

The move has sparked excitement among the Hillsborough faithful, and chairman David Storch is “delighted” with the appointment too. However, the Owls chief issued a strong reminder of the need for patience as Wednesday rebuild.

In his first words on Challinor’s arrival, Storch said: “We are still at the beginning of an ambitious journey. Significant progress has been made in rebuilding the foundations of the club, but we recognise there is much more work ahead.

“Our ambitions remain clear, but our progress cannot be defined by the outcome of one moment or season alone. We must establish a clear footballing identity, consistent standards and a culture capable of delivering sustained success. That journey will require patience, resilience and collective commitment. Progress will not always be straightforward, but we believe Dave has the leadership, character and determination to help us achieve our ambitions.”

There is excitement over Challinor’s arrival at Hillsborough, and it is justified. The new Sheffield Wednesday boss led Stockport County from the National League to the top end of League One, and he was a hit in previous jobs at Hartlepool United and AFC Fylde beforehand.

After moving on Pedersen and bringing in Challinor, fans will set high expectations. The Owls sit 9th, so the hope will be that the new boss can deliver progress and lift them further into the promotion conversation.

However, Storch’s words emphasise the need for patience, and perhaps indicate that Challinor will not be judged on whether or not this season is a success. Success would be defined as a top-six finish at the very least by many, given the strength of SWFC’s squad, but it appears that the club hierarchy are not going to demand that in the immediate future.

Instead, Storch’s comments indicate that Wednesday want to build towards those heights with three goals: a footballing identity, consistent standards and a strong culture. It suggests a quick ascent is not what is being demanded or expected, which does conflict with the hopes of sections of the support after Pedersen’s sacking.

Challinor’s track record and availability meant he was viewed by many as a shoo-in for the Sheffield Wednesday job. His trajectory matches the one desired by SWFC, and he has the promotion credentials to his name to lead the club out of League One.

The Chester-born boss has done an excellent job of building cultures at his former clubs and this is a clear desire of Storch and the other decision-makers at Hillsborough. The chairman’s words indicate he will be given the time to do so with the Owls.

Storch’s vision is a long-term one, and Challinor will help him fulfil the goals set out as Wednesday rebuild after relegation and administration. The fans want results though, so time will tell if the new boss can satisfy everyone in a demanding job at a big club.
