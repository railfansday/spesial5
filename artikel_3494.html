Michael B. Jordan’s New Remake Of 58-Year-Old Thriller Officially Unveiled

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Michael B. Jordan's action thriller remake reveals a new look.

Jordan's list of movies has made him one of Hollywood's most popular actors right now, and his career recently reached a high with the release of Ryan Coogler's vampire movie Sinners. After their collaborations on all the movies Coogler directed before Sinners, including two Black Panther films that stand among the best in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Jordan and the director went on to win it big at the Academy Awards, with Jordan taking home his first Best Actor Oscar in the star's first nomination for the award. The actor's next theatrical release to follow up that vampire hit will be a thriller remake.

Amazon MGM Studios has released a new trailer for The Thomas Crown Affair.

The action heist movie trailer spotlights the cat and mouse game being played between Jordan's title character, the painting-stealing billionaire Thomas Crown, and Adria Arjona's (Man of Tomorrow) Ms. Bennett, who is investigating him as the duo fall into a fiery romance. Jordan also serves as the director of the new take on The Thomas Crown Affair, which stands as the third iteration of the classic heist story, following the original 1968 movie starring Steve McQueen and its first remake, a 1999 thriller of the same name with James Bond star Pierce Brosnan. Jordan's new The Thomas Crown Affair releases in theaters on March 5, 2027.

Jordan and Arjona will come into 2027's The Thomas Crown Affair drawing a lot of attention to the Amazon MGM Studios movie. Jordan is coming off both his Oscar win for Sinners and the animated fantasy movie Swapped, which was released earlier this year and has become Netflix's 8th most-watched movie of all time with 144.9 million views in its first 91 days. As for Arjona, the actress will go from The Thomas Crown Affair into James Gunn's Superman movie sequel, Man of Tomorrow, which flies into theaters on July 9, 2027, and where she is heavily rumored to be playing DC's new Wonder Woman.

Joining them in the film is a slew of other high-profile actors. Among them is another actor-director dual threat in the form of Kenneth Branagh, with other names including the Oscar-nominated Lily Gladstone (Killers of the Flower Moon), Jordan's Black Panther franchise co-star Danai Gurira (The Walking Dead), Pilou Asbæk (Game of Thrones), and more. The Thomas Crown Affair marks yet another action role in Jordan's career, as the actor has also led genre movies like Tom Clancy's Without Remorse, which was written by Yellowstone franchise creator Taylor Sheridan.

The new The Thomas Crown Affair also continues Jordan's career as a director, as this will be only his second film in the chair after having had his directorial debut in 2023's Creed III, the finale of the modern chapter in the Sylvester Stallone-led Rocky franchise. Creed III ended up grossing $276.1 million worldwide, and it now remains to be seen if Jordan's second turn as a director in The Thomas Crown Affair can steal a larger haul at the box office when it releases in theaters next year.
