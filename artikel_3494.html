Netflix Officially Finds Its James Bond Replacement in Henry Cavill

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There has been a lot of noise around one of the most iconic franchises and characters in history — James Bond. In 2006, when Daniel Craig was announced as the sixth Bond, it signaled a new era for the iconic character, one that would come to an end in 2021. After appearing in five Bond films, Casino Royale, Quantum of Solace, Skyfall, Spectre, and No Time to Die, that run ensured that Craig is the longest serving Bond ever. Since then, the race has been on to secure a suitable replacement to serve in His Majesty's Secret Service.

The race would ultimately end with The Batman and The Odyssey star Robert Pattinson, who has now been officially announced as the next James Bond as Amazon plans for an entirely new era for Ian Fleming’s legendary secret agent. Pattinson had to scale through some of the most rigorous and public auditioning processes in entertainment history. Pattinson is set to lead the next 007 movie with Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve set to direct. The Batman actor being 007 means that another favorite, Henry Cavill, misses out on the iconic role.

While Cavill might not be joining the legendary list of Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Craig, the former Witcher star has his own slick Secret Service agent to embody in Netflix's answer to James Bond. Cavill is being paired with Jumanji star Kevin Hart, for the upcoming spy thriller, which is allegedly titled The Serpent and The Snake. The Man of Steel actor has been filmed in several behind-the-scenes news clips filming his new thriller in Budapest, Hungary, which you can see here. The upcoming Netflix movie has sets one could very easily mistake for a 007 ensemble.

The Serpent and The Snake is set to be directed by Joseph McGinty Nichol aka McG, and offers up an espionage thriller with a considerable dose of humor. Cavill and Hart are both cast as secret agents with a license to kill, working for rival outfits. The pair begin their rivalry when they cross paths in a Lamaze class, and their spouses become fast friends. Their double lives subsequently collide in unexpectedly hilarious and dangerous ways, forcing the two men to reluctantly become confidantes and partners on the road to fatherhood. Joining Hart and Cavill as part of the cast are Justin Lupe (Succession), Aja Naomi King (Lessons in Chemistry), and Jason Isaacs. The screenplay is written by Jonathan Tropper, Wendy and Lizzie Molyneux alongside Adam and Aaron Nee.

The Netflix spy action-comedy The Serpent and The Snake does not have a release date yet. Stay tuned to Collider for updates.
