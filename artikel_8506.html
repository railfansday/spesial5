Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: Why do so many people think inflation is bad?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I've often found it curious that people rage so much against inflation. If your grocery bill goes up 10% but so do your stocks, why would that be a problem? You're not going to eat as many groceries in a year as you own in stocks so to me it seems like a net benefit so long as you keep your investment numbers much higher than your consumption of goods numbers. If all assets are going down at the same time and inflation is up, that would seem quite bad but we're not seeing that at all everything is simply worth more of those green pieces of paper from gold to houses to stocks. What am I missing?
Because many of us experienced stagflation in the 1970's, even if it was only watching what our parents went through.
What you're describing is recency bias which is a cognitive trap.
