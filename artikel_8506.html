Choose the balance transfer card whose 0% period gives your payments time to clear the balance, at the smallest fee. For most balances, the Citi Diamond Preferred wins, with 0% for 21 months on transfers made in its first four months, at a 3% fee. Move a Citi card’s balance to another bank’s card instead.

A balance transfer is worth it when its fee costs less than the interest it saves. Paying $300 a month on a $6,000 balance at 22.36%, the average rate the Federal Reserve reported on card accounts charged interest for August 2026, takes 26 months and costs about $1,577 in interest. On a card with 21 months at 0% and a 5% fee, the same $300 a month pays off the balance and the $300 fee in 21 months, so the transfer saves about $1,277. To clear the balance before the 0% ends, divide the balance plus the fee by the months at 0%, and pay that every month. A 3% fee instead of 5% would save $120 on that $6,000, but on an 18-month card the payment rises from $300 to $343.

Each card here earned its spot with the longest 0% period, fewer fees, or rewards once the balance is paid. Check who issued the card you owe on before you apply, since most issuers won’t take a transfer from their own cards. Then move the balance as soon as you’re approved, because most of these 0% periods count from account opening.
