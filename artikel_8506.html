Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Favorite holiday gift baskets?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

You might take a look at Gullah Gourmet, a small business in Charleston, SC. According to their website, they have curated gift baskets of Lowcountry specialties. I have never ordered gift baskets, but I have ordered packages of their she-crab soup mix and a few other items for myself and also as gifts for friends.

(If you order the soup mix, be aware that you need to buy your own crab meat to add to the dry mix when you reconstitute it. I have no trouble finding canned crab meat at the grocery store near the tuna. But if you were to give this as a gift, you might want to include the canned crab meat so the recipients don't need to go on a trip to the store before they make their soup. And add a splash of sherry like the restaurants do.)
