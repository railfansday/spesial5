Download Field Agent if you regularly pass big chain stores and want small cash jobs there, such as shelf photos and price checks that mostly pay $3 to $20. Skip trips made just for one job, because the drive isn’t paid and a denied submission pays nothing.

Field Agent pays you to photograph shelves and displays, check prices, and answer questions at stores such as Target, Walgreens, and CVS, or to buy a product and review it. Anyone 18 or older with a smartphone can join, and each job you reserve usually gives you two hours to finish.

Shoppers whose errands already take them to a Target, Walgreens, or Kroger get the best hourly pay from it, since a $5 audit that takes 10 minutes inside works out to $30 an hour. If you’d be driving out just for the jobs, a delivery app fits better, because the drive itself is the work it pays for.
