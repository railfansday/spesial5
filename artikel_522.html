Best Buy’s gift card bonus deal is live for Black Friday: Spend $100 on gift cards, get $10 free

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Not sure what to buy all those lucky people on your holiday gift list? Gift cards are the perfect pick, and Best Buy is sweetening the deal when you shop this early Black Friday deal.

Spend $100 or more on select gift cards at Best Buy and get a free $10 Best Buy e-gift card as part of the retailer’s early Black Friday discounts. That’s a great kickback on money you were probably going to spend anyway, and it's kind of like rewarding yourself for shopping.

The offer applies to Best Buy’s entire gift card selection, and select brands are included in the promotion. That includes everything from Amazon and Home Depot to DoorDash, Uber, Airbnb, Disney, and Google Play. So if you're looking for a good reason to find the perfect gift card for someone and call it a day while getting a little cash back, Best Buy is the place to shop.

This deal applies to a variety of third-party e-gift cards sold through Best Buy, with plenty to choose from. Included are retailers like Amazon, Home Depot, DoorDash, Uber, Airbnb, Disney, and more. Any mix of brands totaling $100 or more in a single order also qualifies, so you can split your order among a few different people on your list if you prefer.

Not instantly. Best Buy will send your $10 promotional e-gift card after your order is fulfilled or picked up if you choose physical cards. E-gift cards usually process within a few minutes to a few hours, so the bonus gift cards don't tend to take too long to get to you. Any promotional gift cards you get will be sent to the email address on your Best Buy account, so make sure that’s current before you check out. If Best Buy sends a message asking you to confirm your email, you have 60 days to respond, and the bonus is forfeited if you don’t, so watch out for that.

No. Best Buy’s own gift cards aren’t part of the promotion, so buying $100 in Best Buy cards won't get you anything extra at this time.
