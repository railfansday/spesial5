As brands dive into ChatGPT, OpenAI will let them track their mentions

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

If AI chatbots are going to remain a relevant touchpoint for marketers, then agencies and brands need access to the same analytics they use in their other campaigns. Rumor has it that OpenAI is developing new tools in that area, potentially creating new opportunities for advertisers on ChatGPT.

The rumor in question comes from Juozas Kaziukėnas, the founder of research firm Marketplace Pulse. On LinkedIn, Kaziukėnas posted a screenshot that shows a prompt to "claim your business in ChatGPT." Among other perks, those who stake their claims will be able to track how often ChatGPT mentions their business within its replies.
