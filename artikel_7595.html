Senators renew bill to increase number of federal judges

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In an effort to help overburdened federal courts, a bipartisan group of U.S. senators reintroduced a bill Friday that would add 71 new federal judgeships in phases over several presidential administrations. (Image from Shutterstock)

In an effort to help overburdened federal courts, a bipartisan group of U.S. senators reintroduced a bill Friday that would add 71 new federal judgeships in phases over several presidential administrations.

Congress last enacted a significant expansion of the federal courts in 1990, according to a story by Law360.

The federal courts currently have 677 district judgeships and 179 appellate judgeships. Under the bill, judgeships would be added in a number of states, including Arizona, California, Iowa, New Jersey and New York.

The U.S. House of Representatives and the U.S. Senate passed a version of the bill in 2024, but then-President Joe Biden vetoed it during his last few weeks in office.
