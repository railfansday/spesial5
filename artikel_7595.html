BigLaw boosts bonuses and pay for associates, creating new market rate following Cravath, Milbank hikes

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Major U.S. law firms are giving associates raises and establishing a new market rate ranging from $235,000 for first-year lawyers to $455,000 for senior associates, plus additional bonuses. (Image from Shutterstock)

Major U.S. law firms are giving associates raises and establishing a new market rate ranging from $235,000 for first-year lawyers to $455,000 for senior associates, plus additional bonuses.

Cravath, Swaine & Moore’s associate salary scale was increased Tuesday, and it rivals the raises announced in June at Milbank, according to coverage by Reuters.

Milbank increased the associate salary range from $235,000 to $455,000. In July, it offered special bonuses between $6,000 and $25,000 for associates. Reuters is also reporting pay increases at Fried, Frank, Harris, Shriver & Jacobson and Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton.

Cravath’s special bonuses—$11,000 to $40,000—based on class year, exceeded Milbank’s scale. Its year-end bonus scale starts at $15,000 and extends to $115,000, which is a continuation of what the firm and others offered in past years.
