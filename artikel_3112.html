I purchased my first OLED TV a few Black Fridays ago. It remains one of my favorite tech purchases of all time. If you've been looking to go OLED — Black Friday is the perfect opportunity because it's when you'll find the lowest prices of the year.

For example, right now you can get the 65-inch LG C6 4K OLED TV for $1,399. That's a whopping $1,300 off its regular price. The LG C6 OLED TV got a near-perfect 4.5-star review from us for its triple-threat combination of bright visuals, gorgeous colors and excellent gaming features. In other words, it's the OLED TV to buy.

From the iPhone 18 Pro to the MacBook Pro OLED, there are a lot of new Apple devices this holiday season. So you might be asking, what kind of discounts will these devices receive and whether you should bother waiting for the annual Apple Black Friday Sale?

Let's start with the Apple Black Friday Sale. It usually kicks off very close to Black Friday. Last year's sale bundled gift cards of $25 to $250 with select device purchases. If you're trading in an older device and buying direct from the Apple Store, it's a no-brainer way to get some extra credits your way.

However, if you want an actual dollar-off discount — you'll find better deals at Apple authorized retailers like Best Buy or Amazon. For example, I predict the MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, and Apple Watch 12 will all see bigger discounts at Amazon/Best Buy/B&H Photo than the gift cards Apple will offer.

Just look at the Apple Watch 12 for $329 at Amazon. That's already $70 off and better than a potential $50 Apple Store gift card.

If you ask me — every household should have at least one Bluetooth speaker. They're a great way to take your tunes on the go. One of our favorite speakers is the Bose Soundlink Flex (2nd Gen). In our review we said it provides strong, bassy sound with an adjustable EQ. For those who like to customize, there are 7 colors to choose from. It's on sale for $99 right now, which is its lowest price ever.

The Asics Gel-Kayano 30 are one of my favorite running sneakers. Yes, they're an older model, but that only makes them an even better value because they're currently selling at the unbeatable price of just $69.

By comparison, the newer Gel-Kayano 33 sell for $170. In his Asics Gel-Kayano 33 review, my colleague and fellow marathon runner Nick Harris-Fry said they're good, but kind of underwhelming. Given the price difference, the Gel-Kayano 30 are just an amazing bargain.

I've used them for casual runs on the treadmill, HIIT sessions, and long distance running outdoors. Snag this pair while stock lasts.

There are a lot of amazing TV deals happening right now, but one of my favorite deals is this discount on the TCL QM7L. This TV won a Tom's Guide Editor's Choice Award for best Mini-LED TV. It's TCL's first Super Quantum Dot (SQD) TV and it offers better contrast and color than rival Mini-LED TVs. All sizes are selling at an all-time price low right now. (With the exception of the 75-inch model, which has been $200 cheaper before).
