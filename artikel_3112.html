Google Play will show some app developers’ home addresses to the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The EU only requires the details for European buyers, but Google plans to display them everywhere.

Google Play will show traders’ contact details worldwide from March 2027.

Play Store developers must tell Google by February 1, 2027, whether they are traders under the EU’s Digital Services Act. From March 1, Google will show traders’ contact details worldwide, which for personal accounts means a physical address and phone number. Apple shows them only in the EU.

Google will start showing the contact details of many Play Store developers to users worldwide next year, Digital Trends reported. For some who publish apps as individuals, that means their home address.

Developers must tell Google by February 1, 2027, whether they count as “traders” under the EU’s Digital Services Act (DSA). Google treats developers who make money from apps as traders, for example through paid downloads, in-app purchases or regular ad revenue. Those who don’t declare a status risk having updates blocked or apps removed.

From March 1, Google will show traders’ contact details on the Play Store. For developers with a personal account, that means a physical address and a phone number. Google won’t accept P.O. boxes, and the address comes from the developer’s payments profile.

The DSA only requires these details to be shown to buyers in the EU. Google plans to show them to everyone.

Apple, which must follow the same EU rules, takes a narrower approach. It shows trader details only in the EU and lets developers list a P.O. box if they can document it.

Developers who don’t distribute apps in the European Economic Area or the UK can declare themselves non-traders. That leaves little room for solo developers who earn money in Europe to keep their home address private.

The change comes as Google opens Play to more first-time developers. Last month, it said anyone could publish apps for free using its AI Studio. In August, a US judge also told Google to stop making rival app stores hard to install.
