Even though Grand Theft Auto 6 will be taking over the world tomorrow, World of Warcraft: Forever was the first game this month that people flocked to in record-breaking numbers.

While we don't know the exact player count like we do with games on Steam, we can make an educated guess on just how many people dove into the MMO via a recent set of statistics from Blizzard. According to the official WoW X account, over 15 million characters were created in the last few weeks.

Characters aren't individual players. You can create dozens of characters on your account if you want to, but I'd guess most people didn't do that. This is a game where you can put hundreds of hours into one character before ever reaching the level cap. There definitely aren't 15 million total players based on this statistic, but the number might not be massively below that.

I can't say I'm surprised that a spin-off MMO that will remain stuck in the most universally nostalgic era of WoW was a big hit. Everyone who stopped playing for one reason or another came back to remember what it was like to smack a scorpion for 38 seconds.

Whether or not they'll stick around is the question. Blizzard plans on updating the game with new things to do for players at different stages of the long leveling journey. Most people are still weeks away from reaching the level 60 cap, partially because there are so many things to do that don't involve XP. The first raid is around the corner, but I'm curious to see what kind of stuff is added for those of us who are still chilling in the mid-30s. It'd better be good because many of us will be on vacation in Leonida for the next several weeks.
