Glasner made to wait for first win as Trafford thwarts Forest

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

James Trafford became the record signing for a British goalkeeper this summer and he is already proving invaluable for Leeds United.

Signed from Manchester City for an initial £40m in August, Trafford has made an outstanding start and frustrated Nottingham Forest twice in four days.

The England international secured his second successive clean sheet in this City Ground sequel to underline why Leeds moved to eclipse the previous record for Jordan Pickford.

“England’s No 1” was a frequent chant from the travelling Leeds fans and although it may take some time for him to dislodge Pickford as the country’s first-choice, he is loving life at his new club.

Trafford produced a number of fine saves and goals from Daniel James and James Justin secured a comfortable Carabao Cup second-round victory for Leeds, who also won here in the league on Saturday.

Dan James has put Leeds ahead against Nottingham Forest 💥 pic.twitter.com/BdiYMXFJ5h

Leeds manager Daniel Farke said: “He has the potential to be one of the best goalkeepers in the Premier League and that is why Thomas Tuchel has picked him for England.

“I like his natural confidence and aura, he knows that he’s good and he will be outstanding one day.

“He has major chances to be under the spotlight and we are very pleased with him so far.”

Forest manager Oliver Glasner will be pleased to see the back of Leeds. He was appointed by Forest for the primary reason of winning trophies after successful tenures with Crystal Palace and Eintracht Frankfurt, but this particular avenue is now closed for this season.

Four-time League Cup winners, Forest endured a difficult evening following the pre-match news that they have agreed a £45m deal with Chelsea for striker Liam Delap.

Forest have established a reputation for noisy transfer windows but are determined to focus on quality over quantity this year. With Delap to undergo a medical on Wednesday, Forest will then focus on signing a new right wing-back before the deadline.

Newcastle’s Jacob Murphy has been discussed, though a move is now regarded as unlikely. There are understood to be other targets under consideration.

Glasner has insisted he does not want Forest to panic, and it remains to be seen whether they do spring into life on the final day. He said: “I am not a magician or the messiah and we cannot ignore the reality that Leeds are ahead of us at the moment.

“For nine months last season we were fighting relegation, so the players know it is going to take a lot of work. I know exactly what we have to change and that is what we will do.

“We are responsible and made so many mistakes. Maybe we needed this result. At the moment we are far away from what we want but we will get there.”

This was a more open game than the attritional encounter from last weekend, as Trafford was forced into a fine early save to prevent Ola Aina’s cross from arcing into the top corner.

Yet both teams were frequently making mistakes and providing masterclasses in how to misplace passes, frustrating their respective managers in the technical area.

The most impressive moment of the first-half came from Leeds centre-back Joe Rodon, racing back into position to deny Chris Wood from close range. Rodon celebrated the tackle as if it were a 30-yard screamer.

It was always likely to be a mistake which proved the pivotal moment, and Arnaud Kalimuendo’s poor header cost the home team in the 50th minute.

Kalimuendo’s error presented Sean Longstaff with space to run into and his pass found James, who beat Matz Sels with a clinical finish.

“Can we play you every week?” was the inevitable chant from the travelling Leeds supporters.

Forest’s fans were becoming twitchy and Trafford produced another save to frustrate Wood from eight yards after a break down the right from Kalimuendo.

Glasner responded by making a quadruple substitution on the hour, yet Leeds remained dangerous and added a second goal 15 minutes from time when Justin bundled home after a goalmouth scramble.

Nottingham Forest have agreed a club-record deal worth £45m for Chelsea forward Liam Delap.

Delap has been deemed surplus to requirements at Stamford Bridge, and while the clubs were in talks over an initial loan deal, they have now struck a permanent agreement for the striker.

The 23-year-old​ only signed for Chelsea in a £30m deal from Ipswich Town 12 months ago​ ​and is scheduled for a medical on Wednesday​. He will become Forest’s most expensive signing with the deal eclipsing the £37.5m they paid for Omari Hutchinson last summer.

Oliver Glasner has prioritised the addition of a new forward and Delap was a long-term target for the Austrian since his time as Crystal Palace head coach.

Delap held talks with Forest last year over a potential move, and was also understood to have spoken with Glasner while he was at Palace.

Speaking to ITV after the win, Rodon said: “I thought we were a bit sharper going forward, maybe a bit naive in the first half. I thought we had a lot of good chances maybe could have scored a couple of goals but we know it’s a difficult place to come and overall very happy to keep a clean sheet and win the game.”

Of his last-ditch tackle in the first half, he added: “We love it, as defenders it’s as good as a goal. I’m delighted, [keeping] clean sheets is my job and overall I got it right in the end.”

Welcome to live coverage of the Carabao Cup second-round match between Nottingham Forest and Leeds United. This will be the sides’ second meeting at the City Ground in four days after Anton Stach’s late free-kick decided an attritional contest on the Premier League’s opening weekend. Forest’s new head coach Oliver Glasner is something of a cup specialist, though, with FA Cup, Europa League and Conference League titles to his name and he will relish the opportunity to right the wrongs of Saturday in front of the Forest faithful.

The Austrian is Forest’s fifth head coach in less than 12 months and he will look to arrest the churn by bringing to the City Ground the success he enjoyed with Crystal Palace. During pre-season, the Austrian moulded Forest into his preferred 3-4-3 shape, placing summer acquisition Ousmane Diomande alongside the existing central-defensive partnership of Murillo and Nikola Milenković. Here, Diomande gets his Forest debut and Glasner brings in talisman Chris Wood from the start.

Daniel Farke will have been delighted to see his Leeds side continue their excellent form in pre-season – which included victories over Liverpool, Red Bull Leipzig and Augsburg – into the season proper. Although Leeds arrive at the City Ground shorn of attacking options Wilfried Gnonto, Noah Okafor and Mateo Joseph through injury, Farke still shuffles the pack in the forward positions with this weekend’s visit of Brentford to Elland Road in mind.
