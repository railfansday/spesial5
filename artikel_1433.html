GdS: ‘Plays, scores and has fun’ – Pulisic the star in a Milan team rivalling the greats

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Last night, AC Milan added another five goals to their tally in Serie A, making it one of their best starts to a season ever.

With Ruben Amorim, Milan are no longer the defence-first team that Massimiliano Allegri made them. Instead, they’re fluid, attacking and dangerous and the statistics show as much. So much so that their stars are shining brighter than they may have ever done before.

As Gazzetta dello Sport writes, the Rossoneri are on fire, so much so that they’ve only outscored this season’s start twice in their history. With 28 goals in 12 games, everyone is helping things tick, but Pulisic, again, is standing out.

After a fine international break, the American ‘led the way’ again and added a goal to his tally for the season in fine fashion. He dons the superhero cape in a team that ‘plays, scores and has fun’ and with him, the team keep winning.

Every 87 minutes in the league, he’s contributed and for that reason, there’s a sense of inevitability seeing him in the teamsheet. Gazzetta writes that it’s effectively like entering every game with a 1-0 lead already.

He’s now scored in three consecutive games and it’s form that has not been bettered since the start of the 2024/25 season; where he scored four goals in a row. It’s not really a surprise, either.

In Ruben Amorim’s front-foot system, he’s one of the frontmen and that naturally means he’s going to score and create more. A ‘perfect match’ for Amorim, he’s leading in every sense and you’d have to look to the teams of Nereo Rocco and Lajos Czeizler for more goals – both teams scored 29 in their first 12 games of the Serie A season.

Ardon Jashari grabbing a goal also raised the number of scorers this season to eight, which is impressive in itself. It’s a ‘super-attacking’ team where everyone contributes, but Pulisic is again leading.
