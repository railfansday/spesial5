Barcelona set sights on Mario Hermoso

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Mario Hermoso’s impressive performance against PSG didn’t go unnoticed in Spain.

The Roma defender, who scored the opening goal and was named UEFA’s Man of the Match, is reportedly back in Barcelona’s sights.

According to Fichajes.net, the Catalan club is closely monitoring the Spaniard’s situation ahead of next season.

The 1995-born defender would be an attractive prospect for Deco, the Blaugrana’s sporting director, especially given his international experience and ability to build from the back.

The move would also be attractive given his contract expiration date, currently set for June 30, 2027, which could allow Barcelona to consider a free transfer.

The contractual situation, however, is set to change. Roma wants to continue with Hermoso, who has become a key figure in Gasperini’s defense, and significant updates on the extension negotiations have emerged in recent days.

According to Fabrizio Romano, the agreement for the renewal until June 2029 is now close to being finalized, with the signing still to be formalized.
