Spain vs England: Nations League prediction, kick-off time, TV, live stream, team news, h2h results, odds

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

England and Spain both have eyes on top spot in Nations League A3 ahead of a heavyweight showdown this weekend.

The Three Lions guaranteed themselves a top-two finish after beating Croatia on Thursday, but can still topple the reigning world and European champions.

With five matches played apiece, it all comes down to this. England currently trail Spain by three points, which means only a win will see them qualify for the quarter-finals as group winners by virtue of a superior goal difference.

Spain, who have won their last 12 matches in a row, have the luxury of knowing a point is enough to keep them top and thus secure a, potentially, easier knockout phase route.

Spain vs England is scheduled for a 7.45pm GMT kick-off on Sunday, November 15, 2026.

The match will take place at the Metropolitano Stadium, home of La Liga giants Atletico Madrid.

TV channel: In the UK, Spain vs England in the 2026/27 Nations League is being televised live and free to air on ITV, with coverage of the match beginning at 7pm on ITV1.

Live stream: Catch the game live online through the ITVX website and app, which is free with a registration.

Live blog: You can follow all the action with Standard Sport’s live blog, featuring expert insight and analysis from England correspondent Dom Smith.

England could have Ezri Konsa and Djed Spence available for the showdown in the Spanish capital after the defenders missed the win over Croatia through injury.

Thomas Tuchel said in the pre-Croatia press conference that the pair were due to resume team training on Saturday to be ready for the Spain game.

If fit, Konsa and Spence would be options to replace Trent Alexander-Arnold (thus seeing Jarell Quansah move out to right-back) and Lewis Hall respectively, to provide England with greater defensive security.

The front four could be unchanged from the Croatia game with Harry Kane - winning his 127th senior cap - leading the line ahead of Jude Bellingham, Morgan Rogers and Anthony Gordon.

For Spain, the big concern was Lamine Yamal after the Barcelona star sat out the win over the Czech Republic because of a knee issue.

The reverse fixture at Wembley Stadium in September was nothing short of a classic as England had their moments in matching the world champions.

Ultimately, Spain’s class proved decisive as they ran out 3-2 winners and, in truth, they would have won by a bigger margin had it not been for some wayward finishing.

Spain are simply in their groove and we cannot see England ending their long unbeaten run.

England did win this very fixture on their last visit to Spain in October 2018 when Raheem Sterling (x2) and Marcus Rashford had them 3-0 up before half-time.

Paco Alcacer and Sergio Ramos reduced the deficit in the second half, but the Three Lions held on for a famous win.
