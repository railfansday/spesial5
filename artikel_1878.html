‘Not good news’: Mikel Arteta issues David Raya injury update as Arsenal fear lay-off

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

David Raya will on Sunday undergo scans on a potential injury after being forced off during Arsenal’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle.,

The Spaniard has been a central figure in Arsenal’s strong start to the season, keeping seven clean sheets in the opening ten Premier League matches of the season.

However, the Gunners are now sweating over his fitness after he trudged off and went straight down the tunnel in the second half at St James’ Park.

Raya collided with William Osula as he looked to distribute the ball and went down, with the referee calling on the Arsenal medical staff.

The Spaniard looked in a huge amount of pain as his shoulder was treated and he was unable to continue.

Kepa Arrizabalaga came on to replace Raya for the final 20 minutes of the match, joining the action with Arsenal 2-1 down.

Asked for an update after the game, Arteta told reporters: “Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news.

“He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don't know.”

He added: “It was a knock, I don’t know how it happened and what the injury is at the moment. We have to wait and see tomorrow.

“We know how important David is for us. Tomorrow we will have more news.”

The Gunners had taken a half-time lead through Eberechi Eze’s strike but fell apart in the second half as Newcastle turned things around to move in front themselves.

Any spell on the sidelines for Raya would be a huge blow for Arsenal ahead of a crucial run of fixtures.

Mikel Arteta’s side host Manchester City next weekend, before also taking on the likes of Real Madrid, Tottenham and Manchester United before Christmas.
