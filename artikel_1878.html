Transfer news LIVE: Vinicius Jr to Arsenal, contracts agreed; Man Utd deal done; Chelsea, Liverpool latest

The January transfer window is approaching, with Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham all considering moves to strengthen their squads for the second half of the season. Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi and RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa are also admired at the Emirates Stadium, while Vinicius Junior is said to have emerged as a target once again.

The former Chelsea boss is keen to strengthen a defence that has struggled this season, with the Fulham defender potentially providing a solution. Pochettino will face the media ahead of United’s meeting with Liverpool as preparations continue.

Chelsea are confident of securing a deal for Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott when the window opens, despite the Cherries insisting he is not for sale. Liverpool are also monitoring young talent, with Mexican midfielder Gilberto Mora among their targets. The Reds are now leading the race for Lamine Camara, who came close to joining Chelsea on deadline day during the summer transfer window. Chelsea’s former techincal director Michael Emenalo has taken up an advisory role at Leicester City.

The Athletic reports that the Nigerian, who was with the Blues for five and a half years, is part of a team of consultants that is helping the Foxes with their future planning.

Manchester United are set to back Mauricio Pochettino the January transfer window.

Sky Sports reports that the Red Devils are prepared to sanction some signings for their new manager in the winter, with the club currently sat in the Premier League relegation zone.

Pochettino, who arrived at Carrington to begin work on Wednesday, will be tasked with guiding United away from the bottom three and as high up the top-flight table as he can, while also seeing how far he can get his new side in the Champions League.

Mauricio Pochettino has listed the three things he is hoping to bring to Manchester United in his first message to fans.

The Argentine arrived at his new club on Wednesday, touching down in Manchester immediately after overseeing his final game in charge of the United States.

In his first message to United fans, posted to the club’s social media channels, he said: “I am looking forward to meeting you guys.

“I have heard so many good things about you. I hope we bring all the happiness, working together and connect[ion], so that it is going to be a special season, and to make our players fight on the field and get the results we deserve.”

Manchester United have confirmed the departures of five members of staff after Mauricio Pochettino began work as the club’s new manager on Wednesday.

The Argentine was pictured arriving at Carrington for his first day in charge of the Red Devils, having flown immediately over after his final match with the United States.

Gary Neville has called on Mauricio Pochettino to complete an overhaul at Mancheter United, naming five players to be moved on by the end of the season.

The former Man United defender has also suggested another four or five players for the chopping block in a ‘second wave’ of exits.

Having previously commented that the Red Devils’ current defence would lead to relegation, it is no surprise that Neville focused on shipping out defenders.

But Neville fears that a complete reset of the Man Utd squad could take three seasons for Pochettino to complete.

Teddy Sheringham has delivered his reaction to JJ Gabriel announcing his Manchester United exit.

Speaking to BOYLE Sports, who offer the latest football odds, Sheringham said: "I think it's absolutely absurd.

“I don't know all the ins and outs but I wish JJ Gabriel well because he looks like a lovely little footballer but blimey, there are troubles ahead for him at this rate without a doubt if these are the demands when he’s still only 15."

Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott has been urged to reject any advances from Chelsea, and sign for a “bigger” club.

Chelsea’s pursuit of Scott was well documented in the summer, with the Blues seeing two bids rejected by their Premier League rivals, even though the England international had turned down an offer of a new contract.

As such, the 23-year-old, who is out of contract at Vitality Stadium in the summer of 2028, could be allowed to depart in the January transfer window for around £100million.

Standard Sport have previously reported that Bournemouth are open to selling their prized midfielder in the new year if Premier League survival is looking likely and Marco Rose’s side are currently seventh in the table following four straight wins.

Manchester United transfer target Yasin Ayari appeared to pour cold water on any potential move to Old Trafford - before implying that he is keeping his options open.

Ayari has been a key part of Brighton’s bright start to the Premier League campaign, hitting three goals in nine appearances.

Ayari is out of contract this summer leading to speculation regarding his future. United are said to be among the clubs keeping an eye on his situation.

They are far from the only club credited with an interest in the 23-year-old, though. Premier League leaders Arsenal, Serie A outfit Roma, and Turkish duo Fenerbahce and Galatasaray are all reported to be keeping tabs on the Swede.

But, speaking from the Swedish national team camp this week, Ayari appeared to play down reports linking him with a move away from the AmEx: “I’m at Brighton and I’m happy where I am. Things are going well for me right now. Then we’ll see what’s to come.”

JJ Gabriel has confirmed his decision to leave Manchester United in a statement, with Real Madrid expected to win the race to sign the highly-rated young winger.

The 16-year-old has played in United’s academy since 2020 and was expected to start appearing in the first team this season.

Until, that is, he made the shock decision to lodge a transfer request in September, amid reports he and his entourage were frustrated with his lack of first-team opportunities.

United worked hard to convince him to stay and offered him a new contract, but the player has not trained at the club for some time and is now expected to join Real Madrid. He will leave Old Trafford without having made a senior appearance.

After months of speculation as to where he might end up, Gabriel has now issued the clearest update yet on his situation, confirming his decision to leave United in a post to his Instagram page.

Speaking over clips of himself playing for the club, he said: “Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams.

“Here, I grew as a player and a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award, you gave me moments I’ll always treasure.

“To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

“As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ.”
