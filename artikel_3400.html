Prime Video Is Saying Goodbye to Six Decades of James Bond Movies

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

From Sean Connery to Daniel Craig, some of cinema’s most iconic spy adventures are about to disappear

James Bond fans looking to revisit the legendary spy's cinematic adventures might want to clear their schedules before the end of November. Prime Video is preparing to remove a huge chunk of the 007 franchise from its streaming library, with nearly every James Bond movie currently available on the platform scheduled to leave at the same time.

A total of 26 Bond films are listed among Prime Video's upcoming departures, spanning more than six decades of the franchise. That includes everything from Sean Connery's debut as 007 in Dr. No to Daniel Craig's final outing in No Time to Die.

That leaves a pretty substantial gap in Prime Video's Bond collection, particularly for anyone hoping to revisit the franchise in chronological order. The list also includes Never Say Never Again, the unofficial Bond film starring Connery that was released outside the main Eon Productions series.

The departures come at an interesting time for the franchise, too, as Amazon MGM Studios prepares its next James Bond movie. Robert Pattinson has recently been announced as the seventh actor to take on the iconic role, with Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve attached to direct.

While details about the upcoming movie remain limited, Amazon is reportedly targeting a 2028 release. With a new era of Bond on the horizon, now might be a good time to revisit the franchise's previous adventures. Just don't wait until December to do it on Prime Video.
