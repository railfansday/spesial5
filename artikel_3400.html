Kim Kardashian and Jennifer Lawrence Wear Matching Pajamas During NYC Outing

Check out the video ... Jennifer jokingly declares, "We are together!!!" as the two make their way out of the luxury hotel, sharing laughs and looking like they're having the ultimate girls' night.

And while Kim is known for her headvturning fashion moments, it looks like Jennifer has convinced her to embrace a more comfortable dress code.
