Netflix Officially Confirms Its Next Major Crime Thriller Is Coming in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

2026 has been a great year for Netflix's crime thrillers. Some of the platform's most popular shows and movies have been in this genre, cementing Netflix as a great destination for fans seeking high-octane stories. From I Will Find You to Nemesis and The Rip, there have been many stories to enjoy. These releases have dominated global watch charts, sparking widespread fan theories and watercooler debates. I Will Find You remains Netflix's most popular show of 2026, underscoring Harlan Coben's knack for crafting twisty, engaging crime thrillers.

Ultimately, 2026 hasn't just delivered quantity for thriller enthusiasts — it has raised the bar for streaming suspense, ensuring audiences remain on the edge of their seats all year long. But with the year drawing to a close, it is time to look ahead to what the streamer has on the horizon, and 2027 starts in style with another crime thriller, The Twin, that features powerful performers in the lead roles.

Jamie Dornan (Fifty Shades of Grey) plays dual roles as Adam and Lee in the six-part British crime noir series adapted from the acclaimed Norwegian series, Twin. Mackenzie Davis (Station Eleven) also stars as Nicola. The new adaptation trades Norway's rugged archipelago of the Lofoten Islands for the moody, isolated backdrop of the Scottish Highlands. Below, the show's official logline teases the series' events.

"Suffocating in a loveless marriage to Adam, Nicola's life takes a dramatic turn when Adam's long-estranged identical twin brother Lee comes crashing back into her life, and their tangled romantic past threatens to destroy the present. A split-second decision and a terrible accident drive Nicola to protect her children, and over the course of one week, Lee and Nicola are forced together, struggling to maintain a web of secrets and lies. Though they can't ignore their feelings for each other, they both know they're living on borrowed time."

Britain makes more detective television than any country has any business making, and somehow almost all of it is good. Here is the one you belong in, what it says about you, and the case for it being the best of the four.

You want the tea, the gossip and the vicar behaving oddly. Midsomer is a county where the scenery is beautiful, the fete is on Saturday and the murder rate is roughly that of a small war, and absolutely nobody involved thinks this is strange.

You feel other people's grief as if it were handed to you personally. Broadchurch is less a whodunnit than a study of what a small town does to itself when it finds out that the answer was always going to be somebody everybody already knew.

You go through things rather than around them. John Luther is the best detective in London and a liability to everybody who works with him, and the show is completely honest that those two facts are the same fact.

You say the thing and you do not dress it up. Vera Stanhope turns up in a hat and a mac, is rude to almost everybody, and is the only person in the room prepared to ask the question that the family has been waiting years for somebody to ask.

The Twin is written by Sarah Dollard (Neighbors), Hanna Jameson, Scout Cripps, Kam Odedra (Lupin), and Joy Wilkinson (Lockwood & Co.), and is directed by Jeremy Lovering. The series — previously titled The Undertow — is based on the Norwegian series Twin, created by Kristoffer Metcalfe. The original series has a 7.1/10 score on IMDb, indicating mixed reviews from the 2000 users who rated it. Other cast members include Iain De Caestecker (The Winter King) as Marty and Gary Lewis (Billy Elliot) as Keith.

The Twin hits Netflix on Friday, January 1, 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
