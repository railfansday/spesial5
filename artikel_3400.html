Dungeons & Dragons Is Getting A Stunning New Release On November 24

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Dungeons & Dragons has introduced generations of players to sprawling fantasy worlds, unforgettable adventures, and some truly terrifying villains. While the tabletop role-playing game offers countless opportunities to create original characters and stories, its established multiverse also contains an impressive collection of legendary antagonists. Fans will soon have a new way to explore these darker corners through an upcoming illustrated release.

Titled Dungeons & Dragons Fiends & Foes: Beasts, Villains, and Monstrosities from Across the Planes, the upcoming book arrives on November 24, 2026, through Ten Speed Press. Written by award-winning fantasy author Erin M. Evans, this officially licensed release takes an unconventional approach to exploring D&D lore, combining detailed character lore, original illustrations, and a story-driven journey through the franchise's most notorious figures.

Dungeons & Dragons Fiends & Foes: Beasts, Villains, and Monstrosities from Across the Planes is available to pre-order now through this link

Rather than presenting its subjects as a straightforward encyclopedia of monsters, Fiends & Foes frames its exploration around a mysterious delivery mission. The adventure begins with a request from Glasya, the Archduchess of Malbolge and ruler of the Sixth layer of the Nine Hells. She wants a letter delivered to an extraordinarily powerful individual who has deliberately made themselves difficult to find.

That assignment provides the narrative framework for a journey through the D&D multiverse, with Glasya guiding readers from one notorious figure to another. The identity of the letter's recipient remains an important mystery, giving the book an overarching story instead of simply moving between disconnected character profiles. The premise also allows its featured villains to become part of a larger adventure.

Along the way, readers encounter some of the most recognizable names in Dungeons & Dragons history, including Tiamat, Vecna, Strahd, Halaster Blackcloak, Lolth, Xanathar, and Demogorgon. The book explores more than just their reputations, offering information about different categories of supernatural threats alongside closer examinations of particularly significant characters. Antiheroes and morally complicated figures also receive attention.

One of the biggest attractions of Dungeons & Dragons Fiends & Foes is its emphasis on visual storytelling. The book features original, previously unpublished chapter-opening illustrations alongside artwork taken from official D&D adventures. This combination gives readers opportunities to appreciate the franchise's imaginative creature designs while revisiting the visual identity of its established fantasy settings.

The illustrated presentation also makes the release appealing beyond players searching for information to use during their campaigns. Its exploration of legendary enemies, frightening creatures, and complicated personalities offers a more immersive way to appreciate the mythology behind the tabletop game. For longtime fans, the combination of familiar characters and new artwork provides another reason to revisit the multiverse.

Astarion, Karlach, and other fan-favorite heroes are officially returning in 2027 with new adventures that expand the world of Baldur’s Gate 3.

Evans is particularly well suited to this project. She wrote the award-winning Brimstone Angels Saga, which takes place in the Forgotten Realms, and is also the author of the Books of the Usurper series, beginning with Empire of Exiles. Outside publishing, she co-hosts the Writing About Dragons and Shit podcast and participates in the actual-play series Dungeon Scrawlers. Her experience makes this a noteworthy addition to D&D's expanding collection of illustrated releases.

With its November 24, 2026, publication date, Fiends & Foes is also arriving at a convenient time for holiday shopping. Between its in-world narrative, extensive villain spotlights, and original illustrations, the book offers something for both dedicated tabletop players and readers interested in fantasy lore. Fans looking to explore the darker side of Dungeons & Dragons have a very intriguing and exciting release to anticipate.
