GTA 6: How To Unlock Selling Cars

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There are multiple ways to earn money in GTA 6, and not all of them are as efficient. While robbing smaller stores can be less risky than robbing a major bank, the yield is also low, and you still need to clean/fence that merchandise afterward.

In that sense, stealing and selling cars can be even easier to do, but you need to go about it the right way. Plus, you can't just sell cars at the start of the game. You'll need to work up Jason and Lucia's contacts, and eventually find someone who will take stolen vehicles for cash later in the game.

After entering Chapter 2, you'll take full control of Lucia, as you're whisked away to a hotel in the northwest corner of the map. This zone is fairly relaxed, with minimal organized gang activity and lots of woods to lose the cops in.

After completing the first few story missions with Lucia, you'll meet a mutual friend named Moses, who operates out of a garage and junkyard. Moses will walk you through the tutorial for stealing cars and returning them.

You won't be able to steal a car right outside of Moses' shop and turn it in for cash.

Moses will also provide you with the Slim Jim item, which makes stealing cars a lot easier. To use the Slim Jim, you'll press Triangle (Y on Xbox) instead of Square (X on Xbox) to smash the window. From there, you'll need to do another analog-stick-shifting minigame to fully pop the lock, then enter the vehicle without incident.

You can use the Slim Jim for the rest of GTA 6 with both characters. There's no need to equip it or anything; it'll automatically be part of Jason and Lucia's loadout. Keep it in mind if you're trying to steal a car, as it makes less noise and many car alarms don't even go off.

To get paid for stealing cars, drive a car back to Moses, bring it into his shop (the garage door will open), then speak to Moses directly. Make sure you use the L2 method (LT on Xbox) to focus on Moses, and bring up more dialogue options. This will let you sell the car to him directly, so you can complete the transaction.

Note that more expensive cars provide bigger yields, and you can search cars for their worth using the Waink app (the shortcut is holding up on the d-pad). Try to go for bigger sales and avoid picking up junky cars, as it'll only take more time to make a ton of cash.

Also, keep in mind that the Slim Jim is pretty much your main tool for stealing cars until later in the game, when you unlock the key cloner. Once you have that, you can steal higher-end cars with advanced security systems for even bigger profits.

As you progress through the game, Moses isn't the only person who will buy cars off of you. More vendors await in later portions of GTA 6, so you won't need to drive all the way to Moses to make a delivery.
