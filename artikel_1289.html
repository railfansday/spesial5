Chelsea and Manchester United have both been advised to pursue a transfer move for Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey.

Speaking to Simon Phillips, ex-Blue Pat Nevin spoke about what a big fan he is of Bassey, who has proven a rock-solid performer at the back for Fulham in recent times.

Bassey looks like he could be ready to make the step up to a bigger club in the near future, and Nevin feels he’d clearly walk into the Man Utd team, whilst also being a “magnificent” addition to this Chelsea side.

“Calvin Bassey? He’s brilliant. Of course he can walk into these big teams. I think he could walk into Man United, that’s not saying a lot, let’s be fair, but I think he can play at the top level anywhere,” Nevin said.

“Had Arsenal come in for him during the summer, I wouldn’t have been surprised at all. Because remember, Calvin can move about a bit as well, he can play different positions.

“Obviously watching him before at Rangers, he was just a league above. So he’s a top-quality player. As a Chelsea man, if he came to Chelsea, I think it’d be an absolutely brilliant decision. I think he’d be magnificent.”

Bassey could surely be a good option for the likes of Chelsea and United to look at, but we’re also aware of both of these clubs taking a close look at Como’s Jacobo Ramon.

AC Milan’s Koni De Winter is another who’s popped up on these clubs’ radars, so it’s anyone’s guess who’ll end up being a priority, or who will prove to actually be the most realistically available option.

It’s clear Nevin is a huge admirer of Bassey, though, so that could make sense as one to watch, with Fulham perhaps likely to come under pressure to sell if any tempting offers come in.
