Fabrizio Romano: Joshua Zirkzee to Hold Talks With Pochettino Over Man Utd Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Manchester United are already planning for the January transfer window under Mauricio Pochettino before he has even officially taken charge.

The Red Devils have appointed the Argentine to replace Michael Carrick as their manager on a two-and-a-half-year deal until 2029, having won just one of their opening 10 games in the Premier League, which has left them 18th in the table.

Pochettino is scheduled to take over on November 18th officially, with his first game in charge coming just days later against arch-rivals Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday afternoon. But the decisions over who will be in his squad in the long-term have already begun.

Fabrizio Romano shared an update last week that stated the 54-year-old has already hold talks with the club's hierarchy over plans for the winter window, with the board expected to provide funds for him to strengthen his squad.

A left-back and a striker are believed to be the top priorities, and now speaking in his latest video on YouTube, Romano revealed that there are also conversations planned between the new gaffer and current players in the squad to discuss their future, including Joshua Zirkzee.

"Man United, in any case, want to try to get another striker in the January transfer window," he said.

"Could be an opportunity on loan, or could be a striker who can also be for the long term, depending on the opportunities, depending on the finances. But obviously, to understand the future of Joshua Zirkzee is also very important to get to the point where Man United can say, "Okay, this is the striker we want."

"Zirkzee was wanted by Juventus. Zirkzee was wanted by Fiorentina over the summer transfer window. I will not be surprised to see Italian clubs returning and jumping again on Joshua Zirkzee in the January transfer window, because there's always been interest in Zirkzee from Italy after he did fantastic at Bologna and then he joined Manchester United.

"For Zirkzee, there was also an opportunity at Everton in the final minutes of the summer transfer window, but it didn't happen because of one reason: the deal collapsing, also with Monaco and that Balogun story at the end of the summer window. So what's the situation now?

"Joshua Zirkzee is going to have a conversation with Mauricio Pochettino. Joshua Zirkzee is doing his best with Man United, and he's described as one of the best professionals at Manchester United. Always training at the best level, always doing his best for the team. But at the same time, Zirkzee wants to play.

"Zirkzee has wanted to go and play for some time now, not only in the recent weeks or months; Zirkzee wants to go because he wants to play. He loves Man United, but he feels he's not a regular starter, and he wants to play football. He wants to play regular football.

"So let's see what kind of opportunities will arrive on the table. But I will not be surprised to see Joshua Zirkzee on the move in January, unless Mauricio Pochettino can sort the situation with the boy and clarify the situation."

While it's clear that Zirkzee wasn't in the plans of Michael Carrick at Old Trafford, it remains to be seen if Pochettino has the same feeling with him.

The Netherlands international had a strong pre-season before being sidelined by Carrick, but he has scored twice in his last four appearances for the club, all coming off the substitutes bench, against Como and Aston Villa.

His technical qualities are unmatched in the squad, and if Pochettino is capable of getting him to link with the rest of the forwards in the squad, then he could be a really useful member of the squad. But if he doesn't want to be at the club, then there is no point keeping him around.

With that said, Man Utd's squad is far too thin right now, and they cannot afford to let him leave in January without replacing him first. It's imperative that any forward signing is brought in before a move for Zirkzee is sanctioned to ensure they're not left short, and the club will now have the chance to make that happen for the new manager as he looks to revolutionise Man Utd.
