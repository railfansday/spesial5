Spalletti: Only one way to stop speculation over Conte’s Juventus return

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Juventus coach Luciano Spalletti insists the only way to avoid growing speculation about Antonio Conte’s potential return is to ‘win matches’ as the Bianconeri prepare to host Venezia in Serie A.

It kicks off at 12:30 CET [11:30 GMT] at the Allianz Stadium in Turin.

Juventus have won only two of their last five league matches, earning a 1-1 draw against Fiorentina before the international break.

Venezia are in the relegation zone, but have won two of their last five, both under new coach Davide Nicola.

Edon Zhegrova starts for the first time in Serie A, joining Kerim Alajbegovic and Randal Kolo Muani in the Juventus attack, while Nick Woltemade begins on the bench.

“Given our name and the players we have, there are options. We need to keep our balance and be wary of counterattacks, because that’s what they rely on,” Spalletti told DAZN minutes before kick-off.

“[Davide] Nicola has got them well organised and always finds new solutions, so we’ll need to be patient, maintain our balance, and be creative in our decisions.”

Former Juventus coach Conte has openly admitted that he’d like to return to Juventus one day. Spalletti already commented on Conte’s claims on Saturday, and today he was asked whether he wanted to add something more.

“I don’t want to add anything; I just want to focus on winning today,” he replied.

“When you manage Juventus, if you don’t want all the talk swirling around you, you just have to win matches. All of this is quite normal when you reach a level where you’re on TV every day.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
