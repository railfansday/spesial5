Report French Football chief Diallo ready to run against Infantino for FIFA President

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Multiple sources report that the UEFA Executive Committee have unanimously backed French Football Federation President Philippe Diallo to run against Gianni Infantino for FIFA President.

While many national associations have urged Infantino to step down, and Italy have also withdrawn their support for him, they have so far struggled to find anyone willing to run against him in the next election who could gain significant backing.

That would seem to be changing, because multiple sources including Times Sport, RMC Sport and more all confirm that UEFA will formally give Diallo their support this week.

It will be ratified in a meeting of the FFF Executive Committee on Tuesday.

Diallo has held many roles in the game, including the organisation representing French professional clubs, serving within FIFA’s dispute resolution structure, and becoming a member of the UEFA Executive Committee in 2023.

Infantino has so far been able to weather numerous controversies and storms, above all the attempt to privatise some of the World Cup tournaments, and that is largely thanks to the support he has from those nations outside Europe.

It remains to be seen if Diallo will encourage enough nations to switch their vote away from Infantino.
