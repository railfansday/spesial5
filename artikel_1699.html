Incredible, Liverpool on verge of sacrificing their best player - opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liverpool are heading towards a world where they sacrifice their best player. It's time to change course.

Liverpool could change their squad significantly again in 2027 thanks to a series of contract situations. Virgil van Dijk, Joe Gomez, Kostas Tsimikas, and Alisson Becker are all set to leave as things stand.

It's genuinely possible that all depart, too. Gomez and Tsimikas are highly unlikely to get new deals at their ages, given they're not first-team regulars either.

And both Van Dijk and Alisson are expensive renewals. Liverpool may prefer to move on - though, we really do hope that both are given extensions and given them quickly.

But even that's not it. The Reds have more difficult situations to contend with and they're currently hurtling towards a scenario where they lose their best player.

As things stand, Liverpool are cutting Alexis Mac Allister loose. The Reds were actually open to a sale back in the summer, only one didn't materialise.

But they made their choice. Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai received massive new contracts earlier this year but Mac Allister did not. The Argentine then made it public that he wanted a new contract - the club just said he wasn't getting one.

The plan was absolutely to move on from Mac Allister and build around the other two. The problem is that the opposite has happened under Andoni Iraola as he's come to rely on Liverpool's no.10 and drop the pair on the massive contracts.

Mac Allister's start to the season has been fantastic and we'd argue he's been the side's best player so far. But he's genuinely in line to be the sacrifice for any new midfielders, as was the plan in the summer.

That must change. Liverpool have got to change course here and keep Mac Allister, building the midfield around him rather than the pair given the new contracts.

And there's an easy way to show it, too! Give the Argentine the new contract he deserves and right the wrongs of the summer.
