Liverpool’s attack beginning to click after win over Club Brugge, says Isak

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Alexander Isak believes Liverpool's "dangerous" attack is beginning to click after following up their win over Manchester United by beating Club Brugge in the Champions League.

Liverpool boosted their hopes of a top-eight finish with a 3-0 win at Jan Breydelstadion on Wednesday, with Isak netting either side of Florian Wirtz's superb 79th-minute finish.

Isak opened the scoring in the 13th minute, finishing after being teed up by Dominik Szoboszlai following Yann Sommer's sloppy pass that gifted Liverpool possession.

The Reds navigated a wave of Brugge pressure and put the game out of sight thanks to two late goals, with Wirtz scoring the first with a brilliant shot from just outside the penalty area.

Alisson then pumped a long ball forward for Isak to chase, with the Swede getting the better of Brandon Mechele before dinking the ball over Sommer and into the net.

Liverpool have now won consecutive matches by 3+ goals (4-0 v Man Utd and 3-0 v Brugge) for the first time since January (6-0 v Qarabag and 4-1 v Newcastle United).

Among all Premier League players, Isak has scored the joint-most away goals across all competitions this season (eight – level with Erling Haaland).

Bradley Barcola failed to register a goal involvement but looked dangerous, while Wirtz has now scored in three consecutive appearances for the first time as a Liverpool player, and for the first time overall at club level since September 2024 for Bayer Leverkusen (three).

"Perfect away win, I would say. The performance until we got the second one was never obvious which way it would go," Isak told TNT Sports.

"They put on a good game. We spoke about having to get the second goal. They were playing man-on-man, so I was against the defender the whole game.

"It's always difficult to say exactly where you are and how good you are, but I can say I'm in a good place and the team is in a good place, most importantly.

"Hopefully, it gets better from here. The attack in general is beginning to click. We have a lot of different combinations, and whoever comes in, we have a dangerous attack."

6 – Alexander Isak has scored in six consecutive away appearances for Liverpool. In the Premier League era, the only other player to do so in 6+ consecutive away outings for the Reds is Mo Salah (7 – August-October 2021). Delivering. https://t.co/lXKfG9aLZH

Liverpool's win saw them move onto 12 points and fifth in the 36-team standings, with games against Porto, Inter and Lens remaining in the league phase of the competition.

But, more importantly, Liverpool were able to register their first clean sheet in the Champions League in 2026-27, with Alisson making five saves to keep Brugge at bay.

In fact, Brugge were held goalless in the Champions League for the first time in 2026. In their first eight matches in the competition this year, they netted 22 goals at an average of 2.75 per game, but they could not find a way past Liverpool.

In his 18th game in charge, Liverpool have kept back-to-back clean sheets under Iraola for the first time, with the Spaniard thrilled by his team's defensive display.

"It was a very important game for us. It was a key moment to put us in a good position," Iraola said.

"We are guaranteed to be qualified for the knockouts, but now we want top eight. It was a more difficult game than it looks now after the result.

"It was difficult to expect; it would be a very open game, they would man-to-man mark. Big merit in keeping the clean sheet. Overall, it is a very good performance."
