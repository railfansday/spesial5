Prediction: Sunderland vs Tottenham

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Looking to extend on their recent Premier League flurry, Tottenham will travel to Tyneside on Sunday afternoon when they meet Sunderland.

With another stacked schedule from England’s elite this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching Premier League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Sunday’s showdown from the Stadium of Light, you can find all of the key Sunderland vs Tottenham H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Sunderland might have shown some signs of a domestic resurge on either side of November’s international break, Regis Le Bris’ camp are desperately searching for some essential stability. Left frustrated earlier in the week as they were eventually forced to settle for a 1-1 Europa League stalemate at home against Polish outfit Jagiellonia, the Black Cats have drastically failed to replicate the record-breaking form they showed last season. Although Sunderland might have entered November’s fortnight interval with a shock 1-0 victory at home against Chelsea, they still remain stuck in the Premier League drop zone and they have mustered just a pair of wins from any of their first 11 top-flight battles. Struggling to find any real clinical edge in the final third, Le Bris’ squad have notched more than a single strike in just one of their last nine appearances across all competitions.

Limping off in the first-half against Jagiellonia earlier in the week, Sunderland will be sweating over the fitness of former Getafe defender Omar Alderete here. Likewise, suffering from a recent calf injury and missing out on Thursday night, Le Bris’ side are once again expected to be without midfield talisman Granit Xhaka.

Despite putting pen to paper on a new deal at the Stadium of Light this month, Dutch international Brian Brobbey will miss out with a thigh injury. Settling for a spot on the bench in midweek, veteran defender Thomas Meunier should be handed an immediate full recall.

As for the visitors, while Tottenham might have opened up the 2026/27 campaign in the midst of another public Premier League crisis, Spurs have come roaring back in an impressive fashion on either side of November’s international break. With the mood around north London arguably at the highest it has been in recent memory, Roberto De Zerbi’s men have come skyrocketing up the table and have found a route into the top-half of the Premier League standings. Last seen marking their return from November’s fortnight interval with a deserved 2-0 victory at home against Ipswich, Tottenham have now won four of their previous five top-flight appearances and they will be looking to make another domestic statement here. Dreaming of the chance to mount a serious Champions League charge in 2027, De Zerbi’s squad have also shipped just a single Premier League strike in over 400 minutes of action.

Both still recovering from respective long-term knee injuries, Tottenham will once again be without the duo of Wilson Odobert and Dutch international Xavi Simons. Likewise, Spurs have confirmed that ex-Bayern Munich star Mathys Tel faces a lengthy stint on the sidelines after sustaining an Achilles tendon injury against Leeds prior to November’s international break.

Bagging a maiden Premier League strike and producing a stellar display on his first top-flight start last week, 18-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett should keep his spot from the start. Really starting to find his feet in north London, big-money summer recruit Mateus Fernandes will start in the middle of the park.

Enjoying what has become a real flurry on either side of November’s International break, Tottenham will now have their sights fixed on finding a route into the European spots. While Sunderland might have entered the fortnight interval with a shock 1-0 win at home against Chelsea, Sunday’s hosts still remain in the drop zone and they could face a major battle in the race to keep hold of their top-flight status in 2027. However, landing another 1-0 victory when they last welcomed Spurs to Tyneside for a Premier League showdown back in April, we could be set for a cagey affair from the Stadium of Light.
