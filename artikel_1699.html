Liverpool handed major boost as new three-man injury update revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Victor Munoz has avoided serious injury in a boost for Liverpool, who will likely remain without both Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch for Sunday’s Merseyside derby against Everton.

The Reds were handed a fresh fitness scare on Wednesday night as summer signing Munoz limped off shortly before half-time in their 3-0 away win over Club Brugge in the Champions League.

The Spanish winger was hurt after a strong challenge that came at the end of a brilliant run and received treatment on the pitch before going off and being replaced by Rio Ngumoha.

Any fears over a long-term lay-off have now been allayed, however, with Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola revealing that Munoz could return to training as soon as Saturday.

“With Victor, I think the good news is that after assessing him, there is no disruption in the knee, everything is fine,” Iraola said at his pre-Everton press conference on Friday. “But he has a big knock. It’s a big knock.

“He hasn’t trained today but he wants to try tomorrow. We’ll see how the bruise is. But I cannot tell you right now.”

Liverpool will definitely still be missing Gakpo against Everton, with the Netherlands forward set to sit out the third match in a row with a calf injury sustained on international duty.

“Cody definitely not [available against Everton],” Iraola said. “It’s a muscle injury so we have to wait a little bit until the process goes on and it heals.

“This kind of injury is normally two or three weeks. He did it in the last [Netherlands] game against Germany, so normally it’s this timeline.

“But normally I don’t like to say timelines with injuries because every injury is different and some of them go quicker than others.

“The important thing is when he comes back he comes with no pain so he can play as free as possible.”
