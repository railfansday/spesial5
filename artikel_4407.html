The Volkswagen ID. Buzz Beach is an electric van built for camping, but it’s not cheap

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Volkswagen introduced the ID. Buzz Beach, a fun spin on its electric van that’s built for camping and overnight stays. However, it won’t be cheap, starting at over $70,000.

Volkswagen’s original microbus became a cultural icon thanks to its open, customizable interior, making it perfect for large families, road trips, camping, and, of course, music festivals.

The ID. Buzz brings the classic Type 2 microbus into the electric era with a retro-new style, modern tech, and a flexible, spacious cabin.

Building on its reputation as a symbol of freedom and adventure, Volkswagen is adding a new member to the electric people mover lineup.

The new ID. Buzz Beach “brings adventure into the electric age,” Volkswagen said after introducing the new variant on Thursday. Designed for camping, longer road trips, and overnight stays, the electric van will be available in 5-, 6-, and 7-seat configurations.

Volkswagen said it sleeps two comfortably with a removable bed frame and a custom-fitting folding mattress.

The mattress is a decent size at about 6.5 feet long by 4 feet wide (2.0m x 1.2m), roughly the size of an average small double bed.

In the 5-seat configuration, the bed frame tucks away in the trunk, while in the 6- and 7-seat layouts, it’s stored vertically behind the third row of seats.

For camping and other longer stays, Volkswagen added a new Overnight mode that uses the vehicle’s battery to provide up to 48 hours of continuous, off-grid climate control. The new mode can be accessed on the center touchscreen.

It also includes blackout curtains throughout the cabin and ventilation grilles on the front side windows.

The ID. Buzz Beach offers vehicle-to-load (V2L) capabilities. A 230V power socket adaptor comes standard, letting you power an e-bike, charge a smartphone, run camping equipment, or power other external electronic devices. A camping connector is available as an added option.

To top it off, Volkswagen added a hidden foldable table and chairs set that can be tucked away without taking up internal space.

Volkswagen will open orders for the new ID. Buzz Beach in the UK later this month, starting at £53,955 (excluding VAT), or about $71,000. Including VAT and OTR costs, it will start at £65,837, or roughly $86,500.

Nice product at the wrong price. If VW can't lower the price because of the cost, then it has a lot more work to do to compete in this market.

What about those in the US? VW introduced the 2027 ID. Buzz with a new Tourer model that was essentially the same as the Beach variant in the UK, with a fold-out mattress, Overnight mode, blackout curtains, and a table and chairs set.

However, VW told its US dealers in September that it would be delaying the ID. Buzz’s return until the 2028 model year.

The 2025 Volkswagen ID. Buzz started at about $60,000 in the US, which is one of the biggest reasons it failed to catch on with buyers. The original VW microbus was an affordable people mover.
