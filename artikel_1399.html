Crucial comeback in Greece: Instant reaction to Olympiacos 2-4 Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Stefano recorded his reaction to AC Milan’s crucial comeback win against Olympiacos, highlighting the importance of the three points.

After clinching just three points in the first four games of their Europa League campaign, Milan knew that a win was needed against Olympiacos. While it looked for a moment that another defeat was on the cards, they eventually managed to turn things around.

Omari Hutchinson played a key role in the comeback as he not only scored the goal that brought the game back to life, but also assisted the two following goals. In other words, the Rossoneri have him to thank for avoiding negative headlines tomorrow, even if everything wasn’t perfect.

In his post-match reaction, Stefano spoke about the importance of this game and what Milan can take away from the impressive comeback in the second half. Hutchinson, as said, was an exclamation mark and perhaps that can be the start of something good for him.
