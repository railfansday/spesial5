A Legacy Built on Trust and Community

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Long Companies, a member of Leading Real Estate Companies of the World®

Favorite part of your job: Helping agents find opportunities and ways to navigate through them

Motto or work philosophy that you live by: Be curious. We apply our own filters and biases when we think we know the answers, so when faced with a challenge, I get really curious about why things are happening, what people need, what has changed and how we can align around that.

What is the biggest challenge currently facing your brokerage?

Rather than looking at things as challenges, I view them as opportunities—and while I believe there’s an opportunity in everything that happens, it’s about navigating that. Right now we have a market that’s starting to soften a little bit and regulate, so the question is: what do people need, and how do we deliver that?

From an industry standpoint, it feels as though a lot of brokerages and brokerage models appear to be chasing one another, but I’m more focused on quality experiences. And that begins with quality agents and staff.

I also believe the human part of this is really important in real estate. We’ve seen time and again that people want to work with a human, so doubling down on that experience is critical as we deal with a changing market.

Long Realty has reached an incredible 100-year milestone. What has contributed to the company’s success and longevity?

Having survived World Wars, recessions, depressions and pandemics, the company is adaptable and resilient. Communication is key when it comes to educating our agents so they can take that information to the consumer. We don’t make decisions blindly, and people can get behind what we’re doing, as we’ve built up a level of trust over the years.

The other thing I would point to is that we’re community focused. In addition to our own foundation that gives millions of dollars to local nonprofits, we’re also engaged in community activities, causes and sponsorships.

We’ve spent a long time building out our brand. More than just a logo, it’s the way people feel about us. A big part of my job is being a steward of the brand, ensuring that we’re doing things that align with how we want to be known.

What leadership practices have been most important in building and sustaining Long Realty’s success?

For me, it’s about empowering the people I work with. Not only do you need to work together to establish your vision, but you also need to have buy-in and ensure that people are passionate about it. And that begins with having great people who fit the culture. I find that most people want to do a great job and feel fulfilled, so how do we help our staff and our agents do amazing things? It can’t be about one person. It’s a collective, and we’re here to help the collective be better.

How do the partnerships you enjoy as a LeadingRE network member impact your business?

While we have the HomeServices network and our sister companies that we work with, layering on an organization like LeadingRE expands our capabilities and footprint. Through LeadingRE, we’ve built a lot of relationships with brokers around the country and the world, which has allowed us to service more people. We’re also members of Luxury Portfolio, which is important given the fact that Long Realty has nearly 40% market share on the million-dollar-plus side in our community. The members match our culture, and we feel comfortable referring and working with these folks.
