Defense software startup Nominal tripled its valuation to $3 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Lightspeed and Insight led the $240 million round, and the company said its revenue more than tripled this year

Nominal, a startup that makes software for testing and monitoring hardware, raised $240 million at a $3 billion valuation, according to Bloomberg.

The valuation is three times Nominal's value at its previous raise earlier this year, according to Bloomberg. Lightspeed Venture Partners and Insight Partners headed the investment, with a public announcement planned for Monday. Lux Capital, Sequoia Capital and Lockheed Martin $LMT Ventures also took part.

Nominal's platform lets makers of hardware run tests on their designs and track how development is advancing. The software is designed for use across the automotive, energy, space and defense sectors, according to Bloomberg.

Nominal's revenue has grown by more than threefold since the start of the year, the company said. According to Bloomberg, soaring spending on defense technology helped drive that growth. The company said over 80 businesses and government bodies rely on its software, including five out of the seven top defense contractors.

"We're building the platform that puts that knowledge to work," said Cameron McCord, Nominal's co-founder and chief executive officer, referring to the data that hardware makers have built up over decades, according to Bloomberg.

Nominal plans to put the fresh funding into building out its products and entering additional geographic markets and sectors. The company is also considering more acquisitions, following a pair of startup purchases it made earlier in the year, according to Bloomberg.
