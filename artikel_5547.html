Tucows Reports Third Quarter Results In Line with Expectations with Progress on Balance Sheet Obligations

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

TORONTO, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Tucows Inc. (NASDAQ: TCX) (TSX: TC), a global internet services leader, today reported its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. All figures are in U.S. dollars.

"Adjusted EBITDA and gross profit grew year over year, and we remain on track for our 2026 guidance," said David Woroch, President and Chief Executive Officer of Tucows. "We also retired the Ting preferred units valued at $149.3 million, and repaid $6 million on our syndicated loan facility. After quarter end, we agreed to assign Ting's Colorado Springs lease, which is expected to release Tucows' parent guaranty in the spring. Each of these steps makes the business simpler and more flexible than it was a quarter ago."

Consolidated net revenue increased 1.4% year over year to $100.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, driven by revenue growth at Ting.

Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 increased 20.5% to $29.1 million from the third quarter of 2025, and improved 13% sequentially. Both the year-over-year and sequential expansion was driven primarily by a large, non-recurring re-rate from the renegotiation of our mobile contract within the Corporate segment, as well as year-over-year growth from Ting.

Net Loss for the third quarter was $10.3 million (EPS of $(0.92), compared with a net loss of $23.0 million (EPS of $(2.08)), in Q3 2025. Gains and losses from unrealized foreign currency transactions removes the unrealized effect of the change in the mark-to-market values on outstanding unhedged foreign currency contracts, as well as the unrealized effect from the translation of monetary accounts denominated in non-U.S. dollars to U.S. dollars.

Tucows helps connect more people to the benefit of internet access through domain services, communications service technology, and fiber-optic infrastructure. Tucows Domains (https://tucowsdomains.com) manages over 21 million domain names and millions of value-added services through a global reseller network of 32,000 web hosts and ISPs. Hover (https://hover.com) makes it easy for individuals and small businesses to manage their domain names and email addresses. Wavelo (https://wavelo.com) is a telecommunications software suite for service providers that simplifies the management of mobile and internet network access; provisioning, billing and subscription; developer tools; and more.
