PetSmart Brings Back Beloved Holiday Tradition with Free Pet Photos with Santa Dec. 12-13

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pet parents can capture lasting holiday memories with festive photos and seasonal finds at PetSmart

PHOENIX, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Santa's sleigh is making its annual stop at PetSmart Dec. 12-13! From 2-5 p.m. each day, pet parents are invited to bring their dogs, cats and small pets* to PetSmart stores nationwide for a free photo with a store associate dressed as Santa against a festive backdrop.

Pet parents can learn more about the Photos with Santa event at PetSmart.com.

"Photos with Santa is a tradition pet parents and our associates look forward to every year," said Bradley Breuer, senior vice president of marketing at PetSmart. "All year long, our pets give us so much. With this event, we're celebrating the many ways they make our lives fuller and bringing our 'Anything for Your Everything' holiday promise to life. Whether it's continuing a favorite family tradition or bringing a new pet for their very first visit to Santa, we hope everyone will join us in making their season extra special."

This year, PetSmart is introducing an updated in-store check-in process. Upon arrival, pet parents will receive a numbered ticket for their pet. They can browse the aisles and check off their holiday shopping lists while they listen for their pet's name to be called overhead when Santa is ready.

Plus, PetSmart is rewarding its most loyal customers during the event. Treats Rewards VIPP members receive an exclusive Chance & Friends holiday gift at the register as a special thank you to the pet parents who show up for us all year long.

Make a Photo Prep List and Check It Twice From festive outfits to candid moments, PetSmart has a checklist to help pet parents prepare to capture the unique personality and holiday spirit of their pets in every photo.

After checking the holiday photo off the list, PetSmart makes it easy to tackle the rest with thoughtful gifts for every pet. From the techie to the movie lover, explore PetSmart's curated, shoppable gift guides for pet parents and pets, offering recommendations based on interests and style. Plus, kick off the season with PetSmart's Black Friday Savings Event: from Nov. 26-29, save up to 50% on holiday toys, festive apparel, cozy dog and cat beds, plush blankets and seasonal treats**.

To see the full PetSmart holiday collection for dogs, cats, small pets, fish and reptiles, visit PetSmart.com.

*Pets are allowed in PetSmart stores in accordance with the in-store pet policy located here; https://www.petsmart.com/help/about-petsmart-H0013b.html

** Terms apply. Offer activation may be required. See petsmart.com for complete details.
