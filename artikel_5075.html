Metaplanet Cuts 2026 Revenue Target 59%: Is the Bitcoin Playbook Running Dry?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Metaplanet cut its 2026 revenue forecast by 59% on November 13, to ¥6.5 billion from ¥16 billion. Its operating profit forecast fell 74%, to ¥3 billion from ¥11.4 billion. The Tokyo-listed Bitcoin (BTC) treasury firm traced the cut to its own discounted stock. Metaplanet stock fell 4.37% to ¥219 that day, down 53.21% so far
