EcoVadis, KPC and KOTRA Renew Partnership to Help South Korean Companies Strengthen ESG Practices

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

New three-party MoU builds a shared framework for learning, training and exchange across six areas of cooperation

PARIS, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- EcoVadis, the global standard for resilient, sustainable supply chains, today announced a new Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) concluded with the Korea Productivity Center (KPC) and the Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency (KOTRA) to work together in support of South Korean small and medium-sized companies. (SMEs).

The MoU was signed on 19 November 2026 at EcoVadis' Paris headquarters by Pierre-FranÃ§ois Thaler, cofounder and co-CEO of EcoVadis, and Sung-Joong Park, Chairman of KPC, and by Hee-Sang Lee, Senior Executive Vice President, on behalf of KOTRA. It builds on the success of an initial partnership announced in January 2024 which covered training, education and support services on EcoVadis ESG and sustainability products in South Korea. KOTRA takes part within the scope of that agreement.

Since the first agreement, the three organizations have worked together to improve Korean language assessment questions in EcoVadis questionnaires, and produced Korean language guides. Approximately 700 officials from companies and related organizations have attended the jointly held assessment response briefing sessions since 2024. At the end of last year, more than 2,229 Korean companies participated in the EcoVadis evaluation, an increase of approximately 90% compared to 2022. Korean companies achieved an average score of 65, with an improvement rising from 8% in 2021 to 29% last year.
