Bitcoin price is facing downside pressure, with analysts warning that a break below key support levels could trigger another correction toward $67,000. Market strategist Gareth Soloway identified $75,500 as a critical level Bitcoin must defend to avoid deeper losses.

Crypto analyst Dinci Jeremy also warned of further weakness after Bitcoin fell below $76,500. He expects a possible short-term recovery before BTC tests lower support around $72,000.

Both analysts highlighted weakening market sentiment, declining institutional demand, and technical signals as reasons for caution.

Gareth Soloway identified $75,500 as Bitcoin’s most important near-term support level. BTC price is currently trading around $75,600, leaving the cryptocurrency close to a potential breakdown.

A confirmed daily close below $75,500 could expose Bitcoin to further losses toward $67,000. He considers this lower level a potential buying opportunity.

On the upside, Bitcoin needs to reclaim $81,000 to improve its technical outlook and strengthen the possibility of a recovery.

The repeated tests of the $75,500 support could weaken buying pressure. A possible head-and-shoulders pattern could signal further downside if the pattern confirms.

Dinci Jeremy also sees further downside risks after Bitcoin dropped below $76,500.

According to his four-hour chart analysis, the $75,100 to $78,000 range remains an important price zone. He expects Bitcoin to attempt a short-term recovery toward $76,500 before potentially resuming its decline.

If buyers fail to defend the current levels, Jeremy identified $72,000 as the next major support.

This level also aligns with the 61.8% Fibonacci retracement, making it an important area to watch for a potential buying response.

However, Bitcoin would need to reclaim key resistance levels to establish a sustained recovery. Continued weakness across the broader cryptocurrency market could increase the risk of another decline.

Jeremy also highlighted declining demand for Bitcoin exchange-traded funds (ETFs) as a concern for the market.

He expects the next round of ETF data to show additional withdrawals, although the latest figures were not available during his analysis.

He also pointed to a decline of approximately 13,142 BTC in Bitcoin treasury holdings during the period discussed. According to his assessment, most of the reduction came from ETF-related holdings, while publicly traded companies continued accumulating Bitcoin.

These developments could indicate weaker institutional demand, adding pressure to Bitcoin’s recovery attempt.

Market sentiment is another factor influencing Bitcoin’s price outlook.

Jeremy noted that the Crypto Fear and Greed Index was trending lower as Bitcoin declined from around $86,000. However, the index had not yet recorded a new low despite the recent price weakness.

He expects sentiment could move sideways before recovering, although further deterioration remains possible if Bitcoin breaks below important support levels.

Bitcoin’s near-term direction depends largely on whether buyers can defend the $75,500 support level.

A confirmed breakdown below this level could expose BTC to $72,000, followed by the $67,000 target highlighted by Soloway. Meanwhile, a recovery toward $76,500 would be an early sign of improving buying interest.

For a stronger bullish reversal, Bitcoin needs to reclaim $81,000. Until then, weakening ETF demand, negative market sentiment, and bearish technical patterns could keep the cryptocurrency under pressure.
