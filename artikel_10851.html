McDonald’s slashes price of Quarter Pounder meal with new $8 deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

McDonald’s is introducing a new $8 Quarter Pounder meal deal, giving customers a cheaper way to order one of the fast food chain’s most popular burgers.

The promotion arrives as McDonald’s continues expanding its value offerings with deals across breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus.

The latest deal launches on November 2, 2026, at participating US restaurants, although customers will only have a limited time to take advantage of the discounted price.

The new promotion includes a Quarter Pounder with Cheese, medium fries, and a medium drink for $8, down from its full price of more than $11 depending on your location.

The burger features a quarter-pound beef patty cooked to order, topped with slivered onions, pickles, and two slices of American cheese on a sesame seed bun.

McDonald’s confirmed the offer will be available at participating restaurants nationwide starting November 2, with prices and participation varying by location.

The deal is also excluded from delivery orders, meaning customers will need to purchase it through an eligible ordering method to receive the promotional price.

While the company has not announced an exact end date, the $8 meal is being offered for a limited time.

The promotion follows McDonald’s decision to bring back its Extra Value Meals in September 2025, which introduced discounted combinations across its breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus.

At the time, the company said the returning meals offered customers savings of approximately 15% compared to purchasing the included items separately.

Those offerings included an $8 Big Mac meal and a $5 Sausage McMuffin with Egg meal, alongside other combinations featuring Chicken McNuggets, McCrispy sandwiches, and Quarter Pounder burgers.

The $8 meal deal was originally announced alongside McDonald’s first-ever reservation-only Chef’s Table experience, which took place in Brooklyn, New York, on October 29.
