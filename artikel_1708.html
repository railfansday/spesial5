Opinion: He's incredible, Liverpool have found the next Khvicha Kvaratskhelia

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Liverpool have found their next Kvicha Kvaratskhelia, and he's incredible.

Liverpool are entering a new era under Julian Ward, and it's becoming increasingly clear that the club's transfer strategy is undergoing a significant transformation.

For years, the Reds built their success around identifying undervalued players who were ready to make an immediate impact.

Think Mohamed Salah, Sadio Mane, Andy Robertson and Georginio Wijnaldum. Players who arrived at Anfield with something to prove and developed into world-class stars.

But now, Ward appears determined to take things in a slightly different direction.

Rather than waiting for players to establish themselves at the highest level, Liverpool want to identify the next generation of superstars before they become household names.

And it's an approach that makes a lot of sense.

How Liverpool are building a team for the futureThe transfer market has become increasingly expensive. Even relatively unproven players are commanding enormous fees, and Liverpool simply cannot afford to compete financially with the likes of Manchester City and Real Madrid for every established superstar. Instead, the Reds are looking to get ahead of the competition. We've already seen the club become far more aggressive in recruiting young talent from across the world. The arrivals of Mor Talla Ndiaye, Ifeanyi Ndukwe, Samuel Martinez, Armin Pecsi and Lucca Brughmans are all examples of Liverpool investing in players with enormous potential. Meanwhile, the club's academy is already producing exciting talents like Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni, Joshua Abe and Will Wright. The objective is simple. Liverpool want to build a conveyor belt of talent capable of sustaining success for the next decade.

Meet the next Kvaratskhelia - Sander AlamaaFor a long time, the Reds have been on a search to bolster their options on the wing as well. Well, Ward doesn't have to wait much longer - he's found the Reds' next winger. Meet Sander Alamaa. Just like Kvaratskhelia, he is emerging from a very under the radar nation. But except he's from Estonia rather than Georgia. The youngster is one of the most exciting teenage left-wingers playing outside of the traditional top 7 European leagues right now. He's incredibly technically gifted and excels in one vs one situations. Alamaa also loves to cut onto his stronger right foot from the left. The youngster has already made 68 appearances at senior club level and averaged 0.72 goal contributions per 90 minutes. He has 6 goal contributions (4 goals and 2 assists) in 3 UEFA Youth League games this season and helped his side knock Chelsea out of the competition. This is a player Liverpool should be all over because he is simply incredible.
