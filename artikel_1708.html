Manchester United make Leny Yoro red card appeal decision after Liverpool rout

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United have notified the Football Association (FA) that they intend to appeal Leny Yoro’s red card against Liverpool.

The Red Devils were reduced to 10 men just eight minutes into Mauricio Pochettino’s first game as Man Utd boss at Anfield on Sunday.

Liverpool then romped to an easy 4-0 win, with Pochettino afterwards lamenting referee Sam Barrott’s decision to dismiss Yoro as “unfair”.

The Argentine believed that his player did not deny a goalscoring opportunity, meaning that he should not have seen red.

Yoro clipped the heels of Alexander Isak as the Liverpool attacker bore down on goal, but Pochettino and United fans felt that goalkeeper Senne Lammens would have reached the loose ball before Isak, negating the goalscoring potential.

Under current FA rules, clubs have 14 days to submit an appeal, with United back in action on Saturday as they host Brentford in the Premier League at Old Trafford.

With his current three-game domestic suspension, Yoro would also miss the trip to Newcastle and visit of Coventry early next month, but remain available for Champions League meetings with Sporting and RB Leipzig.

Yoro’s dismissal on Merseyside was his second in two months, having also received his marching orders in a Champions League defeat by Atletico Madrid in October.

Former United boss Michael Carrick questioned that decision at the time and stated his belief that it was not a red card, but no appeal was made by the club.
