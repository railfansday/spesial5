Arsenal given asking price to sign Antonio Nusa from RB Leipzig in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal have been told they will need to pay in excess of €100 million (£83.5m) to sign Antonio Nusa from RB Leipzig in the January transfer window, according to reports in Germany.

Arsenal are in the market to strengthen Mikel Arteta’s attacking options and Nusa is one of the options on their shortlist.

The 21-year-old, who joined RB Leipzig in a €21m (£17.8m) deal from Club Brugge two years ago, has been one of the German club’s standout performers this season with nine goals and eight assists in 17 games so far.

At international level, Nusa has established himself as a key part of Norway’s attack, scoring 10 goals in 36 appearances.

Arsenal have been monitoring Nusa’s performances in the Bundesliga and the Champions League ahead of the upcoming transfer window.

But according to Sky Germany, RB Leipzig will only consider selling Nusa if they receive an ‘astronomical offer’, which would be bids over €100m (£83.5m).

RB Leipzig, who sit fourth in the Bundesliga and remain in contention to secure a play-off place in the Champions League league phase, are said to want to keep Nusa for the remainder of the season.

Earlier this year, RB Leipzig held off selling Yan Diomande in the January window and ended up agreeing a deal worth up to £120m with Real Madrid in the summer.
