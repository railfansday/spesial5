Prediction: Lille vs Bayern Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Seeking an immediate response when they turn their attention back to Champions League action on Wednesday night, Bayern Munich will travel to France when they meet Lille.

With a stacked midweek schedule from across the continent, there are plenty of attractive Champions League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Wednesday’s showdown from the Stade Pierre-Mauroy, you can find all of the key Lille vs Bayern Munich H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, marking their return from November’s international break with a toothless 1-0 loss at home against historic domestic rivals Lyon, Lille are searching for some much-welcomed stability. Likewise, opening their Champions League adventure with a rollercoaster 3-2 loss at the hands of Spanish outfit Real Betis on September 8th, Davide Ancelotti’s men face an almighty challenge in the race to book a knockout stage ticket this year. In fact, getting November underway with an uneventful 0-0 stalemate against Bodo/Glimt, that is the only point that Wednesday’s hosts have mustered from any of their first four Champions League showdowns. It should also be highlighted, claiming just a single win from any of their last four consecutive matchups at the Stade Pierre-Mauroy across all competitions, Ancelotti’s side look set for another heavyweight examination in midweek.

Both still recovering from respective long-term knee injuries, Lille will once again be without the duo of Tiago Santos and former Rangers striker Hamza Igamane here. While veteran marksman Olivier Giroud might still be waiting to open his Champions League account, the 40-year-old could be handed a full recall on Wednesday night.

Likewise, former Nijmegen star Calvin Verdonk could return at the heart of Ancelotti’s defence in midweek. Linked with a potential Premier League switch over the summer, 22-year-old Ngalayel Mukau will start in the middle of the park and partner one-time Tottenham starlet Nabil Bentaleb.

As for the visitors, relinquishing their spot at the top of the Bundesliga table over the weekend as they marked their return from November’s international break with a 1-1 stalemate at home against Koln, Bayern Munich are seeking an immediate European response here. Nevertheless, last seen in Champions League action securing an eye-catching 2-0 victory away at Spanish heavyweights Atletico Madrid, Vincent Kompany’s men are regarded as a genuine European contender this season. Not suffering a single competitive defeat across any competition since their Champions League semi-final exit against reigning European champions PSG on April 28th, Bayern Munich should be excited by their extended success away from the Allianz. In fact, Wednesday’s guests have won their previous three consecutive battles on their travels by an aggregate score of 7-2.

In what is a huge blow for Bayern Munich, Kompany’s side have confirmed that newly crowned Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane will miss out on Wednesday night after sustaining an ankle injury against Koln over the weekend. Likewise, picking up his own first-half blow on Friday, the German giants are also expected to be without fellow attacking superstar Michael Olise.

With veteran goalkeeper Manuel Neuer suffering another injury setback, 23-year-old Jonas Urbig will keep his spot between the sticks. Despite recent speculation over both his fitness and long-term future at the Allianz, Jamal Musiala should get some more minutes under his belt.

Sweating over two of their striking superstars on Wednesday night, Bayern Munich are also searching for an immediate response on foreign soil. Left frustrated over the weekend as they were eventually forced to settle for a 1-1 stalemate at home against Koln, Kompany’s side have also shown some worrying defensive issues this season. With the German giants seeing nine of their previous 10 appearances across all competitions finish with both teams finding the net, neutrals will be hoping for another Champions League thriller in midweek.
