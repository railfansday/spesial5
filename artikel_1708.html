Liverpool tee up deal for Senegal wonderkid as transfer ‘option’ agreed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liverpool have successfully negotiated the option to sign Senegal wonderkid Sidy Barhama Ndiaye once he turns 18.

Ndiaye, currently 16, trialled with Liverpool’s academy in the summer of 2025 and now has a clear pathway to Merseyside.

Per reports from The Athletic, the Reds have pipped a number of European rivals to get right of first refusal for his signing in January 2028.

English clubs cannot sign players from overseas until they have turned 18, which Ndiaye will do on New Year’s Eve in 2027.

Ndiaye currently plies his trade in his home nation with Diambars FC, whom he helped earn promotion to the Senegalese top flight last season.

Ndiaye is currently away at the Under-17 World Cup in Qatar, picking up a goal and two assists in a 9-0 drubbing of Cuba earlier this week.

Senegal’s only other match at the tournament so far resulted in a 1-1 draw with Croatia. Liverpool fans hoping to get a glimpse of a future star, though, might be tempted to tune into their meeting with Tajikistan on Thursday, November 26.

Liverpool have made a habit of spotting talent at such tournaments. At last year’s U17 World Cup, their scouts brought home Senegal’s Mor Talla Ndiaye, Austria’s Ifeanyi Ndukwe and Belgium’s Lucca Brughmans.

It remains to be seen whether Liverpool will opt to sign Ndiaye in 2028, and what role he might play at the club if they do.

Ndiaye will hope to follow in the footsteps of one of Liverpool’s all-time greats, his countryman Sadio Mane, in making the move to the north-west.
