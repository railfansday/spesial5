‘No one can chat s***’: Tottenham star defends Cole Palmer amid Chelsea criticism

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tosin Adarabioyo has said that “no-one can chat s***” about his former Chelsea team-mate Cole Palmer despite the England international’s struggles this season.

Palmer has scored five times in his 13 appearances across the Premier League and Carabao Cup this term, adding three assists in the top flight.

The England international has also failed to force his way into Thomas Tuchel’s plans on the international stage, having been snubbed for the World Cup squad last summer.

He also missed out on the Nations League cohorts as the Three Lions secured their place in the knockout stages next year with a 3-2 win over Spain earlier this month.

Now, Tottenham defender Tosin has defended his ex-club-mate amid Chelsea’s dip in form, with the Blues suffering a 4-1 defeat to Leeds over the weekend.

Xabi Alonso’s side have won just once in their last five matches, falling to eighth in the table.

“No one can chat s*** about Cole,” Tosin said.

“For me, he's one of the best players I've ever seen. You can say what you want. Honestly, you guys can say what you want. He's a top level player. He will continue to get better.”

Tosin admitted that he was surprised at Tuchel’s neglect of Palmer for England duty, hailing the attacking midfielder as “the best No 10 in the Premier League.”

The England boss had alleged that Palmer had shown a lack of commitment to his national team, but Tosin laughed off the suggestion.

“False, false, false,” he continued. “I know Cole's dreams, his aspirations. So, yeah, it's completely false.”
