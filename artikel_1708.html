Mourinho stands firmly behind Vinicius amid Real Madrid scrutiny

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jose Mourinho has once again called Vinicius Junior "an incredible player" following a positive international break for the Real Madrid winger.

Vinicius has struggled for Los Blancos this season, scoring just once and providing three assists in 12 appearances across all competitions.

The 26-year-old also missed Madrid's last four LaLiga matches before the break after receiving a straight red card for a hair pull against Villarreal.

However, during the international break with Brazil, Vinicius scored a brace against Japan before netting a hat-trick against Singapore.

Ahead of Madrid's league meeting with Celta Vigo, Mourinho reiterated his support for Vinicius.

"Starter, off the bench, or in the stands, it doesn't matter," Mourinho said about Vinicius' role in the team.

"It's a strange thing because, a couple of weeks ago, people were talking about Vinicius as if he were the huge problem Real Madrid had, and after scoring goals against Singapore and Japan, we can't stop talking about Vinicius: if he plays, if he doesn't, why he has to play.

"I think it's too much. What I say about Vinicius is what I've said one, two, three, four, and five weeks ago: he is an incredible player, we love him, we need him, we have always tried to help him, and that's what we will continue to do.

"Here, we don't have players with privileges. We don't have players who have to play by decree. We have a group of people working for the team, for Real Madrid."

Madrid are second in LaLiga, three points behind early leaders Barcelona, ahead of welcoming 11th-place Celta to the Estadio Bernabeu.

Celta are one of five opponents Mourinho has faced on more than one occasion in LaLiga with a 100% win rate (W2), alongside Athletic Club (W6), Rayo Vallecano (W5), Hercules (W2) and Real Valladolid (W2).

"Celta are much better than the points they have," said Mourinho. They are a team that last season beat us here, at the Bernabeu.

"They are playing in Europe, and perhaps playing matches on Thursdays and then in the league is also costing them some points, but this factor does not exist [for this fixture].

"They will surely present themselves as always: organised and ambitious. They concede few goals because, defensively, they are very organised, and they know how to do it and how to press."

Kylian Mbappe has scored in each of his last seven appearances for Madrid in all competitions (nine goals) and could equal his best scoring run for the club, achieved between September and October 2025 (eight games, 12 goals).

Williot Swedberg has scored more LaLiga goals against Madrid than against any other side (four in five games).

Since the start of 2024-25, he has been involved in five league goals against Madrid (four goals, one assist), the joint-most alongside Raphinha.

Madrid have lost just one of their last 24 LaLiga games against Celta (W21 D2), a 2-0 defeat in December 2025, winning 11 of the last 12.

Los Blancos have won their last eight games in all competitions, their best winning run since a 10-game streak between May and September 2022 under Carlo Ancelotti.

However, Madrid lost their last home game against Celta in LaLiga and have only twice lost consecutive home games against them in the competition (L2 in 1943 and in 2006).

Celta have picked up points in six of their last seven away games against teams from the Community of Madrid in LaLiga (W3 D3 L1), but they lost their most recent such match 3-0 against Rayo Vallecano last October.

Celta have conceded 10 goals and kept six clean sheets in LaLiga 2026-27, both their best records after the first 12 games of a season in the competition since 2002-03 (eight goals and seven clean sheets), under Miguel Angel Lotina.
