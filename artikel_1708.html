Emegha ‘would do anything to win’ at Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Chelsea striker Emmanuel Emegha "would do anything to win" as the Blues prepare to take on Nottingham Forest in the Premier League on Friday.

Xabi Alonso's side have struggled either side of the November international break, highlighted by their 4-1 defeat at home to Leeds United last weekend.

Chelsea lost by 3+ goals while conceding 4+ goals at home in the Premier League for just the fourth time, last doing so against Brentford in April 2022 (also 4-1).

However, in their defeat to Leeds, Emegha became the 31st player to score on his first Premier League start for Chelsea; only Aston Villa have seen more players do so in the competition (32).

The 23-year-old, who joined from Strasbourg in the summer, is now determined to kick on after missing the start of the season through injury.

"Every player is different," Emegha told the club website.

"There are some players, you buy them and straight away they are… boom! Me, I always need some time to adapt, and I've had a lot of injuries.

"Now I'm happy that I'm over it and I do everything for my body to stay as fit as possible. I want to stay like this because playing for a big club, you cannot be having these injuries, injuries, injuries. It will not work.

"I'm a winner, I'm a fighter. I would do anything to win. If I need to block a ball with the front of my face, I will do it.

"I'm quick, I know how to make runs in behind, and I know that I can be a nightmare for all the defenders."

Forest, meanwhile, fell to a 2-0 defeat against high-flying Bournemouth last time out in the league.

Oliver Glasner's side are above the relegation zone only on goal difference, having won just once in their last seven league games (D1 L5).

Forest defender Nikola Milenkovic has urged his team-mates to improve against Chelsea, emphasising the importance of every point.

"We need to review it and try to bounce back in the next game and prepare in the best way possible," Milenkovic said of the defeat at Bournemouth.

"We have to focus on the next game. It will be better next time. Every game is a new story and Chelsea will be another difficult game.

"We need to be ready for it. Every point in the Premier League is massive."

Morgan Gibbs-White has faced Chelsea more often without scoring than any other opponent in the Premier League (11 games).

However, his four assists against the Blues are his most against a top-flight opponent.

With Joao Pedro expected to remain sidelined for this fixture, Morgan Rogers will look to provide an attacking threat again.

He has scored the joint-most goals for Chelsea in the Premier League this season (five – level with Joao Pedro), and he assisted Emegha's goal against Leeds.

Following a 3-1 win at Stamford Bridge in May, Forest are looking to win consecutive league games against Chelsea for the first time since April 1979 (a run of three).

However, Chelsea are unbeaten in their last five away league games against Forest (W4 D1), with their three consecutive victories their longest run of league wins at the City Ground.

Since a run of three consecutive wins in April-May last season (the third of which was against Chelsea), Forest have won just two of their last 14 Premier League games (D5 L7).

Though, since the start of March, no side has lost more Premier League games than Chelsea (13), while among ever-present sides only Crystal Palace (17) have earned fewer points than the Blues in that time (23).

Forest (33% – 3/9) and Chelsea (35% – 7/20) are the two sides with the highest share of their goals in the opening 15 minutes of Premier League games this season, with neither side yet to concede in this timeframe.
