Hila Klein files for divorce from Ethan Klein with celebrity lawyer

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Hila and Ethan Klein filed for divorce ahead of announcing their split to the public, according to a new report from TMZ.

Back on October 22, Hila Klein revealed that her husband, Ethan Klein, had been having an affair for over six months. “I am living in a nightmare I can’t wake up from. My husband has been having an affair. I have been fed lie after lie after lie for months. I am tired of lies. Here is the truth,” she said.

The H3 creator confirmed this by reposting Hila’s Instagram posts and announcing he would be giving up ownership stakes in Teddy Fresh, Teddy Glow, and their other projects. Ethan also removed himself from the H3 podcast, too.

Now, according to TMZ, the pair filed for divorce before actually going public with the split.

As per TMZ, which has seen documents filed by Hila, the pair separated on October 13, 2026. That was three days after their 14th wedding anniversary.

Additionally, Hila is seeking joint custody of their three kids and spousal support. She also wants Ethan to cover her legal fees from the divorce and to also restore her maiden name in the split too.

TMZ noted that Hila has hired Laura Wasser as her attorney for the divorce. Wasser has dealt with a number of high-profile splits in the past, including Kanye West and Kim Kardashian, as well as Ariana Grande and Dalton Gomez.
