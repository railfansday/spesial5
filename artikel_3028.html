We recently attended the 26th Annual Fall Clinical Dermatology Conference, where we sponsored a poster highlighting clinical data on Emrosi's superior skin clearing effects. The post-hoc data analysis from our Phase III clinical trial showed that nearly 50% of patients that achieved the primary endpoint of Investigator Global Assessment or IGA success had complete resolution of their rosacea symptoms at the end of the treatment period with Emrosi.

This equates to nearly 30% of the mild to moderate rosacea patients achieving clear skin or an IGA score of 0 with Emrosi treatment. In comparison, only 12.8% of patients on Oracea achieved an IGA score of 0 or completely clear skin. Notably, clinical research shows that rosacea patients who achieved complete symptom clearance experienced a significant delay in their time to relapse, and they remain free of symptoms longer than those who reach only partial clearance. With over 2.5x more patients achieving clear skin with Emrosi compared to Oracea, we believe that these data are compelling and significantly support Emrosi's best-in-class clinical profile.

The superior head-to-head efficacy demonstrated in our Phase III clinical trials comparing Emrosi to Oracea remains a central message in driving adoption throughout the dermatology community.
