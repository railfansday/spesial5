Why Sunday night’s game vs. the Vikings could be the most important one of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sitting in first place in the NFC, the San Francisco 49ers look to be facing their toughest battle yet in Week 11, as they take on the Minnesota Vikings in primetime in Mexico City.

San Francisco had an outstanding offensive performance last weekend against the Dallas Cowboys, out-dueling their counterparts 46-40 to improve to 8-1, tied for the best record in the NFL. However, their ailing defense struggled in a big way against one of the more talented offenses in the league.

Now, they get a completely different challenge on Sunday night, taking on the league's best defense and best defensive coordinator with the highest stakes on the line: the NFC's No. 1 seed.

The 49ers are in the midst of their toughest stretch of the year. They just played the Cowboys, get the Vikings this week, and then get the Seattle Seahawks, New York Giants, and Los Angeles Rams, all potential playoff contenders, in the weeks after.

So, keeping their lead for the No. 1 seed is paramount, which means a win on Sunday is a top priority. Now normally, I'd vote for divisional games to be the most important ones for the 49ers, as nothing tops taking care of business in the tough NFC West.

But Sunday's game against the Vikings could actually be the most important one of the season for the 49ers, as there are serious implications with the No. 1 seed on the line.

If the 49ers win on Sunday, ESPN's Aaron Schatz, the creator of DVOA, projects San Francisco to have an 81 percent chance of getting the No. 1 seed in the NFC. Should they lose? That number falls all the way to 26 percent in Schatz's simulations, with the Vikings jumping from five percent to 51 percent for the No. 1 seed.

Now, the 49ers are still the team with the second-highest odds to win the Super Bowl on FanDuel at +550, behind the Buffalo Bills at +500. They also currently have the best odds to win the NFC (+250), with the Vikings (+470) second. We'll see how major that change is pending this weekend's outcome.

With the current state of the NFC West and how strong the 49ers have started the season, making the playoffs doesn't seem to be in jeopardy. The 49ers have -700 odds to win the NFC West, and FanDuel gives them -110 odds to win over 13.5 games this season.

But having the No. 1 seed would be huge for January, as the playoffs would run through San Francisco, giving them the best chance at making the Super Bowl in a very wide-open race this season.

San Francisco does get a strong end to its schedule. Three of their last four will be against the Los Angeles Chargers, Philadelphia Eagles, and Arizona Cardinals. But they have to weather the storm in the coming weeks with a couple of matchups against playoff hopefuls, starting with Sunday night against the Vikings.

Minnesota looked rejuvenated last weekend, beating the Green Bay Packers 30-10 on the road in Kirk Cousins's first start back. The 49ers just saw Cousins two weeks ago, and they'll have their hands full on both sides of the ball.

Heading into the game, San Francisco is a 3-point favorite over Minnesota, and they've got a ton at stake in the heavyweight NFC battle.
