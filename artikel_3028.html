Why PDD Holdings Stock Was Bumping Higher Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

An analyst upgraded his recommendation on the Chinese e-commerce mainstay.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 23, 2026 at 3:56PM EST

10 stocks we like better than PDD Holdings ›

Much as shoppers have flocked to its popular e-commerce site Temu, investors eagerly bought the American Depositary Shares (ADSes) of China's PDD Holdings (PDD +4.13%) on the first trading day of the week. In late-session action, the company's U.S.-traded equity was up by almost 4%, thanks in no small part to an analyst update.

Well before market open, Bernstein SocGen's Robin Zhu upgraded his recommendation on PDD to outperform (i.e., buy) from market perform (hold). He also maintained his price target of $110 per ADS.

According to reports, Zhu's revision comes from a greater willingness of management to communicate with investors. This coincides with the continued growth of both Temu, the company's e-commerce portal for customers outside of China, and its Pinduoduo domestic portal.

In his view, the company is meeting the moment by focusing on bargains. This is particularly effective on the Chinese e-commerce market, as the economy has been relatively sluggish of late; consumers are feeling the pinch and looking for shopping deals.

Also adding to the bullish case for PDD's equity is the company's clever sidestepping of U.S. and European restrictions on Temu (through a shift toward local warehousing rather than relying on shipping from China). Meanwhile, Temu remains a popular online shopping destination in a world where many consumers are feeling as constrained as the Chinese population. Temu is an increasingly valuable brand and, I'd argue, one that's rather underrated. Its owner is well worth consideration for a buy.
