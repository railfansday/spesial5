Roborock Robot Vacuum Early Black Friday Deals (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Robot vacuums are more expensive than ever, with many models from popular brands like Dyson and Shark starting at $1,200. But you usually don't have to pay such a high price tag for a great model—you just have to wait for a good sale.

Black Friday marks a good time to find a ton of robot and stick vacuums on sale, but you don't have to wait to score the holiday deals. (In fact, we've already spotted great early Black Friday sales you don't want to miss.)

One of our favorite robot vacuum brands, Roborock, has discounts live ahead of its official Black Friday sale, which kicks off November 19. Two great picks include the Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro (one of my top vacuums overall) and the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2. Each is currently available for less than $700, which is a great price for getting a smart and powerful robot vacuum that doubles as a mop.

The Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro launched in late summer as the newest addition to Roborock's more affordable Qrevo line, and it quickly landed itself as a pick on my guide to the Best Robot Vacuums.

The Qrevo 2 Pro retails at $800, but the best move is to wait for it to go on sale for $600, which is what it costs right now. While I've occasionally seen it drop as low as $550, I think it's worth getting now to have the product in hand.

Overall, Roborock's vacuums are great, but I've been especially impressed by this one's corner cleaning, thanks to its extendable mop arm. It won't always spot cords, but it does a better job at navigating furniture and getting underneath my cabinets than more expensive robot vacuums I've tested.

If you'd rather have a roller-mop style robot vacuum, the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow is another great option from Roborock's Qrevo line. This one came out in January of this year, and our reviewer instantly loved it.

The Qrevo Curv 2 Flow is a more expensive robot vacuum than the Qrevo 2 since it comes with the brand's SpriaFlow roller-mop. The internal roller spans nearly the entire width of the vacuum and can extend to edge-clean your home. It even has a built-in scraper to move dirty water into the tank instead of rolling it back onto the floor. If you're looking for an affordable mopping powerhouse, this is the one to choose. Plus, it's already on sale for almost as little as the Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro.
