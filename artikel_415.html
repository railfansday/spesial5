Play-Doh made a rose bouquet kit for adults — where to buy this romantic Christmas gift

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

TL;DR: Hasbro's $39.99 Blooms by Play-Doh Rose Bouquet Kit lets adults craft 20 display-ready faux roses with baby's breath. It launches exclusively at Walmart on Nov. 13, before expanding to other retailers in early 2027.

Play-Doh is officially getting into home decor. With the launch of the Blooms by Play-Doh Rose Bouquet Kit, the brand has expanded its adult crafting lineup to offer a hands-on project with a display-worthy payoff: a complete, permanent vase of red roses and baby's breath made entirely by hand.

Designed for crafters of all skill levels, the all-in-one box packs everything you need to build an arrangement of 20 bold red roses accented with sprigs of baby's breath. Instead of fiddling with florist wire or messy hot glue, the set comes loaded with Play-Doh compound, flower-making extruder tools, faux greenery, a vase, and step-by-step instructions.

You simply use the blooming tool to press and shape the petals into full blossoms, let the compound dry, mist them with finishing spray to lock everything in place, and arrange the bouquet in the included vase.

Once everything dries, you have a solid, display-ready centerpiece that will not droop after three days on your table. It is an easy, low-maintenance craft whether you are putting it together for a party or just want a relaxing creative project that does not involve a screen.

You can pick up the Blooms by Play-Doh Rose Bouquet Kit now for $39.99 exclusively at Walmart. If you are eyeing one for holiday gifting, Walmart holds the exclusive run through the season before it arrives at other retailers in early 2027.
