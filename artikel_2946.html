Dre Greenlaw, Trent Williams were limited in Thursday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw and left tackle Trent Williams rested during Wednesday's practice, but they had different listings on Thursday's practice report.

Greenlaw was listed with a knee injury and a shoulder injury was listed for Williams. Both players were limited participants.

The only other change from Wednesday's report was that running back Christian McCaffrey (rest) returned as a full participant.

Wide receiver Mike Evans (Achilles), cornerback Renardo Green (back), and cornerback Deommodore Lenoir (foot) missed practice for the second straight day. Safety Siran Neal (knee, foot), wide receiver Deebo Samuel (hamstring), cornerback Upton Stout (shoulder, neck), and defensive tackle C.J. West (knee) remained in the limited category.

The 49ers will issue injury designations for their road game against the Cowboys on Friday.
