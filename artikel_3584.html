Zelda Meets Baldur's Gate 3 In Pirate RPG That's Free To Download Until November 26

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Imagine taking the exploration and secrets of The Legend of Zelda, blending in the branching quests and consequential decisions of Baldur’s Gate 3, and dropping the whole thing onto a cursed pirate island. Sounds like an unusual combination, but one indie RPG brings these elements together, with dangerous creatures, challenging boss fights and multiple endings. Better yet, PC players can claim it for free for a limited time.

Mark of the Deep, developed by Mad Mimic and released in January 2025, is now available on the Epic Games Store for free. The game follows Marcus “Rookie” Ramsey, whose ship is wrecked on an island that most people know only through rumors. With the rest of his crew, the Angry Mermaids, missing, Rookie must explore the island and uncover the mystery behind its curse, which is tied to its native inhabitants, known as the Old Folks.

You see Zelda here once you look closely at how the island unfolds. Mark of the Deep uses an isometric, top-down view and encourages players to explore its connected areas. Rookie gradually gains new abilities that change how he moves through the world and handles enemies. Some routes stay out of reach until he has the right skill, giving players a reason to return to explored places. There are also secrets and side quests to find along the way.

This isn’t a party-based, turn-based RPG, though, so the Baldur’s Gate comparison isn’t about the genre. Mark of the Deep is a single-player action adventure, and its fights take more from the Soulslike genre. Its role-playing side comes through in the people Rookie meets, the quests he can take on and the decisions he makes. The game lets players help characters affected by the island’s corruption, and those choices can influence how the story ends.

For the crucial part of combat, the developers list more than 80 enemies and 16 bosses, including abyssal creatures, undead enemies and cultists. Rookie can unlock new skills, weapons and trinkets as he progresses, allowing him to improve his fighting options. Boss encounters can be challenging, requiring players to learn enemy attack patterns and adjust their approach.

The island also has plenty to explore, although the lack of an in-game map can make navigation frustrating at times. Its atmosphere and exploration are appealing, but players should be prepared to spend some time figuring out where to go next.

There’s a time limit on the giveaway, but not on playing the game once it has been claimed. Search for Mark of the Deep on the Epic Games Store and add it to your library before the offer ends on November 26 at 4 p.m. UTC. You don’t need to download and install the game to claim it for free; just add it and a copy becomes yours forever.

Mark of the Deep is also a fairly modest download by current PC standards: you don’t need a powerful rig to play this Zelda-inspired pirate adventure. For your reference, Epic lists a 5GB storage requirement, 4GB RAM and a dual-core 2.4GHz processor as the minimum specifications.

The Legend of Zelda franchise follows the adventures of Link, an elf-like Hylian, and Princess Zelda as they protect the land of Hyrule from the evil warlord-turned-demon king Ganon. The series is known for its mix of action, adventure, and puzzle-solving elements, often revolving around the collection of the Triforce, a powerful relic left by the goddesses who created Hyrule.
