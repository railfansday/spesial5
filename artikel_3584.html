Party of Five's Scott Wolf Announces Dad's Death: 'He Was My Hero'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Scott Wolf is paying tribute to his dad, Steven Wolf, after his death.

“My father, Steven Charles Wolf, passed away a few days ago,” Scott, 58, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, alongside photos of his dad through the years. “His passing leaves a hole in the center of our hearts, and our lives. He was my hero, and I have lived my entire life trying to be like him. He was the most kind, honest, caring, and loving man. He didn’t just adore his family, he lived for us.”

He continued, “His warmth and authenticity made better the lives of everyone who was lucky enough to know him. He was brilliant, funny, and truly made every room, and this world, a better place. He leaves behind a legacy of kindness, hard work, fairness, service, compassion, and most of all, love.”

Scott described his late father as “the best husband, father, grandfather and friend” who “lives on in the hearts of all of us who were blessed to know his love.”

“I miss you dad,” Scott concluded. “I hope your days are filled with golf, seafood, card games, cashews, and hugs from those you have rejoined. I love you so much. 💔❤️.”

Steven’s death comes two months after Scott and Kelley Wolf confirmed they were moving forward with a divorce after facing a challenging year.

“Since early spring, we have been home together as a family, focusing on healing, rebuilding, and finding stability after an extraordinarily difficult season,” the pair told Us in a September statement. “After much care, consideration, and reflection, we have decided to move forward with our divorce.”

The message continued, “Our hearts are heavy, but they are also filled with gratitude for the years we spent together, for all that we shared and built, for our loving marriage and, most of all, for our incredible children.” (Scott and Kelley share sons Jackson, 17, and Miller, 13, and daughter Lucy, 12.)

The estranged couple shared that they are “deeply grateful” to those who supported them and “sent love and prayers for the healing of our family.”

“We now move forward into this next chapter, guided by love as coparents to our children and by our shared commitment to the best and brightest future for our family,” the statement concluded.

Scott and Kelley previously announced their split in 2025 after more than two decades together. The Party of Five alum was subsequently granted temporary sole custody of the pair’s three children and Kelley was placed on involuntary psychiatric hold. Scott was later granted a temporary protective order against Kelley.

She was arrested in August 2025 on suspicion of electronic communication harassment and electronic disclosure of personal identifying information after allegedly sharing confrontational social media posts about Scott. Us confirmed earlier this year that Scott’s restraining order against Kelley was dropped. The pair have since reunited for the first time publicly for a family outing in February.
