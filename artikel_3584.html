Former Bachelor Peter Weber and Fiancee Jadeyn Carr’s Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Former Bachelor Peter Weber has found love with model Jadeyn Carr.

In a matter of months, the pilot subtly debuted his newfound romance via social media, announced they had twins on the way and popped the question.

Weber, who first appeared in Bachelor Nation in 2019 as a contestant on Hannah Brown‘s season 15 of The Bachelorette and later led season 24 of The Bachelor in 2020, had been linked to former contestant Kelley Flanagan, former show producer Julie LaPlaca and actress Brook Sill before meeting Carr online.

Weber confirmed his romance with Carr via Instagram, in which the model joined her beau on a family trip to the Adirondacks in upstate New York.

“Summer ’26,” Weber captioned his upload, which did not mention Carr by name.

Less than a week later, Weber shared a second post with Carr. “Favorites,” he wrote photos of his then-girlfriend joining him in a plane’s cockpit and cuddling on a beach vacation.

Bachelor Nation members quickly flooded the comments. “The hard launch finally 👏,” Luke Stone wrote, while Clay Harbor chimed in with a trio of clapping emojis.

Weber’s family embraced Carr publicly, too. His mother, Barbara, shared a photo of the couple to her own Instagram with the caption, “SO HAPPY YOU BROUGHT HER HOME!!❤️ … I’m so happy for you two! She fits into all of our hearts❤️.”

Weber’s brother, Jack, left his own note on the couple’s early posts, writing “Love this ❤️.”.

Just weeks after going Instagram official, Weber and Carr revealed a major surprise.

“1 + 1 = 4 ♥️,” they wrote in the joint caption, alongside images of Weber kissing Carr’s bare baby bump.

Weber’s family celebrated the news in the comments. “Wohoo! Can’t wait to meet them!!” Jack wrote, while Barbara added, “Gods sweetest blessings! So So loved already!! You two will be the best parents!! Love you 4!❤️❤️❤️❤️.”

Weber announced on November 11 that he proposed to Carr in New York City’s Central Park.

“Marry Me @jadeyncarr,” he captioned a carousel of photos from the proposal, in which he presented Carr with an oval diamond engagement ring.
