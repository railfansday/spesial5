When is the next Treasure Hour in Fortnite Treasure Hunters? – Complete Power Hour event schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

There’s a lot you need to find and collect in Fortnite’s Treasure Hunters mini-season. If you’re determined to make the most of this one, participating in Treasure Hour events is a great way to make finding everything this season has to offer significantly easier.

Treasure Hours have all kinds of helpful buffs and boosts sure to make finding whatever you’re searching for much more doable. To ensure you make the most of this helpful event, here’s when the next Treasure Hour is and a complete breakdown of the Power Hour schedule in Fortnite.

Treasure Hours is a weekly event where the odds for various items are massively boosted to make finding them around the Fortnite island easier. This includes both seasonal items, like weapons and loot, and special Treasure Chest items, like the Pirate Shop Access and featured Weekly Gold Outfit.

The specifics of each Treasure Hour event vary a bit, as the featured loot items that get a boosted rate vary each time. Ahead of the arrival of these events, the specifics are posted on the official X (formerly Twitter) Fortnite page.

Every Treasure Hour event adds the featured weekly Captain’s weapon to the starting loot pool, which means you’ll already have it in your inventory as soon as you jump out of the battle bus. They also always feature boosted odds and drop rates for Treasure Chest loot.

The Treasure Hour event takes place every Saturday throughout the entirety of Fortnite Treasure Hunters. During each event, there are two separate sessions, offering a couple time slots where you can jump into the action.

Since Treasure Hunters is a mini-season rather than a full one, there are only five Treasure Hour events featured throughout it. There’s one on every Saturday until the season concludes, so you’ve got plenty of time to dive in before it ends.
