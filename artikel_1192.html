Champions League Team of the Week includes Hermoso, Barella and Milla

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There are three Serie A players in the UEFA Champions League Team of the Week, as Roma’s Mario Hermoso, Nicolò Barella at Inter and Como midfielder Luis Milla made it into the ideal XI.

Every week, UEFA’s panel of experts evaluate each game and pick the best performing players for their Team of the Week.

There is plenty of Serie A representation this time around, including two players who got on the scoresheet.

Roma defender Hermoso opened the scoring at Parc des Princes for an exciting 2-2 draw against Paris Saint-Germain, ending the reigning trophy holders’ 100 per cent record.

However, the panel explains he was also chosen for “dominating his duels and making 11 recoveries” against the notorious PSG strikers.

Inter midfielder Barella was included for his performance in the 2-0 victory over VfB Stuttgart, even though the goals were scored by Petar Sucic and Lautaro Martinez.

Como midfielder Milla got the only goal of the game when his side continued their remarkable debut campaign with a 1-0 win over AEK Athens.

It was the 32-year-old’s first ever Champions League goal.

Alisson Becker (Liverpool); Ngoy (Lille), Logan Costa (Villarreal), Hermoso (Roma); Cherki (Man City), Barella (Inter), Milla (Como), Gordon (Barcelona); Bellingham (Real Madrid); Cucho Hernandez (Real Betis), Mbappé (Real Madrid)
