Freebird: Senior Creative Strategy Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Freebird® is a 9-figure DTC brand serving over 2 million customers. We’re hiring a Senior Creative Strategy Lead to develop and scale acquisition creative across multiple products.

You’ll own the creative strategy for your assigned lanes: understanding our customers, finding new angles, writing persuasive hooks and scripts, developing visual formats, and expanding proven winners. Working closely with writers, creators, editors, and Paid Media, you’ll turn customer insights and performance learnings into clear creative direction and ads that drive profitable growth.

We’re looking for a hands-on strategist with strong direct-response fundamentals, visual creative instincts, and experience evaluating Meta creative at scale. You should be able to explain why an ad converts, show how you helped make it work, and identify what to test next. Success means discovering new sources of growth and increasing the profitable spend supported by what’s already working.

The creative portfolio is only as good as the customer understanding behind it. You'll own that understanding for every lane you're assigned.

You'll build new sources of acquisition growth while systematically expanding proven winners.

You'll own how an idea is packaged visually, from the opening hook through the proof that makes it persuasive.

You'll own a living creative territory map for every assigned lane. That map is not a filing system. It's the strategic view that tells us where we are, what we know, and where we need to go.

Strategy that doesn't translate into production-ready direction is not strategy. It's ideas.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
