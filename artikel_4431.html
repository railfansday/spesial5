Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade tier list - Ranking every character

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Updated on November 5th, 2026 - Version: 3.8.4 - Added:  [Effective Range] Mai Zen'in

After multiple delays, Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade was finally released in Japan, and after much speculation, it made its way over to the West as well, allowing fans of Jujutsu Kaisen to try yet another anime-inspired mobile game. To keep our readers up to date on the game’s turn-based combat meta, we’ve created this Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade tier list.

In this tier list, we’ll judge every character’s combat abilities and rank them. All the major characters appearing in the anime are playable, so expect to see some familiar names. However, there are some new faces too. The developers have created a few original characters exclusively for Phantom Parade.

You can find other similar content, including our Dungeon Hunter 6 Lieutenants tier list, One Piece Treasure Cruise tier list since you clearly love anime, and this Fruit Battlegrounds tier list, since that's a Roblox version of one anime that we won't name. But before you dive into any of them, here’s a quick explanation of how this tier list works and what the different tiers represent. Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade tier list explained When making a tier list for Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade, it'd be impossible, and somewhat pointless, to include every character.. So, naturally, this tier list will only rank playable characters. We’re using four tiers to rank them, and here’s a quick explanation of each tier:

With his immense cursed energy and powerful technique, Satoru Gojo has become the strongest sorcerer in the Jujutsu Kaisen universe. And it’s no different here. With skills like Six Eyes and Limitless, Satoru can annihilate enemies in a single blow. That’s something you need on your team during turn-based combat.

Yuji’s best friend, Aoi Todo, has some destructive moves and an even more devastating Ultimate that can deal five-figure damage. Yuji and Aoi make a great pair in the game. Get these two on your team and forget about losing.

Much like Satoru Gojo, the devs also see the potential in Megumi Fushiguro. That’s the only reason a Grade 2 sorcerer has the kind of stats and skills that Megumi has. Although his intelligence won’t be much help in this turn-based RPG, his shikigami and weapon expertise are.

Despite being a non-sorcerer, Maki Zenin has the skills and the combat prowess to hold her own against some strong sorcerers. If you love weapons more than jujutsu, Maki is your fighter.

Toge Inumkai is a one-off. With his cursed speech, he can crush, twist, and explode his enemies just by saying the words. Some of his skill animations are crazy, too. Masamichi Yaga might be the principal of the sorcery school, but he’s not really that strong, especially in the game. However, his ability to take control of corpses makes him a valuable asset.

In the anime, Nobara Kugisaki is a Grade 3 sorcerer. The devs almost copy-pasted her skills, hence tier B. Her weapons are fun to play around with, but she’s better at defense than offense.

Current and former students, a school director, and a sorcerer turned doctor, the C tier is a mixed bag of weak characters. Momo Nishimiya barely has jujutsu, and Kasumi Miwa has no skills apart from a sub-par jujutsu. You won’t get much out of these even after a fully upgraded card.

That’s it for our Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade tier list. If the meta gets shaken up at any point, this tier list will be updated accordingly. Also, note that there are multiple variations of the characters. For instance, there's Hollow Purple version of Satoru Gojo. These variations come and go. That’s why we didn’t use them in our tier list.Stay up to date with what we know so far about the Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade.
