Toyota Tundra Driver Hits 130 MPH in Police Chase, Then Claims He Was Asleep in the Back Seat: Cops

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Police say the driver jumped in the back while the truck was spinning after a PIT maneuver, then tried to convince them he had nothing to do with the chase.

Police officers have heard all sorts of excuses from speeders, from trying to get to work on time to running late for a haircut. Chances are, whatever excuse you try, the police have heard it before. That said, a Toyota Tundra driver that police say recently lead them on a 130-mile-per-hour chase in Florida may have tried the most unique excuse ever: according to Gulf Coast News, the man claimed he was sleeping in the back seat the whole time.

WINK News reports that a Collier County Sheriff's Office Corporal saw a beige Toyota Tundra at about 2:20 a.m. running over a curb while attempting to get to a gas station in Naples, Florida. The Toyota reportedly refused to stop for police; a chase ensued, and authorities claim the truck hit speeds of 130 mph in 45-mile-per-hour zones—impressive velocity, for a Tundra. Deputies reportedly deployed stop sticks, successfully puncturing the Tundra's tires, but that still didn’t end the chase; authorities say it took a PIT maneuver to finally bring the truck to a halt.

Then, it seems, things took an odd turn. According to the arrest report, a passenger told deputies that the driver, identified as Oswaldo Perez-Ramirez, jumped from the driver's seat into the back seat—while the truck was spinning after the PIT maneuver. When officers questioned Perez-Ramirez, he reportedly insisted he hadn't been driving at all; in fact, he allegedly claimed he'd been sleeping in the back seat.

However, that story fell apart when police reviewed the dashcam video, and it showed the driver wearing a white shirt; Perez-Ramirez was also wearing a white shirt when officers removed him from the truck, police say. He was subsequently arrested and faces charges of fleeing and eluding, fleeing and eluding at a high rate of speed, driving without a valid license, failure to stop, and DUI.
