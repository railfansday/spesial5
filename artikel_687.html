Venus and Serena Williams Set to Launch Season 2 of ‘Stockton Street’ Podcast on Dear Media (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tennis superstars Venus and Serena Williams are doubling up again, serving aces as they launch the second season of their lifestyle podcast, “Stockton Street.”

The podcast is named after the street where they grew up in Compton, California. The first episode will premiere on the Dear Media network in Jan. 2027

Speaking with Variety, Venus Williams said, “We are happy that so many people supported us in season one. We found that we got to spend time with each other, where we just sat down and talked. And I think most people don’t get to do that. Serena Williams added, “I feel like Venus and I have so much to say and for so much of our lives, we’ve been motivating people through our actions, our game, tennis and through sport. Now, with all the experiences we’ve been through — good and bad — this podcast is an opportunity to talk about it and learn from it.”

“Stockton Street” also explores the sisters’ personal journey through candid conversations, shared memories and perspectives. Alongside sibling banter, the podcast delves into topics such as opportunities, failures, success and life, with the pair frequently inviting guests from various walks of life to discuss health, wellness and pop culture.

The show’s first season featured guests included Shonda Rhimes, Alexis Ohanian, Mark Cuban, Kevin Hart, Jeanie Buss, Ciara and track stars Gabby Thomas, Melissa Jefferson-Wooden and Sha’Carri Richardson.

Venus Williams teased more unexpected moments between the sisters. She said, “We were telling secrets that we didn’t know — or that we didn’t even know we kept as secrets.”

One upcoming episode will feature a discussion on Serena Williams’ retirement from tennis. “We filmed one episode yesterday, and I told Serena something that I had never told her before about her retirement and how it affected me. There are so many moments where we’ve had challenges; we failed. There are so many moments where we had successes and got through. I think people are looking to be able to hear that from us,” Venus Williams said.

Serena Williams retired from tennis after the 2022 U.S. Open. She is widely considered the GOAT of women’s tennis. With 23 grand slam wins under her belt, she holds the record for most Grand Slam singles titles in the open era.

Earlier this year, Serena Williams made her return to professional tennis, stepping back onto the court after nearly four years of retirement. Meanwhile, Venus Williams — who has won seven Grand Slam singles titles — continues to compete on the WTA Tour.

Looking ahead to season two, Serena Williams shared that the podcast will feature more solo episodes and deeper discussions on important topics. “We’re going to dive into women’s health topics, and important conversations that people don’t normally have.” She added, “We’re bringing more educational things to the podcast. We want to bring awareness to lots of different topics, but no one’s talking about it on a platform that we are so fortunate to have.”

Through “Stockton Street,” Venus and Serena explore where they came from, who they’ve become and what they’re still discovering. Smart, funny, revealing and deeply personal, the podcast tackles themes of ambition, resilience, family and legacy.

“Venus and Serena have shaped sports and culture in ways few people ever will, with influence that extends well beyond the court,” said Michael Bosstick, Founder and CEO of Dear Media. “’Stockton Street’ creates space for conversations you don’t often get to hear, bringing together two extraordinary perspectives and some of the most compelling voices of our time. We’re thrilled to welcome the show to Dear Media for its next chapter.”

The season will consist of 24 episodes with new installments dropping weekly on all major podcast platforms. Dear Media, Isha Price, Jessica Steindorff and Nine Two Six Media serve as producers.
