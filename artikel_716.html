See Kristen Stewart’s Out of This World Transformation into Sally Ride

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Kristen Stewart looked straight out of the 1980s as she transformed into famed NASA astronaut Sally Ride on the set of the upcoming limited series The Challenger.

Where the hell has Kristen Stewart been, loca?

Well, in short, outer space. For the Twilight actress’ latest role as groundbreaking NASA astronaut Sally Ride—a.k.a. the first American woman to fly in space—in The Challenger, she fully changed up her look, rocking a look straight out of the ‘80s.

Stewart donned a brunette bob on the Georgia set of the Prime Video limited series Nov. 24, completing her look with a 1980s style shirt—tied with a bow at the collar—and a dark pair of bottoms. The 36-year-old kept warm on set with a fluffy zip-up sweater as she sipped on a Diet Coke between takes.

The role marks the Oscar nominee’s first regular TV role, with her also serving as a producer on the series alongside wife Dylan Meyers through their production company Nevermind Pictures.

And for Stewart, she couldn’t believe she had the chance to bring Ride’s story to life.

“I’m shaking in boots, but I get to play Sally Ride,” she shared with Collider in December. “The story is incredible and the team is just so inspired and trustworthy and I think I'm in the safest hands.”

The limited series is inspired by Meredith E. Bagby’s 2023 book The New Guys, which chronicled the story of Ride and her fellow astronauts who all broke barriers as NASA’s 1978 astronaut class.

In addition to being the first American woman—and third woman—to fly in space in 1983, Ride also served on the committees that investigated the explosions of the Space Shuttle Challenger in 1986 and the Space Shuttle Columbia in 2003, making her the only person to participate in both.

While she was married to fellow astronaut Steven Hawley from 1982 to 1987, Ride was in a long term relationship with women’s tennis star Tam O’Shaughnessy from 1985 until her death in 2012 from pancreatic cancer at the age of 61. However, Ride hid the fact that she was part of the LGBTQ community during her lifetime, with the news only coming out when O’Shaughnessy was noted to have been her partner of 27 years in her obituary.

Following her death, O’Shaughnessy spoke about the couple’s time together, sharing that while Ride never officially came out to her family, they were all still very accepting of the couple’s relationship.

“Sally never verbally, openly told her mother, her father, or her sister, Bear, that she was gay and that we were a couple. But Sally never hid it from them,” she explained to the Windy City Times in 2013, “you know, I went everywhere with Sally, and she went everywhere with me.”

“Her parents are really smart—especially her mother and her sister,” the former tennis pro added, “they knew a long time ago that we were a couple.”
