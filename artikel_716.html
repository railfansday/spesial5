Why Hoda Kotb Is "Relieved" by Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponement

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Hoda Kotb weighed in after NBC confirmed that the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade would be postponed to Nov. 28: “We waited 100 years for this parade, we’re gonna wait two more days.”

Hoda Kotb knows that sometimes stuffing happens.

After the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed for the first time in its 100-year history, the Joy 101 founder—who was set to host the event alongside Savannah Guthrie and Al Roker—shared why she thinks pushing the event to Nov. 28 was for the best.

“It’s OK,” Kotb said on the November 25 episode of Late Night With Seth Meyers. “We waited 100 years for this parade, we’re gonna wait two more days. We’re gonna have it on Saturday. It’s gonna be a beautiful, sunsplash day.”

As the 62-year-old noted, the date change came about after it was clear the forecast on Thanksgiving Day “was not gonna be fun.”

“The winds were like tropical storm force,” she explained. “There are a bunch of kids who were marching and people with the balloons.”

But Kotb—mom to daughters Haley Joy, 9, and Hope Catherine, 7, with ex Joel Schiffman—is choosing the see the gravy boat half full.

“The good news is, to me, I’m relieved,” the Today alum said. “Saturday’s gonna be amazing. Everybody’s still hanging out. We’re gonna watch it anyway.”

In lieu of the scheduled program, NBC will re-air last year's show at 8 a.m. in all time zones, a spokesperson from the network told E! News in a Nov. 25 statement. And the change in plans won’t rain on Kotb’s parade.

As she put it, “We’re gonna watch an old parade and you won’t know the difference, OK? It’s gonna be spectacular.”

And since the team behind the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has “been working tirelessly for a year-plus” to perfect the event, Kotb teased that it'll be worth the wait.

“The musical acts are incredible,” she said. “It’s gonna be out of this world.”

Kotb’s comments come hours after an NBC spokesperson confirmed to E! News that the beloved event would be postponed due to weather.

“Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28,” the spokesperson shared in a Nov. 25 statement. “More details to follow.”

And while the postponement marks the first in the parade’s 100-year history, they emphasized that the show will go on.

“Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year,” a spokesperson told MSNOW in a statement. “We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”
