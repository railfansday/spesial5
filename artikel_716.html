John Christ, Guitarist With Danzig, Dies at 61

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

"He was an impressively brilliant guitarist and a wonderfully kind man," reads a tribute from Soundgarden.

John Christ, the veteran electric guitarist who wielded the axe for Danzig and Samhain and was widely recognized by the metal community as one of the best to do it, has passed away following a battle with poor health. He was 61. Explore Billboard Danzig See latest videos, charts and news

“It is with a heavy heart that we must inform you of the peaceful passing of John Christ on November 15, 2026,” reads a statement posted on Danzig’s socials. Born John Wolfgang Knoll, in Baltimore, Maryland, the youngest and only boy of five kids, the future rocker got a relatively traditional music education, initially on the piano before picking up the acoustic guitar, and then plugged in at age 13. Both his parents were accomplished musicians.

In the late ‘80s he dropped out of college, relocated to New Jersey and joined forces with ex-Misfits frontman Glenn Danzig on the black-as-night rock band Samhain, which would later become Danzig. Christ auditioned for the spot, and caught the attention of Danzig and Rick Rubin, who was scouting acts for his record label, Def Jam Recordings, with a performance that was both brutal and bloody, thanks to strumming finger gashed on the strings.

The anecdote is retold on Christ’s biography. “Accidentally brushing up against Glenn, he left five bloody lines on his shoulder. Rubin thought this was awesome—Danzig did not agree—he thought Christ was on speed,” it reads. When he didn’t receive an immediate callback, he pushed the process by leaving guitar riffs on Danzig’s answering machine. It was the stuff Danzig couldn’t ignore.

Christ would often catch the attention with his playing, his bluesy hard-rock sound with frequent pinch harmonics earning him a spot in Guitar World’s list of “The 100 Greatest Metal Guitarists,” coming in at No. 63.

As a member of Danzig from 1987 to 1995, Christ worked on four studio albums and one EP—Danzig (1988), Danzig II: Lucifuge (1990), Danzig III: How the Gods Kill (1992), Thrall-Demonsweatlive (1993), Danzig 4P (1994), and featured on Live on the Black Hand Side (2001) and The Lost Tracks of Danzig (2007). Christ and Danzig graced the charts with “Mother,” which peaked at No. 43 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1994, and with a string of albums. All told, 14 Danzig titles have impacted the Billboard 200, including a best of No. 24 for Danzig III: How The Gods Kill.

Christ also contributed to the recording of the final Samhain record, Final Descent, its release delayed until 1990, and he’d go on to share the stage with the likes of Metallica, Black Sabbath, Slayer, Suicidal Tendencies, Soundgarden, White Zombie, Type O Negative, Marilyn Manson, Korn, and Corrosion of Conformity.

In a post from early September, Christ revealed that he’d been focusing on a medical issue that “has been kicking my butt and I am in the process of recuperating.”

Without giving away further details, he explained he’d been advised to “take a few months off to rest up and let my body get back to normal or else they said they will send Glenn to my house to babysit—that is motivation enough to get better.”

The heavy rock and metal community is remembering Christ as a talent, and a fine person.

“Soundgarden are very saddened to learn of the passing of John Christ,” reads a statement from the band. “He was an impressively brilliant guitarist and a wonderfully kind man. John had played with Samhain and was the original guitarist for Danzig when Soundgarden was invited to join them on tour during late summer of 1990.”
