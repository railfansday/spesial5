Tendo Nagenda Named President of Ryan Coogler’s Proximity Media

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Proximity heads Coogler, Zinzi Coogler and Sev Ohanian made today‘s announcement.

Tendo Nagenda, the veteran Hollywood exec and alum of Netflix and Disney, has been named the president of Proximity Media, the outfit founded by Ryan Coogler, Zinzi Coogler and Sev Ohanian.

In the new role, Nagenda, who will begin in his post on Nov. 30, will oversee all aspects of creative development and production across the company’s film, television and nonfiction divisions, reporting directly to the founders.

The founders of Proximity Media said in a joint statement: “As Proximity continues to grow across film, television and nonfiction, we’re excited to have his creative instincts and experience helping guide this next chapter. Nagenda previously served as VP of original film at Netflix, where he oversaw a slate that included Da 5 Bloods, The Old Guard, Ma Rainey’s Black Bottom, The Harder They Fall and Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery. Before Netflix, he was an exec at Walt Disney Studios, overseeing films across the studio’s live-action slate, including Beauty and the Beast, Cinderella and Mulan.

After Netflix, Nagenda founded 10 by Ten Entertainment, and produced the feature The Book of Clarence, released theatrically by Sony Pictures in January 2024.

“I’m honored and excited to join Ryan, Zinzi, Sev and the entire Proximity team,” said Nagenda. “Their commitment to ambitious, meaningful storytelling reflects everything I’ve always believed in, and I look forward to building on what they’ve created, collaborating with extraordinary filmmakers and creators, and bringing compelling new stories to audiences around the world.”

Proximity‘s upcoming slate includes The X-Files for Hulu, starring Danielle Deadwyler and Himesh Patel.
