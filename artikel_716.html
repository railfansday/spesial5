What Is Airing on NBC Instead of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade was interrupted for the fourth time since its debut in 1924, NBC has shared what will be broadcast in its place for 2026.

While it's too late for a Thanksgiving miracle, NBC does have a back-up plan for the Macy's Parade.

Although the New York weather forecast is in fact raining on its parade—with severe wind and rain forcing the 2026 Macy's Day Parade to move from Thursday to Saturday—the network revealed what will air in its place on Nov. 26.

In lieu of the scheduled program, NBC will re-air last year's show at 8 a.m. in all time zones, a spokesperson from the network told E! News in a Nov. 25 statement. And lucky for viewers, given the unforgettable moments of 2025, the second broadcast is sure to make for spectacular leftovers.

The affair began with Wicked star Cynthia Erivo singing a rendition of "Feeling Good" by Nina Simone. From there, the day included performances by Busta Rhymes, Teyana Taylor and Mr. Fantasy—who fans believe is the stage persona of Riverdale actor KJ Apa. And the parade was not complete without an appearance by the Radio City Rockettes—who have been a highlight of the show since 1957—as well as Santa Claus.

As news broke that fans whose Thanksgiving tradition involves spending the afternoon with anchors Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker should expect something different this year, the broadcaster explained the decision was made in the interest of safety.

"Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided," the NBC representative said. "In the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28. More details to follow."

And due to the pivot, some performers may not stay on the 2026 lineup, according to NBC News, as shared by reporter Jesse Kirsch.

"The parade on Saturday may not include everyone who was supposed to be in it," Kirsch said. "I just spoke with the director of one of the bands. He told me they've got flights, they've got hotel rooms that are set in stone, and it's unclear if they would be able to rearrange things."
