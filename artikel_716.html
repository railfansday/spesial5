Hollywood’s Biggest Watch Nerds Are Obsessing Over These Indie Brands

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

From Keanu Reeves to Samuel L. Jackson to Ryan Reynolds, in-the-know insiders favor niche watchmakers that surprise and spark conversations.

Watchmaker Denis Flageollet founded De Bethune in 2002 with art collector David Zanetta. The DB25 Digitale reimagines digital time, with the hour individually displayed above minutes on a rotating disc, while a spherical moon-phase function is positioned in the center of a dial crafted in blued titanium, all set in a 42mm titanium case. At The 1916 Company, Manhattan Beach.

Watchmaker Felix Baumgartner and chief designer Martin Frei co-founded Urwerk in 1997, naming the brand for the Mesopotamian city of Ur, where Sumerians pioneered timekeeping. The UR-300 Time Quantum employs a sculptural approach to time via a T-shape metal frame, on which numbers rotate and rearrange to display hours and minutes. At Urwerk USA, Beverly Hills. A legend in watch circles, Jean-Claude Biver launched his eponymous brand in 2023 — his latest, the 38mm Biver AB, blends brushed and polished steel, while the dial is crafted of solid 18-karat gold beneath a dark gray treatment. Exclusively at Material Good, New York City.

Named after watchmaker François Czapek, who founded his firm in 1845, the Geneva-based brand was revived in 2015 to honor his legacy. The latest model, the 40.5mm Antarctique Tourbillon Victory Green, got its start as a bespoke order and now is available in a limited series of 10 numbered pieces, each one produced on demand. At Cellini Jewelers, New York.

Master watch restorer Michel Parmigiani founded his firm in Fleurier, Switzerland, in 1996. This month, he won the Chronograph Prize at the 2026 Grand Prix d’Horlogerie de Genève, the Oscars of watchmaking, for the Tonda PF Chronographe Mystérieux — its styling is similar to this Toric Petite Seconde Platinum Morning Blue, which debuted in October. At Feldmar Watch Company, Los Angeles.

Geneva’s MB&F debuted in 2005 as a concept house, in which founder Maximilian Büsser would partner with independent watchmakers and artisans. Limited to 33 pieces, the LM1 EVO offers two separate time displays and an oversized flying balance wheel suspended above the movement. At MB&F Lab at Westime, Beverly Hills.

Industrial designer Benoît Mintiens founded Ressence in 2010 in Antwerp, Belgium, crafting pieces that tell time using a system of orbiting, rotating discs. Weighing just 82 grams, the Type 10 DN is seamlessly crafted of two independent sections, allowing the movement to function while the display above is filled with oil. At Feldmar Watch Company, Los Angeles.

Heinrich Moser founded his namesake brand in 1828; 174 years later, his great-grandson relaunched it in northern Switzerland. This Heritage Centre Seconds Espresso Fumé honors the early 20th century moment when wristwatches became the preferred timekeeping device over pocket watches. At Westime, Rodeo Drive.
