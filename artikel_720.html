Players Championship Finals schedule: Price eyes back-to-back major titles

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Gerwyn Price looks to back up his Grand Slam of Darts win by claiming a second straight major title; former champions Luke Humphries and Michael van Gerwen also among the 64-player field in Minehead, but world No 1 and defending champion Luke Littler did not qualify

The 2026 Ladbrokes Players Championship Finals sees 64 players competing to win the PDC's last major event before the World Darts Championship gets under way in December.

The tournament - which has a newly increased prize fund of £750,000 - features the top 64 ranked players following the conclusion of the 34-event Players Championship season, and takes place at Butlin's Minehead Resort from November 27-29.

Gerwyn Price, recently crowned Grand Slam of Darts champion for a fourth time, opens his bid for back-to-back major titles against Sebastian Bialecki on Friday, with record seven-time winner Michael van Gerwen in action against Connor Scutt and Luke Humphries - triumphant in 2023 and 2024 - going up against Darryl Pilgrim.

Top seed Wessel Nijman has been handed a tough opener against exciting youngster Henry Coates, while Beau Greaves makes her Players Championship Finals debut against Christian Kist.

World No 1 and defending champion Luke Littler did not qualify for the Finals.

Friday's first round winners advance to Saturday's double session, with the second round scheduled for the afternoon and round three in the evening.

The tournament concludes on Sunday November 29, with the afternoon's quarter-finals followed by the semi-finals and final in a decisive evening session that also includes the Winmau World Youth Championship final between Cam Crabtree and Jan Schmidt.
