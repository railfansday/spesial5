‘Terminal List’ Prequel Series ‘Cry Havoc’ in Development at Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Amazon Prime Video is doubling down on “The Terminal List” franchise, with the streamer developing a series based on Jack Carr’s prequel novel “Cry Havoc.”

“The Terminal List” is based on Carr’s James Reece novels, while “Cry Havoc” follows Reece’s father Tom Reece during the latter’s time as a Navy SEAL during the Vietnam war. The character of Tom Reece was recently introduced in Season 2 of “The Terminal List,” with David Keeley playing the veteran SEAL in four episodes of the series. “The Terminal List” already has two shows under its umbrella. Including the original series, which stars Chris Pratt as James Reece, Amazon aired the prequel “The Terminal List: Dark Wolf” starring Taylor Kitsch in 2025, with Kitsch reprising the role of CIA operative and former SEAL Ben Edwards. “The Terminal List” originally debuted in 2022, with Season 2 debuting on Oct. 21, 2026. It was recently reported that Pratt would return in “Savage Son,” also based on a James Reece novel, though whether it will serve as a third season of “The Terminal List” or a spinoff project remains to be seen.

The official logline for “Cry Havoc” states:

“‘Cry Havoc’ takes place in 1968 Vietnam and follows Jack Carr’s youngest protagonist, Tom Reece, a brash Navy SEAL assigned to the top secret MACV-SOG unit which ran harrowing cross-border operations in Laos. When Tom’s Recon Team comes across plans for the upcoming Tet Offensive, it unleashes a chain of events that pulls the young warrior out of the jungles and into the shadows of Cold War espionage.”

The show is being developed for television by the team behind “The Terminal List.” David Digilio, Max Adams, Jared Shaw, and Carr are all developing the show, with Adams and Digilio writing the pilot script. All are executive producers on “Cry Havoc,” with Digilio set to serve as showrunner. Digilio developed both “The Terminal List” and the prequel series “The Terminal List: Dark Wolf” and serves as showrunner on both shows.

As with “The Terminal List,” Antoine Fuqua executive produces with Kat Samick for Fuqua Films, with Pratt executive producing via Indivisible. MRC and Amazon MGM Studios produce.
