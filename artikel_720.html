Stevie Nicks Predicted the Midterm Blue Wave in a Moving Poem: ‘The Light Creates a Circle of Angels’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Fleetwood Mac singer said she wrote "Morning Symphony" three weeks before Tuesday's massive rebuke of the Trump agenda.

While many Americans watched Tuesday’s midterm election results with either great anticipation or resigned dread, Stevie Nicks was not sweating the final tally at all because she already knew how the night would end.

The Fleetwood Mac singer and solo star revealed in a post on X that she wrote a poem on Oct. 18 as a kind of wish into the universe. “It was sort of like a little prayer for the coming elections,” she said. “I really believed that what has happened was going to happen; it just had to. Good almost wins … I didn’t have time to get it out before the election — so here it is. That’s the dream …”

The dream came true, as Nicks — who endorsed former Democratic Vice President Kamala Harris in the 2024 election — and the rest of the nation watched the electorate soundly rebuke the second Trump term agenda by giving Democrats a historic blue wave victory that has swept them into the majority in both the House and Senate, in addition to flipping five governorships and racking up massive wins on the local, state and national level.

In the poem titled “Morning Symphony,” Nicks muses on the beauty of early mornings and her dog Lily, her sadness about what is going on in the country, ending with a final, flickering hope that the light will win out over darkness.

I get up and walk to the window and I don’t sleep —

Back in August, Nicks, 78, made a surprise appearance at Olivia Rodrigo’s Daisy Chain Fields festival where they performed the Fleetwood Mac classic “Landslide.” The two women also contributed to the soundtrack of Practical Magic 2, which featured “Landslide” and Rodrigo’s “The Cure.”
