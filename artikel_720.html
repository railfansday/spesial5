Lewis-Potter scores twice as Brentford thump four past 10-man Everton

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Brentford vs Everton. Premier League.

Report and free match highlights from the Premier League clash between Brentford and Everton at the Gtech Community Stadium on Monday night; Keane Lewis-Potter scores twice and James Tarkowski sent off as Bees beat Toffees.

Keane Lewis-Potter’s first-half double set Brentford on their way to a comprehensive 4-0 Premier League win over 10-man Everton.

For David Moyes it was a record-setting night to forget. His 593rd game in charge of Everton, more than any other Toffees manager, ended in misery, as James Tarkowski's red card at 2-0 down left them with no hope of a comeback at the Gtech Community Stadium on Monday night.

Vitaly Janelt added a third for Brentford, while Fabio Carvalho came off the bench to score a fourth, as they moved up to sixth in the table, a point above Everton who slipped to ninth.

It was a deserved breakthrough for Brentford midway through the first half. A lovely touch from Jaidon Anthony teed up Lewis-Potter, who saw his strike deflect past Jordan Pickford.

Four minutes later it was 2-0. This time it came from a free-kick as the ball fell to Lewis-Potter. His first effort was blocked, but he got a second bite at it, and again a deflection from his strike took the ball away from the Everton goalkeeper.

It got worse for the Toffees before the break, too. Tarkowski was first given a yellow card for his follow-through challenge on Yehor Yarmolyuk, before a VAR check saw the referee upgrade it to red.

Moyes introduced Jack Grealish at the start of the second half, his first appearance since being dropped from the squad last month, but it would make minimal difference.

Janelt's rocket on 67 minutes added a Brentford third, as he picked out the top corner from 25 yards, while substitute Carvalho prodded in from a corner in stoppage time to add the icing to the cake.

Brentford boss Keith Andrews to Sky Sports:

"[It ranks] really high. It will probably go under the radar because of the sending off but I thought 11 vs 11 we were outstanding.

"We played with bravery, tempo, energy and desire.

"The expectation levels at this club and maybe in the outside world have changed.

"The fact regardless of the performance or result, we win to go sixth in the league and there is still a little bit swirling in the air: 'we're a little bit unhappy' or 'should they be doing a little bit better'.

"Maybe people need to get a little bit real. I'm really happy with our group and our football club."
