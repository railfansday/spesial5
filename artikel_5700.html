Voters Back Abortion Rights and Transgender Restrictions in Some States

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

— Measures provided a political undercard for a high-profile midterm election

Abortion-rights advocates notched victories in several states Tuesday, and voters in some states backed transgender restrictions as they decided 145 ballot questions in more than three dozen states.

The ballot measures provided a political undercard for a high-profile midterm election in which Democrats took control of Congress and gained power in state legislatures and gubernatorial offices.

Voters defeated a proposed amendment to Missouri's constitution that would have reimposed restrictions on most abortions. The vote Tuesday essentially reaffirmed voters' decision from 2 years ago, when they became the first in the nation to undo an existing abortion ban by approving an abortion-rights amendment.

The Republican-led Legislature then referred a new, less restrictive version of an abortion ban to this year's ballot. The measure would have allowed abortions only for medical emergencies and fetal anomalies, or in cases of rape or incest up to 12 weeks of pregnancy. It also would have amended the constitution to prohibit certain medical procedures for transgender minors.

Abortion-rights advocates, led by national progressive groups, heavily outspent abortion foes in a successful campaign to defeat the Missouri ballot measure.

In Virginia, a voter-approved measure creates a "fundamental right to reproductive freedom" in the state constitution, ensuring that abortions can occur during the first two trimesters of pregnancy. The amendment also creates a right to contraception and fertility care, among other things.

An amendment approved in Nevada creates a fundamental right to abortion until fetal viability, which is generally considered after 21 weeks of pregnancy. The amendment won initial voter approval in 2024 but needed a second vote this year to add it to the constitution.

Since the U.S. Supreme Court overturned a nationwide right to abortion in 2022, voters in more than one-fifth of the states have passed ballot measures protecting abortion rights.

But voters in Republican-leaning Idaho on Tuesday defeated a ballot measure that would have created abortion rights in state law. Current Idaho law bans abortions unless necessary to save the life of a pregnant woman, or during the first trimester of pregnancy in cases where rape or incest has been reported to law enforcement.

Abortion-rights measures failed 2 years ago in Florida and South Dakota.

Transgender Restrictions Pass in Some States

Nebraska voters approved an amendment to add existing transgender medical restrictions to the state constitution. And in Arizona, voters passed a measure that extends public school sports restrictions to private schools.

Voters in Colorado and Washington also were considering measures restricting transgender athletes or medical procedures. That comes as Trump's administration has been pushing for restrictions impacting transgender people, especially youth.

Most Republican-led states already bar transgender women and girls from female school sports teams. In the Democratic-led states of Colorado and Washington, the measures were placed on the ballot by citizen initiatives. A second ballot question in Colorado would ban some transgender medical treatments for minors.
