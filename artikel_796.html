AJ McLean & Rochelle Karidis Rekindle Romance 3 Years After Separation

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Backstreet Boys' AJ McLean and his wife Rochelle Karidis have reconciled three years after announcing their separation, multiple outlets confirmed.

AJ McLean is filling the shape of his heart.

Three years after the Backstreet Boys member and his wife Rochelle Karidis announced they were separating, multiple outlets confirmed the two have officially rekindled their romance.

McLean himself also seemingly confirmed the couple were back together as he reflected on what Karidis—with whom he tied the knot in 2011—loves most about him.

"I think she loves my heart, my generosity, my kindness, my ability to be comforting and loving,” the 48-year-old shared with Fatherly in an interview posted to Instagram Nov. 18. “We went through some tough stuff. We separated, we were living separately, and we both now can look back and reflect on what needed to happen.”

“There are no mistakes,” he added. “I believe everything happens exactly how it's meant to be.”

E! News has reached out to the couple’s reps for comment but has not heard back.

McLean and Karidis—who share daughters Elliott, 13, and Lyric, 9—announced their separation in March 2023, telling E! News in a statement at the time, “Marriage is hard, but worth it. We have mutually decided to separate temporarily to work on ourselves, and on our marriage with the hope of building a stronger future."

As they explained, they were hoping to “come back together” larger than life when it felt right. And as McLean noted later that year, the two were doing all they could to make that dream a reality.

"We talk every day, we are spending more time together," he shared on the Sex, Lies, and Spray Tans podcast. "We just have to rebuild something that was never there from the beginning."

Although, they didn’t always believe there was a way they’d come back together. At the start of 2024, McLean and Karidis shared they’d decided to end their marriage.

"It is with deep love and respect that we have made this decision," they wrote in a joint Instagram post. "Our focus now is moving forward in the healthiest possible way with friendship and coparenting our girls at the forefront of this next chapter."

But since the split, the “As Long as You Love Me” singer was candid about how he was trying to be a better man to help rebuild their relationship.

"There is such a love between the two of us, and we’ve been through f--king hell and back,” he admitted to Us Weekly in 2025. “I have put this woman through the damn wringer, and the fact that she’s still here, either she’s crazy or she loves me or a little bit of both."

And they had been effusive with their praise of each other on social media, showing fans the meaning of their love as Karidis shared how proud she was of McLean as he kicked off his Better Man tour this year.

“You were incredible tonight,” the 45-year-old posted on Instagram in October. “You deserve all the wonderful things coming your way @aj_mclean love you.”
