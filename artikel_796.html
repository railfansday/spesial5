Dubai Darts Masters returns to PDC calendar in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The World Series of Darts will return to Dubai for the 2027 season; the Dubai Darts Masters has not been played since 2017; Gary Anderson was the last player to win the title in Dubai; watch the World Championship live on Sky Sports

The World Series of Darts will return to Dubai in 2027, with the Dubai Darts Masters set to be played for the first time in 10 years.

Dubai was one of the tour's original destinations and had previously hosted a World Series event between 2013 and 2017.

The World Series will kick off its 2027 season in Bahrain on January 15, before heading to the United Arab Emirates for the Dubai Darts Masters.

The tournament will see eight of the world's top PDC players battle it out in a 16-player knockout event between January 22 and 23 at the Coca-Cola Arena.

Scotland's Gary Anderson successfully defended his Dubai Darts Masters title when it was last held in 2017, beating Michael van Gerwen 11-7 in the final.

PDC chief executive Matt Porter said: "We're delighted to be taking the World Series of Darts back to Dubai.

"The city has a great history with the series, and Coca-Cola Arena will provide a brilliant setting for two days of top-class darts.

"We're looking forward to welcoming the world's best players and fans from across the region."

Eight first-round matches will be played on the opening night, before the knockout stages and the final are played on day two.

World Series ranking points will be up for grabs, as players look to secure their spots at the season-ending Finals in Amsterdam.

Ross Smith is out to defend his title after he thrashed Gerwyn Price 11-5 to become the 2026 World Series of Darts Finals champion in September, winning his second PDC major.

Sky Sports remains the home of darts with the World Championship (December 11-January 3) live. Stream darts and more through NOW with no contract.
