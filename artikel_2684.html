Broncos, TV legend Tom Jackson dies at 75

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tom Jackson, a 14-year veteran with the Broncos and a longtime fixture on ESPN’s NFL programming, has died. He was 75.

Jackson was a fourth-round pick of the Broncos in 1973. He became a full-time starter in his second season. He started a run of three straight Pro Bowl berths in 1977, the same year the Broncos made their first Super Bowl appearance.

Jackson played until 1986. The next year, he debuted on ESPN. He worked on the network’s Sunday NFL Countdown show and the must-see NFL Primetime, which featured extended highlights packages years before the best plays could be instantly seen on social media.

“All of us are stunned,” Jackson’s long-time on-air partner, Chris Berman, said in a statement. “I’m stunned, we’re stunned and you’re stunned. We’ve lost one of the best human beings ever to walk the planet, Tom Jackson. I was fortunate to work side by side with Tom for 30 years, primarily on NFL Primetime and Sunday NFL Countdown. It was a labor of love for our whole team, both in front of the camera and behind it. We always hoped our viewers enjoyed the shows as much as we did bringing it to them every week.”

Jackson is a member of the Broncos’ Ring of Fame, the franchise’s 50th anniversary team, and the Louisville Ring of Honor. He received the Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award from the Hall of Fame in 2015.

The recent changes to the Pro Football Hall of Fame’s rules could make Jackson a candidate for the expanded “contributor” category, given that it allows former players to enhance their standing with other contributions to the game. Few have combined an impactful and prolonged playing career with an impactful and prolonged broadcasting career.

We extend our condolences to Jackson’s friends, family, teammates, and colleagues.
