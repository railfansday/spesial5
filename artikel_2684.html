Sean Payton suspends RB J.K. Dobbins for Friday's game vs. Steelers due to 'conduct detrimental to the team'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Denver Broncos head coach Sean Payton told reporters Tuesday that he is suspending veteran running back J.K. Dobbins for Friday's game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Payton said it was due to "conduct detrimental to the team," and the head coach did not elaborate.

Dobbins is in his second season with the Broncos. He began his career with the Baltimore Ravens and spent the 2024 season with the Los Angeles Chargers before joining Denver last year.

He is currently the team's leading rusher with 324 yards and has three touchdowns on 99 carries.

The veteran running back played in 10 games last year, finishing with 772 yards and four touchdowns. Dobbins had a rookie in the backfield with him in RJ Harvey last year, and Denver brought in another this year in Jonah Coleman.

Dobbins has said he is happy to do whatever he can to contribute to the team, but last week Coleman had his breakout game with 111 yards and three touchdowns in Denver's 38-24 win over Las Vegas.

While Coleman had his best game as a rookie, Dobbins had only one touch. With Payton not elaborating, it is unclear whether the conduct detrimental to the team is related to his reduced role in the offense or coincidental.

Coleman has quickly taken over as the lead back, with 302 yards and six touchdowns on 71 carries in six games.

Denver is the second-best team in the AFC, with an 8-2 record. The Broncos, who split with the Kansas City Chiefs this season, currently hold the tiebreaker over their AFC West rivals.

The Broncos will take on the Steelers on the road on Black Friday. With no Dobbins, Coleman is expected to lead the backfield. Harvey and fellow running back Tyler Badie are currently on IR.

Jaret Patterson and/or Cody Schrader will likely join Coleman in the backfield; both are currently on the practice squad and would need to be signed or elevated.
