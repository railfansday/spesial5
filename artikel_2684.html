NFL Punishes Three Falcons Players for Incidents During Chiefs Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The NFL has fined three Atlanta Falcons players for incidents during the Week 10 game against the Kansas City Chiefs.

This weekend, the NFL announced that it has fined DL Brandon Dorlus, DB DeMarcco Hellams, and LB Harold Perkins. Dorlus was fined $7,114 twice for separate incidents (taunting and a late hit); Hellams was fined $6,509 for a hit on a defenseless player, and Perkins was fined $5,203 for use of the helmet.

Dorlus' incidents occurred in the fourth quarter. The first happened at 11:03 when he hit Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes during a second-down play. The second happened with 5:18 remaining after Mahomes threw an incomplete pass. Dorlus was penalized for the hit on Mahomes but not for taunting.

The incidents involving Hellams and Perkins happened in the second quarter. The officials did not penalize either player during the game.

The Falcons lost to the Chiefs 35-13 and are now 3-7. They did not have a game this weekend, as they were one of six teams that were on a bye.

While Atlanta has struggled this season, the team still has a chance to make the playoffs. The Falcons are in last place in the NFC South but just two games behind the Carolina Panthers, who lead the division.

If the Falcons miss the playoffs, it would be the ninth consecutive season they would not participate in the postseason. It would be the longest playoff drought since 1983-1990.

"I've never been in a situation where we're one game out of the division with that record," Falcons WR Jahan Dotson said last week, per Daniel Flick of the Atlanta Journal-Constitution. "But we are. That's the circumstances that we're dealt with right now. So, we have a perfect opportunity to come out of this bye week and make our mind up as to what we want to do as a football team.

"I think we have the guys in this locker room to make this thing right, finish the season strong, and find ourselves in the postseason."

After finishing 8-9 last season, the Falcons made changes to the coaching staff and front office. Kevin Stefanski was hired as head coach, and Ian Cunningham was brought in to be the general manager. The Falcons also created a new position, president of football, and former Falcons QB and 2016 NFL MVP Matt Ryan was hired for the role.
