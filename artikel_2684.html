Game Preview: San Antonio Spurs vs Portland Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After being stopped cold at 12 wins during their 1st win streak of the season, the Spurs have promptly started up another one, chugging right along even in the (one game) absence of their resident superstar.

Whether or not it continues will be decided by a challenging little three-game stretch against playoff teams, starting with last postseason's 1st round matchup, the Portland Trail Blazers.

The Spurs erupted from long-range against Portland in their first matchup this season, but past experience tells that these Blazers are far from pushovers, and are deeper than they were when they were giving the Spurs some degree of trouble in the playoffs. (Or, at least, were, until the injury bug bit them)

It seems unlikely that a Portland team ranking 11th in three-point percentage and 2nd in made threes will shoot 26% from downtown again, and the fact that this is an NBA Cup group stage may add a little zest to the quality of regular season effort in this one.

Still, the Spurs are 9-1 in their last 10 games, and if the Trail Blazers get absolutely manhandled on the boards again, they might not be able to get up enough shots anyway, having fallen eight threes shy of their season average as the Spurs held a 13 rebound advantage on the evening (6 of which were offensive rebounds).

Portland is perfectly capable of handling themselves in that arena, though, as a big team averaging just three rebounds less than the Spurs per game (which is, hilariously, the difference between 1st and 11th in rebounds).

One of the game's biggest questions will be the status of Victor Wembanyama, obviously, but De'Aaron Fox's status will also be a low-key point of interest (after being tripped by Jeremiah Fears against the Pelicans), as Portland's guard play is one of their most significant weaknesses.

Portland ranks in the bottom 20 in assists (22nd), turnovers given up (21st), turnover percentage (22nd), and assist-to-turnover ratio (22nd), all areas the Spurs rank inside the Top 5 in (3rd, 3rd, 3rd, and 1st).

If Wembanyama takes the court, this should be a win for the Spurs, barring another cold spell from three from San Antonio, or a scalding performance from Portland.

But even without Wembanyama, the Spurs might be able to wreak real havoc on the Trail Blazers' front-court with the presence of a healthy Fox.

Minus their availability, things might get a little sticky for the Spurs against a team deep enough for Jrue Holiday to be getting 6th Man of the Year consideration already. Though not if Stephon Castle has his say in the matter.

Here's hoping the Spurs get a little more heath luck than the bit they've already had.

Spurs Injuries: Victor Wembanyama: Questionable (Back), De'Aaron Fox: Questionable (Ankle), Tobias Harris: Out (Calf), Jayden Quaintance: Out (Knee), Dylan Harper: Out (Hip)

Trail Blazers Injuries: Ja Morant: Out (Leg), Shaedon Sharpe: Out (Knee), Chris Youngblood: Questionable (Heel), Vit Krejci: Out (Ankle)
