If you were wondering, and I was, the NHL record for longest winless streak to start a season is 15 in a row by the 1943-44 New York Rangers. They lost 11 straight, tied a game, then lost three more before knocking off the visiting Boston Bruins.

So the Kings (0-4-2) have a long way to get there and haven't even tied the franchise record yet, as the 1984-85 club was winless in its first nine. L.A. still made the playoffs that year. This year's club is still priced +100 for extra hockey with No only slightly favored at -125. The updated season points total is 90.5 – remember, 84 regular-season games now. The Kings' preseason points total was 95.5, so the sportsbooks haven't given up on them yet, nor should they. Still too talented of a roster.

The goaltending has been problem No. 1, with the duo of Darcy Kuemper and Anton Forsberg combining for a 4.17 goals-against average that is third-worst in the NHL and an .861 save percentage that is second-to-last. The penalty kill ranks 31st at 61.9%. Perhaps a trade for Winnipeg holdout Connor Hellebuyck?

But the offense needs help too, putting up just 2.00 goals per game (31st) and converting 14.3% of the time on the power play (26th). L.A. scored eight total goals in its first two games and has scored exactly one in the next four. That's hard to do.

Injuries haven't helped, with the likes of Kevin Fiala, Alex Turcotte and Mats Zuccarello all on injured reserve. Coach Peter Laviolette said that Zuccarello is "trending" toward playing tonight, however, after practicing Monday on a line with center Quinton Byfield and Trevor Moore. No one has more than five points, and only three guys have a positive rating (all at plus-1).

"I'm not seeing [the lapses] from our fourth-line type players, I'm seeing it from our top players," All-Star defenseman and team captain Drew Doughty said over the weekend. "That's why we're losing games. Your top players need to be your best players every night and not just have flashes of brilliance. They need to play 60 minutes."

We should note that the Kings started 1-3-2 last season ahead of a long trip as similar to starts tonight, and they still managed to sneak into the playoffs, although they were then swept by Colorado. But I sure don't see any sure-fire wins on this five-game roadie. It likely will be Kuemper tonight, and in two road starts he has allowed 10 goals on 61 shots.

He remains one assist shy of tying Nicklas Backstrom (762) for the all-time franchise lead.

Ovi already obviously leads in goals and points, so he is on the verge of becoming only the fourth player born outside North America to lead his franchise in career goals, assists and points, behind Florida's Aleksander Barkov, New Jersey's Patrik Elias and Ottawa's Daniel Alfredsson.
