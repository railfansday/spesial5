Joe Burrow can become the first QB ever with 4+ touchdowns in 6 consecutive games

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow is on a remarkable streak of four-touchdown games. If he does it again on Monday night, he’ll do something no player in NFL history has done before.

Burrow has at least four touchdowns (combined running and passing) in each of the last five games. A four-touchdown game against the Commanders on Monday night would give Burrow an all-time NFL record six straight four-touchdown games.

Currently, Burrow and Peyton Manning are tied for the record, with five consecutive four-touchdown games. Manning did it in 2004 with the Colts. Burrow’s last five games have featured:

Four touchdown passes in a 38-18 win over the Steelers.

Three touchdown passes and one touchdown run in a 34-17 win over the Falcons.

Five touchdown passes and one touchdown run in a 43-36 win over the Titans.

Four touchdown passes in a 35-13 win over the Ravens.

Four touchdown passes in a 31-24 win over the Dolphins.

Burrow is the MVP favorite, and with 2,795 yards and 27 touchdown passes through nine games this season, he’s closing in on 3,000 yards and 30 touchdowns, which only four quarterbacks have done within their first 10 games of a season: Tom Brady in 2007, Aaron Rodgers in 2011, Manning in 2013 and 2014, and Patrick Mahomes in 2018. That’s good company for a quarterback to be in, and Burrow is playing right now at a level few quarterbacks have ever reached.
