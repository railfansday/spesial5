The Celtics host the Orlando Magic for the 2nd of 4 games between these two teams this season. The Celtics won the first game in Orlando 108-97 on November 1. They will play again in Boston on March 18 and in Orlando on April 7. The Celtics won the series 3-1 last season, losing one game in Orlando. The Celtics are 80-58 overall all time against the Magic and they are 51-18 all time in games played in Boston.

The Celtics are playing on the second night of back to back games, having hosted and blown out the Dallas Mavericks on Sunday. This is already their 4th back to back set of 16 for the season. They are 2-1 so far on the second night of back to back games, losing the game in Houston on the night after winning in Orlando. The Celtics have 2 more back to back sets left in the month of November.

The Celtics remain in 2nd place in the East, half a game ahead of 2nd place Philadelphia. They are 10-1 against Eastern Conference opponents. They are 6-0 at home and 9-1 in their last 10 games. They have won their last 6 games. The Magic are 8th in the East, 5.5 games behind first place Boston. They are 3-4 against Eastern Conference opponents and 5-5 in their last 10 games. They are 4-3 on the road and just 1-4 at home. They have won their last 3 games.

The Magic have made a few changes since last season. Their biggest move was to fire coach Jamahl Mosley and hire former Spurs assistant, Sean Sweeney, as head coach. They also added former Celtic Nikola Vucevic to a 1 year minimum contract. They drafted Izaiyah Nelson with the 51st pick in the draft and signed him to a 2-Way contract. They lost Moritz Wagner and Jett Howard.

After this game, the Celtics will head out on a West coast trip that takes them through Denver, Sacramento, Golden State, and Washington. They then play one game at home against Atlanta before playing at San Antonio and Dallas. Then it's home against Charlotte before a game at Brooklyn. Next they have a 7 game home stand against Washington twice, Utah, Detroit, Portland, Miami and Cleveland.

After this game, the Magic will host Philadelphia before they play at Brooklyn, and Washington. After the tournament, they will have a home and home series with Philadelphia and host Cleveland before playing at Brooklyn and Atlanta before a road trip through Sacramento, Charlotte, Toronto, and Milwaukee.

Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is always the key to winning games. The Celtics are now 2nd in the league with a defensive rating of 105.6. The Magic are 17th with a defensive rating of 113.4. The Celtics have an offensive rating of 117.4 (6th) while the Magic have an offensive rating of 109.7 (23rd). The Celtics need to continue to make defense a priority and lock down the Magic's shooters.

Rebound – Rebounding is also a big key to winning The Magic are 20th with 42.9 rebounds per game while the Celtics are 9th with 44.8 rebounds per game. In the first game between these two teams, the Magic out rebounded the Celtics 43-41. The Celtics simply have to fight harder to grab rebounds than the Magic. Every Celtic has got to crash the boards and fight for every rebound. The Celtics can't score without the ball and a good way to get the ball is to crash the boards.

Effort and Energy for 48 Minutes – The Celtics have to play with extra effort overall for all 4 quarters. The Celtics are going to need their reserves to come through for them since they are playing in back to back games and when the starters are tired, the bench players will need to come in and bring energy. The Celtics need to make playing with more effort and energy their identity and that goes for all 48 minutes and every player.

Move the Ball Carefully – The Celtics play best when they share the ball and keep it moving. They can't try to do too much individually or the offense gets bogged down. They need to move the ball to find the best shots instead of taking quick shots or over dribbling. That hasn't been a problem as much this season and hopefully they don't fall back into old habits. They have to move the ball carefully, however, and not turn the ball over. The Magic score 19.3 points off turnovers per game. If the Celtics get sloppy, the Magic will make them pay.

X-FactorsCeltics Pride at Home vs Revenge – The Celtics will have the home crowd behind them and that should give them a boost. The Magic will have all the distractions of playing on the road such as travel, hotel stay, and playing on an unfamiliar court in front of a hostile crowd. These two teams played just 2 weeks ago and the Celtics won on the Magic's court. The Magic will be looking to avenge that loss and will come out strong. The Celtics need to be ready for a team that plays very hard for 48 minutes. The Celtics need to match the Magic's effort especially on the defensive end. The Celtics have to show pride and play harder than the Magic to protect their home court and get a win against this motivated Magic team.

Back to Back – The Celtics are playing on the second night of back to back games and so fatigue could be a problem, although Sunday's game was an easy win for them. Both games were at home and so they don't have the added fatigue of travel and so that is good. Queta may be out for rest this game, but no other starters are listed. Even with most of the starters playing, the reserves have to come through to pick up the slack for possibly tired starters.

Officiating – Officiating is always an x-factor in every game. Every crew officiates differently. Some call it tight, others let them play.
