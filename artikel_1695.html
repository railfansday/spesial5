Strops, scruff, and chaos

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

I watched Man Utd v Aston Villa last night. When Villa conceded late on to let a two goal lead slip for the second time in the game, Unai Emery threw a strop and went down the tunnel. It’s not the first time he’s eschewed a handshake with the opposition manager, and his post-game interview where he insisted he thought the game was over was very funny because it was such obvious bullshit.

This is someone who has managed over 1000 games in his career. I’m pretty sure he knows when the final whistle blows and when it doesn’t. Can you imagine if Mikel Arteta did that? You wouldn’t be able to move for all the opinion pieces about what a sore loser he is and all the rest. But perhaps Emery gets a pass because he is ‘the best manager in the league’. Anyway, perhaps it provides a stay of execution for Michael Carrick at Man Utd, which can only be a good thing.

Meanwhile, it looks as if Pierre Sage is gonna get fired by Crystal Palace and replaced by Thomas Frank. That seems a sensible appointment to me. Enough time has passed since his spell at Sp*rs for the toxins to wear off, and he’s clearly a good manager. I’ve never been convinced by Sage, I just think he looks too scruffy. As if someone got a 10 year old to draw man and brought it to life.

I quite enjoyed this quote from Mikel Arteta in his post-game press conference after Saturday’s 1-0 win over Hull:

Sometimes we try to score the perfect goal against low blocks, but we need to be much more chaotic than that. If we open the scoring much earlier also, it changes the game.

I love it as a concept, adding some chaos to our control makes a lot of sense, but it also kinda seems at odds with what the manager asks of his players. Maybe he just means more shots from distance, maybe riskier pass when we’re trying to break the opposition down, or maybe there’s something he’s got cooking for over the Interlull and when we return we see something we never expected. He did kick the shit out of a dugout chair on Saturday, that’s a new enough development to be fair.

I wonder was he referring to the Kai Havertz chance in the first half, when he could have shot on his right foot but tried to cut back/lay it off. If you shoot, maybe you don’t score but as we saw with the winning goal from Martin Odegaard, it might end up in the path of another Arsenal player who can finish it off. Perhaps too there’s a bit of a through line between being more chaotic and what Noni Madueke is doing at the moment. He can be a bit scattergun, but as Nick Wright points out on Sky Sports, his numbers are improving. I don’t think Arteta will ever embrace full chaos, all the time, but if he can add a little more to the mix, it could certainly help.
