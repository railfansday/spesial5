Report: Major Liverpool transfer news dropped in public

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Liverpool have had major transfer news dropped in public. We'll have to see where they go from here.

Liverpool are currently weighing up their options for the 2027 transfer windows. January feels like an important one, too, as this squad is incredibly close to being very competitive.

Right now, though, it isn't. Liverpool are good without being great and lack the kind of consistency that elite teams need. Keep within touching distance ahead of the transfer window, though, and the right signings could put them in the middle of the battles for silverware.

There are a few areas where a signing would make a major difference. Midfield is certainly one, while there's a debate to be had about an imbalanced attack that might just benefit from an extra element.

Above all, there are the full-back roles. Liverpool seem unhappy about both flanks, with Andoni Iraola struggling to settle on preferred options.

Alejandro Balde won't be comingIt seems that one option Liverpool won't have at left-back is Barcelona's Alejandro Balde. They've been linked with the player in a relatively big way. His agent, Jorge Mendes, has reportedly spoken with the Reds about a potential move. Balde isn't playing regularly for Barcelona and looks like a clear candidate for a move this summer - and that has many teams interested. Balde is still only 23 and has the kind of experience that top clubs would love to add. Liverpool, after all, are essentially looking for someone other replace Kostas Tsimikas in the squad and potentially take over from Milos Kerkez.

It seems that one option Liverpool won't have at left-back is Barcelona's Alejandro Balde. They've been linked with the player in a relatively big way.

His agent, Jorge Mendes, has reportedly spoken with the Reds about a potential move. Balde isn't playing regularly for Barcelona and looks like a clear candidate for a move this summer - and that has many teams interested.

Balde is still only 23 and has the kind of experience that top clubs would love to add. Liverpool, after all, are essentially looking for someone other replace Kostas Tsimikas in the squad and potentially take over from Milos Kerkez.

At the very least, any new left-back would need to rival Kerkez for a place. Balde is someone who could do that and he's thought to be available.

But it might not be the case. If Barcelona's Football Coordinator Bojan is to be believed, it's definitely not the case.

“We are not planning for Balde to leave in January," he told TotCosta, as relayed by Fabrizio Romano.

“He’s not playing, that’s not easy and we understand that. But in football there are different moments. He’s always training well."

That would essentially end the chances of Liverpool landing their presumed no.1 target. We're not even sure what the plan would be from there but we'd guess that the Reds simply wouldn't sign a left-back this winter and then revisit the situation next summer.
