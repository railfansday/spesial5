Chelsea XI vs Nottingham Forest: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso still has injury issues to solve as Chelsea look to put damaging defeats behind them on Friday night at Nottingham Forest.

Back-to-back losses to Sunderland and Leeds has put pressure on Alonso and his players as they head to struggling Forest.

In a welcome injury update, key midfielder Moises Caicedo has been declared fit to play less than a week after being forced off during the first half against Leeds.

But Alonso also confirmed that neither Joao Pedro nor Maxence Lacroix will be available.

Joao Pedro has been sidelined for a number of weeks with a thigh injury, during which time Chelsea’s form has taken a significant dip.

Young striker Emmanuel Emegha has deputised in the Brazilian’s absence, and he will hope his first goal for the club, coming last week against Leeds, will be enough to see him retain his place this weekend.

A decision must be made over whether to stick with the 4-2-3-1 system or revert to the 3-4-2-1 formation Chelsea were deploying earlier in the season.

It remains unclear when Joao Pedro, Jorrel Hato or Lacroix will return, amid suggestions that the latter could be back before the start of 2027.
