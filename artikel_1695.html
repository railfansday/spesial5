CM: Cardinale and Mendes discuss USA star Balogun as Milan option – the latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

AC Milan have been offered striker Folarin Balogun by agent Jorge Mendes ahead of January, according to a report.

Calciomercato.com write that Balogun could a potential solution for those seeking a new centre-forward for the second half of the season. The American striker would like to leave Monaco for Serie A and Mendes has spoken with AC Milan and Juventus in recent days.

The fact that he has a contract expiring in 2028 could convince the Monegasque club to accelerate the exit process, having already been close to selling him in the summer before a move to Everton fell through.

Cardinale has direct contact and an excellent relationship with the CEO of Gestifute, who last summer brought Diego Moreira and Goncalo Ramos to the Rossoneri. In recent days, Balogun has been discussed, a player with one goal in 152 minutes under the new coach Filipe Luis.

Milan are focused on their main attacking targets Nicolo Tresoldi and Endrick, but they will keep the USA international in mind given that Francesco Camarda will be leaving on loan and Ramos will therefore need a new deputy.

Balogun was a target for the Rossoneri in the summer of 2023 and he knows Christian Pulisic well, given they play close to each other in the national team. Last season, the 25-year-old scored 19 goals across all competitions, in 43 appearances.

Mendes also discussed Balogun with Juventus, who are looking for a quality striker to partner Woltemade and Kolo Muani. Much will also depend on Monaco’s terms of departure: an asking price under €50m could attract the two Italian clubs.
