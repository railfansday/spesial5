West Ham fans ‘prepare for relegation’ as pressure mounts on new manager Scott Parker

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

West Ham fans are already calling for manager Scott Parker to be sacked - just four games into his tenure at the London Stadium.

The Hammers are yet to win a game since Parker replaced Nuno Espirito Santo as head coach at the Championship side, and lost 1-0 at Portsmouth on Tuesday night.

West Ham struggled to break sides down under Nuno, and it appears Parker is running into the same pitfalls despite boasting supposedly great talents.

One West Ham fan account accused Parker of not having a “clue”, before calling on the former captain to “leave this club immediately”.

Another likened Parker’s football to saying goodbye to attractive football, stating: “Farewell beautiful game.”

Supporters are already suggesting replacement managers, with one fan replying to the club’s Twitter post with their preferred head coach - another ex-West Ham midfielder, recently sacked Manchester United boss Michael Carrick.

After relegation from the Premier League last term, West Ham fans and pundits from the wider football media expected the club to be contending for automatic promotion.

Now, fans are starting to fear a second successive relegation - following in the footsteps of Leicester City.

One fan has told the rest of the supporterbase to “forget the title” and prepare themselves for a relegation battle.
