From bonus points on your next hotel stay to sales on award tickets, we're always looking for travel deals that help you earn more points and miles and redeem them for maximum value. In other words, we want to help you book the vacation of your dreams.

This week, we've got a best-ever welcome offer on this airline credit card, a flash sale for Atmos Rewards members and up to $40 in savings at Uber Eats. Plus, Celine Dion fans can redeem points for hard-to-get concert tickets.

Each week, we help our readers by rounding up promotions so they don't have to scour the internet for the best reward redemptions and offers. This includes everything from frequent flyer programs and hotel rewards programs to credit card offers and rental car deals. Here's our latest list of noteworthy new points and miles promotions for early November.

Airline loyalty programs can offer trip discounts on specific dates, the opportunity to earn more points or miles for future travel and exclusive deals to accumulate points or miles toward elite status. Below are some of the best offers we found for November.

Here at TPG, we love using airline shopping portals to earn extra miles on everyday purchases — and several of our favorite airlines are running lucrative promotions just in time for the the holidays. These bonuses are typically cumulative, so you can hit the minimum spend across multiple purchases at different vendors.

That's up to 6 cents earned per dollar spent, on top of what you'll earn with the credit card you use to pay.

If you've never booked a hotel through American Airlines' hotel booking platform, there are two things you should know. First, you can earn AAdvantage miles and Loyalty Points on every stay (plus bonus points if you hold elite status or a cobranded card). And second, you can score 1,000 bonus miles if you book your first hotel in the month of November.

Book a participating hotel here, and complete your stay by Nov. 30.

Air France-KLM Flying Blue is kicking off November with a fresh batch of Promo Rewards deals. This time around, members can save up to 25% on flights to Africa, China and Europe — with economy tickets starting at just 18,750 miles each way plus taxes and fees. There are also premium economy seats to China and Kenya starting from 48,750 miles each way.

Book by Nov. 30, 2026 for travel through April 30, 2027.

Hotel loyalty programs regularly offer discounted award stays and new ways to earn extra points. Here are some November deals to consider when booking your next stay.

Marriott Bonvoy is offering bonus points at its design-forward CitizenM brand. Stay two nights or more at a participating property, and you'll earn 5,000 bonus Bonvoy points.

Book with promo code S2449 by Dec. 6, and stay by Dec. 13.

If your small business is a member of Hilton for Business, this deal is for you.

For the rest of 2026, both owners and employees can earn double elite night credits on any stay booked under a Hilton for Business rate, up to a total of five additional night credits. Even better, you can combine this with other Hilton for Business offers.

Planning a trip to Canada, Mexico or the U.S.? ALL Accor members can double their points at participating hotels in the Americas. Activate the offer first, then book and stay by Dec. 31.

Plus, if you have an Accor+ Ibis or Voyager subscription, you can score 5,000 or 9,000 bonus points on a single stay. Book by Nov. 20, 2026 for travel through Jan. 22, 2027.

Whether you need a plane ticket, hotel, rental car or cruise, this Chase Travel deal has you covered. We found it on the (see rates and fees).
