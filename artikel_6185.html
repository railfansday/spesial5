Early Travel Tuesday Deals Are in Motion—Save on Bookings With Expedia, Hyatt, and Celebrity Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If there's one thing better than having a vacation on the calendar, it's knowing you didn’t pay full price for it. And right on cue, one of the most exciting stretches of the travel-booking year is kicking off. With Thanksgiving around the corner, Travel Tuesday is already making its presence known, bringing early-bird deals on flights, hotels, cruises, and vacation packages for travelers ready to trade their wish lists for boarding passes.

The annual event, which falls on the Tuesday after Thanksgiving—this year, on December 1—has become a prime moment for savvy travelers to score booking discounts, whether that’s a Caribbean beach escape in the depths of winter, a European summer adventure, or simply a long weekend somewhere new.

The best part? You don't have to wait until the official Travel Tuesday date to start saving. Airlines and hotel groups are getting a head start with early promotions, and some of the most appealing offers—from top-rated cruise lines to flight fares—are already live. I've rounded up nine of the best early Travel Tuesday deals worth booking, or at the very least keeping an eye on as more are announced.

World of Hyatt has already dropped its annual Cyber Sale, slashing rates by up to 25% for its members across more than 1,250 hotels and luxury resorts across the globe. With a travel window stretching all the way through April 30, 2027, you have plenty of runway to lock in trips from a sun-drenched winter escape to a spring break getaway—or that bucket-list vacation you’ve been putting off. If you’re not a member yet, the sale is open to the general public for up to 20% off. (That said, signing up takes two seconds and is totally free, so don’t leave the money on the table!) Just make sure to lock in your dates by December 8, 2026.

Save on Sandals Saint Vincent and The Grenadines—the perfect sanctuary for relaxation within over 50 acres of breathtaking Jurassic Park–like scenery.

Want the best bang for your buck in the Caribbean? Sister brands Sandals and Beaches have you covered. The early Travel Tuesday deals from these all-inclusives ensure up to $750 in air credits to help offset flight costs, alongside up to 65% off hotels and resorts across the entire resort portfolio—from Grenada to St. Lucia, Saint Vincent and The Grenadines, Curaçao, and beyond.

Select bookings promise some exclusive perks too: At Sandals Resorts specifically, travelers can receive up to $2,250 on booking and spa credits to use during their adults-only stay. Families booking Beaches Turks and Caicos (including the new Treasure Beach Village) or Beaches Negril in Jamaica can receive up to $1,650 in booking and spa credits. Just book by Travel Tuesday itself for qualifying travel through December 31, 2028.

Whether you’re an avid traveler or not, there’s a high chance you’ve booked a flight, hotel, or rental on Expedia. Lucky for you, now just so happens to be the site’s biggest sale of the year, with savings on family beach resorts, romantic getaways, cabin retreats, and everywhere in between. If you’ve already got a special destination in mind, simply enter your city of choice and travel dates into the website or app’s search bar, use filters to refine your search, and see which Travel Tuesday deals come rolling in. Up until December 3, those ready to book via Expedia can save up to 50% on stays in cities all over the world—plus, onboard perks on select cruises, eligible discounts on car rentals, and more.

In true holiday spirit, the ever-luxe and tropical hotel group Sun Siyam is offering up to 50% in savings across its six stunning resorts, from secluded barefoot hideaways in the Maldives to lush, high-end retreats in Sri Lanka. The timely sale allows for bookings made before November 30, and for eligible stays through October 31, 2027, depending on the property. Need more incentive to stay at Sun Siyam other than a discount at its fabulous locations? Easy—these stays come with memorable perks, like complimentary seaplane transfers, spa treatments, floating breakfasts, excursions, water sports, and dining experiences galore.

At Siyam World, for one, guests can save up to 50% while enjoying an all-inclusive meal plan. Meanwhile, at Sun Siyam Olhuveli, guests can receive 50% off airport transfers, 30% off à la carte dining, 25% off spa treatments, and 20% off water sports, with complimentary sunset cruises on qualifying stays. And there’s way more where that came from.

It’s not often we find a “someday” trip at such a tempting price—but an early Travel Tuesday deal from Quark Expeditions puts that dream getaway well within reach. For those dreaming of the ends of the Earth, the cruise line is offering savings of up to 40% on select Arctic and Antarctic voyages. Available to book now through December 3, the limited-time promotion brings the world’s most exclusive polar frontiers within closer reach—from the icy wilderness of the Antarctic Peninsula and Svalbard to the rugged landscapes of Patagonia, Greenland, Iceland, and beyond. And yes, bucket-list itineraries—including the time-saving Antarctic Express: Fly the Drake—are also part of the offer.

Celebrity is launching this stunning river ship in summer 2027—and you can sail it for the best booking value yet.

Looking for a value-forward river cruise in picture-perfect corners of Europe? Top-rated cruise line Celebrity Cruises is releasing a set of flash sales over the next week, with its latest being a discount on exactly that—and the perks are pretty enticing. For one, if you book before November 24, you can save 75% on a second guest’s fare on sweet river sailings, from the Danube River to see Vienna and Budapest or venturing from Brussels to Amsterdam on a hunt for tulips. There are also select sailings—including a New Year’s cruise on the Rhine River—where you can save up to $1,000 in air credit per person.

When it comes to savvy ways to fly, it’s no secret that Skyscanner provides heaps of flight options and routes where value is front and center. If you’re looking to score cheap Travel Tuesday flights, you can count on the site for price drops on airfare over the next week through Cyber Monday. Along with vacation packages, hotel deals (at up to 35% off), and an app for price tracking, the Skyscanner website has an entire guide on and around Travel Tuesday itself—from advice on reducing fees to using shared loyalty points and beyond.

Whether you are mapping out a holiday road trip or chasing winter sun, scoring the right car rental deal is half the adventure. While highly regarded car rental company Avis has not yet rolled out an official Travel Tuesday promotion ahead of the December 1 event, you won’t have to wait to lock in some stellar savings. Right now, you can trim up to 35% off base rates simply by choosing the Pay Now option online, or unlock up to 30% off with Avis’s current discount code AWD S169100.

Book a discounted tour to the cloud forests of Costa Rica.

If you’ve got a sudden urge to get up and go, this is G Adventures’s biggest sale of the year—and it includes hundreds of early Travel Tuesday deals with up to 30% off tours and experiences across the globe. With some trips departing in the next 60 days, we’re talking discounts on some of the group’s most-loved and top-rated trips, including Thailand Island Hopping on the West Coast and chasing Costa Rica: Coastlines and Clouds Forests. If your schedule’s flexible and your passport’s ready, this is the kind of spontaneous deal you jump on before it disappears.
