Nvidia said ⁠on Sunday it “is initiating an independent investigation in light of recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort”.

The company’s board said in a statement on that, while it has no reason to believe that either US national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, its inquiry will examine all available information.

Despite those concerns, Huang and his wife, Lori Huang, were invited to a state dinner at the White House in September for Xi Jinping, China’s president, and seated at the top table with Donald Trump and Xi.
