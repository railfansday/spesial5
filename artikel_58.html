Severe Thanksgiving storms leave roads flooded and trees fallen

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Both US coasts have been battered by severe Thanksgiving storms, leaving behind flooded roads, fallen trees, and widespread power outages for hundreds of thousands of people.

Although the weather is expected to improve, hundreds of flights remain delayed or cancelled on Friday.

Beyond the travel disruption, impacts were widespread across the east Coast, and US President Donald Trump said he would consider releasing emergency funds.
