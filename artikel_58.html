Russia attacks Zelensky's hometown, Ukraine reaffirms wish to join NATO

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A Russian ballistic missile attack on President Volodymyr Zelensky’s hometown in central Ukraine on Saturday killed two people and wounded dozens. Hours later, Zelensky reiterated his country's determination to join NATO, following proposals to provide Kyiv with security guarantees similar to those of the military alliance.
