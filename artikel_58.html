November 17 blackout hoax: How a conspiracy theory went viral around the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A conspiracy theory claiming a worldwide “blackout” will hit on November 17 has spread across social media, cutting electricity, internet and banking services. The rumour stems from a routine US FEMA emergency alert test, but was amplified by cryptocurrency accounts, misleading claims about EU preparedness advice and references to The Simpsons. Recent technology outages, flight disruptions and cyberattacks have added to public anxiety, helping turn a routine test into a global blackout hoax.
