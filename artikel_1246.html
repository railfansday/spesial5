Aston Villa Made Massive Error With New Maatsen Deal After Galatasaray Stinker

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aston Villa fell to a sixth game without a victory as Galatasaray scored a late winner to claim victory in the Champions League on Tuesday evening.

The Villans started strongly but registered just a single shot on target across 90 minutes, as substitute Deniz Gul headed home the winning goal while being kicked in the face to grab all three points for the hosts.

The defeat means that Villa have lost their last two Champions League games, and they are now winless in six games across all competitions, meaning Unai Emery must begin to look at ways to change the team's fortunes after a slow start to the campaign.

Villa have been rocked by a number of injury issues across the team this season, including long-term absentee Amadou Onana, while summer signings Aaron Wan-Bissaka and Matteo Ruggeri were both absent from the game due to knocks, as was first-choice right-back Matty Cash, who is dealing with a concussion.

That means that left-back Ian Maatsen was the only natural full-back option fit and available for the game, but he turned in a shocking performance on the night as Villa struggled to create going forward and looked relatively shaky at the back.

The Netherlands international has recently signed a new contract at Villa Park to extend his stay in the Midlands until 2032, and described the decision as an 'easy one', with working under Emery one of his main factors for signing.

“It was an easy decision, the way the club grows in their environment. I have the opportunity to show my qualities, Unai gives me the confidence to play my own game. It is such a good moment and a really proud moment to extend my contract with this beautiful club.”

But after his performance in Istanbul, and with Villa's problems defensively so far this season, the manager will surely be questioning the decision to commit to him in the long-term and essentially confirm that he will be first-choice for the foreseeable future.

Maatsen started in his preferred left-back role with Tyron Mings alongside him and Johan Manzambi ahead of him. But while the team were rather unfortunate to concede in the way they did, they were lucky that it didn't come earlier in the game.

During the game, Maatsen lost possession 21 times, failed to successfully make a tackle, won just 2 of his 13 duel attempts, was dribbled past twice, and conceded two fouls. That shocker of a performance earned him a measly 4/10 rating from the Birmingham Mail, the lowest of any player (alongside Manzambi and Nicolas Jackson).

This was his 7th consecutive start for the team too, so using rust or a lack of rhythm simply won't stand either. He's simply showing that he just isn't good enough to do what the manager wants from him.

After losing Lucas Digne to PSG in the summer, the hope was that Maatsen could step up in his place and take over the mantle, while competing alongside Ruggeri.

But Ruggeri has struggled to adapt to English football so far, and Maatsen hasn't shown anywhere near enough to suggest that he should keep that role. Despite signing ten new players in the summer, a left-back should now be right at the top of their priorities.

Alejandro Garnacho was by far the worst and most pointless signing of the window, with the Argentine not making the bench in Istanbul despite Emery naming two goalkeepers and leaving an empty spot on his team sheet. But there is at least an argument that he hasn't really been given an opportunity.

Maatsen has, and he's failed to take it. For the club to then hand him a new long-term contract anyway is a grave error if he performs this way, and someone must take accountability by ensuring he is replaced in January.

The Dutch forward has had a brilliant time in Italy.
