Short-handed Warriors struggle without Steph Curry, search for answers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A second-straight blowout loss without superstar Stephen Curry in the lineup has caused the Golden State Warriors to look within themselves for answers.

The Warriors fell to 8-6 after losing three consecutive game, 115-94 against the New Orleans Pelicans at Smoothie King Center on Monday, Nov. 16.

Golden State has gotten off to slow starts in consecutive games. They were down 25-5 with 3:30 in the opening period and finished the quarter with a 35-11 deficit against the Pelicans.

Warriors head coach Steve Kerr said the start "deflated" the team. They had a similar lethargic start in their Nov. 13 matchup against the San Antonio Spurs, where they opened the game with a 38-14 deficit after the first quarter.

"Another terrible start," Kerr told reporters after Monday's loss. "We tried to change it up from San Antonio and change the starting lineup, but just didn't seem to have a lot of juice. We're gonna have to change some combinations and find some things that work for us. We got some good looks early and it was one of those nights where shots didn't go. It felt like that really deflated us, and to lose the quarter, like that, 35 to 11, it's so hard in this league to climb out of a hole like that."

Against the Spurs, they started with Will Richard, Brandin Podziemski, De'Anthony Melton, Yaxel Lendeborg and Draymond Green on the floor. At New Orleans,LJ Cryer and Al Horford were plugged into the starting lineup, alongside Green, Lendeborg and Podziemski.

The piece they're missing is a two-time MVP and four-time champion, often referred to as the greatest shooter of all-time.

Golden State has been without Curry since he exited their Nov. 11 game against the Houston Rockets with 6:25 remaining in the first quarter with an injury that an MRI later confirmed was an oblique abdominal tear. He's expected to re-evaluated in another week, but the Warriors will need to find answers within themselves before then.

This season, the Warriors are 1-3 in four games without Curry who, through 10 games, averages 27.8 points, 3.9 assists and 3.7 rebounds, shooting 50% from the floor and 45.7% on 3-point attempts. Kerr is searching for different lineups up to figure out ways to make up for Curry's absence.

"Without Steph, we've got to mix and match a little bit, and, you know, we'll have to experiment," Kerr said. "Without Steph, obviously, we're looking for different combinations that are gonna click, and we've got to find some combos that provide the shooting and the spacing that we need, and the cutting. It's early in the season, so we've got to experiment as well."

The question that Golden State is looking to answer is the same one they've been asking for years: what do they do in non-Curry minutes? Whether he's not on the floor for rest, or in this case, now due to injury. The Warriors have long looked to answer that question. Early in the season, they'll have to find a solution.

Curry isn't coming back anytime soon and the Warriors need to search deep within for answers.

It will take more than a new haircut from Lendeborg to turn things around. After their loss to the Spurs' where he scored three points on 1-of-13 shooting, Lendeborg decided that a change was needed, it resulted in a new look as he chopped his hair.

Kerr hardly recognized him. Podziemski, who knows all about the power of a haircut, embraced it. Against the Pelicans, Lendeborg bounced back with a 28-point performance on 9-of-23 shooting and a poster dunk on Derik Queen.

Still, it's not enough. The Warriors need more than just their rookie to step up. Green had just two points on 1-of-3 shooting. Horford didn't score taking just four attempts. Cryer had seven points on 2-of-10 from the field. Green took accountability for how the team came out.

"That's on me," he said. "Because I'm the leader and I can't let us come out like that, so that's on me."

Green also said that they can't put pressure on their rookie to lead them. He had a moment of mentorship with Lendeborg late in the game on the bench.

"Depend on him, but lower the expectation," Green said. "It's unfair to make him to step into Steph's role 13 games into his career. Right now we're putting too much pressure on him and it's tough to deal with."

They love his demeanor and approach to game. He's a competitor, Kerr said, but ultimately shared the same viewpoint with Green.

"He's a rookie. This is his first year, and we're putting an enormous responsibility on his shoulders," Kerr said. "He has to go through this.  There's no way around it. You can't cheat your way through that. You have to feel the highs and the lows. You heard his comments the other night. He cares about all this stuff, which is a great sign, but you have to learn how to manage that and how to learn from each situation."

With Curry out – along with a number of others such as Jimmy Butler III, Brandon Williams and Moses Moody – the Warriors don't have lots of room for error.

And without Curry, all eyes are on the supporting cast.
