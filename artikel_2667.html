Bears cut Lions lead to 19-17 on Kyle Monangai's second TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Lions scored a flurry of points to close out the first half, but the Lions have answered back to kick off the second half.

Running back Kyle Monangai scored his second touchdown of the game to cap the team’s first possession of the third quarter and the Lions’ lead has been slashed to 19-17.

Monangai also had a 14-yard pass reception on the drive and Caleb Williams picked up 25 more with a strike to wide receiver Luther Burden. The running back now has five touchdowns this season and it is his second multi-score game of the year.

The Lions will try to put more breathing room between themselves and the Bears when they get the ball for the first time since the halftime break.
