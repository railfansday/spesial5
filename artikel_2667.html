Colts sign WR Quez Watkins to the active roster from the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colts signed wide receiver Quez Watkins to the 53-player roster from the practice squad on Tuesday, the team announced.

In a corresponding move, the Colts placed linebacker Jaylon Carlies on injured reserve.

The Colts elevated Watkins in Week 10, but he did not play in the victory over the Dolphins. He originally signed with the team’s practice squad on Oct. 6.

Watkins has appeared in 49 career games, with 25 starts, in his time with the Colts (2026), Eagles (2020-23, 2025), Cardinals (2025) and Steelers (2024). He has totaled 98 receptions for 1,249 yards and six touchdowns. Watkins has also registered 14 kickoff returns for 200 yards (14.3 avg.).

Carlies injured his neck in Week 9 against the Cowboys. He has appeared in seven games with five starts this season and has totaled 19 tackles, one pass defensed and one interception.

He has played 20 career games, with 11 starts, and has compiled 55 tackles, two tackles for loss, a sack, two passes defensed, one interception and one special teams stop.

Carlies entered the league as a fifth-round pick of the Colts in 2024.
