Report: Daniel Jones is likely to wait to sign with the &quot;right team at the right time&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Any team can claim the contract of quarterback Daniel Jones on waivers before the Monday deadline. Given the $10.51 million injury guarantee for 2027, which becomes a full guarantee if he suffers an injury that prevents him from passing a physical by March, it's unlikely that any will.

And so he'll become a free agent, like he did after he was benched (for financial reasons) and then cut by the Giants two years ago today.

Via ESPNFL, Jones is "unlikely" to sign with another team immediately. His preference, per the report, will be to "wait for the right opportunity with the right team at the right time."

There's a cake-and-eat-it option not mentioned by the new report. Jones can sign with a practice squad, giving him the flexibility to jump to another team's active roster in the event the planets align for Jones to step in and play for a contender in relevant late-season games.

That's what he did in 2024, joining the Minnesota practice squad while keeping the ability to jump to a 53-man roster elsewhere.

Why wouldn't he do that again? It would keep him working within the confines of an NFL team, which could result in reports that he's looking much better there than he did during his 2026 season in Indianapolis, which had him dead last among all qualifying quarterbacks with a passer rating of 65.4.

Whether he chooses to do that or not, it becomes an obvious (if not likely) option for Jones. Especially since he did the exact same thing the last time he found himself benched and then released.
