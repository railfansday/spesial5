Brock Bowers is practicing on Wednesday after missing Sunday's game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Raiders could get a key player back for their Sunday night matchup against the Browns.

Tight end Brock Bowers is practicing on Wednesday as Las Vegas begins its work week, according to multiple reporters on the scene.

Bowers did not play in last Sunday’s loss to the Broncos as he deals with ankle and quad injuries. He did not practice on Wednesday before being limited on Thursday and Friday.

Reporters noted that Bowers’ ankle is not taped as heavily as it was last week.

Bowers’ level of participation will be revealed later in the day with Las Vegas’ injury report.

In eight games this season, Bowers has 50 receptions for 676 yards with six touchdowns.
