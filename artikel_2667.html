Stephen Curry fires back after Gilbert Arenas calls Ayesha a ‘blemish’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Steph Curry sounds off in social media video aimed at Gilbert Arenas and Nick Young

As any husband would be, Stephen Curry is very supportive of his wife, Ayesha.

She recently co-starred in a new holiday romantic comedy Roku movie titled "Second Chance Holiday," her first on-screen role in a film.

The rom-com was released on Nov. 5. Many have shared their thoughts, including podcast hosts and former NBA players Gilbert Arenas and Nick Young, and former NFL player Emmanuel Acho on the "We Said What We Said" podcast.

Arenas, particularly, went as far as calling Ayesha a "blemish" for the Warriors guard and compared her to Savannah James, LeBron James' wife.

"We don't want to hear you," Arenas said. "We just wanna know you're there."

Curry has been her champion through and through, and has since responded to people critical of his marriage in a social media video directed at Arenas, Young, and anyone who has something to say about his wife and their marriage.

"I've been hearing a lot of noise and usually the noise doesn't bother me, doesn't bother Ayesha," Curry said in a video posted to Instagram. "But this one in particular, when you're trying to step on the accomplishment of my wife and obviously leading podcast tapings and topics and giving opinions on somebody else's wife. Nick (Young), Gil (Gilbert Arenas), Emmanuel (Acho) and anybody else who feels the need to want to have to jump in and give an opinion on something you don't really know about, something that kind of speaks to a bigger conversation on how you view women in this world what they're allowed and what they're not allowed to do. Even the ignorants behind how acting works. Obviously, I tried to laugh this one off but something needs to be said."

"Anytime Ayesha Curry shows up in your headline, I kindly suggest that you give … congratulations and maybe, just maybe the apology can be as loud as yall leading yall show with this topic."
