The Steelers vs Broncos final score has never happened in NFL history

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Just a couple weeks ago, the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cincinnati Bengals finished with a final score that had never happened before in NFL history. Now 12 days later, the Steelers are at it again. The 27-5 final is the 1102nd different final score in an NFL game.

When we get a new final score in the NFL, it's called Scorigami. Normally when we get a Scorigami it's because a team scores an absolute boatload of points and often something weird happens, usually with two-point conversions. We got a safety and not much else from the Broncos on Friday, resulting in this Scorigami.

I don't need to tell you this was a strange game with all the defensive scores, but the biggest one for our purposes was the Broncos' safety. It was a pedestrian 14-0 when Jaylen Warren was tackled in the end zone, leading to a 14-2 score. The Broncos scored next on a field goal, to make it 5 points on the board.

A 14-5 final has happened, as has 17-5, and 24-5. It wasn't until the Steelers put the 27 on the board that we got a unique score of 27-5.

If Denver had made their field goal, 27-8 was also a Scorigami.

This is the seventh Scorigami of the 2026 NFL season.

September 13: Chicago Bears 59, Carolina Panthers 37

September 13: Minnesota Vikings 39, Green Bay Packers 22

November 1: Cincinnati Bengals 43, Tennessee Titans 36

November 15: San Francisco 49ers 46, Dallas Cowboys 40

November 15: Cincinnati Bengals 38, Pittsburgh Steelers 18

November 22: Dallas Cowboys 51, Tennessee Titans 30

November 27: Pittsburgh Steelers 27, Denver Broncos 5
