Commanders GM Adam Peters on Brandon Aiyuk: Can't dispute his work ethic, excited to see where he's at

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk will visit with the Commanders on Wednesday and that will give their General Manager Adam Peters a chance to catch up with a player who joined the 49ers while Peters was in their front office.

During an appearance on 106.7 The Fan on Wednesday morning, Peters said he has not spoken to Aiyuk for the last couple of years so he cannot speak to what went wrong between Aiyuk and his former team. Peters did say there’s “genuine interest” from the Commanders in the wideout because of what he does know about Aiyuk from their time together.

“One of the things that I don’t think anybody can dispute is his work ethic,” Peters said. “He was one of the hardest workers on the team and that’s how he became an All-Pro type player for us in San Francisco. The last season I was there he had a fantastic season. He was a true No. 1 receiver at the time. He’s a guy who’s a tough cover, he’s great after the catch. He’s got great hands, he’s a smart player. There’s a lot of great things about him on the field and he worked really hard to get there. It wasn’t just that he was the most gifted guy that came out. He’s self made too. . . . .There’s a lot of positives. One thing that stands out to me is his work ethic.”

Peters cited Aiyuk’s work ethic as a reason to believe the wideout will be in good shape, but noted that you “can’t replicate” football shape while being away from the game. The Commanders will assess where Aiyuk is on that front and decide whether to add him to the organization for the final weeks of the 2026 season.
