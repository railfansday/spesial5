Diego Pavia is the first pick in the UFL draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Quarterback Diego Pavia went unselected in the 2026 NFL draft. Seven months later, Pavia became the first pick in the UFL draft.

The Columbus Aviators made Pavia the top selection.

It’s no surprise. He’ll create buzz. He’ll sell tickets.

At some point, he’ll have to show that he can compete with a level of football that isn’t all that far below NFL quality. Every year, plenty of UFL players play their way into NFL opportunities.

Pavia can, too. He’ll get his chance. What he does from there is entirely up to him.

Despite finishing second in the 2025 Heisman Trophy voting, no team picked Pavia in April. He initially was invited to participate in the Ravens’ rookie minicamp before being added to the 90-man roster on a tryout basis.

Pavia participated in the offseason program. He was released not long before the start of training camp. He has had no NFL opportunities since then.
