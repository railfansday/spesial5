Lakers star gets honest on public scrutiny weighing NBA players down

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Being an NBA player comes with its many perks, but it also comes with some struggles.

Lakers star Walker Kessler opened up about witnessing how players lose their love for basketball amid criticism and other factors that come with being in the spotlight.

Walker Kessler #14 of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during the game against the Utah Jazz on November 18, 2026 at Crypto.Com Arena in Los Angeles, California. NBAE via Getty Images

"There's definitely some out there that don't love it as much. Unfortunately, some players go into the league loving it and then all the scrutiny, all the extra stuff, they kind of get blinded or distracted by that.

"So they lose the love of the game because they focus so much on all the other stuff, and it's sad to see," Kessler said on The Old Man and the Three podcast.

"You can see it sometimes with a player — they just don't seem like they're having joy out there. It's not even like they're pouting, it's just like they feel like they've got a weight on their shoulder… There's definitely guys out there that don't love to play."

Luka Doncic (77), Walker Kessler (14), and Quentin Grimes (5) of the Los Angeles Lakers walk off the court during a game against the Toronto Raptors on Nov. 1, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Walker Kessler #14 Luka Doncic #77 and Austin Reaves #15 of the Los Angeles Lakers high five during the game against the LA Clippers on October 23, 2026 at Crypto.Com Arena in Los Angeles, California. NBAE via Getty Images

Kessler signed with the Lakers in the offseason on a four-year, $130 million deal. The big man has seemingly acclimated nicely to the bright lights of Hollywood.

Winning, of course, surely helps the entire Lakers roster feel confident and capable in their individual roles with LA. However, the chemistry of the entire team can't be denied.
