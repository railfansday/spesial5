It’s not just any old Parade this year either, as the iconic event celebrates its 100th edition. Don;t however, confuse that with it’s 100th anniversary, that was back in 2024, but with no festivities taking place during the WWII years, we finally hit triple figures in earnest in 2026.

And we’ll be celebrating in style, with Macy’s delivering a host of All Stars floats, bringing back beloved characters from the past, including Bugs Bunny, Bullwinkle J. Moose, Curious George, Adventure Time’s Finn & Jake, Jimmy Neutron, Popeye & Olive Oyl & Swee'Pea, Pink Panther, Scooby-Doo, Sonic the Hedgehog and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Other new floats to look out for are DoorDash’s Dashing all the Way, GEICO’s Road Trip to Parade and a special Hogwarts Holiday float from HBO to promote their new Harry Potter series. There’s plenty of new balloons too, such as Bluey, Buckarro Bob the Minion, Sorcerer's Apprentice Mickey, Hello Kitty and more.

As for the live performances, an All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration featuring cast members from The Book of Mormon, Chicago, Aladdin, Hamilton, The Lost Boys, Rent, The Rocky Horror Show, Schmigadoon! and Wicked will open the show, while the likes of Megan Moroney, Teddy Swims, Natasha Bedingfield, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, Mckenna Grace, Icona Pop, Wyclef Jean, Queen Latifah, Leslie Odom Jr., Keke Palmer, Anthony Ramos, Smash Mouth, Jack McBrayer, Janet McTeer and Melissa Joan Hart will also appear throughout. Megan Hilty, meanwhile, will lead a special tribute to Dolly Parton.

With Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker anchoring proceedings, it’s sure to be a festive feast of entertainment.
