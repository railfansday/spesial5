George R. R. Martin’s No. 1 'Game of Thrones' Spin-Off Officially Returns in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The rise of popular fantasy shows in the last 10 years can mostly be attributed to the popularity of Game of Thrones, the HBO series that ran for eight seasons throughout the 2010s. Inspired by the famous writings of George R. R. Martin, Game of Thrones is still one of the most-watched shows on streaming years after going off the air. Game of Thrones came to an abrupt and controversial ending in 2019, and although most fans were soured on the franchise after such an unsatisfying conclusion, that didn’t stop people from tuning in to the first spin-off, House of the Dragon. The show has aired three full seasons to date, and HBO has already confirmed that the fourth and final season will shoot in 2027 and air in 2028.

House of the Dragon was the first and only Game of Thrones spin-off, at least until this year brought on the release of A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms. Based on George R. R. Martin’s Hedge Knight series of novellas, the show tells the story of the valiant and brave Ser Duncan the Tall and his young squire, Aegon Targaryen. The first season of A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms was a massive success, but this didn’t come as much of a surprise to HBO, considering the studio renewed the show for a second season months before a single episode hit streaming. A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms Season 2 has already wrapped filming, and while no date has been announced, the show is confirmed to return in the first half of 2027.

HBO has not yet renewed A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms for Season 3, but there are strong signs that the show will continue beyond two seasons. Showrunner Ira Parker told Collider months before the Season 1 premiere that he had the show mapped out for at least three seasons, one for each of Martin’s Hedge Knight novellas. There are also reports that HBO is interested in drawing the show out over multiple seasons and eras, following Aegon as a young boy like he is now, but also as a young man, and eventually, an older leader. Still, it will all come down to viewership.
