Brooke Williamson Reveals What It’s Like Sharing a Kitchen With Bobby Flay

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brooke Williamson is opening up about what it’s really like sharing a kitchen with her award-winning, celebrity chef boyfriend, Bobby Flay.

“We both have opinions, but we also both respect each other’s opinions,” Williamson, 48, exclusively told Us on Thursday, November 19, while discussing her recent partnership with luxury appliance brand Thermador. “So, it’s only two chefs in the kitchen. It doesn’t ever feel like too many and we work well together.

She continued of her romance with the Beat Bobby Flay host, ‘We never argue about food. There’s no argument there.”

The Top Chef winner and Flay, 61, began dating in March 2025 years after meeting in 2022 on the set of his reality TV competition show Bobby’s Triple Threat. (Flay was married to Debra Ponzek from 1991 to 1993, to Kate Connelly from 1995 to 1998 and to Stephanie March from 2005 to 2015. Williamson, for her part, was previously married to Nick Roberts, with whom she shares son Hudson, from 2007 to 2024.)

Flay recently opened up about the pair’s romance and how nice it is to have a fellow chef as a partner.

“Well, first of all, I’ve been friends with Brooke forever,” Flay told People back in October. “I mean, she and I have been friends for a long time and so we shot this show before we started actually officially dating. So it has always kind of been the same for me.”

He continued at the time, “We both cook at home all the time. I love when she cooks. Listen, it’s so nice to have somebody that does 50 percent of the cooking in my house. I went from 100 percent to 50 percent very quickly, but it’s perfect.”

Flay later gifted Williamson a diamond ring, though the pair have both clarified they have no intention of walking down the wedding aisle again.

“I mean, imagine opening like a little blue box with a giant diamond ring inside, and on a park bench on a beautiful day in London, Regent’s Park, in the Japanese garden. It was a perfect day,” Williamson said during a January episode of the “Bobby on the Beat” podcast.

Williamson went on to confess that she blurted out, “What the f*** is this?” and felt that the gift was “a little bit confusing in the moment.”

Flay explained that he gave Williamson a ring as a “sign of commitment” because he views her as his “life partner.”

Now, as the pair prepares for the holiday season, Williamson dished to Us on how the two star chefs will tackle Thanksgiving dinner.

“He wakes up hungry every day, but specifically on Thanksgiving, it’s like he doesn’t eat a big meal for days before,” she said of Flay.
