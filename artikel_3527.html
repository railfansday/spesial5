Selling the OC's Kayla Launches GoFundMe After Son's Medical Emergency

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Selling the OC’s Kayla Cardona is asking for support after her 20-year-old son, Jordan, faced a life-threatening medical emergency.

“What began as unexpected internal bleeding quickly turned into a terrifying fight for survival,” Kayla, 37, wrote via a GoFundMe page. “As suspected, Meckel’s diverticulum did NOT appear on Jordan’s CT scans, colonoscopy or endoscopy. It was only during emergency intestinal surgery that doctors confirmed Meckel’s diverticulum, the very condition they had been urged to investigate.”

According to the Cleveland Clinic, Meckel’s diverticulum is a congenital defect in the small intestine. It occurs in 2 percent of the population and only 2 percent of people who have it develop complications or symptoms.

“Throughout his hospitalization, Jordan required nine blood transfusions, three units of plasma, and one platelet transfusion to survive,” Kayla wrote. “Although Jordan is finally home, his journey toward healing and recovery is just beginning.”

In a separate social media post, Kayla — a Southern California real estate agent who appeared on three seasons of Netflix’s Selling the OC — described her son as a man who has a heart for serving others.

“He works full-time as a behavioral specialist technician, cares for children with autism outside of his full-time work, serves in kids ministry, and is an evangelist and missionary,” she wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22. “He has dedicated ALL of his life to helping others. Truly an angel on earth.”

Kayla continued, “I came so close to losing my baby. There are no words to describe the helplessness of watching your child fight for his life and nearly losing him three separate times.”

Since sharing her fundraiser, Kayla has received support from fans and friends, including Selling the OC costars Tyler Stanaland and Alex Rose.

According to Kayla, her son will be out of work as he needs “significant time to rest, regain his strength and recover.”

“If you feel led, please donate whatever God puts on your heart. And if you’re unable to give, simply sharing his story would mean more than you know,” she wrote. “From the bottom of my heart, thank you for loving my son, praying for him and supporting us through this. All glory, honor, and praise to Jesus for saving his life ❤️‍🩹.”
