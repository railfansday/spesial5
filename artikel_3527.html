Britney Spears Apologizes to Her Family for 'Bringing Up Past Issues'

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Britney Spears is presumably ready to clear the air with her family after a long-held estrangement.

“I’m so blessed to have such a beautiful family,” Spears, 44, wrote via Instagram on Saturday, November 21. “I’m so very sorry for bringing up past issues, but I think I did because I realized I needed to protect my heart.”

Britney is the eldest daughter of exes Jamie Spears and Lynne Spears, who also share son Bryan, 49, and daughter Jamie Lynn, 35.

“It’s the way I love and when I love you, I REALLY love you,” Britney stated on Saturday. “My stubbornness is childish and embarrassing. Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human and there actually might be something to those things I said phycology [sic] wise, either way I’m sorry to my family bringing up those memories.”

She continued, “I’ve never actually spoke to them face to face on it. It’s frankly [because] I just miss them like crazy!!! And I’m very sorry!!!”

Britney claimed in a 2021 conservatorship hearing — her dad ran the guardianship for 13 years following her years of personal struggles — that none of her family members had interfered with Jamie’s alleged mismanagement of the conservatorship. Both Jamie, 74, and Lynne, 71,  have long denied Britney’s accusations, with the matriarch still hoping for future reconciliation.

“[Britney] and her mom still have highs and lows. One day they’re working on their relationship, the next they won’t speak for weeks,” a source exclusively told Us in September 2025. “Lynne tries to treat the situation with as much care as possible, but she also doesn’t want to sit back and do nothing.”

The insider added, “She’d love more than anything to be close with Britney again. They used to be best friends. Lynne understands Britney is still hurt and traumatized by the conservatorship, but she hopes one day they can focus on the future and not the past.”

Britney has also gotten closer to her once-estranged sons, Sean Preston and Jayden James, whom she shares with ex-husband Kevin Federline.

“Happy birthday to my handsome young boy oh dear lord we have this beautiful thing called life in front of us, it’s such a beautiful delicate thing,” she wrote via Instagram in a 19th birthday tribute to Jayden in September. “I hope I can be the best mommy in the world every day of ur life!!!!”

While Britney has also since reconnected with her siblings and her sons, it’s unlikely that she’d mend fences with Jamie.

“There’s zero communication on either side, and that’s how she likes it,” a second source told Us in 2025 of Britney’s dynamic with her father. “Furious doesn’t even begin to cover how she feels about him and what he did to her. She’ll never forgive him. He’s accepted it and lives a very quiet life now.”
