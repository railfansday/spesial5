Megan Rapinoe, Julie Ertz elected to National Soccer Hall of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Megan Rapinoe and Julie Ertz have been elected to the National Soccer Hall of Fame as 2027 inductees. Both were elected on the Player ballot in their first year of eligibility.

Rapinoe and Ertz were both part of the U.S. women’s national team squads that won the World Cup in 2015 and 2019, playing pivotal roles in both campaigns. Rapinoe also won a gold medal in the 2012 Olympics.

Rapinoe earned 203 caps with the USWNT between 2006 and 2023. The winger played for the Seattle Reign from 2013 until her retirement, winning the NWSL Shield three times. Before the NWSL’s conception, she played in WPS, as well as the Australian and French leagues. Rapinoe won the Ballon d’Or in 2019 and remains the only American to do so. She was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2022. Known for her confidence and skill, Rapinoe brought an undeniable flair to women’s soccer in the United States, on and off the pitch.

Ertz debuted for the USWNT in 2013, and was the team’s second-youngest player at the World Cup in 2015. She earned 123 caps before her retirement in 2023. Following a successful college career at Santa Clara, she was selected third overall in the 2014 NWSL draft by the Chicago Red Stars, where she played until 2021. After taking a break from soccer during which she gave birth to her first child, Ertz made seven regular season appearances for Angel City FC in the run up to the 2023 World Cup. Ertz’s grit on the back line and then the middle of the park was key to the USWNT’s success throughout her career; she leaves the program with a legacy of tenacious physicality and skill.
