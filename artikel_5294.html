As mentioned in the introduction, most casinos will require a shared XRP address to receive deposits. The destination tag is what tells the casino which account should receive payment.

You could think of the address as an apartment building, while the tag is the apartment number. The payment itself might reach the building, but without that apartment number, what good is it really doing anyone?

Destination tags on this platform are always presented as numbers and can be up to 10 digits long. They will sometimes be expressed as memos or memo IDs by the casino.

Note also that XRP addresses always begin with the letter R. These types of details sound small, but can be genuinely impactful for players trying to move money.
