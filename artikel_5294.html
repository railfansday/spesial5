ANT Price Falls Below the $83 IPO Price Four Days After Anthropic’s Record Debut: Is the Post-Listing Sell-Off Just Starting?

The ANT price slipped below Anthropic’s $83 IPO price in pre-market trading on Tuesday, November 24, just four sessions after the AI company priced its record IPO on Nasdaq. Anthropic stock fell as low as $81.28 before US markets opened, 2.1% under the price paid by IPO buyers.

That is a sharp reversal. ANT opened its first session at $111.50, 34% above the IPO price, and closed day one at $97.17. Since then, it has lost ground every day, closing Monday at $87.64 even as the Nasdaq jumped 6.5%.

The pressure grew after a Wall Street Journal report that the US is preparing new tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies. Nasdaq 100 futures fell 2.5%, and the ANT price traded around $83 shortly before the opening bell.

The question now is what is dragging Anthropic stock down, and what happened to other giant IPOs that broke their issue price?

The selling started before the tariff news. On Hyperliquid, a crypto exchange that lists Anthropic as a perpetual futures contract trading around the clock, xyz:ANT dropped from $92.65 early Monday to $81.30 on Tuesday morning.

Traders are also active on Hyperliquid. The ANT contract traded about $464 million in the past 24 hours, with its funding rate, the fee one side pays the other to keep positions open, slightly negative. That means traders betting on a fall are paying those betting on a rise.

Falling below the IPO price is common for giant listings. We checked the first year of trading for six of the biggest US IPOs of the past 15 years using daily closes from Yahoo Finance.

So for the ANT price, the break itself is not a verdict. Arm and Alibaba spent only 10 and 15 sessions below their IPO prices in year one. Facebook, Uber and Rivian spent most of the year there.

On the hourly Hyperliquid chart, xyz:ANT trades at $83.08, below its 50-hour exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $87.53.

The ANT price now has to win back $83 in the regular session. If it can’t, Anthropic joins a long list of mega IPOs that spent months below their issue price, and the first bank research after Thanksgiving becomes the next big test.
