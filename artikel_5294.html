XRP Price Prediction: Bears Are Paying to Short XRP, A Short Squeeze?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

XRP is struggling to reclaim $1, but its derivatives market is showing an increasingly unusual setup. Traders betting against the token are now paying funding fees to maintain their positions. This creates an interesting possibility for the XRP price prediction. If buying pressure returns, bearish traders could face mounting losses and forced liquidations.

However, the market is not quite ready for a bullish victory lap. Open interest is falling, long positions have already suffered heavy liquidations, and XRP still needs to reclaim important resistance levels.

XRP traded around $0.95, following another difficult session for the crypto market. Meanwhile, its futures market is flashing a rate that dropped in aggregated funding to -0.0068% every eight hours, equivalent to roughly -7.4% annually.

Negative funding means short sellers pay long traders to maintain their perpetual futures positions. In other words, bears are effectively paying a premium to maintain their bearish exposure.

Hyperliquid showed an even more pronounced imbalance, with XRP funding reaching approximately -10.2% annually. The figures suggest bearish positioning has become increasingly expensive across several derivatives markets.

However, there is another side to this trade. XRP open interest declined 5.74% over 24 hours to approximately $1.97 billion, so traders are reducing leverage rather than aggressively opening fresh positions.

While XRP traders debate whether another recovery is coming, Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER) is approaching an important milestone of its own. The Bitcoin Layer 2 project has raised more than $33.2 million during its presale, with its token currently priced at just $0.0136.

The presale is scheduled to conclude on November 26, leaving a narrowing window before its anticipated token launch. Buyers choosing the staking option are also being offered an advertised 35% APY during the presale period.

Bitcoin Hyper aims to bring faster Bitcoin transactions and decentralized applications to a new Layer 2 environment. Its architecture combines Solana Virtual Machine execution with zero-knowledge proofs and Bitcoin settlement.

The project has already introduced testnet components, including its explorer, wallet, faucet, and bridge. With the presale approaching its scheduled conclusion, the remaining allocation and upcoming launch milestone could attract additional attention from traders looking beyond established crypto networks.

The immediate question is whether bearish positioning has become sufficiently expensive to trigger an unexpected reversal. A negative funding rate alone cannot confirm a short squeeze. XRP needs to attract fresh spot buyers, recover momentum, and force short sellers to close their positions.

The first important hurdle remains $1.00. Reclaiming this psychological level could encourage sidelined buyers to return and create additional pressure on leveraged shorts.

A sustained move above $1.00 would put $1.10 within reach, followed by a potential test of $1.20 if short liquidations accelerate.

However, the bearish scenario remains firmly in play. Losing the recent $0.89 low could expose XRP to another decline toward $0.80, particularly if Bitcoin continues weakening.

For now, the XRP price prediction hinges on whether negative funding translates into actual buying pressure. Bears are paying longs, but bulls still need to deliver the rally. If they do, the same traders betting against XRP could become the fuel behind its next recovery.
