Swansea City set to be rocked by Vitor Matos blow with European switch expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Swansea City sit 6th in the Championship table after a fine first quarter of the 2026/27 campaign. Vitor Matos has been hailed for his work in South Wales, but it has not gone unnoticed with overseas interest starting to gather pace.

On Monday, it was reported that French side RC Strasbourg had made an approach for Matos, and he was keen on making the switch. Now, reporter Ben Jacobs states that the Swans boss is now expected to become the new manager of the Ligue 1 club.

Matos has been in charge of Swansea City for just shy of a year. He was appointed from Maritimo on November 24th and his contract with the Championship club runs until 2030, so Strasbourg will be paying a compensation fee to secure his services.

For the first time in a while, it felt as though Swansea City had a really good thing going under Matos. He impressed last season after taking charge, and he was backed well in the summer transfer window in a bid to take the side to the next level.

Their strong start to the 2026/27 campaign indicated they were well placed to return to the Championship promotion fight too. Form faltered before the break with four defeats in five games, but Matos would have been backed to turn things around again.

The Swans have pressed brilliantly under Matos, and they’ve returned to the forward-thinking, stylish football that was on show throughout the 2010s. Now though, they seem set to lose their boss, and it marks a significant setback.

It remains to be seen just how quickly the move progresses from here, but it appears all signs point towards Matos leaving for Strasbourg.

Swansea City have certainly had more misses than hits in managerial appointments since dropping from the Premier League in 2018.

Graham Potter, Steve Cooper and Russell Martin marked three consecutive successes, both both Potter and Martin were poached. Between then, all of Michael Duff, Luke Williams and Alan Sheehan tried and failed to turn around the Swans’ fortunes.

Matos has been a big hit though, perhaps giving home that the hierarchy can identify a strong replacement. The help of Jamestown Analytics will not go amiss as the South Welsh side face the tough task of filling the big gap left behind by a sudden managerial change.
