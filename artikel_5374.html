Hollywood Prediction Market Faces Insider Scare: James Bond Casting Bets Shift

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Hollywood studios are tightening restrictions on employees betting on prediction market after suspicious activity surrounding the next James Bond casting decision. The prediction market controversy intensified after a platform temporarily pulled its market following a sudden wave of bets backing Callum Turner.

The Hollywood Reporter reported that studios and production companies have been quietly warning employees against wagering on confidential industry information. The restrictions cover casting decisions, movie release dates, and studio earnings. At least one company has reportedly established an anonymous hotline for reporting suspected insider betting.

THR has learned studios and production companies have been quietly issuing new guidelines to employees warning them not to engage in prediction market betting.• The warnings prohibit employees from placing bets on news events like studio earnings, casting, or even release… pic.twitter.com/JbiovSwZC9

The crackdown follows growing interest in entertainment contracts on platforms such as Polymarket and Kalshi. Employees could potentially turn confidential production information into profits before announcements reach the public. They are also being warned against passing information to friends or relatives who could place bets on their behalf.

A prediction market platform temporarily suspended its next James Bond casting market after a sudden wave of sharp money pushed Callum Turner ahead of longtime favorite Jack Lowden. The movement raised concerns that traders might have received information about the casting decision before an official announcement.

Turner’s odds had already improved following casting rumors and reports of screen tests. However, the sudden betting activity attracted additional attention, particularly because Amazon MGM Studios has yet to announce its next 007.

Kalshi’s November 6 market snapshot showed Turner leading with a 62% implied probability. Josh O’Connor followed at 18%, while Lowden held 14%. Prediction market has become an increasingly attractive target for traders seeking opportunities beyond politics and sports, such as Hollywood.

Entertainment contracts can cover award winners, reality television eliminations, celebrity appearances, and confidential production developments. The potential for insider betting is particularly concerning when employees have access to information that ordinary participants cannot obtain.

Hollywood restrictions show a difficult enforcement problem for prediction market platforms such as Polymarket and Kalshi. Blockchain transactions can provide publicly accessible records of wallet activity, but identifying the person controlling an address is another matter. Traders can also use multiple wallets and intermediaries to make their activity harder to connect to their real identities.

The issue is not entirely theoretical. Kalshi previously fined a video editor working for MrBeast’s company $20,000 after the employee reportedly generated approximately $5,000 through bets using confidential information. Beast Industries subsequently fired the employee.
