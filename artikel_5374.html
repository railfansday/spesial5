My Home Delivery Unveils an AI-Enabled Technology Platform to Power the "Uberization of All Things Big & Bulky"

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Revolutionizing local commerce through an upgraded mobile app connecting consumers, retailers, and professional delivery networks for big & bulky delivery, white-glove services, and haul-away solutions.

ENGLEWOOD, N.J., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- My Home Delivery (MHD), an AI-enabled local commerce and direct-to-consumer home delivery technology platform, today announced a major technology upgrade designed to accelerate the "uberization" of all things Big & Bulky, transforming how consumers and retailers access professional delivery and haul-away services for furniture, appliances, consumer electronics, and any oversized merchandise. This new capability features AI-powered routing to get customer orders where they need to go, faster and more efficiently by analyzing traffic, distance, and delivery windows in real-time so consumers can count on their delivery showing up when they want it.

MHD brings same-day, on-demand, and scheduled delivery, room-of-choice placement, white-glove services, assembly, installation, and haul-away into one convenient platform. Available on the Apple and Google Play stores, the My Home Delivery app empowers consumers to independently arrange pickup and delivery from any retailer with a few taps and without the hassle and expense of renting a truck and lifting heavy items.

Whether purchasing a television at Costco, furniture at IKEA, appliances at Sam's Club, building materials from Lowes or merchandise from a local store, consumers can schedule delivery directly and seamlessly through the MHD app. And My Home Delivery goes beyond retail. Consumers can also easily arrange home-to-home deliveries, whether sending an old sofa to their kid's college dorm or first apartment, delivering furniture from an estate sale, or transporting large items, like patio furniture, purchased through local marketplaces.

"Our vision is simple: consumers should be able to shop wherever they want and to move whatever they need without worrying about how they're going to get it from point A to point B," said Arelis Bonilla, Founder and CEO. "We're bringing the 'uberization' of all things Big & Bulky to local commerce by putting white-glove professional delivery services directly in consumers' hands."

Retail Partnerships and Seamless POS Integration

MHD's existing retail partnerships, including Bob's Discount Furniture outlets and Mattress Warehouse, demonstrate how the platform complements traditional retail delivery operations.

For national and regional retail partners, MHD's technology offers optional API integrations to major retailer Point-of-Sale (POS) systems, allowing delivery services to be incorporated into existing checkout and fulfillment experiences. Retailers can also promote the MHD app through approved in-store marketing and QR codes without integrating their POS systems.

Consumers can also use the My Home Delivery app regardless of whether a retailer has partnered with MHD. Retail partnerships simply expand consumer awareness and convenience but are not required for consumers to directly access MHD services from any retailer.

About My Home Delivery (MHD) My Home Delivery (MHD) is an AI-enabled local commerce and direct-to-consumer last-mile delivery technology platform dedicated to the "uberization of all things Big & Bulky". Through its mobile app and optional retailer POS integrations, MHD connects consumers, retailers, and professional delivery providers and is building a technology-driven ecosystem designed to make moving large merchandise as convenient as ordering a ride.
