“Superb” international performance throws Scott Parker a curve ball | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The international break has been full of surprises for West Ham‘s participants. Not all of them good. The humbling of Dinos Mavropanos Greece side by the seven goal Dutch. Tomas Soucek’s return to the Czech side marked by a late mistake. Manor Solomon’s complete exclusion from the Israel side.

Few, surely, would have expected the headlines this morning to be fêting one West Ham player who has been roundly condemned for his domestic form.

Mads Hermansen had conceded eleven goals – without making a save – in his last four appearances in the Championship and had one of the worst shot to saves ratio within the division.

On top form for Denmark – is it the man or is it his defence? Parker has a conundrum.

And yet, yesterday he almost single handedly kept out the Portugal attack as his Denmark national side trounced their opponents 4-1 with the Dane making eight saves.

Pretty much more than he’s managed in fifteen starts for West Ham, in one ninety minute spell when he was only beaten in added time. Labelled as ‘superb’ by his own club web site but as ‘sublime’ by one football.com, the Dane has thrown down the gauntlet (goal keeping gloves) to his critics and has thrown a selection curve ball for Scott Parker, with West Ham fans clamouring for the Danish shot stopper to be dropped in favour of the apparently unfit Alphonse Areola.

85% of West Ham fans, to be exact. Now that leaves Scott Parker with a real dilemma. Fortunately Hermansen will have another outing on Tuesday against Norway before returning to Rush Green, giving the West Ham coach more food for thought.

Several West Ham accounts on line overnight have leapt to the conclusion that Hermansen’s West Ham form must be completely down to the woeful defence ahead of him. They may have a point. It is quite possible also that Hermansen has the confidence to perform better on the international stage in front of a group of supporters who aren’t on his back for 90 minutes. That may also be true.
