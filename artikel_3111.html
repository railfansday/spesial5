Forget the Headlines: Novo Nordisk's (NVO) 4.6% Dividend Yield Is the Real Story for Patient Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The dividend is hefty -- and it's been growing briskly, too.

By Selena Maranjian – Nov 20, 2026 at 8:29AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Novo Nordisk ›

There are exciting headlines galore regarding Danish healthcare company Novo Nordisk (NVO -0.26%):

See? It's enough to make one itch to become a shareholder. The stock is well worth considering for your portfolio. But look beyond the headlines, and you'll see another compelling reason to invest in Novo Nordisk: its dividend. The payout recently yielded a fat 4.6% (and when you add in the effect of share repurchases, the total shareholder yield comes in at about 5.5%). For comparison, the S&P 500 index of America's 500 largest companies recently yielded a mere 1%.

It's a growing dividend, too. Unlike American dividend-paying companies, which generally pay dividends quarterly, Novo Nordisk pays twice a year, offering an "annual" dividend and an "interim" one. Together, they totaled $1.85 per share in 2026, and that's up from $0.81 in 2022 and $0.62 in 2019.

Better still, the company's payout ratio -- the percentage of earnings being paid out in dividends -- was recently just 45%. That number suggests that Novo Nordisk shouldn't have any trouble paying future dividends, and that there's plenty of room for further growth.

So -- should you invest in Novo Nordisk? Certainly consider it, especially if you're seeking both income and growth. The shares seem reasonably or appealingly valued at recent levels, with a recent price-to-sales ratio of 3.5, well below the five-year average of 8.1, and a recent forward-looking price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 12.3, not far from the five-year average of 11.9.

Beyond the stock's valuation, Novo Nordisk's business is attractive. It has long been a force in treating diabetes, but now it's also operating in the weight-loss drug realm, with success. Perhaps most promising is its oral GLP-1 drug, which is preferable to a regular injection for many -- and which will soon be available in 20-plus markets.

The company does face competition, and it has had some setbacks in clinical trials, but its pipeline remains promising.
