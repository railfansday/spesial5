Anthropic Filing Misstep Adds Drama Ahead of IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Anthropic is set to kick off the formal marketing of its blockbuster IPO as early as Tuesday after an apparent snafu over the filing documents added a dose of last-minute drama to a nervous market. Monday’s brief clerical misstep, in which a leaked prospectus mistakenly dangled a $69.3 billion fundraising target before getting retracted, provided an unexpected jolt to investors preparing for the Claude chatbot maker’s offering. Bloomberg Intelligence's Matt Bloxham has the details. (Source: Bloomberg)
