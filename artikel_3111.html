Suncor Energy Inc. (SU -4.52%) reported record financial performance for the third quarter, driven by physical integration between its upstream production and downstream refining assets. Management executed on strategic portfolio pruning by announcing the divestiture of non-core offshore East Coast assets for $1.2 billion while maintaining its long-term free funds flow growth targets through 2028. The company utilized record upgrader utilization and optimized refining throughput to capture synthetic crude oil price premiums and expand global export volumes. CFO Peter Zebedee indicated that the company's strengthened balance sheet, with net debt reduced to $1.8 billion, supports a significant increase in capital returns through a 10% dividend hike and expanded monthly share repurchases.
