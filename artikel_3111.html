When Jack returns to his family's Montana ranch, he asks Hannah to pose as his girlfriend to keep his security troubles from his family. Why you should watch it: I wasn't expecting to enjoy "Guarding Stars" quite as much as I did, but Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki's chemistry won me over. It's a lovely seasonal watch, particularly if you enjoy Christmas rom-coms that put just as much emphasis on family as they do on romance.

What it's about: The latest season of "Bar Rescue" sees hospitality expert Jon Taffer travel across America to help struggling bars and restaurants avoid closure. Alongside his team of chefs and mixologists, he identifies the problems holding each business back, from poor management and financial troubles to dysfunctional staff, before attempting a complete turnaround.
