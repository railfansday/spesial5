AWS adds virtual iPhones to its cloud

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Emulating Android, too, but offers just two handsets and one tablet

AWS now offers two new types of cloudy computers: emulated Androids and simulated iPhones.

The cloudy colossus announced both virtual offerings on Wednesday. They’re part of its Device Farm service, which previously saw AWS offer developers the chance to test their software on actual iOS and Android devices that reside in the Amazonian cloud. AWS will now also host virtual devices running Android and iOS.

AWS touts the virtual mobile devices as superior to physical devices.

“With virtual devices, you can shift testing left to stages that need high speed and volume, such as pull requests, code merges, and smoke tests, and keep real devices for later stages that need high fidelity, such as regression and release testing,” the cloudy business unit argues in its launch announcement.

AWS also says developers can get to work faster on a virtual iPhone or Pixel. “Virtual devices running the latest device and OS typically start in seconds, and others start in around a minute,” the outfit argues.

That may sound appetizing, but Amazon’s menu of virtual devices is short and bland.

When The Register logged on as an AWS user, the only emulated Android phone listed was a Google Pixel 10, running Android 15, 16, or 17. An emulated Pixel tablet running Android 17 is also on the menu.

At the time of writing, we could find only a virtual iPhone 16, running iOS 18.6, 23.61, and 27.0.

Analyst firm Counterpoint’s list of the world’s top 10 best-selling smartphones in Q2 of 2026 does not include a single Pixel device. The iPhone 16 occupies 10th place.

iPhone 17 models occupy the first, second, third, and seventh place on the list.

AWS might excite more developers if it adds emulators for the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, Galaxy A07 4G, A17 5G, A17 4G, and Galaxy S26, as they occupy the fourth, fifth, sixth, eighth and ninth places on Counterpoint’s list.

Amazon’s choice of a Pixel tablet is also an oddity given analyst firm IDC doesn’t list Google among its top five tablet vendors.

Amazon does offer physical Galaxy A17 devices and three versions of the iPhone 17. But Device Farm doesn’t offer any foldable devices, meaning developers struggling to wrap their heads around the iPhone Duo will need either ask Apple for a loaner or shell out $2,000 to acquire one.

At least developers don’t have to change behavior to access the few virtual devices AWS offers, as existing Device Farm APIs and test frameworks will work on real or notional machines. ®
