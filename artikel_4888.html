Where Does Joe Burrow Live? Unpacking the Bengals Quarterback’s Real Estate Portfolio

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

He signed a record-breaking contract with the Cincinnati Bengals in 2023, but where does Joe Burrow live when he’s not on the field?

The NFL quarterback was born in Ames, Iowa, in 1996. His family moved to Ohio when he was eight, and he has resided mostly in the Buckeye State ever since. Burrow played for Ohio State University from 2015 to 2017, then transferred as a graduate student to Louisiana State University, where he won the Heisman Trophy shortly before graduating in December 2019. He was drafted into the NFL the following year by his hometown Bengals, with whom he inked a $36.1 million, four-year contract—plus a $23.9 million signing bonus. Three years later Burrow signed a $275 million five-year extension, making him the highest-paid player in NFL history at the time, according to ESPN.

It’s unsurprising, then, that Burrow has put down roots in the Buckeye State. Below, we’re examining the quarterback’s real estate portfolio.

In May 2020, a month after being selected first overall in the NFL Draft, Burrow revealed that he was spending the pandemic lockdown in his old bedroom at his parents’ house in The Plains, Ohio, just outside of Athens. Interior design apparently wasn’t at the forefront of his mind; the room still had his childhood Star Wars curtains and posters on the wall, though he had given the bed a king-size upgrade in high school, replacing the Star Wars comforter that once rounded out the room’s theme. According to The Sun, the 1997-built residence sits on a cul-de-sac and has four bedrooms and three bathrooms.

The Bengals play at Paycor Stadium, formerly known as Paul Brown Stadium.

In June 2020, Burrow spent $835,000 on his first house: a 2,900-square-foot Cincinnati dwelling just five miles from the Bengals’ home stadium. Located in the city’s historic Columbia-Tusculum area, the two-story property was built in 2014. Further details on the dwelling are scant—including whether or not Burrow still owns it. However, its location would make it a convenient place to stay during the NFL season.
