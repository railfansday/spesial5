New ‘Friday The 13th’ Movie Unveils Cast, Title & Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: We recently announced the first Friday the 13th film in nearly two decades — and now, we have new details on the project, which wraps production today.

The film, directed by the previously announced Steve Miner, is titled Jason. It’s slated for a worldwide theatrical release on Friday, August 13, 2027, with Cineverse overseeing its North American distribution.

The ensemble cast is led by Schuyler White (Haunt) as Jason Voorhees, the enigmatic masked murderer at the heart of the slasher series, and Natalie Alyn Lind (Dutton Ranch) as “final girl” Lauren.

Others in the cast include Matt Cornett (Every Year After) as Asher, Lilia Buckingham (Hocus Pocus 2) as Madison, John Owen Lowe (Unstable) as Jake, Summer Madison (The Summer I Turned Pretty) as Emily, Selena Turner (Will Trent) as Olive, Matthew Keoni Sato (High School Musical: The Musical: The Series) as Lucas, Brook Sill (Stranger Things) as Zoe, Guillermo Diaz (Scandal) as Rick, Johnell Young (Wu-Tang: An American Saga) as Frank, Sencere Messiah Brown (Homage) as Chase and Bryan Gael Guzman (Nickel Boys) as Dylan. White, a stunt coordinator and special effects artist, returns to the role of Jason after playing him in the recent short Sweet Revenge made to commemorate the 45th anniversary of Jason Universe.

On the decision to cast him here, franchise steward Robbie Barsamian told us, “We spent months thinking about how to create the scariest, most imposing Jason and who truly deserved to wear the mask. Beyond his towering physical presence, we knew Schuyler understood what makes Jason tick—and that he had the work ethic and genuine respect for this franchise to get it right. He approached this theatrical feature like the role of a lifetime, packing 30 pounds of solid muscle onto his 6’5″ frame and studying the iconic Jasons who came before him so that he feels like an unstoppable force of nature. On set, we framed him low and tight, so the audience is right there in his space, looking up at him. This will be the most intimidating Jason that has ever been on screen.”

On casting Lind — an actress seen on Dutton Ranch, The Goldbergs and Big Sky — as the franchise’s new final girl, Barsamian said, “Natalie is not only an excellent actress and incredibly hardworking, but also one of the biggest horror enthusiasts and fans of this franchise. When you put all that together you get something special. This franchise has had many strong female leads, but her performance might just be the strongest this franchise has ever had.”

Jason marks the 13th entry of Friday the 13th, an iconic horror franchise that launched with a namesake 1980 slasher. Working from a script by emerging screenwriter David Fliegel, Miner returned to the director’s chair after introducing Jason’s machete in Friday the 13th Part II and unveiling his hockey mask for the first time in Friday the 13th Part III. The film was made independently to afford the Barsamian family, which financed and produced and control the franchise through their company Horror, Inc., total creative control.

Directed by Sean S. Cunningham, the original Friday the 13th watches as a group of counselors at Camp Crystal Lake, preparing to reopen the summer camp, are stalked and killed by an unseen assailant, while the memory of a boy who drowned there years earlier hangs over the grounds. Produced independently for roughly $550,000 and acquired by Paramount in a bidding war, the film provided the foundation for a durable, low-budget sequel machine, with Paramount releasing early entries and and Warner Bros./New Line joining as distributor from the ’90s on. The original film surprised with the revelation of (SPOILER) matriarch Pamela Voorhees as the murderous antagonist, while later entries embraced the machete-wielding Jason himself as the franchise’s terrifying driving force.

Over the decades, Friday the 13th has spawned video games, immersive experiences, comic books, collectibles, apparel and more, alongside the films. Horror, Inc. launched the Jason Universe to usher in a new era of expansion in the franchise in 2024, and most recently, the franchise has been back in circulation via Crystal Lake, Peacock and A24’s prequel series, which premiered all eight episodes on Oct. 15.
