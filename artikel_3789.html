Phasmophobia Resonance update release date and all new features

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It’s nearly time for Phasmophobia’s next major update to arrive, but before it does, there’s still a bit more waiting to do. If you want to be prepared for its release, though, you may be wondering when its release date is and all of the new features it’ll add.

Every new Phasmophobia update introduces some exciting additions, changes, and improvements to the game, so if you’re eager for your ghost hunting experience to get an upgrade, there’s plenty to look forward to with this one. To help you prepare for this launch, here’s when the Resonance update will be released and all the new features it’s bringing to Phasmophobia.

The Resonance update for Phasmophobia is confirmed to be arriving sometime in November. The official Development Preview #22 blog states that it’ll be released in “late November,” so we can likely expect it to arrive somewhere between Nov. 15 to 30.

As of the time of writing, an official launch date for Resonance hasn’t been shared. As the month progresses, though, we’ll likely get more details for this upcoming update, including an official release date announcement.
