Track down this white off-roader for Moses.

The BF Bandito is one of the many cars in GTA 6 that Moses asks you to steal and bring to him for cold, hard cash. This one has a search area out in Buskin Swamp, but when you arrive it's not immediately clear where or how to steal the car.

The owner of this white offroad racer will drive around the map marked search area in a circle, as shown in the clip above. To get him to stop, simply park your car blocking the track and stand next to it. When he reaches you, he'll slow down and call out, at which point you should press L2 to focus on him and use the "Mislead" dialogue option to ask for help.

In my version, Lucia was wearing a ski-mask and had a baseball bat, which quickly convinced them all to run away (after a few swings), but either way, once he stops it's easy to steal the car. The baseball bat probably wasn't necessary, but hey, it's still non-lethal.

As you exit the swamp, you'll get shot at and pursued by a few of his friends, but the BF Bandito is fast enough to outrun them easily. Bring it back to Moses to get $3,400ish as reward, though this may vary based on condition.
