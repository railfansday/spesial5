Overwatch season 5 midseason patch brings buffs and nerfs, Stellar Blade collab, and Mythic Roadhog skin

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Only through conflict (and patch notes) do we evolve.

Winds of change have arrived in Overwatch yet again, and not just because Doomfist has returned to defeat Vendetta in the Reign of Talon storyline.

One final season drop awaits in 2026 before a new narrative begins in February, but before we get there, there’s a hefty midseason patch arriving today that we highlight below including a number of new hero changes, like buffs for Junker Queen and nerfs for Jetpack Cat and Doctrine.

OW’s latest collab is with Korean-made Stellar Blade, featuring these costumes soon appearing in the in-game shop.

It’s finally the big boy’s time. Necrotic Knight is Roadhog’s first Mythic skin, and it’s an ugly beauty. Here’s how it shakes out as you level it up with Mythic Prisms:

This is what you’re here for. Read on below for all of the balancing changes in this midseason patch.

A new event that runs from Nov. 3 to Dec. 7, players can play games and complete challenges to earn exclusive rewards like a new Legendary Doomfist skin and Vendetta’s Mythic weapon.

Want more loot boxes? Log in and win a game from Nov. 3 to Dec. 7 throughout multiple weeks for the free prizes.

Junkrat’s back with double XP and free loot boxes for all players who play matches from Nov. 19 to 23, with “flexible challenges that let you go all-in or pace yourself.”

With this update, Blizzard says it’s improved its tools to “combat gameplay sabotage,” such as “current detection tools that allow us to more accurately detect players engaging in disruptive behaviors, and we’ve already taken action against many players detected by these systems.”

Gameplay sabotage consists of “consistently and intentionally failing to contribute to team fights, repeatedly jumping off the map, and intentionally blocking team progress.” The game will now post a “Thank You” message for reporting players who use disruptive behavior.

Everyone loves a good firesale on Black Friday. From Nov. 3 to Dec. 7, several skin bundles will go on sale including a number of fan-favorite skins and returning skins, like the StarCraft Mega Bundle.
