Owala Dropped Coca-Cola Water Bottles—But Fans Have One Major Complaint

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

One particular group of soda devotees would like a word.

If you need a fridge cig or two to get through the day, stay seated. Beloved water bottle brand Owala has teamed up with none other than Coca-Cola to bring you another way to show off your soda obsession.

Last year, the brands teamed up on an exclusive Diet Coke-inspired Target collection, and now they're back with three new designs, plus a returning favorite. The four 24-ounce tumblers are currently available exclusively on Amazon for $34.99 each, ready to keep your DC ice-cold until the very last sip.

Fans are amped over the new drop. "Never ordered something so fast," one person commented on the social media announcement.

"It's not a want it's a need," another declared. Others weren't about to settle for just one, saying they "might need all of them." And some shoppers had already made their purchases: "Bag secured 🥤," one fan wrote, with another having “never been more excited” to secure theirs.

But while the reception was overwhelmingly positive, there was one major gripe among Coke Zero devotees: "ONCE AGAIN COKE ZERO IS IGNORED! 😢"

The collection includes designs inspired by classic Coca-Cola and Diet Coke, but fans were quick to point out the glaring omission. "No Coke Zero? I would have bought this in a heartbeat 🥹," one person wrote, with another calling them “cute” but still bummed about Coke Zero’s absence.

CZ fans are feeling seriously underrepresented, and clearly, I'm not alone in thinking they deserve some love. As one person demanded, "WHERES MY COKE ZERO?!?!" Maybe next year...
