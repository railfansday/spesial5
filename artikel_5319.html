Jumptuit Introduces Physical-World AI for Biotechnology, Spanning Ten Domains from Green to Red Bio

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Anticipatory Intelligence Framework and Large Dynamic Reasoning Model (LDRM) designed to observe the dynamic, real-world conditions that genomic research and Large Language Models alone cannot reach

NEW YORK, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Jumptuit Group announces biotechnology applications of its Anticipatory Intelligence Framework, a physical-world AI architecture designed to accelerate scientific discovery and advance the human condition. Powered by the Large Dynamic Reasoning Model (LDRM), a non-transformer model, the framework continuously observes cross-domain physical phenomena and the interaction of variables over time. It addresses ten biotechnology domains, from agriculture and marine systems to health, biosafety and governance. Details are available at jumptuit.co/biotechnology.

The physical world and human biology are dynamic open systems. According to industry estimates, 85% of global GDP resides in the physical world. According to UN DESA estimates, the global population reached 8.3 billion in 2026, all of it exposed to environmental factors from prenatal development onward.

Today's LLMs see little of this. The combinatorial space of environmental exposures dwarfs any existing training corpus, including those of frontier LLMs by several orders of magnitude. More than 200 million chemical substances have been cataloged, over 350,000 are registered for commercial production and use, and each can act on a genome of 3.1 billion base pairs. Of the genome's roughly 28 million CpG sites, about a fifth (5â6 million) change methylation state across cell types, and many more may respond to exposures over a lifetime.

Jumptuit's Physical World Intelligence: Built to Observe, Not Just Read

The Anticipatory Intelligence Framework draws on Jumptuit's Live Data Conduit, made up of Global Data Nets (GDNs) and Global Sensory Intelligence (GSI). The LDRM applies multi-mode reasoning (e.g, sign, comparative, causal, analogical, temporal, verbal), dynamic cross-sector threshold driven curiosity queries and urgency drivers to detect anomalies and track how variables change over time. The architecture is supported by the Jumptuit Group's growing USPTO patent portfolio, including the recently granted U.S. Patent No. 12,793,535.

"The limits of genomic research alone, and of Large Language Models, come from the same gap - neither can observe the physical world as it changes," said Donald Leka, Founder, Chairman and CEO of the Jumptuit Group. "Our Anticipatory Intelligence Framework is designed to close that gap, so researchers can see the invisible interactions that precede disease and disruption."

The Jumptuit Group develops physical-world artificial intelligence. The Company's Large Dynamic Reasoning Model (LDRM) and Anticipatory Intelligence Framework are designed to continuously observe, correlate and reason over real-time physical-world data across health, life and environmental sciences, biotechnology and the global economy. Learn more at jumptuit.co.
