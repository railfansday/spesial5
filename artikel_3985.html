CD Projekt Red has revealed its surprise at the animation studio behind Cyberpunk: Edgerunners immediately approving an internal character design instead of asking for something more "anime."

As revealed on an AnsweRED stream, the character of Avida – once King's old flame – was one of only a handful of characters designed in-house at CD Projekt Red.

The design, perhaps more unconventional than most in the anime space, depicted Avida as a tall, broad-shouldered, suit-wearing woman, one who prioritized comfort over fashion.

That is a far cry not only from most female designs in Edgerunners – compare and contrast with Rebecca and Talia, for example – but also the chromed-up citizens of Night City as a whole.

"I was actually really surprised when Studio Trigger accepted Avida's design right off the bat," executive producer Saya Elder said. "They were like, 'Yes, we are going to go with this character design.' "

Elder continued, "I really did love the character design when it first came out, but I thought that she would come out looking a lot more typical anime style, but it wasn't. They accepted our proposal off the bat and I was like, yes! I love Avida's blue lipstick. She looks sick and I'm so happy."

The CD Projekt Red team had also previously revealed how they had to turn down a Studio Trigger request to raze Night City to the ground. Probably for the best – especially if you're looking forward to that Cyberpunk sequel.

Those who still have unfinished business on Cyberpunk 2077 will be pleased to know there's now a new 2.5 update, complete with nods and references to the Edgerunners 2 crew.
