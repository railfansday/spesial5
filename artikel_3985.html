Hollow Knight: Silksong's Sea of Sorrow DLC Launches Next Month, and It's Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Developer Team Cherry has announced the release date and pricing for Sea of Sorrow, the DLC for Hollow Knight: Silksong, and it's great news on both accounts.

As revealed in its recent Cherry Time presentation, Sea of Sorrow will make its launch on 11th December, 2026, and even better, it will be free for all players.

There's a little more detail about the DLC on the official blog post. Here's what it says about Sea of Sorrow:

"In Sea of Sorrow, players will voyage above and beneath Pharloom's forgotten ocean, across islands, through forts and into deep caverns beneath the waves. We've spent the whole year stuffing this adventure full with all the things you'd expect from Hollow Knight: Silksong - crazed characters, high adventure, and epic bosses! It's also overflowing with more exceptional music by the eternally talented Christopher Larkin! We're pretty darn excited to get it into all of your hands on December 11, so please look forward to it."

Anybody who owns Silksong will be able to hop into this expansion at no extra cost, and it's only a few weeks away — pretty exciting stuff.

The Cherry Time presentation also highlighted several games that the company plans to publish under a new Team Cherry Presents label. No details about platforms were shared, but hopefully they'll make it to consoles whenever they're ready.
