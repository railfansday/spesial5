Watch The Carabao Cup Quarter-Final At Hill Dickinson Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Evertonians unable to make the trip to Fleetwood Town for the Club’s Carabao Cup quarter-final can still be part of the occasion, with a special live screening at Hill Dickinson Stadium.

The Blues travel to Highbury Stadium on Thursday 17 December (8pm GMT kick-off), with a place in the Carabao Cup semi-finals at stake.

For supporters not attending the fixture, the Club will open Kendall’s Social, located on Village Street at Hill Dickinson Stadium, offering Evertonians the opportunity to come together and get behind David Moyes’ side on a potentially memorable night.

The match will be shown live on big screens, creating a matchday atmosphere at the Club’s waterfront home as the Blues look to further progress in the competition.

Whether meeting friends and family, enjoying the pre-match build-up or cheering on every moment of the action, supporters can share the excitement of a cup occasion alongside fellow Evertonians.

Tickets for both adults and kids are priced at just £10 per person, including entry to Kendall’s Social and one complimentary drink token, redeemable at the bar.

Secure your place at Kendall’s Social and get behind the Blues!
