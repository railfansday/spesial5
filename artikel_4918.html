Bitcoin Price Fades Near $61,000 as Stocks Soar on Huang’s Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The bitcoin price slipped back to near $61,100 on Monday afternoon, giving up nearly all of its morning spike even as Wall Street held one of its biggest rallies in years on Nvidia’s decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO.

Bitcoin jumped above $64,000 shortly after the Nvidia news broke before 10 a.m. ET and topped out near $64,500. The move reversed within hours, and the bitcoin price dipped to around $60,800 by 2:45 p.m. ET. It remains up only modestly on the day.

The round trip was costly for leveraged traders. Bitcoin liquidations reached about $321 million, with short bets making up $224 million of that total. Liquidations across the broader crypto market topped $762 million.

Stocks kept climbing while bitcoin slid. The Nasdaq Composite was up about 6.9% near 24,170 at about 3:40 p.m. ET, while the S&P 500 gained about 5% and the Dow rose about 3.2%. The S&P 500 had fallen seven straight days before Monday.

Nvidia (NVDA) led the move, trading near $165.66, up about 32% from Friday’s close of $125.53. The board voted unanimously to bring Huang back as president, CEO and director, 12 days after he resigned.

A stronger dollar may be weighing on the bitcoin price. The dollar index rose about 0.65% to 104.40, its strongest level since April 2025, as the stock rebound put more Federal Reserve rate hikes back in play. The Fed meets Dec. 8-9, and a quarter-point hike remains the base case.

Adam Haeems, head of asset management at Tesseract Group, pointed to steady selling during U.S. market hours since Huang first stepped down. “Bitcoin has fallen in seven of the eight U.S. sessions since Huang went, today’s included, and last week each session’s fall lined up with that day’s ETF outflow,” he said.

Crypto stocks held up far better than the bitcoin price. Strategy (MSTR) was up about 9.3% near $85.03 in afternoon trading, after it disclosed a purchase of 457 bitcoin last week. Coinbase (COIN) gained about 6.7% to near $108.78, Robinhood (HOOD) rose about 8.8% to near $67.64 and Circle (CRCL) added about 8% to near $51.33.

Miners with AI data center businesses posted the biggest moves. IREN (IREN) jumped about 23% to near $14.57, CleanSpark (CLSK) rose about 12.6% and MARA Holdings (MARA) gained about 10%. Core Scientific (CORZ) climbed about 9.4% and Riot Platforms (RIOT) added about 7.1%.

Monday’s spot bitcoin ETF flows are the next test. The funds lost about $1.79 billion last week, their fourth straight week of net outflows, as bitcoin slid toward $58,000 on Friday and hit a weekend low near $55,100.

“Tonight’s ETF flow figure is the first real test of this bounce,” Haeems said. Strong inflows would suggest buyers are returning, while another round of outflows would raise doubts about how far the rebound can run.

Traders also face Wednesday’s inflation data and thin trading around Thanksgiving in a holiday-shortened week. Wintermute OTC trader Jasper De Maere said he isn’t ready to call a bottom for the bitcoin price. “A few days of data do not confirm a trend and these series turn slowly,” he said.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $61,100.
