Here’s what happened in crypto today

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Need to know what happened in crypto today? Here is the latest news on daily trends and events impacting Bitcoin price, blockchain, DeFi, Web3 and crypto regulation.

Today in crypto, Jane Street increased its share count in Fidelity’s spot Bitcoin ETF nearly eightfold in the third quarter, US President Donald Trump nominated four new SEC and CFTC commissioners, including a replacement for prominent crypto advocate Hester Peirce and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang abruptly resigned.

Jane Street’s share count in Fidelity’s spot Bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF) rose nearly eightfold in the third quarter, while its holdings in BlackRock’s fund fell 19%.

The trading giant ended September with 7.01 million shares of Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC0, up from about 887,000 at the end of June. The position was worth $509 million on Sept. 30, according to its second-quarter and third-quarter filings.

Jane Street’s holdings in BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT) fell to 20.22 million shares from 24.88 million. A higher share price lifted the reported value of its stake to $957 million from $828 million.

Jane Street was the largest reported institutional holder of BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF at the end of June after boosting its share count by 324% in Q2, according to 13F Pro. IBIT is the world’s largest spot Bitcoin ETF with $56 billion in net assets as of Thursday.

The firm also added nearly 9 million shares (about $249 million) of the ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF (ARKB), while more than doubling its holdings in BlackRock’s iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA) by 7.83 million shares (about $158 million).

US President Donald Trump has nominated Steven Levine to replace former Securities and Exchange Commission Commissioner Hester Peirce, one of the agency’s most prominent crypto advocates, as part of four nominations to fill vacancies at the SEC and Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

The White House’s latest announcement on Thursday named two nominees each for the SEC and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, agencies whose decisions help shape US cryptocurrency regulation, among other functions. All four nominations require Senate confirmation.

The nominations would replenish several commissioner roles. The SEC currently has only two of its five commissioner seats filled, while CFTC Chairman Michael Selig is the sole member of his agency’s five-seat commission.

The nominations come weeks before Democrats are set to take control of the Senate in January, giving a narrow window for Republicans to confirm Trump’s picks.

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as president and CEO and stepped down from the company’s board on Thursday, effective immediately. The board named Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress interim CEO.

Kress will retain her role as CFO while Nvidia’s board considers internal and external candidates for the permanent CEO role. The board also appointed Harvey C. Jones its first chair and said it expects Nvidia’s operations to proceed without interruption.

Nvidia shares fell more than 13% shortly after the US market opened on Thursday, trading at $151.00 as of 2:31 p.m. UTC, according to Yahoo Finance. Bitcoin (BTC) fell alongside Nvidia shares, dropping as much as 3.5% to a multimonth low.

The shares later pared losses to trade at $165 at 4:24 p.m. UTC, down 5% on the day. The stock was down more than 26% over the previous month.
