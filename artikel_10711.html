Final Fantasy 14 players hold in-game funerals across servers after official band bassist dies

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Final Fantasy 14 players lit campfires across the game’s servers after the MMO’s official rock band lost its bassist.

The FF14 community has a habit of showing up for each other, having watered locked-out strangers’ crops during an outage last month so nothing wilted. This time, they came together to celebrate a musician who had played a rollicking Fan Festival set in Tokyo just ten days earlier, and they knew exactly where to gather.

Square Enix confirmed on November 11 that Eikichi Iwai had died suddenly at 57, with its official statement saying the news “has come as a profound shock” to the whole team.

Iwai played bass in Final Fantasy 14’s official rock band, which lead composer Masayoshi Soken started in 2014 to turn the MMO’s music into heavy rock at fan events, and that stage is exactly where players had last seen him.

Within hours, players across several servers began holding in-game vigils, hearkening back to the ones the community staged for Berserk creator Kentaro Miura in 2021 and Dragon Ball creator Akira Toriyama in 2024.

The biggest took shape on the European server Omega, where players formed a ring of campfires around Limsa Lominsa’s Aetheryte Plaza, the seaside city’s main teleport hub, and sat shoulder to shoulder along its edges.

The tributes didn’t stop at Omega, either. Players on Zodiark lined their own plaza with campfires, while on Japanese servers a crowd packed around a glowing blue statue of Ifrit, the fire god players face early in the story, and waved red glow sticks like the front row at a concert.

One group of players even staged an in-game tribute concert, and over in Oceania, a player who started a solo vigil in the glowing flower fields of Elpis found others “still here 7 hours later.”

Square Enix’s MMO communities have rallied around a loss before, too, as Final Fantasy 11’s original developers paid tribute earlier this year to a 91-year-old player who spent nearly two decades in Vana’diel.
