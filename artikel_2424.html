Official: Chatzialexiou to assume Frankfurt sporting director role ahead of schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Joti Chatzialexiou will take over as sporting director of Eintracht Frankfurt on 1 December 2026, succeeding Markus Krösche.

This was officially announced by his current employer in second division side 1.FC Nürnberg that the deal will go ahead prematurely.

Originally, his start date was scheduled for 1 February 2027, after Chatzialexiou exercised his release clause of just under €500,000.

Now, the clubs have agreed on a new deal, upon which Peter Meier, chairman of the supervisory board of the Bavarian club, explains:

“We are pleased that the transition has now been settled and all details clarified," Meier's statement read.

"We would like to take this opportunity to thank Joti Chatzialexiou once again for his time at the club.

"In his approximately two and a half years, he initiated many positive projects and also left a lasting impression on a personal level.

We wish him all the best for his future endeavors, both personally and professionally," Meier concluded.
