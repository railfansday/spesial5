Ethereum Outflows Hit All-Time Low on Binance

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As demand for major crypto assets has increased, Ethereum remains on hold amid growing market fear and uncertainty, while exchange activities across the broad crypto market are showing a bearish trend.

While Ethereum remains one of the most affected cryptocurrencies during the prolonged crypto market volatility, the latest data from the crypto analytics platform CryptoQuant shows a massive slowdown in its exchange outflows.

Amid growing market fear, Ethereum outflows from Binance over the last month have hit a historic low as investors continue to move fewer ETH tokens away from the world’s largest crypto exchange, Binance.

The data shows that about 3.58 million ETH have been withdrawn from Binance over the last 30 days, marking an all-time low in Ethereum outflows on the exchange.

Further data shows that the decline has come as Ethereum continues to struggle to stabilize its price and continues to weaken, trading around the $1,700 mark.

It is important to note that the decline in exchange outflows is expected, considering the growing market weakness and negative shift in investor sentiment over the period.

However, the metric has drawn different attention from market participants, given expectations that the massive security breach Binance suffered should trigger large withdrawals from the exchange.

Although blockchain trackers recently identified multiple large Ethereum withdrawals by the Binance hacker, it appears that the moves did not materialize into increased Ethereum outflows.

Apart from the attackers' withdrawals, it appears that Ethereum holders on the exchange did not respond to the attack in the conventional way, as security incidents like this often make investors more cautious and wary about keeping their assets on the exchange.

While this typically prompts them to transfer the assets into private wallets where they have greater control, it appears that Ethereum holders remained comfortable with keeping their funds on the exchange.
