Ripple’s Voting Stake In XRP Treasury Gets Capped

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Ripple’s Voting Stake In XRP Treasury Gets Capped

Ripple’s voting rights on XRP-only treasury are limited to 9.99%, implying decentralization’s the status quo.

Ripple’s exposure to the XRP-only Evernorth corporate treasury since the NASDAQ debut has been an open debate. Since the October merger, Evernorth’s holdings have imposed a limitation on voting rights ownership, specifically capped at 9.99%.

This gives Ripple Lab’s executives the ability to retain economies of scale through a separate subsidiary class. That implies a strong distinction between financial exposure and voting control. The restriction doesn’t mean Ripple surrendered their additional economic exposure.

Actually, the equity interests exceeding the permitted share allocation remained in the Pathfinder units, which carry distinct rights from ordinary publicly-traded Evernorth(XRPN) shares. The complexity behind Evernorth’s $1 billion corporate treasury fund is laid out in a SEC filing.

The company also had cash from its SPAC combination and later financing like convertible notes & practical warrant exercises.

Other Evernorth fund investors received miscellaneous ownership arrangements. SBI-affiliated investors were subject to a 39.9% ownership threshold, while the sponsor’s allocation reflected a 19.9% XRPN voting threshold. Evernorth’s XRPN debuted on NASDAQ on October 12, 2026.

As previously reported by DailyCoin, Evernorth mulls issuing more than 5.2 million new shares through a financing arrangement with Cantor, which could pull in enough capital for considerable XRP purchases to support the corporate fund’s exponential growth.

At an assumed $10 share price, selling the full allocation would generate approximately $50.6 million before expenses, according to the prospectus. Meanwhile, the Ripple-based exchange traded funds (ETFs) slimmed by $6.14 million over the past 7 days.

With Wall Street’s big boys reducing their XRP exposure based on Spot market’s XRP pricing, it’s clear that crypto whales aren’t keen on buying the dip right now. Judging from the real-time figures on the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) on multiple time-frames, big-time investors are selling.

With the Parabolic Stop & Reverse (SAR) meter flashing above the current XRP price, this trend signal is a signature of ongoing bearish persistence. Alongside the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) dwelling in red, the current $0.90 support level could fall into the broader $0.65 liquidity pocket.
