Amex Platinum Card Bonus: As High As 175K Points Welcome Offer (Check Yours)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The American Express Platinum Card® is one of the most popular premium cards on the market, and it currently has an incredible welcome offer that’s as high as 175,000 bonus points, making it the ideal time to apply. In this post, I’d like to go over all the details of the current Amex Platinum bonus, including who is eligible, and why you might want to apply.

While the Amex Platinum has a steep annual fee, it also has compelling perks that will make it worthwhile for many, ranging from lounge access to credits. If you’re eligible for the current bonus, then picking up the card and giving it a try is a no brainer, in my opinion.

Link: Learn more about the American Express Platinum Card®

The Amex Platinum Card welcome offer is potentially very lucrative, with a twist. The Amex Platinum bonus is marketed as being as high as 175,000 Membership Rewards points after spending $12,000 within the first six months. However, Amex notes how the welcome offers vary, and applicants may not be eligible for that welcome offer.

The way this works, you can apply for the card, and then find out your offer amount. Then you can decide whether you want to accept the Amex Platinum welcome offer or not. That’s an unconventional approach for a welcome offer, though I appreciate that Amex has the “apply with confidence” feature, so there won’t be a hard pull unless you accept the offer.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Membership Rewards points to be worth 1.7 cents each. That means I value 175,000 points at a staggering $2,975, making it among the best welcome offers out there. Of course that assumes you’re eligible for this Amex Platinum bonus, which not everyone will be.

The spending requirement is significant, though you do have six months to complete it, so averaging $2,000 per month in spending might not be that hard for many people. There are lots of creative ways to reach minimum spending requirements, including things like paying quarterly taxes by credit card, if eligible.

This is potentially one of the best welcome offers that we’ve ever seen on the card, and publicly available welcome offers don’t get much better than this. In some cases people may be targeted for even bigger offers, but it’s rare. To maximize your odds of getting a targeted offer, make sure you’re opted in to getting marketing communications (by email and mail) from Amex.

There are lots of great uses for Amex points, as they can be transferred to roughly 20 airline and hotel partners, and those are the best ways to get outsized value.
