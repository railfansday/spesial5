GTA 6 Could Get PC Release Soon to Avoid AI Clones, Says Former Rockstar Dev

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Lauren has experience in the gaming and esports space, writing news, guides, features, reviews, lists, conducting interviews, and more. Before joining the team at DualShockers, Lauren was a Features Writer at Adventure Gamers, a Guides Writer at Gameranx, as well as contributing as a News Writer at PCInvasion and Daily

The launch of GTA 6 is imminent, and thanks to Rockstar Games moving the pre-load date forward, the release of this highly anticipated game is starting to feel more real. However, not even the big dogs are safe from the likes of AI, as there are concerns Generative AI could be used by third parties to port the game on PC.

It’s becoming increasingly common to see an indie hit take off and AI-generated clones quickly following in an attempt to piggy back off of that success. One such example is the viral Needle In A Haystack game, which follows the premise of finding a needle hidden in over 10 million pieces of hay with your friends. Since then, various AI-generated rip offs began appearing on Steam, with one developer even stealing the original game’s code.

Rockstar's 38 new GTA 6 screenshots showcase incredibly detailed faces, Vice City's nightlife, and the gangs and scenes bringing Leonida to life.

Roblox is another platform that Needle In A Haystack, among other trending games, has popped up on. Since Roblox is free to play, anyone can play a near copy of a paid game, with a few microtransactions thrown in.

Although GTA 6 would be difficult to replicate in the same way, former Rockstar North technical director, Obbe Vermeij, believes AI is a threat to an official GTA 6 PC port, as discussed in an interview with Rock Paper Shotgun.

Vermeij acknowledges that Generative AI “really has changed the world," and believes, “I would be concerned, if I was Rockstar, that somebody would just take the PS5 version and get AI to change it to PC, which won't be as good as their effort, but it could still also hurt their sales. So, I wouldn't be surprised if there was an effort to get [the releases] a little bit closer together. Obviously, I don't really know what they're thinking."

"I don't really see how you could protect against that, to be honest, I don't see it," he says, but Take-Two Interactive aren’t completely powerless on the matter. “But a big company like Take-Two, they would be able to get the lawyers on the case, and maybe, like, you said, for a big game, maybe the AI doesn't really do a particularly good job.”

“But yeah, I mean, it's a brave new world. What was the case before, is that there's so much effort in developing the game, that it is not possible to rip it off, and that's completely changed now. So, yeah, we're going to have to see in the next couple of years, how that's going to pan out."

It has been widely reported that GTA 6 will be released on PC during the first quarter of 2027. Neither Rockstar Games nor Take-Two has provided comment on the status of the arrival of GTA 6 on PC.

If GTA 6 does make its PC debut in the first quarter of 2027, it would land just a few months after its release on PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S platforms. Therefore, it would be a lot quicker than the PC release of GTA 5, which came out in April 2015, nearly two years after its roll out on console in September 2013.

Any AI-generated ports would likely face a DMCA takedown and will undoubtedly be nowhere near as polished or safe to run than the real deal.

As AI continues to advance, it will be interesting to see if it’s able to replicate a game as huge as GTA 6. The game is expected to require up to 212 GB of storage on console and is packed to the brim with approximately 80 hours of story content alone.
