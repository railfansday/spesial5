Rico Lewis to push for instant Man City exit after new reveal - verdict

Rico Lewis is surely on track to leave Man City after hinting that he wants his game time to increase in the next 12 months.

Lewis' future was up in the air over the summer as Nottingham Forest registered a serious interest in his signature.

He remained at Man City for the season ahead but has continued to struggle for minutes despite Enzo Maresca's arrival.

The 21-year-old has only started one Premier League game in this campaign so far and doubts over his future have started to rise ahead of January.

New comments around his aims for the near future and favoured position on the pitch both surely point to an inevitable departure from the Etihad in the coming months.

Lewis is bound to attract attention from more Premier League clubs and has all the tools to be a regular at the top level away from a highly competitive City squad.

Lewis is under contract at City until 2030, but seems as close as ever to exiting his boyhood club following a tough start to the campaign.

He has only appeared in six competitive games under Maresca so far, with just two appearances and 102 minutes played in the league.

Lewis detailed his aims for the future and where he would prefer to play in a recent interview with GIVEMESPORT, saying: "I’ve enjoyed playing wherever, but now I’m kind of getting that feeling where I enjoy helping the team going forward. So I think the best place to do that is in the midfield for me. Finding that joy of scoring, assisting, that’s what’s kind of driving me to that feeling.

"The goal in 12 months’ time is playing as many games as I can. Preparing myself mentally, not playing, not playing, not playing, then getting the chance to play, there’s no point in wasting that 90 minutes wishing I was playing those other games I’m playing right now. For me, [my aims] would be literally playing as many games as I can do...having that experience of playing sustained minutes and consistently."

Lewis has only ever featured somewhat regularly at right-back for City, and their outstanding midfield depth surely means he has no chance of breaking through there anytime soon.

Sources have already exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Lewis could look to leave City in January if he continues to struggle for minutes.

His latest comments highlight a player who knows what is best for his career at 21-years-old, but will surely have to move elsewhere to fulfil his aims for the next year.

Using Carabao Cup stats, which are the biggest sample size for Lewis so far in 2026-27, it is clear to see that he stands little chance of nailing down a spot as City's main right-back anytime soon.

He ranks poorly among full-backs in the competition for chances created per 90 (0.54), duels won per 90 (4.88), aerial duels won per 90 (0.54) and defensive contributions per 90 (4.34).

With so many bodies ahead of him in midfield, such lacklustre stats only further the point that he is surely set to leave the club very soon.
