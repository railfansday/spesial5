'Everybody was fighting for the cause' - Crawley Town boss encouraged by hard-fought Fleetwood win

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Goals from Kai Jennings and Pemi Aderoju put the Reds in control in Blackwell’s first game in charge since his appointment until the end of the season on Thursday (November 19).

Will Davies’ late goal for the visitors made it a nervy finale.

But the hosts defended resolutely to earn only their second win in the league this campaign – and their first at home since March.

And Blackwell, while pleased to earn all three points, is keen to quickly build on the positive result.

He said: “It's always great when you win, isn't it? And it's been a long time coming this season, so we will enjoy it but know that we've got a tough game against Barnet on Tuesday [November 24].

“It's just that process now of games coming and hopefully trying to improve the squad and improve the team, and hopefully we get some more results at home.”

Despite the win, Crawley remain bottom of League Two but are unbeaten in their last three games.

Blackwell referenced the high stakes currently at play each time the Reds are in action.

He said: “When you're down where we are, if it can go wrong, it will go wrong. So, we've got to be careful. We don't take risks in some areas. Make sure we fight our socks off. It's as simple as that.

“We're in a dogfight. I want plenty of dogs fighting for us. And you saw today, everybody was fighting for the cause, and that's what I want. They've got to be proud to be a professional footballer, proud to be at Crawley Town, and proud to work for me.”

Blackwell added: “I said to the boys as we went out, ‘this is Crawley Town, this is our home. I don't want anybody coming here thinking, ‘oh, we're playing Crawley, it’s nice and easy’.

“I want them to go back thinking, ‘we've got to get on the bus and do a five-hour trip after being at Crawley’. That's what I want. And I think today, Fleetwood will get on the bus and think, ‘that was a tough one at Crawley today’.”

As the Reds boss alluded to, his side are back in action at the Broadfield against second-placed Barnet on Tuesday night.
