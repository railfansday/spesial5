XRP Price Stalls Below $1 as XRPL Wipes Testnet and Devnet

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

XRP price is still struggling to get back above $1, even after the Nvidia-led market bounce that happened to be just a temporary day. Now, the XRP Ledger has another headline, with developers preparing to wipe its Testnet and Devnet tomorrow following an infrastructure failure.

The reset is scheduled for November 25, between 5 and 6 PM Pacific time. Everything sitting on the two networks goes. Accounts, balances, transactions, AMMs, escrows, offers, and other ledger data will be cleared. With this going on, developers are forced to rebuild their testing environments.

XRP, meanwhile, couldn’t make much of Monday’s rebound. Ripple briefly reclaimed $1 before being pushed back down in a dead cat bounce situation.

The reset is not a routine wipe, so XRPL’s Testnet depended on a single full-history server that crashed and could not be recovered. Ripple says it is increasing storage capacity to address the problem and reduce how frequently the network needs resetting.

Developers will lose their existing setups and need to collect fresh test XRP from the faucet. Those running their own servers will also need to clear their databases and restart them after the reset.

Testnet is used to mirror Mainnet’s amendment status, while Devnet gives developers a place to experiment with proposed features. Both serve as sandboxes, meaning the reset won’t touch real XRP or live ledger activity.

A clean testing environment matters when teams are building new applications, experimenting with features, or checking integrations before they reach the live network. Losing existing test data is inconvenient, but keeping a broken full-history server running isn’t much of an option either.

Ripple says the network URLs, IDs, and validator settings will remain unchanged. Developers can reuse some addresses, but recreated AMM pools will generally have different account addresses.

With XRP struggling to find a catalyst, attention is also turning to Bitcoin Hyper, whose token launch is scheduled for November 26. The project has raised more than $33.3 million during its presale, with HYPER priced at just $0.0136877 ahead of the planned token generation event.

Its launch schedule puts token claims and trading on Thursday, followed by the network’s mainnet launch on December 3.

Bitcoin Hyper is pitching a different kind of utility, aiming to bring faster Bitcoin transactions and Solana Virtual Machine-based applications to a Bitcoin Layer 2. The planned network includes a bridge, staking, and DeFi functionality, putting its ambitions closer to an application ecosystem than a simple token launch.

With just two days left before the TGE, presale holders are approaching the point where months of buying finally meet an open market, making now a final stretch to enter at the current price.

The reset announcement comes at a less comfortable time for XRP traders. The token has been battered through November, and the bounce following Nvidia’s news offered an opportunity to recover some of those losses. But it didn’t last.

XRP briefly moved above $1, but the level quickly turned into a ceiling again. Buyers have struggled to follow through, and another failed attempt leaves the token hovering around $0.94, and it lost momentum for a sustained reversal.

The $0.90 area is now one of the levels XRP price needs to protect. A deeper drop could bring the recent lows back into view, while a clean move through $1 would give the first meaningful sign that selling pressure is easing.

XRP got a chance to ride the market higher this week and couldn’t hold the move. Until buyers can push it back above $1 without immediately giving ground, the recovery remains unfinished business.
