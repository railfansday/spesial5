Anthropic plans to offer 825 million shares at an indicative price of $70–$78 each, targeting up to $64.35 billion through its public offering, according to a draft prospectus reviewed by Reuters. The proposed offering could raise between $57.75 billion and $64.35 billion. The company is also planning a $26 billion private placement involving major technology companies. Potential cornerstone investors include AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC partners. The proposed IPO would give investors an opportunity to buy shares in the AI company through the public market.
