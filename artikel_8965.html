nick cave and india pavilion win the visitors’ lions at the venice art biennale 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

following the resignation of the international jury, this year’s biennale gives audiences a direct role in deciding which artists and national presentations receive recognition.

The public has selected its favorites at the 61st Venice Art Biennale, with American artist Nick Cave receiving the Visitors’ Lion for Best Participant and India winning Best National Participation for its pavilion, Geographies of Distance: Remembering Home. Announced by La Biennale di Venezia on November 25th, 2026, the awards reflect the preferences of visitors to In Minor Keys, curated by the late Koyo Kouoh.

Following the resignation of the International Jury, the institution introduced two Visitors’ Lions, inviting audiences to vote for the Best Participant in In Minor Keys and the Best National Participation throughout the exhibition. The decision placed public engagement at the center of this year’s awards, giving visitors a direct role in determining which artistic presentations received recognition.

Born in Chicago in 1959, Cave receives the recognition for his participation in the main exhibition. Working across sculpture, performance, textiles, and installation, the artist has developed a practice that explores identity, material culture, race, and the possibilities of transformation. His work frequently brings together elaborate material compositions and performative gestures, creating spaces where art becomes an encounter between the body, movement, and collective experience.

India’s winning national participation brings together five artists: Alwar Balasubramaniam, Sumakshi Singh, Ranjani Shettar, Asim Waqif, and Skarma Sonam Tashi. Commissioned by the National Gallery of Modern Art (NGMA), Ministry of Culture, and curated by Amin Jaffer, the presentation occupied the Arsenale. Its title frames the exhibition around questions of distance and belonging, placing the idea of home at the center of a collective artistic presentation.
