New Pokémon Day 2027 Collection Unveiled For January

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Pokémon is forever being celebrated, with The Pokémon Company looking for endless anniversaries and events to put the series back in the spotlight and have an excuse to release even more products. It's great for fans, of course, who relish the opportunity to collect as many new pieces of Pokémon merchandise as they can, as well as get an insight into what is next for the series. Indeed, the numerous 30th anniversary celebrations gave us the best look at the next main game as well as several Pokémon surprises fans weren't expecting.

Now, in celebration of the upcoming Pokémon Day, The Pokémon Company has unveiled yet another collection that prospective purchasers can buy. This new collection offers fans a great way to collect what is undoubtedly one of the best TCGs on the market and potentially even get a few rare cards while they're at it. It joins the pantheon of other recent Pokémon collections that have celebrated the various mainline games, and will almost certainly be accompanied by even more merchandise and reveals when the day finally arrives.

Pokémon Day rolls around on February 27 every single year as a celebration of all things Pokémon and to mark the anniversary of the day the very first games in the series, Red and Green, originally launched. It typically sees The Pokémon Company reveal brand-new Pokémon collections, games, and updates. Crucially, however, several pieces of merchandise are often released to further promote it. Such is the case with the upcoming Pokémon Day 2027 Collection, which bundles together a handful of Pokémon TCG packs and a rare card to celebrate the event.

The Pokémon Day 2027 Collection features three different Pokémon booster packs, a Pikachu from the Ascended Heroes set printed on cosmos holofoil, a commemorative coin with Pikachu's face in the center and Pokémon Day 2027 embossed around it, and a code card. It's a fairly large collection of TCG-related items, which is great for collectors or those just starting to get into the hobby. Of course, these will be made available at select retailers when released.

The exact same collection was released last year as well. The only difference was that the box came with a Pikachu from the Temporal Forces set, the coin said 2026, and the booster packs were from other sets. It's become a bit of a tradition for this collection to drop at the same time and for the same price each year; so, those who enjoyed getting it last year, and especially those eager to collect all of the unique coins, can look forward to it again this year.

The Pokémon Day 2027 Collection box launches on January 29, 2027, almost an entire month before Pokémon Day even begins. It costs $14.99, which is ever so slightly more expensive than the cost of the three booster packs alone (which would be $13.47 together at the standard price of $4.49 per pack). So, if you've been looking for yet another way to celebrate Pokémon, or are interested in a cheap and easy way to get a few cards to start your TCG collection, then it may be worth checking out this Pokémon Day 2027 Collection.
