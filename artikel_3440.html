8 Years Later, Netflix’s Near-Perfect Political Thriller Is Still One of the Best Ever Made

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

From Hostage to The Diplomat, Netflix has slowly but surely established itself as the go-to platform for well-done and fast-paced political thrillers. The Diplomat, for instance, hit the jackpot for the streaming giant, reaching not only unprecedented views as it gets renewed for a fifth season, but critical acclaim too, including 10 Primetime Emmy nominations and one recent win for Allison Janney in 2026 for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series.

But while The Diplomat continues to dominate on Netflix, another 2018 political thriller on the platform is beginning to emerge as the perfect next watch for its viewers. The series Bodyguard, which first released on August 26, 2018, stars Richard Madden in his best role to date, and delivers a twisted, unpredictable tale involving political threats, international politics, and unbelievably high stakes.

Created by Jed Mercurio, Bodyguard follows war veteran and metropolitan police officer David Budd (Madden), as he earns a position as part of Protection Command after helping to diffuse a potential terrorist attack in the first fifteen minutes of the series. In the new role, David, who is currently separated from his wife Vicky (Sophie Rundle), and is struggling to connect with his two kids, is assigned to guard Julia Montague (Keeley Hawes), the UK's ambitious, polarizing Conservative Home Secretary. The catch: he fundamentally disagrees with everything she stands for. David, who suffers from PTSD from his time in combat, vehemently opposes her political agenda, including sending troops (himself included) to Afghanistan, and can't help but to be angered and question Julia's beliefs and policies.

From their first conversation, the tension is felt between the protagonists. David objects to everything Julia is fighting for, and she couldn't care less about his opinion. Nevertheless, she needs his reassurance that she will be protected at all times. While their dynamic is intriguing by itself, the plot soon thickens as David’s protection of Julia is fraught with attempts on her life, almost all of which he has some personal connection to. Soon, David finds himself in the middle of an intricate web of conspiracies connected to either himself or Julia. There's a political power play involving her sweeping new surveillance legislation, which earns her plenty of dangerous enemies in high places. There are people within her own political orbit who want her humiliated. There are people further out who want her dead. And there's at least one person in Budd's immediate circle whose loyalties are not what they appear to be. As he uncovers each of these puzzles pieces, the task of protecting Julia only becomes more and more challenging.

From the premise alone, it's safe to say Bodyguard is a Netflix must-watch. With complex opposing characters at its center, and a web of conspiracies that becomes all the more encompassing and dangerous as David investigates, the series has all the ingredients to be a gripping political thriller. On Rotten Tomatoes, the series was a hit, both with critics and audiences. The series earned 93% with critics, and 84% with audiences. "One of those rare shows that will have you hooked on the screen from the very first scene to the last," wrote one critic's review.

You said you’d watch one episode. That was eight hours ago.

Of course, another highlight is Madden himself, who first rose to fame in roles like Game of Thrones and Cinderella, and is showing a new side of himself in the six-episode series. "There are innumerable twists and turns to what becomes a kind of Whodunnit, but through it all, Madden makes us feel every single moment of what David is experiencing, most especially when confronting his insecurities about his failures as a husband and father," wrote Allison Keene in her review for Collider in 2018. "He has a job to do — several, in fact — and focusing on that is the only thing he believes is keeping him sane."
