Man Fatally Shot Wife Over Fight About Christmas Gifts for Their 4 Children

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A man in Georgia has been accused of fatally shooting his wife after they got into an argument over Christmas gifts for their four children.

The Walker County Sheriff’s Office said in a press release that Gene Allen has been charged with murder in connection to the death of his wife, Bonnie Allen.

Gene, 33, is currently being held at the Walker County Jail without bond.

The killing took place on November 14. Deputies received a report just before 8 p.m. stating that a person had been shot. Officers then responded to a home in Rossville, Georgia, and a child directed them to a bedroom where Gene was giving Bonnie, 32, CPR, according to an incident report reviewed by the Chattanooga Times Free Press.

Authorities reported that Bonnie had suffered a gunshot wound to the head and there was blood spilling out from her chest.

A deputy took over CPR and noted that Bonnie was not breathing and had no detectable heartbeat. Bonnie was then taken to a nearby hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

While speaking to investigators, Gene allegedly said that the couple had gotten into an argument earlier that day about Christmas gifts for their children.

He was then arrested on Friday, November 20, and taken into custody. His bond hearing has not yet taken place, and the sheriff’s office said he will appear in court “at a later date.”

It’s not currently clear whether Gene has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Walker County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.

Following her death, Bonnie was remembered as “a devoted mother and loving family member whose greatest joy was spending time with those she loved. Bonnie was an amazing mother who never missed an opportunity to support her children.”

“Bonnie loved the outdoors and enjoyed camping, spending time at the lake, and making memories at the beach,” her obituary read. “Her love for the Lord was evident in her life, and she often expressed her faith and the goodness of God to those around her. She was a blessing to everyone fortunate enough to know her.”

The tribute continued, “Bonnie had a joy that was contagious and a personality that could light up any room. She was fun-loving, full of life, and always brought happiness wherever she went. A strong, natural leader, Bonnie had a way of encouraging others, making people feel welcome, and leaving a lasting impression on everyone she met. She will be remembered for her beautiful spirit, unwavering love for her family, her faith, and the joy she brought into so many lives.”
