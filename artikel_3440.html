3 Best Movies To Watch On Netflix This Weekend (November 28-29)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Aside from Obsession, the other two projects were Netflix Original movies, and all three movies I recommend watching this weekend fall in that category. According to FlixPatrol, Heartland is the only film not to be trending on the streaming service. Obsession ranks 7th on the coveted chart, while the Jared Padalecki and Leighton Meester rom-com, Guarding Stars, stands even higher, at 3rd.

As for the new recommendations, they are divided into live-action and animated offerings. There is only one of the latter, and it is a new spin on a classic story that speaks to viewers of all ages, making it exciting. When it comes to the live-action releases, one of them is a new war epic that is sure to tug at the heartstrings.

The other movie on our Netflix list is a gritty retelling of another classic, making this an engaging weekend on Netflix. Expect to see stars like Vincent Cassel, Bill Skarsgård, Amanda Seyfried, Daniel Radcliffe, Peter Dinklage, and many more. These are the best movies to watch on Netflix this weekend:

I believe it is best to start with the only animated movie on the list, and anyone who has watched Disney movies over the years will be at the very least familiar with this one. After all, Steps focuses on Cinderella's stepsisters, showcasing why they might be as mean and evil as they seem. It is a fun way to experience a fan-favorite story, and Steps comes with plenty of charismatic performances.

Steps' cast features Amanda Seyfried, who is coming off a surprise turn in one of the movies of the year, Nathan Fielder's You Can See Everything, as Cinderella. The animated fantasy movie also boasts names like Ali Wong, Daniel Radcliffe, Stephanie Hsu, Peter Dinklage, Bette Midler, and more. Steps is fun, colorful, and a story about sisterhood, rather than the traditional Cinderella romance route.

Continuing on the train of new ways to take in classic stories is The Hunchback, Soul of Paris, which was released on Netflix worldwide this Friday, November 27. I believe most have experienced this tale through the Disney animated movie The Hunchback of Notre Dame, which is an adaptation of the 1831 novel of the same name by Victor Hugo. Well, Netflix's new take on the material is disruptive.

Rather than taking place in 1482, the new film sets the action in 1830, changing everything. The Hunchback, Soul of Paris is grittier, with a cholera outbreak making this a more somber approach to the source material. Hugo himself is a character in the French movie, which explores Quasimodo as a hunchbacked stonemason who Hugo really saw in real life. Get ready to take part in a creative, albeit disruptive, ride.

Finally, after The Hunchback, Soul of Paris took our Netflix movie recommendations into a more serious and mature territory, The Mosquito Bowl is the perfect movie to end the list. The war epic was released on Netflix on November 25, and it follows four college football stars who decide to enlist in the US Marines following the attack at Pearl Harbor.

The event that names the movie was a historical football game played by 65 marines on Christmas Eve in 1944. After that would come the Battle of Okinawa, where over a dozen of those who played in the Mosquito Bowl would die. The war epic is heart-wrenching, with solid performances from stars like Nicholas Galitzine and Bill Skarsgård, and is a movie that should stay with viewers long after finishing it on Netflix this weekend.
