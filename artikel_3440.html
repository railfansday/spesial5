It's been nine months since Season 4 of Bridgerton aired. With Season 5 greenlit and scheduled to premiere in 2027, this latest Hulu series could fill the gap, featuring royal drama and a two-episode-per-week release schedule. It has also recently become a streaming hit, even without an official Rotten Tomatoes score. As a result, the series could be the perfect binge for fans waiting for the next season of Bridgerton to arrive on Netflix.

In recent years, several South Korean productions have adapted popular web novels and webtoons, with many finding success. One notable example is Solo Leveling, which began as a web novel before being adapted into an anime. The show has received two seasons, and a movie has been greenlit, making it one of Crunchyroll's most successful titles. Another example is the K-drama series All of Us Are Dead, which premiered on Netflix in 2022 and has a second season in development. Clearly, these adaptations have an audience and also introduce new viewers to the original stories.

Recently, Disney+ and Hulu released their live-action adaptation of The Remarried Empress, based on the webtoon of the same name, which began publication in 2019. The show follows Empress Navier (Shin Min-a), whose marriage to her husband, Emperor Sovieshu (Ju Ji-hoon), falls apart when he falls for a runaway slave named Rashta (Lee Se-young), whom he takes as his mistress. Sovieshu eventually files for divorce, which Navier accepts on the condition that she can marry Prince Heinrey of the Western Empire (Lee Jong-suk). Since its debut, the series has become an instant streaming hit, landing at No. 2 on Disney+'s Top 10 TV Shows Worldwide chart, between VisionQuest and All's Fair. As of this writing, the first four episodes have aired, with six more scheduled and the finale set to premiere on November 25.

As of this writing, filming for Bridgerton Season 5 has ended, and while Netflix has confirmed the series will return in 2027, it has yet to announce an official release date. According to the streaming giant, the upcoming season will take place two years after the events of Season 4's finale and follow the romance between Michaela Stirling (Masali Baduza) and Lady Francesca Stirling (Hannah Dodd). So, while fans wait for the next season, as well as the next episode of The Remarried Empress, here are some other shows worth watching.

One show worth watching is The Crown, a Netflix series based on the reign of Queen Elizabeth II of the United Kingdom. The show ran for six seasons, with its final season covering events from 1997 to 2005. The final season was also filmed during the period of Queen Elizabeth II's death in 2022, which influenced how the series concluded. Just like Bridgerton, this show is filled with royal drama, but its story is inspired by real historical events.

But if you're after something short, then this very specific Doctor Who episode should do the trick. Season 14's "Rogue" follows the Fifteenth Doctor (Ncuti Gatwa) and his companion Ruby Sunday (Millie Gibson) as they travel to 1813 to attend a ball, only to cross paths with a bounty hunter named Rogue (Jonathan Groff), who's hunting down an alien shapeshifting species called the Chuldur. These aliens happen to be Bridgerton fans, "cosplaying" as humans to experience the drama and emotion. Just like The Remarried Empress, this Doctor Who episode is also available on Disney+.
