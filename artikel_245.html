A unified Disney Plus and Hulu app has been a long time coming. Disney started testing its “one-app experience” in 2023, which involved putting a Hulu app tile in the Disney Plus app. This initial experience didn’t include all of Hulu’s content, though, including certain shows, live TV channels, and premium add-ons. This upcoming merger will change this by folding all of Hulu into Disney Plus, creating “a seamless way to discover and engage” with content across both services, according to Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro and CFO Hugh Johnston. Disney still hasn’t confirmed whether it will shut down the standalone Hulu app.

Disney is also expanding vertical videos on Disney Plus, which it first brought to the app in March. The company plans to introduce videos from TikTok creators in December, as well as add more live sports and podcasts to the service.
