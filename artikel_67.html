ICJ ruling on Myanmar genocide: ‘Historic victory for Rohingya, landmark for international justice’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Erin Ogunkeye welcomes Reed Brody, Member of the International Commission of Jurists, Human Rights Lawyer and veteran war crimes prosecutor. The International Court of Justice's ruling on Myanmar marks, according to Brody, "a historic victory for the Rohingya, but also a landmark for international justice." He explains how the court assessed evidence of genocidal intent, rejecting Myanmar's justification of its military operations as counterterrorism. He argues that "the existence of an armed conflict or a security objective doesn't exclude genocidal intent," a finding he believes will have "far-reaching implications in future genocide cases," including South Africa's case against Israel over Gaza. Brody also discusses the Gambia's role in bringing the case and growing challenges to international accountability, warning that "we're in a very dangerous moment."
