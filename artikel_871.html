Fifa hit by cyber attack amid mounting scrutiny of Gianni Infantino

Fifa has been targeted in a cyber attack that compromised what it describes as a "limited set of personal and business information," it confirmed on Thursday.

"Fifa is working closely with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement," the organisation stated in a message.

Fifa did not reveal when the cyberattack occurred or provide the precise nature of the breached data.

Last week, a group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted a screenshot on X of what it claimed were Fifa files. Three days later, the group appeared to take credit when responding to a post stating that more than 12 gigabytes of internal documents had been leaked.

The breach occurs at a time when Fifa and its president, Gianni Infantino, face heightened scrutiny following a foiled, secretive plan to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors.

Infantino is also attempting to extend his 11-year presidency of Fifa to 2031. He remains the favourite when the election is held on March 18, with Kohzo Tashima of Japan and Philippe Diallo of France standing as the other candidates.
