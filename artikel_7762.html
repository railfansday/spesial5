This state senator wrote music for ‘Dragon Ball Z;’ will he go ‘Super Saiyan’ as new Texas AG?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

“Dragon Ball Z,” a popular animation series, has musical scores written by Texas lawyer and state Sen. Nathan Johnson, D-Dallas, who was recently elected as the state’s new attorney general. (Image from Shutterstock)

Before Texas state Sen. Nathan Johnson, D-Dallas, unleashed a “blue spirit bomb” on opponent Mayes Middleton on Tuesday night in the midterm elections for the Texas attorney general spot, the Thompson Coburn lawyer composed musical scores for Dragon Ball Z, a Japanese anime series about martial artists who defend the world from destruction.

Johnson, who got at least 52% of the vote and is a Dallas lawyer, will be the first Democrat to serve as the Texas attorney general in more than 30 years, Chron.com reports.

The show introduced various terms to the United States, including “Super Saiyan,” which means getting a huge surge of physical strength and hair that stands straight up, and “blue spirit bomb,” which is something released on opponents during an attack.

A piano player, Johnson learned how to write music while working as a lawyer and liked it so much that he decided to quit his job and focus on doing film and television composition, according to the article.

Johnson eventually went back to law and started a solo practice. While looking for office space, he met a landlord connected to a Texas company that licensed the English version of Dragon Ball Z in the United States. The company then hired Johnson in the early 2000s, according to Chron.com.

“It gave me a creative outlet and a cultural connection that very few people have had the opportunity to have,” Johnson said in an interview, according to Variety.
