GdS: ‘If not the best’ – Cardinale accelerates by publicly declaring Milan’s Endrick admiration

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A rather significant step was taken on Saturday, as Gerry Cardinale publicly declared AC Milan’s interest in signing Endrick.

La Gazzetta dello Sport have revealed that Cardinale spent six hours at Milanello yesterday, after four at Casa Milan. The owner fully immersed himself in the Milan world again: he spoke with his transfer team, with the team about the match against Frosinone, and with Ruben Amorim about both topics.

While Cardinale did not confirm the imminent arrival of Markus Krösche – though he did admit to being in talks with him – his clear intention is to build a top-tier organisation. Cardinale’s goal is to lead Milan to a trophy – ideally the Scudetto – as early as this season.

To do this, he knows strengthening the squad in January is a necessity. One name stands out in his notebook: Endrick, the forward currently with Real Madrid whom Mourinho – for the time being – is unwilling to let go of.

Regarding a potential move for the 20-year-old, Cardinale confirmed that the club like him: “Endrick’s name keeps coming up in the market. Our project goes beyond any single player, but Endrick is one of the best in the world – if not the best – considering his age and position. I have enormous respect for him, and we’ll see what might be possible.”

In other words, the green light would need to be secured from Mourinho regarding his departure from Madrid. Milan know they can leverage the strong relationship between its owner and Roc Nation, the agency representing the Brazilian.

Given the business already conducted between RedBird and Roc Nation, it is likely that the player would favour Milan over other potential destinations. Meanwhile, Tresoldi has also piqued Cardinale’s interest, though he offers a different profile than Endrick.

Nicolo Tresoldi is also an option for the attack, but more significant progress has been made regarding the Brazilian. Bayern Munich are also watching the Club Brugge striker, with Harry Kane having undergone ankle surgery.

In any case, Milan’s transfer activity will not be limited to a striker. Further arrivals will depend on the opportunities that arise during the winter window, with agents and intermediaries continually ringing the phone lines at Casa Milan.

There is a desire to bolster the defence, potentially with a central defender (possibly through the early return of Zachary Athekame, currently on loan at Lyon) and a left-sided player, should Pervis Estupiñán be sold.

All of this is in addition to the contract renewal for Rabiot, the next player set to be secured following the agreements reached with Strahinja Pavlović and Christian Pulišić. In short, there is a lot in the works.
