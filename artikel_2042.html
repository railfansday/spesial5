Sources: Crystal Palace set Chris Richards sale stance amid contract latest

Crystal Palace have no intention of selling centre-back Chris Richards in January despite interest from Inter Milan, sources have told Football Insider.

The USA international is viewed as being too important for the Eagles to lose in the middle of the campaign, especially with a likely relegation battle on the cards.

However, Richards is out of contract at Selhurst Park next summer, and with Inter lurking, it is expected to be difficult for Palace to agree a new deal.

Sources say that the 26-year-old would "find it hard" to turn down a move to the Italian champions, who can offer Champions League football.

Richards has been with Palace since 2022, and has seen his reputation grow significantly in recent years.

It looks increasingly likely that the South London outfit will be unable to tie him down to a new deal, which in turn will open the door to a move away.

Palace have enjoyed unprecedented success in recent years, but have slowly seen their squad pulled apart by bigger clubs.

The likes of Eberechi Eze, Maxence Lacroix and Marc Guehi have all departed the club within the last two seasons, and Richards looks set to be part of that next exodus.

Jean-Philippe Mateta and Ismaila Sarr both pushed for moves in the summer, and now the team's struggles on the pitch will do little to help their cause.

The interest in Richards from Inter is a huge threat to the Eagles, given the fact that they can offer regular European football.

Palace have allowed his contract to run down to this difficult stage, and are now likely to pay the price by losing him on a free.

Former Tottenham and Everton scout Bryan King told Football Insider on Tuesday that Thomas Frank is currently heading a list of "four names" who could potentially replace Pierre Sage as Palace's boss.

The Dane has been out of work since he was axed by Spurs back on February, but could now be in line to return to the Premier League.

King also believes that Sean Dyche and Kieran McKenna are in the frame for the job, while Eddie Howe is also an outside contender.

The ex-recruitment man has backed Frank to replicate his feats from his days at Brentford, and steady the ship at Palace long-term.
