The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Poster Collection hits new record low price on Amazon — save nearly $10

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Poster Collection is on sale for $41.47 at Amazon, down from its $49.99 list price.

It’s still tough to get well-priced stock for Pokémon’s 30th Celebration expansion, even if Delta Reign is coming out this week.

As of Nov. 3, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Poster Collection has dropped to just $41.47 at Amazon, shaving $8.52 off its $49.99 list price with a 17% discount.

While that might not sound like the biggest saving, it's an improvement on the $51.95 deal we covered on Oct. 27. It's also $9.51 below the previous $50.98 Amazon record low we spotted on Oct. 19. Either way, it’s the lowest price we've seen on the site so far (confirmed with price tracker camelcamelcamel).

Better still, the new offer is sold and shipped directly by Amazon, rather than one of its marketplace sellers — with free delivery set for as soon as Nov. 23.

The Pokemon TCG 30th Celebration Poster Collection contains three booster packs of trading cards from the anniversary expansion, alongside foil promotional cards featuring Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres.

You'll also receive a double-sided poster measuring almost 27 by 39 inches, displaying artwork from more than 160 cards in the set.
