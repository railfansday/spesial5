The best Black Friday headphones deals on Sony, Bose, Apple, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Some Black Friday headphones deals have already started out strong, but with the official start of Amazon's Black Friday sale, they're even better.

Take, for instance, the 45% discount on the second-generation Bose QuietComfort headphones (released just this past August, btw). Prefer a flagship model? No problem, the Sony WH-1000XM6 — our top pick for premium sound — are over $100 off.

As someone who tests headphones year-round and is heading into her sixth year covering Black Friday at Mashable, I only write about deals that are actual deals. That means double-checking accurate list prices, using my price history knowledge from looking at headphone product listings every time I open my work laptop, and consulting the Mashable shopping team's collective knowledge of historical pricing. This year, my criteria also include an awareness of price increases that have affected nearly every area of tech.

With all that in mind, those two aforementioned deals — and the ones below — are the best headphones deals you'll find ahead of Black Friday. And when more inevitably crop up as the shopping holiday draws closer, you'll be sure to find them on this list.

The second-gen Bose QuietComfort headphones are one of my favorite headphone releases of 2026. Though their regular $359 price tag sits on the higher end of mid-range, these headphones come closer to the performance of the QC Ultra headphones, which retail for $449. Their all-day comfort, spatial audio, and excellent ANC make them worth the investment, especially at $199. Though audiophiles will probably be happier with the Sony XM4C (you can read my full breakdown of how these two headphones compare), the Bose headphones are a particularly good buy ahead of Black Friday at this price. If you fall into the audiophile category, it is worth noting that the Sony headphones dropped down to $198 as of Nov. 19. You don’t have to spend Bose money for a pair of everyday headphones. At $38, the Sony WH-CH530 offer up to 55 hours of battery life, multipoint Bluetooth for connecting to two devices at once, and a quick-charge feature that gets you another 90 minutes of listening after just three minutes plugged in. These are on-ear headphones, and they don’t have active noise cancellation, so they’re better suited to shoppers who want an inexpensive pair for music and taking calls.

These headphones also hit the market just this past September, and at $38, are already matching the record-low of their predecessor and a former budget favorite of ours, the Sony CH-520N.

The Sony WH-1000XM6 are our top pick for premium sound, and the discount makes their flagship price a little easier to stomach. Mashable’s testing found improved sound quality, particularly on vocal-heavy tracks, along with excellent active noise cancellation and a comfortable fit for longer listening sessions. Sony also brought back the folding design, so these are easier to pack than the XM5s. Battery life hasn’t improved from the previous generation, but you still get up to 30 hours with ANC on. At $348, these headphones are down to a record-low price, beating their former sale price of $378 that we already recommended picking up. In other words, you're not likely to grab a much better deal than this if you've been waiting on buying Sony's flagship headphones.
