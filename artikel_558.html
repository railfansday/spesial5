Huw Edwards Channel 4 Doc Slammed by U.K. Prime Minister: ‘We Have No Interest in Hearing What He Has to Say’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

U.K. Prime Minister Andy Burnham‘s office has spoken out against an upcoming Channel 4 documentary featuring an interview with disgraced former BBC presenter Huw Edwards.

Announced on Thursday, the doc sees Edwards — who, in 2024, was convicted of three counts of making indecent images of children and given a six-month suspended sentence — speak publicly about his downfall and criminal actions for the first time. Titled “Huw Edwards — Guilty,” it’s set to air on Channel 4 Dec. 1. The doc has stirred up quite a bit of backlash in the U.K., with several members of Parliament speaking out against it, and now Burnham has added his voice to the mix. In a statement to BBC News, an official spokesperson said: “Our thoughts first and foremost will be with the victims of child sexual abuse and exploitation. We have no interest in hearing what he has to say.”

The spokesperson added that these “are extremely serious crimes and anyone convicted of them must face the consequences of their actions” and said that broadcasters “are expected to act responsibly and have appropriate regards to their audiences when making decisions.”

Asked if the prime minister believed Channel 4 has not acted responsibly by making the documentary, the spokesperson said: “Channel 4 is editorially independent and decisions about individual programmes and contributors are a matter for them.”

Representatives for Channel 4 did not immediately respond to Variety‘s request for comment. The broadcaster told the BBC yesterday that the documentary’s director, Patrick Forbes, is “highly experienced” and there were “no questions off limits, revealing his account of what led to his offending and very public downfall.”
