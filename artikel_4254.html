Pikmin 4 Has Been Updated To Version 2.0.0, Here Are The Full Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It's all about Ocarina of Time this week, but next week Nintendo will shift its attention to Pikmin 4's Switch 2 release.

Ahead of its return, Nintendo has today rolled out the game's Version 2.0.0 update, preparing it for this major update. It also comes loaded with some other fixes to improve the overall gameplay experience. A patch for the Pikmin 4 demo has been distributed as well.

The last major update for Pikmin 4 was released in December 2025. And in case you missed the news last weekend, there's now a Pikmin 4 - Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy demo available to download from the eShop.

The Switch 2 Edition of Pikmin 4 includes voice commands for Oatchi (using the Switch 2's built-in microphone), the new mode Dandori Academy, GameShare support, improved visuals and enhanced resolution.
