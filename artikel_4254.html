Bleach: Soul Resonance celebrates a year of service with the SSR Full Hollowfication Ichigo

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With Bleach having finally wrapped airing, perhaps permanently, with the conclusion of the Thousand-Year Blood War arc, I wouldn't blame you for feeling a big ol' shonen-shaped hole in your heart. Fortunately, if you're on mobile, there's plenty of stuff to help fill the gap, especially in Bleach: Soul Resonance's massive first anniversary celebration!

Even if you are feeling a little blue, it can't be worse than for the starring addition in this update, Full Hollowfication Ichigo. Yes, the latest SSR will be joining the roster on November 25th, as a Slash-type character, and a great nod back to a fan-favourite period of the anime and manga.

On top of that, you'll find special anniversary rewards to claim, such as an avatar frame and various in-game resources. All this on top of an anniversary check-in event, Black Friday deals and a whole lot more!

You'll want to keep your eyes peeled this December too, as Bleach: Soul Resonance promises even more excitement, including a new season that's being kept under wraps, and even more check-in rewards for you to collect.
