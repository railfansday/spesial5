Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Test drives round 1:

test drove a bZ today. Wow it is really quiet. The ride was good and steering responsive. The instruments were clear and intuitive except for the transmission selector which would take a little getting used to. I don't know why they don't have a glove box, seems to me that it didn't gain anything in terms of passenger leg room. Overall pretty solid, what I would expect from Toyota.

I was supposed to drive a H-CR also, but it was sold so that will be another time.

I sat in a Nissan Leaf. I know that wasn't in my original list, but poster recommended I look at one. Definitely a different design aesthetic on the interior. I wasn't able to drive it because the Leafs have been put on a sales hold due to a battery related recall. Apparently even the newest ones have an issue, which does not inspire me. I don't know if I will go back for a test drive or not.
I have recently begun a similar journey, but my priorities undoubtedly differ from most those of most people here. I am in an EV desert--except for Tesla. Just finding vehicles to test drive is a chore. I was initially targeting Hyundai Ioniq 5, but after driving one I wasn't knocked off my feet. I liked it ok and might eventually even settle for it despite ICCU issues, but it is really larger than I need as it's just me and a small dog. I did get an estimate from an electrician for running a 220v line to my carport. The estimate came in much lower than I had feared.

I have only recently begun looking at Subaru Uncharted and Toyota C-HR--essentially the same vehicles. These seem pretty close to what I'm looking for. I hope to be able to find one to test drive soon.

Good luck in your quest!
I found a 2026 Toyota C-HR to test drive and liked it enough to buy it. In my limited time with the vehicle I'm liking it a lot.

Backseat room seems to be what was sacrificed to decrease the length of the car. That's not a big deal at all to me, but it could be to others. The driver's seat is easy to get into and out of (one of my priorities), and there's plenty of headroom. Seating is comfortable enough. Acceleration (another of my priorities) is great! There has been a pretty steep learning curve for me for the controls and settings, but I was coming from a vehicle with only basic settings.

My carport had no electrical outlets, but an electrician was able to install a 220v outlet with a short run from the breaker box for a very reasonable (imho) price.
