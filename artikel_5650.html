Shares of ProMis Neurosciences, developer of an amyloid-targeting treatment for Alzheimer’s disease, sold off sharply last week in reaction to an announcement from Acumen Pharmaceuticals that its own Alzheimer’s drug, with a similar mechanism, failed to slow the cognitive or functional decline of patients with Alzheimer’s disease compared to a placebo in a mid-stage study.

“This was a complete overreaction in my opinion and not justified,” ProMis CEO Neil Warma told me, when I asked if investors were correct to assume the failure of Acumen’s drug presaged a similar fate for ProMis’ drug.
