Mini Pumpkin Pies

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Pumpkin pie is a Thanksgiving dinner must-have, but what to do when there are more diners than slices? Or, what if you're more of a crust-lover than filling? Enter these adorable mini pumpkin pies, which rethink the crust-to-filling ratio in the most adorable, snackable package. These mini pies are perfect for people who want just a little treat after the big meal, or if you'd prefer a smattering of desserts, since honestly, who can choose between pumpkin and pecan? No baking experience? No problem. These super-easy pumpkin pies are not just cute, but incredibly easy to make too.

To start, arrange a rack in the center of the oven and preheat it to 375°. As the oven heats, grease the bottoms and sides of a 24-cup mini muffin tin with cooking spray or butter. This will help you get a golden-brown crust that doesn’t stick to the tins.

Transfer the dough rounds into the prepared muffin tin, gently pressing the dough to the bottom and sides of each cup. Once each cup is filled with dough, place the tin into the refrigerator to allow the dough to chill for about 15 minutes.

As the dough chills, whisk the pumpkin puree, condensed milk, egg, vanilla, salt, and baking spices in a large mixing bowl until smooth and well combined.

Remove the muffin tin from the fridge and spoon about 1 heaping tablespoon of filling into each cup. The filling should reach almost all the way up the sides of each mini pie crust.

Place the tin into the oven and bake the pies for about 25 to 30 minutes, until the centers are just set and the edges are golden brown. Remove them from the oven and allow the pies to cool in the tin for 10 minutes before using a small offset spatula or spoon to gently remove the pies from the tin and transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.

As the pies cool, whip the heavy whipping cream in a large bowl until soft peaks form. Top each of the mini pies with a dollop of fresh whipped cream and, if desired, shave chocolate over the tops. Finally, sprinkle the pies with a bit of cinnamon for an extra seasonal touch.

If you want to freeze these pies, let them cool completely, then freeze them on a baking sheet until solid. Transfer to an airtight container once frozen and defrost at room temperature to eat.
