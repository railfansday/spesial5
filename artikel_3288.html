‘Longlegs’ Producer Dan Kagan And His Banner Traffic Ink Partnership With Plan B

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Plan B Entertainment has formed a strategic partnership with producer Dan Kagan and his company Traffic. Insiders say as part of the deal, Kagan and Traffic would bring future projects to Plan B for potential partnerships.

A seasoned producer, Kagan formed his independent label Traffic in 2020 following more than a decade working inside the studio system at Paramount, MGM, Sony and Orion Pictures.

“We believe in the value of curation and Dan’s producorial ethos and focused eye speaks to that — we are excited at what we can build together,“ said Plan B’s Dede Gardner and Jeremy Kleiner.

Kagan recently produced the record-breaking hit Longlegs; Paramount’s sci-fi thriller Significant Other; New Line’s action-horror They Will Kill You; Neon’s Hokum; and Victorian Psycho, which premiered in Un Certain Regard at this year’s Cannes Film Festival and was released theatrically by Bleecker Street. The deal is part of Plan B’s continued structural growth that includes the formation of Plan B Europe, led by Ed Macdonald, which is building out a slate of film and TV series in the UK and Europe, and a joint venture with Oscar winner Kevin Macdonald and his KM Films for unscripted projects, including McDonald’s One to One: John & Yoko and BAFTA-nominated Apocalypse in the Tropics.

“Working with the team at Plan B to build a slate of quality films is nothing short of a dream come true,” said Kagan.

Some of Plan B’s other recent credits include Heart of the Beast starring fellow Plan B principal Brad Pitt and Jane Schoenbrun’s acclaimed Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma. Upcoming releases include The Riders, directed by Edward Berger and starring Pitt, The Bell Jar starring Billie Eilish, and the film and docuseries Isle of Man for Amazon MGM Studios.

Kagan is represented by CAA and Granderson Des Rochers.
