Richard Hammond Says He Was Approached By BBC About ‘Top Gear’ Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Richard Hammond has revealed that the BBC sounded him out about getting back behind the Top Gear wheel for the show’s upcoming reboot.

The presenter said he declined the opportunity to return to the motoring show he helped turn into a global phenomenon alongside Jeremy Clarkson and James May.

Hammond disclosed the BBC’s approach during an interview with Times Radio on the day that Gordon Ramsay was unveiled as the new host of Top Gear.

“I did talk to them about Top Gear relaunching, and I’m sure they went to the others as well. They came to me, and [asked] was I interested? ‘No, I’m too old,’ [I responded],” Hammond said. “I’ve done it. I wouldn’t want to revisit it. That was our Top Gear, wasn’t it? We did it, the three of us, and when we left and went on together, it was the three of us. But that was our show.

“The BBC owns Top Gear and has had it for decades, and I think right now should be making a show that actually tells us what’s happening in the world of cars.”

Times Radio presenter Kait Borsay asked Hammond if he would be interested if Clarkson and May returned to Top Gear.

“We’ve done it. Let somebody else. We made our show in the way we did, and for our time,” he replied. “The BBC should be making a TV show that tells us, the consumers: This is what’s happening in the world of transport.”

Hammond hosted Top Gear between 2002 and 2015, before leaving the BBC with Clarkson and May and launching The Grand Tour on Amazon Prime Video.

Ramsay was announced as the next host of Top Gear four years after Andrew Flintoff’s crash. Ramsay will lead the series alongside a soon-to-be-announced lineup of co-presenters, with The Stig returning.

Filming will take place across the UK and around the world in 2027, with the Top Gear test track at Dunsfold Aerodrome in Surrey once again serving as the show’s home.

Ramsay said: “At its very best Top Gear has always been about the unexpected – surprising, entertaining and just the right amount of chaos. That’s the spirit we want to build on, bringing a fresh energy and ambition to create something truly thrilling for a whole new generation of audiences.”
