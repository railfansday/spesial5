Justin Timberlake Will Open Up About Highs and Lows of Stardom on Four-Part Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A Justin Timberlake docuseries is on its way to Apple TV. The four-part documentary Timberlake will premiere its first two episodes on the platform on Dec. 11. The third and final episodes follows on Dec. 18 and Dec. 23, respectively.

“I’m sure that you’re gonna go back and see these incredibly sweet and goofy things from my childhood,” Timberlake says in the trailer, as flashes of his teenage self showing off a Janet Jackson poster appear. But Timberlake will also reportedly reckon with “some things that are really hard to talk about,” namely, his relationship with Britney Spears, 2024 DUI arrest, 2004 Superbowl “Nipplegate” incident with Jackson, and public backlash regarding past controversial behavior.

Timberlake was directed by Fisher Stevens and was announced earlier this month. Interviews with Timbaland, Pharrell Williams, wife Jessica Biel, Timberlake’s mother Lynn Harless, and SNL creator Lorne Michaels are featured in the trailer, and Timberlake will also include never-before-seen footage of the singer across his 30-year career. Timberlake’s breakthrough album Futuresex/Lovesounds was released 20 years ago, featuring his most recognizable songs “SexyBack,” “My Love,” and “What Goes Around… Comes Around.” His most recent album, Everything I Thought It Was, was released in 2024.

In September, the Prince of Pop and Pharrell Williams appeared onstage at Jay-Z’s 30-year anniversary Reasonable Doubt concert in Paris.
