Duchess Meghan Looks Somber in Monochrome Black at the Launch of the Lost Screen Memorial in Brussels

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

She and Prince Harry held hands as they arrived at the installation, which is part of their ongoing campaign against online bullying

Prince Harry and Duchess Meghan presented a united front as they arrived, hand-in-hand, in Brussels, Belgium, today, to mark the opening of one of their Archewell Foundation Parents’ Network projects.

For her first public appearance since the unfortunate news regarding her father, Thomas Markle, Meghan looked sharp and polished. The duchess wrapped herself up in a luxurious black double-breasted coat that she belted tightly at the waist, pairing it with tailored trousers and pointy patent-leather stilettos.

Meghan’s vintage Dior crystal-and-black enamel door-knocker earrings (which she’s worn out on special occasions before) stood out thanks to her low bun, while her beloved Cartier Love bracelet decorated her wrist. For another pop of light, Meghan pinned a red poppy accent to her coat in honor of Remembrance Day, matching her husband.

Harry, in a navy suit, white shirt, and patterned black tie, never left her side.

The evening saw the opening of the Lost Screen Memorial, an art installation and global advocacy campaign that serves as a moving tribute to children and teenagers who lost their lives due to online harms.

At the event, held at the European Parliament, Harry gave a moving speech criticizing tech platforms’ addictive designs and highlighting the importance of protecting children’s youths. Children “deserve to feel safe wherever they are—in a classroom, on a playground—at any time, no matter where they are,” the prince said.

His comment came days after Meghan’s estranged father was arrested by the FBI and charged with one count of possession of child pornography.

Meghan released a statement regarding the ongoing child porn investigation involving Thomas some weeks ago, but has since stepped back from public engagements. She even reportedly opted out of making a speech at today’s event as she focuses on her wellbeing. “She’s just not strong enough, emotionally, to be doing any public speaking right now,” an insider told the Telegraph.
