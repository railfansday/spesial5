Noah Wrapped the Converse Chuck 70 in Menswear’s Favorite Tartan

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When Converse and Noah collaborated for the first time earlier this year, the most surprising thing was that it’d taken so long for them to link up. The sneaker OGs and New York menswear mavens make as much sense together as peanut butter and jelly—both brands flow effortlessly between worlds, embraced by punks and preps and hoopers and skaters alike. Thankfully, the duo have been making up for lost time, kicking their partnership off with a couple of killer Chuck 70s in the spring before putting out an understated banger of a spin on the Louie Lopez 2 over the summer. Now, they’re capping off their banner year with one last go-round—another jazzed-up Chuck Taylor that’s about as holiday-ready as it gets.

Just in time for turkey season, Noah has wrapped the Converse Chuck 70 Low in one of the most beloved patterns in menswear: Black Watch tartan. The green, navy, and black plaid was originally worn by squadrons patrolling the Scottish Highlands in the 18th century, with its subdued tones meant to help wearers blend into their surroundings. Over the past several decades, it’s been embraced by fashion at large, but particularly Trad Ivy enthusiasts, who tend to sport the striking tartan on everything from sport coats and scarves during the colder months.

In addition to the sharp Black Watch canvas uppers, Noah made a handful of other subtle changes to the classic silhouette, adding its branding to the “license plate” on the shoe’s heel, removing the wing tongue stitch, and swapping in a woven tongue tag. The kicks feature the standard white laces but also come with a mustard yellow pair for an extra boost of color.

If you’re looking to dress festively but aren’t all that into freaky patterned sweaters, the latest Converse x Noah Chuck 70 Low might just be the ticket. The shoes drop tomorrow, November 19, for $112 via both Converse and Noah.
