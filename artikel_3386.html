CBS’ New Sci-Fi Thriller Is Officially Becoming a Streaming Sensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With CBS' fall schedule in full swing, the network has brought back some of television's most popular shows. Mega-hits like Tracker and Marshals are back for new seasons, joined by cult favorites like NCIS and Fire Country. Alongside these returning heavyweights, the network has also rolled out a trio of fresh originals designed to keep viewers hooked. Leading the new wave is NCIS: New York, a gritty franchise expansion starring LL COOL J and Scott Caan. On Thursdays, the quirky vampire sitcom Eternally Yours joins Silicon Valley legal drama Cupertino, which stars Mike Colter as an underdog attorney fighting tech giants.

However, Cupertino is not just your regular legal drama — it is incredibly relevant to today's tech landscape. The series explores numerous issues arising from the latest AI technology as many companies dive headfirst into innovation without caring about the consequences. From rogue chatbots to frightening agentic models, the series feels eerily real because these things are happening every day. Critics have been extremely impressed, giving it a perfect score of 100% on Rotten Tomatoes. "Cupertino revitalizes the legal procedural with sharp wit, addictive courtroom drama, and sophisticated explorations of the tech industry's moral complexities, crafting biting satire with surprising humanity," their consensus reads.

Cupertino was created by seasoned husband-and-wife creator duo, Michelle and Robert King. They are known for other hit shows like The Good Wife, Evil, and Elsbeth. The series reunites them with Colter, who starred in Evil. He plays Michael Price. Rachel Keller plays Olivia Siffre. When Michael and Olivia are fired from their Silicon Valley firm, they team up to defend people caught in the crosshairs of the tech industry. New episodes of Cupertino air on Thursdays at 10 pm. The series has seen massive gains on streaming, with the premiere episode doubling its live viewership after seven days. The series is also one of the most-watched on Paramount+ via Amazon, ranking third at the time of writing, according to FlixPatrol.

The series just came off a special airing schedule, with Episodes 5 to 7 airing between November 5 and 12. This week, the show returns to its regular night with an episode that finds Michael and Olivia defending a journalist framed for murder in a case that exposes an even bigger problem of mass surveillance affecting every American. Meanwhile, the team's AI client, Bo, tries to reconnect with them.
