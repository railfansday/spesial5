‘Caught In The Act: Unfaithful’ Renewed For Season 5 At MTV

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

EXCLUSIVE: Tami Roman is back to help suspicious lovers to catch their cheating partners after MTV renewed Caught in the Act: Unfaithful for a fifth season.

This comes a week after the fourth season, which was split into two parts, ended earlier this month. That season was its highest-rated in nearly three years among people ages 18-49.

As part of the move, previous seasons of the show, which started life on VH1 in 2022, will be made available on Paramount+ from December 9.

The series follows suspicious lovers who enlist former Basketball Wives and The Real World star Roman, Coach Ken Canion and a team of investigators to uncover shocking truths that could end relationships or redefine them altogether.
