Capture Boards Sales On Josh Holloway-Led Western ‘Flint’ — AFM

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

EXCLUSIVE: Capture, the international sales joint venture between Signature Entertainment and Capstone Studios, has boarded international sales rights to Flint, the Western starring Josh Holloway and based on the novel by popular author Louis L’Amour.

Written and directed by Ryan Whitaker, the film features an ensemble cast including Sarah Gadon, Max Martini, Dianna Agron, Mike Vogel, Charles Esten, Forrie J. Smith, Bailey Chase, Nick Searcy and Kristoffer Polaha.

The movie follows James Kettleman (Holloway), “a ruthless East Coast businessman who returns to the Montana frontier he once fled, assuming the name of the notorious gunfighter who raised him. Filmed against the landscapes of Montana, pic is produced by Ken Carpenter of Tirian Films. Mark Pentecost of The Pentecost Group serves as executive producer. Other producers include Jerilyn Esquibel, Josh Holloway, Beau L’Amour and Ari Novak. Beau L’Amour is the son of the late Westerns author Louis L’Amour. Watch on Deadline

Capture will introduce the project to international buyers at the American Film Market in Los Angeles this month. The film premiered at the Nashville Film Festival in September 2026.

Ella Field, EVP of Capture, commented: “Louis L’Amour’s stories have long captivated audiences, and Flint brings together everything that makes the Western genre so compelling — stunning landscapes, memorable characters and a powerful story of redemption. With the renewed enthusiasm for Westerns, fuelled by the phenomenal success of series such as Yellowstone, and Josh Holloway leading a fantastic cast, we feel this is a wonderful time to bring this much-loved story to the big screen. We’re incredibly excited to introduce Flint to international audiences.”
