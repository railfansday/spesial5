Delete GTA 6 For A Chance To Win Another Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 finally releases this week. The game looms so large that other companies are using it to market their own games, including Devolver Digital.

The publisher has announced that people can enter for a chance to win their upcoming game, Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, by reposting the message below with a video of themselves deleting their GTA 6 pre-load. Of course, anyone who deletes their pre-load can install it again, though it might take some time given the game is 212GB.

The lucky winners will get a Steam key for Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, not a console code since the game's console plans haven't been confirmed yet, so that's something to keep in mind if you're taking part in this campaign.

If you were never planning to buy GTA 6 but still want to enter the contest, you can send a video of yourself biting into a raw onion to Devolver.

Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, priced at $5 (or $3.74 until launch), releases on November 19, which is the same day that GTA 6 comes out. Reslimed was originally prototyped as a retro-style 2D platformer for Game Boy Color and was pitched by developer Doinksoft before the GTA 6 conversation really ramped up.

"There is no doubt GTA 6 will have the spotlight before, during, and after November 19, but it’s not for everyone, or releasing on Steam the same day, so we figure there’s a bit of counter-programming that may work in Volvy’s favor," Devolver told Kotaku.

GTA 6 has reportedly already sold nearly 15 million copies and generated about $1.5 billion in revenue.
