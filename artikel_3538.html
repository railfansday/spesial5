Actor Alexander Haskin Dead at 38, Pulled From Water Near Santa Monica Pier

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A background actor known for his roles in You and The Rookie has been identified as the man pulled from the ocean near the Santa Monica Pier in California.

Alexander Haskin was pronounced dead on Monday, November 16, after Santa Monica Police Department harbor guards responded to a call of a man in the water, Lt. Lewis Gilmour told the Los Angeles Times on Wednesday, November 18. He was 38.

Authorities responded to a report of someone in the ocean at around 4:06 p.m. local time. Haskin was pronounced dead just before 5 p.m.

“The evidence shows the man was alone and did not interact with anyone in the period immediately before the incident,” Gilmour told the newspaper. “Security footage corroborates that finding.”

The L.A. Times and TMZ reported that Haskin’s cause of death has been deferred pending further investigation by the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner’s Office.

“On November 16, 2026, at approximately 4:06 p.m., Santa Monica Police Department (SMPD) Harbor Guards responded to a report of a man in the water near the west end of the Santa Monica Pier,” the Santa Monica PD said in a statement posted via Instagram.

The statement continued, “Harbor Guards conducted an underwater search, located the individual and brought him aboard a lifeguard boat, where CPR began. After being hoisted onto the pier deck, Santa Monica Fire Department paramedics continued lifesaving measures. Despite their efforts, he was pronounced deceased at 4:56 p.m.”

Santa Monica PD encouraged “anyone with information related to this incident … to contact the Santa Monica Police Department at 310-458-8491.”

Haskin’s father, Dana Haskin, told the L.A. Times that his son seemed in good spirits before his death and had been looking forward to spending Thanksgiving with his family.

Dana told the paper that his son sent him a text message early Monday and that they last spoke on the phone for close to an hour on Saturday, November 14.

“He was a happy guy,” Haskin’s father said. “He liked to joke around, and he was always getting in trouble, but in a good way.”

In an interview with the California Post, Haskin’s father said that his son’s text message to him on Monday morning only “brings up more questions,” as his family is left wondering whether he had taken his own life. Dana declined to share the contents of Haskin’s message with the outlet.

“In the heat of the moment, who knows what a human brain can do?” Dana asked. “It’s still a big mystery to us. … We keep asking why.”

In addition to background roles in Netflix’s You and ABC’s The Rookie, Haskin was a fitness trainer and U.S. Air Force veteran.
