Keanu Reeves' John Wick Prequel Movie Fate Finally Confirmed At Lionsgate

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Keanu Reeves is definitely coming back for the new John Wick prequel movie.

Following his apparent departure from the franchise with Chapter 4's ending, discussions of a potential return from Reeves have swirled, with Lionsgate eventually announcing in April 2025 that both the animated prequel movie and a proper Chapter 5 were moving forward with him back as the iconic assassin. In the time since, it was also confirmed that Donnie Yen would be directing and starring in a Caine spinoff movie, which wrapped production in July 2026.

Nearly two years after the studio previously confirmed its plans to produce the project, Lionsgate's latest earnings call has confirmed the fate of Reeves' John Wick animated prequel movie, giving it the official greenlight, and putting it currently in the pre-production stage. The film is being helmed by Ultraman: Rising's Shannon Tindle, while Ryan and Kaz Firpo are attached to pen the script.

Development on the animated John Wick prequel movie has been in the works for the better part of three years, with franchise boss Chad Stahelski announcing plans for it in late 2023 and teasing it being done in an anime styling. However, at the time, he indicated that it was going to be a TV series, and didn't originally describe it as being a direct prequel centered on Reeves' eponymous character, with 2025's CinemaCon bringing the latter reveal.

The project's previous announcement did also come with the reveal that the animated John Wick prequel movie would explore the iconic Impossible Task teased in the previous movies that he completed in order to effectively retire from the High Table-run world for a life with his soulmate, Helen. It has not yet been made clear if this will result in other returning characters being featured in the film, namely Willem Dafoe's Marcus, Michael Nyqvist's Viggo or Bridget Moynahan as Helen.

One notable change to come from the official Lionsgate greenlight is that the John Wick prequel movie has shifted its writing team. The film was originally set to be penned by Game of Thrones and The Shape of Water alum Vanessa Taylor, only for the Firpo cousins, who previously developed the screen story and a draft of the screenplay for Marvel's Eternals movie, to now be attached.

With the movie now officially greenlit at the studio, the John Wick prequel may not be the only franchise entry to get major updates in the near future. Given Caine wrapped production four months ago, and it already having been a year since Chapter 5 was announced with Reeves and Stahelski returning for a non-High-Table-driven plot, Lionsgate could have some more exciting updates on the blockbuster action series very soon.
