FC 27 Title Update 1.2.0 Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Title Update 1.2.0 is coming to FC 27 on Nov. 10, and it includes major changes. EA Sports has already announced the highlights we can expect in this major patch, and there will be plenty of gameplay tweaks.

Let’s find out what to expect from the next major gameplay patch.

Gameplay is the main focus of this patch, and EA Sports has considered major community feedback.

Title Update v1.2.0 introduces our first gameplay adjustments for #FC27. Including changes to 95 Depth, fullback positioning, and more. Alongside key updates for Career Mode, FUT, and The Grounds.Read the full Pitch Notes here: https://t.co/QcjkIj9OgJ pic.twitter.com/DlkP0t4Asp

95 Depth will be nerfed, and the position of defenders will be adjusted. Unlike previous instances, they will now drop off sooner while still maintaining the higher line. This allows attackers to have more space to build their attacks.

Fullbacks used to drop deeper than intended when defending attacks. This has been adjusted, and fullbacks will now hold a firmer defensive line.

Similarly, attackers will do a better job at reading the opponent’s defensive line and make better runs to capitalize on the space.

The defensive line will hold a slightly higher position right after kick-off, making it easier to defend against opponents.

Auto-switching will be improved in loose ball scenarios, which also includes instances when a teammate performs a slide tackle, a headed pass, or when a shot misses the target.

Career Mode will also receive improvements in UI and other areas.

The patch will also feature plenty of minor fixes across multiple modes.
