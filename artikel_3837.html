EXODUS ‘Female Jun Reveal + Scrappy Salvager’ featurette

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Archetype Entertainment has released an eight-minute featurette for science-fiction action adventure RPG EXODUS revealing the female version of protagonist Jun, as well as a behind the scenes look at what makes Jun special.

Female Jun is one of two playable versions of the game’s central protagonist. Revealed during a livestream with CohhCarnage, the new footage introduces another side of Jun’s journey, from a scrappy salvager dreaming of adventure to a Traveler exploring extraordinary worlds and confronting the dangers of a new galaxy.

The new featurette offers a closer look at Jun and the character’s role in the EXODUS universe, with insights from the development team on what makes Jun special. Players can choose to experience the story as either Male or Female Jun, with their decisions shaping the journey ahead.
