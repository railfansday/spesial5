Girls und Panzer: All-Star Tank Carnival adds PS5 and PC versions, launches March 25, 2027 in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Tank battle action Girls und Panzer: All-Star Tank Carnival will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on March 25, 2027 in Japan for 6,980 yen, D3 Publisher announced. A limited edition will also be available for 14,080 yen. The PlayStation 5 and Switch 2 versions will be available physically, with the Switch 2 version shipping on a game-key card. The PlayStation 5 and PC versions are newly announced. Cross-play is supported between all three versions.

With the goal of further promoting tankery, the town of Oarai, home to Oarai Girls’ Academy, becomes the stage for a major event. Gathered in the festive town are all the rival schools that Miho and her friends have fiercely battled and built bonds with. The “Special Interleague Match,” which involves the entire town, is set to be held. Miho and her friends can freely roam the town, which is buzzing with festivities, enjoying lively interactions with their rivals and heated tankery battles.

Explore Oarai as Miho Nishizumi and Enjoy an Original Story

Players can control protagonist Miho Nishizumi and freely explore the familiar town of Oarai as seen in the anime. By talking to the other tankery girls scattered throughout the town, players can trigger game-original stories that naturally lead to heated tankery battles. Additionally, players can take commemorative photographs with their favorite characters via Photo Mode. Enjoy a virtual tour of Oarai while progressing through a new story!

Featuring Characters, Tanks, and Maps from Up to Girls und Panzer das Finale: Part IV

The game features characters and tanks from the TV series through the anime film Girls und Panzer das Finale: Part IV. Experience thrilling and immersive tank battles that take full advantage of the terrain, from aquatic combat navigating jungle waterways in the “Special Type 2 Launch Ka-Mi,” to snow battles fought while sliding across vast snowy fields.

Over 90 Characters! All Conversation Scenes Feature New Artwork

All of the conversation scenes throughout the game feature entirely new character artwork. Over 90 tankery girls engage in conversations with a wide range of expressive facial expressions. These dream interactions, which bring together characters from different schools, are a must-see for fans.

Online cross-play is supported across all three platforms. Enjoy heated tankery battles with up to 10 players, regardless of which platform they’re playing on!
