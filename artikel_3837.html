Want to know where to go next in GTA 6? Then the Index is about to become your best friend

There's a lot to see and do in Leonida, to the point that it can be a little overwhelming. That's why it's essential for you to understand how to view tasks, see your goals, and track active missions in GTA 6.

The GTA 6 index is an essential part of the game experience, a menu that you're going to spend a lot of time in as you help Jason and Lucia navigate Vice City. Regardless of where you are in the world or what you're doing, pressing the touchpad will allow you to open up the GTA 6 map.

The map will display all local activities, stores, missions, and opportunities that you've discovered. However, you can see what else is available to you by using the index bar which runs across the side of the screen.

Click Right on the D-pad to open the index in GTA 6 and you'll have the following options:

The index shows all of the missions, tasks, and locations that you've discovered. Once you've navigated to what you're looking for on the map or in the index itself, you just need to hover over the task to learn more about it and then click X to set it as an active destination.

You can also set a destination while in free roam using Quick Navigation (activated by holding Down on the D-pad and pressing R3 on an in-world marker). It's worth noting that you can only set one active destination at a time, and that you should check both the map and index regularly for new opportunities as you explore – anything newly discovered will be marked in the index by an orange diamond symbol.

Tip: While you can only have one active destination set, you can drop multiple markers on the map. Just open the map with the touchpad and press Square on a useful location to leave a marker.
