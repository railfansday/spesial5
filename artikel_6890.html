I Just Doubled My Blush Collection With a Single Product

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

SHEGLAM’s new gray blush makes me feel like a pro makeup artist.

Personally, I’m thrilled that cool-toned makeup is making a comeback. I lived through the warm-toned trend, when it seemed like every palette featured varying shades of orange, orange, and more orange—none of which complement my fair complexion with pink undertones. Now, I’m taking advantage of the latest, cool-toned trend by enhancing my eyes with silver shadow, swiping on milky pink lip glosses, and even transforming my warm, summer blushes into flattering winter-ready shades with the help of the new SHEGLAM Squeeze & Dot Matte Paint Blush in Gray-tiful.

Yes, the blush is actually gray in the tube, but that isn’t as scary as you may think. In fact, it’s quickly becoming the MVP of my makeup bag. Not meant to be worn alone, it’s designed to blend with other blush shades to neutralize bright colors, cool off warm tones, and help you customize the intensity of your blush. I can combine it with any of my cream or liquid blushes, including the rest of the shades in the new SHEGLAM Squeeze & Dot Paint Blush collection in matte and shimmer. Suddenly, I’m getting so much more mileage out of blushes that I used to only reach for during the warmer months, when my complexion is naturally sun-kissed and I feel more pulled toward more vibrant looks.

Gray-tiful is one of eight Squeeze & Dot Matte Paint Blush collection, which also includes shades of peach, mauve, pink, and red. The formulas are infused with hyaluronic acid and glycerin to keep cheeks soft and hydrated. The squeeze-tube dotter makes it easy to pinpoint application exactly where I want it, whether that’s high up on my cheekbones, on the apples of my cheeks, or on my chin and nose for an all-over flush.

Here are my three favorite ways to use Gray-tiful, below.

SHEGLAM Squeeze & Dot Matte Paint Blushes such as Chili Rouge, a candy apple red, and Love Cake, a bright pink, are impressively pigmented. For me, this makes them perfect for special occasions, but for running errands or casual hangs with friends, I prefer a softer look. When I mix them with a dot of Gray-tiful using a dense bristle brush, these bold shades become more subtle, which matches my low-key, everyday vibe.

There’s something so pretty and whimsical about a frosty cheek, especially in the winter. I like to apply two dots of the SHEGLAM Shimmer Paint Blush in the peachy Pink About You on my cheekbones, then add one dot of Gray-tiful, and blend. The result is a luminous, cool-toned flush that catches the light beautifully.

This has to be my favorite way to use Gray-tiful; I feel like an artist mixing up my own custom hues. This is a time for creativity and experimentation, so there’s no one right way to mix colors. I like to squeeze a dab of Gray-tiful on the back of my hand, then add one, two, or even three blush colors on top and mix them all together to see what I can create. Recently, I transformed the Matte Paint Blush in Heart Wish, a warm mauve with two dabs of Gray-tiful and the tiniest hint of Pink About You. The result? My dream winter blush shade.
