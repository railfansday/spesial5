Google and Samsung are opening preorders for AI smart glasses starting at $395

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Warby Parker and Gentle Monster models with Gemini built in are set to go on sale Dec. 10, with Gentle Monster frames at $449 and up

Alphabet $GOOGL's Google and Samsung Electronics opened preorders on Thursday for smart glasses with the Gemini AI assistant, with Warby Parker models starting at $395.

Samsung set a Dec. 10 release in the U.S. and South Korea. Warby Parker glasses start at $395 in the U.S., and Gentle Monster glasses range from $449 to $489, depending on the model. Both collections will launch in the U.S., and the Gentle Monster collection will also launch in South Korea.

The lineup has 30 designs across six Gentle Monster frame styles and three Warby Parker styles, Samsung said. Samsung engineered the hardware, and Google developed the product with it.

The glasses weigh around 41 grams (about 1.4 ounces) and have a battery rated for up to nine hours, Google said. The charging case can recharge the frames up to seven times. The glasses have a 12-megapixel camera and open-ear speakers, and they work with Pixel phones, Samsung phones and iPhones.

Users can say “Hey Gemini” or tap the frames to get help. Google said Gemini can add items to a grocery list, check a user’s Google Calendar when they look at an event poster, and handle calls and messages hands-free. Google Maps provides turn-by-turn directions, and Google Translate offers real-time speech translation in more than 70 languages.

Google said camera visuals from Gemini never go into Gemini History, and they are also kept out of AI model training and ad targeting. The glasses are designed to redact bystanders' faces before Gemini processes images. An indicator light turns on whenever the user captures a picture or clip, and the glasses are built to halt recording should anyone block or disable that light. Samsung said the glasses are designed for users aged 18 and over, with age verification required for activation.

Preorders are live online and in stores at Gentle Monster and Warby Parker, Google said. Customers can sign up for a guided demo at more than 360 Warby Parker locations, and hands-on demos will be available at select Google Store locations.

Google will not sell the glasses directly at launch, according to Bloomberg. The glasses run Google's Android XR operating system, and Meta $META Platforms leads the smart-glasses category.

"As glasses become more capable, our focus is on creating helpful experiences that make both wearers and bystanders feel protected," Shahram Izadi, Google's vice president and general manager of its XR unit, wrote in a separate post on Thursday, according to Bloomberg.
