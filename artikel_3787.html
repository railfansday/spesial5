Warhammer Survivors release countdown: Exact date and time

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Warhammer Survivors is coming soon to gamers worldwide, and you won’t have to wait long to try out this upcoming Bullet Heaven game. Developed by Auroch Digital, it was made by the same team that gave us Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun. Compared to that title, however, this one is an entirely different experience. Here’s when it launches.

Warhammer Survivors goes live on Tuesday, November 10. The release date was announced earlier by Auroch Digital, the developers of the game. The following countdown will expire once the game goes live.

Warhammer Survivors is a Bullet Heaven set in the grimdark world of Warhammer. Featuring over 40 iconic playable characters, the game offers multiple modes and stages inspired by the Mortal Realms and the 41st Millennium universes.

You also get to enjoy over 75 different weapons, including the iconic Boltgun, Astartes Chainsword, Whirlwind Axes, and more. You have to use them efficiently against the mightiest monsters the universe has to offer.

As you level up your characters, you can enjoy a mighty cache of passives and power-ups. These power-ups will add extra abilities that allow you to slay with greater efficiency.

Every run in Warhammer Survivors results in you improving your character builds. You can choose to defeat an endless number of hordes once you have built up the different characters available in the game. Survive for as long as possible, complete the upgrades, and then go again to do better.

If you’re unsure of what the game has to offer, you can check out the free demo available on Steam.
