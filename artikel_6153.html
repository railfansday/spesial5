Reminder: Palm Beach airport code changing from PBI to DJT

On Aug. 18, the International Air Transport Association (IATA) airport code for President Donald J. Trump International Airport in Palm Beach, Florida, will change from PBI to DJT. This is the code used on most customer- and team member-facing systems.

Transition period: Some tools and manuals may continue to reference this airport by its previous name and airport code during a transition period.

If you receive any questions from customers about the change, refer to these talking points:

Why is American using the DJT airport code for President Donald J. Trump International Airport?

American follows airport codes as determined by the International Air Transport Association and other bodies. After Florida passed a law earlier this year to rename the airport, IATA announced they would change the three-digit airport code.

Why is American referring to this airport as President Donald J. Trump International Airport?

Earlier this year, a law was passed in the State of Florida renaming this airport from Palm Beach International Airport to President Donald J. Trump International Airport. American follows all airport names and guidance as determined by international bodies as well as federal, state and local governments.

Why does the screen behind you say, “West Palm Beach, FL”?

Given that this transition is now complete and both the ICAO and IATA codes have changed, GIDS displays will once again say “West Palm Beach, FL.”

American Airlines has dropped “Pres DJT” from the gate monitors of its West Palm Beach flights, going back to “West Palm Beach, FL,” as flagged by aviation watchdog JonNYC. It undoes the unusual decision to give the sitting president billing that other airport namesakes don’t get.

JonNYC flagged the change Wednesday night, then added that he isn’t claiming a connection to the election results. It’s not clear exactly when American flipped the switch.

American even gave agents talking points to explain the change. Their internal memo pointed to Florida’s renaming law and the change in the airport’s three-letter code, saying:

American follows all airport names and guidance as determined by international bodies as well as federal, state and local governments.

That explained why the airport had a new name, but not putting the president’s name on the gate screen. And there’s no change made by the airport itself! Florida’s renaming of Palm Beach International as President Donald J. Trump International Airport took effect July 9. The passenger-facing airport code changed from PBI to DJT on August 18.
