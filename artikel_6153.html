BermudAir Adds 10 New Destinations, 18 New Routes

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bermudian flag carrier BermudAir will nearly double its U.S. footprint next year as it launches new connections across North and Central America and the Caribbean.

The carrier announced Tuesday that it will serve New Orleans; Nashville, Tennessee; New Haven, Connecticut; Burlington, Vermont; Portland, Maine; San Antonio; New Brunswick, Canada; Ocho Rios, Jamaica; San Salvador, El Salvador; and San Pedro Sula, Honduras, for the first time.

Accompanying the 10 new destinations are 18 new routes. BermudAir is also moving to expand service to existing markets, such as Bermuda, Anguilla, Belize, Grand Cayman, Guatemala, and Turks and Caicos.

“From New Orleans and Nashville to San Antonio and New England, we’re seeing tremendous interest in what BermudAir offers,” founder and CEO Adam Scott said in a news release.

BermudAir currently serves eight U.S. cities, all on or close to the East Coast, and three destinations in Canada. Its main hub is L.F. Wade International Airport in St. George’s, Bermuda.
