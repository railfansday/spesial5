Mexico’s President Flew 21 Hours To China In Economy (In A Middle Seat!)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

I’ve gotta say, huge kudos to Mexico’s President, Claudia Sheinbaum, for setting quite the example in terms of how she recently traveled to China, as flagged by View from the Wing…

World leaders have just met in Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders’ Meeting. As you’d expect, most leaders flew on their government planes. There were some exceptions, though I can’t imagine anyone else flew the same way as Mexico’s 64-year-old president.

Viajamos hacia Shenzhen para representar a México en el Foro de Cooperación Económica Asia-Pacífico (APEC) China 2026; la agenda de actividades incluye reunión con empresarios de países miembros. Además, participaré en la conferencia de prensa matutina para informar avances de… pic.twitter.com/F9UqRLjFlb

Media reports suggest she flew to China on China Southern. Specifically, she traveled on Sunday, November 15, 2026, on CZ8032, from Mexico City (MEX) to Shenzhen (SZX), with a refueling stop in Tijuana (TIJ), and the journey covering a distance of 8,803 miles.

The Airbus A350-900 took off from Mexico City at 12:49AM, arriving in Tijuana at 2:02AM, after a flight time of 3hr13min. Then it took off again at 4:34AM, and landed in Shenzhen the following morning at 11:31AM, after a flight time of 14hr56min. All-in-all, the flight took around 21 hours, including the refueling stop.

The shocking thing here isn’t that she traveled commercial, but instead, that she reportedly flew in economy! Reports suggest she was in seat 33B, which would be a middle seat in economy, no less!

That being said, I absolutely commend her for the example she is setting. It’s certainly quite a contrast to what we’ve seen here, where President Trump accepted a Boeing 747-8 as a gift from Qatar, and talked about how he has done it to make Americans be proud, because “our country has to be represented properly.” Heck, just this week he talked about “China was very impressed” and “Russia was very impressed” of his new plane. Lovely, I’m happy we’re impressing Putin, that makes me proud to be an American!

Keep in mind that Mexico has an interesting history when it comes to how leaders fly. In 2016, Mexico’s government took control of a private Boeing 787, which flew around former President Enrique Pena Nieto. The purchase of the plane had actually been arranged under the previous president, Felipe Calderón, in 2012.

Then in 2018, when Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador took charge in Mexico, he fulfilled a campaign promise of selling that 787, which turned out to be pretty challenging, given that demand for a secondhand VIP 787 isn’t exactly that high (the plane ended up going to Tajikistan). Sheinbaum has kept that general legacy going, by flying commercial.

Mexico’s President, Claudia Sheinbaum, traveled to the recent summit in China differently than virtually all other government leaders. Not only did she fly commercial, but she traveled in economy, and reportedly even in a middle seat.

There’s no denying that this is more about optics than anything, but I very much respect that. It’s nice to see heads of state who lead by example, as it’s pretty rare to see nowadays.
