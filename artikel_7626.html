10th Circuit addresses AI hallucinations with new human fact-check rule

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The 10th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals has instituted a rule requiring mandatory human review of all court filings that use artificial intelligence. (Photo by Getty Images)

In an unprecedented move, a U.S. federal appeals court has instituted a rule requiring mandatory human review of all court filings that use artificial intelligence.

The 10th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals at Denver posted a new rule Friday stipulating attorneys and pro see parties certify human verification of all cited authorities and quotations, Law360 reports.

At the start of next year, the 10th Circuit can address violations by waiving the affected issue, striking a noncompliant document or affirming or dismissing an appeal. The judge may also impose monetary sanctions or demand coverage of some of the opponent’s attorney fees.

Four years after generative AI debuted on a mass scale, many fake citations have been discovered in court filings resulting from large language models sometimes producing false information or hallucinations, according to Law360. French data consultant Damien Charlotin found that court cases involving hallucinated case citations worldwide have decreased this year, which he attributes to hallucination awareness and LLM improvements.
