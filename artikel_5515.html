Injective (INJ) remains focused on decentralized finance and on-chain trading infrastructure. Its proof-of-stake network allows holders to delegate tokens to validators and earn staking rewards. Injective has also attracted institutional attention through investment products tied to its native token, adding another potential catalyst for market interest.

Pearl (PEARL) has built its narrative around AI and blockchain infrastructure, with a network designed to support decentralized computing through its Proof-of-Useful-Work model.

The three tokens represent different areas of the crypto market, but their recent gains point to renewed appetite for altcoin exposure.

The altcoin rebound is also putting Bitcoin scaling projects back in the spotlight, particularly those seeking to expand Bitcoin’s role beyond a store of value. Bitcoin Hyper is developing exactly this: a Bitcoin Layer 2 designed to support faster transactions and smart-contract functionality through the Solana Virtual Machine.

The project’s HYPER token is currently offered through a presale priced at just $0.0136, with the presale scheduled to end on November 26, 2026. The project’s official website reports that the sale has raised more than $33.2 million.

That puts Bitcoin Hyper’s presale in its final weeks, with buyers facing a stated deadline before the planned token launch. The current price is a presale price, however, and does not guarantee the token’s eventual exchange value.

Bitcoin Hyper also offers 35% annual percentage yield for users who stake HYPER during the presale. This rate is substantially higher than the reward rate available through Injective crypto staking, which varies with network conditions and validator commissions. The comparison is notable for traders weighing potential staking rewards across crypto assets.

Zcash and PEARL do not offer native proof-of-stake rewards in the same way, as their networks use proof-of-work consensus.

Bitcoin Hyper’s fundraising also provides a point of comparison with other crypto projects. Its $33.2 million presale total exceeds the $25 million seed round raised by Zcash Open Development Lab, a company building products around the Zcash ecosystem.

Injective is close, having raised $40 million in a private token sale, while Pearl Research Labs has reported a larger equity funding total.

Bitcoin Hyper’s approaching November 26 deadline is becoming a key part of its presale story. The project aims to combine Bitcoin’s liquidity with faster transactions and programmable applications, using an SVM-based Layer 2 architecture. Its roadmap includes a bridge, transaction processing, and settlement back to Bitcoin.

The testnet is being developed separately from the planned token launch, with full mainnet functionality remaining a future milestone.

For us watching the altcoin rebound, HYPER offers exposure to a different narrative: expanding Bitcoin into a network for decentralized applications and on-chain finance.

With the November 26 deadline approaching, Bitcoin Hyper is positioning itself as one of the presale projects seeking to convert renewed crypto market interest into demand for a Bitcoin-focused ecosystem.
