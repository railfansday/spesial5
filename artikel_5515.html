Christine Lagarde is resigning as head of the European Central Bank months early

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The departure gives European leaders time to settle on a replacement before France holds its presidential vote in 2027

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde will step down on Feb. 7, 2027, ahead of the scheduled end of her term.

Lagarde informed the ECB's Governing Council and European Council President António Costa of the decision on Thursday, the ECB said. The European Council will appoint her successor following the nomination process outlined in Article 283 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the ECB said. The Governing Council gave her a long standing ovation, the ECB said.

Her eight-year term had been set to expire on Oct. 31, 2027, according to The Associated Press.

In a clip she shared on X $TWTR, Lagarde called it "a great pride and a huge privilege to serve this institution."

Costa said the selection process "will be initiated today and will proceed without delay."

The departure gives European leaders time to settle on a replacement before France holds its presidential vote in 2027. Two other top ECB posts are also changing hands. Chief economist Philip Lane's term ends May 31, and executive board member Isabel Schnabel is leaving Jan. 3 to join the International Monetary Fund, according to The Associated Press.

Leaders are expected to strike a package deal that spreads the three open jobs across E.U. member states, according to The Associated Press.

Klaas Knot, who used to head the Netherlands' central bank, and Pablo Hernandez de Cos, a onetime Spanish central bank governor, are regarded as the leading contenders to take over from Lagarde, according to Reuters.

Lagarde’s announcement landed on the same day the ECB is scheduled to publish its interest rate decision at 1:15 p.m. GMT (9:15 a.m. ET), with her regular press conference set for 1:45 p.m. GMT, according to Reuters. The bank is broadly predicted to leave borrowing costs unchanged, with the deposit rate staying at 2.5%, according to Reuters.
