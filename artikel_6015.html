Thrifty Traveler Is 30% Off For Black Friday—A Year Of Flight And Business Class Award Alerts For $90.99

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

They tell me this is their biggest sale of the year, and the biggest they expect to offer for the foreseeable future. It matches the 30% discount I covered in August.

Readers who took advantage of the offer before their March price increase did better: $79 for the first year and $99 for renewals. That offer’s gone. The regular annual prices are now $129.99 and $189.99, and their price lock guarantee applies to those full prices, not the discounted first year.

I’ve said this is one of the better services for finding rare premium cabin awards. They search departures from more than 200 airports in the U.S. and Canada, and you choose which airports you want alerts for. You can stick to your home airport or include other cities you’re willing to position to.

Deals arrive by email, there’s an online hub where you can browse them, and you can opt into text alerts for mistake fares and their rarest “unicorn” deals. They say they’ve recently sent more than 80 flight alerts in a month, including more than 30 business class award alerts, such as:

Most alerts won’t fit any one person’s plans. I like this most for someone with transferable points who can let award availability help decide where to go. Finding one useful business class award can easily justify the subscription. If you need a particular flight on a particular date, though, a stream of deals to other places isn’t going to solve that.

They’ve also added FareTracker. Premium members can track up to 10 cash flights and get an email when the price drops by at least $20, with instructions to rebook and claim the difference as an airline credit. You still need a fare whose rules allow that, and you have to do the rebooking. But it’s a useful reason to keep watching a ticket after you’ve bought it.

The Premium+ bundle adds hotel deals, including difficult award availability and discounted paid stays. Recent alerts have included Park Hyatt Kyoto and the renovated Waldorf Astoria New York. The extra $42 makes sense if you’d build a trip around finding award nights at a property like that.
