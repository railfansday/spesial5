Zay Flowers limited at Wednesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Baltimore Ravens WR Zay Flowers (ankle) was a limited participant in practice Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Why it Matters: Flowers' status is worth keeping an eye on, but assuming he is able to suit up, he can be considered a WR2 with upside. In two games since Jacoby Brissett became the quarterback, Flowers has 14 receptions on 18 targets.
