Travis Kelce’s feelings about Dancing with the Stars winner Ezra Frech revealed with social media move

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ezra Frech was recently crowned the Dancing with the Stars Season 35 winner alongside Daniella Karagach, and Travis Kelce made a recent social media move in support of his historic feat.

Frech made history as the first-ever amputee and Paralympian to win DWTS on Nov. 24. In honor of the two-time Paralympic gold medalist bringing home the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy, comedian and actor Adam Sandler shared a photo of them holding the trophy on Instagram, congratulating the winning duo.

Sandler didn't add a caption to the post, but Kelce did give the comedian's post a "like" in support.

Frech went to the comment section to thank Sandler for the congratulatory post, "LOVE YOU ADAM!!!!

During Frech's performance, his leg detached mid-routine during his freestyle dance to Linkin Park's "Numb." Despite the mishap, he was able to recover and finish his routine to win a perfect 30-30 score. He later shared how much being a part of the show meant to him.

"I dreamt of getting the opportunity to be a small role in representing my community. And this is the biggest stage there is," Frech said.

As for Kelce, he didn't expand over just sending a "like" Frech's way but as an athlete himself known to have the spotlight on him and his team, he most likely resonated with the power of resistance one feels when achieving a goal. The Kansas City Chiefs tight end recently had a career milestone when he played in his 100th NFL game on Nov. 22 against the Arizona Cardinals. Kelce and the Chiefs won 34–7 over the Cardinals and the tight end had an impressive gameplay with six catches, 74 receiving yards, and two touchdowns.

"Hundred games, dude, it feels pretty awesome right now," Travis told his older brother and co-host, Jason Kelce, on their New Heights podcast this week. "To think about the legacy of that stadium, man, and it's pretty crazy to think they're moving on from that stadium, because of all the history that's happened over the years and history of the franchise and everything."

"You know it's been home for me for 14 years, and it's been one of my favorite places to go to play and to watch not only football, but musical events, and I've had so much history and so many things that mean so much to me happen at Arrowhead that a hundred games, it's pretty awesome to hit that milestone," he said.

Now, Kelce will be suiting up for his 101st game against the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving (Nov. 26) at 8:15 p.m.
