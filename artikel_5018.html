Jensen Huang’s NVIDIA comeback sparks $1 trillion rally and $12 million Polymarket frenzy

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NVIDIA shares surged over 35% after its board reversed Huang's removal, sending prediction-market odds of his return from 6% to nearly 100%.

NVIDIA added about $1 trillion in market value on Nov. 23 as its shares surged more than 35% after the unexpected reinstatement of Jensen Huang as chief executive.

The rally put the chipmaker on course for the largest single-day market capitalization gain in stock market history, reversing part of the selloff triggered by Huang's resignation less than two weeks earlier.

The Kobeissi Letter noted that NVIDIA's shares initially climbed more than 28%, adding approximately $820 billion to its market capitalization, before extending gains above 35% and pushing the increase past $1 trillion.

If sustained through the closing bell, the increase would establish a new record for the largest amount of market value added by a publicly traded company in a single session.

The gains followed NVIDIA's announcement that its board had unanimously reinstated Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately.

Directors said discussions with Huang had satisfied them about his ability to lead the company. The board also consulted senior US government officials before concluding that his reinstatement posed no national security risk.

The decision reversed NVIDIA's Nov. 12 leadership shake-up, which had intensified selling across technology stocks and cryptocurrencies.

The rebound extended beyond NVIDIA, with the Nasdaq advancing more than 6% and the S&P 500 gaining approximately 5% in early trading.

Cryptocurrencies also rallied as traders rapidly increased their exposure to riskier assets. Bitcoin surged more than 4% within an hour of the announcement, reaching $64,427 after briefly falling below $60,000 during the previous week's selloff.

However, smaller cryptocurrencies recorded even stronger gains.

Glassnode reported that altcoins led the market's initial response to Huang's reinstatement. Within 20 minutes of the announcement, nearly every cryptocurrency among the 50 largest was outperforming Bitcoin.

The analytics firm observed the rally spread from altcoins to the Nasdaq and Bitcoin, while the S&P 500 posted a smaller advance.

The rapid rotation showed how quickly appetite for speculative assets returned following the leadership reversal at the world's leading AI chipmaker.

The reinstatement also produced a dramatic reversal in prediction-market pricing.

On Nov. 16, CryptoSlate previously reported that Polymarket traders assigned Huang only a 6% probability of returning to NVIDIA by Nov. 30, despite growing pressure from prominent technology executives and investors.

At the time, the probability of a return by Dec. 31 stood at 14%, while the longer-dated contract covering December 2027 carried a 38% probability.

However, those expectations changed significantly following NVIDIA's announcement. By Nov. 23, the November contract had climbed to 99.3%, before reaching 100% on Polymarket's market page.

Combined trading volume across the four contracts reached approximately $12.34 million, reflecting heightened interest in the leadership crisis.

The sharp repricing illustrates how strongly traders had discounted Huang's prospects of returning before the end of November, only for NVIDIA's board to reverse its earlier decision.

Under Polymarket's resolution rules, Huang needed to return to NVIDIA in a formal executive, advisory, or governance capacity before the relevant deadline.

His reinstatement as both chief executive and director satisfies those conditions, turning what had been considered an unlikely outcome into a confirmed corporate development.
