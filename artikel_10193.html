But you’re reading this on 9to5Google, so how does it impact you as an Android user? That’s a great question.

RedPocket is offering $150 rebates on Android devices like the Pixel 11, Galaxy S26, Galaxy Z Flip 7, Moto G Play, and more. (You can also get $100 rebates for iPhones if you’re into that sort of thing.)

These offers run from November 9 through the 30th at redpocket.com, so don’t wait too long to save big.

It’s OK if you haven’t heard of RedPocket Mobile, because it has been quietly working for 20 years to improve the way people use their phones. Founded in 2006, the company works with all three major US carriers — AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon — and because it doesn’t own any of its towers, it can pass along significant savings to its customers. Here’s what the company says on its website:

RedPocket was built for price-conscious consumers who want real value and freedom from rigid, high-cost contracts. As a no-contract carrier, we’ve stayed focused on making reliable, transparent, and flexible phone service for everyone. Our plans are designed to fit real lifestyles, whether you’re a heavy data user, a frequent traveler, or just looking to cut your monthly bill.

Most MVNOs are owned by the Big Three, which means you don’t really have a choice of network when you sign up. But RedPocket works with all three major carriers and lets you choose which one works best for your location and usage patterns.

RedPocket offers a tool called CoverageGenius that helps you decide which network is right for you. While you can’t switch between networks once your line is set up, it’s a great way to figure out where to start.

I teased that $5/month cost at the beginning of the article, but here’s the real breakdown: you can get a full year of service for $60 if you’re a light data user. RedPocket’s Annual Essentials and Annual Plus plans get you unlimited calling and texting in the US, along with calling and data roaming in dozens of countries around the world. The main difference is how much data you get: 3GB for Essentials and 20GB for Plus.

Annual Premium ups the ante to unlimited data, plus calling and data roaming in even more countries.

Across all three plans, you’re saving between 33% and 50% off the regular price when you sign up between November 9th and 30th.

One thing to note about all of these plans: the annual prices are paid upfront, not billed monthly. Promotional pricing applies to the first year, and after that, annual plans renew at $110 for Essentials, $220 for Plus, and $330 for Premium.

It costs just $10 for the first month to try any of the three plans, with Monthly Plus rising to $20/month after that and Premium $30/month thereafter (Essentials stays at $10/month).
