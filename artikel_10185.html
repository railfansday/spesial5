Google Voice app gets a redesign on Android, and it screams RCS [Gallery]

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Google Voice is a wonderful service that’s often neglected, but with RCS supposedly on the way, a new app redesign is now rolling out on Android that might as well have a big neon sign that says “RCS is coming.”

Google Voice is a US-only service that operates a phone number over the internet. No normal carrier, just an app tied to your Google account that gets a phone number with the ability to make calls and send/receive text messages. The latter, though, has been a point of contention for the past few years as Voice was excluded from Google’s huge push into RCS.

That might finally be changing, though. Recent updates brought mention of RCS in the app’s code.

Now, a slightly newer version (v2026-11-01-1019342246) of Google Voice has debuted a redesign for the app on Android.

The new look isn’t a drastic restructuring, but a huge coat of paint on an app that hasn’t seen any meaningful updates since 2023. The new look feels more modern and very much influenced by Google Messages. The text field at the bottom has a rounded design with the same “+” and camera icons found in Messages, as well as a new long-press gesture that is, again, similar to Google’s primary messaging app. The main page hasn’t been updated beyond a new, rounded search bar up top and some subtly bolder fonts.

Material You colors are hit or miss now. On a Pixel with an orange theme, that color influenced the entire UI, but on a Galaxy Z Fold 8 with a coral/blue theme, the only sign of themeing was a blue message field.

The most important thing here is the “SMS” icon on the send button, which looks just like the button in Google Messages. In that app, it’s used as an indicator as to what type of message you’re sending – SMS or RCS. Combined with code in that other recent update directly pointing to “RCS” being used on a send button, you can absolutely tell Google Voice is finally adding this long-overdue functionality.

A timeline for RCS support in Google Voice is still unclear, but this redesign going live now certainly suggests it’s coming sooner rather than later.
