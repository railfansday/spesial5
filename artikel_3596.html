GTA 6 Has A Surprise Cameo From Someone You Probably Didn’t Expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Grand Theft Auto 6 has plenty of famous faces and voices behind its many, many characters. From Bobby Moynihan as your verbose bestie to Weird Al Yankovic as a radio host, there's plenty of star power here--and you can even spot an influential figure from GTA history making a quick cameo.

Spoilers below

In one of the later missions, players accompany Boobie on one of his errands. Once they reach a new club, Boobie meets up with Big Mike and throws a few jibes at the local help sweeping the floor. That "little Santa" with the broom? None other than Rockstar Games president Sam Houser, who doesn't speak a single word in his brief appearance. As an added touch, Houser's digital doppelganger wears a white t-shirt with the Rockstar Games logo.

Because the thumbnail for it is a big ol' spoiler, you can click this link to see the Houser cameo for yourself.

This isn't the first time that Houser has cameoed in one of his studio's games. In GTA 3, he voiced one of the Ammu-Nation store clerks, and in GTA: San Andreas, a statue of that Ammu-Nation employee could be found in the game's Downtown Square area. Beyond minor background pedestrian and gangster voice roles in San Andreas and GTA 4, Houser has generally kept a low profile, focusing instead on developing the studio's games.

With GTA 6 launching to major critical and commercial success, Houser has also issued a rare statement to mark the occasion. "This is a narrative set in a world of possibilities--and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels and concludes," he said. Meanwhile, Houser's brother, Dan Houser, is working on an open-world comedy game at Absurd Ventures, the studio he founded after leaving Rockstar Games in 2020.
