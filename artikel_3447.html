Cause of Fire on Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith's L.A. Property Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

New details about Will Smith and wife Jada Pinkett Smith’s property fire in Southern California have been revealed.

The Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department — Malibu/Lost Hills Sheriff’s Station confirmed to Us on Monday, November 23, that firefighters responded to a “structure fire” at the couple’s $42 million Calabasas home at approximately 12:04 a.m. local time.

“Security at the location reported a maintenance shed was on fire. Upon arrival, deputies saw a shed type of structure engulfed in flames,” authorities said in a statement. “Los Angeles County Fire Department personnel arrived and extinguished the fire.”

The sheriff’s department added, “No other structures were damaged, and no one was injured. The cause of the fire was accidental.”

Us has reached out to reps for Smith and Pinkett Smith for comment.

Page Six previously reported that a call was made to 911 around midnight local time regarding a shack on the couple’s 150-acre property catching fire but it was extinguished in the early hours on Monday.

Partial 911 audio from the incident, released by the outlet, offered more information on the situation.

“We do have a shed on fire with no spread to the brush,” a firefighter can be heard saying on the call before indicating emergency personnel were already “on scene.”

The responding firefighter also noted that the blaze had been “downgraded to a structure.”

The Men in Black actor, 58, and Pinkett Smith, 55, have experienced fires several times since tying the knot in December 1997.

The couple, who share son Jaden, 28, and daughter Willow, 26, bought the land for their Calabasas compound in 2003. They completed their 13-bedroom estate in 2010. (Smith also shares son Trey, 34, with ex Sheree Zampino.)

The property underwent a second renovation following a September 2021 fire, which broke out in the basement and led to smoke damage. The pair were later forced to evacuate during the January 2025 Palisades Fire that destroyed more than 6,000 homes, businesses and structures.

Smith and Pinkett Smith have weathered relationship struggles in the spotlight as well. Pinkett Smith confirmed in 2023 that she and Smith had been separated since 2016 and were not living together at the time.

The actors reportedly moved back in together the following year, with People citing sources in July that Smith and Pinkett Smith are “committed to supporting each other.” (The couple were spotted in a rare joint outing in Paris earlier that year.)
