The Missing Chapter Between ‘Dune Messiah’ and ‘Children of Dune’ Officially Released

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

There have been plenty of great sci-fi movies and TV shows released this year, but everything has been leading to one of the most anticipated films the genre has ever produced. We’re talking, of course, about Dune: Part Three, the third and final Dune movie in the acclaimed sci-fi franchise from director Denis Villeneuve. Villeneuve is onto James Bond after he completes work on Dune 3, and star Robert Pattinson may even be in line to play the next 007 in the film from Amazon. HBO also has another season of Dune: Prophecy in the works, but there’s still no word yet on when it will be released. PlayStation owners have even been enjoying the latest release for Dune: Awakening, which added a story mode along with several quality-of-life updates that make the game easier to play.

The first Dune novel by Frank Herbert is so sprawling and ambitious that it took a pair of two-hour+ movies to tell the story, and even then, Denis Villeneuve wasn’t able to include everything from the books. The third and final film in the trilogy is adapting the second book in Herbert’s series, Dune: Messiah, but it’s far from the end of the story on Arrakis between the pages. Seven years after the release of Dune: Messiah, Frank Herbert released Children of Dune, which was only the third of six books in the original Dune series. Earlier this month, Frank Herbert’s son Brian, along with Kevin J. Anderson, Michael Shelfer, and Bill Sienkiewicz, released the first installment in a two-part, cinematic graphic novel series for Dune: Messiah. Abrams ComicArts published the first part of the series on November 3. A release date for Part 2 has not yet been announced.

Dune: Messiah jumps forward in time to show how Paul’s reign as emperor has taken a toll on him over the years, particularly how he falls to treachery and corruption like so many other leaders. Paul’s political enemies are closer than ever, and his trust among his inner circle is completely depleted. Fans are especially excited to see what Timothée Chalamet does with the character in the third installment, as he’ll have to play a less morally grey hero and more villain if the movie resembles the book.

Check out Brian Herbert’s new graphic novel adaptation of Dune: Messiah and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Dune: Part Three, which hits theaters on December 18.
