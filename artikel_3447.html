Robert Pattinson’s James Bond Gets Filming Update Amid Casting Reactions

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Robert Pattinson was recently unveiled as the next 007 after months of growing speculation and a long list of potential suitors. Amazon MGM Studios is bringing new life to the spy franchise with Denis Villeneuve at the helm. During a panel at the Doha Industry Days moderated by Variety, corporate affairs director of the UK's beloved Pinewood Studios, Andrew M. Smith, discussed his "wishes" for the franchise to return to its roots.

"I’m thrilled that Bond’s been announced. We’re talking about it. We’re the home of Bond. Fingers crossed."

Pinewood Studios, located in Buckinghamshire, England, has been the home and primary production base for the Bond franchise since 1962's Dr. No. It has been the location for 23 of 25 Eon Bond movies, with Smith hoping Amazon continues the tradition and production to take place where Bond was born.
