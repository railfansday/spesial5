Netflix Officially Revives Sylvester Stallone’s Beloved Action Thriller Before the Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

2026 has been a very good year to be a Sylvester Stallone fan. I Play Rocky, the story of how Stallone wrote the Rocky screenplay and bet everything on himself to play the lead against impossible odds, recently premiered at TIFF and is already being hailed as one of the best movies in an already stacked year. Then Stallone fans got another treat as well, when he revealed that he has completed the script for Rocky 7. Apart from the Rocky universe, Stallone has also been enjoying success on TV with the recently released fourth season of Taylor Sheridan's Tulsa King. Now, another Stallone favorite just got an exciting update.

The film in question is a ‘90s cult classic survival thriller with Stallone as a mountain-rescue expert. A plane heist goes wrong, the aircraft blows up, and three suitcases holding $100 million are scattered across the mountains. The criminals then fake a distress call to lure Stallone’s character and his former partner into the wilderness, and force them to use their climbing skills to track down the missing money.

The film is none other than Cliffhanger, which, ironically, doesn’t end on one. It was directed by Renny Harlin, the same filmmaker behind other beloved classics like Deep Blue Sea and Die Hard 2, and it was a huge blockbuster hit when it came out in theaters. The film grossed a whopping $255 million worldwide against a $70 million budget. Now, it is about to gain a whole new life because it has finally landed on Netflix as of November 1.

This newfound attention is going to be great, because the franchise is being rebooted as a feature film slated for 2027. Stallone was initially attached to a direct sequel, but a major creative overhaul in 2024 led to his departure, and he is completely disconnected from this one. With Stallone gone, the reboot needs all the goodwill it can get, and a Netflix debut is a great way to remind longtime fans why they loved the original.

The upcoming reboot will be directed by Jaume Collet-Serra, who has already proven his directing chops with thrillers like Carry-On, House of Wax, and Orphan. Early plot details pitch the reboot as a father-daughter take on Die Hard. It’ll be a kidnapping story where veteran mountaineer Ray Cooper (Pierce Brosnan) is taken hostage on his weekend retreat, and his daughter Naomi (Lily James) has to face her fears and cross the brutal alpine peaks to save her family.

Cliffhanger is available to stream now on Netflix.
