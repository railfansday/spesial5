This "Unstoppable" Cryptocurrency Is Ready to Deliver Massive Gains in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This fast-rising cryptocurrency is at the intersection of AI and blockchain infrastructure.

By Dominic Basulto – Nov 19, 2026 at 6:12AM EST

10 stocks we like better than NEAR Protocol ›

The crypto market is a sea of red right now. Bitcoin (BTC -0.20%) is down 30% for the year as I write this. Ethereum (ETH +1.68%) is down 40%. And more speculative altcoins are down anywhere from 40% to 65% so far in 2026.

But there are a few "unstoppable" cryptocurrencies that continue to soar in value, regardless of what's happening around them. One of them is NEAR Protocol (NEAR +2.09%), which is up more than 100% for the year.

Like Ethereum, NEAR Protocol is a Layer-1 blockchain network. That means developers can build on top of it, thereby creating the potential for a thriving blockchain ecosystem powered by the NEAR token.

But there's one important twist: NEAR Protocol has been optimized for artificial intelligence. And, more specifically, for AI agents. In fact, NEAR now refers to itself as "the open infrastructure powering the agent economy." If you believe that millions of AI agents will soon be transacting with each other, then NEAR is worth a closer look.

That AI angle helps to explain why NEAR is surging, while Ethereum is collapsing. NEAR Protocol is a play on both artificial intelligence and blockchain infrastructure. While Ethereum could theoretically make a hard pivot into AI, it has not done so. And that has forced investors into the arms of NEAR Protocol.

While NEAR Protocol is currently trading for just $3, there are plenty of hyper-bullish price targets swirling around it. It's commonly accepted that NEAR has the potential to double in value to reach $6.

But that could be just the start. Coinpedia has posted NEAR price targets for 2030, and they are head-spinning. The average price target is just north of $45. The most bullish target is a whopping $72.

So there's definitely upside potential ahead. NEAR might not just double in price -- it might 10x, 15x, 20x, or even 25x.

So far, so good, right? Well, kinda. The problem is that the AI investment boom could end at any time, and that would likely poke all sorts of holes in the case for buying NEAR Protocol. What happens if the Anthropic IPO underwhelms? Or if crisis and controversy at Nvidia (NVDA +3.12%) pull down the whole AI economy?

So buyer beware. Right now, NEAR Protocol is a fantastic play at the intersection of AI and blockchain infrastructure. I'm fully expecting it to soar in price in 2027, just as it has in 2026. But if the AI boom stalls for any reason, all bets are off the table.
