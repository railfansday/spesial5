CuriosityStream: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Licensing contracts with AI developers carry no upfront working capital cost, so the growth story rests on how durable those deals prove to be.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 11, 2026, CuriosityStream (CURI +20.68%) reported third-quarter revenue of $20.7 million. Technology companies developing artificial intelligence (AI) models are licensing the video catalog. This licensing strategy creates high-margin revenue with minimal added operating costs. Three takeaways from management detail the long-term outlook.

Licensing revenue increased 35% year over year to $11.7 million, representing 57% of total revenue. Technology firms developing AI models purchased video and audio content to train their systems. Because CuriosityStream already owns its content library, licensing sales generate cash without requiring heavy capital investment.

CuriosityStream demonstrated significant operating leverage during the period. Because licensing intellectual property, or IP, incurs minimal variable costs, incremental sales flow directly to adjusted EBITDA, which measures earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. Operating expenses dropped after management streamlined operations and reduced advertising spend.

CuriosityStream is securing large licensing agreements with companies developing AI technologies. These deals utilize both proprietary video archives and aggregated partner content. Partner revenue-sharing arrangements affect gross margins on specific deals, but management structures these contracts to avoid upfront working-capital investments.

Recurring subscription revenue covers operational overhead and dividend commitments, positioning new licensing deals to drive expanding net income.
