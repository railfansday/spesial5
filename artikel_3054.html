Celsius: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Management's 2027 portfolio plan is still unproven, and the question is whether pricing discipline can lift a 46.8% gross margin squeezed by can and fuel costs.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Celsius (CELH -2.31%) announced third-quarter revenue of $847 million, up 17% year over year. Demand for the acquired energy drink brand Alani Nu propelled overall portfolio results, while core brand sales were affected by retail inventory adjustments. Investors must evaluate whether corporate restructuring and promotional discipline can expand gross profit margins. Three takeaways from the call highlight the company's path forward.

Alani Nu generated net sales of $410 million, up 24% year over year as retail product distribution expanded. That sales momentum cushioned temporary revenue declines in core CELSIUS products while retailers adjusted inventory levels.

Managing distinct beverage lines under one parent company created operational complexity across sales channels. Leadership established brand-led business units to give each beverage line dedicated executive focus while sharing distribution scale.

Elevated costs for aluminum cans and diesel fuel squeezed third-quarter gross margin, the percentage of revenue left after production and freight expenses, down to 46.8%. Management is implementing structured pricing and promotional discipline across retail accounts to lower trade spending and rebuild profitability.

Product innovation and expanded distribution networks drive current commercial momentum. Non-U.S. market revenue rose 56% year over year as brand awareness doubled across European markets. Management is monitoring promotional trade efficiency, direct-store-delivery distribution integration, and aluminum price fluctuations to safeguard long-term returns.

Supply chain integration across acquired brands unlocks structural scale benefits across major retail channels. Operational discipline positions the business to turn retail shelf expansion into sustained cash generation.
