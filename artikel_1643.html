Arsenal: Mikel Arteta issues fresh David Raya injury update ahead of Man City showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Mikel Arteta provided a coy update on David Raya’s availability for Arsenal’s showdown with Manchester City this weekend.

The goalkeeper missed the Gunners’ draw with Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday night with a shoulder injury that he picked up against Newcastle.

The Spaniard left the pitch at St James’ Park in significant pain and Arsenal feared a lengthy absence, but scans proved to be positive.

The No1 has avoided a serious injury and he will not require surgery, though there is uncertainty over exactly when he will return.

There are suggestions that the goalkeeper will only be out for a matter of days and could be in contention to face City on Sunday.

“No, we have nothing to add today unfortunately,” Arteta said after his side’s 1-1 draw with Dortmund.

“We need to know first how bad it is and whether he has a chance or not [to face Man City].”

In Raya’s absence, Kepa Arrizabalaga started against Dortmund, as Arsenal had to settle for just a point despite a dominant performance.

The Spaniard was fairly comfortable throughout, though he did have some shaky moments from corners and when rolling the ball out to Myles Lewis-Skelly to play him into trouble.

However, it was an improvement on Kepa’s display in the Carabao Cup final last season and solid enough to be confident he will keep his place over Illan Meslier while Raya remains out.

Kepa has had limited football since joining the club, so trusted has Raya become for Arsenal, but Arteta insisted he will have full faith in the goalkeeper if he is required to start in goal against City.

“Him and Illan, they’re both in the same situation,” Arteta said.

“I think Kepa had a really good performance today. We fully trust our keepers and if he needs to play against City, he will play.”
