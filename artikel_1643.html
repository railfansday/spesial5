Sublime England thrash Croatia in best performance for a decade

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

If this is Tuchel-ball, if this is the way Thomas Tuchel wants England to play, then yes, please. More of the same; bring it on. The standard is set.

England’s DNA? This was a total-football annihilation of a top nation. Croatia, 14th in the world and fierce competitors, were cowed and humiliated at home in this Nations League tie. This was the biggest defeat in their proud history – seventh heaven for England, and hell for the hosts.

The visitors were sublime. Harry Kane (twice – 89 goals now for his country), Jude Bellingham and Anthony Gordon all scored and were outstanding. Bukayo Saka also struck twice. Alex Scott made his first start and will take some dislodging. The Bournemouth midfielder was slick with a snap – perpetual, intelligent, smooth motion.

Luka Modric has had the most extraordinary career but he was emphatically second best here and was subbed off on 57 minutes at 5-0. He looked bereft, having had Scott and Elliot Anderson run all over him and Mateo Kovacic.

We came out of the World Cup wondering if Tuchel really had an idea of how he can get England over the line. After last Saturday’s defeat by world and European champions Spain, followed by Tuesday’s win over the Czech Republic we still did not know.

But here the standard was set – it was an exhilarating example of the football England are capable of. On display we saw the speed, the movement, the passes under pressure, the ambition, the control and aggression. This was the full package. Bottle it. Bag it. Believe it.

Croatia have an ageing team and are in transition, but they were left looking callow. It looked as though it was men against boys. There were plenty of boys in the stands, with 6,200 children allowed in for what was supposed to be a behind-closed-doors fixture – Croatia’s punishment for crowd trouble.

Their new head coach Slaven Bilic would doubtless have been relieved that the match was not played in front of the usual, passionate, partisan crowd. Even then some of the kids booed and left early. Bilic looked shell-shocked. As well he might.

It was 4-0 at half-time. It could easily have been 7-1 – Kovacic missed a sitter for Croatia at 3-0 and Gordon should have had a hat-trick. How significant is it that England’s three most dangerous players do not play in the Premier League? Probably not very. But the fact they turn out for Real Madrid, Barcelona and Bayern Munich is. They performed like the top players their clubs think they clearly are.

Kane’s commitment was incredible. At one stage Croatia substitute Kristijan Jakić threatened to break clear but was stopped by England’s last man tracking back. It was Kane, one cap short of equalling Peter Shilton’s all-time record of 125 appearances, and it was already 5-0 when that happened.

These could be exciting times for England. Times when they have shaken off the debate over how well or otherwise they did at the World Cup and moved on. Tuchel has talked up that third-place finish; we have tended to dwell on the semi-final exit to Argentina.

And now? If Tuchel does go and win a trophy for England, he will be talking about this game. Previously he referenced the 5-0 demolition of Serbia in Belgrade, during the World Cup qualification. But the Serbians were a rabble. Croatia are not, but were made to look so on Saturday.

Where will this sit among England performances? There was the 2-1 victory away to Italy at the beginning of the Euro 2024 qualification campaign in March 2023. Before that, the 3-2 win away to Spain in 2018, also in the Nations League. But England were more thrilling here. Do we have to go back to 5-1 in Munich in 2001?

The concern will be the familiar England problem of looking like world-beaters in the autumn and worn out by the time a tournament comes around in the summer. But against Croatia there was control and authority as they raised their chances of finishing in the top two and qualifying for next March’s quarter-finals.

The goals rained in – even if the best remained the first as Marc Guéhi intercepted a clearance, Scott took the ball on the half-turn and found Bellingham. He slid it through to Kane who finished high into the net. What a goal.

However, a caveat in Uefa’s disciplinary regulations allows the home federation in such circumstances to fill the ground to 75 per cent of its capacity with “children up to the age of 14 (duly accompanied) from schools and/or football academies invited to the match free of charge”. Good afternoon and welcome to live coverage of England’s Uefa Nations League Group A3 match against Croatia in Rijeka. Matches between the two are relatively frequent – 12 in 30 years. Croatia have beaten England three times in the 10 competitive meetings, home and away in qualification for Euro 2008, which dealt the England manager a blow from which he could not recover, and the World Cup semi-final in Moscow in 2018.

The extraordinary thing about both the first two defeats – the ‘Wally with the Brolly’ match in November 2007, Steve McLaren’s last in charge, and the home tie on October 10 2006 – is that Luka Modric played in both. And now eight days shy of 20 years since his first appearance against England, he is still running their midfield at the age of 41.

England have had the upper hand since Russia, winning in the Nations League at Wembley in 2018, opening their Euro 2020 campaign with a 1-0 victory by virtue of Raheem Sterling’s goal and then in June when they were at their fluent best, a rare occurrence, in the second half of their first World Cup group game to push on from 2-2 to finish 4-2.

Slaven Bilic, manager from 2006-12, is back in charge for his second spell but his squad still relies heavily on experienced players. Modric is the eldest but is joined by greybeard stalwarts Ivan Perisic, Ante Budimir and Andrej Kramaric, both 35. Spain hammered them 4-1 in midweek after they had started with a 2-1 victory over Czech Republic in Prague.

England, who rattled Spain and should have gone 3-1 up but lost 3-2, were much better in the second half in Prague against a team reduced to 10 men and deserved their 2-0 win, have played at the tiny Stadion Rujevica (capacity 8,279) once before, drawing 0-0 in 2018.

Thomas Tuchel, who changed three of his back four on Tuesday, may stick with Lewis Hall after he made a positive impression in combination with Anthony Gordon. As for Trent Alexander-Arnold, who set up the first goal, Tuchel does not go out of his way to undermine the perception that for all his unique quality, he just doesn’t fancy the Real Madrid right-back at all.

It’s up to Alexander-Arnold to make a compelling case if picked today or at Wembley on Tuesday to persuade the German head coach not to restore his beloved if brittle ‘Reecey’ James at the first opportunity.
