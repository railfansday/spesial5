Kane scores winner but who else made an impression? England player ratings

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Harry Kane (right) became England's all-time leading men's appearance maker

Harry Kane notched his 92nd goal for England from the penalty to spot to seal a slender Nations League win over Croatia.

Here's how I rated the players - and you can see how you scored them below.

Nations League: Kane scores England winner against Croatia on night he breaks caps record

Jordan Pickford - Was left exposed by Jarell Quansah's mistake for Croatia's goal. Was as reliable as ever prior to that. 6

Trent Alexander-Arnold - Played within himself. Some nice link-up play with Morgan Rogers down the right. 6

Jarell Quansah - Dreadful mistake to gift Croatia an equaliser. Was entirely comfortable up to that point. 5

Dan Burn - Not picked in the original squad but started. Unspectacular but largely reliable in the heart of defence. 6

Lewis Hall - Another effective contribution from the Newcastle defender. Is he now England's first-choice left-back? 7

Elliot Anderson - Kept things ticking over nicely in the engine room and always looked to play forward. 6

Declan Rice - Back in the team after missing the previous internationals through injury and conducted the midfield expertly. 6

Jude Bellingham - Not quite the outstanding level as his most recent England displays but still oozed quality in possession. 6

Morgan Rogers - Posed a threat down the right and deserved his goal - his third for England at senior level. 6

Harry Kane - A historic night for the England captain as he became the nation's most capped player. Scored the winner from the spot to boot. 6

Anthony Gordon - Was sensational during the last set of international games. Less so here, but remains crucial for England. 6

Ben White (on for Alexander-Arnold 78). 6

Subs not used: Trafford, Steele, Gibbs-White, Hill, Branthwaite, Jobe Bellingham, Calvert-Lewin, Eze, King

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.
