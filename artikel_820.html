European football: Doué carried off for PSG while Kane vows to return fresh

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Doué injury adds to that of Kvaratskhelia last month

Nice 2-3 PSG; Lazio 3-0 Lecce; Gladbach 0-1 Dortmund

Désiré Doué was carried off in tears after suffering what looked like a nasty knee injury as Paris Saint-Germain beat Nice 3-2 in Ligue 1.

Doué went down after twisting awkwardly late in the first half as he tried to control the ball, shortly after scoring to put PSG 2-1 ahead. The match was stopped for five minutes to allow Doué to receive treatment to his right knee before he was carried off to a backdrop of applause from the crowd at the Allianz Riviera stadium.

PSG will now be dreading discovering the extent of the injury, having lost Khvicha Kvaratskhelia to a ruptured anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee playing against Barcelona last month. The Georgian winger is unlikely to return this season, and Doué looks set for a significant spell on the sidelines too.

“We will need to wait until tomorrow for the test results, but it is obviously a serious injury,” PSG’s coach, Luis Enrique, told the broadcaster Ligue 1+.

Mohamed Amoura had given Nice the lead only for Senny Mayulu to equalise with a penalty awarded after a video assistant referee (VAR) check for a handball.

Doué – who was wearing a mouthguard after one of his teeth became dislodged while playing for France against Italy during the international break – put PSG ahead a minute later, turning in at the back post from an Achraf Hakimi assist for his sixth of the season in all competitions.

His replacement, Ferran Torres, scored PSG’s third in the 85th minute, controlling in the box before finishing on the turn for his eighth goal of the season – but first in two months. Ali Abdi pulled one back for Nice in stoppage time, but their run of three straight wins was ended as PSG made it 12 victories on the bounce.

Lazio will continue their surprise stay at the top of Serie A for at least another week after thumping Lecce 3-0, while a red-hot Donyell Malen scored four at Parma to lift Roma up to second.

Gennaro Gattuso’s Lazio are two points ahead of their local rivals thanks to goals in each half from Tijjani Noslin and a late Davide Frattesi strike. Inter would close to within a point of Lazio with a win at struggling Atalanta on Sunday.

“I am proud of the mentality and the hunger shown today,” said Gattuso to Dazn. “It’s all down to the players, they have the right mentality, hunger and team spirit. What they are doing is incredible.”

Malen took his tally in all competitions for Roma this season to 16 before Wednesday’s daunting Champions League clash at Paris Saint-Germain. The Netherlands forward opened the scoring six minutes before half-time with a brilliant touch and finish, and put Roma, also unbeaten in the league, back in the lead in the 68th minute. Rodrigo Mora added Roma’s third moments later and then Malen capped another superb display with two more expert finishes.

Scott McTominay’s second-half strike gave Napoli a hard-earned 1-0 win over Torino to ease the pressure on manager Massimiliano Allegri, while Como stayed six points behind the leaders in fourth with a 2-0 home win over Cagliari.
