Can Celtic finally end their miserable European away record? Jane Lewis is joined by former Hearts defender Ryan McGowan and Herald football writer Stephen McGowan to preview a huge night for Scotland's European hopefuls. With Martin O'Neill's side facing Portuguese second-tier opposition, is this the perfect opportunity to get their Europa League campaign back on track? Could Jota provide the spark they need, and what does Celtic's latest data recruitment announcement mean for the club? Meanwhile, Monaco arrive at Tynecastle, but can injury-hit Hearts pull off a European surprise?
