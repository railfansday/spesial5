Madonna Calls Dance ‘The Hidden Language of the Soul’ in 2027 Grammy Nomination ‘Love Letter’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Queen of Pop is nominated in all four of the dance categories at the 2027 awards.

Following her nominations in all four of the Grammys‘ dance categories, Madonna has released a message about what this acknowledgment means to her.

“Dance is the hidden language of the soul,” Madonna wrote in a social media post tagging @Grammys, captioned “Incoming love letter.” “When I made Confessions II with Stuart Price, our goal was not just to make people dance, but to find connection with other humans. If Storytelling is how we find our way back to ourselves then dancing is how we get there.

“Thanks to everyone that worked on this record!” the letter continues. “The producers, the writers, the artists and most of all my fans who have been so encouraging and supportive since the beginning of this project. Thank you for sharing this sacred space with me. I’m so grateful to all of you. Love, Madonna.”

The Queen of Pop’s July club album Confessions II received a nomination for best dance/electronic album, best dance/electronic recording for the song “I Feel So Free,” best dance pop recording for “Danceteria” and best remixed recording for the Stuart Price remix of her Kylie Minogue collab “Love Sensation.”

These nominations bring Madonna’s total nominations to 32, with her first coming in 1988. Her seven wins include a few in the dance genre; she got the 1999 best dance recording award for her electronica masterpiece “Ray of Light” and the best dance/electronic album award for her 2007 LP Confessions on a Dancefloor.

Given the warm reception Confessions II has received, there’s reason to believe Madonna could sweep the dance categories at the 2027 ceremony. While the dance categories are typically not televised, the Grammys put one in prime time in 2023, when Beyoncé’s Renaissance was nominated for (and won) best dance/electronic album. Presumably, the Recording Academy may consider this move again given Madonna’s star power.

The 2027 Grammy Awards happen Feb. 7 at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.
