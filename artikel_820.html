DWTS’ Connor Wood Responds to Fans Wanting Him to Date Rylee Arnold

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dancing With the Stars' Connor Wood had a candid reaction when pro dancer Ezra Sosa suggested that fans were "shipping" him and dance partner Rylee Arnold to date.

Dancing With the Stars' Connor Wood is quickstepping past chatter about his friendship with dance partner Rylee Arnold.

In fact, the comedian also known as Fibula had a hilarious response when DWTS pro Ezra Sosa pressed him about fans "shipping" the pair to take their chemistry off the dance floor and date in real life.

"What do you say?" Sosa teased on a TikTok Live during the ABC dance competition series' Nov. 10 broadcast, prompting Wood to briefly stumble on his words before quipping, "I say, 'Thank you for watching.'"

Besides, Wood has made it clear about his actual celeb crush. After all, the 31-year-old was kicking himself when he recently found out that he had once unintentionally snubbed Jennifer Lawrence.

“I saw you on 8th avenue drinking a smoothie and you paid me no mind,” Lawrence commented on one of Wood's social media videos, prompting him to pretend to choke himself in an Oct. 21 TikTok before adding, "This is rolling around in my head.”

As for where Arnold stands with Wood? The 21-year-old—who's been dating college football star Walker Lyon since 2024—considers the two to be great friends.

“It just feels natural,” Arnold told Entertainment Weekly in last month. “I think naturally we just have so much fun together. We feel comfortable with each other.”

Wood agreed, adding that "it was an act of God that we were paired together."

“We don’t really have any missed connects," he continued. "We have this connection that isn’t really faulted by like, ‘Oh, I can’t get this step,’ or ‘I can’t do this move.’”

And though their close bond has left many fans head over heels, Arnold has assured that it's "just dancing."

“I’ve grown up being close to a man’s face since I was 10 years old. Honestly, it’s just second nature," she told TV Insider in September. "To have that chemistry helps you get through the dance, get through the steps."
