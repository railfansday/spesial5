Prediction markets push OpenAI IPO expectations later into 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Anthropic's shaky performance on the public markets since its debut last week and a continuing tech sell-off may be changing the outlook for when prediction markets think OpenAI will go public.

Speculators on prediction market platform Kalshi think there's a roughly 28% chance the ChatGPT developer officially announces an IPO by June 2027. That figure was most recently above 50% earlier this month.

Even by October 2027, odds are still only 50-50 that the private company — most recently conducting a funding round at a $1.25 trillion valuation — will make its move to the public markets.

Kalshi defines officially announcing an IPO as either the Securities and Exchange Commission declaring an S-1 form as effective, the IPO being priced or an exchange has assigned a ticker to the company. Contracts are resolved using reports from media outlets.

OpenAI confidentially filed for an IPO in June, but then in an interview with Fortune published in September CEO Sam Altman ruled out a 2026 public markets debut as questions over artificial intelligence safety grew.

The company's chief rival, Anthropic, debuted on the public markets last week. However, the stock has declined nearly 30% from its peak during its first trading session, and on Tuesday it closed below its IPO price of $83.

Meanwhile, a perpetual futures contract for OpenAI on Hyperliquid has tumbled more than 30% since highs in early October. In late October, OpenAI pared back its goal for its recent funding round to $20 billion from $30 billion.

Disclosure: CNBC and Kalshi have a commercial relationship that includes customer acquisition and a minority investment.
