Broncos HC Sean Payton announces that the team has suspended RB J.K. Dobbins for one game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Denver Broncos Head Coach Sean Payton met with the media today for the first time before their Black Friday game vs. the Pittsburgh Steelers and dropped a bombshell. He announced that veteran running back JK Dobbins has been suspended for one game due to conduct detrimental to the team.

Dobbins was not present for today's practice and will not play in Friday's game vs. the Steelers.

This seemed like a situation that was going to bubble over at some point. Dobbins, who has had minor run-ins with the media, has been ineffective this season and then saw him lose his starting job to rookie running back Jonah Coleman. On top of that, Coleman is all the hype right now after his 100+ yard performance where he totaled 3 rushing touchdowns. Meanwhile, Dobbins, the highly paid veteran running back, saw just one snap and one carry for zero yards.

I have no inside information, but I am assuming this is what triggered whatever went down. Add in the rumors that the Broncos were in the market for a running back at the deadline and it's not hard to put two and two together. Payton did not give any more information outside of the fact he has been suspended, and I do not anticipate him providing that information to the media. It may come out sooner or later from "sources," but until then, we are in the dark.

The Broncos were going to have to make a tough decision on Dobbins sooner rather than later. Tyler Badie was designated to return from the injured reserve today, and second-year back RJ Harvey is expected to come off the IR next week. Those three would all be on the 53-man roster, which would likely leave Dobbins as the odd man out. Harvey would become the 1B to Coleman, while Badie is the trusted third-down guy. So, even after this suspension, his spot on the roster and team could be in danger moving forward.

I would be surprised if Dobbins plays another snap for the Broncos this season. They can easily get out of his contract next season if he sticks around for that long, and the team will move forward with Coleman and Harvey.

Stay tuned to Mile High Report as we will keep you updated on this developing situation.
