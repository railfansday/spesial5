Green Bay Packers News: Packers recovering from a meltdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

You never want to overstate things if you don't have to, but it's hard to avoid hyperbolic language after a full collapse like we saw on Sunday.

The offensive line was never really together to begin with, but even in its decimated state, it was especially bad against the relentless Minnesota Vikings.

And even accounting for the offensive line weakness, the passing game was nothing to write home about. The Packers' dynamic duo of wide receivers was largely neutralized, and Jordan Love missed badly on a red zone throw, resulting in an interception.

Meanwhile, on defense, the Packers were largely solid — except on third downs. Two massive conversions on third and long helped propel the Vikings toward their eventual victory, and the Packers were more or less just passive observers.

But aside from that, things were…well, they were still bad. A win, no matter how ugly or undeserved, was the only worthwhile outcome in this game. When you're scratching and clawing for divisional progress, there are no moral victories, and the gap between 7-3 and 6-4 in the win/loss column is massive, especially with the Rams and Bills looming after the Packers' bye week.

It was a terrible, horrible, no good, very bad weekend for your Green Bay Packers, and there's a lot of work to be done to get back on the right track.
