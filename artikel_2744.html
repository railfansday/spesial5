Brock Purdy misses practice again on Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 49ers remained without the leader of their offense at Thursday’s practice.

Quarterback Brock Purdy missed practice for the second straight day with an ankle injury. Head coach Kyle Shanahan said on Wednesday that Purdy is day-to-day and that he’s not dealing with a high-ankle sprain, but his status for Sunday’s game against the Seahawks is definitely questionable after two missed practices in a row.

Safety Siran Neal (knee, foot) and wide receiver Jordan Watkins (groin) were also out for the Niners.

Wide receiver Mike Evans (Achilles), linebacker Dre Greenlaw (neck), cornerback Deommodore Lenoir (foot), cornerback Upton Stout (shoulder, neck), and guard Carver Willis (illness) were limited participants. Wide receiver Keenan Allen (rest), defensive end Romello Height (hand), defensive end Khalid Kareem (ankle), running back Christian McCaffrey (rest), offensive tackle Austen Pleasants (knee), defensive end Mykel Williams (hand), and left tackle Trent Williams (rest) were listed as full participants.
