Dortmund's contract talks with Manchester United-linked defender dragging on

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Borussia Dortmund are looking to tie Waldemar Anton down to a contract beyond 2028, but negotiations are seemingly proving more difficult than initially anticipated.

According to Sport Bild, talks between the two parties have been progressing slowly, and a swift agreement is not in sight because the proposed salary doesn't meet the player's expectations.

Anton's representatives are reportedly seeking improved financial terms that they believe better reflect the defender's value, with talks expected to continue over the coming weeks.

Manchester United, who have previously been linked with a move for the German international, will likely keep an eye on the situation and could make a move if negotiations continue to stall.

Anton joined the Black and Yellows from VfB Stuttgart in 2024 for a reported fee of €22.5 million.
