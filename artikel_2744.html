Arsenal send scouts to watch Champions League winger

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Graeme Bailey reports for TEAMtalk that Arsenal had representatives in attendance to watch RB Leipzig's match against RC Lens on Tuesday evening, with Antonio Nusa proving the star of the show.

Nusa scored Leipzig's second and third goals in a 3-1 win, keeping his team's hopes of progression to the knockout stages alive.

Arsenal weren't the only ones with scouts at the game, with Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea, and Manchester City also sending representatives, as well as other top clubs from the continent.

Bailey adds that Leipzig also have no intention of selling mid-season, believing they're still in the Bundesliga title race, and attempting to convince the player to extend his existing 2029 contract.

Fabrizio Romano previously reported that Leipzig will be asking for around €70-75m (£58.5-62.7m) for Nusa, but that would be in the summer transfer window. It would need to be a 'completely crazy' bid to let him go in January.

Romano has also confirmed that Arsenal are one of the clubs monitoring Nusa, with their scouts regularly watching the player's development.

Simon Collings had written in early November that Arsenal were tracking Nusa, though he was similarly clear that a deal in the winter window will be hard to pull off.

Leipzig are under no financial pressure to sell mid-season, following Yan Diomande's big-money move to Real Madrid.

21-year-old winger Nusa is having an excellent season so far, with 17 direct goal contributions in his 17 appearances.

Three of his goals have come in his five Champions League games, including one against Arsenal's title rivals Manchester City.

Arsenal have been keeping an eye on Nusa for quite a while now, with reports initially emerging in 2023, when the player was still with Club Brugge. It looks like a move might now be possible in 2027, but it may have to wait for the summer.
