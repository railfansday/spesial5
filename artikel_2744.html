Micah Parsons is cleared to play vs. the Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NFL Network/ESPN's Ian Rapoport has reported that Green Bay Packers edge defender Micah Parsons has been cleared to play against the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday. Parsons has spent this season on the physically unable to perform list, as he's been recovering from his 2025 season-ending ACL and meniscus tears. Parsons will be activated to the 53-man roster later this week, and the team made room for his addition with the release of running back Pierre Strong Jr. (who has since been re-signed to the team's practice squad).

Parsons, whom the Packers gave up two first-round picks for last year, should be a big shot in the arm for a defense that has already played pretty well this season.

Parsons told the press after Monday's practice that he still needed to clear one final hurdle: a check-in with surgeon Dr. Neal ElAttrache. That apparently has gone well, and Parsons will be on the field for the Packers in Week 12.
