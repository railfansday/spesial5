Eagles-Steelers Final Injury Report: DeVonta Smith will play, Dontayvion Wicks OUT

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their third and final injury report of the week on Friday in advance of their Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Eagles ruled two players OUT: Zack Baun and Dontayvion Wicks.

Baun, a rare bright spot for the Birds this season, did not practice this week due to a back injury. His absence means that Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. will start at off-ball linebacker.

Wicks was limited in practice all week. He is also newly listed with a foot injury in addition to his ankle injury. The Eagles are getting DeVonta Smith back, which is nice, but it would've been ideal for both him and Wicks to be available for Joe Milton.

The Eagles ruled one player DOUBTFUL: Smael Mondon.

Mondon missed practice on Thursday and Friday after fully participating on Wednesday. Mondon would've been the top backup linebacker behind Campbell and Trot Jr. if healthy. Instead, the Eagles will likely look to temporarily elevate Chance Campbell and/or Anfernee Orji from the practice squad to have more depth at this position.

The Eagles ruled one player QUESTIONABLE: Will Shipley.

Shipley was limited on Wednesday and Thursday before missing Friday's practice due to illness. The Eagles have until 4:00 PM Eastern on Saturday to activate him from injured reserve in time to play on Sunday. If he can't suit up, the Eagles are left with Saquon Barkley and Jaydon Blue as the only two running backs on the roster. Elijah Mitchell is out of practice squad elevations … so the Eagles will need to sign him to the roster if they want him available to play. Which they shouldn't, unless they're truly trying to tank.

Players notably listed without a game status: DeVonta Smith, Nolan Smith, Saquon Barkley, Jihaad Campbell, Jonathan Greenard, Jalyx Hunt, Andrew Mukuba, Tanner McKee, Michael Carter, Britain Covey, and Jonathan Jones.

Smitty will play for the first time since Week 6. Nice to see the Eagles' top receiver is back in action.

Nolan Smith was upgraded to full on Friday after being limited due to illness on Wednesday and Thursday.

Greenard was full go on Wednesday and Thursday before missing Friday's practice due to rest.

Carter and Jones were both limited on Wednesday and Thursday before being upgraded to full on Friday.

Covey was limited in practice all week but will play and seemingly resume his role as the Eagles' punt returner.

Barkley, Campbell, Hunt, Mukuba, and McKee were full participants in practice all week.

The Eagles opened Shipley's 21-day practice window to return from IR on November 16. Bigsby is currently eligible to return to the roster. Hurts, Sam, and Wilson are out for the season.

The Steelers ruled six players QUESTIONABLE: starting receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson, starting linebacker Patrick Queen, rotational edge rusher Nick Herbig, backup safety DeShon Elliott, and depth offensive lineman Gennings Dunker.

Pittsburgh has until Saturday at 4:00 PM Eastern to activate Pittman Jr. and Wilson from IR in order for them to play on Sunday. Wilson fully participated in practice this week while Pittman Jr. was limited on Wednesday before going full go on Thursday and Friday. Their expected returns should help a struggling Aaron Rodgers.

Queen will probably play. He's never missed a game and he was full go on Friday after being limited the previous two days.

Herbig probably won't play since he did not practice this week. That would be a sneaky loss for the Steelers' defense. He ranks tied for second on their roster in TFLs and fourth in sacks.

Elliott was limited on Wednesday but full on Thursday and Friday.
