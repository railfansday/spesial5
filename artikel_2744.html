Wan'Dale Robinson lands on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tennessee Titans WR Wan'Dale Robinson (ankle) was placed on injured reserve by the team on Tuesday, Nov. 17. In a corresponding move, the Titans added WR Xavier Restrepo to the active roster.
