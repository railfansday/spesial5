Dan Lanning explains scoreboard taunt after fight breaks out between Ohio State, Oregon players

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Oregon Ducks led the Ohio State Buckeyes 38-14 in the fourth quarter at Ohio Stadium on Saturday night when tempers boiled over.

Players from both teams got into a shoving match that spilled off the field and toward the Oregon sideline, and while Ohio State players kept talking, Dan Lanning answered them with one raised arm.

Officials broke it up and called offsetting penalties. Ohio State cornerback Jermaine Mathews Jr. was among the most vocal Buckeyes, jawing at Oregon's players and sideline. As Mathews and others kept at it, Lanning pointed up at the scoreboard.

Oregon head coach Dan Lanning talks to his players as the Oregon Ducks host the Idaho Vandals Saturday, Aug. 31, 2024 at Autzen Stadium in Eugene, Ore.

Lanning was asked about the gesture at his postgame news conference. "They were just talking to our team, and, you know, it was, 'Look at the score,'" Lanning said.

He had described his team's mindset a few minutes earlier, when asked how Oregon turned a 14-0 deficit into a rout.

"Yeah, we're like cockroaches, man. We won't die. And, our guys, truly, they've been battle tested. So, in those moments, they don't blame, right? And we don't really look at the scoreboard. We just focus on working. And I thought they did that tonight," Lanning said.

Ohio State led 14-0 after the first quarter and looked like a team ready to answer its loss at USC on Oct. 31. Oregon then scored 38 straight points. The run included a fake punt Lanning called on fourth-and-10 from his own 37 and an interception thrown by freshman Luke Fahey late in the first half, which set up an Oregon touchdown before the break. By the third quarter, the crowd of over 103,000 was already thinning.

Ohio State added two touchdowns late to make the final 41-28, but the night still ended the Buckeyes' 12-game home winning streak and handed them back-to-back losses for the first time since 2011. Ohio State fell to 6-3, and its College Football Playoff hopes went with it.

Lanning's needle was aimed at the players on the field, and his words about the coach on the other sideline went the other way. In the same news conference, he called Ryan Day an outstanding coach and said he knew what Day was going through, because Oregon had lived through its own slide in October after losing quarterback Dante Moore to a season-ending concussion.

Oregon's social media team was less diplomatic, following the win with a "Shoe Ducks" post aimed at the Buckeyes.

The Ducks are 6-3 and No. 23 in the playoff rankings with back-to-back wins over ranked opponents, and Saturday's result is the biggest win on their resume.
