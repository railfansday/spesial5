NFL trade deadline MADNESS: Vikings get Kirk Cousins, Jaguars are ALL-IN + midseason awards feat. Robert Mays

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Nate Tice & Robert Mays react to the latest NFL trade deadline action and hand out their midseason awards. After reacting to the Minnesota Vikings offense getting dismantled by the Buffalo Bills on Monday night, Nate gives his thoughts on the Vikings making a desperate move for QB Kirk Cousins, plus the Denver Broncos trading for LB Daiyan Henley and the Kansas City Chiefs trading for WR Josh Downs. Next, Nate & Robert react to the Jacksonville Jaguars making a trio of big moves, acquiring S Jessie Bates III, DT Jordan Davis and DT Davon Godchaux. The Jags undeniably got scarier, but was the price too much to justify?

Later, Nate & Robert dive into their nine midseason awards at the halfway point of the NFL regular season, giving their picks for MVP (Joe Burrow, Jordan Love or Josh Allen?), Offensive & Defensive Player of the Year (can Jaxon Smith-Njigba go back-to-back?), Offensive & Defensive Rookies of the Year, Biggest Surprise, Coach of the Year, Assistant Coach of the Year and Protector of the Year. The duo also give their quick reactions to the Bills acquiring S Amani Hooker at the end of the show. Grab your popcorn — it's a packed midseason trade deadline special.
