Cowboys vs. Titans odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's tough being a Dallas Cowboys fan these days. Every step forward is followed by a step back. And if that stays consistent, it will be a third straight year without the playoffs.

The Cowboys weren't favored to win against the San Francisco 49ers, but there was hope since they were at home and got back some of their key players from injury. For one half or so of football, it felt like the dream just may come true. Spoiler alert: It didn't.

They are favored to win next week though. They are currently 7.5-point favorites over the 1-8 Tennessee Titans according to FanDuel.

The Cowboys players and organization will continue talking about winning games and trying to get to the playoffs. There will always be the hope that the New York Giants fall apart and that the Cowboys somehow catch fire. But the reality is it is getting late in the season, and the Cowboys are one of the most inconsistent teams in the NFL, not only from game to game, but literally within a game.
