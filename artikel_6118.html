Wynn is holding to its September 2027 date for the UAE’s first casino resort but it will now decide in February what form the opening takes. The U.S.-Iran war means its first customers may travel from down the road in Dubai and elsewhere in the UAE, rather than being the international guests it primarily wants.

Casino resort operator Wynn Resorts will likely decide in February precisely how to open its Ras Al Khaimah casino resort, CEO Craig Billings said on the company’s third-quarter earnings call on Thursday.

“We will make a decision about the format in which we open. And we’ll probably make that decision around February,” he said.

The $5.7 billion Wynn Al Marjan Island, the UAE’s first integrated gaming resort, remains scheduled for an opening in September 2027. But Billings said the mix of source markets it ideally wants “does impact how we open.” He said that Wynn had “the playbooks for every single iteration of that today.”

Back in August, Wynn said it did not plan a staggered rollout. “We generally don’t do phased openings,” Billings then said. “We would open the doors and open all the amenities exactly as we would any other particular opening.”

Wynn Resorts will decide around February how to open its $5.7 billion Wynn Al Marjan Island resort in Ras Al Khaimah, CEO Craig Billings said on the company's third-quarter earnings call. The UAE's first integrated gaming resort is still scheduled to open in September 2027, but Billings said the mix of source markets it draws "does impact how we open," a shift in tone from August, when he said Wynn generally does not do phased openings. He laid out three demand scenarios tied to the course of the U.S.-Iran war: if conflict continues, the resort leans on people already in the UAE; if it moderates, it adds the wider region; if it resolves, Wynn's original demand thesis applies. That original 2024 thesis skewed international, with 37% of gaming revenue projected from international high rollers and 29% from other international travelers. Billings now describes the UAE's wealthy, largely expat population as the base business that is "easiest to address," and named Western Europeans as the travelers most likely to be apprehensive about the region. Handover of finished zones to operations starts in early January and runs through July, with rooms going on sale "quite soon." Wynn has contributed more than $1.12 billion in cash to the joint venture, in which it holds a 40% stake, and the venture has drawn $1.76 billion on its construction loan.
