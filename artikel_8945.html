Call for Entries: The New Heart of Kraaiennest with OBA Next (Amsterdam)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The City of Amsterdam is holding an international design competition for the project ‘The New Heart of Kraaiennest with OBA Next’. The purpose of the procurement procedure is to select a design team. The assignment calls for a cohesive design addressing the public realm, civic and community facilities, socioeconomic growth, and the spatial integration of the housing programme. Essential to the assignment is the integration of urban design, landscape, architecture and socioeconomic value.

The City of Amsterdam is holding an international design competition for the project ‘The New Heart of Kraaiennest with OBA Next’. The purpose of the procurement procedure is to select a design team. The assignment calls for a cohesive design addressing the public realm, civic and community facilities, socioeconomic growth, and the spatial integration of the housing programme. Essential to the assignment is the integration of urban design, landscape, architecture and socioeconomic value.

The full programme for the competition comprises the following spatial components:• OBA Next library• Youth centre• Huis van de Wijk• Rehearsal space• Complementary neighbourhood programme• Housing and facilities programme• Public realm

The full programme for the competition comprises the following spatial components:• OBA Next library• Youth centre• Huis van de Wijk• Rehearsal space• Complementary neighbourhood programme• Housing and facilities programme• Public realm

The assignment has a strong socioeconomic dimension. The New Heart of Kraaiennest must not only function well spatially but also contribute to the development of K-Midden as a place for social interaction, talent development, entrepreneurship, learning, culture, and community building. Candidates are therefore expected to consider how the programme, public realm, building design and use can help strengthen the neighbourhood socially and economically.
