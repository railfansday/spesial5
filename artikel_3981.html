Poll: How Many Hours Have You Put Into GTA 6 So Far?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

And what's your story completion percentage?

At this exact moment in time, GTA 6 will have officially been playable for 219 hours - going off New Zealand's midnight launch schedule.

That's just over nine days of cruising Leonida with the baddies, Jason and Lucia. We'll admit, GTA 6 fever is in full swing here at Pure Xbox, because for the past week it seems to be the only thing most people have talked about.

But now it's time for the community to, once again, take centre stage to tell us just how many hours you've put into Grand Theft Auto VI so far, and what your current story completion percentage is.

Just like the time we asked you to score GTA 6, we're going to plop some polls at the bottom of this feature and open the comments section up to discussion.

Of course, we'd like this to stay mostly spoiler-free, so please consider your fellow PXers when sharing anything beyond your % completed. If you do wish to talk specifics, we ask that you use spoiler tags - [/spoiler] goes at the end of your text, and [spoiler] goes at the start.

As things stand, I'm ranked second on my friend's list for total time played at 55h 47m and have 49% story completion. Fraser, having reviewed GTA 6 has 100% story completion which took him 42 hours, but his Xbox profile is borked and telling him he's only played 44 hours in total, which is around 10-15 hours off the real number.

So, let's get into it Pure Xbox: how many hours of GTA 6 have you played, and what story completion percentage are you currently at?
