Something Weird Is Going On With GoldenEye 007 And Xbox Game Pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Earlier this month, you may recall that Xbox announced 14 games that would be leaving Game Pass in early November, with arguably the highlight of the bunch being the classic Rare FPS, GoldenEye 007.

We initially thought this might have been a mistake, but then Xbox doubled-down by announcing the game's departure in an official Xbox Wire roundup, confirming that it would definitely be leaving console and cloud on November 15th.

We're now on November 16th, and GoldenEye 007 remains included with Game Pass. Every other title from the list has been removed from the service, but Bond has defied the odds once again to remain part of the subscription!

So, what's going on here? It might take a while until we find out for sure, but we're going to guess that Xbox has either managed to arrange a deal to keep GoldenEye 007 on Game Pass, or they've just pushed back the removal date.

In any case, it's weird that nobody's said anything about this yet, and we're hoping we'll get clarification in the near future. GoldenEye has always been a game that's been tied up in licensing issues (hence why we never got the cancelled Xbox 360 version of it), but perhaps they've managed to figure out a long-term solution.

Anyway, we'll keep an eye on it, and this article will be updated with any important information that's revealed over the next few days. For now though, feel free to keep enjoying GoldenEye 007 on Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and Premium!
