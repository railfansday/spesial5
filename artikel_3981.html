Disney Bringing Game Reveals to The Game Awards Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Disney and The Game Awards have announced a collaboration that will see next month's show streamed live across the Disney+ platform and become the stage for Disney game reveals.

The awards and announcements ceremony will remain available to watch live across all the usual streaming platforms, but now Disney+ is another way to view the proceedings.

Most importantly, however, is a particular line from the press release: "As the evening unfolds, viewers can expect major announcements and surprises from some of Disney’s most anticipated titles in gaming."

Of course, Disney isn't just princes and princesses anymore; it owns the Marvel and Star Wars brands, as well as many publishers that work with external developers to bring its characters to the video game world. A title reveal from Disney could be a significant one — there are Disney characters in Kingdom Hearts 4, for example.

It's also rumoured the announcement of the third Star Wars Jedi game is imminent. Respawn Entertainment and EA will obviously have a say in when it's revealed, but the Star Wars brand ultimately belongs to Disney. Perhaps we'll see Cal Kestis at The Game Awards?

The tease could amount to nothing more than a Disney Dreamlight Valley update, but Disney has said it's going to be there in a big way, and after gobbling up so many brands and IPs in the years gone by, that could mean important news is on the way. The show streams live on 10th December 2026.
