Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Two months ago there were many doom merchants insisting that Aston Villa were heading for a season of struggle, but they are looking foolish now.

After a summer of uncertainty and much change, Villa are stepping into a brave new era and find themselves joint-top of the Champions League table after three matches.

With co-owner Nassef Sawiris in attendance, Villa fans are dreaming of another magical season almost four years after Emery walked through the doors at this historic club.

Sawiris was the key figure driving Emery’s appointment in October 2022 and few could have predicted the success that has followed with the Basque manager at the helm.

Villa 2.0 have made a flawless start to their European campaign and are running into form in the Premier League after a sticky start.

In this comfortable victory over Viking, Villa became the second fastest team to win 20 matches at this level from 30 matches, only behind Real Madrid (who won 20 of their first 29). They have also won 12 home games in Europe in a row.

Much tougher assignments are to come, with powerhouses Barcelona, Paris St-Germain and Borussia Dortmund still to come in the league phase.

Yet Villa are currently level on points with Paris St-Germain, Liverpool and Real Madrid, and Emery remains determined to make an impact in this competition.

He said: “We are delighted with how we are responding and improving things. The Champions League is very difficult, but we have nine points and you can feel confidence. We are comfortable for the next challenge.”

What a difference a few months make. Over the course of the summer, many observers wondered if the Emery fairy tale was in danger of ending.
