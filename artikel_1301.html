Serie A: Milan vs. Frosinone – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Milan will look to Gonçalo Ramos, Christian Pulisic and Adrien Rabiot in a tricky Serie A game against Serie A surprise Frosinone.

It kicks off at 18:00 CET [17:00 GMT] at San Siro.

Samuel Chukwueze, Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Matteo Gabbia and Alexis Saelemaekers are injured for the home side, while Strahinja Pavlović is available but not fit to start, according to Sky and Gazzetta.

Therefore, Sankhoun Diawara is expected to join Koni De Winter at centre-back, with Davide Bartesaghi at left-back and Mario Gila at right-back.

Pulisic has returned from international duty after scoring a banger against Haiti on Tuesday and will start alongside Rabiot behind sole striker Ramos.

Frosinone are without their best scorer, Antonio Raimondo, who is still injured. Left-back Gabriele Bracaglia is another notable absentee as the Italian left-back serves a one-game ban.

The match will be broadcast live on DAZN in the UK and Ireland and on Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog for all five Serie A matches today, including Juventus-Venezia.

Milan (4-4-1-1): Maignan, Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi, Moreira, Musah, Modric, Pulisic, Rabiot, Matias Ramos.

Frosinone (4-2-3-1): Palmisani, Oyono, Calvani, Monterisi, Tchato, Masini, Calò, Ghedjemis, Schmid, Kvernadze, Bîrligea.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
