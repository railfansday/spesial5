Kushner: Russia has agreed to include Europe in Ukraine war talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

U.S. envoy Jared Kushner announced Thursday that Russia has agreed to European participation in direct negotiations to end the Russia-Ukraine war, after three days of talks with delegations from both sides of the conflict.  In the past, Russian leadership has opposed European diplomatic involvement, citing Europe’s financial support for Ukraine in the ongoing war effort. …
