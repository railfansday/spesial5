Christie’s International Real Estate Names Art Tobani National VP of Affiliate Development

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Christie’s International Real Estate announced it has named Art Tobani as its national vice president of affiliate development, bringing the longtime industry business-development executive on to accelerate the network’s expansion across the U.S.

Tobani brings more than 20 years of real estate business development experience, Christie’s noted, including 12 years in brokerage affiliate sales and advisory services. Throughout his career, he has driven business growth through new affiliate signings, mergers and acquisitions, and high-impact recruiting.

“Art has a real talent for looking at brokerage businesses from different angles, identifying opportunities, and bringing the right people together to unlock value,” said Gavin Swartzman, president of Christie’s International Real Estate. “I’ve seen his abilities firsthand, and I’m excited to have him on our team as we enter our next phase of growth. His experience and relationships will be a tremendous asset as we expand our U.S. presence.”

At Christie’s International Real Estate, the brokerage added that Tobani will leverage a similar multifaceted approach, strengthening the brand’s presence in key U.S. markets while helping existing affiliates identify opportunities to expand their businesses.

“While Christie’s International Real Estate is an established iconic brand, I see a lot of potential growth, and that’s what excites me about this role,” said Tobani. “We’re in an interesting time. There’s a lot of industry disruption and market challenges, and brokers and agents are looking for new ways to compete and build their businesses. We’re in a great position to help them do that with Christie’s International Real Estate’s reputation, global referral platform, technology, and relationship with Christie’s auction house.”

In addition to identifying prospective new affiliates for Christie’s International Real Estate, the brokerage stated that Tobani will serve as a resource to existing broker-owners across the network, helping them evaluate opportunities to expand into new markets, attract top-producing teams, and pursue acquisitions that complement their existing businesses. Tobani will also help identify and develop new markets for @properties.

“Our affiliates have had a lot of success with the @properties brand, and it’s ripe for expansion. They’ve embraced our approach to technology, marketing, agent development, and building a strong brokerage culture, and the results have been outstanding. I’m excited to see what Art does with both brands,” said @properties Co-Founder and Co-CEO Thad Wong.

For more information, visit https://www.christiesrealestate.com/.
