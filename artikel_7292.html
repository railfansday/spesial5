Senior Product Designer at DuckDuckGo

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Hi, we're DuckDuckGo, the online protection company and remote-first team of 400+ on a mission to raise the standard of trust online. Founded in 2008 and profitable since 2014, our annual revenue is in the hundreds of millions USD and millions use our browser on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, our search engine, and the DuckDuckGo subscription. We also offer private, useful, and optional AI, including Duck.ai, which lets you chat privately with ChatGPT, Claude, and other AIs, all in one place.

Our culture of trust, inclusivity, and empowered project management underpins everything we do, where each team member takes full ownership of their projects, from scoping and execution to postmortem. If you're seeking end-to-end ownership of your work, you've come to the right place!

6+ years of experience in product design roles, including 2+ years in senior-level design positions at significant global consumer brands.

Proficiency in managing projects of varying complexity, from proposal, through execution and release, all while working transparently and collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders and contributors.

A knack for pushing creative limits while solving challenging design problems and delivering on delight, craft and exceptional quality.

Ability to communicate visual and interaction recommendations with high-quality mockups, prototypes, and rationale.

Accustomed to applying agentic solutions into every day project management and design execution workflows to both accelerate and raise the quality of your work.

Ability to write creative briefs that clearly define and communicate project goals, rationale, and success metrics with stakeholders.

Proficiency in research and experimentation — you can leverage both qualitative and quantitative data in driving decisions forward.

Skills to manage ambiguity and wrangle vague problems, propose innovative solutions, and execute them with a focus on metrics.

Ability to take initiative in generating ideas and carrying them forward with urgency and discretion, while working openly and collaboratively.

A willingness to apply your experience and expertise in educating, guiding, and supporting others, while serving as a steward to maintain quality of execution across designated areas of design.

Experience participating in multiple areas of the product process including prioritization, strategy, and user testing.
