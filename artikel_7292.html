Maybelline Rewrites Its 35-Year-Old Tagline for Gen Z

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

“Maybe she’s born with it. Maybe it’s Maybelline” has one of the most recognizable brand jingles since it was introduced 35 years ago.

Now the company is evolving its famous tagline with a new brand platform, “The Beauty of Infinite Possibility,” launching today, Nov. 10.

A campaign with global ambassador Miley Cyrus rethinks the meaning of “maybe,” aiming make the brand feel more relevant, hopeful, and inclusive for women of all ages—especially younger audiences.

While the original tagline was a question rooted in curiosity about a single woman’s beauty, the brand wanted a more declarative statement that embodies how make-up enables creativity, confidence, self-expression, and reinvention.

Tilde Herrera is a freelance writer and editor based in San Francisco.
