Scan Walmart Clearance With the App – It Can Ring Up Below the Sticker

A Walmart clearance sticker keeps the price it was printed with, and the store may have marked the item down again since. Scan the item with the free Walmart app while you’re in the store. The app shows that store’s current price, and that’s what you’ll pay at the register.

Walmart says each store’s manager can cut prices to clear out extra stock, and that changing prices on paper tags by hand could take hours or even days. The register charges the new price in the meantime.

Walmart is replacing its paper shelf tags with digital labels that show the same price the register charges. It said in March 2026 that it expects every U.S. store to have them within a year. A clearance sticker on the item itself is still paper, though, so it’s the likeliest place to find an old price, along with the shelf tags in any store that hasn’t switched yet.

Say a pair of kids’ sneakers carries a $14.97 clearance sticker, and the store has since cut the price to $5. The sticker still says $14.97, but the app shows $5, and $5 is what you’ll pay.

Look for clearance stickers on the displays at the ends of aisles, along the back wall of each department, and in the seasonal aisle the week after a holiday. Open the app inside the store with location sharing on, since it shows in-store prices only once it knows you’re there. Then tap the scanner icon in the search bar and scan the product’s own barcode, not one printed for shipping or storage.

The price you see applies only at that store. Walmart says prices can vary from store to store and that it doesn’t price match its own stores, so the store won’t match a lower price you found at another Walmart. Clearance is also whatever the store has left, so an item you pass up today may be gone the next time you come in.

For a big purchase you can time, like a TV or a mattress, skip the clearance hunt and wait for its category’s yearly sale season.

Scan every clearance sticker before the item goes in your cart, and buy what scans at a price you’d pay.

Download the free Walmart app and sign in before your next trip.
