Payward, the parent company of crypto exchange Kraken, brought more than 200 UK and European stocks onchain on Wednesday, November 11, as tokenized stocks under its xStocks brand. The first batch includes HSBC, Shell, LVMH and Airbus.

Each token is backed 1:1 by a real share held at a regulated custodian. The tokens trade on Kraken and on blockchains such as Solana and Ethereum from day one.

It is the first time xStocks has gone beyond U.S. listings, and it takes the brand past 1,500 tokenized assets. Hong Kong stocks are next.

The new tokens arrive in a weak month for tokenized stocks. Eight of the 10 largest by value have lost ground over the past 30 days, led by the crypto and chip stocks that onchain buyers favor.

The new tokens cover the most actively traded companies listed in the UK and the European Union, and more will be added over time. Payward built them with GTN, a global broker that buys the underlying shares and holds them in custody.

That last point stands out. A UK investor can’t buy the tokenized version of HSBC or Shell, even though both trade in London.

“A portfolio should not have borders or trading hours,” said Mark Greenberg, Payward’s chief commercial officer, who called the launch “proof it works”.

The brand is already large. xStocks launched in June 2025 with U.S. stocks and ETFs. Payward says it now holds over $1.1 billion in assets for more than 550,000 holders. The London Stock Exchange plans to list xStocks on its LSE 24 venue in 2027, subject to regulatory approval.

Onchain buyers piled into the most volatile names: chipmakers, crypto stocks and SpaceX. Those fell hardest in the past month’s sell-off.

Most of the trading still happens elsewhere. On Hyperliquid, perpetual futures on stocks and ETFs, contracts that track a price without holding any shares, traded $3.1 billion in 24 hours. The 250 tokens traded $1.0 billion. Nvidia’s perpetual alone traded $546 million, against $159 million across every Nvidia token.

None of the new UK and EU tokens shows up on CoinGecko yet.

SpaceX’s perpetual on Hyperliquid, which tracks the stock around the clock, trades at $147.47. That is 20% above its November 5 close of $122.51. It is still 16% below the October 6 high of $176.25.

On the daily chart, the price has just moved back above its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA) at $145.32. The EMA is an average that gives recent prices more weight.
