BitMine Immersion Technologies Inc. (NYSE: BMNR) has emerged as one of Wall Street’s largest corporate Ethereum treasury companies. The BMNR stock price staged a recovery in recent months but has struggled to sustain its gains, with shares retreating from the $25–$26 region toward $18–$19 as broader crypto-market weakness weighed on sentiment.

However, BitMine’s Ethereum accumulation strategy remains central to its investment case. The company held approximately 6.06 million ETH, representing 4.9% of Ethereum’s total supply, as of November 1, 2026. Its combined crypto, cash and other investments were valued at $14.5 billion.

As Ethereum adoption continues to expand, investors are closely watching whether the BMNR stock price can recover and eventually reclaim its previous highs. This BMNR stock price prediction examines the company’s treasury growth, technical outlook, and long-term potential through 2040.

BitMine’s treasury strategy has become the foundation of the BMNR stock prediction. The company now controls one of the largest corporate Ethereum holdings globally.

Ethereum now sits at the center of the BMNR stock valuation model. Treasury growth, ETH accumulation, and staking revenues have become the primary drivers behind the BitMine stock forecast. The company’s expanding Ethereum treasury continues strengthening its balance sheet, while staking operations generate recurring income. The rapid growth of stablecoins, tokenized assets, decentralized finance, and institutional Ethereum products could further improve the long-term BMNR stock outlook.

As BitMine expands its treasury, investors increasingly monitor ETH holdings, treasury value, staking yields, and capital allocation decisions. These factors are expected to remain the primary drivers of the BMNR price forecast throughout 2026.

BMNR stock remains in a broader bearish trend, with the chart showing a consistent pattern of lower highs and lower lows. The recent recovery toward the $25–$26 resistance zone failed to hold, and the stock has since moved lower, falling below its descending trendline and key moving averages. With shares trading near $18.76, the $18–$19 region is an immediate area to watch. A sustained decline could bring the lower support zone around $13–$14 back into focus.

On the upside, the $23–$26 region remains a major resistance area, where previous recovery attempts faced selling pressure. BMNR would need to reclaim this zone and break above the descending trendline to signal a meaningful improvement in momentum. Until then, the technical structure remains weak, with the stock vulnerable to further downside if buyers fail to defend current levels.

CoinPedia expects BMNR stock to remain range-bound during the near term. However, a sustained move above $25 could improve the long-term BMNR stock outlook and strengthen the probability of a higher price target toward $40 during the next Ethereum market cycle.

BitMine reported $124.8 million in revenue for fiscal 2026, but recorded a net loss of approximately $6.31 billion for the year ended August 31. Nearly $6.23 billion of the loss came from unrealized fair-value declines in its cryptocurrency holdings as Ethereum prices fell. Staking and validation generated 97% of annual revenue, highlighting the company’s growing dependence on its Ethereum treasury strategy
