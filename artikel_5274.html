Bitcoin Below $62K After Anthropic IPO: Is a SpaceX Style Crash Coming?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Why is crypto down? Bitcoin broke $62,300 as stocks fell on Anthropic’s IPO day. After SpaceX, BTC bottomed 19 days later.

For traders wondering why crypto is down, the much-anticipated “post-ANT” rebound has turned into another disappointment. Bitcoin’s anticipated post-Anthropic IPO rally has yet to materialize. Instead, BTC slipped below $62,000 on Thursday, November 19, as US stocks fell roughly 2% on the same day the AI company made its Nasdaq debut.

Bitcoin closed at $61,987, marking its first daily close below $62,300 since July 8, before sliding further to around $61,800 on Friday morning.

Anthropic itself had a strong but messy first day. Priced at $83 a share, it opened at $111.50, then faded to close at $97.17. The S&P 500 lost 2.0% and the Nasdaq Composite 2.3%, after the Nasdaq had opened higher.

SpaceX’s June listing is the closest comparison. We tracked Bitcoin’s path after that IPO day by day: it kept falling for almost three weeks before it bottomed.

So could Bitcoin repeat the SpaceX pattern, and when would the bottom come?

The listing was supposed to end the pressure. Bitcoin fell 22.7% in the 24 days before Anthropic’s debut, as traders sold crypto and other risky bets to make room for a mega-listing. Some, including Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse, expected that selling to stop once the shares started trading.

Instead, Thursday turned into a broad sell-off:

When tech stocks fall this hard, Bitcoin usually falls with them. That link is the simplest answer to why is crypto down this week.

SpaceX listed on June 12. We lined up Bitcoin’s daily closes from 24 days before that day to 80 days after, and did the same for Anthropic.

So far, Anthropic is following the SpaceX script with one difference: it skipped the early bounce.

Two listings are not a rule, and the backdrop has changed since June. For anyone asking why is crypto down and when it stops, here’s what lines up and what doesn’t.

If the SpaceX pattern holds, the worst could come in early December, followed by weeks of sideways trading. If stocks keep falling, the bottom could come sooner and lower.

On the daily chart, Bitcoin trades at $61,865. That is below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $74,655, and its 200-day EMA at $75,470.

The next few weeks will show whether the SpaceX pattern holds, and the answer to why is crypto down may come from stocks more than from crypto. A bottom near $58,500 in early December, with stocks steadying, would look a lot like June. A break below $57,800 while stocks keep falling would mean this sell-off is bigger than one IPO.
