Belgium’s Thursday night victory over Turkey all but extinguished the faint possibility of having to contest a relegation play-off. There are third-placed sides elsewhere in League A without the firepower to win three matches.

Their victory in Rome was, indeed, the defining result. Under new management, Italy and Belgium met at the Stadio Olimpico in their opening Nations League fixture in September, with the visitors running out 2-0 winners.

It was Belgium’s first victory over Italy in 11 years and only their fifth in a fixture that has been dominated by the Italians for more than a century.

Thursday could yet prove to have been a pivotal night. Italy welcomed France to San Siro and secured a 3-1 victory, taking them level on points with Les Bleus while maintaining a one-point advantage over Belgium.

Mark van Bommel’s side, meanwhile, overcame an early scare to win in Turkey and keep their quarter-final hopes alive. Kevin De Bruyne and Charles De Ketelaere struck within four minutes of each other to secure the points.
