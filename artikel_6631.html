Egg Noodles Al Limone, Limone, Limone, Limone

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One pot, four hits of lemon: Juice (limone #1) and zest (limone #2) in the cooking water, then more juice (limone #3) and zest (limone #4) to ﬁnish. The contrast of cooked and fresh citrus yields a lemon experience as wild as it gets—at least as far as pasta goes. Crème fraîche and Parmigiano-Reggiano mellow the acid into submission, delivering a creamy yet punchy ﬁnal product just begging for forks… and maybe one more squeeze of lemon.

Want some protein here? Add one pound of peeled and deveined medium shrimp to the pot as soon as the pasta turns uncomfortably al dente.

Remove the zest from 2 lemons with a vegetable peeler in long, lengthwise strips. Chop the strips very fine. (You should have a heaping 2 Tbsp.) Transfer half the chopped zest to a large pot or Dutch oven. Reserve the remaining half.

Squeeze enough juice from the lemons to measure ½ cup (120 ml); reserve the remaining lemon for squeezing at the finish. Transfer ¼ cup (60 ml) of the juice to the pot with the zest and reserve the remaining juice for serving. Add 3½ cups (840 ml) water, 1 large shallot, peeled and thinly sliced, 2 Tbsp. (30 g) unsalted butter, 4 thyme sprigs, leaves stripped and stems discarded, and 1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal kosher salt.

Place the pot over high heat and bring to a boil. Add 1 lb. (454 g) egg noodles. (Don’t worry if they’re not fully submerged.) Cook, stirring, until the pasta is uncomfortably al dente, 5–7 minutes. Reduce the heat to medium to maintain a lively but gentle simmer and add 1 cup (2 oz./60 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano and ¼ cup (60 ml) water, stirring to incorporate and create a pan sauce. Continue cooking and adding water, ¼ cup (60 ml) at a time, until there is a generous amount of sauce pooling in the pot and the noodles are al dente, 3–5 minutes more. Add ⅓ cup (80 g) crème fraîche, the remaining finely chopped lemon zest, and the reserved ¼ cup (60 ml) lemon juice. Season with kosher salt and taste. If the only flavor you’re getting is lemon, continue salting to bring out the creaminess.

Serve topped with a big dollop of crème fraîche and a squeeze of lemon juice. Shower with the remaining 1 cup (2 oz./60 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano and pinches of flaky sea salt. (Yes, this is a lot of Parm! But stay with me here… it’s a vital balance to the punches of lemon.) Top with more thyme leaves, and if desired, cracked black pepper and chopped roasted hazelnuts.
