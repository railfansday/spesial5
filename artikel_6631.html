Despite their reputation for being informal, sweatpants occupy a plum position within our wardrobes. A pillar in travel uniforms and off-duty outfit rotations, this MVP athleisure staple has come quite a long way since its gym wear associations. And for those pairs crafted in luxurious materials and in modern silhouettes, sweatpants can even replace denim or trousers when styled well.

The season’s best sweatpants run the spectrum of fabrication and design. On the traditional side, activewear labels from Nike to Adidas offer classic joggers cut from cotton. Contemporary cult-favorite labels like Jamie Haller, Lisa Yang, and Attersee have masterfully reimagined the sweatpant for a modern wardrobe (Haller’s pintuck pants would look fabulous with a thin turtleneck and check blazer layered on top). And, of course, luxury labels like The Row have always set the bar for the perfect pull-on pant.

The secret to elevating sweatpants is in the accessories. Keep the palette monochromatic, then layer in chic jewelry, a statement bag, and polished shoes for a refined finish. Think effortless minimalism—the Olsen twins remain the gold standard of this, proof that ease can still look directional and considered.

It depends on the fabric, but in most cases, heat is your friend. Washing sweatpants in hot water followed by a high-heat dry will help shrink your selected pair. Just proceed with thought—this method is effective, but not reversible!

Stretching depends largely on the material, and many pairs will naturally relax with wear. For a faster fix, lightly dampen the fabric and gently stretch it by hand into your desired silhouette, then let it air-dry. You may need to repeat the process a few times, but patience will pay off.

Material comes first: Our favorites tend to be in soft natural fabrics, like cotton and cashmere, that also offer a bit of room for maximum comfort.
