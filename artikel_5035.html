Toronto, November 19, 2026 – OmenX, a Base-native leveraged prediction market platform, today announced $2 million in funding from Informed Ventures alongside the launch of its on-demand, 30-second Bitcoin price prediction product.

The new product lets users predict whether Bitcoin’s price will rise or fall over the next 30 seconds, with each trade starting its own prediction window. A position opened at 10:00:12 runs until 10:00:42. Users can act when their view forms, with a full 30-second horizon, rather than adapting to the remaining time in a shared market round.

Featuring zero-slippage execution and rapid settlement, the product extends the familiar five- and fifteen-minute crypto prediction format to a shorter, individually timed experience. It adds to OmenX’s intraday offering, which spans prediction horizons from 30 seconds to 24 hours, depending on the market.

Beyond crypto, OmenX offers directional prediction markets linked to more than 100 global assets, including U.S. equities, futures, ETFs and indices. Its event markets also cover live sports and esports, with sports coverage spanning major football leagues, basketball, American football, ice hockey, baseball and UFC.

Leverage has been central to OmenX’s platform from the outset, allowing traders to amplify their exposure on supported event markets. The latest launch builds on that foundation while advancing a broader goal: making prediction markets a practical financial tool for retail users.

OmenX organizes the trading decision around a defined question: will an asset’s price be higher or lower at the end of a specified period? Users can express a directional view without purchasing the underlying asset, targeting an exact closing price or constructing an options position that requires decisions about strikes, implied volatility and time decay.

“Leverage has been part of OmenX from the start, but our ambition goes beyond adding leverage to prediction markets,” said James, founder and CEO of OmenX. “We want to make global markets easier to participate in. A trader should be able to take a view on Bitcoin, a stock or a sporting event and express it through a clear contract and a timeframe that fits their judgment.”

The platform’s emphasis on short, recurring markets provides faster feedback on trading decisions and can reduce the time capital remains committed to individual outcomes. OmenX plans to expand its coverage of traditional and crypto assets while deepening the trading experience across supported markets.

OmenX is a Base-native leveraged prediction market platform covering crypto, global financial assets, live sports and esports. Through defined outcomes, short settlement cycles and leverage on supported markets, OmenX is building a more accessible way to trade market views.
