Twenty One Capital (XXI) Buys 154 Bitcoin, Its First Purchase of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Twenty One Capital bought 154.32 bitcoin for about $10 million in cash after the weekend selloff, lifting its holdings to about 43,668 BTC.

Twenty One Capital bought 154.32 bitcoin for about $10 million in cash after the weekend selloff, the company said Monday, its first disclosed purchase of 2026 and a small addition to a treasury that had barely moved all year.

The Tether-controlled company said it paid an average of about $64,801 per coin, bringing its holdings to about 43,668 BTC as of Nov. 16. At Tuesday morning’s price near $64,900, that stack is worth roughly $2.83 billion.

“Bitcoin is our benchmark for every investment decision we make. Any business we acquire has to beat simply buying more Bitcoin, and this weekend’s drop in price made adding Bitcoin an easy decision,” CEO Rapha Zagury said in the release.

“Every dollar we spend, whether on Bitcoin, acquisitions, or buybacks, is weighed against maintaining flexibility to run our business well,” he added.

The buy breaks a long pause. Twenty One’s quarterly report, filed Nov. 13, shows it held 43,515 BTC at the end of 2025 and 43,514 BTC on Sept. 30. The only change in between was a single bitcoin transferred on March 31 as a vendor payment.

The same filing shows how much room the company has to keep buying with cash. Twenty One reported $102.8 million in cash and equivalents at Sept. 30, down from $117.7 million at the end of 2025. The $10 million purchase equals about a tenth of that September balance, although the company has not said how much cash it holds now.

Twenty One also carries $486.5 million of convertible notes issued when it went public. Those notes are secured by about 16,116 BTC pledged as collateral, coins the filing says are not available for general corporate purposes while they back the debt.

The company’s bitcoin cost basis was about $3.69 billion at Sept. 30, or roughly $84,900 per coin. The new coins came in about $20,000 below that average, but the treasury as a whole remains underwater at current prices.

Twenty One’s Class A shares closed at $4.56 on Monday, down 7.9% on the day, before the purchase was announced after the market close. The 10-Q lists 346.65 million Class A shares outstanding, which at that price are worth about $1.58 billion, well below the market value of the company’s bitcoin.

That gap has been the central problem for Zagury since he replaced founder Jack Mallers as CEO in July. In an August shareholder letter, he acknowledged investor concerns about the stock trading at a discount to its bitcoin and said Twenty One “must become more than a Bitcoin treasury.” Tether took control of the company in May after buying out SoftBank’s stake.

The company’s latest shareholder letter, released Nov. 13 with third-quarter results, laid out a plan to build cash-generating businesses around the bitcoin treasury. Twenty One reported third-quarter net income of $1.087 billion, almost all of it from a $1.093 billion gain in the fair value of its bitcoin as prices rose during the quarter. For the first nine months of 2026, it posted a net loss of $186.5 million.

Twenty One said further bitcoin purchases are core to its strategy, with the amount and timing set by market conditions, available liquidity and competing investment opportunities. The company did not commit to a size or schedule.

The purchase landed the same day Strategy (MSTR), the largest corporate holder, disclosed buying 485 BTC for $33.4 million, lifting its holdings to 850,849 BTC.

The test now is whether those purchases stay opportunistic and small, or whether Twenty One uses bitcoin, acquisitions or buybacks to close the gap between its share price and its coins.
