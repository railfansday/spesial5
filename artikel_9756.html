SuperPlane is an open-source AI orchestration platform designed to automate routine software development tasks while keeping engineers in control. Rather than requiring developers to manage AI coding agents individually, SuperPlane brings them together in a structured workflow that takes tasks from the backlog to verified, review-ready pull requests.

The platform works by analyzing a team's codebase and backlog to identify tasks suitable for automation. Once a task is selected, SuperPlane coordinates AI agents through planning, coding, testing, and review, applying consistent rules and quality checks throughout the process. If something goes wrong, the system provides feedback and prompts the agent to try again, involving a human engineer when additional judgment is needed.

SuperPlane integrates with existing development tools, allowing teams to choose their preferred AI models and deploy the platform in the cloud or on-premises. The company reports that its approach can autonomously handle 30% of backlog issues, reduce engineer involvement in routine tasks by 70%, and accelerate the process from issue to reviewed pull request by four times.

Founded by Darko Fabijan and Marko Anastasov, the team behind the continuous integration platform Semaphore, SuperPlane aims to reduce repetitive engineering work so developers can focus on more complex problems and higher-value projects.

Now, let’s check out the Projects of the Week.

Slick is a customizable search engine built for people who want more control over how they search the web. Most search engines use a single ranking system that treats every user the same. Slick allows users to adjust their own experience through features like domain preferences, pinned results, blocked websites, custom search recipes, and ranking controls that stay on the user's device. Proof of Usefulness score: +81/1000

Sos-vault is an open-source, self-hosted platform for analyzing sosreport archives (the diagnostic bundles Linux support engineers and SRE teams collect for troubleshooting). It lets you upload, decrypt, unpack, browse, annotate, and compare sosreports through a web interface, in a highly secure isolated environment. A key feature is its AI-assisted analysis pipeline. It's aimed at Linux system administrators and support teams who need collaborative SOS report analysis with shared context and notes. Proof of Usefulness score: +51/1000

GeoBounties is a location-based task marketplace that serves as physical "hands and eyes" for AI agents and humans in the real world. By exposing Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoints, AI models (like ChatGPT or Claude) and remote businesses can autonomously dispatch local bounty hunters for GPS-verified, 10-minute micro-tasks. It bridges digital AI intelligence with real-world physical verification while empowering gig workers and locals with instant payouts. Proof of Usefulness score: +76/1000
