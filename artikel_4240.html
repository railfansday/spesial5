Star Wars Racing Reveals Split-Screen Multiplayer Release Window

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

Star Wars: Galactic Racer has proven to be one of the unexpected slam dunks of late 2026. In the little over a month since it was released, it has swiftly become exceptionally popular, both as another great game set in the Star Wars universe after Zero Company, and a pretty great time of a racing game in general. It's even managed to inspired a healthy modding community. Heck, I don’t even like Star Wars, and I adore this game, having just rolled credits on the campaign the other day.

One of the only major, consistent complaints towards Galactic Racer is its lack of local multiplayer features. The game does have online multiplayer, of course, but if you just wanted to run a few quick races with your buddies on the couch, you can’t do that, at least without everyone in the room having their own Steam Decks or something. This could be the single, crucial factor that definitively revives the spirit of games like Star Wars Episode I: Racer.

Galactic Racer turned a racing dabbler and Star Wars outsider into a stubborn, seething, very happy pilot. Its Roguelite loop earns every restart.

However, it seems the folks at Fuse Games have had their ears to the ground and heard the pleas of racing fans across the galaxy, because not only will Galactic Racer be getting that local multiplayer option soon, it’ll also get a little extra functionality on top.

In a post to the official Star Wars: Galactic Racer X, formerly Twitter account, a representative of Fuse Games announced that the game will be receiving local split-screen functionality some time in early 2027. Rather than as an addition to the existing multiplayer structure, though, this functionality will be added in the form of an entirely new game mode.

“A dedicated team at @Fuse_Games has been working hard to bring a brand new mode, Free Play, to STAR WARS: Galactic Racer,” the post reads.

The game will be receiving local split-screen functionality some time in early 2027.

As the post explains, when you’re playing in free mode, not only will you be able to race freely with up to three friends in split-screen multiplayer, you’ll be able to freely select your vehicle, planet, race track, and character. The post also included a pre-release update build screenshot showing four players racing in split-screen, with the UI in each window adjusted to properly accommodate the radar, afterburner gauge, abilities, and Ramjet meter.

Fuse Games explains how Galactic Racer turns crashes and failed runs into progression, player choice, and spectacular wrecks.

While the post didn’t specifically clarify this aspect, I’d assume players will be able to customize their abilities and vehicle components to some extent, given the ability menu’s presence.

As someone who grew up in the N64 era, I can speak confidently to the power of split-screen racing games in making (and ruining) friendships. I can still remember the shouting matches I had with my sister over who got to be Yoshi in Mario Kart 64. If Galactic Racer can get full split-screen support, plus all the extra game-tweaking elements of Free Play, I think it could just become a contender as one of the best local multiplayer racing games of the current era.

As I mentioned, many fans of this game have likened it to Star Wars Episode I: Racer, an N64 racing game focused on the Podracing elements from the film of the same name. While that game was fun enough on its own, it was the multiplayer that cemented it in peoples’ hearts and minds.

Come to think of it, I hope Free Play lets you roll in Podracers as well, as that’d pretty much be the best possible way to capture that same magic. At the very least, I am confident that all multiplayer parties for Galactic Racer will have at least one argument over who gets to play as Biddy Blas.
