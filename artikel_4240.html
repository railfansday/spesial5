GTA 6 Metacritic User Scores Go Live, Resulting In Mass Review Bombing

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After an incredibly long wait, Grand Theft Auto 6 has dropped to massively positive reviews from critics, who have repeatedly described Rockstar's latest release as one of, if not the best games ever made. While there was plenty of debate over whether the critic reviews and player responses would actually line up or not when it released, the public response has been almost equally impressive so far.

Despite taking the industry by storm and boasting sales that broke a new record for the entertainment industry, GTA 6's review rating has just plummeted on Metacritic immediately after the site opened its user scores.

As noted by users like CharlieIntel on X, while there are plenty of user reviews that note the flaws while celebrating its achievements, the User Review section for GTA 6 on Metacritic has been absolutely flooded with overwhelmingly negative reviews, many of which are from players who admit they "haven't even played the game" themselves.

With GTA 6 currently sitting at a 5.1 user score at the time of writing, over half of the reviews uploaded for the game are negative, with countless politically charged reviews calling it "woke slop" and a "cuck simulator" over its relationship gameplay mechanics, or users straight-up keyboard-smashing negative reviews just so they can contribute to tearing down the game's score.

While it isn't exactly surprising that one of the most widely talked about games in history would wind up being the target of annoyed players or internet trolls wanting to join in on the fun, Take-Two had already expressed their concern about this exact situation happening years before GTA 6 released.

Even Nintendo's recently released Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake wasn't exempt from the same fate either, with Link's return being slammed by players in response to the game's popularity, rather than the actual quality of the remake.

Considering that there are some genuine complaints to be had surrounding GTA 6's console performance, control scheme, or returning franchise features that are currently absent in the newest release, it's hard to pinpoint what the actual score will end up being after the dust of its release settles.

A new survey suggests that most people don't consider it cheating, yet the BBC ran with it anyway.

If the critic scores and the Metacritic rating for Rockstar's previous games are anything to go by, however, Grand Theft Auto 6 will likely wind up with an incredibly high score regardless of the current review bomb storm, especially as the game gets even bigger with the eventual PC port and multiplayer release.
