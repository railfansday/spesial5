To reach the Deku Tree in the Switch 2 Ocarina of Time Remake, Link needs to get the Deku Shield and Kokiri Sword first

To get the Sword and Shield in The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake is one of the earliest challenges you'll be given on the Switch 2. Link is asked to visit the Deku Tree, but you can't leave the village until you've found a Kokiri Sword and Shield. These are found at different locations around the village, and each requires different steps to be gone through. Below I'll show you got to get both, as well as the challenges that Ocarina places in front of you to sort out. Not only that, but I'll explain how to get a Hylian Shield later on, the natural upgrade to your equipment.

The Kokiri Sword – the first weapon you'll get in Ocarina of Time – is found in the Southwest corner of the Kokiri Forest Village, via the tunnel you crawl through in the training ground next to the training dummies (the tunnel's location is shown on the map above).

Once you crawl through, you'll see a chest on top of a high platform with a circular boulder rolling around it. You can make your way around the platform, using alcoves to avoid being crushed by the boulder and let it pass. At the opposite side of this arena is a ladder. Climb up it, and you'll end up on a platform that allows you to jump down to the one with the chest on. Open the chest, and you'll get the Kokiri Sword!

To get a Deku Shield, you need to head to the shop in the middle of the Village, opposite Link's Treehouse. You can buy a wooden shield from the owner for 40 Rupees.

To get the Rupees, you need to explore the entire village. They're scattered around everywhere, and you'll find everything you need before long. Here's some places to check:

This should comfortably bring you up to what you need to buy your first shield. Keep in mind that the wooden Deku Shield is flammable, and can be destroyed permanently if it takes fire damage. If that happens, you can buy more from the village store and replace it, though of course you'll have to pay another 40 Rupees each time.

The metal Hylian Shield is the natural upgrade to the Deku Shield, being non-flammable and harder to destroy. To get it, you need to get to Hyrule Castle Town (which you can go to after completing the Deku Tree dungeon), and go to the Bazaar with the bearded owner on the East side of Town, shown above.

Here, you can buy a Hylian Shield for 80 Rupees. It's heavier and can't be used in the same way as the Deku Shield when you first encounter it, but without spoiling, that will be addressed if you continue with the main story. As a young boy, Link can't hold the Hylian shield and sword at the same time, so you'll have to crouch with it on your back instead of holding it in front of you.
