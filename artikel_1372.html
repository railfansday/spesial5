Revealed: Just two Man United stars are selected in combined XI with Liverpool by stats boffins as Andoni Iraola's defence dominates... but is the Liverpool boss missing a hidden gem?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Published: 01:43 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 01:45 EST, 22 November 2026

All eyes will be on Manchester United's new head coach Mauricio Pochettino when they take on Liverpool at Anfield this afternoon.

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola has freshened up the squad since joining in the summer, including the £123million purchase of Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain, while blending in young emerging talent. They have made a solid start across all four competitions, knocking Chelsea out of the League Cup on penalties, and they currently sit in fourth place in the Premier League.

United have had a dire start to the season, with only one win in 10 games leading to the axeing of head coach Michael Carrick. Will his replacement be able to save the season and galvanise a group of talented but dispirited players?

So, who from United’s struggling side have done enough to break into Sofascore's combined XI based on their performances throughout the 2026-27 season so far?

Manchester United's new head coach Mauricio Pochettino will face his first test against Liverpool on Sunday

United currently sit at 18th in the Premier League after a disappointing start to the season

Between the sticks is Liverpool’s No 1, who since signing for a record fee in 2018, has established himself as one of the best shot-stoppers in the world.

Rated for his positioning, distribution and consistency, Alisson has an impressive record, especially in one-on-one situations.

The 34-year-old Brazilian goalkeeper has kept four clean sheets in 10 league appearances this season, averaging 2.5 saves per game. He has conceded 10 goals, including a brutal 3-0 loss to Manchester City at Anfield last month.

Alisson was poor in Liverpool's 3-2 defeat by Fenerbahce but responded with a fine display in the Reds' 3-1 win over Crystal Palace when back in league action days later.

At right back is Liverpool’s Ronald Araujo, on loan from Barcelona this season, and there are already cries from Liverpool fans to sign him permanently.

The 27-year-old is a natural centre back, but he has done wonders to ease Liverpool’s problems in the right-back position. Jeremie Frimpong started the season in that position but the Liverpool backline were far too exposed. Araujo has helped Liverpool tighten up defensively.

The Uruguayan defender has featured in all of Liverpool’s league matches so far this season, with his defensive actions helping him contribute inside his own penalty box. He has averaged six clearances per game and has won 30 aerial duels so far this season, behind only Virgil van Dijk in the Liverpool squad.

Ronald Araujo (right) has impressed this season for Liverpool, on loan from Barcelona

Jeremy Jacquet is a young player to watch this season after joining Liverpool this summer.

The 21-year-old has a 95 per cent accurate pass rate, including long balls. He had a standout performance against Fulham early on in the season, winning five aerial duels, five recoveries and making a clearance off the line to keep his team in the game.

Despite playing in France's second division just two seasons ago, Jacquet has fitted seamlessly into Iraola's style of play at Liverpool and there are suggestions he is an upgrade on the man he replaced, fellow France international Ibrahima Konate.

Centre back - Virgil van Dijk (Liverpool)

It is no surprise that Liverpool and Netherlands team captain Virgil van Dijk makes it into the XI.

Known for his strength, leadership, speed and aerial ability, he is the only defender to win the UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award, and finished runner-up for both the Ballon d'Or and Best FIFA Men's Player in 2019.

This season, he has shown his aerial ability with 3.8 duels won in the air (79 per cent) per game and 4.8 total duels won per game.

The team struggled against Manchester City, however. In the game at Anfield that ended their unbeaten run, Van Dijk only made four defensive contributions and picked up a yellow card for a frustrated foul on Rayan Cherki in added time.

Van Dijk's contract expires next summer, and the 35-year-old has a big decision to make.

Is Iraola missing a hidden gem? Tsimikas is very much understudy to Milos Kerkez after acting as a reliable deputy to Andy Robertson.

But across all competitions this season he is statistically rated higher than the man who's ahead of him in the pecking order. Kerkez is rated at 6.67 – so it is tight.

Nicknamed 'Greek Scouser' by Reds fans, Tsimikas has only started five games but has produced three assists with 1.78 key passes and 1.56 accurate crosses.

Manchester United, on the other hand, have struggled to fill this position. They were unable to recruit in the summer, and now rely on Luke Shaw – who has been sidelined with a muscular issue since limping off during their 1-1 draw with Tottenham Hotspur in October – and are adapting with makeshift alternatives.

Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk celebrates with the fans after beating Crystal Palace

Centre midfield – Dominik Szoboszlai (Liverpool)

Versatile vice-captain Dominik Szoboszlai signed a new five-year contract with Liverpool this summer, and has proven his worth after scoring a stoppage-time penalty to level the score in their first game of the season against Newcastle.

With his ability to go forward and send crosses into the box and drop back to defend, Szoboszlai has been crucial for ball recovery, key passes and creating big chances.

It is a testament to his creative ability, including registering four big chances and one assist in the Premier League this season, that he makes the XI despite being sidelined with a hamstring injury for three games.

However, the Hungary midfielder has received backlash from fans for playing 90 minutes for his country in a 2-0 win against Georgia in the UEFA Nations League despite early withdrawal against Palace, and injury concerns remain ahead of this afternoon's clash.

Centre midfield – Kobbie Mainoo (Manchester United)

Only one of two Manchester United players in this line-up, Kobbie Mainoo deserves recognition for his creative efforts this season, which have earned him the second highest Sofascore rating in this XI.

The 21-year-old England midfielder is known for his high pressing and long balls to create chances up the pitch. He has also registered four shots from outside the box.

He has been through some turbulent times at Old Trafford but Michael Carrick turned to him regularly this season.

A product of Manchester United's youth system and there is plenty of potential, especially with new manager Mauricio Pochettino’s penchant for developing young players. A chance to refresh and discover his best form.

Kobbie Mainoo is one of only two United players to make the combined XI this weekend

Centre midfield – Alexis Mac Allister (Liverpool)

After his public revelation that he nearly left Liverpool, Alexis Mac Allister has been in form and pulling the strings in midfield and played an important role in Liverpool's season so far.

He headed in Liverpool’s second goal in their 3-1 away win over Crystal Palace and his passing and playmaking abilities have been key for their attacking chances this season.

He has scored five goals in 16 appearances across all competitions this season.

Right forward – Bruno Fernandes (Manchester United)

The Portugal forward was named the PFA Players' Player of the Year last season, but he has divided United fans this season.

Inconsistent performances and publicly questioning Carrick's tactics after defeat in Como.

In the 1-1 draw against Spurs at Old Trafford, the skipper did not record his first successful pass until the 23rd minute, and he missed a big chance against Chelsea.

He has bagged four goals and two assists in the Premier League so far, but this is largely owing to his hat-trick against Ipswich, which earned him a perfect 10 Sofascore rating.

United fans will hope he can get back to his old ways under Pochettino.

United fans will be desperate to see Bruno Fernandes find consistent performances again

Centre-forward – Alexander Isak (Liverpool)

Liverpool’s star striker, transferred for a British record-breaking fee of £125million last summer, has scored eight goals in 14 games this season, showing just how clinical he can be.

Reds fans are finally seeing the form that earned him the big-money move from Newcastle.

Isak edges out United's Bryan Mbeumo to lead the line in this team. He has a 55.6 per cent big chance conversion, scoring a goal every 113 minutes.

The Dutchman is establishing himself as Liverpool's go-to wideman among Iraola's plethora of options. His rapid pace and direct approach proves a massive threat.

This season his end product has improved, notching three goals and supplying six assists in 12 appearances across all competitions.

He is proving to be a reliable outlet while summer signings Bradley Barcola and Victor Munoz continue to settle into life in England.

His stunning long-range strike to equalise against Newcastle United shows just how clinical he can be in front of goal.

He averages 1.9 key passes and one big chance per game, which lands him a spot in this XI.
