D’Amico: ‘Roma want to reinforce in January, not sell Kone’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Roma director Tony D’Amico assures Manu Koné is not leaving in January, as the ‘aim is to reinforce the squad’ mid-season, and explains some of the squad rotation against Parma.

It kicks off at the Stadio Tardini at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The injury crisis is finally starting to calm down for the Giallorossi, as only Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini failed to make the trip to Parma this evening.

Mile Svilar had not played in over two months after fracturing a finger in training, while Paulo Dybala also had not featured since October 14, but they both start today.

Wesley and Matias Soulé are among those rested today, ahead of the upcoming Champions League clash with Paris Saint-Germain.

“The break for international duty always has a certain sense of unknown quantity, but the coach chose the players who remained here, we have some from Argentina and Brazil who only arrived late, but we are confident that we’ll have a good game,” D’Amico told DAZN Italia.

The January transfer window is coming up, so is there a specific budget or list of options drawn up, like Endrick of Real Madrid and Ademola Lookman with Atletico Madrid, who of course worked with Gian Piero Gasperini at Atalanta.

Specifically, D’Amico was asked about the rumours that France international Koné will be targeted by big clubs again after a difficult summer.

“It is premature to talk about this now, as there are many variables that can change from now to January. The aim is to reinforce the squad, and therefore anyone asking about Kone leaving, that is not strengthening the team.

“We discuss the situation with the coach every day, the characteristics of players we imagine can give something extra to the side. The January window is one based on opportunities, so we’ll have to see what happens.”

Roma went into the break for international duty just two points off Serie A leaders Lazio, as they aim to improve on last season’s third-place finish.

“It is not just about technical quality, but temperamental and mental strength too. We believe in the whole squad, and that also means testing those who need a chance to prove they can step up when required,” concluded D’Amico.
