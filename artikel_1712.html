Hurzeler delighted with Brighton youngster Osman

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Fabian Hurzeler expressed his delight with the performance of youngster Ibrahim Osman after Brighton cruised to victory over Universitatea Craiova in the Conference League.

The Seagulls won 4-0 at home on Thursday against 10-man Craiova, with Osman scoring twice while Maxim De Cuyper and Charalampos Kostoulas were also among the goals.

Osman provided an assist to go with his two goals, the first of which came inside three minutes, while he also led all players on the pitch for chances created (six).

Brighton dominated against their Romanian opponents, who saw Vladimir Screciu sent off in the first half for a high challenge on Matt O'Riley.

The Seagulls accumulated 2.63 expected goals (xG) from 27 shots, 10 of which were on target, while restricting Craiova to 0.11 from four attempts.

Hurzeler lauded the display of Osman, though he admitted his side could have been more clinical.

"I think he showed a really good performance today," Hurzeler said of 21-year-old Osman.

"He scored the first goal and created plenty of other moments. We know that his biggest quality is one-against-one, especially against teams who are sitting in a low block.

"It is very helpful to have these one-against-one players, and he showed a very impressive style today.

"Besides that, I'm really happy that he understands, more and more, how important it is to keep working hard for the clean sheet.

"He is also responsible for that as an offensive player, because that's always the foundation of our game.

"I think that was a really good start, and it's also very important to make the game look easy, because especially the first goal helped us force [the opponent] to open up the space.

"That meant we could have more space to combine and more opportunities to attack their box. So I think we were very professional with good standards in the first half.

"Then, of course, the red card definitely helped us to finish – or to change – the structure of the game.

"In the second half we created plenty of chances. I wasn't happy that we weren't clinical and clean enough finishing these chances, because I think we dropped our level a little bit there. Overall, though, winning 4-0 means that we did a lot of things well."
