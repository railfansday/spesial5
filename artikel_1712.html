Moretto: ‘Milan have made the decision’ – Camarda’s fate sealed amid limited minutes

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Matteo Moretto has confirmed that AC Milan have already made the decision to send Francesco Camarda out on loan.

Before a ball was kicked this season, head coach Ruben Amorim delivered a passionate speech about how important he deems Camarda to be, as the only natural deputy to the club-record summer signing Goncalo Ramos.

Yet, the Italian does not seem to have obtained the manager’s full trust yet as he has started just one game all season. That came in the Europa League, and the rest have been cameo appearances off the bench, leading some to question whether it is good for the 18-year-old’s development.

As a result of this, there is growing speculation that the club will sanction a loan exit for the second half of the season. Matteo Moretto also confirmed this in a video on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel.

“Camarda hasn’t found the playing time he himself hoped for. The club certainly continues to have faith in Camarda, but I can confirm that Milan’s intention is to let Camarda go in January,” the journalist revealed.

“The idea is that he can try a new experience to grow, get playing time, and demonstrate his qualities. Milan have made the decision. Then we’ll see if things change between now and January. Anything can happen in football… maybe he scores three goals in a row and the situation changes.

“But as of now, Milan have decided to let Camarda go in January. For this reason, the club is already looking at other forwards, beyond Tresoldi; there are also other names on the list of the club on Via Aldo Rossi [the club’s headquarters].”
