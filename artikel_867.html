On this episode John Bennett, Julien Laurens and Rafa Honigstein take a little trip down memory lane to reminisce about Mauricio Pochettino’s time at PSG, as he’s looking like the preferred choice to replace Michael Carrick at Manchester United. They look at what went right and what went wrong at the French club and if he really is the ‘player whisperer’. Managerial sackings is a bit of theme of this week’s Euro Leagues… Lens have made a change – for the second time this season after a nightmare week before the international break. They lost 9-1 to Como in the Champions League. John, Jules and Rafa asked the question are the stars aligning for a return for Pierre Sage? Not only that, they focus on a very different club in Turkey who have become the country’s surprise package… It’s Amedspor, who are in their first ever season in Turkey’s Super Lig and are sitting in third after a brilliant start to the season. They are joined by BBC News’ Turkish journalist Burak Abatay. And they find out the reaction in Germany to Jobe Bellingham being called up for England after a difficult start at BVB.
