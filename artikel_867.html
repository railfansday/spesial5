Manchester City fans would have hoped that the international break would allow their team to reset after a concerning run of form, but Saturday's 1-1 home draw with lowly Fulham was more of the same.

Enzo Maresca's side are now on a four-match winless run in the Premier League ahead of Sunday's showdown with title rivals Arsenal, although their Champions League form has generally remained strong.

City bounced back from their home defeat by Paris Saint-Germain in gameweek two with a 5-1 thrashing of AEK Athens, before beating RB Leipzig 3-2 in an entertaining clash in Germany that saw Erling Haaland make it five goals in just three European matches this term.

Injured winger Antoine Semenyo is the home side's only absentee, with Matheus Nunes fit again after missing the draw with Fulham.

The good news for City is that Napoli's form is also nothing to write home about, with the Italian side eighth in Serie A and outside the top 24 in the league phase table.

Massimiliano Allegri's men at least managed to stop the rot with their 1-0 win over Torino on Saturday, but qualification for the Champions League knockout stage already looks a tall order following defeats against Arsenal, Villarreal and Porto.

Their defensive numbers are particularly concerning, with only three sides in the 36-team league phase conceding more than their 11 goals across the opening four gameweeks.

That included the 4-1 defeat at Porto last time out, in which centre back Rafa Marin was sent off. He is suspended for the trip to the Etihad Stadium, with Stanislav Lobotka and captain Giovanni Di Lorenzo also unavailable due to injury, but City legend Kevin De Bruyne looks set to face his former club.

City's European form has contrasted with their domestic struggles and they should be able to cash in against a leaky Napoli defence.
