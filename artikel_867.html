Wembanyama sits out Spurs' win over Sacramento

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Victor Wembanyama was forced to sit out of Monday night's clash against the Kings due to a back injury; it came after he scored 50 points against the Pelicans on Saturday; watch the NBA live on Sky Sports

San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama sat out Monday night's 123-107 win against the Sacramento Kings due to back tightness.

Wembanyama, coming off a 50-point, 19-rebound performance in a win over New Orleans Pelicans on Saturday, was ruled out about 90 minutes before tipoff.

San Antonio were also without injured starters De'Aaron Fox [bruised shin] and Dylan Harper [hip] during the match at the Golden 1 Center.

Stephon Castle had 28 points, 11 assists and eight rebounds, while Julian Champagnie made six three-pointers and scored 22 as the Spurs [15-3] improved to 8-0 on the road and remained tied with the Los Angeles Lakers at the top of the Western Conference standings.

"We had some stretches last year when Vic [Victor Wembanyama] didn't play," Champagnie said.

"Obviously, when he's on the court it's a whole different basketball game, and we're able to play better with him for obvious reasons.

Champagnie hit four straight threes in the second quarter to help the Spurs regain the lead and eventually pull away from the Kings (2-15).

"We've got to figure out ways to continue to be good without him [Wembanyama] on the floor, and I think tonight's a good building block for us," he added.

Backup center Luke Kornet got more playing time with Wembanyama sidelined and said his absence can provide a good chance for others to get into a rhythm with more minutes to spread around.

Kornet finished with 18 points and 11 rebounds.

LeBron James scored 23 points during the Philadelphia 76ers' 128-111 defeat to the Portland Trail Blazers, the most the NBA great has scored in a game since joining his new team at the start of the season.

James hit four threes from five attempts as he scored eight baskets during the match.

However, his side were outclassed by the Blazers, with Deni Avdija starring to make five of Portland's 19 three-pointers on his way to amassing 31 points.

Kevin Durant scored 32 points, while Alperen Sengun and Amen Thompson each had 20, leading the Houston Rockets to a 116-104 win over the Los Angeles Clippers on Monday night.

The game was tied midway through the third quarter before the Rockets used a 9-0 run, capped by a three from Tari Eason, to make it 74-65.

Houston held a six-point lead heading into the final three minutes, with Durant scoring consecutive three-pointers to make it 109-97.

Brandon Ingram hit back with a basket late in the fourth quarter for the Clippers, but it was too little too late, with Durant scoring again before the buzzer to see Los Angeles extend their losing run to four games on the bounce.

Elsewhere, Jaden McDaniels scored a career-best 34 points as the Minnesota Timberwolves routed the Brooklyn Nets 142-116 on Monday night.

Ty Jerome made a whopping seven three-pointers to hand the Charlotte Hornets their fifth-straight loss as the Memphis Grizzlies eked out a 117-111 win in North Carolina.

Los Angeles Lakers also avenged a surprising home loss to the Utah Jazz, with Luka Doncic amassing 28 points and eight assists to lift his side to a 109-94 win at the Crypto.com Arena.
