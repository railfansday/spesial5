Arsenal return to Champions League action against Borussia Dortmund hoping to extend their unbeaten start to Europe’s premier competition this season.

The Gunners return to the Emirates after dropping points in the Premier League title race thanks to a Newcastle comeback at the weekend - although rivals Manchester City and Brighton both failed to capitalise and close the gap to the league leaders.

Dortmund are just one point behind them in the Champions League group stage rankings but nine places below, having got past Real Betis 2-1 in their last encounter, while the Gunners beat Slavia Prague by the same scoreline.

Arsenal and Dortmund have not faced off since 2014 - when Mikel Arteta wore the captain’s armband in a 2-0 Arsenal victory.

Arsenal host Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium, London on Tuesday 24 November, with kick-off at 8pm.

Arteta was relieved to announce that the injury to David Raya, which saw him withdrawn against Newcastle after a collision with Will Osula, will not require surgery, but the first-choice keeper will miss out on Tuesday. He will be replaced between the sticks by Kepa Arrizabalaga.

Bukayo Saka remains out for another four weeks while Martin Zubimendi is another injury absentee, although William Saliba could pick up a few minutes as he manages his return from a long-term back injury.

Dortmund are sweating on the fitness of Serhou Guirassy, who picked up a hamstring injury against Real Betis, while Carney Chukwuemeka is another doubt and the likes of Emre Can and Justin Lerma are definite absentees.

Arsenal XI: Kepa; White, Konsa, Gabriel, Hincapie; Lewis-Skelly, Rice; Madueke, Odegaard, Eze; Gyokeres

Dortmund XI: Kobel; Schlotterbeck, Anton, Bensebaini; Ryerson, Nmecha, Bellingham, Svensson; Karetsas, Beier; Silva
