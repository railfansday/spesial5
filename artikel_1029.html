Woods given 'special opportunity' to design new course in Northern Ireland

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bellarena will be TGR Design’s first course in Northern Ireland and the first new championship links to open in Northern Ireland in more than a century; TGR Design are a golf course design firm led by Tiger Woods

Tiger Woods has revealed he will design the first championship-standard links golf course built in Northern Ireland for more than 100 years, describing the project as a "special opportunity".

Reports linking the 15-time major champion with the proposed £30 million course at Bellarena on the north west coast between Limavady and Coleraine had been circulating for several weeks, ahead of Thursday's official announcement.

Hospitality group Galgorm Collection is building the course, with construction set to get under way next year if the required planning approvals are secured.

In a statement, Woods said: "Northern Ireland is home to some of the greatest links golf in the world, and building one on the Causeway Coast is a special opportunity for our team.

"We want Bellarena to feel as though it has always been part of this coastline, with a course that challenges the best players and is fun for everyone who plays it."

Galgorm Collection already owns and runs the championship parkland course at Galgorm Castle in Ballymena and a course at Roe Valley in Limavady, with plans to create an additional venue

Colin Johnston, chief executive of Galgorm Collection, announced that Woods and his TGR Design firm would craft the new links layout.

"Set against the spectacular backdrop of Northern Ireland's rugged northwest coast, our plans represent a once-in-a-generation opportunity to transform Northern Ireland's golfing and tourism future," Johnston said.

Bellarena is around 20 miles from Royal Portrush Golf Club, which hosted Shane Lowry's win at The Open in 2019 and Scottie Scheffler completing the third leg of the career Grand Slam in 2025.

Portstewart - host of Jon Rahm's win at the 2017 Irish Open - and Castlerock also boast acclaimed links courses in the region.

Reef Destinations has previously worked with TGR Design on course construction projects in the United States. Bellarena already has a historical link with the United States as a site of a US Army base during the Second World War.

Woods is poised to return to competitive golf next month for the first time in over two years after being confirmed as an entrant at the Hero World Challenge in the Bahamas from December 3-6.

The 50-year-old has not played competitively since The Open at Royal Troon in 2024 and has played limited golf since a career-threatening car crash in Florida in 2021.

Woods has had at least three different surgeries since his last start and spent time at a rehab facility in Switzerland earlier this year, having been arrested and charged with driving under the influence following a car crash near his home.
