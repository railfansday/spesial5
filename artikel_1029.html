Costa Rica, ranked 54th in the world, also arrive in Córdoba on the back of a friendly defeat against South American opposition. They were beaten 2-0 by Ecuador in Quito on Friday night, having won their previous two matches against Nicaragua and Haiti in the CONCACAF Nations League.

Los Ticos are now coached by 41-year-old Bryan Ruiz, the former attacking midfielder who spent several seasons with Fulham and Sporting CP. He won 147 caps for Costa Rica, making him one of the country's most-capped players, behind only Joel Campbell and Celso Borges.

Neither Campbell nor Borges has featured under Ruiz, and neither played under his predecessor, Fernando Batista, who was dismissed in September following a disappointing spell in charge. Ruiz initially took over on an interim basis before being confirmed as head coach in October.

Argentina, meanwhile, are embarking on a new era but will be without injured defender Cristian Romero and Inter Milan striker Lautaro Martínez. The 29-year-old Martínez has been sidelined for the past month.

This will be the eighth meeting between Argentina and Costa Rica, with the three-time world champions unbeaten in their previous seven encounters. The sides last met in a friendly in March 2024, when Argentina secured a 3-1 victory thanks to goals from Ángel Di María, Alexis Mac Allister and Martínez.

Costa Rica have been boosted by Ruiz's involvement but there's a vast difference in quality that we expect to show in the result.
