Open the Capital One Kids Savings Account if you want a simple online account for your child with no monthly fee and no minimum balance. It pays 2.50% APY, the annual percentage yield, on every balance, and your child gets a sign-in to watch it grow. If you’re chasing a higher rate, compare credit union kids’ accounts first.

Capital One’s 2.50% trails the 3.01% that the Alliant Credit Union Kids Savings Account pays on an average daily balance of $100 or more, but each half percentage point of interest is only $5 a year on $1,000. A parent or legal guardian opens the Capital One account online with a child under 18 as co-owners, and it suits families who’d rather have nothing to pay or maintain than chase the highest rate.

A balance under $100 earns 2.50% here and nothing at Alliant, which makes this the better start for a child who saves in small amounts. A family chasing the higher rate on $100 or more should open the child’s account at Alliant instead, for 3.01% rather than 2.50%.
