One of the questions people often ask us is, "How much is a point or mile worth?"

The true answer varies from point to point and person to person. It also depends on your travel goals and how much you maximize a particular loyalty currency. Still, some rewards are worth more than others, and our goal is to give you a sense of how they stack up.

Rove has grown from an intriguing newcomer into one of the most valuable transferable rewards programs we track. So, we've decided to add Rove miles to TPG's valuations at 2.05 cents apiece.

That puts Rove on a par with our valuation of Chase Ultimate Rewards points and behind only Bilt Points as the most valuable transferable currencies we track. Using our updated methodology, we considered the value available when redeeming Rove miles, as well as the number and quality of the Rove transfer partners.

Rove now has an impressive 22 transfer partners, including multiple currencies that have traditionally been difficult for U.S. travelers to earn, such as Japan Airlines Mileage Bank and Lufthansa Miles & More. Most transfers are at a 1:1 rate, and the roster also includes TPG favorites Air Canada Aeroplan, Flying Blue, Qantas Frequent Flyer and Virgin Atlantic Flying Club.

Rove miles are also easy to earn without opening a new credit card. Members can collect miles by booking flights and hotels or shopping online. New members who join through TPG can earn 1,500 bonus miles, then earn another 5,000 miles after making their first hotel booking of more than $500 with code "BRIANKELLY500."

A points and miles valuation estimates how much a loyalty currency is worth, typically expressed in cents per point or mile. TPG uses these valuations to compare rewards programs and help travelers decide when to earn, redeem or transfer rewards.

TPG valuations are estimates based on factors such as award pricing, redemption opportunities, fees, program rules and extensive redemption data. However, the value you get when redeeming your rewards may be higher or lower depending on your goals, flexibility and how effectively you maximize a particular rewards currency.

A good redemption value is one that meets or exceeds TPG's published valuation for a particular points or miles currency. While exceptional redemptions can deliver even greater value, our valuations provide a practical benchmark to help travelers determine whether they're getting a strong return from their rewards.

Based on TPG's November 2026 valuations, Bilt Points are the most valuable transferable rewards currency at 2.2 cents per point. Chase Ultimate Rewards points and Rove miles are tied at 2.05 cents each, followed by American Express Membership Rewards points at 2.0 cents, Citi ThankYou Rewards points at 1.95 cents, Capital One miles at 1.85 cents, Wells Fargo Rewards points at 1.8 cents and U.S. Bank Rewards points at 1.6 cents.

Based on TPG's November 2026 valuations, Avianca Lifemiles is the most valuable airline currency at 1.65 cents per mile, followed by Alaska Airlines Atmos Rewards points at 1.6 cents each. Both programs can offer strong value through partner awards, though pricing, availability and taxes and fees vary by redemption.

Based on TPG's November 2026 valuations, Accor Live Limitless points are the most valuable hotel currency at 2.25 cents per point, followed by World of Hyatt points at 1.65 cents per point. Accor points have a fixed redemption value tied to the euro, while Hyatt's award charts can help members secure outsize value on some hotel stays.
