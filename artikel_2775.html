Carson Beck listed as questionable, but Josh Johnson is expected to start

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Josh Johnson is expected to start Sunday’s game against the Chiefs, but the Cardinals have not ruled out Carson Beck.

The team lists Beck as questionable with back spasms.

Johnson would become the fourth starting quarterback for the Cardinals this season, following Jacoby Brissett, Gardner Minshew and Beck.

Beck was expected to start the rest of the season after making his first start last Sunday. Beck, though, was listed as limited on Thursday’s practice report, and he did not practice Friday.

Beck was sidelined with back spasms earlier this season, too.

The Cardinals ruled out offensive guard Jon Gaines II, who will miss another game with an ankle injury. He returned to practice on Wednesday but did not participate the rest of the week.

Defensive lineman Zach Carter also won’t play. He remains on injured reserve, in his 21-day practice window, as he works his way back from a knee injury.
