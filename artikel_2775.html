Thursday Night Football: C.J. Stroud, Texans lead Colts 17-3 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans are doing what they need to do. Now, can they finish?

Four days after blowing a 17-0 lead over the Browns, Houston leads Indianapolis 17-3 at halftime.

The Texans outgained the Colts 202 to 125 in the first half but settled for Ka’imi Fairbairn’s 27-yard field goal after stalling at the Indianapolis 9 in the closing seconds.

C.J. Stroud went 13-of-18 for 134 yards with two touchdowns and an interception. He now ranks third in team history in passing yards, surpassing David Carr (13,391).

He has 21 touchdown passes, two short of his career high for a season.

Stroud’s touchdowns covered 42 yards to Nico Collins and 24 yards to Kayshon Boutte.

David Montgomery, who missed the previous four games with a groin injury, has 11 carries for 54 yards. Collins has two catches for 48 yards and a touchdown.

The Colts had four plays of over 20 yards, but went 0-for-1 in the red zone, missed two field goals and threw an interception.

The Colts scored their only points on Spencer Shrader’s 34-yard field goal. But he missed from 56 and had a 62-yarder blocked by Logan Hall with 2:41 remaining in the half.

Daniel Jones was only 1-for-5 for 22 yards with an interception in the second quarter. He finished the half 7-of-17 for 109 yards and an interception.

Jonathan Taylor has eight carries for 17 yards.

Texans offensive tackle Trent Brown was questionable to return with a back injury. He had been alternating with Braden Smith at right tackle, but Smith played most of the half. Brown, though, did return before the half ended.

Texans defensive tackle Sheldon Rankins is questionable with an ankle injury.
