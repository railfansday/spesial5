NFL Week 12 expert picks, score predictions: Bears vs. Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After putting up 31 points on the toughest defense they've seen this year, the Detroit Lions have to bounce back quickly for their Thanksgiving game against the Chicago Bears. Despite being the team that has played the most Thanksgiving games in NFL history, it's not a bright spot for the Lions, with a 38-46-2 record on the day. Like 2024, they hope to send Lions fans home happy and asking for the leftovers on the way out.

"So obviously we got a real good opponent coming up. It's a hot team, 8-2 like we are, division opponent," said Lions head coach Dan Campbell. "Ben's (Johnson) done a hell of a job out there. So's DA (Bears defensive coordinator Dennis Allen). I mentioned this before, I know a lot of those coaches obviously we had them on staff here and then a lot of coaches that I coached with at New Orleans when I was there. Dan Roushar O-line coach, certainly DA, so I got a lot of respect for what they do out there."
