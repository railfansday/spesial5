T.J. Paganetti returns to Eagles as a consultant

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Eagles are bringing back a former member of Nick Sirianni’s coaching staff.

Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that T.J. Paganetti is rejoining the Eagles as a consultant. Paganetti was most recently the offensive run game coordinator for the Eagles, but he was fired earlier this month by New Orleans.

Paganetti was the team’s run game specialist and assistant offensive line coach before leaving for New Orleans with Kellen Moore after winning Super Bowl LIX. He first worked for the Eagles in 2013 and 2014 before spending two seasons at Oregon and then returned to Philadelphia in 2017. He had a variety of job titles and focused on the run game throughout his time with the team.

The Eagles are currently averaging 88.6 rushing yards per game, which puts them at No. 28 in the league and one spot behind the Saints.
