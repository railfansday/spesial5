After an extremely busy Friday, which involved non-tendering four players and a trade, the Philadelphia Phillies made another notable move.

According to MLB.com's Jonathan Mayo, the Phillies have signed slugging 25-year-old catcher Ricardo Olivar to a minor-league deal. Olivar can also play left field.

(Photo by Bob Levey/Getty Images)

Via Mayo on X: "Minor League FA signing: @Phillies have signed C/OF Ricardo Olivar, had been No. 30 on @Twins list. Signed for just $20K in July 2019 by #MNTwins, he did reach Triple-A this past year; career .844 OPS; right knee sprain in '26. Might see more time in LF w/#Phillies"

Olivar had been with the Minnesota Twins organization for his entire professional career before signing with the Phillies. He officially elected free agency on Nov. 2.

GettyFORT MYERS, FLORIDA – FEBRUARY 19: Ricardo Olivar #83 of the Minnesota Twins poses for a photo during Spring Training photo days at Lee Health Sports Complex on February 19, 2026 in Fort Myers, Florida. (Photo by Chris Graythen/Getty Images)

The Twins signed Olivar on July 2, 2019. He is from Barquisimeto, Venezuela.

Olivar reached Double-A for the first time in his career in 2024.

GettyFORT MYERS, FLORIDA – FEBRUARY 19: Ricardo Olivar #83 of the Minnesota Twins poses for a photo during Spring Training photo days at Lee Health Sports Complex on February 19, 2026 in Fort Myers, Florida. (Photo by Chris Graythen/Getty Images)

After spending the entire 2025 season and the start of the 2026 season in Double-A, he earned a promotion to Triple-A in 2026.

Unfortunately, due to his knee sprain, Olivar appeared in just 39 games this year.

GettyFORT MYERS, FLORIDA – FEBRUARY 19: Ricardo Olivar #83 of the Minnesota Twins poses for a photo during Spring Training photo days at Lee Health Sports Complex on February 19, 2026 in Fort Myers, Florida. (Photo by Chris Graythen/Getty Images)

In his limited action in 2026, Olivar slashed a remarkable .274/.348/.603 with 12 home runs,12 doubles and 42 RBI.

Over his minor-league career, Olivar has slashed .279/.381/.463 with 53 home runs, 86 doubles, 238 RBI and 42 stolen bases in 409 games.

GettyFORT MYERS, FLORIDA – FEBRUARY 19: Ricardo Olivar #83 of the Minnesota Twins poses for a photo during Spring Training photo days at Lee Health Sports Complex on February 19, 2026 in Fort Myers, Florida. (Photo by Chris Graythen/Getty Images)

As Mayo noted, Olivar may see more time at left field than catcher with the Phillies organization.

He logged 173 2/3 innings at catcher and 60 at left field this year.

GettyATLANTA, GEORGIA – SEPTEMBER 30: J.T. Realmuto #10 and Andrew Painter #24 of the Philadelphia Phillies celebrate a win over the Atlanta Brave in game two of the National League Wild Card Series at Truist Park on September 30, 2026 in Atlanta, Georgia. (Photo by Kevin C. Cox/Getty Images)

The Phillies had a rough start to the season, leading to the firing of manager Rob Thomson, but turned things around after Don Mattingly took over, finishing the year with a 88-74 record.

As the final NL Wild Card team, the Phillies played the Atlanta Braves at Truist Park in the Wild Card round. The Braves won the series 2-1.
