Handing out grades for Notre Dame after knocking off No. 1 Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

SOUTH BEND —A desperate Notre Dame football team held off top-ranked Miami 31-24 in a must-win scenario on Saturday night Nov. 7.

The Irish (8-1), ranked 11th in the initial College Football Playoff rankings, left no doubt on this night as to their continued worthiness in the postseason discussion.

Here's how the South Bend Tribune graded Notre Dame:

CJ Carr ripped Miami's porous secondary for 205 first-half air yards on 18-of-26 passing and two scores. Carr still wasn't his pinpoint self, but he spread the ball around to 10 different receivers even with Jordan Faison and Micah Gilbert drawing the bulk of his attention. Ground yards were hard to come by against a top-3 rushing defense.

Facing a Miami offense without leading receiver Malachi Toney (ankle), the injury-riddled Irish held Darian Mensah to 74 first-half passing yards. Despite entering as the fourth-best red zone offense in FBS play, Miami settled for a first-half field goal at point-blank range. Luke Talich's seventh career interception, tying Leonard Moore for the active team lead, cooled off Mensah after he heated up with Leonard Moore out of the game.

Jasper Sciafe killed a punt at the Miami 3 and Spencer Porath nailed a 44-yard field goal to open the scoring. Porath later missed badly from 53 with a chance to put the game away. Cooper Barkate filled in for Toney as the primary punt returner but was never a factor. Freshman Micah Drescher yanked his first two kickoffs of the second half out of bounds. That's three in the past two games.

Miami's Mario Cristobal was unable to join Ohio State's Ryan Day (3-0) as the only coach with multiple wins over Marcus Freeman. Maligned defensive coordinator Chris Ash won the chess match this time with Hurricanes offensive coordinator Shannon Dawson. The Irish converted an early fourth down but wisely took the points when the drive later stalled.

This was the so-called "signature win" CFP committee chairman Hunter Yurachek said the Irish were lacking. There will be a new No. 1 next week, and the Irish should vault ahead of Miami and two-loss BYU.
