Forever Young to Take Final Bow in Japan’s Dirt Showpiece, Trainer Confirms

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The horse racing world is preparing to say goodbye to a true global titan.

Yoshito Yahagi, the legendary trainer of Japan's trailblazing dirt superstar, Forever Young (JPN), has officially confirmed the venue for the champion's final career start.

Following months of speculation and a globetrotting campaign that has seen him conquer the world's richest races, the five-year-old son of Real Steel will return home for a spectacular farewell.

He is set to make his 18th and final appearance in the Grade 1 Tokyo Daishōten at Oi Racecourse on December 29, 2026.

The announcement signals the end of an era for Japanese dirt racing. Forever Young has been more than just a champion; he was an ambassador, proving on multiple continents that Japan can produce dirt horses of the highest international calibre.

"Our main priority is always the horse's condition, but after careful consideration, the entire 'Team Forever Young' is unanimous in this decision," Yahagi said in a statement released to Japanese media. "We wanted to give his incredibly loyal Japanese fans one final chance to see him race on home soil. He is a champion of the world, but he started here, and we want him to finish here."

The Tokyo Daishōten, contested over 2,000 meters (approximately 1 1/4 miles) on the dirt, is the traditional year-end showpiece for Japanese dirt racing. For Forever Young, it is a chance to add one last exclamation point to a career defined by unprecedented achievement.

He already won the race in 2024 as a three-year-old.

The decision to target the Tokyo Daishōten means bypassing other major international targets that were naturally on the table for a horse of his quality.

Given his stellar record, the team could have aimed elsewhere:

A Third Saudi Cup Bid (2027): He was the first horse to win consecutive runnings of the $20 million Saudi Cup (2025, 2026). A potential historic triple bid in Riyadh was a possibility.

The Dubai World Cup (2027): He finished a valiant second in 2026 to Magnitude. A return to Meydan Racecourse to try and claim the one desert jewel that eluded him was certainly considered.

American Older Dirt Male Campaign (2027): After winning the Jockey Club Gold Cup (G1) in 2026, there was always the allure of another extended American campaign to defend his title as the 2025 American Champion Older Dirt Male.

"There are always temptations when you have a horse this special," Yahagi admitted, "but we feel the time is right. We want him to retire healthy and at the peak of his powers. The Tokyo Daishōten is the perfect, honourable conclusion."

Forever Young will retire to the prestigious Shadai Stallion Station for the 2027 breeding season, where he will be expected to pass on his immense talent to the next generation of Japanese champions.

A trailblazing global superstar who redefined the possibilities for Japanese dirt horses.
