Pistons at Hawks final score: Detroit outscored by 21 in the fourth, lose 107-97

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

That was an unbelievable collapse by Detroit, and quite possibly the worst game of Cade Cunningham's career.

The Pistons went into the fourth quarter with an 85-74 lead. They outscored the Hawks by 10 in the third quarter, and ESPN had Detroit at a 90% chance to win the game when the final frame started.

That went downhill quickly as the Hawks went on an 18-0 run to open the fourth quarter. The Pistons didn't score their first points until Isaiah Joe hit a corner three with 4:52 left in the game. Detroit's offense stalled, the ball wasn't moving, and for whatever reason, Cade Cunningham and Daniss Jenkins took it upon themselves to try to iso Dyson Daniels and Lu Dort. As you can tell, it didn't work.

Detroit shot better from the field, the three-point line, and the free-throw line. They even scored more points in the paint than the Hawks, but couldn't manage to grab a rebound. Atlanta grabbed 19 more rebounds than Detroit and outrebounded the Pistons on the offensive glass 24-8. If there was ever a game that showed Detroit needed Jalen Duren back ASAP, this was it.

Atlanta had 10 more fastbreak points than Detroit, and second-chance points went in a massive favor of the Hawks to the tune of 27-6. Detroit's rebounding was horrendous – outside of Ausar Thompson's 11 boards, John Collins was second on the team with six.

Cade Cunningham scored in single digits for the first time all season with five points while shooting 2-for-9 from the field. He also added four rebounds and 15 assists in what is surely one of the weirdest stat lines of his career. Kevin Huerter was a -28 on the floor while shooting 1-for-8 for a measly two points.

Oddly enough, it was Isaiah Joe who led the team in scoring with 19 points on 15 attempts. Joe was +11 tonight and knocked down three threes. John Collins and Ausar Thompson each had 18 points. Collins made four threes, and Ausar added five assists. Paul Reed had 16 points off the bench, and Daniss Jenkins had 11.

In what should've been a victory tonight in Atlanta, Detroit blew their double-digit lead and deserved to lose this game. That was one of the worst fourth quarter performances I've ever watched, and it looked like a middle school team that couldn't grab a rebound in a scrimmage against the high school varsity.

Detroit moves to 10-6 on the year and will now travel to Orlando to take on the Magic on Tuesday night. Let's hope this Pistons team has a short memory and burns tonight's fourth quarter tape.
