Broncos activated TE Caleb Lohner from the IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

According to 9NEWS Denver's Mike Klis, the Denver Broncos have activated tight end Caleb Lohner from the injured reserve. Lohner will be at practice today, and this opens his 21-day practice window, during which the Broncos have to decide whether or not to add him to the active roster or keep him on the injured reserve for the remainder of the year.

Lohner, whom the Broncos drafted in the 7th round of the 2025 NFL Draft, earned some early offseason hype due to his performance during the rookie minicamp. Head coach Sean Payton went out of his way to praise Lohner, and had fans anxious to see what he could do throughout the remainder of the offseason program and then training camp/preseason. However, he suffered a lower-body injury during the summer and has been sidelined ever since.

It remains to be seen if Lohner will be added to the 53-man roster and will present the Broncos with a potential wildcard who could contribute to the offense as they make a push for the number one seed in the AFC. The team hasn't had much production from the tight end position, and Lohner is an unknown with elite size and untapped potential as a receiver. Sean Payton has spoken highly of Lohner since he was drafted, so we will have to see what happens with him moving forward.

Right now, the Broncos have veterans Adam Trautman and Evan Engram as the main two tight ends in the offense. Third-string tight end and blocking specialist Nate Adkins leads all tight ends (and the team) with 3 touchdowns but only has 6 receptions in total so far this season. Meanwhile, 7th-round rookie Dallen Bentley made the team, but has been a weekly inactive so far this season. So, there is room for Lohner to make an impact in this positional group if he can show some upside during this 21-day practice window.

Realistically, with his raw background and time missed, we probably will not see any meaningful impact from Lohner this season. However, it would be nice if they gave the Broncos a 6-7 red zone threat for quarterback Bo Nix and the Broncos' offense.
