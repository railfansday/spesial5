NFL Week 11 betting: 5 biggest storylines, including favorites dominating and some huge point spreads

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Los Angeles Chargers are on the board.

The Chargers weren't just the last NFL team to win a game this season; they were the last team that had not covered the spread. They checked off both boxes on Monday night by beating the Ravens as 4.5-point underdogs. The Chargers and the Philadelphia Eagles are the only teams that have covered the spread just once this season.

Here are the biggest betting storylines for Week 11 of the NFL season:

The NFL season became chalky all of a sudden.

For the second straight week, favorites dominated. In Week 9 favorites were 14-1 straight up and 11-3-1 against the spread. Then in Week 10 it almost repeated, with favorites going 10-3 straight up and 9-4 against the spread going into Monday night's game. The Chargers scored one for the underdogs on Monday night with a win over the Ravens.

Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs were one of the favorites that dominated in Week 10.

Part of the reason for the favorites suddenly dominating is there are more than a few teams that are helplessly out of the playoff race already. We saw that at the trade deadline. The contenders got stronger, with many first- and second-round picks being moved for players. That also meant the also-rans got worse.

Oddsmakers will adjust spreads. We can see that a bit in some huge Week 11 spreads. But we're likely to see the gap between the best and worst teams widen. We'll see if the point spreads keep up with that trend.

Anyone still alive in a survival pool will have multiple options to choose from in Week 11.

There are five double-digit favorites in Week 11. There are the two biggest spreads of the NFL season included in that group. The Chiefs are 16-point favorites over the Cardinals, who are quarterbacked by rookie Carson Beck. The Bills are 16.5-point favorites over the Dolphins, who are quarterbacked by Kyle McCord.

It's projected to be a lopsided week in the NFL, though that's often when we see chaos.

With another big game on Sunday night, Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow took over as the clear favorite in the NFL MVP race.

Burrow is -115 to win MVP, with Josh Allen now trailing at +350. The other big change is that Jordan Love went from the top five of the odds to +6000, making it a four-man race, according to oddsmakers.

Burrow has been on fire and his next two games are against the Commanders and Saints, who don't have great defenses.

We get a fantastic matchup for Sunday night in Week 11. It's a showdown between a 49ers team leading the NFL in offensive EPA (expected points added) and the Vikings, who lead the NFL in defensive EPA. Oddsmakers prefer the 49ers, who are at home. They're 3-point favorites, and even -3.5 at some sportsbooks.

The 49ers have had a remarkable season, and the Vikings looked rejuvenated with Kirk Cousins taking over at quarterback in Week 10. It could be a playoff preview on Sunday night.

The Lions lost defensive end Aidan Hutchinson to a torn ACL on Sunday. They were already without linebacker Jack Campbell. Even though the Lions are without their two best defensive players, the betting market doesn't think there will be a big drop.

The Lions are 6.5-point favorites in Week 11 over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, who are coming off a bye. And they are strong favorites to still make the playoffs in a crowded NFC wild-card race. The odds for the Lions to make the playoffs at BetMGM are -525. The odds on them missing the playoffs are +400. Apparently the betting market believes the Lions can continue to outscore everyone.
