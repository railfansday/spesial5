LaunchDarkly: Executive Assistant

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

LaunchDarkly is seeking an Executive Assistant to partner closely with our Chief Revenue Officer and support the broader global Revenue organization as it scales.

This role will help the CRO stay focused on the highest-priority work by managing their calendar, travel, meetings, and competing priorities. The right person will anticipate needs, resolve scheduling conflicts, and make thoughtful decisions about what requires the CRO’s attention.

The Revenue organization spans Sales, Customer Success, pre- and post-sales, Partnerships, and international markets. As the organization grows, the EA may also provide administrative support to other senior Revenue leaders. The right person will be comfortable working across time zones, navigating senior stakeholders, handling confidential information, and learning the CRO’s priorities and working style.
