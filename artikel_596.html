SAVE $90: As of Nov. 17, the Nothing Headphone (a) wireless ANC headphones are on sale for $109 at Amazon, down from their $199 list price — a 45% discount and their lowest price ever, in all four colorways.

Finding a pair of mid-range headphones that stands out from a sea of identical models is rare enough, especially when they outperform a brand's own flagship model. Ahead of Black Friday, Nothing's retro-styled headphones (a) are down to $109 at Amazon — saving you $90 off their $199 list price. This is a brand-new record low across all four colorways: black, white, pink, and yellow.
