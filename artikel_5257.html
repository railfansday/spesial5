Bitcoin price faces $297M liquidation wave – Why $70K is at risk

The crypto markets saw another wave of liquidations. In the past 24 hours, Bitcoin [BTC] and Ethereum [ETH] saw $297.17 million and $491.97 million in liquidations, respectively.

Only 7.9% and 7.8% of those liquidations were short positions, as Bitcoin fell 3.20% and Ethereum plummeted 7.90% within hours.

In recent weeks, the Binance heist of $6.3 billion severely affected BTC price action. The Mercado Bitcoin security incident also impacted market sentiment, and the loss of a key onchain support level was a warning of a deeper correction.

In a post on X, the Kobeissi Letter detailed how the S&P 500 Futures had erased an implied $300 billion in market cap a minute after the 6.00 PM ET open. Under ten minutes later, all losses were erased as traders bought the dip.

Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang had admitted to a “relationship” with a Chinese spy, which helped stage a quickly undone correction, the global capital markets commentary wrote.

The fall in recent hours reached a low of $73.5k. Sentiment was already weak, and the swift liquidation cascade has likely made market participants more risk-averse.

The reaction of the S&P 500 suggested buyer strength, which could translate into strong crypto demand later, during the New York session open.

AMBCrypto reported that Bitcoin prices had fallen below the true market mean at $77.6k recently. Now, it was also trading below the 50-week Moving Average (MA).

Head of Research at Galaxy, Alex Thorn, pointed out that BTC has only slipped below its 50-week MA twice, after reclaiming it as a post-bear bottom. In neither of those times, which were in 2012 and 2019-20, did BTC descend to new weekly lows.

The weekly structure was bullish, following the close above $82,850. The weekly low in question is at $57.8k, which Thorn pointed out was not at risk, based on the historical 50-week MA loss following a recovery from a bear market.

At the time of writing, the momentum was bearish, and a price drop below $70k was feasible. The $65k-$66k area would represent a deep discount opportunity for buyers. In the coming days, more losses appeared likely.
