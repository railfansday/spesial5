West Ham are having the last laugh as big Millwall controversy emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It's rare to see players cross a fierce rival divide to play for both clubs. In Benicio Baker-Boaitey's situation, he never signed professional contracts for both West Ham United and Millwall, but that was his decision.

The 22-year-old is currently under contract at the Den, but came through in the academy of Dockers Derby rivals West Ham. He was a highly coveted star as a teenager, and rejected professional terms to sign for Porto in 2020.

Rejecting terms after coming through your academy can often lead to a fanbase souring on you from the off, and when he joined Millwall from Brighton and Hove Albion in the winter window back in 2025, some Lions fans revelled in the prospect of seeing him flourish with them, instead of at the London Stadium.

That's not been the case, though, and as controversy emerges, Hammers fans are probably happy they got rid of Baker-Boaitey when they did.

It's been nearly two years now since Baker-Boaitey signed for Millwall. He arrived less than a month after his 20th birthday and was an example of the new transfer strategy at the Den, signing younger, promising players to flip for profit.

The 22-year-old's time at the Den, however, has been nothing short of a disaster. He's yet to make his debut for the club, wasn't included in their official EFL squad list for the ongoing campaign, and has now taken to social media to air his displeasure over the whole situation.

Posting to his Instagram story, Baker-Boaitey said, "On my mumma, can't even train with the 21s," before adding more stories and even changing his profile picture to Alex Neil briefly. Now, it's completely blacked out.

Neil is highly regarded as one of the more demanding coaches in the EFL, incredibly strict and not someone who seems to be ready to compromise over much. This, ultimately, is going to lead to some decisions that some simply won't like, and you can't help but feel for the 22-year-old, who must be feeling pretty isolated at his current club.

That being said, airing this online is likely to cause more problems than it will solve, and if there were any route back to the Millwall side for Baker-Boaitey, he'd likely have burnt that bridge with his actions.

Baker-Boaitey was so highly rated as a 16-year-old that, before deciding to move to Portuguese giants Porto after rejecting professional terms at West Ham, he was being courted by Bayern Munich.

Whenever any youngster has links with those types of clubs, you always worry that they'll go on to big things and create some real envy, as it could have happened to you.

Even when he returned to England to sign with Brighton, famed for their youth development, West Ham would have expected the wideman to excel either at the Amex or elsewhere. Ultimately, that's not been the case.

Injuries have more than played their part. A loan spell at Port Vale during the 2024/25 campaign in League Two showed glimpses of quality, but he only played 353 league minutes before being sent back in January amidst troubles staying fit.

It was later that window that he was sold to Millwall, and he hasn't seen the pitch since.

For Millwall fans in 2025, there wasn't much of an opportunity to get bragging rights over West Ham fans.

The two hadn't shared a division since the 2011/12 campaign, and while they themselves were showing signs of improvement, they weren't exactly banging down the door of the Premier League just yet.

So, when the opportunity came to sign a young winger who had rejected the chance to go professional at the London Stadium and, less than five years later, saw his future at the Den, it would have been a big deal.

Now, though, they're regretting signing Baker-Boaitey at all, and those fans who would have used the 22-year-old's arrival as a way to try and get one over on West Ham fans must be feeling quite sheepish right now.

Right now, it doesn't look like Baker-Boaitey is allowed anywhere near Millwall training, regardless of which team it's with. It's an awkward situation to be a part of, and Lions fans will hope that it doesn't prove to be a distraction on the field for them.

It's been a tumultuous campaign so far for Alex Neil's side, summed up perfectly by them following up a 4-1 win over Derby County last Saturday by going 3-0 down within half an hour against Birmingham City on Tuesday night.

There'll still be some expectation that last season's third-placed side can put a run together and climb into the top eight at the very least, but at the moment, things aren't looking as smooth as they could be.

Meanwhile, West Ham haven't had much ground to stand on in terms of bragging rights over their rivals so far this season. Even if they do sit above Millwall in the Championship, they too feel that this year has been a bit of a disappointing one so far.

But they'll be able to have a chuckle at what's happening between Baker-Baoaitey and Alex Neil, and breathe a sigh of relief that their former academy graduate isn't displaying this attitude at their club.
