Birmingham City's £2.2m signing has just given Chris Davies a major headache

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Birmingham City manager Chris Davies has hailed the influence of Max Bird as his side made it four wins from five Championship matches with a convincing 3-0 success over Millwall at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

Having returned to winning ways with a 3-1 success at Lincoln City on Saturday, Birmingham continued their unbeaten run on home turf, with all three goals coming before the half-hour mark.

Alex Cochrane netted his second in as many games with a powerful drive after just 46 seconds, replicating his fine finish against the Lions 12 months ago in the process, before Bird was given acres of space to drill his first goal for the club home on 16 minutes.

Jay Stansfield then netted his second of the season 13 minutes later from Bird's quick thinking, allowing Birmingham to cruise through the second period and up to second in the Championship ahead of Wednesday's remaining fixtures.

Much was made of how Blues were going to perform this campaign given a quieter summer transfer window, as Bird was just one of seven arrivals in B9, joining from Bristol City for a reported £2.2m.

Tuesday's encounter was also his first start since moving from the West Country side, and he certainly made quite the impression on Davies and those in the stands.

Davies' team selection certainly generated interest, with Bird replacing August Priske in the starting lineup, whilst the Dane was omitted from the squad altogether due to a minor knock.

As such, the 26-year-old featured in the '10' role behind Stansfield, and such a decision paid dividends as Birmingham swept Alex Neil's team aside from the get-go.

Having assisted Demarai Gray in the 4-0 rout over Preston North End, Bird marked his full debut with a remarkable performance and his first goal, with Davies paying compliments to the midfielder's 'football brain' post-match.

"As a coach it’s lovely to work with really bright, intelligent players," said Davies. “In little exercises and training sessions you do, he’s tuned in straight away. He knows exactly what tactically you’re wanting."

“I think that’s a real midfield player. He’s thinking about the game, the positioning, the space and everything else," he added. “He’s played as a six, an eight and a 10 so he’s got a real understanding of all the roles. He’s an intelligent lad."

“What he’s got as well is legs. You can be intelligent and technical but maybe not have the other side, but he can do that side of it as well and that’s critical in this team because of how aggressive we are in the press.”

Bird's technical quality at Championship level has been known for many years, having excelled for both the Robins and Derby County before returning to Birmingham this summer, having spent time in the club's academy setup.

Despite sustaining an early-season leg injury, the playmaker has already won plenty of fans over with his contributions.

"If last night's performance from Max Bird is just the start, I cannot wait to watch more of him. Top drawer of a first start for the club," said one thrilled Bluenose.

Another stated: "Blues were so dominant in that first half. Several stand out performances last night, but Max Bird was unplayable at times. If we can keep him fit, we’ve got a serious player on our hands."

"Max Bird was unbelievable today. Haven’t seen a midfielder run a game like that for a while. I didn’t realise how much of a powerful runner he is. When he gets going he looks impossible to tackle," wrote another extremely impressed supporter.

Another summation of the midfielder's impact was: "Max Bird is like a conductor with the three dancers playing off him."

Last season was one of mixed fortunes for Birmingham, as despite finishing 10th after sauntering to the League One title, more was expected on the outside given Knighthead's continued investment.

However, Davies' side are certainly much more complete and efficient this term, and with goals and assists coming from all across the squad, they have become a force to be reckoned with.

Although there are still many games to go, the fact they have reached the Championship's automatic promotion places for the first time under the 41-year-old will be a major confidence boost ahead of Saturday's eye-catching clash with Swansea City.
