El Niño created a skiers' paradise in Chile—now it's Sierra Nevada and southern Rockies' turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

El Niño has a reputation for being a skier's dream, bringing lots of snow to California's Sierra Nevada and the southern Rockies. Tahoe-area resorts were already seeing the effects in mid-November 2026 as several feet of snow fell and some resorts prepared to open early for the season. But El Niño's supercharged precipitation can spell trouble, too.
