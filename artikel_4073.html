Rockstar’s Rob Nelson Explains Why You Can’t Wield GTA 6's Chainsaws

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

GTA 6 is being celebrated for more reasons every day, and rightfully so. However, the most striking thing about the game, since it’s the first thing you see and it’s hitting you over the head every second you’re looking at it, is how stunning it is. That realism is one of the reasons why the option to make Jason and Lucia hyper-violent is taken out of the player’s hands.

Rockstar producer Rob Nelson revealed the level of thinking that went into not only some of the endless things you can do in Leonida, but also some of the things you can’t in an interview with VGC. One of the things Nelson discussed was the decision to not include weapons that would allow for a heightened level of violence that have been present in previous GTA entries, like chainsaws and katanas.

Despite a mountain of leaks, Rockstar managed to keep one of GTA 6’s biggest plot points a secret throughout all of its development.

“As far as the chainsaw – I think it would have been too much for this game at this resolution and these characters,” Nelson explains, adding, “It’s not a resource thing.” Other omissions, such as not being able to play games like darts or bowling, were confirmed to have been left on the cutting room floor because Rockstar simply didn’t think it was worth the time and effort when those resources could be put to better use elsewhere.

The realism that you get with GTA 6 means even standard stuff that has to be in the games, like shooting someone or hitting them with a blunt object, is far more visceral than it has ever been before. When coupled with how much more grounded GTA 6’s lead characters are, giving them the option to carve someone up with a chainsaw just doesn’t fit.

“Even at the most psycho, did Jason and Lucia really need that – what’s that going to look like?” Nelson continues, adding, “You’re always trying to find what is the right sort of line and the right balance, it really just depends, you want to feel like you can interact with as much of the world as possible,” when asked about chainsaws existing in Leonida, but Rockstar choosing not to let players interact with them at all.

It’s a tricky line to walk. A huge part of GTA has always been about players doing whatever they like, and going “psycho,” as Nelson puts it. Once I was done with GTA San Andreas's story, I input cheats to give pedestrians weapons and then made them riot. The whole point of that game for me after that was for it to be as chaotic and unrealistic as possible.

That was a long time ago. Sure, GTA has always been violent, but its violence has never been realised with the same realism as it is in GTA 6. Not just the way it looks, but the way you play and the repercussions that come along with everything you do. Jason and Lucia are the closest the series has ever seen to protagonists that act like real people. They’re not caricatures like the leads in previous GTA games. They might be criminals, but as Nelson says, they’re not psychos who are going to fight their way out of a situation with a chainsaw. It wouldn’t make sense for them, and it no longer makes sense for GTA.
