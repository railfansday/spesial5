Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's Narrative Lead Jennifer Svedberg-Yen Leaves Sandfall Interactive

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Although GTA 6 is set to sweep at The Game Awards this year, even Rockstar's long-awaited RPG doesn't have as many nominations as Sandfall Interactive's record-breaking Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 had last year. While the unique take on the turn-based RPG genre certainly helped, Expedition 33's staggering sales figures are also closely tied to its emotionally gripping narrative.

Even though Sandfall Interactive is still hard at work on a mysterious new game to follow up the release of Expedition 33, a recent announcement confirms that one of the key creators involved in its story has left the studio and won't be coming back for any future entries.

As reported by Eurogamer, Sandfall Interactive's narrative lead Jennifer Svedberg-Yen announced shortly after accepting a BAFTA for her work on Expedition 33 that she was no longer working with the development studio.

“When the dust settles, we will start thinking about DLC, but it's probably not going to be for a while.”

According to Jennifer herself, she had actually left the studio all the way back "in July, after wrapping up some final stuff for Expedition." Despite not giving a reason as to parting ways with the studio, Jennifer did have some ambitious goals for what she wants to work on next.

On top of Jennifer's plans to write "my own projects, my own stories," she also shared her goal of creating a fund to support up-and-coming narrative designers for video games, aiming to "bridge the gap between the seed stage and going to publishers."

Although Sandfall Interactive hasn't exactly been shy about the fact that Expedition 33's sequel isn't going to be an exact copy of the style of the original, the narrative lead's departure only emphasizes how much different the next game in the series will likely be.

Combined with the core principle of the studio being "its own personal taste" that Jeniffer notes won't "bow to pressure from fans to do more of the same," it sounds like Sandfall Interactive's Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 continuation or entirely new titles will still have plenty of surprises in store for open-minded fans while still keeping what made the original so spectacular.
