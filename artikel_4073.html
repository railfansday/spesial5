Video: Sonic's 'Memories And Beyond' 35th Anniversary Animation Released

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Can Sonic and friends defeat Metal Sonic?

Earlier this year, Sega announced a new Sonic the Hedgehog 35th anniversary animation short, titled ' Memories and Beyond'. Now, months on, it's been officially released.

It runs for around 13 minutes and has a story by Takashi Iizuka and written by Ian Flynn. It sees Sonic and friends try to stop Dr. Eggman from harnessing the power of the Chaos Emeralds to upgrade Metal Sonic into the "ultimate fighting machine".

This passion project has been in the making for the last 12 months, and is the fourth collab with GSX productions. It follows Sonic X Shadow Generations: Dark Beginnings, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds: The Animation and Shinobi: The Art of Vengeance DLC Animated Trailer.

To celebrate the launch of this new animation, the Sega Shop is also releasing a special-edition 'Memories and Beyond' t-shirt, featuring artwork inspired by the animation. Metal Sonic will also be appearing in the upcoming Sonic the Hedgehog 4 live-action movie, arriving in cinemas next year.
