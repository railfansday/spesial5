Lego And TT Games Seemingly Tease A New Lego James Bond Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Lego games have been a part of many gamers' childhoods, but TT Games has more than proven in recent years that their family-friendly gaming adaptations can be enjoyed by just about anyone regardless of their age. While rumors have been circulating about what the next Lego gaming project would be after it was leaked to be a Warner Bros. property, some recent teases have fans thinking otherwise.

While it's far from an official confirmation of any kind, both TT Games and Lego themselves chiming in on the topic already has fans fully convinced that a previously cancelled Lego game could be getting the modern-day return it desperately deserves.

First spotted by CrazyLegoMoment on X, a suspicious series of tweets was made after it was announced Robert Pattinson would be taking the mantle of James Bond moving forward, starting with TT Games responding with a pair of eyes emoji when Shai Matheson (voice of Batman in Lego Batman: Legacy of The Dark Knight) joked that he would be "the next Lego Bond".

As if that wasn't enough, even the official Lego account responded by saying "he's got a license to build" when another fan posted about wanting an official Lego James Bond game made by TT Games.

Legacy of the Dark Knight has clocked up impressive enough review scores to make it the highest-rated Lego game ever.

While these could certainly just be some members of the studios trying to join in on the viral sensation and memes coming out of the news, an official announcement wouldn't be much of a surprise considering TT Games had already pitched several James Bond-themed Lego games in the past, one of which saw its entire pitch trailer leaked last year to the excitement of countless fans.

Having supposedly rejected TT Games' take on Bond for the subject material being "too mature" for the Lego in the past, the company has since branched out to countless adult franchises for official sets and even triple-A games like Legacy of The Dark Knight that Bond would feel right at home with for a full-blown adaptation.

Combined with the unique gameplay mechanics, open-world, and sheer level of detail offered by TT Games' previous Legacy of the Dark Knight and Skywalker Saga, a standalone Lego James Bond game would be a perfect fit with countless vehicles, gadgets, and costumes to experiment with.

With 27 films over the past 60 years to pull from, a Lego James Bond game isn't exactly starved for potential when it comes to levels and characters either, which would also offer the perfect tie-in game for whenever Robert Pattinson's upcoming Bond film releases.
