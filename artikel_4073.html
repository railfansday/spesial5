How To Complete The Final Flush In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You can get a free stamina upgrade in GTA 6 by helping out a conspiracy theorist, but there's a dark easter egg to uncover after you're done, too.

I'm convinced that Lucia is a clone in Grand Theft Auto 6 — at least, in my playthrough. If you happen on a little side quest called The Final Flush, where you help a conspiracy theorist uncover the secret machinations of Big Sewage, things get a little weird. Weird enough that yes, I really am convinced Lucia is a clone. Just don't let the FIB hear me posting about that on Snapmatic, yeah?

In all seriousness, this is one of those oddities in GTA 6 where you don't really understand what's happening, things veer a little X-Files, and then you're handed a permanent stamina upgrade for your troubles as though you haven't been wading through literal crap.

Like all good encounters, you start The Final Flush by scanning a QR code on a handwritten poster by a toilet that screams "STOP! DON'T FLUSH." I also found the same poster in the storage compartment of a pawn shop, but if you want a reliable way to begin the mission instead of going on a Vice City crime spree until you happen across one, head to the north of Corsair Key in Mariana County and enter the only building on the entire island - the toilet.

Use the WaiNK IT app to scan the QR code (#NOFLUSHERS) and you'll get a message asking you to reply "TRUTH" to join the movement. If you decide to embrace your inner tinfoil hat, you'll get a follow-up message telling you to meet the sender at Frank's Waffles in Vice City, whereupon arriving you'll need to repeat the code: 00-00-02.

Drive to Frank's Waffles, and you'll see an older man muttering to himself at a booth by the toilets. This is the colorful Dr. Merle, a conspiracy theorist with an ingenious plan: swallow a tracker, poop it out, and follow it to the source.

Of course, as Lucia rightfully points out (you can also do this mission with Jason), he could've just thrown the tracker in the toilet and saved himself the trouble. But he waffles about how Node is too smart for that, and that he needs to be stealthier.

If you return to Frank's Waffles and check the toilet later, well, I decided not to include the screenshot.

The point is, supposedly, Big Sewage is harvesting Leonidan DNA through their poop to make clones of its citizens, and Dr. Merle wants to get to the bottom of it.

Immediately after depositing the tracker, Dr. Merle will ask you to drive his van as he yells directions at you. You can take a guess at where the tracker in the sewage pipes leads — the sewage plant.

Just make sure you have the compass enabled, as you won't get a trail on your mini-map to follow, only sporadic directions from Dr. Merle as he tells you to drive east, north, and in whatever direction the pipes are leading.

Eventually, you will get close enough that a waypoint appears on the beach. Park the van on the coast and follow Dr. Merle on foot along the fence, where he'll start lockpicking a gate to get you inside.

Dr. Merle will ask you to keep watch while he very slowly works a lock that Lucia and Jason could pick in seconds. You can't really do much though, because it's a chain-link fence that security can easily see through.

So, when a van pulls up asking what exactly you're doing, select 'Defuse' to tell the worker you're fixing the gate. They won't believe you, but it will buy you enough time to get inside and start sneaking about.

Once inside, head to the pink area marked on your map. You can either talk to the workers and lie that you're electricians, or sneak past them through the double doors into the adjacent building (like with all interiors, it's marked by a darker shade of blue).

Here, Dr. Merle will break down and ramble about how close he got to finding out the truth, only to be thwarted by concrete, as the tracker is right below your feet. This is where things take a turn. His poop-laden tracker isn't just lurking in underground pipes, there's a secret facility.

Head to the balcony overseeing the room where there's a control panel with three buttons. Turn all the lights on the panel green (press the bottom two) and then interact with the main lever. Right where Dr. Merle is sitting, a secret staircase will be revealed leading to a strange chamber.

Naturally, like with any conspiracy theorist worth his salt, Dr. Merle puts his idea to the test and climbs into the chamber. It quickly fills with a strange, viscous green liquid, and he asks you — Jason or Lucia — to press the button on the control panel and quite literally flush him. Do it.

The room blares with the sound of the agitated water authority warning you that the police are en route and that the chamber is toxic and should not be entered. But if you want a permanent stamina upgrade, I'm afraid you have to get inside and follow Dr. Merle's example.

You'll enter first-person while it fills with that strange green liquid again, and bizarrely, you wake up outside your safehouse, unclear how you got there, stamina upgrade in hand.

Okay, I'm not only convinced that my Lucia is a clone. I have proof. And this is where the guide takes a turn. After completing The Big Flush, you can find a disturbing little easter egg revealing what exactly happened in the chamber.

Head north to Lummox County and travel to the eastern island. Make sure you don't stumble onto Hyde Island, or you'll end up being hunted for sport.

Near the tip, there's a sealed bunker. Enter the code '3612' to open the door, and head on inside. There's no reward here, just a disturbing revelation that will change how you see the rest of the game.

Through the bunker and down a ladder, you'll see three corpses — so long as you've done the mission first: Dr. Merle, whoever you put in the chamber, and an unknown bald man (I checked his face, it isn't Jason).
