Reacts Results: Teetering on the edge of the playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Carolina Panthers get very little respect, both locally and nationally. That is largely because they haven't earned it. An improved roster and an occasionally clutch quarterback in Bryce Young has yet to translate to a consistently successful team. Hence their 5-5 record.

The only thing they really consistently have going for them is that they play in the NFC South. an approximately 0.500 record may be all it takes to win their division while a 10-win NFC North team has the potential to miss the playoffs altogether. The Panthers path to the playoffs is pretty clear, as their chances of making a wildcard berth are virtually non-existent. They need to win the NFC South and, to do that, they really should beat the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Monday Night Football this week.

The fun thing is, nobody thinks they can do it. A 5-5 team is heavily favored to lose to a 3-7 team with an injured quarterback by almost every outlet and oddsmaker out there. ESPN gives the Panthers a 32% chance to win and currently predicts that the Bucs will win the division. The Athletic's playoff simulator barely favors (54%) the Panthers to win the NFC South when looking at the remaining season as a whole. The spread at FanDuel opened favoring the Bucs at home by -2.5 and has shifted to favoring them by -3 Even the Panthers own fans are undecided on if their favorite team can beat the Bucs.

Despite that, the math is fairly clear (even if the playoff predictions from major outlets remain opaque). If the Panthers can beat the Bucs—in what should be a very different game all around than their initial Week 7 clash—then their playoff odds skyrocket. ESPN shifts the NFC South to the Panthers just on the results of Monday Night. The Athletic has the Panthers playoff odds jump to 79%. The team is on the cusp of not having to play for the division title every week until the end of the season as they did—and failed to seize—in 2025. That, and the simple high off their Week 11 win against the Baltimore, may explain why fan confidence is still riding relatively high.

Health, preparation on a long, holiday week, and the Monday night time slot will all have a hand in how this week turns out. One thing seems certain, however, with the stakes high and revenge on their minds, the Panthers seem unlikely to overlook the Bucs a second time.
