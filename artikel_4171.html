Call Of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Tops "Best-Selling" Games In October (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

CoD has dominated for 19 consecutive years in its debut month

October was another action-packed month for 2026, with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 dropping on Switch 2, and other titles such as Nintendo Switch Sports Resort & Final Fantasy Resonance also releasing.

The charts for the US market are now in, revealing the "projected top 10 best-selling video games" of the month. The Call of Duty series has also hit its 19th consecutive year as the best-selling game in its debut month. Here's the rundown via Circana's senior director and video game industry adviser Mat Piscatella. No sales figures are provided.

"October 2026 U.S. Video Game Market Highlights Thread from Circana - Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game of October, marking the 19th consecutive year that a Call of Duty franchise release has been the best-selling game of its debut month."

Circana has also shared an update on the "projected top 10 best-selling video games" of 2026 so far in the US. Call of Duty: Black Ops II is in sixth on this chart. Meanwhile, Nintendo's Switch title Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream remains in 8th place. The same game has already sold more than eight million units globally (approx 8.81 million) since launching in April.

Piscatella also notes how Switch 2 hardware led in "unit and dollar sales" during October and 2026 year-to-date. Nintendo's "limited edition" Zelda 40th anniversary Switch 2 Pro Controller also led the month in "accessory dollar sales". Again, no sales figures were provided.

And that's it for October 2026! November will reveal how Zelda: Ocarina of Time and GTA 6 have performed. Earlier this week, prior to the launch of GTA 6, it was revealed the Ocarina remake was crowned the best-selling physical game of 2026 in the US.
