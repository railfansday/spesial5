Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Switch 2 "Free N64 Box Replica" Announced For Argos Midnight Launch (UK)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As we get closer to the launch of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, more retailers have been revealing all sorts of goodies.

UK retailer Argos has now shared its midnight opening plans for the "Nintendo Pop Up Shop" at the Tottenham Court Road store. Perhaps the most exciting thing about this is the reveal of a "free Nintendo 64 box replica". It appears to be the same PAL version as France is getting, and you'll also get an Ocarina of Time keyring.

Here's the official announcement from the Argos website:

For one night only, join us on Thursday 5th November for our Midnight Launch of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake.

Come along to our Tottenham Court Road store to celebrate the 40th Anniversary of Zelda and grab a free Nintendo 64 Box Replica!

Simply pop in to our store, purchase the game at our midnight opening, and receive a replica box, inner tray and key ring.

This slipcase, as shown in the image below, will allow you to store the Switch 2 version of the game inside of it. The one catch is it's "only instore" at the Argos Tottenham Court Road store during this event, and "whilst stocks last".

If you're attending this event, you'll also be able to play the Switch 2 in-store, shop for exclusive Nintendo merch and browse the latest games and accessories.

Similar offers have been revealed elsewhere. In Canada, EB Games is offering its own version of the classic N64 box art. If you want to find out where else to pre-order the game, you can check out our guide here on Nintendo Life.
