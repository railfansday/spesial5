Oatey Raises Record $437,609 for Cancer Research

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

During this year’s Bike to Cure weekend, 170 Oatey associates from locations around the world came together to ride, volunteer, and raise funds for cancer research at Cleveland Clinic.

Oatey has raised a company-record $437,609 through its 2026 VeloSano campaign, bringing its cumulative fundraising for cancer research to more than $2.1 million.

The plumbing products manufacturer finished as the second-highest fundraising team in this year’s campaign. Over 11 years of participation, funds raised through Oatey have supported 21 cancer research projects at Cleveland Clinic.

The milestone comes as VeloSano, a global fundraising movement supporting cancer research at Cleveland Clinic, surpassed $115 million raised since its launch in 2014. According to the organization, 100% of funds raised go directly toward cancer research.

“Surpassing $115 million in fundraising is an extraordinary accomplishment, but what is even more meaningful is the innovative research and scientific progress those dollars are making possible,” Oatey CEO Neal Restivo said. “We are proud to play a part in its success and deeply grateful to everyone who makes this work possible.”

During VeloSano’s Bike to Cure weekend Sept. 18 to 19, 170 Oatey employees from locations around the world participated by riding, volunteering, and raising money. Cyclists completed routes of up to 100 miles, while others participated virtually or supported riders at the company’s sponsored rest stop.

The fundraising movement has become a significant part of Oatey’s community engagement efforts, with employees, families, and industry partners participating in campaigns throughout the year. VeloSano previously recognized the company with its All In Award in 2021.
