Microsoft stock slips as global outage knocks out Outlook, Teams, and Copilot

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Microsoft said it has no indication the outage stems from malicious activity as it works to repair affected infrastructure

David Paul Morris/Bloomberg via Getty Images

Microsoft $MSFT stock slid 2% before the open on Friday while the company raced to bring services back online following a global disruption of Outlook, Teams and Copilot.

Microsoft's service health page said users may be unable to connect to multiple Microsoft 365 services. The page listed service degradation for Microsoft 365 business and enterprise products and for consumer products. Microsoft posted the information there because administrators may be unable to reach the admin center for service health details.

The stock is heading toward its weakest closing level since Nov. 2 and its poorest weekly showing since the week that ended July 24, according to MarketWatch.

Microsoft reported in a Friday update that the disruption touches many Microsoft 365 products tied to Exchange Online and email, according to The Associated Press. Users reported that Outlook, Teams and Copilot were down, the AP said.

Microsoft said it has no indication that malicious activity caused the outage and is using “a measured approach to identify and repair affected infrastructure,” according to the AP. The company said in an earlier post that the adjustments it rolled out to boost serviceability were “not taking as expected,” the AP said. A more recent Friday update said, “we’re seeing early stabilization and capacity improvements as recovery actions continue.”

Roughly 130,000 users reported problems accessing Outlook on Thursday, according to the AP, citing the outage tracker Downdetector. New reports have dwindled since that high point. Microsoft says its Microsoft 365 apps have a user base exceeding 430 million.

Workhorse Group pushed back its quarterly earnings call as a result of the outage, according to the AP. Singapore's Government Technology Agency said government emails were down. The Philippines' Supreme Court said the outage affected some operations, including email responses. Japan's government said the outage affected its IT infrastructure and that it secured alternative tools such as telephones.

IndiGo said the outage disrupted its email support services but not flight operations. Air India said its flights were unaffected and that it temporarily rerouted email services. OpenAI and Anthropic each reported problems connecting with Microsoft 365 tools, according to the AP.

Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke of Australia said Microsoft had likened the outage to the 2024 CrowdStrike $CRWD incident. "They (Microsoft) don't believe they are dealing with a cyberattack," Burke told reporters in Sydney, according to the AP. He said Microsoft believes it is dealing with something similar to an update that "has gone awry."

The decline follows a rally in which Microsoft stock climbed as high as $551.13 in mid-October, approaching its first record since October 2025. The stock was up about 14% for 2026 at that point.
