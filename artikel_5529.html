New GoDaddy Airo AI Builder Lets Small Businesses Turn Ideas into Websites and Apps

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

An early look at more than 100,000 websites and web apps shows how entrepreneurs are using AI to build their businesses online

TEMPE, Ariz., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- GoDaddy (NYSE: GDDY), the world's largest domain registrar, is bringing advanced innovation to entrepreneurs with Airo AI Builder, an agentic operating system for small businesses. Airo AI Builder turns ideas from users' own words to polished websites, apps, branding assets, commerce tools and marketing content in minutes and keeps it updated just as easily.

Small businesses can go from idea to live site in minutes with Airo AI Builder, with zero coding experience required. By describing what they want to create through written or voice prompts, they can build websites, web apps and online stores, then continue refining them through conversation. And with connectors to more than 90 tools, such as Slack, Notion and HubSpot, entrepreneurs can also link their Airo AI Builder projects to systems they use to run their business, from sending messages and accessing databases to managing customer relationships.

Entrepreneurs are building across industries and business stages

GoDaddy's Airo surpassed $100 million in annualized bookings run rate in the third quarter, more than doubling from the previous quarter. To understand what entrepreneurs are building with Airo AI Builder, GoDaddy analyzed an anonymized sample of more than 100,000 customer-created projects from a six-week period during its beta this summer. These creations range from websites that showcase a business and its services to web apps, portfolios, dashboards, online stores, games and presentations.

For service businesses, winning customers starts online: Health, Wellness and Beauty (13%), Home & Property (12%) and Business Services (11%) together accounted for more than one-third of creations. For personal trainers and salons, a website turns interest into appointments. For contractors and bookkeepers, it helps showcase their expertise and bring in enquiries. Across these businesses, it's where customers go to explore services, build confidence in who they're choosing and take the next step.

From the neighborhood regular to the weekend visitor: Food & Travel accounted for 1 in 8 creations (12.5%). For hotels, bed and breakfasts (B&Bs) and rentals, it turns discovery into direct bookings. For both, it's where customers go first to read reviews and decide where to spend.

Airo AI Builder is also helping established businesses level up their online presence: Nearly 1 in 10 creations were classified as Rebuild/Reimagine, a category for users who already have a website and want to improve the design, or go further and add additional features and capabilities.

2. What's the difference between a website and a web app?

A website typically presents information, such as a business's services, opening hours or portfolio. A web app lets people complete tasks online, such as managing appointments, tracking orders or using a custom calculator. Many sites combine both.

3. What are Airo AI Builder's standout features?

Airo is an AI agent and business partner that helps entrepreneurs bring their ideas to life. Through Airo AI Builder, entrepreneurs can build, test and evolve their online presence through natural conversation, adding capabilities as their business needs grow.

Airo AI Builder supports both first-time founders getting online and experienced builders creating more advanced sites and apps.

5. Which commerce features are available outside the U.S. and Canada?

Airo AI Builder enables entrepreneurs around the world to build and update their online presence through natural conversation. Commerce features and payment options vary by market.
