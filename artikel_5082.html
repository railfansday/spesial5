Just 8% of tokenized assets enter DeFi – Can Solana capture the remaining $45.5B?

No doubt, tokenized equities have become one of the fastest-growing segments of the RWA market in 2026.

Binance Research shows that the entire sector’s market cap increased to $3 billion. That’s a rise of in excess of 4x since the start of the year. Against this backdrop, Solana’s momentum in tokenized equities stands out.

As AMBCrypto reported, Solana has seen its tokenized equities weekly spot trading volumes reach an all-time high of $1.8 billion. This represents 43% of all on-chain equities, marking the highest proportion since June. Furthermore, tokenized stocks issued by Backpack Securities generated $1.39 billion in trading volume on Solana last week, marking their biggest week yet.

In essence, Solana [SOL] is directly benefiting from the growing interest in tokenized equities.

As an example, during the first 24 hours of listing, Anthropic’s tokenized stock raised $134.7 million on Solana, some 2.6x higher than the debut trading volume recorded by tokenized SpaceX shares. Furthermore, it attracted over 15,000 investors, underlining the growing interest in investing in both public and blue-chip listed businesses via blockchain. However, this momentum could extend beyond spot trading.

According to on-chain data, tokenized assets have become one of the fastest-growing DeFi categories in 2026. The key question is therefore whether Solana will be able to leverage its growing tokenized equity momentum to further develop its DeFi network and capture a larger share of this emerging market.

One of the biggest opportunities for tokenized assets lies in DeFi.

The logic is simple: Investors can use these assets as collateral. This could help them back loans and allow borrowing too, hence increasing the practical application of tokenized assets. And while tokenized equities on DeFi have seen an impressive increase in value, rising by almost 1,800% within just one year ($14.9 million to $274 million), their overall adoption remains relatively low.

According to Token Terminal, the tokenized assets market is valued at $49.6 billion, but just $4.1 billion (8.2%) is currently utilized on DeFi platforms. This means that a large proportion of $45.5 billion is unused, highlighting the gap between tokenized asset growth and actual DeFi adoption.

Notably, this is where Solana’s growing tokenized equity activity becomes important.

According to on-chain data, Solana has a 32% stake in total tokenized equity TVL valued at $87 million. Ethereum is next at 20%. Thus, Solana is well placed to continue to attract investment into tokenized assets. The next step is to build on this momentum by bringing more of these assets into DeFi through lending, borrowing and collateral markets.

With this in mind, Solana has the potential to enhance liquidity within its network. It can also enable tokenized assets to be used as collateral. It can then seek to claim more of the $45.5 billion of ‘missed’ DeFi value. This, in turn, makes Solana’s tokenized stocks momentum key to underpinning its DeFi position.

Solana’s tokenized equity activity is growing, with its market share reaching 32%.

Only 8.2% of tokenized assets are used in DeFi, leaving Solana room to attract more capital.
