Cardano [ADA] had reached a high of  $0.282 on the 6th of October. Since then, the altcoin has seen a 48.16% price decline.

Cardano market participants’ sentiment has fallen into pessimism, spurred by a nearly 30% price decline for Bitcoin [BTC] and a 36.8% decline for Ethereum [ETH].

In the past 24 hours, Cardano has bounced by 2.66%, and Bitcoin by 1.19%. This was a brief respite from the steady selling pressure, and not a guarantee of recovery.

Last week, the Cardano Treasury Foundation requested $5 million worth of Cardano tokens to host 12 events in 2027, AMBCrypto reported. The recent price drop has presented a slight complication.

In other news, it was reported that Charles Hoskinson defended Cardano on social media amid frustration over its price movement. Falling token prices do not reflect the network’s utility or progress, he argued.

The weekly chart showed an ongoing bearish price structure for the popular altcoin. The attempted bullish structure flip stalled in October and led to 7 consecutive weekly red candles.

The latest weekly close swept the $0.1382 low from June. Technically, this sweep represented a good buying opportunity with nearby invalidation.

However, the threat of a deeper Bitcoin correction, as well as ADA’s bearish structure, made this bullish setup less likely to succeed.

Zooming in, the 4-hour timeframe also presented bearish obstacles. Given the downward structure, the recent bounce was only a correction. As such, swing traders should maintain their bearish bias.

Earlier this month, the $0.150-$156 area was a consolidation zone. ADA’s downtrend paused there before continuing lower, making the $0.150 area an important supply zone.

A bullish breakout past $0.1516, the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level, would be an encouraging sign. If it is followed by an H4 session close above $0.1566, traders can look for a pullback to buy.
