The Los Angeles Gift Guide for Cool Coastal Girlies

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Whether she’s heading to Pilates, driving up the coast or lounging beachside, these California-cool gifts bring a little piece of LA sunshine to wherever she is this holiday season.

Welcome to gifting season with E!, where we’re making it easier to gift shop for the pickiest people on your list.

Finding the right present for the Los Angeles girly in your life can feel like a challenge. She likely has her favorite beauty products, her go-to workout class and probably a very specific matcha order. And in a city where a beach day can turn into sweater-weather by sunset, a gift she’ll actually use is your best bet.

We’ve been keeping tabs on the pieces and brands that Hollywood shopped all year long. So, let’s just say this lineup of buzzy LA-inspired gifts will be coveted by any Angeleno or pop culture-enthusiast. From roomy tote bags perfect for farmers-market runs to cozy layers for chilly beachside evenings, there’s something special at every price point—with picks starting under $30—for that LA angel on your holiday gift list.

You don't have to live in or be from the City of Angels to find gifts that any LA lover will enjoy. Consider LA-based brands for something a local would appreciate, like Lisa Says Gah!, MadHappy or Aviator Nation.

If she frequents Dodger Stadium, a standout baseball cap—whether with bows or polka dots— will get plenty of use. Is your Angeleno a foodie or hostess? Treat her to something she might not splurge on herself, like a fancy Cali-made olive oil and locally produced honey.

When in doubt, opt for a Gold Coast classic: a luxe robe or travel bag from the official Beverly Hills Hotel collection brings LA luxury to her home. And if she’s picky about her sartorial style, a thoughtful gift for her home, like a decorative ornament or pillow, is a lovely seasonal upgrade.
