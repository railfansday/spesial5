Jax Taylor Steps Out Days After Undergoing Surgery for Kidney Cancer

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Days after Jax Taylor shared he was undergoing surgery for kidney cancer, the Vanderpump Rules star proved he’s moving in the right recovery direction by stepping out alongside girlfriend Lori Krebs.

Jax Taylor is making strides in recovery.

Just days after the Vanderpump Rules star shared he was undergoing surgery for kidney cancer, he shared a glimpse into his post-op journey by stepping out at The Grove in Los Angeles with girlfriend Lori Krebs.

Taylor, 47, appeared in good spirits while indulging in a hot beverage at the outdoor shopping and dining center Nov. 23. He kept his style comfortable, sporting a pair of gray sweatpants, a black T-shirt and baseball cap paired with sneakers.

The outing proves that Taylor is moving in the right direction after a slight setback following his procedure Nov. 19, with a source telling E! News that his release from the hospital was delayed due to “some complications.”

“Physically, he’s very sore from the incisions," the insider explained. “But mentally, he’s feeling relieved to have the surgery behind him, as it’s something that’s been weighing heavily on him for months."

And the source expressed that the reality star was grateful to have Krebs by his side as he recovers.

"The last few months have really taken a toll on him and she’s always been someone who brings him a sense of peace,” they continued. “They’ve been through a lot together and there’s a very deep love and connection between them.”

In going through such an ordeal, Taylor and Krebs have only grown closer.

"They’re truly there for one another,” the insider noted, “going through something like this has only made them realize just how much they mean to each other.”

Although Taylor—dad to Cruz, 5, with ex Brittany Cartwright—was diagnosed in July, he only recently shared his diagnosis, admitting his ongoing cancer battle has been “one of the scariest times” of his life.

“If there’s one thing I hope people can take away from this, it’s never to take your health for granted,” The Valley alum wrote in a Nov. 19 Instagram post. “I did for many years and I won’t ever do that again.”

Despite the difficult few months, Taylor emphasized that his latest health battle has only made him more grateful for his life and sobriety journey.

“Life can really change in a second,” he shared. “I’ve been given a lot to think about these past few years and if there’s one thing I know for sure it’s that nothing else matters more than your health your kids and the people that show up for you when it matters the most.”
