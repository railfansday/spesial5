Michigan football vs. Oregon injury report: Key starters in doubt

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Michigan football has had the same problems all year -- from the anemic offense to poor time of possession to injury concerns.

And against Oregon, they're not getting any better.

The Thursday availability report is out, and a new name is on the list as doubtful: wide receiver Channing Goodwin. He joins offensive tackle Blake Frazier, who was injured before the Indiana game but returned, and eventually was injured again vs. Michigan State.

The rest of the questionable list remains, with both starting edge rushers, safety Rod Moore, and others. Oregon will be without star defensive guru, Teitum Tuioti, for the first time this year.
