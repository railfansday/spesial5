Why Micron Stock Just Dropped Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Micron stock dropped again. And it's TrendForce's fault.

By Rich Smith – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:34AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Micron (MU -11.88%) stock just might be the cheapest stock ever... that investors think is overpriced.

Shares of the DRAM and NAND flash memory manufacturer sell for just 8x trailing earnings and less than 4x forward earnings. Investors slashed Micron's share price by 36% in November. At $566 this morning, Micron stock is down another 10% as of 10:20 a.m.

And once again, I blame TrendForce for that.

Five days ago, if you recall, a report from TrendForce sparked a sell-off in Micron stock. According to TrendForce, Chinese companies were cutting back on memory purchases, causing NAND spot prices to fall 20%.

Five days later, Micron's still falling as TrendForce publishes a new report: "Chinese [cloud service providers] have reduced their 2027 memory orders." And more NAND supply is coming to market, creating an "oversupplied market" and "a downward pricing cycle" in NAND.

The good news is that not all of Micron's sales are NAND -- only about a quarter. The better news is that the reason NAND sales are down appears to be that Chinese companies can't currently get all the GPUs they want, such that they need less NAND to pair with GPUs. This implies that if China does get access to more GPUs in the future, the NAND situation may fix itself.

But the best news of all is the situation with DRAM:

Although prices may "moderate," TrendForce says the DRAM market "is expected to remain undersupplied" in 2027. When Chinese buyers cut DRAM orders, the supply is "quickly absorbed" by American buyers. As a result, TrendForce expects DRAM supply to remain tight in 2027 -- and total DRAM sales could grow 69%!

I don't know about you, but to me, that still sounds incredibly bullish for Micron stock.
