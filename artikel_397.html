Apple might launch as many as 5 Product(Red) gadgets on December 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Apple is about to launch several new gadgets in a red colorway, reports say.

According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, Apple will launch a red HomePod mini 2 and AirPods Max, likely on December 1, which is World AIDS Day.

The new gadgets will be part of Apple's support for Product(Red), a brand that partners with private companies to raise awareness of HIV/AIDS. Apple has supported Product(Red) for two decades, typically by launching red versions of its gadgets. A part of the proceeds from the sale of these products goes to Global Fund, a non-profit organization that fights HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis and malaria.

The new HomePod mini 2 will reportedly have a red mesh exterior, and the AirPods Max 2 are likely to have a silver finish, white mesh ear cushions and headband, and a red Digital Crown. If the amount of red on the AirPods sounds disappointing, it's worth remembering that the AirPods Max 2 currently don't come in silver or white, so these new ones should feel quite fresh in the lineup. If the report is accurate, this would mark the first new Product(Red) releases from Apple in three years; the last one was the red Apple Watch Series 9, which launched in 2023.

Gurman later added that Apple will also launch two Product(Red) accessories: A red AirPods Wrist Strap and red Apple Watch Sport Band.

Finally, 9to5Mac added that Apple has also developed a Product(Red) version of its AirTag tracker. It's unclear whether it will launch alongside the other gadgets mentioned above.

None of these gadgets are likely to have any added new functionality aside from the change in color. Prices are also likely to remain identical to the standard color versions of the same products.
