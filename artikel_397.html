As for what it actually does, the Portal lets you play compatible games installed on your PS5 over WiFi, without needing the TV. So if someone else wants to watch something, you can keep playing with the Portal. It supports up to 1080p at 60 frames per second, plus the DualSense controller’s haptic feedback and adaptive triggers in supported games.

You don’t need a PlayStation Plus subscription for Remote Play, but your PS5 does need to be on or in Rest Mode and connected to the internet. (PlayStation Plus Premium members can also stream select PS5 games from the cloud without connecting to a PS5 at all.) Either way, you’ll need a reliable WiFi connection to play.

The bundle also includes a carrying case to protect it on the go and a charging station to dock it when you’re done playing.
