Dumping to stacking in just three months? What’s going on with XRP whales?

XRP’s price dipped under $1, and most of the market looks ready to walk away. Not the whales, though. At least not yet.

According to recent data, XRP whales have taken out more than $127 million in XRP off Binance since 10th November. Net outflows went between $12-$33 million over the past week, including about $21 million on the 16th alone!

Not too long ago, it was the other way around.

In August, XRP went up by about 72% and back above $1.50. Whales were dumping as much as $75 million on Binance in a single day!

If you look further back, you’ll see that XRP hit $3.55 in the summer of 2025. In that time, some days saw more than $100 million flood in.

When coins leave an exchange, people usually want to hold. However, don’t get excited yet. Some of this might just be Binance moving its money around.

Remember when it was topping up its reserves after the October hack?

The rest of the market isn’t seeing it the way the whales are yet.

XRP closed last week at about $0.97 on the charts. That hinted at its lowest weekly close in two years. It even dipped as low all the way down to $0.89 during the week.

Still, it’s worth thinking. This is where XRP broke out in 2024, right before it sprinted past $3.60.

Futures traders don’t seem to want to be here. The OI dropped from about $1.05 billion on the 9th to around $752 million at press time. Funding went from red on the 14th and to a slim green at 0.0013%.

ETFs haven’t been helping either; $19.1 million in outflows between 9th and 13th November.

Everyone except whales have been heading for the door.

So, here’s the thing. If whales keep taking XRP out of Binance, there isn’t enough of them to be sold. That could help turn $1 into a floor.

Or the rest of the market could drag XRP down anyway. Only time will tell.
