SAP's cloud migration chief is migrating to Ionos as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After more than two decades in Walldorf, Thomas Saueressig takes reins at hosting provider in April

SAP chief customer officer Thomas Saueressig is leaving the German software giant to become CEO of hosting and cloud provider Ionos.

The ERP and biz application vendor confirmed his departure on Tuesday, saying he will remain responsible for his current duties until the end of March 2027. He will succeed Ionos CEO Achim Weiß on April 1.

In January, Saueressig will begin handing over responsibilities to Emmanuel Raptopoulos, currently a regional customer success president, who will take charge on an interim basis. SAP's supervisory board is searching for a permanent successor.

Saueressig joined SAP's executive board in 2019 and became chief customer officer in April 2026. He leads the Customer Value Group and is charged with overseeing sales, customer success, services, and delivery, including adoption of SAP's cloud and AI products.

In previous roles at SAP, Saueressig was responsible for customer services and delivery. He has also led product engineering, and has served as SAP's in-house CIO responsible for IT strategy and cloud transformation. He joined SAP in 2004 through its combined work-and-study program.

His departure follows several changes at the top of SAP in recent years. In September 2024, CTO Jürgen Müller left by mutual agreement following an "incident" at a company event earlier in the year.

In a statement, Müller said his behavior had been inappropriate. The company announced he would get paid €7.1 million following his departure.

Chief revenue officer Scott Russell also left in 2024 by mutual agreement with the supervisory board. Julia White left the role of chief marketing and solutions officer in August 2024.

"Thomas Saueressig has been a valued member of the Executive Board and has made significant contributions to SAP's transformation," said Pekka Ala-Pietilä, chair of SAP's supervisory board. "Over many years, he has helped sharpen SAP's focus on execution, customer value and operational excellence. We thank him for his commitment and wish him all the best for the future."

Saueressig's departure comes as SAP pushes to move the ERP systems underpinning thousands of customers' businesses into the cloud – an effort whose operations and infrastructure he helped oversee. ®
