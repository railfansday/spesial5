Rockstar’s IWGB Union Fails By Majority Vote After GTA 6 Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The industry-shaking release of Grand Theft Auto 6 has overshadowed just about everything else going on in the gaming world right now, but one area that has suffered the most seems to be the efforts of the union trying to form a partnership with Rockstar's employees.

While some developers with the studio have been trying for months or even longer to get the initiative off the ground to protect their employee rights, the latest results shared by the IWGB union on Instagram show that there's still a lot more work to be done.

According to reports from sources like the Video Games Chronicle, a vast majority of Rockstar employees voted against the deal that would have seen IWGB representing them as the studio's official union, which turned out to be roughly 75%, or about 1,200 out of the 1,600 employees that participated in the vote against it.

While IWGB has had plenty of support leading up to the official vote, which ran from November 12 to November 26, several current Rockstar employees have already been expressing exactly why they think the vote failed, or why they voted no in the first place.

Rockstar continues to face allegations of union-busting in the UK, with MPs speaking on behalf of fired GTA 6 devs.

With statements like how "many of us had legitimate concerns that couldn't be answered fully by the IWGB" or dozens of others expressing how their "time at Rockstar has been amazing" with some of the "best working conditions I could possibly dream of," the vote had a lot going against it already.

The main problem many Rockstar employees had with the vote wasn't the concept of a union representing and protecting them itself, but more so the IWGB specifically, which several responses pointed out how they "failed to provide any practical advantages" and made several campaign claims that didn't accurately represent what it was actually like to work at Rockstar during the creation of GTA 6.

The timing of the vote couldn't have been worse either, as while the IWGB was using the release of GTA 6 to provide more attention to their initiative, several Rockstar employees have previously stated they didn't want to take away from the efforts of the developers that worked so hard on the project, or wanted to reap the success of the launch rather than put it at risk over more unsteady union conflicts.

Despite the current results of the vote, however, the IWGB Game Workers union isn't giving up on Rockstar by any means, as they are using the smaller portion of success to "double down" on their efforts and individual representation for Rockstar employees who opt into their organization, as opposed to moving the whole workforce into it.
