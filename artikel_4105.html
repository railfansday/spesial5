How To Unlock Scooter Bros. Delivery In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Don't worry, GTA 6 has a vehicle delivery system - you just have to make some progress to actually unlock it.

There are a lot of mechanics and functions that many expect to appear in Grand Theft Auto 6, including the return of the vehicle delivery system that GTA 5 and GTA Online made the norm.

It's no surprise, then, that GTA 6 also has its own version of a vehicle delivery system, and one that's actually been updated and expanded in some interesting ways. Here's what you need to know.

As with a few other key features, Scooter Bros. is unlocked by making enough progress in the story. It's surprisingly far into the game considering Jason has his own personal vehicle from the get-go, but you'll need to reach Chapter 2 and complete a few of its story missions to get the app. Once you've got the app, you'll be able to use it from then on.

It takes longer than you think to unlock switching between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6. Here's what you need to do.

Scooter Bros. Delivery is a service app that lets the player order any of their owned vehicles to their location for a fee. That fee isn't too much, but it will increase depending on how far the vehicle is, what condition it's in, and whether it's been impounded or not. As you can imagine, the worse the condition and the more expensive the car, the more it's going to cost to have it delivered

Scooter Bros. is also a reference to a series meme that began all the way back in GTA 4. Rockstar sure do love an easter egg, huh?

While it's easier to just pay per vehicle, the app does offer the ability to upgrade to Premium for $12,500, which will give you three daily credits to use on any service for free. If you're using Scooter Bros. a lot, then it might be worth paying the one time upgrade fee, but it's pretty hefty, so don't feel too pressured.

First-person mode sadly isn't in GTA 6 proper, but there are some workarounds both in and out of combat.
