One Of The Most Successful VR Games Of All Time Is Coming To Xbox In January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Say hello to the flatscreen version of Arizona Sunshine

If you've ever owned any kind of VR headset in your life, you've probably come across a game known as Arizona Sunshine, and we're pleased to say that its new flatscreen version is coming to Xbox Series X and Series S in January!

This is a zombie apocalypse game that served as one of the most successful examples of a proper single-player FPS in the early VR era, first releasing all the way back in 2016. It's obviously received a lot of changes for the Xbox release, most notably in the form of a new third-person perspective rather than sticking with first-person.

Its story focuses around a survivor called "Sunny" and his dog known as "Buddy", the latter of which is now playable in two-player co-op as of this new release. Don't be fooled by Buddy's cuteness though - it's still a gory game and certainly not intended for kids, so just keep that in mind before you hit the purchase button or the trailer up above!

Anyway, Arizona Sunshine was a big deal 10 years ago, so it'll be fascinating to see how well it fares on modern non-VR platforms. The game is already available to pre-order on the Xbox Store at a low price of £21.99 / $24.99, and it also seemingly comes with Xbox Play Anywhere support (meaning you get a free PC copy too).

We'll leave you with more details on Arizona Sunshine below, launching for Xbox on January 21st of 2027.

Sharpen your machete and grab your friends. Arizona Sunshine® brings its award-winning zombie apocalypse to explosive third-person action, featuring online and split-screen co-op, brutal combat, dark humor, and Buddy, your loyal zombie-chomping canine companion.
