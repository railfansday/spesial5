Multiple Silksong Expansions Hinted at in Tomorrow's PS5 Physical Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It appears Team Cherry wants you to revisit Hollow Knight: Silksong multiple times in the next year or two, as a page in the manual bundled into tomorrow's new physical edition hints at multiple content packs.

The studio has already confirmed Sea of Sorrow will launch in the future, but there seem to be more expansions coming after it:

"The world of Hollow Knight: Silksong will expand with free content packs," it reads. "Prepare for even more adventures filled with new friends, new abilities, and new ferocious foes!" The page then refers to Sea of Sorrow as the "first giant expansion", suggesting there'll be more after it.

After the support the first game received, this would come as little surprise. Team Cherry supported the first Hollow Knight with four post-launch DLC packs, all of which were substantial and released for free. The PS5, PS4 versions of the game included them all as standard.

Sea of Sorrow was confirmed at the end of last year, and Team Cherry said at the time it would be launching in 2026. It's yet to materialise, but there are still two months left for it to drop — otherwise, we're looking at a delay to 2027.

"New areas, bosses, tools, and more! Hornet’s adventures continue in our nautically themed expansion, coming free for all players next year," is how the DLC was pitched upon its announcement. "We’ll keep further details a secret for now, but expect additional info shortly before Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow releases."

As far as I'm aware, those extra details never actually arrived.

Still, it certainly sounds like Team Cherry has Hollow Knight: Silksong content in store for the long term, and from 10th November, you can own it on a physical PS5 disc.
