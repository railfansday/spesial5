Someone Discovered How To Glitch Into First-Person Mode In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GlitchGrand Theft Auto 6Grand Theft Auto VIGTA 6

Outside of aiming with certain weapons, Grand Theft Auto 6 is a strictly third-person game, at least for now. But one player has already found a way to turn on an unofficial first-person mode using a glitch. It’s fairly simple, and players are already using it to creep on Lucia.

The trick was shared on YouTube yesterday by glitch hunters oneboyler and OrangeGuy and it’s straightforward enough that anyone should be able to do it. It revolves around spamming a button press while Jason is looking at his phone in his car.

The first thing players need to do is go to the controller settings menu and change the touchpad button on the PlayStation 5 DualSense from bringing up the map to controlling the camera. The second thing they need to do his get into a car and pull out Jason’s phone.

Here comes the glitch part. You press up on the D-pad to put away your phone and spam the touchpad button while doing so. Try it a couple of times and if you’ve nailed the timing at least once, you’ll be locked in a first-person view once you get out of the car again. The game should stay this way until the next time you shut it off.

First Person Lucia in Shower byu/knowledgable_ inGTA

I’ve mentioned Jason doing this, but obviously the trick is the same if you’re controlling Lucia. And, well, at least some players have been going into first-person mode as her and then getting showers. Normally, the characters’ backs are to the camera when they’re getting washed, but in first-person mode you are the character and you can see, well, almost everything.Â Presumably, this should also work for Jason when he’s getting changed.

Rockstar Games hasn’t promised a first-person mode will get added to the game in the future, but it did confirm that was an option. GTA 5 and GTA Online didn’t launch with first-person modes either, but each got them when the PS4 and Xbox One versions launched the following year. My guess is we’ll get an official one sooner than that for GTA 6.
