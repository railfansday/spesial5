More than 39 million people have played GTA 6 on PlayStation alone, Sony says, as the game breaks all-time player numbers on the platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sony's revealed staggering statistics relating to the popularity of Grand Theft Auto on PlayStation 5, not least that more than 39 million unique people have played the game there. That's more than the official GTA 6 day-one sales tally of 32.5m copies across all platforms.

Unsurprisingly, with such massive numbers, GTA 6 is a record-breaker on PlayStation. According to the PlayStation Blog, the game has recorded the highest number of concurrent players on PlayStation ever, surpassing any previous record across all kinds of game - multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player.

In addition, PlayStation also saw "the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day" since the console's launch, outside of end-of-year holidays, suggesting that Rockstar's latest game, more than any other PS5 game released so far, has driven hardware sales. GTA 6 is apparently the first game played on 70 percent of new PS5 consoles.

The average GTA 6 playtime, per person, is a considerable 17.4 hours, the blog says, and the total number of hours spent playing the game is 683 million - a number so high I can't contextualise it.

Microsoft hasn't announced equivalent data for Xbox but I assume the game is a record-breaker there, too, given that it broke pre-order Xbox Store records in the same way GTA 6 did on PlayStation. However, GTA 6 is estimated to have sold far more copies on PlayStation - as many as 75 percent of its total sales, according to Alinea Analytics analyst Rhys Elliott.

Elliott estimated that GTA 6 amassed a colossal 44.9m sales by the end of its opening weekend, and that 33.7m of those were on PlayStation 5 (which still doesn't reach the '39 million unique players' stat Sony has just trotted out) and 11.2m were on Xbox. That amount of sales would earn Rockstar something like $4bn in revenue.

The arrival of GTA 6 has, then, been seismic. The game has wowed critics and dominated conversation not only in the games industry but also across entertainment news and mainstream news. Meanwhile, however, we await the verdict in the Rockstar Games employment tribunal in Scotland, relating to the sudden firing of 34 employees a year ago. Was Rockstar union-busting, as alleged? It'll be a historic ruling if deemed so.
