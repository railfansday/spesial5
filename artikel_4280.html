Xiaomi’s 1,548 hp electric supercar is going global, starting in the UAE

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

As a red wave of Chinese-built electric vehicles breaks over Europe and Canada, Xiaomi is selling its electric cars outside of China for the first time ever – and its starting its push for global growth with the UAE in 2027.

Xiaomi is selling its electrified vehicles to buyers outside of China for first time this week, with the SU7 sedan, YU7 crossover, and Skynomad SUV now listed for sale throughout the United Arab Emirates (UAE). The company isn’t going it alone, however.

Xiomi is working local distributor Gargash Prime Automotive, rather than operating its own retail network, with Xiaomi CEO Lei Jun confirming that the company would officially enter Western car markets next year at the APEC CEO Summit in Shenzhen held 17NOV.

CarNewsChina reports that Gargash Prime Automotive is offering 13 variants across the Xiaomi SU7 sedan, YU7 SUV, and Skynomad extended-range electric SUV lineup, with retail prices starting at 169,900 AED ($~46,200 US) for the SU7 Standard and climbing all the way up to 375,900 AED ($~102,000 US) for the range-topping SU7 Ultra.

Not to lump a bunch of nations with their own rich heritage and car culture together, but it’s fair to say that drivers in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Kuwait love their supercars – and the brand’s Xiaomi SU7 Ultra might be one of the most super of them all, thanks to fully 1,548 hp from three high-torque electric motors, the SU7 Ultra can sprint from 0-100 km/h (62 mph) in under two seconds on its way to a towering, 217 mph top speed.

It’s not just a straight-line speed machine, either. Xiaomi gave its go-fast flagship a serious track-focused makeover on its way to becomig the “Utlra,” complete with carbon-ceramic brakes, an aggressive (if controversial) composite aero package, an enormous rear wing, and a chassis tuned to tackle the turns the Green Hell of the Nürburgring.

And, at a relatively reasonable $102,000 equivalent price, I’d be willing to bet that a lot of the hypercar-loving Emiratis will be eager to get their hands on one.

The first customer deliveries of the Xiaomi SU7 Standard and Ultra, YU7 Standard, and Skynomad N70 Pro models are scheduled for December of this year, with deliveries of the remaining variants scheduled for February 2027.
