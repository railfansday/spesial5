Man United's 'gruesome threesome' blasted after 'totally disrespectful' Michael Carrick treatment - ex-manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The treatment of Michael Carrick in his final days as Manchester United manager was completely disrespectful.

That is the opinion of experienced former manager and ex-Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

It was clear that the 45-year-old would be dismissed after an awful start to the season, but he remained in charge as discussions continued last week.

Eventually, Carrick was officially sacked and Man United have since appointed Mauricio Pochettino as his replacement.

The former midfielder had hoped to have bought himself time with a final week that saw his team come from behind twice, once to beat Roma and again to draw with Villa.

But the Red Devils are in the relegation zone after 10 Premier League games and must start producing results immediately.

When he arrived to take over from Ruben Amorim in January, Carrick looked like the saviour Man United had been looking for.

The former midfielder won 11 of his 16 games as an interim manager, qualifying for the Champions League and setting up what could have finally been a fresh start in the summer.

But Man United ultimately failed to get Carrick the players he wanted, in a summer window that has looked more and more miserable since.

"The one thing with Carrick, at least Carrick was trying to introduce the way Alex Ferguson managed," King told Football Insider.

"I'd have let Carrick stay there until the New Year, because all this now, what they've done. Can you imagine that the gruesome threesome [Jim Ratcliffe, Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox] went to Monaco to discuss sacking Carrick, and he's taking training on the day they're sitting in Monaco, having a jolly-up.

"Totally disrespectful. They could have done all that before they left. Carrick showed great dignity, I thought, in the way he handled everything and the way he's managed the football club.

"You can't knock the fella as a player. Won everything with Man United and gave them great service. You rescue the club when you come in. You win 11 games out of 16 and put the club in a decent position, and then you start."

Even despite the woeful start to the season, chants of disapproval at Old Trafford were never aimed at Carrick.

"I want nothing but success for Manchester United. This club has been such a big part of my life and that will never change," he said after his dismissal was confirmed.

"I'll always be proud to have played my part and I'll always want the very best for it.

"I'll be right there with you from now on, watching from the sidelines, cheering the lads on”.
