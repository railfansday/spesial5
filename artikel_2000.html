Liverpool backed to finalise Virgil van Dijk deal after confession - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Virgil van Dijk has been backed to sign a new contract at Liverpool after he confessed he would be happy to extend his stay.

The 35-year-old is out of contract at the end of the season, and it has been suggested that Liverpool could be prepared to let him move on, but his latest comments have changed things.

While he explained that no agreement is close, and they are in no rush to force things through, there is also no panic or concern about his contract situation at Anfield.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Van Dijk's words will have eased pressure on Liverpool, who plan to offer him a new deal in due course.

The Netherlands international remains a crucial part of Andoni Iraola's ambitious plans for success, both on the pitch and behinid the scenes, and his exit would come as a blow.

Van Dijk's reveal that he is happy to stay and extend his contract at Liverpool will now calm a lot of nerves, with all parties now able to look towards the future.

Football Insider revealed last month that Liverpool will be prepared to offer Van Dijk a new contract if he can continue to prove himself to Iraola this season.

The legendary defender has little to prove with regard to his influence, but given his age, concerns have crept in about how much longer he can play at the top level.

But having impressed so far this season, he remains part of the manager's long-term plans, and there is a growing possibility that Van Dijk will be offered a new contract.

"It seems like Van Dijk wants to stay at Liverpool, and that's got to come as a boost," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's still a top-class defender, he's part of the team and playing every week, and he's showing he still has plenty to contribute in the Premier League.

"Obviously there are a few concerns about his age, he's 35 now so it might not be long before he starts to drop off, but he's just as big of an influence behind the scenes.

"When you have somebody like Van Dijk on the books, you want to keep him around for as long as possible, because he offers so much both on and off the pitch.

"After he's come out and said he would be willing to stick around, it's a no-brainer really for Liverpool to offer him a new contract."

Despite the news that Van Dijk is willing to sign a new contract at Liverpool, work has also been ongoing behind the scenes to identify a long-term replacement at the back.

Regardless of his importance to the side, the Netherlands international is now 35 years old, and plans are being made for the future in case his form faces a drop off.

Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Liverpool are keeping a close eye on the progress of Ismaelo Ganiou, who has impressed in Ligue 1 with Lens in recent months.

The 21-year-old is viewed as one of the best young defensive talents in Europe, and despite having only recently signed a new contract, could be available for the right price.

Jeremy Jacquet arrived from France in the summer and has been incredibly impressive, and Liverpool could now follow that blueprint and up their interest in landing Ganiou.
