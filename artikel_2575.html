Brian Madjo, 17, electrifies Villa despite Super Cup defeat by PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Unai Emery’s Aston Villa 2.0 remain under construction but from the wreckage of a difficult summer there has emerged a potential star for the future.

Emery may have now suffered four successive defeats in the Super Cup, yet the big positive from a balmy night in Salzburg was the performance of teenage forward Brian Madjo. Aged 17 years and 212 days, Madjo became the youngest player to start and score in this continental showpiece, just over a week after he was given permission to play for his club.

Signed from Metz for £10m in January, Madjo had been prevented from playing because of Fifa’s rules on the international transfer of minors. Villa’s appeal to the Court of Arbitration for Sport to have him registered was upheld on August 4 and what a prospect he appears.

Born in Enfield but raised in Luxembourg, there has been a huge buzz around him since his arrival in the new year.

Paris St-Germain have produced an abundance of talented teenagers over the years and Madjo did not look out of place here.Désiré Doué’s second-half strike secured PSG the Super Cup – contested by the winners of the Champions League and Europa League – for the second successive year, but Emery will have seen enough to give him renewed confidence.

His team played with all the resilience and bravery that has become a trademark, epitomised by the powerful Madjo and another teenager in midfielder George Hemmings.

Emery said: “Brian has scored goals for us in pre-season and has shown his capacity to work in our idea. I am so happy because the young players were involved in our work and not playing last season.

“Of course there is still work to do but they showed they can play with us. They have responded fantastically and will then feel the confidence. With a loss you can never be happy but overall I am proud of all the players and everything we’ve done. Some changes will be needed but we’re confident.”

This was a fine Villa performance to silence the doom merchants who have been fearing a troubled season. After the painful sales of Morgan Rogers, Youri Tielemans and Lucas Digne, Villa are clearly a team in transition. They are operating under restrictions to comply with Uefa’s financial rules and there is still the possibility of another important player leaving before the transfer deadline day.

England centre-back Ezri Konsa remains a priority target for Premier League champions Arsenal, and was absent here. Konsa, Emiliano Martínez and Ollie Watkins were all granted extended leave after their participation in the World Cup, which has done little to ease the fears of supporters over more departures. The trio will return to training this weekend.

While some outsiders are predicting a difficult season for Villa, do not bother telling Emery. He has turned putting noses out of joint into an art form since sweeping into Villa Park and with him around anything feels possible.

The Basque remains optimistic about the future and those hopes will only have been underlined by the performance of Madjo. He bullied PSG centre-back Willian Pacho for much of the evening and even managed to steal the limelight from PSG’s magician Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.

Hemmings, 19, has emerged from the academy and already made a real impression on his manager. Despite the high regard for those youngsters, this always appeared likely to be a tough proposition against a full-strength PSG team. Indeed, before this game in Salzburg only 39 per cent of Europa League winners have beaten their Champions League rivals in this fixture.

The Champions League and Europa League winners fight it out in the Uefa Super Cup in Salzburg as PSG take on Aston Villa. PSG took on English opposition in the Super Cup a year ago, when they played Tottenham. PSG fell 2-0 down in that game in Udine but scored two late goals to take it to penalties, where they won 4-3. These sides met in the Champions League knockout stages two seasons ago; PSG did progress from the quarter-final encounter as they went on to win the competition but not without a scare as Aston Villa did win at Villa Park and PSG only progressed 5-4 on aggregate. The two clubs have been involved in a transfer this week, with left-back Lucas Digne re-joining PSG from Aston Villa.

Aston Villa have played in the Super Cup once before back in 1982 when they took in Barcelona. They lost the first leg 1-0 over at Camp Nou but fought back to win the second leg 3-0 at Villa Park to claim the trophy. Aston Villa were last in action out in the Far East last Friday, falling to a 2-1 defeat to Bayern Munich in Hong Kong. It has been a big summer of change at the club, with key starters like Morgan Rogers and Youri Tielemans departing and the likes of Alejandro Garnacho and Joao Gomes arriving.

“I feel like the club is in a new era, with this possibility to fight for trophies,” Unai Emery told Uefa. “We are trying to be successful through, firstly, [building] a winning team, and then aiming for the positive consequences after it; winning trophies. To play the UEFA Super Cup is another opportunity to be there, the opportunity to win a trophy, to play against Paris Saint-Germain; the [best] team in the world now and another team we can compare [ourselves] with.”

Emery has confirmed that Ezri Konsa, Ollie Watkins and Emiliano Martinez will not be involved as they are still on their breaks following the World Cup.
