Confirmed lineups: RB Leipzig vs. Manchester City

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

RB Leipzig are looking to continue their red-hot home form this evening, as they welcome Manchester City to Saxony for their fourth outing in the Champions League this term.

Martin Demichelis is yet to taste defeat at Red Bull Arena, while his side also broke their away duck with wins against Rot-Weiss Essen and Schalke across the DFB-Pokal and Bundesliga last week.

Now, they welcome the under-scrutiny Citizens, and could well be sniffing an upset after Enzo Maresca's side suffered successive Premier League defeats against Aston Villa and Brighton.

With kick-off to come at 21:00 CET, both bosses have announced their respective lineups, which you can find below.

RB Leipzig: Vandevoordt - Baku, Orban, Lukeba, Raum - Banzuzi, El Aynaoui, Baumgartner - Gruda, Romulo, Nusa

Manchester City: Donnarumma - Nunes, Khusanov, Guehi, O'Reilly - Fernandez, Anderson - Foden, Cherki, Ndiaye - Haaland
