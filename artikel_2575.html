Husker Volleyball Starts Slow, Digs Deep to Get 4 Set Win Over Purdue: #1 Nebraska 3-#12 Purdue 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nebraska 3-Purdue 1: 21-25, 27-25, 25-11, 25-18

If you were wanting to see Nebraska get tested, tonight was the night for that. Purdue made all of Husker nation hold their breath during the first few sets of the match before the Huskers started to clean it up and play how they have been all season.

The first couple sets of the match had me feeling the same way I did when we played Texas A&M in the Regional Final last year. I'm not usually one to pace when watching sports, but oh boy was I pacing when it came down to the wire in the 2nd.

It was pretty expected for Purdue to come out how they did because of how the match went in Lincoln earlier in the season. The Boilermakers were the first team to take a set off the Huskers in B1G play this season.

What wasn't expected was how bad both sides of the ball was looking for the Huskers. On defense, it seemed like no one was diving for balls and no one was making that extra effort. On offense, all hitters were not being efficient and just doing too much.

Nebraska's middles, Andi Jackson and Manaia Ogbechie, were also a big problem too. Jackson was hitting in the negative all of the 1st and 2nd sets while Ogbechie wasn't getting any kills. The block also wasn't working.

Even with all of these things that went wrong, the Huskers were able to stir up some momentum at the right moments to pull off the 27-25 set 2 win and tie the match at 1.

After longer rest in between sets, it seemed like the Huskers figured it all out and came out like a whole new team playing a completely different match. The Huskers quickly took the 7-1 lead that made Purdue take their first timeout of the 3rd set.

Andi Jackson was also able to quickly pull herself out of the negative early in the 3rd, but still was struggling compared to the numbers she usually puts up. The Huskers were up 15-4 at the TV timeout and ended the set, winning 25-11 and hitting .448%. Just a total 180 from sets 1 and 2. It was amazing to see how they could quickly turn their game around and take control like they are used to having from jump.

After putting in a lot of their bench in the 3rd set, Purdue started their regular lineup in the 4th and quickly took the 3-1 lead. Nebraska said not so fast though and tied it 3-3 pretty quickly after.

Purdue started to find their groove again early in the 4th, but Nebraska was still in theirs too. It's hard to compete against a Husker team that is full steam head and this was the first time tonight that both teams were competing hard at the same time.

While Purdue kept fighting in the 4th, it wasn't good enough to outlast a Husker team that had quickly transitioned from a team that looked like they were having an off night to a team that looked like they had for most of the season.

The Huskers won the 4th set by a score of 25- to send them to the Big 10 Tournament Championship for the first time ever. What a wild ride tonight's match was, but it was a fun one as opposed to the usual sweeps that this team gets.

Teraya Sigler led the team with 18 kills while Harper Murray followed her up with 15 kills of her own. Andi Jackson really turned her night around in sets 3 and 4 as she ended the match in double digits with 11 kills.

Virginia Adriano added 8 kills, Manaia Ogbechie had 6, and Bergen Reilly finished with 3 kills of her own. Jackson recorded 6 the team high of 6 blocks tonight which really shows her grit as she started out rough, but ended on a good note.

The hitting numbers really shows it all. The Huskers hit .143% in the 1st and .043% in the 2nd, but then followed that up with hitting .448% in the 3rd and .378% in the 4th. Purdue had a strong 1st set, hitting .314%, but after that they never hit above .200%, even hitting negative in the 3rd at -.030%.

The Huskers will play tomorrow night for the championship against either Wisconsin or Penn State. The match will be at 7:00pm CST and hopefully the Huskers won't cause us to have a heart attack tomorrow too!
