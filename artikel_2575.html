2026-27 NBA Minutes Report: Brandon Ingram returns, Cameron Boozer is sidelined, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Welcome to the NBA Minutes Report. Every week, I will review each team's updated minutes per game to see which players are seeing the court more or less than in previous weeks and what their impact has been. That can help you with any fantasy basketball decisions you may need to make, but also help you get a lay of the land on what's happening around the NBA and which players are gaining or losing value.

The charts below are also great for exploring on your own. You can track the minutes over the last three, five, ten games, and for the entire season, to see which trends stand out to you. All of this data was made accessible by Kyle Bland, who is incredibly talented and also extremely generous. Be sure to follow him to check out all of his baseball data as well.

We've now seen a full week with CJ McCollum (calf) on the bench, and that's given us slightly more minutes for Corey Kispert(who had 18 points on Sunday) and Lu Dort. However, most of the minutes are just going to the starters. Jock Landale has also been nursing a hip injury, which has created an opportunity for Zuby Ejiofor to enter the rotation.

Mitchell Robinson has been dealing with an illness, but his time in Boston has not gone well. In fact, things are not going well in Boston overall. They beat the Wizards on Tuesday, but had lost four straight games before that, including losses to the Kings and Warriors. Derrick White's shooting has been an issue again this season, and the Celtics just aren't consistently getting strong production from their best unit either.

Sadly, Nolan Traore suffered a hamstring injury that's going to sideline him for a few months. The Nets are likely going to spread playing time around for their young players, but Mikel Brown Jr. got 29 minutes on Monday and scored 17 points with five rebounds and seven assists. He also had six turnovers, but he's gonna have the ball in his hands far more often. The rest of the fill-in players aren't overly interesting.

Kon Knueppel, Grayson Allen, and Dennis Schroder all remain sidelined, so Sion James continues to start and play big minutes. There's not a ton of production, but he's a role player. Christian Anderson scored 25 points in 25 minutes on Monday, and this was a bit of a "hot hand" situation, but there aren't that many options off the bench right now. The rest of the bench totaled just six points in that game.

As we mentioned last week, we've slowly started to see Nic Claxton's minutes tick up. He played 23 in a win over the Cavaliers on Monday but had sat out the game against the Nuggets before. Norman Powell is now dealing with a hamstring injury, so Buddy Hield has stepped into the starting lineup and had 17 points and five rebounds in 27 minutes in that game on Monday. He is going to shoot when he's on the court.

Evan Mobley remains sidelined with a toe injury, which has shortened the Cavs' rotation. Sam Merrill is starting, and Meleek Thomas and Tyrese Proctor have become the two key contributors off the bench. The rest is pretty much status quo here, and the usage sits heavily with the starters.

Caleb Martin's role continues to grow as a starter in Dallas. He played 30 minutes in their win over the Nuggets on Tuesday, scoring 13 points with four rebounds, four assists, and five steals. He's been a great complement to scorers like Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving. Morez Johnson Jr. has also clearly earned the backup big man role, ahead of Santi Aldama.

The Nuggets had been playing well before their loss to the Mavericks, and a lot of that has to do with how elite Nikola Jokić and Jamal Murray are as a duo, which I covered in a video this week. The Nuggets also have a clear-cut starting five, and then like to use Spencer Jones as their sixth man, but the rest of the bench is a rotating group that all seems ot play around 10-17 minutes depending on the matchup.

Cade Cunningham missed Tuesday's win over the Magic with a knee injury, which led to 35 minutes for Daniss Jenkins. We also saw Isaiah Joe start and play 30 minutes in that win, scoring 21 points with four rebounds. He seems to be settling into a larger role now that he's back from injury and Duncan Robinson is still out. The Pistons need his ability to space the floor.

The Warriors have deepened their rotation in the wake of all their injuries, but also 6-7 players are really playing meaningful minutes. Gui Santos did play 23 minutes in a win over the Celtics on Sunday, but that was because he was having a solid game overall. We finally saw De'Anthony Melton play 27 minutes in that game, but he was 0-7 from the field. Charles Bassey has become the starting center and had 13 points, 9 rebounds, 3 assists, and 3 steals in that win over the Celtics. He's been pretty good for Golden State.

The Rockets run one of the tightest rotations in basketball, and not much has changed with it over the last week. In fact, they don't have a single player listed on the injury report.

T.J. McConnell is dealing with a hamstring injury, which has led to more time for Braden Smith, who started and played 24 minutes in Monday's game against the Thunder. Jalen Slawson also played 20 minutes in that game and scored 16 points with 10 rebounds and four blocks, which is insane production in that many minutes. He could earn a larger role for himself.

We saw Brandon Ingram make his season debut on Monday night, which I recorded a video about. That led to a decrease in minutes for Max Strus and Gradey Dick, and Dick could be out of the rotation entirely. Yanic Konan Niederhäuser also returned to the court, which has pushed Isaiah Jackson down the pecking order.

Austin Reaves remains out with a heel injury, so Quentin Grimes and Matisse Thybulle remain in key roles in this rotation. Jake LaRavia has been in the starting lineup and off the bench, but he still pushes 30 minutes a night, and the rest of the team has had sporadic production off the bench. It's a solid bench, but there isn't really one guy they lean on constantly, apart from knowing that Collin Sexton is going to get up shots when he's in the game.

Sadly, the big news in Memphis was that Cameron Boozer will be sidelined for at least a month with a sprained ankle. With Jerami Grant and GG Jackson also hurt, there are some questions about the depth on the wing in Memphis, all of which I discussed in a video this week.

The Heat are starting to figure some things out. They beat the Thunder on Sunday for their seventh win in a row, and that was without Giannis Antetokounmpo, who missed with a groin injury. Bobby Portis Jr. started for him, but he'll move back to the bench on Wednesday with Giannis back. We have seen Ryan Conwell take on a much bigger role off the bench, playing at least 26 minutes in each of the last five games.

The Bucks are really not good and have just two wins this season. Of course, one of them happened in their last game against the Raptors. They essentially used just an eight-man rotation in that game, with Nate Ament, Kel'el Ware, and Brayden Burries as the only bench players playing over eight minutes. Burries has been a tremendous success for the Bucks so far this season, and there's some freedom for him in coming off the bench and just playing with his hair on fire.

The Timberwolves continue to play well with Anthony Edwards sidelined, and a big reason for that is Jaden McDaniels. LaMelo Ball and Ayo Dosunmu have done a good job approximating Edwards' offensive value, but I think McDaniels may be the most important player on Minnesota right now, and I recorded a video discussing why he should make the All-Star Game.

We've now seen an extended period of time after Dejounte Murray demanded a trade, and Jeremiah Fears has unsurprisingly seen his role grow. Trey Murphy III will also play plenty of minutes, but he's not as much of a high-usage player as the other two. Saddiq Bey and Jordan Poole are the key pieces off the bench and fill flash, as they did on Monday against the Suns, but the production is not consistent.

The early-season struggles for Mikail Bridges and Josh Hart seem to be a little bit more in the rearview, and both players are trending back to their old roles with the Knicks finding their footing. OG Anunoby missed Sunday's game with a calf injury, which allowed Landry Shamet to start and Jose Alvarado to play 22 minutes off the bench, but it's not expected to be a long-term injury.

The Thunder lost Alex Caruso to a hip injury in addition to the larger injuries to Chet Holmgren, Cason Wallace, and Jalen Williams. As a result, Bennett Stirtz has seen his role grow. The production has been inconsistent, but the minutes might be there in the short term. Aday Mara missed Monday's game with a shin bruise, but he has been tremendous as a starter and figures to be a key part of this lineup for much of the season.

The Magic are now 7-10 and continue to struggle without Paolo Banchero. Franz Wagner is playing well, but Jalen Suggs is shooting just 32% from the field in November. Anthony Black is providing decent minutes in the starting rotation, but there isn't enough firepower off the bench to help carry this team when the starting lineup isn't delivering.

Not much has changed for the 76ers, but Dominick Barlow did make his season debut, and he'll likely be one of the key reserves, but will play fewer minutes with Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Anfernee Simons, and Dean Wade all in town now. Joel Embiid has continued to play big minutes when he does play, but he's missed four games for various reasons, which will happen throughout the season. The bench guys are gonna have spike weeks when some of the starters sit, but there won't be enough usage to go around when the team is healthy.

Status quo here from the Suns. Ryan Dunn has seen his role decrease a bit, and the Suns' bench unit led by Collin Gillespie and Jordan Goodwin is more interesting than their ill-fitting starting lineup.

Deni Avdija returned after a minor foot injury, so the Trail Blazers are now back to business as usual. Jrue Holiday and Scoot Henderson are the two primary options off the bench, and the only two guys who are playing big minutes. Robert Williams III will mix in to give Donovan Clingan a break, but Clingan has been great for Portland this season and a key reason for their defensive success.

The Kings won a game against the Celtics last week, but that's just their second win of the season. They rely super heavily on their starting five, and the bench behind them is a bit of a mess. Domantas Sabonis still remains the most well-rounded offensive option on the offense, and Darius Acuff Jr. will have big games with his high usage role, but Keegan Murray is really struggling with his shot this year, and this just isn't an exciting team.

We've now seen a full week with Dylan Harper sidelined by a hip injury. That has, as expected, led to a slight minutes boost for Julian Champagnie and both Harrison Barnes and Carter Bryant getting a few more minutes at night depending on the matchup.

Things are not going well for the Raptors, as evidenced by their loss to the Bucks on Monday. Kawhi Leonard (knee) and RJ Barrett (hamstring) remain out, but Scott Barnes is back to his normal role, which is big, and Collin Murray-Boyles finally returned from a concussion on Monday. He played 24 minutes off the bench and didn't shoot well, but it's good to have him back. Allen Graves and Ja'Kobe Walter have been playing more minutes lately, but there's not a lot of production coming along with it.

Jaxson Hayes had been playing meaningful minutes in the wake of Utah's many injuries, but he suffered a knee injury of his own. That has led to consistent minutes for Mo Bamba and an increased role off the bench for Kyle Filipowski. Svi Mykhailiuk has also seen a bigger role as a shooter off the bench, but he went just 1-7 from the field on Monday after a big night the game before, so it's hard to see him as a consistent option.

The Wizards have an incredibly narrow rotation right now. Only seven players played more than eight minutes in their loss to the Celtics on Tuesday. An injury to Julian Champagnie is part of that, but the Wizards also just haven't found anybody on their bench that they feel comfortable relying on, apart from Bilal Coulibaly and Khris Middleton
