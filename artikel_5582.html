XRP Price Could Hit $10,000? From 21Shares’ Forecast to Influencers’ Moonshots

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

XRP price predictions are stretching from Wall Street’s $2.69 bull case to Jake Claver’s extraordinary $10,000 target, as institutional adoption and crypto’s biggest moonshot forecasts collide.

Asset manager 21Shares sees XRP reaching $2.69 in its optimistic 2026 scenario, while veteran trader Peter Brandt has outlined a longer-term $5.40 target. Standard Chartered’s longer-range forecast reaches $28 by 2030, but XRP’s most aggressive supporters are aiming much higher.

Claver continues to stand by his $10,000 XRP price prediction. The forecast would put XRP’s valuation above the combined value of today’s largest financial markets, turning a bullish debate into one of crypto’s most extreme price calls.

21Shares’ January outlook gave XRP a $2.45 base case for 2026, alongside a $2.69 bullish scenario and a $1.60 bearish outcome. The asset manager tied its bullish case to institutional demand, spot XRP ETF inflows, tokenized real-world assets and growing adoption of Ripple’s financial infrastructure.

Peter Brandt’s $5.40 target comes from a longer-term chart pattern that he believes could eventually push XRP beyond its previous record. Standard Chartered has taken the institutional forecast further, projecting XRP at $28 by 2030 as tokenization and stablecoin adoption expand.

EGRAG Crypto has also outlined a $50 scenario based on historical monthly price patterns. This target would give XRP a market capitalization of roughly $3.15 trillion, assuming around 63 billion tokens circulate.

The forecasts become dramatically more ambitious with Claver. The XRP advocate believes the token could first reach $50 to $100 as banks adopt it as a financial asset. He then expects institutional demand and what he calls a systemic repricing to push XRP toward $10,000.

Claver has previously made a $100 prediction for the end of 2025, a target XRP did not reach. His latest forecast nevertheless keeps the five-figure scenario in view.

XRP’s institutional narrative also highlights a question Bitcoin maximalists have debated for years: can Bitcoin become more than a store of value without sacrificing its core security model? Bitcoin Hyper is positioning itself around this opportunity, building a Bitcoin Layer 2 designed to bring faster transactions and smart-contract functionality to the world’s largest cryptocurrency.

The project combines Bitcoin settlement with Solana Virtual Machine execution, aiming to support applications that Bitcoin’s base layer was not designed to handle directly.

Its HYPER token is currently offered through a presale, with a listing price at just $0.0137 and staking rewards offered at 35% APY. The presale has attracted more than $33 million, showing substantial early investor interest.

That combination of a low entry price, staking incentives, and a Bitcoin-native narrative has helped draw attention from crypto investors looking for infrastructure plays beyond established large-cap assets.

The institutional angle is straightforward: if financial firms increasingly use blockchain networks for settlement, tokenization, and programmable finance, Bitcoin’s ecosystem needs infrastructure capable of supporting those activities.

XRP has spent years building its case around payments and institutional liquidity. Bitcoin Hyper is betting that Bitcoin can attract similar infrastructure demand without asking Bitcoin holders to abandon the network they already trust.

Claver’s forecast remains the most aggressive target in the current XRP prediction lineup. His thesis depends on XRP becoming a major liquidity asset for banks and financial institutions, with demand eventually extending into large-scale derivatives settlement.

At $10,000 per token, XRP’s market value would reach approximately $630 trillion based on a circulating supply of 63 billion tokens. That is far beyond the valuation implied by 21Shares’ $2.69 bull case, which puts XRP near $170 billion using the same supply assumption.

The distance between those targets captures the divide in the XRP market. Institutional forecasts focus on ETF demand, adoption, and measurable financial use, while Claver’s moonshot depends on XRP becoming a central component of global financial infrastructure.
