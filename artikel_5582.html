Many Sellers Feel Now Is the Time to Move, Despite High Rates: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Even with uncertainty keeping a hold on housing, many homeowners still feel now is the time to make their move in the market, according to a new report.

Century 21 Real Estate’s Home Seller Confidence Report found that 75% of homeowners planning to sell in the next 12 months believe now is the right time to move, and 82% who already have their homes on the market agree with the sentiment. The survey was conducted via Ipsos and included more than 1,000 current, prospective and recent home sellers across the country.

Despite the phenomena of the mortgage rate lock-in effect, Mike Miedler—president and CEO of Century 21 Real Estate—said that “people don’t make decisions about their homes based on rates alone,” and are often motivated by “new jobs, growing families and other life changes.”

“Sellers aren’t waiting for perfect conditions, but they are approaching that decision with more thought and preparation,” he continued. “They want to understand what their home is worth and how quickly it could sell so they can move forward with confidence.”

Taking a look at how mortgage rates affected seller decisions, rates ranked fifth among 16 factors in the report influencing the decision to move, coming in behind personal motivations such as finding the right next home, seeking a change of scenery and major life events.

The report also found that 86% of sellers surveyed said their mortgage rate was slightly, moderately, very or extremely influential on the timing of their move. Of sellers and homeowners with a mortgage under 5%. 54% did admit leaving behind their mortgage rate would be harder than leaving their home itself.

Even with motivation to move, the report outlined that homeowners do still have concerns slowing down their decisions. Specifically, more than 25% of sellers surveyed considered the choice of moving for one to two years or longer before listing their home.

Additionally, data showed that of sellers who sold in the last 2 years surveyed, 66% seriously reconsidered their asking price before listing at least once, with some revisiting the decision four or more times. Another 58% worried about pricing their home too high or ultimately having to reduce their asking price, while 27% worried about pricing too low.

“Today’s sellers are approaching one of life’s biggest financial decisions with more research and intention than ever before,” added Miedler. “They’re exploring pricing strategies, evaluating buyer demand and looking for trusted guidance before bringing their homes to market. The sellers who feel most confident are often the ones who have a clear strategy from the start.”
