CobbleStone® Announces December 3rd CLE Webinar on Responsible AI Use in Corporate and In-House Legal Practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AI ethics webinar helps legal professionals understand responsible AI use while earning CLE credit.

PRINCETON, N.J., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CobbleStone Software, a recognized leader in contract lifecycle management (CLM) and contract artificial intelligence, announced its upcoming CLE webinar, "AI & Ethics: Responsible AI Use in Corporate and In-House Legal Practice," scheduled for December 3, 2026, from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM ET. The complimentary educational event will provide legal professionals with practical guidance on the ethical implications of responsible AI adoption, including privilege and waiver.

The webinar will be presented by a leading AI attorney and will offer a plain-English explanation of how artificial intelligence technologies function, helping attendees better understand the ethical and professional responsibility challenges associated with AI use by lawyers. Participants will also gain insight into how courts are addressing AI-related privilege and waiver issues and receive actionable recommendations for minimizing ethical risks when leveraging AI tools in legal environments.

Attendees will receive a resource guide featuring key bar association guidance and notable judicial decisions related to AI ethics, attorney-client privilege, and more. Live attendees may also qualify for CLE credit, subject to their state's specific accreditation requirements. Credits have been approved or are pending in numerous jurisdictions across the United States.

Legal professionals today face increasing pressure to adopt AI technologies while maintaining compliance with ethical obligations and professional standards. Understanding how courts, regulators, and bar associations view AI-assisted legal work is becoming increasingly important as organizations evaluate new technologies and workflows. The webinar is designed to address these challenges by providing practical, current guidance tailored to attorneys working in corporate legal departments, law firms, and related legal functions.

Register for the complimentary CLE webinar today to reserve your spot and learn how to navigate emerging AI ethics considerations with greater confidence.

"Artificial intelligence presents tremendous opportunities for legal professionals. It also raises important ethical questions that cannot be ignored," said Bradford Jones, VP of Sales & Marketing at CobbleStone Software.

"This webinar is designed to help attorneys understand AI in terms of what ethical considerations they should be aware of and how they can responsibly use these technologies while protecting client interests and maintaining professional standards."
