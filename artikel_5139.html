Amundi puts European stocks on Ethereum, but access terms stay hidden

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The share class announced Nov. 5 represents fund units, while Amundi's release leaves investor access, transfer and redemption terms unspecified.

Amundi has issued a tokenized share class of its Amundi Core STOXX Europe 600 equity index fund on public Ethereum on Nov. 5, adding a blockchain route to European stock-market exposure with CACEIS providing the technology and infrastructure for tokenizing the fund units

Amundi said the new class remains integrated into the fund's existing operating model, and investors can also continue accessing the fund through traditional channels.

The token represents a unit in an investment fund, with investors gaining exposure through that fund.

The announcement does not specify eligible investors, minimum subscriptions, fees, transfer restrictions, or redemption procedures for the tokenized class.

Those details are key to anyone assessing whether they can buy, move, or redeem the units, since issuance on a public blockchain =does not establish unrestricted retail access or permissionless use across DeFi platforms.

CACEIS's October 2024 description of its general tokenization platform outlined a hybrid model combining traditional ownership records with blockchain units and digital investor checks, providing infrastructure context.

The equity class also extends Amundi's earlier work in cash management. Their EURXT settlement concerned a subscription into a tokenized Amundi money-market fund, and the firm's latest release likewise refers to earlier money-market and stablecoin initiatives.

The issuance establishes a fund-unit route onto Ethereum, with practical access dependent on the new class's terms.
