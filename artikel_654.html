TV Ratings: NBA Logs Winning Streak in First Month of the Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Audiences on national media partners NBC/Peacock, ESPN and Prime Video are up by 37 percent year to year.

Like a jump shooter who can’t miss, the NBA is on a heater with TV audiences through the first month of its 2026-27 season.

National telecasts of league games on NBC/Peacock, ESPN and Prime Video are averaging 2.62 million viewers over the season’s first month, based on Nielsen ratings and Adobe Analytics figures for Peacock’s games. That’s a sizable, 37 percent increase over the same time frame last year — the first in the league’s current media rights deal with NBCUniversal, ESPN and Prime Video. Through last week, every national telecast had topped 1 million viewers, the first time that’s happened since 2023. That’s also the highest mark on record for the first month of an NBA season, excluding lockout-delayed starts in 1999 and 2011 (and with the caveat that changes in Nielsen measurement the past few years have tended to benefit live sports).

NBC’s six national windows lead the way with an average of 4.5 million viewers (including streaming on Peacock), a 27 percent improvement over last season’s first month.. The network’s opening night game between the defending champion New York Knicks and the Philadelphia 76ers (who acquired LeBron James during the offseason) averaged 7.71 million cross-platform viewers.

The first eight games on Prime Video have averaged 2.05 million viewers, and ESPN is at 1.8 million viewers across 13 telecasts. Prime Video has grown by a huge 83 percent year to year, and ESPN’s games are up 13 percent.

Prime Video has the youngest audience of the NBA’s three national media partners (the same is true for Prime relative to other NFL broadcasters); its median viewer age is a little under 46. ESPN and NBC (not including Peacock viewers) have a median age of about 53 for NBA games.
