I love coffee and espresso, but if I’m not careful, I’ll drink six shots a day (which is why I switched to mushroom coffee about a year ago). So a machine that makes espresso, iced lattes, and cold brew at the press of a button is probably not something I need within arm’s reach. But I can appreciate the convenience (and the aesthetic).

Right now, you can get the De’Longhi Magnifica Duo for $699.95, down from $999.95, at Amazon. That’s a 30% discount, or $300 in savings. This offer is for the silver model; if you change the model/color, the price changes with it.

The Magnifica Duo has 20 preset hot and iced drink recipes, including espresso, drip coffee, iced lattes, and cold brew. It also has a built-in grinder and touchscreen display, so you can start with whole beans and choose your drink without buying a separate grinder or manually pulling a shot. The machine handles the grinding, dosing, and brewing.

The milk frother is automatic, too. De’Longhi’s LatteCrema Hot & Cool system makes both hot and cold milk foam, so your options aren’t limited to a hot cappuccino or hot coffee poured over ice.
