Watch the Fiery New Trailer for ‘Prince: 3121 Las Vegas Live’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

As part of the multi-faceted releases around the reissue of Prince’s 2006 album “3121,” a concert film from the era titled “Prince: 3121 Las Vegas Live” will not only be included in the boxed set, it will open in 500+ theaters nationwide, beginning December 3 for a limited run. Tickets are now on sale.

The 154-minute concert film spans hits, deep cuts, covers and extended jams, and is presented in 4K and Dolby Atmos at equipped theatres. The expanded edition of “3121,” which is arguably the best of his post 1990s albums, arrives December 11 digitally and in 3LP + Blu-ray and 2CD + Blu-ray sets. A live album, “3121 Las Vegas Live,” will also be released on triple-LP crystal blue vinyl and 2CD for Record Store Day Black Friday on November 27.

The expanded digital edition pairs the original 12-track album with 10 additional raw recordings, including acoustic versions of “The Word,” “Love” and “Te Amo Corazón,” alongside “Time of Ur Life,” “The Streets of Panama,” “Another Girl,” “Glass Cutter” and more. The physical editions pair “3121” with a Blu-ray featuring the Las Vegas concert.

Originally released March 21, 2006, through NPG Records and Universal Music, “3121” became Prince’s first album to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and his fourth album to top the chart.
