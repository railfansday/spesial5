MLB Commissioner Rob Manfred expects $4 billion Angels sale to Kroenke to close in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NEW YORK (AP) — Major League Baseball expects the proposed sale of the Los Angeles Angels to Kroenke Sports and Entertainment at a $4 billion evaluation to take place in January.

The team announced Sept. 1 that Arte Moreno, who bought the franchise from The Walt Disney Co. in 2003, intended to sell it to the company that owns the NFL's Los Angeles Rams, Premier League champion Arsenal, the NBA's Denver Nuggets, the NHL's Colorado Avalanche, Major League Soccer's Colorado Rapids and SoFi Stadium.

At the time of the announcement, the Angels said they expected to sale to take place in early 2027.

Baseball Commissioner Rob Manfred said Wednesday an update had been given during this week's owners meetings and the sale was on track to be approved subject to closing.

"We expect a closing just after the first of the year," he said.
