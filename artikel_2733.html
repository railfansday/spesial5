Falcons fans praise Bijan Robinson’s response to getting booed after humiliating defeat vs. Kansas City Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bijan Robinson sympathized with the fans who were present at the Mercedes-Benz Stadium, as they had to witness their team suffer a 13-35 loss at the hands of the Kansas City Chiefs.

The Chiefs' offence completely dominated the Falcons' defence, as Patrick Mahomes threw for 348 yards and made 5 touchdown passes. His 5 touchdowns were the most for him in any game since 2022.

Rookie Emmett Johnson also had a sensational game, as he recorded 22 carries for 101 yards.

The Falcons, on the other hand, were left shell-shocked by what they were seeing, which led the fans to boo their own team, as it was also their third consecutive defeat.

Despite all that has been happening around the team, Bijan Robinson has been brilliant for them. He has recorded 951 rushing yards, which is the third most in the league, behind only Jahmyr Gibbs and James Cook III.

Against the Chiefs, Robinson tried to make a difference, recording 16 carries for 71 yards, while also scoring a touchdown. He was asked to react to the fans booing the home team in his post-game interview, and Robinson had a very straightforward response.

"They put in their hard-earned work every single week to try to make it to these games and it's our job to make it count for them. When they boo us, we deserve it. It's our job to make it better and we're gonna evaluate ourselves this week, so we can be ready to go for the Vikings after the bye week," said Robinson.

The RB's comments seemed to impress the fans, as they showed how much playing for the fans and the team meant to Robinson.

One fan said: "Bro is just genuinely a grade A perfect example of how to be an excellent human." Another added: "Bijan is too good for this team. Pair him with Penix and trade them this offseason."

Someone also stated: "Finally, a strong-willed player who can accept when his team isn't giving the effort they should."

"He'd break the single-season rushing record and yards of scrimmage record on virtually every other team this year," an account user expressed. "God bless you, Bijan. You do so much for this team," a fan remarked.
