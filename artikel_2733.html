Panthers activate WR Xavier Legette from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Panthers got wide receiver Xavier Legette back at practice this week and he will be available for Sunday’s game against the Saints.

Legette was activated from injured reserve on Saturday. He has been out since suffering a knee injury in Week 2.

Legette had two catches for 64 yards before his injury. The Panthers also designated cornerback Mike Jackson, and running back Jonathon Brooks for return from injured reserve this week, but neither one of them will be joining Legette in the lineup.

The Panthers elevated cornerbacks Marco Wilson and Corey Thornton from the practice squad. They provide extra depth with Jackson and Jaycee Horn still on injured reserve and Will Lee (hamstring) listed as questionable to play.
