Jeff Lebby's 2027 Mississippi State recruiting class has fourth decommit in a week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

STARKVILLE – A fourth Mississippi State football player since Nov. 10 decommitted from the Bulldogs' 2027 recruiting class. This time, it was the Bulldogs' top-ranked recruit.

Yoshawn Hudson, a four-star running back from Winona, announced his decommitment on Nov. 17. The Clarion Ledger Dandy Dozen selection had been committed to MSU since June 14.

"After much consideration and prayer, I am taking this opportunity to share that I am decommitting from Mississippi State," Hudson wrote in an X post. "I'd like to thank Coach (Jeff) Lebby, (running backs) Coach (Anthony) Tucker, and the rest of the Mississippi State coaches for the opportunity to play for the Bulldogs. I'd also like to thank the Mississippi State fan base for their support. This wasn't an easy decision."

Since Nov. 10, four-star offensive tackle Li'Marcus Jones, three-star safety Hudson Fuqua and three-star cornerback Julian McDonald have also departed the recruiting class. However, the Bulldogs have also received two commitments from junior college players since then: three-star defensive lineman Jekai Lee and unranked tight end Jourdan Butler.

The Bulldogs (4-6) are on a six-game losing streak in Lebby's third season. They play Tennessee Tech (9-2) at Davis Wade Stadium on Nov. 21 (noon, SEC Network+).

Mississippi State's 15-player recruiting class was ranked No. 57 before Hudson's decommitment, according to the 247Sports Composite, last in the SEC and on pace to be the lowest ranked in program history since rankings began in 2000.

Jones is the second highest-ranked recruit who decommitted, but he was only in MSU's class for one day. The former Callaway player who now plays for Brentwood Academy (Tennessee) flipped from Colorado to Mississippi State only to recommit to Colorado.

"After having a hard time sleeping and thinking on my decision I realize that going back home to Mississippi is not for me," Jones wrote in an X post. "I allowed the media to get to my head about where Coach Prime (Deion Sanders) will be at next year but the staff informed me that they will in fact be their so with that being said I'm an 100% locked in with Coach Prime and the buffs."

Fuqua committed to Mississippi State in November 2025 and McDonald on June 8. They have not committed to new teams.

Four-star quarterback Gavin Hampton became the highest-ranked player in Mississippi State's 2027 class after Hudson's decommitment. There are three other four-stars: safety Kennedy Green, running back Christian Alexander and defensive lineman Jaden Bayonne.
