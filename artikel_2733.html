Sean McVay has at least one positive injury update

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

While Puka Nacua's potentially season-ending surgery and possible ending of his tenure with the Los Angeles Rams has dominated the headlines, there is at least one silver lining from Sean McVay's injury updates this week.

Defensive tackle Kobie Turner is likely set to return following the bye in an all-important NFC clash against the Green Bay Packers. Turner suffered a groin injury back in Week 6—LA's first game against the Arizona Cardinals. Since then, Myles Garrett has also returned to health and has wrecked shop in company with Aaron Donald. Turner should add even more juice to an already formidable front.

The Rams won't get back Poona Ford this year. While opposing offenses have not gashed the Rams on the ground, Turner is an important part of the run defense and should help offset what is a significant long-term loss.

But the news ahead of the Green Bay game was not all positive. Davante Adams, who was rumored to potentially be ready to face off against his former team, will likely not be ready to return in time per McVay. Adams is week-to-week and will be evaluated after the Packers game.

And it's a similar story for tight end Terrance Ferguson. The Rams will activate the former second-round pick before his 21-day window closes, which prevents him from being shutdown for the remainder of the season. He won't be able to return to play in this time frame, however, and will take a spot on the 53-man roster.

The Rams can't really afford to lose to the Packers on Wednesday night. In addition to their NFC West rivals, they are indirectly competing against the entire NFC North in the playoff picture. The race against Green Bay could come down the wire. This is an opportunity for LA to bolster their own position while directly putting the Packers at a disadvantage.

Kobie Turner is a welcome reinforcement for that game. Not having Davante Adams nor Terrance Ferguson in time is an unfortunate development.

LA still needs to find a way to get the job done.
