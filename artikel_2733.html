Carolina Panthers 2026 fan mailbag: Injured/reserve slots, NFC South, Celebrity Look-alikes, and more!

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hello CSR! Welcome to Brian Answers, part two of your weekly Panthers fan mailbag for everyone! I've got a special Thanksgiving edition for you today, as we wait for Monday Night Football. Make sure to check your fantasy football teams today, let's dive right in!

hollywud: How many IR designated to return spots are left for the team this year? I can think of 4 spots that need to be filled, Brooks, BC, Horn & Ickey. Could that be an issue? And Thank all the staffers and commenters on Cat Scratch Reader for all the entertainment…

The Panthers just spent the last of their two remaining designations to return from injured/reserve on Thomas Incoom, a core special teamer. That means they will have one spot left, likely for Jaycee Horn. They'll get two more designations if they actually make the playoffs. They allowed the 21 day return window to lapse on OL Brady Christensen, so he reverted back to IR this week. The window is still open for Jonathon Brooks. As far as OT Ikem Ekwonu goes, he's actually still on the Reserve/Physically Unable to Perform List (PUP). So his return would not count against the 8 player maximum for Injured/Reserve. He could return at any point this season.

SnarkyComet: What are your thoughts on Tremble? Like, are you going to pissed if we don't extend him?

As Mike Kaye stated in the Tweet you linked, I think Tommy Tremble is someone the Panthers have to bring back barring a huge contracts, which I don't think he'd get on the open market. He's finally developed into the player we thought he could be, a hugely effective blocker with receiving upside. He has a rapport with Bryce Young who will be here at least through the end of next year. No reason not to bring him back.

@WTMealey: Lets play the future of QB in NFC South? 3 years from now. For each team give me a ratio same guy/different guy 90/10 50/50 you get the idea if you want to do HC that is cool as well

I'd give the Saints a 50/50 on Tyler Shough and probably on their coach Kellen Moore as well. Both seem pretty solid but three years in the NFL is a long time. For the Falcons, probably 0/100 on the QB, I just don't see them sticking with Michael Penix. Kevin Stefanski for me is a 50/50 just because this is his first year there. For the Buccaneers, I'd say 0/100 on both Baker Mayfield and Todd Bowles. I don't see them both leaving now but I have a hard time seeing either of them with the team in 3 years.

For the Panthers… I'm going with Thanksgiving optimism and saying both Bryce Young and Dave Canales are still here in 3 years, and future-us is thankful we stuck around for the ride.

SmoothC: Is the defense noticeably improved under Wash?

I think the defensive line certainly is. The pass rush has been more of a factor since he took over, especially Princely Umanmielen. Obviously, having a guy like Jaelan Phillips back in the fold helps the defense across the board. The secondary has been a bit more aggressive, with Will Lee having his best game as a pro last week. I'm just not sure if I attribute that to Wash as much as him just developing as a rookie. But I like the simplified defense. It is going to be tested in the coming weeks and its first major exam is going to be stopping the run against the Bucs on Monday. The Ravens did a lot of damage on the ground on Sunday, but we'll see if a concentrated game plan to stop the run against a Tampa Bay team that will run at all costs works out.

HighMiles: Is play calling holding back our offense?

We seemed to be scheming T-Mac and Coker open and it made Bryce look like a superhero. Now that seems to be missing.

Is it because our play calling is too predictable now, or because we are forcing our running game now as the priority, or something else entirely?

I think defensive coordinators now have a better sense of what the Panthers are trying to do on offense. Remember, while this is still technically a scheme under Dave Canales's umbrella, this was a new play caller with Brad Idzik. So I think between having some film and the offensive line struggling, the defenses have come up with a better game plan. While I love Idzik's penchant to push the ball down field to Tetairoa McMillan and Jalen Coker, Bryce has to have the time to deliver the football. And defenses know they want to do that now. So between getting pressure and disrupting the routes in man coverage, it hasn't been quite as explosive recently.

Happy Birthday Rob! I'm calling a win because the NFC South is weird and Faker Mayfield can't throw so good anymore. Don't bet money though, never bet on the Panthers.

bulletooftony: Brian, how would you handle the LT situation? Draft one, possibly resign Icky (if he doesn't walk), slide Freeling to LT and draft a RT, other?

I'd probably bring back Ikem Ekwonu and then figure out which one on him and Monroe Freeling make the most sense at left tackle. I would also draft one, maybe not with a first round pick but any other pick would make sense. They'll need the depth since I suspect this is Taylor Moton's swan song either this season or next season. I'd spend the free agency capital on the interior of the offensive line since I expect center and right guard to look very different come the start of next season.

That's all for this week, Panthers fans! Enjoy your Thanksgiving, and stick with us on Monday for a hopeful beating of the Tampa Bay Bucs! KEEP POUNDING!
