Florida’s Todd Golden showers Houston with ultimate praise before Players Era showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Florida and Houston took care of business in the Players Era 8 Tournament on Tuesday, beating Notre Dame and Rutgers, respectively, at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas.

The No. 1 Gators defeated the Fighting Irish, 86-78, to improve to 5-0, while the No. 8 Cougars upended the Scarlet Knights, 85-73, to climb to 4-0.

Something's gotta give between Florida and Houston, as they will battle on Wednesday. Not to mention, their all-time series is tied at 2-2.

Ahead of the big-time showdown, Gators coach Todd Golden was effusive in his praise of the Cougars, as reported by PaperCity Magazine's Chris Baldwin.

"That's why I wanted to come to this event. To play some of the best teams in America, and we believe Houston to be one of the best programs in America, with what Coach (Kelvin) Sampson and his staff have done. They're not the same as they've been in the past, but they have very similar qualities," said Golden.

"I think it's going to be a great test. Obviously, it's going to be a physical game, and we have to step up to the challenge in that way."

It will be a rematch of the national title game in 2025, when Florida came back from a 12-point deficit to beat Houston, 65-63. Golden, then 38 years old, became the second-youngest coach to rule the NCAA Tournament.

He is aware that the Cougars will be hungry for revenge.

"They know us, and we know them. It's going to be kind of mano a mano that way to see who the tougher team's going to be, both mentally and physically," added Golden.

Florida will lean on Thomas Haugh, Alex Condon, Rueben Chinyelu, while Houston will parade Dedan Thomas Jr., Delrecco Gillespie, and Mercy Miller.
