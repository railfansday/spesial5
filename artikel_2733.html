Why Cade Smith Could Have Won AL Reliever of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cleveland Guardians closer Cade Smith is the runner-up for the American League Reliever of the Year Award, as voted on by the Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA). This is the first year for the award.

Oct 12, 2026; St. Petersburg, Florida, USA; Cleveland Guardians pitcher Cade Smith (36) reacts with catcher Patrick Bailey (16) after defeating the Tampa Bay Rays in game one of the ALCS for the 2026 MLB Playoffs at Tropicana Field. Mandatory Credit: Kim Klement Neitzel-Imagn Images

Smith finished behind Toronto Blue Jays closer Louis Varland, who won the inaugural award on Wednesday night. Although Tampa Bay Rays closer Bryan Baker finished third in the voting, he won the Mariano Rivera AL Reliever of the Year Award, which is voted on by a panel of nine retired relief pitchers. The Rivera Award has been given annually since 2014 and features legendary Hall of Famer voters such as Trevor Hoffman, Dennis Eckersley, and Billy Wagner.

Varland, who finished with the highest fWAR and bWAR among AL relievers, earned 16 first-place votes, with Smith securing seven first-place votes and Baker receiving six first-place votes. Varland accrued 146 total points, with Smith receiving 133 and Baker receiving 111. Boston Red Sox closer Aroldis Chapman finished fourth in the voting with 36 points, and Houston Astros closer Josh Hader received a single first-place vote despite missing the first two months of the season with a bicep injury.

Still, Smith didn't leave Wednesday night without some hardware. He was named to the All-MLB Second Team, along with Baker. Varland and San Diego Padres' closer Mason Miller, who won both iterations of the NL Reliever of the Year Award, were named to the First Team.

The 27-year-old right-hander helped lead the Guardians to a division title and played a pivotal role in the team advancing to the American League Championship Series.

In his third professional season, Smith was second in the American League with a 2.6 fWAR while pitching 74 innings. He produced a 1.95 ERA (2.18 FIP) and saved 41 games, which tied for the league lead with Baker. He struck out 13.01 batters per nine innings, which was second in the AL. Also, he ranked fourth in K/BB ratio, producing 4.65 strikeouts per walk allowed. Per Baseball Savant's Pitching Run Value, his +12 PRV ranked in the 80th percentile amongst all pitchers. His chase rate (33.7%) and whiff rate (34.5%) were elite.

Previous winners of the AL Reliever of the Year (the Mariano Rivera Award, not the new BBWAA honor) include Aroldis Chapman (Boston Red Sox in 2025 and New York Yankees in 2019), Emmanuel Clase (Cleveland Guardians in 2024 and 2022), Félix Bautista (Baltimore Orioles in 2023), and Liam Hendriks (Chicago White Sox in 2021 and Oakland Athletics in 2020).

Smith was originally drafted in the 16th round of the 2017 draft by the Minnesota Twins but did not sign. He re-entered the 2020 draft and was not drafted. He would sign with Cleveland as a free agent. His rise from undrafted player to All-MLB selection is rare.

Smith relied on a four-seam fastball that was effective against both left-handed and right-handed batters. His fastball yielded a .213/.274/.316 slash line and produced a 29.9% whiff rate. His splitter completely neutralized left-handed batters, with a slash line of .092/.124/.092 and a 45.3% whiff rate. His arsenal is rounded out by a sweeper that he threw 10% of the time that yielded a .133 xBA and a 35.4% whiff rate.

Smith has placed himself directly in the conversation as one of the best closers in the sport. Dating back to 2024, Smith is second in the majors in fWAR, producing 8.0 wins above replacement in 223 innings, which is also among the leaders for relief pitchers.

During the 2026 postseason, Smith anchored a stout Cleveland bullpen. He pitched 11 1/3 innings and allowed a 2.38 ERA (2.76 xFIP). He produced 11.12 K/9 and 1.59 BB/9 and was one of the most dominant relievers to pitch in the playoffs. Only Abner Uribe of the Milwaukee Brewers pitched in more postseason games this year than Smith.

Smith will look to continue anchoring a sturdy Cleveland bullpen as they aim for another postseason in 2027.
