Patriots’ Drake Maye links up with Tom Brady for incredible photo before Packers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

On Sunday afternoon, the New England Patriots will hit the field at home for a big game against the Green Bay Packers. In town to call the game for FOX is Patriots legend Tom Brady, who won six Super Bowls with the franchise during his iconic run there.

Now, the reins are held by quarterback Drake Maye, and before the game, the two linked up for a photo that will have Patriots fans' hearts beaming.

It didn't take long for Maye to make it to his first Super Bowl in New England, doing so in his second year at the helm last season and losing to the Seattle Seahawks.

He has been able to mostly shake off a rough start to the season this year and lead the Patriots to a solid spot in the AFC playoff picture, and Sunday's test against the Packers should provide a good test for him.

Meanwhile, Patriots fans had plenty of reason to celebrate this weekend when star tight end Rob Gronkowski was inducted into the team's Hall of Fame in a ceremony that Brady was in attendance for.

Maye and the current iteration of the Patriots are trying to reach the heights that Brady and Gronkowski were able to summit together as teammates during the 2000s and 2010s.

In any case, kickoff between the Patriots and Packers is set for Sunday at 4:25 pm ET from Foxborough. Up next for New England will be a game next Sunday against the Detroit Lions in Germany.
