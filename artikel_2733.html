Capitalizing on motion and play-action inside the Jaguars offense

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Jaguars' offense has been exceptional this season. That's not really a secret.

Seemingly anything and everything Liam Coen implements strikes gold.

Among those things is the Jaguars' success with motion, play under-center, and with play-action.

In 2026, Trevor Lawrence is seeing career-highs in use of motion (75.2%), play under-center (45.7%), and play-action (26.8%). The Jaguars just so happen to be about the best team in the NFL in all three departments.

Jacksonville's use of motion over the years is a confusing one. Former head coach Doug Pederson stayed pretty consistent in his use of motion, despite the league seeing a dramatic uptick.

In 2024, his final season in Jacksonville, the Jaguars ranked 31st at just 47.9%.

Since then, Liam Coen has nearly doubled that figure in two seasons. The Jags are now seeing pre-snap motion on 75% of snaps, the fourth-highest mark in the league.

As a whole, the team ranks third in yards per game (281.3), third in yards per play (6.2), and second in EPA per play with motion.

It's no surprise then to see Lawrence has the second-most passing yards in the league with 1,613 yards. On the ground, the Jags rank sixth in yards (873) when utilizing motion.

From under-center, Trevor Lawrence is completing 73.3% of his passes for 665 yards, six touchdowns, zero interceptions, and an NFL-best 142.7 passer rating.

His 11.08 yards per attempt, 73.3% completion rate, 63.3% success rate, and +0.59 EPA per play all rank either first or second in the NFL.

The Jags' ability to run the ball from under-center certainly helps the cause.

Bhayshul Tuten ranks fifth in the NFL with 509 yards gained from under-center, according to Fantasy Points.

It's that success that makes the Jaguars' use of play-action so deadly, a new tool at Trevor Lawrence's disposal.

When using play-action, Lawrence has completed 72% of his passes for 797 yards, six touchdowns and zero interceptions, and another NFL-best passer rating of 133.0. He again is Top-2 in yards per attempt (10.6), success rate (64%), and EPA per play (+0.552).

It's this marriage of coaching and talent between Liam Coen and Trevor Lawrence that has made this Jaguars' offense so scary. Yes, Trevor Lawrence is playing the best football of his career and maybe in the entire NFL.

But, it's also true that Liam Coen is using anything and everything at his disposal to put Lawrence and the rest of his players in the best position to succeed.
