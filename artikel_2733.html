Aaron Rodgers-less Steelers end chaotic week with resounding win over Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Less than 24 hours after their former head coach called the Steelers' present a "dumpster fire," Mason Rudolph and Mike McCarthy — the two members of the organization in the spotlight the most after Aaron Rodgers' scathing rant on Wednesday — proved they might survive the chaos after all.

With Rodgers now released, with signs and pictures of No. 8 removed around Acrisure Stadium, with Pittsburgh just over 48 hours removed from what Prime Video broadcaster Al Michaels described as the "shot heard 'round the football world," the Steelers delivered a resounding statement victory, crushing the eight-win Broncos 27-5 on Friday to move to 7-4.

Rudolph completed 9 of 15 passes for 142 yards and a touchdown, doing what was needed to facilitate a fast start for Pittsburgh. T.J. Watt and Jalen Ramsey returned a fumble and an interception for a touchdown, respectively, and the Steelers defense silenced Bo Nix.

Maybe it was the initial roar from the crowd, the one that followed Rudolph's introduction as the starting quarterback Friday, that suggested the Steelers — and, by extension, head coach Mike McCarthy — might survive. Maybe it was the first touchdown pass from Rudolph to DK Metcalf, who'd been ejected from the game last weekend in what ultimately served as one of the final turning points of the Rodgers era in Pittsburgh.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Mason Rudolph (2) celebrates with Max Iheanachor (71) during an NFL football game in Pittsburgh, Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. AP Photo/David Dermer

On Monday, after the Steelers dropped a second consecutive game, McCarthy reiterated that Rodgers was his quarterback. One day later, Rudolph was named the Steelers' starter, and Rodgers was listed as inactive in an X post at 11:10 p.m.

Then, on Wednesday, Rodgers went on "The Pat McAfee Show" and claimed that McCarthy slept during meetings, broke down a halftime huddle with 1-2-3 Packers and missed walkthroughs. Rodgers was released by the Steelers later that day, and the organization has since reiterated its belief in McCarthy.

With their win Friday, the Steelers demonstrated that the belief was warranted. They jumped out to a quick 14-0 lead behind the touchdowns from Watt and Metcalf, and even after the Broncos got on the board with a safety and crept closer with a field goal, Pittsburgh managed to create some breathing room before halftime. Chris Boswell connected on a field goal, and Ramsey returned an errant pass from Nix 36 yards for a touchdown to make it 24-5.

Neither offense managed much in the second half. Nix struggled the whole game, and Rudolph didn't need to do much more. By that point, the Steelers had already made their statement. By that point, they'd already produced a sample that showed that perhaps the organization isn't scrambling after all. That maybe they can rebound from the chaos Rodgers left them with.

They're now 7-4. They'll be tied with the Bengals atop the AFC North if Cincinnati falls Sunday. They ended a week of questions with a resounding answer.
