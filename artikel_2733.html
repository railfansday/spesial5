LeBron James out for 76ers on Monday vs. Nets due to low-back management; Joel Embiid questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

LeBron James will sit out the Philadelphia 76ers' matchup with the Brooklyn Nets on Monday.

He is listed on the NBA injury report as out "low back/sciatica management," which has been the reason he's missed three of Philadelphia's previous 14 games this season.

Meanwhile, Joel Embiid is listed as questionable for Monday's game with "right foot plantar fascia injury management," which has also sidelined him for three games thus far.

The 76ers are playing the second of back-to-back games after Sunday's 131-124 win over the Indiana Pacers. James played 33 minutes in that contest, scoring 15 points with 7 rebounds and 11 assists.

In his 11 games this season, James is averaging 15.1 points, ranking fourth on the team in scoring behind Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Embiid. He leads the Sixers with 6.7 assists and ranks second with 7.5 rebounds per game.

At 11-3 going into Monday's contest, the 76ers have the second-best record in the Eastern Conference behind the 11-2 Boston Celtics. That mark has them tied for the fourth-best record in the NBA with the Houston Rockets. Philadelphia has won three of its past four games.

Following Monday's contest, the Sixers will have an off-day before visiting the Orlando Magic (5-7) on Wednesday. The team then has two days off until a Saturday matchup with the Milwaukee Bucks (1-13).
