NBA Cup bracket watch: Who's in, who's out and what every Friday game means

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 2026 NBA Cup is set for an action-packed final day of Group Play, featuring 11 games. While one team has already secured its place in the next round, the remaining contenders are gearing up for win-or-go-home matchups, each vying for a spot in the knockout stage.

The Dallas Mavericks are the lone team to have booked their ticket to the next round of the NBA Cup. After the Houston Rockets defeated the Phoenix Suns on Wednesday, the Mavericks clinched the best record in Western Conference Group A. As a result, they can approach Friday's matchup against the Suns with confidence, knowing their spot in the knockout round is secure regardless of the outcome.

Beyond the quest for bragging rights, there are substantial financial incentives at stake in the NBA Cup tournament. Players advancing to the eight-team knockout round earn approximately $50,000 each, with bonus amounts increasing at each subsequent stage. Members of the winning team can expect a payout exceeding $500,000 per player, while the runners-up will receive over $200,000 apiece.
