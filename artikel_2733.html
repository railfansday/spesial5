Two Washington Huskies Ruled Out For Home Finale Vs Indiana

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Washington will officially be without veteran running back Trey Cooley and key special teams gunner Devin Hyde this Saturday when the team hosts No. 8 Indiana in its final home game of 2026.

Cooley, a sixth-year transfer addition for the Huskies this past offseason, is set to miss his second consecutive game after suffering a concussion late in UW's 28-25 defeat against Penn State on November 7. Prior to that, the 5-foot-11, 210-pound Raleigh, North Carolina native suffered a shoulder injury in the fourth quarter at Purdue when he ran for a season-high 113 yards, half of which came on an early fourth quarter 53-yard touchdown run, in the 38-26 victory over the Boilermakers.

Coach Jedd Fisch said Monday that the ex-Troy, Georgia Tech, and Louisville runner would miss Saturday's game against the Hoosiers as he remained in concussion protocol, and made it official Friday as Cooley was downgraded to out after being previously listed as doubtful on the final Big Ten availability report prior to the last game day report that will be released two hours before kickoff Saturday.

Hyde, a 6-foot-6, 225-pound reserve edge rusher and special teamer, had made 23 straight appearances in his first two seasons for the Huskies before being ruled out for the Week 11 contest against IU. The Menlo-Atherton star and former three-star recruit in the 2025 recruiting class has been listed on UW's availability report the past two weeks due to an undisclosed injury.

While Hyde and Cooley won't make an appearance in the 2026 home finale, UW is expected to have veteran edge rusher Logan George, an Ohio State transfer, available again opposite fourth-year starter Jacob Lane.

George has only been listed on the Huskies' availability reports twice this season prior to this weekend, first against Minnesota and last Saturday against the Spartans. In both instances the Pocatello, Idaho native ultimately suited up for second-year defensive coordinator Ryan Walters after missing UW's 48-16 victory against Eastern Washington.

On the visiting sideline will be two key Indiana starters on defense, fourth-year cornerback AJ Harris and third-year linebacker Rolijah Hardy, who were also ruled out for the Hoosiers' road test at UW with separate injuries. Harris was also listed on IU's availability report leading up to last Saturday's game against USC before he was deemed healthy enough to play in the 27-13 win for Curt Cignetti, which saw the ex-Penn State standout make 7 tackles and log 53 snaps against the Trojans.

Hardy did not play for the Hoosiers in their Week 10 victory and will miss his third straight game after being a crucial defender this season, with 5.5 tackles for loss over the first eight games in 2026.
