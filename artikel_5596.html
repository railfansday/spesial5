Accrete Signs Data Agreement with TenderAlpha to Deepen Supply Chain Risk Insight for Federal Customers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

NEW YORK, Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Accrete, the cognitive infrastructure company building Knowledge Engines for autonomous work, today announced that it has signed an agreement with TenderAlpha, a global provider of government contracting and supply chain data. The agreement brings TenderAlpha data into Accrete's Argus for Supply Chain Influence, giving defense and civilian agency customers greater visibility into the companies, suppliers and component parts behind the products and systems they rely on.

Across the government, supply chain visibility has become a priority. The National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) requires closer scrutiny of defense suppliers, and civilian agencies face growing pressure to understand risk in critical food, agricultural and medical product supply chains. Agencies need a clearer view of the sub-tier relationships and sources where risk often goes unseen.

"Supply chain risk isn't a data aggregation problem. It's a judgment and tacit knowledge problem," said Prashant Bhuyan, Founder and CEO of Accrete. "Human expertise doesn't scale fast enough to get ahead of foreign influence threats, and burying analysts under mountains of disconnected data only makes it worse. Accrete's Knowledge Engine captures and scales the tacit knowledge of individual analysts and the organization itself. It grounds reasoning models in that judgment so they become trusted agents. With TenderAlpha's data in Argus, those agents can do in minutes what would ordinarily take teams of exceptional analysts months or years."

"Our customers need to understand supply chain risk faster and at a deeper level than ever before," said Bill Wall, CEO of Accrete AI Government. "Adding TenderAlpha data gives us greater insight into the obscured vulnerabilities and threats inherent in complex global supply chains. It gives defense and civilian agency teams a robust picture of who is really behind the products and components they depend on, so they can act before a vulnerability becomes a problem."

"Exposing complex supply chain risk requires connecting the dots across disparate layers that are very rarely combined," said Martin Tsanov, CEO and co-founder of TenderAlpha. "The fusion of TenderAlpha's global B2B relationship and trade data with government contracting and subcontracting data within Argus' cutting-edge multi-tier graph architecture equips national security agencies with the comprehensiveness and visibility needed to proactively identify hidden dependencies and secure critical supply chains."

The TenderAlpha agreement is part of Accrete's ongoing investment in the data and analytics that federal customers need to protect the Defense Industrial Base and the critical supply chains Americans depend on. Customers using Argus for Supply Chain Influence will gain access to these expanded insights in early 2027.

Accrete builds cognitive infrastructure for government and enterprise customers. Founded in 2017, Accrete's Knowledge Engine platform unifies fragmented systems, siloed data, tacit knowledge, and decision-making into a continuously learning universal system of intelligence. Knowledge Engines give agents the shared context to reason, collaborate, decide, and act across the full complexity of an organization. As AI makes intelligence abundant, judgment is the scarce resource, and Accrete's Knowledge Engines encode the expert judgment organizations accumulate over decades so agents can be trusted with consequential decisions. Accrete's customers span Fortune 500 enterprises as well as U.S. defense and national security agencies. From commercial operations to national security missions, Accrete's platform enables expert agents that deliver superintelligence designed for high-stakes environments. Accrete AI Government LLC, based in Alexandria, VA, is the federal subsidiary of New York-based Accrete, Inc.

Founded in 2014, TenderAlpha delivers high-quality, structured data products designed to support investment analysis, risk assessment, and market intelligence. TenderAlpha serves financial institutions, asset managers, and risk-rating and research professionals, offering deep insights into global G2B and B2B transactional trends. For more information, visit www.tenderalpha.com.
