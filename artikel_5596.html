Under the extreme coordinated institutional restriction scenario outlined above, losing key multi-month support levels would likely trigger powerful measured-move downside extensions and multi-cycle Fibonacci retracements from the prior advance.

Momentum indicators currently sit in neutral-to-weak territory with room to deteriorate into the deeply oversold readings typical of sharp risk-off moves.

Key nearer-term supports sit near $76,000–$77,000 and $72,000–$74,000; failure to hold those zones would keep the broader structure vulnerable.

Within crypto, a series of additional large-scale exchange or protocol security incidents, prolonged regulatory uncertainty in key markets, fading ETF inflows, or a broader rotation out of digital assets into traditional fixed income and commodities would create a challenging liquidity environment that weighs heavily on Bitcoin.
