Man Utd Identify 'Fantastic' Collins as Leading Centre-Back Target

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United already have a preferred centre-back target ahead of the January window, and it's not Calvin Bassey. Mauricio Pochettino already knows he needs a new centre-back in the winter, and Brentford's Nathan Collins is emerging as the main target.

It was a bruising introduction to life at United for Pochettino, who saw his side concede four against rivals Liverpool on Sunday, although they were not helped by Leny Yoro's red card and Dominik Szoboszlai scoring the resulting free-kick.

That was always going to make life difficult at Anfield, especially for a United side that is bang out of form, only winning one of their first 11 Premier League games. The Red Devils are now sat in 19th place and with their ambitions from before the season likely already beyond reach.

But Pochettino's job is to start turning things around, and after mistakes were made during the summer transfer window, the board knows it must back the new boss with some new recruits. The centre-back, left-back and striker positions are of paramount importance in this upcoming window.

United are lacking in depth at the centre-back position, heavily relying upon young Yoro, while Lisandro Martinez has been in poor form this season, and Harry Maguire is not getting any younger.

Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey has been linked ahead of January, which is not going to be an easy window to do business in. Clubs are not going to be keen on the idea of selling a starting centre-back, so prices are not likely to be kind.

“Nathan is playing at a level in the Premier League and showing what a fantastic player he is.” - John O'Shea on Nathan Collins

But it seems Bassey will be secondary to Brentford star Collins, who has established himself as one of the better defenders in the league. The Daily Mail report that Collins is emerging as the leading candidate ahead of January as things stand.

The 25-year-old has been central to Brentford's efforts over the last couple of years, and he is just as important at international level, already racking up 41 caps for his nation.

He has also become a real leader and was made captain by Brentford for this season, with Keith Andrews explaining at the time: “I've seen him really develop as a human being. He's very, very mature for his age.

"There's a lot of experience for his age and his standards are very, very high. He's very ambitious and he has the full respect of not just the players, but all the staff. We have a lot of leaders in our squad but Nathan, for me, was the right decision.”

Collins is under contract until 2029 as things stand, and Brentford also have the option of extending that deal by a further two years. It's likely the Bees would command big money to sell, but it's likely the Irishman would still be a cheaper signing than Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite, who is reportedly valued at around £70million.
