The New Oura Ring 5 Is $100 Off for Black Friday—and I Wouldn’t Hesitate to Hit ‘Add to Cart’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Fingers crossed your size hasn’t sold out already.

It’s never a bad time to invest in your health, but right now is a particularly good one. The biggest shopping holiday of the year, Black Friday, is officially live, and it comes with a deal I didn’t see coming: The brand-new Oura Ring 5, which only came out in June, is $100 off in every single color. New models don’t usually see big discounts their first holiday season, so this is a very pleasant surprise. And trust me when I say Oura isn’t one of those wellness brands that’s all hype and no substance. Just read our Oura Ring review or ask, ahem, the entire Cosmo shopping team.

The Ring 5 does everything the Oura Ring 4 does, just in a much thinner, chicer package. It’s about 40% smaller than its predecessor, so it looks more like a regular ring than a piece of wearable tech, and it still gets up to nine days of battery life compared to the Ring 4’s eight. It keeps tabs on your steps, activity, sleep, stress levels, heart rate, temperature, and cycle, and it keeps all that trackable data in a convenient mobile app.

It also stacks with the rest of your jewelry so effortlessly, whether you’re a gold or silver girly. There are six colorways, and every one of them is $100 off right now: Silver and Black are $299 (previously $399), and Gold, Deep Rose, Stealth, and Brushed Silver are $399 (originally $499). I wouldn’t wait until Cyber Monday hoping for a better deal. Sizes are selling out fast—in fact, more than 2,000 people bought one on Amazon in the past month alone. Waiting is just a gamble, but hey, a good deal is a good deal.

If you don’t need the newest model, the Oura Ring 4 is still on sale too, starting at $249 (previously $349) on Amazon. Many of the Ring 5’s new app features are rolling out to older models, so unless a slimmer ring is a priority, the Ring 4 still does the job for $50 less. It also comes in more sizes (4 through 15, compared to the Ring 5’s 6 through 13), so it’s the one to get if yours falls outside that range.

One shopper called the Ring 4 a “game-changer” as someone living with chronic illnesses and said they’re able to “better understand [their] body, track [their] patterns, and recognize changes.” Another added: “As a woman, the cycle tracking alone has been incredibly valuable. Being able to see how different phases of my cycle affect my sleep, recovery, stress levels, energy, and overall readiness has helped me make much better decisions about work, exercise, and self-care.” That’s a pretty compelling argument alone!
