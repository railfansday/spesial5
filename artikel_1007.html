England Lions hit record score as McKinney runs riot

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Durham opener Ben McKinney smashes 15 fours and 10 sixes in his eye-catching century as England Lions make it five wins from five on West Indies tour; James Rew also scores a ton as Lions eclipse the Ben Duckett-inspired 425-1 the Lions amassed against Sri Lanka A in Canterbury in 2016

England Lions thumped their highest List A score of all time - and the fifth best by any side - as they piled on 487-5 from 50 overs against West Indies A in the second unofficial ODI in St Kitts.

The total, in which Durham opener Ben McKinney looted 145 from 65 deliveries - reaching his century from just 43 balls - and James Rew scored 107 not out from 56, eclipsed the 425-1 the Lions racked up against Sri Lanka A at Canterbury a decade ago.

In that match, current England Test and ODI opener Ben Duckett plundered 220 not out from 131 balls and Kent's Daniel Bell-Drummond 171 from 139 after Dawid Malan had fallen for 23.

Ten years on, McKinney and Rew were the stars, with the former smoking 15 fours and 10 sixes and the latter - who made his Test debut against New Zealand at The Kia Oval in June, keeping wicket with Jamie Smith on paternity leave - struck 15 fours and two sixes.

McKinney was linked with a Test call-up earlier this summer when it became clear that England were going to drop Zak Crawley but the spot as Duckett's partner at the top of the order went to the youngster's Durham team-mate Emilio Gay instead.

McKinney and Rew flogged a bowling attack that included full West Indies international Oshane Thomas - the seamer has played 46 white-ball games for his nation, most recently in February 2024.

The Lions won the match by 276 runs after skittling their hosts for 211 in reply in Basseterre, thanks chiefly to a five-wicket haul from Hampshire quick Eddie Jack, who has been included in England's T20I squad to take on Australia later this month.

Glamorgan leg-spinner Mason Crane bagged three wickets.

Earlier on with the bat, Lions captain Dan Lawrence - in England's Test squad for the winter trip to South Africa - chipped in with 76 from 56 deliveries and Hampshire youngster Ben Mayes notched an unbeaten 58 from 46.

The Lions have won all five of their matches on the tour so far, following a 3-0 sweep in the T20 series by taking a 2-0 lead in the five-match one-day international leg.

The remaining 50-over matches are also in St Kitts, on Wednesday, Saturday and next Monday.

Jack, Rew's younger brother Thomas - who made 22 in Monday's run-fest - and James Coles are the uncapped players in the senior England T20 international squad to battle Australia in five matches from November 21, with the opening fixture in Melbourne.

Jack, Thomas Rew and Coles will fly out to Australia after the third unofficial ODI against West Indies A.
