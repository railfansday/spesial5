Candace Cameron Bure Disputes Jodie Sweetin's 'Full House' Residuals Claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Candace Cameron Bure has some questions regarding Jodie Sweetin’s recent claims that Full House residuals are far from impressive.

“I actually saw that, and she said she doesn’t get much now, and I was like, ‘Jodie, you got to check with your agent or you got to call your bank ’cause I still get residuals,’” Cameron Bure, 50, said on the Tuesday, November 24, episode of the “McBride Rewind” podcast.

When podcast host Josh McBride asked whether the residuals are enough to pay for a mortgage or rent, Cameron Bure said every check is different.

“I mean, depending on what it is, they are,” she explained. “They’re substantial. I think it’s like quarterly.”

Following an April appearance on “The McBride Rewind,” Sweetin, 44, made headlines for revealing how much she received from a recent residual check for her starring role on Full House.

“I got a one-cent check the other day,” Sweetin said. “There’s no syndication anymore because it’s all in streaming. Who gets paid for that? Nobody gets paid for that.”

She continued, “Honey, I drive my 2023 used Hyundai Sonata that I love. I rent my house. I have credit cards that are maxed out. I live a normal life. There are moments when you’re like, ‘This is going well,’ and there are times when you’re like, ‘I need a day job.’”

While Cameron Bure acknowledged that every actor will have a different experience, she is confident main cast members from Full House should be receiving some fair compensation.

At the same time, the Special Forces winner pointed out that she has a large body of work that dates back to before she played D.J. Tanner from 1987 to 1995.

“Even pre-Full House, post-Full House and then all of the Hallmark movies too. I would say I at least get a few every single week,” she shared. “And yes, some of them are for two cents. If I did a commercial way back when and for some reason it just ran somewhere or I had done voice-over work and you get some that are, like, $7, $6. I do get tons and tons of those.”

Cameron Bure added, “But the checks for Full House are still substantial. I’m always happy when I open that check and deposit it.”
