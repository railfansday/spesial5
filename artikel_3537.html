‘Silo’ and ‘Breaking Bad’ Stars Join Apple TV’s New Godzilla Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

With a purposeful grimace and a terrible sound, another MonsterVerse series is rising from the depths. The series, a spin-off of Monarch: Legacy of Monsters, will star the father-son duo of Kurt Russell and Wyatt Russell, and has recruited a bevy of intriguing guest stars. Now, it's recruited two titans of the silver and small screens: they're not exactly King Kong and Godzilla, but they're bound to make an impact when they drop into the new show.

The series, which is still untitled, will center around Lee Shaw, a founding member of the monster-tracking organization Monarch. As in Monarch: Legacy of Monsters, he'll be played by Wyatt Russell in the past and Kurt Russell in the present day. The series will center around Shaw being sent behind enemy lines in 1984 as the Cold War is threatening to heat up; he's on the trail of a Soviet plan to unleash a monstrous kaiju that could change the course of history. Joining the Russells in the series are Corey Stoll (Ant-Man), Ralph Ineson (The Witch), Millie Brady (Surface), Pilou Asbaek (Foundation), Nyasha Hatendi (Seven Dials), Connie Nielsen (Gladiator), and Stana Katic (Castle).

Now, the Legendary Entertainment series is adding two notable actors as guest stars: The Shawshank Redemption's Tim Robbins and Breaking Bad's Giancarlo Esposito. Robbins is no stranger to Apple TV, the MonsterVerse's streaming home, as he currently stars in their mind-bending science-fiction hit Silo; Esposito, meanwhile, can currently be seen in The Only Living Pickpocket in New York and Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother and heard in The Cat in the Hat. Character details for both characters are being kept hidden in the Hollow Earth for now. The series, which is showrun by Joby Harold (Obi-Wan Kenobi), does not yet have a release date.

The two biggest stars of the MonsterVerse are set to collide once more on March 26, 2027 in Godzilla x Kong: Supernova, which will pit them against a threat from outer space. Meanwhile, Monarch: Legacy of Monsters has not yet been renewed for a third season, but earlier this week we learned that it's in development, with the search now on for a new creative team for the series.

The Lee Shaw MonsterVerse series is in development; no release date has yet been announced. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
