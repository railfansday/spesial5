Boxer Lee Reeves Dead at 31 After Suffering Brain Injury During Fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pro boxer Lee Reeves has died at the age of 31, months after he suffered a traumatic brain injury during a fight.

Reeves had been in a coma since a bout against Gary Cully in Dublin on August 1. He died at University Hospital in Limerick, Ireland, on Monday, November 16.

The boxer lost consciousness during the 10th round of his fight against Cully, 30. Reeves received medical attention in the ring and was then taken to a nearby hospital, where he was described as being in “critical but stable condition.”

Reeves was transported from Dublin to Limerick in October in an effort to get him closer to his family. He died “with loved ones by his side,” according to The Athletic.

The late boxer’s trainer, Jonathan O’Brien, shared a series of emotional social media posts after Reeves’ death.

“Goodbye son sleep tight you fought so hard till the end,” O’Brien shared via Instagram on Monday. “We love you so much rest in peace with your mam and brother waiting at the heavenly gates look over your sisters and your father give them the strength to get true this we love you Lee 🥹💔🥹🙏🫶🏼.”

In another post on Tuesday, November 17, O’Brien wrote, “I’ll see you again Son 🥹.”

O’Brien’s wife, Emma, also remembered the late boxer on social media.

“We will never forget you 💔,” she shared via Instagram on Monday, adding, “I’ll miss seeing your beautiful face pop up on my phone, I’ll miss hearing your wise powerful and always positive words 😔 You fought your hardest and you did yourself and everyone proud 💯 like you always have 💪🏼 El Champo your legacy will live on forever 🇮🇪.”

Cully, the boxer who Reeves was fighting, offered his condolences after news of his former opponent’s death.

“We shared the ring back in August but we [had] been going back for years,” Cully shared via Instagram on Monday. “I still remember the stadium days when I used to watch Lee in the nationals the weight above me and always be impressed by his fights and his flashy gear 😂.”

He added, “What a character and a gent. It’s never been nothing [but] love between us champ can’t really believe I’m writing this but RIP brother ❤️.”

Queensbury Promotions founder and promoter Frank Warren called Reeves’ death “heartbreaking.”

“Lee was a fine young man and a credit to the sport,” Warren wrote via X on Monday. “He will be sorely missed, and my thoughts and prayers are with his family and all who loved him.”
