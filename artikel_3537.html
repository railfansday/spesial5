Elizabeth Hurley Wows in Red-Hot Naked Dress on Date Night Billy Ray Cyrus

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Elizabeth Hurley and boyfriend Billy Ray Cyrus turned the red carpet into their own personal date night runway.

The couple held hands as they posed for photographs at the Saturday, November 14, Baby2Baby Gala Presented by Paul Mitchell in West Hollywood, California and commanded attention with their bold looks.

Hurley, 61, stunned in a Monique Lhuillier embroidered tulle gown featuring a high neck and long sleeves, with sheer fabric and red floral embellishments. Underneath the dress, Hurley opted for a red one-piece. She styled her hair down in loose waves, completing the look with a silver clutch and silver earrings.

Not to be outdone, Cyrus, 65, donned a black velvet jacket with a plunging neckline. He accessorized with silver rings and two cross necklaces as well as a pair of sunglasses.

Hurley’s son, Damian, also smiled for photographs with the pair on the red carpet of the event led by co CEOs Kelly Sawyer Patricof and Norah Weinstein. Damian, 24, wore a cream velvet jacket with an open shirt, revealing a cross necklace on his chest. (Hurley shared her son with ex Steve Bing, who died in 2020.)

Hurley and Cyrus’ initially met while filming the 2022 holiday movie Christmas in Paradise and later reconnected, making their romance public in 2025. Since unveiling her relationship with Cyrus, Hurley has been candid about their families merging.

“Well, it works very well, I think,” Hurley said on an October episode of the “Off Air with Jane & Fi” podcast of the pair’s blended family. “When I dated a previous boyfriend of mine, Shane [Warne] — who sadly, of course, isn’t with us anymore — he had three kids and they were all sort of the same age as Damian and then a few years above, but they were kids.”

She continued, “They were at school. There were still school runs, school holidays. There was an awful lot to juggle. And now, Billy and I are empty nesters. All our children are independent and Damian is the youngest of all of them.”

While Hurley is a mother to Damian, Billy Ray is the father of son Christopher, 34, with ex-girlfriend Kristin Luckey and kids Miley, 33, Braison, 32, and Noah, 26, with ex-wife Tish Cyrus. Billy Ray also adopted Tish’s children Brandi, 39, and Trace, 37, from a previous relationship.

“It’s very different. It’s more grown up and it’s easier. Sometimes I miss being a hands-on mummy,” Hurley said. “I don’t miss the school runs. I don’t miss running around the house screaming because I can’t find a tennis racket. I don’t mind missing that. But you do sort of miss, I suppose, being so hands-on. It’s an adjustment to have to make.”
