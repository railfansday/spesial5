Bloodborne Officially Returns May 2027 In New Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Despite first releasing over a decade ago, Bloodborne is still home to an impressive fanbase, all of those who want to see that highly anticipated PC port, remake, or remaster. Or even just a 60fps update. Anything. However, with Sony seemingly dragging its feet despite FromSoftware itself being on board with the project, it may be another decade until we see Bloodborne make its grand return.

That being said, there is still plenty to look forward to if you want to immerse yourself in the Bloodborne universe, and in May 2027, the FromSoftware magnum opus returns with a stunning new collection.

Courtesy of Good Smile Company, pre-orders for a re-release of its Hunter Figma are now open and showcases our protagonist as they appear in Bloodborne's The Old Hunters DLC. Complete with the iconic hat and garb, the figure is also capable of being transformed into different poses thanks to adjustable joints, and not only that, but the upcoming release also comes with multiple weapons from both the base game and DLC which can be attached depending on your chosen pose. Last but not least, the Hunter Figma statue comes alongside a stand to help display your collectible even easier.

Set to release in May 2027, Hunter Figma showcases the iconic Saw Cleaver, Hunter Pistol, Hunter Blunderbuss, Hunter Axe, Whirligig Saw, and Ludwig's Holy Blade, and fans will be pleased to learn that the Saw Cleaver can even be extended. So how much will all of this cost? The upcoming re-release retails at ￥12,800, which is the equivalent of 80.30 USD, so, as figures go, it is a pretty respectable price for Bloodborne collectors. The full list of features include:

・Using the smooth yet posable joints of figma, you can create a variety of poses.

・A flexible plastic is used for important areas, allowing proportions to be kept without compromising posability.

・Both the Saw Cleaver and Hunter Pistol are included as weapons for the Hunter to wield.

・The Saw Cleaver can be transformed into its extended cleaver form just like in the game.

・The Old Hunters Edition also includes the Hunter Blunderbuss, the Hunter Axe, the Whirligig Saw and Ludwig's Holy Blade.

・The Hunter Axe, Whirligig Saw and Ludwig's Holy Blade come with interchangeable parts to display them in their transformed forms.

・Special attachment parts are included to display the Whirligig Saw and Ludwig's Holy Blade on the back of the Hunter.

・Hand parts to display the Hunter holding each weapon are included.

・An articulated figma stand is included to display the figma in a variety of poses.

Limited to three figures per person, this re-release from Good Smile Company is not one that Bloodborne fans will want to miss out on again, and whilst it may not be the Bloodborne PC port or remaster we have all been waiting for, it is definitely the next best thing. With the protagonist sporting one of the most iconic looks in gaming history, you can now commemorate it on your display case for good, and for quite a reasonable price.
