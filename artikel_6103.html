When you're applying for a credit card, you may wonder: "How good is the welcome offer I'm applying for?"

There's no simple answer to this question, but knowing the previous offers a card has had can help you determine what constitutes a good welcome offer. Getting any welcome offer is better than getting nothing at all, but you wouldn't want to apply for a card only to see a better offer a month later.

Generally, you’ll want good to excellent credit to qualify for many of the Capital One cards on this list, though other factors can also affect your approval odds.

We’ve collected and analyzed the offer history over the past few years for Capital One's most popular cards and determined the best time to apply for each.

Limited-time offer on the Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card: Earn more than $1,000 in travel bonuses with a limited-time $300 Capital One Travel stays credit, plus earn 75,000 bonus miles once you spend $4,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

It's important to note that we'll only discuss publicly available welcome offers. However, you might receive targeted offers that offer more bonus miles or cash back. You can also check for preapproved Capital One offers without affecting your credit score, which may help you find targeted offers that are higher than the publicly available bonus.

Keep in mind that offers may change at any time, and some of those mentioned below may not be available.

The bonus offer values throughout this guide are based on TPG’s November 2026 valuations and are not provided by the credit card issuer.

The Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card earns an unlimited 2 miles per dollar spent on every purchase, plus 5 miles per dollar spent on hotels, rental cars and vacation rentals booked through Capital One Travel.

The card’s modest $95 annual fee, simple earning structure and solid return on everyday spending make it a keeper once you’re approved for it.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Venture Rewards:

Earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first three months of account opening (public offer).

The highest offer we've seen on this card allowed new cardholders to earn up to 100,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases within the first three months of account opening.

When the bonus offers more than 75,000 miles — whether through additional miles or a travel credit — it meets our criteria for applying.

Learn more: Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One Venture Business card is a straightforward option for business owners who want consistent travel rewards without having to track bonus categories. With a $95 annual fee, it offers simple earning rates, easy-to-use credits and flexible redemption options that help offset the cost year after year.

While it doesn’t come with premium perks like lounge access, its simplicity and solid earning structure make it a reliable long-term choice for everyday business spending.

The Venture Business's launch offer of up to 150,000 miles (75,000 miles after spending $7,500 on purchases in the first three months, plus an additional 75,000 miles after spending $30,000 on purchases in the first six months) is the best offer we've seen so far.

As a recently launched card, the Venture Business has only two bonus offers. Since there’s limited historical data, we recommend applying when the bonus is over 100,000 miles.

With plentiful, easy-to-understand perks, the Capital One Venture X is a no-brainer for all travelers, from luxury seekers to the more budget-minded, and the card practically pays for itself year after year.

Though the card carries a $395 annual fee, it offers a $300 annual credit toward bookings made through Capital One Travel and rewards cardholders with 10,000 bonus miles each account anniversary.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Venture X:

The highest offer we've seen on this card offered 100,000 bonus miles earned after spending $10,000 on purchases within the first six months of account opening.

When the bonus offer is 75,000 miles or higher, it's a good time to apply.

Learn more: Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card

The Venture X Business carries the same $395 annual fee as the personal version and offers the same major perks, including a $300 annual travel credit for bookings made through Capital One Business Travel and access to Capital One and participating Priority Pass lounges. It also carries the same earning rates as the Venture X.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Venture X Business:

The highest offer we've seen on this card is up to 400,000 bonus miles: Earn 200,000 miles after spending $30,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening, plus an additional 200,000 miles after spending $150,000 on purchases in the first six months.

When the bonus offer is for more than 150,000 miles, it meets our criteria for applying.

Learn more: Capital One Venture X Business

While it comes with fewer perks, the Capital One VentureOne Rewards offers the same great redemption options as its sister cards, the Capital One Venture Rewards and the Capital One Venture X, with no annual fee. You’ll earn a flat 1.25 miles per dollar spent on everyday purchases.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One VentureOne Rewards:

The highest offer we've seen allowed new cardholders to earn 40,000 bonus miles after spending $1,000 in the first three months of account opening.

When the bonus offers more than 20,000 miles — whether through additional miles or a travel credit — it meets our criteria for applying.

Learn more: Capital One VentureOne Rewards Credit Card

The biggest downsides to cash-back cards are low earning rates and a lack of bonus categories, but that’s definitely not true of the Capital One Savor Cash Rewards.

For no annual fee, the Savor offers strong cash-back earning rates on popular and often overlooked categories, including 3% back on dining, entertainment and popular streaming services.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Savor Cash Rewards:

The highest offer we've seen included a $100 Capital One Travel credit to use in the first year. Plus, new cardholders could receive a $200 cash bonus after spending $500 on purchases within the first three months of account opening.

When the bonus offer is $250 or more, it meets our criteria for applying.

Learn more: Capital One Savor Cash Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One Quicksilver Cash Rewards is another great card with no annual fee. It earns a reliable 1.5% cash back on all purchases, making it a perfect go-to card for the odd purchase that doesn’t fit in any bonus categories.

While many other cards offer higher earning potential in specific spending categories, this card offers the safety of knowing you’ll earn a solid 1.5% back no matter what you buy.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Quicksilver Cash Rewards:

The highest offer we've seen included a $100 Capital One Travel credit to use in the first year. Plus, new cardholders could receive a $200 cash bonus after spending $500 on purchases within the first three months of account opening.

When the bonus offer is $200 or higher, it meets our criteria for applying.

Learn more: Capital One Quicksilver Cash Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One Spark Cash Plus earns 2% cash back on all purchases and is ideal for business owners who want to earn reliable cash-back rewards on all their expenses. It is worth noting that this is a no-preset-spending-limit card, meaning cardholders must pay off their balances in full each month.

The card has a modest $150 annual fee that can be refunded after spending at least $150,000 in your card anniversary year.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Spark Cash Plus:

The highest welcome offer we've seen on this card offered up to $3,000: $500 after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening and $2,500 after spending $50,000 on purchases in the first six months from account opening.

When the bonus is $2,000 or higher, it meets our application criteria.

The Capital One Spark Cash earns 5% cash back on hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Business Travel and 2% cash back on all other purchases. With the flat rate earning structure, the card is ideal for businesses with spending across different categories.

The card has a $0 introductory annual fee for the first year, then a $95 annual fee.

The Spark Cash is a traditional credit card that allows cardholders to carry a balance, offering more flexibility for businesses that don’t want to pay their balance in full each month.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Spark Cash:

The highest welcome offer we've seen on this card is $1,500 in cash back after spending $15,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

When the bonus exceeds $1,000, it meets our application threshold.

The Capital One Spark Cash Select is a solid option for small-business owners who want to earn straightforward cash-back rewards without paying an annual fee.

The card earns unlimited 1.5% cash back on all purchases, plus 5% cash back on hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Business Travel.

Here are the offers we've seen on the Capital One Spark Cash Select:

The highest welcome offer we've seen on this card is $750 cash back after spending $6,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

When the bonus exceeds $500, it meets our application threshold.

Learn more: Capital One Spark Cash Select
