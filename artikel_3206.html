El Pollo Loco: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Restaurant margins held inside the company's long-term target range while the brand pushes into New York and other new states.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, El Pollo Loco (LOCO -0.06%) announced that systemwide same-store sales -- sales at restaurants open at least a year -- grew 5% year over year. The restaurant operator is expanding its digital loyalty network while securing multi-unit development deals outside its core West Coast footprint. Three things management said stand out.

El Pollo Loco is accelerating restaurant openings, using experienced franchise operators to establish footholds in markets such as New York. Management expects second-generation site conversions--rebuilding existing restaurant properties--to reduce construction costs as national expansion gains speed.

Digital orders reached 31% of total sales during the quarter. The company relies on its Loco Rewards loyalty program to increase order frequency and lock in repeat customer visits during tight spending cycles.

Restaurant contribution margin -- the percentage of restaurant sales remaining after direct store operating costs -- landed inside target ranges. Management balances targeted promotional deals against wage costs to protect store-level profitability.

Franchise demand is expanding beyond the West Coast, and management shows clear confidence in regional growth. The company relies on lower-cost site conversions and digital order growth while management monitors pricing discipline.

Operational execution will stay essential for driving recurring customer visits.
