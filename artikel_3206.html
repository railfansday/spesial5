Hyster-Yale (HY) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Bookings rose 45% as Hyster-Yale launches a new efficiency push.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Hyster-Yale, Inc. (HY +5.42%) reported a sequential improvement in operating results and revenue during the third quarter as bookings reached $550 million. Management announced a new efficiency initiative targeting $100 million in annual cost reductions by 2029 through plant consolidations, organizational simplification, and reduced capital intensity. To focus on its core Lift Truck business, the company is discontinuing several noncore programs, including hydrogen-related solutions and the global electric terminal tractor program. Management stated that these actions are intended to create a more resilient operating model that can perform effectively throughout the industrial cycle. Although revenue in the Americas was impacted by delivery delays and component shortages, the company expects higher production volumes and manufacturing absorption to support a return to positive adjusted net income in the fourth quarter.
