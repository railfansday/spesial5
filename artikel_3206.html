iOS 27 adds new Messages app setting I instantly started using

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

iOS 27 brought several new features to the Messages app, including a setting that’s long been a popular request: the ability to customize or turn off the audio button. Here’s how to use it.

With iOS 27, Apple fixed a lot of bugs and also granted many feature requests from over the years.

Siri AI is the biggest example of that, offering a big upgrade over the old Siri. But smaller changes in apps like Calendar, Mail, and Messages reflect this focus on wish fulfillment.

In Messages, there’s a new setting that I instantly starting using. It lets you turn off the audio button that normally lives on the right side of the text input field.

The audio button has been conveniently accessible there for years, but I never use it. I don’t think I’ve ever sent an audio message. Sometimes I use dictation, but that’s always available from the bottom-right of the keyboard.

Since I never need that button, the only time it gets pressed is by accident. And as I suspect a lot of users have encountered—including Justin Bieber—it’s very easy to hit by accident.

Thankfully, iOS 27 has the fix. To disable the audio button, follow these steps:

Even if you turn the audio button off, you can still send audio messages by hitting the + button to open your iMessage apps then selecting ‘Audio.’

If you prefer to keep the button active, you can alternately now select ‘Record Audio’ or ‘Start Dictation’ as its function. That way the button will always stay set to your preference instead of dynamically changing like it did before.
