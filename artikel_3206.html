Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Full-year guidance rose as the reopened Royal Palm South Beach ramps up.

Park Hotels & Resorts Inc. (PK -8.31%) reported a net loss of $15 million for the third quarter, while comparable RevPAR increased 8.4% to $201.02. Management reported that broad-based strength in group and leisure demand drove performance, particularly in urban markets like Washington DC, Chicago, and Boston. The company raised its full-year 2026 guidance for RevPAR, Adjusted EBITDA, and Adjusted FFO per share following results that exceeded internal expectations. Strategic capital allocation efforts included the reopening of the Royal Palm South Beach following a $110 million renovation and the commencement of a $100 million project at the Hilton Hawaiian Village. Management also continued its noncore disposition strategy, completing the sale of the W Chicago – City Center for $19 million in October 2026.
