Gayle King Taking Next Week Off From ‘CBS Mornings’ Following Son-In-Law’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Gayle King will be absent from CBS Mornings for one week beginning Monday, November 30, following the death of her son-in-law, Virgil Miller, over the Thanksgiving holiday.

CBS Mornings executive producer Jon Tower revealed the sad news about Miller’s death Friday in a memo to staff, which was obtained by People.

Tower noted that King will take next week off to help with family matters, returning the week of December 7 after “private arrangements” have been made, according to People. Watch on Deadline

Miller is survived by his wife Kirby Bumpus and their two children, son Luca, 5, and daughter Grayson, 2.

“I’m so nuts about this child,” King said during a March 2022 episode of Parents magazine’s We Are Family podcast afte she becane a first-time grandmother.

“I’m certainly not the first person to have a grandchild — I realize that, but what I like is seeing Kirby become a mother herself. She’s so good at it. She and her husband, Virgil, are such great partners.”
