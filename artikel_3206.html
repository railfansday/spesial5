Puma: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Puma's U.S. NERLYNX demand is heading toward a second year of growth, yet China royalties are shrinking, a split the company must balance.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Puma (PBYI -15.56%) reported total revenue of $55.1 million for the quarter. For long-term investors, the business is shifting focus toward clinical drug development while managing international revenue headwinds. Three things management said stand out.

Puma cleared its remaining loan balance to investment firm Athyrium during the second quarter. The payoff frees up operating cash flow and leaves the business with $108.5 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities to support research and development.

Puma recorded a $2.1 million cumulative reduction to Q3 2026 royalty revenue after generic versions of NERLYNX, its prescription breast cancer drug, gained market share in China. Management lowered full-year royalty guidance to a range of $18 million to $19 million as a lower contractual royalty rate takes effect.

Interim results from the company's ALISCA-Breast1 trial of alisertib, an investigational cancer drug, showed activity in specific biomarker groups. Management plans to engage global health regulators in the first half of 2027 to design a pivotal phase III late-stage clinical study.

Demand for NERLYNX in the U.S. is heading toward a second consecutive year of growth, providing baseline commercial revenue. Management is controlling operating expenses to maintain positive net income for the full year.

With zero debt and $108.5 million in liquidity, Puma is balancing the commercialization of its core product against clinical trial expansion for alisertib.
