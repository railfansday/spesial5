Could Investing $500 a Month in Nebius Help You Retire a Millionaire?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The neocloud stock looks like a steal at current levels.

By Marc Guberti – Nov 21, 2026 at 1:37PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Nebius Group ›

Holding on to Nebius (NBIS -16.11%) stock recently has required an incredible conviction, but that resolve could pay off for long-term investors. Nebius' fundamentals remain robust and continue to strengthen despite its shares losing more than 40% of their value over the past month.

Those types of mismatches create long-term opportunities for investors who can see beyond the short-term noise and focus on the big picture.

Although Nebius delivered a solid earnings report, it's good to deal with the blemish first, especially with the stock’s declines over the past month taking a firm grip on current sentiment.

The neocloud had to delay the delivery of some new capacity due to issues with a third-party provider. That wasn’t Nebius' fault, and the company is building its future data centers to avoid third-party risk moving forward. The end result is that Nebius says it will not hit the $7 billion annual recurring revenue (ARR) target it forecast for December until February.

Anyone who is holding Nebius stock is doing so for the long run. While this two-month delay ruined short-term call options, it didn't affect the long-term thesis much. Artificial intelligence is still heating up, with demand scaling upward.

Financing and profitability have been among the main concerns people have about the neocloud, but Nebius squashed them both in its third-quarter shareholder letter. Its new contracts provide prepayments that cover all capital expenditures. Furthermore, the company expects to be breaking even on a pre-tax basis by 2027.

Within five to 10 years, it's possible that the conversation will shift from Nebius raising money to management initiating stock buybacks. It's quite feasible, since the company's payback period for new data centers has dropped to one year and seven months, and that excludes prepayments.

Its annual contract value per megawatt now exceeds $25 million for long-term deals, and Nebius wasted no time mentioning key enterprise AI leaders that recently became new customers.

Nebius believes it can generate $12 billion to $13 billion in ARR by the end of 2027. Granted, unforeseen delays could push some of that revenue out into 2028, as investors witnessed when Nebius said it wouldn’t reach its $7 billion ARR target until February.

Importantly, though, that guidance shows that growth is not slowing down. In Q3, Nebius delivered 571% year-over-year revenue growth to $981 million. That was also a 68% sequential growth rate from the second quarter.

Nebius has said that it expects to be operating at breakeven on a pre-tax basis by the end of 2027, and that its data centers are recouping the capital investments required to bring them online in just 19 months. However, that's still underselling Nebius as an investment.

One key detail investors may be overlooking is how negotiations will change when it's time to renew contracts. Nebius has to negotiate prepayments to fund its data centers. Receiving capital up front makes it more viable to build data centers at scale.

Customers make prepayments in exchange for lower annual contract values for each megawatt. Once Nebius operates data centers that have been fully paid off, it won't be as reliant on prepayments. That will give Nebius the flexibility to set higher prices per megawatt, and the soaring demand for compute implies that the elevated prices will stick.

Nebius revealed in its Q2 shareholder letter that it gets annual contract values of $40 million to $50 million per megawatt for short-term deals. Customers aren't afraid of those prices, and long-term deals may get closer to those levels once prepayments are no longer necessary.

This detail, combined with Nebius' current performance and pipeline, suggests it can produce 100% or higher year-over-year revenue growth for multiple years. That's a pretty ambitious prediction, but Nebius has been delivering on that minimum growth rate in recent quarters. Guidance implies that ARR will roughly quadruple in a single year.
