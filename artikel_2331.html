Germany reveal squad omission and shirt numbers ahead of Serbia clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Following his first-ever Germany call-up, Borussia Mönchengladbach youngster Wael Mohya has been left out of the matchday squad for Friday night's UEFA Nations League clash with Serbia, the German Football Association (DFB) confirmed on Thursday.

Germany coach Jürgen Klopp has named a 24-man squad for the upcoming games against Serbia and the Netherlands, but is only permitted to select 23 players for each match.

Mohya will be the player to miss out this time around, but will still travel to Belgrade with the rest of the squad.

The DFB also confirmed that Germany will wear their black away kit for the encounter and announced the shirt numbers for Friday's game.
