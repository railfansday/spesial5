3 managers Swansea City must target ASAP as Vitor Matos leaves for France

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Swansea City have been dealt a big blow ahead of club football’s return this weekend. RC Strasbourg are set to appoint Vitor Matos as their new boss after reaching a compensation agreement with the South Welsh side.

It marks a significant blow for the Swans, given Matos’ success and short time in the job. The Portuguese coach has been in charge at the Swansea.com Stadium for just shy of a year, and has turned them into Championship play-off contenders with impressive and intense pressing football.

Now though, a replacement must be identified. Here, we put forward three contenders that Swansea City should consider…

If Swansea City want Championship pedigree and a similar coaching profile to Matos, then Carlos Corberan is the ideal candidate. The Spaniard is available after being let go by Valencia earlier this season, and although reports have said he’s eyeing a top-flight job, the chance to lead a Championship promotion push will be tempting.

Corberan likes his sides to press intensely and high up the pitch, much like Matos. He took inspiration from Marcelo Bielsa, who he worked under at Leeds United, and has found a great level of success.

No compensation fee would be required, yet Corberan would still be an exciting and ambitious appointment.

Next up is Belgian boss Karel Geraerts, who is also out of work and thus wouldn’t require a compensation fee. Many at Swansea City will wonder whether Jamestown Analytics influence the search for Matos’ replacement, and if they do, Geraerts could come into consideration.

The 44-year-old was manager of Union SG for the 2022/23 season and oversaw 35 wins in 57 games. They scored 116 goals in the process and averaged 2.04 points per game before FC Schalke came calling.

That stint didn’t work out for Geraerts, and he was sacked in his last job by Stade Reims after missing out on Ligue 2 promotion. However, his similar high-pressing tactics and focus on wide overloads would fit the Swans’ squad.

At 34 months, Geraerts falls just short of the 36 months of head coach experience needed to be granted automatic qualification. However, through the Exceptions Panel, his Jupiler Pro League title challenge and run to the UEFA Europa League quarter-finals with Union SG would likely see this approved.

Last but not least is Mike Tullberg, who also falls short of the required head coach experience. But, like Geraerts, Swansea City would likely be successful in an exception appeal given his UEFA Pro License, elite pedigree at Borussia Dortmund and immediate silverware wins at FC Midtjylland.

The 40-year-old took charge of the Danish club in 2025, finishing 2nd and winning the Danish Cup in his first season. He held a variety of roles at Borussia Dortmund from 2019 to 2025, including a brief spell as their caretaker boss which yielded two wins and a draw.

Tullberg’s side are currently unbeaten in the Danish Superliga with nine wins and five draws. He too focuses on high-pressing football, allowing for continuity after Matos’ exit. As he is in a job and under contract until 2029 though, the Swans will have to pay compensation for his services.
