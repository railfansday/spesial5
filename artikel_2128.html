Real Madrid injury, suspension list and return dates vs. Valencia: Vinicius Junior, Rodrygo updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

By Matt Law | 05 Nov 2026 09:00, Last updated: 05 Nov 2026 09:01

Real Madrid will continue their La Liga campaign against Valencia on Saturday.

Los Blancos are third in the La Liga table on 27 points, three points behind the leaders Barcelona, and Jose Mourinho's side have been victorious in their last seven games in all competitions.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up Real Madrid's injury and suspension news ahead of the game with Valencia, who sit in 19th position in Spain's top flight.

Rodrygo suffered a severe knee injury earlier this year, but the Brazil international has stepped up his recovery and is working towards being available at the start of 2027.

Mendy is still dealing with a long-term thigh problem, and the Frenchman may not be available for selection until the final stages of 2026.

Asencio recently underwent an operation on a stress fracture in his leg, and the centre-back is expected to be on the sidelines for the next three months or so.

Possible return date: November 21 (vs. Celta Vigo)

Vinicius completes his four-game domestic suspension this weekend, with the Brazilian able to play in Spain's top flight after the November international break.
