National Newspaper names new ‘keeper being tracked by West Ham ahead of January

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In terms of ‘hot topics’ during this international break there’s little doubt that the thorny problem of West Ham’s goal keeper sits front and centre. Mads Hermansen’s form has been shocking for West Ham but on international duty he’s seemingly turned into a world beater.

Second ‘keeper Alphonse Areola is either injured or overweight – or both – depending on which article you read: Even third choice ‘stopper Fin Herrick had to leave the England u20 training camp, no explanation offered, suggesting there was more trouble ahead.

Little wonder, then, that supporters have been clamouring for a change between the sticks and a move in January for a new keeper. Whilst fan surveys suggest 85% of supporters want Hermansen replaced by Areola, others point out the Frenchman was little better during his last stint between the sticks in West Ham’s relegation season and demand a new signing instead.

Which might make The Sun.co.uk’s report especially pertinent:

“BRISTOL CITY face a fight to hold onto Wales keeper newbie Joe Duncan, who is being tracked by West Ham and Ipswich. Duncan, 20, who is currently on loan at Kieron Dyer’s Southend in the National League, was a surprise call-up by Craig Bellamy into the Nations League squad for the games with Norway and Portugal.”

Already described as having an ‘unbelievable future’ ahead of him, Duncan could be an exciting addition to West Ham’s ranks if the club choose to part company with Areola who is out of contract in the summer anyway.
