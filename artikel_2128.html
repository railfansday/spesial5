Ex-EFL player picks out ‘massive issue’ at West Ham at the moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

West Ham are positioned in 9th place in the Championship table after their first 15 fixtures of the season. WHUFC are one point off the play-offs behind QPR and Southampton.

The Hammers lost 2-1 away at Blackburn Rovers last time out before the international break at Ewood Park. They are back in action next weekend with a home clash against Preston North End at the London Stadium as they aim to return to winning ways.

Parkin, who used to play for the likes of Ipswich Town, Luton Town and Swindon Town, has delivered his honest verdict on the London outfit on the Benjamin Bloom YouTube channel: “They’re conceding far too many goals. They’ve been incredibly open in the last few games. They showed great powers of recovery in that game at Cardiff. Defensively is a massive issue.”

He added: “There’s not much Championship experience. Who are those leaders if you’re putting guys fresh into this XI in a really challenging league where you’re going to be so tested in every game. Every game at the London Stadium is the biggest for the opposition.”

West Ham will get the chance to bolster their options at the back with some reinforcements in the upcoming January transfer window. WHUFC will have the opportunity to freshen up their ranks ahead of the second-half of the campaign.

The Hammers have conceded 23 goals already this term. They have the worst defensive record in the top 10 right now.

At the other end of the pitch, they have no problems scoring goals and have found the net on a whopping 37 occasions. In fact, they have the best attack in the division.

West Ham have turned to former midfielder Scott Parker as their new head coach. He was picked as their replacement for Nuno Espirito Santo, who was given the sack after a poor run of form.

West Ham would benefit from a new goalkeeper. WHUFC’s number one Mads Hermansen has struggled over recent times, whilst Alphonse Areola has slipped out of favour.

The Hammers could also do with another centre-back to help them to stop leaking goals. They need someone to compete with Konstantinos Mavropanos and Max Kilman.

Parker is an experienced coach and knows what it takes to get out of the second tier. He has guided Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley up in the past.

It may take him a little time to get used to his new squad and patience will have to be required from the fanbase. When West Ham, they should have enough quality to rise back up the league and challenge for the top two.

They will fancy their chances of a win against Preston North End next Saturday. Their next opponents are in the drop zone with Burnley and Derby County.
