NHTSA is investigating more than 1.2 million Ford vehicles over airbag burns

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The preliminary evaluation covers 2021-2026 Ford Explorers after eight complaints and four early warning reports of burns

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration launched an initial review on Monday covering upward of 1.2 million Ford $F vehicles, citing burn injuries tied to deployments of seat-mounted side airbags, according to Reuters.

The agency has logged eight complaints, along with four early warning reports, that describe burns connected to the airbag deployments in certain Ford Explorers from the 2021-2026 model years, according to Reuters.

Drivers, along with passengers in the front seat and some police officers in Explorer Police Interceptor vehicles, sustained second- and third-degree burns when side airbags went off, according to Reuters.

The injuries occurred during crashes in which the seat-mounted side airbag deployed, according to The Wall Street Journal. Those burns required medical treatment.

The evaluation will look into how widespread the alleged problem is, how often it occurs and what safety risks it could carry, according to The Wall Street Journal.
