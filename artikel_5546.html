Stakeholder relations director Meredith Wimbrow departs FHFA

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

FHFA director of stakeholder relations Meredith Wimbrow is leaving the agency after about 20 months to become a vice president at Federal Hall Policy Advisors.   AI Summary

Meredith Wimbrow is leaving her role as director of stakeholder relations at the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) after about 20 months to become a vice president at Federal Hall Policy Advisors, she told HousingWire.

Wimbrow, a former FHFA Director Bill Pulte aide, is joining the private sector. POLITICO first reported on her transition. FHFA did not immediately respond to a request for comments.

“I had a great experience at FHFA. It gave me firsthand understanding of the housing finance system and the policy making process, and the importance of the relationship between the government and the agency,” Wimbrow said. “My role there was stakeholder relations. So I had a great opportunity to engage with the industry and work with folks in the housing space.”

During her tenure, Wimbrow said key discussions with industry participants focused on affordability, credit score modernization, oversight of the government-sponsored enterprises (GSEs) and the future of the housing finance system, as well as insurance modernization.

“The industry was very positive with the engagement, and they appreciated the focus of making housing affordable,” she said.

Wimbrow framed the career move as a natural shift from public service to advisory work.

“I wanted to take what I learned on the agency side, particularly in stakeholder relations and industry engagement, and apply that experience in the private sector,” Wimbrow said. “It’s a natural step for me to decide on Fed Hall; they lobbied me, and they’re the best in the business.”

Before joining FHFA, Wimbrow spent three years at the National Association of Home Builders (NAHB), most recently as director of Build-PAC, NAHB’s bipartisan political arm focused on federal campaigns.
