Kraft Heinz is projecting a return to sales growth in second-half 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Kraft Heinz expects 2027 organic net sales to be relatively flat for the year and adjusted operating income to decline

Kraft Heinz $KHC expects to return to sales and profit growth in the second half of 2027, the company said on Thursday as it hosts its 2026 Investor Day.

For full-year 2027, the company expects organic net sales to be "relatively flat," with growth returning in the second half. It expects adjusted operating income to decline by a low single-digit percentage versus 2026, with a return to growth in the second half. Free cash flow conversion is expected to remain at least 100%.

Kraft Heinz said it will provide formal 2027 guidance on its fourth-quarter earnings call. Management is sharing the initial contours of its expectations at the investor event, which has a webcast starting at 10:30 a.m. ET.

The company laid out milestones through 2029. In 2026, it said it is narrowing market share losses, recovering its top line from 2025 levels and improving volume trends in all regions. It expects consistent top-line growth and margin expansion in 2028. By 2029, it expects to deliver double-digit total shareholder return, with a balanced contribution from earnings growth and capital returned to shareholders.

"With the right team, a business-unit-led operating model, and greater, consistent investment behind our brands, we are positioning Kraft Heinz to create long-term value for shareholders," Chief Executive Officer Steve Cahillane said in a statement. The company said its strategy centers on volume-led profitable growth.

Cahillane has led the turnaround since taking the helm in January, according to Bloomberg. Having shelved the proposed breakup, he directed $700 million toward marketing, pricing, additional staff and research and development. The company's breakdown of that spending is $325 million for marketing, $250 million for pricing, $75 million for new employees and $50 million for products and packaging.

Kraft Heinz projects that new launches will represent 5% of its retail net sales in the U.S. in 2027, versus 3% in 2026. The company said its worldwide market share rose 30 basis points this year relative to 2025, and that sales of its macaroni and cheese line are growing again, according to Bloomberg.

Cahillane told analysts last week that Kraft Heinz is likely to lift prices in 2027 as inflation persists, according to Bloomberg.

Kraft Heinz last week raised the low end of its 2026 organic net sales forecast when it reported third-quarter results. The company now projects a full-year organic net sales decline of 0.5% to 1.25%, improved from the earlier outlook of a 0.5% to 2% drop.

In the third quarter, net sales fell 2.0% to $6.1 billion and organic net sales fell 1.1%. North America net sales fell 3.4%, Europe and Pacific Developed Markets net sales fell 5.6%, and Emerging Markets net sales rose 13.8%.
