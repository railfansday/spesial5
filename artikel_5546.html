Socket Mobile Reports Third Quarter and Nine Months 2026 Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

FREMONT, Calif., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Socket Mobile, Inc. (NASDAQ: SCKT), a leading provider of data capture and delivery solutions for enhanced workplace productivity, today reported financial results that are determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("GAAP") for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

"Our third-quarter results reflect progress in stabilizing revenue while executing our cost management initiatives," said Dave Holmes, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Revenue increased 12% sequentially from the second quarter and 9% compared with the prior-year quarter, reflecting improved performance amid continued market uncertainty. At the same time, we continued to align our cost structure with business conditions, reducing operating expenses by 12% from the prior quarter and 10% year over year. These results reflect our ongoing focus on operational efficiency and financial discipline while maintaining our ability to support customers and pursue future growth opportunities."

During the quarter, the Company continued to expand its commercial reach across multiple vertical markets through strategic partnerships, including 3Eye Technologies, which supports industrial mobility solutions for Apple-based frontline workflows, and Orda, which provides integrated mobile ordering and self-checkout solutions for enterprise workplace dining environments.

The Company expects to continue its cost management initiatives through the remainder of the year while pursuing opportunities to expand its data capture solutions across key markets. Management believes that disciplined expense management, stabilizing revenue, and expanding commercial opportunities will help position the Company for continued operational improvement.

About Socket Mobile, Inc.          Socket Mobile is a leading provider of data capture and delivery solutions for enhanced productivity in workforce mobilization. Socket Mobile's revenue is primarily driven by the deployment of third-party barcode-enabled mobile applications that integrate Socket Mobile's cordless barcode scanners and contactless readers/writers. Mobile Applications servicing the specialty retailer, field service, digital ID, transportation, and manufacturing markets are the primary revenue drivers. Socket Mobile has a network of thousands of developers who use its software developer tools to add sophisticated data capture to their mobile applications. Socket Mobile is headquartered in Fremont, Calif., and can be reached at +1-510-933-3000 or www.socketmobile.com.
