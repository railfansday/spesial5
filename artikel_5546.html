Oil slips below $100 as Trump played down further Iran strikes

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Brent crude fell 16% last week, while Iran's foreign minister said talks with Washington had made no progress

President Donald Trump said Sunday that he considered additional U.S. strikes on Iran improbable while negotiations to wind down the war kept advancing, and oil prices dropped on Monday.

Brent crude futures traded below $100 a barrel. Brent was down 3.5% at $94.93, and West Texas Intermediate was down 3% at $88.13.

Trump had suggested he might resume attacks on Iran once last week's midterm elections were over, but there was no indication of a major American military campaign, according to Reuters. Iran has warned that it would retaliate against U.S. and Israeli targets.

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said negotiations with Washington had yielded no headway, while Tehran was waiting to find out whether the U.S. administration would shift its stance once the elections were over, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Saxo Bank analyst Ole Hansen said the slide in oil reflects optimism about a U.S.-Iran peace agreement more than any tangible evidence of headway, according to Reuters. Brent fell 16% last week, marking its sharpest weekly decline since the COVID-19 pandemic began.

Attacks and shipping incidents continued over the weekend. An administrative office run by Saudi Aramco in Riyadh was lightly damaged on Sunday, according to The Wall Street Journal. The Saudi Energy Ministry said the building was hit by debris raining down after an attempted attack, with no casualties reported. The Iran-aligned Houthis claimed responsibility for the attack, according to Reuters.

The U.K. Maritime Trade Operations said an unidentified projectile hit a tanker leaving the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, according to The Wall Street Journal. The crew was safe. The U.K. Maritime Trade Operations also relayed a report from military authorities of a burning tanker roughly eight nautical miles to the northwest of Khasab, Oman, where the crew was evacuated. The origin of the blaze was not disclosed.

Trump said the United States had no immediate plans to strike Iran last Wednesday, the day after Democrats won majorities in the House and the Senate. Brent crude futures traded at $101.02 that day.

The U.S. military had conducted defensive strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island the day before, aimed at anti-aircraft missiles fired at American assets, a U.S. official said. A senior Iranian official said Tehran and Washington were a long way from an agreement to end the war.
