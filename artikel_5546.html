Alix Earle’s Feud With Alex Cooper Resurfaces—Inside Her Family’s $4.1 Million New Jersey Home Where She Told Her Side

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Social media sensation Alix Earle’s falling-out with Alex Cooper is back in the spotlight after Sofia Franklyn revisited her own split from the “Call Her Daddy” co-founder in her new explosive memoir, "Daddy Issues."

Earle, 25, previously pulled back the curtain on her side of the story from inside her family’s $4.1 million New Jersey home, where Netflix’s "Earle Meets World" cameras captured how the feud took an emotional toll on her.

Now, that drama has been brought to a boiling point after Franklyn shared her own experience with Cooper, 32, in her tell-all book, detailing the experience that took the once-co-hosts from friends to enemies.

Following the release of her book, Earle made it clear whose side she was on when she shared a TikTok video of her reading Franklyn's memoir.

Earle and Cooper were once known to share a close friendship, but their relationship appeared to sour after Cooper dropped Earle's podcast, "Hot Mess," from her Unwell Network in early 2025, sparking rumors that a feud had erupted between the two.

After Earle was publicly seen liking negative comments about Cooper on social media, the latter posted a video urging her online nemesis to air her grievances in public.

Earle responded to Cooper's video with a brief, "On it," but stopped short of sharing her side of the story at the time. Instead, she later teased that fans would finally get answers about what happened between the pair in the Netflix series.

"She's trying to paint it as I'm just like this crazy lunatic who is hating her for no reason. There's a reason," Earle said.

In her Netflix series, Earle opened up about the drama with Cooper, telling her family, “From the start, I didn’t realize things were going wrong because I was obsessed with her. I was like, ‘This is my idol. I’m so happy to work with her,’ and then [I] constantly felt taken advantage of and used and mistreated and manipulated.”

Holding back tears, she said, “What I was thinking—which makes me so sad for me at the time—is I was so happy. I trusted her so much, and all she did was just s--- on it.”

She explained, "We’re filming her podcast, and she’s having us drink before and then asking me really sexual questions I wasn’t comfortable with. But then I’m also answering ‘cause I’m drunk. I’m imagining myself in her shoes, to take someone in college, who’s young, impressionable, you know looks up to you, and then ask them these raunchy questions. It’s stuff that I didn’t necessarily want to live on the internet forever.”

The series, which hit screens on Sept. 4, also offered fans the the most revealing look yet at the behind-the-scenes drama that will be played out on screen for the world to see, including the difficult relationship between Earle's mother, Alisa, and her stepmother, Ashley Dupré, as well as the TikTok star's split from NFL player Braxton Berrios.

"Earle Meets World" take fans inside the star's world as she navigates her booming career, complicated relationships, family dynamics, and the hardships that come with being in the spotlight.

And while Earle has become known for documenting everything from her nights out to her acne struggles, with her millions of followers, the series promises to offer a much more intimate look at what happens when the cameras—or, in her case, the phone—would normally stop rolling.

"There's so much more to my life that people don't get to see,” Earle admitted in the trailer.

The reality series also brings Earle's family firmly into the spotlight, giving viewers a glimpse at the people closest to the influencer as she attempts to balance her increasingly high-profile career with her personal life.

"Every time I step out, there's a story," Earle lamented in the clip.

And among the familiar faces featured in the series is another backdrop that longtime Earle followers may recognize: her family's sprawling New Jersey mansion.

Earle's father and manager, Thomas "TJ" Earle, and her stepmother purchased the property in 2013 for $2.25 million, property records previously reviewed by Realtor.com® reveal.

The pair scored quite the deal on the home, which had previously been listed for $3.35 million. Today, the dwelling is estimated to be worth around $4.1 million, according to Realtor.com.

TJ and Ashley share the home with their three children, Izabel, Penelope, and Thomas, while Alix and her sister, Ashtin Earle, regularly return to New Jersey to spend time with their family.

Over the years, Alix has offered her millions of social media followers glimpses inside the property, revealing that the family has made numerous changes to the dwelling since purchasing it.

Now, the home is poised to serve as one of the backdrops for the family's next chapter as Netflix cameras capture the highs and lows of their lives for Earle's first reality series.

"My family is full of chaos," Earle said in the trailer.

In addition to putting Earle's close-knit blended family front and center, the show will also highlight the TikTok's romantic life, specifically her relationship with former boyfriend and NFL star, Berrios.

In one scene of the trailer, Earle's family can be heard confessing that they are "team Braxton." It then cuts to a clip of the social media icon crying as she admits she struggled to balance both her "break up" as well as the news of her mom's cancer diagnosis—both of which occurred while she was filming "Dancing With the Stars."

Earle's mother, Alisa, can also be seen opening up about the complicated relationship she shares with Dupré, telling the camera: "I'm side by side with someone who had an affair with my husband. You don't forget what happened. It doesn't mean we can't enjoy the people that we love."

Ashtin can then be seen telling her family members that they have "such a special dynamic."

The series also documents the enormous changes that have taken place in the influencer's own life since she first found fame filming "Get Ready With Me" videos from her student housing at the University of Miami.

Since graduating in 2023, Earle has transformed her enormous online following into a sprawling career, complete with lucrative brand partnerships, red-carpet appearances, and business ventures.

"I started sharing a more unfiltered look into my life, and then it just amped up," Earle says in the teaser.

But “Earle Meets World” promises to go beyond the polished snapshots of that success, documenting the more complicated reality of navigating fame, family, friendships, and relationships while millions of people watch.

Earle's sister Ashtin will feature prominently alongside her, as well as several of Earle's close friends.

While Earle's followers are already accustomed to seeing snippets of her family life online—including her visits home to New Jersey—the Netflix series will offer much greater access to the people and places that have played an integral role in her life.

That includes the multimillion-dollar family mansion that has appeared repeatedly in her social media content and will now help set the scene as the Earles invite viewers further into their world than ever before.

The family appears to have made numerous upgrades to the dwelling in the six years since they bought it, with Earle's videos showcasing a much more modern and elegant interior than featured in the original listing images.

Meanwhile, records show that Earle's mother, Alisa, resides in a newly-built dwelling in nearby Point Pleasant.

When they are in New Jersey, the TikTok star and Ashtin, who resides in New York City, split their time between the two dwellings and it is understood that both parents will feature heavily in the Netflix series.

All episodes of “Earle Meets World” are scheduled to premiere on Netflix on Sept. 4.

In a previous press release announcing that Earle would be stepping into the world of reality TV, the TikTok star confessed that partnering with Netflix felt "unreal," adding that she was excited to show fans her life behind the scenes.

"It honestly feels unreal to be partnering with Netflix, and I’m so excited to be creating this show with them," she shared. "I’ve shared my life for years through my own camera, but this felt like the next phase because it’s a chance to go deeper.

"Not just the highlights, but the real moments, too. The vulnerable ones, the hard ones, and the fun, chaotic ones in between. It’s the same authenticity people know me for, but now with a different lens."

The social media mogul revealed that she was most "excited" for her fans to see her "fun" and "modern family."

"People either think we’re insane or they’re like, wait, this is exactly like my life. And honestly, both are true. I’m excited for fans to experience the full picture as it happens. The chaos, the emotional moments, the stressful parts, and all the little things in between that you don’t always see online," she added.

She admitted that the process was a "little scary," adding, "I’m so used to being in control of what I share and what I keep private, so having cameras around in moments I normally wouldn’t post is definitely an adjustment.

"But the real, human moments are what connected me to my audience in the first place. If letting people in even more makes someone feel less alone, or even just makes them laugh, then it’s worth it."
