‘Vanderpump Rules’ Star Jax Taylor Reveals Secret Cancer Battle—Inside the L.A. Home Where He’ll Recover

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jax Taylor had been quietly facing one of the most frightening chapters of his life while settling into a new home with his son, Cruz.

Now, after revealing that doctors found cancer in his left kidney, the "Vanderpump Rules" alum is looking ahead to recovery following a five-hour surgery to remove the tumor.

Taylor, 47, took to Instagram on Nov. 18, to share his diagnosis, explaining that the discovery came after he was hospitalized with kidney stones in July.

A CT scan also showed a tumor on his left kidney. After a series of tests and appointments, he learned it was cancer—a revelation he had kept private for months.

"This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life. Back in July, I woke up one morning in horrible pain and ended up in the hospital with kidney stones. What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined," he penned on his candid social media post.

He added, "My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer. I lost my father to cancer, so hearing that word just broke me. It put me in a really dark place. The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine. It’s crazy how you can act like everything is normal when deep down you’re a mess."

The reality TV star revealed that he underwent a five-hour surgery and is awaiting the results.

"One thing this experience and the last couple years have taught me is when life gets really hard, you find out fast who’s really there for you. And believe me.. it’s been eye opening. I honestly don’t know how I would have gotten through everything without my amazing girlfriend by my side. She’s been my rock through all of this," he added referring to his girlfriend, Lori Krebs.

"If there’s one thing I hope people can take away from this, it’s never to take your health for granted," he added at the end of his post.

While speaking to People about his diagnosis, Taylor told the outlet, “It’s been a really scary few months. The tumor was larger and deeper than originally expected, which made the surgery more complicated. Thankfully, my surgeon is hopeful they were able to remove everything, but we’re still waiting on the final pathology results. I have five incisions on my stomach, so I’m pretty sore and know it’s going to take some time to recover.”

The health scare unfolded as Taylor was beginning a new chapter at home. In May, he gave fans a look inside his new place, showing off a comfortable, neutral-toned interior with warm wood details and a polished but relaxed feel.

Brown paneled walls and a round midcentury-modern mirror add character, while a beige couch covered in pillows and throws makes the living room feel like a place to settle in.

The home has a masculine style, with natural wood accents and soft, neutral colors throughout. Taylor’s son, Cruz, whom he shares with ex-wife Brittany Cartwright, also appeared in the photos, looking at ease in the space.

Taylor said the home was made special for both of them, thanking RENWIL Decor for helping bring the rooms together.

The move followed a series of changes in Taylor’s personal life. Earlier in the year, he announced that he was moving into a new place, describing it as a “new chapter” and saying the move had gone smoothly.

By the time he shared the May photos, the house appeared furnished and lived in, with cozy touches that made it feel like a home for father and son.

Taylor’s latest update now gives that glimpse of domestic life a more emotional backdrop. He said he had kept the diagnosis to himself because the experience had been frightening.

His father, Ronald Cauchi, died from throat and esophageal cancer in 2017, and Taylor said hearing the word “cancer” brought him to a dark place.

More than anything, Taylor said, he wants to be healthy and present as Cruz grows up. He also thanked his girlfriend, Lori Krebs, for standing by him during the ordeal, saying her support had meant everything.

As he begins the next stage of recovery, Taylor said he is taking things “one day at a time.”

His new home reflects a fresh start he had been building with Cruz; the diagnosis has made his priorities clearer.

Taylor said the experience reminded him not to take his health for granted and to value the people who show up when life becomes difficult.

With the tumor removed and further results still pending, his attention is now on healing and being there for his son.
