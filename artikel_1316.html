Sacked English Footballer Madelene Wright Making a Fortune on Adult Site

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Professional athletes are always on the hunt for ventures that can increase their earnings away from whichever sport they participate in.

However, the journey from footballer to OnlyFans model isn't a career path many take. But that's the exact path England-born Madelene Wright took in 2021.

The footballer-turned-social media star saw her popularity skyrocket after being brushed aside by the football world in 2020. Wright became a social media influencer, with her Instagram following exceeding 250,000 and brands lining up to work with her.

She may never have ventured into the luxurious lifestyle she has since created for herself if she hadn't been sacked by Charlton Athletic in 2020.

Wright was still a young player making her way in the women's game when a controversial video promptly ended her career with the Addicks.

Her football career hit a brick wall in 2020, when Snapchat videos emerged showing Wright inhaling balloons and drinking from a champagne bottle in her Range Rover.

It has been claimed that she is no longer in contact with the former Premier League star.

Charlton's chairman delivered the news that her contract with the club would be terminated after an internal investigation. Speaking to The Sun, she revealed how hard the news hit her:

"I was young, I was messing around with friends, and I didn’t really understand the position I was in and the responsibility I had.

"I loved Charlton, my coaches and teammates so the decision left me heartbroken.

"I felt guilty, embarrassed and disappointed in myself that I showed myself in that light."

That was the catalyst behind Madelene's venture into social media, and she eventually set up an OnlyFans account in 2021. But it wasn't the end of her football journey.

After four years out of the game, she was signed by Leyton Orient. Wright has since played for Chesham Women and is now under contract with Chatham Town Women.

Upon joining Orient in 2024, she posted: "Four years out of the game. Sometimes you have to take a step back, rebuild and go again even harder.

"I’m far from perfect but I don’t live in my past. Here’s to more hard work, discipline and determination. On to the next."

She still operates her OnlyFans and social media accounts alongside her football career. Wright admitted that it was a life-changing decision to pursue the online avenues.

In a separate interview with The Sun, the 28-year-old claimed she made around £500,000 in the first year of her OnlyFans journey. "In the first year I made in the ballpark of half a million pounds. I can't lie, it has completely changed my life," she said.

It's only reasonable to assume those earnings will have grown significantly in the years since. As a result of the cash injection, Madelene has been able to travel more and live a more lavish life.

She explained: "I was able to get my own place, I have been able to travel the world and have enjoyed a lot of luxurious things.

"As long as you learn from your mistakes, make the right moves going forward, there is light at the end of the tunnel."
