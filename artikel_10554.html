Game dev reveals huge difference in money made on Steam vs Epic Games Store

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The developer behind a popular indie game has weighed in on a discussion about cuts between Steam and the Epic Games Store, revealing their income from both.

If you’re an indie developer making games for PC, there are a few platforms where you can put your games up for sale. Steam is the most well-known of them, but there is also the Epic Games Store, as well as GOG.

Hosting on Steam, however, does mean that you end up paying a cut to Valve. This is set at 30% for games that make under $10 million, but they bring it down once you hit that mark. Obviously, not every game reaches that figure, though, meaning some developers will miss out on some cash.

It is a topic that comes up regularly on social media, with one developer – Evgeny Grishakov – questioning fellow devs on the money they’ve made between the two.

VuVuu Inc., the sole game dev behind Tiny Terraces and Otok, noted that there is a pretty huge difference for them between the two platforms.

“In a year for Tiny Terraces, I got $136,195 pre tax from Steam vs $916 pre tax from Epic,” they said on November 17.

“About 79% of that is roughly the after tax final number I kept,” the developer added after another user quizzed them on the exact amount they made.

VuVuu Inc also criticised Epic’s dashboard as being “kinda doo doo” and noted that it is “much harder” to see the stats they want to see.

“Steam is where the players are because it’s obviously that vastly superior service for both players and devs,” another user chimed in.

The Tiny Terraces dev noted that they also made money on both Apple’s App store and Android’s Play Store than the Epic platform.
