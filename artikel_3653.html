Rosé, Salma Hayek, Sienna Miller and More: An Exclusive Look Into the 2026 LACMA Art + Film Gala After-Party

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Some of Hollywood’s biggest main characters, including Artificial co-stars Andrew Garfield and Monica Barbaro, Nicole Kidman, Kim Kardashian, Sarah Paulson, and Viola Davis posed by the Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s famous Urban Lights installation last night to celebrate the 15th annual LACMA Art+Film Gala, which was co-founded in 2011 by museum trustee Eva Chow and actor Leonardo DiCaprio and continues to be sponsored by Gucci.

After a performance by Rosé, who serenaded guests with renditions of her hits “Number One Girl,” “APT.,” and “New Trick,” in addition to a cover of The Killers’ “Read My Mind,” some of the starry guests carried on at an exclusive after-party hosted by Chow. The fashion designer, arts patron and Khee Soju founder welcomed guests including Julia Garner, Sofia Vergara, Paris Hilton, Alix Earle, and Salma Hayek Pinault and her husband, Kering chairman François-Henri Pinault to her private residence.
