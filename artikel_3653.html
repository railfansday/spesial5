Grand Theft Auto 6 might feel more grounded compared to its predecessors, but that doesn't mean Leonida isn't full of weird and supernatural ongoings. One such paranormal activity is GTA 6's aliens—if you know where to look, you'll be able to find a UFO which'll eventually lead you down a strange road of cows, radiation, and the aliens themselves.

And if you don't know where to look, here's where I can help you. If you want to find GTA 6's UFO and a weird little alien guy, here's exactly where you need to go and what you need to do.

The alien abduction is actually a three-parter mission, and to get started you'll first need to locate the abandoned car. You'll need to look for it at nighttime, and it can spawn in a number of locations. For me, it was a little ways east of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. For our own Chris Livingston, he discovered the car somewhere north of Capri, also in Kelly County. You know it's the one if the car is pointed toward a forest and the doors are open.

Once you've found it, you'll need to follow the path it's parked along. You'll know you're going the right way by the clothes strewn along the path. If it's a bit too dark or you're stuck, you can press L3 + R3 to activate your focus and see the clothes more easily or equip your flashlight. Eventually you'll see the lights of the UFO and a very naked lady amongst it all. Head over until a cutscene plays out.

Once that's over, you'll get into a light scuffle with the woman before she instructs you to go get her car. Head back to where you first found the vehicle, where one more cutscene will play out and you'll be done… for now.

You'll need to wait for some time to pass. Make sure you're heading home and going to sleep each night, preferably as whichever character you discovered the UFO on. Eventually, you'll trigger a cutscene that'll bring you to a white room.

Find the dark oval which signals the way to a corridor and run through it. You'll be met with a terrifying maze of eggs. Get close to them and they'll start glowing blue. Get too close and they'll turn yellow, eventually cracking and causing your character to be yoinked back to the start by tentacles.

Carefully make your way through the eggs, finding large gaps where you won't cause them to break. Once you've made it to the other side, you'll be met with another room containing the solar system. Take a look at earth—this is actually showing you where you'll need to head for part three of this mission—and continue onwards until you reach a code.

To decipher the code, simply look at how many lines are in each of the four symbols above the keypad. For me, the code was 3124. Punch in the code, and head to the next room.

Here, you'll need to push the button on the control panel and light it up. The aim is to have four green lights, so keep pushing until all four are the same colour and then pull the lever to the left.

Once that's done, you'll be met with three metal doors. The middle one'll do some nasty damage if you step into the room and hang around too long, so avoid it. Instead, head to the room on the left—yes, the one with the cows—and run in to grab the bottle of milk on the table.

Run back out and head for the door on the right. Walk to the end until you see a contraption where you're able to insert your milk bottle. Shortly after, you'll wake up back in your bed and ready to tackle the final part of this mission chain.

You'll want to head over to the location shown to you in the globe. More specifically, you'll want to head south of Hamlet, at this abandoned building just west of the river.

There'll be a gap in the fence you can hop over, or you can shoot the padlock, or alternatively ram into the gate with your car. From here, you can either climb on top of the roof and drop down or find the double doors and kick them down, or use the snap gun. Either way, you'll want to make sure the door is unlocked for later in this mission.

Turn around and head down the stairs, towards the keypad at the end of the corridor. The code here is the same as the one you used earlier, so punch it in again and head into the elevator.

Walk through the tunnel and you'll once again be met with a control panel and three doors, plus a little audiotape with some fun exposition about alien experimentation. You'll be doing the exact same thing here as you did earlier—get every light to green, push the lever, and head into the left room to retrieve a bottle of milk. The middle room once again doles out gnarly damage, so don't hang too long or avoid it all together.

Head over to the door on the right, down the corridor and into the room. Insert the milk bottle into the contraption and after a few seconds, out will pop a little alien guy! It's unconscious, though, so you'll need to pick it up.

As soon as you've picked up the alien, the bunker will begin to fill with toxic gas and you'll need to head out ASAP. Go back the way you came—through the tunnel and into the elevator, and then through those double doors you snap gunned/kicked down earlier to get outside.

Sadly, your time with this alien fella is brief, as it'll quickly get beamed up into the sky by a UFO. It also doesn't take long for special forces to show up, but don't worry. The UFO will return to laser them all down, allowing you to get out without any further issues.

For your troubles, the aliens will give you a permanent buff to your health, stamina, and focus, so helping out these extraterrestrials is more than worth the effort. And, y'know, the trauma.
