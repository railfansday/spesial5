GTA 6 is extremely long as games go. It's more comparable to Red Dead Redemption 2's wild west saga than it is to GTA 5, with multiple chapters, missions, and locations you'll move between as you follow Jason and Lucia's story.

As you'll see with the Vintage Vice City Pack and Ultimate Edition bonuses, quite a lot can change between each game's chapter, as you slowly explore the map. So, here's each chapter and mission in GTA 6's main story, plus a little reference summary for each, though some are excluded for spoiler reasons:
