All eyes on the justices’ next private conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

The Senate confirmed Justice Joseph Story to the Supreme Court on this day in 1811. When he joined three months later at age 32, he became the youngest justice in Supreme Court history, as we noted in a Closer Look in May.

On Tuesday, the Supreme Court indicated that several notable petitions for review will be considered at the justices’ next private conference on Friday, Dec. 4. The disputes address such issues as the Trump administration’s ban on transgender servicemembers and its approach to appointing acting U.S. attorneys. For more on the conference, see the On Site section below.

The December argument session will begin on Monday, Nov. 30.

Kelsey Dallas is SCOTUSblog's managing editor and the primary author of the SCOTUStoday newsletter.

Nora is an editorial assistant at SCOTUSblog, based in Washington, D.C.
