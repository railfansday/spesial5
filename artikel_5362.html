JOYY Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results: Total revenues record consistent increases, driven by sustained growth of three core businesses

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

SINGAPORE, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- JOYY Inc. (NASDAQ: JOYY) ("JOYY" or the "Company"), a leading global technology company, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

In the third quarter, JOYY maintained solid growth momentum. Total revenues reached US$624.6 million, up 15.6% year over year and 5.7% quarter over quarter. Social entertainment revenue was US$441.0 million, up 8.8% year over year. BIGO Ads, including both first-party and third-party businesses, generated US$145.7 million in revenue, up 39.6% year over year, with its third-party advertising business sustaining strong growth of 52.3% year over year. SHOPLINE revenue reached US$37.8 million, up 24.7% year over year. The contribution from non-livestreaming revenue further increased to 32.8% of total revenues this quarter, driven by the Company's multi-engine growth strategy.

JOYY's non-GAAP operating income reached US$46.6 million, up 14.6% year over year. Non-GAAP EBITDA reached US$55.7 million, up 10.1% year over year. Operating cash inflow for the quarter was US$102.2 million. As of September 30, 2026, JOYY held US$2.94 billion in net cash. Supported by the Company's strong cash flow generation and solid financial position, it continued to return capital to its shareholders for their long-term support through cash dividends and share repurchases. As of November 13, the Company has returned a total of US$536.0 million to shareholders this year.

Ms. Ting Li, Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of JOYY, commented, "We have continued to execute our multi-engine growth strategy, strengthening our core Social Entertainment business while developing Ad Tech and Smart Commerce as two new growth engines. Following our investments and business model validation over the past few years, the results of this strategy are becoming increasingly evident in sustained topline expansion. Looking ahead to 2027, we believe JOYY will enter an important phase as we advance our multi-engine growth strategy and translate our progress into stronger operating results. With increasingly clear growth paths across our three businesses, we are confident in the Company's ability to sustain solid revenue growth in 2027. We also expect greater scale, a stronger earnings mix, and improved operating efficiency to drive further growth in operating profitability."

In the third quarter, Social Entertainment continued to deliver steady growth, with year-over-year growth momentum further accelerating. The Company's global average mobile MAUs reached 278.8 million, up 4.7% year over year. Livestreaming revenue reached US$419.8 million, up 8.1% year over year and 4.3% quarter over quarter. Driven by effective and refined operations, the number of core livestreaming paying users grew 8.1% year over year and 4.8% quarter over quarter.

Bigo Live, the Company's flagship product, continued its steady revenue growth, supported by ongoing improvements in content operations, its streamer ecosystem, and AI applications. In content operations, Bigo Live continued to build on its strengths as a global social platform to better cater to users' cross-regional content consumption and social interaction needs. Bigo Live further refined its cross-regional content distribution algorithms, expanded live translation coverage, and enhanced speech recognition in complex acoustic environments, making it easier for cross-language communication. These improvements drove continued growth in cross-regional gifting among users in developed markets. In terms of the streamer ecosystem, Bigo Live continued to invest in its community by refining growth and incentive mechanisms for new streamers. In particular, Bigo Live improved onboarding and guidance to help newly signed streamers successfully launch their first livestreams. These efforts contributed to a 4.7-percentage-point increase in the share of newly signed streamers completing their first livestream compared with the previous quarter, bolstering Bigo Live's high-quality content pipeline. Bigo Live continued to integrate AI into livestream interactions and virtual gifting, expanding its AI-generated interactive content and virtual gifts to create a more personalized experience for users. In September, the share of total virtual gift consumption attributable to AI-generated interactive virtual gifts increased further to 35.4%.

Furthermore, Bigo Live continued to deepen local community engagement and foster vibrant creator ecosystems. In the Middle East and North Africa, as an official content partner of the 2026 Esports World Cup, Bigo Live delivered an immersive viewing format that integrated tournament livestreams with interactive experiences. Across Southeast Asia, Bigo Live hosted multiple mid-year galas in local markets, supporting the growth of local creators and the sustainable development of its ecosystem. In North America, BIGO Academy combined online growth challenges with offline recognition, highlighting the vitality of its creator ecosystem and strong user engagement. In South America, Bigo Live held its inaugural offline event in Brazil and organized roadshows in Colombia and Peru, expanding public awareness of the platform and fostering a more vibrant and inclusive local creator ecosystem.

In the third quarter, BIGO Ads continued to expand its global traffic network, diversify its advertiser verticals and demand mix, while improving its algorithms and ad delivery efficiency. As a result, BIGO Ads' revenue reached US$145.7 million, up 39.6% year over year and 9.0% quarter over quarter. Its third-party advertising business, BIGO Audience Network, grew 52.3% year over year and 9.2% quarter over quarter.

On the supply side, BIGO Ads continued to expand its global developer ecosystem and broaden the high-quality traffic coverage. In the third quarter, SDK traffic grew 55.6% year over year. In October, BIGO Ads SDK became generally available via Google AdMobâ¢ Mediation as an in-app bidding partner. Developers can now seamlessly access the BIGO Ads SDK through Google AdMob Mediation and tap into massive demand from global premium advertisers. This integration creates new opportunities for BIGO Ads to expand its premium traffic supply and reach a broader range of app categories.

On the demand side, BIGO Ads continued to strengthen its presence in key verticals by expanding its local advertiser base and accumulating more advertiser conversion data. These insights helped further refine its algorithms and optimize ad delivery solutions across different web-based verticals. Web-based advertising demand continued to accelerate, growing by approximately 328% year over year and 30% quarter over quarter, becoming a key driver of BIGO Ads' growth this quarter. Meanwhile, in-app demand maintained healthy growth momentum.

On the technology side, BIGO Ads continued to enhance its data and AI-driven algorithm capabilities to improve the precision and efficiency of ad delivery. As its cross-channel attribution systems improved and advertiser conversion data accumulated, the platform further upgraded its model architecture to support multiple conversion objectives and types of traffic, improving algorithm accuracy and iteration efficiency. Meanwhile, BIGO Ads continued to refine its bidding strategies to better assess the value of each ad impression and match advertiser budgets with available inventory more effectively. These improvements helped advertisers meet their ROI targets while enhancing the efficiency of BIGO Ads' traffic acquisition and monetization.

In the third quarter, SHOPLINE continued to focus on acquiring high-quality brand merchants, strengthening its presence in key markets, and enhancing its omnichannel commerce infrastructure and value-added services. SHOPLINE's revenue reached US$37.8 million, up 24.7% year over year and 10.0% quarter over quarter.

In the cross-border business, SHOPLINE enhanced its service offerings and local support to help these merchants migrate to its platform, manage operations across markets, and process cross-border transactions more efficiently. In the third quarter, revenue from cross-border merchants maintained strong momentum, growing 77.8% year over year. To support the growing base of brand merchants, the Company continued to enhance SHOPLINE Payments with more local payment options, stronger multi-currency payment support, and improved transaction management. These enhancements helped merchants manage cross-border payments easily and efficiently.

In AI Commerce, SHOPLINE's strategy revolves around helping merchants capture demand from AI channels and improve their operational efficiency. As AI agents change how consumers discover and purchase products, SHOPLINE continued to strengthen its capabilities and expand integrations with the broader AI ecosystem. While volume from AI channels is still in an early stage, adoption is building quickly. In the third quarter, page views and order volume from AI channels grew nearly 500% and 550% year over year, respectively. Notably, AI is demonstrating distinct advantages in categories such as consumer electronics and furniture, where shoppers weigh multiple options before buying. In these categories, AI can better interpret consumer needs, recommend relevant products, and guide shoppers toward a purchase. SHOPLINE Omi, an AI-powered commerce assistant, has rolled out features spanning product management, customer segmentation, promotional events, guidance on platform features and settings, and detailed store performance analysis, bringing AI deeper into merchants' day-to-day workflows and improving operational efficiency.
