As HR Weighs AI's Risks, Financial Finesse's Human-Backed Platform Wins Personal Finance Tech of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Recognition for AI coach Aimee marks the company's second US FinTech Awards win since 2024

EL SEGUNDO, Calif., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AI is moving quickly into every corner of the workplace. In SHRM's 2026 CHRO Priorities and Perspectives report, 92% of CHROs said they expect AI to be further integrated into their workforce this year. But HR's confidence has limits: Culture Amp's 2026 AI in HR survey found that while 77% of HR professionals are bought in on AI, only 24% are comfortable letting it act autonomously.

That caution matters most when AI guides employees' financial decisions, from retirement savings to benefits elections, where a wrong answer can follow someone for years.

Financial Finesse was named Personal Finance Tech of the Year November 19th at the 2026 US FinTech Awards in New York City, its second win in the category since 2024.
