In the news release, Finzly Appoints Former Early Warning Services CEO Paul Finch to Its Board of Directors, issued 16-Nov-2026 by Finzly over PR Newswire, we are advised by the company that changes have been made. The complete, corrected release follows, with additional details at the end:

Finch helped launch Zelle and brings extensive experience scaling bank-led payments as Finzly accelerates adoption of BankOS.

CHARLOTTE, N.C., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Finzly, a leader in modern banking technology, today announced the appointment of Paul Finch, former Chief Executive Officer of Early Warning Services and a key architect behind the launch of Zelle, to its Board of Directors.

Finch brings decades of experience helping financial institutions bring new services to market at scale. As a Finzly director, he will provide strategic guidance as the company expands adoption of BankOS, its modern bank operating system that enables banks to modernize payments and introduce new banking capabilities alongside their existing core systems.

As CEO of Early Warning Services from 2003 to 2019, Finch led the expansion of the bank-owned company's capabilities in payments, fraud prevention, and identity verification. Under his leadership, Early Warning worked with leading U.S. financial institutions to launch Zelle in 2017, making real-time payments available to millions of Americans through their banks.

"Paul has already helped transform how America moves money. Now, we have an opportunity to transform how America banks," said Booshan Rengachari, Founder and CEO of Finzly. "Paul understands what it takes to earn banks' trust, build industry-wide collaboration, and bring new services to market at scale. His experience will be invaluable as we bring BankOS to more financial institutions, helping them innovate faster and deliver next-generation banking experiences without being constrained by legacy technology."

Finzly is advancing its strategy to enable banks of all sizes to rapidly introduce new products, connect to emerging payment networks, seamlessly integrate acquired businesses, harness AI capabilities, and deliver differentiated financial experiences while maintaining control of their infrastructure and customer relationships.

"The financial services industry is entering a new era of transformation, and banks need the freedom to innovate without being held back by legacy infrastructure," said Paul Finch, incoming member of Finzly's Board of Directors. "Booshan and the Finzly team have built a compelling platform that addresses this challenge. BankOS allows banks to modernize progressively while preserving their existing investments and accelerating innovation. I'm excited to join the board and help Finzly scale its impact across the banking industry."

"Finzly has built a strong foundation for its next chapter of growth," said Shamit Mehta, a member of Finzly's Board of Directors and Partner at TZP Group. "Paul's experience in building and scaling one of America's most successful payment networks brings invaluable expertise and industry perspective to our Board. I believe the combination of Finzly's technology, entrepreneurial drive, and Paul's industry leadership positions the company to play an increasingly important role in shaping the future of banking."

Finzly is a banking technology company on a mission to modernize the financial infrastructure that powers banks and credit unions. Its flagship platform, BankOS, is an AI-native, API-first, event-driven bank operating system enabling financial institutions to progressively modernize legacy systems and rapidly introduce new banking capabilities.

Through its modular platform spanning payments, accounts, digital banking, foreign exchange, tokenized deposits, and agentic AI, Finzly enables financial institutions to innovate faster, operate more efficiently, and grow through mergers and acquisitions without technology becoming a barrier to their strategic ambitions.

Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, Finzly is helping shape the future of banking with secure, scalable, and interoperable infrastructure.

For more information, visit www.finzly.com.
