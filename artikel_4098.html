Poll: Which PS5 Games Did You Buy During the Insane Pre-GTA 6 Rush?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Over the last couple of months, we've been subjected to one of the most packed release schedules in recent memory — and it's all been GTA 6's fault.

The now imminent launch of Rockstar's world-beater changed everything when it was first announced. Publishers from across the globe scrambled to avoid its inevitable impact, opting to push all of their games out between the end of August and the beginning of November.

The result was, as mentioned, a truly crazy schedule. It's been a pretty incredible period for PS5 gaming in general, but not many people will have had the time — or the money! — to play everything that was on offer.

And so this poll (originally recommended by regular Push Square reader themightyant) should make for an interesting study. Basically, I want to know which games you actually managed to sit down and enjoy during the pre-GTA 6 rush.

I imagine that a whole load of you opted for Marvel's Wolverine given the popularity of the character and its status as a PS5 exclusive, but what else was on the docket? I feel like we were absolutely spoilt for choice.

Some of the highest rated releases of the entire year dropped between September and now. Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter, Control Resonant, Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve, Star Wars: Galactic Racer, Final Fantasy Resonance, Phantom Blade Zero... Sheesh!

We even had the likes of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered vying for our already limited time. And while Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen is an expansion, it honestly might as well be counted because it's bigger than most full games.

In short, it's been brutally busy few months — but now that the chaos has subsided, which titles did you deem worthy of your attention? Vote in our polls, and then tell me all about your adventures in the comments section below.
