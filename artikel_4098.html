Square Enix's Big Reset Is Working As It Increases Its Full-Year Forecast

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Publisher reports "solid performance" for FF7 Rebirth

Square Enix has released its latest financial data for the six-month period ending 30th September 2026.

In short, it looks like the company's efforts to reset its business is working. It reported increased sales compared to the same period in 2025 and has therefore increased its overall forecast for the remainder of the fiscal year.

Net sales for the period totalled ¥163,179 million, an increase of 21.9%, while operating income totalled ¥38,793 million, an increase of 41.3%. Square Enix has cited strong sales of games, including Final Fantasy VII Rebirth on Switch 2 and Xbox Series X|S along with The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales.

The company now forecasts total net sales of ¥310,000 million over its previous estimate of ¥298,000 million, with total operating income now forecast at ¥59,000 million over its previous estimate of ¥49,000 million.

It also provided a fresh breakdown of physical sales vs. digital in the entertainment sector. The figures in the west are particularly telling, with physical sales down year-on-year from 1.09 million to 1.01 million. Digital sales, however, increased substantially from 5.83 million to 8.19 million.

Overall across all regions, digital sales made up just over 89% of Square Enix's total sales.

Looking ahead, Square Enix will release Final Fantasy VII Revelation on 8th April 2027. Unlike the previous two entries, the Switch 2 version will launch day-and-date alongside all other platforms.
