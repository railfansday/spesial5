All Awards And Nominees At The Game Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Geoff Keighley has just revealed all nominees for The Game Awards 2026, and you can find them right here.

The Game Awards 2026 is just around the corner, and today its host and creator, Geoff Keighley, has revealed the nominees.

It's likely to be a controversial year, with Grand Theft Auto 6 being eligible for the awards, despite not having been released yet, but it makes for an exciting event. It's unlikely anything will beat Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's record-breaking year last year, but if anything does, Rockstar's title is a solid bet.

Below, you'll find all nominees as they're announced.

The big winner of this year, unsurprisingly, is Grand Theft Auto 6. It received seven nominations across the 30 categories, including in the overall Game of the Year. These seven nominations mean it won't be able to beat Clair Obscur's record from last year.

Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem both received six nominations, while 007 First Light and Phantom Blade Zero got five each.
