You Can Visit The Original Vice City in GTA 6 With This Hidden Easter Egg

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Patrick Souza is a writer for DualShockers who has been writing for over 5 years, specializing in JRPGs, roguelikes, and other genres such as MMOs. He previously worked for Prima Games, The Escapist, and The Gamer, and holds a degree in Journalism.

GTA 6 closely follows many of the franchise’s traditions, such as adding tons of easter eggs to be found. Many of them relate to past Rockstar titles, and a very dedicated player has figured out how to uncover one of the game's best-hidden easter eggs, rewarding you with some nostalgia.

Through his X account, content creator Memeio revealed that he managed to visit the 2002 version of Vice City and unlock Tommy Vercetti’s signature T-shirt and motorcycle in the game, while also encountering one of the game’s most prominent figures.

Players need to follow a very specific order of steps to visit the city in its original glory. Memeio claims that it took him 3 days of wandering around the map trying to piece out the clues and actually finding something. And looking at how cryptic figuring this out can be, I’m surprised it didn’t take him longer.

By first visiting the Radio Station in Key Lento, you can find a small room with posters, an old TV, and a power switch you can turn on or off. By interacting with the switch, you’ll immediately hear the Vice City sound effect for finding health, raising eyebrows at the spot.

The next clues are in the two posters on the wall. One is for LPL - Leonida & Light, the power company in the game, and another has a controller image, and it says “Lose track of time”. You can find 12 LPL substations around the world map, all with multiple switches or buttons just like the one in the Radio Station. The substations are also marked with letters on the wall, which spell “Life is passing me by”, a time-advancing cheat code in the original GTA Vice City.

It doesn't take much to figure out that the phrase and the poster are connected. The switches, however, all represent a different key on your controller. Almost as if you could input a command by using them. And you can do just that.

By hitting the correct buttons in each of the 12 substations, spelling out the phrase in order, you’ll receive a phone message from an unknown source, asking you to visit the Turtle Head Power Station between 7 p.m. and 6 a.m. If you do, you’ll see a strange portal forming inside the station, and you can jump on it for a true wave of nostalgia. You’ll be sent to Malibu Club from the original Vice City, with old graphics and all.

Hiding a game inside a game is just the thing that Rockstar loves to do. You even get to meet Kent once again, with his old VA and all. He seems to be doing fine, and he’s a lot less irritated than his last appearance in San Andreas. Which only makes sense, as this is presumably happening during Vice City’s events.

However, the visit is brief, and you’ll be back to the current timeline soon. But you’ll have unlocked both Tommy’s Hawaiian shirt and his motorbike for Jason when you’re back, so all that work wasn’t in vain.

This is far from the only easter egg from the previous game you can find. The Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers’ description mentions the Vercetti State, which can also be visited in the game. This is the mansion Tommy took over during the game, and it serves as a hub throughout the story. Other iconic locations can also be found as you explore Vice City, albeit a bit different from their 2002 counterparts.

This was certainly one of the weirdest, most specific easter eggs I’ve seen, even for GTA standards. But it only shows how much Rockstar has dedicated itself to bringing a unique experience to their long-awaited title. Fortunately, it was much more than just a lot of money.
