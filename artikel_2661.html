How to watch Boston Celtics vs Washington Wizards: TV, live stream, info for tonight's game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The reeling Boston Celtics are on the road to face the surging Washington Wizards in an NBA Cup group stage game Tuesday night on NBC and Peacock.

Boston (11-6) has lost four consecutive (the team's longest losing streak since May 2021) but can clinch Group C of the NBA Cup with wins over Washington and Atlanta on Friday.

Washington (7-11) has won three in a row after an eight-game losing streak.

The Celtics are wrapping a four-game road trip, and it's the second of a three-game homestand for the Wizards.

The Celtics are trying to avoid their first five-game losing streak since the 2014-15 season.

The past three defeats came after Boston blew leads of at least 10 points. In their 88-87 loss Sunday to Golden State, the Celtics were held to a season-low 12 points in the fourth quarter.

Boston has struggled offensively during the slump with its scoring average dropping by 18 points per game while shooting 38.7% from the field (down from 46% in an 11-2 start).

"We're just kind of in like a team-wide funk right now," guard Derrick White said. "Just got to stick together and find a way to get through it. So, can't feel sorry for ourselves. Can't point my fingers. [We have to] figure it out together."
