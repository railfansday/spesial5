Steph Curry injury update: Warriors star’s return date pushed back

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Golden State Warriors will have to wait longer for Steph Curry to return as the team continues to manage his recovery from a torn oblique. The injury has already forced the veteran guard to miss time, and his absence will now extend through another stretch of the schedule.

Curry has been making progress in his rehabilitation, but he has not yet reached the point where he can return to game action. The Warriors have provided a new timeline for his recovery, giving fans a clearer idea of when he could be evaluated again.

The Warriors have ruled Curry out for their next three games.

The 38-year-old has started individual on-court workouts as he works toward a return. He is making progress, but Golden State will not rush him back before he is ready.

ESPN's Anthony Slater reported that Curry will be re-evaluated next Wednesday, following the Warriors' game against the Utah Jazz.

Curry initially suffered the injury during the Warriors' loss to the Houston Rockets. He left in the first quarter after appearing to hurt his right side following a made three-pointer.

An MRI subsequently confirmed an oblique abdominal tear, with Golden State initially announcing that he would be re-evaluated in two weeks.

Before the injury, Curry was enjoying another productive season. He averaged 27.8 points, 3.9 assists and 3.7 rebounds across his first 10 appearances.

Golden State will continue relying on its supporting cast while Curry works toward a return. The Warriors face the LA Clippers on Sunday, at 8:30 p.m. ET.
