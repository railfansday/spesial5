Jadarian Price scores in Week 10 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Seattle Seahawks RB Jadarian Price had nine carries for 36 yards and a touchdown and caught one pass for eight yards against the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 10.

Why it Matters: Although Price found the end zone, he saw fewer touches than Zach Charbonnet for the second game in a row. In fact, Price has 18 fewer carries than Charbonnet over the last two contests. Price does appear to be the preferred option in passing situations, but with Charbonnet handling a bulk of a carries, Price looks like more of a boom-or-bust RB3 as the Seahawks head into a bye week.
