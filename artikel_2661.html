Goo Goo Dolls will play hometown stage for Thanksgiving Day football game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Thanksgiving Day football game lineup will each come with star-studded halftime shows and rock band The Goo Goo Dolls will close out the turkey day games on their hometown stage while the Buffalo Bills take on the Kansas City Chiefs.

The Goo Goo Dolls are proud Buffalo natives, and front man John Rzeznik even recently met a penguin at the Aquarium of Niagara named after the band's biggest hit, "Iris."

The game kicks off at 8:20 p.m. Eastern Time and NBC and Peacock.

The Goo Goo Dolls' performance is just one of a slew of halftime performances with legendary artists this Thanksgiving.

Rock legends U2 played the first Thanksgiving Eve halftime show on Wednesday, Nov. 25 during the Green Bay Packers vs. Los Angeles Rams game.

Legendary hop-hop group Wu-Tang Clan played the halftime show for the 1 p.m. Thanksgiving Day game between the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions

Country music star Miranda Lambert played the halftime show during the 4:30 p.m. Thanksgiving Day game between the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys on Fox

As usual, Thanksgiving Day – Thursday, Nov. 26 – will have three football games:

4:30 p.m. – Philadelphia Eagles vs. Dallas Cowboys

8:20 p.m. – Kansas City Chiefs vs. Buffalo Bills

Here is how to watch each Thanksgiving halftime show and football game:

Watch primetime Thanksgiving football on Peacock

Green Bay Packers vs. Los Angeles Rams with halftime show by U2 – Wednesday, Nov. 25 at 8 p.m. on Netflix

Chicago Bears vs. Detroit Lions with halftime show by Wu-Tang Clan – Thursday, Nov. 26 at 1 p.m. on CBS and Paramount+

Philadelphia Eagles vs. Dallas Cowboys with halftime show by Miranda Lambert – Thursday, Nov. 26 at 4:30 p.m. on Fox

Kansas City Chiefs vs. Buffalo Bills with halftime show by Goo Goo Dolls – Thursday, Nov. 26 at 8: 20 p.m. on NBC and Peacock
