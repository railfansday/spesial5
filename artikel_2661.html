Cincinnati Bengals at Washington Commanders, Week 11 ‘MNF’ live game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In case you hadn't had your fill of the Washington Commanders this season, luck is in the cards this evening. Tonight, the 7-2 Cincinnati Bengals are in Landover, MD for a primetime clash against the 3-5-1 Commanders.

You're undoubtedly in Washington's court this evening, hoping the Bengals somehow fall flat and further back in their pursuit of the AFC one seed. Cincinnati has proven to be a thorn in the Buffalo Bills' side in recent seasons, and there's little love lost between two fan bases.

It's going to take a monumental effort for the Commanders to pull off a win tonight. Jayden Daniels has shown to be an incredibly talented and capable quarterback, and Washington will probably need everything he has if they're to outduel quarterback Joe Burrow, wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase, and the rest of the Bengals.

With that, here's your open thread to close out Week 11 of NFL action with Bengals at Commanders for Monday Night Football! As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
