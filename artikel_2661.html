Wan’Dale Robinson injury could cost Giants in 2027 NFL Draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Tennessee Titans are putting wide receiver Wan'Dale Robinson on injured reserve with an ankle injury, and that could be costly for the New York Giants.

According to the compensatory pick formula, Robinson is the only former Giant the team is expected to receive a compensatory pick for in the 2027 NFL Draft.

Compensatory formula expert Nick Korte of Over the Cap believes that Robinson missing time could drop the compensatory pick the Giants receive for losing Robinson in free agency from the fourth to the fifth round.

After Robinson compiled the only 1,000-yard season of his four-year career with the Giants last year, catching 92 passes for 1,014 yards, the Titans gave the 5-foot-8, 185-pounder a four-year, $70 million contract with $38 million guaranteed.

Robinson has caught just 38 passes for 272 yards for the Titans. He is averaging just 4.2 catches per game after averaging 5.5 and 5.8 per game the last two seasons with the Giants. Robinson's 7.2 yards per catch is also a career low.
