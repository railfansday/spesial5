20 Best Christmas Ornament Picks, Vetted by AD (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There’s no universally correct way to decorate a Christmas tree, though everyone seems to have an opinion about it. Some people swear by coordinated color palettes and perfectly spaced ornaments. Others treasure the funky glass baubles that have graced their family trees for decades, a hand-carved wooden figure picked up at a German Christmas market, or the lopsided ornament made in elementary school. The sentimental ones usually come with better stories. A Christmas tree, after all, is as much a collection of memories as it is a decorating opportunity.

And yet, there’s always room for one more. Maybe it’s a handblown glass ornament, a miniature version of your favorite food, or a decoration that’s so uniquely you it somehow feels essential. Your tree doesn’t need a theme, and your ornaments certainly don’t need to match. We’ve rounded up our favorites, including sculptural glass baubles, handwoven treasures, cheerful little oddities, and keepsakes you’ll look forward to unpacking every December.
