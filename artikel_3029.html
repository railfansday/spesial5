Nano-X (NNOX) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liquidity pressures mount as the company pivots to equipment sales.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 3:07PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Nano-X (NNOX -12.28%) reported a 29% increase in total revenue for the third quarter of 2026, primarily attributed to the consolidation of its Health IT business and growth in teleradiology services. Management stated that the company is prioritizing operational efficiency and commercial execution in the U.S. market as it manages limited liquidity and works to restore compliance with Nasdaq listing requirements. The company is transitioning its sales strategy from a per-scan service model to capital-intensive equipment sales to accelerate cash generation and support long-term sustainability.

Our primary commercial focus remains the U.S., where we continue to strengthen and activate our network of channel partners and build our Nanox Imaging Network infrastructure. Through closer collaboration with our distribution partners, joint sales initiatives, and targeted engagement with healthcare providers, we are working to expand our market reach, develop qualified commercial opportunities, and improve the conversion of these opportunities into operational installations. Our focus is now on working closely with our partners to translate these foundations into system sales, installations, and over time, revenue-generating operations. Alongside these efforts, we continue to support our installed base, extend clinical utilization, and strengthen the physician and customer relationship essential to sustainable commercial growth. Beyond the U.S., we are making measured progress in selected international markets. In Peru, we received the first payment for a Nanox.ARC system, and the system has been shipped. In Argentina, we obtained import approvals and have shipped the systems designated for the market, both under consignments arrangement. Our preliminary third quarter 2026 revenue reflects year-over-year growth. Nevertheless, we recognize that revenue from imaging system sales remain limited relative to our long-term ambitions. At the same time, our current liquidity position requires decisive action.

We are pursuing operating cost reductions, commercial initiatives, and financing and strategic alternatives aimed at strengthening our financial position and supporting our long-term objectives. Our commercial progress provides the foundation for the next phase. To accelerate execution while preserving the capabilities essential to long-term growth, we are focused on four priorities. First, Nanox.ARC and the Nanox Imaging Network, or NIN. Here, we are working to accelerate U.S. commercialization throughout our current channel partners, drive system installations, clinical adoption, and utilization, and activate our constructed NIN sites. On the network itself, we are seeing early scanning and reimbursement activity as we work to expand it.

As a reminder, NIN deploys Nanox systems in care segments such as workers' compensation and concierge practices where reimbursement can be more favorable. Our preliminary models suggest each site could potentially generate $0.5 million-$1 million in annual revenues, depending on utilization, reimbursement, and payer mix. At our first NIN site in Philadelphia, initial activity shows a minimum average of 9-10 patients per day, with average revenues per scan ranging from $163-$249 per patient, based on the scans already performed. Second, our commercial partnerships. We are strengthening our distribution relationships, exploring strategic opportunities for Nanox.AI and our OEM capabilities, and advancing our teleradiology and healthcare IT activities, all with the aim of supporting revenues growth.

This agreement is intended to support the expansion of our AI commercial presence in the Italian market. Nanox.AI signed a distribution agreement with Dosy Health, a Thailand-based healthcare technology company, for Nanox.AI cardiac solution, HealthCCSng, to support its planned introduction and commercialization in Thailand. In Poland, Nanox.AI entered into a distribution agreement with a local distribution partner for commercialization of HealthCCSng, our cardiac solution, and HealthOST, our bone health solution. This agreement further supports our European expansion through local distribution partners.

We continue to expand our commercial and clinical footprint, including new pilot programs with two additional hospitals in India, as we continue to drive broader adoption of Nanox.AI solutions. Moving on from Nanox.AI to our OEM business, revenue in Q3 grew compared to the prior quarter, largely due to our project with the U.S. department of energy and portable X-ray applications for the military. This specific project, if successful, has the potential to be commercialized in 2027. Rounding out our commercial partnerships, the Nanox Health IT acquisition has proven to be a valuable addition to Nano-X and continues to contribute meaningful revenue, providing nearly $1 million during the third quarter.

That brings me to our third priority, operational efficiency, where we are reducing operating costs while preserving our clinical innovation, regulatory, supply continuity, and commercial capabilities. To that end, we continue to align our cost base with our commercial priorities. As previously announced, the additional measures, together with the previously implemented initiatives, are designed to achieve an annualized reduction of 25%-30% in the company operating expenses, excluding related charges once the measures are fully implemented, which the company currently expects to occur by the end of Q1 2027. Fourth, financial flexibility. We are evaluating financing and strategic alternatives to support commercialization and liquidity.

Before I review the third quarter results, I will place them in the context of priorities described earlier. As we work to convert our commercial progress into recurring revenue, we continue to manage our cost base with discipline and to strengthen our financial position. During the quarter, we continued to execute the restructuring of our South Korea operation, including the transition of chip production to our OEM partners. Together with the earlier headcount reduction in Israel, we continue to expect these steps to generate annualized cost savings of approximately $2 million beginning in 2027. We continue to evaluate financing and strategic alternatives as we are addressing the applicable Nasdaq listing requirement with careful attention to impact on our existing shareholders.

Q3 2026 revenues were $4.4 million compared to $3.4 million in the comparable period, representing a year-over-year increase of 29%. The company generated revenues of $3.2 million from our teleradiology services, $0.9 million from our AI and software solutions, and $0.3 million from our sales of imaging system and the OEM services. Q3 2026 adjusted EBITDA loss was $10.1 million compared with the adjusted EBITDA loss of $10.2 million in the comparable period.

Q3 2026 GAAP operating expenses were $11 million compared to GAAP operating expenses of $11.4 million in the comparable period. Q3 2026 non-GAAP operating expenses were $9.8 million compared to a non-GAAP operating expenses of $10.2 million in the comparable period. Q3 2026 GAAP net loss was $12.3 million compared to a GAAP net loss of $13.7 million in the comparable period.

Q3 2026 non-GAAP net loss was $10.6 million compared to a non-GAAP net loss of $9.9 million in the comparable period. Cash and cash equivalents and restricted deposits as of September 30th, 2026 were $26.6 million.
