Grocery Outlet: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Slowing new store openings in favor of infill sites is a bet that the company can grow profitably without leaning on promotions.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Grocery Outlet (GO +1.08%) announced a return to positive comparable store sales, which measure sales at stores open at least a year. The discount grocer is refocusing on its core model of buying deeply discounted surplus inventory to draw budget-conscious shoppers. Three things management said stand out for long-term investors.

Management phased out synthetic broad promotions and refocused on buying surplus brand-name products at deep discounts. That shift expanded customer visits to stores as inflation continued to squeeze household budgets.

After applying a targeted playbook to revitalize surplus grocery items, management repeated the process in frozen and deli. Replacing standard items with higher-margin opportunistic inventory accelerated sales growth across both departments.

To improve returns on capital expenditure, which means money spent on building stores, management is slowing store openings and prioritizing infill sites. Building within existing markets provides predictable returns and lowers execution risks.

Sales momentum accelerated entering the fourth quarter as customer traffic offset smaller basket sizes. Management is monitoring freight costs and maintenance expenses while leaning into established store footprints.

Management expects gross margins to expand year over year as higher-margin surplus deals replace artificial promotions. Capital allocation discipline will guide store expansion and supply chain efficiency into 2027.
