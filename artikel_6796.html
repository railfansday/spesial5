The “Brainy Heel” Is This Season’s Shoe MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

They’ll make every outfit feel more thoughtful

Sometimes, you just want to dress for yourself. It’s not about man-repelling so much as just responding to your own needs: I want to feel good, look well-dressed, and be drawn to what I’m wearing—for me, not them.

Looking at the new fall/winter 2026 shoes, from the feathered hiking styles at Prada to the peaked-toe lace-up shoes at Jil Sander to the grown-up Mary Janes at Chanel, I found myself coming back to that thought. These are heels for me, not them: attractive, but not designed purely to attract; chic, but not flashy; and, dare I say it, made with the wearer’s comfort in mind.

They’re shoes that make you think—and make you wonder what the woman wearing them is thinking too. You’re intrigued by her sartorial choices, especially her heels. Brainy heels? Perhaps. But like the woman herself, maybe they don’t need a title. Just a formula.

As with any well-designed object, what makes these shoes appealing is the law of proportions—the relationship between shapes that creates a visual balance. And what makes them compelling is that there’s always something just a little off, something to make you look again or make you wonder why, something that opposes and attracts at the same time. We’ve written about the wrong-shoe theory, the comfort-first commuter shoe, and plenty of the season’s chicest styles. The brainy heel brings them all together in one slightly off-kilter package.
