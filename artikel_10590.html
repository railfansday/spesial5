YouTuber blasts “coward” Airbnb host after copyright strike for reaction video

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A YouTuber has blasted an Airbnb host as a “coward” for hitting her video with a copyright strike as she responded to a TikTok from him.

Over the years, plenty of content creators have ended up in beefs because they’ve had their videos hit by copyright strikes from someone else. The strike usually means that their video or stream is quickly removed from view and could harm a channel in the long term.

Well, in the case of YouTuber brookesharks, she revealed that she had a video taken down through a strike.

“If you noticed my most recent video is gone, it’s because this f**kin guy copyright striked it because I responded to his tiktok,” the YouTuber said in a community post on her channel.

“This results in a copyright strike on my channel which can get my channel removed if it happens again, even though this is fair use content and protected by copyright law. F**king coward.”

Viewers quickly urged brookesharks to appeal against the strike, so that the video could be reuploaded and help get the strike removed from her channel.

“Frivolous copyright strikes are against terms of service. I hope he has the kind of day that he deserves,” one said. “YouTubes copyright system is laughably broken. I dont think it’ll get any better, another added. “Appeal that s*it! You didnt break any rules,” another commented.

Others noted that the video had gotten on the TikToker’s radar as screenshots from the YouTuber’s video had been posted in his comments.

Some creators have even successfully sued YouTube after being banned, but their channels still haven’t been reinstated.
