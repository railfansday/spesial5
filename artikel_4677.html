How restaurants can make sustainable dining choices easier while still making a profit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Canadians are eating out less. A 2025 survey found 57% of them are cooking at home more often to save money, while 69% of restaurant operators say customers are dining out less because of affordability. According to Restaurants Canada, 41% of operators are either losing money or breaking even.
