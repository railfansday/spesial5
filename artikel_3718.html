There’s An IRL GTA 6 Live Event And You’ll Need To Win A Lottery To Get In

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cardi BGrand Theft Auto 6Grand Theft Auto VIGTA 6MusicRockstar Games

Rockstar Games is billing the event as “Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival.” It’ll take place in Miami, Florida later this month, feature live performances from Cardi B, Travis Scott, and others who appear on the game’s soundtrack, and the only way to get a ticket is to enter a lottery.

Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival is scheduled for November 21 in Mana Wynwood, the city’s convention center. That’s the Saturday after the game launches on November 19. For those who would rather go see live music than be at home playing the game, the “one-of-a-kind launch event” will feature two stages hosting “nonstop music.”

Entry is limited to people 17 and older, and the only way to get a ticket is through an online lottery. People have until 4:00 p.m. ET on November 10 to enter. Winners will be notified later that day. Tickets will then go on sale for the lottery winners on November 11 at 12:00 p.m. ET. Rockstar hasn’t said how much the tickets will actually cost yet.
