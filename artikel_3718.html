Bunnie Xo Reacts to Her Pin-Up Poster Being Removed From Jelly Roll’s Bar

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

UPDATE — 11/12/26, 19:00 p.m. ET: Bunnie Xo has delivered a shady response to a massive pin-up poster of her being removed from ex Jelly Roll’s Nashville bar.

Hours after the emergence of a viral video showing the poster— which originally hung over the Goodnight Nashville bar and featured Bunnie X0, 46, in a swimsuit and heels — being loaded onto a truck and driven away from the establishment, the “Dumb Blonde” host reacted by lip syncing to 50 Cent‘s iconic “Why am I in it?” rant for an Instagram video.

“When the life-size cutout of you gets loaded onto the back of a dump truck in broad daylight and you weren’t even offered it,” she quipped in the Thursday, November 12, caption, adding, “Who has it? I want it.”

Jelly Roll, 41, has not commented on the poster’s removal from Goodnight Nashville.

Jelly Roll’s Nashville bar is getting a makeover with a little less Bunnie Xo flair in the wake of their recent divorce.

Photographers captured a pin-up poster of Bunnie Xo (real name Alisa DeFord) being hauled out of her ex-husband’s Goodnight Nashville establishment on Wednesday, November 11, per TMZ. The massive poster — which depicts Bunnie Xo in a sparkling bathing suit and heels — used to have a central spot in Goodnight Nashville right above the bar.

The outlet reported that the Bunnie Xo pin-up was loaded into the back of a truck and driven away from the property on Wednesday, though it’s not clear where the prop will end up.

Jelly Roll (Jason Bradley DeFord) filed for divorce from Bunnie Xo after 10 years of marriage in May. Us subsequently learned in July that the DeFords reached a quick settlement, with a judge signing off on “an absolute divorce” on the grounds of “irreconcilable differences” that same month.

The former couple have repeatedly maintained that their separation and subsequent divorce was entirely amicable.

The three-time Grammy winner encouraged his fans not to believe speculation about his divorce during a Saratoga Springs, New York, concert in June.

“I wasn’t going to talk about this tonight, but while we’re talking about liars… the internet is a liar,” Jelly Roll insisted in footage shared via his Instagram. “It’s the only time and the only city I’m going to speak about this so break your camera phones out now.”

He added, “Me and my wife are best friends. We will always be best friends. We just got off the phone earlier today … Nobody cheated on nobody. She just did a whole podcast about it. You can go watch it. Every word of it is the truth. That will be my best friend forever. This is the only time I will ever speak about it.”

During a June 18 edition of her “Dumb Blonde” podcast, Bunnie Xo mentioned that a lack of communication was a key reason why she and Jelly Roll separated.

“J and I have never really been good at having disagreements. So, we were the type of couple who never argued. So, he would be holding things in. I would be holding things in, and that’s a recipe for disaster,” Bunnie told podcast listeners. “We always preach you gotta be comfortable having the uncomfortable conversations, and we did that for eight years in. Then the past year and a half, we kind of got away from that because, we’re in a different life right now.”

Their disagreements apparently came to a head during argument on Mother’s Day when Bunnie Xo encouraged Jelly Roll to “file the f***ing divorce papers.”

“In our relationship, that is the one cardinal thing that you don’t say, even though my husband has said it numerous times,” she acknowledged. “But, when I say it, it really holds weight because I’m not the type of person who says what I don’t mean.”

Bunnie Xo has recently started spending time with Calabasas Confidential’s Dylan Wolf, who she credited with giving her her “smile back” following her marriage breakdown. (They were first spotted kissing at Jelly Roll’s bar in Nashville during the 4th of July weekend.)

Wolf, 34, exclusively confirmed to Us in October that he was “having the time of my life with an amazing person.”

“I’m happy,” he declared. “I’m happy in this chapter of my life, and I hope it doesn’t change anytime soon.”

Bunnie Xo indicated on her podcast that Jelly Roll has also started dating other people.
