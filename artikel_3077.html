Nike Is Down 79% From Its Peak. Here's How Much $10,000 Invested Pays in Dividends Now.

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The sneaker giant's stock is down, but its yield is up.

By Jennifer Saibil – Nov 22, 2026 at 5:40AM EST

Nike (NKE -4.11%) is still the king of athletic wear, but its sales have been sinking for a few years now. The activewear market has been inundated with new brands that are attracting the premium shopper, and Nike hasn't kept up.

Nike is now 79% off its all-time high. However, Nike pays a fantastic dividend. If you'd invested $10,000 in Nike stock today, here's how much you'd get in dividends.

Nike Mercurial Vapor 17 and Superfly 11 soccer shoes. Image source: Nike.

Nike is a blue chip dividend stock, which is a status that implies reliability. It's the top stock in its league, with years of growth and dividend history, and even with its sales declines, its trailing 12-month revenue of $46 billion is much higher than that of any of its direct competitors; second-place Adidas (ADDYY -4.25%) has $31 billion.

However, sales fell 4% from last year in the fiscal 2027 first quarter (ended Aug. 31), and management expects a high-single-digit decline for the full year.

If you'd invest $10,000 in Nike stock today, you'd get 263 shares at the current price of $38.

Nike stock yields 4.3% at this price, just off a 10-year high, and it pays $1.64 per share in annual dividends. Owning 263 shares gets you $431.32, which could be a reliable component of a retirement portfolio. For investors who have time to wait it out, it could also give you exposure to a Nike rebound.
