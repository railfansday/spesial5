Black Friday and Cyber Monday have become two of the biggest shopping events on the Australian retail calendar, making them an ideal time to tackle those larger purchases you've been putting off. Whether you're upgrading your TV, replacing an ageing mattress or investing in a new appliance, many retailers reserve some of their strongest offers of the year for this end-of-year sales period.

While it's impossible to know every deal ahead of time, there are a few trends that tend to repeat each year. Amazon's usually among the top retailers offering with major discounts, particularly across its own range of devices, including Kindle ereaders, smart speakers home security products.

JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys are also major players across popular tech categories, though buying direct from brands like Dell, Dyson and Samsung can often be the best option in terms of savings. If you've been eyeing a new laptop, desktop PC or other big-ticket electronics purchase, holding off until the sales begin could be a smart move. With competition between retailers at its peak, Black Friday is often when some of the year's most attractive tech deals appear.

Black Friday 2025 falls on Friday, November 27, 2026, with sales extending through Cyber Monday, ending at 11:59 pm (AEST) on Monday, November 30, 2026.

With that in mind, you can anticipate deals starting well ahead of the official launch date. Retailers like Amazon, The Good Guys, Dell and Myer are known for rolling out early discounts, so be sure to watch for great offers in the weeks leading up to the big day.

In Australia, plenty of retailers will be vying to offer the best Black Friday deals and discounts. Which retailer has the best offers really depends on what you're looking to buy. For example, Amazon, The Good Guys, JB Hi-Fi, and Harvey Norman typically provide the biggest savings on 4K TVs, smartphones, and appliances.

If you're in the market for a new PC or laptop, it's a good idea to buy directly from brands like Dell and HP. Also, keep an eye on eBay Australia, which usually has discounts on almost everything, with even bigger savings available for eBay Plus members.

Originating in the United States, the term "Black Friday" in relation to shopping dates back to 1961. It was first used in Philadelphia to describe the heavy pedestrian and vehicle traffic that followed the Thanksgiving holiday.

The term gained popularity and spread nationwide by 1975. Over time, Black Friday took on a new meaning for retailers, signifying the point in the year when businesses went "in the black," finally turning a profit. This allowed them to offer major discounts on leftover stock, resulting in massive deals leading up to the holiday season.

Although the timing differs from one country to another, the Black Friday sales event has become a global phenomenon, which is why Australia now participates.
