Cathie Wood Keeps Buying Archer Aviation Stock. Should You?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The tech-focused investor sees promise in electric vertical takeoff vehicles.

By Brett Schafer – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:30PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Archer Aviation ›

Archer Aviation (ACHR -8.24%) is down 72% from its highs. This is not deterring Cathie Wood from buying the dip. The founder of Ark Invest has kept scooping up shares of the electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) upstart, keeping it a sizable position in the defense- and autonomous-themed exchange-traded funds (ETFs) she manages.

eVTOLs as a theme have gone through a rough patch in 2026, but Archer Aviation is getting close to making a commercial debut with its Midnight aircraft. Does that make the stock a good buy-the-dip candidate today?

eVTOLs are a novel aircraft solution that combines the mobility of helicopters with fewer nuisances, such as dangerous landings and noise pollution. These are small vehicles propelled by multiple rotors and electric motors, designed to carry people over traffic in urban areas, such as for airport visits.

Archer Aviation has been pushing to get its Midnight vehicle approved by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), a slow process for a type of aircraft that has never flown commercial customers before. It is conducting numerous tests in California, including a recent test flight across the San Francisco Bay Area, and is setting up flight infrastructure in Los Angeles ahead of the 2028 Olympics, where it is a promotional partner.

The company hopes to start flying passengers in 2027, beginning in California and the United Arab Emirates (UAE). Timelines can be finicky with FAA certifications, but this looks like the long road to certification is nearing its end, with a huge boost in marketing coming with the Olympics next year. Then, Archer Aviation will start generating real revenue.

In addition to commercial flying, Archer is now working with defense start-up Anduril to develop an eVTOL for military use, such as shuttling soldiers in and out of combat. The concept is in its early days, but this could help Archer Aviation diversify beyond its commercial eVTOL ambitions and bring reliable military revenue to its income statement.

Plus, it recently acquired some businesses from Boeing, including a company called Insitu that has $200 million in existing revenues from reconnaissance drones. This will play into Archer's long-term ambitions in commercial and defense autonomous eVTOL systems (along with other aircraft), and will immediately contribute revenues to the business.

Right now, Archer Aviation is not generating much revenue. However, with the start of its commercial eVTOL operations, along with the defense partnership and the Insitu acquisition, the company could see a meaningful inflection in sales over the next few years, which is perhaps what Cathie Wood is looking at when buying shares right now.

Because it is investing in airport infrastructure (its eVTOLs need to land and take off somewhere) and manufacturing its Midnight aircraft, Archer Aviation is burning a lot of cash. Free cash flow was negative $751 million over the last 12 months, with nearly zero revenue. Expect this type of cash burn to continue in the quarters ahead, even many quarters after commercial passengers start flying.

This could be a concern because of the dwindling cash on Archer's balance sheet. It had $1.35 billion in cash on the balance sheet at the end of last quarter. Unless it can turn around its cash burn, the company will run out of funds within the next two years. Cash burn has only worsened in recent quarters, I may add.

Continued progress on commercialization may help the stock price in the interim, allowing Archer to raise more funds. However, this will be a drag on long-term value creation.

Archer's market cap is already at $3 billion, pricing in significant future growth from its eVTOL network. These aircraft may be the future of transportation in cities, but the thesis will play out over decades. Archer stock does not look like a good investment at current prices.
