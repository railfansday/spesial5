GTA 6 players furious as physical copies reportedly arrive with missing or already-used codes

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Some GTA 6 players who purchased physical copies of the highly-anticipated game are reporting issues with missing or already-redeemed download codes.

GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19, 2026. However, in a controversial departure from traditional physical releases, boxed copies of the game contain no disc, instead providing a digital download code that allows players to preload the title ahead of launch.

As these copies began arriving ahead of release, numerous players took to social media to report that their boxes contained either no download code or one that had already been redeemed.

According to Twisted Voxel, numerous prospective players who purchased their copies from retailers including GameStop and Best Buy, as well as directly from Rockstar Games, have not been able to redeem their code successfully.

Some claimed they were denied replacements or were directed to Rockstar’s support team, with one player alleging their support ticket was closed without a resolution.

Meanwhile, reports of already-redeemed codes have prompted speculation that thieves could be obtaining them without opening the packaging.

Some have suggested that shining a flashlight through certain international versions of the packaging could reveal the printed code, potentially allowing it to be redeemed before purchase.

However, this theory remains unverified, with Kotaku reporting that its own editor-in-chief was unable to replicate the trick.

The controversy has also prompted attention towards PlayStation’s dedicated GTA 6 support page, which offers guidance for players experiencing missing, damaged, invalid or previously redeemed codes. Sony advises affected customers to contact their retailer for assistance, including those seeking refunds.
