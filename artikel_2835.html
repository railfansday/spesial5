BTSC Thursday Night Football thread: Jaguars at Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It's an AFC battle to kick off Week 9 of the NFL season with the Jacksonville Jaguars traveling to take on the Ravens in Baltimore on Thursday Night Football.

The Jaguars, at 5-2, have emerged as one of the best teams in the league this season, and are hoping to gain another win against the struggling Ravens, who recently lost both starting quarterback Lamar Jackson and backup Tyler Huntley to injured reserve. As a result, Baltimore will be starting Joe Fagnano, a 2026 undrafted free agent from UConn who saw his first-ever NFL action in 40 snaps last week, in an attempt to stay above .500 and stay in the mix in a competitive AFC North.

What will you be watching for in this game? Have anyone in fantasy football? Join the Behind the Steel Curtain community and let us know in the comments below ahead of a (hopefully) stress-free Steelers bye week.
