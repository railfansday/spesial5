Bebé completes CD Teruel move 16 years after £7.4m Manchester United transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Former Manchester United attacker Bebé has officially joined Spanish club CD Teruel for the remainder of the 2026/27 season.

Bebé joined Ibiza in February 2025 after mutually terminating his Racing Ferrol contract, only five months after arriving at the Galician club. He ended that campaign with six goals and four assists from 18 appearances.

Ibiza renewed his deal in June 2025, but the forward confirmed in May 2026 that his contract was expiring and said his representative would take care of his future once the season had finished (via Flashscore).

The 36-year-old subsequently became a free agent. He was already training at Teruel's Pinilla base by Wednesday, with the club considering whether to add him to their squad (via Diario de Huesca).

Teruel have now completed the move, with Bebé registered as a first-team player for the 2026/27 campaign (via COPE).

The Spanish outlet highlighted the Cape Verde international's positional flexibility, stating that he "can play on both wings as well as through the middle of the attack".

United signed Bebé from Vitória Guimarães in August 2010 after paying his £7.4 million release clause. The move – championed by superagent Jorge Mendes, came only weeks after he had joined Vitória from Estrela da Amadora. Sir Alex Ferguson famously admitted to never having seen him play before he signed him.

He made seven competitive appearances for the Red Devils and scored twice, finding the net in the League Cup and Champions League. His two substitute appearances in the Premier League came during United's title-winning 2010/11 campaign.

Bebé later had loan spells with Beşiktaş, Rio Ave and Paços de Ferreira. Benfica bought him for £2.4 million in July 2014 after he was omitted from United's pre-season tour of the United States.

His subsequent Spanish clubs included Córdoba, Rayo Vallecano, Eibar, Real Zaragoza, Racing Ferrol and Ibiza. After representing Portugal at youth level, he made his senior Cape Verde debut in March 2022 and scored against Guadeloupe.

Having already trained with Teruel before signing, Bebé is likely to be integrated quickly into the first-team squad.
