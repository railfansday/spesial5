Shaquille O’Neal hits Angel Reese with all-time troll job at LSU vs Alabama game

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Angel Reese will never stop being trolled from her own celebration.

Just before LSU fell, 31-13 to Alabama at Tiger Stadium, Reese was seen hanging out with her fellow LSU alumnus, Shaquille O'Neal, and that's when he continued, what's been a recent tradition of trolling the former National Champion.

Reese was filming O'Neal on the LSU sideline, and when she pointed the camera at him and told him to say something, he put his two fingers together and hit her with the "too small" celebration.

After he taunted her, Reese is heard in the video saying, "it's okay, it's okay," and then jokingly forces her camera in his face, pretending to be taking a shot at the four-time NBA champion.

Shaquille O"Neal at Fanatics Fest over the summer. ZUMAPRESS.com

The "too small" celebration has been thrown at Reese's way since she did it in Game 3 of the WNBA Finals.

During the series, the Valkyries ended up getting the last laugh after the two teams went a full seven games, resulting in Golden State Prevailing with a 68-63 victory.

In Game 7, Gabby Williams led the Valkyries with 30 points, where she scored 28 in the second half.

Once the game was called, Warriors rookie sensation Yaxel Lendeborg, who was in attendance was seen running onto the court and started taunting Reese with her own celebration.

The trolling continued into this past week, during the Valkyries Championship Parade, when former Warriors big man, Festus Ezeli, was seen holding a shirt of Reese doing the gesture.

Ezeli later put on the shirt and was doing the "too small" taunt to the fans in attendance.

Shaquille O'Neal dunking as a member of the Lakers. NBAE via Getty Images

While O'Neal was taunting Reese, it comes from a place of love, as he's grown to be a father figure to her.

O'Neal went to LSU from 1989-1992 and was the National Player of the Year winniner for the 1991 season.

After spending his first four seasons with the Orlando Magic, O'Neal signed a record-breaking deal with the Lakers in 1996 — that would be followed up with a second contract in 2003.

Angel Reese sitting courtside at an NBA game between the Timberwolves and Pelicans. AP Photo/Matthew Hinton

Reese played two seasons at LSU, from 2022-2024.

After her senior season ended, she was drafted with the No.7 overall pick by the Chicago Sky.
