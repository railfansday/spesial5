Bills news: Final thoughts on Buffalo’s 26-9 win over Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Buffalo Bills (9-1) accomplished their goal of going 1-0 each week thanks to Sunday's 26-9 triumph over the Miami Dolphins (1-9), but just because the Bills dispatched of the Dolphins, doesn't mean there aren't a slew of issues for Buffalo to address following a lackluster offensive performance.

Miami's defense put up a valiant effort, picking off Josh Allen twice while pushing around Buffalo's top-notch offensive line, and while the Bills were able to amass more than 400 yards of total offense, the Dolphins forced the Bills to settle for field goals on three of their five drives into the red zone.

But in the NFL, they don't award style points, and all that matters is the final score. Today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks leads off with some final thoughts and observations from a ho-hum effort by the Bills in picking up their sixth straight victory.

Check out the news items below and then let us know your thoughts on the win in the comments section below.

After limiting the Kyle McCord-led Dolphins offense to just three field goals, Buffalo's defense has now not allowed a touchdown over its last 45 possessions dating back to the first quarter of a 36-10 home win over the Baltimore Ravens in Week 8.

On a sunny, uncharacteristically warm late November afternoon at Highmark Stadium, Josh Allen completed 27 of 45 passes for 304 yards with one touchdown and two interceptions, while Khalil Shakir hauled in five passes for 101 yards and D.J. Moore caught eight balls for 91 yards with a touchdown. James Cook rushed for 89 yards and found the endzone, the ninth straight time he has rushed for at least 75 yards with a rushing TD, as the Bills led from start to finish against the last-place Dolphins.

Edge rusher Greg Rousseau finished with two sacks and tallied six quarterback hits, while Buffalo's secondary broke up nine passes. The Bills held Miami to just 2 of 14 on third downs, and Amani Hooker has been an instant upgrade to the back of the defense.

But despite the relative ease of the win, the Bills have some issues to clean up before they face Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving.
