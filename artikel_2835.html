Reacts poll: Throw it to Bryant more

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

While Steelers fans are still figuring out WTF just happened with their team in the last five days, Broncos fans are wondering when Pat Bryant is going to become the primary target for Bo Nix.

Well, that may be an exaggeration, but Broncos Country does believe No. 13 is the most reliable receiver on a team with a slew of pass-catchers.

By a huge margin, Broncos fans think Bryant is one of the best targets for Nix.

With just 21 targets, Bryant has caught 16 this season for a total of 221 yards and 2TDs, for a 13.8 average on yards per catch.

By comparison, Jaylen Waddle and Courtland Sutton each have more total catches and yards, but the number of drops is much higher. Waddle is 33 of 58 for 398 yards and 2 TDs (12.8 ypc average) and Sutton is 25 of 48 for 266 yards and 2 TDs (10.6 ypc).

No wonder only 15% of fans picked Sutton as most reliable and just 1% said Waddle, the receiver who is supposed to be our new super star.

Not surprisingly, when Nix is throwing to Bryant his passer rating is gooooooooood.

While the Broncos have a lot of receiving targets — and all want the ball — second-year Bryant has earned a few more targets than the rest.
