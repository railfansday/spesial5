Monero’s Full Chain Membership Proofs++ (FCMP++) eliminates the need for decoys. It replaces rings by providing zero-knowledge membership proofs across the entire blockchain output set. In other words, it mathematically proves that the spent XMR belongs to the Monero blockchain, making every previous output created part of the decoy set.

A mainnet activation date has not been confirmed for the FCMP++ and Carrot, the addressing protocol and wallet key hierarchy upgrade designed for the FCMP++ network upgrade. AMBCrypto reported that zkSecurity’s audit of FCMP++ was “complete, zero-knowledge, and sound”.

Using zero-knowledge cryptography, private ZEC balances and transactions are tracked in shielded pools. Zcash has five of these shielded pools, as well as its transparent pool. Once funds are inside, the older pools can not be shut down, but can be made spend-only.

The Ironwood (NU6.3) upgrade was launched on the 28th of July, 2026, addressing the Orchard “infinity” bug found in May. It also introduced a new shielded pool and restricted the Orchard pool. Funds exiting Orchard must now pass through Zcash’s turnstile, for supply integrity verification, to enter Ironwood.

The NU7 upgrade implemented in November aims to reduce block processing times by 67%, from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. It also reduced the wallet scanning workload required to perform private payments.

When transactions are shielded in some form, anti-money laundering (AML) tracing is hindered or prevented. This brings strict regulatory restrictions on shielded transactions, as they require visibility in fund flows to prevent illicit financing.

The latter argues that oversight is a necessity to prevent serious crimes and to detect and disrupt illicit activity.

Selective disclosure, such as in Zcash, Midnight, or using Identity Frameworks in Ethereum, can achieve this by sharing transaction details without revealing the entire financial history.

Zero-knowledge compliance is another approach that can prove that a transaction meets certain criteria, without exposing other relevant details.
