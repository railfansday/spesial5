Biofrontera (BFRI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Liquidity concerns and a new skin cancer indication shape the quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Biofrontera Inc. (BFRI -24.39%) reported third-quarter results highlighted by the FDA approval of Ameluz for the treatment of superficial basal cell carcinoma and a favorable ruling from U.S. Customs and Border Protection regarding its redesigned RhodoLED XLC lamp. Management stated that the new indication makes Ameluz the first photodynamic therapy approved in the United States for a skin cancer. The company filed a supplemental New Drug Application to expand the use of Ameluz to the extremities, neck, and trunk, with a regulatory decision date assigned for Aug. 2027. Operations during the quarter were impacted by legal and remediation costs associated with the International Trade Commission matter, though the company reported a reduction in the sales volume affected by the exclusion order. Management detailed a mitigation plan to address liquidity concerns, including focusing on revenue growth from the new indication and potentially drawing on a working line of credit.
