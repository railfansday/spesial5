Walmart drops Apple Pay support from its app and website

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Walmart appears to have dropped support for Apple Pay on its website and in its iPhone app. This comes just a few weeks after the retailer finally added support for Apple’s payment platform to its app and website.

Appleosophy spotted the change last week. There’s no indication whether the feature was removed deliberately or if this is some sort of bug.

In the “Payment method” section of the checkout page on Walmart’s app and website, it no longer lists Apple Pay as an accepted payment method where it previously did. Not only that, but it doesn’t show up under the “Other ways to pay” or “More ways to pay” sections of the checkout page either.

Walmart still accepts Apple Pay in its retail stores. The retailer began rolling out contactless payment support in August, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, contactless cards, and more. For years, Walmart refused to support Apple Pay, instead trying (and failing) to convince shoppers to use its own Walmart Pay platform.

We’ll update this post if Walmart comments on Apple Pay’s disappearance from its website and app. Hopefully, it’s a bug that the company fixes sooner rather than later.
