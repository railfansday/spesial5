Legal sector jobs hit record high in October, new data shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Data from the U.S. Department of Labor shows that employment in the U.S. legal sector hit a record high in October. (Image from Shutterstock)

Legal sector jobs have reached a record high, hitting more than 1.25 million positions for the first time in October, according to seasonally adjusted data released by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics on Friday.

Last month, 1,251,200 people worked in lawyer, paralegal and other law-related professional roles, up 2,400 jobs from September and 3,200 jobs from August, according to Law360, which reported on the government statistics.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics also notes that jobs in the legal sector have increased each month since March, Law360 reports. They have grown by 22,800 year over year.

The broader economy in the United States added 165,000 jobs in October, according to Reuters, which has additional reporting. The unemployment rate fell to 4.1% last month.
