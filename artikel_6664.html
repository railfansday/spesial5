Chili’s Customers Suffered Chemical Burns From Margaritas Contaminated With Cleaning Product

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A simple mistake behind the bar had some seriously frightening consequences.

A North Carolina Chili’s is facing questions after a margarita mix-up left customers and employees with chemical burns.

A group of customers stopped by Chili’s Grill & Bar in Forest City, North Carolina shortly after doors opened on October 28. After ordering a round of margaritas, three diners immediately experienced discomfort and chemical burns in and around their mouths.

According to a statement from the Foothills Health District, the customers notified Chili’s staff before leaving to seek medical attention. Public health officials immediately suspended the location’s food service permit while they investigated the incident.

Investigators soon identified the culprit behind the chemical burns: a granulated degreasing chemical.

Two days before the incident, a third-party contractor was power-washing the restaurant’s exterior when a Chili’s employee received some of the cleaning product. The employee placed it in an unlabeled container, which was then brought inside and left at the bar.

On the morning of October 28, an employee setting up the bar mistook the degreaser for sugar and used it to prepare the margarita station for lunch service.

The three customers weren't the only ones to suffer from chemical burns. After the guests reported their injuries, two Chili’s employees tasted the substance in the container and experienced similar symptoms.

The Foothills Health District completed its investigation on October 29 and reinstated the restaurant’s food service permit after officials confirmed it met applicable food safety requirements. While local health officials have closed their investigation, Chili’s is still conducting its own.

“We are aware of an isolated incident at our restaurant in Forest City, North Carolina, that resulted in injuries to two of our guests and Team Members,” a Chili’s spokesperson tells Delish. “Our team took immediate action to support those affected and avoid any further injuries. We are actively investigating the circumstances related to this incident as the health and wellbeing of our guests and Team Members remain our top priority.”
