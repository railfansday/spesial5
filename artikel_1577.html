Çelik pulled from Türkiye XI with left hamstring tightness as Kalulu is already out

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Zeki Çelik was withdrawn from Türkiye's starting line-up in Bordeaux after feeling tightness in the same left thigh muscle he injured in October. With Pierre Kalulu already sidelined, Juventus face tests that could leave them without both main right-sided defenders for Venezia and Omonia.

Zeki Çelik’s international week ended before kick-off in Bordeaux. The Juventus full-back was named in Türkiye’s starting line-up for Sunday’s Nations League match against France. His left hamstring tightened up as he went through the warm-up. He was taken out of the matchday squad as a precaution. Barış Alper Yılmaz started in his place.

An Emirhan Topçu own goal and a goal from Eli Junior Kroupi put France ahead. Abdülkerim Bardakcı scored a late reply for Türkiye. The result left France top of Nations League Group A1 with 13 points. They are through to the quarter-finals, which are due next March.

Çelik now returns to Turin for tests at J|Medical. Those results will decide how Juventus line up at the back after the international break.

The location is what worries Juventus. Çelik was substituted at half-time in the 2-2 Europa League draw with Rennes. His scans took place on the morning of Friday 23 October. He began a rehabilitation programme straight away and was expected back after the November break.

Tuttosport counted him among Spalletti’s first returning players, alongside Vicario and Gatti. The paper placed the Bordeaux problem in the biceps femoris of his left thigh. That is the same area as the October injury.

Çelik had only just returned after weeks out. That is why the warm-up incident has raised fears of a relapse. No diagnosis has yet been confirmed.

Juventus were already without Pierre Kalulu. He picked up the injury in the game against Fiorentina. He began treatment and was withdrawn from the France squad. Sky Sport Italia expected him to be out for about three weeks. That would rule him out of both the Venezia and Omonia matches.

If Çelik’s problem proves to be a relapse, Spalletti could be without both of his main right-sided defenders for those two games. The schedule offers little room for recovery. The Omonia match is the second of four games in 12 days.

The league position adds pressure. After 11 rounds Juventus are seventh in Serie A with 18 points. Their record reads five wins, three draws and three defeats.

Venezia visit Turin first, at 12:30 on 22 November in round 12 of Serie A. Omonia then come to the Allianz Stadium at 21:00 on 26 November in the Europa League league phase.
