Sky: Milan have ‘expressed an interest’ in teenage QPR sensation Scarlett

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

AC Milan are one of the teams interested in signing a talent from Queens Park Rangers, one journalist has claimed.

Milan are always on the lookout for ways to bolster the talent pool at their disposal, and this was seen in the summer when the likes of Alphadjo Cisse and Sankhoun Diawara were welcomed. They have an eye on the English market, as the signing of Aurelien Guernier demonstrated.

According to Anthony Joseph of Sky Sports in the UK, there is a new forward that plays for a Championship club who has caught Milan’s eye: Leon Scarlett, who has already broken into the first team in west London.

“Scarlett, who can play on both wings, joined QPR from Arsenal’s youth academy in April last year and is already a Wales U21 international. His impressive rise has alerted the attention of Milan and Frankfurt, who are continuing to monitor his situation.”

What is intriguing is that Eintracht are the other suitors in the running, the club that Markus Krösche has just left. He is expected to become the Rossoneri’s new Head of Football, so the interest from both red and black teams might be no coincidence.
