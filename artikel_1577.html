Bundesliga: Kane and Olise out due to injury, Bayern Munich surprised by Cologne

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bayern Munich conceded a surprising draw against Cologne this Friday evening at the Allianz Arena. For his return to the bench after almost a month of absence due to meningitis, Vincent Kompany was certainly hoping for a better evening. The Belgian technician, who appeared with his left arm in a sling, quickly saw his plans thwarted by injuries to Kane, who came out in the 20th minute, then to Olise, replaced at the break and seen on crutches as he left the stadium.

On the pitch, the visitors even opened the scoring in the 12th minute thanks to Moore, perfectly served by a backheel from El Mala. Brown finally equalized for the Bavarians in the 37th minute with a beautiful shot from the left, without managing to mask his team’s difficulties. Despite the return of Jamal Musiala and the first minutes of young Frenchman Noah Codjo-Evora, Bayern Munich remains under pressure from Borussia Dortmund.
