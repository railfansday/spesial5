Forget Kroupi: Tottenham Can Sign Next Harry Kane Nobody Is Talking About

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ten games into the 2026/27 Premier League season and it is fair to say that Tottenham are lacking a little bit in firepower.

Spurs went the first four games of the campaign without scoring and are among the lowest scorers in the league now, with only Sunderland and Nottingham Forest having scored fewer.

Centre-back Jan Paul van Hecke is the club's top scorer in the Premier League with two goals, with seven players on one goal each.

GIVEMESPORT understands that Tottenham will enter the January transfer window looking for a striker, with senior football correspondent Ben Jacobs saying in September: "Tottenham are prepared to add another striker mid-season in the winter market if goals continue to dry up."

Eli Junior Kroupi is a name who has been linked with a move to N17, with Spurs holding an interest in him in the summer transfer window before he picked up an injury that kept him out of action for the first few months of the season.

He is back for Bournemouth now, but will command a hefty fee, with the Cherries reluctant to sell mid-season. Igor Matanovic at Freiburg is another name that has been tipped for a move to the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, but Jacobs added in his most recent GMS newsletter that he was "unlikely to be available in the winter market".

With Dominic Solanke and Omar Marmoush on one goal apiece and Richarlison left out of the club's Premier League squad altogether, Spurs are in need of firepower if they are to continue their charge up the table. And there is one player that nobody is talking about who could just be Spurs' next Harry Kane.

Replacing someone of Kane's stature is no mean feat, given he has just won the Ballon d'Or with Bayern Munich to become one of the oldest Ballon d'Or winners. Richarlison, Solanke and now Marmoush have tried to live up to those lofty expectations and it would be unfair to even expect them to get anywhere close to Kane's standards. He is the club's all-time top scorer, after all.

The 26-year-old is 6ft 1ins, one inch shorter than Kane, and has something of a similar path in football to the England captain. Sinayoko has paid his dues in the lower leagues in France, as Kane did in loan spells with the likes of Norwich City, Leyton Orient, Millwall and Leicester City, before making his mark in Ligue Un.

Kane became a revelation in the Premier League at the age of 21, scoring 21 goals in 34 games for Spurs in 2014/15 - Mauricio Pochettino's first season in charge - on his way to becoming one of the greatest Premier League players of all time. Sinayoko started to make his mark aged 23 in 2023/24, scoring nine and assisting five in 35 appearances for Ligue 2 side Auxerre.

The same number of goal contributions followed in 24/25, albeit with five goals and nine assists this time. But that was after a step up to Ligue Un following Auxerre's promotion.

Last season Sinayoko bagged 12 goals and four assists in 32 games, but, aged 26 now, he has shown he is well and truly up to speed in one of Europe's big five leagues. He already has nine goals and five assists in 10 appearances for Paris FC, having made the move in the summer transfer window.

He is the joint third top scorer in Europe's top five leagues this season with nine in the league, the same number as Manchester City star Erling Haaland.

Sinayoko's all-round ability, to score goals and create them, makes him comparable, to a much lesser extent of course, to Kane, while his career trajectory from lower league to the very top, perhaps slightly slower than those who make the grade as teenagers, is also similar. He can also play as a support striker or off the two wings.

Sinayoko is not a name on many people's lips at the moment, but if he carries on his top form, his star will rise. He is unproven in one of the bigger domestic leagues, and that would be a risk for Spurs, but he clearly knows where the goal is and that is what Roberto De Zerbi and Spurs are primarily missing right now.
