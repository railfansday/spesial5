Bladder leakage might not come up a lot (or at all) in the group chat, but a lot of women experience it. In fact, a large scientific review found that one in three women experiences bladder leakage between six weeks and a year after giving birth — whether it’s their first child or their third. So you’re probably not the only mom at the baby music class who’s dealing with the occasional accident. It only seems like a rarity because no one talks about it, when really it’s a legitimate and very normal part of recovering from birth.
