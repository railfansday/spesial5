GQ’s 2026 Men of the Year Celebration Begins Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

GQ’s December issue has arrived, and with it comes our annual Men of the Year blowout. Except, for the 31st year, we’re going bigger than ever.

We’re honoring the brightest figures in entertainment and culture with festivities as epic as their accomplishments. And for 2026 we’ll be celebrating across three nights in Los Angeles next week—GQ’s annual Men of the Year Party, as well as two new-for-2026 events.

A lucky few will join us Tuesday, November 10th at the newly opened, architecturally stunning Lucas Museum of Narrative History to honor Denis Villeneuve, GQ’s Director of the Year. Guests will get to hear GQ’s Zach Baron, host of the new show One More Question, in conversation with Villeneuve, followed by a screening of Dune: Part Two.

The GQ Director of the Year screening is presented by OMEGA.

Wednesday night, we’ll head into the Hollywood Hills for a house party at the breathtaking Sheats-Goldstein residence with our Breakouts of 2026: 13 of the actors, directors, comedians, podcasters, and more who stole the spotlight this year. The view from the terrace will be awe-inspiring; the vibe inside will be electric.

The GQ Breakouts party is presented by Hot Wheels.

Thursday, November 12th, GQ will host a private dinner honoring our Men of the Year followed by its annual Men of the Year party, at the iconic Chateau Marmont. The event will be hosted by Global Editorial Director Adam Baidawi, alongside co-hosts Chris Rock, Andrew Garfield, and Matt Damon.

The guest list, as ever, will be star-studded, and impeccably dressed. And the red carpet will be hosted Benito Skinner—tune into GQ’s Instagram for the biggest moments from carpet (and inside the party).

GQ’s Men of the Year Dinner and Party is presented by Johnnie Walker Blue Label.

GQ’s Global Editorial Director Adam Baidawi will co-host this year’s bash alongside three leading men: Chris Rock, Andrew Garfield, and Matt Damon.

We’re honoring the Creative Director of Celine, Michael Rider, as Designer of the Year, along with Dune’s very own Denis Villeneuve, as Director of the Year.

Benito Skinner, creator and star of Overcompensating and co-host of the podcast “Ride,” will bring his high-energy charm to the red carpet of the storied Chateau Marmont.

GQ’s annual Men of the Year party is presented by Johnnie Walker Blue Label. The “Director of the Year” screening is presented by OMEGA, and the GQ Breakouts Party is presented by Hot Wheels.
