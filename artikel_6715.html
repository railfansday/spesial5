Hailey Bieber Reveals Her 2-Year-Old Son Jack Is Already Making Music in a Sweet New Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Justin and Hailey Bieber’s son Jack Blues Bieber is only 2, but he’s already getting into music. Hailey posted a rare video of Jack playing the keyboard at home. “Blues loves the keys,” she wrote.

Hailey shed more light on Jack’s interests earlier this year. During her discussion on Therapuss in February, she revealed that Jack was already reciting the lyrics to his dad’s hit song “Baby.”

“He’s been saying, ‘Baby, oh baby, oh baby oh.’ And I’m like, ‘Whoa, that is so crazy,’” she said.

Jack is also drawn to one particular sport: “So he talks a lot about basketball,” Hailey said. “He says ‘basketball’ all the time. I went to get him up from his nap today, and he was like ‘basketball.’ He’s just—it’s insane. So funny. He also just started saying ‘please.’ He knew how to sign please for a really long time. So if I would say, ‘Hey, say please,’ he would go this [the sign language for please], as please. And now he goes, ‘please,’ oh, it’s so cute. It’s so fun.”

Speaking on motherhood more broadly, the model added, “I think it’s just the coolest thing to watch a little person become their own little person. I’m watching him get his personality. I’m seeing what he’s into. He likes basketball. He likes this thing. You know? It’s so fun.”

While Hailey has been open about Jack in interviews, she only shares glimpses of his life on occasion. The model last posted a photo of Jack during Halloween weekend, revealing he dressed up as a ghost.
