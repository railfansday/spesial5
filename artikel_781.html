Liverpool hand Pochettino nightmare Manchester United start after Yoro sees red

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Green shoots of recovery have lasted longer in a blast furnace than in the Mauricio Pochettino era at Manchester United. On another abysmal Anfield day for the fallen giants of Manchester, Pochettino endured a baptism of fire from the eighth minute onwards when Leny Yoro was sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity. Liverpool would not be denied anything else while tormenting their old rivals yet again.

Andoni Iraola indulged in his first fist-pumps towards the Kop after an utterly dominant, comfortable win illuminated by Rio Ngumoha and Florian Wirtz brilliance plus goals from Dominik Szoboszlai and Víctor Muñoz. Liverpool’s two creative forces were also on the scoresheet as Ngumoha became the youngster scorer in the fixture’s Premier League history.

“Poch is at the wheel” was the mocking, merciless soundtrack that accompanied the end of the former Tottenham, Chelsea and USA coach’s first traumatic game in the job. There may well be more. On this evidence, 19th in the Premier League is not a misleading reflection of United’s position.

The latest calamity in United’s worst start to a season for 40 years cannot be pinned on a head coach who met his new charges only on Wednesday. There can be no genuine takes on Pochettino’s vision for United either, given how quickly he was forced to turn his first game into a damage-limitation exercise. But there was one firm conclusion to be drawn. His first big call in the job proved a serious mistake with destructive repercussions for United’s entire evening.

• Accounting for 3pts/win all-time, Manchester United’s eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since 1972-73 (7). That is also the previous season in which the Red Devils managed only one win through their first 11 league matches.

• Mauricio Pochettino (pictured) suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a Manchester United manager in his first game (0-4), along with a defeat by the same scoreline for Lal Hilditch in October 1926 (0-4 v Bolton Wanderers in the top flight).

• Manchester United end the day in 19th position in the Premier League, with this the first time since November 1986 that they have been within the bottom two places of the league table having played at least 10 matches (21st in that case).

• Mauricio Pochettino was the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club. Opta

That ill-fated decision was to hand Matthijs de Ligt a first Premier League start in 12 months at the expense of Harry Maguire. Maguire may be a symbol of United’s inertia, but his replacement at centre-back was heavily responsible for the chaos that quickly unfolded. The Netherlands international was at fault for three goals. Liverpool, under instruction from Iraola to start strong and “make them feel pressure from the beginning”, could hardly believe their luck at the first mishap in the visitors’ central defence.

De Ligt lost the flight of the ball and his man, Isak, when Ronald Araújo pinged a routine pass down Liverpool’s right-hand channel. Isak was suddenly away, De Ligt all at sea, and to compound matters Yoro was slow to react to the danger. By the time United’s covering defender was across to challenge Isak it was too late. Yoro clattered into the back of the Sweden international just outside the area and was shown a straight red card by the referee, Sam Barrott. Isak’s heavy first touch could not save Yoro. The free-kick could not save United from double jeopardy. Szoboszlai swept a powerful set piece into Senne Lammens’ bottom right-hand corner from 19 yards.

Pochettino claimed the dismissal was wrong and United should have had a penalty moments earlier for a Jérémy Jacquet challenge on Bryan Mbeumo. It was a desperate take. A simple detail showed the extent of the job United’s latest head coach has on his hands: the wall that lined up to face Szoboszlai’s free-kick. Just getting that right was beyond this group of players. White shirts lined up to protect the left side of Lammens’ goal but, with the isolated exception of Bruno Fernandes, no one covered the spot where the ball was ultimately placed.

Liverpool devoured the gifts. When the hosts doubled their lead three minutes later thoughts of another 7-0 humiliation for United did not seem unreasonable. Ngumoha tormented Diogo Dalot from the first whistle, as everyone inside the ground expected he would. Having been found by Isak’s cross-field pass the teenager breezed past Dalot to the byline and lifted a cross slightly behind Wirtz. The German playmaker, left in space by De Ligt, improvised brilliantly to hook a volley into the roof of the net while floating in mid-air. United were sinking into the abyss.

The first chants of “sacked in the morning” flowed from the Kop towards a disbelieving United head coach. There were 14 minutes on the clock. It was, of course, a ludicrous chant. It might be months before United’s hapless owners are seeking another scapegoat.

Pochettino responded in the only way he could to the onslaught and introduced Maguire into a rejigged five-man defence. Whether in the knowledge that United possessed neither the ability nor the inclination to hurt them, Liverpool eased off for the remainder of the first half although Isak and Alexis Mac Allister could have extended the lead.

• Since the start of the 2021-22 season, Liverpool's four wins by 4+ goals against Manchester United are the most any Premier League team has achieved against another across all competitions.

• Andoni Iraola is unbeaten in his seven Premier League matches against Manchester United (W3 D4). The only manager to record a longer unbeaten run against the Red Devils in the competition is Jürgen Klopp (8 between 2018 and 2022).

• This was the quickest Liverpool had gone 2-0 up in a Premier League match against Manchester United, going two ahead after just 12 minutes and 40 seconds.

• Rio Ngumoha (18y 85d) became the youngest player to score and assist in a Premier League game for Liverpool since Michael Owen on Boxing Day 1997 (18y 12d v Leeds United).

• Dominik Szoboszlai (pictured) scored his fifth direct free kick for Liverpool in the Premier League; only Jamie Redknapp (8), Steven Gerrard (7) and Trent Alexander-Arnold (6) have netted more for the club in the competition.

• Florian Wirtz created 10 chances in open play for Liverpool, the most by a player in a Premier League match since Manchester City’s David Silva against West Brom in March 2015 (11). Opta

Liverpool did not let their rivals off so lightly after the restart. Bradley Barcola could and should have provided Wirtz with a hat-trick of assists but squandered three beautiful touches from his fellow £100m+ recruit. Virgil van Dijk put a free header wide from another fine delivery by Wirtz. Barcola turned provider in devastating style, however, to create the goal Ngumoha’s display richly deserved. The France winger burst away from Lisandro Martínez and Fernandes with one flick of the ball before delivering an assist on a plate for the teenager to convert.

Iraola’s thoughts started to drift towards the midweek Champions League trip to Club Brugge and he started resting players from the 72nd minute onwards. But Liverpool were not done. Another mistake by De Ligt, this time a sloppy pass infield, was pounced on by Wirtz who released Muñoz inside the box. The Liverpool substitute stroked an emphatic finish past Lammens. All Sir Alex Ferguson could do up in the directors’ box in the closing stages was laugh with his friend the actor James Nesbit. The alternative is too bleak for a proud United man to contemplate.
