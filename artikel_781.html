Gwen Stefani & Gavin Rossdale Reunite to Support Son Kingston at His L.A. Concert Alongside Blake Shelton

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The exes split in 2015, and the No Doubt frontwoman married her Voice co-star six years later.

After all that they’ve been through, Gwen Stefani and Gavin Rossdale are cool — at least, cool enough to reunite to support their son Kingston at his recent concert, with the No Doubt frontwoman attending with husband Blake Shelton at her side.

The three musicians were all in the audience Tuesday night at The Troubadour in West Hollywood, California, as Stefani and Rossdale’s eldest son rocked out onstage, according to photos circulating online. TMZ also shared video of Shelton and his fellow The Voice coaching alum holding hands as they left the venue, with Stefani at one point saying goodbye to her parents before getting into her car.

Rossdale was also captured interacting with fans and signing autographs outside of the venue, at one point telling paparazzi that while he might love to perform with Kingston someday, his son “doesn’t want anything to do with me musically.”

“He wants to do his own [thing],” the Bush rocker said proudly. “He’s really big on that. I was lucky I was even allowed to come.”

Stefani and Rossdale also share younger sons Zuma and Apollo. They got married after six years of dating in 2002 before filing for divorce in 2015.

“I never thought I’d ever get divorced,” Rossdale said on the Amy and T.J. podcast in 2024. “Sometimes I wish that, when you see the kids, there’s a loss. It would be nice if there was more of a connection with the person that made them with me.”

The “Just a Girl” singer would go on to marry Shelton in 2021 after they met on The Voice. The pair recently returned to the singing competition series for its 30th-anniversary special, which aired in September.

Kingston is clearly paving his own way in music, taking after his famous parents. The 20-year-old’s show at The Troubadour came a few days after the release of his EP Louise Street.

“Last night was amazing!!” he wrote on his Instagram Story the day after his concert. “Huge thank you to everyone that showed up love you guys can’t wait for the next one.”
