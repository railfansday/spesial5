Kim Kardashian Reveals She Eats "Prop Food" on All's Fair Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kim Kardashian admitted she often eats the prop food on the set of Hulu's All's Fair, costarring Sarah Paulson, Glenn Close, Niecy Nash-Betts, Teyana Taylor and Naomi Watts.

For Kim Kardashian, all's fair on the set of All's Fair.

That's why she has no problem eating prop food while filming scenes with costars Sarah Paulson, Niecy Nash-Betts, Naomi Watts, Glenn Close and Teyana Taylor for their Hulu legal drama.

"It's real food and I eat it the whole time," Kardashian said during the cast's Nov. 4 appearance on Good Morning America. "I'll be walking by after they cut and I'm like, 'Can I take a plate of that pie with me?'"

And her castmates have objections to her unusual snacking habit. As Nash-Betts quipped, "Do not let that tiny body fool you: She eats everything."

"I said, 'This is prop food! Why are you eating this?'" she continued with a laugh. "This is the greediest woman I've ever met in my life."

But there's a reason why Kardashian's costars have dismissed prop food.

"We don't want to eat it all day," Paulson explained, with Watts adding, "It's a thing called continuity. If you eat it in the scene, you have to keep doing it."

However, their testimonies haven't swayed Kardashian one bit.

"Because I'm new to this, they tried to tell me, 'Hey, continuity. Pace yourself. Eat a grape or half a grape because you're gonna have to do that the same time 50 times,'" the 46-year-old shared. "By the end, I am like, 'What did I get myself into?' But I love it."

In fact, the Keeping Up With the Kardashians alum has even recreated some of her own snacks with Rice Krispies off set. As she put it, "Last night in my room, I made Rice Krispie treats because I wanted all the girls to try them."

And while the jury's still out on Kardashian as a cook, her costars have been thoroughly impressed by her acting skills.

"Her ability to remember her lines, she's so good with that," Taylor raved to E! News last year. "She'll keep reading it over and over and over again. It's dope to see."

Added Close, "She's a very sweet woman, she does her homework, she has great people around her, which says a lot."
