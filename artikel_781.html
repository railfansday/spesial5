Blake Shelton Shares Best Part of Being Stepdad to Gwen Stefani’s Kids

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Blake Shelton revealed what he loves most about being a stepdad to Gwen Stefani's kids Kingston, 20, Zuma, 18, and Apollo, 12, sharing how they spend most afternoons.

Blake Shelton has taken to fatherhood like a honey bee to a honeysuckle.

The “God’s Country” singer shared what one of his favorite parts of parenthood is after becoming stepdad to wife Gwen Stefani’s kids with ex-husband Gavin Rossdale: Kingston, 20, Zuma, 18, and Apollo, 12.

“The most cliche dad thing I do every single day when the kids get home from school, Apollo—our youngest—we throw the football for about 30 minutes,” Shelton told Taste of Country in a clip posted to Instagram Nov. 15. “He’s got this thing, he loves to jump on the trampoline while I throw the football to him, so he can do the crazy Odell Beckham catches, DeAndre Hopkins stuff.”

And while the 50-year-old is happy Apollo is able to get his energy out, Shelton is keeping his feet firmly planted on the ground, quipping, “Just throwing the ball wears me out.”

Thankfully, he and Zuma enjoy a less strenuous activity, as Shelton shared that he and the teen spend time playing the guitar together.

“It’s not me showing him stuff,” the Grammy nominee added. “It's him showing me stuff. ‘Have you heard this song?’ ‘You heard of this artist?’ ‘Listen to this song.’”

The duo share musical moments “almost every single day,” and that along with Apollo’s football antics are just a few of the memories Shelton values as their stepdad.

“I love it,” he said. “I can't wait ’til they get home.”

The “Sangria” singer has also been there to support Stefani’s eldest son Kingston with his musical career, as the couple attended his concert at the Troubadour in Los Angeles Nov. 10, alongside Stefani’s ex Rossdale.

Since tying the knot with Stefani in 2021, Shelton has been candid about how his life has found new fulfillment as a stepdad.

"I didn't know what I was signing up for, but I was all about signing up for it," Shelton said during the 2022 Country Radio Seminar. "And every day I've fallen in love with the boys as much as I do with Gwen."
