Priscilla Presley Still Faces Fraud Claims in Legal War With Former Business Advisers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Elvis Presley's ex-wife is accusing two former business partners of elder abuse, while they say she's actually in the wrong.

A judge has rejected Priscilla Presley’s bid to dismiss fraud claims brought against her as part of a messy legal dispute with two former business partners.

Elvis Presley’s ex-wife has been locked in a back-and-forth legal battle with her former advisers Brigitte Kruse and Kevin Fialko since 2023. Presley claims the duo committed elder abuse by stealing her money, while Kruse and Fialko allege they’re actually the ones who were wronged.

Kruse and Fialko’s fraud claims are premised on the notion that while they were working tirelessly to monetize Presley’s name, image and likeness rights — including for a skincare line and in connection with the 2023 Sofia Coppola biopic Priscilla — she concealed that those rights had actually been sold years earlier to Authentic Brands Group. Presley’s lawyers sought to throw out those claims as legally deficient, but Judge Mark H. Epstein ruled on Monday (Nov. 16) that they pass muster, for now.

“The court believes the causes of action are sufficiently pled,” wrote the judge in his decision, obtained by Billboard.

Judge Epstein similarly rejected Presley’s bid to dismiss Kruse and Fialko’s wrongful firing claims, saying that whether they were employees in the first place is a “fact-heavy question” that must go through evidence discovery. He also declined to trim some of the more sensational parts of the advisers’ countersuit, including paragraphs that alleged Presley “pulled the plug” on her daughter Lisa Marie Presley and “exerted undue pressure on Elvis, pushing him to his death.”

“The court sympathizes with Presley that much of it seems gratuitous and mean-spirited,” wrote the judge. “But it is out there and has been for a while. The court sees little purpose to be served by striking allegations, so the motion to strike is denied, largely as moot given that the cross-complaint was filed long ago.”

The judge did, however, trim a few of Kruse and Fialko’s counterclaims, including one that accused Presley of contract interference and misappropriating her own name, image and likeness. Kruse and Fialko were given permission to amend these allegations.

Kruse and Fialko’s attorney, Jordan Matthews, told Billboard on Monday that his team is “pleased” with the ruling. “We intended to vigorously enforce our clients’ rights,” added Matthews.

A lawyer for Presley, Wayne Harman, said in a Monday statement that his client “continues to seek justice for the manipulation, elder abuse and financial exploitation that she suffered at the hands of Brigitte Kruse and her co-conspirators.”

“In an effort to deflect from their significant liability to Ms. Presley, Kruse and her co-conspirators filed a set of salacious-sounding counterclaims,” said Harman. “In today’s ruling, Judge Epstein appropriately found that certain of the Kruse parties’ counterclaims, including that Ms. Presley somehow misappropriated her own name, image and likeness, and that Ms. Presley is obligated to indemnify the Kruse parties for their own wrongful conduct, should be dismissed. While the Kruse parties have another opportunity to amend their claims, Ms. Presley is thankful that her case is moving closer to trial, and she looks forward to obtaining justice (for her and her family).”

This story was updated on Nov. 16 at 6:53 p.m. ET to add a statement from Presley’s attorney.
