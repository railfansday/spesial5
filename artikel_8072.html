If your adventures take you to anywhere with truly atrocious water, the Grayl GeoPress is a reliable companion. Unlike most filters, the GeoPress can reliably remove viruses like norovirus from water sources in addition to bacteria, protozoa, and microplastics, while activated carbon removes heavy metals and chemicals like benzene. Using the GeoPress is simple: Fill up the 24-ounce outer cup, place the filter module inside, and press down. An integrated soft pad helps prevent hikers’ hands from getting sore, a common complaint with testers who tried earlier versions. Unlike hollow-fiber filters like the popular Sawyer Squeeze, the Grayl is safe to use after freezing up to two times.
