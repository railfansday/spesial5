No major injury issues for Giants as they open practice for game vs. Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The only New York Giants player who did not practice Wednesday as the team began preparing for Sunday's game against the Jacksonville Jaguars was reserve cornerback Nic Jones. He has a knee injury.

Special teamer Malik Harrison, who missed Thursday's game against the Washington Commanders with a groin injury, was a limited participant. So, too, was safety Jason Pinnock.

Cornerback Deonte Banks (shoulder), running back Cam Skattebo, and long-snapper Ben Mann (illness) were full participants.

Head coach John Harbaugh revealed on Wednesday that in the game against Washington the Giants chose to receive the opening kickoff because Banks had injured his shoulder during warmups and was still being checked out. Banks was in the game when the Giants went on defense.
