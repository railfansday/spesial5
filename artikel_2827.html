Run Through the Jungle: Is the Bengals’ defense good? Plus, “Buy or Sell” and Commanders Preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

When Demetrius Knight Jr. ran back a 27-yard fumble recovery for a touchdown in Week 1, it was more than just the Bengals' first score of the season. It set the tone that this Bengals' defense was going to change games this season and be a lot better than the last two seasons.

On this week's episode of "Run Through the Jungle," Alex Frank and Jason Garrison discuss how the Bengals' defense may actually be good. It's Knight's scoop-and-score and other plays so far this season that have set the tone for that side of the ball, and it's not far-fetched to say that Dexter Lawrence's sack late in the fourth quarter against Tennessee saved the season.

Jason wrote a column this week on whether he would buy or sell 15 hot Bengals takes that have been scattered throughout the Internet this week. Alex reveals his answers to those takes, including whether he's buying this being the best Bengals team in franchise history.

The Bengals are in Washington on Monday Night Football this week to face the Commanders. Alex and Jason preview the game and give their biggest keys to the game.
