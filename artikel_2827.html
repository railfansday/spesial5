Knicks' Mikal Bridges drills buzzer-beating 3-pointer to defeat Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The New York Knicks are rounding into form, and maybe Saturday night is what Mikal Bridges needed to get back on track, too.

The 30-year-old wing, who came into a matchup with the Portland Trail Blazers shooting just north of 40% from the field and a smidge above 30% from deep, drilled a buzzer-beating 3-pointer to deliver New York a 97-96 victory in Madison Square Garden.

Bridges finished with 12 points on 5-of-8 shooting. He connected on two of his three attempts from long range. The last one extended the Knicks' win streak to four games.

Following a pair of Deni Avdija free throws that put the Trail Blazers (10-6) up 96-94, New York (10-6) took a timeout. With 3.2 seconds remaining, Josh Hart inbounded the ball to Bridges.

He caught the pass to the right of the top of the arc, used a quick fake to get an overzealous Toumani Camara to bite, took one more dribble and launched.

The ball rattled in, the buzzer sounded and Mike Breen exclaimed, "Bang! Bang!"

Bridges was swarmed by his Knicks teammates, eventually wearing a smile as MSG reveled in utter pandemonium.

He came to the rescue on a night when New York squandered a 26-point lead. The Knicks shot 13 of 27 from 3-point land in the first half but scored only 31 points over the final two quarters.

Bridges admitted afterward that Portland played "harder" than New York in the second half.

But he also noted in that on-court postgame interview: "It's 48 minutes. We just kept persevering through the time and ended up winning the game."

After trailing 94-84 with less than five minutes to go, the Trail Blazers went on a 12-0 run, which culminated in the aforementioned Avdija free throws. Additionally, that stretch featured a game-tying 3-pointer from Damian Lillard.

Avdija was sent to the line because of a head-scratching foul by Knicks center Andre Drummond, who switched and committed the personal on the floor well beyond the arc.

Avdija had 20 points. Center Donovan Clingan spearheaded Portland's effort with 22 points and 13 rebounds. Lillard put up just 15 points and shot a meager 1 of 9 from outside.

New York was paced by Jalen Brunson's 30 points. Fellow guard Landry Shamet poured in 20 points.

Bridges, though, was the hero, sinking a redemptive shot 16 games into what's been a trying season for the ninth-year pro.
