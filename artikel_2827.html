NFL Power Rankings for Week 12: Epic Thanksgiving showdowns are coming

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Who says you don't get presents on Thanksgiving?

Football fans will be gifted two of the most anticipated games of the season Thursday, with apologies to Eagles-Cowboys in the second leg of the tripleheader.

The Bears-Lions Round 1 winner will be the slight favorite in an NFC North where all four teams could go to the playoffs. Round 2 is Week 17.

The latest installment of the great Bills-Chiefs rivalry will crown one team or the other as the favorites to come out of the AFC (at least until the Broncos can say their piece against the Steelers on Friday).

The games also should provide some clarity at the tightly bunched top of The Post's NFL power rankings for Week 12:

Buffalo Bills running back James Cook III (4) scores a touchdown against the Miami Dolphins on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in Orchard Park, N.Y. AP Photo/Jeffrey T. Barnes

Greg Rousseau added two sacks to push his season total to 10 and Damar Hamlin notched an interception as the defense feasted on a fourth straight backup quarterback in a 26-9 win against the Dolphins. James Cook became the second player in NFL history to rush for at least 75 yards and score a touchdown in nine consecutive games.

Brock Purdy cracked the code of the NFL's stingiest defense by throwing for three first-half touchdown passes, including the first two to George Kittle, to demolish the Vikings, 35-10, in Mexico City. If that wasn't enough, Purdy engineered a 98-yard touchdown drive in the second half as traveling 49ers fans chanted "M-V-P!" Mike Evans added 109 receiving yards and a score.

Rookie Jonah Coleman rushed for 111 yards and three touchdowns and the defense honored the late franchise great Tom Jackson by collecting five sacks, 10 other quarterback hits and seven passes defended in a 38-24 win against the Raiders. The Broncos blew a 17-0 lead but got it together and sealed the win on Ja'quan McMillian's 35-yard pick-six.

In-season addition Jeffery Simmons looked the part of a game-wrecker, tallying three sacks in a 21-13 win against the Saints. The rest of the defensive scorecard included Montez Sweat with a safety, Dillon Thieneman with an interception and Austin Booker with a fumble recovery. Caleb Williams threw two touchdown passes to extend the Bears' winning streak to five games.

It looked like the good old days as Patrick Mahomes threw two of his three touchdown passes to Travis Kelce in a 34-7 rout of the Cardinals. Emmett Johnson, filling in for Kenneth Walker III, carried 23 times for 105 yards and scored a touchdown for the fourth straight game. Embattled kicker Harrison Butker converted all six kicks, including two field goals.

In his second start with the Vikings and second start against the 49ers in three weeks, Kirk Cousins threw a pick-six to Ji'Ayir Brown in the ugly loss. He became the first player ever to throw a pick-six for two different teams against the same opponent in one season. Will Reichard's would-have-been record-setting 71-yard field-goal attempt fell just short.

Detroit Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs (0) runs for a touchdown past Tampa Bay Buccaneers linebacker David Walker (51) in Detroit, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Paul Sancya

Jared Goff threw for two touchdowns and Jahmyr Gibbs ran for two more to fuel a 31-21 win against the Buccaneers. The first game without Aidan Hutchinson (in addition to Jack Campbell) ended up being a gem by the defense, which has held four straight opponents to 275 or fewer total yards for the first time since 1976.

Drew Lock has led the Seahawks to four wins (3-0 as a starter) but the plan calls for Sam Darnold to resume the reins after the bye as long as his health complies. Zach Charbonnet's workload and production increased in each of his first three games of the season. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (1,207 receiving yards) has been great no matter who is throwing.

It's been anything but an easy road for the preseason "SuperTeam," but the Rams return from their bye on a four-game winning streak. But they return without Puka Nacua, who underwent season-ending knee surgery. The Rams are a combined 23-7 after their bye over the last three seasons combined, including 7-1 in 2023 after a 3-6 start.

The Bengals allowed a decisive 64-yard drive over the final 46 seconds and lost, 22-20, when the Commanders kicked their fourth field goal of the second half. Joe Burrow threw for 300 yards and two touchdowns but took a costly sack on his final snap. Ja'Marr Chase (31 yards) was unusually quiet in the end of a five-game winning streak.

Abdul Carter's 12 quarterback pressures and two sacks terrorized Trevor Lawrence and made things easier for a secondary that created two takeaways and allowed just six points on three other second-half red-zone trips in a 23-18 win against the Jaguars. Malik Nabers caught a touchdown pass and Cam Skattebo did a back flip after a game-sealing first-down run.

Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow (9) throws a pass against the Washington Commanders on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, in Landover, Md. Getty Images

Elevated from the practice squad, 11-year veteran Sterling Shepard caught two touchdown passes for the first time in his career to spark a 27-24 win against the Chargers. Geno Smith completed 17-of-21 passes for three touchdowns in all. It was the first game in franchise history with zero punts, zero turnovers, zero turnover on downs, and zero missed field goals.
