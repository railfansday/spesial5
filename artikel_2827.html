Hawks reveal Kingston Flemings' status for Wednesday vs. Thunder

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Atlanta Hawks rookie Kingston Flemings will miss Wednesday's road game against the Oklahoma City Thunder, according to the team's official injury report.

Flemings didn't play Sunday in a 113-106 win over the Milwaukee Bucks because of a lumbar strain. He began experiencing discomfort last week and was forced to leave early Friday in a 125-117 overtime loss to the Chicago Bulls.

The eighth overall pick appears to be trending toward a return after practicing with the team Tuesday at the Paycom Center. He was seen participating in on-court activity with coach Quin Snyder, according to Lauren L. Williams of the Atlanta Journal-Constitution.

Flemings is averaging 7.8 points, 4.1 assists and 2.5 rebounds while shooting 42.9% from 3-point range in 12 appearances. He is third in the rookie class in assists and one of 12 first-year players to score 20 or more points in a game this season.

The 19-year-old Flemings recently moved into the starting lineup at the urging of veteran CJ McCollum, who has since led their second unit. Snyder has been pleased with Flemings' play in that role, citing his poise and ability to play within himself.

Flemings' impending return will be much needed, with McCollum expected to miss at least four weeks with a calf strain. He has been a key player early and will be counted on for the short-handed Hawks.
