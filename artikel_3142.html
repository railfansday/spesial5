Even without its current discounted price, Surfshark One is the best plan in Surfshark's lineup – and one I happily recommend to almost everyone. It gives you pretty much everything Surfshark has to offer, with only Incogni and identity theft coverage from the top-tier Surfshark One+ plan left out. But honestly, most people can do just fine without those extras.

For starters, you get a pretty capable antivirus solution backed by Avira. It offers real-time protection that checks apps and files as they're downloaded, installed, and used, along with scheduled scans to check your device for threats. There's also webcam protection, which blocks apps and websites from accessing your camera without permission.

Surfshark Alert keeps an eye out for your personal information online and notifies you if details linked to your email, credit card, or ID show up in a data breach. You also get regular personal data security reports, so you can keep tabs on potential leaks.

Then there's Surfshark's Scam Protection, which checks SMS messages for signs of fraud, uses AI to spot suspicious emails and phishing attempts, and blocks access to malicious or unwanted websites.

All that comes on top of Surfshark's already near-flawless VPN, which is both fast and incredibly private. Along with a strict no-logs policy and a unique twist on typical kill switch functionality through Everlink, it offers post-quantum encryption, RAM-only servers, MultiHop (its Double VPN feature), and VPN obfuscation through its Camouflage Mode.

Alternative ID, meanwhile, lets you create an entirely random identity, complete with a fake but realistic-looking name, email address, birthday, and physical address. I've used it countless times for those one-off sign-ups where I'd rather not hand over my real details.

But Surfshark's real party trick? Unlimited simultaneous connections. This means you can run virtually all your household devices through the VPN without worrying about hitting a device limit.
