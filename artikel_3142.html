Why SpaceX Stock Is Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SpaceX's debt is getting expensive to insure.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:45PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Space Exploration Technologies ›

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX +1.30%) stock is down just 1.4% as of 1:42 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 25, though it dipped as low as 9.4% earlier in the day.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite are down 2.7% and 4.1%, respectively.

Over the past two trading sessions, five-year credit default swap (CDS) spreads -- what it costs to insure against the company defaulting on its debt -- hit all-time highs for SpaceX. So too for Alphabet (GOOG -2.02%) (GOOGL -2.13%), Oracle (ORCL -5.13%), and CoreWeave (CRWV -0.08%). With inflation still running hot and a fresh round of tariffs that will add more upward pressure on prices, borrowing costs are likely to rise. That's not great news for a company that is likely to need to tap the debt markets to a substantial degree in the coming years.

Free cash flow (FCF) -- the money left over after accounting for core operations and investments in things like AI data centers -- is running at a $35 billion shortfall through the first nine months of 2026.

There are also reports that the company has lowered its prices for the compute it offers. That doesn't exactly bode well for its AI division long term.

But the stock gained much of it back through the rest of the trading, potentially from excitement around the potential merger of SpaceX and Tesla.
