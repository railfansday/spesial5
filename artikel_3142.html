Hulu, Disney+, Apple TV, HBO Max and More Streamers Up to 80% Off For Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Black Friday is one of the best times of year to reassess your streaming subscriptions. Following another year of price hikes across Hollywood’s biggest streaming services, nearly every major platform is offering steep discounts on monthly and annual subscriptions ahead of the holiday weekend.

Among this year’s standout deals are HBO Max‘s yearlong subscription for just $5.49 a month, Apple TV’s six-month subscription for $6.99 a month and two-months of Paramount+ for $2.99 a month — including its normally pricier, ad-free Premium plan. Meanwhile, Peacock is back with a Black Friday promo after sitting out last year’s sales event, and Disney+ and Hulu have once again slashed the price of their streaming bundle. Below, check out the best Black Friday streaming deals to take advantage of this year.

Original programming includes Emmy-winning series such as “Severance,” “The Studio” and “Ted Lasso,” in addition to acclaimed dramas such as “Slow Horses,” “Pluribus” and “Shrinking.” Among its upcoming releases is “Neuromancer,” Apple’s highly anticipated adaptation of William Gibson’s seminal science-fiction novel.

$2.99/Month for Two Months (Up to 79% Off)

Paramount+ has brought back one of its steepest subscription discounts of the year, offering both its ad-supported Essential plan and its largely ad-free Premium plan for $2.99 a month for the first two months.

In addition to Showtime programming such as “Yellowjackets,” the streamer is home to Taylor Sheridan’s sprawling television empire, including “Landman,” “Tulsa King” and “Dutton Ranch,” alongside franchises such as “South Park,” “Star Trek” and “Mission: Impossible.” It’s also one of the better streaming options for sports fans, with coverage of the NFL on CBS, UEFA Champions League and UFC events.

After forgoing a Black Friday deal last year, Peacock is offering discounts across two of its subscription tiers this year. Its Select plan, which includes programming from NBC, Bravo and Telemundo, is available for $2.99 a month for three months or $29.99 for an entire year.

Those looking for access to Peacock’s full programming library can sign up for its Premium plan for $8.99 a month for three months or $89.99 for a year, down from its usual annual price of $129.99.

Disney+ and Hulu are once again teaming up for one of the best yearlong streaming deals of Black Friday. Through Nov. 30, the ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu bundle is down to $4.99 a month for 12 months. This year, the companies are also offering a discount on their Premium bundle, which brings the price of ad-free streaming across both platforms down to $14.99 a month for a year.

The bundle encompasses Disney’s extensive collection of Marvel, Pixar and “Star Wars” programming, alongside Hulu and FX hits such as “The Bear,” “Only Murders in the Building,” “Shōgun” and “The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives.”

Amazon is also discounting digital purchases of Christmas classics, including “The Polar Express,” “A Christmas Story” and “The Grinch,” starting at $7.99 apiece through Nov. 26.

HBO Max is slashing the price of its Basic With Ads plan by 50% for an entire year, bringing its monthly subscription down from $10.99 to $5.49. The deal, which runs through Nov. 30, amounts to $66 in annual savings.

The streamer’s highly anticipated “Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone” series premieres on Christmas Day, followed by the third season of “The Pitt” in January. New seasons of “Heated Rivalry,” “The White Lotus,” “The Last of Us” and “A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms” are also slated for 2027.
