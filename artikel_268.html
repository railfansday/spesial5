British hackers face prison for GTA 6 data breach and crypto thefts

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Two British hackers are facing lengthy prison sentences for separate hacks as part of their roles in long-running, prolific cybercriminal hacking groups. The two are accused of hacks that stole millions of dollars in cryptocurrency and leaked pre-release footage from Grand Theft Auto VI.

Tyler Buchanan, 24, from Dundee, Scotland, has been sentenced to six years by a U.S. court after he was extradited to the United States in 2025. Buchanan was accused of stealing at least $11 million in cryptocurrency from dozens of companies as part of a phishing scheme. The attacks would impersonate IT helpdesks and call up unsuspecting employees and trick them into giving over their credentials, which the hackers would use to break in and steal cryptocurrency.

Meanwhile, Arion Kurtaj, 21, from Oxford, England, has pleaded guilty to several cyberattacks that he carried out while a teenager. One hack included the theft of data and extortion of Rockstar Games, the maker of Grand Theft Auto VI, which was released Wednesday after several delays.

Kurtaj was previously ordered by a U.K. court to be detained indefinitely after deeming him unable to stand trial due to his autism diagnosis and his perceived high hacking risk to the public. But U.K. authorities, tasked with charging Kurtaj with cybercrime offenses, confirmed Monday that he had admitted to several hacks, including leaking footage of the long-awaited video game, and would subsequently face a public trial.

U.K. authorities confirmed earlier reporting that Kurtaj hacked several companies, including while he was confined to a hotel amid police protection from rival criminal gangs. The authorities said Kurtaj used an Amazon Fire TV stick to hack the companies, which breached his bail conditions.

Both Buchanan and Kurtaj were members of overlapping cybercriminal groups, dubbed 0ktapus, Lapsus$, and later Scattered Spider. These prolific hacking gangs mostly consist of teenagers and young male adults, lending to the term “advanced persistent teenagers,” whose focus and motivations were to steal as much money as possible through various cyberattacks.

Over the years, the hackers were collectively responsible for hundreds of cyberattacks targeting big companies, including Coinbase, DoorDash, Mailchimp, and Nvidia.

By late 2024, both U.K. and U.S. authorities say they had indicted five of the hackers whom they believed were key members of the hacking groups, including Buchanan. The U.S. Department of Justice said this week that Buchanan is the fifth and final defendant to be sentenced in this case.
