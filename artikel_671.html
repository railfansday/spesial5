Rauw Alejandro Is Fulfilling a Childhood Dream With Rockstar Games: ”GTA’ Has Been Part of My Life’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Alejandro chats with Billboard about joining the GTA VI soundtrack and bringing throwback reggaetón energy to the game with "Macacoa 2000."

Like many adults right now, Rauw Alejandro is probably glued to the couch playing Grand Theft Auto VI. Age is nothing but a number for millennials who grew up reveling in the criminal escapades and explosive gameplay Rockstar Games has offered gamers over the last two decades. Now, Rauw isn’t just reliving his childhood; he’s part of one of the biggest video games in history, thanks to his inclusion on the blockbuster soundtrack, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. “When I heard about the opportunity, I was like, ‘Let’s gooo! We gotta bring that Puerto Rican underground sound to GTA y más que nuevamente regresa a Miami, home of many Latinos,’” he tells Billboard. “Growing up playing GTA, I never imagined I’d get to be part of its soundtrack. As a fan, it’s crazy to experience something like this. GTA has always been about the culture, and now getting to bring my own sound into that world is just insane. It’s a full-circle moment for me, de verdad. Something really special.”

Alejandro’s electric track, “Macacoa 2000,” is a love letter to throwback reggaeton that complements the game’s Miami-inspired setting. With music juggernauts including Travis Scott, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen, Dua Lipa and JENNIE also featured on the soundtrack, Alejandro is in great company. He also joined the all-star cast of performers in Miami this past weekend at Grand Theft Auto: The Festival, capping off Rockstar’s massive opening week.

Billboard spoke with Alejandro about joining Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the making of “Macacoa 2000” and Rockstar’s decision to go global with its music.

What’s your first memory of playing Grand Theft Auto as a fan?Wooo Wee! Mano, GTA has been part of my life desde chamaquito, for real. I remember spending hours just driving around, exploring the city, huyéndole a la policía [Laughs]. Sometimes I wasn’t even doing the missions, I was just vibing, discovering new things, listening to the music on the radio. GTA is one of those games que tú empiezas a jugar y cuando vienes a ver, se te fue el día entero [Laughs.] I have so many memories growing up with it. It’s such a crazy experience, y hasta el día de hoy lo sigo disfrutando igual. ¡Eso nunca pasa de moda!

When you recorded “Macacoa 2000,” what kind of energy were you trying to capture, especially knowing the game would return to the Miami-inspired Vice City?With “Macacoa 2000,” I wanted to bring back the roots of reggaeton, ese perreo de antes, but with a fresh twist. Vice City has that whole nightlife energy, the cars, the Miami vibe, and that connects so much with Puerto Rico. I wanted the track to capture that feeling of driving around late at night, windows down, music blasting, running from the cops on our way to the club — just vibing. Con ese sazón boricua, el joint en la mano, and just enjoying the moment. That’s the energy I wanted people to feel when they hear it.Music has always been a driving force behind the GTA experience. As an artist and a fan, what do you think makes the relationship between music and video games so special?Music has that power to make moments unforgettable, you know? Especially in GTA,  the music makes that whole experience even better. For me, that’s what it’s all about. As an artist, I want my music to make people feel something, whether they’re at one of my shows, driving around, or just playing GTA. Creating memories through music, eso es lo más brutal de todo esto. That’s what I love.With GTA becoming a global phenomenon, how important is the opportunity to introduce your music to new audiences, especially listeners who might discover you for the first time through the game?My music reaches people in different parts of the world, but I think what’s really cool about this is connecting with people who are experiencing GTA for the first time, or maybe hearing my music for the first time. Imagine someone just cruising around Vice City with a new custom car, “Macacoa 2000” comes on the radio, and they’re like, “Yo, what song is this?!” [Laughs.] That’s the beauty of it, you never know who’s gonna discover your music or where. Ya tú sabes! ¡Nos vemos en Vice City!
