Mel Gibson’s ‘The Resurrection of the Christ’ Bought For Italy by RAI Cinema and Leone Film Group

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Italy’s RAI Cinema and Leone Film Group have acquired exclusive Italian distribution rights for Mel Gibson’s two-part biblical drama sequel, “The Resurrection of the Christ,” from Lionsgate.

More than 20 years ago, Gibson shot the original “The Passion of the Christ” at Italy’s Cinecittà studios in Rome. It became one of the largest-grossing independent films of all time with a worldwide box office of $610 million. Shooting on Gibson’s long-delayed follow up also took place at Cinecittà and in other Italian locations including Matera, Bari, Ginosa, Craco and Brindisi.

The follow-up has been split into two movies, with “Part One” set to debut in U.S. theatres on May 6, 2027 and “Part Two” scheduled for May 25, 2028. Each installment will open in theaters on Ascension Day, a Christian holiday that celebrates the ascension of Jesus into heaven.

The project “explores the period between the crucifixion and the resurrection, employing a narrative that encompasses both symbolic and spiritual dimensions, underscored by a powerful and evocative visual style,” according to a statement from RAI Cinema. The broadcaster’s banner 01 Distribution will replicate Lionsgate’s release pattern day-and-date in Italy.

“The Passion of the Christ” followed Jim Caviezel as Jesus of Nazareth and Monica Bellucci as Mary Magdalene in the final 12 hours before the crucifixion of Jesus Christ.

Gibson has described the sequels as an “acid trip,” adding that he “never read anything like” the scripts, which the filmmaker has been writing alongside Randall Wallace. Finnish actor Jaakko Ohtonen (“The Last Kingdom”) has replaced Caviezel in the role of Jesus with Mariela Garriga taking over from Monica Bellucci as Magdalene. They are starring alongside Pier Luigi Pasino, Kasia Smutniak, Riccardo Scamarcio and Rupert Everett.

The screenplay is written by Gibson, Randall Wallace, Christopher Gibson, and Alan Munro, based on a story by Mel Gibson and Randall Wallace.

Lionsgate had previously enlisted a slew of international distribution partners including Leonine (Germany), Metropolitan Film (France), Diamond (Spain), Dutch Filmworks (Benelux), Mis. Label (Scandinavia), Sun Africa (South Africa), Monolith (Poland), Vertical Entertainment (Eastern Europe), Blitz Film (Ex-Yugo), Ascot Elite (Switzerland), Pris Audiovisuais (Portugal), and Acme (Baltics). Icon will release in Australia andNew Zealand.
