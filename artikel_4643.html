Growing up, I never understood the hype around the concept of gratitude. I always thought gratitude was a glorified term for being thankful for what’s right in front of you. I’ve learned that gratitude is much more than that. Gratitude is actually your superpower.

It can reduce your stress levels, increase your self-esteem, and shift your attention towards positive experiences. The simple act of being thankful for the smallest thing can cause your body to release “feel-good” chemicals that can improve your mood and motivation.

Living a life filled with gratitude can also help you move forward in life. It allows you to believe that there is a brighter tomorrow despite the difficulties you encounter. Life can be very challenging, and gratitude can help you build a more solid foundation.

November is a month committed to gratitude. Our Thanksgiving holiday is often a favorite because there is no pressure to give gifts. Instead, it’s a time to take a break, enjoy each other’s company, and give thanks. And this season of gratitude makes people feel good. People like to especially thank, acknowledge, and appreciate others this time of year. So as we kick off the holidays, take the opportunity to start enhancing your gratitude superpower.
