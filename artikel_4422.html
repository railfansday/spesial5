I like to start every Monday the same way: coffee in my Ember Mug 3 and AirPods Pro 3 in my ears. Now I’m ready for my first strategy meeting of the week. I go through lots of other office accessories, test tons of personal audio gear, but these are two things I’d replace immediately if they were lost or broken. And, right now, they’re on sale for Black Friday at Amazon, along with dozens of other recommendations to boost your productivity and caffeine intake.

The smart mug that keeps my coffee hot through a full morning of calls.

Coffee at my desk used to go cold the minute a meeting ran long. The Ember Mug 3 holds whatever temperature you pick, and Ember says the included sipping lid stretches the battery to three hours away from the coaster. Parked on its charging coaster, it stays warm all day. You can set the temperature on the mug itself or fine-tune it in the Ember app, and Ember says this is the first of its mugs that can go in the dishwasher (top rack only). The 14-ounce white version is $94.99 at Amazon, down from $149.95.

I live along a busy stretch of road, and the AirPods Pro 3 make it sound a lot less like a raceway. With noise cancellation on, brake squeal and traffic fall back so that the hi-hats and bass lines in my music come through clean again, and the voices in my conference calls are all clear. The foam-infused tips seat deeper than the Pro 2’s; the buds and case are now rated IP57; and Apple rates battery life at up to eight hours. They launched at $249, and Amazon has them for $149.00. My AirPods Pro 3 impressions cover the fit and sound in more detail.

The same familiar design, with even better sound and noise cancellation thanks to Apple’s H2 chip.

If headphones are more your style than earbuds, the AirPods Max 2 look identical to the originals but run on Apple’s H2 chip and a new high-dynamic-range amplifier that delivers more expressive audio. Apple says that pairing makes noise cancellation 1.5 times more powerful, particularly against low rumble like planes, and rates battery life at up to 20 hours with ANC on. Plug in a USB-C cable, and they play 24-bit/48kHz lossless audio with under 15 milliseconds of latency, which matters if you edit audio or game at your desk. All five colors are $419.00 at Amazon, down from $549.00.
