Charlotte aiming to take goal-scoring momentum into Chicago clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Dean Smith believes Charlotte FC have the ability to ride their end-of-season momentum in front of goal as they prepare for another campaign in the MLS Cup playoffs.

Charlotte signed off the regular season with an entertaining 3-3 draw with Inter Miami, with the Crown coming from three goals down to claim a point at Nu Stadium.

Liel Abada's brace and Rodolfo Aloko's 90th-minute leveller ensured a share of the spoils, though Charlotte were pipped to fourth place by the Chicago Fire in the process.

And that is who they will face in their Round One best-of-three series, with the first match coming this weekend at SeatGeek Stadium against Gregg Berhalter's side.

But Charlotte come into the match off the back of an unbeaten three-game run, a stretch that saw them plunder 11 goals, with eight of those coming in 4-0 routs over Orlando City SC and CF Montreal.

"I'm disappointed because we were aiming for a top four finish," Smith said. "If we had beaten Miami last time out, we would have found ourselves in that top four.

"I'm proud of the boys, though. There has been a lot of change this year. We've brought new players in during that time and I think we have grown overall as a team.

"You have seen from the World Cup break how much we have grown, and we will go into the playoffs knowing that we can certainly go and cause problems and score goals.

"We ended the season with 11 goals in three games and going into the playoffs, we know we have got that behind us. And we've got two clean sheets as well, so we've got momentum at both ends of the pitch, so I'm looking forward to it."

Chicago are, however, another side in rich goal-scoring form. Their final regular-season match saw them go down 4-3 to the Columbus Crew, though they still sealed fourth.

The Fire have not appeared in an MLS Cup final since 2003, when they lost to San Jose Earthquakes.

They also lost in the 2000 final to the Kansas City Wizards, and their only MLS Cup success came 28 years ago, when they defeated D.C. United 2-0 in the 1998 final.

If anyone in Chicago's squad knows about winning major trophies, then Lewandowski is certainly the man for the big occasion.

Lewandowski has managed nine goals and five assists in 19 appearances since leaving Barcelona, and he will be keen to lead Chicago to glory here.

But Charlotte have their own creative midfield maestro in Biel, who leads the MLS for assists so far this season (18).

The Spaniard also has 10 goals for Charlotte, while his 65 chances created rank as the most of any player within Smith's ranks this season in MLS.

Despite being favoured by the Opta supercomputer, Charlotte have failed to win any of their last four meetings with Chicago in the MLS, losing all four. The Fire during that period have outscored their opponents 10-4.

Indeed, Charlotte's last win away at Chicago came back in May 2024, when Ashley Westwood, who looks likely to start this weekend, scored the only goal in the 60th minute.

Smith's side also struggled on their travels last year, only managing five road wins throughout the regular season, while Chicago picked up nine home victories, a tally only seven teams across both divisions could better.
