Klopp fell ‘head over heels in love’ with Germany squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jurgen Klopp declared he fell in love with his Germany squad at first sight, as he heaped praise on their 5-0 destruction of the Netherlands in the Nations League on Monday.

The former Liverpool boss, appointed after the shock last-32 exit to Paraguay at the 2026 World Cup, assembled a huge 44-man squad for his first games in charge in September, splitting the group in two for their four matches.

Germany started slowly under Klopp and won just one of those fixtures – a 2-0 victory over Serbia – before the coach selected a standard 24-man squad for this international break which produced the goods.

After defeating Serbia 3-1 last Friday, they remarkably thrashed Xavi's Netherlands in Berlin to book their place in the Nations League quarter-finals.

"In the summer, you got the feeling that the new coach, which was me, had to get drunk to make the German national team look good. But I arrived and was head over heels in love – with 44 men," Klopp told ARD.

"Those who are here now are wonderful footballers, great characters. But the perception from the outside was different.

"We have a good idea, and thought if we can get these highly talented players moving, it could work well; then it won't be fun to play against us.

"And now everyone will go home with a smile and won't question the national team for at least three or four months.

"Instead they will think, 'This could be something special'."

Derry Scherhant curled his first international goal into the top corner just before half-time, before Florian Wirtz struck twice, with his goals coming either side of Nico Schlotterbeck's header.

Brajan Gruda impressed and he added to his pair of assists by rounding off the scoring with his second goal in as many international appearances.

"The game was extraordinary, also exceptionally difficult. We scored incredible goals, it was just insane," said Klopp.

"A fantastic football match, a magnificent experience. I wouldn't have dared to wish for something like this.

"This is a very special group at the moment.

"All problems have not been solved – that's nonsense, of course. But we have shown our potential. We have set the bar high for ourselves and we have to keep surpassing it."
