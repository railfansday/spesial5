6 Best Travel Dresses of 2026, Tested by a Style Writer and Travel Expert

The best travel dresses are the kind you can throw in a carry-on, forget about for hours, and still slip on looking put-together. As a style writer who’s constantly on the move (I checked off eight countries last year alone), I don’t say that lightly. I’ve learned the hard way which pieces wrinkle beyond the help of a hotel room iron, feel restrictive mid-transit, or just don’t pull their styling weight once I land. The ones worth packing work just as well with sneakers as they do with sandals, and make getting dressed in an unfamiliar place easy.

I tested 30-plus styles in real-life conditions, paying close attention to how they pack, wear, and hold up after long days of travel. The best ones strike the sweet spot between comfort and style with breathable fabrics, packable silhouettes, and just enough versatility to earn their spot in my very limited luggage space.

I’ve packed the below dresses for trips to Cancún, Paris, Marrakech, St. Lucia—and plenty of destinations in between—putting them through everything from long-haul flights to full-day itineraries. These are the ones I’ll reach for no matter where I’m headed next.

Since traveling involves lots of packing, travel expert Addie Bell says to look for wrinkle-resistant materials like spandex, nylon, or polyester. These fabrics are great for maintaining a polished look after being packed in a suitcase. Travel days often include a lot of walking, so you'll also want to make sure your dress's fabric is breathable—cotton and linen are great choices, but they are prone to wrinkling.

If you’re only traveling with a carry-on or trying to maximize storage, stick to neutral tones like black, navy, white, and beige, which are versatile and easy to mix and match with other wardrobe pieces. "Subtle patterns can also help hide wrinkles and stains, making the dress more travel-friendly," says Bell. But bright colors can add a fun, vibrant touch. "I always recommend sticking within a similar color palette to ensure they match other items you’re bringing.”
