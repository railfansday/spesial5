Sony wants to buy your PS5 for up to $400 if you’re not using it

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sony is encouraging PlayStation owners to trade in their PS5 consoles for up to $400, with notifications being sent to players offering cash in exchange for their old hardware.

The PS5 launched in November 2020 and has since gone on to sell over 90 million units. However, with the system approaching its sixth anniversary, some owners may have stopped using theirs.

But rather than letting those consoles gather dust, Sony is now encouraging players to sell them through a third-party service, with the money paid directly into their bank accounts.

As shared on ResetEra, PlayStation has begun sending notifications to users in the US advertising the offer. “Trade in your PS5 console for up to $400,” reads the message, dated November 2.

The scheme is being handled by Back Market, a company specializing in refurbished electronics. According to the notification, the $400 figure is based on previous quotes for a standard PS5 console in Grade 0 condition, bundled with at least two controllers.

Those estimates were collected between August 23 and September 22, 2026, meaning the amount isn’t guaranteed for everyone.

Instead, the final offer depends on several factors, including the console’s model, storage capacity, physical condition, and any accessories included. Back Market will also inspect the hardware before determining its value.

Players interested in selling their console can request an estimate through the service, with successful trade-ins resulting in a cash payment rather than PlayStation Store credit.

At the time of writing, it appears that the notification has only been sent to US players. It remains to be seen if a similar offer is extended to users in other regions.
