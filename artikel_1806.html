Man Utd chief Omar Berrada breaks silence on Man City charges and denies involvement

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada has insisted he was not involved ‘in any wrongdoings’ during his time at Manchester City amid the ongoing case against his former employers.

An independent commission found City guilty of breaching more than 100 of the Premier League’s financial rules in September between the period of 2009 and 2018.

City have appealed against the ruling and repeatedly denied any wrongdoing.

Berrada joined United in January 2024 having spent over a decade working in various roles for City Football Group.

He was named the club’s CEO in 2016 and remained in that role until 2020 when he was named City Football Group’s chief football operations officer for the next four years.

Berrada’s time at the club’s CEO overlaps with the period in which City were investigated. But the United chief has no concerns over any implications for him in the case.

‘I’m not concerned,’ Berrada told Sky Sports.

‘I want to be very clear about this: I wasn’t involved in any wrongdoings. I wasn’t involved in the proceedings. There are no allegations against me.

‘In that sense, there will be no surprises.’

Asked whether United could look to pursue compensation if City fail in their appeal, Berrada replied: ‘I don’t want to comment on that because right now, it’s an ongoing process.

‘When it runs its course, then we’ll take a view on what is our position as Manchester United.’

Berrada and the rest of United’s hierarchy have just presided over their third mid-season managerial sacking in three years with Mauricio Pochettino the latest to be tasked with lifting the club from its malaise.

‘If you think about the fact that we’ve had three permanent managers in less than three seasons, clearly we haven’t achieved the stability that we want to – that’s for sure,’ Berrada said.

‘There are different circumstances as to why it didn’t work out with Erik, Ruben and now Michael. And we’re always learning. We’re always trying to improve and understand what has failed.’
