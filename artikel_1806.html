No doubting Thomas as Doncaster Rovers boss Grant McCann hails winger - plus message to Bradford City hecklers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In many respects, his strike to make it 1-1 at Valley Parade was the icing on the cake. The winger was already enjoying his best day in a Rovers jersey since his summer move.

McCann said: "He was really good, He came up against a really good player, probably one of the best in the division at the moment at right-back in Josh Neufville.

"Jordan was good against him. He defended him pretty well and didn't let him hit many crosses, because that's his game.

"He took him the other way and even when Kayden Jackson went there, I thought he handled him really well - another experienced player.

"I think that will make him grow in stature - not in terms of anything else."

Having been beaten in embarrassing fashion in their previous two away matches at Kidderminster Harriers and MK Dons - where they conceded a combined aggregate of nine goals—Rovers showed plenty of character at Bradford City to equalise not once but twice.

Barracked by some Doncaster fans at Aggborough and Stadium MK, McCann got a bit of stick from Bantams followers when his side trailed in the second half. He would have the last laugh, courtesy of Sean Grehan.

He continued: "We've never shown any sort of lack of belief. The belief will always be there. It's probably the people from the outside looking in.

"Maybe the Bradford fans who were singing my name will probably think other things, but there's a real determination inside this group and inside me."
