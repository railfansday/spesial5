Southampton v Middlesbrough FC: Spygate backdrop disguises huge top-of-Championship clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The sides met in May, when Southampton won over two legs, but were expelled from the play-offs for spying on Middlesbrough in the build-up. Boro, perhaps drained by an affair which ran deep into final week, lost to Hull City at Wembley.

It means both sides are still in the second tier but well placed to get out this season, with Boro set to go top with victory at St Mary’s, and Saints capable of moving to third despite a four-point deduction for that and other spying offences.

Kim Hellberg would rather forget the history but anyone playing a drinking game watching Sky's coverage is guaranteed to be paralytic by kick-off if they take a drink every time it is mentioned.

"We've moved past that one," insists Boro's Swedish coach. I'm just looking forward to playing against a very good side, against a good coach (in an) interesting game to watch.

"I don't know what to expect in terms of (the atmosphere).

"A Friday night game is always a nice atmosphere to play in and I'm just looking forward to a tough opponent, a tough place to go to, energetic supporters and a good side we're facing.

"A lot of the players were at St Mary's last time which was also quite intense. They also know that I will be on their side whatever happens."

An away win would put ten points between arguably the two best sides in the division.

"It's a good look for the moment but that could change quickly," cautioned Hellberg. "Two points per game I think is a good benchmark to look for, especially at the beginning of the season. That's more important than maybe if you're first, second or third.

"We have focused this week on the other two opponents and first started talking about Southampton on Thursday.

"We face a team that haven't lost at home in 20 games.

"I think it's the two best football sides in the league. It's two brilliant squads and two very good coaches that try to play in ways that maybe are a little bit different to what many are used to.

"A win for either team gives a massive difference. It will be ten, seven or four (points between them) depending what the score is, which is still a quite good position for us."

Boro are without the injured Alfie Jones, Connor Barron and Rokas Pukstas.
