Brighton confirm another major injury blow ahead of Conference League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Brighton midfielder Malick Yalcouye face a lengthy spell on the sidelines due to a knee injury.

The Ivory Coast star sustained the injury while on international and missed last Sunday’s 2-0 Premier League loss at Hull City.

Head coach Fabian Hurzeler confirmed that Yalcouye is now ruled out of Thursday’s Uefa Conference League clash against Romanian League leaders Craiova and could be out for a ”several weeks.”

“Several weeks, definitely,” confirmed the Albion boss. “It could be anywhere from six to 10 weeks, so unfortunately it's a long-term injury.

“But I think he's a quick healer and he will come back as quickly as possible.”

The Ivory Coast midfielder has made 17 appearances in all competitions during the 2026/27 campaign, scoring four goals and providing no assists. His Premier League contribution stands at 10 appearances and two goals, while he has also found the net in the Conference League and Carabao Cup.

The 21-year-old has particularly impressed with his energy, athleticism and willingness to drive forward from midfield.

Hurzeler added: “Of course, every injury hurts us because we feel for the player. We build relationships with the players, and every player wants to be on the pitch and express themselves on the pitch. If they can't do that, you feel for them.

“I think that's the worst thing that can happen to a player: being injured, being out and seeing their teammates performing on the pitch from the sidelines.

”We have a great environment where we can really help these players and give them the belief that they can come back as quickly as possible. That's also what we're doing with Malick.”

Elsewhere, Diego Gomez will be rested against Craiova while Olivier Boscagli misses out due to illness. Left back Jaouen Hadjam is out due to injury and the match will also arrive too soon for Stefanos Tzimas.

“Yes, too soon [for Tzimas] said Hurzeler to Sussex World. “I think his first step will be a game with the Under-21s, and then we'll go from there.

“But, as I said at the last press conference, it's a joy to see him training, and huge credit to him so far.”

On Hadjam, Hurzeler said: “Hadjim has a small issue, definitely a small issue, but we have to take it day by day.

“I just spoke to him and he feels quite confident. He has a lot of trust in his body. For tomorrow it's too early, but hopefully he'll be available for Sunday.”

On the plus side, summer signing Femi Azeez is fit and could make his debut on Thursday night.
