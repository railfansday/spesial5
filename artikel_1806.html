Three Bridges FC launches new post-16 academy

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Following the club's successful promotion to Step 3, they are building a pathway that connects education, youth development and senior football.

The two-year academy will be delivered in partnership with JACE Training and include UEFA-qualified coaching, competitive football, GPS performance tracking, Veo video analysis, strength and conditioning, and gym access.

The club said: “Current Year 11 students can combine their passion for football with study towards a Level 3 qualification in Sport and Physical Activity.

“Based at Three Bridges FC in Crawley, the programme offers UEFA-qualified coaching, competitive fixtures and structured education in partnership with JACE Training. Teaching and football coaching will be led by Jamie Crellin, an experienced teacher of 10 years and the club’s First Team Manager.

“Our ambition is to develop talented footballers while helping young people gain the qualifications, skills and confidence needed for their future.”

The club are holding their first Open Trial & Information Evening on Wednesday 16 December 2026, from 5pm–8pm, at Three Bridges FC.

For more information, visit https://threebridgesfc.co.uk/academy/.
