When is FA Cup draw? Date, start time, second round ball numbers and TV channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The FA Cup continues apace this weekend with the draw for the second round.

League One and League Two clubs have joined the action, with one of the pick of the ties seeing Robbie Savage facing off against his son, Charlie, as Peterborough host Reading.

The FA Cup first round ties begin on Friday night with 2021 winners Leicester taking on Oxford City. Elsewhere, 2013 winners Wigan are aiming to avoid an early exit at the hands of Boreham Wood.

Eighth-tier AFC Rushden & Diamonds and Stafford Rangers - the lowest-ranked teams remaining in the competition - face off against Burton Albion and Grimsby Town respectively, raising the prospect of a major giant-killing.

The weekend’s action will see 80 teams whittled down to 40 for the second round, with Championship and Premier League teams entering the fray in the third round.

Here’s everything you need to know about the FA Cup draw for the second round...

The draw for the FA Cup second round will take place on Monday, November 9, 2026, before the clash between non-league clubs Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted.

Television coverage of the game starts at 6:30pm GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live on TNT Sports 1.

Live blog: You can follow all draw via Standard Sport’s live blog.
