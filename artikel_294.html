The Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration Ultra Premium Collection at market price in Walmarts Black Friday sale — save up to $130

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection is on sale at Walmart: Buy the Day Box for $309, the Night Box for $339.77, or a randomly selected edition for $316.52.

The flagship Ultra Premium Collection boxes for the Pokémon TCG's 30th Celebration expansion are currently sold out at Amazon, but Walmart's latest batch of Black Friday deals has delivered.

As of Nov. 25, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection Day (Espeon Edition) is available for $309 at Walmart ($85.95 off), while the Night Box (Umbreon) is going for $339.77 ($60.18 off). If you're not particularly attached to either Eeveelution, you can buy a randomly selected edition for $316.52 — a $133.47 saving (30percent off).

With TCGplayer's market prices currently at $299.81 for the Day Box and $334.27 for the Night, these are some of the most competitive prices we've seen outside the specialist marketplace.

In fact, Walmart's Night Box is $60.22 cheaper than Amazon's Nov. 16 restock.

Each collection comes loaded with 29 anniversary booster packs, a Classic Collection pack containing three etched trading cards, and exclusive Pikachu ex and either Espeon ex (Day) or Umbreon ex (Night) promos. You'll also get a themed playmat, acrylic card display, card sleeves, deck box, metal coin, and damage-counter dice.
