Score $70 off our favorite budget iPad at Amazon this Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SAVE $70: As of Nov. 26, the 11-inch iPad (A16 chip, 128GB, WiFi) is on sale for $379 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $70 off its list price of $449.

After a round of price increases earlier this year, it's been a relief to see some Apple devices discounted for Black Friday.

iPads are among the deals we've spotted, with the 11-inch iPad (our favorite budget pick) even popping up on sale for $70 off its post-price-hike price of $449. As of Nov. 26, this discount has brought the price of this iPad down to $379. This price only applies to the blue, pink, and silver models. If you don't want to splash out on a fancy tablet this year, this iPad sets you up with the essentials. It comes with 128GB of storage, the A16 chip, a vibrant Liquid Retina display with True Tone (which adjusts the display's color temperature based on the lighting conditions), a 12MP Center Stage front camera, and a 12MP Wide back camera. Plus, it has an all-day battery life.

As for why it's our top budget iPad, Mashable's Samantha Mangino writes that "Even though its tech is basic compared to the advanced iPad Air and Pro, it's great for someone who wants a portable screen for streaming, playing games, and reading."

If an iPad has been on your seasonal shopping list, this is a great opportunity to save $70 on the 11-inch iPad at Amazon.
