TPG member offers are special deals, discounts and bonuses available to TPG members. These offers can help you save money or get more value from travel tools and rewards programs we think are worth knowing about. Simply create or sign in to your free TPG account to access available member offers.

To become a TPG member, simply create a TPG account using your email address. Once you’re signed in, you’ll have access to TPG member benefits, including special member offers and the ability to join the conversation in the comments on TPG articles.

There is no fee to simply become a TPG member; just set up an account using your email address to get started.

Sign in to your TPG account and click “Get offer” on the deal you want to use. You'll be directed to the page where you can access that specific offer.

Our favorite part about The Points Guy is the community of people who love deals, points and travel just as much as we do.

That's why we're excited to build a community where TPG readers and viewers can connect with us — and each other. When you create a free TPG account, you can join the conversation in the comments on TPG articles to ask questions, share your own experiences and offer real-world advice when you've already been there and done that.

But creating a TPG account unlocks more than just the conversation.

TPG members get access to special offers that can help you save on our favorite travel tools and earn more with top rewards programs. This includes tools picked by our rewards experts that can help you find award flights, get through the airport faster, automatically save money when flight prices drop and more.

Depending on the special offer, you might get a bigger new-member bonus, additional free months of a service, a discount or other bonus perks when you sign up through your TPG account. At times, we've even seen TPG-exclusive credit card bonuses and some of the highest available bonuses on points purchases.

As just one example, TPG members who purchase a year of point.me via the TPG member offers page can currently get four bonus months added to their membership, worth $43. This is a TPG member-exclusive deal you can't access elsewhere.

That offer gives you access to point.me's unlimited award search tool that finds flights you can book with points, lets you set price alerts and more.

The point.me Premium membership level also includes a points strategy consultation, a $100 annual concierge booking credit and the ability to use multi-city search.

You'll currently also find special offers for TPG members from ExpertFlyer, Clear, Blacklane, Junova, Flighty and Rove, just to name a few.

And there’s no need to pick just one deal: You can take advantage of as many TPG special offers as you'd like.
