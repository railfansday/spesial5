3 things from Charlotte's 111-97 loss to Atlanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Charlotte Hornets suffered another loss Friday night, as the Atlanta Hawks eliminated them from NBA Cup contention. Although the Hornets led at halftime, their effort couldn't be sustained, as Atlanta outscored Charlotte 31-19 in the third quarter. From there, the Hawks were well in control, extending their lead as high as 19 points, ultimately earning a 111-97 victory.

Moussa Diabate was dominant for the away team, as the Frenchman tied a career-high with 21 points to go along with 15 rebounds, two assists and one steal on 9-for-12 shooting. Naz Reid also had a double-double, tallying 12 points and 10 rebounds, while Brandon Miller scored 19 points. All-Star Jalen Johnson stole the show for Atlanta, finishing with 26 points, six rebounds, six assists and four steals. Ahead of Charlotte's next matchup against the Grizzlies, here are three things to take note of as the season progresses.

The Charlotte Hornets missed six of their 16 free-throw attempts Friday night, as Naz Reid, Moussa Diabate and Sion James missed a pair each. Free-throw shooting has been a strength for Charlotte this season, shooting 81.4% from the line, third-best in the NBA. Grayson Allen is shooting 92.7% from the stripe, while rookie Christian Anderson Jr. has yet to miss a foul shot.

In a game where Jalen Johnson and Dyson Daniels tallied four steals each, the Hornets finished with one block and four steals for the entire game. In 15 contests to begin the season, the Hornets rank 25th in blocks per game and 29th in steals per game. Although shooting is at the core of Charlotte's playstyle, they could afford to add a defensive stopper on the perimeter.

Despite hoisting 12 fewer attempts, the Hawks finished with more made 3-pointers than Charlotte. Now 15 games in, the Hornets attempt the most 3-pointers per game (45.4) but remain dead last in 3-point percentage (31%). While offensive rebounding can often keep the Hornets within striking distance of their opponents, shooting will have to improve to end Charlotte's four-game losing streak.
