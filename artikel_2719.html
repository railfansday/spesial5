Three predictions for the Bucs offense down the stretch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bucky Irving has already established himself as the centerpiece of Tampa Bay's rushing attack, and the second half of the season could bring an even bigger accomplishment. Through nine games, Irving has rushed for 825 yards and seven touchdowns on 167 carries, averaging 4.9 yards per attempt. The prediction is that Irving finishes the season as the Buccaneers' single-season rushing record holder. The franchise record currently belongs to James Wilder, who rushed for 1,544 yards in 1984. Irving needs another 720 yards to surpass that mark, which would require a strong finish but isn't out of reach if Tampa Bay keeps leaning on him. With uncertainty surrounding Baker Mayfield's health, Irving's importance could become even greater. Establishing the run will be essential to take pressure off whoever is under center, and Irving can make that approach successful.

Getting Jalen McMillan back on the field could be one of the biggest developments for Tampa Bay's offense during the second half of the season. His absence has been hard to overlook, especially as the Buccaneers need more consistency and explosive plays from their passing attack. During his rookie season in 2024, McMillan finished with 37 receptions for 461 yards and eight touchdowns. Seven of those touchdowns came over his final five games, showing how quickly he can become a difference-maker when he finds his rhythm. His ability to separate, create downfield opportunities, and make plays in contested situations gives the offense what it has been missing. He doesn't need to lead the team in targets to make that impact. His presence alone can force defenses to account for another legitimate threat, creating opportunities for the rest of the receiving group.

The Buccaneers may have originally viewed Jalon Daniels as a developmental quarterback, but circumstances have given him an opportunity to prove he belongs in the NFL. After Baker Mayfield dislocated his thumb, Daniels stepped into the starting lineup and gained valuable experience. He also earned his first NFL victory against Dallas, showing that he can manage an offense and compete when given the opportunity. With Mayfield now dealing with foot and ankle injuries, Daniels could be called upon again against Detroit. The prediction is that he makes multiple additional starts before the season concludes, whether because of Mayfield's health or the Buccaneers' decision to give the rookie more opportunities. Daniels has shown some encouraging qualities, particularly his mobility and ability to extend plays. Through his first three starts, he completed 61.2% of his passes for 501 yards and three touchdowns, while also throwing five interceptions. There is clearly room for improvement, but the experience he is gaining could be valuable for his development.
