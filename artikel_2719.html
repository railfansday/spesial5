Eagles add consultant who coached under Chip Kelly, Doug Pederson, Nick Sirianni

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Eagles add consultant who coached under Chip Kelly, Doug Pederson, Nick Sirianni originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

T.J. Paganetti, who spent 10 seasons in two separate stints with the Eagles in a variety of roles and was part of both Super Bowl championship staffs, is returning to the club as a consultant, a league source confirmed following a report by ESPN's Adam Schefter.

Paganetti will serve in a similar role to previous consultant Jim Bob Cooter in 2021 and Marcus Brady in 2022 and 2023, advising Howie Roseman and Nick Sirianni on numerous topics.

"I don't comment on things like this often, but T.J. Is an outstanding coach," Jason Kelce tweeted last November after Paganetti was fired by the Saints. "He brought a ton of value for us in Philadelphia, and was a big part of our success up front.

"One of my favorite coaches from my time with the Eagles. He's extremely knowledgeable, driven, and always worked his ass off to put the players in positions to succeed, I respect him greatly.  A lot of teams are in need of good coaches, the team that hires him is getting a great one."

Paganetti, 37, began his coaching career in 2009 under Chip Kelly while he was still a student at the University of Oregon. When Kelly became Eagles head coach in 2013, he brought Paganatti with him as an offensive and quarterbacks analyst.

Paganetti returned to Oregon for two years in 2015 and 2016 and worked under new head coach Mark Helfrich, who Paganetti had worked with under Kelly. But Doug Pederson brought him back in 2017 as an offensive quality control coach and assistant offensive line coach under Jeff Stoutland. He remained in that role for two years.

In 2019, he became assistant running backs coach and in 2020 assistant run game coordinator.

Nick Sirianni kept Paganetti when he replaced Pederson and named him an offensive quality control coach in 2021. He became run game specialist and assistant tight ends coach in 2023 and then run game specialist and assistant offensive line coach in 2024.

Paganetti joined Kellen Moore's Saints staff as run game coordinator in 2025 after the two had worked together with the Eagles in 2024. But Paganetti was among a handful of assistant coaches fired halfway through the season with the Saints 3-6.

Paganetti's specific responsibilities are unclear, but the Eagles are an NFC-worst 2-7, have lost seven straight games and are buried in last place in the NFC East two years after winning the Super Bowl. So clearly management is looking for a fresh voice with varied NFL experience who can help turn the operation around.

Pagnetti's brother, Ryan, was with the Eagles from 2018 through 2020 as a defensive coach and is now a popular NFL analytics social media expert.
