Michigan State defense stymies Michigan, 15-6

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

ANN ARBOR — Led by a defense that forced four turnovers and constantly pressured quarterback Tommy Carr, Michigan State defeated rival Michigan, 15-6, on Saturday, Nov. 7.

Michigan had won four straight in the series.
