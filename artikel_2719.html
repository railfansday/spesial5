WWE star Bronson Reed dies at age 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

WWE star Jermaine Haley, known as Bronson Reed, died at the age of 38 on Friday, Nov. 27.

WWE announced the death later in the day and said he died "due to medical complications."

"Jeremaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague," WWE said in a statement. "Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine's entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as his friends, co-workers and fans."

A member of The Vision stable, Reed was last at Crown Jewel 2026 on Nov. 7 and interfered in the match between Oba Femi and Bron Breakker. He suffered an injury during the match and reportedly had to stay in Saudi Arabia due to the severity of it.
