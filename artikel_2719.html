Travis Hunter now has two games with a catch and an interception. Deion Sanders had nine.

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Jaguars cornerback Travis Hunter intercepted a pass on Sunday against the Titans, and doing his part-time job as a wide receiver, he also caught two passes. It was the second time Hunter has both caught and intercepted a pass in the same game.

That’s a rare feat in the modern NFL, where few players play both ways. It’s been four years since any player other than Hunter had both a catch and an interception in the same game. The last player to do it was New England’s Marcus Jones, who did it twice in 2022.

Since the 1970 AFL-NFL merger, the player who has had both a catch and an interception in the same game the most times is Hunter’s college coach, Deion Sanders, who did it nine times. Sanders did it three times while playing for the Falcons and six times while playing for the Cowboys.

Hunter is primarily a defensive player: He played 56 defensive snaps and only six offensive snaps on Sunday, and that’s been typical for him this season. Overall in 2026, Hunter has played 84 percent of the Jaguars’ defensive snaps and 9 percent of the Jaguars’ offensive snaps. But the Jaguars say they view Hunter as a two-way player for the long term, so there’s a good chance that he’ll have more games with both a catch and an interception, and perhaps eclipse Sanders’ modern-era record of nine such games.

But Hunter is unlikely to break the all-time record of Packers Hall of Famer Don Hutson, who had 21 games with both a catch and an interception between 1940 and 1945. Hutson had some statistics that don’t look possible, such as the game when he outscored the Chicago Cardinals all by himself with six catches for 86 yards and two touchdowns, plus two interceptions for 84 return yards and a pick-six, in a 35-14 Packers victory. Hutson was by far the best receiver of his era and one of the best defensive players of his era, and he was also a pretty good kicker by the standards of the 1940s. There will never be another Don Hutson.

What Hunter is doing in 2026, however, is a special accomplishment that also deserves celebration. There’s no one else playing the game in quite the same way Hunter is playing it.
