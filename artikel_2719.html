Week 11 NFL picks: Games with playoff implications for Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Cleveland Browns are on the bye this week, but there's still ample action for fans to tune into. In past seasons, at this stage of the year, Browns fans would be rooting for better draft position. But this year, we're keeping an eye on how certain games can impact the Browns' playoff chances.

The most important games center around the other three AFC North teams. All three — Baltimore, Pittsburgh, and Cincinnati — are on the road. However, all three of them figure to have a reasonable shot to win. Cincinnati's offense should trounce Washington's, the Steelers are facing a third-string quarterback, and only the Carolina game against Baltimore figures to be a bit of a toss-up.

The Patriots are on the bye this week, and they're the closest wildcard contender to Cleveland. If you disregard the AFC North, the AFC West is the division to look out for. The Raiders are two games behind Cleveland right now, but if they somehow pick up a win this week, then they face the Browns next week in a game that would have a lot more wildcard spot implications (for both teams). Based on the way Denver has played, though, it would be a surprise if they fell to the Raiders.

It'd also be a stunner of the Cardinals upset the Chiefs. Nonetheless, with both Kansas City and Denver at 7-2, if one of them lose this week, that presents the opportunity in future weeks where Cleveland could surpass one of them for a higher wildcard spot.
