NFC East standings: Giants on top, but division crown far from assured

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The New York Giants helped themselves immensely in the race to the NFC East title with their 23-18 victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday. Unfortunately for the Giants, the rest of the division kept pace.

The 5-6 Dallas Cowboys overwhelmed the hapless Tennessee Titans, the Philadelphia Eagles upset the Pittsburgh Steelers, and perhaps most importantly in the big picture the Washington Commanders upset the Cincinnati Bengals 22-20 on Monday Night Football.

That is a 4-0 week including three victories over teams with winning records for a division that has been snickered at most of the season.

The Athletic's playoff simulator gives the Giants a 66% chance of winning the division and 67% chance of reaching the playoffs. No one else is close.

Giants 6-3-1 | 66% chance to win the NFC EastCowboys 5-6 | 19%Commanders 4-5-1 | 15%Eagles 3-7 | <1%

The Giants, though, aren't nearly as comfortable atop the division as those percentages make it look This is a classic 'objects in the rearview mirror are closer than they appear' situation.

Especially when it comes to the Commanders.

Before the Bengals stumbled in the rain at Northwest Stadium in Landover, Md. and the Commanders pulled off a last-second 22-20 upset victory I would have pegged the number of victories the Giants would need to win the NFC East at nine. Remember, because of the tie with Washington nine wins is really 9.5.

That means Dallas would have to get to 10 victories. That means the Cowboys would have to go 5-1 the rest of the way. Even Cowboys fans can't feel good about their chances to do that.

Now, though, the Giants might need to reach 10 victories.

Because it would appear that, despite what the odds posted by the simulator, the Commanders have become a bigger threat.

Washington owns the tiebreaker over the Giants after their Week 10 Thursday Night Football victory, a game the Giants could not close out despite two interceptions of Jayden Daniels in the final six minutes. If the Giants fall short in the division, tying and losing to the Commanders in winnable games might be what they look back upon with the most regret.

After a 1-4-1 start, the Commanders have won three of four. Washington's next four games are as follows:

at Arizona Cardinals (3-8)at Tennessee Titans (1-9)vs. Houston Texans (4-6)vs. Atlanta Falcons (3-7)

That is four teams with a combined record of 11-30. With seven games remaining, it seems within reach for the Commanders to go 5-2 and get to nine victories. Could they go 6-1 and get to 10?

at Indianapolis Colts (3-8)vs. San Francisco 49ers (9-1)at Seattle Seahawks (6-4)vs. Cleveland Browns (6-4)

That is four teams with a combined record of 24-17.

The Giants remain in a good spot since they are the team everyone else has to chase. Still, they absolutely cannot afford to stumble against the downtrodden Colts. Even if they beat Indianapolis, the race is far from over.
