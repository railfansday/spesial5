Open Thread: What Wemby’s 50-point game says about the future of the NBA

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

On Saturday night, Wemby notched his third game scoring a minimum of fifty points. This harkens back to November 13, 2024 when he dropped a half-century mark on the Washington Wizards. His second fifty spot was earlier this season as he led the Spurs to a win against the Dallas Mavericks scoring a career-high 52 points.

More than a confluence of planets aligning in perfect harmony, the Alien's 50-point outing signals a new era as he evolves into the face of the NBA. His choice to stay on after the game was far enough out of reach for the Pelicans to finish with members of the second unit made a statement. He wanted fifty and nothing was stopping him.

After the game Spurs head coach was asked if he could have pulled Wemby before his fiftieth point. Mitch arched his eyebrows, leaned in, and simply stated "no comment."

A night after James Harden notched his 26th 50+ point game surpassing Kobe Bryant's career best, Wemby put his stamp on the record. He's currently tied with Richie Guerin, David Robinson, Shaquille O'Neal, Dwyane Wade, Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, Gilbert Arenas, and Anthony Edwards who all have 3 career 50+ point games. He's one of fifty-two players to notch 50-point games. That's fifty-two players all time.

Meanwhile, on the same evening, Tyler Herro also scored 50 points. The difference? Herro and his Bucks lost. While Herro was losing to LeBron James, Wemby was smiling at Selena Gomez.

In another nod to Wemby, this 50 point game —paired with 18 rebounds and 5 blocks — was the fourth time in NBA history a player scored 50+, grabbed 15+ rebounds, and tallied 5+ blocks. The only three players to accomplish the feat are Bob McAdoo, Anthony Davis, and Wembanyama, who has now done it twice.

After the gamer, Wemby was asked if his staying on the court defied his belief in ethical basketball. "Ethical basketball isn't really a clearly defined term. What it means is creating a culture as a team." Wemby went on to explain that Mitch Johnson gave him 90 seconds to raise his total to fifty points, sharing that not only was that enough time, but everybody got what they wanted from the ultimatum.

When asked it he was okay with taking the additional shot, Victor responded through his smile "I made it, didn't I?"
