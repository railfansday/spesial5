Jazz vs Mavericks recap: A wild finish in Salt Lake City

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It was looking like another depressing night for Jazz fans. The young Jazz were plagued with turnovers (20 by the end of the third quarter), Darryn Peterson was struggling from three again (he's 0 for his last 15), and more injuries started piling up with Jaxson Hayes leaving early and Keyonte George turning another ankle. And once again, the Jazz slowly watched the Mavericks' lead grow and grow to near-insurmountable odds.

Then Isaiah Collier came in, and it felt like the Jazz had thrown in the towel.

With the game seemingly over after the third quarter, Svi Mykhailiuk scored 23 points in the fourth quarter and went absolutely nuclear. For the game, Mykhailiuk had 39 points and shot 10/15 from three. With less than 10 seconds left, Isaiah Collier scored on a layup to tie the game. On a side note, it's great that Isaiah Collier also had a hand in this one. He's had a great attitude despite being benched, and deserves some credit for his energy and hustle.

Heartbreak ensued when Ace Bailey fouled Cooper Flagg with four seconds left in the game, and Flagg hit the free throws. Then, an inbounds play ended with Kyle Filipowski missing a three to finish.

Yes, it was a dissapointing end. And yes, it was depressing to see our young players struggle again on a night they probably should have done better. It was the back end of a back-to-back for the Mavericks, after all. But this felt like something that could carry over into the next game and the rest of the season. It probably built some confidence for the bench, or at least in Svi Mykhailiuk, and might be the thing that reminds the Jazz they can win these games even when things aren't looking good. At least that's what you hope.

If I'm Keyonte George or Darryn Peterson, I'm looking at what Svi Mykhailiuk did, and I'm learning from it. The reality is that Utah has had two truly explosive games this season, one from Brice Sensabaugh, and one from Svi Mykhailiuk. We've had some dominant nights from Keyonte George but haven't experienced consistent explosive play from the starting lineup. If there's one thing you hope for from this one, it's that the Jazz starting lineup can take the confidence Svi showed tonight and incorporate it into their own play.

Hey, it might even improve their turnover issue. Believing in their ability could mean that the other things fall into place more, we'll find out tomorrow when they take on the Lakers once again.
