George Pickens rolls up three touchdowns in Week 10 vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys wide receiver George Pickens went for 11 receptions, 145 yards and three touchdowns on 18 targets in Week 10 against the San Francisco 49ers.

Why it Matters: Everything was coming up aces for Pickens on Sunday, and he had a monster fantasy stat line. Generally, he is considered a high-end WR2, but this was obviously a WR1-type performance. However, as long as CeeDee Lamb is also healthy, Pickens has a little bit of a fantasy ceiling. We'd bump him up if he outperforms Lamb again in Week 11 against the visiting Tennessee Titans.
