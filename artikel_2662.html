Red Sox Make Surprising Pitching Trade As Craig Breslow Continues Roster Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Boston Red Sox continue making changes to their roster, and their latest trade involves a pitcher who barely had an opportunity to contribute during the 2026 season.

Boston traded right-hander Johan Oviedo to the Arizona Diamondbacks on Friday in exchange for minor league left-hander Luke Dotson and a player to be named later or cash considerations. The move was part of a busy stretch for chief baseball officer Craig Breslow, who has already made several transactions as the organization prepares for the 2027 season.

Oviedo's departure is particularly interesting because of the circumstances surrounding his brief time in Boston.

The 28-year-old appeared in just one major league game for the Red Sox this season before an elbow injury essentially ended his year. Now, rather than bringing him back for another opportunity, Boston has decided to move in a different direction.

Oviedo arrived in Boston after previously pitching for the St. Louis Cardinals and Pittsburgh Pirates. He made 32 starts for Pittsburgh in 2023, posting a 4.31 ERA and demonstrating that he could handle a regular role in a major league rotation.

However, injuries interrupted his progress.

Oviedo underwent Tommy John surgery and missed the entire 2024 season before returning during the following year.

His opportunity with Boston proved similarly frustrating.

The right-hander pitched 3 2/3 innings in relief against Houston on March 30 before being placed on the injured list with a right elbow strain.

He eventually made a rehabilitation appearance for Triple-A Worcester in September but never returned to the major league mound for the Red Sox.

That left Boston with limited opportunities to evaluate how Oviedo might fit into its future pitching plans.

Arizona now gets the opportunity to determine whether he can regain his previous form.

The return gives Boston another developmental arm.

Dotson, 22, was selected by Arizona in the 11th round of the 2025 MLB Draft. The left-hander spent time with Single-A Visalia and High-A Hillsboro during the 2026 season. He is not an immediate replacement for Oviedo at the major league level, but the acquisition provides the Red Sox with another pitching prospect to develop.

The trade also reflects the broader approach Breslow has taken during the offseason.

Boston has already added catcher Nick Fortes from Tampa Bay and made several decisions involving its arbitration-eligible players. Connor Wong became a free agent after the Red Sox declined to tender him a contract, while the club retained control of its remaining unsigned eligible players.

The organization continues to evaluate both its major league roster and its minor league depth.

Trading Oviedo won't generate the attention of a blockbuster free-agent signing, but it demonstrates that Boston is willing to reshape its pitching inventory.

For Arizona, the appeal is a pitcher with previous starting experience and the possibility of renewed production if he can remain healthy. For Boston, the return is a younger prospect and additional potential compensation.
