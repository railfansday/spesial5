BTSC Wednesday Night Football thread: Packers at Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Happy Thanksgiving Eve — and it's enough of a holiday that the NFL has scheduled a football game.

At 8 p.m. EST, the Packers and Rams, both 6-4, will clash on… Netflix. Both teams are firmly in playoff bubble territory, with Green Bay as the current No. 7 seed and Los Angeles the current No. 9 seed in NFC.

The big news in this one, besides two NFC heavyweights hoping to stay alive in the postseason race, is that Green Bay has activated star edge rusher Micah Parsons, who plans on making a season debut against LA.
