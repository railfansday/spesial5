James Jones: I'm on text threads with former Packers, none are on Aaron Rodgers' side

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In the dispute between Steelers coach Mike McCarthy and ex-Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers, players who were with both of them in Green Bay are squarely on McCarthy’s side.

That’s the word from former Packers wide receiver James Jones, who echoed the comments of former Packers offensive lineman T.J. Lang, both of whom say that in text threads of former Packers players, everyone agrees that Rodgers’ criticism of McCarthy was out of line.

“How do you jump on Pat McAfee’s show and say all this? Come on, man,” Jones said on the Speakeasy podcast. “My text thread is full of texts and group chats with a lot of my teammates right now, and none are on 12’s side, especially the ones that know Mike.”

Jones said that even if everything Rodgers said about McCarthy’s shortcomings as a coach is true, airing dirty laundry in public is unacceptable. Jones said that when a coach and a player have the kind of personal history that McCarthy and Rodgers have, they should address their differences privately.

“I don’t even care if it’s the truth,” Jones said. “Have the conversation with Mike in there. This is not something you go to the media bashing somebody. I would not do this to people that I love.”

It’s safe to say that there’s no love anymore between Rodgers and McCarthy. But there is a lot of love for McCarthy among former Packers players, who are not happy that Rodgers chose to publicly attack their coach.
