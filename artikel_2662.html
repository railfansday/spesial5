ESPN captured Tom Jackson’s ‘surprise’ visit to studio weeks before death

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tom Jackson visited ESPN's Bristol, Conn. campus to surprise Chris Berman, best friends and longtime broadcast partners, three weeks before the Broncos legend died on Friday.

As of Monday, a cause of death for Jackson, who was 75, had not been disclosed.

During Sunday's "NFL Countdown" on ESPN, Berman teared up after the Worldwide Leader aired a behind the scenes video of Jackson's Nov. 1 studio visit to celebrate 40 years of "NFL PrimeTime" in Bristol.

"We're going to surprise Berm and just express how much the friendship means to me," Jackson said in the video.

Berman was all smiles when Jackson showed up on set.

Tom Jackson on the ESPN set before the game between the Green Bay Packers and the Carolina Panthers on Sept. 13, 2004 at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina. Getty Images

The pair — who hosted "NFL PrimeTime" for 18 seasons during its original run from 1987 to 2005 — shook hands and reminisced on their 29-year broadcast partnership.

"We were more family than friends," Jackson said in the video. "It's like brothers."

ESPN named its NFL studio in Bristol after the duo in 2017 for their on-air contributions. It is the only named studio on ESPN's campus.

ESPN commentators Tom Jackson and Chris Berman during the ESPN Monday Night Football game between the Chicago Bears and St. Louis Rams in St. Louis, Missouri on Dec. 11, 2006. Getty Images

Berman, who announced Jackson's death during "Sunday NFL Countdown," explained that he couldn't make it through the video without tearing up.

"I had no idea he was coming [to visit], I mean, my God, talk about the weight that that is now three weeks later," Berman said.

"It was the last time we saw him. I'm glad we did."

Berman explained that Booger McFarland, who replaced Jackson on "NFL PrimeTime" in 2020, was "part of" Jackson's surprise visit — adding that McFarland left the set because "his mic had a problem" just before Jackson arrived.

Jackson's family informed ESPN that he died.

Berman said Sunday that Jackson's death was "unexpected" and that he spoke with Jackson "at length" on Tuesday.

"He was a vital 75," Berman said. "Tom was poignant. He was active. This is a shock."

Linebacker Tom Jackson #57 of the Denver Broncos looks on from the field during a game against the Buffalo Bills at Rich Stadium on Oct. 21, 1984 in Orchard Park, New York. Getty Images

Jackson is survived by his wife, Jennifer, and his two daughters, Taylor and Morgan.

He retired from ESPN in 2016 following the Pro Football Hall of Fame's enshrinement ceremony that year, his last assignment.

Jackson was awarded the Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award by the Hall of Fame in 2015.

A fourth-round pick by the Broncos in the 1973 draft out of Louisville, Jackson spent 14 seasons with Denver and made the Pro Bowl three times.

He played in 191 regular-season NFL games, including 177 starts and 10 playoff games.
