The Met and the Shanghai Museum Sign Collaborative Agreement

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The collaboration comes just weeks after MoMA, Paris’s Centre Pompidou, and Hong Kong’s M+ Museum embarked on a similar alliance.

Fluorescent foliage, crisp breezes, dogs in sweaters, and international museum partnership announcements — one of these is not like the others, but you can check them all off on this autumn's bingo card. The Metropolitan Museum of Art in Manhattan and the Shanghai Museum have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) codifying their relationship ahead of a dual-collection historic jewelry exhibition opening at the Shanghai institution this week.

The MoU between the two museums was announced less than three weeks after the Museum of Modern Art, Centre Pompidou in Paris, and M+ Museum in Hong Kong publicized their collections alliance at the end of October, further illustrating an institutional shift toward resource pooling, joint acquisitions, and other collaboration initiatives popularized in the wake of pandemic-induced budget deficits.

The Met and the Shanghai Museum, which have worked together on myriad projects and exhibitions since 1980, stated in a press release that their shared goals in scholarship and presentation advancement will manifest through “exhibitions, research initiatives, and curatorial exchange.”
