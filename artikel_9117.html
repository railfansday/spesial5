Dezeen Awards public vote reveals 16 winning designs

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The results of the design public vote for Dezeen Awards 2026 have been revealed, with 16 design projects selected as winners by Dezeen’s readers.

Projects voted as readers’ favourite include a temporary festival venue made from an eclectic mix of salvaged materials, including fabric from hot air balloons and sheep’s wool.

Another public vote winner is a set of jacquard textiles composed like music to create layered spatial compositions.

Dezeen Awards 2026 public vote winners revealed this week

Dezeen Awards 2026, in partnership with Trimble, will reveal all public vote winners this week. The architecture winners were announced on Monday and the interiors shortlist was announced yesterday.

Readers were invited to choose their favourite projects out of the 392-strong shortlist in this year’s vote.

The vote took place between 28 September and 16 October, spanning shortlists in each of Dezeen Awards’ three main category sectors.

All the winning design projects are listed below, each with a link to a dedicated page on the Dezeen Awards website where you can find an image and more information about the project.

The public vote is separate from the main judging process, in which entries are assessed by our panel of industry-leading judges. The overall winners of this year’s programme will be announced at the Dezeen Awards 2026 party on 16 November at the Royal Opera House in London.

Read on to see the list of this year’s public vote winners for the design categories:

Lijn Seating by Carole Baijings for Geiger

The Inverted Earth: Fugue for Windows by Aya Kawabata

Room by Lidewij Edelkoort for El Espartano
