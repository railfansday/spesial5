The saga of It Ends With Us has lasted for years, with the two leads becoming synonymous with the book-to-screen adaptation and the long legal battle afterward. Namely Blake Lively and Justin Baldoni had been battling in court due to allegations made about about the film. The saga has taken another twist, as Baldoni reportedly cut another tie in LA, selling his home in the wake of the brouhaha.

Blake Lively's complaints against Wayfarer Studios started a years-long legal battle between her and Justin Baldoni, who also directed and starred in It Ends With Us. Despite the pair issuing a joint statement and settling, there have been changes in the actors' personal lives, particularly for Baldoni, who left Tinseltown. The latest update comes from Page Six, which claims that Baldoni sold his home in Los Angeles for over $2 million, after moving to Nashville in the midst of his legal battles.

The outlet claims the actors sold the house in August, and ended up making a profit of around one million dollars. Considering how wildly expensive lawyers fees were for both Lively and Baldoni, I'm sure any money coming in helps.

The report also clarifies the actor/producer/director still has a home on Ojai, so there are still some roots in California. Although it seems like his primary residence will be in Tennessee, where he'll presumably be able to operate with more anonymity than staying in California... especially as the It Ends With Us drama continues to make headlines.

As previously mentioned, the legal drama surrounding It Ends With Us has been going on for years, starting shortly after the movie arrived in theaters back in 2024. After his co-star's complaint Justin Baldoni filed his own defamation case, naming both Blake Lively and Ryan Reynolds in the process. Both of the movie's stars made allegations about what the other allegedly did on set, with the Jane the Virgin star claiming Lively tried to take control of the movie, using famous friends like Taylor Swift to try and influence the narrative.

The saga definitely captured the attention of the Internet, with the court of public opinion making their own judgments. It also seems to have negatively impacted both of its stars' careers. For his part, Baldoni largely hasn't been working while dealing with his legal battle again Lively. Luckily for him that's about to change, as Wayfarer Studios will produce Gina Rodriguez's upcoming directorial debut. We'll just have to see if/when he gets in front of the camera again.

It Ends With Us is streaming now for those with a Hulu subscription, and is, by critics accounts, a pretty decent movie. Baldoni didn't have projects on the 2026 movie release list, but maybe that'll change now that his legal life is settling.
