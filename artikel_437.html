Anthropic Executive Team Rings Nasdaq Opening Bell

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthropic co-founders and siblings Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei rang the Nasdaq opening bell, alongside key employees. The Nasdaq rose sharply at the open before ANT shares begin trading. The company behind the Claude chatbot and its seven co-founders raised $81.3 billion in an IPO priced above range, topping SpaceX’s record for the biggest-ever first time share sale, and another $26 billion in a concurrent private placement. The deal values Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis. (Source: Bloomberg)
