Robinhood CIO on Retail Access to Anthropic’s IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Stephanie Guild, CIO over at Robinhood Markets, discusses the record breaking IPO of Anthropic and what it says for the AI Trade. She also says customers have continued to buy Nvidia on a net basis on days the stock has fallen following recent news, even as it remains one of the platform’s highest-volume stocks and some customers sell. She speaks with Romaine Bostick & Ed Ludlow on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
