ImmuCell (ICCC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Higher calf values and a new $8 million expansion fuel the growth.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ImmuCell Corporation (ICCC +5.19%) reported a strategic shift toward its First Defense product line, exiting development of the Re-Tain mastitis treatment to prioritize bovine scours preventatives. Management indicated that high market values for beef and dairy calves supported increased demand for premium antibody products, driving top-line growth and improved manufacturing utilization. The company is executing an 8 million capital expansion project to alleviate manufacturing bottlenecks, specifically in freeze-drying and liquid processing, with completion targeted through late 2027. This transition included repurposing existing facilities and personnel to support First Defense production and sourcing capabilities.

As I mentioned in my initial remarks, ImmuCell made the decision in late 2025 to focus on our scours preventative products known as First Defense. The combination of factors led to this decision, and let me touch on calf values before discussing commercial activity and our new products. Our industry continues to benefit from historically strong calf values. Whether it's a calf born in a traditional beef herd or one originating on a dairy, their value has risen fivefold since 2021. Calf values are expected to remain strong in 2027 and 2028 as tight cattle supplies continue to underpin the market.

These higher calf values are reshaping farm-level economics, making newborn calf health an increasingly important priority. In addition, as dairies adopt more beef genetics to meet demand for beef, there are fewer dairy replacement heifers to support dairy herd expansion, which makes those heifers more valuable. For ImmuCell, this means that producers are interested to learn more about our premium products. The return on investment from using Tri-Shield has always been highly positive, even back in 2021 before calf values increased so dramatically. Certainly, protecting a calf worth more than $2,000 with a $15 product makes a lot of sense today.

Another driver for our top-line growth is our commercial activity, and we've made significant investments in our team and capabilities. Since last November, we have expanded our commercial team from nine to 13 people. The expanded sales team continues to increase customer engagement. As new hires gain traction in their territories, quality sales calls increased significantly from the second to the third quarter, and revenue per field sales representative also grew year-over-year. New customers contributed about 46% of our net growth in the third quarter of 2026, and increased utilization of existing customers contributed the remaining 54% of growth.

In the third quarter, dairy customers largely drove demand, as relatively few traditional cow-calf operations calve during this part of the year. This is great news for our business, as dairy demand tends to be stable from quarter to quarter. The last driver I will touch on is our new product line, Functional Feed. This line was developed for the calf ranch segment that we believe raises more than 4 million calves. Calf ranches manage large populations of primarily beef-on-dairy calves sourced from multiple farms that have varying newborn calf health protocols. Managing the inherent variability in these incoming calves that are two to seven days old when they arrive at the ranch can be a challenge.

Ideally, these calves benefited from Tri-Shield at birth, but that's difficult for the calf ranchers to determine. We developed a Functional Feed product line that is based on the same hyper-immunized colostrum we use for our biologicals line and is manufactured using a different and lower-cost drying technology. It has a much lower price per dose to encourage calf ranchers to administer it to all incoming calves. We have demonstrated 50% lower pre-weaning removal rates at these customers, which increases the number of calves that get to the market weight and generates a robust return on investment for the calf ranchers.

Although we do not report on this product line as a separate segment, we are very pleased to have met our full-year targets already and are optimistic about the runway for further growth. Sales of First Defense are recovering after setbacks related to contamination issues that created back orders. We estimate that First Defense captures about 34% of the spending on bovine scours biological products in the U.S., and we are very pleased with our premium positioning. Now, we have shifted our focus to managing yields to increase output. Ultimately, output drives our gross margin, given our relatively fixed manufacturing cost structure. So far in 2026, we have produced an average of about 435,000 manufacturing units per month. Manufacturing units are a standard measure we use to account for differences between Tri-Shield and Dual-Force products in our product line and are not the same as doses or revenue. Our average monthly output in 2026 has been 14% higher than 2025, and that compares to just 252,000 units per month in 2023 during the height of our contamination issues. Essentially, we now have a better understanding of our bottlenecks. We plan ahead to maximize their output.

We have changed our shift schedules to accommodate all these efforts, and we have made some changes to technology and equipment. While we look forward to bringing new equipment online for greater First Defense capacity by the end of 2027, we believe we can meet customer demand in the meantime with improved yield management. This quarter, we spent significant effort looking further back in our supply chain, specifically sourcing of colostrum and manufacture of the vaccines that we use to hyper-immunize the cows that give us the colostrum. We hired a team leader for our farm team, we developed a new value proposition for our source farms, and we are running trials to improve yields from the collected colostrum.
