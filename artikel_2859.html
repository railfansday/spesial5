Ravens release TE Nick Vannett, waive G Kyle Hergel

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Ravens parted ways with guard Andrew Vorhees in a trade with the Falcons on Tuesday and they also cut a couple of other players from the active roster.

They announced that they have released tight end Nick Vannett and guard Kyle Hergel. They did not make any corresponding signings, but they did add center Ryan Neuzil in the Vorhees trade.

Vannett played in the Ravens' last five games and made three starts. He was used primarily as a blocker during his time in Baltimore and did not catch any passes. The 11-year veteran has 108 catches for 1,012 yards and nine touchdowns over his entire career.

Hergel made one appearance for the Ravens and played two special teams snaps.
