Tanggal: 2026-11-05

That's six Lions defenders and nine total players with their status up in the air this week. It's far too early to declare any of these players definitively out this week (outside of Kerby Joseph—Dan Campbell already said he won't play this week—and Jack Campbell), but there's a pretty good likelihood the Lions will be missing at least a couple of these players.

My answer: It feels like the answer has to come on defense. With Campbell and Rolder out, there will certainly be a ton of extra pressure on the linebacker room. Derrick Barnes, Malcolm Rodriguez, and Trevor Nowaske are the most likely starters for Detroit this week, and we've seen a lot of ups and downs from this group. The good news is that they all have plenty of experience on defense. The bad news is that none of them bring the consistent play-making ability of Campbell, nor the leadership of Detroit's defensive captain.

But relief can also come from a different position. Covering the middle of the field has been a challenge, and it's not necessarily a strength of the current linebacking corps. This would be a huge week for the safeties to step up. While it's unclear if Branch will be available this week, I'm really looking for Chuck Clark and Keith Abney to elevate their games. Abney has been up and down, as you'd expect from a rookie who made a midseason shift from cornerback to safety. But I've been pretty disappointed with how Clark has played. He was supposed to bring communication and discipline to the position, but mental errors have plagued the back end (admittedly, not all his fault).

Malik Willis is not afraid to throw deep or utilize the middle of the field, so I'd love to see a clean game from the safeties to help out the ailing linebacker position.
