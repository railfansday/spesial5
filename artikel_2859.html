Game Discussion Thread: Knicks vs. Trail Blazers, November 21, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After three straight road victories, the Knicks (9-6) return to Madison Square Garden tonight to face the Portland Trail Blazers (10-5), one of the early surprises out west. Under new coach Micah Nori, the Blazers have won seven of their first 10 road games and average 117.5 points while surrendering 111.2. Damian Lillard is back in Portland after a year away with a torn Achilles, and the old gunslinger recently dropped 33 points on his former Milwaukee employer. He'll have plenty of help from Deni Avdija (24.2 points, 6.2 rebounds, 6.1 assists), should the forward's sore foot allow him to play (as of this writing, a game-time decision).

Portland also has 7-foot-2 Donovan Clingan patrolling the paint (10.7 rebounds, 2.2 blocks per game), while Jrue Holiday and Toumani Camara provide pesky perimeter defense. The Knicks have their own impressive big man, however: Karl-Anthony Towns has recorded a double-double in all 15 games this season. With Ja Morant and Shaedon Sharpe sidelined, and Avdija questionable, the good guys have a good chance to extend their winning streak to four games and start this five-game homestand on the right foot.

Tip-off is 7:30 pm EST on MSG and NBA TV. This is your game thread. This is Blazer's Edge. Please don't post large photos, GIFs, or links to illegal streams in the thread. Mind your manners. And go Knicks!
