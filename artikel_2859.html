Warriors’ latest embarrassment ties franchise mark team would like to forget

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Warriors have lost four straight games by 20 or more points, tying the longest such streak in franchise history which was set in 1997-98.

After Steph Curry suffered a right oblique abdominal tear on Nov. 11, the Warriors have been flailing.

Golden State Warriors' Steph Curry plays during the NBA season opener against the Los Angeles Lakers in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026. London Entertainment

They went from having won eight games in a row to dropping their last five contests, including a 38-point loss to the Spurs, a 21-point loss to the Pelicans, a 22-point loss to the Mavericks and a 28-point loss to the Grizzlies.

It's an unfortunate break for a Warriors team that was surging behind the 38-year-old Curry, who's averaging 27.8 points (50% shooting from the field, 45.7% from beyond the arc), 3.7 rebounds and 3.9 assists per game.

Curry is expected to be sidelined close to a month, per ESPN.

The Warriors have been decimated by injuries this season.

Jimmy Butler (torn right ACL) and Moses Moody (ruptured left patellar tendon) aren't expected to return until midseason. Kristaps Porzingis stepped away from basketball indefinitely while dealing with an undisclosed health matter. And Brandon Williams has missed five straight games because of a mild left hamstring strain.

Yaxel Lendeborg (1) of the Golden State Warriors handles the ball against the Memphis Grizzlies on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, at FedExForum in Memphis, Tenn. NBAE via Getty Images

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, who turned heads at the top of the season with an impressive Rookie of the Year campaign, has greatly struggled without Curry. Over the Warriors' last four games, he has averaged 13.7 points on 26.8% shooting.

"It's unfair to him to try to make him step into Steph's role 13 games into his career," Draymond Green told reporters Nov. 16. "So, depend on him like we need to, but lower what we're expecting out of him to allow him to then just flow into it. I think, right now, we're putting too much pressure on him, and it's tough to deal with in game 13, 14."
