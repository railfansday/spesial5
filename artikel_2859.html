2026 Austin Championship tee times, TV coverage, viewer's guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The PGA Tour returns to Texas for the first time in three years with a brand-new tournament that also doubles as the penultimate event of the FedEx Cup Fall. Nearly 80 percent of the World Wide Technology Championship field will make the trip to Austin and tee it up again this week, most notably recent winner Blades Brown.

The 19-year-old (and the youngest PGA Tour winner in the modern era) will lead the charge of phenoms at the Fazio Canyons Golf Course, with other young up-and-comers Miles Russell (recent three-time AJGA player of the year), Christiaan Maas and Tommy Morrison all in the field this week. The 6'9" Morrison won the Evans Scholars Invitational over the summer on the Korn Ferry Tour and has already earned his PGA Tour card for next year.

One of Texas' best golf resorts is the Omni Barton Creek, located just 25 minutes outside of Austin. The resort features four 18-hole designs, and the highest-ranked layout is the Fazio Canyons design, a former Golf Digest America's 100 Greatest Public winner. This signature Tom Fazio design, which offers scenic views of Austin's Hill Country, recently underwent an extensive renovation. View Course

Along with those youngins, Max Homa, Billy Horschel, Joel Dahmen, Matt Kuchar and Lucas Glover are among the notable names still looking to secure exempt status for next season by finishing inside the top 100 on the FedEx Cup points list.

This is the first Austin Championship presented by Compliance Solutions, so no matter what happens, we're going to soon have a 72-hole record at this event. We'll find out soon if Brown has another win in him before his 20th birthday.

This week's winner will receive a $1.08 million cut of the $6 million purse.

Thursday-Sunday: 2-5 p.m. EST, Golf Channel
