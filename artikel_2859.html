Reality star has cheeky response after revealing Clippers courtside photo goes viral

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The 3-8 Los Angeles Clippers aren't producing the best product on the court this season.

Therefore, a lot of the best entertainment to come from Clippers games isn't the actual basketball.

This was shown once again when a viral moment unfolded during the Clippers' 113-112 win over the Thunder on Friday, when a photo of a woman courtside reacting to a woman's revealing outfit spread online.

Kris Dunn of the LA Clippers handles the ball against the Oklahoma City Thunder in an NBA Cup game at Intuit Dome in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 13, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Photographer Wally Skalij posted the photo on Instagram with the caption, "Fans sit courtside to watch the Clippers/Thunder game as one walks back to her seat."

The woman in the photo is wearing a revealing outfit, and several of the people behind her appear to be looking at her backside as she walks back to her seat.

Photographer Wally Skalij's photo of a group of people sitting courtside at a Los Angeles Clippers game. (Instagram/@wallyskalij).

The photo was reposted on social media and went viral, with one X post amassing over six million views.

It turns out that the woman was Brittany Renner, who is a social media influencer and fitness model who has a son with Dallas Mavericks forward P.J. Washington.

Renner was also a cast member on VH1's "Basketball Wives" show from 2023 to 2025 and divorced rapper Kevin Gates in 2025 after 52 days of marriage.

"Basketball Wives" star Brittany Renner poses for a photo. (Instagram/@bundleofbrittany).

Renner responded to the courtside photo of her on X on Saturday, writing, "Who wears this to a game…"

The 34-year-old Renner is clearly being cheeky with this response, just as she was being cheeky at the game.
