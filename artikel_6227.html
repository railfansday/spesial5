End Of An Era: American Retires Boeing 777 Flagship First Class

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

American Airlines has just officially “retired” its Flagship First product on the Boeing 777-300ER, meaning that no US carrier offers an international first class product anymore. The good news is that an updated business class product is in the process of being rolled out, and that’s something that should benefit a lot of travelers.

As I’ve covered in the past, American is in the process of eliminating its “premium” first class product, and that change was just implemented for flights as of this week on the carrier’s biggest planes.

Specifically, the Boeing 777-300ER is American’s “flagship” aircraft, with 20 of the planes in the fleet. As of this week, the airline is no longer selling first class on these planes. Now, plenty of 777-300ERs with these cabins are still flying around, as American has just started reconfiguring its 777-300ERs with new cabins, including the new business class.

Still, for flights effective immediately, first class seats are just being sold as standard business class seats, and any business class passenger can select seats in the first two rows (you’d think they’d try to charge extra or at least reserve them for select elite members, but nope). So there will no longer be a differentiated soft product… not that there was much of a difference as of late anyway!

I expect we’ll continue to see these planes flying around well into late 2027, or possibly even into 2028, depending on how fast the retrofit project happens. American’s dedicated first class ground product has also largely been discontinued, as Flagship First Dining has turned into Flagship Dining, so a la carte lounge dining is now available to eligible business class passengers as well.

It’s great that these planes will be getting new business class suites, as they’ll also be the aircraft belonging to a US carrier with the most business class seats, as they’ll have 70 of them… that’s lot!

While American has now eliminated its international first class product, it’s worth emphasizing that the airline still technically has a “premium” first class, in the sense of a cabin above business class.

Specifically, American still has its specially configured Airbus A321T aircraft, which are used primarily for flights between New York (JFK) and Los Angeles (LAX) and San Francisco (SFO). The planes have 10 reverse herringbone seats spread across five rows, in a 1-1 configuration.

American also plans to eliminate this product, as the A321Ts will be reconfigured into standard A321 domestic configuration aircraft, and A321XLRs will be taking over the transcon routes.

American has stopped selling first class on these planes for flights as of March 28, 2027. After that date we’ll still see some of the planes flying with the cabins, but the first class seats will simply be sold as business class.

American Airlines has officially eliminated its international first class product, as Boeing 777-300ERs no longer have Flagship First for sale. These planes are being retrofitted, as first class seats are being eliminated, and new business class suites are being introduced. However, the planes will keep flying around with the current cabins for quite some time, and business class passengers can freely assign the first class seats.

In the spring, we’ll see American eliminate its last “premium” first class product, which is the A321T first class cabin, as those planes are replaced by A321XLRs.
