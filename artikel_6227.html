Brit Arrested After Flight Attendant Finds Hidden Camera In Airplane Bathroom, Now Faces Voyeurism And Cover-Up Charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A flight attendant discovered a hidden camera while using the lavatory on a Scoot flight from Japan to Singapore. A British passenger now faces charges of voyeurism and obstructing justice, with police alleging that he remotely activated the camera and later tried to erase evidence after realizing it had been found.

Hugh Lutley, a 52-year-old British national, was charged on November 5, 2026, following an incident aboard Scoot flight TR821 from Osaka (KIX) to Singapore (SIN) on October 7.

He faces one charge each of voyeurism and obstruction of justice. It appears Lutley intends to plead guilty, was offered bail of S$10,000, and has a pretrial conference scheduled for November 17 (which of course is not a conviction or a sentence).

The Singapore Police Force’s account describes an alleged attempt to record people inside an aircraft toilet. Police say the passenger concealed a camera in the lavatory, left, and activated its recording function through an application on his phone.

A cabin crew member subsequently used the lavatory and discovered the device. She removed it and immediately alerted a colleague. The passenger allegedly returned to retrieve the camera, found it missing, and deleted the application and related photographs from his phone in an attempt to avoid detection.

Police detained him when the flight arrived at Singapore Changi Airport and seized his phone and the camera.

Those are statutory maximums, but the alleged attempt to destroy evidence exposes him to a charge carrying a substantially longer maximum prison term than the underlying voyeurism charge.
