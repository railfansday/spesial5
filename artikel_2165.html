Ex-Rangers star signs for new club in second tier after weeks as free agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Former Rangers midfielder Greg Docherty has signed for Preston North End as a free agent.

The 30-year-old has been without a club since leaving Charlton Athletic by mutual agreement near the end of the summer transfer window, despite having captained them to promotion from League One a year before.

With the Addicks continually strengthening for the Championship, he found himself out of favour and it was agreed he could move on in search of first team football.

Former Motherwell boss Graham Alexander recently took over at Preston and was tipped in recent weeks to make a move for Docherty, after admitting he was looking at free agents. That has now come to fruition.

Preston announced the signing today and Docherty told their website: “I’m really proud to be here, to have this badge on my chest and to be part of a club that’s steeped in history. When I told my family, they were all excited and willing me to get it done.

“I’m just thankful that we managed to do it. Coming down from Scotland and playing in England, Preston’s a very good team to get to for my family and I’ve always kept an eye on them.

“I’ve always loved playing at Deepdale but haven’t won very often there, so hopefully that will change now. When I was an opposition player, I always found it really difficult. I’ve got family in the north of England and this move just really ticks all the boxes.

“To be a Preston player is something I’ve always wanted to do and I’m really proud to have finally done it.”

Docherty has been training with Alexander's side already and added: “From the moment I walked in here, I got a really good feeling from speaking to the manager and all the coaching staff.

“When I see how passionate the manager is about turning our current situation around, it excites me and I want to also help change things. I see real quality in the dressing room and I know we can improve if we keep working hard.

“The manager’s training demands are very high, and I’ve come in and really enjoyed it. I’ve enjoyed the soreness after it, I’ve enjoyed the tiredness after it, but I feel much better for it and all the lads do too.

“The boys are saying they’re getting fitter, which is massive in this league, and I just can’t wait to get going alongside them out on the pitch.”

Alexander said: “We’re really pleased to bring Greg in. He’s been a captain, won promotions and knows the game inside out.

“Greg’s vocal, fit and motivated, which are all the key things you look for in a professional footballer.

“He has settled into the group really quickly and has a real willingness to show what he can do.”
