Woman escorted from concert by security after ex-boyfriend drama with friend: ILoveCallie9 drama explained

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A woman has gone super viral on TikTok after finding out that her ex-boyfriend had been contacting her friend while the pair were at a concert together, ending in her being escorted out.

On November 14, TikToker Upperperla uploaded a clip of a woman kicking and slapping the back of seats before the start of a Treaty Oak Revival concert in Houston, Texas. The woman, known as ILoveCallie9 on TikTok, could be heard screaming at her friend as well.

In the short clip, which has gotten around 10 million views, the woman was eventually shown as being escorted away by security staff.

As the video was only 14 seconds long, plenty of viewers left comments, asking Upperperla whether or not she had a longer version. As it turned out, the TikToker did, with the extra six seconds of footage racking up close to 50 million views in just one day.

Upperperla followed that super-viral footage up with an explanation from her side of things, noting that Callie was “acting out” before the concert got underway.

“The girl messaged me saying like ‘hey, stop making TikToks out of me, my mom is going to call the cops, get in contact with people because I’m in high school,’ but she is eighteen,” the TikToker said.

“At any age, y’all shouldn’t be acting like that. No one should be acting like that after a certain age, not even my kids act that bad.”

Callie’s friend, J_Moneyy14, who accompanied her to the concert, also posted a TikTok about the situation, noting that the outburst happened after Callie saw that her ex-boyfriend had contacted her friend.
