6 EFL clubs who must eye £0 Wilfried Zaha transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Championship clubs will already have one eye on the January transfer window as they look to strengthen their squads, even if there is a lot of football to be played between now and the New Year.

Another problem facing clubs trying to do deals in January is the reluctance of rivals to sell players halfway through the season.

It’s obvious why that is the case, so deals in the winter window often come at a premium, which makes business harder to do.

However, one market clubs could explore is the free agent one, as there are still some talented individuals who are without a club.

Wilfried Zaha certainly falls into that category, with the Crystal Palace legend yet to return to the game since his Galatasaray contract expired in the summer.

The forward last played on loan at Charlotte FC in May, so he would take time to get up to speed, but, even at 34, you still get the feeling Zaha could contribute a lot.

The Ivory Coast international had been linked with a move to Lazio last month, but nothing materialised, and, given his age, he may want to get back playing in the UK, where he has spent most of his life.

With that in mind, we look at SIX EFL clubs who should consider a move for Zaha…

Wrexham have signed several big-names in their journey from non-league to the Championship, and Zaha could help them take that next step to the Premier League.

His profile would make this an intriguing signing, but, more importantly, he would beef up Phil Parkinson’s squad, which is lacking in the final third. The likes of Josh Windass, Nathan Broadhead and Sam Smith have suffered injuries, with the latter ruled out for the season.

So, in January, Wrexham are widely expected to bring in at least one attacker, but they could still benefit from Zaha as well, who could play in the three attacking roles in Parkinson’s XI, and he would provide the team with some much-needed pace and spark.

Birmingham are another ambitious club at this level, and they will feel that promotion to the Premier League is a very real possibility considering they sit top of the league right now.

But, the one concern for Chris Davies will centre on the squad depth, as the lack of quality behind their best XI is a worry. So, adding a player like Zaha would make a lot of sense, as he could provide quality cover down both flanks.

Given his pedigree, Zaha would back himself to force his way into the best XI, and his experience and know-how could be just what Blues need to get over the line this season.

As mentioned, Zaha has spent most of his life in London, so, he would surely be open to playing in the capital once again, and West Ham would be a good fit.

After a positive start, Manor Solomon’s form has dipped, and with Edvin Austbo and Jarrod Bowen also at the club, you could argue the Hammers are one winger short, so Zaha could add to the squad for this season at least.

This campaign is all about promotion for West Ham, so a short-term solution can’t be dismissed, and if Zaha helps the Irons back to the top flight, it would be the perfect outcome for all parties.

Following on from that, if Zaha wants a London club that offers a more realistic route to the first-team, then QPR could appeal, and they would benefit from the attacker in the squad.

Julien Stephan’s side will also fancy their chances of winning promotion, but there will be concerns if they have the squad and the experience to cope with the demands of the Championship.

So, Zaha would help on both fronts, and he is the sort of unpredictable, skilful player that the R’s fans enjoy watching.

If first-team football is the priority, then Zaha may have to lower his standards, but he would walk into many mid-table Championship sides, including Sheffield United.

Chris Wilder doesn’t have the biggest budget to work with at this level, so he will have his eye on free agents, and he would surely back himself to get Zaha back enjoying his football, which would make him a huge asset at this level.

The Blades are lacking players who have speed and can carry the ball, which is where Zaha excels, so he is the profile that could enhance the Yorkshire outfit as they seek a top eight finish.

Finally, Derby are in a desperate situation right now, so it’s perhaps unrealistic to think that they could attract a player like Zaha.

However, if the ex-Lyon man wants regular football, he knows he would get it at Pride Park, and, at this stage of his career, that may be something that Zaha decides to prioritise.

Derby are going to need to improve John Eustace’s squad when they get the chance, otherwise they will drop to League One. Whilst they must strengthen defensively, a player with pace and the ability to threaten on the counter is also imperative, which is what Zaha is all about.
