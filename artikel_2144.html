Southampton should target January move for ex-West Brom man as talk of a return is quashed: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Southampton had a mixed summer transfer window but one major blow was the sale of Taylor Harwood-Bellis. The Saints brought in multiple defenders in a bid to replace him and one of which was Zach Abbott who joined on loan.

Abbott has since struggled for gametime and fellow summer signing Keven Schlotterbeck has impressed but is now out injured. With that in mind, Tonda Eckert may may be hoping to bring in another centre-back during the January window.

Southampton are in an excellent position to push for automatic promotion this season, something that they cannot afford to take any risks with. With that in mind, they should be keeping an eye on Torbjorn Heggem’s situation.

The former West Brom man looks likely to leave Bologna this January, sparking talk of a return to the Baggies. However, while talk of a move to the Hawthorns has been quashed, a move to Southampton should be under consideration.

The 27-year-old has struggled for regular opportunities under Raffaele Palladino and has made it clear that he does not want to spend the remainder of the season sitting on the bench.

With the January transfer window approaching, the Saints could have an opportunity to bring an established international back to English football.

Heggem joined Bologna from West Brom in August 2025 after an impressive Championship campaign. He has since made 45 appearances for the Serie A side, but his involvement has decreased this season. That could leave Southampton well placed to explore a deal.

The Norwegian international has already demonstrated his ability to perform in the Championship. During the 2024/25 campaign, he made 45 league appearances for West Brom, scoring once and providing two assists. His consistency earned him both the Players’ Player of the Season and Supporters’ Player of the Season awards, underlining how highly regarded he was at The Hawthorns.

Heggem offers physical presence, aerial ability and impressive distribution. He is naturally a centre-back but can also operate at left-back, while his preference for his left foot makes him an especially useful defensive option.

His current Serie A statistics also demonstrate his technical qualities. Across seven appearances and 525 minutes this season, he has completed 93.4 per cent of his passes, recorded 43 line-breaking passes and made 27 clearances. He has also won 12 aerial duels and earned 25 caps for Norway.

Southampton would face competition for his signature, and his wages could prove a stumbling block. Nevertheless, he is approaching his prime, has proven himself in the Championship and possesses experience at a higher level.

With promotion ambitions potentially shaping their January business, Southampton should seriously consider swooping for Heggem. He could be an outstanding addition to their defensive options.
