'I've seen him crying' - Sam Clucas reveals Nathan Jones truth at Stoke City

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Charlton Athletic boss Nathan Jones has never been one to hide his true emotions, with the Welsh boss wearing his heart on his sleeve, as former Stoke City midfielder Sam Clucas has revealed exactly what he is like behind the scenes.

Nathan Jones has cultivated an excellent coaching career for himself, working his way up the ranks at Yeovil Town, Charlton, and Brighton & Hove Albion before taking the top job at Luton Town, guiding the Hatters from League Two to League One.

He'd leave Kenilworth Road for Stoke City in January 2019, with Luton sat 2nd in the third-tier table at the time and the Potters struggling in the Championship following relegation from the Premier League.

His time at the bet365 Stadium went far from planned, but not for lack of trying, as revealed by former midfielder Sam Clucas.

Clucas, 36, is now without a club as he enters the twilight of his playing career, but having worked his way up from the National League with Hereford United to Hull City in the Premier League, he joined Swansea City in the summer of 2017 for £16.5 million.

His time in South Wales didn't go as he had hoped, and 12 months later, he joined Stoke in the Championship for £6 million, initially under former Derby County boss Gary Rowett. Six months later, Jones was appointed as the Potters' new head coach, and Clucas has revealed exactly what he was like behind the scenes.

Speaking to The Football Lowdown podcast, Clucas said: "I loved him, to be honest with you.

"I think you'll see from a lot of his interviews that he's definitely a heart-on-his-sleeve type of guy, and believe me, that's not just for show.

"I've seen him crying in dressing rooms after games and stuff; a really emotional guy, but he puts his heart and soul into everything every day in training, his meetings, everything.

"I wanted it to work out so badly for him, to be honest with you, at Stoke, because you could see how much work he was putting in, and I believe he actually slept over at the training ground a few times."

Having succeeded Rowett at the helm in the Potteries, Jones would guide Stoke to 16th in the Championship that year, and over the summer the likes of Nick Powell, Jordan Cousins, Adam Davies, and Liam Lindsay joined under his watch.

However, the Welshman failed to get a tune out of his side, winning two of his first 14 league games in charge the following campaign and was swiftly dismissed.

With a win percentage of just 15.79 per cent, only Paul Lambert and Chris Kamara have a worse win percentage than Jones for Stoke, and of managers to have undertaken 20 games or more for the Potters, he is statistically the worst of all time.

Whenever he and Stoke meet, there is no love between the Welshman and the Potters' supporters, as despite his best efforts, he ultimately flopped in North Staffordshire.

Since leaving Stoke, Jones has returned to Luton, and followed that up with a brief spell with Southampton and now Charlton, with whom he has helped to establish in the Championship after winning promotion with the Addicks back in 2025.

Jones has tended to thrive as an underdog during his coaching career, and taking the Stoke job with the Potters just having been relegated from the Premier League, it was a disastrous fit for all parties.

The Welshman is certainly not the most popular of head coaches, though his heart-on-the-sleeve personality certainly makes for some box office moments, as he looks to steer Charlton up the second tier table this season.
