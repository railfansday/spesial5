Man City star signs - What Charlton Athletic’s perfect January transfer window looks like

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Charlton Athletic find themselves set for a hard-fought relegation battle down in the Championship and it could well be that good business done in the January transfer window is what saves them.

Having started the season with three successive victories and then a draw away at promotion challenging West Bromwich Albion at The Hawthorns, things looked as though they were on the up for Charlton.

Having survived the drop in their first season back in the second-tier last season, the expectation within the fanbase would have been to kick on this year and the start suggested that they will.

However, The Addicks then endured a ten-match winless run of form which only ended just before the international break with a 2-1 defeat of Sheffield United at The Valley.

Joint lowest scorers in the division, it is clear that Charlton need a spark in the final third and a general shift around in their squad and so we have taken a look at what would be an ideal winter window for the South Londoners.

A bit of a gamble but someone who could be a major surprise point of difference for Charlton in the second half of the season would be Younes Ibrahim, if they can get Brighton and Hove Albion to agree to loan out their starlet in the winter.

A mobile attacking midfielder will be a key addition for Charlton this winter with plenty of players having been tried but underwhelmed in that position – and Ibrahim, while very inexperienced, could be someone to provide a much-needed spark.

Brighton youngsters thriving in the EFL would not be a new thing and is perhaps a model for many to follow, with Amario Cozier-Duberry the best example on loan at Middlesbrough after his impressive stint with Bolton Wanderers last season.

The 18-year-old Egyptian is the latest prodigy coming through at the AMEX Stadium and an exciting first-team debut against Kauno Zalgiris in their UEFA Europa Conference League league phase opener, in which he earned an assist in a 4-0 victory over the Lithuanian champions, may well be a prompt for some EFL interest this winter.

A bit more known and recognisable to EFL followers and Charlton supporters but someone who is only who is even younger than Ibrahim at 17 would be winger Jeremy Monga.

Monga came through at Coventry City and then Leicester City before making his Premier League debut at the age of just 15 for The Foxes in the 2024-25 campaign before featuring on 27 occasions in the Championship last season.

He joined Manchester City over the summer before being loaned out to Swansea City but the sheer quality and depth of attacking talent that the promotion challenging Jacks have has limited Monga to being on the bench, and remaining an unused substitute, on just three occasions this season.

Monga is surely destined to have that loan stint cut-short but a Championship move still remains the likelier option for him and Charlton are crying out for some exciting wide talent and pace.

Moving away from the recruitment of young starlets to provide a spark in the final third, Charlton may well require a poacher in order to finish off whatever creativity is sought and produced.

As a result, they could do a lot worse than moving for Reading’s 32-year-old striker Jack Marriott, who hasn’t played Championship football since the 2021-22 campaign when he scored nine goals in 28 appearances for Peterborough United.

He has remained a consistent threat in the EFL, notching 16 in 24 League One games for The Royals last season before racing to nine across all competitions this season, albeit with injuries often disrupting him. With Jacob Brown and Jamie Reid now in Berkshire, though, it wouldn’t be a surprise to see Reading deem a sale, for any fee but surely a relatively low one given his age, acceptable.

Matty Godden has had critics this season, albeit he did score their winner against The Blades at the weekend, and finding an upgrade on his profile; an experienced finisher for the level could well be a priority for Charlton.

Zach Mitchell came through the Charlton academy setup in 2022 but the 21-year-old defender has only gone on to make 20 appearances across all competitions for the club with loan spells at Colchester United, Saint Johnstone twice and Hibernian in recent seasons.

Still young, it could well be that Mitchell moves away from The Valley on a temporary basis initially but out of contract next summer and without a squad number this season, his departure feels fairly inevitable at this stage.

Another defender who could well depart this winter would be Kenya international Collins Sichenje, who signed for Charlton from Serbian outfit Vojvodina in February.

The 23-year-old managed just six appearances in the second half of last season with just three starts. This time around, he came off the bench to defend 2-1 victories against Derby County and West Ham United in August but hasn’t been seen since in the league, with a couple of other substitute appearances in the EFL Cup and his departure would also feel quite likely.

Following their promotion in 2025, Charlton brought in a fair few attackers that had previously thrived at a lower level and many have failed to make too much of an impact and one of them is Harvey Knibbs.

The former Cambridge United and Reading man struggled to find form and consistency in the conservative approach and setup of Jones last season and he managed just two starts thus far this season in defeats against Middlesbrough and Bolton Wanderers. To free up space in attack and also for himself to get game-time, a move this winter would surely suit all parties.
