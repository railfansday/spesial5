Five goals, eight assists: Wolves loan flop hits electric form with UEFA Champions League club

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Wolves’ downfall has been widely credited to failed recruitment. In recent times, valuable assets like Matheus Cunha, Ruben Neves and Pedro Neto have been sold without suitable replacements coming in to keep the standards high at Molineux.

One player who struggled to fill gaps in Wanderers’ attack in the 2024/25 season was Carlos Forbs. The Old Gold brought him in on loan from Ajax, where he had struggled to hit the ground running after an exciting youth career at Manchester City.

Forbs’ time at Molineux did not go as planned. However, over the past 18 months or so, the Portuguese winger has hit flying form and is catching the eye in Europe.

At Wolves. Forbs played only 11 times across all competitions and contributed with one assist. He played just 16 minutes of Premier League football over the second half of his 2024/25 season and upon the return to Ajax, they decided to cash in.

Forbs was sold to Belgian side Club Brugge, and the 22-year-old has started to show why he was so highly rated in his youth. He performed well in his debut season with 11 goals and 12 assists in 51 games across all competitions and has taken things to another level this term.

In 15 appearances, Forbs has already notched 13 goal contributions with five goals and eight assists. He is starring in the Jupiler Pro League, but his success is really shown by his form in the UEFA Champions League.

The Wolves loan flop has two goals and three assists in four games in the prestigious competition. Club Brugge have won three games on the bounce in the Champions League, with Forbs setting up three goals in a 4-3 triumph away at Inter Milan before scoring in wins over RC Lens and PSV.

Forbs has been recalled to the Portugal first-team squad as a result of his surging stock.

Forbs’ fine youth career at Manchester City meant there was real excitement over his future. However, having struggled to make a telling impact at Ajax, it was perhaps a bit of an oversight to expect him to deliver in the Premier League at Wolves.

The Eredivisie is a strong league for player development, so the fact that Forbs struggled there suggested he had more progress to make before playing in the English top flight.

The winger has come on leaps and bounds in Belgium though. On his current form, it won’t be long before top clubs start to take notice.
