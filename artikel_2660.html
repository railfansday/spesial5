Aaron Glenn: We'll know more on Geno Smith's ankle this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Jets quarterback Geno Smith left Sunday's game with an ankle injury and head coach Aaron Glenn didn't have any update on his condition after their 31-12 loss to the Bills.

Glenn was asked about the nature of Smith's injury and Glenn said he didn't have any information about the severity or how long he might be out of action. Glenn said he expected to have more information during the week.

Glenn said the same was true for rookie wide receiver Omar Cooper. Cooper left the game after the first offensive possession.

Smith also underwent a concussion evaluation ahead of the second half and rookie Cade Klubnik opened the third quarter at quarterback. Smith returned for the team's next possession, but was done for the day after being injured during that drive.
