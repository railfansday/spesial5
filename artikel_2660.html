Victor Wembanyama named Western Conference Player of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

We've reached the point where a "normal" performance from Victor Wembanyama is something like a 28-point double-double with four blocks. For the second time this season and fifth time in his career, Wemby has been named Western Conference Player of the Week. He joins Tim Duncan, David Robinson and Tony Parker as the only Spurs to win it five or more times.

Wemby's biggest perforamnce of the week came in Phoenix, where he recorded 40 points, 11 rebounds and eight blocks — all in just 25 minutes of action — in a 122-96 win that even had the opposing crowd (one that is usually very anti-Spurs) cheering him on. He joined Wilt Chamberlain as the only the second player in NBA history to record multiple games in a season with at least 40 points, 10 rebounds and 8 blocks. He also had 32 points, 9 rebounds and 5 blocks in the Spurs come-from-behind 124-119 win against the Bulls.

It was the Week of the Freaks, as he is joined by Giannis Antetokounmpo as Eastern Conference Player of the Week, who helped lead the Miami Heat to a 3-0 week while averaging 33.7 points, 11.3 rebounds, 4.7 assists, and 1.3 blocks.
