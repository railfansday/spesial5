Mike Vrabel: No timetable for Drake Maye, he won't be available this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Word late last week was that Patriots quarterback Drake Maye fractured his fibula and that he could miss the rest of the season, but head coach Mike Vrabel isn’t looking that far ahead.

The Patriots had a bye in Week 11, so there was no chance for head coach Mike Vrabel to discuss Maye’s diagnosis in the days after he was injured against the Lions. On Monday, Vrabel made an appearance on WEEI and he was asked how knowing the extent of the injury affects the team’s preparation for the rest of the campaign.

“Won’t be available, so I don’t think it necessarily matters,” Vrabel said. “We’ll see how he progresses and how long that takes. I don’t think there’s any timetable right now.”

Vrabel repeated that Maye “won’t be available this week” when asked a followup about whether he believes it will be a short term absence. Tommy DeVito is in line to start with Behren Morton as the backup and Vrabel did not indicate that a move to add another quarterback is imminent.
