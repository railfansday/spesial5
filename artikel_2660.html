Bucks’ Tyler Herro faces uncertain future after Taylor Jenkins’ fourth quarter benching against the Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tyler Herro struggled to find his rhythm in the Milwaukee Bucks' 113-106 defeat to the Atlanta Hawks on Sunday, prompting head coach Taylor Jenkins to keep him out for the entire fourth quarter. The 26-year-old's offensive struggles raised questions about his place in the rotation as the team looks ahead to its next games.

The Bucks guard managed only five points in 20 minutes, converting two-of-13 field-goal attempts and missing all five shots from beyond the arc. He added three assists but also gave away possession five times. With his offense struggling, Jenkins turned to Kasparas Jakucionis and Gary Trent Jr. for more minutes after halftime, while AJ Green also saw an expanded role.

Milwaukee is now looking for a response from Herro after his early-season momentum faded. He has struggled to find his shooting touch, connecting on just 37.8% of his attempts while averaging 18.4 points across his past nine outings. Given the heavy workload Milwaukee has maintained for him, his recent efficiency has become an important concern for the coaching staff.

The Bucks' struggles have continued, with Milwaukee dropping its 10th straight game and falling to an NBA-worst 1-13 record after letting a 22-point lead slip away against Atlanta. Taylor Jenkins has already shuffled his rotations in an effort to turn things around, and taking Herro out of the starting lineup would mark another significant change.

Herro joined Milwaukee in June through the trade involving Giannis Antetokounmpo, with Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware and other draft assets also included in the deal.

However, his recent struggles have put his starting role in question, especially with Ryan Rollins potentially returning after an illness. Jenkins has not revealed whether he plans to make another lineup adjustment, but Herro's usual minutes may no longer be assured.
