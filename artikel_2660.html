SCORIGAMI: The NFL’s list of unique scores has added more in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

There had been 1095 unique final scores in NFL history as of the conclusion of the 2025 season. We are now up to 1102 with the ones added during 2026.

With teams scoring more points in games, the extra point moving out to a 33-yard kick, and teams deciding to go for two more often to gain edges, never-before-seen final scores are still happening despite the NFL playing for more than 100 years. These unique scores are also called "Scorigami".

Many of the Scorigami we are seeing are for 40-plus point games that have high scoring from both teams, but we've also been seeing scores in the 30s that have never happened. It's not just high scores that have never happened, though. A game has never finished in a 2-2 tie, for instance. The NFL didn't allow two-point conversions until 1994, and we've never seen an 8-7 game. A game has never ended in a 9-9 tie, among other scores still out there.

Since 1992, there have been three or more Scoigamis in every season. The average since the start of the 2000 season is seven per year with the high of 14 in 2013 and a low of three in 2022 and 2009.

The 2026 NFL season didn't wait to get the Scorigami train rolling. Two NFC North teams won games on the Sunday of opening weekend with scores that had never happened before. They were the 1096th and 1097th unique scores in NFL history.

Normally when we get a Scorigami, it's because something weird happened in the game. This one wasn't that. The Bears just laid the smack down. Chicago scored 14 points in the first, third, and fourth quarters along with a 17-point third quarter. All of those are regular scores, there were just a lot of them. We had a late extra point blocked, but 59-38 also has never happened.

The Packers missed an extra point in the first quarter, but that didn't trigger a whole lot of unique scores on its own. Everything stayed pretty normal until the fourth quarter when Minnesota scored to take the lead. They converted a 2-point conversion to go up 25-22. Scoring 25 points in an NFL game is weird. Then Minnesota added two more 7-point touchdowns on top of it. A 39-point day is pretty rare. (A 32-22 score has also never happened.)

After a two-point conversion made the score a very normal 21-21, things got weird. A Bengals safety made it 23-21. The Bengals couldn't get a two-point conversion on a later touchdown after the Titans scored, making it 29-28. Then the Titans got a two-point on their next score to make it a seven-point lead at 36-29. The Bengals scored and kicked an extra point and the game sat at 36-36 at the end of regulation. It should be noted that an overtime touchdown would have won the game without an extra point before this season and 42-36 has happened before.

The Cowboys didn't get the extra point after scoring a touchdown, which made it 16-10 in the second quarter. In the fourth quarter, the score was 38-19 when the Cowboys got in the end zone and tried to make it an 11-point game but failed at 38-25. That's when things got weirder. Dallas made it 38-32 then the 49ers scored a long touchdown with a 2-point conversion to get the to the very strange 46 points. 46-32 was a Scorigami, as was 46-39, but the Cowboys went for 2 after their last TD and got it to 46-40. Also a Scorigami.

For the second time on the same day, we saw a Scorigami. And it was the Bengals' second in three weeks. With the Bengals up 31-2, the Steelers scored and went for two to make it 31-11. it's weird because it's still a three-touchdown game and doesn't really help him close the gap but whatever. While 31-11 would have been Scorigami, both teams scored again to make it 38-18. The only 38-point Scorigami winners left now are 38-2 and 38-4.

For the second straight week, the Dallas Cowboys were part of a Scorigami and the Titans get on the board for the second time in 2026. Dallas seems to have the formula — score a ton of points. There are a ton of 51-point games that have not hit in the history of the NFL. Down 51-23, the Titans scored with 31 seconds left. 51-23 and 51-29 have both happened before as has 51-31. The Titans kicking the extra point to make is 51-30 was the difference.

The 27 points is normal, but 5 points is not. A safety and a field goal were all the Broncos could muster on Black Friday. A lot of other scores have happened against 5 points, but not 27. When the Steelers added a fourth-quarter field goal for the only points of the second half, it moved from 24-5 (which has happened once before) to 27-5, which has never happened. Scorigami.

After seven Scorigamis in 2024, there were four NFL games to hit new finals in 2025, two including the high-scoring Buffalo Bills. here is how each of them got there in chronological order:

This game features two high-powered offenses at the height of their powers, but the Bills started going for two in the third quarter and missed on consecutive attempts. Halfway through the fourth quarter, they sat at 25 points, a rare number. A missed extra point by the Ravens put Baltimore at 40 — not an entirely uncommon number on its own — before the Bills' final push. Buffalo made it 40-38 after two touchdowns and an extra point but failed to convert on the game-tying two-point conversion. They got the ball back and kicked a field goal as time expired to win 41-40.

There are several ties still available on this list, but not as many as you would think. The NFL didn't have sudden death overtime until 1974 when they added a 15-minute frame, so there were a bunch of ties prior to 1974 but then a lot fewer. They modified the rule in 2010 so it wasn't sudden death for a first-drive field goal then shortened the time to 10 minutes in 2017. In 2025, they gave every team a possession. With those recent changes, ties can be more common.

Those rule changes directly impacted this game between the Dallas Cowboys and Green Bay Packers. Dallas didn't win after their first-possession field goal because Green Bay was guaranteed a possession. The Packers only had 4:40 on the clock and drove deep into Dallas territory. After shots to the end zone failed, they settled for a 40-40 tie as time expired.

In the third quarter, the Jaguars were up 23-10 and scored another touchdown. Up 19, they knew the Texans had to score three times to win so they tried to make it a 21-point lead to make it three touchdowns to tie or win. That play failed, and they sat at 29 points with a 19-point lead.

The Texans then scored a touchdown plus a two-point conversion to make it an 11-point game. Another touchdown with a failed two-point conversion made it a 5-point game. Houston scored again to go up 30-29 and tried another two-point conversion to ensure a late field goal wouldn't beat them, but that try failed with 31 seconds on the clock.

Here's where things get nuts. A 30-29 score has happened before. But the Texans returned a fumble for a touchdown to make it 36-29 as time expired. A final of 37-29 and 38-29 has happened, but because there was no time on the clock, there was no extra point attempt, and the final stood at Scorigami.

For Buffalo's second Scorigami of the year, they also topped 40 points. The Buccaneers started the shenanigans in the third quarter when they scored a touchdown to make it 26-21. They went for two to try and make it 28-21 but the conversion failed. Buffalo got to 31 the old-fashioned way, with four touchdowns and PATs plus a field goal, and the score was 31-26. After another touchdown, the Bucs tried to make it a three-point lead with another two-point conversion that failed, sticking them at 32 points. Buffalo scored another touchdown to make it 37-32 and failed the two-point conversion to try and make it a seven-point game. The Bills' final touchdown game them a 43-32 lead and Buffalo pushed it to 44-32 with an extra point to make it a two-touchdown lead with 2:35 left.
