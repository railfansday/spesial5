Aaron Jones accounts for Minnesota's lone touchdown on SNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Minnesota Vikings RB Aaron Jones carried the ball eight times for 30 yards and a touchdown in Week 11 versus the San Francisco 49ers. He added a 13-yard catch on his lone target.

Why It Matters: Jones was one of the few Vikings that brought the juice on Sunday night, trucking a 49ers defender on his way into the end zone for what turned out to be the team's only touchdown. It was the veteran's second straight game with a score, which is good, but it was also the fifth consecutive week that he has rushed for fewer than 50 yards, which is bad. With the team leaning more on the passing game, and Jones splitting snaps with Jordan Mason, the 31-year-old back should be considered an RB3/flex candidate.
