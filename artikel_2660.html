Mexico City altitude will affect the kicking game on Sunday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

When the Vikings and 49ers face off in Mexico City on Sunday night, they’ll be playing at the highest elevation any NFL game is ever played at: Estadio Azteca’s altitude is around 7,300 feet. And that could have an impact on the kicking game.

Kickers and physicists alike have agreed that high altitudes can easily add five yards or more to field goal range. The air is thinner at altitude, and in Mexico City the altitude makes the air about 20 percent less dense than at sea level. In Denver, which is about 2,000 feet lower than Mexico City, the air is about 15 percent less dense than at sea level, and Denver’s Mile High Stadium is well known for the boost it gives to kickers.

Vikings kicker Will Reichard has a career-long field goal of 62 yards, and 49ers kicker Eddy Pineiro’s career long is 59 yards. Both kickers made their career longs at sea level, and they both should feel confident if called upon to attempt a kick from at least a few yards beyond 60 yards in Sunday night.

Kickoff strategy may also be a factor at altitude. Only 16 percent of all kickoffs in the NFL this season have gone for touchbacks, as teams believe it’s better to try to pin their opponents deep on their own end of the field than boot the ball into the end zone. In Mexico City, it may be hard for a kicker to avoid kicking the ball into the end zone — or kickers might be able to get a higher trajectory on their kicks and make it even easier to pin their opponents deep.

And punts will travel several yards farther, which could be especially exciting for Vikings return man Myles Price, the NFL’s top punt returner, who might be able to reach top speed before the coverage can get to him if 49ers punter Corliss Waitman outkicks his coverage.

There’s a lot of talk before games at altitude about how teams should acclimate so their players don’t get out of breath in the thinner air. But that’s not the only part of the game that players will need to adjust to on Sunday night. The kicking game will be significantly impacted.
