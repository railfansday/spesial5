Jaguars Reacts Survey: Grade Jacksonville’s 2026 Trade Deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Jaguars fans and fans across the country.

Jessie Bates, Davon Godchaux, and Jordan Davis are now on the team.

Godchaux and Davis will help replace DaVon Hamilton (quad), while Bates will help replace Eric Murray (elbow).

These moves were about pushing the chips into the middle of the table — not just for this season, but also for future seasons. Davis is entering his prime on a relatively cheap contract; Bates is still in his prime and will likely be extended before he hits free agency in 2027.

The Jaguars certainly paid a pretty penny to make these moves, and they still don't have a dominant No. 3 pass rusher like so many fans have long wanted. But hey. The fact that they're making moves is pretty darn cool.
