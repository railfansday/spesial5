With the season on the line, should Patriots sign QB Aaron Rodgers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The New England Patriots have every reason to dampen the effect of Drake Maye being ruled out for at least the next six weeks with a fractured fibula by signing Aaron Rodgers in free agency after he was released by the Pittsburgh Steelers

It's a proposed move that would make perfect sense for the Patriots amid their 5-5 start to the year.

According to The Ringer's Tom Pelissero, Rodgers cleared waivers on Thursday after going unclaimed, making him a free agent who the Patriots now have a wide open window to strike a deal with.

The shocking release comes directly on the heels of Rodgers voicing his displeasure with the increasingly unsettling situation that brewed in Pittsburgh leading up to his recent benching on Wednesday's episode of "The Pat McAfee Show."

Rodgers sounded off on Steelers coach Mike McCarthy, expressing frustration about learning of his benching from teammate Pat Freiermuth as opposed to McCarthy. He also accused his head coach of falling asleep and skipping meetings.

With the Patriots' season suddenly taking an unexpected turn with Maye sidelined, a potential opportunity to land Rodgers could significantly impact New England's playoff hopes. Tommy DeVito is set to take over as New England's starting quarterback, but the chance to sign Rodgers has the potential to ensure that the team's playoff hopes remain alive. New England is currently the No. 8 seed in the AFC behind the Steelers (6-4), and with Rodgers in town, they might be able to overtake Pittsburgh and Cleveland (6-4) in short order.

Rodgers makes sense on too many fronts. The four-time AP MVP and former Super Bowl champion has recorded 2,241 passing yards, 16 touchdowns and six interceptions this season. He has still played like a quarterback who is capable of leading a team to a Super Bowl this season, even despite his down play in the last few weeks and blowback being heard in NFL circles about the potential for the Patriots to sign him.

With the all-time great resume that Rodgers has, coupled with the capabilities that he still has, he himself might be able to carry New England to a Super Bowl win this year. The Patriots have a veteran receiving corps that is conducive for Rodgers to work with. The unit is expecting Romeo Doubs back soon, and they might also get A.J. Brown back before the end of the season, not to mention a strong tight end unit with veteran Hunter Henry and promising rookie Eli Raridon.

When it comes to DeVito, he has the ability to be dynamic as a dual-threat quarterback. Nevertheless, completing only 50 percent of his passes this season with 132 yards, one touchdown and one interception, along with limited experience starting in the league, the Patriots would find much more success going with Rodgers over him.

The Patriots' season has a chance to be salvaged, and a move for Rodgers would be the best way to ensure that they make the playoffs.
