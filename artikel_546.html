Marvel's heroes and villains are stepping into the world of Magic: The Gathering.

The Marvel Super Heroes Play Booster Box brings iconic characters from the Marvel Universe into the collectible card game, giving fans the chance to build their dream team and discover their favorite characters in card form. And as of Nov. 4, you can pick up this booster box for less, with a $79.81 saving on the usual list price of $209.70 at Amazon.

This Booster Box lets you build a team of iconic heroes and villains, equip them with recognizable gear, and unleash their abilities. Each booster contains 14 cards, including at least one Traditional Foil card and one to four cards of Rare rarity or higher, with the chance to find special borderless alternate-art designs.
