How Long Can Jensen Huang and Nvidia Defy Gravity?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

His reinstatement at Trump’s urging is a perilous high-wire act.

Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang speaks at the annual Consumer Electronics Show in Las Vegas, Nev., January 6, 2026.

It’s foolish to be astonished by anything anymore. Still, Nvidia’s reinstatement of Jensen Huang as president and chief executive officer, just eleven days after his resignation and six days after the board of directors said it had no intention to reinstate him, is surprising, to say the least.

The Wall Street Journal reports Nvidia’s statement that the board had “received input from senior levels of the United States government” and is “confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”
