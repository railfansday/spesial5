As of this writing, Amazon and Walmart have the lowest price on the Apple Watch SE 3, so take your pick.

In looking for the best Black Friday Apple Watch deals, we found great savings on both the Apple Watch Ultra 3 and Ultra 4. Apple's Ultra watches are designed for rugged outdoor use and performance athletes. They have astounding battery life, lasting up to three days in low-power mode. They also offer both GPS and cellular connectivity.

The older Apple Watch Ultra 3, usually $799, is now $250 off at Best Buy, and we've never seen this previous-gen smartwatch priced so low. To be honest, the Ultra 3 offers a lot of the same features thanks to the recent watchOS 27 update, but if you want the more future-proofed option, then opt for the latest and greatest version, i.e. the Ultra 4.
