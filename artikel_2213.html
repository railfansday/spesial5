Wigan Athletic vs Barnsley predictions as ‘decisive’ game awaits League One strugglers

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Wigan Athletic appear to have found their feet following a turbulent start to the campaign, thanks to a morale-boosting 3-2 victory over fellow strugglers Bromley before a stalemate with Mansfield Town last time out.

After comfortably progressing past National League table-toppers Boreham Wood in the FA Cup, Gary Caldwell – who has been linked with the vacant post at Aberdeen this week – is finally seeing the fruits of his labour.

Barnsley meanwhile will be aiming to end their eight-game winless run in the league against Wigan Athletic this weekend. In their last League One game, a share of the spoils in a 1-1 draw with high-flying Oxford United showed their character after Jack Shepherd’s early dismissal, but their next test will be a true measure of whether they have the credentials to retain their third-tier status.

The Tykes navigated a tricky first hurdle in the FA Cup, teaching Aldershot Town a lesson in clinical finishing with an emphatic 7-2 win. It was the remedy Barnsley were after, with the triumph marking their first home win since the start of September, and they will be desperate to build upon that result in Greater Manchester.

“Finding the back of the net has been Wigan Athletic problem so far this term and Scott Hogan’s injury against Mansfield Town has only intensified their attacking woes. However, the Latics have picked up of late and they seem to have turned a corner after a tough start to the campaign.

“Barnsley haven’t been happy travellers so far this term, picking up a solitary win away from Oakwell, but will fancy their chances against a fellow side tipped for the drop.

“Key to a vital three points will be Vimal Yoganathan, who has netted three goals in his last four outings. But, Daniel Stendel may opt to take a more defensive approach after it proved effective against Oxford United last time out in the league.

“With both sides misfiring in attack and leaking goals at the back, it is difficult to envisage anything other than a closely-contested affair. I’ll be predicting a draw here.”

“Based on what we’ve seen from Wigan Athletic and Barnsley this season, this game could end up being a decisive one in the League One relegation fight further down the line.

“The Latics have had a couple of better results of late, beating Bromley and getting a point against a good Mansfield Town side. There is more to get from this group with some talented players like Jensen Weir and Connor Barrett on the books, and it will be hoped that Christian Saydee’s return to fitness can provide the goals needed to push away from danger.

“As for Barnsley, you have to be worried about them. We’ve known for a while that the problems run deep at Oakwell, but they are all-at-sea at the back and big names like Reyes Cleary are not delivering.

“Although neither have fared well this term, I am leaning towards a home win. That will leave the Tykes in big trouble.”
