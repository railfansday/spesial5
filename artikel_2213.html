Positive Sheffield Wednesday injury update emerges with 75 mins per goal star fit for Barnsley clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sheffield Wednesday are back in action on Friday as they face Yorkshire rivals Barnsley at Oakwell. Dave Challinor will be eager to build on his first SWFC win against Plymouth Argyle, and he’ll be helped by an improving injury picture at Hillsborough.

Speaking to the press ahead of Friday’s game, Challinor revealed that star forward Louie Barry is fit for the clash with the Tykes after making his return off the bench against Plymouth. The 23-year-old has come through another week of training scot-free, but the boss remains wary that he needs to be managed carefully after his long-term ankle injury.

Elsewhere, Ashley Maynard-Brewer and Ali Al-Hamadi have trained with no issues, and internationals Kieran Morrison, Jordi Liongola, Sean Fusire and Will Grainger returned with no injuries. With no new blows, the Owls only remain without Max Lowe, Reece James, Mason Burstow and Tyler Onyango.

Sheffield Wednesday have lacked a killer instinct in attack at times this season. The failure to convert control into goals played a part in Henrik Pedersen’s downfall, so it is down to Challinor to right the ship.

The 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle showed big attacking improvements, and their frontline only grows stronger with Barry’s return. He is a proven star at League One level and has already shown SWFC fans how potent he can be in his limited time on the pitch since joining in the summer.

Barry’s comeback will give Challinor a welcome but tricky selection dilemma to resolve. The former Aston Villa and Barcelona prospect can play at striker or on the left-hand side, and there is strong competition for both of these positions.

Davis Keillor-Dunn‘s form has seen him solidify a place in the team, while it was Callum Slattery and Barry Bannan who played in the most advanced roles behind the number nine against Plymouth Argyle.

If Challinor is to play with a three-man frontline, Barry may be perfect for the place on the left. However, the Owls boss will have to work out who drops from the team to facilitate his inclusion.

There is an understandable eagerness to see Barry back in the first XI. He is one of League One’s standout players and has the potential to be a real star in an exciting chapter at Sheffield Wednesday.

However, Challinor is right in saying that he can only play such a role if they are smart with his return. If they rush him into too much too soon, he will be at risk of re-injury or separate setbacks.

By managing his minutes over the coming weeks, SWFC give themselves the best chance of having Barry fit and available for longer. He will be a key influence in Challinor’s attack, but only if they’re careful in the early days of his comeback.
