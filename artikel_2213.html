Tributes paid after death of Tenerife striker following on-field collision

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tenerife player Jesus De Miguel has died after an on-field collision in a match on Monday, his club has announced.

The collision occurred as Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono raced from his goal to challenge De Miguel for the ball in stoppage time of a Spanish second-tier game.

The Canary Islands club said in a statement on Tuesday: “Racked with pain, with a lump in our throats and unable to believe that this is true, from CD Tenerife we communicate the tragic passing, this Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 8:00 a.m., of Jesus de Miguel Alameda.

“There are no words of comfort; there is absolutely nothing that can alleviate this feeling overflowing with sadness.

Late on Monday night Tenerife issued a statement confirming De Miguel had been admitted to hospital and was initially described as “stable despite the severity of his condition”.

An update in the early hours of Tuesday morning said his condition had “worsened neurologically”.

Owono was immediately sent off after he charged out of his goal to challenge De Miguel in the centre circle, his knee appearing to connect with the Tenerife player’s head.

Andorra and Tenerife players beckoned on medical staff immediately and appeared visibly upset by De Miguel’s condition.

The match was suspended after the incident.
