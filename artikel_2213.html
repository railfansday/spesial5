Jermain Defoe in line for coaching role with 46-year-old set to become Bromley boss

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bromley parted ways with Andy Woodman last month, in a controversial dismissal after five successful years in charge. Woodman had taken the side from the National League to League One, however, they have struggled this season and are bottom of the third tier table.

Byron Webster has been in charge on an interim basis. Now though, reporter Jason Burt states that Karl Robinson is set to join as the Ravens’ new manager.

Robinson has been without a job since June, following his departure from Salford City. He led the side to the League Two play-off final last season, losing out on promotion to Notts County.

Robinson, 46, is set to bring an assistant of his own with him to Hayes Lane, as Burt adds Jermain Defoe is poised to join alongside the new boss. He will serve as no.2 to the experienced manager following a maiden managerial stint of his own at Woking.

At the time of his first managerial appointment, Robinson was aged just 29 with MK Dons – the youngest boss in the EFL at the time. He spent six years with the Dons and has since managed Salford City, Charlton Athletic and Oxford United.

At each of his sides, aside from Charlton Athletic, Robinson has managed over 130 games. He averaged over 1.5 points per game at all of Salford, Oxford and MK, with his Addicks stint being the only exception again at 1.36 PPG.

Robinson often operates with a 3-4-1-2, but his vast experience has seen him switch systems and utilise four-man defences. He likes his sides to have high possession, with wide overloads, aggressive pressing and fluid midfield rotations.

A past shift to a 5-2-1-2 could be repeated at Bromley to provide them with more defensive solidity in their bid to secure League One survival.

In his time as a manager, Robinson has gained most of his experience in League One. He has won 796 points in the division, including 222 wins (Transfermarkt).

Defoe’s experience is more limited, but he is a budding coach determined to forge his way in the dugout after a short spell in charge of Woking. The former England striker has had roles at Tottenham Hotspur and Rangers alongside his stint with the Cardinals, and his experience as a top player could mean he has valuable nuggets of advice to offer to the players.

Bromley have mainly played in a 4-1-4-1 system this season, and it was regularly used last term alongside a 3-4-2-1. They may have to adjust to new demands under Robinson.

Bromley do not have a league fixture until November 21st now, but will hope Robinson can make an impression in cup matches against Rotherham United and MK Dons as he gets to work at Hayes Lane.

Robinson has the experience and tactical nous to bring success at Bromley, but time will tell whether the squad is truly strong enough to achieve such a feat after their quick ascent to League One.
