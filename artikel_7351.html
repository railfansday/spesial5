YouTube enables custom Shorts thumbnails

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

All users can now add custom thumbnails for their Shorts clips.

After initially telling creators that Shorts thumbnails were not particularly important for discovery, YouTube has reversed course and has now enabled Shorts thumbnail customization options for all creators.

As shown in this example screen, which was posted by YouTube CEO Neal Mohan on X, creators can upload a custom thumbnail image in YouTube Studio’s Shorts flow.

YouTube’s head of editorial Rene Ritchie explained that creators on mobile or desktop can upload a thumbnail image at the upload stage, while they can also change the video thumbnail after publishing.

“If you don’t want a custom image for your thumbnail, you can pick from a suggested frame from the Short to use as the thumbnail,” Ritchie added.

Ritchie also said creators can use YouTube’s artificial intelligence-powered Ask Studio assistant to brainstorm thumbnail ideas.

Shorts thumbnails were initially made available to creators in the YouTube Partner Program in June, with YouTube then gradually expanding access to the option over time.

Many creators have been asking for Shorts thumbnails, but initially YouTube wasn’t planning to add them at all.

In 2024, YouTube said that Shorts thumbnails were not as important as regular video thumbnail images because most users come across Shorts clips when they’re scrolling through full-screen video displays, as opposed to tapping on a smaller thumbnail to activate a Shorts clip.

Eventually, YouTube added in Shorts thumbnail customization anyway, and this latest update is the final step in enabling Shorts thumbnail creation.
