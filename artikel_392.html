Weighing just 135 grams, the DJI Neo is designed to make aerial photography a little easier. You don't even need a remote controller to get started. It can take off and land directly from your palm, and you can control it using the DJI Fly app or voice commands. There's also the option to pair it with a controller if you'd prefer.

There are plenty of clever features packed into its tiny design, too, including Subject Tracking that lets the drone follow you around automatically. It also has eight QuickShots modes that help you capture cinematic footage without needing to master complex flying techniques.

It shoots in 4K UHD, with DJI stabilization to help keep your videos looking smooth. Level-4 wind resistance and full-coverage propeller guards also help make things easier for beginners.
