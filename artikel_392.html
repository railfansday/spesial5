Boxed copies of GTA 6 are already out in the wild. Heres whats inside.

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Grand Theft Auto VI doesn't launch until Nov. 19, but someone appears to have gotten their hands on a boxed PS5 copy more than a week early.

The unboxing video, posted by GTA 6 Intel — a fan account on X who claims to be "The largest GTA 6 fan community" — racked up over 4 million views in 24 hours. It shows the person carefully opening the case to reveal a download code for the game and a folded, double-sided poster.

Rockstar said back in June that the boxed edition would come with a download code versus a physical disc, but knowing that in advance didn't make the empty holder any less comical. "Making a disc slot for no disc is diabolical," one X user wrote in the replies. Another wrote, "#NoDiscNoBuy," a popular hashtag some gamers are using to push back against games going digital-only.

Their frustration goes beyond just GTA 6 — Sony says it will stop producing discs for new PlayStation games altogether starting in January 2028. As for the poster, it’s double-sided, with the game's cover art on one side and a full illustration of the woman on the cover art on the other. German retailer MediaMarkt had shown off the artwork in an Instagram post five days ago, along with some other GTA 6 fan pages, so for die-hard fans, this isn't exactly news.

And if the sight of folded paper in a GTA case made you think of the maps that came with earlier games, that's a separate item this time (much to the dismay of some gamers online). Rockstar will release a free printable map of Leonida on launch day, then sell a physical version with the map on one side and the cover art on the other for 99 cents, or throw it in with another purchase from the Rockstar Store.

As for the code, Rockstar expects boxed copies to reach retailers starting Nov. 12, and anyone who gets theirs early can redeem it and start downloading right away.
