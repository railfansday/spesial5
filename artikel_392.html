New iPhone 20 Pro rumors: Titanium frame, ceramic parts in a limited edition model

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

When it comes to the iPhone Pro's frame, Apple has historically gone back and forth between aluminum and titanium.

The iPhone 15 Pro models, introduced in 2023, as well as iPhone 16 Pro, had a titanium frame, but Apple went back to aluminum unibody in the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro. However, the iPhone Air and foldable iPhone Duo still have titanium frames, likely due to the material's lighter weight and better structural strength when compared to aluminum.

The company also uses ceramic parts on some of its devices, notably the Apple Watch Ultra and some variants of the Apple Watch Series 12.

Now, two rumors claim Apple is going to use both these materials in the upcoming iPhone 20 Pro. A leaker called Fixed Focus Digital (via MacRumors) claims that Apple plans to manufacture parts of the phone's mid-frame using 3D-printed titanium. This does not necessarily mean the entire frame will be made of titanium, but it could improve the frame's strength and durability. Separately, the same leaker said on Weibo that he obtained ceramic components for Apple's long-rumored anniversary iPhone, which should launch next year alongside the regular iPhone 20 Pro. Said components will be produced in very low capacity, he said, implying that they'll only be used in a limited edition release.

The nature of the ceramic parts in question is unclear, so once again it's hard to say whether the phone's frame will be ceramic-made, or will the material only be used for a smaller part.
