Here’s what happened in crypto today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Need to know what happened in crypto today? Here is the latest news on daily trends and events impacting Bitcoin price, blockchain, DeFi, Web3 and crypto regulation.

Today in crypto, stablecoin redemptions echoed a pattern seen during the 2022 bear market, CleanSpark’s chief financial officer said the company could consider selling more Bitcoin than it mines to reduce treasury risk and Coinkite disclosed how a software error left Coldcard users’ Bitcoin recovery phrases vulnerable to hackers.

Net redemptions across the 11 stablecoins tracked by crypto research firm Kaiko reached about $12 billion in the 30 days to Nov. 13, echoing a pattern analysts saw during the 2022 bear market.

Tether’s USDt (USDT$) and Circle’s USDC (USDC), the two largest stablecoins, each ended the period with about 4% fewer tokens in circulation. Since Nov. 13, total stablecoin supply has fallen just 0.4%, while USDC supply has edged higher.

“The contraction looks like capital leaving crypto rather than rotating within it,” Kaiko analyst Andrew Yang told Cointelegraph.

Yang said that the redemptions left traders with fewer stablecoins on hand to buy crypto.

The cash-out coincided with outflows from US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds and a shift in expectations toward tighter Federal Reserve policy.

Bitcoin miner CleanSpark is considering selling more Bitcoin than it mines each quarter to reduce its treasury risk, according to chief financial officer Gary Vecchiarelli, potentially further drawing down its holdings, last valued at $806 million.

CleanSpark held 13,530 Bitcoin at the end of September after mining 1,708 Bitcoin and selling 2,102 Bitcoin during the quarter, according to Cointelegraph’s tally of the company’s monthly operating updates. It marks the first time since at least 2024 that the company ended the quarter with less Bitcoin than it began with.

During a Q4 earnings call on Tuesday, Vecchiarelli said CleanSpark already aims to sell virtually all of the Bitcoin it mines to cover day-to-day operating costs, and told an analyst that selling slightly more than it produces in a quarter was one way to reduce treasury risk.

“We see the selling of a small amount of Bitcoin in excess of the quarterly production to be one way of de-risking,” said Vecchiarelli.

“But again, we are going to be sensitive and very reactionary to markets, and we are going to use that capital to make sure that we prevent dilution or bringing additional debt just to grow the company,” he said.

The remarks came as CleanSpark recorded a $987 million net loss for the fiscal year ended Sept. 30, compared with a net income of $364 million a year earlier. The company attributed two-thirds of the swing to noncash adjustments in the value of its Bitcoin holdings, as Bitcoin’s price fell from about $114,100 to $83,600 over the fiscal year.

Bitcoin hardware wallet maker Coinkite has detailed how a security flaw in its Coldcard devices went undetected for more than five years despite several independent security reviews, including those using artificial intelligence (AI).

In a Nov. 23 postmortem report, Coinkite said that while its security checks confirmed the wallets had the correct system for generating secure recovery phrases, the company never verified whether the wallets were actually using it. As a result, a software error caused the wallets to use a weaker system instead, making the recovery phrases easier for hackers to guess.

A Bitcoin hardware wallet is a physical device that stores private keys offline, while a recovery phrase is a series of words that allows users to restore access to their funds if the device is lost or damaged.

Coinkite said at least 15 independent security reviews and research efforts failed to catch the flaw before the thefts began. It added that the weaker system went undetected because the recovery phrases looked normal and could still pass some security tests.

The flaw allowed hackers to steal at least 1,789 Bitcoin (BTC), worth around $114.7 million at the time of the thefts, Galaxy Research reported in August. Coinkite said it fixed the flaw on July 31 and introduced further security measures in August.
