This ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Judge Is Worried About Being Fired, Allegedly Says He Has “No Plans to Walk Away”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

There’ve been some rumors about the judges’ panel being switched up on Dancing With the Stars—specifically that Mark Ballas’s mom, Shirley Ballas, could replace Bruno Tonioli. But please be advised that (1) Bruno has heard said rumors, and (2) he has no plans to retire.

“Bruno knows the noise about Shirley, and the chatter about shaking up the panel,” a source tells The Sun. “He has been vocal to everyone: ‘I love the show, I have no plans to walk away or even consider retirement.’ It is about as loud as you can be without saying: ‘Don’t fire me!’”

Bruno’s been a judge on Dancing With the Stars since season 1 and is pretty well respected, so the chances of him being fired seem low. That said, he “knows how the industry can be,” and the insider emphasizes that “Bruno has seen it many times. It is never about anyone doing something wrong, but keeping the wheels turning and the fans hooked.”

Meanwhile, the source says Shirley “really shook up the show with fresh energy and tough marking” when she guest judged this season, but for what it’s worth, she doesn’t seem willing to leave Strictly Come Dancing over in the UK. As she put it to Chris Evans on the Breakfast Show, “I am not moving to the United States, although my son and my grandson are there…He has made it through to the final, I was up all night so if I look a little dark under the eyes.”
