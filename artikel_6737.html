Megan Skiendel Sat Down to Perform at a KATSEYE Concert While Recovering From a Concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The girlies of KATSEYE are still on the road for their Wildworld Tour after reaching another career milestone at last month’s Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show, and their latest show looked a little different for Megan Skiendel.

During their November 10 tour stop at the Moody Center in Austin, Texas, Megan performed sitting on the side of the stage in sunglasses as the remaining members, Daniela Avanzini, Lara Raj, and Yoonchae, pushed through the show’s regularly scheduled programming.

It was Megan’s first time on stage since recovering from a concussion, and based on these fan-captured clips, it seems like all she wants to do is dance again.

Of course, Eyekons remained concerned about Megan’s health during the performance.

“I can’t believe this is the state of KATSEYE right now,” one person wrote, while another added that her condition should be taken more seriously.

“like that's gonna do the job........ girl say goodbye to your brain and hello to CTE like it IS that serious,” they wrote. “fire whoever allowed her on the stage and cancel this fuck ass tour like this is crazy...”

Megan’s sit-down moment on stage comes after she confirmed she was returning with limitations.

“I wanted to give you a little update and let you know that while I’m feeling so much better, I am still actively recovering from my concussion so I’ll need to take some precautions as I continue to heal,” she wrote in a statement shared on KATSEYE’s November 9 Instagram Story.

“I won't be going full out just yet!” she added, explaining that she will have to be reassessed by a doctor before she can return to “unrestricted performances.”

“That said, I’m so excited to be back on stage with the girls and see all of you in Austin! I’m incredibly grateful for the opportunity to be there, even if I have to do things a little differently this time,” she wrote.

The global girl group seems to be persevering through the tour since it kicked off its North American leg on October 13. Not only are two members, Manon Bannerman and Sophia Laforteza, absent from performances, but several shows have been canceled or postponed due to illness. The Grammy nominees previously canceled tour stops in Detroit, Minneapolis, and Chicago.

“The label has made the decision to postpone our shows in Chicago and Minneapolis as Megan is recovering from a concussion,” the group wrote in its Instagram Story on November 5. “We are incredibly sorry to disappoint our fans. We know how much you’ve been looking forward to these shows, and that many of you have traveled long distances to be there. We don’t take that lightly, and we’re truly sorry for the disappointment.”

“We’ll announce the rescheduled dates as soon as possible, so please hold on to your tickets,” they continued. “We can’t wait to see you in both cities again soon. Thank you for your continued love, support and understanding.”

Wishing Megan and the girls nothing but the best as she continues her recovery!
