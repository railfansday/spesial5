Trail Correspondents: S5 Episode 15 | Interview With Your Former Self

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every great journey starts with questions, but not all of them get answered until the very end. In our Season 5 finale, the 2026 Trail Correspondents look back at a time capsule they created before hitting the AT, PCT, and CDT, revisiting the questions they once asked their future selves. From expectations and unanswered curiosities to hard-earned perspective and personal transformation, these final reflections bring the season full circle and reveal just how much can change between the trailhead and the finish line.
