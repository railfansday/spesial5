Aaron Rodgers' brother blames Steelers GM for quarterback's benching

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The benching of Aaron Rodgers became a family affair on the airwaves of the "Worldwide Leader."

Jordan Rodgers, Aaron's younger brother who is an analyst for ESPN, was on "Get Up" Wednesday, Nov. 25, less than 12 hours after news broke that Mason Rudolph will replace Rodgers under center for the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, Nov. 27, against the Denver Broncos.

"There's something amiss, and it's not just between McCarthy and Rodgers," ESPN analyst Jeff Saturday said.

Jordan Rodgers then noted that given the short week and adjusted schedule, Tuesday was not a normal off day and Aaron Rodgers was presumably the starter during practice.

"So something had to happen, I'm with you," Jordan Rodgers said. "Him and (Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy) have been together so long and have spent every aspect that you could with a head coach and quarterback, so I don't think that was the issue."

The younger Rodgers put the blame at the feet of general manager Omar Khan. He was surprised Rudolph was named the starter instead of the two young quarterbacks on the roster (Will Howard and Drew Allar).

Pittsburgh has lost two consecutive games, with Aaron Rodgers and the passing attack looking worse than pedestrian and averaging 17.0 points per game in the defeats.

"Their lack of success this year – and Aaron hasn't played great the last couple of weeks, but there was also a month ago where you say 'He looks like vintage (Rodgers)' ... is more of an indictment of Omar Khan and how he constructed this roster, because they're in the middle of the 'Last Dance' and a rebuild," Jordan Rodgers said. "There's problems everywhere. To me, Mason's not solving that this week."

Aaron Rodgers, who will be 43 on Dec. 3, signed a one-year deal this offseason after once again pondering retirement. He has been adamant that 2026 is his final season.

Jordan Rodgers has been a studio analyst for college football with ESPN and SEC Network since 2016. Aaron Rodgers was famously estranged from his family for several years before a recent reconciliation.
