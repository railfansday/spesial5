Daily Norseman Reacts Survey: A Different Border Battle Awaits

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year, we ask questions of the most plugged-in Vikings fans and fans across the country.

It's time for our SB Nation Reacts survey for this week, everybody! Time for us to, once again, take the pulse of the readers of the best damn Minnesota Vikings website on the internet to figure out how everyone's feeling about our favorite football team.

We've got two questions for you this week, one of which is the same question we ask every week: Do you think the Vikings are heading in the right direction? And boy howdy. . .what a difference a week makes, eh? The Vikings are coming off of the completion of a season sweep of the Packers, Kirk Cousins has given the offense a new lease on life, and they're getting ready to play the game of the year (of the week) as they head to Mexico City to face off against the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday Night Football. It sure does feel like this team is heading in the right direction, doesn't it?

Speaking of that big matchup south of the border, that's the focus of the other half of our survey for this week, specifically how you think this one is going to turn out. It's a matchup, once again, of one of the NFL's best offenses against the NFL's best defenses. The big difference this time is. . .well, this time the Vikings actually get to play offense, too. So, we're doing an extended version of "call your shot" for this one. Who do you think is going to come out on top this Sunday night, folks?

As always, we invite you to let us know what you think by voting on our poll questions, whether it's here or over in the Feed, and by sounding off in the comments section.

Have at it, ladies and gentlemen! We'll have the results of our survey up and posted on the site here for you before things kick off on Sunday night.
