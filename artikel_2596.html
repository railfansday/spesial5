Bronson Reed (Jermaine Haley) Shockingly Dies At 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

WWE Superstar Bronson Reed shockingly died at 38 amid a serious kidney issues that led to undisclosed surgery earlier this month following his cameo at WWE Crown Jewel 12026. After taking a bump off the stage through multiple tables, Reed remained in Saudi Arabia after suffering multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney following a garden-variety Beal toss from rising star Oba Femi. The spot appeared to go as planned, but Reed underwent surgery and recovery following the incident.

Bronson Reed was a rising star in WWE, positioned prominently with top heel stable The Vision alongside the deliberate future of WWE in Logan Paul, Austin Theory, Bron Breakker and Paul Heyman, who has churned out countless mentees who became Hall of Famers under his guidance.

Reed was known for his fast-paced style despite being a big man, and really excelled when he developed his flying splash finisher otherwise known as the Tsunami. Despite being a basic finisher in the context of 2026 pro wrestling, which is more high-risk than ever, Reed's theatrical splash was a weekly highlight on WWE programming. The former New Japan Pro Wrestling standout was released by WWE in 2021, an incident which took him by surprise. The former NXT North American champion returned to WWE the following year and went on to reinvent himself as a more svelte big man, dropping over 100 pounds ahead of his 2026 return from injury.

After suffering injuries at Crown Jewel, Reed's surgery was believed to have been a success.

"In great news, we are told Bronson Reed has made it back home and is continuing his recovery from surgery. Continued best wishes," reported Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer Live (h/t WrestleZone.)

Reed had previously suffered a fractured talus bone in November 2024 and a torn distal biceps in February 2026.

Bronson is survived by his wife, high school sweetheart Paige Haley, and their two children, one of which is on the way. Reed was finally hitting his stride as a legitimate potential main eventer, and similar to fellow international star PAC—who passed away earlier this year—Bronson Reed is gone too soon.
