Trevor Lawrence takes the blame for Jaguars' loss to Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jacksonville Jaguars quarterback Trevor Lawrence didn't mince words when evaluating his performance in Sunday's 23-18 loss to the New York Giants. After three consecutive wins, the Jaguars struggled to find their rhythm on offense, and Lawrence was quick to shoulder much of the blame.

"I played really, really poorly today," Lawrence said in a post-game press conference. "And you're not going to win many games if I don't play better than that. So I take a lot of it on myself."

Lawrence completed 25 of 47 passes for 301 yards, one touchdown, and two interceptions. The Jaguars struggled to establish a running game, while an injury-riddled offensive line allowed Lawrence to be sacked three times and under constant pressure. Abdul Carter alone logged a career-high 12 pressures, the second most by a player so far this season, and those were just pressures from one defender.

While that understandably made Lawrence's job harder, he didn't manage to rise above it and keep the offense moving. Jacksonville managed just one touchdown in six red-zone trips, repeatedly squandering opportunities to close the gap against New York.

"I did not make enough plays, didn't make enough of the routine plays, the easy ones," Lawrence said. "So really, really frustrated in that and my performance."

Despite how much the offense struggled, the Jaguars defense kept the team within striking distance, holding New York to just 238 total yards and allowing only three points in the second half. Josh Hines-Allen forced a fumble that Antonio Johnson returned 42 yards in the third quarter, giving the Jaguars another opportunity to score. However, the ensuing possession ended with a turnover on downs.

"We let our defense down today," Lawrence said. "They got us a bunch of turnovers and gave us plenty opportunities to win the game. And we didn't capitalize."

Lawrence also admitted that the Jaguars lacked the aggressiveness that had characterized their recent performances, saying the Giants "punched us in the mouth a little bit."

But despite the disappointing loss, Lawrence expressed confidence in their ability to respond. "We're going to be fine moving forward," Lawrence said. "But we've got to learn from it and get better."
