The console release of GTA 6 is here, in case you hadn't noticed. And us PC gamers are desperate to get hold of a version of our own, particularly as modders say it'll be playable on god's own platform within a week. Be careful what you click on, though, as NordVPN's threat intelligence team has discovered a nasty-sounding scam package on the dark web.

The company has posted a warning on X of a "GTA 6 PC scam kit" doing the rounds on the more dubious parts of the internet, using a fake GTA 6 PC port as bait.

A “GTA 6 PC scam kit” — yours for just $200. That’s what our Threat Intelligence team found on the dark web: a crypto wallet drainer, a fake “GTA6 Keygen.exe” that steals accounts, a YouTube Shorts bot to spread it, and a hidden crypto miner. The bait? A fake “GTA 6 PC Port.” But GTA 6 is console-only — there's no PC version coming on launch day. If you see one, it’s a scam. If it doesn’t come from the creators, don't click it!November 19, 2026

The package is said to be for sale for $200, or twice what a single copy of Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition costs at retail. Included is a crypto wallet drainer, a fake "GTA6 Keygen.exe," and a hidden crypto miner. There's also a YouTube Shorts bot within the package, designed to help it spread.

All of which sounds comprehensively designed to screw over PC gamers looking to get their hands on GTA 6 ahead of an official release.

Speaking to Tech Radar ahead of the games launch, NordVPN CTO Marijus Briedis said: "Someone has packaged the whole scam and is selling it to anyone with $200. You don’t need any technical skills anymore."

"People who play on PC have been waiting for news, and any link promising an early PC version will get clicks," says Briedis. "There is no PC version on November 19. If you see one, it's a scam."

NordVPN isn't the only security provider warning of GTA 6 scams. Malwarebytes has detailed a number of dodgy sites that supposedly offer access to the game, each using the hype as a lure to convince you to give away your details and funnel you into various schemes.

All of which compels me to restate the facts: GTA 6 does not have a PC release at the time of writing. In fact, it's not even been announced by Rockstar at this point. The game is available in Xbox Series X/S and PlayStation 5 versions only, and while modders seem to be beavering away on an unofficial PC version based on a leaked Xbox build, it's not available yet.

Don't let yourself be fooled into clicking shady links—and if you don't have a console like my good self, be prepared to wait. May I suggest playing all the other excellent games in your Steam backlog instead? It's not like we're short of PC games in general, you know.
