GTA VI made $3.79B on launch day, Take-Two says, a record for entertainment

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The game sold 32.5 million units before release, more than four times GTA V's first-day haul.

Official Grand Theft Auto VI artwork featuring Jason and Lucia.

Take-Two says Grand Theft Auto VI generated $3.79B in sales worldwide through the end of its launch day, and sold 32.5 million units before release. The company calls it the biggest entertainment launch in history, ahead of any film, album or game.

Grand Theft Auto VI made $3.79B in sales worldwide by the end of its launch day on Thursday, publisher Take-Two said on Friday. It called the game the biggest entertainment launch in history, across any medium.

The game also sold 32.5 million units before release, a record, according to Take-Two. The sales figure is a company estimate, and Take-Two did not say how it was calculated.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, Rockstar’s founder and president.

For comparison, GTA V made $800M in its first 24 hours in 2013, and $1B in three days, Bloomberg reported. The biggest film opening, Avengers: Endgame, took about $1.2B worldwide over a weekend in 2019.

Take-Two had said pre-orders beat expectations, and the game tied Metacritic’s record with a score of 99.

Analysts estimated it cost up to $1.5B to make, which Take-Two has not confirmed. Take-Two’s CEO has said Rockstar built it without generative AI.

The game costs $80, or $100 for an edition with extra in-game items. A PC version and an online mode will follow later. Take-Two shares rose about 2.7% on Friday.
