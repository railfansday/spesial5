HH Princess Sophia Wolkonsky brings royalty, global business leaders and esports figures together in Vienna for Castillia 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Castillia founder and CEO hosted an exclusive three-day Heritage Gala and marked the launch of AVOR, with HRH Prince Omar bin Faisal of Jordan, HRH Prince Gabriel of Nassau of Luxembourg, Johann Strobl, Vlad Marinescu and TNW CEO Alexandru Stan among the attendees.

From left: HH Princess Dr. Michaela Wolkonsky, ⁠HIRH Archduchess Herta Margarete Habsburg-Lothringen, HRH Princess Fenosoa Ralandison Ratsimamanga of Madagascar, HH Princess Sophia Wolkonsky.

VIENNA, Austria, HH Princess Sophia Wolkonsky, founder and CEO of Castillia, brought together members of royal families, international business leaders, technology executives and esports figures in Vienna from October 30 to November 1 for Castillia’s Heritage Gala 2026, an invitation-only celebration of cultural heritage, innovation and international connections.

Under her leadership, the three-day gathering connected guests from royalty, finance, entrepreneurship, technology, sports and culture across several of Vienna’s historic venues.

Among the attendees were HRH Prince Omar bin Faisal of Jordan, President of the Jordan Esports Federation; HRH Prince Gabriel of Nassau, a member of Luxembourg’s Grand Ducal family; Johann Strobl, former CEO of Raiffeisen Bank International; Vlad Marinescu, President of the International Esports Federation; and Alexandru Stan, CEO of The Next Web (TNW).

Founded and led by Princess Sophia Wolkonsky, Castillia focuses on historic properties, cultural heritage and curated experiences that connect tradition with contemporary international communities. The Vienna Heritage Gala reflected that vision, pairing formal celebrations with conversations about the future of cultural preservation.

The programme, themed The Enchanted Forest, included an opening reception, a Heritage Salon with panel discussions, a formal gala dinner and a farewell brunch. Its central milestone was the launch of AVOR, a new nonprofit cultural initiative.

Esports was among the emerging industries attracting interest from participants at the Vienna gathering, although it was not the official focus of the gala.

HRH Prince Omar bin Faisal has played an influential role in esports through his leadership in Jordan and involvement with international esports organizations. Vlad Marinescu, President of the International Esports Federation, also attended, bringing an international perspective on competitive gaming and its institutional development.

Filip Šoć, President of the Alliance of Electronic Sports of Montenegro and founder of Montenegro Future Festival, was also present. HRH Prince Gabriel of Nassau also expressed interest in the sector.

The gathering also included Johann Strobl, former CEO of Raiffeisen Bank International, alongside entrepreneurs and leaders from other sectors. Their presence illustrated the event’s breadth beyond traditional cultural and royal circles.

The inauguration of AVOR was a central moment of Castillia 2026. According to the official invitation, the international nonprofit initiative is developing a Global Cultural Atlas, with young people documenting the stories and places that shape cultural heritage.

The launch took place on October 30 at Vienna Private Members, Wiener Rennverein, at Palais Esterházy. On October 31, the Heritage Salon was held at Park Hyatt Vienna, followed by the formal Heritage Gala at Palais Pallavicini. The programme concluded on November 1.

The event was held under the patronage of figures including HRH Prince Omar bin Faisal of Jordan, Princess Ratsimamanga Ralandison Fenosoa, Maharani Sahiba Nivritti Kumari Mewar of Udaipur, Duke Michael zu Mecklenburg and Prince Constantin Sturdza.

By bringing historic families, cultural leaders, business executives and figures from emerging industries into the same setting, Princess Sophia Wolkonsky’s Castillia offered a meeting point between the preservation of the past and the opportunities of the future.
