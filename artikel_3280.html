‘Our Blues’, ‘Midnight Diner’ & ‘C’Est La Vie’ Among Projects Backed By TAICCA Joint Venture Funds

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Four content funds, jointly established by Taiwan Creative Content Agency (TAICCA) and local companies, presented the projects they’ve been investing in over the past year in a TCCF showcase today.

The line-up from the four funds – Chunghwa Digital Cultural and Creative Fund, Momomini Kids Content Fund, Joint Journey Creative and Enjoy Entertainment – include a wide range of films, series, live performances and immersive exhibitions.

Opening the presentation, TAICCA chair Sue Wang said: “These ventures are managed by professional fund managers, based on rigorous risk assessment and management. The fund has so far made 15 investments since launch one year ago, including into Our Blues, a Taiwanese remake of CJ ENM’s Korean series, which will premiere on Netflix on December 3; and two recent hit films: Taiwanese horror film The Rope Curse 4: Kuntilanak and action film 96 Minutes.

Gary Tsai, FET’s COO of Entertainment Service and GM of Far Reach Entertainment, said Rope Curse 4 had recently grossed $2.5M (NTD80M) in Taiwan theatres and has also been released in Malaysia, Indonesia, and soon Cambodia. “The film has Taiwanese taste and flavor but is still resonating with audiences across Asia,” Tsai said.

Enjoy Entertainment has also invested in the ID Music Festival in Huashan cultural park, live musicals Her Voice and Don’t Cry Dancing Girls and an immersive exhibition created by illustrator Jimmy Liao.

TAICCA is also partnered with Momomini, a Taiwanese media brand and YouTube content channel, in the Momomini Kids Content Fund, which specializes in kids content and has made six investments over the past year. In addition to TAICCA support, the fund is backed by the Fubon Cultural & Educational Foundation and DaWin Creative.

Phoebe TSAI, COO of Momomini Kids Content Fund, introduced the slate which includes animations such as comedy adventure Who’s Our Sub Today, and Feifei, based on stories by Taiwanese artist and author Bei Lynn; as well as live-action shows such as Mogu & Perol, a collaboration with France’s Zephyr, and reality series Having Fun In The Mountains.

The Momomini fund has also backed drama series Hello Mr President, about a kid who travels to Taipei to meet Taiwan’s president. “Very few production houses in Taiwan make drama for kids and teenagers – it’s been many years since any kids drama appeared on TV. So we’re very honored to have the opportunity with the support of TAICCA to invest in this drama,” said Tsai, adding that the show has finished shooting and will be on air from January next year.

Founded in 2025, Joint Journey Creative (JJC) is a joint venture fund between SET Group, TAICCA, Chunghwa Telecom and Japan’s The Far Eastern TV Entertainment.

Focused on investments in the transmedia and IP development space, the fund aims to connect Taiwanese creators with Asian and global markets. As one of its shareholders is Japanese, the fund has been building bridges between the content industries in Taiwan and Japan. Its investments include joining the production committee for multiple seasons of Japanese drama Midnight Diner, including season six.

“We’re able to create relationships and trust between Taiwan and Japan to pave the way for more collaboration with Japanese parties,” said JJC President Joanne Tsai.

JJC has also been running talent incubator programs including a romance drama series workshop that introduces Taiwanese talent to drama producers from Korea and Japan. “We’ve identified several quality scripts and here in TCCF we’re talking to our collaborators in Japan and Korea to see how we can bring them to screen,” said Tsai, adding that the fund is also working with partners in the rest of Asia, Europe and the U.S.

The fourth fund, Chunghwa Digital Cultural and Creative Fund, was jointly established by TAICCA, Chunghwa Telecom, and E.Sun Bank, again with a mission to help Taiwanese cultural content reach global audiences, with capital of around $30M (NTD1BN).

General Manager Lin Kun-feng explained that the fund is making equity investments into Taiwanese production company Damou Entertainment (The World Between Us) and IP development and publishing company Horiza Creations.

Chunghwa is also investing in a wide range of film and TV projects, including C’est La Vie, produced by and starring Esther Liu, Call Of Lobster, starring Chang Chen and Choi Siwon, and indie band Shallow Levee’s tour Speak in Silence.

While Chunghwa is the biggest shareholder in the fund with a 64% share, TAICCA holds 30%, E-Sun has 5% and CDCC Capital Management has a 1% share.
