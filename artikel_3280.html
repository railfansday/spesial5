Supriya Ganesh, Benito Skinner, Amy Landecker & Bradley Whitford Round Out Cast Of Mary Beth Barone Rom-Com ‘Still We Met’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

EXCLUSIVE: Still We Met, the indie rom-com written by and starring comedian Mary Beth Barone, has wrapped production in New York with four major additions to the cast: The Pitt‘s Supriya Ganesh, plus Overcompensating‘s Benito Skinner, and the husband & wife duo of Amy Landecker (Misty Green) and Bradley Whitford (The Diplomat).

Character details are under wraps. The film follows a young woman at a crossroads who meets a charming British stranger and spends one unforgettable night wandering New York, revealing more than either intended.

Still We Met marks Barone’s feature screenwriting and acting debut. The film is directed by Zackary Drucke, in his narrative feature debut. Other new additions to the cast include Daniel Liu (The Devil Wears Prada 2), Adam Faison (Fallout), Corteon Moore (Overcompensating), and Johnny Sibilly (Hacks). Jack Heller and Caitlin de Lisser-Ellen produced for Assemble Media (The Man I Love, Skeletons), alongside Alex Bach and Daniel Powell of Irony Point (I Think You Should Leave with Tim Robinson). Lena Dunham and Michael Cohen exec produced under their Good Thing Going banner, with Madison Wolk co-producing for Assemble Media alongside Blake Mars for Good Thing Going. We scooped the project in September.

Best known for her role of Dr. Samira Mohan on HBO’s hit medical drama The Pitt, heading into its third season, Ganesh will be seen coming up in Netflix’s reboot of 13 Going on 30. She is repped by Untitled and Granderson Des Rochers.

Skinner is the creator and star of Overcompensating, the Prime Video comedy returning for its second season on February 17, 2027. He works with Barone on that show and will next reteam with her on her Hulu rom-com series Hard Feelings. He is repped by WME and Entertainment 360.

Best known for starring in Prime Video’s Transparent, Landecker has most recently been seen in Chris Rock’s A24 film Misty Green. She is repped by Gersh, Ayala Cohen Management, Marsha McManus of Principal Entertainment LA and Toczek Suddleson.

Emmy nominated this year for his work on Netflix’s The Diplomat, Whitford is repped by Gersh, Greenlight Management and Peikoff Mahan.
