JJ Redick gets real on Lakers ‘ceiling,’ frustrating blown leads

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Before the season began, he raised eyebrows with a bold claim: he was "confident" the Lakers could become "a championship-level team this year."

They were miles behind the Thunder and Spurs in the Western Conference.

But 14 games into the season, Redick is the one chuckling at his detractors after the Lakers won eight games in a row and are tied for the best record in the league at 12-2.

Some of the wins haven't been pretty, including Sunday's 131-123 win over the Suns in which they watched a 31-point lead disintegrate to five points in the fourth quarter before Quentin Grimes banked in a 3-pointer and Luka Doncic made a pair of free throws to save the day.

Despite Lakers coach JJ Redick having his analytics department prepare a 21-page audit detailing where LA can improve offensively and defensively, it's clear the team has wildly surpassed expectations.

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick looks on during the first quarter against the Milwaukee Bucks at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026. Getty Images

And Redick thinks this is just the beginning.

"I don't think we should put a ceiling on this group," Redick said. "This group is committed to getting better every single day, recognizing that this is a process to become a high-level team. And that's what I want from this group, is hold each other to that standard every single time. And that's the frustrating part at times because you see how good we can be. There's no reason to ever play less than that standard, you know?"

Redick is like the never-satisfied father who sees the potential in his son. He rides him. He pushes him.

That style is working with this Lakers team.

Luka Doncic is playing MVP-level basketball, including finishing with his seventh 40-point performance in 12 games on Sunday, with 43 points, three rebounds and five assists.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Walker Kessler had his fifth straight double-double against the Suns with 14 points and 12 rebounds. And Austin Reaves, who was sidelined Sunday because of a bruised heel, looks like the best No. 2 in the league.

They also have some things going wrong: When Doncic isn't on the floor, the Lakers blow leads. Their third unit can't be trusted. And defensively, they're ranked No. 18.

But they're the top-rated team in the league on offense and they entered Sunday's game leading the league in scoring (122.2 points per game), field goal percentage (50.3%), steals (11 per game) and points off turnovers (24.5 per game).

Walker Kessler of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Redick is right not to put a ceiling on the Lakers.

He got them to buy into their championship potential.

And now he's pushing them to believe they haven't even tapped into who they could become despite being atop the league.

"Honestly, I think we have a lot of space for improvement," Doncic said. "Obviously one thing for now is blowing some leads. I think we got to work on that, for sure. But I think every aspect of the game, I feel like we have a lot of improvement left."

The Lakers blew a 25-point lead to the Warriors in a 112-95 loss on Oct. 30. At least Sunday, they pumped the brakes and recovered. The Lakers are still figuring things out.
