Mike Macdonald "really optimistic" Sam Darnold will be able to return Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Seahawks are anticipating having Sam Darnold back for their next game.

Seattle will have its bye in Week 11. But head coach Mike Macdonald said in an interview with Seattle Sports 710 on Monday that Darnold could be back for the Week 12 matchup against the 49ers.

“I’d say we’re really optimistic that he’s going to be able to get a full week’s worth of work and be able to play against San Francisco,” Macdonald said, via John Boyle of the team’s website.

Macdonald also noted that backup Drew Lock won’t be replacing Darnold, saying, “Sam’s our starting quarterback.”

Darnold has missed the last two games with quad and rib injuries.

While Darnold’s performance has dipped in 2026, Macdonald has been steadfast in his support of the quarterback the club won the Super Bowl with in February.

Seattle is 3-4 in Darnold’s starts this season — with Drew Lock playing most of the Week 1 win over New England. Darnold has completed 60.2 percent of his passes for 1,478 yards with 10 touchdowns and nine interceptions this season.

Lock, however, sports a 3-0 record this season. He’s completed 73.1 percent of his throws for 837 yards with seven touchdowns and no picks.
