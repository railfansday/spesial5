Cavs sign sharpshooting wing to two-way deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cleveland Cavaliers are making another change with their two-way players. The team announced on Wednesday morning that they signed Zack Austin, who's been playing in the G League with the Cleveland Charge, and waived Riley Minix. Austin will join Ernest Udeh Jr. and Norchad Omier on two-way contracts.

Austin turned heads on Tuesday evening when he dropped 40 points for the Charge on 14-for-18 shooting, which included going 5-for-9 from beyond the arc in a 138-126 win over the Iowa Wolves.

Zack Austin is a 25-year-old who finished his college career at Pittsburgh. The 6'5" wing spent part of last season with the Oklahoma City Blue, the Thunder's G League team. He's averaged 15.3 points, 3.7 rebounds, 2 blocks, and 1.0 steal while shooting an efficient 51.7% from the field and 42.9% from three in his 23 career G League appearances.

The Charge have placed an emphasis on targeting players who can shoot and defend at a high level. Those are the two skills that they believe translate best from the G League to the NBA, and more importantly, fit with the Cavs' core group. Austin fits this mold.

Cleveland has had to turn to some unconventional lineups in recent games due to injuries. We've seen Tyrese Proctor and Meleek Thomas get opportunities to shine in larger roles than what they were expected to play going into the season. That has extended to some of the two-way players as well, with Norchad Omier providing key contributions in the Cavs' last two wins.

Austin has the skill set to slot in and help a team battling injuries. He may get his opportunity to prove what he can do in the NBA sooner rather than later.
