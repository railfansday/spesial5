Why Alabama football sat Keelon Russell vs Chattanooga

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Keelon Russell didn't play a snap for Alabama football in its 47-0 win against Chattanooga. Austin Mack, Jett Thomalla and John Gazzaniga did the work at quarterback for the Crimson Tide.

But Alabama coach Kalen DeBoer made it clear that Russell's absence was not because of further injury.

"He didn't re-aggravate it last week (against Vanderbilt)," DeBoer said of Russell. "We really feel good about the direction he was going this week, and we didn't want to risk anything with putting him in a vulnerable position. You know, just there's a little bit of carryover that carries over into the next week when you're just out there and active, and so he's on the home stretch. He's looking really good, and I'd expect him to be, you know, going full, full speed, full go all week next week in practice."

Russell had three touchdown passes in Alabama's 21-6 win against Vanderbilt, completing 22 of 33 pass attempts for 217 yards.

Alabama takes on Auburn Saturday, Nov. 28 on ABC. A kickoff time is to be determined.
