Titans agree to trade S Amani Hooker to Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Bills are bringing in some help for their secondary.

Buffalo has agreed to acquire veteran safety Amani Hooker from Tennessee.

Tom Pelissero of Netflix was first to report the trade. Multiple reporters note Buffalo will send Tennessee a second-round pick in exchange for Hooker and a fifth-round pick.

Hooker, 28, currently leads the league with five interceptions in 2026. He’s also recorded 52 total tackles with one tackle for loss.

A fourth-round pick in the 2019 draft, Hooker has appeared in 103 games with 74 starts. He’s tallied 17 career interceptions with 45 passes defensed and five forced fumbles.
