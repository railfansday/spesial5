Jack Ham will return to Penn State radio booth following medical emergency

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Pro Football Hall of Fame linebacker Jack Ham announced he will return to the Penn State radio booth on Saturday, two weeks after a medical emergency.

Ham, a color analyst for the Penn State Sports Network since 2000, was transported to the University of Washington Medical Center during pregame of the Nittany Lions’ Nov. 7 game. He missed Penn State’s game against Minnesota last week.

I’ve spent a lot of Saturdays in Beaver Stadium, first on the field and then up in the booth,” Ham, 77, said in his statement. “I’ll be back alongside Steve [Jones] this weekend, and there’s nowhere else I’d rather be.”

Jones treated Ham at the scene, saving his life, according to Penn State coach Matt Campbell. Ham remained in the hospital several days before returning to Pennsylvania on Saturday.

“I’m feeling good, and I owe a great deal to the people who took care of me and stood by me through all of it,” Ham said.

Ham was a six-time All-Pro with the Steelers, with whom he won four Super Bowl championships. He also made eight Pro Bowl appearances. He earned All-America honors at Penn State in 1970.

He entered the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1988 and the College Football Hall of Fame two years later.
