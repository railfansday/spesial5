Josh Jacobs questionable to return with a hand injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Packers running back Josh Jacobs may be done for the day in New England.

Jacobs went to the locker room during the Packers’ third possession of the first quarter of Sunday’s game against the Patriots. The Packers announced that he is considered questionable to return due to a hand injury.

Jacobs ran four times for five yards before heading inside. He got his hand caught in the facemask of Patriots safety Craig Woodson on one of those runs and it seems likely that’s when he was injured.

MarShawn Lloyd is inactive, so Pierre Strong and Kaleb Johnson are the other available running backs for Green Bay. The Packers lead 14-0 with just over two minutes to play in the first quarter.
