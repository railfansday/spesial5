JD Vance Should Not Be Finger-Pointing in the Midst of War

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The vice president ought to recognize the important role that diplomacy plays in a U.S. commitment to victory.

Vice President JD Vance speaks at a midterm campaign rally in Monroe, Mich., October 15, 2026.

President Donald Trump told Fox News’s Jacqui Heinrich that as for the Iranians who continue to find new ways to threaten Americans, “I’m going to finish it off — soon. One way or the other, we’ll finish it off.” In that same interview, he said he never liked the infamous Memorandum of Understanding, which gave the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps a cash infusion even as it continued firing on commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.

The president has removed Vice President JD Vance, the face of and defender of the MOU, from leading negotiations with the Iranians, whom Vance claimed the U.S. had reformed. Weeks and dozens of attempts to kill Americans later, in response to Senator Tom Cotton’s questioning, National Counterterrorism Center Acting Director Joseph Weirsky told Congress what every American can see: “There’s no indication there’s any turning over of leaves with the regime.”

Vice President JD Vance speaks at a midterm campaign rally in Monroe, Mich., October 15, 2026.

Rebeccah Heinrichs is a senior fellow at the Hudson Institute and the director of Hudson’s Keystone Defense Initiative.
