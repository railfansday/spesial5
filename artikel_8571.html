List clothes, electronics, toys, and other small items on Mercari when you’d rather ship them than arrange a local pickup. You pay 10% of the price plus any shipping the buyer covers, ship on a prepaid label Mercari emails you, and must accept a return only for an item that arrives damaged, fake, or far from its description.

Say you sell a $30 item in a small 1-pound package and pick Mercari’s cheapest label for it, a $7.99 USPS one. By our math, you keep $26.20 when the buyer pays the $7.99, since the 10% fee applies to it too, or $19.01 when you offer free shipping and pay for the label.

Mercari works best for casual sellers of clothes, electronics, toys, and everyday collectibles who’d rather ship than meet a buyer. Find local buyers for furniture and appliances instead, and put rare collectibles up for auction on eBay, where bidders compete on price.
