Ford Mustang Dark Horse Driver Leads Police on Wild Chase Through Los Angeles Before Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The driver couldn't outrun the cops—but his passenger, apparently fresh out of jail for felony evading, insisted he could have.

Los Angeles County’s fabled Mulholland Drive is the perfect place to enjoy a Ford Mustang Dark Horse. Packed with tight curves, dramatic views, and homes owned by the rich and famous, the scenic route is one of the most famous roads in America. However, one Ford Mustang driver and his boastful passenger allegedly decided to turn it into a setting for a high-speed police chase.

According to reports by Fox 11 Los Angeles and The California Post, the pursuit began shortly after 1 a.m. on Thursday, November 5. Police reportedly had reason to believe that the Mustang was stolen, although authorities later specified that it was not. But their attempt to pull it over kicked off a chase that lasted nearly a half hour, with the Ford reportedly hitting speeds of about 80 miles per hour.

The driver reportedly didn’t stick to Mulholland Drive; he also jumped on Highway 101 and then Interstate 405, before eventually losing control and crashing on Woodcliff Road in Sherman Oaks. Authorities say that despite being injured, the driver tried to flee on foot after the crash but was quickly caught. The passenger was also detained, and he had some words for the police. “I just got out of the joint for felony evading,” the passenger told authorities, as captured on video. “If I was driving, you wouldn’t have caught me, I swear to God.”

In any event, the passenger was released after police questioned him. As for his buddy behind the wheel? He’s now facing felony evading charges of his own. A 500-horsepower Mustang Dark Horse may be made to eat up corners, but if those corners are on Mulholland Drive in the middle of the night—with police officers behind, no less—it might be better to keep those horses in the stable.
