Gemini Unveils Crypto Bloodbath's Biggest Victims as XRP, ETH Sink

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Leading crypto exchange Gemini has shared the names of the major crypto losers in the ongoing crypto market bloodbath.

Major crypto assets such as XRP and Ethereum (ETH) have seen double-digit percentage losses in the past week.

As per the latest data, ZEC has seen a massive 22.67% drop over the past seven days, making it the worst-performing cryptocurrency among those shared by Gemini.

XRP, which is linked to Ripple, has seen an 18.89% drop over the past seven days. It is the second-biggest loser among the assets shared by Gemini.

Notably, the percentage drop in XRP is quite significant, considering it is one of the top crypto assets by market capitalization.

Ethereum (ETH), the world’s second-largest crypto asset, has also seen a major drop. ETH has seen a 13.98% drop over the past seven days.

Apart from XRP, ETH and ZEC, several other major crypto assets have seen major price drops amid this ongoing market downturn.

Ethena (ENA) has seen a 16.68% drop. It is the third-worst-performing cryptocurrency on Gemini's list.

Furthermore, Pump.fun (PUMP) has seen a 15.60% drop over the past seven days.

Decentralized exchange token Uniswap (UNI) has seen a 13.82% drop. Furthermore, Hyperliquid (HYPE) has seen a 12.92% drop.

Notably, all seven crypto assets shared by Gemini have seen their prices drop by more than 12% over the past seven days.

This further illustrates the extent of the ongoing crypto sell-off. Notably, this sell-off has hit altcoins the hardest.

However, the recent downturn has wiped out a lot of gains.
