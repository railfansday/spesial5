Amazon puts Arnold Schwarzenegger in Santa suit to extend holiday push

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A new spot ties into the upcoming movie “The Man with the Bag,” showing Amazon again tapping into its own programming to promote its range of offerings.

Amazon is once again tapping into its Prime ecosystem to integrate one of its entertainment properties into an advertising campaign. Taking inspiration from “The Man with the Bag,” which premiers Dec. 2, the e-commerce platform has created a light-hearted holiday ad featuring a familiar face.

“Santa Schwarzenegger” follows the actor as he uses a Santa suit, delivered by Prime, to explore New York City unnoticed. During his adventure, Schwarzenegger is able to watch a game with bar patrons, catch a holiday movie and use his strength to help a family get a Christmas tree on the roof of their car. It ends with the actor using an Amazon Locker to pick up a pair of skates so he can enjoy time on an ice rink. The minute-long spot is set to “Make Someone Happy” by Jimmy Durante.

Schwarzenegger will also appear as Santa throughout the season outside of the spot and movie, per press details.

Amazon has made finding moments of joy a central part of its holiday strategy. “Santa Schwarzenegger” follows the longer “Joy Delivered” spot, which tells the story of a young woman going on a “Planes, Trains and Automobiles”-style journey to make it home to her family in time for the holidays, giving away the gifts she bought along the way.

This is not the first time Amazon has drawn inspiration from a Prime Video title in its advertising. In May, Amazon’s Brand Innovation Lab released a spot with Liquid I.V. featuring the leading actors from its hockey romance show, “Off Campus,” marking the first time Prime Video leads appeared in a co-branded spot.

“Santa Schwarzenegger” allows the e-commerce giant to promote its delivery services while also showing how its properties can be used for advertising.

Amazon’s ad revenue hit $19.6 billion in Q3, a 12% year-over-year increase, per an earnings statement. However, this is a significant dip from the 26% YoY growth rate seen just a quarter earlier, leading to a stock dip for the company.
