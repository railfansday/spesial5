Backblaze (Nasdaq: BLZE) | Remote, US | AI and application storage Solutions Engineering | Reports to the Solutions Engineering Manager (the VP of Solutions Engineering until the manager is hired) | Works with Account Executives, Product, Support, and the Solutions Engineering team

We started by building storage servers from scratch in a Palo Alto apartment to save 90% on hardware. Turns out hard constraints make for pretty good engineering. That discipline forged a platform that performs as well or better than competitors, scales to multi-exabyte workloads, and costs a fraction of what AWS or Google charge. AI infrastructure runs on data. Storage gets hard at scale. We've been breaking, rebuilding, and improving that storage layer for 20 years.

Today, the world's leading AI infrastructure providers, neoclouds, and data-intensive businesses run on Backblaze. Our platform includes:

500,000+ customers. 175 countries. Nasdaq: BLZE. backblaze.com

Backblaze sells a technical product, so Solutions Engineers join the deal at the first discovery conversation, before it is qualified. You will partner with Account Executives to learn what a customer needs, run the proof of concept that shows Backblaze fits, and stay close after the deal closes. This role focuses on AI and application storage customers.

Nearly every customer is replacing something they already run. Your job is to show where Backblaze slots into what they have already built, not to design their stack from scratch. You will earn trust by being curious, hands-on, and straight with customers about what the test shows.

This is not a demo-and-handoff role. If your instinct is to open a terminal, test a customer's real workload, and write down what you find, keep reading.

You will have four conversations: a recruiter screen, the hiring manager, our principal solutions architect, and a member of the Solutions Engineering team. We will ask you to walk us through a proof of concept you ran, including a result the customer did not hope for and how you reported it. We will ask about a technical problem you dug into yourself, and how you work with customers and Account Executives.
