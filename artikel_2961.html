Chicago Bears vs. New Orleans Saints: Week 11 Players to Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Though the Chicago Bears have won 4 of the last 5 games, including three straight prime-time matchups, the BYE week couldn't have come at a better time as they embrace the much-needed opportunity to rest their wounded and prepare for the final stretch of the 2026 season.

That starts in earnest this Sunday as Caleb Williams and Co. host the New Orleans Saints. A team that has struggled mightily this season despite being one game behind in the race to win the putrid NFC South.

Make no mistake, this is as winnable a game as the Bears will see the rest of the season, but a conference matchup in such a tight race atop the NFC is also a must-win at this point in the season, and it also happens to be against Bears' defensive coordinator's former team, so there will absolutely be some added emotions this week. Either way, here are a couple Saints players to keep an eye on. Starting on offense.

The number 8 overall prospect selected in the 2026 NFL and the 2nd WR taken, the former Arizona State Sun Devil has been inactive for most of the season dealing with some nagging soft-tissue injuries. While Tyson was only targeted 5 times in his first action as a pro two weeks ago against Cleveland, he led the team with 13 targets (more than doubled the next most player) last week against Carolina and converted 11 of those into receptions for 80 yards.

Tyson is an explosive play-maker who might not be quite as physical after the catch but reminds me of Luther Burden III with his burst in and out of cuts and how he can make defenders miss in the open field. Tyson is the type of receiver who can give our corners fits, so I will be eager to see how Dennis Allen plans to slow him down while not losing track of the big-play weapons like Chris Olave, Barion Brown and fellow rookie Bryce Lance. With a depleted offensive line, I expect much more quick passes from QB Tyler Shough, so Tyson figures to once again receive the bulk of the targets. If Chicago can limit his YAC, they should force New Orleans to turn the ball over and score enough points on offense to put this one away. That is, if they can slow down the Saints pass rush.

With two players in the top 5 of the current NFL sack leaders, the Saints boast as potent of a pass-rush threat as any team in the league. Carl Granderson holds the slight edge with 8.5 sacks to Chase Young's 8.0, but both players will be key in containing Caleb Williams and forcing errant throws like we saw in Chicago's last action against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Not only does Granderson lead the team in sacks, but he also leads the team in tackles for a loss with 10.5, and that is despite missing a game this season already.

While I have the utmost confidence in Chicago's OL and how Ben Johnson schemes up protection, fending off these pass rushers will be a primary focal point this week. As our own Mongo Peanut pointed out, Bears Left Tackle Theo Benedet will have his work cut out for him this week. If Chicago can limit their production and prevent them from making any game-wrecking plays, the Bears should march up and down the field and put up points in droves. If not, New Orleans has a chance at an upset in Week 11.
