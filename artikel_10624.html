Meta’s AI can now make phone calls you don’t want to make

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Meta has introduced a new feature for its Muse AI assistant that allows users to make phone calls without having to speak to anyone themselves.

The feature lets Muse contact businesses on a user’s behalf, handling everything from restaurant reservations to customer service inquiries and waiting on hold. However, there is a catch for anyone hoping to try it.

According to Meta’s chief AI officer Alexandr Wang, Muse can now handle calls to businesses such as insurance providers, cable companies, restaurants, and even the DMV.

Rather than making the call themselves, users can open Muse and ask the AI assistant to contact a business with instructions about what they need.

For example, someone could ask Muse to call a restaurant to make a dinner reservation or contact their cable provider to ask about an unexpected charge. The AI can then place the call and speak to the business on their behalf.

The feature is not available on Muse’s free plan. Subscriptions start at $20 per month, which includes 500 million tokens per week, while a $100 monthly plan provides 3 billion tokens for heavier users.

Meta is also not alone in developing the technology. Google’s Gemini and AI startup Instinct have introduced similar capabilities that allow AI assistants to contact businesses on behalf of users.

The expanded rollout comes as Meta continues building Muse into a consumer AI assistant. The company revealed in October that the service had surpassed 10 million users.

The expansion comes after Meta’s Muse AI previously raised concerns when it reportedly shared a YouTuber’s home address with a stranger on Facebook Marketplace while handling a potential sale.

The incident highlighted concerns about giving AI assistants greater control over everyday tasks, particularly when they involve communicating with other people or sharing personal information.

With Muse now able to make phone calls on behalf of users, Meta is continuing to expand the assistant’s ability to interact with businesses and complete tasks without requiring users to handle every step themselves.
