“Endless” cruise ship seized by authorities after passengers abandoned in Cambodia

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Villa Vie Odyssey ship has been seized in Singapore just weeks after passengers were told that the “endless” cruise had come to an abrupt end.

Back on October 27, 200 passengers on board the Villa Vie Odyssey were told they had to get off the ship in Sihanoukville, Cambodia. The operator, Villa Vie Residences, blamed a rise in fuel costs as they were “unable to secure the fuel necessary to continue operations.”

A number of the passengers had sold their homes so they could live on the “endless” cruise, with many expecting the journeys to last for at least 15 years. Other travellers didn’t get to set foot on the ship despite paying for the trip either.

Now, the Villa Vie Odyssey has been placed under a court order in Singapore and seized.

As per the BBC, the reason for the seizure has not been disclosed, but CBS News has reported that it is “likely” from a fuel supplier.

Singapore’s port authority told the BBC that the ship has been “placed under arrest pursuant to a court order,” and that “arrangements for their welfare and wellbeing are being managed by the vessel’s creditors.”

A local law firm, Helmsman LLC, has been named the “arresting solicitor” for the seizure too. Naturally, no passengers are on board the ship, seeing as they got off in Cambodia.

According to the Singapore Courts, the boat was “arrested” on November 8 and remains docked at the Changi General Purpose Anchorage.

A number of passengers still have pieces of personal property onboard the ship after only grabbing the “absolute essentials” upon being kicked off. Randy Cassingham, who has blogged his experiences on the ship, noted that if they had more of a heads-up, arrangements could have been made to protect the property.
