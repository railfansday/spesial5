Crypto Liquidations Top $1.32 Billion as 'AI Bubble' Deflates: Bitcoin, Gold, XRP in Focus

Liquidations on crypto exchanges reached $1.57 billion over 24 hours on Nov. 20, according to CoinGlass data. Bitcoin (BTC) fell 4.11% to $59,370, its lowest level since July 2.

According to CoinGlass, 265,525 traders were liquidated over 24 hours, and long positions accounted for $1.43 billion of the total.

Bitcoin saw $554.28 million in liquidations, and 91.3% of that amount came from long positions. Ethereum (ETH) fell below $1,700 on Nov. 20, and ETH traders saw $331.08 million in liquidations. Binance accounted for 45.8% of all liquidations, and the largest single order was also on Binance: an $11.98 million ETHUSDT long position that was liquidated at $1,638.78.

XRP saw $14.67 million in liquidations, and $13.86 million of that amount came from long positions.

Stock markets fell on the same day. The Nasdaq Composite has given back all of its gains for 2026, and the S&P 500 fell 1.82% to 7,045.5 points. The S&P 500 and the Dow Jones are now down 10% from their record highs. The VIX volatility index jumped 8.09% to 30.73.

Nvidia has lost $2.55 trillion in market value since its Oct. 6 peak. Anthropic shares started trading on Nasdaq on Nov. 19. On Nov. 20, the stock fell about 10% in premarket trading, and it was close to its IPO price of $83.

Nvidia futures on crypto exchanges saw $26.79 million in liquidations, according to CoinGlass. Anthropic contracts saw $15.29 million in liquidations, and 90.7% of that amount came from long positions.

On Nov. 19, Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said on CNBC that investors had been moving money from crypto into big listings. "People are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included — Bitcoin, XRP — and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Anthropic IPO," he said. Garlinghouse also expected a crypto rally after the listing. "I think you'll see that happen here as well," he said.

Gold fell 2.27% to $3,542.12 an ounce, and silver dropped 3.51% to $41.32. The gold-to-silver ratio rose 1.43% to 85.686.

Gold and silver futures on crypto platforms saw $64.9 million and $40.3 million in liquidations, respectively.
