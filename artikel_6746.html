A Complete Timeline of Zendaya and Tom Holland’s Romantic Relationship

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

In 2021, Spider-Man: No Way Home co-stars Zendaya and Tom Holland confirmed they are in a romantic relationship after years of off-and-on rumors that they were dating. In 2025, TMZ confirmed that they were engaged after Zendaya debuted her engagement ring at the Golden Globes. In June 2026, Holland confirmed they had secretly gotten married months before.

Zendaya and Holland have been playing Spider-Man love interests Peter Parker and MJ in the franchise since 2017, but met on set in 2016. They’ve obviously been close, but it was in July 2021 that the paparazzi caught them kissing at a stoplight, giving those rumors some more legitimacy.

The couple still keeps their personal lives private, but these are the notable moments in the timeline of their relationship so far. Here, their full history.

Holland was the first to post a photo of Zendaya, but soon they were appearing on one another’s Instagram feeds regularly. In July 2016, he shared a pic of them with another friend at the pool, captioned, “Summer Sunday’s.”

Zendaya posted their shared The Hollywood Reporter cover just a few months later, writing, “Amidst all the chaos and sadness...this one good thing did happen to me today. Honored to share it with the very best...Spider-Man himself.”

Holland and Zendaya’s chemistry on-screen transferred easily to the red carpet, and photos of them being playful with one another at the San Diego Comic-Con were widely circulated by fans.

In May 2017, they started attending events together, possibly because of promotional opportunities, but photos taken of them show a more personal relationship, like this photo from the MTV Movie and TV Awards.

Throughout the month, they were frequently photographed together as they promoted Spider-Man, but it seemed like the easy chemistry of two familiar colleagues.

In July 2017, a source confirmed to People that they are together.

“They started seeing each other while they were filming Spider-Man,” the source said. “They’ve been super careful to keep it private and out of the public eye, but they’ve gone on vacations with each other and try and spend as much time as possible with one another.”

A second insider added, “They’re both really ambitious, and they challenge each other—but, most importantly, they make each other crack up. They seem to have a really similar sense of humor and love joking around together. They have great banter back and forth.”

Before the Spider-Man: Homecoming premiere in June, Holland was gushing to People about his friendship with Zendaya, saying, “We are like the best of friends. She’s so great and amazing. I’m a little worried [about dealing with fame … [but] Zendaya is super famous and she’s been through this and I just call her up and say, ‘How do I manage being famous?’ I’m very glad I have a friend like her.”

The pair addressed the dating rumors above by tweeting to one another about the idea they’ve gone on vacation together.

“Wait wait...my favorite is when it says we go on vacations together HA! I haven't been on a vacation in years! hbu?” Zendaya wrote to Holland.

He joked back, “Does the press tour count?”

The next month, Zendaya denied the rumors again in an interview with Variety.

“We are friends,” she said. “He’s a great dude. He’s literally one of my best friends. This past how many months we’ve had to do press tours together. There’s very few people that will understand what that’s like at 20 years old.”

The next month, Zendaya told fans she’d been cheated on in the past and why it left her a bit skeptical about relationships early in life.

“I’m so anti being in a committed relationship when you’re young and people are learning and growing, because when people are young, they make bad decisions sometimes because they don’t know any better,” she explained. “It doesn’t mean they don’t know the difference between right and wrong—it just means that they’re still in the experimental phase in their life where they haven’t made the right decisions yet…it’s very hard to be in a relationship when the both of you are still figuring out life. You cannot change anybody. You cannot make someone grow up faster than they’re supposed to.”

At this point, Holland had spent time with Zendaya’s parents, meeting them at a steakhouse in New York.

In March 2018, the two stars sat together at the Oscars, though they walked the carpet separately. They were seen later that month at a comic book store in Los Angeles.

Zendaya attended the Met Gala, wearing a Joan of Arc-inspired chainmail Versace gown, and Holland posted a photo of her to his Instagram writing, “All hail the queen. Killing it mate.”

The next month, Zendaya posted a video of Holland goofing off with a lightsaber to wish him a happy birthday. She captioned the video: “Happy birthday loser…”

In September, Holland celebrated Zendaya’s birthday with a paparazzi photo from their date at the comic book store and a statue of Spider-Man.

“Me, myself and the birthday girl. Happy birthday mate - keep killing it and paving the way for the rest of us,” he wrote, adding a heart.

But then a few days later, Skai Jackson’s mother said on Instagram that they are, in fact, a couple.

“Yes. It’s true. They been on the low for a while,” she wrote.

She then denied she was the commenter using her screen name. “Nothing posted or said was me. I could [sic] care less or know who was dating who. Please stop asking.”

Holland and Zendaya began their Spider-Man: Far From Home press tour, and when Holland posted an Instagram of himself, Zendaya, and co-star Jacob Batalon, he accidentally tagged her name in a very suggestive place.

“I’m assuming because you don’t know how to work ig, that’s the reasoning behind my name placement,” she commented with five laughing emojis.

Holland edited the tag and wrote, “Thanks @zendaya for making me aware of my obvious blunder.”

Holland shot down the Zendaya dating rumors again. The actor told ELLE in an interview that he is single and not romantically involved with anyone. He admitted he is “definitely a relationship person. I’m not the fleeting type at all; it’s not my way of life.”

Zendaya was seen with her Euphoria co-star Jacob Elordi on vacation in Greece. Zendaya never confirmed she was dating Elordi, but paparazzi did catch Elordi giving her a kiss in New York City in February 2020. By September of that year, Elordi seemed to be dating Kaia Gerber. (They ultimately broke up in November 2021.)

Tom Holland appeared to go Instagram official with Nadia Parkes, who he was supposedly spending lockdown with in London.

Then, two years after denying they’re a couple, the Spider-Man co-stars were spotted together in Holland’s car sharing a kiss at a red light in L.A. They were then seen in the Silver Lake neighborhood the same day, leaving Zendaya’s mother Claire Stoermer’s residence.

Later that month, Zendaya posted photos of herself on the red carpet for the Venice Film Festival in her Balmain dress. Holland commented with three flame emojis.

“Easy, Tom Holland!” Chalamet answered immediately, setting Zendaya off into a fit of giggles.

During the London photo call for Spider-Man: No Way Home, Holland was interviewed and pointed to Zendaya, saying, “There she is—looking beautiful, might I add.”

On December 13, the Los Angeles premiere of the film included this adorable moment when Holland sees Zendaya walk in and gets stopped in his tracks. They do look stunning together on the red carpet, too.

“Thank you @tomholland2013 for coming back again to @anticapesa for your surprise night with @zendaya & friends,” he captioned the shot.

“Zendaya. Easily,” he replied after they asked for names.

“Our relationship is something that we are incredibly protective of and we want to keep as sacred as possible,” he explained. “We don’t think that we owe it to anyone, it’s our thing, and it has nothing to do with our careers.”

He also joked about how his Spider-Man co-star sends him constant content on social media.

“She sends me too much, it's like, I can't keep up,” Holland said. “I delete my Instagram for days at a time. I download it to post and then I have to check my messages, and I'll have hundreds and hundreds and hundreds of things from her.”

On the July 3 episode of the Wondery podcast SmartLess, Holland spoke with hosts Jason Bateman, Sean Hayes, and Will Arnett about why he and Zendaya work so well.

“I’m lucky that I have someone like Zendaya in my life,” Holland said. “It’s interesting being in a romantic relationship with someone that is in the same boat as you.”

He continued, “You can share your experiences and all that sort of stuff—and that's worth its weight in gold.”

Zendaya spoke to ELLE about whether fans liking her and Holland as a couple so much makes it easier to accept the public’s interest in them.

“Parts of my life, I accept, are going to be public,” she replied. “I can’t not be a person and live my life and love the person I love. But also, I do have control over what I choose to share. It’s about protecting the peace and letting things be your own but also not being afraid to exist. You can’t hide. That’s not fun, either. I am navigating it more than ever now.”

Holland and Zendaya had a day date at the BNP Paribas Open final day in Indian Wells, California, on March 17. The couple was photographed in the stands together in their biggest public appearance together of the year so far:

Zendaya spoke a little about Holland in her Vogue interview. She praised how Holland handled overnight fame when Spider-Man: Homecoming came out in 2017. “We were both very, very young, but my career was already kind of going, and his changed overnight,” she said. “One day you’re a kid and you’re at the pub with your friends, and then the next day you’re Spider-Man. In an interview on The Rich Roll podcast, Holland teased a bit about the next Spider-Man film and how he had shared it with his girlfriend.

“We have a creative and a pitch and a draft which is excellent,” Holland said. “It needs work but the writers are doing a great job and I read it three weeks ago and it really lit a fire in me.”

Like this is a real movie worthy of the fans’ respect, but there [are] a few things we need to figure out before we can get that really going. He added that he wants to give fans “the best version of what the next chapter for Spider-Man looks like.”

We’re trying to get it going as soon as possible. Things are looking great but there’s still a lot of moving pieces that need to come together for us to hit the ground running.”

The morning after, TMZ reported that Zendaya and Holland are indeed engaged. Holland proposed “in one of Zendaya’s family homes in the United States” between Christmas and New Year’s, sources close to the couple said. It was a “very romantic” and “intimate” proposal between just them.

On January 6, she attended a special screening of Challengers wearing the ring.

A few days later, Holland’s father, Dominic, released more details about the engagement in a Patreon blog post. The English actor and author shared in his post that he knows some of the people following him are doing so to glean more details about the celebrity couple. Dominic wrote it “amuses” him, but he was happy to have something to report this time.

“The big news this week is a good example of just such an opportunity for me—are they aren’t they engaged?” he wrote. “Zendaya wearing a ring and Tom nowhere to be seen adding to the intrigue.”

Dominic then stated that he would not be “adding anything new and juicy like a date, venue, guest list,” but he would be sharing “the different approaches of father and son to proposing marriage.”

“Tom, as you know by now was very incredibly well prepared. He had purchased a ring. He had spoken with her father and gained permission to propose to his daughter,” Dominic explained. “Tom had everything planned out… When, where, how, what to say, what to wear.”

Dominic shared that when he proposed to his son’s mom, Nikki, it was “nothing like my eldest son who has obviously watched all the slushy movies because growing up I never saw him reading a book.”

He added, “For most blokes, the stress of purchasing an engagement ring is being able to afford it. I suspect that this was the least of Tom’s worries, more concerned with the stone, [its] size and clarity, its housing, which jeweler.”

The Spider-Man star’s dad mentioned how he does not “envy those who are gawped at wherever they go,” adding, “I do fret that their combined stardom will amplify their spotlight and the commensurate demands on them and yet they continually confound me by handling everything with aplomb.”

He continued, “And even though show business is a messy place for relationships and particularly so for famous couples as they crash and burn in public and are too numerous to mention… I am completely confident they will make a successful union.”

While promoting The Odyssey, Holland and Zendaya shared several new details about supporting each other during its filming.

In a People interview on July 15, Holland recalled discussing the role of Telemachus with Zendaya after learning that its production schedule would overlap with Spider-Man: Brand New Day. He said, “To be honest, when I got home after our meeting, I sat down with Z and was like, ‘I’ve been offered this movie.’ And she was like, ‘By who?’ And I was like, ‘It’s the big one.’ And she was like, ‘Chris?’ I was like, ‘Yeah, it’s Chris Nolan.’”

“And then we had Spider-Man that was literally scheduled to go the same day that we would start shooting,” he continued. “Z said, ‘I’ll leave you if you don’t do The Odyssey.’”

Zendaya also revealed that Holland was the one who told her Christopher Nolan wanted her to play Athena. “He was...allowed to be the one to tell me, and that was really special,” she told the Associated Press. “I was already on cloud nine. The fact that I was even got to be involved as well was like just this cherry on top.”

At the film’s New York premiere on July 14, Zendaya told Extra that Holland’s presence helped calm her nerves on her first day of filming. “You know, some locations he wasn’t at, but he was on my first day, which I was very grateful for, because as you can imagine, something like this is quite a daunting task,” she said. “It’s the scale, the scope, it’s absolutely extraordinary. So walking onto a Chris Nolan set for the first time is no small feat, so I was happy to have some support.”

Holland was equally enthusiastic about sharing the experience with her. “I mean, it’s awesome,” he told the outlet. “We still, to this day, cannot believe we got to go through this together and experience this epic movie on the same set, on the same days. You know, she’s fantastic in the film. She had a really tough job. I think when you see the movie and you see what she has to do, it’s really difficult, and as always, she nailed it.”
