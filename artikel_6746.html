I’ve experienced my fair share of adult acne over the last five years (see: persistent chin pimples and a two-year stint on spironolactone), so I’ve learned just about every skincare hack to keep my breakouts in check. Like, did you know even oily skin types need hydration to keep your skin barrier healthy? And that you should be ditching those makeup wipes in exchange for a cleansing balm that simultaneously washes your face and has nourishing benefits?

Below, I’ve rounded up team Cosmo’s must-have products to keep acne at bay. You’ll always find these in our purses or stashed in our vanities. And the best part—everything is under $30, so you don’t need to break the bank to have clear skin.

One Cosmo editor says: “Taking off makeup is arguably the most important step in any routine to avoid breakouts, and this cleansing balm is my go-to for getting every last bit off, all the way down to my mascara. It melts into a nice, creamy texture and skips the chalky feeling that many cleansing balms leave behind. After adding some water and rubbing, it leaves my skin feeling hydrated, fresh, and ready to start the rest of my skincare routine.” —JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “Hyaluronic acid is fab for plumping and hydrating acne-prone skin without feeling too heavy. But a lot of these serums leave an annoying tacky feeling on my skin. Not this drugstore pick though. It sinks right in and immediately makes my face look more moisturized while keeping my skin barrier in check.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “Hypochlorous acid—a soothing, anti-bacterial ingredient that’s used in doctor’s offices—is an acne-fighting must-have. And this affordable spray from e.l.f. comes in a large bottle that I can keep at my vanity to spritz on before makeup, after my workouts, or whenever my skin just needs a refresh.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Speaking my truth: I am the Gen Z coworker showing up to the office with stars all over my face. But when they’re coated in salicylic acid to actually help get rid of a pimple instead of just cutely hiding it, why wouldn’t I? It also keeps me from picking at my pimples to help them heal faster.” —BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Made with 10 percent benzoyl peroxide, this spot treatment immediately gets to work when I dab it on a zit, killing bacteria to help flatten it. My pimples go away in about two days when I use this on them.” —BG

One Cosmo editor says: “My dry skin barrier needs all the hydration it can get, and I also have hyperpigmentation on my legs, so this workhorse formula is a win-win.
