McInnes reveals Hearts Naderi interest before Rangers move as new deal discussed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Rangers signed Ryan Naderi from German third-division side Hansa Rostock in a £4.7m deal in the winter window; the striker's current deal runs until 2029 but Sky Sports News revealed he wants to extend that stay; he has 11 goals and seven assists in 19 games across all competitions

Derek McInnes hoped the signing of Ryan Naderi would fall through 10 months ago.

But the Rangers boss is now encouraging Ibrox chief executive Jim Gillespie to do what he can to tie down the Czechia international for the longer term.

When Hearts manager, McInnes asked sporting director Graeme Jones to check how the striker was rated by data partner Jamestown Analytics as Rangers negotiated with German third-division outfit Hansa Rostock.

McInnes said: "You're always curious. It's brilliant when you see other teams round about you signing a player that's not rated, it's just natural.

"But he was someone that was well known to them [Jamestown Analytics]. I think Club Brugge had an interest in Ryan and Union Saint-Gilloise potentially had an interest in him as well. But the fee just went away from them.

"We were told that he was a good player before I'd even set eyes on him, and the feeling was that he could help Rangers and make them better.

"So when I'd seen a lot of the comings and goings, it was on, it was off, it was on again, I was kind of hoping that it would fall through, being the Hearts manager."

Naderi scored his first Scottish Premiership goal in early April to help Rangers go top of the table for the first time in more than two years, but he soon went off with an injury and watched from the sidelines as Danny Rohl's team blew their title hopes.

The German-born forward has proven a key player under McInnes, taking his season tally to 11 goals and seven assists in 19 games across all competitions this season, and Rangers are looking at Naderi's long-term future.

Sky Sports News revealed on Wednesday that the player was keen to extend his Ibrox contract, which currently runs until 2029.

"These conversations I have with Jim on a regular basis about players that we should be speaking to and give them a wee bit more security and a wee bit more comfort for us," McInnes said.

"So these conversations have been ongoing. The club's well aware of what needs to be done and what we shouldn't be doing. And Ryan's been part of that conversation."
