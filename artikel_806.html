Yoro sees red as Ngumoha shines - Liverpool v Man Utd player ratings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Ngumoha stars as Liverpool thrash 10-man Man Utd

Mauricio Pochettino's reign as Manchester United manager got off to a dismal start as his side were beaten 4-0 by rivals Liverpool after playing 82 minutes with 10 men.

Here's how BBC Sport rated the players - and you can see how you scored them below.

Alisson Becker: Caught one cross and took a few goal-kicks. Made a good save to deny Bruno Fernandes in stoppage time. Had little else to do. 6

Ronald Araujo: Never really tested defensively. Made a few contributions going forward. 7

Jeremy Jacquet: Went shoulder to shoulder with Bryan Mbeumo in the box at 0-0, although a penalty against the French defender would have been harsh. 6

Virgil van Dijk: Organised the defence well. Also had a header well-saved at 0-0 and nodded another wide in the second half. 7

Milos Kerkez: Always keen to drive Liverpool up the pitch. Had some nice link-ups with Rio Ngumoha. 7

Dominik Szoboszlai: Fantastic driven free-kick to open the scoring. Went close a few more times with long shots and generally bossed the midfield. 8

Alexis Mac Allister: Did all the simple things right and allowed others to shine around him. Forced Senne Lammens into a top save in the first half. 7

Bradley Barcola: Missed a few chances to score his first league goal for Liverpool when through one-vs-one. Showed his electric pace to set up his side's third. 7

Florian Wirtz: Readjusted well to net Liverpool's second and assisted their fourth. Five goals in four games for club and country now and looks close to his creative best. 8

Rio Ngumoha: So direct and skilful. Scored Liverpool's third with a calm finish. Also set up their second after easily beating Diogo Dalot. Club and country have a real talent on their hands. 9

Alexander Isak: Led the line well but missed some big chances. Won the free-kick that resulted in Szoboszlai's goal and led to Leny Yoro's sending off. 6

Victor Munoz (on 72, Barcola): Good cameo and took his goal well, curling it into the far corner. 7

Trey Nyoni (on 72, Mac Allister): Looked bright. Had a shot go just over. 6

Subs not used: Chiesa, Mamardashvili, McConell, Tsimikas.

Senne Lammens: Could he have done better with the opener? Good save to deny Mac Allister and some decent stops after that. 6

Leny Yoro: Did he have to make the tackle on Isak? It was almost as though he thought about whether he should and decided the answer was yes. Given Liverpool scored from the free-kick it was a bad decision. 4

Matthijs de Ligt: First Premier League start in almost 12 months in middle of a back three turned into a nightmare with the early error that led to opening goal and Yoro red card. Struggled from that moment on. 4

Lisandro Martinez: Fully extended by Liverpool's attack. The least bad of the visitors' starting defence, but that isn't saying much. Exposed completely by Barcola for Liverpool's third. 5

Diogo Dalot: Given a torrid time by Ngumoha, which prompted Pochettino to bring on Maguire. Tries hard but it's not enough. 5

Youri Tielemans: Concerns about a midfield partnership with Mainoo emerge again. Not having the sustained impact required for a player of his experience. 4

Kobbie Mainoo: Twenty-one-year-old being asked to shoulder so much responsibility in an ailing team. New midfield recruits have not helped him. 5

Bryan Mbeumo: Couple of decent bursts came to nothing, then sacrificed after Yoro red card. Received consoling pat on the head from his new boss. 5

Bruno Fernandes: Early free-kick just wide and shot straight at Alisson. Given the carnage at Old Trafford this season, no wonder contract talks on hold. Furious at free-kick given against him when he thought he was through. 5

Patrick Dorgu: Asked to do an awful lot, initially as wing-back, then further back. Lucky Barcola fluffed his lines or his failure to keep an offside line would have been exposed. 5

Matheus Cunha: Tried to keep simmering frustration in check. Not easy in a losing side with no service. Replaced at the break. 5

Harry Maguire (on 22, Mbeumo): Couldn't have expected to be called on so soon. Went into middle of back three and immediately blocked an Isak effort to prevent a third goal. Tried hard. 5

Joshua Zirkzee (on 46, Cunha): Pre-match rumours of a rare start proved unfounded. Replaced Cunha at half-time and asked to bring a different skillset to the number nine role. More physical but not as good and lost possession too easily. 4

Mason Mount (on 65, Tielemans): Only on for a couple of minutes when attempted crossfield pass went to Liverpool player and eventually a third goal. 5

Noussair Mazraoui (on 65, Dorgu): Not really sure what he was supposed to do. Tried to push forward as United's poor control brought more pressure. 4

Subs not used: Heaton, Amad Diallo, Heaven, Lacey.
