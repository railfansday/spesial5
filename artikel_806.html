Rochdale sack boss Watson after Northampton defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Ian Watson was in his first EFL managerial role at Rochdale

Rochdale have sacked head coach Ian Watson after only five months in charge of the League Two club.

The former South Shields boss replaced promotion-winning manager Jimmy McNulty in June after he joined Stockport but leaves the club just two points clear of the relegation zone.

Watson took South Shields to the National League North play-off final last season where they were beaten by Kidderminster Harriers before taking up his first Football League role at Spotland.

After returning to the EFL by beating Boreham Wood in last season's National League promotion final, Rochdale have won only four of their 16 League Two matches so far this season.

He departs following Saturday's 3-2 defeat by Northampton Town which was their fourth successive loss and left them 22nd in the table.

"We would like to thank Ian for his hard work and commitment during his time at the Crown Oil Arena and wish him every success in the future," said a club statement., external

"The process to appoint a successor is already underway, and the club will give a further update in due course."

Rochdale are next in action at home to Barnet on Saturday, 28 November (15:00 GMT).
