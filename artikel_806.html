Cody Johnson on Why He’s Skipping 2026 CMA Awards: ‘Going 100 MPH All the Time Isn’t Healthy’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The 2026 entertainer of the year nominee says he's taking a step back due to "mental and physical exhaustion."

Cody Johnson has four nominations heading into the 2026 CMA Awards, but the country star revealed Tuesday (Nov. 17) that he won’t be attending the ceremony.

In a note posted to Instagram, Johnson addressed his “CoJo Nation” fans, his peers and the country music industry at large to let them know why he wouldn’t be at Wednesday night’s show.

“I won’t be attending the CMA Awards tomorrow, and I want to be honest about why,” Johnson wrote. “After getting sick at the end of my UK tour earlier this month, I realized much of it stemmed from mental and physical exhaustion. I want to be open about this because I believe we help one another by revealing our truths, and this time my truth isn’t in the words of a song; it’s real life.

“At the end of the day, I’m a human being, no matter how much I tell myself and others I’m Superman,” he continued. “I have everything to be thankful for, but sometimes I put too much pressure on myself. I’m going to spend some time recouping at home; being grounded by my family is what I need most right now.”

Johnson is nominated for five awards at Wednesday night’s show, including the top prize of entertainer of the year. In his note, he acknowledged just how hard he’s worked to reach that upper echelon of country music.

“I’ve waited my whole life to be where I am in my career, and I wouldn’t have it any other way,” he wrote. “It was well worth the wait and the hard work it took to get here. However, going 100 mph all the time isn’t healthy, and I see that now.”

He closed by thanking people for understanding and “for your love, support, and grace during this downtime through the end of the year. I will see you all in 2027.”

In addition to entertainer of the year, he’s also up for male vocalist of the year as well as single of the year and music video of the year for “The Fall.” He’s previously won four CMA Awards, including male vocalist of the year at last year’s show.
