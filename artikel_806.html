DP World Tour player vows to 'kick butt' in battle with brain tumour

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

MJ Daffue secured his DP World Tour card via the HotelPlanner Tour, having won twice this season and finished fifth in the season-long rankings; Daffue revealed on social media that doctors had found tumours after he was hospitalised with an infection

DP World Tour player MJ Daffue has vowed to ‘kick butt and be ready to go soon’, having been forced to miss the start of the season after being diagnosed with a brain tumour.

Daffue won twice on the HotelPlanner Tour this season and finished fifth in the Road to Mallorca rankings, securing his DP World Tour card, before being hospitalised earlier this month for an infection that unearthed further health issues.

The 37-year-old has since revealed two tumours were found, including one benign on the liver, with Daffue now undergoing treatment to address the one found in his brain.

In a statement on his Instagram, Daffue said: "It's taken a while to post anything about this year because of the difficult time the past two weeks has been. This year has been incredible and we have been so blessed with good golf.

"Unfortunately, I ended up in the hospital with an infection about 10 days ago and after lots of tests they could not find the source. However they did find a tumour on my brain and my liver.

"Many more tests later, it was established that the one on my liver is benign, so we are very grateful. Unfortunately, the one on my brain is still a mystery.

"I am currently on strong medication to see if we can attack the legion on the brain but I am struggling a bit physically. I am in great spirits, and the care team and neurosurgeon has a perfect plan.

"I feel very confident that this is something that I will also kick butt and be ready to go soon. I appreciate all the messages so far and can't wait to be fully healthy and attacking the top of the leaderboards again soon."

Daffue previously held the halfway lead at the 2022 US Open, won by Matt Fitzpatrick, then graduated from the PGA Tour from the Korn Ferry Tour later that season.

He struggled for form over the following years and entered 2026 outside the world's top 800, only to post three top-10s on the DP World Tour and impress on the HotelPlanner Tour.

"I started the year with little to no ranking on the HotelPlanner Tour or the DP World Tour, but I worked hard, played intensely and was able to notch up a couple of early wins," Daffue said earlier this month.

"That made it possible for me to also fight for a card through the DP World Tour non-members points list, where I came up just short. I was pleased to finish the season with third place at the HPT Grand Final in Mallorca, which meant I finished fifth on the rankings.

"Starting the year with no ranking meant I had to take every playing opportunity I got. This meant it was hard to plan a schedule and so I spent weeks on the road, away from family and friends, which was hard.

"Most importantly, thanks to my wife for the love and being my rock - I can't be anything without you. So I'm super proud to come through the other side and it all be worthwhile."
