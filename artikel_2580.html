Dejounte Murray Trade Rumors: Warriors Make Intentions Clear After Rich Paul Approached Front Office

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dejounte Murray has one foot out the exit door in New Orleans after reportedly demanding a trade from the Pelicans' front office less than two weeks ago.

According to Brett Siegel, the Warriors' insider with ClutchPoints, while the Pelicans have had some conversations with other front offices about a trade, it is, in fact, Rich Paul of Klutch Sports who has taken the lead on reaching out to multiple front offices to find his client a new home in the NBA.

"Now that a formal trade request comes in, they [the Pelicans] are actively looking for trades with teams. They've had some conversations but nothing really in depth, to my knowledge, yet."

"It's been more so from Murray's agency side, through Klutch Sports, a.k.a. Rich Paul; he has been the one who has been looking for a destination for his client at the point guard position," said Siegel in the latest episode of the 'Clutch Scoops' podcast.

"He's been talking to several teams about making a trade. I had one source tell me that Rich Paul reached out to the Warriors among several others to see if there was a way to make a move."

"From what I'm told, Golden State isn't interested, especially since they'll have to move a guy like Moses Moody to make the salaries work, and that's not something they want to do."

"From other teams' perspective, there's a reason why they aren't jumping the gun and are interested: the fact that Dejounte Murray is making $32.7 million and around $31 million next year. It's all guaranteed money; that's just something a lot of teams can't take on."

"And honestly, it's not something they want to take on right now without any compensation in the form of draft picks from New Orleans. As of right now, that doesn't seem like something that's going to be on the table. So Dejounte Murray is kind of in limbo at this point."

"His time in New Orleans is done; he will be on a new team, from what I believe, sometime in the next month, around that December 15/16 window, when a lot of contracts in the league open up to become trade-eligible. I think that's when we're going to see a deal materialize for Dejounte Murray," Siegel concluded.

The former All-Star is currently averaging 13.4 points, 4.6 rebounds, and 4.4 assists while shooting 43.1% from the field and 29.6% from beyond the arc.

No team in the league wants a $63 million+ burden on its salary cap over the next two years for a player averaging less than 15 points per game and shooting below 30% from the three-point line.

Therefore, the Golden State Warriors have reportedly made it clear they have no interest in landing a player like Dejounte Murray. Especially since they need to retain key young pieces like Moses Moody for their long-term future and also want to wait for Jimmy Butler's return from injury.

Now Murray does claim that he is being sabotaged in New Orleans, and it is possible that his performance could improve exponentially on another team. But it will be a major challenge for his representation and the Pelicans' front office to find him a new landing spot.

Reports say the Suns are among the teams that have emerged as a potential landing spot for Murray, but it remains to be seen where the 30-year-old former All-Star ends up this season.
