What channel is the Steelers vs Bengals game today? Start time, schedule, streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

After a couple of weeks of uninspiring matchups, NBC's "Sunday Night Football" should be getting a good one Nov. 16.

The Pittsburgh Steelers will travel to face the AFC North rival Cincinnati Bengals in a prime-time rematch that could prove critical in determining which team claims the division crown at season's end.

Both teams are riding four-game winning streaks that have led to identical 6-2 records. They've seized control of the division over the 5-3 Cleveland Browns and banged up 4-4 Baltimore Ravens.

Pittsburgh prevailed by a 30-27 score in a Week 3 quarterback duel in which the Bengals' Joe Burrow and Steelers' Aaron Rodgers both played well. The duo combined for 574 passing yards in an entertaining one at Acrisure Stadium.

The Bengals will enjoy the comforts of home at Paycor Stadium this time, so it will be interesting to see to what degree they can get the better of the Steelers in the first SNF game between teams with winning records since Seattle bested Kansas City back in Week 7.

The Steelers vs. Bengals game kicks off at 8:20 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 16.

The Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Cincinnati Bengals game will be broadcast on NBC and be available nationwide as the only game happening in its primetime kickoff window.

Those without cable or over-the-air TV sets can watch the game online via Peacock, NBC's companion streaming service. Cable TV alternative service Fubo also has a free trial and carries NBC, as well as CBS, Fox and the ESPN family of networks, meaning fans can catch NFL action all season.

The NFL+ streaming service will also carry the matchup.
