A weekend to remember in Paris!

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Matchday Live on Tour with Etihad headed to the French capital, where City fans came together at The Blue Moon to watch our Premier League clash with Fulham.

Before matchday, City legend Joleon Lescott got behind the decks in Paris, then joined Matchday Live host Cel Spellman to explore a local market.

On Saturday, the pair reunited at The Blue Moon, where Cel hosted the show live from Paris and Joleon shared his thoughts before kick-off, at half-time and after the final whistle. Supporters in the French capital watched alongside City fans following the show from home.

There was a goal for the Blues to cheer as Elliot Anderson volleyed home in the second half to earn City a 1-1 draw with Fulham.

Thank you to everyone who joined us in Paris! Keep an eye on mancity.com for more Matchday Live on Tour stops.
