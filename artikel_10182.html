The OnePlus ColorOS 17 update is proving to be a disappointment for fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

As OxygenOS is slowly being replaced by ColorOS on OnePlus devices, long-time OnePlus fans are expressing their disappointment with several missing OnePlus features.

If you’re a OnePlus user who has enjoyed 3D Wallpaper and AI Motion Wallpaper, per-game performance modes, Mind Pilot, AI Photo Agent, the OnePlus Dialer, lock screen music lyrics, or even the OnePlus calculator Easter egg, you may soon be joining the ranks of OnePlus fans who are finding the ColorOS 17 update more than a little disappointing. Several fan-favorite features like the ones previously mentioned are either not available at all in ColorOS 17, or are only available in some regions.

OnePlus ran a ColorOS 17 AMA on November 23, but failed to clearly answer many of the questions put forth by the community. Essentially, the answer for most of these missing features runs along the lines of “we’re working on it, but availability will depend on your region.” The ColorOS 17 global rollout has been slower than initially anticipated, and my own OnePlus 13 has yet to receive the update at all.

Other models, like the OnePlus 15, are now receiving the updated builds of ColorOS 17 in Europe and some other regions, which include a few feature additions and bug fixes, but don’t look to resolve the biggest complaints raised during the AMA. OnePlus officially shut down its presence in North America, Europe, India, and other regions back in July, and Oppo has taken over software updates since then, swapping out OnePlus’s OxygenOS Android build with Oppo’s ColorOS.
