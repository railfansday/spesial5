Hadley Designs LLC: Amazon Brand Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Our purpose is to help families feel more connected, confident, and supported at every stage of childhood. We design the tools that shape childhood, support families, and brighten life’s meaningful moments.

Hadley Designs is a fast-growing lifestyle and early education brand trusted by millions of families and teachers. We create products that make learning joyful, celebrations meaningful, and everyday life more organized and intentional. We are a fully remote global team of creators, thinkers, problem solvers, educators, and operators who care deeply about impact, craftsmanship, and heart.

Hadley Designs is a family-founded, female-led brand dedicated to shaping brighter futures through creativity, connection, and purposeful design.

We create screen-free learning tools that spark curiosity, build confidence, and turn everyday moments into meaningful growth. Every product begins with a hand-drawn concept and is crafted with teachers, parents, and child development experts so learning feels joyful, accessible, and engaging at every level.

Our mission is simple. Help families and educators unlock children’s potential through tools that inspire curiosity, strengthen connection, and support learning without screens. What started at a kitchen table has grown into a brand trusted by more than two million customers, with products used in homes and classrooms worldwide.

We do not just design educational materials. We create experiences that make children feel supported, capable, and excited to learn. Creativity is our craft. Heart is our blueprint. Impact is our standard.

If you want to work with a company that designs with purpose and builds with passion, you will feel at home here.

Own Your Catalog Like a Business, and Win on Amazon.

Hadley Designs is seeking an Amazon Brand Manager to take full ownership of a multi-million dollar group of our products on Amazon, as the product manager, the merchandiser, and the CEO of that catalog. This is a high-impact role for an e-commerce brand builder who brings real judgment, reads a market quickly, and knows how to grow net profit, not just sales.

You will report to the leader of our Amazon channel and work as part of a small, high-performing Amazon team. While this role operates deeply within Amazon, we welcome candidates who have built brands across e-commerce, DTC, or marketplace environments and are excited to bring that experience to a scaled Amazon catalog.

You bring the judgment. We bring the brand, the catalog, and the team.

We are looking for thinkers who are coachable and confident, not people waiting for a playbook.

This is an ownership role for an experienced brand builder, not a virtual assistant position.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
