ChatGPT&#8217;s Agent Writes Its Own Queries to Serve Ads. Advertisers Don’t Get to See Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Buyers have been clamoring to understand more about ChatGPT’s ads system since it launched in February. Executives have been slowly revealing more.

OpenAI matches ChatGPT ads to search queries written by its own AI agent, three executives who run the AI lab’s ad system told ADWEEK.

When a user writes a prompt in ChatGPT, an AI agent OpenAI calls the orchestrator reads the conversation and works out what the person might want to buy. It can also draw on the user’s previous chats when the user’s ad personalization settings allow it. It then writes its own search queries, ranks the ads it finds in its system, and sends the best candidates to an auction that weighs advertisers’ bids against how useful each ad is likely to be to the user.

Trishla is an Adweek staff reporter covering AI and tech.
