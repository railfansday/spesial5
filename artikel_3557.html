Below Deck Med's Gael Breaks Silence on Night of Nathan's Arrest After Abuse

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Below Deck Mediterranean‘s Gael Cameron is speaking out for the first time about Nathan Gallagher‘s alleged abuse that led to his arrest for domestic violence.

Cameron appeared on the Tuesday, November 17, episode of Paul C. Brunson’s “We Need To Talk” podcast where she made a rare comment following her breakup from Gallagher, saying, “He’s a very charming person. I give him that. And I think even when I speak to certain people from [Below Deck] production, they still scratch their heads because no one would have seen this coming from him right. And I didn’t either.”

Bravo viewers met both Cameron and Gallagher when they joined season 9 of Below Deck Med. Their relationship got off to a rocky start as Cameron navigated a breakup from an offscreen boyfriend before moving on with Gallagher.

Their romance ultimately fizzled out after production wrapped. Cameron and Gallagher later reconciled — and announced a surprise pregnancy. Their son was born in June 2025 and they returned to season 11 of Below Deck Med with Nathan working on deck while Cameron raised their child nearby.

News later broke in August that Gallagher was arrested on domestic violence charges in Australia in December 2025. Gallagher was charged with “assault occasioning actual bodily harm, intentionally chok[ing] a person without consent and common assault” following an alleged incident with an unnamed victim, which Cameron has since confirmed was herself.

A police memo revealed that Gallagher was eventually granted conditional bail. He was later convicted on three counts of domestic violence after failing to appear in court. Us also confirmed that Gallagher is the subject of an Apprehended Violence Application that was filed on behalf of Gael in New South Wales.

“I’m not claiming I was perfect. I wasn’t. I made mistakes, and I will take responsibility for my own actions,” Gallagher told Rolling Stone in a statement in August. “I will not accept being portrayed solely through one account of a relationship when there is another side and evidence relating to it. I have contemporaneous video footage from the night in question, as well as messages and other records, which I believe provide important context.”

Cameron remained largely tight-lipped before Tuesday’s podcast, where she answered burning questions about her life now.

Flashing back to their first meeting, Cameron clarified that she didn’t immediately plan to explore a romance with Gallagher, saying, “I spent a lot of time where I really did just see him as a friend, and things move very fast on a boat. He would be very playfully flirty with me, and I was very like, ‘Stop it! No, stop.’ Like, I would say it in a funny way, but I’d be like, ‘You need to stop.’ And I would even push him towards some of the other girls.”

Cameron slammed the insinuation that she was unfaithful with Gallagher.

“So there is a scene where we hug and we’re hugging on the stairs and he kisses me on the neck. I was still technically with my partner,” she explained. “And so that’s where people do the whole ‘She cheated. She cheated.’ So technically, in that scene, I didn’t physically cheat. I didn’t actually ever have an intimate relationship with him until after my ex and I had broken up.”

She continued: “But in saying that, the reason why I carry it and I don’t bother trying to change everyone’s minds on it is because I’d be lying if I said I didn’t have an emotional connection to him before I had fully broken it off. … Things had ended with my ex. So I do carry so much of that guilt because it’s not something I’ve ever done before. I carry a lot of that guilt.”

Cameron broke down the timeline between her and Gallagher leaving season 9 in a good place to them calling it quits off screen, saying, “It’s not like it wasn’t official but we traveled together for six weeks after. I think we went like two weeks without a call and essentially he ghosted me for like five days and then he said like he’d spoken to his ex and they wanted to make it work.”

The pair got back together when Cameron made an appearance in season 10.

“Ultimately I guess they got their fairytale ending from that,” she added. “I guess, I fell in love.”

Cameron accused Gallagher of making her feel “love bombed.” After she found out she was pregnant, Cameron claimed Gallagher made her feel guilty for questioning their future plans.

“But if I ever said‘I don’t know if I can do this. Like, I don’t think it’s the right time.’ He would make me feel very bad about myself. He would tell me, ‘Abortion is wrong and bad.’ He would make me feel very guilty and just terrible about myself. Like I was a bad human being for thinking that way. … I have quite a conservative mum. I couldn’t talk to anyone. So he was kind of my only sounding board because I was so afraid to tell anyone.”

“It was a very rough pregnancy. Like, we were very up and down. We were fighting a lot over very simple things that I did that would seem to trigger him. I think I spent 90 percent of my pregnancy in tears and I think it was very hard for my parents to see,” she recalled. “At first it was very emotional isolation. I would be told that what I said was wrong. And even if I apologized, it was like, ‘You’ve already made me feel this way. So I now will retract my love.’”

Cameron recalled confiding in her mother, adding, “What I learned and what I was told multiple times [by Nathan] is you’re not allowed to speak about your relationship to other people. … We had another really big fight and things had escalated and he’d got on the drink. I was running around Sydney very pregnant, having just finished a full day at work. … I called my best friend and I said, ‘Hey, can you just stay on the phone with me? Nathan’s upset with me at the moment. Can you please just stay on the phone to me?’ And he jumped out of the car. The moving car. So he opened the door, I slammed the brakes and he jumped out.”

She continued: ”The way that I was treated, I would be told to shut the f*** up, I’d be called a bitch, a s***, and all these horrible things. There were multiple times when I was like ‘No no no, this cannot continue because my son cannot grow up thinking that this is how you speak to women.'”

After giving birth to son Kayden, Cameron was asked to move to Croatia while Gallagher filmed Below Deck Med.

“Obviously, I didn’t think it was a good idea to go [to Croatia]. In a perfect world and with a supportive partner, I would 100 percent say, ‘Yep, we can do this, no problem.’ But obviously the situation wasn’t that,” she noted. “And DCJ, which is essentially Child Protective Services, had gotten involved.”

Cameron recalled trying to get help. “The social worker deemed the situation to be unsafe,” she recalled. “I had even gotten onto the phone to one of the producers, and I couldn’t tell him everything. But I was just saying, ‘Look, I’m in a really bad situation and I don’t think it’s a good idea if I go [to Croatia].'”

She continued: “It felt as though he was very worried about who I spoke to or who I was around when he wasn’t around. So it did feel like there was this element of control that he would lose over me if I wasn’t in the same country as him.”

While watching season 11 back, Cameron wasn’t thrilled to serve as the “scapegoat” for Gallagher, sharing, “It was kind of like he was not treating everyone on the boat quite so nicely and the excuse was he was getting a hard time at home, when we were just all getting a hard time.”

Cameron ultimately had to find a way to get her friend to Croatia after Gallagher allegedly isolated her.

“They did offer me to have someone there. They said we will pay for a flight for someone to come and help you [production]. And in discussions with Nathan about me coming there, I was told that I couldn’t have any one of my family or my friends come because he has to feel comfortable also. My options were his sister or his mom,” she noted. “I had to essentially devise this thing with my best friend. … She can’t take too much time off work so I said, ‘OK, how about I get there and then I say that I can’t do it on my own and then maybe ask him in front of people that maybe I need a little bit of help. I know this sounds really bad but it was the only way that I could get the help that I needed.”

Cameron tried to alert a producer to her issues, to which Gallagher responded by “crying.”

“He said, ‘I’m so sad that I made her feel this way.’ And I just sat there in absolute shock and awe,” she recalled. “This was the first time he had ever cried or ever showed any remorse to my sadness or to my hurt. He’s never ever shown. It was always anger towards my sadness.”

Following a tense day, Cameron claimed Gallagher was “angry” she spent time with her family.

“He started calling me certain names. I put my foot down and said I don’t want you sleeping in this room tonight. He came back in, pushed past and he was recording, and this wasn’t the first time he’s done this to me,” she said. “So it wasn’t a surprise where he was shoving himself into the room.”

Cameron alleged that Gallagher would fake being on the receiving end of her abuse on footage he captured, adding, “When he was behind me, holding me, I had a moment where I was like, ‘Why am I allowing this?’ Like, I couldn’t breathe, I couldn’t [do] anything, and all I could do was throw an elbow behind me as fast, as hard as I could just to get out of it. And obviously in this scenario that made him see red and that just escalated things so much more.”

She continued: “When I was in the bathroom and he had his hands around my neck and attempted to lift, my son was in my arms. At that moment, all I could think of was, if I fall, so does he. So in that moment I step up and do anything I could and thank God there’s a toilet behind me. That’s probably the biggest part that I’m like from that moment, I should have known there’s no return.”

Cameron said she was worried she wouldn’t survive the harrowing moment.

“There was a moment when he had me pinned down and he was striking me on the jaw… I actually sat there and I asked if he was going to kill me. There was a moment where I actually asked that question. The reason I say this is because if things had not gotten that bad, I would never have gotten out. That’s the scary thing. As bad as that night was, it wasn’t the worst,” she added. “Me being me, I would have scraped, crawled, gotten myself out of there so much earlier. But the reason I couldn’t was because if I had pushed past and my son would be collateral from that, I would never forgive myself. So I was stuck. When I was able to get out, I ran barefoot down the fire stairs. He chased me all the way down until we got to the first floor. And after that, there’s cameras and he stopped there. I ran straight to my brother’s house who lives just a block away.”

Amid Gallagher’s legal issues, Cameron recalled “paying for hotels” for her then-ex-boyfriend.

“He didn’t have any money,” she claimed. “I was paying him money from what I had to pay for my mortgage and my bills and things like that. But he would still come back to my house anyway. I tried to get out. I tried so hard. The amount of times I said, ‘This is over.’ And the threats that I was met with, I was told that he would destroy me publicly.”

She added: “He chose not being able to come back to the same country that his son lives in over facing the court system. And the thing is, if he had faced this court system, and this is what I’m very shameful to admit, I would have had his back. I did try to change my statement to the police. The police wouldn’t speak to me.”

Cameron “tried everything to support” Gallagher, saying, “Shamefully so, I would have stuck up for him in court. I would have given the police nothing from me as a witness. How sad is that? It’s so sad. And the thing that got me out was him leaving, and it was the biggest weight off my shoulders. All of a sudden, I could have my family over to help me look after my son when I went to work. I could breathe again.”

For Cameron, life has now “felt so bright” without contact with Gallagher.

“I haven’t been in contact with him since he left the country,” she concluded. “I know that he is not afraid to do anything to throw me under. He’s not afraid to lie. He’s always had this idea that he has ownership or control over me. And you know what? I still feel that. And I am still scared of him. And that’s why I’ve not said anything, even though he’s thrown all these accusations out there.”
