Court Grants Request by KJ Biermann's Younger Siblings to Challenge Ruling

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A Georgia court has granted a motion made by Kroy “KJ” Biermann Jr.’s three younger siblings in connection to his sexual battery case.

According to court documents obtained by Us, the Georgia Court of Appeals granted a motion filed by the minor children’s attorney which seeks to stop their forensic interviews from being used in KJ’s sexual battery case.

Prosecutors for KJ’s case filed an emergency motion on September 17 requesting court-ordered forensic interviews of minor children, which was later granted by a judge. On October 15, the matter was placed under review.

Attorneys for KJ and his siblings separately filed applications for interlocutory appeal in the matter. Both were granted on November 12.

KJ, 15, Kim Zolciak and estranged husband Kroy Biermann’s son, was previously arrested in Alpharetta, Georgia, on August 17 for aggravated sodomy and aggravated sexual assault. (He has denied the allegations and pleaded not guilty to all charges.)

A judge later granted KJ a $200,000 bail on September 11. After posting bail, KJ was ordered to wear an electronic ankle monitor under 24-hour house arrest.

Court documents also revealed that KJ would live at Zolciak’s house, with the judge strictly barring any child 16 years or younger — including his own siblings — from staying at the home or visiting the residence.

(In addition to KJ, Zolciak, 48, and Kroy, 41, are parents of son Kash, 14, and twins Kaia and Kane, both 12. Kroy also adopted Zolciak’s eldest daughters from a previous relationship: Brielle, 29, and Ariana, 24.)

As KJ’s case proceeds, his attorney continues to maintain his client’s innocence.

“This is not the kind of information that should be for public dissemination. These are juveniles,” KJ’s attorney Jason Sheffield told Us in an August statement. “Secondly, my client is 100 percent innocent and tried to present evidence to the arresting officer, including a passed polygraph by a notable and respected sex offender polygrapher.”

Sheffield continued, “Additionally, the officer’s report notes the GBI [Georgia Bureau of Investigation] found no male DNA on or inside the areas the alleged victim claimed she was assaulted. Had the officer sat down with me, I feel certain she would’ve never made an arrest.”

In a separate statement, Zolciak denied her son did any wrongdoing .

“The allegations being made against my son are nothing short of reckless and deeply troubling,” she said in August. “He categorically denies ever being involved with this woman or engaging in the conduct she has alleged.”

The former Real Housewives of Atlanta star continued, “We remain confident that the facts and evidence will establish the truth, and we ask that people allow the proper process to take its course rather than spreading unproven allegations.”
