Kim Kardashian Slams Claim She Was 'Rude' While Visiting BF Lewis Hamilton

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kim Kardashian shut down claims she was “rude” while visiting boyfriend Lewis Hamilton at a work event.

In a sneak peek at the Thursday, November 26, episode of The Kardashians, the reality star, 45, was asked about her time at the F1 Grand Prix over the summer, to which she replied, “I would never in a million years show up to my boyfriend’s job and make it all about me and start doing interviews.”

Kim’s response to the rumors came as a screen grab from an article rehashed her being approached by Sky Sports reporter Martin Brundle at the event in Monaco. The journalist’s attempt to capture a pre-race interview took an awkward turn when Kim didn’t respond to his questions before the Formula 1 races.

The interaction caused some critics to label Kim as “rude” while others defended her for keeping the focus on Hamilton, 41. Earlier in the season, Kim spoke about how she manifested her relationship after she collected over 50 attributes that she was looking for in a potential romantic partner.

“Let me just say this, I am just really, really happy,” Kim gushed to the cameras in an October episode. “That’s all I’ll say for now.”

Kim was seen answering a “private call” with Hamilton while filming the reality series. In another confessional, she opened up about finding someone who checked every box.

“Isn’t that crazy? Every single [box is checked]. 55 [of them],” she gushed. “To just look up one day and be like, ‘Wait, let me check the manifest list.’ And then be like, ‘Yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes.’ It is a crazy feeling.”

Kim, who has been linked to the Formula 1 driver since February, previously discussed her attempt to manifest the perfect man.

“[I need him to] protect me [and] fight for me. Good hygiene, and that is a given, so I should maybe take that off. [He should be] calm. No mom or dad issues. [I want someone who is] patient, supportive, genuinely happy for me [and] successful,” she shared in a June 2023 episode of the Hulu series. “[He should be] spontaneous, fun [and] my friends and family love him. [I want] someone that can be a role model for my kids — especially someone my boys can look up to.”

Kim later elaborated on her search for The One.

“I do [have a list]. But I’m also not so delusional to know that someone will check every single box,” she shared in a 2024 episode of SiriusXM’s “This Life of Mine With James Corden” podcast. “[I’m looking for someone who is] accountable for actions. Takes accountability, [has] good teeth, can hold their own and I don’t have to babysit [them] all the time. Just a good, genuine person that’s driven and has their own motivations in life.”

She concluded: “There’ll be moods where I’m just like, ‘You know what? I understand my life is really big and I understand it takes a really, really, really special, unique person to want to deal with that.’ I think my life is really fun and whoever comes into my life will have a really good time, but it’s a lot. I’m not lonely, so I’m good. I have my kids, I have work, I have my family, but it would be nice to share your life with someone, but when you do, it’s such a big … I don’t take that lightly.”
