Donald Trump Reacts to Ex 'DOGE' Leader Elon Musk Claiming He's 'Not MAGA'

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

President Donald Trump is reacting to the former leader of the so-called Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) Elon Musk claiming he’s no longer “MAGA” in the wake of the Republican party’s historic midterm election losses.

“He’s a friend of mine,” Trump, 80, said while speaking to NBC News on Thursday, November 19. “What can I tell you? I mean, you have to ask — there’s a lot of definitions out there. MAGA, to me, means something very specific, and when people veer from it, they don’t do very well.”

The president continued, “But Elon is — Elon’s got very good intentions. I mean, he was with me the whole — he traveled with me.”

After Musk, 55, spent nearly $500 million on the 2026 midterm elections in an attempt to prop up GOP candidates in various states — only to have the Democratic party turn a number of so-called “red” states blue, including Texas, Mississippi, Alaska and more – the once leader of the infamous DOGE initiative denounced the political movement he once shamelessly championed.

“I mean, I’m not MAGA,” the world’s first trillionaire — who earned more than a reported $200 billion in Trump’s first year of his second term and over $913 billion during year two of the former reality TV star’s second stint in the Oval Office — told The New York Times on Wednesday, November 18, during the outlet’s DealBook Summit.

“I’m not sure what MAGA means,” Musk, who campaigned for Trump during the 2024 presidential election on multiple occasions, continued. “But I, I don’t think I’m, like, if, if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican party, then I certainly don’t have that. I think there are, there, there are positives and negatives in both parties.”

Musk went out to express his regret for spending so much money on what proved to be an ultimately failed effort to prop up the Republican party amid the president’s abysmal approval ratings. (Musk reportedly spent $200 million dollars alone on Texas Senate nominee Ken Paxton’s campaign, only for the scandal-ridden politician to ultimately fall short to Democrat James Talarico.)

“I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it,” Musk told columnist Andrew Sorkin. “Or maybe it would’ve been much worse without that. But it certainly shows that American elections are not just about money.”

In the wake of his attempt to separate himself from the MAGA movement, singer Grimes — one of four women whom Musk shares a total of 14 children— denounced the tech giant’s alleged obsession with fake women amid the artificial intelligence boom and multiple outages in the tech, banking and the aviation sector.

“Grimes opposes unelected power, and that includes Mr. Musk’s unelected power,” Grimes’ spokesperson Mitchell Jackson told Us in a recent statement. “Given Mr. Musk’s power, Grimes believes it’s ridiculous that Mr. Musk behaves this way and spends his free time promoting AI slop while needlessly lowering morale and alienating the people.”

Jackson added, “Grimes is disappointed in the lack of maturity and leadership from the tech giants. She will continue to speak out on this important topic and welcomes the discourse surrounding these tweets. Grimes believes we cannot afford any more of this aggressive nihilism from those in power. She will continue to speak out about these important issues.”
