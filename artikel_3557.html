Patriots QB Drake Maye and Wife Ann Michael Maye's Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Drake Maye was one of the first players picked in the 2024 NFL draft, and his then-girlfriend, Ann Michael Maye, was by his side when he joined the New England Patriots.

“Drake, words cannot describe how proud I am of you,” Ann Michael gushed via Instagram in April 2024. “What an incredible blessing it has been to be by your side through all of this❤️ I love you and can’t wait for this next chapter!”

Drake, who previously was the quarterback for the University of North Carolina Tar Heels, was drafted by the Patriots in the first round. While making strides on the football field, Drake and Ann Michael got engaged in January 2025 before tying the knot that June.

The newlyweds had a wild first year of marriage, including Drake leading the Patriots to his first Super Bowl appearance with a win against the Denver Broncos in January 2026. Ann Michael was by her husband’s side every step of the way — including two snow-filled playoffs ahead of the February 2026 Super Bowl, which the team ultimately lost to the Seattle Seahawks.

Keep scrolling for a look at Drake and Ann Michael’s sweet love story:

Drake posted his first Instagram photo with Ann Michael on New Year’s Day in 2020.

“Bringing in ‘20 with a 10🤤,” he captioned a photo of the pair standing cheek to cheek.

“Somewhere on a beach 🏖,” the football player captioned a photo of the couple enjoying a trip to Isle of Palms, South Carolina.

Drake gave Ann Michael a shout-out as she prepared to embark on a new chapter of life.

“Happy 18th to my girlfriend & best friend. Enjoy your day and college move in 🥰I love you❤️,” he wrote alongside two pictures of the couple, one of which was seemingly a prom photo.

The twosome posed by the ocean together in a photo dump shared by Drake.

“Spring Bling✨,” Drake wrote alongside a photo of the duo dressed up for a formal event. The athlete donned a crisp suit with a bowtie while Ann Michael wore a cream-colored, spaghetti-strap dress with a multilayered skirt.

Ann Michael showed her support for Drake ahead of the NFL draft.

“Today’s the day!” she wrote via her Instagram Story alongside an ESPN video of Drake, per Sportskeeda. “So excited for you @drakemaye.”

On the big day, Ann Michael attended the ceremony with Drake when it was revealed he’d be joining the Patriots.

Ann Michael attended one of Drake’s first NFL games. “Just the beginning,” she captioned an Instagram pic of the couple on the sidelines.

The couple got engaged when Drake dropped to one knee in a gorgeous oceanfront proposal.

“Love doing life with you,” Drake shared via Instagram. “Can’t wait to marry you!”

Drake and Ann Michael didn’t waste much time tying the knot, getting hitched six months after their engagement.

“What a night! Cheers to forever🥂I love you!” Drake wrote via Instagram, sharing a series of photos from their ceremony.

“Happy birthday to my better half, my best friend, and lucky enough to call you my wife❤️ I love you,” the NFL player wrote via Instagram on August 11 while celebrating Ann Michael’s birthday.

A few weeks later on August 30, Ann Michael toasted Drake’s birthday, writing via social media, “Happy birthday to my forever person❤️ I love you more than you know and am beyond lucky to be your wife! So thankful I get to do this life with you🥰.”

During the 2025-2026 NFL season Ann Michael started to go viral on TikTok for her cooking skills — especially when her videos featured her husband or were about feeding the football player.

A November 5 video of Ann Michael’s spiced apple cider donut loaf was viewed more than 175,000 times within one week and received more than 550 comments.

“Thank you for taking care of Drake Maye, First Lady of New England,” one fan wrote.

The following month, Ann Michael hosted a “Merry Bakemas” baking countdown where she cooked a sweet treat or bread ahead of the holidays. Drake was a frequent taste tester and guest in the TikTok series.

Drake gave fans a glimpse at his home life with Ann Michael during a January 21 press conference, revealing they are both pretty competitive.

“She started playing mahjong,” Drake said. “I haven’t gotten into that. Something like that. Playing Battleship or cards or anything that goes off of me playing her.”

He noted that the couple picked up playing tennis in the offseason and love to make a game out of it. “She’ll try anything we can compete in. She’s a great athlete,” Drake added.

Later that month, Ann Michael was by Drake’s side when he led the Patriots to a victory over the Denver Broncos in the AFC Championship Game, securing a spot in Super Bowl LX — the first of his career.

While most fans were thrilled to see the Pats make their way back to the big game, others couldn’t stop talking about Drake’s body language towards Ann Michael after the January 25 victory.

“Why does he always seem so standoffish around her? She was so excited,” one user wrote in the comments section after the NFL shared a video of Ann Michael congratulating Drake after the game where he appeared to be looking everywhere but at his wife who is trying to hug him.

A separate user noted, “He looks like he couldn’t care less that she’s there — and this is coming from a die hard patriots fan. It hardly looks like a couple celebrating going to the Super Bowl. Awkward.”

“I’m sure that he loves her. He’s just locked in,” one fan wrote in defense of the player. “He knows that he has one more game to win.”

Ann Michael, for her part, didn’t appear to be phased by the critics, sharing her own celebratory post via Instagram on January 26.

“WE ARE GOING TO THE SUPER BOWL!!!!!❤️💙❤️💙 Words can’t even begin to describe how proud I am of you, #10!!!! Watching your dreams come true is the biggest blessing and I love you!!!!!” she captioned a series of photos with Drake, who looked happy to be by her side.

Drake addressed a question about getting married when he was just 22 years old during Super Bowl media availability, explaining, “My teammates have the right to whatever they choose but I would definitely advise them to eventually get married. It’s one of the best things I’ve experienced in my life. There’s no better feeling than coming home to a wife that loves you and cares for you.”

He continued, “I know my teammates, some of them are experiencing different parts of their life. I chose to get married young, and I don’t regret it one bit.”

That same month, Ann Michael showed Drake some love after the Patriots lost Super Bowl 2026.

“Not how we wanted it to end but still thanking God for this incredible season❤️,” she wrote via Instagram on February 11, sharing photos from the big game. “We are blessed to be surrounded by such an amazing group of people, who made this season what it was. This is only the beginning!!”

After a whirlwind 2025-2026 football season, the Mayes jetted off to Japan for a well-earned vacation. Ann Michael documented their trip via social media in early March.

Drake and Ann Michael headed to Georgia to watch the 2026 Masters Tournament. “I love @themasters ⛳️💚,” she wrote via Instagram alongside several photos on the green with her husband.

Shortly after their 1st wedding anniversary, Ann Michael opened up about the most significant obstacle the couple has been forced to navigate during married life.

“Moving to somewhere so completely different, being away from all of our friends and family is probably the most challenging part,” she exclusively told Us. “Luckily, we have great teammates and great friends here that have really made the New England area feel like home, which is great.”

She continued, “But you know, we’re both from North Carolina. Our parents live in the same neighborhood, and then we went to college in North Carolina, so we were never too far from family. That’s been the biggest adjustment.”

After Drake suffered an ankle injury during the Patriots game against the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany, on November 15, Ann Michael showed her husband support via social media.

“ILY always,” she wrote via her Instagram Story on November 17, alongside a red heart emoji. Ann Michael also shared a photo of the couple kissing before Drake took the field in Europe.

Drake exited the game after being sacked in the first quarter. The team hadn’t revealed his official diagnosis or timeline for recovery at the time of Ann Michael’s post.
