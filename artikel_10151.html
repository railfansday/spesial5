WhatsApp officially adds scheduled messages, and the rollout is scheduled too

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It’s about time. WhatsApp announced today that it is finally adding support for scheduled messages in its app.

A staple of many other messaging apps, WhatsApp is adding scheduled messages to its app. This gives users the ability to write a message but schedule sending it to another user or group chat at a later time.

Scheduled messages on WhatsApp can be sent up to 14 days after you draft them in both 1:1 chats and group chats. You can schedule through a long-press on the send button, with a menu popping up to let you pick the future time when the message will be sent. You can also see scheduled messages before they’re sent to cancel sending or edit the message.

WhatsApp notes that these messages are sent even if your phone’s battery is dead or if the device is off, and everything is still end-to-end encrypted. It works with text, photos, and videos, but apparently not with voice notes.

That said, the rollout is also scheduled out. WhatsApp says the feature will start showing up for users “over the coming weeks” globally, so you might be waiting a bit longer for this long overdue release.
