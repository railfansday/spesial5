‘If this was Liam Rosenior’: Xabi Alonso’s Chelsea future questioned amid Liverpool ‘snub’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Paul Merson has questioned Chelsea’s decision to appoint Xabi Alonso in the summer.

The Blues sank to a new low over the weekend when they were thrashed 4-1 by Leeds United at Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

It saw the west Londoners slump to eighth in the Premier League table, five points off the top four.

They have now registered just one win in their last five games as pressure piles on Alonso in his first term in the hotseat.

On Sky Sports, Merson, the former Arsenal striker, disputed the Spaniard’s appointment and rhetorically asked why Liverpool had not hired their former midfielder, despite him being a free agent and Arne Slot being sacked at the end of the season.

"My biggest worry is that Liverpool could have had Alonso and they chose not to,” he said.

“Obviously, Liverpool didn't think he was good enough.

"Alonso took the Chelsea job, and Liverpool sacked Slot two weeks later. You can't tell me that Liverpool couldn't have spoken to Alonso and say: 'Don't take the Chelsea job, we're going to take you'.

"He was out of a job as well, Liverpool could have had him and he'd have gone there all day long. So why didn't they take him? Did they know something that we're seeing now?

"It just always sits in my mind. That was my worry - and now it's playing out.”

Merson compared Alonso to his predecessor, Liam Rosenior, who was dismissed after just three months in charge, having lost five consecutive league games without scoring - the club’s first time since 1912.

"If this was Rosenior in charge at Chelsea and getting these results, he'd be in major trouble,” Merson continued.

“100 per cent, he'd be in trouble. And remember, Rosenior didn't have Morgan Rogers in his team, bought for £116million.

"But it's because of the unbelievable job [Alonso] did at Bayer Leverkusen that everybody is thinking: ‘It will be alright."
