Vic Fangio sends vote of confidence to Cowboys’ Christian Parker after losing playcalling

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dallas Cowboys defensive coordinator Christian Parker may have lost his playcalling duties, but not the confidence of at least one fellow defensive coordinator in him.

Speaking to reporters on Wednesday, Philadelphia Eagles DC Vic Fangio shared his thoughts on Parker, who will not be calling plays for the Cowboys defense after losing the duties to senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine.

The 68-year-old Fangio even cited examples to support his belief that everything will be just fine for Parker, who is in only his first year as a defensive coordinator in the NFL.

"Well, patience is not in overabundance in today's society, especially in the sports world," Fangio said, via a video shared by Eagles reporter Eliot Shorr-Parks of 94WIP.

"But, Christian will recover from that. You know, it happened to Sean Payton many years ago in New York. But I think he's recovered nicely. You know, Joe Brady, who's the head coach of Buffalo, he got fired during the season, then he kept on. I think he's recovered nicely. Mike LaFleur in Arizona, the Jets fired him during the season a couple of years ago. He's recovered nicely. Christian will do the same thing. He'll be fine."

Fangio also has some familiarity with Parker, and vice versa. Before he was hired by the Cowboys, Parker was with the Eagles from 2024 to 2025, serving in the roles of passing game coordinator & defensive backs coach.

The latest development in Parker's role in managing the Cowboys' defense came on the heels of Dallas' 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers in a Week 10 game at AT&T Stadium in Arlington last Sunday, in which Christian McCaffrey and company went off for 584 total yards.
