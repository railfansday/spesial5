George Pickens the Cowboys' shining light for a change in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cowboys wideout George Pickens finally broke out catching 11 passes for 149 yards while recording his first, three touchdown game of his career. For a moment, it appeared as if Dallas was going to be able to outscore San Francisco and upset the team with the best record in the NFL.

On his 35-yard TD reception, Pickens surpassed 2,000 receiving yards becoming only the third Cowboys WR to go over that milestone within their first 26 games joining Terrell Owens (2,208) and Bob Hayes (2,136). In his Dallas career, Pickens now has 143 receptions for 2,111 yards and 14 touchdowns.

This season, his 678 receiving yards and five receiving touchdowns rank twelfth in the NFL. His 11 receptions against San Francisco was the first time this season Pickens has caught double digit passes and his 145 receiving yards was only the second time he has gone for over 100+ yards this season.

Pickens becomes an unrestricted free agent at season ends but his taunting penalty with 1:25 remaining in the fourth quarter is exactly the reason why Dallas, or other teams, may be hesitant to hand out massive amounts of money for a player who has shown to have mental lapses.
