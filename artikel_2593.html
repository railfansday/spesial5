Falcons vs. Chiefs: What will Week 10’s win or loss mean for Atlanta?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Falcons cap off a very eventful week which saw them trade away major contributors on both offense and defense in Jessie Bates III and Ryan Neuzil, with a meeting against the team that built the dynasty of the modern NFL in the Kansas City Chiefs.

Atlanta is currently in a unique situation, sitting in between playoff contention due to their pathetic division and a top 10 pick, and their decision to ship out contributors indicates that they have no delusions on the present as one eye firmly stays looking toward the future.

With the bye week ahead, the Birds have an opportunity to really lock in on having the best of both worlds with a victory, and possibly a more gleaming future with continued losses. It's a rare scenario where the perspective can be that of glass half full either way.

Here is the scenario the Falcons face if they beat the Kansas City Chiefs in Week 10, as well as if they lose.

They'll stamp in another victory against an opponent they were wholly unlikely to defeat, as their resume will have very impressive victories over the Packers, 49ers and now Chiefs up until this point. Their continued identity of showing up against the good teams will be solidified.

It's hard to believe, sitting here at 3-6 and in the basement of the NFC South, but the Falcons would put themselves in a decent position in their division going into the bye week. With Tampa on a bye, Atlanta would move ahead of them and they would jump the loser of the Saints – Panthers matchup this weekend to control sole possession of second place, just 0.5 games back of first.

The odds won't shift much in either direction for Atlanta as far as the playoffs are concerned, according to playoffstatus.com. They currently sit at 17%, a win would increase that to 21% regardless of other outcomes around the NFL.

The Falcons would break a three game losing streak at the hands of the Chiefs, earning their first victory in this matchup since 2012.

They would go into the bye week likely with an even greater eye toward the future. I would predict that coming out on the other side we would see the likes of Aveion Terrell, Billy Bowman and other young pieces be given more of an opportunity over veterans.

They would remain in play in the division no matter what, sitting at 1.5 games back of either the Saints or the Panthers. If Panthers are victorious and if they win their matchup again next week while Atlanta is on the bye, the Birds would find themselves at a full 2.0 games back with no tiebreaker essentially needing to make up three full games in the second half of the season. With games against the Lions and Vikings on the other side, the playoff dream is likely to be slipping quickly.

The playoff odds would decrease from 17% to a meager 13% and then even lower than that depending on what happens next week during the bye.

As unlikely as it may be, a Cardinals victory against the Rams would see Atlanta's pick at minimum reach as high as 8th at the end of this week.
