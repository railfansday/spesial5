Fantasy Football Start 'Em, Sit 'Em: RBs in timeshares who can help your lineup in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Let's take a look at the backup RBs who have a Yahoo roster rate under 60% and could help fantasy football teams to victory in Week 12. Players are ranked in order of their Week 12 appeal, and their current roster rates are listed in parentheses.

The Bucs had to vacate their preferred run-heavy game plan when they fell behind to the Lions in Week 11. But Gainwell continued to find ways to contribute, gaining 57 total yards on four carries and four catches.

Bucky Irving continues to be the unquestioned RB1 in this offense. But the Bucs WRs have disappointed this season, which has opened an opportunity for Gainwell to be more involved in the passing attack. He would normally rank a bit lower on this list but is a viable flex option for a Week 12 matchup against a Panthers defense that has allowed the third-most fantasy points per game to opposing RBs.

Hill is starting to turn heads as a viable weapon in Baltimore's offense. The 29-year-old was as dangerous as any Ravens skill player in Week 11, when he tallied 69 yards and a touchdown on six carries while also gaining 27 yards on four catches. Hill has caught at least three passes in each of his past six games and has amassed at least 69 rushing yards in three of his past seven contests. He could be a viable flex option no matter what the game script looks like for the Ravens. After all, while franchise QB Lamar Jackson was likely to tuck the ball and run, new starter Jacoby Brissett is more likely to dump it off. Hill is heading into a Week 12 matchup against an average Texans defense.

Johnson is sitting on waivers in most leagues, as many managers have forgotten that he produced 110 rushing yards and a touchdown in the Packers' two games before their Week 11 bye. With Josh Jacobs expected to miss another game with a broken finger, Johnson should lead the team in carries during their Week 12 matchup against the Rams on Wednesday night. There are two things preventing Johnson from ranking at the top of this list – he has no involvement in the passing game, and the Rams have allowed the second-fewest fantasy points per game to opposing RBs.

Perine outperformed Chase Brown on Monday Night Football, gaining 97 total yards on six carries and four catches, in comparison to 36 total yards on six carries and five catches. Although Brown has received a little more than double the carries of Perine this season, both backs are averaging 3.8 yards per carry, while Perine has averaged nearly twice as many yards per catch. After a strong effort in Week 11, Perine could be used more than normal in Week 12, when the Bengals host a Saints defense that has allowed the second-most fantasy points per game to opposing RBs.

Knight did his part to keep the Cardinals offense afloat in a difficult Week 11 road game with Josh Johnson at QB by gaining 35 yards on seven carries and catching his only target for a 10-yard gain. The injury-riddled Cardinals desperately need a more reasonable matchup, which they will get in a home game against the Commanders in Week 12. While the fantasy upside of any member of Arizona's offense remains limited by the QB situation, Knight is the favorite to lead Arizona in touches for that game.

Although Rodriguez wasn't called upon often in the Jags' loss to the Giants in Week 11, he produced 41 total yards on four catches and one catch. The former Commander would normally rank lower on this list, but he and the rest of Jacksonville's offense have tremendous potential for their Week 12 home matchup against the Titans.

Since trading Jeffery Simmons on Oct. 29, the Titans have allowed an average of 45 points over three games. Rodriguez should get more touches than usual if the Jags jump out to a big lead. It's also worth noting that Bhayshul Tuten was banged up during the Week 11 loss. If the Jaguars opt to rest Tuten for a game in which they are two-touchdown favorites, Rodriguez would jump to the very top of this list.

In four games since returning from a season-opening stint on injured reserve, Sampson has had a clear role as the Browns' pass-catching back. The second-year pro has averaged five carries and four catches per game, and with 4.2 yards per carry and 9.5 yards per catch, he is making the most of his opportunities. Sampson should be able to approach 10 points in PPR formats during his Week 12 game against a Raiders defense that has allowed the fifth-most fantasy points per game to RBs.

After gaining at least 50 yards on the ground in three consecutive games, Black had a dismal night (six carries, eight yards) in the 49ers' lopsided win over a stout Vikings defense. One bad game isn't enough to knock the rookie off this list, as he still ranks 30th in the NFL in rushing yards and has earned at least six carries in each of his past seven games. While Black remains on the radar for flex consideration, it's worth noting that he is heading into another tough matchup when the Niners host the Seahawks in Week 12.

The bad news for Washington is that Week 11 served as a redemption game for Ashton Jeanty, who averaged 7.2 yards on 11 carries while continuing to be a weapon out of the backfield. Fortunately, Washington had a solid game of his own, averaging 4.7 yards on nine carries. Washington will remain low on this list each week due to his lack of involvement in the passing game, but he still gets 6-10 carries in almost every game.

The good news is that Kamara has scored three times in the past two weeks. The bad news is that he has gained just 16 total yards in those two contests. The Saints clearly trust Kamara in the red zone, which gives him some potential in deep leagues. And perhaps he will get more opportunities overall when the Saints face the Bengals in a possible Week 12 shootout.
