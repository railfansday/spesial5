NHL Power Rankings: Hurricanes mowing down everyone in their path on quest for Stanley Cup repeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

I don't know whether the Carolina Hurricanes have an ample supply of ibuprofen or Pedialyte, but there has been no Stanley Cup hangover in Raleigh. In fact, the reigning champions look even better than they did at this time last year.

The Hurricanes own the NHL's best record at 16-3-2, and they're outpacing last year's performance, even when you factor in two extra regular season games. Carolina played an 84-game pace of 115 points last season. The team is tracking for 136 points in 2026-27.

It's not just that the Canes are winning. It's how they're winning. Carolina is bludgeoning its opponents to the tune of a plus-40 goal differential, which leads the league. The next closest team is the Edmonton Oilers at plus-30.

If you're looking for an area in which the Canes don't excel, good luck. They rank second in goals scored (87), first in goals allowed (47), eighth in power play (25.4%) and fifth in penalty kill (85.9%). Under the hood, Carolina is far and away the best team in five-on-five expected goals share at 62.0%.

Concerns about goaltending have been quelled by Pyotr Kochetkov, who's saved 7.64 goals above average while posting a .920 save percentage. Up front, Carolina's scoring depth is unmatched, and the defensive corps is nearly impenetrable.
