Has J.K. Dobbins played his final game for the Broncos?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

How rare is it for the Denver Broncos to suspend its own player for conduct detrimental to the team? The last such case I could find of this happening was back in August 2009 when Brandon Marshall was suspended by Josh McDaniels. If memory serves me, he punted the football out of practice to show his displeasure with McDaniels and the coaching staff. Marshall did end up playing the whole season that year, but McDaniels traded him soon into the 2010 offseason.

Given that's all the history we have to go on, perhaps J.K. Dobbins' tenure with the Broncos in 2026 isn't done for good and this one game suspension is exactly that: one game.
