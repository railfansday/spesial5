Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Golden State Warriors are looking to build on Sunday's momentum, after they snapped a five-game losing streak with a shocking victory over the Boston Celtics. The good news for the Warriors is that they're facing the 2-15 Sacramento Kings. The bad news for the Warriors is that they're still not very healthy.

Here's the full injury report for both squads.

The Warriors remain optimistic that Curry's injury won't linger for too much longer, but there's still no timeline for a return.

Same stuff as usual with this trio. Nothing of note!

Questionable — Brandon Williams (left hamstring strain)

The Warriors have to wait a while longer for Curry, but thankfully may be getting Williams back. Williams had a very honest assessment of how Golden State's offense has looked without their two-time MVP, and hopefully his lightning bolt scoring off the bench can help fix that.

Needless to say, the Warriors will be in much worse shape if their star rookie is unable to play toinght. Here's hoping he's good to go.

Out — Ben Simmons (right Achilles tendon repair surgery)

Count me among those who were very curious to see how Simmons would look with the Kings this year. What a bummer to have him suffer a severe injury in training camp.

Out — Maxime Raynaud (right patella subluxation)

After a very impressive rookie season, Raynaud has yet to make his sophomore debut. Thankfully the second-round pick should be back on the court before too long.

Questionable — Zach LaVine (right knee soreness)

LaVine can still fill up the points column when he wants to, though he's openly ceded first option status to rookie Darius Acuff Jr. Even so, the two-time All-Star is averaging 20.3 points per game, and if he's unable to play tonight, that will be a big hit for Sacramento.

Enjoy the game, Dub Nation. It tips off at 7:00 p.m. PT on NBC Sports Bay Area.
