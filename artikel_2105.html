Arsenal vs Hull City preview: Arteta sticks with winning formula as Gunners target another three points

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Arsenal have resisted the temptation to rotate after their victory at Anfield, with Mikel Arteta naming the same starting XI for Saturday’s meeting with Hull City.

Arsenal welcome Championship side Hull City to the Emirates Stadium, looking to strengthen their grip on the top spot after collecting 24 points from their opening nine Premier League games. However, their tally of just 14 goals suggests Arteta’s side still have work to do in the final third.

Arteta has again chosen to partner Ezri Konsa and Gabriel Magalhães in defence. William Saliba was left out despite pre-match talk that he would be named in the starting XI. Bukayo Saka and Max Dowman are also missing from the squad.

Noni Madueke will carry the right. Tzolis starts on the opposite flank. Kai Havertz will lead the line. Viktor Gyökeres, Eberechi Eze and Mikel Merino add attacking options from the bench.

Arsenal predicted starting XI: Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Guimarães, Rice, Ødegaard; Madueke, Havertz, Tzolis.

Substitutes: Arrizabalaga, White, Mosquera, Hincapié, Lewis-Skelly, Zubimendi, Merino, Eze, Gyökeres.

Hull are coming into this game ranked 11th in the league after five draws in their first nine games. They’ve not won in their last eight games but can cause problems for any opposition.

They look like playing a defensive game with Slater, Iroegbunam and Gourna-Douath in the midfield to protect the backline. McBurnie and Cho will give them some attacking options.

Hull starting XI: Tzolakis; Coyle, Ajayi, Egan, Mendy, Stroud; Slater, Iroegbunam, Gourna-Douath; McBurnie, Cho.

Substitutes: Butland, Giles, Thomas, Gelhardt, Hjerto-Dahl, Norton-Cuffy, Herrington, Mouzakitis, Vaz.

McBurnie is just one booking away from suspension for the clash against Brighton on November 22 but may make an important impact in the physical battle with Gabriel.

Hull have not scored at the Emirates since 2014 but will look to turn that around. However, their main concern will be to ensure they keep Arsenal quiet for as long as possible.

Arsenal will need to find ways to break down the likely tight Hull backline.

Ødegaard and Rice will be key in keeping possession and creating chances, whilst Madueke and Tzolis will create width and movement.

Hull may be exposed to threat in case they are trapped in defence for too long, but Arteta can use Gyökeres if Havertz is having difficulties doing anything productive.

A win for Arsenal will give them a six-point lead. Manchester City will be looking to get all three points when they face Nottingham Forest later on Saturday.

The visitors will look to create an uncomfortable afternoon for the Gunners.

Arsenal have enough quality to take all three points, but Hull will not make it easy for them. I expect this to be a tight game, and the Gunners will win by a small margin.
