Southampton will regret missing out on transfer deal for 25-year-old to Fulham: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Southampton were reportedly keen on luring the defender to St Mary’s in the last transfer window. However, they were beaten to the punch in the end by Premier League side Fulham.

Affengruber, 25, was snapped up by the London outfit on a four-year contract running until June 2028. He has since made seven league appearances for the Whites and has become a regular in their starting XI over recent weeks under the guidance of their head coach Alvaro Arbeloa.

The Austria international, who has three caps to his name so far in his career, was identified by the Saints as a potential replacement for Taylor Harwood-Bellis, who was sold to Aston Villa. However, a move to Hampshire wasn’t to be and Tonda Eckert’s side weren’t able to get a deal over the line in the end.

Southampton will rue not being able to land Affengruber following Keven Schlotterbeck’s recent injury that will see him ruled out for four to six weeks. They are now short of options at the back as they look to close the gap between them in the play-offs and the top two.

Southampton will have seen Affengruber as someone to bolster their defensive department. If he had joined them, he would have given them more competition and depth in his position.

The Saints will have to make do without Schlotterbeck over the coming weeks which isn’t ideal. Either Nottingham Forest loan man Zach Abbott or Nathan Wood will need to partner Jack Stephans in the heart of their backline.

Affengruber would have got plenty of game time under Eckert if he had linked up with the Championship side. He initially had to be patient at Fulham for his opportunity but he has now cemented his place in their team.

The Red Bull Salzburg academy graduate made his name at Sturm Graz. He then spent two years in Spain at Elche and played 84 games altogether, chipping in with two goals.

Southampton should sign a new defender in January. Affengruber is off the cards though after his recent emergence into the Fulham team.

Stephans isn’t getting any younger, Abbott could be recalled by Nottingham Forest and Wood has seen his minutes dry up. Therefore, they need someone to come in and perform next to Schlotterbeck when he returns.

The Saints lost 1-0 away at Preston North End last time out. They face Derby County at home after the international break.
