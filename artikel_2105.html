Crystal Palace twist hands major boost to Steve Gibson and Middlesbrough

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It has been an outstanding start to the season for Middlesbrough in the Championship.

After losing to Hull City in last season's play-off final, Middlesbrough have started strongly as they look to bounce back from their Wembley heartbreak, and head coach Kim Hellberg has earned plenty of admirers for his work at the Riverside Stadium this campaign.

Middlesbrough spent big in the transfer window this summer as they look to finally secure a long-awaited return to the Premier League, and striker Will Lankshear was the club's marquee signing as he joined from Tottenham Hotspur for an initial fee of around £10 million, plus a further £10 million in add-ons.

Lankshear was not the only headline addition of the summer for Boro, with the likes of Radek Vitek, Ashley Phillips, Max Arfsten, Sebastian Berhalter, Rokas Pukstas and Kyle Joseph also arriving for significant transfer fees, and the club's ambitious squad rebuild has certainly had the desired effect so far.

It was a disappointing week for Boro before the international break as they picked up just one point from two away games at Wrexham and Watford, but while they were knocked off top spot on goal difference by Birmingham City, they currently sit second in the Championship table.

However, it seems Boro's impressive form has had some unwanted consequences for the Teesside outfit, with reports emerging over the weekend claiming that Hellberg was on the radar of top flight side Crystal Palace as they prepared to part company with Pierre Sage.

Hellberg was not the only name linked with Crystal Palace, with Thomas Frank, Frank Lampard, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira, Vitor Matos, Tony Popovic, Jens Berthel Askou and Ole Werner also believed to have been under consideration.

After a poor start to the season that has left them bottom of the Premier League table, the Eagles confirmed Sage's departure on Thursday after just 16 games in charge, but while Middlesbrough may have feared that the Frenchman's exit would lead to an approach for Hellberg, it seems that will not be the case.

Frank was understood to be the leading contender to replace Sage, and Palace are now reportedly in advanced talks with the former Brentford and Tottenham Hotspur boss about taking over at Selhurst Park, albeit a deal has not yet been finalised.

Given that Frank is out of work and has Premier League experience under his belt, it is easy to see why the Eagles have opted for the Dane in their current predicament, but the fact that Hellberg's name was mentioned shows that his reputation in English football is growing.

Having lost Rob Edwards to Wolverhampton Wanderers just before the November international break last season, Middlesbrough owner Steve Gibson could have been forgiven for fearing the worst when the speculation arose linking Hellberg with Crystal Palace.

In an eerie similarity to the present situation, Boro were also second in the Championship table last November when Edwards made the controversial decision to swap the Riverside Stadium for Molineux after just 16 games in charge, but it appears that history will not be repeating itself.

While Edwards was widely criticised for his response when quizzed about the Wolves job prior to his eventual departure, infamously stating that his daughter had informed him of the rumours when they first surfaced, Hellberg immediately dismissed any talk about him making the switch to Selhurst Park and insisted that he remained fully committed to Boro.

Earlier this week, when asked by Swedish outlet Fotbollskanalen how he felt about the Palace reports, Hellberg replied: "Absolutely nothing, absolutely nothing.

"It's always nice to be on lists like that, if that's the case — it shows you've done something right. But I think it's mostly just talk, and, for me, it's all about doing a good job here.

"We actually have a chance to get promoted this year. We feel that there is a chance, but then it's clear that it will be difficult.

"We are one of five, six teams, maybe not the biggest favourites, but, behind them, I think we have a good chance.

"My focus is here and hopefully we can get up, that would have been fantastic of course."

While Hellberg's loyalty to Boro has ultimately not been tested by any formal approach from the Eagles, it is clear that the 38-year-old's head has not been turned by the gossip about his future, and his comments will be music to Gibson's ears after his frustrating experience with Edwards.

Hellberg has become a hugely popular figure on Teesside over the past year, and he is regarded as one of the most exciting managers outside the Premier League, having built a team that plays an attractive, attacking brand of football, even if Boro have been a little vulnerable defensively at times this season.
