West Brom officially announce Shilen Patel action and it's triggered a response

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

West Bromwich Albion's impressive start to the Championship season has now got even better in the eyes of the club's supporters, courtesy of a major off-field development involving owner, Shilen Patel.

The American businessman took control of the Baggies for £60m in February 2024, bringing a welcomed end to Guochuan Lai's controversial tenure in The Hawthorns boardroom.

Not all has been straightforward for the Black Country outfit since then, with activity linked to former ownership groups proving to be a strong stumbling block in Patel's aims of moving the club forward.

Albion narrowly avoided a first relegation to League One in 33 years last season through a strong end-of-season run under James Morrison, who has since vindicated Patel's decision to make his managerial role permanent with his side sat fifth after 15 games played, just one point off the Championship summit.

Supporters are now believing a bright future for their club is ensured after confirmation that Patel has paid off an outstanding £3.7m loan was confirmed on Wednesday afternoon.

West Brom were hit with a two-point deduction in May for breaching the EFL's PSR regulations, which left many supporters perplexed given the high-profile sales of Torbjørn Heggem and Tom Fellows in 2025.

12 months previous, it was confirmed Patel would pay off the aforementioned loan, which was taken out by West Bromwich Football Holdings in 2014 by former owner Jeremy Peace, after an investigation saw the liability of the funds passed to his company, Bilkul Football WBA.

"At that time, the club confirmed arrangements had been put in place to ensure it would not sustain any loss in respect of the loan," a statement on Albion's website read.

"The club can confirm the outstanding liability, including interest, has now been repaid in full by the current ownership group."

This isn't the first loan Patel has paid off during his tenure, having wiped out an outstanding sum to Wisdom Smart Corporation Limited in March 2024, although a further loan to MSD Holdings is still outstanding.

Nevertheless, supporters in B71 have been quick to hail the 46-year-old's work, with one saying: "Saved the club when it was at its lowest point. Top people who deserve all the respect possible from fans. Here's to the future."

Another Baggies fan wrote: "We should forever be grateful to Shilen Patel and the ownership group. I don't know where they will eventually take us, but whatever happens in the future they deserve our patience and support."

"Words won't ever express what Shilen has done for us, from the worst excuse for an owner to someone like him who cares and loves the club and has saved us along the way, forever grateful," further summed up the feeling Albion's fanbase have towards the Florida-born entrepreneur.

"Another huge weight lifted off the shoulders of the club. Grateful to Bilkul and Shilen," was another comment full of praise.

Summing up the loan payment in a rather humourous way, one Albion fan added: "BILKUL AND PATELLLLL 90+12 years."

Another posted: "This is brilliant from Shilen. But the next statement in Dec saying the MSD loan is fully paid off is gonna be unreal!! Cannot thank Shilen for saving our club and giving us a direction!"

With West Brom returning to action this weekend following the international break, their supporters will certainly be in a jubilant mood as they prepare to take on Swansea City in South Wales, with their hosts seemingly in disarray as Vitor Matos nears his expected move to RC Strasbourg.

Albion's future is certainly in safe hands right now, though, with Patel finally able to repay the aforementioned loan, whilst the transfer strategy adopted in the summer means there are several names within Morrison's ranks capable of delivering in the here and now before potentially being sold on for millions.
