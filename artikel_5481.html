TheHolyCoins Launches Crypto Presale ROI Dashboard to Track Token Performance, Revealing 99.34% Average Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The crypto presale ROI tracker gives investors one place to compare token prices, track returns and assess the risks of new token sales.

TEL AVIV, Israel, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- TheHolyCoins (https://theholycoins.com) has launched a crypto presale ROI dashboard tracking 48 projects, with an initial dataset showing a fundraising-weighted average decline of 99.34% from final presale prices. The dashboard helps investors examine what happened to tokens after previous sales before committing money to new ones. TheHolyCoins plans to expand coverage beyond the initial 48 projects.

The crypto presale performance tracker lets users compare final presale prices with current market prices, alongside reported funds raised, all-time highs and listing dates. Investors can search individual projects, filter by category and sort the results to see which tokens have retained value and which have collapsed.

BlockDAG and Pepe Unchained illustrate the scale. The tracker records $440 million in reported fundraising for BlockDAG and $73.9 million for Pepe Unchained. Those two projects alone account for more than half a billion dollars in reported fundraising. Their performance gives investors researching new crypto presales a reason to look beyond fundraising milestones and projected listing prices.

The list includes apparent scams alongside stalled projects. Bitcoin Pepe's team disappeared amid complaints of undelivered tokens, while Qubetics halted core development after reporting over $18 million raised. Others, including Pepe Unchained, continue developing products despite steep price declines and exchange delistings.

"Retail investors can face experienced developers and professional marketers who know how to sell the promise of getting in early," said Shay Feldman, editor-in-chief of TheHolyCoins. "Influencer endorsements, countdowns and ambitious price predictions can make a speculative token sale look like an opportunity investors cannot afford to miss. We built this dashboard so people can check what happened after previous presales ended, before putting their money into another one."

Coverage focuses on presales conducted from 2021 onward, primarily those raising approximately $1 million or more, with selected smaller projects included. Prices are sourced mainly from CoinGecko, while fundraising information comes from public materials and reporting.

Returns compare market prices with final presale prices; individual investor outcomes depend on entry prices and selling decisions. Calculations exclude vesting schedules, liquidity depth and OTC transactions. The dataset covers selected projects, rather than the entire crypto presale market.
