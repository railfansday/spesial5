GTA 6: Should You Kill Or Spare Arnold

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

GTA 6, like many other Grand Theft Auto games before it, offers several missions with a choice. Generally, it's "should you kill or spare someone," but that choice alone can feel huge in that moment. I can't tell you how many times I've known people who have paused the action and looked up the consequences of a choice.

That said, in GTA 6, not every choice is as heavy as it seems. Here's what you should do about Arnold, and whether you should kill or spare him early in the game, when you're just getting your bearings as Jason in the Florida Keys.

Early on in GTA 6, you'll meet Brian, the character who's essentially your first crime boss. Brian runs a marina out in the Florida Keys, getting up to all sorts of debauchery facilitated through his vehicles and associates. As Jason starts to become more and more entwined with Brian's interests, he'll start asking him to do bigger and bigger jobs.

One of those jobs deals with Brian's old friend Arnold. Brian and Arnold have had a working relationship for many years, but now that Arnold has stopped answering Brian's calls, it's clear something is going on. Brian asks him to investigate the issue on-site, leading to a confrontation with Arnold and his crew.

A car chase ensues, and Jason is quickly pulled into an out-of-control situation, chasing Arnold across dangerous bridges and into a neighborhood. Depending on how the chase goes, Arnold generally gets out of his car and starts running on foot, and Brian gives him the go-ahead to "take care of him."

Here's where the choice comes in. You can either let Arnold get away, or take him out from afar and end the mission right there.

Killing him will result in a "hardened" boost to your criminal meter, which can be good if you're trying to roleplay that type of Jason, or bad if you're not. Either way, it's functionally negligible, so if you prefer to handle the mission this way, this is one of the fastest methods. Once Arnold is taken out, you'll head back to meet up with another associate of Brian's, who will herald in a new deep-sea-diving mission type.

Letting him get away will have no negative impact on the storyline, and you won't "ruin" your relationship with Brian. In fact, Arnold will make an appearance later on, so you can get a better sense of what kind of character he is, rather than taking Brian's word at face value based on the 30 seconds you see him. In any case, it's OK to make either choice, and you won't need to reload a save or anything like that.

These kinds of choices are generally framed as big gravity-shifting alterations to the story of GTA 6, but that's not often the case. Don't stress too much about making decisions regarding minor characters, because the focus really is on Jason and Lucia. Their relationship and their individual hardened criminal profiles are the factors that will influence the dialogue and the story the most as you progress.
