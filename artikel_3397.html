12 Years Later, Star Wars Officially Crowns Its Canon Mara Jade Replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Warning! This post contains SPOILERS for Star Wars: The Fall of Kylo Ren #4

Star Wars has officially crowned one of the best canon replacements for Mara Jade. Although it's extremely unlikely we'll ever see Luke Skywalker's wife reintegrated into the official timeline and continuity, a brand-new character may be the next best thing in the canonical Star Wars galaxy.

First appearing in Timothy Zahn's Heir to the Empire novel, Mara Jade was one of the most popular Expanded Universe characters before it all became non-canon Legends. A former assassin for Emperor Palpatine, Mara Jade eventually turned to the light and married Luke Skywalker himself during the New Republic Era.

Now, Charles Soule and Will Sliney's recent Fall of Kylo Ren series has cemented the idea that Tava Ren is essentially the best replacement we may ever get for Mara Jade in the official Star Wars canon, especially after the newly released Fall of Kylo Ren #4.

Teeing up Episode IX, The Fall of Kylo Ren #4 features major revelations about how the Knights of Ren reunited with their former leader between The Last Jedi and The Rise of Skywalker.

In Charles Soule's previous Legacy of Vader series, it was confirmed that Supreme Leader Snoke ordered Kylo to abandon the Knights before The Force Awakens. From that point, the dark side faction would be led by a new character known as Tava Ren. Following an initial encounter with Kylo Ren in Legacy of Vader, and the start of a rather flirtatious rivalry between herself and Kylo Ren, Tava and her Knights have since returned in the new Fall of Kylo Ren series.

In The Fall of Kylo Ren #4, Kylo had no choice but to help Tava destroy a powerful assassin droid hell-bent on killing all of the Knights after they claimed a powerful artifact known as the Umbral Shard, as Tava intentionally put the Supreme Leader in the droid's sights as well after gifting him the artifact. In the aftermath of the droid's eventual destruction, the Knights offer to renew their loyalties and serve Kylo Ren once more, motivating Tava to reject them all, determined to rebuild the Knights of Ren as they once were rather than a "shattered reflection."

Surprisingly, Kylo agrees with her assessment and lets Tava go, identifying her as the last true Knight of Ren while still intending to let the former Knights serve him in Rise of Skywalker.

All things considered, it's pretty remarkable that we now have a canonical red-haired, Force-using warrior assassin who also has a flirtatious relationship with a Skywalker. She may not be exactly like Legends' Mara Jade, but there are certainly enough similarities to make Tava Ren's future very interesting.

Clearly, Tava Ren's story is far from over. On this issue's first page, Tava was shown watching a mysterious holo-recording of a more ancient-looking Ren than audiences have seen before, supporting her claim to Kylo later on in the issue that she now knows what the Knights of Ren are supposed to be. As such, it stands to reason that Tava will be looking to create a truer Knights of Ren in the post-Rise of Skywalker galaxy, which is a super exciting prospect to look forward to.

With Kylo Ren's death in Episode IX, Tava could very well serve as a key bridge between the sequel trilogy and whatever comes next for the galaxy far, far away.

Star Wars: The Fall of Kylo Ren #4 is on sale now from Marvel Comics.
