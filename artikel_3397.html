All 9 Major New & Upcoming KPop Demon Hunters Releases, From New 6-Episode Series To A Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

With very little advanced hype, KPop Demon Hunters changed everything for Netflix in a way that made their lack of preparedness look like a mistake. The animated musical is now Netflix’s biggest movie of all time, of course, and yet neither the streaming giant nor Sony were confident enough to have plans to expand the franchise in place.

There have been other KPop Demon Hunters releases since, of course, including books, trading cards, a long-awaited LEGO set, and video games, but the bigger releases won’t start landing until 2027, by which time it’ll be two years since the original movie released. And it’ll take even longer for the sequel and a reunion with Huntrix (and possibly The Saja Boys).

With KPop Demon Hunters fans still hungrily running to pick up new releases and find out news about other upcoming drops, now’s the perfect time to fill you in on what’s just arrived and what’s coming right up to 2029.

By far the most exciting upcoming release for KPop Demon Hunters fans is the sequel, but unfortunately, it's going to take an awful long time to get here. When KPop Demon Hunters 2 was quickly announced after the success of the first movie, early reports claimed that the studio's goal was a 2029 release. While it's good news that it's happening, having to wait another 3 years for it feels like torture.

Beyond that, fans can probably look forward to more adventures and possibly spin-offs, especially if the studio heeds the excitement for Sajja Boys' project. Again, we're talking about a long time in the future here. It seems likely that Netflix will fill the gap with more shorts and more releases, release events like merch drops, and perhaps more books, until eventually we get to 2029.

Details on the sequel are somewhat understandably absent, but it would seem inevitable that Huntrex will return, that they will face another battle to save Earth, and that Rumi's backstory will be further explored. Hopefully, we'll also see the return of Geno, at the very least, to rekindle his romance with Rumi. Of course, if the first movie is anything to go by, we'll also get a new crop of addictive songs.

A little over a year after the arrival of the first movie, Netflix introduced a brand-new hub page for KPop Demon Hunters with new content. As well as hosting the movie and the sing-along version, the hub also came with a new six-episode playlist series, Stories. The stop-motion episodes tell more stories for Huntrix and The Saja Boys from behind the scenes, and it's now available to stream on Netflix.

On top of that, Netflix also released a three-episode mini-series titled KPop Toy Creations, which comprises mini episodes inspired by the film, and also a four-episode Play Games with Us series, which is also stop-motion and includes competitions and challenges inspired by the original film. It's essentially companion content to keep the fans engaged, just as the new weekly release of choreography videos was unveiled ahead of the hub's release.

For fans looking for the best edition of the movie and a way to ensure it remains in your collection forever, the Criterion Collection has just dropped its own KPop Demon Hunters physical release on November 3. It’s widely expected that Netflix and Sony will release shorter-form spinoffs of KPop Demon Hunters before the sequel arrives, with major clamor from the fans to see some Derpy and Sussie stories at the very least. That expectation was somewhat set by the apparent reveal of short film, Debut: A KPop Demon Hunters Story, back in September 2025, for which an MPA Classification and Rating Administration filing appeared.

Speculation suggests Debut will be an origin story revealing how the band came together, thanks to the title, but little is actually known. So far, a release date hasn’t been revealed, with Netflix typically keeping that information locked down.

Just launched on October 27, 2026, and running through February 1, 2027, the Toca Boca World x KPop Demon Hunters collaboration saw stylized depictions of the movie’s characters added to the popular open-world game. If you haven't already, you can pick up iconic looks inspired by Huntrix and The Saja Boys, both in terms of their everyday style stage outfits that can be mixed for personalization. There's also a new in-game music player that includes four of the movie's original songs - “Golden,” “How It’s Done,” “Soda Pop” and “Your Idol" - which can be used as players' own soundtracks up until November 23.

So far, we've only had one LEGO set for KPop Demon Hunters with the Derpy and Sussie set that landed on shelves back in August, but the original announcement came with a promise of 2027 sets. So far, there's not been any information on what those sets might be, but we can reasonably assume we'll get a Huntrix set, a Saja Boys set, and possibly a larger set featuring Gwi-Ma and the underworld. Leaks posted by BrickFanatics suggest fans can expect five KPop Demon Hunters LEGO sets and a polybag release on May 1, 2027, ranging from $24.99 up to $129.99. Nothing is confirmed there, but they have a good track record.

November has also seen another new KPop Demon Hunters release, as the KPop Demon Hunters: The Official Screen Comic Boxed Set is finally available to buy. Bundling two volumes together, the special edition revisits the story as a graphic novel across 700 pages and has some special features worthy of its title as the ultimate collector's item, including the lyrics to both of the original songs added for the deluxe version of the soundtrack:

There are other 2027 books coming for KPop Demon Hunter fans, including KPop Demon Hunters: Official How to Draw Netflix KPop Demon Hunters, which is set for March, and KPop Demon Hunters: Official Paper Dolly Dress-up Book, coming in April. Neither really expands the franchise in any way, but nice to have for fans.

KPop Demon Hunters fans now have a chance to enjoy the world in a completely new way, as Immersive Gamebox is teaming up with Netflix to launch an interactive gaming experience across select U.S. locations - starting with Dallas, which launched on November 12.

The game will be playable by two to six players over a 60-minute session and uses lightweight motion-tracking headsets, projection mapping, and touch-sensitive walls. Players will be able to create their own animated avatar, joining Huntrix to protect the Honmoon through a series of mini games. Tickets start at $39, while the VIP Package, priced at $99, includes all-day access to the VIP Lounge, including complimentary snacks and refreshments and a choice of a VIP-exclusive tote or plush bag clip, as long as supplies last.

In what will surely be a major revenue driver, Netflix and AEG Presents have announced that a KPop Demon Hunters world tour is on the way. Tour locations have not yet been announced, but it’s expected to kick off in 2027, with 150 stops across the world. You can sign up for more information here.

It’s unclear who will actually be performing - whether it would be the vocal stars of the movie or officially endorsed Huntrix and Saja Boys stand-ins as tends to be the case in smaller-scale unofficial events. Either way, it’s going to pull in a lot of families. And it will be a remarkable shock - and a major business mistake - if Netflix don’t release a KPop Demon Hunters Concert Film both theatrically and then on the platform.
