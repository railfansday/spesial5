WoW: Forever’s Secretly Best Item Changes Everything

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

You might have heard of one of the new features in Blizzard's remix of classic World of Warcraft, WoW: Forever: camping. This social feature encourages players to build campfires out in the wild and sit around them for a minute to get useful gameplay buffs, perhaps exchanging a friendly word while they do.

A couple of weeks ago I started noticing players lying around campfires in thick, quilted sleeping bags. They looked comfy. I didn't know what these items were, but assumed them to be another buff-granting camping feature, like the tent and camp chair I, as a leatherworker and skinner, can craft. I didn't stop to consider that the sleeping bags didn't seem to grant a buff to the whole group of players like the other camping features do.

In fact, the sleeping bag is not a craftable camping feature, but a quest reward that lies at the end of a long and mysterious quest chain. It's called the Cozy Sleeping Bag, it's new to WoW: Forever, and, as someone who has been playing WoW for almost 22 years, I can assure you that it's an absolute game-changer.

Players use the Cozy Sleeping Bag at campsites because it, too, grants a buff if you spend a minute in it. They're just maximizing the efficiency of their chill time, as WoW players are wont to do. The Cozy Sleeping Bag gives you a 1% bonus to XP if you spend 1 minute lying in it, stacking up to 3%. A nice little perk for leveling, but nothing major. What's the big deal?

But wait. Lying in the sleeping bag also grants rested XP. Rested XP, which is usually gained by hanging out in inns or capital cities, fully doubles the experience gained from killing monsters, up to 150% of your current level. It's a massive leveling boost, and it accrues while you're offline. You get 5% of a level for every eight hours you spend resting, so you need to spend about 10 days resting to max it out.

Rested XP has been in World of Warcraft since the very beginning, and it's a handy catch-up boost for players who can't be online all the time. But you have to make it back to an inn or capital city to earn your rest. That's great immersive design from a roleplay perspective. But if you need to log out in a hurry when your character is out in the open world, the feeling of missing out on that delicious 100% XP buff is nagging.

I didn't realize until I happened across a random reply on Reddit, in a thread about something else, that the Cozy Sleeping Bag operates as a portable inn. You can get in your sleeping bag and log out absolutely anywhere to gain rested XP, without breaking the flow of your adventure.

This is a tremendous, game-changing convenience. It might seem odd to find it in Forever, a game that has mostly been about stripping away the immersion-breaking, high-speed convenience of modern WoW to get back to the friction, slow pace, and sense of discovery of the classic game. But that's the great thing about the Cozy Sleeping Bag: it's not immersion-breaking at all, and in fact, it's fully harmonious with Forever's overall theme. What could be more redolent of a hard-won spirit of adventure than snuggling down in your sleeping bag under the stars?

I don't have my own Cozy Sleeping Bag yet. I need to make some time for the quest chain, which will lead me, by way of cryptic clues and at a relatively low level, deep into enemy (read: Alliance) territory. It sounds like a grand adventure; I can't wait. A daring expedition in pursuit of a legendary item that just happens to be the comfiest sleeping bag imaginable. What could be more WoW than that?
