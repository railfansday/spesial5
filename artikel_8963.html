Casa Amigó / Estudio Travertino

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Text description provided by the architects. Some renovations update spaces; others reorganize how they are lived in. Casa Amigó belongs to the second group. Located in Barcelona's Sant Gervasi-Galvany neighborhood, this 1970s family home starts with a clear idea: to change how the space is perceived and inhabited. The project turns the flat into an interior landscape where color, material, and light reorganize the relationships between rooms.
