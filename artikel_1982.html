Heartbreak for Oakwood FC as Dial Square strike late to snatch win – the match in 12 pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Adam Aziz had given the visitors a half-time lead from the penalty spot. The Oaks then drew level on 68 minutes through Luca Intelligenza’s close-range effort.

Oakwood looked to have done enough to claim a hard-earned point – until Dial substitute Danny Taylor latched on to Daniel Wanyonyi’s through ball in second half stoppage time and rounded the Oaks goalkeeper to slot into an empty net.

The defeat leaves Oakwood 16th in SCFL Division One going into this Saturday’s trip to third-from-bottom Ringmer AFC.

Dial Square, meanwhile, are up to eighth and host bottom-of-the-table Shoreham this weekend.
