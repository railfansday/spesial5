Liverpool vs Arsenal - Premier League RECAP: Mikel Arteta finally wins at Anfield to take his side three points clear at the top after perfect game plan with Kai Havertz's first-half goal the difference

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

By HARRY BAMFORTH, LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE REPORTER

Relive Daily Mail Sport's coverage of Arsenal's first win at Anfield in the Premier League for 14 years.

After 14 years of pain at Anfield, Arsenal have finally managed to secure three points at this stadium.

Was it easy? Far from it. Liverpool threw almost everything at the Gunners' defence in what was an electric performance at times from the hosts.

But Mikel Arteta's side showed incredible heart, putting in a defensive masterclass to keep the hosts at bay.

It means the defending champions have gone three points clear at the top of the Premier League, and good luck trying to catch them.

Anyway, I'm now heading for a cheeky rest after what was a breathtaking evening on Merseyside. Thanks for joining us and we will see you all soon.

Mikel Arteta, Arsenal boss, speaking on Sky Sports:

On whether this is a big win in the title race:

Is it time for this conversation again? I think it is.

We are nine games into this Premier League season and Arsenal have already kept six clean sheets and have conceded just five goals.

Remember, three of those goals came in their shock defeat away at Brighton. Bar that odd afternoon, barely anybody has been able to break that rock-solid defence down.

The famous goals conceded record from Chelsea's 2004-05 has been deemed impossible to break. 15 goals in 38 games is just outrageous, isn't it?

But if anybody is capable of breaking that record is this Arsenal team, they are just so hard to score against.

Maybe this is the year they break it? A long way to go, mind.

Andoni Iraola, Liverpool boss, speaking to Sky Sports:

On whether Alexander Isak should have had a penalty:

Andoni Iraola has a real problem on his hands.

Trey Nyoni's performance today showed why he needs to start every game for them now. He dictated the game from deep, winning a midfield battle against Bruno Guimaraes and Declan Rice.

Nyoni's 53 passes were the most during the 59 minutes he was on the pitch, while he also had the highest pass accuracy in 96 per cent and made the most line-breaking passes with 14.

After he came off, Liverpool lost their momentum.

Dominik Szoboszlai is clearly a fantastic footballer who has produced so many big moments for the Reds over the past few years, but he doesn't quite suit this system.

The Hungarian works incredibly hard but also slows Liverpool's game down. He was taking two or three touches on the ball before making each of his passes.

I think Iraola needs to commit to a Mac Allister and Nyoni midfield behind Wirzt because it clearly works. But that does mean keeping the likes of Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch out of the team.

Arsenal couldn't have hoped for a much better start to the season than they have had.

Their 24 points are the most by a team after nine games of a Premier League season since the Gunners themselves in 2022-23 (also 24).

Only in 2004-05 and 2007-08 have they ever had more at this stage of a PL campaign (25 in both).

It's very much a catch us if you can scenario now.

Now, this isn't a post bashing Liverpool because they played rather well. But I guess I am about to...

I still can't believe that the Reds didn't manage to register a single shot on target, despite applying so much pressure on the Arsenal goal.

It's the first time EVER that a Liverpool side have failed to have a shot on target in a Premier League match at Anfield. That's a crazy stat.

In fact, Liverpool have scored just four goals at Anfield in the Premier League this season, their joint-fewest after five home matches in a top-flight campaign (also four in 1913-14 and 2012-13).

That is something Liverpool need to sort out, and if they do, they will be a proper team.
