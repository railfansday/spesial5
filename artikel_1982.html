Scott Parker is being backed to lead West Ham United back to the Premier League this season, sources have told Football Insider.

The 46-year-old was quickly chosen as the man to replace Nuno Espirito Santo following his dismissal earlier this week, with the board keen to make a rapid appointment.

It wasn't the best of starts for the new boss, who suffered a 2-1 defeat against Blackburn at Ewood Park in his first game in charge, despite dominating the game.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Parker already has more support than Nuno did behind the scenes and in the stands.

Part of the reason West Ham moved so qucikly to appoint there new boss was a feeling that he would get the fans on side thanks to his previous spell in East London as a player.

Parker was a fans' favourite during his time with the Hammers, and there is optimism that their backing will help turn around the club's early fortunes in the Championship.

Former West Ham scout Mick Brown warned Football Insider on 26 October that Nuno was struggling to win over the fans, making his position at the London Stadium unstable.

The decision to appoint Parker as his replacement was at least partly an attempt to win over those frustrated supporters, by bringing in somebody they already know and trust.

Parker's previous spell at West Ham is likely to earn him more time and support from the fans, and will give him space and a platform to build success upon.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Obviously you mentioned his time there as a player. That obviously has played a part in sort of bringing him in.

"I think there might have been a plan from West Ham to get the fans on side, you know, bringing in a former player, they know him, they trust him, and all that sort of thing.

"It'll be interesting to see if the fans do immediately get on side and start pulling in the right direction.

"I think that was a bit of a problem that Nuno had there. The fans never quite seemed to be behind him, and ultimately that played quite a big role in his dismissal."

After Nuno's dismissal earlier this week, West Ham moved extremely quickly to land Parker as his replacement, leaving no time for doubts about their next boss.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that Parker was immediately identified as the ideal replacement due to his previous experience managing in the Championship.

The 46-year-old has previously led Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley to promotion, and there is optimism that he will do the same with the Hammers come next summer.

West Ham are determined to land promotion back to the Premier League as soon as possible, and have already set lofty goals of finishig in the automatic promotion places.

Having slipped down the table after a poor run of form, they are now hopeful that Parker is the right man to help them reach their goals between now and May.
