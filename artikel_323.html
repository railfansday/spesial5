Black Friday streaming deals 2026: The best deals live now and what to realistically expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's been yet another rough year in the streaming world. Many of our favorite streamers raised costs once again, including Peacock, Apple TV, Disney+, Hulu, Netflix, ESPN, and Paramount+. Bank accounts everywhere are pleading for some relief, and this year's Black Friday streaming deals are slowly rolling in to deliver it.

As Mashable's resident streaming expert, I've been tracking streaming prices for years now and can say without a doubt that Black Friday is the best time of year to sign up if you want to save some money. I'm pumped that despite all of the price hikes in 2026, it's proving to be another banger year for streaming deals. I highly recommend taking advantage of these prices if you're looking to diversify your streaming this season, as we're unlikely to see discounts this steep for a while.

Most of the heavy hitters have already thrown their hats in the ring, but more streaming deals will likely roll in throughout Cyber Week. I'll be keeping a watchful eye out and will update this list as any other noteworthy streaming deals pop up. In the meantime, here are all the best Black Friday streaming deals live. Most of these deals expire on Nov. 30 (Cyber Monday), unless otherwise noted.

HBO Max's Black Friday deal is top dog once again in the streaming world. This year, you can secure an entire year of streaming for half the usual cost. That drops it to just $5.49 per month, or $65.88 total for the year. Better yet, the offer is available as a standalone deal, a Prime Video add-on, or a Roku subscription.

Yes, you'll have to watch with ads, but for the price, it's easy to look past that. With a Basic subscription, you also won't get access to live sports. Everything else is yours to enjoy — including recent box office gems like Backrooms, Wuthering Heights, and The Drama, as well as HBO Max's many excellent original series like The Pitt, The Comeback, A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, and Industry.

Paramount+ offers two plans: Paramount+ Essential, which includes the standard Paramount+ library with ads for $8.99 per month, and Paramount+ Premium, which removes ads and includes the additional Showtime library for $13.99 per month.

The new Black Friday deal (available as a standalone deal or an add-on) drops both plans to $2.99 for the first two months. So there's really no reason to not go with Paramount+ Premium. You save 67% with the Essential deal, compared to almost 80% with the Premium deal. You’ll gain access to hit shows like Yellowjackets and Tulsa King with Paramount+ Premium, and you won’t have to deal with ads. Not to mention, with the Premium subscription, you'll also unlock access to Showtime in its entirety, and your local CBS channel live. It's a no-brainer.

Apple TV's outstanding library is well worth the subscription, even if you cancel after the six-month promotional period. It's stacked with Emmy-winning TV series like Widow's Bay, Pluribus, and Severance, as well as Oscar-winning and nominated films, including Coda, F1: The Movie, and Killers of the Flower Moon.

Disney has shockingly revived its exact same Black Friday bundle deals from last year, despite raising costs across the board earlier this fall. If you're looking for the most bang for your buck and prefer to watch without ads, you can get an entire year of both Disney+ and Hulu Premium for only $14.99 per month. Considering an ad-free standalone Disney+ Premium plan typically costs $21.49 per month, the typical bundle price of $21.99 on a normal day is already an excellent deal. But this knocks an extra $7 off, getting you both streamers for less than the cost of one.

You can also opt for the ad-supported plan and get both Disney+ and Hulu Basic for only $4.99 per month for a year, which is an even steeper discount.

A Disney+ and Hulu bundle subscription is your ticket to the Super Bowl this season, as well as select live ESPN College Football games, the entire Skywalker Saga and most of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, new releases like Toy Story 5, Hulu and Disney+ Originals, hit ABC TV shows, and so much more.

Normally I wouldn't recommend Peacock's Select tier, as it's extremely limited in scope and not worth it for the usual cost. However, with this Black Friday discount, it's definitely worth a second look. At $29.99 for one year, which breaks down to about $2.50 per month, you'll get access to all of your TV favorites from NBC, Bravo, and more. It doesn't include Peacock Originals, hit movies, or live sports — in order to unlock access to those, you'll need at least the Premium tier. Fortunately, that tier is also discounted for Black Friday. You can get Peacock Premium for $89.99 for one year, which saves you $40.

I'm going to hold your hand when I say this: most streaming deals aren't going to blow you away. In fact, over the last few years, Black Friday streaming deals have gotten significantly less impressive. Still, any and every discount is welcome in a time where prices keep rising.

Most streamers have announced their Black Friday deals at this point, but you may be wondering if there's more to come. Aside from some stragglers later this week, I would say the best streaming deals have already arrived.

I'm shocked that Peacock's Black Friday deal is as good as it is for 2026. Last year, the streamer didn't offer a deal at all, so this is lightyears better than I was expecting. If you've been holding off on subscribing, now is likely the only time you'll get a decent discount. Paramount+, HBO Max, and Disney+/Hulu are pretty much on par with their Black Friday deals of years past. I would lock in HBO Max and the Disney+/Hulu bundle for a full year, as more price increases could pop up in 2027.

If you're hoping for a Netflix Black Friday deal, don't hold your breath. Netflix has never offered a Black Friday discount, or any kind of discount for that matter. I don't expect this year to be any different.
