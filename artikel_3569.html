Patrick Mahomes Reacts to Fan Claiming 'He Was Done' After 3rd Child

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick Mahomes is ready to welcome his fourth child with wife Brittany Mahomes, responding to a fan who claimed he was “done” after his third.

“I thought Patrick said he was done after the last one,” the fan commented on a joint baby no. 4 Instagram announcement shared by Patrick, 31, and Brittany, 31, on Tuesday, November 10. The comment prompted the Kansas City Chiefs quarterback to reply, “ayyy chill chill,” before adding three laughing-crying emojis.

The fan’s comment had referred to Patrick speaking about his growing family to reporters in July 2024. “I’m done. I’ll say that. I did three and I’m done,” he told members of the press according to the Associated Press at the time. (Brittany gave birth to the couple’s third child, daughter Golden, 21 months, in January 2025.)

Patrick’s amused reaction to the fan’s reminder sparked further discussion between social media fans. “People are allowed to change their minds,” wrote one fan, while another added, “Words said by almost every parent.”

Patrick’s mom, Randi Mahomes, meanwhile only had praise for the happy news. “I cannot wait!! Congratulations, love y’all,” she commented on the joint post.”

Brittany and Patrick’s announcement featured a string of photos of the couple’s children, Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, gathered around their mom by the beach while placing their hands on her belly. “Another blessing from God,” the couple captioned the post. “& then there were 6.”

The official NFL and Chiefs Instagram accounts shared congratulatory words in the comments section, while fellow Chiefs star Travis Kelce’s official “New Heights” podcast account also wrote “Congratulations” and added four heart emojis.

The couple’s news came as a surprise to fans of Brittany, also, after the WAG had similarly stated publicly once that she was unsure about welcoming further children into her family. During a June 2025 episode of the “Whoops” podcast, Brittany said, “’We’re done, we’re done for a while. I don’t know. I mean, I feel like you can never say you’re done. I think three is all I need.”

The doting mom revealed an everlasting tribute to her family on her arm recently, sharing a photo of a tattoo that showed the side profiles of her three children connected to “Patrick’s initials into their names and birthdays.”

She said of the September 29 Instagram Story reveal, “I guess I never have showed my tattoo and people are so intrigued.”

Patrick and Brittany met in high school and began dating in 2012. Eight years later, they tied the knot in March 2022 in Maui, Hawaii, after Patrick had popped the question in a private suite inside Arrowhead Stadium, the Chiefs’ home ground.
