Kaitlyn Bristowe Reveals Dog Is Back in the ER Amid Serious Health Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Former Bachelorette Kaitlyn Bristowe has revealed that her beloved dog Pinot has returned to the ER following his near-fatal overdose.

“Watching DWTS from the ER. Pinot had a very scary episode that I would never wish on anyone to have to witness that. Second time in ER today. I just want answers and I want my goofy baby back,” Bristowe, 41, wrote over an Instagram photo of herself in a car beside Pinot on Tuesday, November 24.

The heartbreaking image showed the reality TV personality with tears in her eyes as one of her two golden retrievers appeared limp while laying beside her on the seat.

The medical emergency comes one month after Pinot accidentally consumed 120 prescription pills that had been issued to help manage a preexisting heart condition. The October incident took a significant toll on the animal’s health, with Bristowe revealing earlier this week via Instagram that he was recently given feeding tubes and a balloon in his esophagus that required air to be pumped into him “twice a day for six weeks.”

Pinot’s health decline also comes after Bristowe, who is also a fur parent to Ramen, shared on October 31 that she had started taking Pinot’s temperature whenever she administered his medication: a task that was required throughout the night.

She said in Tuesday’s Instagram update that Pinot would remain in emergency care overnight. “In the waiting room, to go in and say goodnight. We will have more answers tomorrow but he needs to be monitored tonight. I am trying to stay positive and [DTWS] is good for the soul,” she captioned the post. “I can’t believe I’m asking for these again, but prayers for Pinot. I feel broken that I can’t fix him.”

Her caption concluded, “I thought I was going to have a positive update today for you guys. Thank you for all the DM’s asking about him.”

An Instagram Story from Bristowe, shared after her grid post, showed Pinot lying on the ground of the ER with his head perched on Bristowe’s leg. “I don’t want to leave him,” she wrote over the clip, which captured her stroking one of his paws.

A subsequent Instagram Story also reassured fans that she was hopeful for Pinot to make a swift recovery. “It’s important to note that he is himself most of the time, and still has so much life to live!! He just keeps having little set backs but the vets all say that we will figure this out and we are in NO place to even think about giving up,” his fur mama wrote.

Bristowe rose to fame on season 19 of The Bachelor before leading season 11 of The Bachelorette. Spreading her wings beyond Bachelor Nation, she went on to win season 19 of DWTS in 2020 and is now the cohost of Hulu’s Are You My First? reality TV dating series.
