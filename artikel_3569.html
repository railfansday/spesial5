Xbox Game Pass Kicks Off December With Horror, Cozy Adventures, And More

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One last month of 2026 brings two more waves of Xbox Game Pass titles to close out the year. Subscribers can once again look forward to some new arrivals in the Xbox Game Pass library, ranging from gruesome co-op action to wholesome adventures featuring helpful mice on a mission. These announcements typically come in two waves, with a sneak peek split between them, so while this list isn’t complete yet, we do know what you can expect in the first half of December. As usual, we’ll update this page once Xbox reveals the full lineup for this first batch.

For now, you can look forward to an eclectic mix of games, starting with The Outlast Trials. Released in 2024, it’s a co-op survival-horror game that throws a team of up to four players into a gauntlet of deadly trials, where evasion is key to victory. You can also play it solo, and if the onslaught of festive season cheerfulness is getting to you, then this will be a nice–but brutal–change of pace.

If you’d prefer to focus on the coziness of the season, then Hela: Of Mice and Magic looks like a fun time. Set in a magical forest inspired by Scandinavian lands, the game follows a group of mice on a journey to help people, at the behest of an old witch who can’t make the trip herself. This is also a day-one release for Xbox Game Pass, and it’ll be joined by World of Tanks Blitz on the same day. On December 3, Freestyle Football 2 launches, bringing fast-paced, 5v5 multiplayer street soccer to Xbox and PC, and on December 14, you can grab Nubby’s Number Factory, a roguelite game all about making big numbers.

Xbox Game Pass December Wave 1 Games

December 1

  • The Outlast Trials (Cloud, Xbox Series X|S, and PC) — Game Pass Ultimate, Game Pass Premium, PC Game Pass
  • Hela: Of Mice and Magic (Cloud, Xbox Series X|S, and PC) — Game Pass Ultimate, PC Game Pass
  • World of Tanks Blitz

December 3

  • Freestyle Football 2

December 14

  • Nubby’s Number Factory

If you missed it, November brought a solid selection of releases. The first wave added the dungeon-crawling roguelike deck-building game Dungeon Crawlers to more Game Pass tiers, along with Warhammer Survivors and John Carpenter’s Toxic Commando. The Game Pass library expanded further later in November, with some Paw Patrol fun for the kiddos, High on Life 2, and the pretty good long-distance stealth game Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2.
