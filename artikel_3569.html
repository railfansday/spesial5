“The “F**k Cut”– Resident Evil Movie Has A Version With A Lot More Swearing

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Zach Cregger's Resident Evil movie includes a lot of swearing, with main character Bryan (Austin Abrams) saying the words "f**k" and "shit" over and over again as he faces a nightmare in Raccoon City. But the film could have had a lot more swearing.

Cregger said in a new interview with Sony for the movie's home release, that, "The whole thing was carpeted with f**ks. You think there's a lot of f**ks in there now? You should have heard the rough cut. It was insane."

Abrams said he isn't sure how many of the swear words were written into the script, saying there was "definitely a lot improvised."

Cregger, who co-wrote the script with Shay Hatten, said the original script indeed had a lot more f**ks in it. "We tried to go in and take as many as we possibly could out of the movie. I think we probably pulled 70%," he said.

Cregger went on to joke that there is a "F**k Cut" of Resident Evil out there.

Similarly, there is a more comedic cut of the movie that exists. Test screenings showed that it was too funny. "So over the course of my testing, I stripped out as much of the jokes as possible,” he said previously.

Cregger was previously in the sketch comedy group Whitest Kids 'U Know and has some fantastic comedy chops. Perhaps one day we'll get to see Cregger's original version of Resident Evil with more swearing and more comedy.

The Resident Evil movie made more than $400 million globally and ranks as the highest-grossing film of Cregger's career and the top-grossing Resident Evil movie ever. Cregger won't come back for a sequel, though. He got a lot of hate from core gamers about his Resident Evil movie, which didn't feature any storylines or characters from the games, and is now pursuing only original movies as a director.
