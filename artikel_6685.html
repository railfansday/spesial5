13 Menswear Sales Worth Shopping on Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Here’s how to save big on Lacoste polos, Filson jackets, and designer menswear.

If you’re looking hard enough for sales on menswear, you can almost always find a discount on something decent, a pair of shoes, a shirt maybe, but you have to scrounge. Every other week of the year, that’s just how it is. But this week, the week of Black Friday, the opposite is true. Every store and their mother is having a sale and suddenly, it’s not easy to tell which markdowns are gonna go fast and which ones will still be there when you log on again in December. Luckily, you have me. Year-round, it’s my job to keep tabs on deals so I know which ones to pounce on this Black Friday.

On Amazon, there’s my favorite Lacoste polo, which is actually on an outrageous sale going up to 40 percent off if you’re the right size. Filson jackets are 25 percent off. Lululemon isn’t just for the gals, it has a ton of great stuff for men and its Black Friday sale is absolutely stacked. And REI is doing its classic “We hate Black Friday but are doing a sale anyway” thing, and as always we are here for it.

Let’s start with the personal favorites; after that we’ll just list deals alphabetically by brand. First, Amazon. Yeah, yeah, everything is Amazon now. But when Bezos is offering big discounts on Lacoste polos and Levi’s, we listen.

Nothing is ever going to beat the cool of a beat-to-hell Filson jacket. If you’ve been holding off on buying one, I can’t recommend it enough. Plus, Black Friday is the perfect time to get one for 25 percent off.

I tell regular—meaning normal guys, not fashion-y dorks like me—that Huckberry is really the answer to 90 percent of what they want. All of a man’s casual wardrobe can be bought here; the only thing it doesn’t stock well is suits and tailoring. I’m seeing up to 40 percent off on seasonal shirts, and up to 30 percent off jackets, sweaters, and pants; that’s as good as it gets, folks.

Any time Lululemon does a discount is noteworthy. The brand is averse to any seasonal sales, so Black Friday is your best bet here. Through Cyber Monday, you can get big discounts (I’m talking 40-plus percent off) on the usual best sellers—just note that for the most part, only certain colors are discounted.

If you’re not a regular Huckberry guy but a man who considers himself “into fashion” or at least “stylish,” you ought to be hitting the Mr Porter sale. The sale is absolutely massive—over 12,000 items are up to 50 percent off—so I’d go in with a plan. Have one item, or even one brand, you want to check out; do that and that only. My hunt is for a really good jacket—sport coat, blazer, suit separate, or whatever you want to call it. That’s what I’m showing, but you need to do some of your own hunting on this one.

All right, the rest of these I’m presenting brand by brand in alphabetical order. I’ll spare you a spiel on each one, but if you need some guidance, let me call out a few heavy hitters.

Del Toro, which has for a long time just been a really good rich guy made-in-Italy shoe brand, has started putting out some really lovely elevated casual clothing—all vintage Italian-inspired stuff—and a majority of it is 20 percent off. And J. Crew has its usual sale going on, but an extra 40 percent off sale styles thrown in with the code FRIDAY on top of that. Todd Snyder is giving 30 percent off a ton of items with the code BLACKFRIDAY. And of course, you can’t go wrong at perennial favorite outlets REI and Nordstrom—both are featuring affordable menswear at a discount for Black Friday.
