The Best Boots to Wear With Barrel Jeans This Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ankle boots keep barrel jeans looking sleek, balanced, and intentional.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Whether or not you’ve jumped on board, barrel jeans remain a favorite silhouette among the fashion set. They’re quirky, fashion-forward, and, yes, undeniably comfortable, qualities that explain their staying power. I’ve been devoted to them since I got my first pair in 2022, and they’ve been my main go-to jean ever since. Now that boot season is here, we set out to find the best styles to wear with them.

Our conclusion: ankle boots are the best match for this sculptural cut, because they tuck neatly beneath the curved hem. That said, not all ankle boots are created equal. A sleek Chelsea boot keeps the proportions clean, while a pointed-toe style adds a bit of length to balance the leg’s volume. If you lean casual, a lug-sole pair gives the look an easy, utilitarian edge. Whatever you choose, aim for a hem that skims the top of the boot, so the curve of the denim stays the star of the outfit.
