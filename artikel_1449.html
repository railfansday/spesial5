Francisco Conceição has a hand in three goals as Juventus beat Omonia Nicosia 4-1

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Juventus came from behind to beat Omonia Nicosia at the Allianz Stadium on Thursday night, scoring four times in the fifth round of the UEFA Europa League league stage after falling behind in the 15th minute.

The visitors went ahead through Šatka, with Tanković providing the assist. Omonia had already had Tănase booked in the 10th minute, and Juventus saw Lucumí cautioned in the 34th.

Luciano Spalletti’s side, set up in a 3-4-2-1, levelled three minutes after that booking when Cambiaso scored from Francisco Conceição’s assist. Six minutes later Ekhator put Juventus in front, again with Conceição supplying the final pass, and the home side went in at half-time a goal to the good.

Conceição got on the scoresheet himself in the 68th minute, and Boga made it four five minutes later, with Cambiaso the provider this time. Balkovec was booked for Omonia in the 81st minute.

Cambiaso was rated 8.93, the highest on the pitch, after a goal, an assist, 4 shots and 3 key passes. Conceição, rated 8.61, was involved in three of the goals and had 7 shots and 4 key passes before being replaced with 86 minutes played. Boga and Ekhator also scored, the latter in 66 minutes on the pitch, while Neto made 4 saves.

Juventus had 60% of the ball and completed 570 of their 619 passes, against 351 of 410 for Omonia. They had 23 shots to 13, with 9 on target to 5, and finished with expected goals of 2.42 to 1.13. Kaminski made 5 saves in the Omonia goal.

— OMONOIA FC (@OMONOIAfootball) November 26, 2026

The win is Juventus’s second in a row with four goals, following the 4-0 victory over Venezia, and leaves them unbeaten in five matches in all competitions since their defeat to Genoa. In that run they have also beaten Napoli and AZ Alkmaar and drawn with Fiorentina.

For Omonia, it is a second defeat in their last four matches, after a draw with Apoel Nicosia, a win over Anorthosis and a loss to NEC Nijmegen.

Juventus return to Serie A on Sunday 29 November away to Como. Omonia go back to the Cypriot First Division on Monday 30 November, when they visit Nea Salamis.
