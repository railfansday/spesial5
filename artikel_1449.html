Como: Nico Paz in Manchester United’s sights

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

While the future of Bruno Fernandes at Old Trafford becomes more and more dotted as the last six months of his contract approach, Manchester United are actively preparing his succession. According to The SunMancunian leaders are closely following Como’s nugget, Nico Paz, with a view to the next summer transfer window. Already targeted even before his recital last month – author of a double and an assist during the correction inflicted on the Red Devils in C1 (4-1) which precipitated the fall of Michael Carrick – the 22-year-old Argentine playmaker displays impressive statistics under the orders of Cesc Fàbregas with 6 goals and 8 assists in only fourteen matches this season.

Eighteen months before the end of his lease in Lombardy, the former Madrilenian could be seduced by the imminent arrival on the Mancunian bench of his compatriot Mauricio Pochettino, a strong asset to convince the player to cross the Channel. The file, however, promises to be complex on a financial and contractual level since Real Madrid, which sold it in 2024 and suffered a refusal to return last summer despite a low-cost option, still has a buyout clause that can be activated next summer. If the presence of Jude Bellingham in the center closes the door to a repatriation to the Spanish capital for the moment, United will have to deal with this Merengue lock to offer themselves the much sought-after successor to their Portuguese captain.
