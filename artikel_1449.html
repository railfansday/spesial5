Bruno Guimarães cupped his ears and had a plastic cup thrown at him as the abuse came down from the stands. Despite the missiles, the Brazilian wore a look that made it clear he was very much enjoying his first goal in an Arsenal shirt.

Mikel Arteta has laid bare his quirky management techniques so his players are ready for any eventuality. Late meals and making the dressing room hotter are among his tricks, although nothing can prepare a player having 40,000 fans calling you a “sad Geordie b------”.

So it was a sweet moment for Guimarães, even more so after coming just after Sunderland had a penalty saved. What an important week it has been for Arsenal, who have shown their squad depth by winning in Napoli then travelling to the Stadium of Light and extending their perfect start to the season to six games.

Other teams with aspirations of the title dropped points earlier in the day, while Arteta could battle to a win with key players brought back into the team and Guimarães coming off the bench at half-time. A trip in the Champions League followed by an away game the following weekend is a barometer of where teams are, and Arsenal have sounded a warning after coming through a bruising encounter.

Bukayo Saka scored a stoppage-time penalty to seal the win but the evening belonged to Guimarães, the £75m summer signing who has been waiting for this moment to ignite his Arsenal career.

“Everyone knows I had a difficult start through injuries in my short time. But I’m glad to be back and glad to play,” Guimarães said. “I feel very good. It was unbelievable to score a beautiful goal, beautiful moment for me and my family and I am happy.”

These are ominous signs for Arsenal’s title rivals, with Guimarães’s return to fitness adding to the midfield options. Arteta’s changes to his starting line-up restored Riccardo Calafiori and Kai Havertz – two of the in-form players in the Premier League – to the team, rather than squad players being rotated.

With Arteta’s changes altering the pace of the game, the match turned just before the hour mark. “But I loved the save from David, the way the team reacted, the way we go again and we go against everything, but this should never happen, it’s not fair, and I am sad, very sad, in that sense.”
