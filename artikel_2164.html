West Brom reporter makes ‘record sale’ prediction for 21-year-old ace amid excellent form

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Brom recruited French prospect Rabby Nzingoula from RC Strasbourg in the summer, and the 21-year-old has quickly impressed in English football. He wasn’t a nailed-on starter upon arrival, but he has now started eight of the last Championship matches with his performances increasing eye-catching.

Nzingoula has drawn wide praise, and after Wednesday’s 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers, Full Throstle’s Steve Tudgay stated on X that he believes the Lille-born starlet can usurp WBA’s £16m record fees received for Salomon Rondon and Matheus Pereira.

⭐️ Hopefully it won’t be anytime soon but one day Rabby Nzingoula will become Albion’s record sale, currently Pereira & Rondon £16m. Just love watching him play # wba

The Baggies signed Nzingoula to a three-year contract until 2029 in the summer. He had interest from Swansea City, but it was Albion who managed to get a deal done with Strasbourg.

Nzingoula arrived at The Hawthorns with some decent pedigree behind him. Despite his youth, he had amassed 37 appearances in Ligue 1 and enjoyed a solid half-season loan in Germany with 2. Bundesliga outfit FC Nuremberg in the 2025/26 season.

He was eased into things with West Brom, with his first four Championship appearances coming off the bench. The youngster impressed in a maiden start against Newcastle United in the Carabao Cup though, and he has only gained momentum since then.

Used as a central midfielder or on the right-hand side in James Morrison’s flexible midfield setup, Nzingoula is a great carrier of the ball with 17 successful dribbles in the Championship this season. His smart footwork and fluid yet powerful running allows him to win plenty of fouls (22 this season – FotMob), and he isn’t afraid of getting stuck in.

Morrison has spoken about how Nzingoula plays on the edge, and his aggressive style out of possession has seen him pick up five bookings, which means he will be suspended against Bristol City this Saturday. This is an area where WBA will hope to tame the midfielder a little, but without taking that bite out of his game.

Nzingoula has been a real hit at West Brom. The speed at which he has broke into the starting XI and impressed in Championship football speaks volumes about his existing ability and room to develop.

At 21, the Frenchman has an excitingly high ceiling. If he maintains his trajectory, there is no reason why a club wouldn’t be ready to come in with a record-breaking fee of over £16m for his services.

As Tudgay made clear though, WBA will have little desire to let him go any time soon. Like with Ousmane Diakite, Nzingoula looks like a talent capable of moving to a higher level, but Albion will hope he can do so in their colours.
