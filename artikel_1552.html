Report: Liverpool target now available for €23m, clubs have made enquiries

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool's first-team squad are in need of an upgrade or two.

Without these moves getting made, the Reds will fall even further behind their title rivals and could end up finishing in a worse position than they did last term.

This would be far from ideal as, last term, Arne Slot was sacked for finishing fifth, never mind what would have happened if they'd have finished any lower.

Right now, seven points separate Liverpool and first-placed Arsenal which, in the grand scheme of things, isn't that big of a gap to break down.

But, for them to definitely be able to do this, transfer will need to be made in 2027 to bolster their ranks.

And now, they seem to have been handed a major boost in their pursuit for one player.

Archie Brown has a €23 million release clause in his contract

One main position that Liverpool need to upgrade is their left-back.

Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas have failed to meet expectations this term with fans left wondering what's happened to their defence.

Indeed, Kerkez did have a decent game against Club Brugge on Wednesday evening but, it still feels like more should be coming from him in other games.

Tsimikas is more or less a lost cause and, with his contract set to expire in June 2027, his time at Anfield is over.

These facts have led to questions being asked about the Reds' transfer plans with a fresh update from Florian Plettenberg about Archie Brown certainly something for fans to be excited by.

"Archie Brown has a release clause worth around €23m, valid for both the winter and summer transfer windows. The 23 y/o is a regular starter at Fenerbahçe and has also impressed with some very strong performances in the Champions League," he wrote.

"Under contract until 2028, a move is planned, with Premier League clubs already making enquiries."

Archie Brown would be an interesting signing for Liverpool

Brown has probably only just started to be known by Liverpool fans.

Before the 2026-27 season, it would've been hard to find someone who knew the Englishman within L4.

He's spent most of his career away from England and is currently playing for Fenerbahce.

The left-back had a superb game against Liverpool during the Reds' 3-2 defeat against the Turkish Super Lig outfit in the Champions League which caused several clubs to show an interest in him.

Liverpool were seemingly one of them and, now that it's been revealed that he has a €23 million release clause embedded into his contract, the 24-year-old feels like an absolute steal.

Indeed, it would mean that Liverpool have three left-backs among their ranks for half a season but, it would give the Englishman plenty of time to get settled and start battling Kerkez for the starting role in Iraola's plans.

Brown would also be classed as homegrown which, as everyone knows, is an essential thing for Liverpool at the moment.
