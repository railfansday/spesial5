PSG: Luis Enrique reveals his truths about Lucas Digne’s concerns

Barring any major surprises, he should stay there after his trip to Normandy. The HAC is dead last in Ligue 1 with only two points on the clock, and is still looking for its first victory of the season. Above all, we will have to take advantage of a relaxation in Paris to hope for something. And again.

Will Luis Enrique take advantage of this to rotate his squad? There is a trip to Villarreal in the middle of next week that must be on his mind somewhere. It’s very bad to know him.
