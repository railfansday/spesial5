For those of you who have been keeping an eye on Switch 2 console prices since its launch earlier this year, you'll have noticed that they've not enjoyed the customary steady decline. In fact, there was a massive price increase in September! But why?

Well, you can blame hungry AI data centers! Tech giants spent 2026 gobbling up the global supply of memory chips, triggering a RAM shortage that sent component costs skyrocketing. Add in messy international hardware tariffs (I wonder who was responsible for those?!), and Nintendo’s profit margins suddenly started looking dangerously thin.

Rather than lose money on every console sold, Nintendo had to hike the price of all things Switch. But they're not alone. It's an approach other tech giants, like Sony and Apple, also took.
