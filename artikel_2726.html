Astros hire JP Martinez as new pitching coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Astros hire JP Martinez as new pitching coach appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Houston Astros are changing who oversees their pitching staff after winning the American League West and exiting the playoffs in two games. J.P. Martinez is set to replace Josh Miller as pitching coach on new manager Brad Ausmus' staff.

Houston initially planned to retain Miller when it parted ways with Joe Espada. That decision came before the Astros hired Ausmus. Miller spent roughly 16 years in the organization, including eight on the major league staff. He served two seasons as bullpen coach and six as pitching coach or co-pitching coach.

Martinez will inherit a staff that includes starter Hunter Brown and closer Josh Hader. The Astros finished 81-81 in 2026 and clinched the division on the final day of the regular season. The Chicago White Sox then swept the Astros in the AL Wild Card Series.

The Athletic's Chandler Rome shared news of the hire Monday on X, formerly Twitter, and linked to his coverage with colleague Ken Rosenthal.

Martinez spent five seasons on the San Francisco Giants' major league staff. He served as assistant pitching coach from 2021 through 2024 before taking over as pitching coach in 2025. He joined the Atlanta Braves as bullpen coach in 2026, when their relievers posted a 3.44 ERA.

Ausmus is also bringing in Aaron Bates as hitting coach. Bates spent 12 seasons coaching in the Los Angeles Dodgers organization, including four alongside Robert Van Scoyoc on the major league staff. Houston also named former catcher Bobby Wilson its bench coach last week.

The pitching change gives Martinez his second opportunity to lead a major league staff. His tenure in San Francisco included work as both an assistant and a department lead, while his time in Atlanta put him back in a role focused on relievers. In Houston, he'll oversee a staff with established arms in both the rotation and bullpen for 2027.
