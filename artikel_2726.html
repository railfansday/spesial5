Vikings can’t keep pace in NFC North playoff standings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Minnesota Vikings lost on Sunday night to the San Francisco 49ers and with wins from the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions, they have fallen a game back in the NFC North. The Green Bay Packers were idle on their bye and sit a game behind the Vikings plus a tiebreaker.

The Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Rams were also on a bye, so the Vikings didn't really have a chance to be caught from behind. The closest team in the rearview is the Dallas Cowboys who won to get to 5-6.

The Vikings' favorable schedule down the stretch should help them secure a playoff spot and then once you're in, it's win or go home.
