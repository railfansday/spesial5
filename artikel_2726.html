Utah Jazz vs. Los Angeles Lakers: Recap and Final Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 2-13 Utah Jazz knock off the 13-2 Lakers, who had the best record in the NBA heading into tonight, with a final score of 109-97. What a huge win for this Jazz team!

The Lakers were missing Austin Reaves, but the Jazz were also missing key players in Lauri Markkanen, Jaren Jackson Jr, and Jusuf Nurkic.

The first half was a little bit of a rollercoaster. Utah's offense was looking smooth and the defense was solid. Svi Mykhailiuk finally had a good shooting night, hitting 4 threes in the first half, although he wasn't great on defense and struggled with turnovers. Darryn Peterson struggled against the Lakers full court pressure, and had some early foul trouble which took him out of the game for most of the first half.

Although Luka Doncic looked unstoppable, getting to the rim and drawing fouls whenever he felt like it, Keyonte George fueled Utah's 2-point lead heading into halftime. Doncic must be so annoying to officiate. He gets plenty of foul calls but still argues endlessly over every little detail.

Doncic finished the half with 22 points and 3 rebounds, and George had 18 points and 6 assists.

In the third quarter, the Lakers turned up the intensity, flying around for loose balls and forcing Jazz turnovers. The Jazz were extremely sloppy, but managed to calm down and take back control.

Ace Bailey continued his shooting slump, but still found ways to contribute to winning basketball. His defense and rebounding have been so impactful this year, not to mention how he has become a great connective passer.

Utah held onto their 2-point lead heading into the fourth. The Jazz weren't even particularly playing well, but still managed to be ahead. However, the game was far from over, and Doncic is inevitable. Or maybe he isn't?

With the game tied at 83 a piece, Utah went on a 15-0 run to essentially put the game away. After committing 21 turnovers in the first three quarters, Utah took care of the ball in the fourth, only committing two.

Doncic couldn't get going, finishing the game with 9 turnovers and hitting only 2/12 from three. You've got to give a lot of credit to Josh Green, who not only scored an efficient 11 points, but also held Luka to just 7 points in the second half.

Darryn Peterson had an okay night for his standards, scoring 11 points. It's too bad he could only play 18 minutes because of the foul trouble. Hopefully the knee injury isn't bothering him too much anymore. His potential is through the roof.

The unsung hero from this game was Mo Bamba, who finished the game with 6 points, 16 rebounds and 2 blocks. Shout out to Walker Kessler ($32.5M AAV) for getting outplayed by Mo Bamba ($3.5M AAV) and shooting 40% at the charity stripe!

Mo Bamba and Josh Green are both looking like great moves by the front office. Utah would've been in a very dark place without their play this season.

Ace Bailey finished with 19 points and 8 rebounds.

Keyonte George had his second straight 30-points, 10+ assists game, shooting 9/17 from the field and 5/10 from distance. He was undoubtedly the best player on the floor, and deserves to be in All-Star considerations.

Just two games ago, this team was looking distraught and desperate. The Jazz defense has been good, and just held the Lakers, one of the best offenses in the league, to 97 points. It also looks like they're beginning to figure out the offense.

Now they've come out of LA with two wins, and a lot of confidence. When Jaren and Lauri come back, things could get very interesting.

Utah plays Phoenix at home on Friday, November 20th. Can they extend the winning streak to three?
