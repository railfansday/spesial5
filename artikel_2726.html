Commanders HC Dan Quinn praises rookie LB Sonny Styles as a blitzer

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Washington Commanders have found themselves a generational talent in linebacker Sonny Styles, who fell to them as the No. 7 overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft. As a rookie, Styles has been the best defender for the Commanders so far.

He leads Washington in total tackles with 80 and is on track to have over 150 tackles this season. Styles also leads the Commanders in solo tackles with 37 and is tied for second in tackles for loss with five. An underrated aspect of his game has been his ability to blitz as Styles has at least seven blitzes in four games this season, including Washington's most recent win against the New York Giants.

"I wasn't as aware of how good a blitzer he was going to be. I thought he would, but he didn't do it as much at Ohio State. So to see that part of his game come to life, that's also been good," head coach Dan Quinn told Cris Collinsworth. "But I think what, for me coaching him, to see the attention to detail that he wants to have, the professionalism, his constant searching for improvement. That, to me, is what jumps out, his humility, his willingness to work. He's going on the way up and it's already at a good level. So, I'm excited to see where he can take it. He's got good instincts. He made a fantastic play at the end of the game [against the Giants], but I loved his remark afterward just as much. In the first game, he commented they did this on third and three. I thought something would be different in that spot. So, here's a guy applying what he learned from the first game against the Giants and then put it into the second game. So, he's been an excellent addition for us."

With how well he has played this season, Styles should certainly be a finalist for Defensive Rookie of the Year. He will have an opportunity to continue to bolster his resume on Monday Night Football against one of the NFL's best offenses, the Cincinnati Bengals.
