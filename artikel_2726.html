Ben Stokes registers for IPL 2027 auction: Three Indian Premier League teams who can sign ex-England all-rounder

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ben Stokes has registered for the IPL 2027 mini-auction at a base price of ₹2 crore.

He needed special permission to enter after skipping the 2025 mega auction.

Kolkata Knight Riders, Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings all have room to bid.

The auction register handed to the ten franchises on Tuesday evening carries 1,220 names, and the one that changes the shape of the December 16 event in Goa belongs to a player nobody expected to see on it.

Ben Stokes has enrolled for the mini-auction with a base price of ₹2 crore, as reported by Cricbuzz. The 35-year-old retired from international cricket earlier this year, which removes the England scheduling conflicts that kept him away from the tournament for most of the last decade, and he arrives in a pool thin on marquee names.

His IPL record runs to 45 matches across five seasons, with 935 runs and 28 wickets for Rising Pune Supergiant in 2017, Rajasthan Royals between 2018 and 2021, and Chennai Super Kings in 2023. The more relevant line on his CV for several franchises is that he captained England in Tests for four years.

Stokes fits a specific brief, which is a seam-bowling all-rounder who bats in the top six and can lead a side. Three franchises go to Goa needing some version of that.

Kolkata released Ajinkya Rahane at the retention deadline and have no internal candidate to replace him as the captain of the side, with Sunil Narine having never wanted the job and Varun Chakaravarthy and Rinku Singh untested as leaders. They were also linked with Hardik Pandya before he moved to Punjab Kings, and no formal offer ever materialised.

They have the resources to act on it, carrying ₹41.65 crore and eight slots, three of which can go to overseas players. The complication is Cameron Green, whose ₹25.20 crore already commits one of those places to an overseas all-rounder.

Hardik's departure to Punjab Kings in the trade that brought Arshdeep Singh the other way leaves Mumbai without the seam-bowling all-rounder their XI has been built around since 2024. Stokes is the only player in the pool who covers that role at the same level.

Mumbai have ₹32.60 crore and nine slots, but only two of those are overseas places, and they also released Allah Ghazanfar, Keshav Maharaj and Mayank Markande, which leaves Mitchell Santner as their lone specialist spinner. Spending one foreign slot on Stokes would mean solving the spin problem with an Indian signing.

Chennai were among the sides who looked at Hardik before Punjab got him, which told its own story about how they view a batting group where Shivam Dube at ₹12 crore is the only all-rounder of substance. They also know Stokes, having bought him in 2023 for a season cut short by a toe injury.

They go to Goa with ₹28.05 crore, six slots and two overseas places, and a bowling attack that lost Matt Henry, Zak Foulkes and Spencer Johnson at the deadline. Ruturaj Gaikwad's side need more than one player with that money, which is the argument against going hard for Stokes and the reason they may have to anyway.
