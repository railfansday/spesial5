Puka Nacua injury report is devastating news for Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua is expected to miss the rest of the season, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter.

Nacua underwent MCL surgery and it is expected to take him four to five months to fully recover.

He is currently on injured reserve, while also serving a three-game suspension for violating the NFL's personal conduct policy.

Injuries have plagued the Rams this season and this news might be their worst yet.

Nacua had played in the season opener but was then sidelined several weeks due to a hip and groin injury. He returned in Week 4 but he hasn't played since Week 9 and now won't for the rest of the year.

Defensive tackle Poona Ford and kicker Blake Grupe are among the other players ruled out for the season for the Rams.

Fellow standout receiver Davante Adams suffered a hamstring injury in recent weeks and is not expected back until later this season. Myles Garrett also missed significant time after playing in the season opener before recently returning.

Nacua is unlikely to return for the season and the playoffs, if the Rams are able to qualify.

Los Angeles did not make a significant move before the trade deadline to bring in a receiver, leaving the Rams to count on receivers that have low production this season.

CJ Daniels, Tutu Atwell and Konata Mumpfield will be asked to move forward as the primary receiving options for now with Nacua out of action and Adams still out.

Daniels has played in four games this season but has just one reception for 22 yards. He was a sixth round selection (No. 197 overall) out of Miami by the Rams in 2026.

Mumpfield has started three of the 10 games he's played in this season, producing 14 catches on 27 targets for 243 yards and a touchdown. He had not seen more than four targets in a game, outside of his four catches on eight targets during a game against the Denver Broncos on Sept. 27.
