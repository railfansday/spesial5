Brock Purdy receives major injury update ahead of 49ers-Seahawks game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The 49ers won't have quarterback Brock Purdy when they take on the Seahawks on Sunday.

Purdy is dealing with an ankle injury suffered during last week's game against the Vikings and Mac Jones will start in his place, coach Kyle Shanahan announced Friday.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy reacts before an NFL football game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AFP via Getty Images

Purdy sustained the injury in the second quarter of last week's 35-10 victory over the Vikings in Mexico City. He had his ankles taped for the second half before he finished 21-of-26 passing for 255 yards and three touchdowns while helping solidify his case as an MVP candidate.

Shanahan told reporters it was a mid-ankle sprain that didn't heal as quickly as the team would have hoped. Purdy missed practice the entire week, leaving Jones to receive practice reps with the starters.

"Any time it's not a high ankle sprain, you get pretty relieved. But it was more mid than low, which is a little bit worse," Shanahan said. "(I) kinda had a feeling just the way it was by the time Wednesday came around. So didn't heal up as fast as we wanted so we had to shut him down."

Shanahan was asked if Purdy would be available for the following game when the 49ers travel to play the Giants. He said it was too soon to tell.

"But I'm pretty optimistic about it. But I was about this week to start, and then it changes. Hopefully it'll be all right," he said.

The 49ers signed Jones before the 2025 season to a two-year, $8.4 million contract to upgrade their backup quarterback situation after struggling through stints with Brandon Allen and Joshua Dobbs the season prior.

"Mac's been awesome," Shanahan said. "He's had a great week. We went through this a lot last year, so we got a ton of confidence in Mac. Mac's had a hell of a camp, and he's the same guy every day. So it's been a good week with Mac."

Mac Jones #10 of the San Francisco 49ers attempts a pass against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

The 49ers believe Jones is one of the NFL's best backup quarterbacks after relying on him heavily when Purdy was injured in 2025. Teams called about acquiring Jones at the trade deadline, but the price was reportedly too high. The Vikings were reportedly among those teams and instead opted to swap a third-round draft pick with the Raiders for Kirk Cousins.

"Any time you have a backup quarterback you believe could be one of the better starters in this league, that's a hell of a deal," Shanahan said. "It's always hard and tempting when someone offers a lot of stuff, because you know how much that can help your team. But you're kinda glad you don't have to make that decision if because of how bad it is if your starter gets hurt and you're missing that guy. So really grateful that he's here and that we didn't make that trade."

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy listens to a question after an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

San Francisco went 5-3 in Jones' eight starts last season while Purdy was dealing with a turf toe injury suffered in Week 1. Jones completed 69.6% of his passes, averaging 269 yards per game with 13 touchdown passes and six interceptions.

Jones kept the 49ers afloat while they finished 12-5 and made the playoffs. Sunday's game will mark the 11th Purdy has missed due to injury since the start of the 2024 season.
