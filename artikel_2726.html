Josh Downs "excited to keep building" connection with Patrick Mahomes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Chiefs wide receiver Josh Downs didn’t waste any time making an impact for his new team.

Downs joined the Chiefs in a trade with the Colts on Tuesday, which meant that he had limited practice time with quarterback Patrick Mahomes ahead of Sunday’s game in Atlanta. Their lack of familiarity with one another didn’t get in the way of Downs hauling in an 18-yard touchdown pass from the quarterback in the second quarter and he finished his Kansas City debut with four catches for 53 yards.

“Pat gave me a chance, and I went up and made a play,” Downs said, via Nate Taylor of ESPN.com. “I’m excited to keep building off of that. First game and first touchdown, you can’t write it better than that. Playing with a guy like him is incredible. Doing anything you can to get open for him and making plays is what I want to do.”

Downs only played 10 offensive snaps in the 35-13 Chiefs win, which makes his production stand out all the more. It also leads to thoughts of what his connection with Mahomes will look like once they’ve had more time working with one another and those thoughts will be pleasant ones for Chiefs fans to ponder heading into Week 11.
