Why hasn't Jacoby Brissett's passing production turned into wins?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jacoby Brissett has thrown for 616 yards in his first two Baltimore starts. The Ravens lost those games by a combined five points. His 52-yard touchdown pass to Rashod Bateman at Carolina showed what the offense can do with him under center. The drive that followed showed how a productive possession can still come up short.

Baltimore drove 69 yards on 15 plays in the first quarter and reached the Carolina 11. Brissett threw three straight incompletions, and Riley Patterson kicked a 28-yard field goal. That was a drive with completions to Zay Flowers, Bateman, Matt Hibner, and Justice Hill. The Ravens moved the ball across the field and still left four points available inside the red zone.

Baltimore converted four of 10 third downs and gained 421 yards. It also got nine points from a kickoff-return touchdown with its extra point and a safety. Brissett's late interception ended the final possession, but a fuller assessment starts earlier. The Ravens have enough ways to gain yards. Turning more of those gains into touchdowns will matter against a Houston defense that held Indianapolis to three points.
