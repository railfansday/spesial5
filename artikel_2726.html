Jets end touchdown drought, lead Chargers 10-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Jets have found their way back to the end zone.

Running back Breece Hall caught a pass from quarterback Geno Smith and found his way into the end zone with 13:34 left to play in the first half in Los Angeles. The score put the Jets up 10-3 and ended a touchdown drought that stretched back to the third quarter of Week 8.

It was a 13-play, 70-yard drive that ate up nearly seven minutes and the first Jets drive covered eight plays before a Jason Sanders field goal. Smith is 5-of-6 for 54 yards and Hall has teamed with Braelon Allen for 51 yards on 14 carries.

The Chargers had an 11-play field goal drive of their own and will be looking for their first touchdown of the day before the Jets can add to their lead.
