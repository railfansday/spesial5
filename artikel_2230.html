Birmingham City fans will love Jude Bellingham's reaction to England news

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Jude and Jobe Bellingham have received call-ups to the England senior squad under Thomas Tuchel for the first time as a duo, with the former paying tribute to their early-career spells at Birmingham City.

The Premier League and Championship will halt their respective campaigns after the upcoming weekend's round of fixtures, with the Three Lions in Nations League action against Croatia and Spain.

It has been a relatively successful year for England on the footballing stage, with a third-placed finish at the FIFA World Cup hosted in the United States, Canada and Mexico being the nation's best in the tournament since winning it in 1966.

Jude Bellingham was an integral part of the side's run to the semi-finals and has continued to impress in the four Nations League matches during the previous international window.

Many of the high-profile names at Tuchel's disposal can credit spells at various EFL clubs for aiding their eventual development, including Ballon d'Or winner, Harry Kane.

Unsurprisingly, Bellingham was quick to reference his boyhood club yet again in a proud family milestone.

Jude and Jobe Bellingham are the most high-profile names to have graduated from Birmingham's Wast Hills academy setup this century, with the former becoming the club's youngest-ever player and goalscorer seven years ago before initially moving to Borussia Dortmund.

The West Midlands outfit then opted to retire the '22' shirt to help create a legacy, and that has only grown stronger through the 23-year-old's current exploits with Real Madrid and England.

Jobe, 21, played 26 times for Blues between 2022 and 2023 before moving to Sunderland for £3m, and after the Black Cats' Premier League return, also signed for Dortmund in a £27.8m deal.

He has since shone for the Bundesliga giants, and after impressing domestically and in the UEFA Champions League, the 12-time Under-21s international earned his first senior Three Lions call-up on Friday.

Such feats were marked by his older brother on social media, who posted a picture of them sporting Birmingham colours during their academy days.

During the early years of his career, Jobe has often been compared to his older brother, despite rightfully wanting to create his own story on the pitch.

That has clearly been the case given his rave reviews at Dortmund, whilst he also racked up over 100 Championship appearances for Birmingham and Sunderland.

Like Jude, the Stourbridge native has never lost sight of the impact Blues staff had in order for him to flourish at such a young age.

“I’m so grateful for everything they’ve (Birmingham) done for me over many years, since I was really young," he said shortly after leaving B9.

“At Birmingham, a lot of the academy coaches helped loads, just with my understanding of the game. I think they’ve made me a better person, I believe. There’s also a lot of players in the first team who helped me grow into the first-team environment.

Whilst they have their own career paths to focus on, both Jude and Jobe will be loving the developments at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park right now, which is a stark contrast to how the club was run during their respective breakthrough years.

Knighthead's plans for the Sports Quarter and Powerhouse stadium have seen Jude involved in promotional videos, whilst he was also present at the pre-season friendly against FC Barcelona.

On the pitch, Chris Davies' side are doing their utmost to end a long-standing Premier League exile, as Blues sit third in the Championship having lost just two of their first 14 games.

Joyous feelings will be reciprocated by those associated with the club for the progress made by the duo, who, in turn, will hope to see the club back in the top-flight very soon.
