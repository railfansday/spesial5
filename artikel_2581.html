Cleveland Browns: Run defense must right itself in final seven games

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cleveland Browns have been sound on defense through the first 10 games of the season, with a couple of big exceptions.

The first is the opening drive of the game. Cleveland has allowed a scoring drive in nine games, including six touchdowns, which, coupled with the slow starts from the offense, have left the Browns playing from behind each week.

The second has been the run defense, which has been porous, even by the low standards of the Browns.

Heading into the bye week, the Cleveland Browns' run defense is ranked:

No. 24 with seven runs of more than 20 yards

No. 15 (tied) with eight rushing touchdowns

When they come out of the bye, the Browns will have seven games left on the schedule, starting with a Sunday night tilt at home against the Las Vegas Raiders, and finishing with a road game against the Cincinnati Bengals that could hold major playoff implications.

While Cleveland's defense shouldn't expect an easy game against anyone, it has a chance to stabilize the run defense in the first two games after the bye against the Raiders and Cincinnati Bengals, though it still faces some tricky matchups.

The Browns are currently holding the final playoff spot in the AFC, with hopes of improving their position over the final seven weeks of the season.

And fixing the run defense will go a long way toward improving their position in the conference.
