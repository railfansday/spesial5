Romeo Doubs, Alijah Vera-Tucker return to Patriots practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A.J. Brown remains out of Patriots practice, but they got another wide receiver back on the field on Wednesday.

Romeo Doubs took part in practice as a limited participant. He has been out with a shoulder injury since Week 6 of the regular season.

The Patriots also got guard Alijah Vera-Tucker (hamstring) back on the field. Both players are now in 21-day windows to be activated from injured reserve.

Brown is sidelined by a hamstring injury. Quarterback Drake Maye (ankle), cornerback Carlton Davis (knee), and offensive lineman Walter Rouse (concussion protocol) were the other Patriots out of practice ahead of Sunday’s game against the Chargers.

Fullback Reggie Gilliam (knee) was also limited and guard Ben Brown (knee) was a full participant.
