What are the worst losses for each Super Bowl-winning team?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Minnesota Vikings are coming off a particularly tough showing in a 35-10 loss to the San Francisco 49ers in Mexico City on Sunday night. One thing I hear a lot after games like this is, "Well, you're definitely not a Super Bowl contender if you can lose a game by 25 points."

So that got me thinking — what's the worst loss each Super Bowl team had in the year they won the big game?

The last team to lose a game by 25-plus points and win a Super Bowl in the same season? It wasn't that long ago — the Tom Brady-led Tampa Bay Buccaneers six seasons ago lost 38-3 in Week 9 to the New Orleans Saints.

The average deficit for the 59 previous Super Bowl winners who lost a game — pop that champagne, remaining surviving 1972 Miami Dolphins — was 15.4 points.

I don't know that any of these numbers truly mean anything, but it felt too interesting not to share.
