Travis Kelce moves past Rob Gronkowski for No. 3 all-time in TD catches by a tight end

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

For the first time since Sept. 27, Travis Kelce has gotten back into the end zone.

And Kelce has also secured another milestone in his storied career.

Kelce capped the Chiefs’ first drive with an 8-yard touchdown catch, giving Kansas City an early 7-0 lead over Arizona.

The score also moved Kelce past Rob Gronkowski for No. 3 all-time in touchdowns by a tight end. Only Antonio Gates and Tony Gonzalez are ahead of Kelce on the list.

The possession lasted 11 plays and went 72 yards to the end zone, taking 5:14 off the clock.

Patrick Mahomes was 4-of-5 on the drive for 36 yards, converting a pair of third downs. He hit Rashee Rice for a 15-yard gain on one third down and the touchdown to Kelce was the second.

Rookie Emmett Johnson is once again off to a strong start, taking six carries for 36 yards while also catching a screen for an 8-yard gain.
