Recap: Colorado erases 3-0 deficet; beats Minnesota in a shootout

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After securing a 6-0 win over the Montreal Canadiens on Wednesday night, the Colorado Avalanche kicked off a back-to-back tonight with a game against the Minnesota Wild. While the Wild found themselves up 3-0 after the second period, the Avs were able to completely erase that deficet in the third period, and ultimately secured a win in the shootout by a final score of 4-3.

Let's take a look at how it all happened!

Joel Eriksson Ek would open the scoring for the Wild, as he was able to split through the Colorado defense and put a wrist shot on net that beat Mackenzie Blackwood up high.

Blake Coleman made it 2-0 next by driving in towards the Colorado net all the way from Minnesota's own zone, and was able to sneak the puck past Blackwood.

Eriksson Ek scored his second goal of the game with less than a minute left in the second period off a great feed for Brock Faber, who found Eriksson Ek down low driving towards the Colorado net.

With the net empty and just over six minutes to play in regulation, Devon Toews finally got Colorado on the scoreboard, as he was able to rip the puck across his body and past Jesper Wallstedt.

Artturi Lehkonen would score his 17th goal of the season to bring the Avs within one, off a shot from Cale Makar that he was able to tip past Wallstedt.

T.J. Hughes would be the one to tie the game up at 3-3 with less than a minute left in regulation. Makar again took a shot from the top of the zone, which did not get fully through to the net, but Minnesota was unable to find the puck, Hughes found the rebound, and scored on a bar-down slapshot to tie this game and send it to overtime and the eventual shootout.

Both teams had some good looks in overtime, but were not able to convert obviously, and we would eventually head to a shootout. Makar was the fourth shooter for the Avalanche, and he would be the one to ultimately beat Wallstedt in the shootout, as he was able to sneak the puck past the Wild goaltender, and secure the 4-3 win.

After absolutely taking care of business against Montreal a couple of nights ago, for a very wide majority of this game, it definitely looked like the same outcome was not to be had. Minnesota was kind of having their way with the Avs through the first and second periods of this game, but their inability to put the game away with under 10 minutes left in regulation gave the Avs the opportunity to pull the goalie and attempt to tie the game, which they were able to do, of course. During the shootout, Minnesota's players had a handful of good looks, but they were unable to get the shot off, ended up missing the net, or were stopped by Blackwood. I've said it before, and I'll say it again, playing a good 20 minutes (if that) is not always going to be enough for you to win hockey games, especially against teams of Minnesota's caliber, but it's always very impressive when it is enough in games like this one. Also, how great it is to have Cale Makar back in the lineup.

The Colorado Avalanche take on the San Jose Sharks on the second night of a back-to-back on Saturday, November 28. Puck drop will be at 7:00 p.m. MT.
