Inside the Editors’ Table: New York

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

On Tuesday, November 17, Vogue Business Executive Members gathered in New York for our annual Editors’ Table dinner at the exclusive private members' club Moss.

Executive editor Hilary Milnes and US editor Madeleine Schulz brought together senior leaders and founders from across fashion, beauty and luxury for a night of thoughtful conversation. In an intimate setting, guests shared perspectives on the challenges and opportunities defining the year ahead, building connections that extend well beyond the dinner table.
