PSA: Nintendo Ending Google And Apple Sign-Ins On Nintendo Accounts In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

You'll need a Nintendo Account from here on out

If you currently use a Google or Apple account to sign in to your Nintendo Account, Nintendo has got some news. In an official notice, it's announced the discontinuation of Google and Apple account sign-ins on Nintendo Accounts.

This will take place on 26th January 2027, so we're just a few months out. "As of" this date, it will no longer be possible to link a Google or Apple account. Nintendo notes this exact end time "may be delayed depending on the progress of the system change work".

Nintendo: "We apologise for any inconvenience to those who used a Google or Apple account to sign in to their Nintendo Account."
