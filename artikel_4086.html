Fallout Game From Hidden Object Developer Leaks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A new Fallout game was just trademarked in collaboration with Bethesda by developer DEVCATS.

Dubbed 'A Wasteland Full of Cats', this is a brand-new hidden object game set in a vault... full of cats (via Gematsu), following in the paw prints of A Mission Full of Cats, A Planet Full of Cats, A Tower Full of Cats, and Flats Full of Cats, just to name a few.

"You play as one of the cats in the Vault," the description reads. "One day there is an accident in the Vault and all of the cats who live there escape out into the Wasteland. It is up to you with the help of some NPCs you meet along the way to find them and return them to the Vault."

There's no release date attached to the trademark, but it does tease 14 locations, 33 rooms, "and a vault of recognizable levels from the Fallout franchise." There's even a "final boss fight where the player has to shoot some radroaches in order to save the last remaining cats and humans."

I have absolutely nothing against hidden object games as an avid and awful Mystery P. I. player, but for the first new Fallout game in 8 years, it's understandable that the community's response boils down to, 'huh? Really?'

The monkey's paw curls once again - u/Grimey_Rick.

Over on Reddit, fans muse that it might steal GOTY from GTA 6, or that it "could potentially earn them tens of dollars," but in fairness, DEVCATS make some solid hidden object games, and while sure, it might not be Fallout 5 or New Vegas 2, it's a harmless collaboration that doesn't take resources away from Bethesda or Obsidian Entertainment's main teams.

It will be available in real-life, and then eventually added to Fallout's in-game universe.

As u/Dinoverry put it, "The most interesting thing about this is that Bethesda is potentially looking at giving their IPs to other studios for spinoffs like this."

It'll be a long time before we hear anything about Fallout 5, and Obsidian has only recently begun work on a new spin-off, so smaller games like these are clearly here to tide us over along with the TV series, Fallout 76 expansions, and the upcoming Fallout 3 and New Vegas remasters.

It might not be my cup of tea, or even yours, but these kinds of spin-offs are clearly huge for the franchise, as Fallout Shelter amassed over 230 million downloads across all platforms. So, I wouldn't write off A Wasteland Full of Cats just yet.

Bethesda just acknowledged Fallout 3's existence out of the blue, but even diehard fans are feeling toyed with at this point.
