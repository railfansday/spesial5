Xbox Playback Is Now Available, Take A Look Back At Your Xbox History

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

"Your games. Your milestones. Your story."

One of my favourite things to do is look back at the Xbox games I've been playing, the achievements I've unlocked, and all of my cool unique stats that make me the Xbox gamer I am.

This year, Xbox is doing something a bit different. Unlike previous Xbox 'Year in Review' roundups, which cover the past 11 or 12 months, Xbox Playback takes things all the way back to the beginning, celebrating your story through Xbox's 25 years.

You can access your own Xbox Playback from the official Xbox site, where you'll need to sign in with your Microsoft account. There's this whole song-and-dance animation, sorta like Spotify's Wrapped, which takes you through stuff like the first game you played, what year you played the most, what % of most played time across all of Xbox, achievements, and loads of other really cool stuff. Then, you can share it to social media if you fancy.

As you can see above, Xbox CTO Scott Van Vliet created his account in 2006, has earned 28,528 gamerscore, and has 4,375 hours played: which is only in the top 4.8% of hours played, whereas yours truly is in the 0.2%. What can I say? I guess I'm an Xbox gamer after all.

Another thing that's really cool about Xbox Playback is that, for the first time ever, players will be able to access and view it directly from their console. No browser required. According to Xbox, it should appear soon, so keep an eye out!

We're keen to find out what your Xbox Playback says about your journey with Xbox through the years, so please do let us know what that looks like down in the comments. What percentage of hours played are you? What was your first game? When did you join? Let us hear it!
