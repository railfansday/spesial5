HBAR’s Path To $3 Relies On Volume, Not Promises

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Home » Altcoins » HBAR’s Path To $3 Relies On Volume, Not Promises

Analyst floats a $3 target for Hedera’s native digital currency as the DLT network snatches a fresh record.

Hedera Hashgraph’s (HBAR) price trades near $0.067 with a market capitalization around $3 billion and roughly 44.3 billion tokens in circulation out of a 50 billion supply.

A move to $3 would imply a valuation in the $133 – 150 billion range. That is aggressive, yet the case rests on capacity the network already demonstrates rather than planned upgrades.

Hedera has processed more than 20 million transactions in the recent month and recorded daily peaks above 800,000. Its governed throttle sits at 10,000 transactions per second for crypto transfers, with production bursts already surpassing that level.

🚨 #Hedera processed 21 MILLION transactions in 30 DAYS!!This is an ATH, $HBAR is averaging around 8 transactions EVERY SECOND, 24/7!This highlights the consistent activity on Hedera as its ecosystem expands across payments, tokenisation and enterprise applications… 🔥

The network does not require a consensus redesign to handle enterprise-scale settlement or micropayments. Most chains describe future capacity. Hedera is operating within measurable headroom today.

Several live deployments sit on the network. Verra is integrating the Guardian platform for carbon markets, while BCarbon has already moved millions of issued credits on-chain, shortening digital MRV timelines that previously stretched up to two years. Avery Dennison’s atma.io has tracked tens of billions of products on Hedera.

Lloyds and abrdn completed FX trades using tokenized money-market funds and gilts issued and managed on the network via Archax. Agent Accounts allow AI agents to execute bounded transactions with programmable limits, approved recipients, and audit trails. These are operating systems, not roadmap items.

HBAR remains roughly 88% below its 2021 all-time high near $0.57. Daily RSI has sat in the low-30s. In prior cycles, Layer-1 networks with measurable usage and institutional integrations saw large re-ratings once macro liquidity returned and the narrative shifted from pure speculation toward settlement infrastructure.

From there, continued volume growth on the live use cases above would be required to treat $1 as an intermediate level and $3 as a cycle high rather than a stretch target.

The pieces for higher sustained volume already exist: digital carbon registries tied to major standards bodies, production supply-chain tracking at billions of units, institutional collateral workflows, and AI-agent payment primitives.

When the next liquidity wave arrives, networks that can absorb real volume without congestion or unpredictable fees are the ones positioned to re-rate. Hedera is one of the few that already shows that capacity in production.

The path from current HBAR price levels to $3 is volume plus narrative plus cycle conditions. It is not a single announcement. The infrastructure is running. The rest remains speculative.
