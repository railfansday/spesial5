We’re almost inside of 24 hours before the U.S. launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 and Sony has just revealed that a last-minute software update is needed to play the game on PS5. Hurry up and download it. It’s apparently a pretty big one.

It’s not immediately clear what the software update is for. An extra level of security to make sure people don’t accidentally manage to unlock their pre-loaded versions of GTA 6 early? Or is it some sort of fix aimed at the recent scourge of jailbreaking and piracy on the platform?

Anyone who hasn’t already done so will also need to download a small but not insignificant day-one patch for GTA 6. Version 01.002.001 began rolling out earlier this week. It’s only 602MB, even smaller than the 1GB update that went live back on November 13.

GTA 6 goes live in the U.S. at midnight on November 19, but anyone who wants to jump through some hoops and tempt fate can can try to set up a New Zealand PSN account to play the game early. The global launch is staggered, and begins in the earliest time zones at 6:00 a.m. ET on November 18.
