Jeans are what we like to call ‘hard pants,’ so it’s not always ideal to commit to them for a long day ahead. However, we do love the look of them. Quite the debacle, huh? Well, if you want the style without the discomfort, opt for sweatpant jeans. They’re the clever pants that feel like sweats but look like denim.

Big brands are just getting wind of the sweatpant jeans trend, and we’re hoping to see the style more in the fall and winter seasons! These pants are designed with smart printing to give them the appearance of denim with the additional cozy factor. The best part is that no one will realize they’re not really jeans! Since the style is still new, not too many retailers have hopped on the trend, but you can still find them on Amazon, Walmart, Nordstrom, Walmart and more — starting at $12.
