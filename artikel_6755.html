The ‘All’s Fair’ Season 2 Premiere Was a Mount Rushmore of Maximalism

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Last night, as the 2026 CFDA Awards celebrated achievements in American fashion at the American Museum of Natural History, another fashion-filled event was happening downtown at Crane Club: the All’s Fair season 2 premiere.

The Ryan Murphy Cinematic Universe™ assembled to celebrate the second installment of the campy legal drama, and its stars—Kim Kardashian, Sarah Paulson, Glenn Close, Naomi Watts, and Niecy Nash-Betts—dressed the part. But unlike the season 1 premiere, the show’s stars seemed to settle on a shared color palette.

Sarah Paulson, Glenn Close, Kim Kardashian, Naomi Watts, and Niecy Nash-Betts.

Kardashian, who will reprise her role as divorce attorney Allura Grant, tapped Ashi Studio for a custom premiere look that mirrored the show’s more-is-more ethos. The gray lace body-conscious gown featured panels of croc-embossed leather and a matching choker, plus shreds of leather draped over her arms and hips.

All’s Fair season 1 premiere, October 2025.

Paulson also got the croc memo in Roksanda, hitting the carpet in a black one-shouldered dress with a croc-embossed pannier skirt. Watts and Nash-Betts also embraced the LBD—Watts in a sleek tea-length Mugler strapless dress outfitted with the house’s signature sharp neckline, Nash-Betts in a theatrical hooded LouBe dress finished with gold embroidery.

Close also swung for the fences; the actor embraced the buzzy, celebrity-approved label Matières Fécales, posing alongside her co-stars in a cream-colored dress, replete with ultra-padded shoulders, a cinched waist, and a shredded hemline, and styled with black leather gloves and boots.

While the All’s Fair cast ran the gamut from sexy to avant-garde at the season 2 premiere last night, they all brought the drama—onscreen and off.
