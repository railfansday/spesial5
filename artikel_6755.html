18 Innovative Hair Products ELLE Editors Chose for 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

From a split-end repair balm to a tangle-free hairbrush, these are our Future of Beauty winners.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Should you try an overnight mask? Or a serum? What about a repair balm? And no, not for your face—for your hair. Though products like these used to be exclusively reserved for skin care routines, in recent years, our hair has gotten the multistep treatment. But with so many new products on the market, it can be difficult to figure out which are worth buying and which are better to skip. For the 2026 Future of Beauty Awards, ELLE testers sprayed their hair with leave-ins, massaged scrubs into their scalps, and styled with various gels and creams to find 18 game-changing hair products.
