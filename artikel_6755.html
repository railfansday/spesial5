Beyoncé Just Revealed Where 9-Year-Old Rumi Carter Loves to Shop

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Beyoncé gave fans an early holiday gift this year: a rare new interview, this time with i-D magazine. While she kept the majority of her comments focused on her hair care line Cécred, she did offer a little insight into her youngest daughter Rumi Carter’s sense of style at age 9, as well as her two favorite places to shop.

The outlet asked about Beyoncé’s House of Deréon archive. After reflecting on revisiting the pieces for the 20th anniversary rerelease of her album B’Day, the singer revealed that her daughters Blue Ivy and Rumi would be inheriting all her clothes.

“I’m so excited to pass all my archives to my kids, especially now that they’re all discovering their own personal style,” she said. “I’m going to enjoy seeing how they pull from their parents’ carpets and tours if they’re ever interested. Rumi’s favorite place to shop right now is Ross and TJ Maxx these days.”

Beyoncé also looked back at her wedding day when discussing her favorite piece that her mother, Tina Knowles, ever made for her. “My favorite dress my mother designed was my wedding dress—it was gorgeously simple and made with her love,” Beyoncé explained. “We had a very small wedding in our home [in April 2008]. We told the guests we were having a party, and no one knew they were coming to our wedding but our parents and a few friends. Wearing a dress she designed was very important to me, and more important than any designer dress.”

Knowles has long been impressed with her granddaughter Rumi’s style, too. She praised it during an interview with Oprah Daily in July 2022.

“Rumi loves fashion. She picks out her own clothes,” Knowles said. “We recently went to Disneyland, and she put on her Mickey Mouse shorts. Then she wanted to put on a sparkly printed top which had the same colors. It actually looked really cute.” The moment impressed Knowles, who noted Rumi was just 5 at the time.
