Office Administrator & Team Engagement Coordinator at Yodo1

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

You will: Run the day-to-day operations of Yodo1’s Beijing office, coordinate company-wide and business unit offsites, and find creative ways to help team members build stronger connections in our Work From Anywhere (WFA) culture.

Must have: Strong organizational skills, reliable follow-through, the initiative to bring people together, and a growth mindset: you’re curious about AI and ready to experiment with it to improve your everyday work.

At Yodo1, we don't just build and grow games, we turn them into global successes that become household names.

With 14+ years in the games space, we've reached 3.5 billion+ players and supported 6,000+ developers worldwide. From games like Transformers: Earth Wars, Rodeo Stampede and Ski Safari, to bringing iconic IPs from Hasbro, AMC, Netflix, Paramount and Sony Pictures to leading studios like Rivergame, Candivore, 37Games and Tencent Studios, we connect creativity with global expertise.

Our work spans game development, publishing, IP licensing and ad monetization across mobile, PC and console. We build games, grow franchises and help developers reach players worldwide. We love games. We play games. And together, we grow games that last.

We believe in the power of games and that everyone deserves the opportunity to build a life they love, from anywhere in the world.

We're fully remote by design. We embrace Work From Anywhere, with no fixed hours or limits on time off. We trust our people to own their work and deliver results.

We're driven by performance, curiosity, and continuous improvement. We experiment often, challenge assumptions and value impact over titles.

Yodo1 is looking for an Office Administrator & Team Engagement Coordinator to run our Beijing office, own logistics for company-wide and business unit offsites, and help colleagues build stronger connections in our Work From Anywhere (WFA) culture.

Focused on impact. You understand the 80/20 rule and focus on activities that matter.

Performance-driven and growth-oriented. You have an intrinsic will to succeed while unafraid to try, fail, and learn by experimenting with new approaches and testing ideas.

Cooperative. You help others succeed; you give and receive feedback well.

Humble. You ask for help; you are quick to admit and learn from your mistakes.

SMART. You work and communicate in ways that are Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Run Beijing office operations. Manage the day-to-day needs of the office, including facilities, vendors, supplies, and internal requests. Plan ahead for recurring needs, resolve issues promptly, and look for practical ways to use AI to simplify routine work and automate repetitive tasks.

Own logistics for company-wide and business unit offsites. Work with corporate and business unit teams to turn each offsite’s goals into a clear plan. Manage budgets, timelines, vendors, attendee communications, and on-site arrangements. Flag risks early and use feedback to improve future offsites.

Bring people together in our WFA culture. Plan and run online and in-person activities that help colleagues who don’t normally work together get to know each other. Make it easy for people in different locations and time zones to take part, and use turnout and feedback to decide what to repeat or change

Hands-on experience in office administration, strong organizational skills, and reliable follow-through. You can prioritize competing requests, keep track of details, and follow up without waiting for reminders.

Initiative and the ability to bring people together. You’re comfortable starting conversations, getting people interested, and turning ideas into action. You’re willing to learn how to run online and in-person events.

An open, hands-on AI mindset. You look for ways to use AI in everyday work, try it on real tasks, and check and improve the results.

Proficiency in Chinese and English. You can listen, speak, read, and write proficiently in both languages.

Willingness to travel for offsites as needed.

Experience coordinating work across teams, locations, or time zones.

Experience helping organize offsites, team activities, or community events.

Compensation. We pay based on qualifications, experience, and fit for the role. We provide an allowance to purchase your own device or equipment and offer variable compensation through our annual bonus program.

Growth. We offer an environment where you will have the trust and freedom to try, fail, learn fast, and make a real impact while accelerating your career, and where anyone with a great idea can pitch it directly to our management team.

WFA. Work from anywhere in the world, travel when and where you like, get paid in your currency of choice, and enjoy flexible hours and unlimited paid time off.

Community. Work with an energetic, fast-learning, supportive, and diverse team of Yodies across six continents and 30+ countries who share and support new ideas and get things done.
