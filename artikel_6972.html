Docker: Renewals Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Docker is the platform at the center of how intelligent software gets built. For more than a decade, Docker has helped millions of developers and thousands of organizations build, ship, and run software consistently across any environment. Today, Docker extends that same trust to the AI era. Docker Hardened Images form the foundation agentic code is built on, local and cloud Docker Sandboxes isolate where agents run, and Docker AI Governance sets enterprise-wide policy. Together, they give builders and organizations the confidence to adopt any model, tool, or agent without compromising security, governance, or control.

_______________________________________________________________________

As a Renewals Manager at Docker, you will be responsible for driving the renewal process for our customers, ensuring that they continue to see the value in our products and services. You will work closely with our Account Executives and customers to identify opportunities for upselling and cross-selling, and to negotiate and close renewal contracts.

A successful Renewals Manager at Docker will be able to build strong relationships with customers and communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. They will have a deep understanding of our products and services, and be able to articulate the value that we provide to our customers. They will be proactive and able to manage multiple accounts simultaneously, while also being able to identify and prioritize opportunities for upselling and cross-selling.

Please note: French and English language fluency is a requirement for this role.

_______________________________________________________________________

Manage the renewal process for a portfolio of customers end-to-end, ensuring timely and accurate renewals.

Build strong relationships with customers, and identify opportunities for upselling and cross-selling.

Negotiate and close renewal contracts on time, working closely with Account Executives or independently.

Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, articulating the value that we provide to our customers.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure that customer needs are met and issues are resolved.

Identify and mitigate churn risk early by taking ownership over outreach, data review and other signals

Maintain accurate forecasts and Salesforce hygiene according to provide clear visibility into risk and outcomes

_______________________________________________________________________

Bachelor's degree in business, marketing, or a related field.

4+ years of experience in a sales or account management role preferably in a SaaS company, but not required.

Experience working with Mid Market and Enterprise level accounts

Strong communication and negotiation skills.

Ability to build strong relationships with customers.

Proactive and able to manage multiple accounts simultaneously.

Experience with Salesforce or other CRM tools is a plus.

_______________________________________________________________________

Onboarding and training on Docker's products and services.

Introduction to the Renewals team and cross-functional teams.

Review of current customer portfolio and renewal pipeline.

Management of a portfolio of customers and renewals.

Identification and pursuit of upselling and cross-selling opportunities.

Collaboration with cross-functional teams to resolve customer issues and meet customer needs.

Continued management of a portfolio of customers and renewals, with a focus on driving customer satisfaction and revenue growth.

Identification and pursuit of strategic opportunities for upselling and cross-selling.

Active participation in cross-functional projects to improve the customer experience.
