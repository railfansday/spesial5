Ukrainian Solos New Route in Gokyo Valley, Near Everest

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mykyta Balabanov of Ukraine has soloed a new route on the northeast face of Nepal’s Phari Lapcha (6,017m). Also known as Machhermo Peak, it sits 24km west of Everest in the Gokyo Valley.

Balabanov is still trekking back to Lukla, but has given ExplorersWeb some details and photos about this impressive new route.

The Ukrainian set off on his own up an unclimbed line on the northeast face on November 19. He made two bivouacs on the way up and one more on the descent.

“The level of difficulty is hard to tell, as always when solo, but I’d say it was around 80° on ice, M5+, 5c on rock, and lots of poor snow!” Balavanov said.

He estimates the new route is 1,200m long, and spans some 850 vertical meters from base to summit.

Rough topo of Balabanov’s solo route on the northeast face of Phari Lapcha. Photo: Mykyta Balabanov

Another side of Phari Lapcha saw action just one day before Balabanov, when U.S. guide Bennie Lieber led Canadian Emma Deguara up the Stone(d) Yeti route on the south face.

“I was not related to that climb, as I climbed alone after approaching from a different valley,” Balabanov explained. “However, I saw their tracks on the summit and spotted their bivy place at the bottom of the south face.”

Lieber and Deguara made the second ascent of their chosen route. “While the technical grade (M3, AI2+) is easily digestible, the room for mistakes, due to exposure, is zero,” Lieber said.

Mykyta Balabanov and fellow Ukrainian Mikhail Fomino opened a new route on Ama Dablam in 2024, but they first became widely known for their new route on the Southeast Ridge of Annapurna III in 2021.
