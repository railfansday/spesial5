Can personalized cutoffs for CA19-9 levels help spot early pancreatic cancer? (Gastro Hep Advances)

There's room for improvement when it comes to performing repeat colonoscopy in patients with an inadequate bowel preparation on the first go-round, an analysis of the GI Quality Improvement Consortium indicated. (American Journal of Gastroenterology)

In other colonoscopy news, combination strategies involving computer-aided detection may be best for spotting colorectal neoplasia, a meta-analysis of randomized trials suggested. (Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology)

Amid layoffs, drugmaker nChroma Bio halted enrollment of a phase I/II trial for its liver-directed epigenetic gene silencing therapy in development as a potential functional cure for chronic hepatitis B. (Fierce Biotech)

Three brothers, three liver transplants, and all at the same hospital. (People)

An analysis in Liver Transplantation offered reassurance on transplant outcomes following donation after cardiac death from donors 70 and older.

A meta-analysis of randomized trials on intravenous albumin for decompensated cirrhosis showed a survival benefit only for spontaneous bacterial peritonitis and hepatic encephalopathy. (Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology)

Exploring the links between bile acid metabolism disruptions and pediatric short bowel syndrome. (Gastro Hep Advances)

The American Gastroenterological Association issued new guidelines on the endoscopic management of Zenker's diverticulum and cricopharyngeal bar. (Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology)

Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology published year-in-review articles covering developments in irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) and the contemporary management of Crohn's disease.

Continuing advanced therapies before elective Crohn's surgery may reduce serious complications, according to research in the Journal of the American College of Surgeons.

Meanwhile, experts argued that starting advanced therapies within a year of an inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) diagnosis may be appropriate in many clinical scenarios. (American Journal of Gastroenterology)

A single-center retrospective study warned that two-thirds of IBD patients relapsed after stopping biologic therapy, with a median time to relapse of 23.5 months. (Scandinavian Journal of Gastroenterology)

Data from the VIVID-2 extension study showed mirikizumab (Omvoh) to be a good option in Crohn's disease for patients who don't respond to ustekinumab (Stelara). (American Journal of Gastroenterology)
