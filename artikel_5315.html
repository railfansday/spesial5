Stellar Activates Protocol 30 Bell Testnet, Begins Testing Quantum-Resistant Signatures

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Stellar’s Bell upgrade reached its next major milestone today, according to the data shared by the Stellar Development Foundation. The Bell (Protocol 30 upgrade) went live on the testnet on Tuesday, November 3. The update rolled out smart-account features for enterprises and post-quantum signature verification, enhancing transaction and security efficiency.

As announced today, Stellar activated the Bell upgrade on its testnet (test network) on Nov. 3. Stellar’s move to enable Bell integration on the testnet is a crucial step as it prepares for a Mainnet vote set for Nov. 23.

Bell’s activation on the testnet allows developers to test the upgrade’s capabilities. Through the test environment, developers explore the capabilities of new smart contract features for businesses and the effectiveness of post-quantum signature verification.

The Bell upgrade, also commonly recognized as Protocol 30, aims to improve smart account capabilities for businesses. At the same time, it prepares Stellar’s infrastructure for future security requirements, including quantum-safe signatures and faster ledgers.

Its mainnet launch is scheduled for Nov. 23, subject to community voting, which could shape the future capabilities and direction of Stellar’s blockchain technology. With the upgrade, businesses can use more sophisticated, efficient accounts, while quantum security remains a long-term necessity.

Stellar entered an important phase on Tuesday as the Bell upgrade went live on its testnet. The upgrade represents another step in the blockchain’s commitment to supporting financial utilities and institutional use cases.

The Protocol 30 upgrade seeks to expand what enterprises can do with accounts on the Stellar blockchain and also lays the foundation for quantum-safe digital signatures.

Digital signatures are important for blockchain security as they allow customers to authorize transactions and prove ownership and control of their accounts.

Advancing quantum computing poses a serious threat to cryptographic blockchains. This explains why Stellar is preparing to make its network quantum-resistant to address such potential risks.
