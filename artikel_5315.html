Chatbot subscribers are too fickle for AI companies to consider charging more

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Americans are ditching AI chatbots in large numbers. Anthropic and OpenAI can’t raise prices without losing more of them

Matteo Della Torre / NurPhoto via Getty Images

If an AI service raised its prices, it would likely bring in less money, not more. Across consumer AI services, the economists estimate that raising prices by 10% would cut what people buy by nearly half. Anthropic has been lowering what businesses pay instead, cutting customers' bills by about $600 million in the third quarter through new model versions and contract discounts, Acuity found, and Anthropic's revenue still grew 47% from the second quarter, to $17 billion.
