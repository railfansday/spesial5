The Pedalling Prescription: 12 Ways Cycling Can Change Your Mind (P)

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

From instant cognitive boosts to lasting emotional resilience, cycling offers powerful, science-backed brain benefits.

Cycling is widely celebrated for its cardiovascular and physical health benefits, but its effects on the mind may be just as remarkable.

Emerging research suggests that pedalling can influence mood, stress, memory, attention and motivation in many different ways.

These mental shifts do not follow a single timeline: while some cognitive and emotional benefits appear almost instantly after a few minutes in the saddle, others unfold gradually over weeks or months of regular riding.
