As its air business softens, MakeMyTrip is pushing harder into everything else. Quick Ride gives it a product people use on a random Thursday, not just before a holiday. The test is whether those commuters become MakeMyTrip travelers.

MakeMyTrip Friday announced the acquisition of a majority stake in Bengaluru-based ride-pooling platform Quick Ride, expanding its ground transportation business.

The online travel company said the acquisition gives it an entry into the shared mobility segment, complementing its existing ground transport offerings, which include bus bookings through redBus and intercity car rentals through Savaari. MakeMyTrip did not say how much it invested in Quick Ride, or its precise ownership, beyond now holding a majority share.

Quick Ride connects commuters traveling along similar routes, allowing them to share rides and split commuting costs. The platform also works with corporates, IT parks and business campuses, giving it access to a customer base beyond individual travelers.

MakeMyTrip has acquired a majority stake in Bengaluru-based ride-pooling platform Quick Ride, adding daily urban commuting to a ground transport business that already includes redBus for buses and Savaari for intercity car rentals. The company did not disclose the price or its precise ownership level. Quick Ride moved its ride-pooling business and brand into a new entity, Co Ride Mobility, which MakeMyTrip now controls, while the electric vehicle taxi business stays with iDisha Info Labs and Quick Ride's existing promoters continue to run the business. The deal continues a run of acquisitions — Savaari in 2023, corporate expense platform Happay in 2024, Flamingo Transworld in group holidays, and a minority stake in visa processor Atlys — as MakeMyTrip pushes beyond flights and hotels ahead of a reported India IPO. The timing tracks with its results: non-air businesses reached 63.2% of gross bookings in the fiscal second quarter ended September 30, 2026, up from 61.5% a year earlier, and the "other" segment that includes cabs, insurance, foreign exchange, and activities posted adjusted margins of $24.4 million, up about 30% year over year. Ride-pooling gives MakeMyTrip something its other products do not: a reason for customers to open the app on days they are not traveling.
