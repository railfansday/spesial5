Pay at Costco with a Visa, the only credit card its U.S. warehouses take. Get the Costco Anywhere Visa if you buy gas there, since it pays 5% on up to $7,000 a year. In the warehouse its 2% only matches a no-fee 2% card, and Robinhood’s Gold card beats it at 3% for a $50 yearly membership.

So the first question is whether you buy enough Costco gas to carry its card, and the second is which Visa should pay for your cart. Costco’s gas stations accept Visa and no other credit card as well, according to Costco’s membership terms, while Costco.com also takes Mastercard.

The store’s own cards come first, then flat-rate cards that pay the same rate on every purchase, then cards that pay more as your balance at their bank grows.

Both pay cash back once a year, after the February statement closes, as a certificate you redeem at a U.S. Costco warehouse for merchandise or cash.

At 5%, a household that buys $3,000 of gas a year at Costco’s pumps earns $150 with this card, against $60 on a 2% card.

Business owners who buy gas and supplies at Costco get the personal card’s rates on business spending, and each employee who holds a Costco membership can get a card at no fee.

For more information, read our full Costco Anywhere Visa® Business Card by Citi Review.

With Robinhood’s $50-a-year Gold membership, it pays 3% in the warehouse and at the pump, and after that fee it beats a no-fee 2% card once you charge it more than $5,000 a year.

For more information, read our full Robinhood Gold Visa® Card Review.

Members who want one card with no annual fee and nothing else to join get 2% from it in the warehouse, at the pump, and everywhere else.

Fidelity investors earn 2% in the warehouse, as Costco’s own card does, but the cash back goes into a Fidelity brokerage, retirement, or college savings account instead of onto a Costco certificate.

For more information, read our full Fidelity® Rewards Visa Signature® Credit Card Review.

U.S. Bank customers with $10,000 already in Smartly Checking, and a Smartly Savings account, earn 2.5% in the warehouse, and 4% at $100,000, on up to $10,000 of purchases a billing cycle.

For more information, read our full U.S. Bank Smartly™ Visa Signature® Card Review.

Bank of America checking customers in its BofA Rewards program earn 2.2% at wholesale clubs like Costco, or 3% once their deposit and investment balances there average $100,000, up to a quarterly cap.

For more information, read our full Bank of America® Customized Cash Rewards Credit Card Review.

At Costco, gas is where its own card leads, and the cart is where a flat rate or a bank balance decides what you earn. Put Costco gas on the Costco Anywhere Visa and pair it with a no-fee 2% Visa for the cart, or with the Robinhood Gold Card if you’d put over $5,000 a year on it.
