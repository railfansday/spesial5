Shop for next year’s ornaments, lights, wrapping paper, and cards in the week after Christmas, when holiday decor goes on clearance at 50% off or more and gets cheaper again by early January. Buy only what you’ll use again and have room to store, and you’ll stop paying full price every December.

The ornaments and wrap on store shelves in December are the same ones that go on clearance the day after Christmas. On Dec. 26, 2025, Target put select holiday decor at 50% off and up in its post-holiday clearance.

At 50% off, a $40 box of ornaments costs $20. That’s rarely the last markdown. Whatever’s left usually drops to 70% to 90% off by the first week or two of January, when the same box would cost $12, then $4.

The longer you wait, though, the less is left to choose from. Buy what you want most at the first markdown, and go back for extras as prices fall. Bigger purchases, such as TVs and mattresses, have yearly sale seasons of their own.

Stock up on what your household uses every year:

Skip anything printed with the year, and anything you’d buy only because it’s 90% off.

For what you need before Christmas, buy things that last for years, and use what you already have. ENERGY STAR says its certified LED light strings use about 92% less energy than incandescent ones and can last up to 10 times longer. A sturdy gift bag can be reused for several Christmases, while wrapping paper is used once. Evergreen clippings from the yard and the decorations already in your boxes cost nothing.

While you decorate and wrap this year, keep a list of what breaks and what runs out, and take it to the clearance aisle on Dec. 26.
