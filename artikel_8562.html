CreditStrong, a division of Austin Capital Bank, sells two credit-builder loans, Instal and MAGNUM, that lock the money you borrow in savings until you’ve repaid it, plus Revolv, a credit line sold by subscription. Instal, the entry loan, releases $1,010 after 48 monthly payments, and by our math its interest and fees add up to $349.

CreditStrong suits borrowers with a short or damaged credit history who can keep each payment on autopay for the full term. Borrowers who can join a credit union should price its credit-builder loan first, and anyone who might miss a payment is safer with Revolv’s yearly plan, which requires no monthly payment.
