This is one for the Xbox players, so PS5 readers, check back in a moment because I swear I love you all equally.

The Nacon RXU is my favorite controller for Xbox overall. It's one of the premium, elite gamepads for that console and features maybe the best microswitch buttons I've ever pressed. Alongside its prowess as a great gamepad doing the basics well, it has four back buttons, two additional bumpers, and a screen to help you customize it on a software level.
