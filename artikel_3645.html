Brielle Biermann Marries Billy Seidl, Brothers Absent Amid KJ's Assault Trial

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kim Zolciak’s daughter Brielle Biermann is officially a married woman.

The influencer and former Don’t Be Tardy star married baseball player Billy Seidl in front of a group of close family and friends in Naples, Florida, on Saturday, November 21. Brielle shared footage of herself and her husband in their wedding attire, as waves rolled in on a nearby beach.

“Mrs. Seidl,” Brielle captioned the clip.

Brielle wore a traditional white, flowing wedding dress with a large black bow adorning the left side of the gown while Seidl opted for a traditional tuxedo with a black bowtie. Zolciak was reportedly in attendance with her boyfriend, Kyle Mowitz, per TMZ.

Brielle’s sister Ariana Biermann was one of many to wish the couple congratulations, writing in Instagram comment section, “I’m crying. I love you so much.”

The bride’s former stepdad, Kroy Biermann, was reportedly absent at the Naples, Florida, wedding as he did not receive an invitation. Also absent was Brielle’s half-brother KJ Biermann amid his legal troubles. (Twins Kaia and Kane were reportedly absent as well.)

Months before the ceremony, Brielle was able to enjoy a bachelorette weekend with her closest friends, including sister Ariana.

“God forbid I have some fun before becoming mrs. 🎀 ,” Brielle wrote via Instagram on August 13 after celebrating in Turks & Caicos.

One month later, Brielle gathered with family and friends for a bridal shower.

“Can’t believe my baby is getting married 🤭🙈 to the most incredible man @bseidl21 ❤️,” Zolciak wrote via Instagram at the time.

Despite some happy celebrations, Brielle and her family have also been embroiled in scandal after her brother Kroy “KJ” Biermann Jr. was arrested for aggravated sodomy and aggravated sexual assault on August 17. (His lawyer has denied all charges.)

Although KJ was released from a youth detention center in September, a judge’s ruling orders the high school student to wear an electronic ankle monitor under 24-hour house arrest.

“The allegations being made against my son are nothing short of reckless and deeply troubling. He categorically denies ever being involved with this woman or engaging in the conduct she has alleged,” Zolciak said in a statement to Us days after the arrest. “My son voluntarily took a polygraph examination regarding these allegations and passed. Additionally, an examination was conducted, and no DNA belonging to my son was found.”

On Thursday (November 21), court documents obtained by Us confirmed that KJ’s accuser “will be made available to testify at trial and be subject to cross-examination.” Earlier in the week, Georgia prosecutors opposed a motion by KJ’s lawyers to have his case moved to juvenile court on the grounds that the teen showed “sophistication and premeditation” in his alleged assault.

“This behavior has become part of a deeply troubling pattern when it comes to this defendant forcing sexual compliance through a personal relationship,” the docs read. “This defendant has proven to be beyond the rehabilitation offered within the Fulton County Juvenile Court process.”

Regardless of any drama going on in her family, Brielle has remained focused on her love story with Seidl.

One year after the pair confirmed their relationship status, Seidl proposed to Brielle in Arizona.

“Forever with you 🤍🥹🫀🫶🏻,” Brielle wrote via Instagram in February 2024 with photos from the romantic moment.

In a separate post, Zolciak wrote, “I’m so happy for @briellebiermann @bseidl21. Nothing makes me happier than seeing my kids happy! 💍It’s the best feeling EVER!! Love you both to the moon and back and look forward to alllll the years to come 🙏🏻❤️😍.”

Despite growing up on reality TV, Brielle hasn’t shared every aspect of her relationship online. When it comes to major milestones, however, the former Real Housewives of Atlanta star can’t help but celebrate.

“I can’t believe it’s been 1 year since we got engaged!!” she wrote via Instagram in February 2025. “Happy anniversary billy. I can’t wait to marry you !! 🤍🩵🩷🥹🥰 .”
