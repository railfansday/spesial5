Kaitlyn Bristowe Reveals Dog Is on Feeding Tubes Weeks After Overdose

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Former Bachelorette Kaitlyn Bristowe’s beloved dog Pinot has been battling additional health issues weeks after suffering a near-fatal overdose.

“Pinot cannot catch a break,” Bristowe, 41, wrote via Instagram on Saturday, November 21. “He has scar tissue built up in his throat (strictures) some of the worst they have seen. He has feeding tubes and a balloon in his esophagus that we have to pump air into twice a day for six weeks.”

Bristowe, who is the dog mom of golden retrievers Pinot and Ramen, revealed late last month that she was “told to say goodbye” to Pinot after he accidentally overdosed on 120 prescription pills for his preexisting heart condition. The dog returned home after five days in a critical care unit weeks before his latest health scare.

“Pinot and I would love to thank his doggy dad and my partner,” Bristowe added on Saturday, referring to her mystery beau rumored to be Zac Clark. “He has been a hero, the amc, and for your prayers to keep him going. I’ve never met a dog willing to fight so hard through all of this.”

Bristowe further promised to “keep everyone posted” on Pinot’s condition.

“Just know when you see me online, you might not know what’s going on behind the scenes,” she continued. “And sometimes my coping mechanism is sleeping, sometimes it’s trying to be funny, sometimes it’s letting loose and sometimes it’s crying until I can’t cry anymore. Thank you guys for all the support. I know I’m not perfect, but I will continue to be open and honest.

Bristowe is hoping that her pup will make an eventual recovery.

“My fighter. My actual earth angel,” she captioned her Instagram post. “The next six weeks will be all hands on deck and a lot of prayers.”

“And we are already on our way back to the ER,” the “Off the Vine” podcast host wrote via her Instagram Stories later on Saturday. “His feeding tube has a clog. He is OK.”

After returning home again, Bristowe captioned a selfie with her pooch, “And now. We rest. (Till 6 a.m. meds.)”

Pinot’s most recent health setback began when Bristowe was out of town on the Bachelor Nation fan cruise.

“I had such a wonderful experience on the Bachelor cruise. I felt so much shame for being out there when this happened,” she wrote via her Stories on Saturday. “My Bachelor family and all the wonderful people on the cruise made me feel so happy. And, again, my person at home taking care of Pinot did everything right.”
