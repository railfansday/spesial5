Klopp advice and Iraola confidence boosts Wirtz

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wirtz moved from Bayer Leverkusen to Liverpool for Â£116m in 2025

Florian Wirtz says he is enjoying his best form yet at Liverpool and benefiting from Andoni Iraola's confidence and Jurgen Klopp's advice.

Wirtz, 23, scored in Liverpool's 4-0 win over Manchester United on Sunday and has three goals and six assists this season.

The Germany midfielder made the Â£116m move from Bayer Leverkusen in the summer of 2025 and after failing to make his mark in his first campaign in England, is beginning to showcase his quality for Iraola's side.

Speaking in Belgium before Liverpool's Champions League game against Club Brugge, Wirtz said: "I can't really say that I've changed that much.

"I've become a bit better on a physical level and that's the only reason that could explain it."

He also said he has been picking the brains of Germany manager Klopp, the former Liverpool boss, while on international duty.

"He tells me all the time that he loved his time here, the people love him here and tries to give me some advice to get successful over here as he has shown how it is done," Wirtz said.

"He can give me some good advice. It's always small things. More private things.

"But he is speaking good about the club, the region, the people, and that helps to hear someone who has experienced it for a long time."

On the influence of Iraola, who replaced Arne Slot as Liverpool head coach in the summer, Wirtz added: "He is bringing me back in a place where I was maybe two years ago where I played really good football, and I can feel that he gives me the confidence and I'm really happy about that.

"I'm just trying to give him back what he gives me."

Liverpool have nine points from their four Champions League games, having won their first three before losing to Fenerbahce in Istanbul this month.

Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch are both absent from the travelling squad with injuries, with Iraola confirming that Gakpo will be out for the Merseyside derby this weekend, while adding that it is "difficult to say" for Gravenberch.
