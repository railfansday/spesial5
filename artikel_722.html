The Best Gifts for Musicians and Music Fans in 2026: Fender Guitars, DJ Decks, Vinyl Sets and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Everyone has a music lover in their life, and the holidays are the perfect time to get them something special, whether it’s a record player, a CD box set, a nice pair of headphones or some earplugs to protect their hearing at concerts.

Variety has curated this list of the best music books, home stereo systems and personal listening devices — as well as instruments and gear for both beginners and veteran musicians — so you can surprise your audiophile friend or guitar-playing relative with the perfect present this holiday season. While Squier is Fender’s entry-level guitar line, it feels slightly unfair to call one a “beginner guitar” considering its quality. In the Squier Classic Vibe Series, Fender has expanded its collection of vintage-inspired designs at an affordable price, with Stratocasters, Jaguars and two limited-edition Telecasters . The guitars come in all sorts of colors, from Fiesta Red to White Blonde to Lake Placid Blue. This guitar is the perfect gift for a new shredder who is serious about upping their game.

For the serious musician, touring guitarist or passionate hobbyist looking for a more substantial product, the Fender New American Professional is a sleek, sexy and strong piece of hardware. The Telecasters have a comfortable, flexible bridge and single-coil pickups that deliver the snappy twang synonymous with the brand. Plus, it comes with a Deluxe Molded hardshell case for taking music on the go.

For musicians who wish to record music on the go, the Fender AudioBox Go is a basic interface that lets you plug in and set up anywhere. It may not be as dynamic as higher-tech options, like the classic Focusrite Scarlett 2i2, but it’s more compact and — at its low price point — offers everything a beginner will need for laying down audio tracks. Each purchase also comes with a six-month Fender Studio Pro trial.

Be the life of the party with the Akai Numark Party Mix III, a Bluetooth DJ controller with stems control and a beat-synced LED light show. Beginner DJs can learn how to mix tracks and pump up the jams from their phone or tablet.

Chances are you’ve heard a Shure SM7 without knowing it: on the radio or even on your favorite podcast. It’s the industry standard mic that makes voices sound rich, warm and close. The SM7dB is the newest and most headache-free version. Its built-in booster makes it sound great plugged into a simple home setup, and a couple of switches let you fine-tune the tone. For the friend starting a podcast or recording the next great American album, this mic is an upgrade they’ll hear instantly.

A simple sound bar can work wonders on a home television setup. Yamaha’s brand new B200A, perfect for sound enhancement in TV, gaming and music streaming, features a built-in subwoofer and Dolby Atmos. You’ll never complain again about not being able to hear the dialogue in your favorite show. Check out Variety’s home theater awards with more soundbar and audio options here.

For those looking to further optimize the sound in their living rooms or home theaters, Yamaha’s RX300A AV receiver is an intuitive system that serves as a flexible hub for all sorts of speaker layouts. With an easy setup, you can tailor immersive audio to any space at home.

A wireless smart speaker with built-in Dolby Atmos, the Denon Home 600 is a powerful, standalone stereo device for the home. It’s for casual music listeners and audiophiles looking for one speaker with built-in subwoofers and an eight-driver array creating a three-dimensional, immersive sound. If this price point is too high, Denon also makes two smaller models, the 200 and the 400.

Turn your living room into a full-fledged home theater with Sony’s Bravia Theater Trio, all you need to create a simple surround-sound experience at home. The three-speaker design works best with large televisions, and the product was created in collaboration with Sony Pictures Entertainment so the sound lives up to the blockbuster standard of today’s filmmakers. With the Bravia Connect app, the system can be calibrated and controlled from your smartphone.

For the new guitar player who hasn’t quite found their desired feel, this Tortex Variety Pack comes with 12 picks across six different gauges. The matte surface makes them easy to grip, and the bright colors will make any guitarist stand out.

As far as high-end headphones go, the Bowers & Wilkins Px8 S2 are top of the line. They’re sleek and comfortable, and the sound quality is excellent. These premium headphones are made in four color options, and they come with a flat, zipper case for portability. With excellent noise cancellation, the Px8 S2s are perfect for flying.

For the person in your life who has been collecting records for years but still sticks to streaming, this fully automatic, analog turntable from Denon is a great introduction to playing vinyl. It has an easy, two-button setup and premium sound quality. And it features a built-in phono pre-amp, meaning it can plug directly into speakers or a stereo without extra gear. These Pioneer speakers can help upgrade your DJ setup or even just your home office. These compact 4-inch monitors can pair over Bluetooth for everyday listening, or plug into a mixer when it’s time to light up the party. Sleek enough for a dorm or living room, and available in white too, the DM-40D-BTs are a gift that gets cranked up the day they’re unwrapped.

Do you know someone who can’t stop talking about their guitar? Or someone who can’t seem to ever find their keys? Well, if those happen to be the same person, the Fender Jack Rack is the perfect household accessory. The wall-mounted key holder is a pint-sized replica of a classic Fender amp — it looks and feels exactly like one, with four input jacks across the front. And it comes with four quarter-inch-cable keychains, so you can plug in every time you get home.

The only downside to being a concert junkie is the morning-after ring. Most music fans don’t want to dilute the sound of live music with crappy foam earplugs, and custom plugs can be quite expensive. The Loop Experience 2 earplugs have an acoustic channel and mesh filter that turn the volume down by 17 dB while keeping the music clear. Plus, they’re shaped like jewelry and come in a variety of colors. For those with a front-row habit who are conscious about their hearing, the Loops are a perfect and affordable gift.

Long before you could play a synth on your smartphone, there was the Stylophone: a palm-sized instrument you play by touching a brass-tipped stylus to a metal keyboard. Invented in 1967, it buzzed its way onto David Bowie’s “Space Oddity,” Kraftwerk’s “Pocket Calculator” and the White Stripes’ “Icky Thump.” This affordable reissue is faithful to the original instrument’s charming tone and offers a vibrato function. Anyone can find a melody in minutes, which makes it a perfect gift for seasoned musicians and total beginners alike.

A must-read classic for anyone interested in punk or rock — or a loved one who recently moved to New York — “Just Kids” chronicles Patti Smith’s young adulthood in Manhattan, when she met and fell in love with the artist Robert Mapplethorpe. It’s a music memoir, yes, but it’s more than that. Smith’s impeccable prose teems with possibility as she writes of friendship, music and growing up. It won the National Book Award in 2010. Check out more celebrity memoirs to shop here.

Often considered one of the best rock books ever written, “Trouble Boys” tells the exciting but often heartbreaking story of the Replacements, a band that should have been huge but did everything possible to make sure they weren’t. With access to its surviving members, author Bob Mehr wrote about the foundational alternative rock band behind “Can’t Hardly Wait” and “I Will Dare.” Behind the music, it’s a gripping story about brotherhood and self-sabotage.

In “Dilla Time: The Life and Afterlife of J Dilla, the Hip-Hop Producer Who Reinvented Rhythm,” Dan Charnas tells the story of the Detroit producer who changed hip-hop and died tragically at 32. This biography explains how J Dilla revolutionized rhythm itself and became one of the most influential beat makers of all time, a music titan who continues to loom large over his genre.

Bob Dylan’s Rolling Thunder Revue is one of the most famous tours in music history. Now you can listen to the live recordings from the second leg with this sprawling box set. Spread across 39 CDs are 22 performances from spring 1976, with 371 of its 381 tracks never before released. Dylan sounds explosive and raw, furnishing his most iconic songs with a full band including Mick Ronson, Scarlet Rivera and T Bone Burnett, with Joan Baez dropping in for duets. Shop more new vinyl releases here.

For the Tom Petty fanatic in your life, the “Origins” box set covers the rocker’s early years in the 1970s, when he and the Heartbreakers churned out some of the best rock songs of all time, like “American Girl” and “Breakdown.” The four-CD set includes 50th-anniversary editions of the band’s first two albums and a blistering 1977 set from the Record Plant, as well as a DVD of their 1978 Old Grey Whistle Test appearance, a Blu-ray of Atmos mixes and a 46-page fanzine.
