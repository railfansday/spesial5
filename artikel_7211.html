Apple’s Heartfelt Holiday Ad Gifts the Power of Belief

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Believing in someone can make all the difference.

That’s the message of Apple’s 2026 holiday campaign, which launches today (Nov. 12). The 4-minute short film, “The Dreamer,” was created in partnership with TBWA\Media Arts Lab and directed by Melina Matsoukas.

“The Dreamer” tells the story of one man’s relationship with art, from infancy to young adulthood. Like other Apple short films—such as “Someday” and “Welcome Home,” both directed by Spike Jonze—“The Dreamer” forgoes dialogue to rely on visual storytelling. The film’s cinematic images were shot by cinematographer Bradford Young.

The film opens with an infant lying beneath a colorful mobile with dangling shapes.

Richard Lowe is a freelance writer based in Los Angeles.
