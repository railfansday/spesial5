Clover is reinventing health insurance by working to keep people healthier.

The Actuarial Strategic Operations team serves as the operational and financial nerve center for Clover's Medicare Advantage business. Embedded within the Actuarial and Finance organization, the team combines deep business expertise with advanced analytics and AI to integrate financial, operational, and actuarial insights, monitor enterprise performance, evaluate strategic initiatives, and deliver timely recommendations that improve affordability, member outcomes, and financial performance.

At Clover, AI is part of how we work. We expect leaders to thoughtfully incorporate tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and emerging enterprise AI technologies into their daily workflows to improve analytical quality, accelerate execution, and enable better decisions.

The Enterprise Performance Lead serves as a strategic business partner within Clover's Actuarial Strategic Operations, owning financial and operational partnership for one or more areas across the Medicare Advantage business. This role partners across Finance, Actuarial, Clinical, Revenue, Pharmacy, and Operations to align operational execution with financial performance, ensuring enterprise initiatives deliver expected outcomes. As a member of the Actuarial Strategic Operations team, you will leverage automation and AI-enabled ways of working to enhance analytical workflows, improve reporting, accelerate insight generation, and strengthen executive decision support.

The Enterprise Performance Lead reports to the Director of Actuarial Strategic Operations and serves as a strategic business partner within Clover's MA Plan Actuarial Strategic Operations team.
