Vote now for 2026's RPS 100: Reader Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

For the past six years, the team at RPS have got together to pick what we consider to be the best 100 games you can play on PC. There's very little science involved, and we try not to get too hung up on what games should make the cut. From year to year, our rankings and choices vary wildly as the formation and tastes of the team changes.

However, our definitive list isn't always met with a chorus of approval and cheers of 'Hear, hear!', "'Well done!', and 'Couldn't have said it better myself!', so we also have the RPS 100: Reader Edition. This is your chance to submit your ten favourite PC games of all time. Your votes will be tallied up with all the other submissions from our other readers and we will produce a list of the community's top 100.

We've just finished revealing what we currently consider to be the Best PC games of all time and now it's your turn.

Using this very Google Form you can submit up to ten votes for your favourite PC games of all time. I will then tally them up with all the other votes from RPS's readers and produce a list of the community's top 100.

Now, a note on scoring. The game you put in 10th place will be awarded one point, 9th place will be worth two, 8th place will be worth three, and so on until you reach your top pick, which will be worth ten points. Please write the full game name (and include the year of release if there are multiple titles in a series with the same name, such as Doom (1993) and Doom (2016)). If in doubt, check how the game is titled on Steam.

Last year, I implemented a change to create a greater variety of games on the list. I combined series scores, so if one reader voted for Civilization IV and another voted for Civilization VI, the scores of both were combined and the entry would be for the Civilization series. I also shared the breakdown of the votes for each title in the entry, so you could see which was most popular within the series.

I will be doing the same again this year, as we saw a lot more games that wouldn't have made the cut otherwise. However, I will try to do a better job of separating out games that are very different from one another even if they are in the same series. Warcraft 3 and World of Warcraft are both Warcraft games, but, admittedly, they aren't similar games. I would ask for some leeway on this, though, as not every example is clear cut. Should Alpha Centauri be counted among the Civilization games? Are Final Fantasy XIV and Final Fantasy Tactics - The Ivalice Chronicles truly worlds away from the other games in the Final Fantasy series?

This does also mean that one-shot games can struggle to compete against games which are part of longrunning series. After all, Crimson Skies isn't just competing against Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020), it is competing against votes for any and all Microsoft Flight Simulator games ever released. Unfortunately, this is a cost of the system, but I prefer the variety that comes from seeing more games represented instead of several separate entries for games within the same series.

We also invite you to tell us why you've chosen each game. This is entirely voluntary, you can simply enter the game titles if you would prefer. However, I'll choose a selection of what readers have said about winning games so we can better understand why the RPS community loves these games. Try not to write any more than 100 words. If you have more to say you can always drop it in the comments of the list when we publish it at the end of the year.

To cast your votes, please make sure you complete this form by 5pm GMT on Sunday, December 6th.
