WoW Forever Beta Patch Notes (Nov. 3, 2026) – What’s coming before release?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Blizzard dropped one final patch before World of Warcraft: Forever launches.

We’re barely a day and a half away from World of Warcraft: Forever‘s launch, and Blizzard released one last round of patches before our characters re-enter Azeroth for the first time. It’s no bad news, though – everyone is getting a power-up.

This batch of fixes came after some older patches that made the Forever beta feel a bit dull to some players. Blizzard devs admitted that limiting for the sake of balancing was them turning to old habits.

“Some of this round of changes reflects areas where we were likely hasty in turning to Burning Crusade- or Wrath-era solutions rather than carving out new ones that better fit into the Forever combat model,” Community Manager Kaivax said in the patch notes.

Read on below for the biggest changes ahead of WoW: Forever‘s launch day.

Naturally, the newest patch affected WoW Forever classes across the board. But surprisingly, this round of updates weren’t nerfs that kept everyone at the same power levels. That’s old-era stuff, which would defeat the ethos of Forever.

“We came to the realization that trying to hold ourselves roughly to Classic power budgets for each class meant that every new element we added needed to come at the expense of something else,” Kaivax said in the post. “Those reductions left some iconic abilities feeling lackluster, and closed the door to other potentially exciting design options.”

“Our aim in class design is not perfect numerical equality, but rather for every class to feel valuable to the groups they join,” Kaivax added, highlighting the team’s desire to reward players for mastering their class. “In Forever, this is accomplished by doubling down on the strengths and differences that make each class unique, rather than overly unifying them.”

Blizzard has finally done it – WoW: Forever is fully controller-compatible. You can use the Edit Mode and the Options menu to customize movement, spells, interactions, and a whole host of other options.

The devs also fixed bugs related to keyboard and mouse vis-a-vis gamepad functionality. Expect smoother progress when using the gamepad with action bars, add-ons, inventory, combat, navigation, and the like.

Racial abilities seem to have cut crit bonuses and added buffs to weaker ones.

Everyone’s going to level up during launch, and respawn rates are adjusted to accommodate this. Expect faster respawn rates in starting zones and lowered slowly as you level up. This will be lowered eventually, once the surge settles.

Bags also don’t auto-equip anymore. You have to manually equip them to your chosen slot. And if you dropped the flag in Darkspear Islands, you no longer get a debuff.
