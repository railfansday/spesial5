D2B: E-commerce & Operations Coordinator (Pharma/Supplements) - NZ

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The client is a New Zealand-based wellness brand specialising in nutritional supplements and wellness products. The company is committed to developing high-quality, science-led products that support everyday health and wellbeing. With a growing presence across New Zealand and Australia, they continue to expand its e-commerce, wholesale, and retail operations while maintaining a strong focus on product quality and customer experience.

We are looking for a highly organised and proactive E-commerce & Operations Coordinator to support the day-to-day operations of a growing wellness brand across New Zealand and Australia.

This is a broad, hands-on role suited to someone who enjoys working across multiple areas of a business, including e-commerce, customer experience, wholesale account management, supply chain, inventory coordination, and project administration.

Working closely with the Head of Operations, you will play an important role in keeping business operations running smoothly, coordinating with customers, retailers, suppliers, manufacturers, and logistics partners, and ensuring that operational processes, documentation, and reporting are maintained accurately.

The ideal candidate is commercially minded, detail-oriented, resourceful, and comfortable taking ownership of tasks in a fast-paced, growing business.

We are particularly interested in candidates who have worked in growing consumer brands, especially within the wellness, supplements, food and beverage, or FMCG industries.

You don't necessarily need experience in every area of the role, but you should bring a strong foundation in e-commerce and business operations, with the versatility to support different functions and take ownership of day-to-day responsibilities.

If you're someone who enjoys bringing structure to operations, solving problems, coordinating people and processes, and contributing to the growth of a dynamic business, we'd love to hear from you.
