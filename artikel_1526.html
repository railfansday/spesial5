From Germany: Roma renew interest in De Cuyper

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

According to reports coming from Germany, Roma are interested in Brighton defender De Cuyper.

The English club signed him in the summer transfer window for €25 million and have no desire to let him leave in January, so much so that they’ve reportedly set their asking price high: €60 million, reports Fussball News.

The player has a contract until 2030. AC Milan and Juventus are also reportedly interested in him in Italy.

Several Premier League clubs are also interested in De Cuyper.
