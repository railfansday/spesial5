Opinion: Liverpool's next Trent Alexander-Arnold passes audition, what a player

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool's next Trent Alexander-Arnold may now be known. He's passed his audition in front of Andoni Iraola and co. - now he can be signed.

Liverpool lost Trent Alexander-Arnold to Real Madrid in 2025 and with that, one of their best creative players disappeared. A unique one, too - no one in the history of football has done what Alexander-Arnold can do from right-back.

We don't think that's too controversial. The Scouser's ability to create and progress the ball from a full-back position is unheard of and he was a core reason why Liverpool were such a standout side during his time at the club.

But with Alexander-Arnold's exit, the Reds knew they had to move in a different director. Conor Bradley was chosen to be first-choice, though injuries have essentially ruined that plan. It might get back on track, but it's a waiting game involving a lot of hope.

Jeremie Frimpong is an entirely different full-back to Alexander-Arnold. To the extent that he's arguably not even a full-back but a wing-back. The Dutchman hasn't ever made the role his own, unable to offer solidity while his attacking game feels too limited to be a threat with another wide player on that side.

So how about going back to the Alexander-Arnold way? It's difficult for as we've said, he's essentially unique in football for what he can do. But there is a player who looks like he could replicate it.

Make no mistake: Club Brugge are a fantastic side and Liverpool's 3-0 win in Belgium was a brilliant one. They kept a clean sheet against a team that usually dominates, even if they had to ride their luck at times.

One notable facet of that match is that Liverpool didn't have the majority of possession against a Belgian side. This Brugge iteration is a brilliant one with some brilliant players.

One of those is Kyriani Sabbe, who is doing notable things from right-back. He's someone averaging over 100 touches per 90 minutes - a ridiculous figure for a right-back but he's a player Brugge turn to for progression.

It was the same against Liverpool on Wednesday. Sabbe had the fourth-most touches in the match and second-most for Brugge as they won the possession battle.

Notably, Sabbe used these touches to move his side forward. His 12 line-breaking passes were five more than any of the four players with more touches than him, and only Jeremy Jacquet managed more in the match (13).

And that number is fewer than Sabbe usually manages. He's averaging an absolutely ridiculous 17 per 90 minutes in Belgium this season, to go along with a long-ball success rate of 56 per cent.

So here's a 21-year-old with the kind of passing numbers you'd only really see from Alexander-Arnold. And he's doing it in the UEFA Champions League and in a successful side that's used to lots of possession.

It makes Sabbe an incredibly interesting prospect and one that Liverpool simply must look at as they look to solve their right-back problem. Wednesday felt like an audition of sorts and one that the player certainly passed.
