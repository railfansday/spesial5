Barcelona targeting January move for Tottenham Hotspur defender

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Tottenham could face growing interest in one of their key players ahead of the January transfer window, with Barcelona president Joan Laporta reportedly keen on bringing Pedro Porro to the Nou Camp.

The Spanish right-back has attracted attention following his performances for Spain at the World Cup, but Spurs have several reasons to feel confident about keeping hold of the defender.

According to Catalan broadcaster 3Cat, Porro is one of Laporta’s preferred defensive targets as Barcelona consider whether to strengthen their backline in January.

The Catalan club have been dealing with a series of defensive injuries involving Eric Garcia, Jules Kounde, Andreas Christensen and Xavi Espart. However, Barcelona have not made a final decision on whether to bring in another defender, with the club waiting to assess the injured players’ recoveries.

Laporta’s admiration for Porro reportedly grew during Spain’s World Cup campaign. The Tottenham defender became an important part of the national team, developing a strong understanding with Lamine Yamal on the right flank and scoring twice during the tournament, including in the semi-final against France.

Laporta watched that semi-final in Dallas and was reportedly impressed by Porro’s performance. Barcelona had also gathered information about the defender towards the end of the previous summer transfer window, but their enquiry did not progress into a formal proposal to either the player or Tottenham.

Porro’s contractual situation gives Spurs a strong negotiating position. The 27-year-old renewed his contract shortly before the World Cup, committing his future to the north London club until 2031.

Reports have also indicated that Tottenham would demand a significant fee to consider allowing the defender to leave. Real Madrid have also been linked with an interest in the Spain international, potentially creating further competition for his signature.

Barcelona’s admiration for Porro is a reminder of the defender’s standing in European football, but there is currently no confirmed bid or formal approach.

Tottenham will be keen to retain a player who remains an important part of their plans. His long-term contract also means the club are under no immediate pressure to negotiate a sale.

The situation could develop if Barcelona decide their defensive injury problems require January reinforcements. For now, however, Spurs remain in a strong position, and any interested club would likely have to make a substantial offer to test their resolve.
