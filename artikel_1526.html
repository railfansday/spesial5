Mercato: FC Barcelona is thinking of an Algerian international for January

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

“We are open to the idea of ​​signing the best players for the team, any player who can help us progress. I said it during the transfer window: we always discuss what is best for the team, how we can improve it. This is what we are doing now. We’ll see. We still have a few weeks left before the transfer window opens. We discussed it with Deco and Bojan as well as with my staff. This is how we do it. If we can, maybe we will”. At the end of October, Hansi Flick was very clear: Barça plans to recruit this winter.

It must be said that even if the Blaugranas are coming off a great 2-1 victory against Atlético de Madrid, the defensive sector worries the supporters a lot. He is considered the team’s weak point, and several defenders have experienced physical problems in recent weeks, forcing the German coach to tinker. Especially since there could be departures, like Alejandro Baldé, not in the coach’s plans. Jules Koundé is also presented as a possible starter by the Catalan media.

New faces could therefore arrive, in central defense – the name of Lisandro Martinez (Manchester United) comes up a lot – as well as for the left-back position. As indicated SportFC Barcelona really appreciates Rayan Aït-Nouri (25 years old), the Algerian player from Manchester City. He has had very little playing time under Enzo Maresca, having not played a single minute in the Premier League this season, and is expected to leave the Etihad Stadium in January. He is already coveted by neighbors Manchester United.

The Catalan media indicates that Barça really appreciates the player’s profile, and that he corresponds to what the club is looking for in a full-back. A particularly offensive corridor player, good with the ball, in small spaces, and who helps to create excess numbers in the final meters. He was already followed by the Catalans before his signing for City in 2025 for 36 million euros, while he played at Wolverhampton. It now remains to come to an agreement with the Skyblues. Above all, you will have to sell first to have cash available…
