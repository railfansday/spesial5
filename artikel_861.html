Bournemouth make European history after coming from behind to beat Celta Vigo

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Celta Vigo vs Bournemouth. UEFA Europa League Round 1.

Estadio Abanca-BalaídosAttendance: Attendance20,002.

Report from the Europa League game between Celta Vigo and Bournemouth at Estadio Abanca-Balaidos; Iago Aspas put home side in front before Alvaro Rodriguez and Rayan struck to earn the Cherries a historic victory as Bournemouth extended their winning run to 10 matches in all competitions

Bournemouth continued their magnificent run of form with a 2-1 victory against Celta Vigo, creating two bits of history in the process.

It was a club-record-extending 10th successive win for Bournemouth in all competitions, with the victory also marking the first time an English club have won their first five matches in major European competition.

Veteran former Liverpool striker Iago Aspas put the home side in front in style in the first half, before Bournemouth were reduced to 10 men when Bafode Diakite was sent off for a late challenge.

But Alvaro Rodriguez equalised early in the second half, with substitute Rayan coming on to score a late winner to give Bournemouth an unlikely win.

Bournemouth were fortunate not to go behind after 10 minutes, with an offside flag chalking off Pablo Duran's poke home from close-range.

But the Cherries fell behind 10 minutes later when record Celta Vigo goalscorer Aspas scored a screamer.

The 39-year-old had seen an overhead kick saved well by Michele Di Gregorio, but he picked up the ball on the right side of the area in the second phase of play, before cutting inside to curl past the goalkeeper and into the far corner off the post.

Things went from bad to worse for Bournemouth moment later when Diakite saw a yellow card upgraded to a red after a VAR check following a late challenge on Couhaib Driouech.

Tyler Adams replaced Justin Kluivert at half-time and the tactical switch drew an instant reward when Alvaro Rodriguez glanced home Amine Adli's cross to get Bournemouth back on level terms.

James Hill then drew a fine save from Ionut Radu - who is the only player ever to play for both clubs - before Di Gregorio was called into action at the other end to divert a long-range Aspas strike away.

But three Bournemouth substitutes combined for a late winner.

Adrien Truffert stole possession and broke before finding Marcus Tavernier the area. He laid the ball off for Rayan to sweep home and seal victory as Marco Rose's side made more history with a brilliant win at the Estadio Abanca-Balaidos.

"Now we're enjoying a good run of form and things are going well for us," said Bournemouth midfielder Amine Adli. "We're in a good moment with confidence and a positive atmosphere.

"We're trying to maintain this energy and all the good things we're doing on the pitch, and keep reproducing them in every game."
