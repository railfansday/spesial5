Jamie Foxx's Girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp Gives Birth, Welcomes Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Jamie Foxx and Alyce Huckstepp welcomed their first baby together after three years of dating, marking the comedian's third child following daughters Corinne Foxx, 32, and Anelise Bishop, 17.

Jamie Foxx is about to be driving around his third baby.

After all, the Ray actor has welcomed his third child, his first with girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp, E! News has learned. People was first to report the news.

And the precious little one put the Baby Driver star's two older kids—Corinne Foxx, 32, with ex Connie Klein, and Anelise Bishop, 17, with ex Kristin Grannis—back in action as big sisters.

But even though he's raised two daughters, looking after a newborn with the fitness instructor wasn't a life change the 58-year-old took lightly.

In Foxx's words to E! News, "Being my age, I was a little on the fence."

But he had fellow Oscar winner Al Pacino to look up to as he wrapped his head around breakin' all the rules as an older dad.

"Then I ran into Al Pacino a few months ago," Foxx quipped of his run-in with the 86-year-old, who became a dad again in 2023. "'I said, ‘Al, you gonna be back in the drop off line?' I said, aw man, shoot I’m good."

And the new era may lead Foxx—who was first linked to Huckstepp in August 2023 when they were seen on a date night at Nobu Malibu—to another way home, away from a U.S. zip code. Indeed, it seems the pair began family planning with Huckstepp’s Australian roots in mind.

During a visit Down Under, the Django Unchained actor said he’d “of course” be open to moving his family to the continent in an interview with Daily Mail published Nov. 2. As he put it, “Imma find a place."

And Foxx has already forged a bond with the 31-year-old's family, revealing that inviting her relatives to visit during the couple's vacation in Melbourne was a no-brainer because "my girl is from Australia.”

But no matter where they choose to settle down, Foxx will be all in. In fact, he previously shared his favorite part of fatherhood is simply hearing his daughters call him “dad.”

That, he said during an appearance on Ellen Digital’s series Dad Confessions in 2017, and “watching them light up when you come into a room."
