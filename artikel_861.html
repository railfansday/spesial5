Where Alabama Barker Stands With Landon Barker After Estrangement

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Alabama Barker revealed during the Nov. 5 episode of The Kardashians how she and brother Landon Barker were able to make up after a period of not speaking.

Alabama Barker’s rift with Landon Barker turned out to be just a small thing.

After the 20-year-old revealed she and her older brother stopped speaking for weeks following a disagreement, there’s still true care between the two naturally.

"You know what? It was really sibling-coded,” Alabama told stepmom Kourtney Kardashian during the Nov. 5 episode of The Kardashians about the pair making up. “He looked at me—and then that's how we started communicating again."

And even though their squabble is now water under the bridge, as Alabama explained, instead of discussing the issue, they simply ran over it.

As she told Kourtney, “We never established—we never spoke about it.”

ICYMI, Alabama shared she and Landon, 23, had hit a bump in the road when it came to their close relationship. Bible.

“We’re in an argument,” Alabama—whose mom is Shanna Moakler—previously admitted to Kourtney on The Kardashians. “We just had a disagreement.”

Though Alabama didn’t get into the specifics about what went down, she did share that it ended up involving dad Travis Barker.

“I found something out, and I told my father,” she explained. “And [Landon] didn’t like that,” noting that at the time, they were estranged for “going on four weeks.”

While Landon may not have agreed with Alabama’s decision, she explained why she felt like she needed to talk about the issue with their dad (who also shares son Rocky Barker, 3, with Kourtney).

“It wasn’t that I was tattletaling,” Alabama noted, “it was for his own good.”

And after Kourtney suggested Alabama's move was more so for Landon’s “safety,” her stepdaughter couldn’t help but agree that she was thinking past just herself in the moment: “Because I love him.”

But if there was still any doubt that all is well, look no further than this past summer when Alabama posted a TikTok alongside Landon, simply captioning her June video, “I love my brother.”
