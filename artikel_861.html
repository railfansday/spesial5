Bristol Rovers being let down by poor players - Evans

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Steve Evans has criticised the performances of his Bristol Rovers players

Steve Evans insists he will leave Bristol Rovers if he is not wanted following an eighth defeat in 10 league games, but says their poor form is down to his "inconsistent" players.

A 2-0 loss to Chesterfield on Saturday leaves the Gas just four points above the relegation places, with manager Evans lambasting a first-half performance that saw him make a double substitution before the interval.

The Scot was blunt in his message to supporters after the final whistle, saying he is not one to hang about for a payoff, but called on his players to take responsibility for their performances.

"My message has always been the same," he told BBC Radio Bristol. "If I'm not wanted, I'll leave. I'm not like the others that hang on for paydays.

"Let's not mince my words. I sat on the bench for 10 seconds to have a conversation with Paul Raynor [assistant head coach], and somebody shouted when I came out, 'He's come out from hiding'.

"I answer them. I don't hide. I'm out the trenches. I don't hide. I'm not like the others hiding behind the ownership. I don't do that. I'm not doing my job, neither's Paul, neither's Byron [Anthony], neither's Tomo [Lee Tomlin].

"We're not doing our jobs, but we're being let down by inconsistent players. Poor players, in my opinion."

The 64-year-old says he will now reflect on the job he is doing at the Memorial Stadium as he looks to reverse Rovers' stuttering form but wants to see his players perform the basics week to week.

"I'll go away and think about everything," he said. "I'll go away and think about my job, am I doing my job effectively?

"Can I affect the players differently to how we're affecting them at the minute because it's certainly not right, we've got players who turn up one week and disappear for two weeks. You wouldn't recognise some of them. Paul said coming off the pitch, we have to reflect on whether we are doing our jobs.

Rovers now face a tricky away trip at neighbours Cheltenham next week and Evans says he will pick a team hungry for three points.

"It's always going to be tough," he added. "You're going to Steve Cotterill's Cheltenham side, who are very good, particularly at Whaddon Road. We have to pick a team first and foremost that works hard and fights.
