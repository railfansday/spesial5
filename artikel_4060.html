A Complete Guide To The Dune My Arrakis, My Superdrop Secret Lair MTG Drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This new sci-fi Secret Lair contains three drops in total, but will have a slightly different format due to the availability of nonfoil cards.

We're gearing up for another Magic: The Gathering Secret Lair drop, and this time it's Dune-themed to celebrate the recent trilogy's conclusion.

Three bundles are coming to the Secret Lair at the end of the month, and there are some changes being made to the options on offer, specifically in relation to the foiling options that are available, alongside the absence of some nonfoil bundles, too. It's not clear if this is anything to do with the ongoing availability issues, but in some cases, nonfoil bundles are due to be available at WPN game stores "at a later date."

The My Arrakis, My Superdrop Secret Lair is launching on Monday, November 30, 2026 at 9 am PT (12 pm ET, 5 pm GMT, 6 pm CET). This is scheduled to be a normal Secret Lair drop, meaning the waiting room will be open from one hour before the sale time, and you can put any products you wish to buy into a basket ready to check out once you make it through the queue.

As for prices, the Desert of Arrakis and Balance of Power bundles both cost $39.99 for the nonfoil variants and $49.99 for the foil alternative. With the Spice drop, it is only available in Halo Foil, and will cost $59.99.

Finally, the promo card for this superdrop is Emperor's Hutment, which is a reskinned and renamed Reliquary Tower. You get a copy of this card for each $149.99 spent on an order.

This bundle contains five card and a token. Three of the cards a renamed reprints, one is just an art swap, and one is a brand new to Magic card.

Shai-Hulud is the new-to-Magic card and is a nine-mana green 12/12 creature with Trample and Indestructible. When it enters, you destroy all artifacts and enchantments. For each player who controller a permanent destroyed this way, search your library for a basic land card or a Desert card. Put those cards onto the battlefield tapped, then shuffle.

It's an immensely powerful card that comes with a huge cost attached to it. But even at nine-mana with three green pips, you can ramp up to this card soon enough in the right deck. However, holding onto a card like this until the right moment will be key, as you want to make sure you're disrupting everyone's boards as much as possible by taking out valuable artifacts and enchantments.

The rumors were true and a Magic: The Gathering x Dune Secret Lair Superdrop is coming later this month.

This bundle contains eight cards, all of which are new-to-Magic.

This is an immensely exciting bundle, especially for fans of Dune, but even for general Magic fans, some of these potential Commanders are looking very interesting to build around.

The final bundle from this superdrop contains six cards, four of which are renamed reprints, one is just a reprint with fresh art, and the final card is a token.

Most notable here is a reprint of Darksteel Forge, a $40+ card, but we don't expect the value of other printings to be impacted by this release too much given that it has been renamed and comes with Dune-themed art. Secret Lair cards can sometimes help lower rare card prices, but when they're a Universes Beyond release, the market tends not to react.
