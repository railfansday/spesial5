Tips on how to defeat Bai Chilian, Lady of the Twin Serpents in Phantom Blade Zero

Beating the Phantom Blade Zero Bai Chilian boss fight isn't easy as this serpentine woman moves erratically and quickly, and can deliver some devastating blows. However, as always with these boss fights, knowledge is power, and so is blocking, parrying, and dodging. Learn Bai Chilian's attack patterns and take in the tactics I've laid out below, and the fight will become much easier to manage – just be aware that there's a second phase that mixes things up a bit!

Bai Chilian, Lady of the Twin Serpents, is a story-critical boss, so you can't progress until you've defeated her. Despite her often-rapid movement, she isn't as difficult to bring down as she seems – if you've already beaten the Phantom Blade Zero Three-Faced Puppet, you should have very few problems! With all of that in mind, here are my tips for beating Bai Chilian in Phantom Blade Zero:

Once you've depleted Bai Chilian's health bar, she'll transform into the Sacrificial Serpent for a second boss fight – thankfully there's a checkpoint! Despite having a radically different moveset since she can now swing around using puppet-like strings, much of the same logic and tactics from phase 1 applies, but here are some additional tips:

Once you've beaten both phases of the Bai Chilian fight, you'll get the Broken Illusory Pipa, a crucial story item that's tied to the Phantom Blade Zero Whence The Weeping quest. You'll also get a very good dual-wield weapon, the White Serpent & Crimson Viper swords, and the Resonance Talisman Accessory, which increases your maximum Sha-Chi – both very useful to have for the journey ahead.

This also marks the end of the Crimson Forest region, as you'll be immediately whisked to the next region. Hopefully you've already completed the Phantom Blade Zero A Healer's Mercy quest and answered the Phantom Blade Zero Nimble Thief questions though!
