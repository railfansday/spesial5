We Got These 102 Things Before GTA VI

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grand Theft Auto VI is here. Let’s pat ourselves on the back; we made it. While it's a day of jubilation for many, it’s also a day of mourning as we’re losing an important piece of our cultural fabric: the “We got ________ before GTA VI” meme.

It became the sarcastic cry of a generation, highlighting important, improbable, or absurd events that still managed to occur before Rockstar released its next mainline GTA game, serving to highlight how long it really took for this day to come. The meme is officially retired, so let’s remember it by looking back at a random assortment of memorable and, in many cases, unlikely events, in no particular order, that did indeed occur during the 13-year wait between GTA V and GTA VI.

What else happened before GTA VI? Share your memories one last time in the comments below!
