Need to learn the identity of the Three-Faced Puppet? Here's what you need to do to complete the Triple Threat quest

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The Phantom Blade Zero Triple Threat quest is one of the earliest side quests you can pick up, but you won't complete it until almost the end of the game. Starting with a pretty tough boss, followed by many hours of nothing, this quest suddenly picks up once you reach the Mirage Estate. There's a lot to do, it's a bit complicated, and it doesn't help that if you've not got a particular weapon, you can't get all the quest rewards either, so here's how you complete the full Triple Threat quest in Phantom Blade Zero.

With the Three-Faced Puppet gone for good, that's the end of the Triple Threat quest in Phantom Blade Zero – finally! As a reward, you get Freezing Chaos, a very good one-handed sword that lets you switch between the powers of the three puppets. And if you beat the third phase, you also get Chaos Iron Fragment II. This combines with the first fragment from completing four Long Pillars to give you Chaos Twin Daggers, which you can use to Soul Temper the Sanguine Twin.
