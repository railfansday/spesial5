Monster Hunter Rise now available worldwide for mobile on Netflix Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Early in October, we got probably our most exciting addition to the Netflix Games lineup in a while, as Monster Hunter Rise made a surprise drop in select regions. Now, those of you who've been waiting with bated breath will be excited to learn that Monster Hunter Rise is now out fully on Netflix Games for all eligible regions!

If I were a business guy, I'd probably wonder whether this might split the audience. And in truth, although I'm glad you'll have more options as a Monster Hunter fan, it does raise the question whether the split in attention will affect MHO negatively.

Still, that's a question for the philosophers- for now, it just means that if you want to experience Monster Hunter on mobile, you're more spoilt for choice than ever! And if MHO and Rise are any indication, the new standard has already been set for the monster-slaying franchise.

If Monster Hunter Rise is just a little too action-packed for you, though, there're other ways to get your fix of mobile fun. Why not try something a little more heartfelt and check out my review of Vampire Therapist to find out why I think this therapy-themed visual novel is well worth playing for vampire fans and narrative lovers alike!
