GTA 6 Pre-Orders Outperform Even Take-Two's Expectations, GTA 5 Just Keeps on Selling

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We won't know how well GTA 6 is selling during its pre-order phase until after the game's launch next week, but with the biggest entertainment release of all time projected and now Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick talking up the numbers, we could be looking at some absurd sales stats.

Speaking alongside the publication of financial reports, Zelnick said GTA 6 pre-orders are "running far in excess of our expectations". The game has been available to pre-order 25th June 2026, so they'll have been available for almost five months when GTA 6 finally comes out on 19th November. That's a long time to build up millions of pre-orders.

"That’s a good thing. That said, we never claim success before it occurs,” Zelnick continued when talking to Variety. “So we’re just days away from beginning to see the fruits of our labour."
