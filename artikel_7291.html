Disney Advertising Hopes to Give Brands Multiple Layups This College Basketball Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

An action-packed college basketball calendar is underway for Disney and its media sports properties, giving advertisers and brands access to a passionate, loyal audience ready to follow their teams all the way to the men’s and women’s tournaments in March.

For the first time, Disney’s college basketball offering this season includes exclusive coverage of the Players Era Tournament, as well as exclusive coverage of ACC and SEC games, two of the sport’s most competitive conferences.

In addition, the conglomerate will also air the State Farm Champions Classic, ACC/SEC Challenge, Champ Week, NCAA Women’s Tournament, and Jimmy V Classic.

All in all, more than 6,000 men’s and women’s basketball games during the regular season will air across ESPN, ESPN+, ACC Network, SEC Network, and SEC Network+.

“College sports fans choose their team once and stay for life, which makes them really rare for audiences and brands,” said Jacqueline Dobies, vp, revenue and yield management at Disney Advertising, to ADWEEK.

The Disney executive noted that they connect with fans differently, saying they don’t make fans come to them; instead, through its various rights, it reaches fans across linear, streaming, digital, and social, giving advertisers and brands multiple options to access this ravid fan base.

Dobies pointed out that its data stack enables Disney Advertising to ensure each access option works for the brands it serves.

“You know, a brand can reach everything from like a prime broadcast fan to a weeknight streaming fan to a fan that’s viewing the highlights on mobile or social,” she said, adding, “and we can consistently measure across all of those things.”

More than half of 200 advertisers from last year are back for another season, and they are growing their investment in Disney’s college basketball programming across the portfolio, according to Dobies. Disney has added more than 20 new advertisers and two new product categories this year – exercise equipment and athletic wear.

This extensive advertiser inventory comes as ESPN comes off its most-watched men’s college basketball regular season in 11 years, with viewership up 25% year over year. On the women’s side, ESPN had its most-watched regular season since 2008-09, with viewership up 19% from the year prior.

Dobies disclosed that the media company does not publicly share how much ad income Disney generated from its college basketball programming, but said this year they are “pacing double digits ahead when we look at men’s and women’s combined.” She added that income from both men’s and women’s college basketball is growing.

Disney Advertising is also seeing strong demand for college basketball in its longer upfront marketplace. Dobies says that as teams and matchups become more competitive and individual games become sought out, they will see an increase in the scatter marketplace for those really competitive matchups that couldn’t be predicted at the beginning of the season.

Even surrounding college basketball programs are being sought after by brands and advertisers.

College GameDay, for example, has additionally seen growth in sponsorships, with seven brands activating across men’s and women’s programming, three of which are new to College GameDay.

Dobies says College GameDay “is definitely a very sought-after space for advertisers” because they can also activate on-site, and viewers can see that, providing additional reach.

Disney Advertising has also secured more than 20 sponsors across the season, seven of which are new. They have also sold additional title and presenting sponsorships across more than 40 multi-team non-conference events this fall.

With audiences growing, returning advertisers increasing their investment, and a packed slate of marquee matchups, standout players, and top teams giving brands even more reasons to lean in, Disney is hoping for multiple slam dunks this college basketball season.

“Because we have such a robust college basketball schedule, when you invest with Disney college basketball, you’re more likely to be a part of those lightning-in-a-bottle moments, those upsets, the rushing of the court. Those are the things that you can’t predict, that you only see in college sports,” said Dobies.
