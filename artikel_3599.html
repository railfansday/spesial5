Sold-out Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 Is Coming Back Very Soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Good news for Zelda fans who didn't manage to get their hands on this special Switch 2

In September 2026, Nintendo announced a new Switch 2 and controller set to commemorate the Zelda franchise's 40th anniversary and the release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake. While the game quickly became a massive hit, overshadowed only by the launch of GTA 6, the Switch 2 set unfortunately sold out in a matter of seconds. But there's good news: The Zelda 40th anniversary edition Switch 2 is coming back soon.

The item's page on the Nintendo Store still lists it as sold out, but keen-eyed fans spotted a message saying that "more inventory is on the way in January 2027." This is consistent with previous statements from Nintendo that reassured fans more Zelda Switch 2 consoles were on the way.

The special-edition Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 sold out on every storefront as soon as preorders were up. The only way to get one of the consoles was to find a reseller on eBay​​​​​​​, which meant paying significantly more than the retail price. Speaking in an interview, Bill van Zyll, Nintendo’s vice president and general manager for Latin America, said the company "underestimated how well it would be received." However, he also reassured fans that Nintendo was "working on replenishments."

The fact that the special-edition Zelda Switch 2 immediately sold out everywhere shows just how dedicated the franchise's fanbase is. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina​​​​​​​ of Time remake released on Nov. 5, 2026, selling more than 5 million copies in the first four days and quickly becoming the second-best-selling Switch 2 game. It also became one of the best-reviewed games in recent years, before a certain relationship sim hogged all the spotlight.

It doesn't hurt that the special Zelda hardware looks pretty slick, too. The controllers and the Switch 2 feature the black, gold, and green color scheme of the franchise's protagonist, Link, along with the Triforce symbol and the Hylian Crest.

Hopefully, this time Nintendo will not underestimate the popularity of one of its biggest franchises, and there will be enough special-edition Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 consoles​​​​​​​ to satisfy fans' demand, without giving scalpers another field day.
