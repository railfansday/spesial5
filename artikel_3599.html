Kate Gosselin Slams ‘Disgusting’ Haters Over Her Dog's Amazon Wishlist

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kate Gosselin has a pointed message for her “haters.”

“Somebody yesterday asked me to post a list for Koda, as well, so I did,” Gosselin, 51, began a Friday, November 20, TikTok video. “I posted a short list and noticed that yesterday the entire list was purchased. I was like, ‘Oh my gosh, everyone’s so amazing.’ That’s so kind and so sweet.”

Upon further evaluation, Gosselin realized that none of the items were actually purchased.

“Most of the items were purchased by one person and it was marked ‘purchased from elsewhere,’” she claimed. “It occurred to me that, yeah, that was not purchased. It was marked as purchased so that no one else will purchase it for Koda, and I just have a message for haters.”

Gosselin continued, “You’re disgusting. If you are that desperate to hate me through my dog, rest assured, Koda will not go without those items. I will make sure she gets them.”

The Kate Plus 8 alum recently shared a list of holiday presents for her dog, including bedding, grooming supplies and toys. After discovering the alleged hater’s actions, she edited her Amazon list and “unmarked” the gear as purchased so they could be available for those actually intending to buy the items.

“I’m not asking anyone to [buy anything], but I’m just saying make sure you buy them from Amazon, so it’s marked correctly and I know that it’s purchased,” she noted. “I’m saying I will buy them if need be, she will not go without, and if you’re that desperate to hate me through my sweet rescue dog who had a rough life? Gosh, your life must suck because that is just ridiculous.”

Gosselin further stressed that the alleged hater’s actions didn’t “upset [her] in any way.”

“It just tells me what an awful person you are and how much hate you must have in your heart,” she concluded. “Go, be gone!”

Gosselin captioned her post, “My sweet girl Koda deserves and will get ALL of her wish list items, worry not!”

Kate, who shares eight children with ex-husband Jon Gosselin, also adopted dog Aneika. Both canines have since received a handful of new gear from the Amazon list.

“Thank you to Joanna, Lisa, Piper, Angie and ‘Dog Mom’ for making Koda and Aneika’s days brighter and more fun,” she captioned a Saturday, November 20, unboxing video. “We can’t thank you guys enough for the love!”

Another TikTok user promised to donate to a local humane society in Koda and Aneika’s honor in lieu of a present.

“YESSSSS! Thank you!!” Kate replied via TikTok comment.
