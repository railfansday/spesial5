Mass Effect Creator Is ‘Optimistic’ the Series Will Live On

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Fans and developers remain confident that Mass Effect will return

BioWare's shutdown has been one of the hardest studio closures in gaming history. The Edmonton-based studio made its way into thousands of gamers' hearts, and it was set to continue this mission with the development of Mass Effect Universe, the next entry in the Mass Effect series. The project may have been canceled, but hope still lingers.

In 2020, in celebration of N7 Day, the annual celebration of the Mass Effect franchise, BioWare announced that it was working on a new Mass Effect game, but it was only in the early stages of development. Besides a handful of teasers, not much was heard about the project from then on. When private investors bought EA, Mass Effect 5 seemed increasingly unlikely. Unfortunately, that proved true: EA shut down BioWare and canceled the project on Oct. 21, 2026, nearly six years after its announcement.

However, neither fans nor developers were ready to give up on Mass Effect just yet. Petitions were created, billboards were put up to raise awareness, voice actors showed support, and developers even leaked an early trailer to fuel the fire. The community has come together, celebrating N7 Day in a uniquely special way.

Casey Hudson, project director for the Mass Effect trilogy, also spoke up on N7 Day. He showed confidence in the series' future and thanked fans for continuing to make N7 Day a special occasion. Hudson underlined that "when Mass Effect makes its triumphant return, it will be thanks to the outpouring of love from players around the world." The message was particularly special coming from one of the series creators. While Casey's words shouldn't be taken as an official statement nor a promise, as the series' IP remains firmly in EA's hands, it was a boost of morale that gave fans another dose of much-needed hope.

Commander Shepard's voice actors, Mark Meer and Jennifer Hale, have also shown immense support for this cause, alongside other cast members. The two have made videos on social media promoting the petition in hopes that another company will pick up the project, rescuing it from the graveyard of IPs affected by EA's studio closures. It has been weeks since the project was canceled, but the community remains hopeful that this is not the end of Mass Effect. Only time will tell.
