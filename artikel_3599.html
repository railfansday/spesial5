Married to Medicine's Lateasha 'Sweet Tea' Files for Divorce From Dr. Gregory

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Married to Medicine alum Lateasha “Sweet Tea” Lunceford has filed for divorce from Dr. Gregory Lunceford after three years of marriage, Us confirmed.

Lateasha, 35, filed for dissolution of marriage at the Fulton County Superior Civil Court on Tuesday, November 10.

The former Bravolebrity stated in the paperwork that her marriage is “irretrievably broken with no hope of reconciliation,” according to court docs obtained by TMZ. While Lateasha did not specify a date of separation, she noted that they split before the filing, the outlet reported.

Lateasha requested spousal support, stating that she “lacks sufficient income and resources to maintain the standard of living established during the marriage.” Additionally, she asked for equitable division of their marital assets and to each keep the cars in their possession.

Lateasha and Greg, 59, met in 2020 after she slid into his DMs. The pair got engaged two years later and tied the knot in Atlanta, Georgia, in April 2023.

“I said what’s up and he responded and from there, you know, FaceTime calls … that turned into a flight to Atlanta, and we met, and we hit it off,” Lateasha told The Daily Dish in 2023, adding that they both knew they wanted to start a family someday.

During season 11 of Married to Medicine in 2024, Greg revealed that he had a two-year timeline for having a baby with Lateasha. The couple explored in vitro fertilization (IVF) in February 2025, although Lateasha, who struggles with endometriosis, later revealed that they had taken a break from their fertility journey.

“Honestly, after getting off the show, we have decided to just really focus on our marriage,” she said in a social media live in October 2025. “At first, I was trying to do it right away, but honestly, with all the drama, I just think that it’s important for us to establish a solid foundation, and of course, like I do want kids.”

Lateasha continued, “I definitely do want a family, but in order to have a family, you’ve got to have a solid foundation.”

Lateasha’s revelation about their family plans came shortly after her exit from Married to Medicine after two seasons.

“Never ever been a quitter. And I never ran away from anybody. It just is what it is. There are better things in store for me,” she said in May 2025. “That platform was what it was at the moment and I think God has something better, and I’m going down the right path.”
