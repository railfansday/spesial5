Sandra Bullock Shares Rare Pics of Teen Children During Disneyland Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sandra Bullock is soaking up some quality time with her children at the happiest place on Earth.

Bullock, 62, took to Instagram on Sunday, November 22, to share family vacation photos featuring kids Louis, 16, and Laila, 13, during a trip to Disneyland in Anaheim, California. In one image, Laila looked at Bullock as they rode a rollercoaster, while another picture showed the actress taking a selfie as the group walked ahead.

“Sometimes you just need to stop worrying and remind yourself of what it feels like to be a kid,” she captioned the series of photos. “FYI There are no calories in ice cream if you ride the Guardians of the Galaxy ride and don’t cry 😉.”

Bullock and her children were also joined by longtime friend Josh Holloway, wife Yessica Holloway and son Hunter, 12. While tagging them in the caption, Bullock added, “Family and friends ♥️.”

Bullock adopted her son in 2010 and added her daughter to their family in 2015. Five years later, Bullock’s partner, Bryan Randall, was diagnosed with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Randall died in 2023 at age 57.

Earlier this year, Bullock opened up about how her children navigated Randall’s health battle and recalled how Louis and Laila “could see” what was happening.

“They’re very, very astute kids,” she said during an August appearance on the “Smartless” podcast. “They not only see the physical change, there’s a behavior that changes in all of us. I’m hypervigilant … I became manic in my vigilance.”

From Bullock’s perspective, Louis “handled it very different” than Laila.

“About two years before Bryan passed, he saw it was a dynamic that wasn’t healthy for him to be around. So, he’s very evolved, he’s like, ‘You know what, I’m here for you’ … but he chose to just sort of be there and support and help me on the things that were easy [for him]. But he smartly disengaged himself and said, ‘This is a safer space for me to sit,’ and I said, ‘Absolutely, you go. I got this,’” she said.

Laila, meanwhile, “was more bonded until about six months” before Randall’s death. “And then she says, ‘Can you please say no when they call me over to go visit?’ and I said, ‘Absolutely, but if you do want to go visit, I will come with you and I will be right there and if you tap out, I’m right there,’” Bullock recalled.
