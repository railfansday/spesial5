Man Sentenced for Killing Parents for Money After He 'Discovered' Their Bodies

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A California man will spend the rest of his life in prison after prosecutors said he killed his parents out of greed, while he was set to inherit half of their estate.

A judge sentenced Mark Andre Tomas Navarrete to two consecutive life sentences without parole in his parents’ deaths, the Sacramento County District Attorney’s Office announced in a news release on Friday, November 13.

Navarrete, 46, was found guilty in October of two counts of first-degree murder, with the jury agreeing to the special circumstances that he killed Augusto Tacsuan Navarrete and Marjorie Jane Tomas Navarrete for financial gain, The Sacramento Bee reported.

Before the murders in Elk Grove, Mark was allegedly scamming his friends and family members with fraudulent investment schemes, according to prosecutors, who said he made promises to “repay them at exorbitant interest rates.”

Mark also started to resent his parents, who repaid family members for his debts, investigators learned.

He murdered Augusto and Marjorie the morning of June 28, 2024, when prosecutors said he drove from his Elk Grove home and parked a few blocks away from their residence in the city, located about a 15-mile drive south from Sacramento.

Mark put on a mask and gloves, then hopped on a kick scooter, which he rode to his parents’ home, according to prosecutors. He allegedly entered their property through a side gate so their Ring camera on their front porch wouldn’t detect him.

After meeting Augusto and Marjorie at their back door, Mark had them go into their garage, where he beat them both in the head with an object before fatally strangling them, prosecutors said.

“Navarrete then drove away in his parents’ car, parked it a short distance from where he originally parked and rode away on the scooter back to the car,” the district attorney’s office said in the release.

That same morning, he sent a text to his parents inviting them over to have dinner, according to prosecutors.

Mark later drove his children to his parents’ home and “discovered” their bodies after his sister grew concerned when she couldn’t get into contact with them, prosecutors said.

Multiple factors linked Mark to the murders, including evidence from Flock cameras and traffic cameras that recorded his movements, according to the district attorney’s office.

Detectives also discovered his DNA beneath his mother’s fingernails and found the kick scooter he used to ride to his parents’ home inside his garage, according to prosecutors.
