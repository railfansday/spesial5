PolyPid Ltd. (PYPD +4.60%) is moving toward a potential U.S. commercial launch of its lead product candidate, D-PLEX100, following the acceptance of its New Drug Application and the establishment of a strategic partnership with Azurity Pharmaceuticals. Management reported that the company is currently engaged in labeling discussions with the FDA ahead of the Nov. 28, 2026, PDUFA date. The company also regained full commercial rights for the European market and is pursuing label expansion into broader abdominal surgeries while advancing its clinical pipeline with PD01B for diabetic foot infections. Financial results for the third quarter showed a decrease in net loss and a cash position supported by milestone payments from its U.S. partner.
