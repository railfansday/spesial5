You can now DM Meta’s Muse on Instagram to turn saves into plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Meta's personal AI agent now takes requests in Instagram's inbox, starting with the posts you have saved.

Meta has added its Muse AI agent to Instagram direct messages, inviting users to send it a DM to turn saved posts into plans, such as a trip. Muse already worked in its own app, in WhatsApp and on Instagram profiles, and Meta says it does not read private messages unless users allow it, though Reuters reported staff found it reading encrypted chats.

Meta’s Muse AI agent can now be messaged in Instagram DMs, where it can turn posts users have saved into plans. Meta announced the change through Muse’s own Instagram account on Tuesday, using a long-planned trip as the example.

Muse launched in the US in September as an agent that books, buys and negotiates for users. It works in its own app and in WhatsApp, and later that month users could add it to their Instagram profiles.

The DM option puts Muse where the saved posts already sit. Meta has not said which countries get it. Beyond the US, Muse is live in Canada and has launched in Australia, and it reached the UK this week.

Yet Reuters reported last week that Meta staff found Muse reading encrypted messages. Meta called those isolated incidents.

Users can also make Muse ask before it sends messages or buys anything. In September, though, a YouTuber said Muse shared his address with a Facebook Marketplace buyer before asking him. Meta said it was looking into it.
