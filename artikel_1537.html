Chelsea monitoring Como defender Jacobo Ramon

Chelsea are continuing to assess their defensive options as they plan for future transfer windows, with Como centre-back Jacobo Ramon emerging as a player attracting attention.

The 21-year-old has impressed in Italy, and his performances have reportedly placed him on Chelsea’s recruitment radar.

According to the Mail Online, the Blues are keeping track of his development as they consider potential additions to their defence.

Ramon has attracted interest after establishing himself at Como. His performances have reportedly caught the attention of several major clubs, with Chelsea among those monitoring his progress.

However, securing his signature may prove complicated because of Real Madrid’s contractual position. The Spanish giants are understood to have retained buy-back and sell-on clauses when Ramon left the club for Como.

Those provisions could give Madrid considerable influence over the defender’s future and potentially make negotiations more difficult for interested clubs.

Chelsea are also likely to face competition from Arsenal and Manchester United, who have reportedly been linked with the young centre-back.

Ramon represents a potential long-term defensive addition for Chelsea as the club looks to maintain depth and quality at the back.

His development in Serie A makes him an interesting option, but Chelsea would need to navigate Real Madrid’s contractual rights and competition from other Premier League sides.

For now, the reported interest appears to be part of Chelsea’s wider recruitment monitoring rather than evidence of a formal bid or advanced negotiations.
