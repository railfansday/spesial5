Dovbyk open to joining Valencia in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Artem Dovbyk’s time at Bologna is set to end in January.

The Ukrainian isn’t part of Raffaele Palladino’s plans, and Roma has no intention of seeing the striker lose value.

A return to Spain is one of the most likely options for the 1997-born striker, but several factors need to be considered.

A Spanish club has reportedly shown interest in the striker from the Roman club.

Valencia is reportedly open to a loan move if the striker were to return to Trigoria early. And the first hurdle—the player’s desire—seems to have been overcome.

According to reports from Spain, Cadena SER, Dovbyk has opened his eyes to Valencia. The player is reportedly happy to return to Spain, where he excelled and convinced Roma to shell out millions to bring him to Trigoria.

The striker has instructed his agents to find a solution to make the transfer a reality. The Pipistrelli are unable to meet the Trigoria club’s financial demands and can only offer a loan with an option to buy.

The ball is now in the clubs’ court, who want to find a solution to close the deal. The Spanish club is expected to pay €1.5 million to the Roman club and 50% of the player’s salary, following the terms established for the 1997-born player’s transfer to Bologna.

The Giallorossi paid €35 ​​million plus bonuses for the Ukrainian, and are currently struggling to find buyers.
