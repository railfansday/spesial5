WHO sounds the alarm on antimicrobial resistance threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The spread of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is threatening modern medicine despite progress in combating drug-resistant bacteria, senior World Health Organization officials said Monday. WHO officials said countries had expanded efforts to tackle AMR, but warned that progress in prevention, diagnosis and the appropriate use of antibiotics remained too slow to meet global targets. FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney speaks with Jean Pierre Nyemazi, Acting Director of the WHO's AMR Department.
