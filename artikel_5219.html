Anthropic stock has just completed one of the most dramatic post-IPO shakeouts in recent market history, and that violent reset may be exactly what sets the stage for an extraordinary rally. Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts that what looks like a failed debut could instead launch a move that redefines the stock’s valuation before the calendar turns to 2027.

Anthropic (ANT) priced its IPO at $83 and began trading on November 19. Shares exploded higher on the open, hitting $111.50 and briefly touching $115.10 before closing the first day near $97. The euphoria faded quickly. Over the next three sessions, the stock sold off hard, closing on November 24 at $81.91, below the IPO price, after printing an intraday low of $75.61.

In less than a week, the stock went from a multi-trillion-dollar peak-valuation fantasy to trading below its offer price. That kind of rapid digestion of early gains often clears out weak hands and resets sentiment for the next leg.

The hypothetical landmark catalyst is this: In the final weeks of 2026, Anthropic unveils a breakthrough version of Claude that achieves a decisive, independently verified leap in both capability and reliable alignment.

The release is paired with the announcement of exclusive, multi-year enterprise and government contracts totaling tens of billions in committed spend, including a landmark partnership that positions Claude as the preferred frontier model for several major sovereign AI initiatives.

The combination of technical superiority and locked-in, high-value demand triggers a violent re-rating as investors conclude the company has pulled decisively ahead of competitors just as the market was questioning AI valuations.

Several recent developments already lay the groundwork for renewed optimism. Despite the sharp post-IPO decline, retail buying has remained notably firm on the dips.

Analyst coverage has largely stayed constructive, with multiple firms highlighting Anthropic’s growth trajectory and addressable market. The company’s revenue run-rate and product momentum heading into the IPO were exceptional, and the broader AI infrastructure narrative remains intact even amid short-term volatility.

The very speed of the selloff has also compressed the valuation from the frothiest first-day levels, creating a cleaner technical base for any positive surprise to build upon.

However, a looming ‘JUNK’ credit rating could be coming for Anthropic, which could lead to a deeper slide before any rally can get going, potentially putting the ‘Meta AI predicts’ price target on hold.

The price action since the November 19 debut has been violent but potentially constructive for bulls. The stock gapped up aggressively, failed to hold the initial spike, and then flushed below the IPO price on heavy volume, a classic post-IPO washout pattern.

Critically, the decline has occurred in a compressed timeframe without a prolonged grinding distribution phase. On short-term charts, the $75–$80 zone now represents a clear capitulation low. A sustained reclaim of the $90–$97 area (first-day close and early support) would signal that the selling has been absorbed and that buyers are regaining control.

Momentum indicators are deeply oversold after the multi-day drop, leaving substantial room for a sharp mean-reversion squeeze. In high-beta AI names, these kinds of rapid flush-and-recover sequences have historically preceded outsized upside moves when a fundamental catalyst appears.

In this over-the-top but not entirely impossible scenario, where a genuine technical breakthrough collides with locked-in sovereign and enterprise demand just as the post-IPO overhang clears, Anthropic doesn’t just recover its debut highs.

It enters a new valuation regime, and by January 1, 2027, Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts that Anthropic (ANT) could reach as high as $310.
