Cosmos has spent years grinding lower, but the weekly chart is showing a potentially important test. The ATOM price is holding near the lower boundary of a long-term descending channel, with support around $1.23 to $1.385. Now, a Paxos listing gives the token a fresh fundamental development to work with.

The chart shows ATOM trading near the channel’s lower boundary after an extended decline. The marked support area sits around $1.23 to $1.385, making this zone important for the current setup.

Still, holding support isn’t the same as confirming a reversal. The descending channel remains in place, and buyers need to demonstrate that the latest bounce has staying power.

The bigger development comes from Paxos. Its listing of ATOM enables fintech companies and brokerages on its platform to offer the token to their customers through existing infrastructure.

That’s useful distribution. It gives participating businesses a route to provide ATOM access without building an entirely separate system themselves. Whether that translates into meaningful demand remains to be seen.

Cosmos Hub governance approved the arrangement through Proposal 1054. The proposal covers the full cost from the community pool, with no monthly fees.

That removes a recurring payment obligation under the approved arrangement. But the listing itself doesn’t guarantee increased buying or a sustained price recovery.

The chart marks potential upside levels at $2.10, $3.60 and $5.50, followed by $10 and $15.50. These are conditional targets, not confirmed destinations.

For now, the ATOM price needs to defend its lower-channel support and establish a convincing bounce. Paxos adds a new access route, but the chart still has to do the talking.
