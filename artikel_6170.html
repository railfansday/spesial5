A welcome bonus can be a compelling reason to open a Citi / AAdvantage credit card, especially if you're saving miles for your next trip. But before you apply, it's worth checking how the offer compares to previous bonuses.

These offers change throughout the year, and waiting for a higher bonus could mean earning thousands more AAdvantage miles for similar spending.

Here's a look at the offer history for four popular Citi / AAdvantage cards, along with our recommendations on when to apply.

We track publicly available welcome offers. You may receive a targeted offer with a different bonus, and the historical offers below may no longer be available.

Citi® / AAdvantage Business™ World Elite Mastercard®

The Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select is a good fit for American Airlines flyers who want useful travel perks at a relatively modest cost. Cardholders receive preferred boarding, a free checked bag on domestic American Airlines itineraries and no foreign transaction fees.

You can also earn a $125 flight discount after spending $20,000 or more on purchases during your cardholder year and renewing your card. (The discount increases to $150 starting Jan. 24, 2027.)

The annual fee is waived for the first 12 months. It is currently $99 and will increase to $150 starting Jan. 24, 2027.

*Bonus offer value is based on TPG’s November 2026 valuations and not provided by the credit card issuer.

The highest offer we've seen allowed new cardholders to earn 80,000 bonus miles after spending $3,500 on purchases within the first four months of account opening.

If the offer is for at least 75,000 miles, it's a good time to apply.

Learn more: Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select World Elite Mastercard

The Citi / AAdvantage Globe Mastercard is a good fit for American Airlines flyers who want some lounge access without paying for the airline’s top-tier card. Cardholders receive preferred boarding, a first checked bag free on domestic American Airlines itineraries and four Admirals Club Globe passes per calendar year.

Other benefits include a statement credit for inflight purchases and a companion certificate (after each renewal, starting in the second cardholder year). The certificate is redeemable for $99 (plus taxes and fees) on a qualifying domestic round-trip Main Cabin flight.

*Bonus offer value is based on TPG’s valuations and not provided by the credit card issuer.

The highest welcome offer we've seen gave new cardholders the opportunity to earn 90,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases within the first four months of account opening.

If the offer is for at least 90,000 miles, it's an ideal time to apply.

Learn more: Citi / AAdvantage Globe Mastercard

The Citi / AAdvantage Executive is a good fit for frequent American Airlines flyers who regularly use airport lounges. Cardholders receive an Admirals Club membership, including access for up to two guests or immediate family members when traveling on eligible flights.

For travelers who would otherwise pay for a membership, this benefit alone can justify the card’s $695 annual fee.

*Bonus offer value is based on TPG’s valuations and not provided by the credit card issuer.

The highest welcome offer we've seen gave new cardholders a chance to earn 125,000 bonus miles after spending $15,000 on purchases within the first five months of account opening.

If the offer is for at least 100,000 miles, it's an excellent time to apply.

Learn more: Citi / AAdvantage Executive World Legend Mastercard

The Citi / AAdvantage Business World Elite Mastercard is a good fit for business owners and employees who regularly fly American Airlines. Cardholders receive preferred boarding and a first checked bag free on domestic American Airlines itineraries.

*Bonus offer value is based on TPG’s valuations and not provided by the credit card issuer.

The highest welcome offer we've seen is also the current offer: Earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases within the first five months of account opening.

If the offer is for at least 75,000 miles, you should consider applying.

Learn more: Citi / AAdvantage Business World Elite Mastercard

Citi generally limits the total credit it extends to you rather than the number of cards you can hold. It can also be sensitive to recent credit inquiries.

Commonly reported application guidelines include:

Exceptions exist, and good-to-excellent credit generally improves your approval odds.

You can hold multiple Citi / AAdvantage cards and earn welcome bonuses on different products. However, each card has its own eligibility restrictions.

Generally, you won't qualify if you've received a bonus on the same product within the past 48 months. The 48-month clock starts when you receive the bonus, not when you're approved.

Related: What you need to know about earning another American Airlines card welcome bonus

If you're considering a Citi / AAdvantage card, use its offer history to help decide whether to apply now or wait for a better bonus.

You don't necessarily need to hold out for the highest offer we've ever seen. A bonus near that level can still be a good opportunity, provided the card suits your travel habits and you can meet the spending requirement with purchases you'd make anyway.
