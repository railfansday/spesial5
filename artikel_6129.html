Aer Lingus to Introduce First Premium Economy Cabin

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Aer Lingus is moving to upgrade the widebody aircraft operating its transatlantic network.

The carrier said Wednesday that it will introduce its first-ever Premium Economy cabin on 10 refurbished Airbus A330s. The aircraft will also get upgraded Business Class seats – now enclosed as suites – and an improved Economy section.

“[This] is a transformation of the entire experience across our A330 fleet, elevating Business Class, Economy, and, for the first time in Aer Lingus history, introducing Premium Economy,” CEO Lynne Embleton said in a news release. “We want to give our customers greater choice, comfort, and connectivity while creating an experience that feels unmistakably Aer Lingus.”

The upgraded Business Class will have 28 Collins Elevation suites in a 1-2-1 configuration, giving each passenger direct aisle access. Each seat will have a fully flat bed, a closing door, enhanced bedding, and “well-being amenities.” A self-service snack and refreshment bar will also be available.

The carrier will also roll out new Economy seats with more personal space and six-way adjustable leather headrests.

New interior elements will tie the three cabins together, officials said. Plans call for mood lighting, artwork reflecting Irish landscapes, and custom fabrics in colors inspired by the sea.

All seats will have larger 4K high-definition entertainment screens, Bluetooth headphone connectivity, USB-C charging ports, and access to Starlink wireless internet. Starlink is already available across all of Aer Lingus’ transatlantic routes.

The first of the overhauled A330s will reenter service in October 2027, flying between Dublin and New York-JFK. The new cabin experience will become available on additional North American routes as work on the remaining A330s is completed.

Aer Lingus serves a total of 20 destinations across the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, including Boston, Newark, New Jersey, Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, and Cancún.
