These San Juan Airbnbs Show Off the Best of the City’s Architecture and Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

My favorite thing about San Juan? You can have a morning that feels plucked out of a history book, an afternoon that commits you entirely to the beach, and a night that’s topped off with a frosty Medalla, sweet rum cocktail, and flirty bachata dance. It’s glitzy yet lush, historic yet ever-evolving, a city delightfully full of contrasts, and one I will never run out of reasons to revisit.

My father’s side of the family is from Puerto Rico, so I’ve been coming back to the island for years, and I love that there’s always another corner to get to know. There’s Old San Juan, with all its pastel-painted façades, galleries and shops, blue cobblestone streets, and tucked-away restaurants; Condado and Ocean Park for long, sea-salty afternoons by the ocean; and Santurce, where street murals, galleries, haute dining, and a lively bar scene bring a fresh, creative vibe to the city.

Of course, there’s no shortage of hotels in San Juan, but sometimes a vacation to the island’s capital calls for a little more room to sprawl, or a front door that opens onto a lush little neighborhood rather than a buzzy hotel lobby. The city’s best Airbnbs offer just that, along with wonderful amenities, whether it’s a private pool hidden behind swaying palms or a breezy courtyard for sipping on some local morning coffee.

Consider this your invitation to soak up the sun in Puerto Rico's capital for a few days. Read on for nine of the best Airbnbs in San Juan to consider for your next trip.
