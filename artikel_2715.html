Mavericks vs Jazz Preview and Injury Report: Dallas attempts to break a bad streak while starting a new one

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Dallas Mavericks (9-7) are on the road to start this week, facing off against the Utah Jazz (3-14) on Sunday. Dallas is playing night two of a back-to-back, tipping off just 20 hours after they beat the Los Angeles Clippers at home. The Jazz lost on Friday to the Phoenix Suns, 111-101. Dallas has lost five straight road back-to-backs, but they're also on the cusp of a three-game winning streak. Here are the main things to know heading into this matchup.

WHAT: Time to beat Utah? Or lose yet another road back-to-back?

WHERE: Delta Center, Salt Lake City, Utah

The Mavericks injury report isn't too much different from yesterday. First, the good news: Kyrie Irving is going to play the second night of a back-to-back for the second time. Second, Walter Claton Jr. is also playing despite some tendinitis. Unfortunately, everyone else who was out against the Clippers is also out against the Jazz. Alphabetically that means Tarik Biberovic, Sergio De Larrea, Tobi Lawal, Dereck Lively, and PJ Washington.

The Jazz are without Jaren Jackson Jr. , Lauri Markkanen, and Jusif Nurkic.

A road game that's played this soon on the second night of a back-to-back is maddening. It's a built-in loss excuse at times, and Dallas has lost many, many second nights of back-to-backs in a row. Kyrie playing is a great sign though, and hopefully these Mavericks take advantage of the situation. The Jazz are better than their 3-14 record and Dallas needs to be prepared for a lot of rim pressure.
