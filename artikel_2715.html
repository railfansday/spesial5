Lions plan for Kerby Joseph to play this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Lions lost Aidan Hutchinson for the rest of the season, but they expect to have another defensive player in the lineup for the first time this year when they face the Buccaneers in Week 11.

Head coach Dan Campbell said on 97.1 The Ticket on Tuesday that the plan is for safety Kerby Joseph to be activated from the physically unable to perform list ahead of Sunday’s game. Joseph has been practicing for the last couple of weeks and was listed as a full participant in practice last Thursday.

Joseph has been working his way back from knee issues that also limited him to six games last season. Joseph led the league with nine interceptions and was named a first-team All-Pro while playing in every game during the 2024 season.

It has been more than 13 months since Joseph has played in a game, so there will likely be some rust that needs to be knocked off once he returns to the lineup but the prospect of getting anything close to the kind of player he was prior to the injury is an appealing one for the Lions defense.
