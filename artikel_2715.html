Devin Booker Calls Peterson ‘My Fav Rook’ After Rookie Gives Jazz Reason for Hope

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Darryn Peterson missed all seven of his three-point attempts against Phoenix. He still finished two assists short of a triple-double ... and received a signed jersey from one of the players he grew up watching.

Devin Booker's message to the Utah Jazz rookie: "Stay on the gas! My fav rook!"

Utah had its own reasons to appreciate Peterson's night. With his jumper failing him and the lineup missing three veteran big men, he kept finding teammates and retrieving missed shots.

Peterson finished Friday's 111-101 loss with 20 points, 10 rebounds and eight assists. He shot 7-of-20 but committed just one turnover in nearly 32 minutes. Five of his rebounds came on the offensive glass.

The scoring efficiency needs work. The rest of that performance gives Will Hardy something to build on.

Keyonte George shot 4-of-17, leaving Utah's starting guards a combined 11-of-37. Lauri Markkanen, Jaren Jackson Jr. and Jusuf Nurkic were unavailable. There was little room for Peterson to disappear because his outside shot wasn't falling.

He accounted for eight of Utah's 20 assists while leading the team in scoring.

Even with Phoenix getting the win, Peterson helped generate offense without repeatedly surrendering possessions through turnovers.

According to The Salt Lake Tribune's Shreyas Laddha, Booker praised Peterson's maturity and pace. Booker also indicated the two could connect next summer, a potential opportunity for Peterson to learn directly from a player he models his game after.

That would give them plenty to discuss beyond shotmaking.

Peterson is learning when to pursue his own offense and when to move the ball. Against Phoenix, he had to keep making those decisions without a made three-pointer to settle him. His passing gave teammates opportunities while the offensive rebounds helped extend possessions.

Those contributions are especially useful for a rookie whose scoring will fluctuate. Hardy needs to be able to leave Peterson involved without requiring him to shoot his way out of every difficult stretch.

Friday offered a reason to do so ... though one productive all-around performance still leaves plenty to prove.

Markkanen and Jackson returning would give Peterson more established scorers to find. Some nights, making the right pass will help Utah more than trying to force his own shot into rhythm.

Friday, he handed out eight assists with one turnover despite missing every three he attempted. That's a reason for Hardy to keep trusting him with the ball ... even when the next possession needs to end with somebody else shooting.
