Who is playing in NFL Week 10? Here's the full schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Who is playing in NFL Week 10? Here's the full schedule originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

It's time for the second half of the 2026 NFL regular season.

There are 14 games on tap for the upcoming week, including another contest in Europe and seven divisional games with four teams on their bye.

What's in store for Week 10? Here's the full schedule ahead:

The slate opens with a matchup of NFC East rivals. Jayden Daniels and the Washington Commanders (2-5-1) will visit J.J. McCarthy and the New York Giants (5-2-1) in a rematch of their infamous Week 5 tie.

Sunday's action starts early in Munich, Germany, for the final international game in Europe this season. Drake Maye will lead the New England Patriots (5-4) against Jared Goff and the Detroit Lions (6-2) at Allianz Arena, home of Bayern Munich. After that, there are seven games in the early window and three in the late afternoon.

The 1 p.m. ET slate includes several high-stakes games, including the Minnesota Vikings (6-2) at Green Bay Packers (6-3), Houston Texans (3-5) at Cleveland Browns (5-4) and Kansas City Chiefs (6-2) at Atlanta Falcons (3-6). In the late window, the San Francisco 49ers (7-1) visit the Dallas Cowboys (4-5) and the Las Vegas Raiders (4-5) host the Seattle Seahawks (5-4).

NBC's Sunday Night Football will be a matchup of AFC North rivals, both riding four-game winning streaks. Joe Burrow and the Cincinnati Bengals (6-2) will host Aaron Rodgers and the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) with the division lead on the line.

Week 10 will conclude with a showdown between two of this season's disappointments. Newly-acquired quarterback Jacoby Brissett and the injury-riddled Baltimore Ravens (4-5) will host Justin Herbert and the winless Los Angeles Chargers (0-8).

The four teams on a bye in Week 10 are the Chicago Bears (7-2), Denver Broncos (7-2), Philadelphia Eagles (2-7) and Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6).
