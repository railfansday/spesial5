After loss to Virginia, Bill Belichick's bowl hopes come down to NC State game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It all comes down to next Saturday, potentially.

After a 41-24 loss to Virginia, Bill Belichick’s North Carolina team has fallen to 5-6. Bowl eligibility hinges on the last game of the season — an a visit from NC State.

The Tar Heels have had two shots at getting a sixth win. They loss to Louisville last Saturay, before falling again today or their sixth loss of the season.

Could Belichick’s future come down to whether UNC gets to a bowl game? Even with a win next weekend and a 6-6 finish, that may not be enough to get him a third season.

He also may not want one, given that the early weeks of the campaign were clouded by the investigations and suspensions and eventually the resignations of G.M. Michael Lombardi and defensive coordinator Steve Belichick, Bill’s son.
