Geno Smith ruled out with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Jets announced in the fourth quarter that Smith has been ruled out with an ankle injury. Smith initially missed a series while being evaluated for a concussion, but returned to the game for one drive in the third quarter before exiting for good.

Smith was 13-of-21 for 148 yards before the ankle injury.

Fourth-round pick Cade Klubnik is now at quarterback for the Jets and he’ll try to lead the team back in the final minutes of the game. The Bills lead 24-12 with under six minutes left on the clock at MetLife Stadium.
