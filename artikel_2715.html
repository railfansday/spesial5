Lakers use fourth quarter run to win weekly match-up with Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A fourth quarter run saw the Lakers open up a double-digit lead that they carried to a 109-94 win over the Jazz on Friday.

After another back-and-forth affair against Utah through three quarters, the Lakers turned it up a notch in the final frame. In total, it was a 15-3 run that saw LA jump ahead 93-80, an advantage they carried to the final buzzer.

Employing a zone defense for much of the night, the Lakers turned Utah over 16 times, leading to 20 points. That helped them overcome a poor shooting night as they went just 12-41 from three.

Luka Dončić got off to a hot start, scoring 11 of their first 13 points. Jake LaRavia was the only other Laker to score on a dunk, which came off an assist from Luka. Ace Bailey was leading the Jazz with four points.

Thanks to Luka fast start, the team shot 57% from the field.

He wasn't just scoring, but was also connecting with teammates on shots. Cameron Carr had a productive showing off the bench, draining a triple and scoring five points.

Los Angeles ended the quarter shooting 52% from the field and were up by 10.

The Lakers did not have a particularly good start to the second period, going 2-9 from the field. Matisse Thybulle had the only points in the quarter. He was also one of the main reasons defensively why Los Angeles remained in the lead despite the slow offensive start.

Utah wasn't shooting great either, going 4-15 from the field. LaRavia and Thybulle continued their strong play, combining for six more points.

Kyle Filipowski had a nice showing off the bench for the Jazz, scoring 10 points. He was their only double-digit scorer.

Unfortunately for Luka, he picked up his fourth foul.

The purple and gold had built a 16-point lead, but on the shoulders of rookie Darryn Peterson, Utah chipped away, cutting the deficit to 11 at halftime.

The third period began with LA on a five-minute scoring drought as Utah kept inching closer. Luka stopped the drought with a midrange jumper, but the Jazz kept their feet on the gas pedal, led by Josh Green draining two triples.

Los Angeles was forced to call a timeout as Utah took the lead after a 3-pointer. Out of the break, Thybulle knocked down a triple.

LaRavia once again came up big, scoring four in a row to put LA back in the lead. It didn't last long, though, as Utah kept pace and tied it back up. Green now had eight points for the Jazz.

Utah was on an extended 25-12 scoring run.

Williams was fouled from behind the arc, made the shot, and converted on the four-point play. Carr added another four points to his strong night, giving nine total.

The Lakers were up by one at the end of the third.

Extending from the end of the third, LA were on an 11-0 scoring run to start the final frame. They opened up a double-digit lead. Thybulle scored the first basket of the quarter for the team, pushing his point total to 11.

The run extended to 17-3, and Los Angeles was up by 13 with 7:31 left.

George tried to get the Jazz going, putting up six points, but LA did a great job keeping the lead and keeping the pace where they wanted, not allowing Utah to make a run.

LaRavia knocked down a triple, his second of the night. He had a productive 15 points. With 1:28 left, Utah waved the white flag and subbed in their bench.

Luka finished with 28 points, four rebounds and eight assists. Kessler once again had a slow offensive showing against his old team, logging 10 points with 12 rebounds and two blocks. LaRavia had 15 points with 10 rebounds.

Thybulle continued his stretch of exceptional thievery with two steals to go along with 14 points and two blocks. Carr had his best showing of the season with nine points. Grimes pitched in with nine points and eight assists.

Williams ended with nine points and four rebounds. Sexton tallied 12 points and four assists.

The Lakers' next game will be against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday at 6:30 PM PT.
