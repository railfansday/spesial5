Midweek Warm-up: It’s gut-check time for Bayern Munich; Champions League predictions; and MORE!

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bayern Munich has been battered by injuries—there is no denying it.

Yes, the squad is stretched thinner than anyone would like. And yes, having half the medical department on speed dial is not exactly ideal preparation for a Champions League showdown—but welcome to Atlético Madrid. Nobody is handing out sympathy points at the Metropolitano.

Diego Simeone is not going to look at Bayern Munich's injury report and politely agree to make things easier or offer to let the Bavarians borrow a few subs. Atlético Madrid will press, scrap, frustrate, kick, counter, and generally make ninety minutes feel like an appointment with the dentist. They will smell vulnerability, and Bayern Munich cannot afford to show any.

This is precisely the kind of match that separates a talented team from a genuinely resilient one. Harry Kane, Dayot Upamecano, Joshua Kimmich, and the rest of the available squad need to show some leadership. The youngsters and squad players need to embrace their opportunities rather than look around wondering who is missing.

Bayern Munich has had two consecutive Champions League draws, a bruising injury situation, and plenty of reasons to feel sorry for themselves (though, credit to them, they have not gone down that road).

This is gut-check time. No perfect lineup, no ideal circumstances, and absolutely no sympathy from Simeone. Just Bayern Munich against one of Europe's most uncomfortable opponents.

It is time for Bayern Munich to roll up its sleeves and remind everyone what this team is made of.

This won't be a walk in the park, as Diego Simeone's side will bring its usual combination of physicality, defensive discipline, and shithousery designed to frustrate Bayern Munich's attack. However, Bayern Munich—even in an injured state—has too much firepower to be contained for ninety minutes. Harry Kane will be the difference-maker, while Michael Olise's creativity and Joshua Kimmich's control in midfield should give FC Bayern the edge. Manuel Neuer will also have a big moment or two to remind everyone why he's still one of Europe's elite goalkeepers. Atlético Madrid will make things uncomfortable and find the net, but Bayern Munich's top-end quality—despite dealing with so many injuries—will ultimately prevail.

Prediction: Atlético Madrid 1-2 Bayern Munich (W)
