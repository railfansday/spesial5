Steelers score first as T.J. Watt recovers Bo Nix fumble in end zone

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

There hasn’t been any offense on display in Pittsburgh so far on Black Friday, but there has been a touchdown.

On third-and-15 at his own 19-yard line, Broncos quarterback Bo Nix dropped back to pass and was immediately pressured, and he did about the worst thing he could have done: He dropped the football, which rolled into the end zone, where Pittsburgh’s T.J. Watt fell on it for a touchdown.

The player everyone is watching today is Steelers quarterback Mason Rudolph, making his first start of the season after Pittsburgh benched and then cut Aaron Rodgers. Rudolph hasn’t done anything of note — he has just five passing yards through two Pittsburgh drives — but he hasn’t made any big mistakes either.

Nix made a very big mistake, Watt capitalized, and that’s the difference in the game so far.
