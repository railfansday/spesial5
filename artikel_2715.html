1 Colts player fined for unnecessary roughness vs. Dolphins in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

One Colts player received a fine from the NFL in the aftermath of Indianapolis' tight win over the Dolphins in Week 10. Colts defensive tackle Adetomiwa Adebawore was hit with a $11,941 fine for unnecessary roughness stemming from a face mask call on Adebawore in last week's game.

No Dolphins players were fined for their actions in Week 10.

The NFL deducts fine money from players' game checks, and the funds are donated to the NFL Foundation to support the health, safety and wellness of athletes at all levels of football and the Professional Athletes Foundation to support NFL Legends in need.

Matt Goncalves, $8,847: Unsportsmanlike conduct, illegal celebrations and vulgar acts, Week 2

Akeem Davis-Gaither, $11,941: Unsportsmanlike conduct, taunting, Week 4

Austin Ajiake, $5,583: Unnecessary roughness, hit on a defenseless player, Week 5

Spencer Shrader, $7,639: Unnecessary roughness, tripping, Week 7

Cam Taylor-Britt, $7,791: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 7

Adetomiwa Adebawore, $11,941: Unnecessary roughness, face mask, Week 10
