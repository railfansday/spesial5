Broncos vs. Steelers injury report for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers have released their final practice participation report and game statuses ahead of their Black Friday matchup tomorrow afternoon.

They elevated running back Tyler Badie to the active roster despite the questionable game status and moved defensive lineman Sai'vion Jones to injured reserve. Safety Taylor Rapp has also been ruled out, but the good news there is that fellow safety Brandon Jones is returning to active status and will play tomorrow. That Broncos' secondary is finally back to full strength after nearly a month of various injuries holding guys out.
