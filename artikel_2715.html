Ravens’ Calais Campbell gives strong endorsement of Jacoby Brissett before MNF vs. Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Baltimore Ravens are set to take on the Los Angeles Chargers for a "Monday Night Football" clash to wrap up Week 10. Jacoby Brissett will make his debut with the club after being traded to Baltimore from the Arizona Cardinals. Heading into the contest, defensive lineman Calais Campbell hyped up Brissett with a strong endorsement.

Hours before kickoff, Jamison Hensley of ESPN shared what Campbell had to say about the 33-year-old quarterback. Campbell is familiar with Brissett, as the two were teammates at one point with the Cardinals, and the 19-year veteran likes what he's seen at Ravens practices heading into Monday's game against the Chargers.

"Calais Campbell, who was a teammate of Brissett's in Arizona, said, 'You can just see Brissett's belief in himself.' Campbell pointed out that a lot of times players are hesitant when they arrive with new teams, and Brissett has been ripping the ball in practice," Hensley said.

Brissett appeared in eight games for the Cardinals before ultimately being benched in the 33-30 Week 8 win over the Dallas Cowboys for Gardner Minshew. Shortly after, the franchise traded the 11-year veteran and a 2027 fifth-round pick to Baltimore in exchange for a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick.

Jacoby Brissett makes his debut with the Ravens, having recorded 1,719 passing yards, 10 touchdowns, and seven interceptions this season with the Cardinals. He's recorded those numbers while completing 63.3% of his pass attempts.

With Lamar Jackson ruled out for an indefinite amount of time, the former third-round pick gives Baltimore a chance to keep its playoff hopes alive. Especially if he leads the team to a win over the Chargers on "Monday Night Football."
