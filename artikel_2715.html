New York Jets News: Familiar Face Returns in Bills Clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Look at that. Former New York Jets defensive back Jim Leonhard is the defensive coordinator for the Buffalo Bills.

Leonhard played for the Jets from 2009 through 2011. Ah yes, the glory days. The Jets went to back-to-back AFC Championship games and it felt like the team was on top of the football world. Looking back on that era and living through those moments reminds me of the line from Andy Bernard in "The Office": "I wish there was a way to know you're in the good old days, before you've actually left them."

Jets fans thought we'd be riding that wave forever, and then suddenly, out of nowhere, it was over.

Leonhard was asked about his many connections to the Jets organization during his media availability ahead of Week 10.

Here's a did-you-know segment: Leonhard played against Geno Smith as a player in the NFL. During Leonhard's final year playing in the NFL in 2014, he played under first-year coach Aaron Glenn with the Cleveland Browns. Leonhard has known Jets running back Braelon Allen since he was 16 years old. Before making the jump to the NFL coaching world, Leonhard was in the college ranks at Wisconsin and got to recruit Allen as a high schooler.

Plenty of interesting connections to think about as we head into the next opportunity for Glenn to get his first win against the AFC East as the HC of the NYJ.
