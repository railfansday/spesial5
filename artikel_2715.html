Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 10 Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Thursday injury report for week 10 of the NFL season against the Miami Dolphins.

Right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) missed practice again today. Travis is trending in the direction of being unavailable this week which would be a huge loss to the offensive line. The likely replacement would be starting Luke Tenuta but they could also reshuffle the line by moving Matt Goncalves to right tackle.

Linebackers Jaylon Carlies (neck) and Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) both missed practice today. Both look set to miss this weeks game. If both are out this week expect a CJ Allen and Bryce Boettcher starting linebacker duo on Sunday.

Tight end Mo Alie-Cox (groin) has missed both practices this week. Ali's-Cox looks like he may miss this weeks game. If so expect a bigger role for Drew Ogletree and an activation for Will Mallory who has been a healthy inactive the past few weeks.

Long snapper Luke Rhodes (hamstring) missed practice today. Rhodes injured his hamstring during the Cowbiys game last Sunday. If he can't go expect a practice squad enervation to fill the void in Sunday.
