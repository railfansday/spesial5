Steelers try for first win in Philadelphia since LBJ was president

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

That's the last time the Pittsburgh Steelers won a regular-season game in Philadelphia.

LBJ was president. Bonanza was the top show on TV. Yesterday was the No. 1 song.

The Steelers entered that game 0-5. The Eagles were 2-3. Pittsburgh's quarterback was Bill Nelsen. Philly's was Jack Concannon.

The 20-14 final score was fueled by a pair of defensive scores. Willie Daniel returned a fumble 17 yards for a touchdown. Jim Bradshaw had an 82-yard interception return for another six. (He had three interceptions in the game.)

The Eagles otherwise dominated. They gained 388 yards. The Steelers gained 132. Pittsburgh fumbled five times, but recovered four of them. Running back Dick Hoak, who passed away recently, led Pittsburgh with 46 yards from scrimmage.

Since that day, the Eagles are 11-0 at home against the Steelers. Today, in a game that was flexed out of the 4:25 p.m. ET window because the Eagles are in a soft-tank free fall, the Steelers are 6-3 and the Eagles are 2-7.

For the Steelers, a loss today could see 6-3 become 6-7 quickly; their next three games are against the Broncos, the Texans, and the Jaguars.

So beyond ending a 61-year streak of futility in Philly, the Steelers need to win this one today. Even if they do, those next three games could alter the trajectory of the season and throw the Steelers into a scramble for a playoff berth in a stretch run that includes the Ravens, Panthers, Titans, and Ravens.
