Chiefs terminate veteran LB, two-time Super Bowl champion in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Kansas City Chiefs terminated the practice squad contract of veteran linebacker Cole Christiansen on November 18.

Christiansen started his career as an undrafted free agent with the Los Angeles Chargers in 2020 and signed with Kansas City in 2022.

A member of the Chiefs' Super Bowl LVII and LVIII rosters, Christiansen exclusively saw action as a special-teamer for Kansas City and Los Angeles, logging a total of 399 NFL snaps without a single defensive down played.

Two-time Emmy-winning Sports Producer Nick Jacobs of KSHB 41 shared the news of Christiansen's termination in a post to his official Twitter account:

While his role with the Chiefs' special teams unit was relatively niche, Christiansen was quietly one of the team's longest-tenured veterans. Fans shouldn't be surprised if the former Army Black Knights standout reunites with Kansas City later in the 2026 regular season, especially if injuries affect the team's depth at the linebacker position heading into the playoffs.
