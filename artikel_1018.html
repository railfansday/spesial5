JJ Gabriel says goodbye to Man Utd with Real Madrid move close

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Real Madrid reached an agreement with Manchester United earlier this month to sign JJ Gabriel; The teenager requested to leave United in September and has not trained with the club since; the 16-year-old and his camp are confident there is a clear pathway to the first team at Real Madrid

JJ Gabriel has announced he is leaving Manchester United as he looks forward to his "next chapter" at Real Madrid.

Sky Sports News revealed earlier this month that Real have reached an agreement to sign the 16-year-old prodigy from United.

One source has told Sky Sports News a deal to take Gabriel to Real Madrid could be finalised in the next two to three days.

Gabriel is registered with Manchester United's academy until the end of the season but Real Madrid are intending to sign him in January.

There is a FIFA loophole that allows European clubs to tie down young players to professional contracts if they have a European passport which Gabriel qualifies for through both parents.

In a farewell post on Instagram he said: "Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams.

"Here I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award.

"You gave me moments I'll always treasure. To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

"As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything. JJ."

Sky Sports News understands United are disappointed Gabriel's video last night confirming his exit was posted without any contact with those at the club beforehand.

Club sources have reiterated, as Sky Sports News reported last month, they have attempted to check in on Gabriel on multiple occasions but have yet to successfully get in contact with him or his family.

Gabriel remains registered with United's academy until the end of the season and the club have not yet been contacted by any governing body around his potential 'de-registration'.

United will challenge any move away from the club with 'every legal means possible' if there is evidence of what club sources describe as 'inducement' from another team.

Sky Sports News has previously reported Gabriel's camp are not concerned by any potential legal challenges by the club.

The teenager requested to leave United in September and has not trained with the club since. When Gabriel's camp made it clear he wanted to move on, they asked to be de-registered immediately for the player to become a free agent.

Sky Sports News understands a big factor as to why Gabriel is leaning towards a move to Real Madrid is because he and his camp are confident there is a clear pathway to the first team at the Bernebeu.

"Those on the outside of Carrington's walls will understandably view Gabriel's impending departure as a major blow for Manchester United. The reality, however, is that it has been expected for some time."Gabriel and his family have no longstanding ties to United as a club. He was not born and raised in the north of England, where a sense of local connection might have carried greater weight in his decision to stay."Over the last 12 months, United have pulled out all the stops to convince Gabriel and his family that there was a pathway to the first team. They met Sir Alex Ferguson, Ruben Amorim and Michael Carrick. Gabriel also trained with the senior squad and made his first-team debut at the age of 15 against Atletico Madrid in pre-season."Ultimately, it was not enough to keep him at the club. As disappointing as it will be to lose a talent of Gabriel's calibre, United will no longer have to make further concessions to accommodate a young player and his family - a situation that can risk upsetting the balance of an entire academy system."There is a wider consideration, too. Parents and agents connected to other academy players will inevitably take note when they see one individual receive such tailored treatment. It can create expectations that are difficult for a club to manage across its youth system."This situation now appears to be reaching its conclusion. But the focus will soon shift to the 16-year-old himself. His talent is undeniable, but will the circus surrounding his exit become a hindrance to his development, or will it prove to be little more than a footnote in what could be a long and illustrious career?"Time will tell. And I imagine Jose Mourinho will have something to say about it, too."

"This kid's a good player. He could have been a superstar. Man Utd will be gutted.
