Koepka 'disappointed' with PGA Tour season but 'beyond happy' to be back

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brooks Koepka will tee up in the RSM Classic this weekend; the American left LIV Golf in 2025 and returned to the PGA Tour earlier this year but has endured a tough season; watch the RSM Classic live on Sky Sports Golf

Brooks Koepka has dubbed his 2026 season a "a disappointment" but insists he is hungry to secure a spot on Team USA's 2027 Ryder Cup squad.

The American returned to the PGA Tour via the one-time-only Returning Member Program in January following his LIV Golf exit at the end of 2025, a decision he said he was "beyond happy with".

After being welcomed back to the tour with open arms, Koepka made his return at the Farmers Insurance Open in February. He'd go on to make 16 starts on the PGA Tour this year but would miss the cut at five events and record only one top-10 finish.

Koepka, who was not selected as one of Keegan Bradley's captain's picks for the 2025 Ryder Cup and was also omitted from Brandt Snedeker's President's Cup team in 2026, hopes to raise his game and earn a spot in the roster for next year's Ryder Cup.

"Adare Manor is a big goal of mine," Koepka said. "But this year has just been a little bit of a disappointment, so I know I need to pick it up a little bit.

"It's something I don't enjoy, sitting back watching the Presidents Cup.

"I know I've only played in one [2017], a lot of those [omissions are due to] injuries. But those team events are a lot of fun, I really enjoy them.

"Getting to be around the guys and representing your country is pretty cool.

"So, anytime you can do that, or you have the opportunity to, and you're not able to go play, it kind of infuriates me sitting on the sidelines."

The 36-year-old has featured in four Ryder Cup's in the past, notably helping Team USA to victory in at Hazletine in 2016 and Whistling Straits in 2020.

He boasts an overall individual record of 7-6-2.

Koepka was the lone player to elect to sign up to the PGA Tour's Returning Member Program, with Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau and Cameron Smith all snubbing the deal, instead choosing to remain at LIV Golf in 2026.

However, following the dissolution of the formerly Saudi-backed breakaway league, Rahm, DeChambeau and Smith have all elected to rejoin the DP World Tour for the 2027 season, along with multiple other former LIV Golf players.

Koepka, meanwhile, was asked ahead of this week's RSM Classic whether he felt back at home after returning to the PGA Tour, replying: "Yeah, I felt very at peace with coming back.

"The reception was fantastic from all the players, caddies, just everybody on tour.

"I think I kept a good relationship with everybody even when I left.
