Whether you’re looking for a small, low-watt charger for your phone, a versatile travel charger, or something powerful enough to keep your laptop and multiple other devices topped off, these deals on USB chargers and portable power banks can help you save money while improving your charging setup.

After testing more than 20 chargers, ranging from basic, low-wattage models to a 140W multi-device charger, we learned that choosing the right one isn’t as complicated as you may think. Modern charging standards have made today’s chargers more efficient, smarter, and safer than ever. But some still stand out, as you'll see in our Best Chargers 2026 article.

In addition to the chargers, we also recently tested 13 power banks with various capacities and outputs. From 5K mAh 22W models to massive 25K mAh 220W devices, we covered charging speed, charging capabilities, and capacity by using a desktop charger and a powerful laptop to drain the battery packs. In the end, we found it’s best to buy what you need for what you’re charging. If you're powering a laptop, get something more powerful with more capacity; if you’re only charging smartphones, watches, and low-power devices, a smaller capacity/output bank is fine for most people.

While cheaper options exist for both types of devices, we stand behind these because we have tested or used most of them ourselves, or they are part of the same family with the same or similar attributes.

One of the best times of the year for sales is the week of Black Friday where you can find all kinds of deals on these products.
