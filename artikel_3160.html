This 65-Inch Samsung Mini-LED TV Is $200 Off Ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Early Black Friday deals are popping up everywhere from Amazon to Costco. For those looking to upgrade an old but smaller TV to a newer model without splurging on an OLED, the 65-inch Samsung M80H Mini-LED TV is 29% off and down to a record-low $497.99 (originally $697.99). If you want to downsize (or go massive), the 55-inch, 75-inch, and 85-inch models are also discounted ahead of the holiday.

While it’s considered an entry-level 4K smart TV, the M80H is packed with Samsung Vision AI features, and like the rest of the brand’s TVs, it runs on the Tizen interface. A high 144Hz refresh rate, as well as VRR and  AMD FreeSync Premium, makes it a decent choice for gamers compared to the standard 60Hz of cheaper models.

It has AirPlay support and built-in Alexa, but lacks local dimming and may struggle in very bright, sunlit rooms, so keep that in mind if you do a lot of daytime viewing. That said, Filmmaker Mode delivers reliable color accuracy for standard dynamic range (SDR) content out of the box. At under $500, the 65-inch Samsung M80H Mini-LED TV is a solid option if you want a big screen and a high refresh rate on a budget.
