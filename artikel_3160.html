Nvidia reports without Jensen Huang as options bet on a 7% swing

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Interim CEO Colette Kress takes the Nvidia earnings call for the first time. Analysts want answers on Southeast Asia sales, the 70% growth forecast and Nvidia’s backing of AI customers.

Nvidia reports its third-quarter results after the US market closes on Tuesday. It is the first report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO. Analysts expect revenue to jump 91% to $109.1bn and earnings of $2.47 a share, LSEG data cited by CNBC shows.

Interim CEO Colette Kress, Nvidia’s CFO since 2013, will lead the call with analysts for the first time. In the past, she read the prepared remarks and Huang took the questions. The call is due to start around 5pm ET.

Nvidia’s shares fell 4% on Monday to close at $156.14. They have lost about 35% since their all-time high in October. The stock has fallen for six straight weeks, its longest losing run since 2012, CNBC reported.

Options traders are pricing a 7% move in either direction after Nvidia earnings. That is the largest implied move since February 2025, CNBC’s Oliver Renick reported. The median move over the past eight quarters was 3.2%.

Analysts want to know how much of Nvidia’s sales in Southeast Asia end up serving Chinese companies. On Monday, the US moved to curb remote access to AI chips through overseas data centres. On Tuesday morning, Nvidia’s board set up a committee to oversee export control compliance.

UBS analysts flagged new scrutiny of sales into Singapore and Malaysia, according to Axios.

“In a worst case, we estimate this could cut up to ~10% out of revenue,” UBS wrote.

HSBC downgraded the stock to hold last week. It said 11% of revenue over the past six quarters came from outside the US and Taiwan. Nvidia has said it gets essentially no data centre sales from China. It has warned customers that new rules on remote access could expose them to US “legal action”, sources told CNBC.

In August, Nvidia forecast revenue growth of roughly 70% for the fiscal year ending January 2028. Analysts expect about $125bn in revenue guidance for the current quarter.

“What would be good for them to do is reiterate that and confirm that is still intact, if they have the ability,” Bloomberg Intelligence analyst Kunjan Sobhani told CNBC.

Investors also want to know if Kress will keep using Nvidia’s balance sheet to back customers, from neoclouds to AI labs. Nvidia plans to invest about $10bn in Anthropic after its IPO this week, according to the prospectus, Axios reported. The Wall Street Journal has reported that Kress is expected to rein in that financing.

The board is not considering bringing Huang back, a person familiar with the matter told CNBC. On prediction markets, Arm CEO Rene Haas and Nvidia executive Ian Buck lead as permanent successors, Business Insider reported.

Analysts are split. Goldman Sachs kept its buy rating and a $300 price target, Business Insider reported. Truist cut the stock to hold. Morgan Stanley cut its data centre sales estimates for 2027 and 2028 by about 10% after Huang’s exit, CNBC reported.
