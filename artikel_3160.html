OpenAI says its AI found 14% gains in AMD’s flagship GPU design

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Some of the model's circuit changes are already in a chip AMD has sent for manufacturing.

OpenAI says its models improved power, performance and area by up to 14% on AMD’s flagship GPU designs, with some changes in a chip sent for manufacturing and single-digit gains on AMD’s next flagship CPU. It also detailed AI-made gains on its own Jalapeño chip and is training a GPT-Synopsys model with Synopsys.

OpenAI says its AI models have improved the design of AMD’s flagship graphics chips, with some changes already in a chip sent for manufacturing. In a blog post on Wednesday, it reported gains of up to 14% in power, performance and area, the three measures chip designers trade off, against AMD’s heavily optimised designs.

The model rewrote the code that describes a chip’s circuits, and used Synopsys software to check each change still worked. OpenAI says this was about a year’s worth of progress in under five months. AMD also confirmed single-digit gains in key parts of its next flagship CPU, according to OpenAI.

OpenAI used the same approach on Jalapeño, its first in-house chip, which went from design to manufacturing in nine months. Changes suggested by its models cut the size of two key parts of the chip by about 13% and 10%, and worked in the finished silicon. Startups are chasing the same idea: Architect Labs raised $24M in June to help companies design custom chips with AI.

OpenAI also tested five generations of its models on designing two small open-source processors. Its newest, GPT-6.2 Astra, scored highest both when improving existing designs and when building a processor from written requirements. OpenAI noted the second test was easier, because models could leave out features the originals had.

Next, OpenAI is training GPT-Synopsys with Synopsys, which is refocusing on AI design, to run the design tools engineers already use. Not everyone is fully convinced yet. OpenAI’s own chip chief, Richard Ho, said last month he still does not fully trust AI design changes, so every one is checked.
