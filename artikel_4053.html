Xbox Series X|S Estimated At 38M Sales, And Here's How That Compares To Other Consoles

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

They've just celebrated their sixth anniversaries

The Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S officially celebrated their sixth anniversaries back on November 10th, and to mark the occasion, Niko Partners analyst Daniel Ahmad has been looking into a whole bunch of sales estimates.

In a post over on social media, Ahmad estimated that the Series X and S are now at a combined 38 million sales worldwide, and then compared that figure to loads of other consoles that we've seen throughout the years.

The comparison is specifically referencing the sales of each console after six years on the market, so when the Xbox 360 is referenced at 60 million sales, for example, it still racked up another 20+ million until it was discontinued.

As you can see, it doesn't make for great reading overall. Sony has shipped a lot more PS5 consoles than Microsoft has Xbox consoles throughout this generation, and even compared to past Xbox systems, the Series X and S have struggled to keep pace. That said, the original Xbox only racked up around 25m sales, so that one's been beaten at least.

Ahmad also points out that "console players are monetizing at levels significantly higher than any prior gen", so even though hardware sales have been poor for Xbox, content and services revenue has grown massively in recent years.

People will point to this kind of stuff as evidence that Xbox is "failing", but Microsoft is much more interested in the content and services side of things, and that's been a much bigger success for them since the Xbox Series X and S first launched. With Project Helix around the corner though, they'll still hope they can attract a bigger piece of the pie in the hardware space, although that'll be a very tough ask amidst an ongoing component crisis.
