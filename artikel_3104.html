This award-winning game where you deliver packages to folks is about establishing human connection. This over-the-top, modern-day adventure follows an amnesiac ex-yakuza member who has reinvented himself as a swashbuckling pirate. It’s a wacky beat’em-up game that lets you build a pirate crew, board other ships and live out your pirate dreams.

The Windscale fire was the worst nuclear disaster in the UK’s history, and it’s estimated that it caused between 100 and 240 cancer-related deaths. And Atomfall asks, “What if Windscale was similar in magnitude to the disaster at Chernobyl?”

This game takes place five years after a more devastating Windscale disaster. You’ll encounter gangs of bandits, cults and rogue government agencies throughout the quarantine zone in the Lake District, Cumbria. It’s up to you whether you avoid conflict or charge into the heat of battle, but resources are scarce, so maybe don’t go in guns blazing every chance you get.
