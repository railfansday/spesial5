BigLaw expects uptick in congressional investigations work after Democrats’ wins in midterm elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

President Donald Trump speaks at a campaign rally at the Liacouras Center on June 22, 2024, in Philadelphia. (Photo by Anna Moneymaker/Getty Images)

BigLaw attorneys are expecting work to pick up in several areas, including congressional investigations, after Democrats took control of Congress in the Tuesday midterm elections.

“Democrats ran on an investigative agenda, and they will seek to execute that agenda with a mandate from the voters,” Thomas Moyer, the head of Bracewell’s congressional investigations practice, told Law.com. “Any industry, company or individuals who have had business before the Trump administration could be pulled into the mix, if they haven’t been already.”

While congressional investigations could target many of BigLaw firms’ clients, Law.com reports that the nine law firms that made pro bono deals with the Trump administration also could face scrutiny in coming months.

Mergers and acquisitions are another practice area that could experience increased activity, as companies will likely try to complete deals before Congress changes next year, Law.com reports.

“Some deals may push forward to lock in the certainty of the current regulatory environment,” Francesca Odell, the head of the corporate group at Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton, previously told Law.com.
