The Republican Party Must Be Bigger Than Any One Person

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The GOP’s future depends on putting principles first.

President Ronald Reagan gives his acceptance speech at the 1984 Republican National Convention in Dallas, Texas

In 1967, Ronald Reagan offered a reminder about the Republican Party that is as relevant today as it was nearly 60 years ago: “Unity does not require unanimity of thought.” Reagan understood something fundamental about political leadership: A party is not a personality cult. It is a coalition of Americans united by shared principles, even when they disagree about policy specifics.

In the aftermath of the midterm elections, Republicans have serious questions to confront. The results demand reflection and an examination of what we believe and what kind of political institution we intend to be. I believe in the Constitution, limited government, individual liberty, fiscal responsibility, and personal accountability. Those convictions have not changed because of political pressure, nor are they dependent on who occupies the White House.

President Ronald Reagan gives his acceptance speech at the 1984 Republican National Convention in Dallas, Texas

Brad Raffensperger is the secretary of state for Georgia.
