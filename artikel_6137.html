Why Entitlement Reform Is Easier Abroad Than in America

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Privatization, among other proposals, requires a particular set of conditions that the U.S. seems to lack.

Pedestrians walk along a street in the Old Town of Stockholm, Sweden, October 17, 2024.

Senator Ron Johnson’s proposal to sustain Social Security payouts even after the projected depletion of its funds in 2032 has attracted new interest in entitlement reform. Fifteen years ago, Paul Ryan became the victim of character assassination over his plans for modest entitlement reforms. In 2005, George W. Bush attempted to reform Social Security, yet his efforts were in vain.

Johnson’s proposal is poorly conceived, even by the low standards of populists. It would set the United States on course to default even faster than it currently is.

John Gustavsson is a writer from Sweden and holds a doctorate in economics. He is Senior Editor of Nation & Citizenship at Restoring the West and a former adviser to the Sweden Democrats in the European Parliament.
