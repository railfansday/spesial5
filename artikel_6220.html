Inside Washington Dulles’ New Concourse E, Light Years Ahead Of United’s “Temporary” Gates

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Concourse E at Washington Dulles International Airport is now open for business and it’s a beautiful terminal that stands in stark contrast to the “temporary” C and D Concourse that United Airlines has used since the mid-1980s.

I was present for the ribbon-cutting yesterday, which featured United executives Andrew Nocella (Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer) and Toby Enqvist (Global Chief Operations Officer) as well as Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger and U.S. Senator Tim Kaine and a number of other elected officials and airport leaders. Steven Bradbury, President Trump’s Deputy Secretary for Transportation, also spoke.

While everyone is going to play nice at these sort of events, I was happy to see Republicans and Democrats talking together about investing infrastructure, one of the many opportunities for bipartisan collaboration as the new Congress assumes power. There’s reasonable discussions over cost overruns and the massive time and money drain this project has been already, but it has to be done and maybe just maybe everyone can come together to accelerate this project and hold everyone more accountable.

But my main point here it to showcase the beautiful new terminal. It’s world class without a comparison, but we cannot help but compare it to the C/D Gates and it’s truly night and day; the difference between first world and third world.

First, it’s very easy to reach. Take the train to the C gates stations and now instead of walking a half miles back to the terminal, you can simply walk upstairs to the new terminal:

There’s plenty of natural light and and the Untied Club is conveniently located on at the top of the stairs/escalators on the right side. Many new concessionaires are slated to open, with several highlighting local food and beverage options from Northern Virginia.

And yes, the mobile lounges remain. Passengers arriving on an international flight will still be directed to a mobile lounge and driven to the mid-field immigration facility. Eventually, a new facility will be opened in Concourse E.

This is phase one. The terminal expansion will continue in 1-2 more phases and the entire concourse will not be done until the end of the decade, at the earliest (sad, but predictable).

I wrote about the new United Club here. It’s posh and United will also build a new Polaris Lounge in this Concourse.

Bottom line: it’s a bright new day for Washington Dulls and the new terminal expansion is a fitting upgrade for the capital city of the United States.
