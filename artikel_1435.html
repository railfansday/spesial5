Como beat AEK with eight players missing, then faded late before hosting Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Como beat AEK Athens 1-0 to reach 12 points in the Champions League, despite eight unavailable players and a late drop in intensity. Juventus visit the Sinigaglia on Sunday, but must first play Omonia on Thursday.

The narrow victory over AEK was Como’s third straight win in the Champions League. It leaves them close to the knockout stage. It also offers the latest look at Juventus’s next league opponents, on the same ground.

The win came with a stretched squad. Fàbregas told Sky Sport that the absentees were either injured or left out of the Champions League squad list. Making so few changes was, he said, a first in his coaching career. That is a thin bench ahead of a league weekend.

The scoreline flattered AEK in places. Lucas Da Cunha missed a first-half penalty, and Nico Paz hit the post. At the other end, goalkeeper Robert Sánchez made two important saves before the break. Milla’s goal made him the 11th different player to score for Como in this season’s competition. No other team in the Champions League has had that many different scorers. UEFA named him Player of the Match.

Como had been scoring freely before Matchday 5. On Matchday 4 they won 9-1 away to Lens, a result that put them fourth at the halfway point of the league phase. AEK proved a harder problem. They held Como up for long spells and were difficult to break down.

The final stretch was the concern. Fabio Capello put it to Fàbregas that Como had dropped off badly in the last 20 minutes. The coach replied that he did not yet have the experience to stay calm in such moments. The late fade is consistent with a group short on fresh legs.

Fàbregas also set out his priorities. He said the win brought Como close to at least a play-off place, but that qualification was not yet secured. In the same breath, he stressed that Serie A matters more to the club. That message points straight at Sunday’s meeting with Juventus.

Juventus have their own European commitment first. Kick-off in Turin, in the Europa League league phase, is at 20:00 UTC. Juve go into that game fifth with 10 points from four matches. Omonia are 18th with six. Sam Barrott of England will referee, with Darren England on VAR.

That schedule cuts into any advantage from Como’s fatigue. Both sides will carry European minutes into the weekend. Juve, however, will have had two fewer days to recover.

The league table adds weight to the fixture. Como sit fourth in Serie A, eight points ahead of Juventus. A home defeat would leave a depleted Como squad with a reduced cushion. A Juve win would close the gap at the end of a demanding week.

Como’s Champions League campaign continues away to Real Betis on 9 December, and the league phase ends with a trip to Barcelona on Wednesday 27 January 2027. After Sunday’s 13th-round meeting at the Sinigaglia, Juventus host Udinese at 18:00 on Sunday 6 December.
