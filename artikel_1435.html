Gasperini after PSG draw: “I will rewatch it, we were great. Soule is becoming a top player.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After PSG-Roma, Gian Piero Gasperini spoke to Sky Sport.

“I’m definitely very happy and very proud of tonight’s performance. It was clear we wouldn’t lose our composure even against the strongest, and these guys are undoubtedly that right now, not only because of the players’ ability, but also because of the team’s organization and the variety of plays they used.”

“It was a very important match for us, and I’ll rewatch it because we had some really strong performances against this team, which will have to help us move forward in the league, in the upcoming matches, and even in the Champions League.”

“I’m particularly satisfied this evening. I knew there was some risk, but not for me, because I see these guys, Pisilli, Castro, and then Ghilardi and Lulli came in. I see them really improving, and so, if you overcome these types of games like you did this evening, it’s a great boost for everyone.”

“Soule? Meanwhile, besides being a strong player, he’s a very positive guy, who understands every situation to improve and grow. Let’s remember that he’s a 23-year-old, relatively young and in the period in which he can evolve significantly, as he is doing. Furthermore, he’s a much more complete player than before, because tonight he played on the left, whereas until last year he never got out of that space. He played centrally behind two strikers. He’s a player who is reaching extraordinary technical, physical, and athletic maturity. I’m really happy for him, because it’s not just tonight’s match. He’s been putting in great performances for a month and a half, two. He did well at the start of the season, too, but in these last two months he’s really done something beyond that, something superior.”

“I’ve always said I simply have to try to bring out the best in these guys, but those are their characteristics; I can’t add anything. I can try to help them bring out the best in their qualities, which are many. Hermoso is right: we don’t give up on anything.”

Gasperini: “I’m happy and proud of my team. Castro deserved a chance, Hermoso was brilliant.”
