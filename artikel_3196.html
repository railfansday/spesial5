New Book Alleges Former Disney Chief Bob Iger Impregnated Employee Leading To “Traumatic Termination Procedure”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A bombshell book about former Disney boss Bob Iger alleges that the exec impregnated an underling employee in the early 1990s that resulted in “a traumatic termination procedure.”

Details of the book went public in a New York Times article today.

The New York Times report, which features excerpts from The House of Mouse by Wall Street Journal reporter Robbie Whelan, was published in the paper and claims that a consensual extramarital relationship between Iger and a woman described as an “ABC marketer” resulted in a pregnancy. In his new book, Whelan, according to The Times, “contrasts Mr. Iger’s public support for the #MeToo movement in the late 2010s with an extramarital relationship he had with an ABC marketer in the early 1990s.” Whelan “points to a power imbalance: Mr. Iger, then running ABC Entertainment, was rungs above her. The woman became pregnant and ‘suffered through a traumatic termination procedure.'” Watch on Deadline

Whelan has defended the inclusion of the episode in his book as an example of the “disconnect between Mr. Iger’s public principles and private conduct.”

According to Whelen, the 75-year-old Iger declined to be interviewed for The House of Mouse, but “enlisted a lawyer who sent letters to the publisher raising concerns about a potentially ‘false and defamatory’ account.” Disney also declined to cooperate, Whelan said, adding “Their instinct was to bully me.”

Disney had no comment when contacted today by Deadline.

Disney did provide a statement to The Times, but did not directly address Whelan’s claim about the affair. “Bob Iger is one of the most respected C.E.O.s of his generation,” the company said, citing, among other things, his “bold creative and strategic decisions” and saying he left Disney “stronger and more innovative.” A representative for Iger declined the Times’ request for comment.

Elsewhere in today’s article, the Times writes that The House of Mouse depicts Iger “as insecure, vindictive and preoccupied with money.”

“I am frustrated about compensation!” Iger wrote in one of several private emails obtained by Whelan. “I had a nasty and distasteful negotiation to extend my contract this last time.” The 2014 message went to Ike Perlmutter, then Marvel’s chairman and a major Disney shareholder.

HarperCollins releases The House of Mouse on December 1 – the same day Iger is scheduled to attend his final Disney board meeting.
