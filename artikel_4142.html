GTA 6: Where to Find Helicopters

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Adam Beck is a Canadian who has covered the gaming industry for over twenty years. He has a passion for RPGs, action, stealth and horror games.

How you travel in Grand Theft Auto 6 is very important. Whether it’s by plane, train or automobile, there’s a slew of different ways to get around the vast world Rockstar has created. One of them is a helicopter. This is an iconic vehicle from the franchise that gets you to places faster than most, but securing one isn’t exactly the easiest thing to do. Thankfully, with this guide, you’ll be able to find them with ease.

Getting a helicopter is one of the better ways to travel in Grand Theft Auto 6. While planes are faster and bring you further distances, there isn’t always a good way to park them. You can definitely try landing on some rugged piece of land or on the freeway, but the chances it will work or stay there when you come back is unlikely – not to mention the chances of getting back into the sky are unlikely.

You can definitely jump out and parachute to where you’re going, but then you’ve lost your means of transportation. That’s where helicopters come into play. They are a good middle ground where they can cover a good amount of ground fast and be more versatile when it comes to escape vehicles.

Heck, during robberies, parking this out back is always handy if cops aren’t at the ready when you exit the building. While you’ll be unable to register helicopters as personal vehicles, having a good idea where they are located is always good.

Early on, there are very few actual helicopters to speak of. You will be able to find a couple of helicopters that can land on water, but even they are rarer than water planes. Where you will need to look is in Vice City itself.

The easiest tell is looking at the map and looking for a helipad. The thing, though, is that not all helipads have helicopters on them. Some are meant for you to land on them instead of housing something for you to take.

You will want to look in three different districts of Vice City: Tequesta, Downtown and Washington Beach, specifically in that order to look for them.

Tequesta has arguably the most to choose from, but we’re assuming you’re coming from the south. From here, there will be a hotel all the way to the southeast of the area up against the water. Enter from the front and take the elevator all the way up.

If you’re coming from the south, the first place you should hit is the Rico Huxley Hotels. This is located on the river just east of the Zenith gym. Take the elevator up into the lobby and then break into the roof access area.

If you’ve made it to the fourth chapter, you’ll be pretty familiar with the area, but the Crimson Palms Condominiums is the place you need to go. This is up against the water to the north, two buildings south of the Aichi Condominiums Outdoor Gym. Like the others, take the elevator up and get onto the roof.
