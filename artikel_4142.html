How To Find Dog Island In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Here is how to reach Dog Island via Cool Dog in Grand Theft Auto 6.

Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally arrived, and it's as chaotic, grandiose, and weird as you'd expect. While there are a comical number of secrets and hidden interactions you can discover, one of the oddest is Dog Island.

As the name suggests, it's a small island filled with lifeguard dogs that you can visit. However, finding it will require getting lost, but it's worth the effort if you're just starting. Here is everything you need to know about reaching Dog Island and what you'll get for your troubles.

You can get to Dog Island as soon as you complete your first day as Jason, but it can also be reached on Lucia if you've progressed further into the campaign. Once you wake up at his house and finish getting ready, go out to the nearby dock and get into his boat. Now we need to head out to open water, so go west from Key Lento into Leonide Bay.

When you arrive, look for any open spot of water, kill your boat's engines, and jump in the water. Start swimming away from your boat. After around 20–30 seconds of swimming, you will hear barking. Activate your Focus Mode to locate the source of the noise, which is a dog in a life vest driving a jet ski.

This is Cool Dog, and he functions as a way to get back to shore if you're stranded or your boat is destroyed. Approach Cool Dog, hold down L2 to focus on him, and then select the Praise option when you meet him. This will allow Jason to climb onto Cool Dog's jet ski.

Once you do, sit back and relax as Cool Dog brings you to shore. However, if you stick with Cool Dog he will eventually bring you to Dog Island, which is located in the southern portion of the Grassrivers region. How long this takes depends entirely on where Cool Dog picks you up, which is why I suggest doing it early, since Jason's first home is right by the Grassrivers.

You'll know you've reached Dog Island, as Cool Dog will get off the jet ski and join a few other dogs under an overturned boat. When you arrive, there are a couple of things you can find. The first is a wallet that Cool Dog will dig up for you, which contains 1,200 dollars. Cool Dog will carry it around in his mouth, so approach him to get this new furry friend to drop the wallet for you!

This is a nice amount of cash to start you off if you visit Dog Island early. Additionally, there will be the Sports Wrap Shade sunglasses you can snag in the small hole to the left of the boat near the toys. Picking them up will automatically equip the glasses for Jason and will permanently add them to his wardrobe.

You should make sure to throw either the Frisbee or mini-football for the dogs on the island, as this will make progress towards increasing your Focus meter. It won't be a lot, but every little bit counts, and who doesn't want to play with all the cute dogs running around anyway?

When you're ready to leave, you can actually just take Cool Dog's jet ski. It's quite fast, and he doesn't seem to mind you borrowing it! Don't worry about your boat either, as it will respawn back at your house free of charge. While that's all you can do on Dog Island, Cool Dog will still be around to rescue you if you're ever stranded at sea, so just make sure to listen for the barking!

Don't worry, GTA 6 has a vehicle delivery system - you just have to make some progress to actually unlock it.
