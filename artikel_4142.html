Helldivers 2's Ever-Changing Universe Evolves Again with New Venomous Terminids

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

One of the best things about Helldivers 2 is its shifting warfront across the shared online galaxy.

Developer Arrowhead has been able to constantly update the game with new missions, narrative beats, and features in this sort of "live" scenario, reacting to how players are doing and shifting the balance to keep things constantly engaging.

It's a really cool aspect of the game, and the latest addition is a dangerous new strain of Terminids.

As detailed on the PS Blog, these new bugs are venomous, and are burrowing beneath the surface, creating highly toxic new cave systems on various planets.

These Terminids move faster, but are more fragile than the regular species. The advice is to use powerful but less precise weaponry, such as shotguns, flamethrowers, and grenades to deal with them quickly.

If you let them attack you, you'll be poisoned. Light poison steadily chips away at your health, while heavy poison stacks over time.

Again, I think it's pretty cool how the game is able to change things up like this. I reckon it's a big reason why Helldivers 2 took off and continues to be popular.

I just fought the new faction with three randoms (all of them very good) on superhelldive. Long story short the new spiders mix come in two variants, a little very fast one and a big slow one. The big ones are slow but bite harder and spit as well as bite. Both boast venom that not only damages Helldivers but prevents them from running. Bugs attack in hoards (the spiders mix in with regular bugs like chargers, flyers and bile titans) so constant strategic repositioning aka running is the key to not getting overwhelmed.

I was never killed by poison but I did die a couple times when poisoned. Venom can be cured by the healing stimulants every Helldivers carries into battle (4 is standard though some suits hold 6).

Like bile spewers the big spiders have big venom sacks and explode in a cloud of poison if you shoot them but that is good provided they are close to other bugs and far from divers. It’s worth noting Helldiver’s poison gas works very well on even venomous bugs.

The team I played with was all very good but while we got the mission and all the side objectives done only one of us made it to the ship (three of us died on the tarmac of the extraction point a few seconds before our rescue ship landed).

Another new thing included (theoretically a temporary strategem though I’m sure it will become permanent) is a jeep with a flamethrower which works out pretty well though as the gunner I can personally attest a gunner who points in the wrong direction can accidentally set the jeep and all its occupants on fire 😭.
