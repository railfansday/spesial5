French team: relive the announcement of Zinédine Zidane’s list

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

“Rennes? Looking at the players they have released, it is clear that they have a very good training center, with real skills. This is exactly what is needed. We should not envy anyone given the pool we have in France. We have everything we need here, and Rennes is a perfect example. They put things in place and the work is done very well. They are all very good footballers, and that is what interests me. »

“He can play anywhere. Afterwards, it depends on the moment, the match, the choice we make but he can play anywhere. Honestly, I think he’s better on the left side because he can come in and hit. He can go very far but he will continue to progress. We improve all the time even when we are very good, even when we are very old (laughs)”.

“Worried about French football? No, I’m not. It’s a football that lives well. I see great things there, great matches and great players. Everyone is free to think what they want, but we must remain vigilant and ask ourselves certain questions. As for Fonseca’s departure from OL? It’s his decision. It’s certainly a complex situation for him, but it was a choice of the heart. It’s going to be hard for OL, because he was a very good coach, with good ideas and who did an excellent job. And when we find ourselves like this during the season, it can quickly become tricky. »

“Really, I can’t tell you that right now. The thing was to make the list with my three goalkeepers and now we are going to talk with Fabien about the hierarchy to make decisions”.

“In any case, I saw an abnormal match. Both on the result, of course, and on the manner: there is a team which was effective and another, rather good with good players, but which was stifled and taken out of its match. That said, what I expect from my players is that they realize all the potential we have. We must show that we have incredible potential, that we are capable of achieving great things, of being better than the opponent and proving how to achieve it. We prepare to win, without being intimidated. »

“I don’t want to get into that, I’m sad and extremely touched, it gives me goosebumps, we don’t want to see that on a football field, that’s just the message I want to give. I’m especially sad for the family, we don’t want to see that. I hope we never see that again.”.
