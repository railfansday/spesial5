“Important to take time” – Gabbia admits January return is possible with right precautions

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Matteo Gabbia has admitted that he could return to the field in January for AC Milan, but he doesn’t want to rush back.

It has been a difficult season for Milan, at least where injuries are concerned. In defence, they have had issues reminiscent of the 2023/24 season, where Gabbia had to be brought back from his loan spell at Villarreal.

This season, though, it seems Gerry Cardinale is going to go down a different route and spend to help Ruben Amorim with his defensive options. Indeed, there have been plenty of rumours about recruitments at the back.

However, Gabbia could again be a key returnee in January, with him hoping to return from injury early in the new year.

Speaking to Sky Sport (via MilanNews) – at an event organised by the Milan Foundation at Milan’s “Arturo Toscanini” secondary school, where they met with several children with disabilities – Gabbia discussed the importance of the event, as well as his recovery.

“It’s wonderful, very enjoyable to spend hours with them. It’s also beautiful to see how they manage to create a group, something that really shows the importance of teamwork, the importance of friendship, even among themselves.

“We’re happy; we thank Fondazione Milan for the project, which is always beautiful and important: we’re happy to be a part of it and we’re enjoying the afternoon.”

Are you able to rejoin the group in January?

“Yes, that could be the time: we’re trying to work hard and do everything as best we can. I obviously have to thank the physiotherapists and the doctor because we’re really doing everything we can every day to try to heal the injury and get back on the pitch feeling good.

“It’s definitely been an unlucky season for me so far: I don’t want it to happen again or for any recurrences, so it’s also important to take the right time, get back in shape, and support the team right now.”

“Although it’s bad for the team that there are so many injured players, we’re together right now, supporting each other and trying to encourage each other through therapy, gym work, and the entire recovery process. Therefore, we’re trying to work as a team this week too, to overcome all the players’ injuries.

“From a personal standpoint, I’m trying to take this time calmly, in the sense that these things are part of sport, part of life, and not all moments are the same.

“This is certainly a difficult time, but it still allows me to stay close to the team, to try to lend my support, even in the good and bad times. That’s what I’m doing, in addition to obviously doing what I can to try to return as soon as possible and make my contribution on the pitch.”

How do you explain the team’s ups and downs?

“I think it’s something we need to get our heads around; we need to understand the importance of every game.

“The other day I heard our coach give an interview in which he said something important: we need to understand that every game, when you don’t win, is taken as a defeat, a heavy defeat. We need to truly understand the importance of every game and approach training to prepare 100%, as best as possible, for every match.

“Therefore, I think this is room for improvement, but I see a group that is really eager to improve, to work, and as a result, I’m sure we’ll be able to fix this flaw as well.

“Although we’ve had a good start to the year in the league and less so in the Europa League, we have plenty of time to improve and have a strong year.”
