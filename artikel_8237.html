10 Best Resorts in the West for Parking (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Parking isn’t as glamorous as some other categories in our Resort Guide, but it’s one of the most important factors. You probably don’t think twice about it until you’re paying for a spot or being turned away on a powder day.

Save yourself the stress and start your ski day off right. Here are SKI readers’ Top 10 resorts for parking in the West.
