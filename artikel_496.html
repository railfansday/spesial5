Nvidia Board Meets to Consider Replacing Jensen Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Technology-stock-driven rally came to a sudden halt as traders confronted a leadership crisis at Nvidia Corp. that risks reverberating across the global artificial intelligence supply chain. Nvidia dropped 7% in premarket trading, with the firm’s board discussing Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang’s future after reports of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of spying for China.
