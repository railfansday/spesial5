Inter & Co, Inc. (INTR +4.67%) reported its 14th consecutive quarter of profit growth, attributed by management to its BanTech model that integrates banking services with a scalable technology architecture. The company reported a significant strategic focus on artificial intelligence, including the deployment of over 700 internal models and a new strategic partnership with SpaceXAI. Leadership changes were implemented during the quarter to align organizational structure with objectives for growth, monetization, and operational excellence. Management stated that the credit portfolio expansion currently emphasizes secured lending products, such as mortgages and private payroll loans, to navigate a challenging macroeconomic environment. Profitability remains a priority, with the company maintaining an efficiency ratio near 42% while continuing to grow its active client base.
