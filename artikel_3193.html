‘Avengers: Doomsday’ Final Trailer Clocks 563M Views, Best Ever For Disney; 2nd Most Viewed Of All-Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EXCLUSIVE: Distribution sources always like to downplay the straight line between a telling analytic about a pic and its opening box office. However, it’s pretty clear from the traffic for the final Avengers: Doomsday trailer, the Russo Brothers-directed Disney/Marvel movie is bound for some sort of box office opening record.

In its first 24 hours, the trailer racked up 563 million views, making it the most viewed Disney trailer of all time, beating out the Avengers: Doomsday teaser (503M in 24-hour traffic) and Deadpool & Wolverine’s 2024 Super Bowl teaser (365M including broadcast, $211.4M U.S./Canada opening). Final trailers typically get fewer views than first trailers, but not Avengers: Doomsday.

Among all trailers, the final Doomsday was the second-most-viewed movie trailer of all time behind Sony/Marvel Studio’s Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which was watched by 718.6M in its first 24-hours, and wound up opening to a domestic box office record of $360M. Watch on Deadline

The final Doomsday trailer outperformed the final trailer for Spider-Man: Brand New Day in its first day by more than 3x, the latter pulling in 131M.

Other stats show that the last Doomsday trailer had close to a million mentions on social in its first 24 hours. It was also the second-most-discussed trailer since 2023, only below its own teaser (1M).

By Tuesday morning, the trailer had ranked as No. 1, No. 2, and No. 3 on YouTube Movie Trending Charts. The trailer following its release was No. 1 on Twitter/X trending for Doomsday related terms, and it remained in the Top 5 for eight consecutive hours. That also is the highest concurrent viewership for the Doomsday Countdown Clock livestream on YouTube (306K).

The global publicity tour kicked off at the Formula 1 Race in Las Vegas this past weekend, with appearances from Avengers stars Chris Evans, Anthony Mackie, and Simu Liu; an Avengers: Doomsday takeover of the sphere; an Avengers wrapped midnight air taxi; and the Williams team displaying a Doctor Doom inspired show car. The pic’s global tour will make stops in São Paulo, the Gold Coast, Rome, London, Seoul, Shanghai, Toronto, and Los Angeles.

This week, massive OOH Avengers installations popped up in Oakland, New Orleans, Santa Fe, New York, and New Jersey, each one tied to an MCU character, which has driven additional social chatter.

Back in March 2025, the cast announcement for Avengers: Doomsday broke livestreaming records, generating 275M digital views and 3.1 million interactions across its 5½ hour runtime.

Across all trailers thus far, Avengers: Doomsday has generated a jaw-dropping 3.3B views and 4.8M social mentions. This includes performance on the RDJ SDCC Reveal (views only), ‘Chairs’ announcement, four character teases, teaser, trailer and final trailer.
