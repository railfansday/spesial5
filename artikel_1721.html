Mason Mount offering Michael Carrick a midfield solution which could have legs

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It is not uncommon to hear football club executives talking about the importance of the “money being on the pitch”.

In other words, managers having their best players fit and available most if not all of the time. Quality at the top level is essential but so is robustness. They go hand in hand.

Unfortunately for Manchester United, the money has not been on the pitch down the years nearly as much as the club would want or expect.

Bruno Fernandes stands out for what he does with the ball primarily, but also because he plays almost every minute of every game and, while his sheer indefatigability is extreme, United know they need more of his ilk.

This is why the forthcoming season is such a big one for Mason Mount. His quality has seldom been in doubt. The problem is he has rarely been on the pitch long or consistently enough to show it.

Indeed, the one-time England midfielder has started just 25 Premier League matches since his £60m move from Chelsea three years ago and that perennial unavailability is one of the reasons doubts were being cast about his Old Trafford future going into this summer.

Yet Michael Carrick was keen to keep Mount, United have rebuffed inquiries around him and intriguingly the 27-year-old could yet have an impact on what the club do during the remaining six weeks of the transfer window.

For the second time in the space of six days, Mount was deployed in a deeper midfield role alongside new £48m, signing Andrey Santos for Friday’s resounding 5-0 victory over Norwegian side Rosenborg in Trondheim, just as he had done against Wrexham last weekend.

Mount has spent the majority of his career playing further forward, either off the right or as a No. 10 but Carrick likes him in a fluid No. 8 role, a position Erik ten Hag had identified for the player only for persistent injuries to detonate those plans.

The logic is simple: Mount is mobile, can cover the ground, is adept under pressure, has line breaking passing, vision and a nose for goal. He is also a good presence in the dressing room and a leader in his own way. Watch him imparting advice to Shea Lacey as they walked back to the centre circle after the youngster’s goal opened the scoring against Rosenborg and you get a glimpse of that.

The question, of course, is whether he can stay fit long enough to impose those qualities that help to explain why Liverpool and Arsenal had also been so keen to sign him back in 2023 and Carrick’s predecessor Ruben Amorim was also such a fan.

With United’s summer budget only stretching so far, Carrick was always going to have to think on his feet in certain areas of the pitch.

What will be interesting to see over the coming weeks is whether the manager feels Mount is robust enough to complement Santos, fellow summer signing Youri Tielemans and Kobbie Mainoo.

Much of the chatter among United fans at present centres on who the third midfield signing will be after Santos and Tielemans.

Given Mount’s injury record, it would be a significant risk not to recruit again in that department in the hope he could stay fit over a full season. Four options in two positions behind the No 10 of Fernandes sounds fine in principle but what happens if Mount’s season was to follow a similar trajectory to the previous three?

If United are going to strengthen in other areas, there will need to be compromises or alternative solutions that help to free up cash along the way but gambling in midfield may not be the best way to go.

With Champions League football to factor in this season, it is still hard to believe United will not seek to strengthen again in midfield but the club seem ready to take their time on that front. And all the while an assortment of names continue to fly around.

After spending £85m on Santos and Tielemans, a massive outlay on a third midfielder at this stage would be a bit of a surprise.

Could that negate a move for Real Madrid’s Aurelien Tchouameni? Alex Scott is greatly admired by United but Bournemouth are determined to keep him and have put a prohibitive pricetag on him.

AS Roma’s Manu Kone is not thought to have universal backing internally. Tyler Adams’ name has been floated again but the Bournemouth and US midfielder’s injury record counts against him.

There has not yet been evidence of a serious appetite to go for Brighton’s Carlos Baleba, whom they considered signing last summer. Sander Berge at Fulham and Borussia Dortmund’s Felix Nmecha are among a number of other midfielders who have been linked with United.

Another midfield signing could well emerge but United are keeping their cards close to their chest. Whatever materialises, though, it will still not change the fact this is a huge season for Mount.

There is the final whistle and Manchester United have cruised to a 5-0 win over Rosenborg in Norway. Goals from Lacey, Zirkzee, Devaney, Amass and Williams have seen Michael Carrick’s men ease to victory. A pretty one-sided game.

The first yellow card of the game is given very late on as Devaney is booked for a high boot.

A late change for Rosenborg as Hald-Hernes replaces Borgersen.

The visitors have dominated this game and they now have their fifth goal. It is created by great work from Kamason on the right side of the box before he squares it to Williams, who has the easiest of tap-ins at the far post.

Other than a few short periods in this game, Rosenborg have looked poor. They may be midway through their league season but you can see why they are down in 10th in the Norwegian top flight with just four wins from their opening 13 league games.

You would expect that Michael Carrick will be very keen to see his side keep a clean sheet.

Manchester United have their fourth of the evening. Kamason is found making an overlapping run on the right side of the box and his cross fired towards the back post is tapped in by Amass. That was a goal assisted by one full-back and scored by the other.

Good work from Soboczynski results in Rosenborg winning a corner out on the left. They take it short before a shot is hit from the edge of the box straight down the throat of Vitek.

Rosenborg hit the post. Manchester United should probably have been given a free-kick in the build-up as Soboczynski appeared to push Devaney over but play goes on. The ball comes to E Ceide at the far post but his effort strikes the outside of the post.

Just a few minutes after making a raft of changes, Manchester United have their third goal of the evening. Rosenborg fail to clear a corner from the right and the ball falls to Devaney on the six-yard box. He takes one touch before firing into the top of the net.

Bearing in mind Rosenborg are in the middle of their league season, this has been an encouraging showing so far from United. Joshua Zirkzee has been lively. He’s still more likely to leave than stay at Old Trafford this summer but performances like this won’t hamper his value. He set up Shea Lacey for his goal with a good pass after again dropping deep into space and he’s taken that goal to give United a 2-0 lead quite brilliantly. Patrick Dorgu has been a threat and Mason Mount has dovetailed well in central midfield with new signing Andrey Santos.

Both sides are going to make a raft of changes on the hour mark, with the rest of the Manchester United starting XI being changed other than Vitek in goal.

Santos brings down Borgersen not far outside the Manchester United penalty area to give Rosenborg a decent opportunity. It is a pretty decent effort from E Ceide that goes just wide of Vitek’s right post.

The visitors have not been great since the break but they have their second and it is a great solo goal from Zirkzee. Lacey, out on the left touchline, squares it for Zirkzee, who takes the ball towards the Rosenborg penalty area. He fakes a shot, which takes numerous Rosenborg defenders out of the equation as they slide past. He then sits down the Rosenborg keeper before slotting into an empty net. A fine goal from Zirkzee.

Soboczynski has his second chance since coming on at the break after good work from E Ceide down the left but the former’s effort goes wide.

That is a really poor decision from the linesman. Duris was played in behind the Manchester United defence and thought he was through for a one-on-one chance but the offside flag goes up. However, replays prove that Duris was clearly being played onside by Amass, who has looked shaky since coming on at the break.

Zirkzee receives a pass from Dorgu on the D and takes one touch before shooting. His effort takes a deflection and goes behind for a corner, which Wahlstedt slightly unconvincingly punches away.

That is a very good chance for Rosenborg to equalise early in this second half. Amass and Heaven makes a mess of things deep in their own half, presenting Rosenborg with a good opportunity inside the Manchester United penalty area. Two Rosenborg substitutes combine as Soboczynski squares it for Borgersen but the latter makes a mess of it and shoots well wide from 10 yards out.

Michael Carrick has decided to make just a few changes at the break with Amass and Kamason coming on. Shaw and Maguire have gone off, which has seen Yoro move inside from right-back to centre-back.

With no delays, the first half is brought to an end and Manchester United take a 1-0 lead into the break courtesy of Lacey’s goal. Expect a raft of chnages at the break.

As this half has worn on, Manchester United’s dominance has only increased.

Mbeumo appeared to be racing through one-on-one but Mortensen just about gets back to outmuscle Mbeumo and allow the ball to go back to his keeper. There were a few appeals for a foul by Mortensen but that challenge was just about fine.

Other than those early stages, Manchester United have dominated the rest of this first half and are pressing for a second. For the second time in the half, they strike the frame of the goal. Mbeumo clips in a cross from the right into the Rosenborg penalty area and it picks out the head of Yoro. However, from eight yards out, the Frenchman’s header comes back off the bar.

Moments after they hit the frame of the goal, Michael Carrick’s side take the lead. Zirkzee picks up the ball inside his own half and, from the halfway line, feeds a pass into the on-running Lacey. Dorgu wants Lacey to play the ball across to him at the back post but the latter opts against it. Instead, just inside the Rosenborg penalty area, he cuts onto his preferred left foot and curls one into the far corner. A good finish from Lacey.

Just as I was saying that both sides were lacking a cutting edge in the final third, Manchester United are inches away from taking the lead as they strike the post. Santos feeds a delicate pass into the path of Dorgu on the left side of the box. Dorgu strikes his effort powerfully but it comes off the outside of the far post.

Both sides have lacked a cutting edge when they get into the opposition final third, hence why there has not been a plethora of good chances.

Mbeumo beats his man down Manchester United’s right and cuts it back to Mount on the edge of the Rosenborg penalty area. Mount hits it first time but his shot is blocked and spins behind for a corner. It is Manchester United’s first of the game but it is headed away.

That was nearly the moment for Rosenborg to take the lead. Pereira’s cross from the left-hand side of the box takes a deflection and nearly ends up at the feet of Chiakha at the back post but it is just out of his reach.

Rosenborg looked the sharper in the early exchanges but Manchester United are growing into the game and starting to gain more control.

Manchester United create their first opening of the game. Rosenborg poorly five the ball away deep inside their half and immediately Zirkzee plays it into the path of Mbeumo, whose shot from the right side of the box is saved by Rosenborg keeper Wahlstedt, who initially spilt the shot but gathered at the second attempt.

Mount trips Sahsah just outside the Manchester United penalty area, which will give Rosenborg a good shot to take an early lead. Rosenborg look a little sharper unsurprisingly in these early stages.

The Rosenborg captain Selnaes takes but hits it straight into the wall.

With Rosenborg in the midst of their season and Manchester United just in their second pre-season game, you would expect the hosts to have more rhythm and flow in their game.

Manchester United to appear to be lining up with a back four, with Yoro starting at right-back rather than his more customary centre-back position.

Manchester United’s preparations for the new season continue in Norway as they take on Rosenborg in their second pre-season game. These sides did meet in a pre-season friendly two years ago in July 2024, with Rosenborg winning that encounter 1-0.

Manchester United kicked off their pre-season against Wrexham nearly a week ago, which they lost 1-0 in Helsinki. Michael Carrick opted to deploy two different XIs for each half, meaning 22 players got a run-out. Manchester United kick off their Premier League campaign at newly promoted Hull City on August 22.

They have added two midfielders to their ranks over the last few weeks, both from the Premier League. Brazilian Andrey Santos has joined from Chelsea whilst Belgium’s Youri Tielemans has arrived from Aston Villa. Santos made his first Manchester United appearance in the loss to Wrexham whilst Tielemans is yet to feature having made the knockout stages with Belgium at the World Cup. A number of players are still yet to return from World Cup duty, including the likes of Lisandro Martinez, Marcus Rashford and Kobbie Mainoo, although Rashford still faces an uncertain future.

Former Leeds goalkeeper Karl Darlow has also arrived but he is currently recovering from a minor procedure. Benjamin Sesko is still trying to shake off a shin injury whilst Matthijs de Ligt is not yet available due to a back injury which plagued for most of last season. Manuel Ugarte faces a lengthy spell on the sidelines having picked up a serious injury whilst playing for Uruguay at the World Cup. After this game, the level of competition for Manchester United for the rest of pre-season ramps up a number of notches, with the likes of Atlético Madrid and back-to-back European champions PSG to come over the next couple of weeks as Carrick’s men prepare for a season in which they return to the Champions League.

Rosenborg are in the midst of their league campaign in Norway and are experiencing a tricky campaign. They are currently 10th in the Norwegian top flight, having won just four of their 13 league games thus far, but their form has picked up of late as they have won three of their last five games. They won their last match 3-0 against Start six days ago.
