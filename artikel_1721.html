ESPN Brasil: Real Madrid open to permanent Endrick sale – Milan have ‘two aces’ to play

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Another source in Endrick’s homeland Brazil have gone big with the story that a permanent move to AC Milan is possible.

This morning, we relayed the news from TNT Sports Brasil, who claimed that Milan are readying a package worth €70m to sign Endrick. They claimed that a loan deal is now ‘off the table’ and that a winter switch is possible on a permanent basis.

Now, further confirmation has arrived from ESPN Brasil. They state that Real Madrid want €60-70m in order to sell the young forward, which is the amount that their colleagues at TNT suggested the Rossoneri would offer.

Furthermore, Real Madrid have not ruled out including a buy-back clause in the operation should Endrick reach a high level at San Siro. President Florentino Pérez holds Endrick in high regard, so he fears the regret of losing him.

ESPN adds that neither Real Madrid nor Endrick are considering another loan and are working on the prospect of negotiating a permanent move in January. In all of this, Milan have two ‘aces up their sleeve’, as per the report.

The first is the fact Endrick is represented by ROC Nation. They are an agency that Gerry Cardinale has a good relationship with, and they have expressed a certain ‘fatigue’ towards Los Blancos due to Endrick’s lack of opportunities since returning to the Santiago Bernabéu after his loan at Lyon.

The second ace is that at Milan he would be a key starting player, with a tailor made role for him on the right side of the attack. With this comes the guarantee of more minutes, which could boost his profile also when it comes to the national team.

ESPN report that Jose Mourinho is firm on his stance: he will deploy Endrick when he deems it appropriate and depending on the context of each match or each situation. The manager will continue to prioritise the team over the individual, but wants Endrick to say.

As mentioned, if he is sold then Real Madrid’s desire is to recoup a large portion of the sum invested in his 2022 purchase, when he was still at Palmeiras. At the time, the transfer was finalised for around €70m, including bonuses.
