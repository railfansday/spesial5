Anthropic IPO launch: How tech stocks are performing Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Anthropic had a huge IPO launch day on Nov. 19, making history as the first major AI lab to go public. The company's stock opened at $105 after setting an $83 IPO price well above expectations. The stock then reached a high of just over $115 shortly after opening.

But that was yesterday. Friday, Nov. 20 is a different story.

Anthropic stock has since fallen by more than 12 percent. It's currently trading at around $86, which is just above the IPO price.

The rest of the tech-centric NASDAQ has also fallen with it. NASDAQ is currently down as a whole by 3.6 percent. Nvidia is among the hardest hit, down 7.19 percent. Nvidia's stock troubles, however, shouldn't come as a surprise as the chip company is still reeling from the fallout of former CEO Jensen Huang's resignation following reports of an affair with a woman suspected of working for Chinese intelligence.

Other chipmakers such as Intel and Micron Technology also fell by 4.5 percent and 5.2 percent, respectively.

Meta stock is down 4.48 percent, Amazon is down 2.88 percent, Microsoft stock fell 1.96 percent, and Apple is down 1.27 percent.

Despite all the interest surrounding Anthropic's IPO, its many investors still have concerns regarding the AI boom. Right now, it's unclear exactly what that means for the industry and the U.S. economy at large in the long term.
