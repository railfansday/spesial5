I regret the thread seemed to focus more on the payment methods. My reason for posting was to point out how much my life has shifted to working with this Thumbtack community. They've been extraordinarily helpful at broadening my network of handymen and helpers - unlike my neighbors who all wait weeks for the same handyman. As a caregiver whose needed to make so many big and small changes to the home, it's been great having Thumbtack for the smaller and frequent stuff. I wanted to encourage other folks (seniors, those rehabbing from injury, those with mobility issues) to be aware of it, as a source of labor. I found Thumbtack much better than the Angie's list of yore.
Thank you for introducing me to these resources! At 72 and about-to-be 77, we are realizing that we (a) can't and (b) don't want to do a lot of this stuff any more. We've had enough miserable experiences flying over the last 4-5 years (and then look at today!) that travel has lost a lot of appeal, so we don't mind paying others to make our lives more enjoyable at home.

- and we too keep cash on hand at home. Not just for paying for help, but after covid and then an inland hurricane in the mountains that the area is still recovering from, there are times that cash is king. Try no internet, no cellular, no electricity and see how tap-to-pay works...
