Your Wedding Date Sets the Venue’s Price – Ask for Friday, Sunday & Winter Rates

Wedding venues charge the most for Saturdays from late spring through fall, and many charge less for a Friday, a Sunday, or a date in their slow months. Before you set your wedding date, ask every venue you’re considering for its prices by day of the week and by month.

Venues price by demand, and most of that demand falls in six months of the year. About three in four weddings take place between May and October, according to The Knot.

Any day but Saturday is one a venue would rather fill at a lower price than leave empty, and so is almost any date in its slow season, usually January through March in much of the country. The discount can come off the rental fee or the food-and-drink minimum, the least you must spend on the venue’s catering and bar. Say a venue sets a $10,000 minimum for June Saturdays and $6,500 for February Fridays. Booking a February Friday instead of a June Saturday saves $3,500 before anything else changes, and a brunch or afternoon start can lower the catering bill further.

Other vendors vary. Some will lower their rate for a slow date, while others charge the same all year. Hotels near the venue usually charge less in their own slow months, too. That lowers the rate on a room block, the rooms a hotel holds for your guests at a group price.

Find out which dates each venue counts as peak, since some charge Saturday prices for the Sunday before Memorial Day or Labor Day. Then ask your other vendors whether a different date changes their quote.

Weigh what the date costs your guests, too. A Friday or a midweek date means time off work for many of them, a Sunday means a late trip home or a Monday off for anyone traveling in, and winter weather can delay flights and make driving risky. Check the local calendar as well, since a festival or a college graduation weekend can fill nearby hotels and push up room rates.

Ask each venue to quote the same wedding on three dates, a Saturday in your first-choice month, a Friday or Sunday that month, and a Saturday in January, so you can see what moving the date would save.
