BofA Rewards, the free program that replaced Preferred Rewards in May 2026, raises the card’s rate for members, and anyone with one of the bank’s eligible checking accounts can join. Your tier, set by your three-month average balance across qualifying Bank of America and Merrill accounts, decides by how much.
