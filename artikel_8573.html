Take your used toys where they’ll be resold, handed to a child, or played with, such as thrift stores, foster care programs, child care centers, and neighborhood giveaway groups. Check that each is clean, complete, and not recalled, and skip children’s hospitals and holiday toy drives, which usually take only new ones.

Sort the pile before you call anyone, because each place wants a different kind of toy.

Toys in good shape with all their pieces can go almost anywhere below. Stuffed animals are harder to give away, since child care centers, doctors’ offices, and many family and domestic violence shelters avoid them for hygiene. Many animal shelters take ones too worn for a child.

Thrift stores and charities turn away recalled toys, since selling a recalled product is against federal law. Look up any toy you’re unsure of on the Consumer Product Safety Commission’s recall search.
