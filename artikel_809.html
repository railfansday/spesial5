Collins: I got choked up before I came on for my Wales debut!

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Collins discusses making his senior Wales debut and his incredible start to the season; the 29-year-old has scored 17 goals in 17 games for MK Dons so far in 2026/27; watch MK Dons vs Notts County live on Sky Sports+ on Saturday; kick-off 12.30pm

Football Journalist @DanLong__

On Friday November 6, Aaron Collins was notified by the FAW that he was on the standby list for Wales' Nations League matches against Norway and Portugal.

He knew it was a step in the right direction, but his form - 17 goals and three assists in 16 games in all competitions at the time - seemed more than worthy of an official call-up.

A first chance for the MK Dons striker to represent his country since an U19 friendly defeat to Germany in April 2015 was tantalisingly close, yet just out of reach.

Wrexham's Kieffer Moore withdrew from Craig Bellamy's squad and Collins was called up to take his place.

"That was a call I've been waiting for for a long time," Collins tells Sky Sports.

"I don't score goals to be like: 'I'm going to get called up now', because I know the level I'm at, and I've got to prove a lot to get that chance.

"But after being a pro for 12 years, I did think the older I was getting, the less chance I was going to get. Of course that crossed my mind.

"It was a shock to finally receive the call and know I was going to represent my country at international level."

In Oslo, Collins was an unused substitute as Wales suffered a 3-1 defeat to Erling Haaland, Martin Odegaard and co.

Hearing 'Land of My Fathers' for the first time stirred something inside.

"It really choked me up. I grew up watching the rugby team and the football team singing it and you get goosebumps then. But to be able to stand there and sing that national anthem as a proud Welshman, as one of the players, was something that I would have never even thought I'd be able to do."

Back in Cardiff against Portugal, Bellamy told him at half-time to make sure he was ready to go if called upon.

After 63 minutes, he came on alongside Brennan Johnson to make his senior debut.

"I got choked up again then!" Collins says.

"To actually be able to achieve that and have the honour of having a Welsh badge on my chest entering the pitch was just crazy.

"I enjoyed it and I just played with no fear, like I had nothing to lose. I did what the manager wanted from me; I didn't manage to get any real chances, but you're at the elite level and it's not going to be as easy."

Having not played against Norway, he did not go searching for a shirt swap "out of respect for the lads who played".

But against Portugal "I was walking down the tunnel I saw Diogo Dalot and I got myself a shirt to treasure along with my debut shirt".

Those two shirts are not the only new additions to the room where Collins puts his accolades on display.

In the last league game he played before his Wales adventure, on October 31, he scored four in MK Dons' 6-1 demolition of Doncaster.

That mammoth effort earned him another match ball for the collection - the second of the season and fourth of his career - as well as the Compare the Market Goal Hero of the Month award.

"I'd never scored four in a game before - that's a new achievement ticked off!"

"The only thing I can put my form down to is confidence. Any attacker knows that when you're in your flow, you're in your flow, and when you're scoring and assisting, there's that feeling where you feel like you're not going to miss.

"I'm in hot form and I do expect everything that I hit to go in. It's a feeling I need to cherish and try and make last as long as possible."

Collins' hot streak comes after a turbulent 2025/26 season, during which he helped Paul Warne's side to promotion from Sky Bet League Two, but suffered three significant injuries that greatly reduced his playing time after a reported £800,000 move from Bolton.

"Coming into this season, I had this different kind of hunger to make up for last season where I came in as, maybe you could say the marquee signing, and didn't really get to live up to that," he adds.

"Being fit and ready to kick on - and to start of the season the way I have - has been a real achievement. Now I can show why I've been signed, and hopefully fans can go, 'Aaron Collins was a good signing!'

"To get to 17 in 17 games is a huge achievement so far, and there's plenty more games to come. My goal is to get to that 20 mark as early as possible, and even get to that 20 mark in the league as early as possible, and carry on kicking on."

If Collins carries on in this fashion, he might be a shoo-in for another Wales call-up in March.
