Ryan Seacrest Debuts First-Ever Spray Tan—and the Reactions Are Golden

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Ryan Seacrest's fresh spray tan—the 51-year-old's first—triggered a big reaction from his co-hosts during a Nov. 10 taping of his show, On Air.

Even after the On Air With Ryan Seacrest host learned that his first-ever spray tan turned out more subtle than he intended, he maintained that he was proud of his UV-free, sunkissed look.

“My debut spray tanning was last night. Never done it before,” Seacrest said during a Nov. 10 taping of his radio show, noting that his selected tan level was “two.”

Though his co-host Patty Rodriguez suggested the American Idol star opt for at least a level three, Seacrest understandably wanted to avoid accidentally recreating Ross Geller’s infamous tan fail in Friends.

As he put it, “I was nervous because they told me if I go too far, you just can't really downgrade it.”

And as contributor Tanya Radz also confessed she couldn’t tell a difference after the treatment, the 51-year-old stood by his understated aesthetic, replying, “Maybe that's good.”

As for one reason the producer’s reveal was so underwhelming?

Seacrest eventually admitted he had L.A.’s Sugared + Bronzed beauty spa skip his face to avoid disturbing his oily-prone skin.

“I didn't do my face. Why would I do my face?” he asked with a laugh. “You do your face in a spray tan? I don't want that stuff on my face to cause a zit.”

In addition, though the radio DJ recalled that before leaving the salon, “They told me to let it sink in,” he also divulged that he took a shower soon afterward because he wasn’t aware the recommended waiting period was a minimum of six hours.

Still, the Wheel of Fortune host has been showing off his well-groomed physique while enjoying the single life following his breakup with model Aubrey Paige in 2024 after three years of dating.

"I love 'Love Is in the Air,'" he told Vanna White, referencing the evening’s theme during a March episode of the game show before joking, “And I’ll be on Tinder tonight, so I can find it.”
