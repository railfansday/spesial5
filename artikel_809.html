Lionel Messi at center of red-card controversy as Inter Miami face early MLS playoff exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Messi booked for throwing ball into opponent’s face

Columbus coach insists Argentine deserved red card

Defending champions thrashed 3-0 in series opener

Columbus Crew coach Laurent Courtois said Lionel Messi should have been sent off after throwing a ball into an opponent’s face as Inter Miami crashed to a 3-0 defeat in the MLS Cup playoffs on Saturday.

Messi’s frustration boiled over on the stroke of half-time when he hurled a throw-in into the face of Columbus forward Gonzalo Tapia, with 10-man Miami trailing 1-0 at the time.

Messi and Tapia were both shown yellow cards for the incident by referee Allen Chapman, but Courtois said afterwards that the Argentina superstar should have been given a red card.

“Everybody knows that he deserved more than a yellow card,” Courtois said at a post-game press conference when asked about the incident. “It’s just really sad.”

Chapman later defended his decision to show Messi only a yellow card.

“The force used was negligible and the offense did not reach the level of violent conduct,” Chapman told a pool reporter.

Miami had already been reduced to 10 men when the incident occurred and were trailing 1-0 after an early penalty from the Chile international Tapia.

The reigning MLS champions went on to suffer a 3-0 defeat, with Taha Habroune doubling Columbus’s lead shortly after half-time before Tapia added his second at Miami’s Nu Stadium.

The result means seventh-seeded Columbus, the 2023 champions, can eliminate Messi and Miami from the playoffs with a victory in Game 2 of the best-of-three series in Ohio next Friday.

“It was a game where nothing went right for us, and we effectively created their chances for them,” Miami coach Kily González said afterwards.

“We didn’t produce the great performance we had hoped for. Everything went wrong, but we have to learn from it because next time, if we don’t win, we’re out.

“There’s no better challenge for us as a team than this. It’s a test of character, and I know my team has that character.”

Joaquín Valiente scored twice in the space of two minutes as FC Dallas beat the Portland Timbers 3-0 in the opening game of their best-of-three Western Conference first-round series.

The 25-year-old Uruguayan midfielder broke the deadlock in the 53rd minute, volleying home a rebound after Daniel Job’s shot was parried by Portland goalkeeper James Pantemis.

Valiente doubled the lead two minutes later, timing his run to meet another pass from Job before redirecting the ball between Pantemis’s legs.

Samuel Sarver completed the scoring in the fourth minute of stoppage time, finishing a cross from Logan Farrington on the counterattack.

Dallas goalkeeper Daniel de Sousa Britto, known simply as Daniel, made four saves, while Pantemis finished with five for Portland.

The Timbers also lost Ariel Lassiter to an apparent injury in the 33rd minute.

Second-seeded Dallas can advance to the Western Conference semi-finals with a victory in Portland next Saturday.

Teenager Peyton Miller scored one goal and helped create another as the New England Revolution beat the Philadelphia Union 2-0 in the opening game of their Eastern Conference first-round series.

The 19-year-old Miller became the fifth-youngest goalscorer in MLS Cup playoffs history when he volleyed home a cross from Jack Harrison in the 73rd minute, his effort bouncing off the crossbar and into the net.

New England had taken the lead in the 10th minute when Miller’s shot from the edge of the penalty area deflected off Philadelphia defender Nathan Harriel for an own goal.

Harrison, who came on as a substitute in the 62nd minute, was making his first appearance since 9 September after missing more than two months with a quadriceps injury.

Philadelphia nearly equalized in the 37th minute when Cavan Sullivan turned and fired a shot from outside the penalty area that struck the left post.

The Union fielded the youngest starting lineup in MLS Cup playoffs history, with an average age of 23 years and 207 days, breaking the record set by the New York Red Bulls in 2022.

Matt Turner made two saves to preserve the shutout for New England, who can advance to the Eastern Conference semi-finals with a victory in Philadelphia next Saturday.
