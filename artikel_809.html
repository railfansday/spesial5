Miley Cyrus Marries Maxx Morando After 5 Years of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando tied the knot in Palm Springs, Calif., on Nov. 21 after five years together.

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are having the ultimate party in U.S.A.

Nearly one year after the Grammy winner and Liily drummer revealed their engagement, the couple tied the knot on Nov. 21 in Palm Springs, Calif., following five years of dating, Miley confirmed on social media.

Alongside sweet black-and-white photos of the newlyweds, shot by photographer Jose Villa, the "Climb" singer wrote on Instagram Nov. 22, "M + M 11.21.26."

As for who was there to watch something beautiful happen as Miley, 33, and Maxx, 28, exchanged their vows? Well, her father Billy Ray Cyrus and his partner Elizabeth Hurley were among Miley's loved ones in attendance, as well as Elizabeth's son Damien Hurley, per photos obtained by DeuxMoi.

Meanwhile, Miley’s sisters Brandi Cyrus and Noah Cyrus as well as their mom Tish Cyrus and stepdad Dominic Purcell also witnessed the climb to the Last Song star's next chapter.

And the Hannah Montana alum wasn’t having visions of white, but was the embodiment of one as she stunned in a long-sleeved Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello gown that featured a low-cut back while saying "I do." Meanwhile, the groom matched the guests in a black suit, which was also designed by Saint Laurent, according to a press release from the French fashion house.

Miley—who was previously married to Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020—began dating Maxx in 2021 after they were set up on a blind date through his bandmate's sister's friend, he told Wonderland magazine in July.

As for the proposal, the couple proved that true love hit them like a wrecking ball when Maxx popped the question during a vacation in Japan, getting down on one knee in a beautiful Japanese garden.

“It was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation,” Maxx told Wonderland. “She was taking videos, and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”

And although the “Toro” singer admitted that he was so nervous he “could feel my heart pounding,” he was able to pull off the surprise, which Miley shared wasn’t a simple feat.

“I am not easy to surprise,” she admitted during an appearance on Good Morning America following her engagement news. “I love to control every situation. I had completely surrendered. I’m telling you, I was so, so, so surprised.”

And before long, she was teasing that her and Maxx’s special day was on the horizon, releasing “Let’s Get Married” on her September 2026 album Bass Persuades, with the pair also giving an intimate glimpse into their romance in the song's music video.

“Let’s go get married tonight / Let’s get married / I’m having visions of white,” Miley sings in the track's chorus. “Let’s get married / Let’s turn the rest of our lives to a holiday / Anytime, any place, we can run away / Get married tonight / Let’s get married.”

But the couple didn't only work together on the romantic track, as Maxx additionally produced several other songs on the album.

And there’s a reason they always find their way back home to the studio, as Miley told Harper’s Bazaar in 2024, “Maxx just inspires me so much.”

But beyond their musical collaborations, the “Flowers” singer also credits Maxx with helping her with “starting over” and “building again” after she and ex Liam's California home burned down in the 2018 Malibu fires, before later separating.

“After I lost my house in the fires, that wasn't all that I lost,” Miley shared during a December press conference, per Hello! magazine. “I really actually went down to the ashes and rebuilt my life personally and physically [with] my home.”

And as Miley and Maxx enter the new chapter of their romance as newlyweds, keep reading for a look back at where their relationship began…

Miley Cyrus and musician Maxx Morando began dating in 2021. "We got put on a blind date," she recalled to British Vogue in 2023. "Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, 'The worst that can happen is I leave.'"

Miley didn't leave and, as their romance blossomed, the pair went red carpet official at the Gucci Love Parade event on November 2, 2021.

Miley and Maxx, who is a drummer in the band Liily, enjoyed a night out seeing Chicago on Broadway.

The "Flowers" singer and her boyfriend stepped out together at a Versace runway show in West Hollywood on March 9, 2023.

Maxx was by Miley's side when she won her first-ever Grammy at the 2024 award ceremony. The couple even shared a sweet smooch at their table during the event, and Miley referred to Maxx in her acceptance speech as "my love."

They returned to the Grammy Awards in 2025 for another public date night. "He’s very similar to me," Miley told Harper's Bazaar in an interview published November 2024. "We just don’t take life too seriously."

Maxx, who is six years younger than the Hannah Montana star, joined Miley on the red carpet at SNL50: The Anniversary Special, to celebrate 50 years of Saturday Night Live.

The musicians got glammed up for a big night out at the 2025 Oscars ceremony.

And later that night they stepped out in fresh looks for an appearance at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party.
