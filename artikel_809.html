Dorgu makes dramatic impact - Man Utd v Aston Villa ratings

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Patrick Dorgu rises to head Manchester United's injury-time equaliser in a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa

Manchester United fought back from two goals twice to draw 4-4 with Aston Villa as Patrick Dorgu made a dramatic impact off the bench.

But who impressed and who didn't in the Old Trafford epic?

Senne Lammens: One of Man Utd's best performers this season but a calamitous start to this one. First goal went straight through him and he spilled a shot straight from Nicolas Jackson for the second. Went wrong way for penalty too. 4

Diogo Dalot (off 66, Dorgu): Got his body in the right position to close half the goal off for the opener but was in the wrong position to make challenge on Alysson at start of second half to gift Villa a penalty. 4

Leny Yoro: Moved back into the centre, intelligently stepped up to win the ball on halfway, then spoiled good work by attempting a lofted pass when Matheus Cunha wanted it on the ground. Almost headed into his own net in build-up to Villa's fourth goal. 5

Harry Maguire (off 46, Heaven): Managed to clear his head quickly enough to lead United's defence after their shocking start. A doubt pre-match, he went off at half-time. 5

Noussair Mazraoui: Struggled along with many others this season. Asked to push forward, which he seems to find easier for his country but not his club. 4

Youri Tielemans (off 66, Mount): Baited by the Aston Villa fans given his Â£35m summer move from them, but answered in the best way by driving home an equaliser for his first United goal. 6

Kobbie Mainoo: Threatened with a long-range effort and nearly picked out Amad Diallo's run with a precise chipped pass over the visitors defence. 6

Amad Diallo (off 66, Lacey): Kept his place ahead of crowd favourite Shea Lacey but one chase back into his own box showed why, defensively, it made sense. Came close to an equaliser and should have ended an 11-month wait for a goal but Zion Suzuki saved well. 6

Bruno Fernandes: For the second game running, he kept his cool to covert from the spot when the pressure was at its highest. Looked unhappy at the end despite the comeback. 7

Matheus Cunha: Driving run into the visiting box brought a crucial penalty. Curling effort was heading for the corner but turned around post by Suzuki. United's most dangerous attacker. 7

Bryan Mbeumo (off 83, Zirkzee): Did a lot of chasing in the first half to try to pressure the Villa defence into mistakes. 6

Ayden Heaven (on 46, Maguire): Booked for late tackle on Alysson after being introduced at break. 6

Mason Mount (on 66, Tielemans): Hit the bar with first time shot but sliced later effort over. 6

Shea Lacey (on 66, Amad): Tried to create. 6

Patrick Dorgu (on 66 Dalot): What an ending as he changed the outcome of the game with an assist and goal. 8

Joshua Zirkzee (on 83, Mbeumo): Scored in stoppage time and goal proved crucial. 6

Subs not used: Darlow, De Ligt, Andrey Santos, J Fletcher.

Zion Suzuki: Kept busy as the hosts hunted an equaliser. One excellent save from Cunha and a couple when he benefited from a kind rebound, but bad error for United's late equaliser. 5

Victor Lindelof: Found himself under a lot of pressure on a ground he knows so well and turned in an accomplished performance. 7

Taylor Harwood-Bellis: Had to remain concentrated and wasn't faultless but emerged with credit in the bank. 6

Pau Torres: Guided a header over as Villa attacked but biggest task was trying to stop Cunha. As it turned out, a stride too late getting out when Tielemans scored. Couldn't keep United out in the end. 6

Ian Maatsen: Testing day, trying to stop Amad attacking and looking to beat Mazraoui at the other end. Was doing well until the late drama. 7

Alysson (off 71, Barkley): Even the Brazilian must have been a little bit surprised his first-minute shot ended up in the net. His shot created the second and came so close to adding another to his tally after being released by Goretzka. Won the penalty at start of second half too. 8

Boubacar Kamara: Ill-advised attempted tackle on Cunha led to penalty that offered the hosts a way back into the game. Struggled to contain the home attack. 7

John McGinn (off 71, Hemmings): Everything looked so good in the opening minutes but then had to try to ensure Villa didn't fall apart as United fought back. Kept his concentration to score from the spot and created panic with a free-kick. 7

Leon Goretzka (off 78, Mbaye): Tried to relieve pressure on Villa defence as the hosts fought back and drove home Villa's fourth on a crazy day. 7

Johan Manzambi (off 78, Gomes): Not the easiest game for the Swiss international to shine given its general chaotic nature. Tried to get control but no real chance of that. 5

Nicolas Jackson (off 86, Madjo): Alert to snaffle home the rebound after Lammens had spilled McGinn's shot. Not the best performance but much effective than in his Chelsea days. 7

George Hemmings (on 71, McGinn): Came on to shore things up but move didn't work. 5

Ross Barkley (on 71, Alysson): Won't remember ending with fondness. 5

Subs not used: Bizot, Mings, Buendia, Cisse.
