Kraft combines mac and cheese with Cinnamon Rolls in bizarre new flavor

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Kraft Mac & Cheese has introduced a new Cinnamon Roll flavor, combining its signature cheesy pasta with the warm spices of the popular pastry.

Kraft announced the limited-time release on November 2 alongside another new flavor, Chili Crisp, as it expands its lineup with combinations inspired by changing consumer preferences.

According to the company, more than 60% of its fans are interested in trying new and unexpected flavors beyond its traditional mac and cheese offerings.

The new Cinnamon Roll Mac & Cheese combines cinnamon with Kraft’s familiar cheese sauce to create what the company describes as a creamy, slightly tangy flavor.

Inspired by the popular fall pastry, the product brings together sweet and savory elements in a single dish.

Kraft says the release was influenced by the growing popularity of cinnamon as a seasonal flavor, claiming it has surpassed pumpkin spice in consumer preferences in recent years.

The Cinnamon Roll flavor is available exclusively at Walmart for a limited time. The company has not announced an official end date for its availability or provided pricing information.

Alongside the cinnamon-inspired release, Kraft is introducing Chili Crisp Mac & Cheese, which combines its traditional cheese sauce with the heat of chili crisp.

According to Kraft, chili crisp has experienced 923% growth on US restaurant menus over the past four years, helping inspire the new addition.

Unlike Cinnamon Roll, the Chili Crisp variety will be sold at multiple retailers, including Meijer, Walmart, and Amazon, for a limited time.
