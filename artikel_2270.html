QPR confirm Sky Sports decision and it's left a lot of R's fans unhappy

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Sky Sports' decision to move EFL games for TV coverage hardly ever goes down well with fans, and the new year's announcement of the live picks has frustrated some QPR supporters.

It's been a solid opening few months for Julien Stephan's side this season. The Hoops have dropped out of the top eight after just one win in their last four, but they resided exclusively in the play-offs before then.

And, with that one win in that run coming by way of a 5-0 rout against former league leaders Swansea City, there's still enough to suggest that they could be in the mix for the postseason come May.

To do so, the fanbase will want as many games at Loftus Road to take place either in the evening or at the traditional time of 3pm to help build the atmosphere on one of the tighter grounds in the Championship, and for away games not to be at awkward times, too, so they can back the squad on the road.

However, three of QPR's home games from January to the end of February against Birmingham City, Southampton and West Ham United have all been moved to a 12:30pm kickoff on a Saturday.

Meanwhile, Julien Stephan's side will travel to Lincoln City on a Friday night in late January, which is not just a six-hour round trip with no traffic, but comes off the back of an away day in Burnley just three days earlier.

It's safe to say that R's fans have taken umbrage at the decision to move those games for TV coverage.

"How can they move Lincoln to the Friday when we play away on the Tuesday that week? Absolute farce," one fan commented in response to the decision. "3 of the best home games at 12:30 zombie hour," another said.

"Well these are awful. 3 home 1230s. Away at Lincoln Friday night after being away at Burnley on the Tuesday," was also among the replies, combining the two.

"Absolutely dreadful selections for us. 3 home games and a decent away day ruined. No football fans that I know enjoy the 12.30pm kick offs so why are they doing this to the game?" another fan asked.

And a fifth simply said, sarcastically, "Cheers Sky, thanks… The one game we didn’t want moved to a Friday night."

The movement of games against Birmingham, Southampton and West Ham, three sides currently in the top eight and who are expected to stay there come the new year, indicates that Sky Sports are hoping to televise some potential clashes between sides in the top eight.

It's a credit to how well QPR are currently doing under Julien Stephan, but it could be detrimental to their performances in those games.

As mentioned, Loftus Road can be quite a difficult place to go when the crowd are raring to go and up for the game. Swansea know how that feels, as do Derby County and, while in a draw, Preston North End, who allowed chance after chance and squandered a 2-0 lead despite having a man advantage.

In these big matchups against fellow strong-starters who have the spending power that QPR don't, the Hoops are going to have to rely on those in the stands to level the playing field and push them towards a win.

At 12:30, it can often take a little while to heat up, which may be more of a benefit to the away sides, who could take advantage of a quieter-than-usual Loftus Road.

When pairing that home lunchtime triple-header with a trip to Lincoln, the one away day that the majority of Championship mainstays were looking forward to doing, given how long it's been since the Imps were in the division, there's arguably no positive to these TV selections.

That being said, though, QPR won't be the only side aggrieved by having their schedule switched to accommodate the cameras. In fact, it's almost a given that every side has some grumbles about those timings being changed.

On the bright side, at least there isn't a 12:30 away from home. People travelling down from Birmingham or up from Southampton to Loftus Road for those meetings may have it a little harder due to the early wake-up.

QPR will at least be hoping that this decent start can be maintained for the rest of the calendar year, so the decisions to move these games for a national audience can be justified.
