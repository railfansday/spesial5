£300k Birmingham City man faces exit: Bolton Wanderers & Oxford United must react

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As the winter months roll on, it won't be long until we're looking at how clubs across the EFL can improve their squads in the January transfer window.

Birmingham City will be looking to strengthen for what they hope will be a push towards securing a top-two finish and a place in the Premier League for the first time in 16 years.

But Chris Davies' side does have some deadwood that they'll need to rid themselves of, too, none more so than Scott Wright, who really hasn't settled at Birmingham at all since making the switch from Scottish giants Rangers in the summer of 2024 for around £300,000.

The 29-year-old has made just 24 appearances in the nearly two-and-a-half years he's spent at St Andrew's and was loaned out last January back to Scotland to see out the season with Dundee FC.

With just one one-minute cameo appearance in their EFL Cup to his name this season, he'll surely be angling for more game time, and Birmingham should be in a position to grant that.

Wright's career is a peculiar one. The former youth Scotland international was on the books at Rangers for two-and-a-half years, but never during his time at Ibrox was he a certified starter.

The winger was a very solid squad player during his time with the Gers, and a move south of the border for the first time in his career to a side expected to dominate League One in 2024/25 presented a good opportunity for him to take a more important role elsewhere.

That never came to fruition at Birmingham, though. Despite scoring a late winner on his debut against Wigan Athletic, injuries stopped Wright from playing much of a part in Chris Davies' side's record-breaking campaign.

He played just 437 minutes of League One action, and a knee injury in just his third league start for the club in February 2025 not only curtailed his season, but also saw him out of the picture for the first half of last year's Championship return.

At that point, Birmingham had invested even more into the squad, and even though he did get off the bench for a few games back in January, it was always going to be a struggle to get into starting contention, especially as he wasn't while the Blues were in League One.

Wright needs a fresh start, and these two EFL sides should be on hand to give it to him.

Bolton Wanderers have had a fine start to life back in the Championship after a seven-year exile, sitting 17th with 17 points from 14 games and an away record which is amongst the best in the division.

Not many expected the Trotters to pack such a punch going forward, especially given the amount of quality they lost in those areas in the summer due to loanees returning to their parent club and the number of perceived top players they lost out on during the off-season.

Steven Schumacher has done well with the tools he's had, but it's evident that he still wants more, especially in the wide areas. Against Charlton Athletic on Tuesday night, he opted for Max Lott on the left wing, a 19-year-old they signed from Aston Villa's academy in October, for his first senior start for the club.

Some saw that as a potential cry for some reinforcements in January. Thierry Gale aside, the Trotters don't look stunning on the wings. Sam Illing-Junior hasn't excelled, and Kyliane Dong hasn't done much to nail down a starting spot.

Additionally, both of those wingers are on loan at Bolton, so they won't be theirs in the future.

If Bolton can agree on a minimal deal to bring Wright to the Toughsheet Stadium, they'll have a permanent player entering their prime and with a big point to prove that he's good enough to play regular Championship games after not getting a sniff at Birmingham.

The likelihood is, though, that if Wright does remain in the EFL in January, he may find himself dropping down to the top of League One.

If Oxford United's transfer embargo, which forbade them from investing in Aaron Ramsey's squad this past summer, is lifted by January, as the higher-ups at the Kassam Stadium are confident it will be, they should have an eye on Wright to increase their attacking depth.

The Yellows will be in for a busy winter window if the embargo is lifted. Oxford have started the campaign brilliantly under Ramsey, who has taken to his first senior managerial role very well. After their win over Reading on Wednesday, they sit third in League One, three points off the top and with a game in hand.

However, there are some worries about the lack of senior depth in the squad. Ramsey has been brilliant at utilising players from the academy, and it's led to 18-year-old Mo Missanga being one of the more exciting stars in the division.

But if they can bring in some experience out wide, that would be optimal, especially with Villa loanee Luka Lynch only at the club until January.

Wright would automatically be one of the more veteran attacking players in the Oxford side if they moved for him, and he could provide a good boost, if given the right minutes, as the Yellows look to maintain promotion form.
