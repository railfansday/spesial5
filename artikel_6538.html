The New <i><em>Werwulf</em></i> Popcorn Bucket Is Dividing Fans—And Christmas Might Be To Blame

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Santa definitely didn’t sign off on this one.

Cinemark has revealed a new popcorn bucket inspired by Werwulf, and it’s probably safe to say Santa won’t be adding this one to his present delivery. The aptly named Claw Bucket resembles an enormous gray, furry werewolf paw, complete with long, menacing claws and some particularly blood-soaked fingers. The opening where the wrist would normally be is where your popcorn goes.

The collectible is tied to Robert Eggers’ upcoming horror film Werwulf, which arrives in theaters on December 25. Starring Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Lily-Rose Depp, and Willem Dafoe, it’s set in medieval England and follows a farmer dealing with a rather unfortunate supernatural curse.

Cinemark shared the first look on Instagram, captioning its post with, “Do not dread the darkness. Our Werwulf claw popcorn bucket is COMING SOON to theaters!” And judging by the comments, some people are already prepared to make this horrifying little accessory part of their holiday plans.

“That's so ugly LMAOOO I want it,” one person wrote, capturing a sentiment I suspect plenty of popcorn bucket collectors can relate to.

“I’ll be on the hunt for this 🔥," someone else declared, while another commenter wondered what the gruesome design might reveal about the movie itself, calling it a “nice hint to what kinda werewolf we’ll be seeing.”

Of course, there’s also the small matter of the calendar. Christmas is hardly the first holiday that comes to mind when you see a furry, blood-covered monster hand, and some people aren’t thrilled about the timing.

“Ngl that’s creepy , should of came out for like Halloween or something,” one person commented. Another took considerably greater issue with the movie’s release date, writing, “Christmas is about Christ and gifts and Santa and all above not scary evil movie that belongs on December of Christmas. It should’ve been October they should’ve released it.”

I can understand expecting a little less bloodshed from your Christmas Day entertainment. Then again, for anyone who doesn’t celebrate Christmas—or whose ideal holiday involves escaping the family gathering for a horror movie—this sounds like a perfectly reasonable way to spend December 25.

The whole thing is another memorable entry in the increasingly bizarre popcorn bucket competition we’ve been following. Between the Hunger Games collectibles and AMC's Dune: Part Three sandworm buckets, movie theaters have been getting pretty creative with how they serve their snacks. A giant severed werewolf paw was the logical next step, of course.

The Claw Bucket will be available at participating Cinemark theaters while supplies last, complete with an extra-large popcorn and a free same-day refill. There’s no confirmed price or exact on-sale date yet, so anyone planning a particularly bloody holiday outing might want to keep an eye on their local concession stand.
