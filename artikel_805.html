Toney loses bid to avoid Euro 2028 court clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ivan Toney's trial will take place during the Euro 2028 tournament

A bid to move the date of England striker Ivan Toney's assault trial to avoid a clash with Euro 2028 has been refused.

The 30-year-old footballer, from Northampton, is accused of headbutting and punching Saber Da Silva during a confrontation at a nightclub in Wardour Street, central London, on 6 December.

Toney denied assault occasioning actual bodily harm at a court hearing last month and a date for a trial of up to four days was fixed for 12 June 2028, which is three days into the Uefa Euro 2028 tournament.

He represented England at this summer's World Cup and has made 10 international appearances. It is not yet known if he will be selected for Euro 2028, but the application was made in case.

At a hearing at Southwark Crown Court on Wednesday, where Toney did not appear, his barrister Mozammel Hossain KC said: "It came to our attention thatâ¦ there may be potentially a football match called the European Championship in 2028 between 9 June and 9 July."

Hossain proposed a trial date in August that year, adding: "We ask you please that the matter be put back to then for a trial so that if the defendant is included in this football tournament he can play.

"My knowledge of football is very limited but I am told he was included in the last one in 2024 and the England squad for the World Cup."

Judge Tony Baumgartner called it a "speculative" application.

He refused the request, saying: "It does not seem to me to be in the interest of justice to disturb the date that was already fixed."

The judge said the application could be made again if Toney is selected for the England squad.

Toney missed out on the latest squad for the country's Nations League ties.

Toney started his football career with Northampton Town and played for teams including Wigan Athletic and Peterborough United before signing for Brentford in 2020.

He scored 72 goals during four years with the Bees before making a surprise switch in 2024 to Saudi Pro League club Al-Ahli.
