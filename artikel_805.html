For the runner or fitness enthusiast who never wants to be without their music, the Shokz OpenRun Pro 2 offers an alternative to traditional earbuds. Available in five colors, its open-ear design combines bone and air conduction technology for balanced audio while allowing wearers to remain aware of their surroundings. With up to 12 hours of battery life, a secure fit and water resistance, it’s designed for active lifestyles. Best Buy’s November promotion offers savings of up to 30% on select Shokz headphones.

Not everyone enjoys having earbuds tucked inside their ears, which makes the Shokz OpenDots 2 an interesting alternative. These lightweight, clip-on headphones feature an open-ear design that lets users enjoy music while staying connected to their surroundings. Dolby Audio, customizable sound settings and up to 40 hours of total listening time add to their appeal. Available in four colors, they’re a thoughtful pick for commuters, walkers and anyone who’s always listening to music, audiobooks or podcasts but still values comfort.

Home security might not be the first thing that comes to mind when planning Christmas gifts, but it’s a practical upgrade that could make a meaningful difference for someone you care about. Ring’s Battery Doorbell 2K offers a rechargeable design, 2K video and motion notifications delivered directly to a smartphone. Live view and two-way talk also let homeowners communicate with visitors remotely. For someone who has been meaning to improve their home security, this is a useful gift that offers added peace of mind for you and your recipient.

For homeowners looking to keep a closer eye on their property, the Ring Outdoor Cam Pro brings more advanced monitoring capabilities to outdoor spaces. Its Retinal 4K video, 10x zoom and low-light technology are designed to capture details even when lighting conditions are less than ideal. Radar-powered 3D Motion Detection also sends activity alerts to a phone. Available in black or white, it’s a thoughtful investment in everyday security for someone who might not think to purchase one for themselves, not to mention that it’s known for its easy, DIY installation.

For the person already thinking about their fitness goals for the new year, the Google Fitbit Edge is a timely holiday surprise. The advanced tracker features a colored display, built-in GPS and personalized fitness coaching powered by Gemini. Users can monitor workout performance, heart rate and sleep patterns while receiving guidance tailored to their routines. With up to a week of battery life and quick charging, it’s designed to make keeping track of everyday wellness a little easier.

For active people who want health insights without another screen competing for their attention, the Google Fitbit Air stands out as a different kind of gadget. This screen-free tracker quietly monitors your activity, sleep and recovery through built-in sensors, with all your insights available in the Google Health app on your phone. Its lightweight, adjustable design is comfortable enough to wear around the clock. You also get personalized coaching powered by Gemini, up to a week of battery life and a tracker that lets you focus on moving more and scrolling less.

From personal audio to home security and fitness technology, this year’s tech gifts offer something for different interests and everyday needs. Best Buy is bringing several leading brands together in one place, making it easier to tackle multiple names on your Christmas list. Shoppers can also take advantage of November deals running Nov. 15 through Nov. 28 and the shipping benefits and rewards that come with a My Best Buy Plus membership.
