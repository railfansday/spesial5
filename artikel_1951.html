Duncan Ferguson has been making headlines.

The former Newcastle United and Everton player took on his latest manager’s job five weeks ago.

Duncan Ferguson becoming manager at Glentoran Football Club on 19 October 2026.

It hasn’t been a stellar start with his new club, with five points from his opening four league matches.

The latest match was a 3-2 defeat to Cliftonville.

A result that the normally shy and retiring Duncan Ferguson didn’t take lying down.

Launching a tirade against the match officials, the former Newcastle United player has now been hit with a seven match suspension.

His club isn’t contesting the ban, an official statement declaring: ‘Glentoran Football Club confirms that First Team Manager Duncan Ferguson has been handed a seven-match suspension following his dismissal during Friday night’s Irish Premiership fixture against Cliftonville at Solitude. The Club accepts the sanction, which takes effect from tonight’s BetMcLean Cup tie against Larne at The BetMcLean Oval.’

As for Duncan Ferguson himself: “I want to apologise to the match officials for my behaviour on Friday night. I was frustrated by what happened on the pitch and let my emotions get the better of me. I went too far in the way I expressed that frustration towards the officials, and I should have handled the situation better.”

The one-time Newcastle United striker not contesting the ban either: “I accept the suspension and I’ll learn from what happened. My focus now is on the players, the staff and the work we’ve got to do together, both on and off the pitch.”
