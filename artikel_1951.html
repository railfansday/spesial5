What is the latest Carlos Baleba and Marcus Rashford injury update?

Marcus Rashford has not featured since United’s Champions League meeting with Atletico Madrid on October 3. His absence has left Pochettino waiting to work with the forward since taking charge.

Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino has provided an encouraging update on Marcus Rashford, revealing that the forward has started training on the pitch as he works towards a return.

Speaking ahead of United’s upcoming fixture, Pochettino was asked whether he was looking forward to working with Rashford once he returns.

“Yes, I’m looking forward to work with all the players that are injured. Hope that they can recover as soon as possible. I think to have the full squad fit and available to be selected, that is the most important thing for every single coaching staff, no?” he replied.

Pochettino also provided an update on summer signing Carlos Baleba, who picked up an injury during United’s Champions League comeback victory over Roma at the start of November.

The midfielder faces a longer spell out, with the medical staff initially estimating that his recovery could take between 10 and 12 weeks.

“Baleba, the moment was Roma, [when he got] injured, and the medical staff were talking about 10, 12 weeks,” Pochettino explained.

There was more positive news on Rashford, who has now started working on the pitch as he gradually increases his workload.

“Marcus, today I think, started being in the session on the field. I think he is starting to feel much better,” the United boss added.

Rashford’s return would give United another attacking option, as Pochettino still waits for his first win the united colours.

Manchester United play Brentford next on Saturday.
