York City boss Scott Lindsey ‘taking encouragement’ from goalless draw with Lee Grant’s Walsall

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

York City manager Scott Lindsey “took encouragement” from his side’s goalless draw with Walsall.

The Minstermen could not make a breakthrough against the Saddlers, who sit just one point behind the North Yorkshire outfit in the League Two table.

It is not often York are kept at bay in the final third, but Lindsey did not read the riot act.

He said: “It was a really good game and as good as a 0-0 draw can be. We maybe deserved a bit more than a point, but there weren’t too many chances in the game.

“We had goalscorers all over the pitch by the end of the match and it’s not like us not to score, but we tried as hard as we could and I think the players carried out the game plan really well on the ball.

“Alex (Hunt) had a big chance in the first half, but there weren’t too many chances in the game and you have to give the opposition credit as well.

“I am still taking encouragement from the performance, as I believe whoever finishes above Walsall this season will be promoted and I’m also delighted with the way we defended.

“That’s our third clean sheet in a row (excluding the Jersey Mike’s Trophy) and we’ve put a lot of work into improving that side of our game.”

Walsall boss Lee Grant applauded his 10-man team’s “great character” after they became the first league opponent to keep a clean sheet at York since Solihull Moors in September 2025.

The Saddlers held out for a 0-0 draw despite losing Courtney Clarke to injury in the 81st minute, having made all their permitted substitutions.

Grant said: “Going down to 10 men for the last 15 minutes, I’m proud of the great character we showed and it’s nice to be able to not concede a goal despite those circumstances at a place where so many teams have found that hard.

“There’s a reason why they have the ball more than anybody else in this division and score lots of goals and that’s because they have good technical players and are well coached.

“But, through the course of a season, there are so many parts of your game that you have to be able to demonstrate to continue performing well and I think we are showing all of those sides and I thought both teams defended their goals well.

“We maybe had enough of the game to warrant better chances than we created at times but, in the first half especially, we showed how we can control matches.”
