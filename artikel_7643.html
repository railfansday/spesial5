SCOTUS grants asylum ban case

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

Justice Pierce Butler, who served on the Supreme Court from 1923 to 1939, died on this day in 1939. Butler was the first Minnesotan appointed to the Supreme Court and the only dissenter in the infamous case of Buck v. Bell (though he did not write anything).

On Friday, the justices met in a private conference to discuss cases and vote on petitions for review. That afternoon, they announced that they had added two cases to this term’s oral argument docket: Mullin v. Refugee and Immigrant Center for Education and Legal Services, on whether the Trump administration can suspend access to asylum for immigrants at the U.S.-Mexico border; and Robinhood Markets, Inc. v. Sodha, a dispute over whether a financial-services company violated federal securities laws when it failed to disclose some interim financial data as part of its initial public offering. For more on these cases, see the On Site section below.

Additional orders from Friday’s conference are expected this morning at 9:30 a.m. EST.

The December session of oral arguments will begin on Nov. 30.
