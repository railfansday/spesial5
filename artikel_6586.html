How to Style Straight-Leg Jeans This Winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

All denim is timeless, but straight-leg jeans might be the most trend-resistant silhouette of the bunch. That said, they’re trending! The classic style has been making a comeback (or perhaps it never really went away), cropping up in the fall collections of Dior, Stella McCartney, Toteme, and Balenciaga. Amid the revolving door of denim trends, from ultra-baggy shapes to the much-debated return of the skinny jean, there’s something about the simplicity of a straight leg that feels very much like an aesthetic homecoming.

With their clean lines and easy proportions, straight-leg jeans are a true wardrobe chameleon. They’re a key part of the model off-duty look when worn with a white tee, leather jacket, and retro sneakers, but they also look chic dressed up with a pump or heeled boot. Their streamlined silhouette lends itself well to winter layering too, balancing out both oversized knits and heavy coats. And while the shape is relatively straightforward, there’s plenty of room for interpretation: a faded blue pair can feel laid back, while a dark-wash style can look almost as polished as trousers; a bone-colored pair works a palette cleanser, while something warm and brown brings softness to the look.

Below, a few of our favorite styles—with ways to wear them this season.

A pair of dark-wash straight-leg jeans can look just as polished as a pair of trousers. Lean into the sophisticated vibe with a pussy-bow blouse, a sharp leather jacket, and a pair of sleek, kitten-heel boots.

Play up the Americana roots of classic blue jeans with a plaid flannel shirt and a chocolate-brown suede jacket. A pair of supple leather loafers and Balenciaga’s City bag keep the look from feeling too rugged.

Take a cue from the model off-duty set and style your straight-leg jeans with a zip-up hoodie and a pair of retro Nikes.

A washed-brown pair of jeans makes a surprisingly versatile foundation for colorful separates - like an argyle knit and a bright-red suede jacket. Keep the look grounded with black leather accessories.

Pale blue jeans pair particularly well with various shades of brown. A belted, fur-collared suede jacket and matching ankle boots lend the look a subtle ’70s feel.

The streamlined silhouette of a straight-leg jean works well with voluminous outerwear, like a plaid, fringed-trimmed scarf jacket. A pair of ivory ballet flats worn with tonal socks doesn’t detract from the action up top.

Style slightly more relaxed straight-legs with a collared cashmere cardigan, a lived-in leather jacket, and a pair of suede moccasins for a look that strikes a balance between polished and nonchalant.

LOOK 8: Black straight-leg jeans are a winter wardrobe workhorse. A navy crewneck layered over a classic white tee and a belted wool coat make for understated extras.
