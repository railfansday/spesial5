School Administrator Charged With Sex Crimes Involving Children Is Fired

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Authorities are looking for possible victims of a New York school administrator and longtime youth leader who’s charged with sex crimes involving children.

John Henry Griffiths, of Yorktown, who most recently worked as interim assistant principal at Grady Elementary and Dixson Primary Schools in Elmsford, was arrested Monday, November 16, according to the Westchester County District Attorney’s Office.

He’s charged with promoting a sexual performance by a child younger than 17 and possessing a sexual performance by a child less than 16, the district attorney’s office said in a news release.

“John Henry Griffiths has spent [the] last 30 years working in education and volunteering with local youth organizations, affording him access to children throughout Westchester County,” Westchester County District Attorney Susan Cacace said in a Tuesday, November 17, statement. “We encourage anyone who may have been victimized or who may have information about other potential victims to contact my office.”

Since 1996, Griffiths has worked at schools throughout Westchester County, including Croton-on-Hudson, Somers, Pelham and Garrison in addition to Elmsford.

He previously served as the principal at the Garrison School, attended by students in kindergarten through eighth grade, and as the assistant superintendent of the Croton-Harmon Union Free School District, per The Journal News.

Following a tip from the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, state troopers executed a search warrant at Griffiths’ home and seized his digital devices, according to New York State Police.

On Friday, November 13, Griffiths, 62, was found to have child sexual abuse material of a girl estimated to be between 6 to 8 years old, the district attorney’s office said. He also allegedly was found to have shared child sexual abuse material of a boy around 11 to 13 years old.

That day, Griffiths was fired from his position as interim assistant principal at Grady Elementary and Dixson Primary schools in Elmsford, according to the office.

Griffiths is involved in Boy Scouts of America, which has been rebranded to Scouting America, the district attorney’s office said. He’s been the scoutmaster of a local troop in Yorktown Heights since 2013.

Griffiths also serves as the minister of music at St. Mary’s Church in Mount Vernon, according to the church’s website. He’s held the position since 2008, according to authorities.

Scouting America and the church didn’t immediately return requests for comment from Us.

Elmsford Union Free School District Superintendent Susan Yom said in a statement that the district learned of the investigation into Griffiths on Friday and fired him, according to News 12 Hudson Valley.

“The District takes its responsibility to protect students and staff extremely seriously,” Yom said. “Our commitment to the safety and well-being of our school community is unwavering, and we will continue to take appropriate measures to protect our students, families, and staff.”

Following an arraignment, Griffiths was detained at the Westchester County Jail, according to the district attorney’s office.

It’s not immediately clear whether he retained legal counsel or entered a plea.

Griffiths is scheduled to return to court on November 24.
