GTA 6 Has A Secret Mission That Feels Like Hard Target Meets Saw

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In the mood for a little bloodsport? In GTA 6, you can teach one loud-mouthed hunter that Lucia (or Jason) is the deadliest game, as this secret mission will require cunning and sharp shooting to survive. It's Hard Target meets SAW, and the victor gets a pretty sizable stack of cash. Here's how you can be the most elusive of quarry.

Go to Hyde Island

Located north of Lummox County and the Moreau River, Hyde Island is where you can start this mission. All you have to do is land on the island, and after a few steps, a dart will hit you and knock you out.

Listen to your captor's speech

Now for the boring part. You'll wake up inside of a shipping container, with a fashionable explosive necklace locked in place. Once your captor finishes his rant, the game begins, and you'll want to move.

Run to the tent directly in front of you

You'll have 10 seconds to reach a tent in front of you, where you'll find a gun and a paltry two rounds for the sidearm. After the count is up, a hunter on a quad bike will show up, and he'll be gunning for you. To make things worse, he also has a dog with him that'll track you throughout the island. Don't engage him; he's heavily armored, and two bullets aren't nearly enough to stop him—besides, you'll need those rounds for his dog, Caesar. Sorry, animal lovers.

Search for more outposts

You can find more ammo, adrenaline, and a shotgun at nearby outposts. Each one is lit up, so be fast, be sneaky, and try not to get caught. The hunter is going to be tracking you constantly, even without his dog, and he has a sniper rifle that can take you out in just a few shots if he catches you.

Prepare for a showdown

Get as much ammo as you can, and choose your spot for the last stand. The hunter's dog will be a pest, so deal with it first. It's easy to spot, with a red light on its collar. Trees also make for good cover against the hunter, so once he's in range, start nailing him with shots to critical areas. Once he stumbles, unload a few shotgun rounds and finish him off.

Collect your rewards

Once the hunter is down, search his body for the Island-Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle and ammo for it. Head west to the boathouse to reclaim your personal items and $15,000 in cash. Not a bad haul for a deadly night's work.

What happens if you fail?

If the hunter does kill you, you'll lose a chunk of cash and wake up near one of your safehouses. The good news is you can retry the mission by heading back to Hyde Island, but the bad news is you'll have to listen to the hunter's speech again.
