AI May Be Scanning Your Private Signal Chats

If you use Signal, it's likely you care at least a little bit about keeping your communication private so only you and those you're chatting with can see your message threads. Signal's end-to-end encryption keeps prying eyes out, but if you use agentic AI on your device, your chats may not be as locked down as you think.

Signal has already blocked screenshot capabilities for Microsoft's Recall AI, which by default was able to take and store screen grabs of Signal messages on Windows 11. While Signal cannot guarantee that AI won't have access to your data, there are a few protections now available for those on iOS and Android as well.

The main workaround in Signal for now is to restrict screenshots and screen recordings inside the app, though this does not prevent AI agents from potentially grabbing content from other parts of your device. According to Signal's support documents, these protections will now be enabled by default on iOS 27 and Android with the latest versions of the Signal app.
