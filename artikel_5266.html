Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had an intimate relationship with another Nvidia employee, Reuters reported on Thursday, citing two people familiar with the matter. It would be the third relationship linked to Huang to come to light, after his reported affairs with Alice Huang, whom US intelligence suspects of spying for China, and Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of healthcare. Reuters did not name the employee and said it could not reach her for comment. Nvidia declined to comment, and neither Huang, Powell nor a representative for Nvidia’s board responded to requests. Huang resigned as Nvidia’s CEO, president and board member on Thursday, four days after admitting to an affair in a statement that did not name the woman involved. He is also expected to testify before Congress before Thanksgiving.
