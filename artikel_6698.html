The 6 Best Places to Find Black Friday Handbag Deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Tory Burch, Balenciaga, and Chloé are up to 40 percent off.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

You don’t have to hold out for Cyber Monday to shop for a new handbag—Black Friday fashion sales are worth your time today. Retailers like Amazon, Nordstrom, and Revolve marked down dozens of styles from popular labels like Tory Burch and Mansur Gavriel, to name a few. And with discounts up to 60 percent, many of these designer finds will sell out before the weekend is over, so we don’t recommend waiting to shop.

To help you navigate an overwhelming number of sales, we’ve shortlisted the six best sites for early Black Friday handbag deals. Whether you’re looking to upgrade your everyday work tote, pick up a new party bag before the holidays, or check a few stylish people off your gifting list, these online destinations are worth your time.

Everyday bags from entry-level designers like Marc Jacobs, Kate Spade, and Michael Kors are already deeply discounted on Amazon. Plus, the affordable, celeb-loved label JW Pei is offering 15 percent off an assortment of styles, including Anne Hathaway’s Lucia bag and Selena Gomez’s Delphine bag.

Nordstrom rolled out hundreds of Black Friday deals, including handbags from in-demand brands like Tory Burch, Reformation, and AllSaints. Warning: Stock of discounted designer items is already running low, so don’t leave that Balenciaga bag lingering in your cart for long.

Those looking to invest in a designer bag should start their search at Mytheresa, where big names like Chloé and Valentino Garavani are currently up to 40 percent off. On sale for under $300, Taylor Swift’s Vivienne Westwood Granny Frame bag makes a fashionable gift for Swifties.

Revolve is where cool girls shop for IYKYK labels like Verafied, By Far, and Margesherwood—all of which are included in the retailer’s Black Friday markdowns. Neutral-colored shoulder bags and totes are going fast, and we don’t expect prices to drop further on Cyber Monday.

You can count on Saks Fifth Avenue to have great handbag deals, no matter the time of year. This Black Friday, totes and shoulder bags from Veronica Beard, Staud, Vince, and Polo Ralph Lauren are 30 to 60 percent off. Keep an eye out for short-lived flash sales and promo codes that offer extra discounts on existing markdowns—the department store is famous for them.

Fashion insiders flock to Net-a-Porter for its rare handbag deals from sought-after designers. Invest wisely, and you can easily save over $1,000 on Alaïa, Toteme, and other luxury labels. Often, only one or two of each style are in stock, so you’ll need to finalize your cart fast to avoid missing out. Use the code CYBER20 for an additional 20 percent off your purchase.
