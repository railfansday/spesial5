ClearSign: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Gulf Coast field installation is the reference refiners want to see before committing capital, and it is still ahead of ClearSign.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 27, 2026 at 8:36AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, ClearSign (CLIR -3.45%) announced a gross margin expansion to 55.6%, up from 35.8% in the prior-year period. The company relies on an asset-light strategy that focuses on intellectual property (IP), or proprietary technology designs, rather than high-cost manufacturing. Three things management said stand out.

ClearSign expanded gross margins by granting fabrication rights to an original equipment manufacturer (OEM) that builds equipment under license, enabling the business to earn licensing fees without incurring hardware assembly costs.

Refineries require field data from Continuous Emissions Monitoring Systems (CEMS), which stream stack measurements to regulators, before committing capital to replace legacy pollution control equipment.

ClearSign standardizes engineering designs for midstream gas-processing burners, which cut turnaround times and enable repeatable volume across West Texas gas fields.

Field deployments at major oil facilities are advancing, shifting focus from initial development to commercial execution. Management says computer modeling gives ClearSign a design edge by bypassing physical testing cycles, while management watches project execution and proposal conversion.

Scaling through verified site references remains the central path to reaching break-even volume, which management has pegged at about 160 burners a year
