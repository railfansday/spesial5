Loads of limited-time Black Friday-related streaming sales are happening right now, but one new discount that’s here to stay is DIRECTV’s brand-new streaming bundle that rolls Netflix, HBO Max and Apple TV all into one plan that can be added to almost any of their subscription packages.

This week, DirecTV rolled out a bundle that combines Netflix, HBO Max and Apple TV at a discount while aiming to help streamline billing in one place. Customers with any DirecTV Signature or Genre Pack can add this bundle to their plan, with two notable exceptions: the MyEntertainment genre plan or Premier package are not eligible, as both plans already include HBO Max.

There are two tiers to choose from with the new bundles: a Value plan, which runs $28 per month (in addition to any monthly live TV subscription fees) and carries Netflix Standard with Ads, HBO Max’s ad-based plan and Apple TV, or the Deluxe plan, an ad-free option that costs $43.50 a month and includes Netflix Standard, HBO Max Standard and Apple TV. Either bundle can be added to or canceled from your existing plan at any time.

Right now, if you want to watch all the best shows, live sports and news programs, they’re scattered across multiple services, and this bundle offers you three of the most robust services out there, while skirting their latest price increases.
