ScottsMiracle-Gro CEO on Earnings Beat, Growth Outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

ScottsMiracle-Gro shares rose about 10% after its earnings report, with investors focusing on guidance that exceeded expectations. Nate Baxter, the company’s President and CEO, says fiscal 2026 revenue grew about 1%, partly because the company exited lower-margin commodity products and changed its product mix. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Vonnie Quinn on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
