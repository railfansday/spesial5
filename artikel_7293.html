Ticker: Willie Geist to Depart MS NOW, Committing to NBC News Full-time

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Top of the Ticker: After a nearly 20-year run, Willie Geist will be departing MS NOW for an expanded role at NBC News. Geist will continue to focus his efforts on Sunday Morning and broaden his presence across Today and other NBC News platforms.

In announcing his departure on Monday, during Morning Joe, Geist said, “I am so grateful to both NBC and MSNOW for the unique opportunity to do what I love at both networks. He added, “This is a bittersweet goodbye to Joe, Mika, and the place that made possible everything else in my career over the last two decades, but I am thrilled to settle in with my family at Today and dedicate all of my time to that special place.”

Geist was the only remaining on-air talent still pulling double duty for MS NOW and NBC News after NBCUniversal spun off its cable network assets into a new media company, Versant, in 2025.

Continued independence: Skydance CEO David Ellison, during The New York Times’ DealBook Summit held on Wednesday, reiterated that its two news outlets, CBS News and CNN, will continue to operate as separate entities and be editorially independent. He did say there might be “some news sharing in the future,” but was happy with how they are currently positioned.

Distribution agreement: The free streaming Newsmax channel, Newsmax2, will now be available over the air across 112 television stations owned by HC2 Brodcasting. This widens Newsmax2’s footprint, as it will now be available in all of the top 40 U.S. television markets and 57 of the top 60.

Milestone unlocked: ABC News’ Sunday public affairs program This Week celebrated its 45th anniversary on Sunday, November 15. To toast the occasion ABC News hosted a party on November 12 at the Jefferson Hotel in Washington, D.C., which featured remarks from ABC News president Almin Karamehmedovic, anchor George Stephanopoulos, and co-anchors Martha Raddatz and Jonathan Karl.

Highest honor: Meanwhile, Martha Raddatz received the National Press Club’s Fourth Estate Award at a gala on Nov. 12 in Washington, D.C. Present to witness this occasion were Raddatz’s ABC News colleagues, including Mary Bruce, Rachel Scott, Mark Burstein, and daughter Greta Williams.

Paging Dr. Gupta: MS NOW now has its own doctor named Gupta, as the news network announced a new exclusive series for its Membership platform, On Call with Dr. Vin Gupta, where he will explain medical stories, conduct interviews, and take questions from MS NOW Members. Rival CNN has its own Dr. Gupta, of course, chief medical correspondent Sanjay Gupta.

Revolving Door: CNN has announced that Shery Ahn will join CNN en Español as an anchor and correspondent beginning Jan. 4, 2027. Based in New York City, Ahn, formerly the anchor and editor-at-large at Bloomberg Television in Tokyo, Japan, will strengthen and expand CNN’s business and economic coverage while contributing to national and international reporting across CNN en Español and the wider network.

Ali Bauman is joining CNBC as a general assignment reporter beginning November 30. Bauman was previously at CBS News, where she was a New York-based reporter and served as a Northeast correspondent for CBS Saturday Morning and the Saturday edition of the CBS Evening News. Meanwhile, Kylie Cohu joins CNBC as a senior equity analyst and correspondent on Dec. 7, reporting to Christina Cheddar-Berk, senior editor of stock analysis, and Katie Tarasov has been promoted to technology reporter, reporting to technology editor Megan Morrone.

In addition, CNBC announced a slew of appointments to its editorial team, including Francesca Levy who joins as director of podcasts and digital shows on Dec. 7, reporting to Matt Quayle, senior executive producer of program development. Gary Cotton joins as a homepage editor today, and Penelope Patsuris joins as a weekend editor starting on Nov. 30, both were previously with CNN, and will report to deputy managing editor, Elisabeth Cordova. The network also announced that in September it hired Marshall Ritzel as a senior data visualization editor reporting to Kamelia Angelova, executive producer of digital video and audience development.

Court TV announced that Ted Rowlands has been promoted to executive editor, and will continue his role as an on-air host. He joined Court TV in 2019.
