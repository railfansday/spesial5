Black Friday is becoming as well known for its annual 3D printer deals as it is for TV deals and laptop deals. Elegoo’s Black Friday sale is already live, with deals of up to 54 percent off 3D printers, including deep discounts on models that proved to be favorites during our testing.

Looking for the best 3D printers of all brands, sale or no sale? Check out our guide to the Best 3D Printers we tested. And don’t forget to check out our just-updated guide to Everything You Need to Set Up a 3D Printing Lab at Home.

We crowned the original Elegoo Centauri Carbon the best value among 3D printers after putting it through its paces, praising its fully enclosed design, budget price point, and fast, accurate printing. We reviewed Centauri Carbon 2 with up to four filaments straight out of the box. A plastic dome enclosure covering the PTFE tubes also reduces noise and fumes during operation. Centauri Carbon 2 is more affordable for printing parts in volume than a resin printer, although its level of fine detail isn’t quite as good.

Resin 3D printers like the Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra, which use a cure liquid resin, are better suited than FDM 3D printers for creating extremely finely detailed parts. That’s why we crowned the Mars 5 Ultra the best 3D printer for making miniatures. Ease of use was the theme here. We found navigating the settings on its 6.6-inch 4K LCD screen streamlined and intuitive, and its tilt-release bed broke the suction of printed parts, making them easy to remove once a print job finished. This bundle includes the Mercury Plus V2.0 wash and cure station, which washes freshly printed parts in water to remove dust and particulates left over from the printing process.

The Saturn 4 Ultra 16K resin 3D printer is larger and offers higher batch volumes than the Mars 5 Ultra. With the right resin, its level of detail approaches that of professionally manufactured miniatures. Although it and the Mars 5 Ultra both have a maximum printing speed of 150 mm/hour, the physically larger Saturn 4 Ultra 16K has more room to print larger objects. It also has a heated vat that reaches up to 86 degrees Fahrenheit and keeps the resin at a steady temperature, improving consistency and reducing the chance of poor layer adhesion.

With a 420 x 420 x 480 mm printing platform, the Neptune 4 Max has one of the largest platforms available in a mid-priced 3D printer. If you routinely print large parts, such as cosplay props or other large objects that need to be created as a single piece, the Neptune 4 Max is one of the most affordable ways to do so. As an FDM (fused deposition modeling) printer, it’s cheaper than a resin 3D printer when all other aspects are equal, which matters especially when you’re printing large objects.

Elegoo’s Jupiter 2 combines an enormous 14.7-liter build chamber with the fine detail afforded by a resin printer, a heated resin vat that reaches up to 86 degrees Fahrenheit, and a 14-inch 16K LCD. The Jupiter 2 can print both large objects and large volumes of very small, finely detailed objects. The trade-off is that it’s a larger machine than more affordable 3D printers and takes up more room in your home 3D printing lab.
