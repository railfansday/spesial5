If you are a frequent American Airlines flyer, you have plenty of credit card options to help you earn additional miles and make the most of every American Airlines flight.

For a limited time, new Citi® / AAdvantage Business™ World Elite Mastercard® (see rates and fees) cardholders can earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first five months from account opening. This matches the highest offer we've seen on the card.

There have also been some recent changes to Citi's American Airlines credit card lineup. The premium Citi® / AAdvantage® Executive World Legend Mastercard® (see rates and fees) recently added a new dining credit. Plus, Citi and American Airlines have also announced upcoming changes to two other cobranded cards.

Here’s a rundown of the top Citi / AAdvantage credit cards and their current offers. Holding one of these cards could be a straightforward way to score a business-class ticket on American or one of its Oneworld partners.

*The offer value is based on TPG's November 2026 valuations.

**Annual fee will increase from $99 to $150 starting Jan. 24, 2027

The information for the Citi / AAdvantage MileUp card has been collected independently by The Points Guy. The card details on this page have not been reviewed or provided by the card issuer.

The Citi / AA Executive is American’s top-tier personal card.

Best for: Those who want Admirals Club access

Welcome bonus: Earn 80,000 bonus miles after spending $8,000 on purchases within the first three months of account opening.

According to TPG's valuations, the welcome bonus is worth $1,160. We recommend waiting until at least 100,000 miles are on offer.

This card is best suited to frequent American Airlines flyers who can take advantage of its Admirals Club membership, statement credits and elite-status benefits.

At $695, the Citi / AAdvantage Executive has a much higher annual fee than other options but comes with a notable benefit for frequent flyers: Admirals Club access for you and up to two guests traveling with you (when you have a same-day boarding pass for an eligible flight).

†A qualifying stay is one of two or more consecutive nights booked directly through Omni Hotels & Resorts on omnihotels.com. Enrollment in the Champion Status benefit is required.

The card also offers travel protections and the ability to earn up to 40,000 bonus Loyalty Points per elite qualification period, making it particularly useful for those pursuing American elite status.

To learn more, read our full review of the Citi / AA Executive.

Apply here: Citi / AAdvantage Executive World Legend Mastercard

The Citi / AA Business is Citi’s business card for American Airlines loyalists.

Welcome bonus: For a limited time, earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first five months from account opening.

According to TPG’s valuations, the welcome bonus is worth $1,088. This matches the highest offer we've seen in the card's history, so now is a great time to apply if you are interested.

Annual fee: $0 introductory annual fee for the first 12 months, then $150 thereafter (increases from $99 on Jan. 24, 2027)

With this card, you (and up to four companions) get a free first checked bag when flying a domestic American Airlines itinerary on the same reservation.

If you spend $30,000 with the card in a cardholder year and renew your card, you’ll earn a companion certificate, which allows you to take a companion on a flight for $99 (plus taxes and fees).

The certificate can only be used for a round-trip, domestic itinerary (i.e., one to and from the 48 contiguous states, unless you live in Alaska or Hawaii) in the Main Cabin. The certificate is granted upon account renewal, and the account must remain open for a full billing cycle after the anniversary date.

To learn more, read our full review of the Citi / AA Business Mastercard.

Apply here: Citi / AAdvantage Business World Elite Mastercard

The Citi / AA Globe is best suited for those who fly often but not enough to justify having the premium card.

Best for: Semifrequent American Airlines flyers

Welcome bonus: Earn 60,000 bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

According to TPG’s valuations, the welcome bonus is worth $870. This is the standard offer on the card, so we recommend waiting to apply.

The Globe's standout perks include four Admirals Club passes each calendar year and a 5,000 "Flight Streak" Loyalty Point boost after four eligible American Airlines flights. You can do this up to three times per year for a total of 15,000 Loyalty Points.

The card also provides an annual companion certificate each cardholder year (starting in your second cardholder year), allowing you to pay $99 (plus taxes and fees) for a round-trip domestic Main Cabin ticket each year. Plus, you'll receive the first checked bag free (for the cardholder and up to eight companions on the same reservation; on domestic flights), preferred boarding and a Splurge credit.

It's a decent option for those who can take advantage of the card's companion certificate, lounge access and AAdvantage-related perks in exchange for a more modest $350 annual fee.

To learn more, read our full review of the Citi / AA Globe.

Apply here: Citi / AAdvantage Globe Mastercard

The Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select is a solid option for American Airlines flyers who want basic airline perks without paying for a more premium card.

Best for: Casual American Airlines flyers

Welcome bonus: Earn 50,000 bonus miles after spending $2,500 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

According to TPG’s valuations, the welcome bonus is worth $725. This is the card's standard offer, so we'd recommend waiting for a better bonus if you're not in a rush to apply.

Annual fee: $0 introductory annual fee for the first 12 months, then $150 thereafter (increases from $99 on Jan. 24, 2027)

*Earning rate for these purchases starting Jan. 24, 2027

If you fly American Airlines often enough to want an upgraded flight experience but aren't interested in Admirals Club access, this is a solid card option. It’s affordable and easy to keep in your wallet.

You’ll receive benefits like preferred boarding, a free first checked bag (for you and up to four companions on domestic American Airlines itineraries), and discounts on inflight snacks and beverage purchases.

As a cardholder, you’ll also earn 1 Loyalty Point for every eligible AAdvantage mile earned from purchases, and this card charges no foreign transaction fees.

Another great benefit of this card is the ability to earn a $150 American Airlines flight discount (after spending $20,000 or more on purchases during your card membership year and renewing your card; increasing from $125 starting Jan. 24, 2027).

To learn more, read our full review of the Citi / AA Platinum Select.

Apply here: Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select World Elite Mastercard

The Citi / AA MileUp is Citi’s entry-level American Airlines product.

Best for: Beginners looking to earn American Airlines miles

Welcome bonus: Earn 15,000 bonus miles and a $100 statement credit after spending $500 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

According to TPG’s valuations, this welcome bonus is worth $318 (including the statement credit). The statement credit is a sweetener on top of the standard amount of miles, making now a good time to apply.

If you’re a beginner looking for an inexpensive way to earn American miles, this could be a good entry point into the Citi AAdvantage credit card lineup.

As a no-annual-fee card, it offers few perks, but you will get 25% savings on inflight drinks and snacks.

However, anyone taking more than a couple of American Airlines flights per year will likely be better off with an American Airlines card that charges an annual fee, since those cards offer higher earning rates (in select categories) and more valuable perks.

To learn more, read our full review of the Citi / AA MileUp.

Citi doesn’t limit the total number of credit cards you can hold, but it does cap your overall credit line across accounts.

You can only apply for one card every eight days, no more than two cards in 65 days and just one business card every 90 days.

Finally, Citi’s 48-month rule means you can’t earn a welcome bonus on the same AAdvantage card if you’ve received that bonus within the past four years.

Whether you’re after entry-level earning, mid-tier perks or premium Admirals Club access, there’s a Citi / AAdvantage card for a range of American Airlines flyers.
