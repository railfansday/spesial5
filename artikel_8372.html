This likely does not apply to you but since you asked and it may apply to others, I will mention this. I am working f/t and trying to save as much money for retirement as possible. I've always been interested in consulting and side hustles. I have multiple websites....most of which nobody looks at. I have an LLC.....which usually makes no money. This year I figured out a way to easily monetize a side hustle and the best part, is that I opened a simple IRA and am now putting money into that (from the side hustle). This gives me tremendous satisfaction, despite not being a lot of money. (fyi: The maximum contribution to a simple IRA is about 18K annually.)

Good luck OP. You can do this.
For OP a simple IRA may be a decent choice. However, since you also have a full time job it seems a poor choice since the contributions to a simple IRA limit your 401k contributions at your main job. I'd look at a SEP IRA or a solo 401k.
