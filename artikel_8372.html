Top 10 Resorts in the East for Dining (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Umbrella Bar (Photo: Courtesy Killington)

If you’re the kind of skier who likes to squeeze every last run out of the day, a proper lunch break might feel like lost time. After all, you can always shove a granola bar or a squished sandwich in your pocket and keep moving. But why settle for that when there’s actually good food to be had? These 10 Eastern resorts earned the highest dining marks from SKI readers, thanks to food scenes that deliver on both taste and value—whether you’re grabbing lunch between laps or sitting down after a day on the slopes.

In other words, save the pocket sandwich for the drive home. You’re going to want to stop and eat.
