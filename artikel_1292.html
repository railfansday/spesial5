Official: Atalanta hand new contract extension to Bernasconi on 23rd birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Lorenzo Bernasconi is celebrating his 23rd birthday with a new Atalanta contract, the club has confirmed.

Bernasconi, who turns 23 on Monday, has put pen to paper on a new and improved contract with Atalanta, the club confirmed in a statement at the start of the week. La Dea have not formally disclosed the length of Bernasconi’s new deal, but it is understood to be a long-term contract given that his previous deal already ran until the summer of 2030.

“Having joined the Nerazzurri before turning 18, Lorenzo progressed through the youth ranks and gained valuable experience with the U23s before making the step up to the First Team,” read a statement on the club website.

“Since then, he has steadily earned an increasingly prominent role through humility, dedication and hard work, setting an example with his attitude both on and off the pitch.

“The Percassi and Pagliuca families, the sporting management and everyone at the Club wish Lorenzo every success, both individually and collectively, as he continues his journey in the Nerazzurri shirt.”

The contract extension comes shortly after Bernasconi’s 50th competitive appearance for Atalanta, which came in their 1-0 win away against Monza in their last match before the November international break.

A new contract could help Atalanta fend off interest from Juventus, who had been linked with a January move for the left-sided defender last month. The Old Lady are actively seeking a new left-back heading into the January window.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
