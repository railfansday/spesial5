Three games, three goals, three wins: Midfield maverick stars again as Norwich City thrash Derby County

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Norwich City cruised past Derby County with much of the hard work done by half time. NCFC were 3-0 up at the break, and Jovon Makama lashed home a stunning striker to seal the victory in the second half.

It was another strong team performance for the Canaries, as displayed by the fact there were four different goalscorers. However, one man who stood out and got himself on the scoresheet once again was attacking midfielder Paris Maghoma.

Maghoma had Rams midfielder Alex Mowatt biting on every dummy before he fired home from outside the box for the Canaries’ second goal of the night. It made it three goals in three consecutive wins for the maverick playmaker, who looks to be back at his best.

Maghoma made an impression early on in his Norwich City career, scoring in his first starts in the FA Cup and Championship after joining in January. His trickery and flair has frustrated even manager Philippe Clement at times, but it isn’t easy to curtail someone as expressive and skilful as the 25-year-old.

The attacking midfielder was influential in the Derby County win. Alongside his goal, Maghoma created one chance and one big chance, also completed five passes into the final third (Fotmob).

Maghoma had only started two Championship games before his recent three-game scoring and starting run. His per 90 stats show just how impactful he has been when on the pitch though, offering a creative streak and valuable abilities in progressing the ball through passes and dribbling.

Across his 442 league minutes, the NCFC ace is averaging 2.44 chances created, 6.11 line-breaking passes, 57.83 touches and a 60% dribble success rate per 90. His three goals from 10 shots and just 0.87 xG shows how clinical he has been when given an opportunity at goal too – although that does beg the question whether such goalscoring form will continue (all stats via Fotmob).

Maghoma has been a standout player in Norwich City’s three-game winning run, and there will be confidence that he can continue in this vein. However, both he and the entire team face a challenge in the effort to maintain their form.

The last three games have been against Derby County, Watford and Charlton Athletic – three of the league’s worst form teams. If NCFC are to keep pushing up the table and come good on their promotion ambitions, they have to maintain these high standards against tougher opposition.

A trip to Michael Skubala’s Bristol City marks a step up for the Canaries, but it is another winnable match. It would be a real morale boost to enter the upcoming international break off the back of four wins, and Maghoma’s recent form indicates he will have a key part to play in continuing the run.
