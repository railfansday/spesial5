Cardiff City’s injury struggles continue as Brian Barry-Murphy confirms new blow for attacker

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cardiff City face West Ham on Wednesday night, the league’s highest scorers. It marks another tough game in a challenging run of fixtures, and the Bluebirds will be without forward Isaak Davies after a new injury blow.

Brian Barry-Murphy confirmed to BBC Sport Wales that Davies will face four weeks on the sidelines with a hamstring issue. Unfortunately for the City boss, the Welsh attacker is not the only concern, with Perry Ng and Alex Robertson in need of assessment while Dylan Lawlor, Calum Chambers, and Callum Robinson have been out too.

Robinson has been omitted from the last three matchday squads, but the decision has not been expanded on since Barry-Murphy first said ‘certain standards’ had not been met.

Aside from a loan spell in Belgium, Isaak Davies has always played for Cardiff City. He joined the club when he was seven, making his professional debut against Middlesbrough in October 2021 and scoring his first goal against Preston North End in January 2022.

Fans have a natural affection for the player because of his affiliation with the club. However, he has struggled to make a mark on the first team for the most part, and that has continued this term.

Davies has only played 74 minutes of Championship football so far this season, and he is yet to score. His highest goalscoring season for the Bluebirds is six goals, compared to 12 scored in a productive loan spell with KV Kortrijk.

He’s had injury absences to deal with too, impacting his bid to find consistency. It will be hoped this latest blow does not result in an extended spell on the sidelines with four weeks the initial timescale.

Although the news about Davies is a blow, defensive injuries are more of a concern at Cardiff City currently. The Bluebirds will operate with Yousef Salech up front and have the option of Jack Moylan should he be required.

However, defensively, they are without Lawlor, Chambers and Bielik, amongst others. It meant Barry-Murphy had to utilise Joel Colwill and Ollie Tanner as emergency full-backs at the end of the 3-0 defeat to Wolves.

Despite their defensive injuries, City haven’t done too badly at the back in terms of their performances. They have conceded 20 goals, less than nine other teams in the division.

The Bluebirds will be looking to take advantage of their upcoming fixtures, with games against the likes of Norwich City and Preston North End on the horizon. They will be hoping their injury problems ease as soon as possible to give themselves the best possible chance of taking the points in those matches.
