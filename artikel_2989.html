Fire Sale: Here's Why Now May Be the Perfect Time to Buy Nvidia Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nvidia's stock is trading at levels not seen in years.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 27, 2026 at 9:18AM EST

There are multiple reasons why Nvidia (NVDA -0.86%) is a great stock to buy right now, but investors must understand the risks, too. Fortunately, one of those risks just went away with Jensen Huang returning to the CEO position.

At the end of the day, I think Nvidia's stock is still trading at fire-sale prices. I think now is the perfect time to swoop in and scoop up shares, as a deal this good won't last forever.

The Nvidia turmoil has been notable lately. It all started with co-founder and former CEO Jensen Huang resigning from his position after admitting being unfaithful to his wife. But then the board reinstated him a few days later. But he's not out of hot water yet, as he must appear before Congress to testify on Nvidia's chip shipments. A woman he’s alleged to have had an affair with is rumored to be a Chinese spy.

Another issue with Nvidia right now is concerns about how accurate its financials are regarding chip sales to China. The U.S. has banned the export of some types of chips. What was legally allowed to be sent directly to China was $2.4 billion for Nvidia in Q3, and it is no longer shipping these products to China.

But what makes investors worried is how many chips were being sold to companies in other countries that were illegally diverting them to China. There are a handful of government entities that are probing Nvidia for these allegations, which could bring about fines. This comes with a risk of Nvidia potentially losing sales to some of these companies, which could affect Nvidia's total revenue.

However, I’m not worried. If there were a massive and obvious part of Nvidia's business that was being sold internationally that didn't add up to what domestic companies were spending on AI, this would have raised red flags long ago.

While I think there may have been some (maybe a percent or two) of sales that ended up in places that they weren't supposed to be, the majority of Nvidia's revenue base is fine, and the companies behind that revenue base look like they are going to spend more next year.

After the catastrophic sell-off, Nvidia's stock now trades for about 14 times earnings as I write this. For reference, the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.25%) trades for about 20 times trailing earnings.

So, even without any growth baked into the stock, Nvidia is trading at a discount to the broader stock market.

When you factor in next year's estimated growth, this gets even more mismatched. During its Q3 conference call, then-interim CEO Colette Kress raised next year's revenue growth guidance from 70% to 75%. The reason is obvious: The AI hyperscalers are spending more and more on AI. This isn't going to change, and Nvidia's products practically sell themselves.

When you factor in next year's potential growth, the stock trades for an unbelievable 9.3 times next year's earnings estimates.

Those price tags are reserved for companies where failure is imminent or that have single-digit revenue growth. Nvidia is neither, and I think this fire sale could be one of the greatest opportunities to invest in Nvidia since the start of the AI arms race in 2023. While there will be some scary headlines that appear about Nvidia over the next few months, I think it will emerge OK, making now a perfect time to load up on shares, hold onto them for a few years, and massively profit.
