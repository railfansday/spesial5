Steelers WRs Michael Pittman Jr., Roman Wilson expected to play vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Pittsburgh Steelers travel to Philadelphia to take on the Eagles on Sunday, a place they have not won in since 1965. As the Steelers look to get back on track against a struggling Eagles team, it looks like they will have two of their top receivers back on the field.

Gerry Dulac of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette reported on Saturday afternoon that Steelers wide receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson are both expected to play in Week 11.

Earlier in the week, the Steelers opened up the 21-day practice window for four players, including both Pittman Jr. and Wilson after their stints on injured reserve. Pittman Jr. has been dealing with a plantar fasciitis injury that has plagued him throughout the season. The veteran last played in Week 3 against the Cleveland Browns. Wilson had been dealing with a dislocated finger that he suffered in the team's Week 5 game against the Indianapolis Colts.

The Steelers' passing game has struggled to find consistency over the course of the last few games, making the return of the receivers a welcome sight for Aaron Rodgers, Mike McCarthy, and the entire Steelers team.

At the time of this writing, the Steelers have not yet officially activated either of the receivers for their game. With this latest information, we should expect the news of both Pittman Jr. and Wilson being activated to the 53-man roster sometime on Saturday afternoon.
