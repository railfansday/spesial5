Hornets vs. Lakers predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Preseason darlings of many prognosticators, the Charlotte Hornets (4-9) have struggled with injuries and a subsequent lack of offense. As a result, they head to Crypto.com Arena Tuesday night trying to halt a two-game skid. The challenge is a bit greater than simply looking to snap a modest losing streak. Their opponent is one of the NBA's best and hottest teams, the Los Angeles Lakers (12-2), who have won eight in a row. The Lakers are scoring a league-best 122.8 points per game and shooting 50.3% from the field, while the Hornets are producing just 99.1 points per game and shooting 40.0%.

Charlotte comes in off a 113-105 road loss to the Clippers on Sunday, its second consecutive defeat and eighth in 10 games. The Hornets actually attempted 18 more field goals than Los Angeles but shot only 38%, compared with 55% for the Clippers, while losing the assist battle 32-23. Coby White led Charlotte with 21 points on 7-of-13 shooting, and rookie guard Christian Anderson Jr. supplied a career-high 16 points. As mentioned earlier, injuries have complicated Charlotte's offensive problems, with Kon Knueppel (leg) and Dennis Schröder (soreness) listed out. Naz Reid, meanwhile, has been Charlotte's most productive scorer this season at 17.2 points per game while adding 8.2 rebounds, and Brandon Miller has averaged 15.3 points over his last 10 appearances.

The Lakers arrive in dramatically better form after extending their winning streak to eight with a 131-123 victory at Phoenix on Sunday. Luka Dončić erupted for 43 points in that victory and remains the centerpiece of an offense that has overwhelmed opponents throughout the opening month. Los Angeles has gone 9-1 over its last 10 while averaging 120.6 points, and Dončić has averaged 32.4 points in that span. Center Walker Kessler gives the Lakers a presence in the paint, averaging 14.5 points while shooting 68% from the floor, and Los Angeles also ranks among the NBA leaders in transition scoring. LA, though, has not been immune to the injury bug. Austin Reaves is out with a heel injury.

The teams split their two meetings last season, with each winning on the other's home floor. The most recent matchup came Jan. 15, 2026, when Charlotte stunned Los Angeles 135-117 at Crypto.com Arena, avenging the Lakers' 121-111 victory in Charlotte on Nov. 10, 2025.
