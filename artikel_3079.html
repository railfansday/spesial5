The Real Test for Nvidia Could Come After Dec. 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jensen Huang meets with Congress next month, as questions still loom over his involvement with a potential Chinese spy.

By David Jagielski, CPA – Nov 24, 2026 at 9:36AM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -8.43%) has brought back Jensen Huang as CEO, amid all the drama of the past few weeks that saw the company's co-founder resign only to end up being reinstated. It's put the stock on a roller-coaster ride, with it rising more than 30% yesterday on the news of Huang's return.

While many investors appear to be feeling pretty bullish about Nvidia of late, the reality is that the issues around Huang haven't completely gone away. A key date for investors to circle on their calendars is Dec. 1 -- that's when Huang is scheduled to first meet with members of Congress. And how that plays out could have a significant impact on the tech stock.

A major reason for Huang's return to Nvidia appears to be assurances the U.S. president gave the company's board, as he believed there wasn't a serious national security risk. The most concerning issue surrounding Huang's affairs is that one of them may have involved a Chinese spy, possibly resulting in illegal shipments of billions of advanced Nvidia chips to China.

While Trump appears convinced that there is little to no risk based on the information available, that doesn't mean everyone is. Republican Sen. John Cornyn's response to the news of Huang's reinstatement effectively says it all: "You gotta be kidding me."

Even if it turns out to be a non-issue for Nvidia, members of Congress may scrutinize the situation more closely given the reinstatement, with Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer going so far as to accuse the president of "putting his own financial interests and the needs of his billionaire buddies before our national security."

There was a lot of excitement around Nvidia's stock yesterday, but it may prove to be premature. Congress still wants answers from Huang, and it may be a while before investors know the full ramifications for the company. While it may come away unscathed, the risk hasn't disappeared. If new issues arise related to the investigations into Huang, they may weigh on the stock in the future.

While Nvidia has a highly profitable business and a dominant position in tech, there may continue to be significant volatility around the stock. It can be a good buy at its reduced levels, as even with yesterday's rally, the stock entered Tuesday down 32% from its 52-week high, leaving much more room for further gains. It may, however, be a bumpy ride.
