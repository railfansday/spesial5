Apple’s streaming service will soon have a video podcasts section, the New York Times reports. It will start testing the feature in a developer release today, so expect a new update for iOS 27.3 beta testers to roll out in a few hours. Once it rolls out fully, the new video podcasts section will be available to everyone using an Apple TV device or app on all supported platforms.

The expansion follows other streaming services’ push into podcasts. Netflix announced it was adding video podcasts to its platform last year, and debuted its first original video podcast series in January. Hulu and Disney Plus also offer podcasts, including a mix of companion podcasts for their shows and licensed podcasts like We’re Here to Help, hosted by Jake Johnson and Gareth Reynolds. Spotify has had support for video podcasts for several years now, and last year expanded them to its tvOS app. YouTube’s TV app has long been an option for watching podcasts, as well.

Besides bringing its video podcasts to more screens, Apple is also planning to add more video podcasts to its regular Podcasts app, according to the Times. Apple added support for switching between audio and video podcasts in its iOS Podcasts app earlier this year, and added video podcasts on the tvOS Podcasts app in September.
