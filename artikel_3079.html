Prediction: This Is What Nvidia Stock Will Be Worth by 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Nvidia is an absolute bargain if it can get its house in order.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 26, 2026 at 2:34AM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -3.91%) stock has gotten slammed over the past few weeks. It all started with co-founder and former CEO Jensen Huang resigning from his position. While he's back in the CEO role, news of investigations into Nvidia's export controls are also weighing down the stock.

When the outlook is uncertain, that's normally the best time to buy stocks, as fear controls the market rather than logic. When you look at the growth Nvidia is expected to deliver, I think the stock is an absolute buy, and I wouldn’t be surprised to see it rise to $660 per share by 2028.

That's a huge gain from today's levels, but how is that possible? Let's take a look.

There is no doubt that Huang built an incredible company, and he was an integral part of it. With him back in his role, the company he built remains intact. However, he's not the only one in the building. Nvidia has built a tech-centered company that has given it a huge advantage in the AI realm. Nvidia's products are better than the competition and practically sell themselves.

Investors should keep an eye on the future, as product development is a major reason why Nvidia has created a huge lead. Huang returning to the CEO role is major news for Nvidia's product development. Huang has made several tough decisions over the years as to how to shift product development, and his vision and technical knowledge are unparalleled in the industry. Nvidia’s stock skyrocketed 31% on the day he was reinstated, underscoring this.

There will certainly be huge growth in the field, as several AI hyperscalers anticipate spending more in 2027, building on an already record-breaking 2026. AI demand is real, and Nvidia can produce enough computing units to meet demand. In fact, Nvidia estimates revenue will grow at a 75% pace in its next fiscal year. Some companies don't forecast that far into the future because visibility that far out is hard to achieve. But with the industry in a supply-constrained state, it's easier for Nvidia to estimate how many chips it will be able to sell.

Nvidia looks set to have another year of solid growth in 2028, although a 75% clip again likely won't be in the cards.

So, where will that put the stock price by then?

Thanks to the turmoil surrounding the leadership transition, Nvidia's stock now trades at a dirt cheap 16.6 times trailing earnings. I don't think that's a reasonable price for the stock to trade at, as I think a 30x valuation is far more reasonable, given its market position and growth prospects.

Wall Street analysts estimate Nvidia will generate around $424 billion in its next fiscal year. Assuming management’s 75% growth rate is accurate, that brings its total to $742 billion. Over the past 12 months, Nvidia's profit margin has eclipsed 60%, but I'll set it at 60% to bake in a bit of conservatism. That would mean Nvidia will generate around $445 billion in profits.

If we assume Nvidia can grow at an additional 20% year-over-year pace in 2028 (which is Nvidia’s fiscal 2029), that would bring its profit total to $534 billion. At a 30x earnings valuation, Nvidia’s market cap would be $16 trillion, or a $660 per-share stock price.

That's a huge gain from today's levels, and it requires Nvidia to return to a reasonable valuation and meet projections. If it can do that, Nvidia's stock is a generational buy at today's price tag.
