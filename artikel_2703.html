2026 Minnesota Golden Gophers in the NFL: Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Week 9 was another quiet one as many former Gophers were either relegated to special teams work or did not play at all. The honeymoon is over for the Gophers in NYC as both JMS and Tyler Nubin have lost their playing time. Antoine Winfield Jr is hurt again, and Blake Cashman just keeps playing his heart out even as the Vikings offense stinks. But Max Brosmer got in the game and did well….so that's a win? Here is how the former Gophers did in Week 9 and a look ahead to Week 10.

Gibbons played 49 defensive snaps and six on special teams in the Cardinals loss to Seattle. He recorded four tackles, two of them solo. He was targeted once in the passing game and gave up one reception for 16 yards.

Bateman played 35 snaps in the Ravens loss to Jacksonville. He was targeted twice and made one catch for 6 yards.

Mafe played 50 defensive snaps in the win over the Falcons in Spain. He had three tackles, one of them solo.

Span-Ford played 55 snaps on offense and another 16 on special teams as Dallas defeated Indianapolis. He was targeted three times making a pair of catches for 20 yards. He also added a solo special teams tackle.

St. Juste got into the Packers blowout win and played 12 defensive snaps and 19 more on special teams. He made one tackle.

Ersery played all 70 offensive snaps and 8 more o special teams in the Texans win. The team did not allow a sack in the game.

Walley was inactive for the second straight week with a knee injury.

Faalele played seven special teams snaps.

Striggow played 19 defensive snaps and six on special teams in the win over Baltimore. He recorded three tackles, one of them solo and added a QB hit. He may see his playing time decrease with the Jags adding Jordan Davis from Philly before the trade deadline on Tuesday.

Lindenberg played 18 special teams snaps in the loss to San Francisco. He made one tackle.

Brosmer entered the game in the fourth quarter making his season debut. He entered to loud applause and went 6-7 for 68 yards and was sacked once as the rest of the Vikings offense was putrid in the loss to the Bills.

Cashman played 69 defensive snaps in the Vikings loss to Buffalo. He led the Vikings with 12 tackles, six of them solo and one for a loss. He added a pair of pass defenses as well.

Howden played 24 defensive snaps and 28 on special teams in the Saints OT win after Cleveland went for 2 and the win and failed the conversion. He had one solo tackle.

Schmitz still lost his job after the bye as he was dressed but did not play a snap in the Giants win over the Eagles.

Nubin also has not regained his job as he played 23 special teams snaps and not a single defensive snap in the win. He picked up a pair of special teams tackles, one of them solo.

Eastern remained on the practice squad and did not play.

Kallerup played a season high 27 offensive snaps and three special teams snaps in the win over Arizona. He did not record a stat.

Kieft played one offensive snap and 12 special teams snaps in the loss to the Bears. He had one tackle.

Winfield played just 29 snaps before leaving the game in the third quarter with an ankle injury. Before he left he recorded four tackles, two of them solo and a pass defense.
