Steelers WR Michael Pittman Jr. says the quiet part out loud about this week's win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers went into Friday's game under an incredible amount of pressure, thanks to the drama between Aaron Rodgers and Mike McCarthy from the week. We can argue all day about why the Steelers played so well against the Denver Broncos but at the end of the day they did enough to get a big 27-5 to improve to 7-4 on the season.

Pittsburgh got back injured wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. and while he only had one reception for seven yards, he might have had the most honest quote of the night about the importance of this game.

"Obviously this was a must-win, because if we didn't win, then the shitstorm keeps brewing," Pittman Jr. said after the game.

In the span of just 36 hours, the Steelers became a meme and a joke across social media and everyone was preparing to see this team fall flat on its face. If the Steelers had slipped up against Denver, the media would have pounced on this team but at least for the week, the storm that Pittman Jr. talked about will be quiet.

Next up for the Steelers is a revenge game at home. The 4-6 Houston Texans are coming to town as the team that blew out the Steelers in the first round of the playoffs last season.
