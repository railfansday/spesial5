Tyler Bass hits walk-off 57-yard field goal, Bills beat Chiefs 13-10

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The weather in Western New York delivered nasty conditions with freezing rain for Thanksgiving night.

Buffalo’s kicker sent a walk-off, 57-yard field goal through the uprights to give the Bills a 13-10 victory over the Chiefs, putting the club further in the driver’s seat for the AFC’s No. 1 seed.

The game was not without controversy, particularly late in the contest. But the weather conditions also affected both teams, as neither offense got much going.

Josh Allen finished the game 10-of-21 for 81 yards with two interceptions, rushing for 43 yards on seven attempts. Allen’s passer rating of 18.3 is the second lowest of his career in any regular-season game.

Patrick Mahomes completed 16-of-29 passes for 174 yards with no touchdowns or interceptions.

James Cook ended up pacing the offense for Buffalo, rushing for 117 yards on 32 carries with Buffalo’s only touchdown — a 5-yard run in the second quarter.

The Chiefs got a 26-yard field goal from Harrison Butker to close the first half and a 1-yard touchdown from Emmett Johnson to open the second half. But the team was not able to score again.

After Bass connected on a 54-yard field goal in the fourth quarter to tie the game at 10-10, Butker missed his eighth field goal of the year — this one going wide right from 46 yards out.

Kansas City had the ball with another shot to get Butker in field goal range. But what looked like a third-down completion from Mahomes to Rahsee Rice was overturned on replay, allowing Buffalo to field another punt.

The Chiefs got yet another opportunity to possess the ball after Allen was sacked for the second time on the night. But the club’s special teams continued to have issues, with Josh Downs fielding a punt inside the 10, returning it to just the 11-yard line.

And when Mahomes’ third-down pass to an open Kelce fell incomplete, Matt Ariza’s punt was short and skipped to Greg Dortch, who returned 17 yards to the Kansas City 39 with four seconds on the clock.

Somehow in the weather conditions, that was enough for Bass, who sent the 57-yard attempt through the uprights to give the Bills their 10th win of the season.

Between Butker, Downs’ returns, and Ariza’s punts, the Chiefs special teams had a rough night, arguably costing the team the game.

On the injury front, Chiefs receiver/returner Jalen Royals (ankle) exited and was not able to return.

With the win, the Bills are now 10-1 and will have at least a one-game lead on the rest of the conference entering Week 13 for the No. 1 seed. Buffalo will play division-rival New England in Week 13.

The Chiefs are now 8-3 and won’t have extra rest in the coming week, as they’ll be in Los Angeles to face the Rams next Thursday night.
