Fantasy Basketball Week 5 High Score Perfect Lineup: 3 players from Bulls-Nuggets OT track meet make the list

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It's Thanksgiving week in the NBA and looking back, fantasy basketball managers have plenty to be thankful for (if they were able to avoid the injury bug after the first month-plus of the 2026-27 season).

In Yahoo High Score fantasy basketball, it was a pretty eventful Week 5 with a few 50-burgers and three players landing in the perfect lineup thanks to an overtime track meet between the Bulls and Nuggets on Sunday. Let's dive into the perfect lineup and break down the top performers.

Josh Giddey: The Bulls guard had been off to a slow start to the season, averaging just 8.5 points and 7.5 assists per game in six contests in the opening month. But since November came along, Giddey has upped his game a bit, though managers would like to see him be more aggressive on offense. But that unselfishness is why Giddey landed in the perfect lineup, posting 23 points, 14 rebounds and 22 assists for 84 fantasy points in the Bulls' OT loss to Denver on Sunday. Chicago has three gettable matchups in Week 6, so be sure to get Giddey in those lineups.

Jamal Murray: The Nuggets' guard is pacing toward his best NBA season, averaging 26.2 points per game after Sunday's 45-point outburst in the win over Chicago. That point-per-minute performance awarded Murray 79 fantasy points and one of the guard spots in the perfect lineup. Murray also chipped in 5 rebounds, 7 assists and 5 stocks — pretty good category coverage. A consensus second-round pick preseason, Murray is looking like an early league-winner if he can keep this up.

Victor Wembanyama: Wow, we didn't expect Wemby to land in this article again (wink). But really, Wembanyama is putting together an MVP campaign so far in 2026-27, and has been arguably the best asset for High Score fantasy basketball (Luka Dončić is really the only one ahead of him right now). After last week's 50-point outing against the Pelicans, Wembanyama now has the two highest single outputs of the season for High Score (110, 97). He'll get another shot to try and top the High Score leaderboard in Week 6 with four games on tap.

Nikola Jokić: The Joker benefitted from that overtime contest on Sunday, same as Giddey and Murray. It was the Nuggets big's second-highest fantasy score of the season with a 37-18-11 triple-double, just his fourth triple-double of the season. Yes, we can say "just" there because we're used to Jokić doing that pretty much every night. While this feels like a slow start by Jokić's standards, he's still the No. 3 asset in fantasy as we're just past the first month of the season.

Bam Adebayo: The Heat and Adebayo started off the Giannis era with four straight losses but have since gone 10-2 since Oct. 28. Adebayo is starting to find his groove on offense, averaging north of 20 points per game in November with 8.4 rebounds, 3.9 assits and nearly 3.0 stocks per contest. Other than a game in which he left early due to injury, Adebayo has at least 40 fantasy points in six straight games. Miami has a rough sledding up in Week 6 with road games against Eastern Conference rivals — the Sixers, Knicks and Cavaliers.

Tyler Herro: The former Heat guard who was the centerpiece of the Giannis deal this offseason, Herro was expected to join Milwaukee and lead the team in scoring. That's been the case, though it hasn't led to much winning for the Bucks. That doesn't matter to us in fantasy, just as long as Herro is filling up the score sheet. Last Saturday, Herro scored a career-high 50 points with 11 boards and 7 assists in a close loss to Philadelphia. Milwaukee has a four-game week with some spots where Herro can showcase that scoring touch again.
