NFC Playoff picture: The 49ers are sitting pretty at the top; there’s a new No. 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NFC playoff picture is set through ten weeks. The San Francisco 49ers are leading the way with one loss. Let's take a look at the playoff picture:

2. Detroit Lions (7-2, 1-0 vs CHI/MIN, 2-0 NFCN)

4. Carolina Panthers (4-5)5. Minnesota Vikings (7-2, 1-1 vs DET/CHI, win over CHI)6. Chicago Bears (7-2, 0-2 vs DET/MIN, loss to MIN)

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4, 2-1 NFCW)9. Los Angeles Rams (6-4, 0-2 NFCW)10. Dallas Cowboys (4-6)

11. Washington Commanders (3-5-1)12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6, 3-3 NFC)13. Arizona Cardinals (3-6, 2-5 NFC)14. New Orleans Saints (3-6, 1-4 NFC)

In the NFC North, the three-way tie goes to the Detroit Lions because of their win over the Vikings. They are undefeated in the three-way head-to-head. The Vikings are next because they have a win over Chicago.

The Vikings humbled the Packers in Week 10. Staying in the division, we still believe the Lions and Bears are the biggest threats to the 49ers. They have elite offenses, and we saw what happens when the Niners run into an aggressive passing attack. Unlike Dallas, Chicago and Detroit are just as dangerous on the ground as they are through the air.

The Rams and Seahawks are still on the outside looking in. Green Bay currently holds the tiebreaker over Seattle based on best win percentage in conference games. It would be hilarious if the reason the reigning Super Bowl champs missed the playoffs was because they lost to Marcus Mariota.

Speaking of the Commanders, they just beat the top team in their division. J.J. McCarthy looks lost against a 2-high structure, and their cornerbacks are bound to come back down to earth. A tie may be the reason New York sneaks into the playoffs. There's a lot of football to be played. Another slip-up by the Giants opens the door for the Cowboys.

If you rank the NFC by point differential, here's how the standings would look:

49ers +85Rams +61Lions +58Bears +57Vikings +50Seahawks +48Buccaneers +24

I think it'd be easier to convince the masses that these are the conference's seven best teams.
