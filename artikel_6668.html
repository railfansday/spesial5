Suki Waterhouse Has Been Dropping James Bond Clues for Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In an interview in the December issue of British Vogue, Judi Dench, of M fame, suggested that the next James Bond should be “a young actor we’ve not yet heard of.” There has, of course, been no shortage of speculation about who might inherit the role of 007 since Daniel Craig bowed out following the release of No Time to Die in September 2021, with Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, and Josh O’Connor (bless) all touted as potential successors. But, lo and behold, it has just been announced that the lead in the new Denis Villeneuve-helmed era of the spy franchise will go to little-known up-and-comer Robert Pattinson.

And perhaps it should have been obvious all along, thanks to the woman on his arm: his wife, Suki Waterhouse. In the two years since releasing her second studio album, Memoir of a Sparklemuffin, the singer has cultivated a vintage wardrobe of Penny Lane coats, flares, and newsboy caps. But around June of this year—when she stepped out in New York wearing a Saint Laurent tuxedo jacket, cycling shorts and patent pumps, perhaps just as Pattinson was starting his own campaign for the role—Suki Waterhouse took a dramatic style pivot, swapping her bohemian signatures for something altogether more cold-blooded.

Consider also the outsized Zimmermann blazer she threw over a lingerie-strapped bodice for a Manhattan outing in September; the almost-transparent lace minidress she wore to the Venice Film Festival that same month; and the cut-out Michael Kors bodycon dress she chose for the CFDA Awards just two weeks ago. The Pattinson-Waterhouse household would doubtless have been bound by some of the world’s most punishing NDAs, but as any spy worth their double-O status knows, secrets hide in plain sight.

All this is to say: while the role of Bond girl has yet to be cast, might we suggest a woman you’ve definitely heard of?
