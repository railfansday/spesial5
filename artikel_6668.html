Pumpkin S’mores Cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Recipe by Lauren Habermehl, Pewaukee, Wisconsin

Preheat the oven to 350°F and line two sheet pans with parchment paper.

Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Cook, stirring often with a whisk, until the butter has melted, begins to foam, and turns a rich golden brown color. Remove the saucepan promptly and pour the butter into a bowl of a stand mixer. Cool the brown butter at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes. (This is important! You don’t want the butter to scramble the egg when it’s added to the bowl in the next step.)

Editor’s Tip: Keep a close eye on the butter, as it can burn easily if you’re not careful. Browning butter typically takes 3 to 5 minutes and is ready when the butter has a warm, nutty aroma.

Add the brown sugar and granulated sugar to the cooled brown butter. Stir to combine, and then whisk in the vanilla and room-temperature eggs. Beat everything until the mixture thickens slightly and turns paler in color, 3 to 5 minutes.

Add the all-purpose flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt to the wet ingredients. Gently fold in the dry ingredients using a rubber spatula until just combined—a few streaks of flour are OK.
