Luminate Bank buys Real Genius mortgage unit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Minneapolis-based Luminate Bank is acquiring selected assets and the mortgage origination operations of online consumer-direct lender Real Genius, which will remain as a division and consumer-facing brand.  AI Summary

Minneapolis-based Luminate Bank has agreed to acquire select assets and the mortgage origination operations of Real Genius, expanding the bank’s reach into the digital mortgage channel, the companies announced Wednesday.

Online consumer-direct lender Real Genius, which was originally a division of FirstBank, will operate as a division of Luminate Bank and remain as a consumer-facing brand. Financial terms of the deal were not disclosed.

Nathan Raich, executive vice president of mortgage lending at Luminate Bank, said the move is aimed at giving borrowers more flexibility in how they work with the institution.

“Some homebuyers want a mortgage professional beside them at every step, while others prefer a more self-directed, digital experience,” Raich said in a statement. “Mortgage lending isn’t becoming exclusively digital or exclusively relationship-driven. The opportunity is to be great at both and give customers the freedom to choose.”

Luminate Bank was formed after a team of mortgage executives acquired the bank in 2020, with a strategy of building a “concierge” financial experience that blends traditional mortgage lending with depository services, co-founder and president of mortgage lending Eric Lovins said in a recent interview with HousingWire’s Reverse Mortgage Daily.On the Real Genius deal, Lovins said that Luminate has been working on integration planning and evaluating the organizational structure.

“Our focus is on creating a strong platform for sustainable, profitable growth while ensuring we have the right people, resources and infrastructure to support the business,” he said.

According to Lovins, Real Genius’s leadership team previously helped build and operate FirstBank’s ConsumerDirect mortgage platform, which delivered more than 50,000 loans over nine years and contributed to a mortgage business that generated approximately $6 billion in annual production. In 2012, the division of FirstBank became Real Genius under the leadership of CEO Jamie Thornton.

“We see tremendous value in that experience and the opportunity to build on it,” Lovins said.

He added that the deal leverages Luminate’s broader mortgage and banking platform and represents another important step in expanding its national mortgage presence. The company’s strategy continues to focus on both organic growth and strategic acquisitions.

Earlier this year, Luminate acquired assets of Illinois-based First State Mortgage Services, extending its footprint across the Midwest and central U.S. Many members of First State Mortgage’s team, led by CEO and president Rene Shaffer, joined Luminate.

Luminate has originated about $3.8 billion year to date, according to mortgage technology platform InGenius. The company had 433 sponsored loan officers as of Wednesday, per the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System.

In December 2022, Luminate Home Loans — a subsidiary of the bank that has operated since 1998 — brought the more than 200-member NEO Home Loans team, previously part of Celebrity Home Loans, onto its platform. This boosted Luminate’s distributed retail presence at a time when many independent mortgage banks were contracting.

In October 2024, however, Luminate announced that it would shut down the NEO Home Loans division. The company said the decision was driven by a desire to “focus on integrating mortgage lending into its core banking services to better serve clients and build long-term financial relationships.” The NEO group is now partnered with AI-focused mortgage lender Better.com.
