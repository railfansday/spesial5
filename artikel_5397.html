Li Auto posted its third straight quarterly loss even as vehicle sales rose

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Net loss narrowed from 624.4 million yuan a year earlier, while revenue rose 5.4% and free cash flow turned positive

Li Auto reported a net loss of 309.7 million yuan ($46.2 million) for the third quarter of 2026 on Wednesday, even as deliveries and vehicle sales increased. The net loss attributable to ordinary shareholders was 314.1 million yuan.

The loss is the company's third in a row, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The net loss narrowed from 624.4 million yuan a year earlier and 1.7 billion yuan in the second quarter. Non-GAAP net loss, which excludes share-based compensation, was 112.9 million yuan.

Total revenue rose 5.4% from a year earlier to 28.8 billion yuan. Vehicle sales rose 4.7% to 27.1 billion yuan, which the company attributed to higher deliveries, partly offset by a lower average selling price from a different product mix. Deliveries totaled 99,964 vehicles, up 7.2% from a year earlier.

According to The Wall Street Journal, analysts had forecast revenue of 27.14 billion yuan and a net loss of 985.6 million yuan.

Gross margin reached 16.7%, a gain on both the 16.3% of a year earlier and the 11.0% of the second quarter. Vehicle margin was 15.3%, down from 15.5% a year earlier, which the company attributed mainly to product mix. Loss from operations was 699.6 million yuan, down 40.6% from a year earlier. Operating expenses fell 2.3% to 5.5 billion yuan.

Free cash flow was positive 2 billion yuan, compared with negative 8.9 billion yuan in the third quarter of 2025. Net cash provided by operating activities was 3.2 billion yuan.

"Despite ongoing industry-wide cost headwinds, our gross margin continued to improve sequentially, reaching 16.7% in the third quarter, and free cash flow turned positive," Chief Financial Officer Tie Li said in a statement.

For the fourth quarter, the company expects deliveries of 110,000 to 115,000 vehicles, a year-over-year increase of 0.7% to 5.3%. It expects total revenue of 31.7 billion yuan to 33.1 billion yuan, up 10.1% to 15.1% from a year earlier. The company delivered 29,025 vehicles in October.

China's auto industry is contending with weak demand, and fierce rivalry has squeezed profitability in every segment, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Li Auto's U.S.-listed shares receipts gained roughly 4% before the market opened on Wednesday. Li Auto stock is down about one-third this year.
