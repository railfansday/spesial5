Acura Makes Japanese-Market Debut with the U.S.-Built Integra Type S

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Integra Type S is now for sale in Japan, making it the first Acura-branded vehicle to reach the Japanese market.

The 2026 Acura Integra Type S is officially for sale in Japan. That might not seem like big news, considering Honda Motor Company is headquartered in Japan, but the release marks the first Acura-branded vehicle to be exported to the Japanese market. While this is Acura’s first time in Japan, it’s strangely not the Integra’s, which spent a few decades wearing a Honda badge from 1985 to 2006.

Most Acuras are designed and built in the United States and sold throughout North America. The new Integras for sale in Japan are the same as those sold stateside, meaning they’ll be left-hand-drive anomalies in a predominantly right-hand-drive market.

That also means they’ll get the same 320-hp turbocharged 2.0-liter inline-four that we’re quite fond of in the United States. The front-wheel-drive sport compact is only offered with a six-speed manual transmission and features a limited-slip differential, Brembo brakes, and 19-inch wheels wrapped in summer tires. The whole package comes together to reach 60 mph in a brisk 5.1 seconds.

Honda builds every Integra Type S at its assembly plant in Marysville, Ohio, and the engines come from Honda’s Anna Engine Plant, also located in Ohio. Honda began exporting vehicles from Ohio to Japan in 1988 and has shipped nearly 300,000 Hondas to Japan since then.

Honda says the Teg’s introduction in Japan marks a strong global demand for an enthusiast-centered performance sedan. If the Type S is well-received in the Japanese market, it’s likely that more Acuras could start making their way to Japan in the future. Pricing and the exact number of Integra Type S models available in Japan haven’t been released yet, but considering they’re identical to the Type S models sold on our shores, they’ll likely come in around $56,000.
