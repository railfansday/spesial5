'Pikmin 4 - Switch 2 Edition' Overview Trailer Showcases More Dandori Academy Exams

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

It's always Ocarina of Time this, Grand Theft Auto VI that. Have we all forgotten about November's biggest release? The one launching one week today, no less?

That's right, Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is on the horizon, and Nintendo has released a new overview trailer to remind us all that it exists showcase some of its fresh features.

Things start off with the new Switch 2 technical features, like the resolution bump to 4K and the chance to shout out commands to Oatchi via the built-in microphone. There are also some Dandori GameShare opportunities inbound, so you can go head-to-head across systems with only one copy of the game.

Moving on to the new game content, we have the bonus Dandori Academy mode. This one is all about testing your treasure hunt strategies to bring items back to the S.S. Beagle as quickly as possible. Six Dandori stages will be released each month, with exams cropping up every weekend for you to prove your skills.

It looks like the Dandori Academy updates will keep coming throughout the next 12 months, with 72 stages being added in total. Performing well in the exams will net you in-game prizes like photo stamps and user icons.

We'll admit that it feels like this Switch 2 Edition is going to be a little swept under the rug this month, but hey, more Pikmin is always good news!
