Temtem: Swarm for Switch launches January 21, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Switch version of roguelike action game Temtem: Swarm will launch on January 21, 2027, developers Crema and GGTech Studios announced.

Temtem: Swarm first launched in Early Access for PC via Steam on November 13, 2024, followed by a full release for PlayStation 5 and PC on April 2, 2026.

Here is an overview of the game, via Crema:

Temtem: Swarm mixes the simple, nail-biting fun of survivor games with Temtem‘s strategic battles, letting you cook up all kinds of clever strategies with friends or randoms in a world full of familiar creatures and vibrant vibes.
