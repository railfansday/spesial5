Digital Foundry Analyses Whether GTA 6 Could Benefit From 40FPS On Xbox Series X

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

"A 40fps mode with VRR could still make sense"

Over the weekend, there was a whole lot of buzz amongst the GTA 6 community about a Quick Resume glitch that unlocks the game's frame rate on Xbox Series X and S, potentially allowing it to reach 60FPS and higher.

Follow-up tests showed that the results were averaging more around the 30-35FPS range consistently, and some of the game's animations appeared to be running at double speed. The experts at Digital Foundry have now weighed in on this, and they've come to pretty much the same conclusion - that it's not really a viable way to play.

Rich Leadbetter of DF found that he reached an average of 36FPS on Xbox Series X in the first hour of the game:

"Unfortunately, while the unlock glitch is academically interesting, it doesn't provide a huge improvement to responsiveness. Our capture tools show frame-rates in the 30fps to 40fps range on Series X, with Rich recording an average frame-rate of 36fps during the first hour of the game."

The sped-up gameplay is also mentioned, described as an "oddity" that "makes the game inconsistent and harder to control", although this is said to be more likely a result of the bug rather than GTA 6's code being tied to frame rate.

Nevertheless, there's one specific advantage that's brought up as part of this glitch, which is that it reduces the game's input lag. Digital Foundry's tests showed that the game provided around 170ms of lag at the 30FPS mark, while a 40FPS scene dropped it to around 120ms. However, the varied frame rates are said to provide an "unsettling" feel overall, and one that probably outweighs the smoother feel during specific 30FPS+ moments.

The outlet says that a 40FPS mode when using a VRR supported display "could still make sense" on Xbox Series X, but "the game's CPU limits remain the largest barrier against such a mode" due to occasions where GTA 6 can't even reach 30FPS as things stand, while it might also require extra work such as implementing FSR upscaling tech.

"A 40fps mode with VRR could still make sense, but it's far from an open-and-shut case to say that such a mode would be an improvement overall against the current 30fps cap. It might also require extra work, such as integrated FSR 4 on Series X in service of a higher frame-rate when GPU limited, which wouldn't be the work of a moment. Finally, it looks like a 60fps mode is fundamentally unachievable on current console hardware."

Who knows whether Rockstar Games will be able to put in the work to provide a 40FPS mode in the future, but we're going to bet they'll stick with 30FPS and simply aim for 60FPS when Xbox Project Helix and the PS6 hit the market. They've got plenty of work to do with the PC version of GTA 6, not to mention the GTA Online version of the game that's surely in the works, so pushing to hit 40FPS on current-gen consoles seems unlikely.

@AccessibleDaydream FSR 4 intentionally lowers the rendering resolution and then uses AI to reconstruct the image. This is less demanding for the GPU than rendering directly at the native resolution because there are fewer pixels to process. As a result, the GPU saves rendering time and can use those saved resources to generate more frames per second (FPS). The end result is higher FPS with the same image quality, or in some cases even slightly better image quality
