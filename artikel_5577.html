STAT+: Invivyd reports positive results in Sisyphean Covid antibody quest

An experimental monoclonal antibody showed 70% to 90% protection against symptomatic Covid-19, Invivyd, a small biotech firm, said Monday.

The result, from a 2,359-volunteer clinical trial called DECLARATION, included three arms: a single 250-mg dose of the antibody, VYD2311; monthly doses of VYD2311; or a saline placebo. Results were released by press release. Invivyd said that it plans to file for accelerated approval for VYD2311 for preventing symptomatic Covid.During the pandemic, monoclonal antibodies were employed both as treatments and preventatives for Covid. The technology, however, fell into disuse as the market shrank and people gained protection against the infection. Still, Covid causes tens of thousands of deaths in the U.S., and Invivyd executives argued that there is a need for a monoclonal antibody. Moreover, they say, for some patients the monoclonal could be more effective than existing Covid vaccines. Covid vaccines reduced symptomatic infection by about 30%.

“Clinically, I really do believe that most Americans who are interested in using a pharmacological agent to protect themselves from Covid would probably move over to a monoclonal if given the option,” said Michael Mina, the former Harvard University infectious disease expert who is Invivyd’s chief medical officer. “And I think we’re going to aim to have volumes that can accommodate that over the coming couple of years.”
