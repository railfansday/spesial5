Google is unveiling two new specialized AI chips to widen its challenge to Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The TPU 8tx and 8i Max, due to be available next year, target confidential computing and long-context AI workloads

Google $GOOGL is launching a pair of tailored tensor processing units, or TPUs, to expand its AI chip lineup against Nvidia $NVDA, according to Bloomberg. The TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max will be unveiled Monday, with availability set for next year, Google chip chief Amin Vahdat told Bloomberg.

The 8i Max is meant for AI applications that need long context windows, meaning the volume of information a model can take into account within one request. Vahdat said that matters for drug discovery, math and coding. The chip packs two TPU chips together, giving it extra processing muscle and memory.

Both variations are reconfigured versions of existing silicon rather than new designs, Vahdat said. Google changed them to handle tasks customers have requested. They follow two TPU models Google released this year, according to Bloomberg, which said the chip unit used to release one new processor every two years.

"We could build a single chip that tries to address the whole market, or by building specialized versions of the chips, we actually can deliver much more value," Vahdat said, according to Bloomberg.

Google's TPUs compete with Nvidia's graphics processing units. Customers include Apple $AAPL, Meta $META Platforms and Oracle $ORCL, according to Bloomberg. Anthropic holds the right to acquire up to $150 billion of TPUs from 2028 through 2031, according to Bloomberg, which cited a regulatory filing.

Nvidia is also moving toward specialization, according to Bloomberg. It offers its own central processing units and inference-focused chips that use technology from startup Groq.

Oracle and Google said in October that TPUs will be available through Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in calendar year 2027. That marks the first time the chips will be offered on a hyperscale cloud platform other than Google's own. Google designs its chips with Broadcom $AVGO and manufactures them at TSMC $TSM.

Supply is tight. Every TPU unit Google has is already committed to a customer, the company said in its earnings report, according to Bloomberg. Google faces shortages of memory and manufacturing capacity and is seeking to diversify its suppliers, Bloomberg said. Vahdat declined to comment on the role of Marvell Technology or MediaTek in the TPU business.
