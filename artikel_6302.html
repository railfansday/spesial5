We've Tested Dozens of Toiletry Bags—These Are Our Top Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If there's one travel truth I live by—and hold dear to my heart—it’s that a dream vacation deserves a dream toiletry bag. After all, what’s a perfectly packed carry-on without a designated home for your favorite lipstick, beloved serums, sunscreen, and all those little beauty essentials that somehow multiply before every trip? The best toiletry bags make room for the whole glam squad, without adding any more suitcase chaos to the packing equation.

On all of our worldly journeys, Condé Nast Traveler editors (myself included) have tested dozens of travel toiletry bags to find the ones worth your precious packing space, from streamlined makeup pouches for the less-is-more crowd to roomy, cleverly compartmentalized cases for those who believe in a 10-step skincare routine. It's no easy job finding the best ones: Some tear after just a few months of use, others have awkwardly sized compartments. Many are too big for a standard carry-on, while others have proven too small to fit the simplest of our travel necessities.

With wipe-clean linings and durable construction, plus smart pockets and ultra chic designs, the carefully selected, editor-tested toiletry bags that earned a spot on this list are the crème de la crème. Read on for our recommendations to keep your essentials organized and your travel beauty routine in check.
