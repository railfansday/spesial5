Black Friday sales are live, and while a portable power station won’t fix, well, gestures at everything, it can at least keep your phone charged when the lights go out. Portable power stations have been in high demand this year, so it's well worth shopping sale models during Black Friday.

Below, you’ll find the best Black Friday power station deals we’ve spotted so far, including Mashable-tested favorites from Anker, Bluetti, EcoFlow, and DJI.

The Anker Solix C1000 Gen 2 is a Mashable fave, and for good reason. Our power station expert, Lauren Allain, gave it 4.5 stars and a Mashable Choice Award after spending two weeks using it to charge her devices and run appliances, including a rice cooker, blender, and air fryer.

It weighs just under 25 pounds and can fully recharge in 49 minutes. (You’ll need to turn on UltraFast charging in the Anker app to get that speed.) You can’t add an expansion battery, so make sure its 1,024Wh capacity works for you. But for weekend camping trips or occasional power outages, it has a lot to offer.

And if you missed it during October Prime Day, you’re getting another shot at the same price. The Anker Solix C1000 Gen 2 is on sale for $469.99, down from $799, at Amazon. That’s a 41% discount, or $329.01 in savings.

If your packing list includes a drone, cameras, and a laptop, this one’s for you. Allain calls the DJI Power 1000 V2 a “near-perfect” portable power station for creators, especially DJI drone owners. It’s super quiet, so you’re not adding a bunch of fan noise to whatever you’re filming. And with four AC outlets, two USB-C ports, and two USB-A ports, there’s room for the rest of your gear. A full recharge from a wall outlet takes 56 minutes; solar charging requires a separate DJI adapter.

Worrying about food spoiling during a power outage is a major concern. Groceries are expensive these days, and power outages tend to always happen right after a giant Costco haul. The Jackery FridgeGuard with an expansion battery is on sale at Amazon for $260 off. Together, you'll have 2.048Wh to keep your fridge cooling for up to three hours, according to Jackery's numbers.

Unlike the standard design of most portable power stations, the Jackery FridgeGuard has a slim design, measuring 2.6 inches, so it can sit on top of your fridge, nearly invisible. The Jackery stays charged up, and it'll automatically take over when the grid fails.

Maybe we're stretching the "portable" aspect of portable power stations, but if you need more than a weekend of backup power, the EcoFlow Titan 5000 is worth the investment. Plus, it's over $1,000 off for Black Friday.

The Titan 5000 offers 5,017Wh for keeping your tech and appliances online during the next power outage. It has three standard AC ports, a TT-30R, one USB-C, and one USB-A.
