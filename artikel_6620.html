10 Airbnbs in Sweden for a Stylish Winter Getaway

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For all the lakeside escapades and long, light-filled days of Scandi summer, something about winter in Sweden feels especially apropos. True, the sun is basically a missing person until April; but between the Christmas markets, the reindeer running loose in the northern part of the country, the sauna traditions, and the copious amounts of glögg being poured at cafés, it’s about as cozy and festive as it gets. (Provided, of course, you pack the right layers.)

Experience all this up close is by booking an Airbnb; whether it’s an apartment on the outskirts of Stockholm or a quiet escape that’s significantly more remote, a home of your own is the best way to truly get a feel for this special time of year.

Try an 18th-century cottage surrounded by fields and wetlands for brisk winter walks; or perhaps a colorful jewel box apartment right in Stockholm (complete with a terrace, if you’re willing to brave the cold for your morning coffee). We even found a cabin on your very own personal island; if you want to go truly off the grid, WiFi-free and everything, it’s the vacation rental for you.

Keep reading for our favorite Airbnbs in Sweden to book this winter.

Located in Midsommarkransen, a residential neighborhood dotted with boutiques and cafés, this bright studio does color well. From pine-green limewash in the bedroom to terracotta-colored windows, it’s the antidote to winter blues—it even has a large balcony. (Admittedly, a less-tempting amenity in the winter months; but if you’re feeling brave enough to take your mug of glögg al fresco, the twinkle lights set a cozy scene.)

Known for its farm shops selling local delicacies and small fishing villages, Österlen is a great option for your Swedish trip. Set the scene for your stay with this cozy home. It dates back to 1901, with soaring ceilings and whitewashed wood panels that make it feel even more spacious than it is. It comes with four bikes you can use for the duration of your stay—in more temperate months, cycle out to one of the aforementioned farm stands for dinner ingredients.

Amenities: Bicycle rental, washer, piano, record player, exercise equipment, wood-burning fireplace, hammock, outdoor furniture, backyard

This two-bedroom home is waterfront living at its finest. Designed by an architecture, it sits on the contemporary side with brutalist-like concrete walls and floor-to-ceiling windows. Rent a car to explore the archipelago—you’ll be near both Stockholm and the beaches for day trips.

Amenities: Washer, TV, grill, outdoor dining, pool, free parking

The coastal town of Landskrona has a lot to offer, not least of all this unique property. Part-historic landmark, part-vacation rental, this 18th-century “Fortification House” is deceptively large (it can actually sleep six people) and full of charm. Antique furnishings and high ceilings give it a stately feel, with plenty of decor ideas to bookmark—starting with the lace canopy curtains in the bedroom. Want to bring back a piece of your holiday? All the furniture in the apartment is actually for sale; inquire within to purchase.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, TV, sound system, fire pit, outdoor space, grill, shared boat slip, shared outdoor pool available seasonally, housekeeping available at a cost

Vacation amongst the locals in this sunny cabin on Lidingö, an affluent island on the Stockholm archipelago. The house itself is bright and well-equipped, with architectural details like stained glass and barrel-vaulted wood ceilings to take note of. The biggest draw, however, is its location: You’ll be steps from the beach, which takes on a more rugged form in the winter months, and a wood-fired sauna is within walking distance.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, outdoor dining, free parking

You get city and country all wrapped up in one with this circa-1910s home, which is just outside of the center of Stockholm. It has seven beds and a sunny garden, with several family-friendly amenities like a travel crib that’s available upon request and a large outdoor dining table for meals at home. Inside, cozy details put you instantly at ease. From the plant-lined windowsills to the roomy clawfoot tub in the bathroom, you’ll feel like you’re staying at your stylish friends’ house.

Amenities: Washer, HDTV, piano, Bluetooth sound system, indoor fireplace, outdoor furniture, pets allowed

It’s not every day you get the chance to stay in a renovated chapel. This house, located on the island of Gotland, offers you the opportunity: Originally a 1912 Baptist church, it’s now a well-decorated vacation rental that’s a great size for a large group. It sleeps up to eight, with a main house and a guest house on the property. When you’re feeling stir-crazy, the nearest beach is a quick two-kilometer bike ride away.

Amenities: Washer, TV, ping pong table, indoor fireplace, backyard, grill, private pool

For a lakeside vacation, book this home. It’s right near Siljan, one of the country’s largest lakes, and sleeps five with use of the large sofa in the living room. Inside, Scandinavian design elements steal the show. Expect colorful gingham bedding, paper lanterns, and even a traditional Swedish fireplace to warm up by—you may just leave scheming to redesign your own home.

Amenities: TV, high chair, outdoor dining

For an outlandishly extravagant holiday, go all out and book your very own island. (Which isn’t actually as extravagant as it sounds: This cabin goes for less than $200 a night.) Welcome to Aurora Isle, a tiny island in northern Sweden’s Västerbotten where this sweet cottage is the only thing around—except for a wood-fired sauna, of course.

This spot is ideal for the couple willing to get a bit adventurous: There’s no electricity or running water here (the lamps are solar-powered), so you get to fully lean in to an off-the-grid experience. Get around with the property’s small boat, cook all your meals outside, and spend evenings stargazing—in the winter, you can even see the Northern Lights.

Amenities: Outdoor shower, firepit, grill, pet-friendly

Situate yourself in Nyköping, the capital of the castle-riddled region of Sörmland in southeastern Sweden, in this cozy cottage. It’s from the 18th century but has been beautifully restored: Mauve toile wallpaper, two wood-burning fireplaces, and a gorgeous, fully equipped kitchen (which even comes with a bread maker) are among its highlights. Step outside to the private garden in the morning to take your coffee in the fresh air.

Amenities: Washer, Bluetooth sound system, wood-burning fireplace, backyard, outdoor furniture, grill, pet-friendly
