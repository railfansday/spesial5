Stylish Winter Staples to Secure From Reformation’s Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Hailed as a go-to among both celebrities and It-girls, LA-based fashion brand Reformation is best known for its well-considered mix of hardworking wardrobe staples, stylish accessories and versatile eveningwear. Its designs are reliably trend-led, yet there’s a timeless quality to the collections too; pieces feel current without being so tied to a particular moment that they’ll date by next season. Since launching in 2009, Reformation has built a loyal following for good reason— from its much-loved denim and everyday separates to its occasionwear, which has become a fixture on the party circuit.

Right now you can shop the entire collection at discounted prices. The site-wide Black Friday sale, which offers 25% off across all categories, is now live, with reductions on cult items endorsed by the likes of Hailey Bieber and Sienna Miller (both of whom love the brand’s Inez pump). Whether you’ve been waiting for the right moment to invest in one of its signature dresses for wedding season, have your eye on a pair of its much-photographed shoes or simply want to refresh your everyday winter rotation, there are plenty of reasons to take a look. Plus, this is a once-yearly opportunity to snap up some of the brand’s most coveted designs for a little less.

Pieces from Reformation’s collaboration with Alexa Chung also feature, including the striped Hug sweater that was all over social feeds after the drop, the sumptuous velvet dress coat and the playful “me again” slogan T-shirt. “I always start with my own wardrobe first; what I feel on a personal level that I’m missing or dreaming about,” Chung previously told Vogue of building the collection, which was heavily inspired by her love of vintage shopping.

Alexa Chung wearing the velvet coat from her collaboration with the brand.

The party-ready dresses that the brand is perhaps best known for are also on sale in abundance. In a recent story on why Reformation is the ultimate destination for festive dresses, Vogue’s shopping writer Augustine Hammond noted how Alexa Chung’s recent collaboration is just one example of Reformation’s knack for getting occasion dressing right.

The brand’s hero styles regularly return in new fabrications and colourways each season. Take the Lilibet, a silk cowl-neck maxi dress finished with a detachable sheer cape, or the Jillian, which features a diamond halter neckline, contrast panelling at the waist and an open back. For fall 2026, the latter arrives in a particularly festive velvet jacquard. Both of which, you’ll be pleased to know, are now also on sale.

Whether you’re on the lookout for a champagne-appropriate ensemble, or simply a new cashmere knit to cherish, there are plenty of stellar pieces to choose from. Shop our edit of the best Reformation buys from the Black Friday sale below.
