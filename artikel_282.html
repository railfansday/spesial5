U.S. Received $12 Billion From Venezuela’s Oil, Report Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The workings of a U.S.-controlled bank account shed light on how Washington manages Venezuelan revenue after capturing the country’s president.

The U.S. government received more than $12 billion in revenue from Venezuela’s natural resource exports in the first nine months of this year, according to a confidential report by the global financial services firm KPMG.

The vast majority of the funds were distributed back to Venezuela soon after they were received, the report shows. It is unclear if the money stayed in the country or who the ultimate recipients were.

The report, provides the most detailed portrait so far of the financial control exerted by the United States government on Venezuela since U.S. forces toppled the country’s president in January. The report was reviewed by The New York Times and its contents have not been previously reported.

The Trump administration installed a pliant interim government and charged it with opening up Venezuela’s oil industry to American investment.

The report was written just days before the midterm elections and Democratic lawmakers had repeatedly vowed to bring more transparency and accountability to Washington’s policy toward Venezuela.

Now that Democrats have won control of Congress in the midterm elections, several aides to Democratic lawmakers said they would continue pressing the Trump administration to describe what it was doing to prevent corruption in Venezuela after taking control of the country’s finances.
