With The Blood Of Dawnwalker’s New Game+ Mode, It’s Worth Going Back To See How Much You Can Get Done

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Blood of Dawnwalker‘s latest patch 1.2 is now live, adding a new game+ mode which increases the level cap from 50 to 100 and, perhaps most interestingly, removes zone scaling. In the original game, protagonist Coen could, in a bit of player-guided misadventure, stumble into a zone he was wholly underleveled for and get his shit rocked by a pack of wild boars or the errant bear. That possibility added an extra dash of tension to a game already fraught with time pressure and some tough decision-making, and I’d be curious to see what the game feels like without that added bit of danger.

New game+ comes after developer Rebel Wolves added a bunch of extra bells and whistles to the game post-launch including a new difficulty tier that introduced additional combat assists for those having an issue with the game’s directional parry system. Speaking of, the devs also bolted its directional parry system onto your ability to pet the various cats Coen can encounter, letting you stroke them this way and that because why not?

Dawnwalker‘s new game+ mode might go on my list of odds and ends to try before the end of the year. The massively increased level cap is appealing just for the prospect of seeing exactly how chonky the boars can get now that they scale to my level. The game’s time management system gives you ample opportunity to get the biggest quests done, but not everything can be completed in the 30 days you’re given to save your family.Â  I’m curious to see just how much you actually can get done, both within the time allotted and after everything’s over.
