Why William Saliba is not in Arsenal squad against Hull despite injury return

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

William Saliba is expected to play for Arsenal’s Under-21s against Aston Villa on Sunday.

The Frenchman has not played a minute this season, with his last competitive appearance coming in July in the World Cup semi-finals.

He has been out with a serious back injury since then, an issue that Arsenal have been extremely cautious with.

However, Saliba has made good progress and recently played 45 minutes in a behind-closed-doors friendly.

He was then named in Arsenal’s squad against Slavia Prague on Wednesday night, though he did not come off the bench.

Clubs are able to name more players on the bench in the Champions League than they are in the Premier League and Saliba was not selected in Arsenal’s matchday squad to face Hull on Saturday.

Mikel Arteta has confirmed that was not due to any setback, but rather a plan to get Saliba back up to full fitness.

He is set to be involved when Arsenal’s Under-21s host Villa on Sunday afternoon.

“Obviously, the Champions League allows you to bring more players into the bench, but we believe that it’s the best thing to do, to play some minutes on the weekend with the Under-21s and then be fully fit for the international break.”
