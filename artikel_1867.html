Is Barnsley FC v Oldham Athletic on TV? Channel and streaming details ahead of EFL Trophy meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Barnsley are set to host Oldham Athletic in their final EFL Trophy group stage game.

Daniel Stendel’s side are heading into a dead rubber, with their elimination from the competition having already been confirmed.

The Reds were beaten 4-3 by Leeds United Under-21s in September before following it up with a 4-1 reverse away at Fleetwood Town.

However, there is a clear desire for momentum to be built in Barnsley’s pocket of South Yorkshire. They recently battled their way to a valuable point against Oxford United and then thumped Aldershot Town 7-2 in the first round of the FA Cup.

Reds midfielder Vimal Yoganathan said: “Look, we know the form hasn't been good and this is a chance now to kick on, especially away at Wigan next week. I think we're all in good spirits and we know the quality we've got.

“It's just about putting results together and hopefully we go there, [put in a] good performance and [get] a good result.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details ahead of Barnsley’s EFL Trophy meeting with Oldham.

Kick-off at Oakwell is scheduled for 7pm tonight (Wednesday, November 11).

Yes - those not planning to visit Oakwell can watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

Supporters can stream via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Sheffield.
