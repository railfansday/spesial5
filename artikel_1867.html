Swansea City next manager: Leeds United icon and ex-Middlesbrough FC boss emerge as early favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The favourites for the Swansea City job - including ex-Leeds United and Middlesbrough men

Figures familiar with fans of Leeds United and Middlesbrough have emerged as the early favourites for the Swansea City job.

Vitor Matos has been in charge of the Wales-based outfit since November last year and has turned the club into promotion contenders.

Swansea sit sixth in the Championship table but reports have indicated they are set to lose Matos to French outfit Strasbourg.

As speculation regarding a potential exit intensifies, supporters have been left to ponder who will take the reins if his depature is confirmed.

Here is an early look at the BetVictor favourites to take the reins at Swansea if Strasbourg manage to secure Matos’ signature.
