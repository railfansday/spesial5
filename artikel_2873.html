Eagles place Zack Baun, Dontayvion Wicks on IR in roster flurry

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles place Zack Baun, Dontayvion Wicks on IR in roster flurry originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles on Saturday afternoon placed LB Zack Baun (back) and WR Dontayvion Wicks (ankle/foot) on Injured Reserve in a flurry of roster moves.

They will miss at least the next four games.

Baun missed all week of practice and was ruled out on Friday. Without him, the Eagles will turn to Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. as starters. Before Campbell missed a couple games with injuries of his own, those two split reps next to Baun. This will give the Eagles an extended look at both of those young 'backers.

Baun, 29, was playing at a very high level again this season. The two-time Pro Bowler had 65 tackles, 2 sacks, 8 TFLs, 5 QB hits and 5 pass breakups in 8 games this season.

Wicks, 25, missed the three games before the bye week with an ankle injury but was able to return to practice this week. He then was ruled out for this game on Friday as the foot injury was added to the report. At least DeVonta Smith (hamstring) will return this weekend for the Birds.

In addition to placing those two players on IR, the Eagles made the following roster moves on Saturday:

• RB Will Shipley (foot) was activated off Injured Reserve, where he had been since Oct. 16. Shipley was able to return to practice this week. He joins Saquon Barkley and Jaydon Blue as the running backs on the roster.

Expect Shipley to slot in between Barkley and Blue in the pecking order. He'll also take a significant role on special teams. Shipley will retake his role as a kick returner.

• Both DT Gabe Hall and LB Anfernee Orji were elevated for the Steelers game.

Hall will be the fifth defensive tackle after the Eagles traded Jordan Davis ahead of the deadline. He's behind Jalen Carter, Moro Ojomo, Byron Young and Ty Robinson. Sunday will be Hall's 2026 debut; he played in two games in 2025.

Orji first joined the Eagles' practice squad in early October. This is his second stint with the Eagles. He hasn't played in a regular season game since 2024 with the Saints.
