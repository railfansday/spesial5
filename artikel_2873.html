LSU football's Ty Benefield ejected for targeting vs Tennessee

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

KNOXVILLE, Tenn. — LSU football senior safety Ty Benefield has been ejected due to targeting late in the first half against No. 9 Tennessee.

Benefield was making a tackle on a Volunteer receiver DaSaahn Brame on a pass play over the middle and appeared to lower his head and lead with the crown of his helmet while making the stop.

The Tigers were leading Tennessee, 6-3, at the time of his ejection with less than 20 seconds left in the second quarter. Three plays later, the Vols scored the game's first touchdown and took the lead for the first time Saturday, Nov. 21 inside Neyland Stadium.

Officials reviewed the play after it was not initially ruled targeting and came out of it ruling that Benefield targeted while making the tackle.

Benefield will sit the rest of this game and it'll be a big loss for LSU's defense as he's one of the top players on that side of the ball.

Sophomore Jhase Thomas replaced Benefield in formation.
