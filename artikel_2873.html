With Matt Gay dealing with a hip injury, the Raiders worked out three kickers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Raiders kicker Matt Gay had a limited practice Wednesday with a hip injury. The Raiders have not yet released their injury report for Thursday.

His injury is concerning, though, based on the fact that the team worked out three kickers on Thursday.

According to the NFL’s personnel notice, Lucas Havrisik, Younghoe Koo and John Parker Romo tried out for the Raiders.

Havrisik last kicked in 2025 for the Packers, seeing action in three games. He has kicked in 12 games, making 19-of-24 field goals and 26-of-31 extra points.

Koo kicked for the Falcons and Giants last season, and has kicked in 98 games in his career. He has made 185-of-217 field goals and 186-of-194 extra points.

Romo has kicked in 11 games, including seven with the Falcons last season, and he has made 22-of-26 field goals and 19-of-21 extra points.
