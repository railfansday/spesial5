Stampede Blue Reacts Survey (Week 11): Can the Colts restore fan confidence?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Colts fans and fans across the country.

The Indianapolis Colts finally snapped their five-game losing streak Sunday, escaping with a narrow 24-22 victory over the Miami Dolphins to improve to 3-7 on the season. While ending the losing streak was certainly a welcome development, the performance itself did little to suggest that Indianapolis has turned a corner. The Colts nearly squandered a 21-7 third-quarter lead against a struggling Dolphins team, benefiting from two missed field goals and a failed two-point conversion to secure the victory. With another difficult divisional matchup against the Houston Texans approaching Thursday night, there are still plenty of questions surrounding the team's ability to compete consistently.

Perhaps the biggest concern remains the offense, particularly quarterback Daniel Jones and head coach Shane Steichen's continued commitment to him. Jones completed just 11 of 22 passes for 108 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions against Miami, while Jonathan Taylor and Seth McGowan combined for 160 rushing yards on only 28 carries. Tyler Warren, meanwhile, finished with just two receptions for one yard, despite being one of the team's most talented receiving options. With the Colts having traded several established offensive players before the deadline, Steichen faces increasing pressure to adjust his game plan and make better use of the talent still available. Continuing to rely on an ineffective passing attack while the team's most productive players receive limited opportunities is hardly a formula for sustained success.

Last week's Reacts survey revealed that only 17 percent of Colts fans believed the franchise was headed in the right direction, continuing a steady decline from 47 percent just three weeks earlier. The recent trades, disappointing performances and uncertainty surrounding the coaching staff and quarterback position have understandably left much of the fan base frustrated. However, the emergence of several younger players, combined with the organization's efforts to accumulate draft capital, could provide some hope for the future. With the Colts now preparing for a divisional matchup against Houston and a new general manager expected to take over during the offseason, the question is whether Sunday's victory has restored any confidence or whether fans remain convinced that Indianapolis is still years away from returning to contention.
