Heroes, zeros from Giants’ loss to Commanders: Jayden Daniels remains unbeaten against Big Blue

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Heroes, zeros and the full blitz from the Giants' 24-21 loss to the Commanders on Monday:

Jayden Daniels remained unbeaten in five career games against the Giants as he overcame two fourth-quarter interceptions to throw his third touchdown pass for the lead in the final minute.

Washington Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels (5) runs the ball against the New York Giants at MetLife Stadium, Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026. Charles Wenzelberg/NY Post

All-Pro punter Jordan Stout shanked a 12-yard punt to set up the game-winning touchdown drive.

New Jersey native Rasul Douglas came down with the game-clinching interception on a 50-50 ball against Malik Nabers before the Giants could get into range for the potential tying field goal.
