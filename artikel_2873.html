LeBron James misses Nets clash with back injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

LeBron James sat out the 76ers' 135-123 victory in Brooklyn on Monday, one day after moving even higher on the NBA's all-time assists list.

The 41-year-old James missed his fourth game of the season — all wins — due to management of a lower-back injury, sitting out the second night of back-to-back games.

The NBA's all-time scoring leader passed former Nets point guard Jason Kidd for third on the all-time assists list in Sunday's win over the Pacers.

Philadelphia 76ers forward LeBron James walks onto the court during a game against the Brooklyn Nets in Brooklyn, New York, Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Sixers also posted wins over the Hornets, the Bucks and the Raptors in the previous games James missed during his first season in Philadelphia. He is averaging 15.1 points, 7.2 rebounds and 6.9 assists in his first 11 appearances.

Joel Embiid netted a season-best 37 points in his return to the Sixers lineup after sitting out their win over Indiana to manage a foot issue.

Nets forward Josh Minott was fined $25,000 by the NBA for making an obscene gesture late in Saturday's game against the Grizzlies.

The 23-year-old Minott is averaging 3.4 points in 6.7 minutes per game, including just two minutes played in Sunday's loss. The Nets signed the 6-8 forward to a two-year deal worth $9 million over the summer.

Nets leading scorer Michael Porter Jr. had 25 points in his return to the starting lineup for the Nets after he sat out Sunday's loss in Memphis for personal reasons. He replaced Noah Clowney, who had played 35 minutes in his first start of the season against the Grizzlies.

Nolan Traore replaced Danny Wolf and tied his career high with 23 points on 7-for-12 shooting in 30 minutes.

Wolf had started the previous four games at shooting guard after receiving five DNP's earlier in the season. He averaged 11.3 points as a starter since contributing 13 off the bench Nov. 4, in a win over Charlotte.
