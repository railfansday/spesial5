Burger King's New Advent Calendar Has A Big Surprise Behind Its 12 Doors

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Last year's disappeared in minutes. Consider yourself warned.

Remember when Advent calendars were just little cardboard boxes with a piece of chocolate behind each door? Nowadays, you can find them filled with everything from beauty products to booze—and apparently, miniature fast food restaurants.

Burger King's Advent calendar is returning for a third year, and this time, you can practically open your own franchise.

If you remember last year's edition, it was packed with some pretty unexpected throwbacks, including Cini Mini-inspired candles, a tiny board game, and a JD the Dog plush. Fans ate it up, too, as Burger King said its last two calendars sold out in less than five minutes.

For 2026, the chain has ditched the assortment of random collectibles for something you can actually put together. Joel Yashinsky, Burger King's chief marketing officer, said fans can “build their very own BK restaurant.”

Behind the 12 doors are miniature versions of just about everything you'd find at your local BK: There's a Whopper with its own box, fries, nuggets, a soda fountain, a booth, employees, and customers. And of course, who can forget the essential Christmas tree and a reindeer pairing, too.

The only thing missing is food you can actually eat, although the final compartment does include a $5 digital offer for eligible Royal Perks members.

Now, if you want to get your hands on one, you'll have five chances. Burger King is releasing just 110 calendars each day from November 16 through November 20, with sales starting at 12 p.m. ET exclusively at BKAdventCalendar.com.

Each costs $19.54, a reference to the year Burger King was founded, and orders are limited to one per household, with free standard shipping to the contiguous U.S. and Washington, D.C. If you manage to snag one of the Advent calendars, expect it to ship the week of November 30.

So have yourself a merry little Whopper this Christmas. And I do mean little.
