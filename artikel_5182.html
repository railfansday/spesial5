Stellar (XLM) Beats XRP in Daily Tokenized Fund Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Amid the growing adoption of tokenized assets, XRP’s rival Stellar (XLM) has continued to gain traction following increased participation from DeFi users.

The latest on-chain data from Token Terminal shows that Stellar has outperformed XRP in daily tokenized fund growth after it recorded the highest amount of tokenized funds on November 26.

The data shows that tokenized funds on the Stellar network grew by about $80.4 million over the last 24 hours, showing its dominance in the market over the period.

While the network had outperformed XRP, recording the largest amount of tokenized funds over the period, XRP ranked next as the second-highest network, recording $36.3 million in growth.

Although XRP saw substantial growth in its tokenized activity, the amount it recorded was less than half of Stellar's daily increase, signaling Stellar's stronger performance in attracting tokenized fund capital over all other blockchains during the period.

It is important to note that the massive growth in Stellar’s tokenized funds market does not necessarily mean investors purchased $80.4 million worth of XLM.

Rather, this measures the value of tokenized funds on the blockchain amid growing demand from investors.

Furthermore, other networks saw considerably smaller gains in tokenized funds that day when compared to XLM and XRP. Robinhood Chain ranked third on the list, recording about $3.6 million, while Base and the Plume Network followed with $1.9 million and $1.2 million, respectively.
