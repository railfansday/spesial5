Vero Security raises $11M seed to police AI agents at work

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The startup turns security rules into controls that stop AI agents from overstepping.

Vero Security raised an $11M seed round led by TLV Partners.

Vero Security has raised an $11M seed round led by TLV Partners for software that discovers the AI tools and agents employees use and enforces security policies as they run. It cites research showing most large companies give AI security to someone who already has another job.

Vero Security, a year-old startup that helps companies control the AI tools and agents their staff use, has raised an $11M seed round, it announced on Wednesday. TLV Partners led the round, with GTM Capital, Maple Capital and angel investors joining. Vero, based in New York and Tel Aviv, says several Fortune 500 companies already use it.

Its main product checks prompts and actions as AI agents run, and blocks those that break company rules. For example, it can stop a coding agent reading keys it has no need for, the kind of leaked tokens behind last month’s LiteLLM hack. Security teams set the policy, and Vero turns it into controls that update as AI tools change.

Vero is betting that few companies have staffed this job. Among firms with at least $10B in revenue, 65% have someone leading AI security, but only 18% made it a dedicated role, according to IANS and Artico Search. At the rest, it was added to an existing job.

“CISOs have seen enough dashboards and tables that tell their teams what’s wrong and leave the work to them,” said co-founder and CEO Elad Tessler.

Tessler previously led product at Astrix Security, which Cisco bought this year. Vero is also launching a free online course and community for AI security staff, which it says contains no sales material.

The field is crowded. Zenity raised $125M in August, Tel Aviv’s Bay raised $50M last week, and Pluto Security emerged with $42M in October. Security teams often cannot see the AI their companies use.
