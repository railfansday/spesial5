Anthropic Investor Sees Huge Runway Beyond Coding

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anthropic shares are retreating after their blockbuster debut, but T. Rowe Price’s Emma Norchet says the volatility doesn’t change the firm’s long-term outlook. One of Anthropic’s largest private investors, T. Rowe also participated in the IPO. Norchet discusses the company’s improving compute economics and the growth opportunity beyond coding, from knowledge work and cybersecurity to biology and robotics. She joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
