Mayville Engineering Company, Inc. (MEC -0.31%) reported that third quarter results were driven by project volumes in the data center and critical power end market and improved demand in commercial vehicle segments. Management stated that the company is currently navigating a transitional period characterized by significant investments in new capacity and the absorption of startup costs for recently secured programs. The company indicated that operational performance across its manufacturing footprint varied as teams responded to evolving customer schedules and labor constraints in rural facilities. Leadership noted that capital allocation remains focused on expanding organic capacity, specifically through the recent acquisition of a manufacturing facility in the Southeastern United States.
