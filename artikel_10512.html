$300 dating app promises “marriage or refund” if you don’t find love

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

A Chinese dating app has a ‘marriage-or-refund’ guarantee after charging users $300 to sign-up and get one match at a time.

The ultimate goal of every dating app is for users to find love so that they can delete the app and enjoy their long-term relationship. However, it isn’t always a quick process, with plenty of users sticking around for years before they find the one.

In the meantime, pretty much every app has premium features that are supposed to help speed up the process. So, if you’re stuck on there for a while, you could run up a pretty big bill of app subscriptions.

Over in China, though, a new dating app is promising to refund users if they don’t get married within three years, but it does come with a big sign-up fee.

It’s called Liangpei, which roughly translates to Good Match, and costs $300 to sign up. However, as noted, the app promises to give you that $300 back if you don’t get married after three years.

As per Sixth Tone, users have to answer 25 questions about their dating history, while also having a 20-minute-long interview with an AI matchmaker, all to get their first match. You are only allowed one match at any one time, too.

“One-on-one allows both parties to invest more focus and effort into building a relationship,” founder Zeng Xinxun told Sixth Tone. “Even if it doesn’t work out, it’s a conclusion reached after genuine communication.”

As noted, this app is only available in China, so if you’re outside of the country, then you’re out of luck.

However, there are other apps available. Hinge founder Justin McLeod has launched Overtone, which takes inspiration from Black Mirror’s iconic ‘Hang the DJ’ episode.
