Unilever’s Creator Army Has Grown From 75,000 to 500,000 in Two Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Unilever now works with more than 500,000 creators, up from 75,000 in 2024, a sign of just how quickly the CPG giant has accelerated its shift to social-first marketing.

Unilever’s chief marketing officer Leandro Barreto revealed the number earlier in November at Unilever’s Capital Markets Day. The company has since released a statement with more details about its ongoing marketing transformation.

The creator expansion follows a commitment Unilever CEO Fernando Fernandez made in March 2025 to move half of the company’s digital media spend to social and work with 20 times more influencers. This shift marked a major change for the Dove and Hellmann’s owner, which spends $9 billion on marketing each year, 70% of which goes on digital channels.
