Another injury concern for Juventus as Zhegrova withdraws from Kosovo squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Juventus have another injury concern to contend with as confirmation arrived on Monday morning that winger Edon Zhegrova has been forced to withdraw from the Kosovo national team squad.

The exact nature of the issue remains unclear and Zhegrova is expected to report for further medical assessments in Turin on Tuesday.

He will be joined by Zeki Celik, who was forced to drop out of the Turkey national team starting line-up after limping out of the warm-up with pain in his thigh ahead of his country’s Nations League defeat against France on Sunday.

Pierre Kalulu was also forced to drop out of the France national team squad at the beginning of the November break due to a muscular issue he sustained during Juve’s 2-2 draw against Fiorentina just before the latest round of Nations League fixtures.

They add to an injury list that also includes Kenan Yildiz, Khephren Thuram, Manuel Locatelli, Francisco Conceicao and Nico Gonzalez.

The Juventus players who were not involved in international fixtures over the last week returned to training on Tuesday, and according to the latest updates, Nico Gonzalez and Andrea Cambiaso trained individually.

There have also been suggestions that the injuries to Celik and Zhegrova are not major causes for concerns, but more precise information will be made available after their medical tests on Tuesday.

Juve will next be in action against Venezia on Sunday at 11.30 GMT.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
