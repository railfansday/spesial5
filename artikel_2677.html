This Red Sox Trade Idea Would Swap Curtis Mead, Jarren Duran For Three-Time All-Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Boston Red Sox's offense is already in a better spot than last season with the addition of Jake Bauers and key players getting healthy.

Chief baseball officer Craig Breslow recently noted that he does not envision any big moves on the horizon, but anything can happen. Right now, the Red Sox seem to have an everyday player at every position across the board, and their regular alignment could look something like this:

There's not really room for an outside addition, unless the Red Sox trade one of those players for an upgrade. The Athletic's Jim Bowden proposed this trade idea that would net the Red Sox All-Star second baseman Ketel Marte:

Diamondbacks receive: Curtis Mead and Jarren Duran

Duran is a non-tender candidate and does not really have a fit on the team anymore. However, he has shown great potential in the past and is a change-of-scenery candidate. The Diamondbacks may be ready to move on from Marte after some off-the-field issues, and they need to get better against right-handed pitching.

Duran gives them a left-handed bat that could compete for an everyday job, and Mead would replace Marte. Meanwhile, the Red Sox get a two-time Silver Slugger Award winner at second base with club control through 2030.

Even in a down year, Marte had a .748 OPS and would have been a better option at second base than what the Red Sox had last season.
