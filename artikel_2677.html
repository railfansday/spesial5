George Pickens limited again Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cowboys wide receiver George Pickens remained a limited participant on Thursday. He has a hamstring injury that kept him out of Sunday's victory over the Colts.

The Cowboys had five changes to their practice report.

The team added safety Alijah Clark (illness) and safety Juanyeh Thomas (hamstring). Clark missed practice, and Thomas was limited.

Safety Malik Hooker (personal), outside linebacker Von Miller (rest) and linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring/back) were upgraded to full participants. Hoker and Miller did not practice Wednesday and Overshown was limited.

Outside linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee/ankel), running back Tyler Goodson (knee/ankle), safety P.J. Locke III (concussion), wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle/foot) and cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) did not practice a second consecutive day.

Wide receiver KaVontae Turpin (ribs) was limited again.
