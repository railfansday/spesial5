Brock Purdy not practicing on Friday, putting Mac Jones in line to start

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It looks like 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy will not be able to play Sunday’s game against the Seahawks.

Purdy is not practicing on Friday, according to multiple reporters on the scene. He has not been able to practice all week due to an ankle injury.

That puts Mac Jones in line to start against Seattle.

Jones has thrown only one pass this season in three appearances. Last year, he started eight games for San Francisco, going 5-3. In 11 total appearances for the club last year, he completed 69.6 percent of his passes for 2,151 yards with 13 touchdowns and six interceptions.

Receiver Jordan Watkins also was not practicing on Friday.

The 49ers’ full injury report with game statuses will be released later in the day.
