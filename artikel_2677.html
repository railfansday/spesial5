4-Star Washington Edge Commit Rips Off 90-Yard Pick-6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Future Washington Huskies edge rusher and St. Joseph Regional senior Chaz Gray made sure his first—and potentially last— high school interception would be one he won't ever forget.

The 6-foot-4, 250-pound Montvale, New Jersey native bullied his way into the face of Delbarton sophomore quarterback Jayden Schmalz, deflected a pass attempt on the opening drive of the game and took it back 90 yards for a pick-six last Saturday to give the Green Knights an early lead on their way to a 38-21 victory in the quarterfinals of the NJSIAA Non-Public playoffs.

Gray, the older brother of highly coveted five-star 2028 linebacker Tahj Gray, didn't register another stat in the 17-point win, the first time in three seasons as a varsity defender for St. Joseph Regional that the standout defender didn't record at least one tackle in a single game two weeks after recording the first forced fumble of his prep career in a 35-14 victory over Paramus Catholic.

Through 10 games in 2026, the four-star recruit in the 2027 class, per ESPN, has totaled 35 tackles, 6.5 tackles for loss and 2.5 sacks, similar numbers that he boasted last fall as a junior with an equal number of TFLs and one fewer sacks going into Saturday's semifinal playoff matchup against Bergen Catholic.

Gray is the lone edge commit for coach Jedd Fisch in the '27 class, ranked as the No. 7 prospect from the Garden State and No. 41 edge, based on the 247Sports Composite, and the second New Jersey recruit to commit to UW over the last two cycles.

Four-star DePaul Catholic quarterback Derek Zammit opened the pathway in the 2026 class as Fisch and co. have expanded UW's footprint following the move from the Pac-12 to the Big Ten in 2024. Zammit made his college debut midway through the fourth quarter in the Huskies' Week 2 win over Utah State when he converted a third-and-1 with a 2-yard run that led to a 43-yard field goal by Tyler Robles to extend UW's lead to 16-7 with 6:28 left in the game.

Along with the St. Joseph Regional star, fellow three-star pledge and St. John Bosco linebacker Ethan Coach has the potential to play both inside and outside linebacker for defensive coordinator Ryan Walters once he arrives on campus next year, though Gray is viewed as the more likely of the two to remain in outside linebackers coach Aaron Van Horn's room as a true pass rusher.
