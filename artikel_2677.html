Xavier dominates, struggles, dominates, then survives 88-83

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Having watched that game I cannot determine if it was hideous, amazing, or both. Xavier was at times incredible, at times awful, and occasionally somewhere in between. Texas A&M was relentless, blazing fast, rough in the post, and slap happy. This game was compelling, but it wasn't pretty. At the end, it was the Musketeers who were standing, just. They don't ask how…

Xavier came out and immediately started doing what they needed to do to beat the Aggies. They were playing fast and knocking down shots. Mike Nwoko (20/10/3) was a monster inside against Zach Clemence, who looked nothing so much as he looked frightened. Then it was Gabriel Pozzato (14/2/0) who scored nine straight to keep Xavier rolling before Kalek House (9/3/4) went on a hot streak. This all sounds like a team buiding an unassailable lead and it would have been, except Texas A&M is never out of a game.

X was up 12, and then they were up two. The Aggies ripped off a 10-0 run that spanned halftime and turned this right back into a game. The officiating had been decent in the first half in that it let pretty much anything go. In the second half, things tightened up. The refs called 41 fouls in this game and it felt like most of them came in a 10 minute span in the second half. Xavier had built an 18 point lead on the back of a 16-0 run that came in under four minutes.

And then the ref show started. That was not the reason that X threw the lead away, that was because they threw the ball away. All of the sudden, though, there were no fouls. Anything goes! Do whatever you want! For ten minutes that was the vibe and A&M fought their way back into the game. Full credit to Bucky's team, if they are allowed to play, they won't quit. X's lead dropped back down to four. And then there were fouls again.

For the last five minutes it was a matter of hanging on. Tru Washington (15/6/4) had a massive and one and then found House for another three. Washington scored again to stake X to a five point lead with a minute to play, but the Musketeers still needed five free throws and two stops to salt this thing away. It wasn't easy, but they grabbed an absolutely massive win.

Goodness me. The Musketeers finished 19-33 from the line, and that felt an improvement from where they had been. Tru Washington shot 74% from the line last year and put in another 4-7 performance, Gabriel Pozzato was 2-5, he shot over 70% from the line last season, Mike Nwoko shot 75.8% last season and was 3-8 tonight. This team was bad form the line tonight and somehow survived. It seems like that will punish them some time.

Xavier's turnover rate for the season is still 17.8%, which isn't dreadful. Tonight it was 28.3%, which is dreadful. Nwoko had four, Washington had six, Aligbe had three in just 16 minutes. Ruben Dominguez (3/0/0) didn't have any, but you could be forgiven for not knowing he played. His start to forget is starting to transition into a mid-season to forget. The only other player to not have a turnover was Filip Borovicanin (9/6/5), who played 33 excellent minutes and was maybe the only reliable set of hands Xavier had.

Call it toughness, call it bloody mindedness, call it what you will, Xavier fought themselves into a win. They very nearly tossed the game away, but they also just kept making the plays they had to to win the game. This is a game that will resonate in March, and the Musketeers found a way to get it.
