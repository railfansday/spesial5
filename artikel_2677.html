The New England Patriots and the Detroit Lions are the latest NFL teams to hit the international circuit — in Week 10, the two teams will meet in Germany, playing at FC Bayern Munich Stadium.

This Sunday's international game in Munich will air on Fox starting at 9:30 a.m. ET; out-of-market viewers can tune in with NFL Sunday Ticket. (Note that this is a change from previous weeks; past international games this season have all aired on NFL Network.)

Here's everything you need to know about how to watch this game and how to watch every other Week 10 game on the schedule.

YouTube TV gets you nearly every channel you need to watch the NFL, including Fox, NBC, ABC, CBS, ESPN and NFL Network. Plus, YouTube TV is the home of NFL Sunday Ticket. The Sunday Ticket package, while pricey on its own, gets you all the Sunday out-of-market games (160+ games per season).

Current pricing for new users for the 2026 Sunday Ticket is $27/month for four months. Factor in the cost of YouTube TV's Sports and News plan at $49.99/month for your first five months, then $71.99/month thereafter.

That's $76.99/month for the next four months of YouTube TV + NFL Sunday Ticket. You can also opt, with or without a YouTube TV subscription, to pay for the rest of the season of Sunday Ticket upfront on its own for $108.

Fubo's Sports package gives you access to ESPN, NFL Network, Fox, ABC, CBS, NBC and 20-plus more live channels. Starting at $54.99/month for your first month, the live TV streaming service offers most channels you'll need to watch the 2026 NFL season.
