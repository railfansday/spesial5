Who plays on 'Monday Night Football' tonight? Time, TV channel, schedule, live stream for NFL Week 11 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

There is no quarterback hotter than Joe Burrow right now. The Bengals star is fully healthy and playing like an MVP, with 17-game paces that could put his season among the best of all-time.

On Monday night, he returns to the site that brought his rookie season to a crushing end six years ago.

The Commanders, with renewed NFC East hopes after a win over the Giants, have their sights set on a charge up the division standings, but they have yet to prove they can sustain a win streak this season.

To knock off Burrow and the Bengals, fellow LSU Heisman Trophy winner Jayden Daniels is going to have to play with the kind of confidence he did when he broke out against the Bengals three games into his rookie season in 2024.

With that, here's what you need to know to watch "Monday Night Football" in Week 11.

Location: Northwest Stadium, Landover, MD

"Monday Night Football" will see the Commanders host the red-hot Bengals at Northwest Stadium, pitting former Heisman Trophy winning quarterbacks from LSU against each other.

Bengals vs. Commanders will kick off at 8:15 p.m. from Northwest Stadium on Monday night as Week 11 wraps up.

"Monday Night Football" will be simulcast live on ESPN, with Joe Buck (play-by-play) in the booth alongside Troy Aikman (analyst) from the Washington D.C. metro area.
