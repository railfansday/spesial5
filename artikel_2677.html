Ravens waive returner LaJohntay Wester

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After he had a rough Sunday in Charlotte, the Ravens are moving on from LaJohntay Wester.

Per Jeff Zrebiec of TheAthletic.com, Baltimore has waived Wester on Wednesday.

Wester muffed a pair of punts in the loss to the Panthers, with Carolina recovering one of them.

A sixth-round pick in last year’s draft, Wester averaged 8.2 yards per punt return and 23.7 yards per kick return in 11 games this season. He averaged 12.4 yards per punt return and 24.6 yards per kick return in 2025.

Wester also contributed five catches for 58 yards in 2026. But with four fumbles this season and three in the last three games, Baltimore has elected to move on.
