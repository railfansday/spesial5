Jordan Addison to Avoid IR; Vikings Make Several Other Moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Minnesota Vikings made a handful of roster moves on Wednesday, and also got some very positive news about one of their best wide receivers.

Jordan Addison had surgery to correct the thumb injury we mentioned on Monday, and it turns out that he will not be going on short-term injured reserve after all. That means the team expects him to miss less time than the four games mandated by a stint on IR. We'll see how many games Addison misses, but the team's decision not to put him on injured reserve should be seen as a positive sign for him.

Unfortunately, outside linebacker Bo Richter was not so fortunate, as the team placed him on IR on Wednesday. Richter suffered a foot injury in Sunday's win at Green Bay, but the team is hopeful that he can return at some point later in the season. A third-year player from the Air Force Academy, Richter has played almost exclusively on special teams for the Vikings this year.

The Vikings have also signed wide receiver Jeshaun Jones to the active roster from the practice squad. Jones has been with the Vikings for a couple of seasons, primarily on the practice squad, and recorded the first catch of his NFL career against New Orleans in Week 5.

In one last move for the day, the Vikings signed former Seattle Seahawks and Miami Dolphins wide receiver D'Wayne Eskridge to the practice squad. Eskridge, who was selected by the Seahawks in the second round of the 2021 NFL Draft, worked out for the Vikings back in October during the same workout that saw the Vikings bring in veteran JuJu Smith-Schuster.

So, to give everyone a full update, the Vikings' practice squad now looks like this, as the team also lost safety Tavierre Thomas to the New York Giants on Tuesday.

That should catch everyone up on the Vikings' roster moves for today.
