Celtics have bigger issues, but can strengthen grasp on NBA Cup bid in DC

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Celtics have bigger issues, but can strengthen grasp on NBA Cup bid in DC originally appeared on NBC Sports Boston

The Boston Celtics wrap up what's been a thus-far winless four-game road trip on Tuesday night in the nation's capital. And while the NBA Cup is not likely front of mind for the team — and simply righting the ship amid a four-game losing streak is priority No. 1 — the team has a chance to strengthen its grasp on East Group C during a visit to the Washington Wizards.

The Celtics own a 2-0 record in Group C play, while both the Wizards and Hawks — their final two group opponents this week — have both stumbled in group play. Boston has two pathways to being one of the four teams to advance in Cup play: Win Group C outright, or earn the East wildcard spot by having the best point differential among the three East runner-ups.

Boston currently owns the best point differential in the East at +53. Both the Hawks and Wizards sit at +13 in the event that a Group C tiebreaker is necessary.

With wins over Washington and Atlanta this week, Boston would win Group C outright and likely host the East wildcard winner in Round 1, if they maintain the best point differential among all group winners.

A win over Washington but a loss to Atlanta would still leave Boston with a strong chance to advance as the East wildcard, but would require a healthy point differential. A loss to Washington but win over Atlanta would leave all three teams tied at 3-1 in Cup play and make point differential the tiebreaker for both winner and runner-up in the group.

Two losses this week and Boston would be eliminated from Cup play and would instead compete against two non-Cup qualifiers over the second week of December.

When the Celtics won their first two Cup games, we wondered if there was any chance that Boston and Philadelphia could meet in later-round play, potentially accelerating Jaylen Brown's first game back at TD Garden.

Philadelphia is still alive in Group B play, but a 1-1 record and negative point differential complicates its path. The Sixers would need wins over Miami and Indiana, plus the Knicks to beat Cleveland, in order to win Group B via head-to-head tiebreaker over Miami. Two wins could help Philadelphia push for the East wildcard spot, but a minus-19 point differential through two games basically requires them to dominate those final games.
