Houston Dash hire former NWSL Coach of the Year Seb Hines as next head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Houston Dash have hired Seb Hines, formerly of the Orlando Pride, as its next head coach, the club announced Tuesday.

Hines won the 2024 NWSL Shield and Championship with the Pride, firsts for the club, and was also voted Coach of the Year that season. The Pride fired him in late August 2026, and he will be the seventh head coach in Dash history.

"Seb brings a track record of success that truly aligns with our ambitions for the Houston Dash. He understands what it takes to compete for championships in the NWSL, because he has done it before, and we are confident in his ability to maximize the potential of our roster and establish the standards necessary for sustained success," said Dash president of women's soccer Angela Hucles Mangano in a club statement.

"Our ambition is bigger than a single season or postseason appearance; we want to build a team that consistently competes at the highest level and contends for trophies. We believe Seb is the right leader to help us realize that ambition," Hucles added.

The Dash finished 12th in the league despite a strong rookie class and a bright start to the season, in which they won three of their first four matches. Forward Kat Rader was one goal shy of tying the record for most goals scored by a rookie in a season (10) and earned a Rookie of the Year nomination, and midfielder Linda Ullmark was named to the U.S. roster for the U-20 Women's World Cup, which took place this September in Poland. Veteran Dash goalkeeper Jane Campbell was also nominated for a Goalkeeper of the Year award this season.

In September, the Dash parted ways with former head coach Fabrice Gautrat and technical director Twila Kilgore, who was the interim head coach of the senior U.S. women's national team. Then-assistant coach Jaime Frias was promoted to interim head coach for the remainder of the Dash's season.

Hines will likely hire a new coaching staff in his new role in Houston. The two assistant coaches he worked with in Orlando have since become head coaches. Yolanda Thomas was promoted to interim head coach during Hines' absence for the rest of the Pride's season and was hired permanently last week as head coach, becoming the first Black female head coach in NWSL history. Giles Barnes, also a former Pride assistant coach, left the club in July and leads Al Qadsiah FC as head coach in the Saudi Women's Premier League.

"Ultimately, we want to build something this city and the fans can be proud of, give them a team they are excited to watch and create sustained success for this club," Hines said in a statement. "I look forward to working closely with Angela, our ownership group and the players as we turn that shared vision into results and that work is underway."

With Hines and Thomas as managers, the NWSL will have two Black permanent head coaches for the first time in the league.
