Four Projects in Thailand and Indonesia Recognized at the Steel Architectural Awards ASEAN 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

An innovation campus, a corporate headquarters, a family home, and a community development in a remote peatland environment are the four winners of the Steel Architectural Awards ASEAN 2026. Located in Thailand and Indonesia, the projects explore how coated steel can respond to different climates, construction conditions, and patterns of use. The winners were announced at the ASEAN Awards Ceremony in Bangkok, Thailand, on November 20, 2026. They were selected from 12 finalists representing Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, and Vietnam, following awards programs in each country.

An innovation campus, a corporate headquarters, a family home, and a community development in a remote peatland environment are the four winners of the Steel Architectural Awards ASEAN 2026. Located in Thailand and Indonesia, the projects explore how coated steel can respond to different climates, construction conditions, and patterns of use. The winners were announced at the ASEAN Awards Ceremony in Bangkok, Thailand, on November 20, 2026. They were selected from 12 finalists representing Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, and Vietnam, following awards programs in each country.

Under the theme "Shaping Resilient Futures: Timeless Design with Coated Steel," the awards examined how architectural ideas, construction details, and material choices contribute to a building's capacity to remain in use over time. Entries were assessed on their response to context and climate, technical resolution, innovation, and long-term material performance. Design Excellence accounted for 50% of the evaluation, Innovation for 30%, and Sustainability for 20%.

KJL Innovation Campus (KiN), ThailandArchitect: DesireSynthesis

KJL Innovation Campus (KiN), ThailandArchitect: DesireSynthesis

Located in Thailand, KJL Innovation Campus (KiN) brings together workplace and industrially related functions within a campus conceived around research, testing, manufacturing and exchange. The project was developed for Kijcharoen Engineering Electric Public Company Limited. The consolidated Awards record identifies COLORBOND® steel in Off White colour for the roof.

Located in Thailand, KJL Innovation Campus (KiN) brings together workplace and industrially related functions within a campus conceived around research, testing, manufacturing and exchange. The project was developed for Kijcharoen Engineering Electric Public Company Limited. The consolidated Awards record identifies COLORBOND® steel in Off White colour for the roof.

The architecture is characterised by stacked standing seam roof forms from Lysaght®, pleated façades and thermal chimneys, while elements of the project were fabricated in house by the client. During the finalist presentation, the relationship between the architectural concept, curved elements, fabrication and steel selection became a significant part of the jury discussion.

Desa Binaan Tampelas, IndonesiaArchitect: PT Pandega Desain Weharima (PDW)

Desa Binaan Tampelas, IndonesiaArchitect: PT Pandega Desain Weharima (PDW)

Set within Central Kalimantan, Desa Binaan Tampelas shifts the scale of the Awards from the individual building to a community and its relationship with a sensitive landscape. The project is located in an off grid peatland setting reached primarily by river. Its buildings are elevated on stilts, while solar panels are integrated with roof systems. The programme also connects architecture with local economic activity, including facilities associated with the processing of locally sourced products.

Eight ASEAN Finalists Receive Commendations

Eight further projects received commendations, offering responses to adaptive reuse, climate, landscape, heritage and community across the region.

Eight further projects received commendations, offering responses to adaptive reuse, climate, landscape, heritage and community across the region.

Jakarta Premium Outlets by PT Pandega Desain Weharima (PDW): An archipelago-inspired layout integrates landscape, shaded promenades and indoor and outdoor spaces into an open retail environment. TUAH 1895 by GDP Architects Sdn Bhd: The conversion of a sales gallery into a dining and lifestyle destination uses curved metal recladding and an opened ground plane to connect with a retained heritage gateway. Can Gio Pavilions by DE-SO Asia: Two V-shaped COLORBOND® steel roofs rise above a laterite plinth within a mangrove landscape. The jury noted the simplicity of the project's architectural expression. Nova Organic Office and Warehouse by Atelier of Architects: The project combines office and distribution functions while organizing truck, car, motorcycle and pedestrian circulation across the industrial site. Pandora Crafting Facility – Binh Duong (HCMC) by Archetype Vietnam Ltd.: A steel canopy derived from Pandora's visual identity connects a manufacturing campus planned for thousands of craftspeople. IC House by Angkasa Architects: A nine-grid arrangement, pocket courtyards, cross ventilation and a steep gabled roof support passive environmental design, recognized by the jury for its exploratory approach and sustainability performance. Pusat Rehabilitasi PERKESO Sultan Nazrin Shah by RSP Architects Sdn Bhd: A radial plan inspired by Perak's tekat embroidery brings healthcare facilities and landscape beneath a monumental COLORBOND® steel roof, earning recognition for innovation and sustainability. Chiang Mai University Main Stadium by A49CH: Working within a limited budget, the renovation translates the form and colors of the Thong Kwao flower into its roof and facade. The jury highlighted the slender roof structure.
