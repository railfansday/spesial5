Norwich City consider potential loan exit for midfielder

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Norwich City are considering the youngster’s next steps as they prepare for the January transfer window. NCFC are currently positioned in 12th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures and they are three points off the play-offs behind Southampton and QPR.

Towler, 19, has made an impressive start to the season for the U21s and has seven goals to his name already. He is under contract at Carrow Road until June 2028 meaning he has just under two years left to run down in East Anglia and he is highly rated by the Canaries.

In this latest update regarding his immediate situation, ex-Birmingham City and Luton Town man Lee has said, as per a report by The Pink Un: “Obviously the key for us now is, what’s the next step for Luke? Can he maintain this (form) until January? Then can we see whether it’s a loan or whether it’s pushing on and knocking on the first-team door.”

Norwich City won 5-0 away at Bristol City last time out before the international break. They are back in action this weekend against Cardiff City at home.

Norwich City are well stocked up with options in Towler’s position at the moment. Therefore, his pathway into NCFC’s starting XI is blocked right now.

The Canaries have the likes of Pelle Mattsson, Sam Field and Jacob Wright, among others, at their disposal in the middle of the park. Towler would benefit from a temporary exit to get some senior experience under his belt and some exposure to men’s football.

Towler rose up through the academy at Ipswich Town before his switch in 2025. He was a regular for the Tractor Boys at various different youth levels before his departure.

The teenager is yet to make a first-team appearance for the Canaries. Nevertheless, he is an important member of their development side and has flourished under the guidance of Lee.

Towler is showing a lot of potential and promising signs with Norwich City U21s. NCFC should now try and find him a club lower down the pyramid to help him showcase more of what he can do.

A loan move would allow him to boost his development which will help him in the long run. He is unlikely to break into the Canaries’ team anytime soon, despite his glowing youth displays.

Philippe Clement‘s side are on a roll as they eye promotion to the Premier League. They have won their last four on the spin and are carrying some momentum.
