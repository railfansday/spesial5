Bolton Wanderers dodged a transfer bullet in walking away from £3m striker pursuit amid new blow: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On Deadline Day, both Bolton Wanderers and Preston North End were in the market for a striker. At the last hour, Celtic striker Johnny Kenny, who had spent the second half of the season prior at the Trotters, was made available.

Steven Schumacher‘s side would make a move for the 23-year-old, but Celtic’s price tag and PNE’s £3m offer would see BWFC walk away from a deal. And, Republic of Ireland international Kenny duly made the move to Deepdale.

Now though, according to The Lancashire Post’s Dan Murphy, Kenny is now out for the next couple of months with a hamstring injury. The striker’s injury struggles have meant that, in hindsight, Bolton Wanderers were better off looking elsewhere.

At the start of summer, Schumacher made it clear he’d welcome back a number of Bolton Wanderers loan stars, including Johnny Kenny. However, BWFC would not have the same spending power they had in League One, and being frugal with their budget would be at the top of the priority list.

The Trotters made 13 signings and Kenny would have been their marquee addition of the summer. The 23-year-old scored six goals, with a total of nine goal involvements in his time with the Trotters. In the end, the £3m price tag put Wanderers off, and they look right to have walked away.

Kenny has two goals so far for Preston North End but has played just eight games, having also suffered an injury that made him miss the play-offs with BWFC. The North West outfit would not have been able to bring in as many players if they had signed the forward, and so far they are nine points above their Lancashire rivals.

So far, Kenny has missed three games for the Lilywhites and could now miss at least the next 15 as he recovers from his new hamstring injury. The striker was one of Preston North End’s big signings, and with both them and Bolton Wanderers in a relegation fight, it is easy to see which team is in a better position after their summer recruitment.

For the Trotters, £3m is a lot of money, if not all their transfer budget, and it looks like they’ve dodged a serious problem by walking away from the deal.

Theo Bair, who signed the same day as Kenny joined PNE, has three goal involvements whilst Sam Dalby has six goals, sitting sixth in the Championship goalscoring charts. BWFC may still need more firepower at the top of the pitch, but they also need players on the pitch, and right now their double act is doing just that, whilst Kenny remains sidelined.
