When Yang could make Celtic return as O'Neill provides update after training return

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Yang could return for Celtic against Kilmarnock but Besiktas is likely to come too soon for him.

The winger has been back in training with his teammates after picking up an injury during South Korea under-23s' Asian Games campaign.

However, with the Europa League clash with the Turks coming tomorrow, that is too short a turnaround for the 24-year-old to be ready.
