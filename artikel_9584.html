Creamy Pumpkin Gnocchi With Italian Sausage

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

During the fall, I can’t get enough of all things pumpkin. While sweet recipes might get the majority of the spotlight (lookin’ at you, pumpkin pie), I’m here to make the case for adding pumpkin into your favorite savory dishes. For this creamy gnocchi dinner, I added pumpkin to create a comforting, nutty, and slightly sweet sauce that pairs perfectly with pillowy gnocchi and Italian sausage.

The sausage:Sweet Italian sausage is so fantastic in this dish, but you can also substitute mild or hot Italian sausage or another fresh sausage in the butcher’s aisle to mix things up a bit.

The pumpkin:Hands down, our favorite brand of pumpkin puree is Libby's: They just know how to do it right! What to do with the rest of the can of pumpkin puree? Try out these pumpkin puree recipes to use it up.

Serving ideas:Serve with bread to sop up the extra sauce—you aren’t going to want to waste a drop—and a nice salad alongside to complete the meal.
