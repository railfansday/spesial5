Pokemon collector reveals $660,000 “dream card” was actually fake

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A Pokemon collector has revealed that they spent over $600,000 on their “dream card,” only to find out that it was actually a fake slab.

Collecting Pokemon cards has remained a big business over the last few years, with people regularly spending hundreds of thousands of dollars to get their hands on cards from their childhood.

In the case of Jesse Peng, he has been chasing a Call of Legends Shiny Rayquaza card from 2011. He managed to pick up a PSA 10 version for over $660,000, and everything seemed to be above board. However, after doing some investigative work into the card’s background, the Pokemon collector discovered that it was, in fact, a fake.

“It looked like a very clean card and the label looked very very real,” he said, noting the transaction happened through a “well respect middle man” in the TCG world.

“This is so clean that it’s a little bit suspicious to me,” he added, noting that his own system flagged it as fake. “The card is real, the label is real, the only thing that they couldn’t do is get the fugitive ink right.”

Peng said that the “fugitive ink” on the slab doesn’t match the authentic version on other cards in his collection.

“Greed has destroyed our hobby over the last few months and this is not the hobby that I fell in love with 25 years ago,” Peng added, noting that there is a part of him that wants to quit collecting.

The owner of PDGR noted that he has the resources to fight the problem and is “coming after you scammers,” with his app and verification technology.
