Watford set to appoint former Romania boss Iordanescu

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Edward Iordanescu's most recent job was with Legia Warsaw, who he left in October 2025

Watford are set to appoint Edward Iordanescu as their new manager.

BBC Three Counties Radio report the Romanian is due to become the Hornets' sixth manager inside 18 months after they sacked head coach Alessio Dionisi following Saturday's 4-0 defeat at Sheffield United in his 18th game in charge.

The 48-year-old is understood to be arriving in the UK on Tuesday to sign his contract and plans to bring three members of staff with him and hopes to be in the dugout for Wednesday's Championship fixture at home to Blackburn Rovers (19:45 GMT).

Iordanescu guided Romania at Euro 2024 where they topped their group ahead of Belgium with all four sides ending level on four points from three games before a 3-0 defeat against the Netherlands in the Round of 16.

Iordanescu - whose father Anghel had three spells in charge of the Romanian national team, leading them in two World Cups and two European Championships - left his last role at Polish club Legia Warsaw in October 2025.

It was the 48-year-old's ninth club over a 15-year coaching career which has taken in Bulgarian club CSKA Sofia as well as Steaua Bucharest and Cluj among others in his homeland.

Dionisi, who departed with the Hornets in 21st, four points above the relegation zone, was only appointed in June after the departure of Ed Still, who himself lasted only three months at Vicarage Road.

Still, Javi Gracia and Paulo Pezzolano were all also given full-time contracts after Tom Cleverley left in May 2025.

The 46-year-old Italian was Watford's 12th head coach, not including interim bosses, since the end of the 2020-21 season, and leaves the Hornets searching for a 25th since the Pozzo family took over the club in 2012.

The club have had 57 permanent managers in their 145-year history.
