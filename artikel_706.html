Imax Posts Record Revenue, Profit Growth With ‘The Odyssey’ in Third Quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Christopher Nolan's epic on its screens helped the film technologies company record best-ever key earnings metrics from a mix of Hollywood and local language titles.

Imax saw big gains from Christopher Nolan‘s The Odyssey on its screens as the film technologies company posted record global box office, revenue and profit growth in the third quarter.

Lifted by Hollywood’s ongoing recovery at the multiplex, Imax posted record quarterly global box office at $712 million during Q3, well up on a year-earlier $368 million. The big audience draw had been a 70mm run for Nolan’s epic as it recorded premium ticket sales surpassing $550 million worldwide.

During the latest quarter, Imax saw revenue rise 64 percent to $174.6 million, compared to a year-earlier $106.7 million. Net income rose over 400 percent to $117.1 million, against $22.6 million in the same period of 2025. Adjusted EBITIDA hit $121 million, up 133 percent from a year-earlier $51.8 million, another quarterly record, as was the adjusted earnings per-share at $2.21, compared to a year-earlier 47 cents.During the latest quarter, content solutions revenue rose 110 percent to $94 million in the third quarter, driven by the global box office increase, while technology products and services revenue increased 28 percent to $77 million.

Heading towards the end of 2026, Imax has the upcoming release of Dune: Part Three and the Chinese release of Avengers: Doomsday, and a 2027 slate to include movies shot with proprietary Imax cameras, including Star Wars: Starfighter, and the Superman sequel Man of Tomorrow. Avengers: Secret Wars and the Imax debut of the original Star Wars will be presented in 70mm film for the first time. Imax said it had already drawn $30 million in pre-sales for Dune: Part Three, its second highest pre-sales ever.

And after Imax merchandise for The Odyssey included a first-ever popular popcorn bucket, the company is rolling out a Godzilla Minus Zero apparel line, including a t-shirt, and a line of Imax-branded products to target the December release of Dune: Part Three.

“What Imax achieved goes beyond simply a ‘great quarter’ and transcends the many records we shattered in the period. What we believe we are witnessing is an awakening to the power and potential of our platform to impact global culture, unlock new commercial opportunities, and inspire a new level of demand and awe in audiences around the world,” Rich Gelfond, CEO of Imax Corp., said in a statement on Thursday.

Imax forecasts it will exceed its 2026 full-year guidance for a record $1.4 billion in global box office after signing agreements for 105 theater systems worldwide so far this year. Gelfond on a morning analyst call pointed to a 13-theater agreement in Europe expected to shortly close and announce.

Imax is also at work on pilots for new concept auditoriums, including smaller, ultra-premium venues for better immersive aspect ratio, depth of view and sound. Gelfond also said it is looking to expand its 70mm film locations by 10 to 15 new locations.

The Imax chief also touted the upcoming Imax release for the sequel to Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, the Brad Pitt-starrer The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. Cinemark and Regal among major chains have decided against their own sequel releases due to Netflix’s ultra-short two-week release window on offer, while AMC Theatres will screen the David Fincher film, starring Brad Pitt, on its Imax screens.

Gelfond argues the Cliff Booth release on his screens “marks an exciting experiment for us, releasing across more than 550 Imax locations worldwide ahead of its debut on Netflix this Christmas.” Cliff Booth will debut in theaters on Nov. 25, then hit Netflix on Dec. 23.
