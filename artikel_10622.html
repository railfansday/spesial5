Jimmy John’s is giving away free Picklewiches to customers who dress as pickles

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Jimmy John’s is celebrating National Pickle Day with a buy-one, get-one-free Picklewich promotion, but customers will have to dress for the occasion to take advantage of the deal.

The sandwich chain is encouraging fans to show up dressed as pickles on Saturday, November 14, with everything from full pickle costumes to head-to-toe green outfits qualifying for the promotion.

The one-day offer is part of a larger National Pickle Day celebration that also includes free pickles, limited-edition collectibles, and a meal built around the chain’s signature pickle sandwiches.

On November 14, customers who visit Jimmy John’s dressed as a pickle can receive a free Picklewich when purchasing another one.

The chain says customers don’t need an elaborate costume to participate. Wearing an entirely green outfit is enough to qualify, although fans can also show up in full pickle costumes.

Jimmy John’s is also offering a separate promotion for JJ Rewards members from November 13 through November 15. Customers who purchase any sandwich through the chain’s website or app can use the promo code FREEPICKLE to receive a free pickle.

Alongside the food promotions, Jimmy John’s has introduced five limited-edition Pickle Pals, collectible pickle plushie keychains that are now available to purchase individually in stores and online.

The collectibles are also included in the Triple Pickle meal, which combines a Picklewich, Pickle flavored Jimmy Chips, and one mystery Pickle Pal packaged in a blind bag.

Customers can choose between six Picklewich varieties for the meal, including the Vito, Turkey, Ham, Roast Beef, Tuna, and Veggie options.
