Spotify finally lets users turn off its AI DJ: Here’s how

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The streaming platform announced the changes as part of a broader update to its personalized DJ feature, which also introduces several new voices for listeners to choose from.

Spotify’s AI DJ launched in 2023, offering personalized music recommendations accompanied by an AI-generated voice that introduces tracks and provides commentary based on a listener’s music preferences.

Until now, that commentary has been a central part of the experience. However, Spotify is introducing a setting that allows users to disable the voice between songs while continuing to receive personalized recommendations.

Once disabled, DJ will continue playing music without speaking between tracks. Users can still make requests by typing or speaking to the feature, and the DJ button remains available to switch genres, artists, moods, or recommendations.

However, the feature will not become completely silent. Spotify says listeners will still hear brief acknowledgements when starting DJ or pressing the DJ button to change the music.

The company explained that the option is designed for situations where users want music playing in the background without commentary interrupting the experience.

Alongside the commentary setting, Spotify is expanding its selection of AI DJ voices with nine additional options across English and Spanish.

The update introduces seven English voices with American, British, Irish, Australian, and Indian accents, alongside two Spanish voices.

The new English options include Casey, Tess, and Lane from the US, Sam from Australia, Nell from the UK, Declan from Ireland, and Nisha from India.

Spanish-speaking listeners can also choose between Nico and Fabi, two new voices featuring Mexican accents.

Each voice has a different personality and delivery. For example, Spotify describes Casey as laid-back with a slight drawl, while Declan is characterized as straightforward, passionate, and knowledgeable about music.

Listeners can change their preferred DJ voice through the same three-dot menu used to adjust commentary settings.

According to Spotify, 80% of DJ listeners return to the feature week after week, making it one of the company’s most popular Premium offerings.
