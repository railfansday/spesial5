From Work Mode to Wind-Down: The Benefits of Programming a Connected Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

How a tech content creator uses Hubspace from The Home Depot to streamline the smart devices that power his everyday.

A tech and lifestyle content creator by trade, Gyasi Linje looks to the latest gadgets to make his days more efficient. He relies on Hubspace from The Home Depot to control many of the smart devices in his home in one convenient place.

Hubspace, The Home Depot’s exclusive smart home platform, powers more than 250 products across select private brands—like Hampton Bay, Husky, and Vissani—as well as national brand partners. It effortlessly connects everyday essentials, including smart security, indoor and outdoor lighting, appliances, switches, watering controls, and more.

At Linje’s desk, his computer screen and speakers are plugged into a smart surge protector. He uses the app to program the lights and ceiling fan in his home office and the lock on his front door. And the presets allow Linje to work without distraction all day. “I’ve got a work-mode lighting preset,” he says. “Even my fan and dehumidifier run on a schedule.”

When the workday is over, Linje can typically either be found gaming or entertaining friends at home. Using the app, Linje can shift his living room into a colorful atmosphere for play, or into low, golden lighting that makes guests feel at ease. Plus, he can switch his freezer to fridge mode to make room for extra drinks. Hubspace even lets him know when the switch is complete, so he can time it to his guests’ arrival.

Hubspace is free to download. Once you’ve synced your home’s smart devices to the app, you can enable voice control for screen-free commands. The Hubspace app runs on WiFi and Bluetooth, so even if the WiFi is down, you can still program your home smoothly. And if you work remotely as Linje does, you can use it to set your smart devices on an automatic schedule, to help you shift in and out of your WFH routine with ease.
