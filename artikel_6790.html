The ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Dance-Off Pairings Have Been Revealed for ‘Grammy Night’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Reminder: Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson are not participating.

The finale theme week of the season is going down Tuesday night on Dancing With the Stars, and Grammy Night is kicking things up a notch. Aside from the cast performing their usual routines, we’re also getting “Dance Offs” where couples go head-to-head while performing the same dance style to the same song.

As the show puts it in a press release: “Two at a time, couples face off against one another on the ballroom floor as they dance head-to-head in the same style at the same time. Winners of each of the three Dance-Offs will be selected by the judges, and the winning couple from each dance-off will receive bonus points added to their judges’ totals for the night.”

Amber and Pasha vs. Ezra and DaniBoth couples will perform a rumba to “The Boy Is Mine,” performed live by Brandy and Monica.

Jenna and Val vs. Tatyana and JanBoth couples will perform a cha-cha to “The Middle,” performed live by Maren Morris.

Connor and Rylee vs. Maura and MarkBoth couples will perform a Cha Cha to “All I Wanna Do,” performed live by Sheryl Crow.

Meanwhile, Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson have immunity from the Dancing With the Stars Dance-Offs due to getting the highest scores last week, and they’ll get a bunch of bonus points automatically added to their score.

Dancing With the Stars is back to its usual Tuesday night programming after airing Monday last week due to ABC’s election coverage. So…see you November 10 at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC!
