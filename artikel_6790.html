Why Wallpaper Is the Secret to Making Even the Smallest Bathroom Feel Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A little pattern can turn into a true design moment.

When you’re working with limited space, it’s almost instinctive to tone down your design details to avoid feeling overwhelmed—but even small spaces need character. Our 2026 Whole Home in Charleston is a master class in excellent design, no matter the room size or purpose. This year’s top recommendation for a chic bath is wallcoverings.

When installed properly, wallcoverings are one of the most reliable ways to add an extra touch of flair to interiors. Here’s how the designers of our 2026 Whole Home used them to bring big personality to small spaces.

For many designers, powder rooms are the perfect opportunity to play. Designer Mark Kennamer, founder and principal designer of his eponymous Birmingham, Alabama–based firm, used wallcoverings to add bespoke character to a nearly monochromatic powder-pink scheme. In the guest bath, the Zoffany mauve moiré wallcovering lends a subtle shimmer and dimension, while its wood-like grain adds an unexpected, welcome layer of texture.

Anissa Zajac of House Seven Design outfitted the primary bathroom with a botanical-printed wallcovering by Sanderson that incorporated nearly every hue in her palette while adding a dose of whimsy. The pattern’s vertical lines play off the texture of the fluted vanity cabinets, making a bold statement without straying too far from the design scheme.

In the gallery bath, the team at R&R Interior Design 365 took inspiration from Charleston’s famed cobblestone roads. They opted for a blue-brick wallpaper by Perennials to channel the perfect level of city flair while keeping the feel warm, intimate, and inviting.
