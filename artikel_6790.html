<em>Off Campus</em> Season 2: What We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

After the smash success of Heated Rivalry, production studios clamored to find the next big drama to keep both hockey and romance fans glued to the TV—and got their wish with the arrival of Elle Kennedy’s Off-Campus. The book series, which follows elite hockey players and their love interests as they navigate the ups and downs of life at Briar University, is now a Prime Video series with the same name.

Much like Bridgerton, the books feature a different pair of leads with each installment, which has led to fans of the books wondering what’s in store for the show’s second season. Here’s everything we know about Off Campus season 2 so far.

Yes! Deadline reported on Feb. 12, 2026—just over three months before the show’s May 13 premiere—that Off Campus received a season 2 renewal for Prime Video.

“Off Campus captures the kind of emotionally charged, character-driven storytelling that truly resonates with our global Prime Video audience,” said Peter Friedlander, head of global television at Amazon MGM Studios. “With its passionate fan base, rich source material, and an incredible creative team bringing Briar U to life, we knew that this series had the depth and momentum to continue beyond its first season. Set against the high-intensity world of college hockey, the series explores deeply personal journeys and is brought to life by a tremendously talented cast whose chemistry and authenticity make every moment feel real. We have great confidence in the way Louisa has mastered this adaptation, and we’re excited to give fans even more of the romance, friendship, and heart that define the series.”

On May 12, 2026, season 1 leads Belmont Cameli and Ella Bright—who play Garrett Graham and Hannah Wells, respectively—confirmed that they will be returning for the second season of the show. “I totally understand your concern,” Cameli responded when asked if Off Campus would focus on a separate couple in season 2. “We will be along for the ride the whole time.”

Bright added they’ll be “sticking” around, while Cameli said that they’re “excited to see what season 2 holds for [us].”

Prime Video later confirmed that season 2 series regulars include Mika Abdalla, Stephen Kalyn, Antonio Cipriano, Jalen Thomas Brooks, and India Fowler, with Philipa Soo in a recurring role.

On Aug. 13, 2026, the show revealed via Instagram that Shay Rudolph (Keeps Me Young) has been cast as Summer Di Laurentis, Dean’s sister in the franchise. Fans eagerly awaited her casting, as Summer is the love interest of hockey player Colin “Fitzy” Fitzgerald in The Chase, the first novel in Kennedy’s Briar U book series.

“Can’t stop screaming I’m SO EXCITEDDD,” Rudolph wrote in the comments section of the show’s Instagram post announcing the news.

Dean (Stephen Kalyn) and Allie (Mika Abdalla) will be the central couple of season 2, Prime Video announced. Much like Bridgerton, each book spotlights a different duo in the Off-Campus universe, but Garrett and Hannah will still be along for the ride.

Show creator Louisa Levy said in a statement, “We are so excited to continue Allie and Dean’s story as our primary romance of season two after kickstarting their romance in season one. But if you fell in love with Hannah and Garrett, don’t worry—they will still be an integral part of our robust ensemble. We’re looking forward to telling the next chapter of everyone’s story. There’s a lot for fans to look forward to!”

Season 1 takes a great deal of its storyline from The Deal (book 1 in the Off-Campus series). With season 2 following Allie and Dean’s romance, it seems the show will skip to book 3, The Score, which focuses on the couple.

While specific plot details are still unknown, here’s the synopsis for Kennedy’s novel:

“Allie Hayes is in crisis mode. With graduation looming, she still doesn’t have the first clue about what she’s going to do after college. To make matters worse, she’s nursing a broken heart thanks to the end of her longtime relationship. Wild rebound sex is definitely not the solution to her problems, but gorgeous hockey star Dean Di Laurentis is impossible to resist. Just once, though, because even if her future is uncertain, it sure as heck won’t include the king of one-night stands.

“Dean always gets what he wants. Girls, grades, girls, recognition, girls…he’s a ladies’ man, all right, and he’s yet to meet a woman who’s immune to his charms. Until Allie. For one night, the feisty blonde rocked his entire world―and now she wants to be friends? Nope. It’s not over until he says it’s over. Dean is in full-on pursuit, but when life-rocking changes strike, he starts to wonder if maybe it’s time to stop focusing on scoring…and shoot for love.”

On November 5, the series announced via Instagram that Off Campus season 2 will arrive on April 28, 2027. In a video, Kalyn and Abdalla opened up the door of a dean’s office to deliver the news.

“What? You miss us?” he says, to which Abdalla chimes in to add, “Dean and Allie are officially back for season 2.”
