Celtic look to keep Rangers at bay in title race as Frick starts Aberdeen rebuild

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Rangers are away to Motherwell live on Sky Sports on Saturday from 7.30pm; Celtic host St Mirren; Mario Frick's Aberdeen are at Kilmarnock; Falkirk take on St Johnstone; Dundee United are away to Hibernian; Hearts face Dundee at Dens Park

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

The Scottish Premiership returns after the international break and with it comes the guaranteed drama.

Celtic know any slip-ups could hand Old Firm rivals Rangers the opportunity to edge closer at top of the table again.

There is a new man in charge at Aberdeen, with Mario Frick handed the challenge of turning the Dons' dismal season around.

Here, we take a look at the big talking points ahead of the weekend…

Rangers head to Motherwell on Saturday evening, live on Sky Sports, aiming to carry on the momentum that has seen them win 10 games in a 12-match unbeaten run.

Derek McInnes described his team's performance following the previous international break as "disjointed" after they gave up a series of chances in a 3-1 win over Kilmarnock.

"It's something we're really mindful of, that they all go away to their different federations, they eat differently, they travel differently, they're coached differently, some are playing, some are not playing, so they get thrown out of kilter a bit," the manager said.

"So we just like to bring them together and get that focus again on what we're doing here at the club and the importance of knuckling down, being focused to win this game."

Their opponents are unbeaten in their last three after a run of seven defeats, with boss Alfred Johansson confident his squad is finally getting to the position he wanted before the season started.

Paul McGinn, Callum Hendry and Oscar Priestman have returned from injury off the bench in recent weeks and Alex Lowry could make a comeback against his former club.

After taking the last international break to consider his future after three consecutive defeats in all competitions, Martin O'Neill returns from this one with a more positive outlook.

They are four points clear of Rangers, albeit having played a game more, ahead of welcoming St Mirren to Parkhead.

O'Neill has already said there are "things that we can definitely do much, much better," but against a team who have won just one of their last six league games, he will be feeling positive.

Kasper Hogh is one player who could "do better" as he struggles to live up to his record transfer fee.

The striker has played 18 games since joining Celtic for £11m and scored in just one - a hat-trick against Kilmarnock back in August.

New manager Frick is intent on making Aberdeen a more prolific team as he bids to enjoy the type of longevity at Pittodrie that he had at previous club Luzern.

The 52-year-old former Vaduz boss has been appointed as Stephen Robinson's successor and will take charge of his new team for the first time in Saturday's bottom-of-the-table showdown at Kilmarnock.

The 11th-placed Dons are the joint-lowest scorers in the Scottish Premiership, with just 12 goals from 12 league games.

"I'm known for high-scoring clubs," Frick said as he faced the media for the first time since his appointment. "Two times with Vaduz we had a high-scoring goal record.

"Aberdeen is lacking to score, and so I'm here to change that.

"We need time, but we don't have it, so more important now is to show the basics. Body language, willingness to work, aggression, intensity - that's what I and the supporters want to see in Kilmarnock."

While the new boss is targeting the top-six, defeat at Rugby Park would see them end the weekend bottom of the table.

Killie have their own issues - they have managed just one home win in the Premiership so far this campaign. If Frick makes a winning start, they will be cast five points adrift.

Falkirk are on a five-game winning run during which they have beaten champions Celtic and ended Hearts' incredible 19-month unbeaten run at Tynecastle Park.

The Bairns return to action against St Johnstone sitting third in the Scottish Premiership - at the last international break, they were down in 11th after winning just one of their first six games of the season.

Their last win before the break came at a cost though, with captain Brad Spencer ruled out until the end of January after having surgery on a knee injury suffered against Hearts.
