Shopping Black Friday Deals? These Brands Offer Free Shipping, Too

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In case you needed any more reason to shop the best Black Friday deals, here's another: free shipping.

The only thing that could make Black Friday deals better is free shipping. After all, the annual shopping event is the perfect time to finally save on the items you’ve had sitting in your cart for months—and free shipping makes the wait feel extra worth it.

Whether you’re shopping for beauty, fashion, home or tech, many of the brands you likely plan to shop post-Thanksgiving dinner offer free shipping as an added bonus. So, we rounded up sites that offer free standard shipping in addition to can't-miss deals.

Certain brands like lululemon and Quince always offer free shipping, while other retailers like Sephora require you to sign for their respective loyalty programs (don't worry it's free).

Other sites offer free shipping with a minimum purchase value, typically starting at $35. Then there are subscription services like Amazon Prime and Walmart+. These memberships do come with a small price tag, but the cash you pay upfront will save you money in the long run.

If free shipping is the incentive you need to shop Black Friday, consider yourself influenced.

All Beauty Insiders enjoy free standard 1-3 day shipping on every order, no minimum purchase required. Not a Beauty Insider? Sign up for free now.

Standard shipping at Nordstrom is always free. Need faster delivery? Nordy Club members get free two-day shipping on select items, and it's free to join.

If you want to get a good deal while still getting that reliable shipping, Nordstrom Rack is your girl. Shoppers get free standard shipping on orders of over $89. Nordy Club members with Ambassador status or higher receive free standard shipping automatically.

Amazon Prime members enjoy free shipping and receive unlimited free two-day, one-day, or same-day shipping on eligible items with no minimum purchase requirement. Non-members get free shipping on orders $35 and over. Want free, lightning-fast shipping? Sign up to become a Prime member here and get a 30-day free trial.

Standard shipping is always free at Quince, no exclusive code or membership necessary.

All lululemon shoppers automatically get free standard shipping (2-6 days) on all online orders, no minimum purchase amount required.

You score free shipping at Walmart on orders of $35 or more. Want free shipping without an order minimum? Sign up for Walmart+ and receive free shipping on all your orders.

At Ulta Beauty, standard shipping (3-8 business days) is free with all purchases over $35.

Right now, Lulus is offering free standard shipping on orders over $50, free 3-day shipping on orders $125 and up, and free 3-day shipping on select orders. If you order on the Lulus app, there is no minimum purchase amount needed to secure free shipping.

Need a candle ASAP? Bath & Body Works offers free standard shipping (3-7 business days) on orders over $50.

IGK just announced 30% off sitewide for Black Friday and Cyber Monday, plus free standard shipping on orders $50 and over (3 to 8 business days).

At Free People, you get standard shipping on orders over $100 or enjoy express shipping on app orders valued at $150 or above.

If you need a stylish new outfit for a last-minute event, Revolve has so many great options, and their shipping is super fast. The retailer offers free two-day shipping, with no minimum purchase amount required.
