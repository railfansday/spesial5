Alcaraz and Zverev headline the ATP Finals in Turin - live on Sky Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Alexander Zverev chases a third crown at the season-ending tournament, while Carlos Alcaraz targets his first, with Ben Shelton, Daniil Medvedev and Frances Tiafoe also involved; The ATP Finals from Turin is live on Sky Sports Tennis from 15-22 November

The climax to the 2025 ATP Tour season is upon us again with the season-ending ATP Finals set for Turin, live on Sky Sports Tennis from Sunday.

World No 1 Alexander Zverev is chasing his third crown at the event, while Carlos Alcaraz will try to lift the trophy in Turin for the first time.

Ben Shelton, Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe and Rafael Jodar make up the field.

The Nitto ATP Finals is structured as a round-robin format, with eight singles players/doubles teams divided into two groups of four.

All singles matches are the best of three tie-break sets. All doubles matches are best of three sets, with the first two sets played as tie-break sets with no ads and the final set played as a match tie-break.

Each player/team plays the three other players/teams in his group.

The winner of each group is placed in separate semi-final brackets, with the top player/team in Group A playing the runner-up in Group B, and vice versa.

Brits Henry Patten and Neal Skupski have both qualified.

Patten and Harri Heliovaara - No.1-ranked team and defending champions - will be the team to beat in Turin this year.

The British, Finnish duo have won five ATP titles this season, including their second Wimbledon trophy during the summer.

They will be joined by Skupski and American partner Christian Harrison.

Skupski and Harrison have won two titles this year, with both coming at Grand Slam events - the Australian Open and the US Open.
