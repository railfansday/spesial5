KFC brings GTA-inspired Cluck n’ Bell to life with delivery car straight out of GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

KFC is bringing a GTA-inspired chicken delivery experience to the real world with Cluck n’ Bell, a limited-time promotion that will see fans receive food from a bright pink Cadillac.

The fast food chain announced the promotion on November 17, 2026, with plans to deliver meals to select customers in London on November 19. The event is inspired by Grand Theft Auto’s fictional Cluckin’ Bell restaurant, although it is not an official collaboration with Rockstar Games.

The promotion comes as anticipation continues to build around GTA 6, with KFC incorporating elements of the game’s Vice City setting into its latest marketing campaign.

On November 19, KFC will send a hot pink 1959 Cadillac convertible through London to deliver meals to a limited number of customers.

The vehicle will feature custom Cluck n’ Bell branding, while its driver will wear a Hawaiian shirt and sunglasses inspired by the aesthetic of GTA 6.

When the Cadillac arrives at a winner’s address, the driver will open the trunk to reveal a selection of KFC menu items before presenting the food on a custom serving tray.

The Cluck n’ Bell feast includes a Tenders Bucket, Tower Burger, Twister Wrap, Mini Fillet Burger, KFC Fries, Iced Coffee Kwench, and Kwench Cloudy Lemonade.

KFC says the promotion is designed around allowing gamers to eat while continuing to play, describing the experience as a “one-handed gaming feast.”

Entrants must follow KFC’s link starting November 17 and complete the Cluck n’ Bell competition form. The competition is intended for eligible London residents aged 18 and older, subject to the official terms and conditions.

Winners will be selected and contacted on November 18, with deliveries scheduled for the following day. Those selected must be available to receive their food on November 19.

Fans outside London will not be eligible for the Cadillac delivery experience, but KFC is also planning a nationwide promotion through Just Eat.

Starting at 6 pm on November 19, customers across the UK will be able to take advantage of free KFC delivery through the platform at participating restaurants. However, KFC notes that the offer remains subject to final confirmation and participating restaurant terms.

“When you’ve waited 13 years for a game to drop, the last thing you want to do is leave the house. So, we thought we’d make things a little easier,” said Phoebe Syms, Fame & Partnerships Lead at KFC UK & Ireland.

“Cluck n’ Bell is all about keeping one hand on the controller and the other the chicken. And if we’re going to deliver to gamers on 19th November, we might as well do it in a pink Cadillac.”

This isn’t the only GTA 6 promotion KFC has planned. In Australia, the fast food chain is hosting a fake funeral on November 19 to give fans an excuse to take the day off work and play Rockstar’s new game.

The event, taking place at a KFC restaurant in Robina, Queensland, will celebrate the life of a fictional person named Ann Alibi, complete with a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, and a eulogy. Attendees will also receive free fried chicken, giving Australian fans another way to take part in the launch celebrations.
