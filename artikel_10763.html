GTA 6 devs worried about layoffs despite making “biggest game of all time”

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A Rockstar Games developer says he and his colleagues are worried about losing their jobs after GTA 6, despite the successful launch.

The long-awaited sequel arrived on November 19 to rave reviews, after racking up well over $1 billion in pre-orders, and is likely to be one of, if not the biggest, video game releases ever.

GTA 5 sold more than 230 million copies, and Rockstar animation programmer Nestor Uriarte says the follow-up is expected to surpass it.

But for some of the people who made it, launch week also brings questions about what happens in the weeks and months that follow.

Uriarte, who works at Rockstar’s Edinburgh studio, raised those concerns in an opinion piece for The Guardian. He described the pressure game developers have come to expect on either side of a major launch.

“While the highs of a game release like this can be incredible, game workers have also come to expect other, less welcome side effects: intense overtime in the buildup and mass redundancies in the aftermath,” Uriarte wrote.

He pointed to job losses across the games industry to explain why those fears persist, even at Rockstar.

“It says a lot about the industry that even my colleagues and I, working on the biggest game of all time, are worried about getting laid off. But the truth is that workers across the sector are helpless to resist the waves of redundancies crashing around us, until we get organised.”

As a result, Uriarte said he was voting in favor of union recognition because he wants staff to have a say in pay, bonuses, and working conditions. He said Rockstar has already introduced some overtime pay and wants to build on that so workers are paid for every hour they put in.

He went on to admit that there has been no suggestion from Rockstar that layoffs are imminent, but that the fears are still there among staff.

“After the big day, we have had no indication that layoffs are coming, but it’s hard not to worry about what the future holds,” he added.
