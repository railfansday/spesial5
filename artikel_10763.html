Xbox PC app gets major update that makes it faster & easier to navigate

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Microsoft has announced a major overhaul of its Xbox PC app, introducing faster loading times, redesigned navigation, and improvements to how players access their games.

The update was revealed on November 5, 2026, as part of Microsoft’s ongoing efforts to improve the Xbox experience across PC, web, and cloud gaming platforms.

Xbox Insiders will be the first to test the changes, which include significant performance improvements throughout the app. Microsoft has not yet confirmed when the update will become available to all users.

According to Microsoft, the updated Xbox PC app launches up to 60% faster than the current version, while its Home, Store, and Game Pass pages load up to 50% faster.

The company also claims that in-app windows open up to 65% faster, reducing the time players spend navigating between different sections.

Beyond performance, Microsoft is redesigning the app’s game library to make it easier to find and organize installed titles. Improved filters and navigation will help users manage their collections and access games more quickly.

The changes make a major difference in navigating the app with a controller, like with the Xbox ROG Ally X20 and the new Nano controller.

Search functionality is also being expanded, allowing players to find games, installed titles, add-ons, and other users through an updated interface.

Social features are receiving attention as well, with changes designed to make friends lists, chats, and Xbox parties easier to access.

The update also introduces refreshed game cards, settings menus, and animations throughout the app.

Microsoft is also making changes to Xbox Cloud Gaming across supported TVs, VR headsets, and web browsers as part of the broader update.

These improvements are intended to make the Xbox experience more consistent across devices, allowing players to access their games through different platforms. For now, the redesigned PC app is being introduced through the Xbox Insider program, giving participating users an opportunity to test the changes before a wider rollout.
