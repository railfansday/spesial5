Xfinity Conjures John C. Reilly To Get You Off Wi-Fi This Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The ghosts of Charles Dickens’ A Christmas Carol—Past, Present, and Future— are well known. But this holiday season, Xfinity is introducing a fourth: The Ghost of Christmas Very-Recent-Past.

And this ghost has an unusually specific power: Showing those distracted by their devices what they just missed in real life.

In a campaign that launches today (Nov. 9), the Ghost of Christmas Very-Recent-Past is played by Academy Award-nominated actor John C. Reilly. The spot, directed by Jim Jenkins and created in partnership with Goodby Silverstein & Partners, features several vignettes of holiday gatherings where someone has missed a special memory.

In the first, two young people kiss under a mistletoe while the crowd “aws.”

Richard Lowe is a freelance writer based in Los Angeles.
