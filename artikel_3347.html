‘Avatar: Seven Havens’ Sets Book 2 Release Date At Paramount+

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Paramount+ has set a spring release date for Avatar: Seven Havens Book 2. The 13-episode sophomore season premieres globally on the streamer on Friday, May 21, rolling out weekly with Episodes 1-3 on May 21, 4-6 on May 28, 7-9 on June 4, and 10-13 on June 11.

The news was revealed Thursday during an exclusive Reddit AMA with Seven Havens executive producer Ethan Spaulding, and co-executive producer Sehaj Sethi.

Book 3, also comprised of 13 episodes, will premiere on Paramount+ later in 2027. As previously announced, the animated series will conclude with Book 4, with premiere details to be announced at a later date.

Avatar: Seven Havens, the latest installment in the Avatar animated universe, hails from Avatar: The Last Airbender co-creators Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko. Watch on Deadline

In Book 2, titled “Discovery’, Pavi (Saheli Pavi) continues her chakra journey to the Floating Isles and learns more about what happened to Korra. Meanwhile, Nisha (Aishu Devan) discovers vital information about the mysterious spirit energy Pavi released from Ellora. The girls are brought together in New Ba Sing Se, along with all their unresolved issues.

Returning cast for Book 2 also includes Akshay Khanna as Karthik, Major Curda as Jae, Sakina Jaffrey as Agam, Darren Barnet as Daemin, and Dee Bradley Baker as Geet and Ruhi.

DiMartino and Konietzko serve as co-creators and executive producers for Avatar: Seven Havens. Spaulding is executive producer and Sehaj Sethi is co-executive producer. The series is produced by Avatar Studios and Nickelodeon Animation Studios.
