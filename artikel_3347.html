I have tested a lot of the best vacuum cleaners over the years. From corded uprights to cordless lightweight stick models. Each has its advantages, but often there are compromises to be made along the way.

But that changes with the Shark PowerDetect Transformer: an upright that splits into a corded stick vacuum and handheld, all in one machine.

Shark has also packed in the technology with this vacuum. Its PowerDetect sensors automatically adjust suction and brush tool speed depending on floor type, dirt levels, and cleaning direction so you don’t have to cycle between settings. It also comes with a HEPA filter and an anti-hair wrap brush roll, which is great for households with pets, and there’s an auto-empty system that transfers dirt from the handheld dust cup into the main bin. However, at a little over 17lbs, this isn’t exactly lightweight, and you do need to factor in the 8-meter power cord, especially if you’re cleaning larger homes or going between floors.

But is it enough to convince you to ditch the convenience of cordless and go back to corded? Find out what happened when I put it to work.

The Shark PowerDetect Transformer was launched in September 2026 and retails for $649.99/£499.99 at SharkNinja or selected retailers like Amazon UK, Amazon US, Curry’s and Best Buy.

It comes in two colors, Midnight Steel and Sagewood, although if you're in the UK and want the Sagewood you'll need to get it from Amazon as Shark don't stock that color.

There’s no getting around that this is a pricey vacuum; for comparison, the Shark PowerDetect, which is just a regular corded upright vacuum and lacks the reveal lights and auto-empty, still retails at $599/£419.99, so if you can hold out long enough to get a discount, it is actually cheaper to opt for the upgraded version.

The question is whether the three-in-one design really offers enough convenience to make that higher price tag worth it.

If you already own a handheld or stick vacuum, then the Transformer may feel like an expensive way to add an upright vacuum to the list, especially when you consider that the Shark Classic Upright is priced at just $379.99 / £219.99, which is reduced to £129.99 at sale price for UK buyers.

In terms of non-Shark alternatives, the Dyson Ball Animal 3 is the most obvious comparison. This corded vacuum is suitable for all floor types and has a telescopic handle which rotates in three directions for 360º cleaning. It’s even cheaper than the Shark, priced at $499 / £329.99, but you do miss out on the HEPA filter, reveal lights, and smart sensors.

Honestly, there’s nothing quite like the Shark PowerDetect currently on the market unless you’re willing to make some sacrifices in terms of features and functions.

When you first look at the Shark PowerDetect Transformer, you’ll be forgiven for thinking it looks like just a regular upright vacuum.

But look a little closer, and you’ll see that the design is actually the thing that makes this cleaner stand apart.

It uses a modular design that can transform it from an upright to a stick to a handheld, all with the click of a button.

Shark isn’t a stranger to the idea of modular upright vacuum cleaners. The PowerDetect Powered LiftAway is another good example where the main unit detaches, allowing you to effortlessly switch between different cleaning modes.

However, that model retains the traditional hose design, whereas the PowerDetect Transformer does away with the hose altogether for a much smoother and more flexible experience.

It’s a weird vacuum to describe, because when it is fully together, it’s not the smallest or lightest machine, measuring 46.3 x 11.2 x 12.5 inches and weighing over 17lbs.

But when you pop off the wand for the stick and handheld functions, it suddenly becomes very light and flexible, so it’s a bit of an odd hybrid of a vacuum cleaner in that it’s heavy and bulky whilst being lightweight all at the same time.

The controls are extremely uncomplicated. There’s an on/off button at the top of the machine; this is all you really need, as that will set the vacuum straight into auto mode. However, if you do feel like you want a little more control, there is also a mode button which will allow you to toggle between eco, detect, and boost.

Personally, this machine does a great job of detecting what type of floor you’re vacuuming and the level of dirt it can detect, so I’d just leave it on auto every time to ensure you’re getting the best clean.

You get plenty of accessories with this vacuum, including a duster crevice tool, stair tool, HairPro pet power brush and an anti-allergen dusting brush. The main floor head uses a dual-roller system which uses Anti-Hair wrap technology, so ideal if you have pets or you’re like me and shed more than the dogs.

Another interesting design point for this vacuum is that it has two bins, there’s the main bin, which is a very generous 1.3 litres, but then there’s also a smaller 0.2L bin, which is used when in handheld mode and the great thing is that when you reattach it, the smaller bin self-empties into the main bin so you don’t have to empty the bin twice. Of course, this vacuum is corded so you don’t quite have the same convenience you do when it comes to cordless but with an 8-meter cord, it does cover quite a lot of ground before you run out of wire.

This vacuum comes in two colorways, Midnight Steel and Sage, which is more interesting than the typical black or grey look most vacuums have. The colours give it a distinct look without being too flashy, and whilst it’s not exactly a piece of home decor you’d actively want on display, it certainly doesn’t look hideous if you don’t have anywhere to store it when not in use.

In my opinion, this is a really well-built vacuum; the plastics are thick and don’t feel as though they’re going to break after heavy use. The attachments secure well, although surprisingly they don’t click in, so it can be a bit of a struggle to pull them out if you want to reattach the wand or change attachments. The buttons to release the bin and the wand are easy to push; they don’t feel too stiff. The build quality feels as though this is a good choice for busy households, as it’s certainly robust enough to withstand knocks and bashes of family life.

To assess the Shark PowerDetect Transformer’s cleaning performance, I ran a number of tests on a range of flooring, including hard floors, long-pile carpet, and short-pile carpet. I used seeds, Rice Krispies, and hair on each surface to see how much the vacuum cleaner picked up.

Straight off the bat, I was super impressed by the performance of the Shark PowerDetect Transformer. I thought my floors were clean before I started using this vacuum, and to say I was a bit shocked is an understatement.

The PowerDetect Transformer uses a DuoClean Detect floorhead, which has two brushrolls that have been designed to tackle a range of different flooring.

This is ideal if you have a mixture of flooring types throughout your home, as it automatically detects the surface it’s working on and adjusts the suction and speed accordingly. I thought this particularly useful, especially when vacuuming downstairs where I have carpet, hard floors, and tiles.

I’ve been using a Dyson V12 Absolute for the last couple of years in conjunction with a robot vacuum, so I didn’t think there could possibly be much dirt and debris left in my carpets. But from the first use of this vacuum, my carpets instantly looked cleaner and the amount of dust and fur that found its way inside of the dust bin was quite frankly insane.

The vacuum uses sensors to identify areas where additional cleaning power is needed. Once it detects those areas, it increases the suction and speed at which it works.

I’ve tested enough vacuum cleaners over the years to have heard all these marketing claims before, so I wasn’t too convinced this would be a feature that I noticed, but I was really blown away by just how well it worked. Especially around the areas where my dogs’ beds are kept.

These, of course, are some of the dirtier spots in the house, but when going over those areas, the vacuum LED went purple, which signified a dirty area and the power increased. It’s a really useful feature, especially if you have pets, because it means you don’t have to practice guesswork by repeatedly going over the same areas.

The reveal lighting is another welcome addition, especially when vacuuming the wood and tiled floor. It did a great job of showing up dog hair on the floor, which I couldn’t see at all when the light wasn’t illuminating it. It’s one of those features that you may not think you really need until you see it in action and then you can’t do without it.

This is a really nice vacuum to manoeuvre around. It gets up close to the edges so the whole floor really does feel like it’s had a good clean. However, because of the size of the bin, if you’re hoping to get underneath furniture like sofas, you may struggle.

You can of course, use it as a stick vacuum, but then you don’t have a large head to be able to cover a large area, so there is a little bit of a problem there.

Whilst the PowerDetect Transformer isn’t specifically a pet vacuum, it’s a great choice if you do have pets. The Shark Anti-Hair Wrap technology means that hair doesn’t get tangled around the brush rolls.

This isn’t just relevant to pets either; my hair gets everywhere, and my Dyson always needs the hair cut away from the brush. I’ve been using this vacuum for a couple of months now, and not once have I had to cut my hair from the roll, so it really does work.

There’s also a HairPro pet power brush included, which is for upholstery and other areas where pet hair accumulates. I thought this did a great job of getting rid of dog hair on the sofa, but I didn’t really rate it when doing the stairs; it didn’t quite get into the edges to pick up the fur that had accumulated, so I still had to manually brush it down first.

As for the collection, the 1.3-litre dustbin is incredibly generous. I have seven rooms in total in my house and I empty it after each clean, it isn’t full to the top but it is pretty full so you can certainly do a full clean through the house without having to stop and empty the bin every five minutes.

The smaller 0.2 litre bin that is attached to the handheld section is ok, I find the limit is just doing the stairs with it before I need to empty it into the main bin, so if you’re planning on doing any areas that are heavily soiled, you may have to empty midway through the job which could get a little annoying after a while.

You can open and empty the small bin manually if you want, but to activate the self-emptying you just have to re-dock the wand onto the main body, so if you’re finished with cleaning, it makes more sense to just do it that way.

This Shark does have a HEPA filter, and also the bin has an Anti-Allergen complete seal, so it catches and traps dust within the bin rather than pushing them back into the room. This makes it a great choice for anyone with allergies, but it’s also good to know that your hard work won’t be undone by emptying the bin.

One thing that’s worth noting is the weight. At a little over 17lbs, this isn’t a very lightweight vacuum when being used as an upright, so if you are going to move it between floors, then it’s worth just thinking about. However, the powered floorhead means that it’s light and easy to move across carpets; I certainly don’t feel like I’ve done a workout when I’ve finished vacuuming.

The Shark PowerDetect Transformer performed well on hard flooring. The built-in reveal lights do a good job of highlighting any dust and fur that can’t be seen by the naked eye. The head also glides around the flooring with ease and the auto-detect did a great job of recognising when the vacuum was going from hardwood to vinyl to tiles.

Vacuuming up the Rice Krispies and the seeds was ideal, it didn’t crush anything, just sucked it up in one go, making the job quick and easy. Nothing got pushed out to the sides either so it didn’t spread it across the floor.

This vacuum does incredibly well on carpeted floor. It's powerful suction gets deep in to the fibres and removes dust, debris, pet hair and anything else that may be hanging around. I was blown away how easily the vacuum picked up the test products. The Rice Krispies especially as they didn't get crushed to smashed in to the carpet, the moment the vacuum went over the top, it just sucked them up. It didn't have any problems picking up the chia seeds either which are small and could have easily been lost within the fibres but it vacuumed it all up leaving none behind. It was really interesting when it came to vacuuming up the hair as it also took in more debris that it found, and that was after the floor had already been vacuumed, so if there's anything on your carpet, this vacuum is going to suck it up.

Noise-wise, I don’t think this vacuum is over the top loud, I used the Decibel X app around 3 feet away from the vacuum and it recorded a reading of 71.1dB whilst it was cleaning.

If people are working or relaxing nearby, then it could be distracting, but it certainly isn’t the loudest vacuum that I’ve tested.

Despite being a three-in-one vacuum, the Shark PowerDetect Transformer doesn’t really need much space. It’s not the smallest vacuum, measuring 46.3 x 11.2 x 12.5 inches.

But one advantage of it being a corded model is that it can be kept in its upright configuration and doesn’t need to be attached to the wall, placed on a charging dock, or be anywhere near a power outlet. I’ve stored mine under the stairs, and there’s plenty of room.

Shark has made some effort to think about how to store the accessories too. It ships with a bag so you can keep the additional tools together if you don’t want to risk them getting lost. But it also comes with some clips which create onboard accessory storage if you’d rather keep them with the unit. There are also dedicated hooks to keep the power cable tidy.

Maintenance is also pretty straightforward. The bin is bagless, so there’s no need to buy replacement bags. The bin also detaches from the main unit so you can take it outside directly to the bin to empty. You will want to clean the dust bin, as, speaking from my own experience, it does get very dusty. The bin lid opens from the bottom and the top so it’s easy to get a cloth in there and give it a good clean.

The vacuum does have multiple filters, which are washable and require a little more maintenance. The filters are really easy to get out; they just pull out from their area, so there’s no real issue with removing them.

As with most upright Shark vacuums, there is an anti-odor cartridge at the front of the floor head. This keeps things smelling fresh when the vacuum is running and needs to be replaced every six months. These cost $7.99/£7.99 direct from SharkNinja, but you can buy off-brand versions if you prefer.

Overall, this isn’t a machine that you’re going to have to spend a lot of time maintaining. But it is important to keep it clean and tidy so that you get the best performance out of it throughout its lifetime.

The Shark PowerDetect Transformer is an impressive upright cleaner, and you don’t have to compromise on the convenience of a stick or handheld.

To say that this vacuum cleaner leaves nothing behind is an understatement. It performed consistently well across all three surfaces, collecting almost all of the test items that I laid down for it.

It brings together an innovative design with a powerful performance that’s enough to convince me to ditch my cordless stick and go all in on this corded upright. I was really impressed by how well this vacuum got to work on carpets. It lifted embedded fur and dust that I had no idea was even there. I also found that being able to switch between different cleaning configurations made giving the house a full deep clean a lot easier.

There are a couple of drawbacks, of course: being corded means it’s not so easy to just grab it and vacuum up any small spills because you have to consciously get it out and plug it in. Also moving between floors is also a bit of a pain, again you have to carry it about.

However, when you consider that I managed to vacuum the whole house in under 20 minutes, that’s a pretty good indication that this vacuum does a great job quickly.

For anyone looking for the cleaning power of an upright with the flexibility of a stick and handheld vacuum, the Shark PowerDetect Transformer is a brilliant option.
