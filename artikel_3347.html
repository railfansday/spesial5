Netflix’s New Harlan Coben Series Based On 12-Novel Franchise Hits Major Filming Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Harlan Coben's upcoming adaptation of his Myron Bolitar books just reached a major production milestone.

Costume designer Claire Dille Parkinson shared an Instagram Story announcing that Myron Bolitar has now wrapped filming, saying, "That's a wrap! Until next time Myron Bolitar!" The accompanying image appeared to show various outfits featured on the show.
