Tottenham wonderkid Luca Williams-Barnett signs new contract: ‘This is only the start’

Tottenham teenager Luca Williams-Barnett has committed his future to his boyhood club by signing a new contract.

The 18-year-old caught the eye during his full Spurs debut in Saturday's 2-0 Premier League victory over Ipswich, striking after 15 minutes before driving forward from deep inside his own half to assist Dominic Solanke six minutes after the second-half restart.

With his previous agreement entering its final 24 months, Spurs have now moved to extend the youngster's contract.

"This is a special moment for me and my family, and one I have worked really hard to reach, but this is only the start," the Academy graduate told the official club website.

"Spurs is a family to me. I have been here for so long – it’s a second home, where I trust the people and coaches who have pushed me to become the player I am today.

"The club has trusted and believed in me, now I’m ready to repay that."

Spurs head coach Roberto De Zerbi believes far more is to come from the England Under-19 international.

"Luca is a player I think the world of, and I’m so happy that he has signed a new contract," De Zerbi said.

"He has benefitted from being around the first team every day, growing in stature and consistency, and has deserved his place in the matchday squad over recent weeks.

"Everyone can see how technically gifted Luca is on the pitch, but I also love his calmness and decision-making under pressure.

"The best players have confidence on the pitch and humility off it. There is still plenty of room for Luca to improve, and I know he will continue to work hard every day to be the best version of himself."

Spurs sporting director Johan Lange feels "special talent" Williams-Barnett fully merits his fresh contract.

"Luca’s progression through the Academy to this point is a great example of what our pathway is designed to achieve and what is possible for young players here," Lange said.

"We are excited to watch Luca continue to develop and be an important player for the club, both in the present and the future."
