DWTS’ Jenna Johnson Shares Update on Broken Nose After Accident

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dancing With the Stars pro Jenna Johnson confirmed to E! News that a “little accident” such as a broken nose can’t stop her and Harry Shum Jr. as they look ahead to the semifinals.

Jenna Johnson is putting her best nose, er, foot forward.

Despite taking an accidental hit to the face the day before the Dancing With the Stars quarterfinals on Nov. 10, Johnson confirmed she isn’t going to let the broken bone affect her and Harry Shum Jr.’s performances.

“It's so funny, these little accidents always happen,” she exclusively told E! News following the show’s Grammys night. “Dance is a physical contact sport, and I want to always represent dancers as athletes. Just like any other professional athlete would get a little dinged up here or there, they would still go compete.”

“They kept asking me,” she added, like, ‘Do you want someone to dance for you?’ And I was like, ‘No, I love this dance. I've loved dancing this week with Harry. Like, no one is keeping me off the freaking dance floor.’”

And a good thing, too, since their salsa to “Die With a Smile” earned an impressive 29 out of a possible 30 points.

Plus, it helped that the people in Johnson’s life all have a nose for how to best carry her through the performance, despite the injury.

“I've been so supported,” the 32-year-old said. “So, I have to thank all the teams that were there for me last night. But yeah, it's just part of the show.”

Shum, however, wasn’t ready for his partner to brush off her accomplishment.

“It also speaks to you,” the Grey’s Anatomy star emphasized. “Dancers don't get enough credit for how much pain they go through—their feet, injuries in their knees, their back, and like your nose.”

He added, “And to say like, ‘Nope, I'm going to keep going,’ because you love to perform. But also, you work so hard for something and you said, ‘No, I'm not going to let this stop me if it doesn't need to.’”

And stop her it won’t, with the two ready to lead each other on a merry dance all the way through the semifinals.

“Jenna always has a strategy,” Shum emphasized, “and I follow whatever she has in store.”

Meanwhile, Johnson noted it truly takes two to tango.

“You are so smart when it comes to things,” she added. “I love that leading up to this moment, I think we were very thoughtful about what we wanted to do in the semifinals if we had the opportunity to be here. And I'm super excited.”
