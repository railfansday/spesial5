Tonight's meeting with Liverpool will stir up a mix of feelings for Club Brugge fans of a certain generation. The fixture conjures happy memories of the Belgian team's heyday in the 1970s, but also the pain of losing their only two European finals.

Brugge's Class of 2026 are doing their best to emulate their predecessors, though. After reaching the Conference League semi-finals in 2023/24, the club have qualified for the Champions League knockout stage in each of the past two seasons and look well-placed to do so again, with nine points from four games.

Ivan Leko's side responded superbly to their 3-2 defeat by Aston Villa in gameweek one, beating Italian champions Inter Milan 4-3 at the San Siro and following it up with wins against Lens and PSV Eindhoven. In fact, they have put together an 11-match unbeaten run — including 10 wins — since losing to the Villans.

Brugge's dangerman is 22-year-old German forward Nicolo Tresoldi, who has scored 15 goals in 16 matches this season, including five in the Champions League. Winger Jan Virgili has not featured since injuring his groin against PSV.

Those two victories over Brugge in the 1970s were part of a golden era for Liverpool, who won six major European trophies between 1973 and 1984.

Fast forward half a century and the Reds head into their third meeting with the Belgians after thrashing rivals Manchester United 4-0 on Sunday, in what was surely their best result of the Andoni Iraola era.

The six-time winners are above tonight's opponents only on goals scored, so a defeat would not only see them leapfrogged by the Belgians but also allow a cluster of teams to steal a march in the race for the top eight. Liverpool won their opening three matches in the league phase but suffered a shock 3-2 defeat at Fenerbahce in gameweek four, so they cannot afford to slip up again.

Ryan Gravenberch remains an injury doubt with a heel issue, while Cody Gakpo is unlikely to recover from his calf problem in time. Conor Bradley, Hugo Ekitike and Giovanni Leoni are also still sidelined.

This is likely to be a tougher game for Liverpool than some people may think, but we are backing them to claim a narrow win.
