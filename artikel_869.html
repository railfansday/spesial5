York City player pleads not guilty over alleged punch of Rochdale fan

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hiram Boateng allegedly hit a Rochdale AFC supporter after a pitch invasion by fans in April

A York City FC player has appeared in court after allegedly punching a Rochdale AFC supporter during a pitch invasion.

Hiram Boateng, 30, of Leeds, pleaded not guilty at Manchester Magistrates' Court on Monday to using threatening, abusive or insulting behaviour with intent to cause fear of, or provoke, unlawful violence.

The charge relates to an incident involving fans who entered the pitch during the final-day match between the two sides at the Crown Oil Arena on 25 April.

York City FC said it was "aware of developments" following the incident and added Boateng, whose trial is due to begin on 23 June 2027, "maintains the full support of everyone at York City".

Rochdale supporters entered the pitch during the match after believing their side had secured the National League title. The alleged incident happened during a second pitch invasion later in the game, after a late goal instead gave the title to York.

A spokesperson for York City FC said: "We are aware of developments regarding the incident involving Hiram Boateng during the game against Rochdale in April.

"We are continuing to work with him and all the appropriate authorities to address this and to understand the circumstances that led to this.

"Hiram maintains the full support of everyone at York City and is a much-valued member of the squad, both on and off the pitch."

Boateng is charged with a Section 4 public order offence which carries a maximum sentence of six months in prison.
