Mavericks vs Nuggets Preview and Injury Report: For the Cup!

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dallas Mavericks (10-7) are on the road Tuesday night in a pre-Thanksgiving matchup against the Denver Nuggets (10-5). It also happens to be a Group Play game for the NBA Cup! The Mavericks are riding a three-game heater (of a sort) where they've played some ugly basketball, but are winning anyway. Dallas last escaped Utah on Sunday after a near collapse in the final few minutes. The Nuggets are riding a three-game streak of their own, last holding off the Chicago Bulls in overtime. Here's the main things you need to know:

The injury report is familiar. PJ Washington is, as of this writing, still questionable with whatever illness he has. I have no insight as to if he plays. Then it's the usual cohort of players missing the game: Tarik Biberovic, Sergio De Larrea, Tobi Lawal, and Dereck Lively. Denver doesn't seem to have anyone on the injury report.

Both teams enter tonight 2-0 in Cup group play. Denver clinches the group with a win. Dallas would need both a win and Suns loss either tomorrow at Houston or when Dallas plays in Phoenix on Friday. Dallas has a TALL task ahead of them, the Nuggets are good on offense and in areas where Dallas stinks. The defensive approach of forcing turnovers is going to be a bear against a team with Nikola Jokic, one of the best passers alive. I'm looking forward to this one despite the late game.
