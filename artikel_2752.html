College Football Playoff rankings: Indiana plummets in latest poll before regular season ends

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Indiana football's loss to Washington sent the Hoosiers falling all the way down to No. 14 in the latest set of College Football Playoff rankings.

The Hoosiers moved up to No. 8 from No. 9 last week after beating USC, currently No. 15, at home. A pair of losses to N0. 21 Ohio State and No. 24 Washington have pushed Indiana all the way out of the field, with the No. 11 spot representing Indiana's lone shot to remain in the field.

Indiana will need substantial help to make its way back into this year's playoff field, either through losses from teams ranked above it or one of four scenarios that lands the Hoosiers in the Big Ten Championship Game, winning it if they get there and securing the league's automatic bid. We'll have more this week on what the Hoosiers need to move up.

Indiana has the second best odds to win the conference's final title game, sitting at +290 per FanDuel Sportsbook, behind Oregon at -105. The Hoosiers are no longer favored to make the playoff field, sitting at +220.

Indiana is set to take on rival Purdue in the annual battle for the Old Oaken Bucket this Saturday at 7 p.m. ET at Memorial Stadium in Bloomington.
