Chiefs elevate 2 practice squad players for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ahead of Week 11's home matchup against the Arizona Cardinals, the Kansas City Chiefs announced a pair of practice squad elevations for the contest.

Running back Nate Carter and center Pete Nygra will get the nod on Sunday. As elevated players, they will immediately revert to the practice squad after the game.

Second-year pro Carter made his Chiefs debut last week against the Atlanta Falcons as star running back Kenneth Walker sat with a groin injury. He carried the ball twice for six yards in relief of rookie Emmett Johnson, who finished the game with 171 scrimmage yards and two touchdowns. Walker is ruled out again, but rookie running back Jadyn Ott has no game status after missing Week 10 with a hamstring injury. Assuming Ott is available to spell Johnson, Carter may only be an emergency option against Arizona.

Nygra signed with Kansas City in April as an undrafted free agent out of Louisville. He has been a member of the practice squad the entire season after failing to make the final roster. Sunday will make Nygra's NFL debut.

Earlier this week, longtime Chiefs center — and current Sports Radio 810 WHB host — Tim Grunhard suggested that third-year pro Hunter Nourzad would start at right guard with starter Trey Smith in concussion protocol. Over three seasons, the Chiefs have seemed to view Nourzad as a center-specific backup while frequently bringing him in for six offensive lineman formations.

Having another center in Nygra available would suggests merit to Grunhard's report that Nourzad — and not Mike Caliendo — may truly get the start on Sunday.

Also on Saturday, the Cardinals downgraded rookie quarterback Carson Beck to out with back spasms before departing to Kansas City. Beck had been listed as questionable on Friday after missing practice. 40-year-old Josh Johnson will officially start at quarterback against Kansas City on Sunday.

Arizona also elevated veteran wideout Zay Jones from their practice squad.
