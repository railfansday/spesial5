Nnamdi Madubuike misses second straight practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Ravens had one player out of practice due to injury on Thursday.

Defensive lineman Nnamdi Madubuike (hamstring) was on the sidelines for the second straight day. Madubuike has started all eight games he’s played this season and has 22 tackles, a sack and nine quarterback hits this season.

Cornerback Marlon Humphrey was also out of practice, but that was due to being sent home by the team after making derogatory comments about the city of Baltimore earlier this week. Ravens head coach Jesse Minter said Humphrey will not play against the Panthers as well.

Safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) was limited for the second day in a row. Linebacker Mike Green (biceps), tight end Matthew Hibner (hand), guard Vega Ioane (knee, shoulder), and running back Adam Randall (neck, hamstring) remained full participants.
