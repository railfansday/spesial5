Sterling grad Presley Winters named CCIW Women’s Diver of the Week for Augustana College

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sterling grad Presley Winters named CCIW Women's Diver of the Week: For the third time this season, Augustana College sophomore Presley Winters was named the CCIW Women's Diver of the Week. At the Carthage Classic over the weekend, she took fourth in the 1-meter dive with a season-best score of 331.90, which leads the CCIW by more than 50 points. She also took third in the 3-meter dive with a CCIW-leading 316.95.

Winters also earned the award in Week 1 and Week 2.

She was the one-meter diving champion at the Augie Invite as the Vikings took first on Oct. 31.
