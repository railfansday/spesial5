NFL calls out Mike Florio, Chris Simms for 'inappropriate and offensive' comments comparing Mike Vrabel's mustache to Adolf Hitler's

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The NFL has called out both Mike Florio and Chris Simms for "inappropriate and offensive" comments they made on a recent "ProFootballTalk" podcast episode.

The league told Front Office Sports on Wednesday that comments the two made about New England Patriots coach Mike Vrabel's mustache being similar to that of former German dictator and Nazi party leader Adolf Hitler had gone too far. The league has reached out to NBC Sports about the comments, too.

"We found the remarks inappropriate and offensive," NFL executive vice president of communications, public affairs and policy Jeff Miller told Front Office Sports in a statement.

Florio and Simms were talking about Vrabel and the Patriots' 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich on Sunday in a recent episode. The conversation quickly turned into a discussion about Vrabel's mustache.

"He got the German mustache to go along with that right now, too, so he's hitting all themes there over in Germany," Florio said.

"You've gotta be careful trimming that thing. If you're in Germany, you've gotta be careful trimming that thing," Simms responded.

An off-mic producer was apparently thrown off by the comments, too, and Florio told him to lighten up.

"Gary's groaning. It's funny, Gary," Florio said. "It was 80 years ago, let us have some fun for crying out loud."

Mike Vrabel and the Patriots fell to the Detroit Lions in Germany this past weekend.

Neither Florio nor Simms actually invoked Hitler's name once during the exchange, but it's clear who they were referring to. Hitler oversaw the Holocaust, which killed millions of Jewish people and other minorities throughout Europe.

Florio has been a staple in NFL media for decades now, and his site, Pro Football Talk, partnered with NBC back in 2009. Simms was a quarterback in the league for five years himself, most recently in 2009, and he joined NBC Sports in 2016.

Vrabel and the Patriots sit at 5-5 on the season after Sunday's loss, which marked their second straight. The Patriots declined to comment to Front Office Sports about the exchange. Patriots owner Robert Kraft is Jewish, and has long led efforts against antisemitism.
