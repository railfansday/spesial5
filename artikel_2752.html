Embiid questionable, LeBron OUT for second of back-to-back in Brooklyn

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hopefully this is just all a part of the normal stagger.

The Sixers released the injury report for their second leg of this back-to-back against the Brooklyn Nets Monday and have listed Joel Embiid as questionable and LeBron James as out. The same reasons have been listed, right plantar fascia management and lower back/sciatica management, respectively.

Nick Nurse said ahead of their win over the Pacers that the team's load management program is what dictated Embiid missing that game. That would indicate he'd be good to go in Brooklyn, but the questionable designation raises an eyebrow a bit. Nurse also said earlier in the week he thought Embiid wasn't moving that great in some recent games.

The question was also broached to Nurse as to if it's better to play Embiid and James together in one leg to build chemistry. Nurse says that is something that's been considered, but the team's long-term management plans take priority to that. ESPN's Dave McMenamin just reported that James has asked the Sixers' coaching staff to be patient with him as he hopes to find his rhythm as the season goes on, hoping for a ramp-up in December.

The rest of the injury report hasn't changed. Adem Bona and Dominick Barlow remain out with their respective neck and back injuries. Brooklyn comes into this one with a clean slate of health. They're only listings on the injury report are two-way assignments, which includes the No. 28 overall pick in this past year's draft Joshua Jefferson, as well as 2025 first-round picks Ben Saraf and Drake Powell.

Should Embiid be able to go, this should be an easy win for Philadelphia. If he's not able to, things will get really tricky at the center position without James as an option there either.
