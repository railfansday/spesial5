Eagles’ Joe Milton unleashes ‘Bazooka Joe’ with bonkers TD pass to DeVonta Smith

The Philadelphia Eagles' offense remains severely limited, but there is one factor that could give them an intermittent boost for the remainder of the season. When Joe Milton becomes "Bazooka Joe," electricity pulsates through the entire team. A big arm and exploitable Dallas Cowboys defense created the perfect formula for an incredibly impressive Philly touchdown late in the first half of this NFC East Thanksgiving showdown.

Facing second-and-4 with a little more than four minutes remaining in the second quarter, Milton let it rip and linked up with wide receiver DeVonta Smith for a 50-yard score. FOX Sports broadcasters Kevin Burkhardt and Tom Brady could not contain their excitement, as a packed AT&T Stadium crowd tried to process what just transpired in the end zone. Milton's rocket put the Eagles within 11 points of the Cowboys and infused a welcome burst of energy into the visitors' sidelines.

Unfortunately, there were still a few minutes left before halftime.

Dallas marched right down the field and capped off an eight-play, 71-yard drive with Javonte Williams' third TD of the night. The Cowboys squashed the momentum that Milton had built with one mammoth throw and plunged the Eagles back into a three-possession deficit with four seconds left on the clock.

The 2024 sixth-round draft pick may need to uncork a couple more bonkers touchdown passes if Philadelphia is going to pull off a Turkey Day comeback. This is surely an unusual experience for Joe Milton, who was celebrating the holiday season with the Cowboys this time last year. Now, they are in his way.

Eagles fans are thankful for Milton's big-play upside, but they would like to see more of it. Dallas led 28-10 midway through the third quarter.
