Instant reaction: Broncos bamboozled by Steelers on Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers battled on the gridiron on Black Friday. With all the drama surrounding the Steelers, many thought the Broncos were in line to get their ninth victory of the season. Alas, that wasn't the case and Denver was shellacked on the road by Pittsburgh 27-5. Here are some instant reactions from today's matchup.

Anything that could have possibly went wrong for the Broncos this afternoon did. For all the talk about the Steelers potentially coming out flat, the exact opposite transpired. It was Denver who came out of the gate absolutely lifeless.

Quarterback Bo Nix played the worst game of his career. We have seen him perform bad in the elements the past two seasons. However, today's awfulness was on an entirely different level. Early on, Nix had a fumble that resulted in a touchdown for the Steelers in the first quarter. At the end of the second quarter, he threw an errant pass that was picked off by cornerback Jalen Ramsey and returned for a touchdown.

You can't spot an opposing team fourteen points. As I wrote earlier today, winning the turnover battle was going to be mission critical for the Broncos. They certainly wound up on the wrong side of that equation. Of course, the poor weather didn't help matters. That being said, it's no excuse for how poor he and the offense played today.

It was absolutely dreadful from start to finish. Though he certainly wasn't the only player that struggled. A handful of wide receivers dropped the ball which stalled out drives and any chance of a comeback. On defense, even Patrick Surtain II had an off day, and those rarely ever happen.

Overall, I'm still quite perplexed at how off kilter the Broncos looked. They have struggled to be consistent throughout the year, but outside of the opener against the Chiefs, this is probably the worst they have looked all season. Quite embarrassing.

NFL games are won in the trenches. Coming into today's matchup, it was well-known that the Steelers had a tremendous defensive front. Throughout the season, their trench players have proven they could get after the passer and be elite against the run. They dominated Denver's offensive line from start to finish.

Left tackle Garett Bolles hadn't given up a sack in over 1,000 pass blocking snaps. Today, he had two in the first half. Right tackle Mike McGlinchey also had a rough outing. Throughout the game, he allowed a lot of pressures off the edge and was beaten to his inside on several occasions by standout T.J. Watt. The interior lineman didn't fare much better either.

The running game? Absolutely absent. They needed to show out in this regard to take down a Steelers team that as undefeated at home. It was a poor game for rookie running back Jonah Coleman. It's almost impossible to win without a good running game, especially in the elements, and Denver's effort in that respect just didn't cut the mustard this afternoon.

On defense, Denver's lineman also struggled. With Eyioma Uwazurike and Sai'vion Jones out, rookie Tyler Onyedim and Jordan Jackson received more playing time. They didn't do well and even veteran nose tackle D.J. Jones struggled and was often blown off the ball. Being outclassed and outmatched in the trenches isn't a way to win football games.

Denver's Week 12 matchup provided them with an opportunity to gain some breathing room in the AFC West and the conference's playoff picture. The Kansas City Chiefs lost last night, and both the Jacksonville Jaguars and Cincinnati Bengals lost last week.

Unfortunately, the Broncos weren't able to capitalize on any of those after shooting themselves in the foot against the Steelers. With the Buffalo Bills now at 9-1, it will be hard for the Broncos to catch them and earn the top seed in the AFC. Unless they are able to take down the Bills at Christmas, the odds of that seem infinitesimal.

Next Sunday, the Broncos will host the Miami Dolphins at home who have only one win this season. The Broncos looked awful today. It was one of the worst games Broncos Country has witnessed in the Sean Payton era. There is really no other way to describe it.

In my opinion, they have to dominate next weekend and earn a decisive victory in front of a hometown crowd to get back on track. As of now, I think most fans can put a pause on any thoughts of this team being a legitimate Super Bowl contender until further notice. They have a lot more to work on that some folks may want to admit despite being 8-3 on the season. All three of those losses? Double digits to winning teams.
