Report: NFL Primetime documentary is in the works

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The recent passing of Tom Jackson has sparked much discussion about ESPN's NFL Primetime, which was for years a mandatory Sunday evening destination for football fans everywhere.

As it turns out, a documentary is coming regarding the show.

Via Sam Neumann of Awful Announcing, the final product is believed to be on track for a release in connection with the Super Bowl, which is being televised by ESPN and ABC.

It could be part of the game-day programming. Three years ago, CBS debuted You Are Looking Live! — a documentary about The NFL Today — before the launch of the official pregame show in advance of Super Bowl LVIII.

For those who were actively following the NFL in the late '80s, NFL Primetime provided the first opportunity to see extended highlights of NFL games on the day they were played. Years before the Internet, social media, Sunday Ticket, and RedZone, Primetime became appointment viewing for fans who wanted to see more about what was happening beyond the two or three games available in their local markets on Sunday afternoon.

And the team of Chris Berman and Tom Jackson became the tour guides for the opportunity to essentially watch all of the important plays from every game in a compressed but meaningful package, with Berman's cadence and the music accompanying the words creating the loose impression that it was all happening live. Even though everyone knew it wasn't.

It became a unique moment in time, which at the time didn't seem to have a shelf life. It's just the way things were, with fans having no concept that there would ever be a way to watch all of the games live or to see any and all relevant plays long before 7:00 p.m. ET. It was a new frontier that no one had reason to believe would not remain a permanent fixture of each and every NFL Sunday.

As Neumann explains it, Jackson recently made a surprise return to ESPN as part of the filming of the documentary. ESPN shared footage of the reunion on Sunday.

It's worth your time to watch it, if you were one of the millions who climbed aboard the Primetime Express every Sunday night of football season for something much more meaningful than a glimpse of the entire day of NFL action. And the documentary will undoubtedly bring back fond memories of the final years before advances in technology caused a narrow range of football options to explode into today's endless menu of choices.
