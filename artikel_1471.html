Rabiot grateful for Amorim’s trust and offers insight into renewal plea: “Makes us feel important”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Speaking to French TV, Adrien Rabiot has looked at the interest from Ruben Amorim in a renewal at AC Milan and praised the Portuguese.

It’s no secret that Rabiot is one of Milan’s most important players. Gerry Cardinale admitted that he’s something of a ‘franchise player’ already and the love from Amorim has not been kept quiet. Indeed, the former Manchester United head coach is a huge admirer of his.

After a summer of questions about his future, a renewal has seemed to be on the cards for the majority of the season and with continued good performances, the talk of one coming soon won’t go away. In fact, it could be completed before Christmas, with a deal reportedly close.

Speaking to Telefoot during the international break, Rabiot revealed a little more about his renewal and Amorim’s dream to offer him a new deal quickly into his tenure, as MilanNews have relayed.

“My excellent start to the season with good stats? It’s also positive to have players capable of being decisive without being attackers. It also encourages those around us. At Milan we have quite young players and it’s important for them to have role models in their everyday lives.

“I had been back from vacation for a week and he was already talking to me about a contract renewal. I told him: ‘Coach, calm down’ [he laughs]. But obviously, it’s important for a player to have this trust. The coach is counting on us; he has a high regard for us and makes us feel important.”
