PSG wants to extend Achraf Hakimi but…

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

PSG shows its muscles. With a very overhauled team, the French champion managed last night to score a 9th consecutive victory in all competitions. This time, it was Le Havre which was no match. Despite Josh Maja’s opener, the capital club never panicked, confident in its strengths to take 5 (5-1 victory) against the Normans, more than ever last in the Ligue 1 standings. Captain of the evening in the absence of Marquinhos, Achraf Hakimi again produced a great performance, in line with his start to the season.

With a hat-trick and two assists, the Moroccan was undoubtedly named man of the match by our editorial team (10/10). “It’s incredible for a full-back! My training? It’s that of a full-back. Afterwards the coach helped me a lot, by giving me freedom, to take space, to cover me when I am in attackhe reacted yesterday at the microphone of League 1+. I am happy and comfortable on the pitch. I feel very good. I feel comfortable playing with these teammates, it’s easy. On the pitch, I am well in front of the goal. I try to have fun.»

His trainer is also won over, he who has made him one of his base men. “We know its quality perfectly. He’s an attacker. He is higher than the attackers sometimes. He is the best right back in the world. I’m not excited about his three goals. What I like is to see him all the time at a very high level“, he explained at a press conference. No surprise then to know that PSG wishes to extend its contract, which currently runs until 2029, reveals The Team. It doesn’t matter what legal case the Moroccan is facing.

As a reminder, his trial for rape should be held in 2027, after the Court of Cassation confirmed his referral to justice. Presumed innocent, the side has always firmly contested these accusations. His club does not seem any more worried than that either, showing its desire to extend it. Except that according to the sports daily, Hakimi’s entourage has somewhat slowed down the enthusiasm of the Parisian management. He believes that there is still time before thinking about what comes next, especially since the last extension is only two years old. Simple temperance or first signs of a possible departure? Hakimi has never hidden behind the scenes his desire to one day return to Real Madrid…
