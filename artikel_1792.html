MN: Maignan ‘working towards Frosinone’ with training plan going smoothly

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mike Maignan will hopefully be back in goal for AC Milan this weekend, after initial fears that he could be out with an injury.

When Maignan went off in France’s game against Turkey, there was immediately a bit of panic regarding their options moving forward. Lorenzo Torriani was the only fit back-up and they’d not exactly been planning to invest in a goalkeeper.

However, they were reassured quickly. Initial tests done in France came back positively and Milan’s own tests were the same, ruling out any injury.

So, he was free to begin training again ahead of this weekend’s return to San Siro against Frosinone. Now, it’s simply a case of whether he’ll be ready to return for the fixture.

According to MilanNews, the Frenchman took part in the first part of training on the pitch today, as he was scheduled to do. So, the expectation is that his work will be ramped up slightly over the coming days.

He will be available to Ruben Amorim this weekend and with a few sessions to go, he could well be the starting choice. However, Torriani has been working too, so there’ll be no rush to have him back if he’s not 100%.
