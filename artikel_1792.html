10 man Billingshurst lose heavily at home to Hassocks

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A challenging first half for Billingshurst meant they fell to defeat against promotion challengers Hassocks. The away team were quickly out of the traps & on 7 minutes only a great block from Jamie Smith prevented the away side taking the lead.  It was a short term reprieve when 5 minutes later, ‘hurst gave the ball away and Omar Abdsami finished well across goalkeeper Fraser Trigwell. Worst was to follow when from a corner, a Hassocks header made it 2-0.  At this point the officials intervened & decided that the ball hadn’t crossed the line and gave a penalty for a handball against Nat Guest. The penalty was duly despatched by Lewis Finney to make it 2-0 against now 10 man  Billingshurst.  Within 2 minutes, it was 3-0 when Finney latched on to a free ball and scored from 25 yards. For the next 20 minutes, Hassocks continued to dominate play and it was little surprise that just prior to half-time, Dan Perry added to his tally against Billingshurst having scored a hat-trick for East Grinstead Town a few weeks earlier. 4-0 at half time.

Into the 2nd half, Billingshurst changed their shape & to their credit battled hard without creating a huge number of chances.  Hassocks continued to dominate but did not make that dominance pay but still ran out comfortable 4-0 winners.

Manager Eddie French said “I wouldn't normally talk about the officials but i am  personally disappointed with four big decisions. Look, Hassocks were  worthy winners but big early calls spoilt the game and in the end it was  just about damage limitation & pride.  An offside in build up to  their 1st wasn't given. They scored a 2nd from a corner that wasn't even  a corner, the resulting header went over the line, but then the  officials decided it was a handball but our player was behind the goal  line.

“This  resulted in a red card and they scored the penalty anyway,  so 2-0 down and down to 10 men. A leading arm in the face of our centre  back was missed and they smashed in the 3rd from distance. We move on,  not much we can do now but you need a bit of luck & help against the  top teams and we had nothing today.  I did enjoy the spirit &  togetherness"
