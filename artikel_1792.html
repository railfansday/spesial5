Ex-Premier League ref calls Mikel Arteta a ‘nightmare’ to deal with

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former Premier League referee Graham Scott has named Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta as the toughest person to deal with on the touchline.

Last month, Arteta received his second yellow card of the season during Arsenal’s 2-0 win over Everton but said after the game that he had a dossier of other managers committing similar offences which have gone unpunished.

The Arsenal manager now faces a one-match touchline ban if he receives a third yellow card.

Scott, who refereed in the Premier League for a decade before retiring in 2025, claims Arteta ‘does push all the limits all of the time’ and described the Spaniard as ‘hard work’.

When asked who was the worst manager to deal with on the touchline, Scott told The Athletic: ‘Well, the trite answer is all of them when they’re losing.

‘The honest answer, I’m afraid, I know whatever I say at this point just gets me pelters but it is Arteta. He is the hardest work.

‘He really does push all the limits all of the time. I don’t mind that in many ways, he’s trying to get a competitive advantage for his team. That’s fine. That’s part of his job. And of course, he’s entitled to do it, and it’s for us to place them, but he does make your life extremely difficult.

‘He’s out of his box [technical area] all the time. I watched him recently in the game against Everton and every time Everton had the ball anywhere near the touchline on his side, he was like joining in the press, his toes are on the touchline.

‘As fourth official, it’s a nightmare, because if you step forward and do anything about it, you’re being pedantic, being petty. He does get away with rather too much. The officials do need to be a bit tighter. And I was one of them who let him do that. I’m not going to deny that. But he was just hard work. Really, really hard work all the time in a way that others tended to be hard work some of the time.
