When is the Scottish Cup fourth round draw? How to watch as Celtic, Rangers and other Premiership sides enter

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It is that time of year when the Scottish Cup starts to come to prominence.

Twenty third round ties take place this weekend, culminating in the draw for the fourth round, which is when holders Celtic and the 11 other Premiership clubs enter the competition.

Here are all the details for the Scottish Cup fourth round draw:

The Scottish Cup fourth round draw takes place on Sunday, November 29, starting at 7.15pm.

The draw will be screened live on the BBC Scotland channel and the BBC Sport website. Former Hearts, Rangers and Motherwell midfielder Andy Halliday will make it.

Aberdeen, Celtic, Dundee, Dundee United, Falkirk, Hearts, Hibs, Kilmarnock, Motherwell, Rangers, St Johnstone, St Mirren.

Friday, November 27, 7.30pm: Largs Thistle v Ayr United.

Saturday, November 28, 3pm: Annan Athletic v Greenock Morton, Bo’ness United v Brechin City, Clydebank v Livingston, Cumnock Juniors v Arbroath, Dumbarton v Johnstone Burgh, East Kilbride v Clyde, Elgin City v Partick Thistle, Inverness Caledonian Thistle v Cove Rangers, Montrose v Hamilton Academical, Musselburgh Athletic v Airdrieonians, Peterhead v Edinburgh City, Queen of the South v Dunfermline Athletic, Queen’s Park v Spartans, Renfrew v Cumbernauld Colts, Ross County v Brora Rangers, Stenhousemuir v East Fife, Stranraer v Clachnacuddin, Threave Rovers v Alloa Athletic, Troon v Raith Rovers.
