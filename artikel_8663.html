Get the Bank of America® Business Advantage Unlimited Cash Rewards Mastercard® Credit Card if your business keeps a combined $50,000 or more at Bank of America and Merrill. Enrolling in Preferred Rewards for Business lifts the card’s 1.5% cash back to at least 2.25% on every purchase. With less banked there, a no-fee card paying a flat 2% earns more.

It’s a business Mastercard that pays one rate on every purchase, so it suits owners who would rather not track spending categories. Preferred Rewards for Business requires a business checking account at Bank of America, and your tier in the program depends on your three-month average balance across Bank of America business deposits and Merrill business investments.

On $60,000 a year in purchases, a business enrolled in Preferred Rewards for Business with a combined $50,000 at Bank of America and Merrill earns $1,350 here, by our math, against $1,200 from a flat 2% card. Owners with smaller balances earn more on their spending with Wells Fargo’s Signify Business Cash, which pays a flat 2% and has no annual fee.
