Icon Place Shibakoen Renovation / Gensler + Rockwell Group

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Text description provided by the architects. Gensler has renovated a 30-year-old office building in Tokyo's Shibakoen district, repositioning it as ICON PLACE Shibakoen, a hybrid building that combines offices, residences, and publicly accessible open space. The area sits close to Tokyo Tower, with cherry blossom-lined streets and a long history alongside green space, and the renovation was designed to connect the building more closely to that setting.
