KYY Unveils 18.5-Inch Laptop Screen Extenders With Two or Three Displays

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

KYY's 15.6-inch portable monitors are available on Amazon for Black Friday and Cyber Monday, ahead of the larger models' December debut.

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- KYY introduces two 18.5-inch laptop screen extenders for the U.S. market. The X90K adds one display on each side of a laptop, giving users three screens in all. The X90P adds a third display above the laptop for a four-screen workspace. KYY plans to bring both models to Amazon in the U.S. in December.

Each of the X90P's three Full HD (1920 Ã 1080) IPS panels has about 41% more viewing area than a 15.6-inch panel of the same proportions. A video editor can keep a timeline on the laptop, a full-screen preview overhead, and source clips and notes on the side displays.

The side displays adjust independently. When a presenter folds the top panel back to face a client across the table, a built-in sensor turns the picture right side up. The presenter can keep notes on the laptop. The aluminum frame has its own tabletop stand and folds to fit the included carrying case.

After the KYY display driver is installed, one USB-C data cable connects either extender to a compatible Mac or Windows laptop. The X90P gets power separately from the included 45-watt adapter.
