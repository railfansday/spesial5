BYD Barred from Selling Ti7 SUV in EU Because of a Copycat Design

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A Dutch court barred BYD from selling the SUV in the European Union, ruling that the company copied the design of the Land Rover Defender too closely.

A Dutch court has ruled that BYD cannot sell the Ti7 mid-size three-row SUV in the European Union, according to a report from Automotive News. According to the report, a preliminary ruling from the Dutch court found BYD guilty of copyright infringement, with the Ti7 ruled to be a dupe of the Land Rover Defender.

Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) brought the case against BYD earlier this year after the Chinese automaker announced that it would launch the car in the United Kingdom in 2027. The preliminary ruling marks a significant setback in BYD’s recent explosive expansion into Europe.

“In these summary proceedings, the preliminary relief judge rules that the Ti7 infringes the EU design of Jaguar Land Rover,” the court said in its November 20 ruling, AN reported. “The characteristic and distinctive elements of the design are reflected in the BYD Ti7.”

The Defender is JLR’s best-selling vehicle globally, with the automaker shipping more than 100,000 units last year. Both vehicles have boxy designs with flat rooflights, squared wheel arches, and, notably, body-colored panels in place of the rear side windows.

Unfortunately for JLR, the judge also ruled that the court does not have authority over BYD’s U.K. arm. To that end, JLR is reportedly planning to bring the same claim to the courts in England.

According to AN, BYD “strongly disagrees” with the ruling and has plans to appeal the result. “The BYD Ti7 is the result of BYD’s own independent design work and infringes none of JLR’s rights,” the company said. “We will pursue every legal avenue to demonstrate that this decision is incorrect.”

While the final ruling will likely take some time before it is announced, the Dutch court has prohibited BYD from producing the Ti7 at its new factory in Hungary, where it was due to begin mass production early next year.
