One Person Was the “Angriest” at Val and Jenna’s Elimination on ‘Dancing With the Stars’ and Had “Smoke Coming From Her Ears”

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The rest of the season should be interesting to say the least!

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were eliminated from Dancing With the Stars this week, a somewhat shocking plot twist given their consistently high scores. But apparently one contestant was extremely upset over their exit: Val’s wife Jenna Johnson.

“Val’s wife, Jenna, was the angriest of everyone,” a source tells The Sun. “You could practically see smoke coming from her ears when it was announced Val and Jenna were eliminated. She feels very protective of Val, just as he was very protective of her during the Carrie Ann firestorm.”

Despite heightened emotions, Val and Jenna seem to be handling their elimination just fine—though Jenna gave a post-show interview speculating that “online noise” may have played a part in them going home.

“I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it,” she told Extra. “I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”

Meanwhile, Val added, “Every week’s a shock. It’s very competitive. Lot of great couples out there, wishing everybody the best, gonna be rooting from home. Definitely a sad result, but no regrets, we had a great season.”

As for who they’re rooting for to win, Jenna cryptically said “Clearly as you see you can’t count anyone out in this competition. I have the best hopes and wishes for all of them, I hope they have a beautiful rest of their journey and I’ll be cheering them all on.”
