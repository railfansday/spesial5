Céline Dion Trades the Stage for the Stands at Metallica’s Las Vegas Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Céline Dion might have wrapped the first leg of her Paris residency, but that doesn’t mean she’s not still spending her time in arenas. This weekend, the Harper’s Bazaar cover star headed to Las Vegas to catch Metallica’s Life Burns Faster show at the Sphere, spending an evening soaking up some heavy metal with her sons, Eddy and Nelson Angélil.

Spotted in a box, Dion was captured rocking out to the band’s “Nothing Else Matters,” singing along and playing air guitar (even when she was the only one standing up in her section). Rather than wearing a classic band T-shirt, she dressed up for the occasion in a blue suit, featuring a fitted blazer with a wrap detail at the waist. Her hair was slicked back into a low bun, and she appeared to keep her jewelry minimal. Nothing could get in the way of her dancing, after all.

Back in September, the Grammy-winning artist kicked off her Paris residency at the Plénitude Arena, making an emotional return to the stage after a full six years off. Dion, who hadn’t performed a full-scale concert since her 2022 stiff-person syndrome diagnosis, hosted a total of 16 shows throughout the month, with a starry crowd that rivaled Metallica’s (think Rihanna and Michelle Obama).

She honored her host city with a wardrobe full of pieces from the country’s top couture houses, including a dramatic black Dior gown made custom by Jonathan Anderson and a dusty-pink Givenchy cape. In May, she will head back to Paris for an additional 10 nights.
