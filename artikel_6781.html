Kylie Jenner Dips Into the Roberto Cavalli Archives for Her Governors Awards Date Night With Timothée Chalamet

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kylie Jenner notably skipped the red carpet at the Governors Awards last night, leaving boyfriend Timothée Chalamet to walk it solo. But, of course, her gown was well worth the wait.

Once inside, Jenner revealed a stunning vintage Roberto Cavalli gown pulled from the designer’s Fall/Winter 2006 runway collection. The rare design came in a silky champagne satin, with tiny sleeves and a crisscrossing back. Little crystals and gems embellished parts of the gown, while a subtle floral print adorned it. As Jenner walked, a romantic train trailed behind her.

The new Victoria’s Secret angel wore the dress with a pretty, elegant blowout and a lined pink lip. She shared a photo of the look on her Instagram, but did not grace us with snaps of herself with Chalamet at the ceremony. Maybe one day.

The Dune: Part Three star was dapper in a classic black tuxedo from Giorgio Armani.

Photos and videos shared on social media show the two holding hands inside, as well as Jenner chatting with fellow guests, such as Penélope Cruz.

Since they began dating in early 2023, Jenner has accompanied Chalamet to many a starry red-carpet event. They made their striking red-carpet debut at the 70th David di Donatello awards in Rome, Italy, in May 2025, after having attended the Golden Globes and the Oscars together without walking the carpet. Months later, in December, they made a statement in matching orange Chrome Hearts looks for the red-carpet premiere of the actor’s viral film Marty Supreme in Los Angeles.

Their last joint red-carpet moment was in March 2026, when they attended the Vanity Fair Oscars party. Then, the couple contrasted in black and white looks, with Jenner wearing a body-hugging McQueen gown covered in inky beads, and Chalamet going for an icy Givenchy suit.

We can’t get enough of these two, and it seems they can’t get enough of each other.
