Shop Our Favorite Celebrity Looks of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Fall is here! If you’re wondering what to wear to embrace the drop in temperatures, we’re here to simplify matters with outfit ideas, courtesy of our favorite celebrities spotted on the streets. Consider this week’s edit your guide to everything from transitional fall looks to clever layering techniques, all infused with plenty of off-duty flair.

This week, celebrities embraced the seasonal chills, stepping out in their best fall outerwear—which for many meant a faux-fur jacket and jeans. Hailey Bieber wore Phoebe Philo’s sold-out shearling Dancer jacket with straight-leg Levi’s jeans and strappy mules for an event in New York, while Bella Hadid layered her faux Mango topper (also sold out!) over a sporty zip-up, bootcut denim, cap-toe heels, and Coach’s Chelsea bag while in Aspen. Julia Garner stepped out in a faux-fur-trimmed coat from The Andamane, pairing it with light-wash jeans, Loewe’s Amazona bag, and quilted ballerina flats. If there’s one cold-weather layer worth investing in this season, a faux fur jacket is certainly it.

Other outerwear inspiration came from Olivia Munn, who wore a minimalist black mac coat over a taupe turtleneck sweater and Ossou’s Flex jeans in an indigo wash—the perfect everyday outfit. Rihanna, also a Phoebephile, took a more playful approach to her black leather bomber and jeans formula, adding a dose of color with Dries Van Noten’s rainbow-striped sneakers and a coordinating oversized tote.

Keep reading to discover these ideas and more, bringing timeless classics to life with a few standout finds, all reworked by the stars and completely shoppable.

Hailey Bieber ushered in the season of statement outerwear with Phoebe Philo’s sold-out shearling Dancer jacket for an event in New York this week. She dressed up her favorite pair of Levi’s jeans with strappy mules, a sleek black clutch, and diamond studs. Consider this the perfect formula for date nights and holiday gatherings alike.

Looking for some easy everyday inspiration? Olivia Munn’s recent look is a good place to start. The actress wore a minimalist black mac coat over a taupe turtleneck sweater and Ossou’s Flex jeans in an indigo wash, finishing with black pointy pumps. We’d simply add a structured top-handle bag to complete this timeless pairing.

Jennifer Lawrence is leaning into all things lavish for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping press tour. Think embroidery, velvet, jacquard, feathers—nothing is off limits. Her styling trick? Balancing elaborate pieces with something simple, like a white V-neck T-shirt and jeans. Take a similar approach, adding strappy mules and her Completedworks earrings for a striking finish.

Bella Hadid is already in après-ski mode. The supermodel was spotted in Aspen, layering her sold-out, faux-fur Mango topper over a sporty navy zip-up and bootcut denim for a cool off-duty look, while Coach’s Chelsea bag and cap-toe heels added polished touches. Whether just off the slopes or city-bound, this one’s worth bookmarking.

Katie Holmes stepped out with boyfriend Jason Bard Yarmosky for Old Navy’s festive fête in New York in the ideal party-season pairing. Recreate the look with an oversized gray sweater, sequin-fringe skirt, and contrasting leopard-print kitten heels. The mix of cozy knits, playful sparkle, and unexpected pattern keeps the outfit feeling interesting without looking overly put together.

Julia Garner stepped out in a faux-fur-trimmed coat by The Andamane (another sold-out style), but you can recreate the look with a similar wrap silhouette. Ground the statement topper with light-wash jeans, Loewe’s Amazona bag, and black ballet flats for an off-duty take, then finish with a pair of oversized sunglasses from Side Eye for an extra dose of downtown cool.

Sarah Pidgeon demonstrated the art of set dressing at Chanel’s Watches and Fine Jewelry dinner in a matching gray shirt-and-trouser combination. She wore the shirt open over a white T-shirt, breaking up the monochromatic look, and accessorized with classic cap-toe pumps and a tiger’s eye beaded necklace. It’s the cool-girl take on a tailored two-piece combo.

Rihanna, another Phoebephile, took a more playful approach to her black leather bomber by Ms. Philo. Paired with baggy jeans, the casual look received playful contrast from Dries Van Noten’s rainbow-striped sneakers and a coordinating oversized tote, proving the most laid-back uniform can feel fresh with a little color therapy.

Minimalists can still have a little fun with evening dressing, even if it comes down to the subtlest details. Ella Rubin wore a black Chanel jacket accented with candy-colored stone buttons, pairing it with slouchy slim jeans and satin slingbacks for a dinner celebrating the house. It’s the kind of low-key glamour that works well beyond office hours.

Finish with a top-handle bag, oval sunnies, and a simple belt for an effortlessly polished look that transitions from day to night with ease.

Another take on the peplum trend, Louisa Jacobson chose a full look from Ashlyn’s spring 2027 show for a Tiffany & Co. event in New York. Until the runway look is shoppable, capture the idea with a curved cardigan from the brand and a slinky green tube skirt. Peep-toe mules, a petite shoulder bag, and diamond jewelry completed the ensemble—an elegant yet unexpected option for evening events and the many holiday gatherings ahead.

Meghann Fahy knows that when all else fails, an all-black ensemble never disappoints. The actress offered an easy everyday outfit formula while promoting The Good Daughter, wearing a leather coat, V-neck top, and velvet trousers, all in rich noir. Pointy pumps and shield sunglasses gave the monochromatic look a Matrix-inspired style point of view with a bit of downtown attitude.

Paloma Elsesser demonstrated how to make a single-tone ensemble feel more interesting, with texture and unexpected silhouettes serving as the secret sauce. The supermodel chose a military-inspired Junya Watanabe bomber jacket and sheer lace dress, both in olive green, for eyewear brand Gentle Monster’s latest launch party. Metallic heels, a conceptual handbag, and glasses from the brand brought even more dimension to the tonal pairing.

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley stepped out in Phoebe Philo’s burgundy leather bomber jacket, pairing it with a plain white T-shirt, gray pleated trousers, and pointed black ankle boots from Christen.
