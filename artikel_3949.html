Star Wars: Galactic Racer Reaches 1.5 Million Players, Teases Future Updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Developer Fuse Games has announced its debut title, the brilliant Star Wars: Galactic Racer, has amassed 1.5 million players.

The news comes via a recent social post, featuring the one and only Biddy Blas himself:

It's unclear if the number equates with sales or is purely the number of people that have logged in, but either way, it's a pretty impressive milestone for the arcade racing title.

Galactic Racer has amassed this amount of players in a little over a month, when it launched to an overwhelmingly positive reception.

Its relentlessly fast racing, silky smooth controls, and moreish roguelike structure made it all stand out, and speaking personally, it's easily one of my favourite games of 2026. I wrote about it recently, and used it as an excuse to mention Burnout far too many times.

Anyway, the post also suggests the team is not done with Galactic Racer yet. Fuse Games says it "can't wait to share more about the future of Star Wars: Galactic Racer", which is a very exciting prospect.

Many are hopeful for additional locations and courses, even more abilities and perks for build variety, and features like creating custom events. I'd love for all of this to be added eventually, but for now we don't know exactly what the team's plans entail.
