Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United release date moved up to February 4, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Square Enix has moved up Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United from its previously planned February 16, 2027 release date to February 4, 2027, nearly two weeks earlier than planned. It will be available for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store.

Here is an overview of the game, via Square Enix:

Romancing SaGa 3, the acclaimed JRPG first released in Japan in 1995, returns in a full 3D remake. Whether you’re a longtime SaGa fan or a newcomer, this reimagining offers a fresh take on a classic adventure.

Every 300 years, Morastrum, the Star of Death, rises, dooming all born beneath it except the mysterious Child of Destiny. Nearly twenty years after its last appearance, an insurrection in Loanne uncovers a far greater threat: the Four Sinistrals seek to reopen the gates to the Abyss. Eight heroes bound by fate must unite to save the world.
