DokeV Set for 2028 Launch, But You'll See It Again This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

But the Crimson Desert dev is embracing AI

Remember DokeV? The bizarre Pokémon-esque game announced over five years ago from Pearl Abyss? The developer has gone on to make Crimson Desert, but in a new financial report, it's shared its wild take on Pokémon is still coming.

Now its Charting the Unknown expansion for Crimson Desert is out, it's shifting more of its developers onto DokeV, and the firm is targeting a launch in the second half of 2028. Before the end of this year, however, it plans to reveal its latest "development progress".

DokeV was originally revealed at Gamescom Opening Night Live in 2021, a show run by Geoff Keighley. There is a good chance the re-reveal of the game will take place next month at The Game Awards, then.

Here's that wacky announcement trailer as a reminder:

Having shown it again, Pearl Abyss will have hands-on demos in the second half of 2027. It said: "We will continue to communicate with players by sharing updates at optimal timing to effectively highlight the game's direction and core strengths."

The problem is it sounds like the developer is embracing more AI development to achieve that.

In the financial report, Pearl Abyss confirms that it's "integrating AI technologies to enhance development productivity and overall quality". AI is being used across "the automation of repetitive tasks, content creation support, and test optimisation", and its use is being expanded to the team's "entire development pipeline".

"This enables a more efficient allocation of development resources and enhances overall production speed," it added.

For now, though, the studio claims AI serves "strictly as a tool to support concept validation and pipeline productivity". Humans are still behind the "core gameplay experience and actual content creation".

Crimson Desert got into hot water shortly after its release for including AI portraits in some of its environments. It did remove them from the game and issued an apology where it said the assets were "unintentionally included in the final release. This is not in line with our internal standards, and we take full responsibility for it."

Now, as it looks to the future, Pearl Abyss seemingly plans to lean even more into AI development.
