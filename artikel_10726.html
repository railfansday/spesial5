Lego reveals massive $450 Godzilla set with over 5,000 pieces

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lego has unveiled its first Godzilla set, confirming that the King of the Monsters is getting a 5,363-piece model with a $450 price tag.

The Lego Ideas set is based on Godzilla’s appearance in Legendary’s Monsterverse movies and stands over 27.5 inches tall with an attachable Heat Ray.

The set releases on November 27, 2026, priced at $449.99 in the US and £349.99 in the UK.

Following earlier leaks, Lego has now revealed the finished model, complete with blue dorsal fins, a movable head, and adjustable arms, wrists, and fingers.

The set also includes an interchangeable Heat Ray effect that can be attached to Godzilla’s mouth in place of his tongue. His tail can be built facing either left or right.

According to the Godzilla website, the model is the largest Lego Ideas set released to date.

The design originated from a submission by Lego Ideas user Matthew Esposito, known as MattE720, whose original concept reached the required 10,000 supporters after more than two years.

Lego has also confirmed a separate Godzilla Logo and Footprint set, numbered 40888, which will be available as a gift with purchase of the main model during its Black Friday launch, while stocks last.

The 359-piece bonus set features Godzilla’s footprint alongside a brick-built logo and miniature cityscape, complete with a Mothra detail.
