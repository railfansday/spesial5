Discord teases long-awaited mobile feature as it prepares major app upgrade

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Discord has confirmed that its long-awaited mobile account switcher is on the way, giving users the ability to move between multiple accounts without repeatedly logging in and out.

The feature will arrive as part of Discord’s new Rust-based client, which is currently being tested with a focus on improving performance across devices.

While Discord already offers account switching on desktop, mobile users have been waiting for the same functionality. Now, the company has shared an early look at how it will work on smartphones.

On November 10, Discord developer Stanislav shared a video demonstrating the account switcher running on an iPhone, showing how quickly users can move between profiles.

“Fast launch times are cool, but actual usage is what matters,” he wrote. “Check out the speed of the long-requested mobile account switcher on iOS on the new Rust client. And yes, it is just as fast on Android and Desktop.”

The demonstration shows a user opening Discord’s account menu and switching between profiles in a matter of seconds, without needing to manually enter login credentials each time.

Discord subsequently shared the video through its official X account, confirming that the new Rust client will include the long-awaited feature.

The account switcher could be particularly useful for people who maintain separate Discord profiles for personal conversations, gaming communities, or other activities.

Beyond account switching, the new client is also being developed with performance in mind. Stanislav’s comments suggest that Discord is focusing on improving both launch times and everyday responsiveness rather than simply making the app faster to open.

However, users will have to wait a little longer before trying the feature themselves.

“We’re still in testing for now but hope to get it out to you as soon as we can,” Stanislav added.

The mobile account switcher is the latest in a series of changes Discord has made in response to user feedback. In September, the platform rolled out several highly requested features after receiving more than 3,000 suggestions from its community, including the ability to pin DMs on desktop, expanded profile customization, and new moderation tools.

Discord also confirmed it was working on additional features, including larger Group DMs and the ability to hide profile badges. Those improvements are part of a broader effort to address community requests, with the company saying more updates are planned for the future.
