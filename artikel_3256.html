Jeff Bezos Describes How Amazon Decided On Robert Pattinson As The Next James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If anyone knows the glitzy, globetrotting world of James Bond it’s Bezos, Jeff Bezos.

The Amazon founder and world’s second-richest man today revealed that he brought his real-world knowledge of that milieu to the process of choosing the next James Bond, who, if you haven’t heard yet, is Robert Pattinson.

“I was heavily involved,” said Bezos at the Semafor Silicon Valley & The World conference today.

“We ran a really tight process, and we made the final decision on Friday,” the Amazon MGM Studios overlord revealed. “There were a big group initially, and we down-selected to five finalists, and then we did real old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists. Watch on Deadline

“It was really interesting. You know: full costume, real sets, other actors with them and three different scenes with each of the five finalists. And then we got together on Friday to spend a couple hours looking at all those screening tests or looking at all of that.”

While Bezos said, “all the actors were extraordinarily talented,” Pattinson stood out, even if they initially resisted selecting him.

“By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob Pattinson and his performances, and it was kind of immediately un-debated. Denis, the director, was…we all sort of gravitated to him. By the way, initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him because he’s already so famous. We sort of thought, maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown. But in the screening tests, it was so obvious: He is James Bond.”
