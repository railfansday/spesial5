Gang Dong-won & Kim Go-eun To Lead Netflix Korea Romance-Action Period Drama ‘The Twilight Between Us’ From Kim Won-suk

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gang Dong-won and Kim Go-eun will lead The Twilight Between Us, an action-romance period drama for Netflix.

Kim Won-suk (When Life Gives You Tangerines) is directing a script from writer Park Ji-eun (Queen on Tears) about heirs to a pair of houses locked in a century-long feud who are forced into marriage by royal decree. Further production and casting details haven’t been released at this stage.

The story follows Mu-yeong (Gang) and Su-ryeon (Kim), whose union is intended to end generations on conflict. However, it soon spirals in unforeseen directions. Mu-yeong, the illegitimate son of the House of Kang, carries the ominous title ‘Demon of the North’ and live sin obscurity despite his exceptional martial skill and noble heart. Su-ryeon, the only daughter of House Han, finds herself bound by a royal marriage ban after failing to become the Crown Princess, until her union with Mu-yeong changes her fate. Watch on Deadline

Netflix is also today announcing reality competition series Vocal Class Wars has gone into production ahead of a 2027 launch date.

The series follows on from Netflix’s existing Culinary Class Wars and is billed as a “vocal survival competition that pits ‘White Singers’ – who the producers are calling ‘established artists whose names alone define a genre’ – against ‘Black Singers’ – challengers who erase their names and identities to prove their worth through voice alone. A similar black-and-white format device was used in Culinary Class Wars.

The series reunites Kim Hak-min, the producer behind Culinary Class Wars, and Park Sung-hwan, the producer who devised the Just Makeup beauty-survival format, following their previous collaboration on Sing Again. Studio Slam is making the show, with Choi Hye-jung attached to write.

The Vocal Class Wars production team said: “Following Culinary Class Wars, we’re excited to expand the ‘black-and-white’ universe to a new stage — music. Watch for whether the legendary artists appearing as ‘White Singers’ can protect their reputation, or whether the ‘Black Singers’ set to shock the world will flip the script — expect another thrilling showdown and unpredictable twists in this next class war.”
