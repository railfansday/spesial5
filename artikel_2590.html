Report: Ravens to waive QB Kedon Slovis

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Ravens are reportedly making a change to their quarterback room.

Mike Garafolo of NFL Media reports that they will waive Kedon Slovis. Slovis was signed off of the Packers’ practice squad three weeks ago as the team scrambled to build depth in the wake of injuries to Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley.

Per the report, the Ravens would like to bring Slovis back to the practice squad if he clears waivers.

Slovis did not appear in any games for Baltimore, but served as a backup to Joe Fagnano and Jacoby Brissett over his brief time on the roster. The Ravens do not have another quarterback on the roster or practice squad, so they’d likely be in the market for another addition if Slovis does not wind up returning.
