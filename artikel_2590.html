FIFA confirms personal, business data recently stolen by hackers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Nov. 26 (UPI) -- FIFA on Thursday confirmed personal and business data has been stolen after its systems were hacked.

International soccer's governing body said a "limited set of personal and business information" was stolen in the cyberattack.

The data breach did not include documents related to the controversial FIFA Forward Enterprise proposal, the failed sell-off plan by FIFA President Gianni Infantino, the BBC reported.

The Handsome Boy Hacking Club, an online group, has claimed responsibility for the attack and posted screenshots last week of FIFA documents. The group says it stole more than 12 gigabytes of data, which include identity and immigration documents, executive information, financial records, and legal documents and internal governance records.

FIFA personnel reportedly said the hackers impersonated staffers to gain access to its systems and not because of a technical failure.

The hack comes three months after FIFA said "there is a concerted and ongoing effort by some to undermine FIFA and its President."
