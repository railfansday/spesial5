Real Madrid and Spain star Marc Cucurella knows he "can never play in Germany" following Euro 2024 handball controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Marc Cucurella has been booed by German fans ever since Euro 2024, when he controversially blocked a shot from Jamal Musiala with his arm inside the penalty area.

The referee decided against awarding Germany a penalty, and Spain went on to win the quarter-final 2-1 after extra time thanks to Mikel Merino's late winner.

Speaking to COPE, Cucurella reflected on the backlash the incident has generated, admitting that a move to Germany is no longer an option for him as a result.

“I know that I can never play in Germany. They remind me of it every time,” Cucurella said.

When it was pointed out that the refereeing team, rather than him, was responsible for the decision, the Real Madrid defender replied:

“Yes, yes, yes, without a doubt. But I don't know why they've taken it out on me. Well, that's how it is.

“I know I can't play in Germany. I'm very much loved there,” he added with a laugh.
