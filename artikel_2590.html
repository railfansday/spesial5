Ex-Colorado State AD, former players raise alarm over Jim Mora and staff conduct

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Things appear to continue to get messier for Jim Mora at Colorado State.

According to The Coloradan — part of the USA TODAY Network — nearly two dozen former Colorado State players are asking the university's governing board to address concerns in what they describe as "inappropriate behaviors" from Mora and his staff and "the lack of response by athletic department leadership."

Mora is in his first season at Colorado State after being hired from UConn last offseason.

The Colorado State players who wrote and sent the letter to the Colorado State University System Board of Governors on Nov. 25 include former Rams quarterback and athletic director Jack Graham, former defensive back Erik Olson and former linebacker Nate Kvamme.

The letter does not specifically ask the Colorado State University System Board of Governors to fire Mora or Colorado State athletic director John Weber, but brings forth their worry about the "welfare of the student athletes" in the program.

"As former CSU football players, we want to convey a sense of urgency and resolve to support the University in rebuilding the integrity, culture and reputation of the Athletic Department and our football program," the letter reads. "Our experiences at CSU delivered great lessons and guidance for our adult lives.

"We learned to compete and win. We had support to study and succeed in our academic careers. We were given the opportunity to be part of teams that represented commitment, hard work, integrity, respect and pride. We left CSU as better people."

Graham additionally brought up several "red flag" incidents that he believes are egregious enough for Colorado State to fire Mora "for cause" under the terms of the five-year contract they signed him to last December. These "red flags" include: the program's entire equipment room staff reportedly quitting at the beginning of the season or late spring due to "abusive behavior toward them by Jim Mora," damage allegedly done to the visitors' locker room following a loss at Texas State on Oct. 25, not believing Mora's side of the story on the firing of former Rams defensive coordinator Tyson Summers in the middle of a game against Oregon State on Oct. 3 and a physical altercation between strength and conditioning coach Shelton Bynum and offensive lineman Toriyan Johnson amongst others.

"Is the University providing the culture, leadership, support and resources these athletes need to be good human beings, strong competitors and lifelong proud alumnae of CSU?," the letter reads. "... We believe not; rather, they are seeing the antithesis of these critical values."

Under Mora this season, the Rams have the No.4 scoring offense in the Pac-12 at 33.7 points per game, but one of the worst defenses in the conference and country. The Rams rank dead last in the Pac-12 in both scoring defense at 47.7 points per game and total defense at 548.1 total yards allowed per game.

The Rams have won just once in their last nine games and have lost each of their last four games. In their four-game losing skid, Colorado State has been outscored by opponents 217-101.

Colorado State ends its regular season at 6 p.m. ET on Saturday, Nov. 28 against No. 21 Boise State.
