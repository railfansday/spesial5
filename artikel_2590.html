Titans place guard Peter Skoronski on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Tennessee Titans placed guard Peter Skoronski on injured reserve today with the hamstring injury that he suffered against the Cincinnati Bengals. Skoronski will miss at least the next four games. The Titans were already thin at guard, with Fernando Carmona and Jackson Slater already on IR. The line has still been really good with those 2 guys out. Will they be able to hold up as well with the absence of Skoronski?

Punter Tommy Townsend was also placed on IR. Thomas Morestead was signed to the active roster and will handle the punting duties while Townsend is out.

With both Anthony Hill and Cody Barton out for this game, the Titans elevated LB Dominique Hampton from the practice squad for tomorrow's game against the Jacksonville Jaguars. WR Xavier Restrepo was also elevated for the second straight week.

Tomorrow will be a tough test for a Titans team that will be missing a lot of starters on both sides of the ball.
