‘I’ve been a Democrat my whole life’: Alex Karp suddenly wants you to know that Palantir is progressive

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It was only a matter of time until tech CEOs rethought their alliances with the Trump administration and pivoted back to the left now that the blue wave has swept the midterm elections.

But while many had their eyes on larger tech figures closer to the president, like Meta’s Mark Zuckerberg or on-again-off-again presidential ally Elon Musk, Palantir CEO Alex Karp beat them to the punch.

In a sit-down interview with Politico, Karp soft-launched his avowed support for the Democrats and voiced concern over the current administration’s policies.

“I’ve been a Democrat my whole life. I grew up in a far-left environment. I know more about being a progressive than almost anybody on the planet, and I absolutely believe Palantir—all of our products are essentially progressive,” Karp told Politico.

While in the past, Karp has focused on supporting the military industrial complex through technology, he turned his focus onto economic injustice, suggesting Democrats have an advantage over Republicans when it comes to developing a solution.

“I’m really not pitching Palantir,” Karp said. “I’m actually pitching, ‘What are you going do about economic justice in this country?’ For many Republicans, there’s an orthodoxy that capitalism solves all problems. Maybe that was once true. I never thought it was true. It’s definitely not true now.

Past controversies, lucrative Trump-friendly contracts

The statement has undoubtedly raised some eyebrows on social media, particularly as the technology company—which Karp once referred to as “anti-woke“—has gained heat in the past years for its lucrative contracts with the Trump administration.

Perhaps Palantir’s most notable—and controversial—contract is the $30 million deal for ImmigrationOS, the software it developed to help U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) track and deport undocumented immigrants. Karp addressed the controversy during the interview, commenting on Trump’s approach to immigration enforcement.

“I was really happy to hear the president be honest that—you know, his words, I believe, were something like, ‘Yeah, we kinda went too far,’” Karp continued. “And I’m glad he’s saying in public now what I’ve been saying in private.”

Karp’s donation history is actually a mixed bag. This year, he donated $100,000 to Americans for Security PAC, a conservative political action committee, according to OpenSecrets, a nonpartisan nonprofit that tracks the flow of money in American politics. But his individual candidate donations show that he has financially supported Republican Rep. Jay Obernolte of California, as well as Democratic Sen. Mark Warner of Virginia and the Democratic Party of Virginia.

“My giving is equal Democrat, Republican,” Karp said in the interview. “I can tell you, without going into things I’m not gonna disclose, I’m very balanced, and everyone in the political arena knows it. And I’ll tell you what—I’m also not the village idiot.”

‘Pure survival grift’

Still, social media users aren’t quite buying it.

Some have taken the interview’s revelations as fodder for memes, using AI to transform Karp into a woke figure with pink-colored hair, piercings, and pronouns in sight.

But for many others, the contradictory messaging reads as opportunistic.

“Karp spent years selling surveillance and control tools to the regime, then flips the second the midterms hit. Pure survival grift,” a user said on X.

Another added: “The back peddling gonna be legendary with every passing week that Trump gets even less popular.”
