Wingstop reveals new Sizzlin’ Sichuan wing flavor with mouth-numbing heat

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Wingstop is introducing a new limited-time wing flavor that promises a different kind of heat, combining spicy ingredients with a distinctive tingling sensation.

The chicken chain announced Sizzlin’ Sichuan on November 16, revealing plans to bring the flavor to participating US restaurants later this month. However, some customers will have an opportunity to try it before the nationwide launch.

The release comes ahead of the holiday season, with Wingstop positioning its latest addition as an option for group orders and game day gatherings.

Wingstop’s new Sizzlin’ Sichuan flavor will officially launch nationwide on November 24, 2026, with Club Wingstop members receiving early access beginning November 20.

The flavor combines Sichuan peppercorns, spicy ginger, savory soy, and garlic. According to Wingstop, the ingredients create a mouth-numbing sensation that goes beyond the traditional heat associated with spicy chicken wings.

Sichuan peppercorns are known for producing a tingling or buzzing sensation in the mouth, which is a defining characteristic of Sichuan cuisine. Wingstop has incorporated that effect into its latest flavor, giving customers a different experience from its existing spicy offerings.

“We are dedicated to bringing uniquely Wingstop flavors to our fans, and Sizzlin’ Sichuan delivers a sensory kick unlike anything we’ve done before,” said Michael Skipworth, President and CEO of Wingstop.

“This isn’t just standard heat, it’s a physical sensation that’s equal parts craveable, shareable and exciting. It’s the perfect way to spice up the holidays this season.”

Customers will be able to order Sizzlin’ Sichuan across several menu items, including classic wings, boneless wings, chicken tenders, and chicken sandwiches. The flavor can also be paired with Wingstop’s housemade ranch.

The company is additionally promoting the new flavor as part of its Watch Party Bundle, which starts at $7 per person.

Those interested in trying Sizzlin’ Sichuan early can join Club Wingstop through the chain’s website or mobile app. Membership provides access to the flavor four days before its wider release, alongside other member-exclusive benefits.

Wingstop’s latest release comes just weeks after the chain teamed up with Sour Patch Kids to introduce its Wicked Punch flavor for Halloween. The limited-time offering combined a sweet-and-sour Redberry glaze with Sour Patch Kids Bitz, bringing actual candy pieces to its chicken wings.
