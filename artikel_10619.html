UK considering banning under 16s from eBay to “keep children safe”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The UK government is considering banning children under 16 from using eBay, Vinted, and other online marketplaces as part of its growing crackdown on platforms that allow young users to interact with strangers.

Britain is already preparing to ban under-16s from major social media platforms, including Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat, with restrictions expected to take effect in spring 2027.

However, the government could go even further, potentially preventing children from using popular shopping websites over concerns about online grooming.

According to Sky News, Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy confirmed that officials are considering expanding the ban to online marketplaces.

Asked during an appearance on the BBC’s Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg whether eBay and Vinted could be included, Nandy responded: “We are looking seriously at doing so in order to keep children safe.”

The Sunday Times reports that Depop and Facebook Marketplace could also be affected, with officials concerned that messaging features allow adults to contact and potentially groom children.

Research from UK regulator Ofcom found that 19% of children aged eight to 14 use eBay monthly, while 15% use Vinted.

The Children’s Society has also warned that vulnerable children using resale platforms have been targeted by adults offering money in exchange for explicit photographs or attempting to lure them with drugs.

Interestingly, eBay already requires account holders to be at least 18 years old, while Vinted generally has the same minimum age but allows parents or guardians to create supervised accounts for minors.

The proposals are the latest sign that governments are looking beyond traditional social media when deciding which online platforms children should be allowed to access.

Earlier in 2026, UK officials considered preventing children from talking to strangers in games like Roblox, Fortnite, and Minecraft.

Canada has taken a similar approach, introducing legislation to restrict social media access for under-16s before considering expanding its rules to gaming platforms.

For now, the UK has not confirmed whether online marketplaces will be included in its restrictions, with further details expected as officials finalize which platforms will be affected.
