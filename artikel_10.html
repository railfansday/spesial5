Tech executives' investment in Trump 'clearly paying off' as Huang reinstated at Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nvidia shares fell on Tuesday as the historic rally sparked by Jensen Huang's return as CEO quickly lost steam, with investors refocusing ​on the political ‌and governance risks surrounding his reinstatement. Speaking with FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney, The Hill Senior Technology Reporter Miranda Nazzaro says that the reinstatement of Jensen Huang at Nvidia represents "the culmination of a two-year push of tech executives to get closer to President Trump and their investment in the President is clearly paying off".
