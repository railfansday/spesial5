Huddersfield Town boss provides update on Derensili Sanches Fernandes and three other players ahead of Doncaster Rovers derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Cape Verde international came off late on in last Saturday’s home loss to Leicester City with a slight issue, but he is fine for the trip across the border into South Yorkshire.

Drury, whose side won in the Trophy at the Club Doncaster Sports Village earlier this term, said: "He's fine and available tomorrow. He got a whack on his foot and then a little bit of cramp, but he's fine.

"Every player is really important, so any injury that we get does hit us. The importance of Derens with his performances this season; with any injury, then you're always a little bit worried.

“He'd had a difficult week the week before, he'd obviously been on international duty and he'd been unwell. So energy-wise, it was difficult for him on Saturday, he'd not eaten very much, he was still suffering a little bit.

"I thought the way that he still wanted to play and committed himself to the game was amazing."

On other injury issues ahead of the game at Rovers, Drury added: "We've got a couple of little ones, but moreso from training and stuff.

“(David) Kasumu’s got a little niggle that we're going to have to keep an eye on. Samson Tovide has got a quad problem, which he felt in training Monday. So, yes, there’s one or two, but it’s just little niggles.

"Cam Ashia has a little niggle, didn't train yesterday, but came back into the squad and trained this morning.

"Kas is out and Samson Tovide's out. With Cam, I need to speak to the physios after the session.

"But it looked to me like he got through the session OK, without any problems, so he should be OK."

Town will move back to the summit, should they pick up three points in Doncaster and win by a three-goal margin.
