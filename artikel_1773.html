Premier League table predicted after fresh Leeds United, Hull City, Liverpool and Tottenham Hotspur boosts

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Opta have issued fresh Premier League forecasts for Leeds United, Hull City, Liverpool, Tottenham Hotspur and more

Data experts Opta have deployed their Premier League supercomputer after a hectic weekend of action in England’s top flight.

Leeds United secured their first win at Stamford Bridge since 1999, completing a remarkable 4-1 rout of their old rivals Chelsea. Emmanuel Emegha gave Chelsea the lead but Daniel Farke’s men roared back in style, cruising to victory courtesy of a Noah Okafor brace and efforts from Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronson.

Hull also claimed three valuable points as Oli McBurnie notched a stoppage-time brace to secure a 2-0 win over Brighton & Hove Albion.

Away from Yorkshire, Liverpool heaped misery on Manchester United with a 4-0 win and Everton were thumped 4-0 by Brentford.

Manchester City had to settle for a 1-1 draw with Fulham, AFC Bournemouth saw off Nottingham Forest and Aston Villa shared the spoils with Sunderland.

Coventry City were 3-2 victors over Crystal Palace, Newcastle United drew 2-2 with Arsenal and Tottenham Hotspur swept Ipswich Town aside.

With the latest results taken into account, Opta have predicted how the Premier League table will look at the end of the season.
