Frank Leboeuf makes shock Manchester United relegation prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Manchester United have the look of genuine relegation candidates, according to former Chelsea defender Frank Leboeuf.

The Red Devils dropped into the bottom three on Saturday following Nottingham Forest’s shock victory against Manchester City at the City Ground.

United host Aston Villa at Old Trafford this afternoon and defeat would see Michael Carrick’s side reside in the drop zone over the course of the upcoming international break.

The former United midfielder oversaw a dramatic upturn in results when he was appointed interim boss last term and a superb run culminated in Champions League qualification.
