Walsh lauds Chelsea’s ‘massive’ Champions League win over PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Keira Walsh is confident Chelsea are in an excellent spot after their "massive" Women's Champions League victory over Paris Saint-Germain.

Veerle Buurman turned home from Lucy Bronze's delivery in the 87th minute at Plough Lane on Wednesday.

Sjoeke Nusken had just seen an effort cleared off the line, with Buurman converting from the resulting corner.

While Chelsea had 17 shots and created chances worth 2.67 expected goals, Sonia Bompastor's team were not at their free-flowing best.

Yet they got over the line to avoid back-to-back goalless draws and, in the process, move top of the Champions League standings.

"It was a massive win. It was tough for us," Walsh told UEFA.

"They defended really well and they're aggressive. It was a difficult game physically, but to get the win was really important. Hopefully it will push us into the top four.

"We spoke about it before the game about wanting to finish the evening on top. We are in a good spot going into the last game."

Chelsea have kept a clean sheet in all five of their Champions League outings this season, and Walsh said: "Whoever plays in the back three, we're really solid. It's something we've concentrated on compared with last season."

The Blues have now kept six straight clean sheets in the competition, which is the longest such run by any team since Paris Saint-Germain in 2021 (six games).

Chelsea are unbeaten across their last 29 group stage/league phase games in the Champions League (W22 D7), while they have never lost a home group stage game in the competition (W13 D4).

However, goalscorer Buurman believes the Blues must be much better.

"A good team performance. We tried really hard and that I can score a goal is really important for the team," she told Disney+.

"We had the right spaces on the outside, it’s just the crossing and the finishing that needs to be a little better."

PSG, meanwhile, have won just one of their last 13 Champions League outings, and are winless across their last seven away games in the competition (D3 L4).

Emilie Osteras told Disney+: "It hurts a lot. We are disappointed. We really wanted the one point. We had a good game and did well defensively, so we are disappointed now.

"I think we did what we set out to do. Everyone went for the plan we had. We created a lot of chances and could have had a goal."
