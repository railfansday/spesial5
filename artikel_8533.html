Use Safeway forU if you buy most groceries at Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, or a sister chain, since it’s free and its clipped coupons cut prices below the member price. Its Points are worth 1% to 1.5% of your grocery spending. Redeem them by the end of the month after you earn them, or they expire.

Safeway forU is the free loyalty program of Albertsons Companies, so one account works at Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Acme, Shaw’s, and their sister chains. Member prices need only your phone number at checkout, but the app’s digital coupons and personalized deals count only when you clip them before you pay.

Anyone who buys most groceries at these chains should use forU on every trip, since a $150 weekly shop of eligible groceries earns 150 Points, about $2.25 off at the 1,000-Point reward by our math, plus what the coupons save. Occasional shoppers can skip the app and enter their phone number at checkout for member prices.
