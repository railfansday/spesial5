'Ellen DeGeneres Show' Producer Matthew Wright's Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Producer Matthew Wright's cause of death has been revealed after his sudden passing late last month.

According to a death certificate obtained by PEOPLE, Matthew died from cardiopulmonary arrest. Hypertension was listed as the underlying cause ... while the contributing conditions were identified as obstructive sleep apnea and Class II obesity.

According to a GoFundMe supporting Matthew's family, he passed away on October 30 "suddenly but peacefully in his sleep" in Los Angeles.

His family called his death an "unfathomable tragedy" ... and they've been struggling to cover the costs of bringing his body home to New York, since he and his husband, Justin, were both laid off over the summer.
