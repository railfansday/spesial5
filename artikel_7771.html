Trial opens for suspect whose multiple email threats include ‘skinning’ federal judge

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A series of threatening emails sent to the Northern District of California last year included “skinning U.S. District Judge Charles Breyer” and accused judges from the Golden State of being “enemy combatants.” (Image from Shutterstock)

A series of threatening emails sent to the Northern District of California last year included “skinning U.S. District Judge Charles Breyer” and accused judges from the Golden State of being “enemy combatants.”

David Brooks Pokorny, 45, of El Cerrito, California, allegedly sent 11 email threats in a span of 44 minutes to assault, kidnap and murder Breyer, a senior judge for the Northern District of California, according to U.S. Assistant Attorney Kent Stejskal in opening statements Tuesday.

“Words within those 11 emails constitute what are called true threats,” said Stejskal, according to coverage by Courthouse News Service.

He allowed the 14-person jury to view snippets of the emails during the trial. Pokorny is charged with making felony threats against a judicial officer and faces a maximum sentence of 10 years in prison.

According to Pokorny’s public defender John Reichmuth, his client’s words were “truly shocking, truly offensive, truly ugly, but not true threats.”

Reichmuth said the emails were free speech, and the prosecution would have to prove beyond a reasonable doubt that Pokorny, who has a history of sending threats to public officials and media outlets, meant to perform the actions described in his emails.

Presiding U.S. District Judge Jon Tigar of the Northern District of California noted that the prosecution does not have to prove that Pokorny intended to carry out the threat for it to supersede the protections of First Amendment free speech rights.
