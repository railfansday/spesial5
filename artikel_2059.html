Looking at this Newcastle United team v Arsenal.

Matthias Jaissle and his players looking to get back to winning ways at St James’ Park.

Or maybe more realistically, pick up a point.

United up against the reigning champions and current league leaders who have won nine of ten Premier League matches.

It is nine weeks since Newcastle United picked up any points at St James’ Park, thanks to a combination of defeats to Villa and Everton plus ridiculous international breaks meaning only two home games since the win over Hull City on 19 September.

A positive performance AND result would certainly lift the mood.

Especially with you know who coming back to St James’ Park for a very first time after jumping ship.

As a reminder, this was the Newcastle United team v Fulham two weeks ago:

Hornicek, Dedic, Thiaw, Burn, Hall, Gonzalez, Steur, Barnes, Wissa, Murphy, Osula

Ewen Jaouen remains out and on Thursday Matthias Jaissle confirmed Joelinton is also still an absentee.

However, it sounded like apart from those two, the NUFC boss has a full squad to choose from, unless there are any nasty surprises we weren’t aware of.

This is my predicted Newcastle United team v Arsenal:

Hornicek, Dedic, Thiaw, Burn, Hall, Gonzalez, Steur, Miley, Fernandez-Pardo, Elanga, Osula

If I am proved correct it would mean three changes from two weeks ago.

My thinking is that especially against Arsenal, we need another body in midfield and putting Lewis Miley in with Nico and Sean Steur can make us competitive in the middle of the pitch.
