Chelsea predicted lineup vs. Leeds: Pedro, Palmer, Rogers decisions for Xabi Alonso

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

By Oliver Thomas | 19 Nov 2026 16:05, Last updated: 20 Nov 2026 21:27

Chelsea will be without five players for Saturday’s Premier League clash with Leeds United at Stamford Bridge.

Star striker Joao Pedro remains sidelined with a thigh injury and is joined in the treatment room by Jorrel Hato (back), Reece James (knee), Maxence Lacroix (hamstring) and Jamie Gittens (ankle).

However, Moises Caicedo (illness) and Josh Acheampong (unspecified) have recovered from minor issues. Emiliano Martinez, meanwhile, has been out for four games with a broken jaw sustained in the win against Everton last month, but the Argentine represented his country during the international break and should be ready to start in goal ahead for Mike Penders on Saturday.

Acheampong could return to play alongside Wesley Fofana and Levi Colwill in a back three, unless Xabi Alonso favours sticking with a four-man defence with Marco Palestra and Pep Chavarria as full-backs.

Jordan Henderson made the 700th appearance of his club career in the 1-0 defeat to his former side Sunderland a fortnight ago and he will hope to continue in centre-midfield role next to Caicedo and or Romeo Lavia.

Depending on Alonso’s formation, Pedro Neto could operate either out on the right flank or in a central attacking role next to Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer, while Estevao Willian is another attacking option pushing to start.

In the absence of Pedro, Danny Welbeck will hope to earn a place up front after netting a brace in Chelsea’s 6-3 EFL Cup win over Leeds in September. However, Alonso may prefer to use both Rogers and Palmer in central positions – Palmer also scored twice in that cup tie against Leeds while Rogers provided four second-half assists.

Martinez; Acheampong, Fofana, Colwill; Neto, Caicedo, Henderson, Palestra; Estevao, Rogers, Palmer
