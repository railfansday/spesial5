Southampton had scouts attending in Aberdeen to watch Rangers talent David Odunukan on Friday, sources have told Football Insider.

The highly-rated defender started as Rangers Under-17s played Aberdeen U17 at Cormack Park, with the Gers coming from behind to win 3-1.

Odunukan, 15, has represented Scotland at the U16 level, but was recently called up by England for the U16 Val de Marne tournament in France.

He started against Italy U16 at the tournament, but would fail to feature against Japan or France.

Rangers will hope to keep hold of Odunukan, who joined the club in 2025 and has emerged among the brightest sparks in his age group.

It is unclear how advanced Southampton's interest is at this stage, with other clubs potentially also keeping tabs on the youngster.

Odunukan joined Rangers from Motherwell in 2025, having spent his youth career with the Steelmen before the switch.

The defender, who most commonly appears in the centre of the defence, was born in London but moved to Scotland as a young child.

It is yet unclear which international team the 15-year-old will eventually represent, having spent time with both Scotland and England.

Odunukan appears to be the latest in an effort to recruit young Scottish talent at Southampton.

16-year-old defender Harris Afzal joined the Saints from Queens Park FC earlier this year, having already made senior appearances.

Rory Whittaker, another defender, arrived in 2025 from Hibernian, and has featured regularly for the U21s since.

As the hunt for younger and younger talents has progressed over the years in the Premier League, nowhere in the UK is safe.

Scottish clubs have been hit especially hard, with countless talents to have made the trip down south in recent years.

The likes of Alfie Osborne, Harry Grierson, Evan Mooney and Dara Jikiemi, among many others, have made the move just this year.

And there appears to be no end in sight for the drain, which will continue to plague Scottish clubs for years to come.

It remains to be seen whether David Odunukan will be the next in line to make the move - Rangers hope not.
