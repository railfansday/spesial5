Middlesbrough make £70m Will Lankshear transfer decision as major Man Utd, Arsenal update emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Middlesbrough striker Will Lankshear is a player who is set to dominate the headlines over the next couple of months with the January transfer window drawing closer, as Boro set their valuation on the Englishman amid interest from an array of European clubs.

Lankshear, 21, has enjoyed an incredible start to life at Middlesbrough following his move from Tottenham Hotspur in the summer, with 15 goals in 16 games across all competitions, announcing himself as one of the best players in the Championship in the process.

Naturally, the young forward hasn't quite managed to go under the radar; instead, the exact opposite, as Boro have made their stance on Lankshear abundantly clear to interested parties.

According to the Sun Sport, Middlesbrough have slapped a £70 million price tag on Lankshear ahead of the January window, hoping to deter potential suitors.

If any club were to seal a deal worth that amount for the Englishman in the next couple of months, he would become the second most expensive export in Championship history after Mateus Fernandes' £85 million move from West Ham United to Tottenham Hotspur in the recent summer window.

It was Spurs who decided to part ways with Lankshear in the summer, with Boro paying an initial £10 million, with the potential for a further £10 million in add-ons, for the forward.

That appears to be a bargain already, as though Middlesbrough will surely be tempted by the enormous profit they can make on him already; a decision on his future appears to have already been made by Steve Gibson and Kim Hellberg.

According to TEAMtalk, Middlesbrough dismiss any suggestion they could be persuaded to part ways with Lankshear in January, though the forward is not short of interest.

TEAMtalk reports that Manchester United, Brighton and Hove Albion, Everton, Newcastle United, Sunderland, Leeds United, Arsenal, and Borussia Dortmund are all closely monitoring the situation on Teesside, though Middlesbrough's latest stance may set back their pursuits.

Lankshear previously spent time in the youth system of Arsenal before moves to Sheffield United and Spurs eventually followed, and though the Gunners will be frustrated at missing out on the youngster now, they are looking to make amends by bringing him back to the Emirates.

Man United are looking for their new star striker for years to come, and Lankshear could well be the heir, though he won't come cheap, and whether he would consider a move to the Red Devils at this stage remains to be seen.
