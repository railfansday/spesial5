Arsenal vs Hull City preview: Gunners face draw specialists at the Emirates

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Arsenal have the opportunity to strengthen their position in the Premier League table when they welcome Hull City to the Emirates Stadium on Saturday.

Mikel Arteta’s side secured a three-point lead after their 1-0 victory over Liverpool at Anfield and then followed it up with a 2-1 win in the Champions League against Slavia Prague on Wednesday.

The Gunners have won four games in a row but are looking to stretch their lead to six points ahead of Manchester City’s clash against Nottingham Forest later on Saturday. They are undefeated at home this season.

However, Hull will be looking to cause problems in North London.

Sergej Jakirovic’s side sit 11th after collecting 11 points from their opening nine league matches.

They have drawn five times, sharing the highest number of stalemates in the division. Three of their last four league fixtures have also ended level.

They drew 2-2 with Ipswich Town in their last game, but they needed to get back on track after falling behind. Recently they have failed to convert good performances into wins.

Despite Arsenal expecting to dominate possession, Hull’s 5-man defence may cause them problems.

The visitors have also had defensive issues recently, as they have failed to keep a clean sheet in their last seven games in all competitions. This may lead to chances being created for the Gunners attackers.

Arteta has to deal with his squad after the midweek trip to Prague. William Saliba is back in contention after recovering from his back injury but Cristhian Mosquera may stay in the squad if he is not rushed back in.

Ezri Konsa is a doubt after being withdrawn from Wednesday’s game with a knee problem. Bukayo Saka is also still sidelined as is Max Dowman.

Hull will be missing Matt Crooks who is suspended. Eliot Matazo, Matt Targett, Darko Gyabi, Ilyas Ansah and Charlie Hughes are also expected to miss out.

Raya; Timber, Mosquera, Gabriel, Calafiori; Guimarães, Rice; Madueke, Ødegaard, Tzolis; Havertz.

Tzolakis; Coyle, Ajayi, Egan, Mendy, Giles; Belloumi, Iroegbunam, Slater, Stroud; McBurnie.

Arsenal will face Hull City at the Emirates Stadium on Saturday, November 7. Kick off is at 3pm GMT.

Despite the fact that Hull has a tendency to draw, Arsenal remains a strong favorite to win this match. Arteta’s team will want to earn as many points as possible ahead of the international break.
