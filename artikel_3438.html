Nintendo Switch Online Adds Another GameCube Classic

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nintendo Switch Online's expansive library of retro games is a treasure trove of nostalgic experiences, and it just got a little bit better. That's because the 2001 GameCube racing game Wave Race: Blue Storm has just been added to the Nintendo Switch Online Plus Expansion Pack library. With the chilly season fast approaching, it's the perfect excuse for a little tropical getaway from the comfort of your couch.

While I often think of Wave Race as a series that got its start on the Nintendo 64, the first Wave Race actually came out all the way back in 1992 for the Game Boy. The N64 version marked a big leap forward with fully 3D environments, and Wave Race: Blue Storm goes a step further, thanks to the addition of unique weather conditions to mix up the action. Wave heights and visibility will vary depending on conditions, which can be sunny, foggy, rainy, or choppy. In addition to the weather, there are also hazards to keep an eye out for on each course, like leaping killer whales or trees falling into your path after being struck by lightning.

Blue Storm has an eclectic mix of courses too, like a Venice-inspired city, a frosty blue glacier, and a jungle river surrounded by ancient ruins. There's even one level where you bust through an intricate stained-glass window. Take that, church!

Blue Storm includes five modes for your sporting pleasure. Championship mode is the core single-player experience, where you'll go head-to-head with seven other riders across four courses, doing three laps on each one. Time Attack mode is exactly what it sounds like: a way to familiarize yourself with one course and winnow down your lap times. In Stunt Mode, you'll need to perform sick tricks and jumps across courses full of ramps and rings instead of simply getting to the finish fastest. Multiplayer provides couch co-op fun for up to four players, while Free Roam lets you explore the courses without any time pressure.

November marks the 25th anniversary of the GameCube's 2001 debut, and Wave Race: Blue Storm was one of its launch titles. It was the third-best-selling launch title for the platform in North America, behind Luigi's Mansion and Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader. Sadly, Nintendo hasn't revisited the series since, scrapping plans for a Wii installment that would have used both the Wiimote and the Balance Board peripheral. It's not too likely we'll see another Wave Race anytime soon, but it's definitely worth taking Blue Storm for a test-drive if you're a Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack member.
