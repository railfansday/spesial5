‘GTA 6’ Has At Least 9 Distinct Endings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

(Don’t worry, this post contains no specific spoilers about the possible “GTA 6″ endings.)

With a reported play-through time of at least 80 hours to complete its primary storyline alone, it’s safe to say that most “GTA 6” players have yet to reach the end of the line for Jason Duval (Dylan Rourke) and Lucia Caminos (Manni Perez).

But while you continue to work your way through the criminal world in Vice City, it could be good to know that when you do get to the game’s conclusion, there are at least nine possible final outcomes to the story that Variety has confirmed. That news is sure to please fans who have endured the 13 year wait between “Grand Theft Auto V” and “Grand Theft Auto VI” and paid at least $80 for the standard edition of the game (the “ultimate” costs $100) and want to make sure they got their time and money’s worth out of the Rockstar Games title. Online speculation suggests there are even more possible endings, with rumors of 18 outcomes spreading across social media. Rockstar Games has not commented on the total number of options.

The number of confirmed “GTA 6” endings fulfills a promise repeatedly made by Rockstar Games throughout the game’s marketing campaign, where the importance of player choice was addressed over and over. The developers made it very clear in all promotion that how you chose to play the game as Jason and Lucia would ultimately have major consequences on the game experience you received. This aspect of the game was one of the key reasons that media industry analysts anticipated the title would not only be the biggest game release of all time, but the biggest release for any piece off entertainment ever.

As Variety previously reported, “GTA 6″ currently sits at a 99 aggregate score from critics on Metacritic, tying it with 1998’s “The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time” for the platform’s highest-rated game of all time. “GTA 6″ has also been nominated for several awards at the upcoming Game Awards, including Game of the Year.

Following “GTA 6’s” Nov. 19 debut, Rockstar Games parent company Take-Two Interactive announced the game reached $3.8 billion in its preorder and first-day sales alone, though number of units sold has not been confirmed. The title was available across PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S and X consoles (as well as Xbox One through Xbox’s Cloud Gaming service) but is not currently offered to PC gamers.

According to PlayStation, the game has racked up more than 39 million unique players on PlayStation 5 consoles as of Monday, setting a record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation. Xbox has not yet disclosed comparable gameplay figures.
