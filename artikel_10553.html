Forget GTA 6, Taylor Sheridan’s New 8-Part Crime Series Officially Releases Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The impact of Grand Theft Auto VI truly can't be overstated. The latest installment of Rockstar Games' franchise has been dominating the conversation as of late, as players discover all the debauchery that can be found in the fictional Vice City. If GTA 6's high-stakes antics somehow leave you wanting more (or you're just waiting to get the right console to play the game on in the first place), the latest Taylor Sheridan epic to hit the small screen might be able to help.

Frisco King, the new crime television series created by Sheridan, is set to debut on Paramount+ on Friday, December 18th. While that day is already stacked from a pop culture standpoint, thanks to the "Dunesday" double feature of Dune: Part Three and Avengers: Doomsday, the premiere episodes of Frisco King will still offer an interesting set of stakes.

Led by Oscar-nominated actor and Marvel Cinematic Universe star Samuel L. Jackson, Frisco King is a spin-off of Tulsa King, Sheridan's hit series that stars Sylvester Stallone as a mob boss setting up a criminal enterprise in Oklahoma. Tulsa King has already run for four seasons since it first premiered in 2022, and was renewed for a fifth season this past October. Now, the series' general concept will be headed to the Dallas-Fort Worth suburb of Frisco — an area that is definitely a lot less intense than any of the locales featured in Grand Theft Auto, but will still be fun to see Jackson explore.

According to Paramount Television Studios' official description for the series, Frisco King follows seasoned New Orleans gangster Russell Lee Washington Jr. (played by Jackson) as he lands in Frisco, Texas, and discovers a hustler’s playground hidden beneath the polished surface of American suburbia. Recruiting an ambitious protégé, a loyal driver, and a growing network of eccentric partners, Lee transforms small hustles into big business. But, every new score raises the stakes, and as his influence grows, so do the risks, forcing Lee to stay one grift ahead. Frisco may not know it yet, but its new king has arrived.

The cast of Frisco King also includes Asa Germann (Gen V, Scream 7) as Teddy, a sharp, savvy, entrepreneurial college dropout who becomes Lee’s protégé; Kai Caster (Spider-Noir, Yellowstone) as Keith, a Texas-bred polite valet at the local Sandman Hotel recruited by Lee; Lilah Pate (The Runarounds, The Summer I Turned Pretty) as London and Savanna Gann (Rebel Moon - Part One: A Child of Fire, His & Hers) as Avery, local cheerleaders recruited by Lee who are not afraid to use their charm to get what they want. Stallone will also reprise his Tulsa King role of Dwight "The General" Manfredi.

Jackson executive produces Frisco King alongside Stallone, Sheridan, David C. Glasser, Ron Burkle, David Hutkin, Bob Yari, Christina Alexandra Voros, Michael Friedman, LaTanya Richardson Jackson and Keith Cox. Voros and Friedman each directed four episodes of the series.
