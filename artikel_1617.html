Report: Andoni Iraola wants to bring versatile defensive sensation to Liverpool soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Andoni Iraola's time as Liverpool's manager has delivered a bit of a mixed bag of performances so far.

The Spaniard was brought in to replace Arne Slot after the Dutchman led his title-defending team to a fifth-place finish.

Clearly, this performance was nowhere near good enough for a title-defending team and, change was definitely needed.

And, even though over £150 million worth of change came through the Anfield doors in the summer, more deals need to be struck so that Liverpool can start competing for silverware again.

So, even though the transfer window isn't open yet, there's no reason for the rumour mill to not continue turning.

Liverpool are eyeing a move for Mohamed Simakan

Alongside Liverpool's attack, their defence has been a big problem for them.

Whether its been a lack of form or just the sheer number of injuries they've suffered, things just haven't gone to plan for Liverpool's back line over the last couple of years.

That's why players such as Jeremy Jacquet, Ronald Araujo and Giovanni Leoni have all been brought in.

Right now, things are in a good place for the Reds' back four but, that could all change if they choose against signing Araujo permanently.

Nothing is set in stone yet but, the fact that Caught Offside have shared that Mohamed Simakan is now wanted by Liverpool proves that Iraola and Julian Ward are trying to line up some back-up options just in case things don't go to plan.

"Liverpool and Manchester United are both monitoring Mohamed Simakan as the Premier League rivals consider defensive reinforcements ahead of 2027," they wrote.

"The 26-year-old has remained an important figure for Al-Nassr since leaving RB Leipzig and offers the kind of profile that could appeal to both clubs.

"Comfortable at centre-back or right-back, Simakan combines pace and physical strength with experience playing in an aggressive defensive system.

For Liverpool and United, however, January may be too early for anything concrete. For Liverpool, Simakan’s versatility is particularly attractive.

"With long-term succession planning required in central defence, signing a player capable of operating centrally while also covering right-back would give the squad valuable flexibility."

Mohamed Simakan could become Liverpool's next Ronald Araujo

One of the best things about Araujo is that he can comfortably play as a right-back or a centre-back.

No matter which position he's used in, he's always been seen as one of the Reds' best players this term with fans demanding that his deal is made permanent.

Clearly, this hasn't happened yet and, since rumours have been emerging that the price of Araujo would have to be slashed in half for Liverpool to even consider doing that, the deal doesn't feel all that likely to come to fruition.

This isn't ideal news but, the fact that Simakan can be used as a centre-back or a right-back does mean that change is coming to Anfield.

He may have spent the last couple of years in the Saudi Pro League with Al-Nassr but, he's still only 26 years old so, even if he isn't up to scratch right away, he could develop and become better.

Nothing is set in stone yet but, since Iraola wants to bring him in, Simakan could end up being a name that Liverpool's fans get rather used to.
