Manchester United hounded off pitch by own fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

After a humiliating defeat by Grimsby Town in their previous League Cup campaign, Manchester United found a different way to exit the competition this time around. The jeers heard at the full-time whistle after throwing away a two-goal lead pointed to more worrying times a year on from Blundell Park.

For the first time in half a century, United lost a game at Old Trafford having led 2-0, highlighting the defensive problems Michael Carrick needs to solve after turning results around last season.

“What the f---ing hell was that?” shouted the home fans, in a chant that may have been aimed at the referee but equally applied to United’s players, who led through Shea Lacey and Mason Mount’s early goals.

Brighton cut through Carrick’s defence several times before staging their comeback with goals from Charalampos Kostoulas, Pascal Gross and Maxim De Cuyper. Carrick and his analysts will rewatch the footage of all three goals in horror at how they conceded.

“I totally understand the feelings of the supporters,” Carrick said. “We have to accept it. I totally accept it. It’s on me ultimately: as a team what we look like, how we play and what results you get. I take that. I’ll take the praise, I’ll take the criticism and the upset.”

Erik ten Hag used the domestic cups to taste success while struggling in the Premier League, but one of those opportunities is now lost to Carrick, who must be careful not to spiral from a poor start to the campaign into crisis. Sunday’s trip to Fulham before the international break now feels like a big game for United and Carrick.

“We’ve gone out of the cup and the feeling around the place feels bigger than that,” Carrick said. “I’ll be honest about that, that’s how it feels, but it doesn’t have to be like that. If we win football matches again, it can go in a different direction.

“We have a big game on Sunday that we have to prepare for. We’re out of this competition and we have to go back to winning football matches.

“It doesn’t feel good at the moment. It doesn’t feel good. We have to go back to winning football matches. We want to win and want to win quickly. The next game is Sunday. That’s all I’m thinking about right now.”

At the start of the evening it seemed that the tie would be remembered for Lacey scoring on his first start for United, the first teenager to do so since Marcus Rashford.
