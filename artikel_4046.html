Sakurai Celebrates Kirby Air Riders' First Anniversary And Seemingly Ponders His Future As A Game Creator

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

He's apparently still working at "full throttle"

As we reflect on 25 years of Super Smash Bros. Melee, it seems game director Masahiro Sakurai is taking a moment to remind everyone that Kirby Air Riders has now been available for a year.

As highlighted by Sakurai on social media, the Switch 2 exclusive is officially one year old as of this week. It's received multiple updates since launch and Nintendo recently rolled out the last two amiibo.

In the game director's message, he also touches on his future as a game creator. Here's a rough translation of this part of Sakurai's post via EventHubs: "Time flies. I wonder how much more I'll be able to make".

Sakurai admittedly isn't getting any younger, and Kirby Air Riders included "everything" at launch, so it seems this is a genuine reflection on his future as a game creator. He has reportedly claimed to be semi-retired in the past, but this eventually led to Kirby Air Riders. He's also raised his future within the game industry on multiple other occasions.

Just earlier this week at the Famitsu 40th Anniversary Festival, Sakurai apparently revisited a column he had written about retirement, and how his decision depends on the demand for his work, and if he remains capable of delivering it (via OtakuKart). He's also questioned when his "spirit" might break. For now, though, he's still apparently working at "full throttle".

If we do hear any significant updates about Sakurai's future projects, or anything else he is up to, we'll be sure to let you know.
