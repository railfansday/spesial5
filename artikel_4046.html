Helldivers 2 Devs Say Bouncy Stratagem Fix Is Still “In Development”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Helldivers 2 has had a rollercoaster of fan reactions since its release, flipping between one of the most hyped PvE games in recent years and an incredibly controversial sci-fi shooter. While much of this criticism springs from just how popular and surprising the success story behind Helldivers 2 is compared to its predecessor, Arrowhead Studios still seems to be working on issues that have been plaguing the game since release.

This isn't to say that fans have been starving for content, with a whole new faction, numerous collaborations, and plenty of live-service events to keep them busy, but even dedicated members of the community are feeling frustrated by just how long it took for Helldivers 2's upcoming gameplay change.

If you've ever played Helldivers 2 before, you'll definitely have encountered the problem of throwing down a Stratagem on a rocky surface, or even a flat building, before it winds up bouncing dozens of meters away from where you intended.

While you might have assumed this was an intended feature to limit their effectiveness, Arrowhead Studios themselves have revealed it's actually a bug on X that they're actively working to fix.

Rather than being happy about the change, fans have been using it as a point of criticism over wondering how they are expected to be "hyped over a bug fix that should've happened in 2024," alongside other players noting that "modders fixed this and hellpod steering long ago."

As if that wasn't enough, the long-awaited bug fix is not even being added to the game yet, with "more information coming in the new year" to the disappointment of many players.

While it's still certainly great to see Helldivers still getting updates and quality of life fixes years after release, it's not hard to see why players are so frustrated either, especially after the implementation of stricter anti-cheat that ended up disabling countless fan-made mods.
