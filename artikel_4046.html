The Expanse: Osiris Reborn Spotlights Its Female Protagonist in RPG's Promising New Footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

OwlCat Games has unveiled fresh gameplay from The Expanse: Osiris Reborn, and the sci-fi RPG continues to look really promising.

Now that we're past all of the drama with the game's male protagonist — his default appearance was recently changed along with his voice after a lot of critical feedback — it's time to take a look the female lead.

She's all over this new gameplay montage, which showcases characters, dialogue, a little bit of exploration, and a bunch of zero-g combat. It's probably one of the title's best trailers to date.

However, we still don't have a release date for this one. All we know for sure is that it's launching next year, and that it's got a spring 2027 target — but if that's going to stick, then we'll be needing a concrete date sooner rather than later.

I suppose it's also worth noting that a direct competitor to Osiris Reborn — fellow Mass Effect-style RPG Exodus — is locked in for the 7th April. Ideally, these two don't want to go head-to-head and potentially cannibalise each other's initial sales.
