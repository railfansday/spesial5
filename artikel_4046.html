Ember Outpost offers the Rimworld experience on mobile, out now for iOS and Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Space, the final frontier. And like all frontiers, there's always someone willing to take a chance on building a new life there, no matter how much of a ludicrously ill-advised idea it may be. It's a concept that offers rich ground for colony management sims, but there haven't been many options for that on mobile, well, until Ember Outpost arrived.

Available now on iOS and Android, Ember Outpost is a colony management sim that sees you guiding a group of settlers on an alien world. Everything, from food to water and other supplies, will need to be crafted or collected, and you'll also have to deal with the drastically contradictory personalities of your colonists. Oh, and let's add natural disasters and murderous raiders, just for some extra spice.

It's not all sunshine and roses, though. Ember Outpost does have the lingering spectre of AI in some bizarrely high-res assets scattered throughout, and promotional imagery. Which is a shame because I think part of the appeal of colony sims such as RimWorld is how easily readable and minimalist they are. But you can't have everything.

For what it is, Ember Outpost offers exactly what those who want RimWorld on mobile have been waiting for. Densely detailed colony sim action, where there's just as much potential for disasters inside the walls of your compound as outside of it.
