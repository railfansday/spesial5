Shade of the Marionette: The Story of Piyorita debut trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Kiwi Walks has released the first trailer for Shade of the Marionette: The Story of Piyorita, its upcoming story-driven 3D action RPG based on the world of WitchSpring.

Here are the latest details, via Kiwi Walks:

The studio’s previous title, WitchSpring R, has sold over 300,000 copies worldwide across PC and consoles. With Shade of the Marionette: The Story of Piyorita, Kiwi Walks introduces its first action RPG. The new trailer shows protagonist Piyorita in real-time combat against towering boss dolls, alongside exploration, crafting and upgrades presented through gameplay footage.

Each weapon gives Piyorita a different attack speed, reach and set of skills. The trailer shows her switching weapons mid-battle and reflecting enemy magic. It also highlights tactical elements such as exploiting openings in a giant boss’s attacks and using elevated terrain to perform plunging attacks. After absorbing a boss doll’s life force, Piyorita can summon that doll to fight alongside her.

Materials gathered while exploring are used to craft tools and upgrade weapons. In the White Nest Room, the base where Piyorita first awakens, players can craft a variety of tools and spells for combat, then strengthen their weapons and skills before setting out again.

Shade of the Marionette: The Story of Piyorita introduces a new protagonist and story within the world of WitchSpring. Piyorita is a doll born without emotions or memories. Following the instructions of a familiar Voice, she explores the world beyond the mirror and absorbs the life force of other dolls to become the Masterpiece. The trailer also offers glimpses of the game’s atmosphere and story, showing varied spaces connected by mirrors and a girl in a black dress whose face is identical to Piyorita’s.

Shade of the Marionette: The Story of Piyorita is due out for unspecified PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo consoles, as well as PC via Steam in the second half of 2027.
