Two Games Release Today On Xbox, Including A National Park Simulator (November 17)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

"Take on the everyday responsibilities of a real park ranger"

While GTA 6 might be literally just around the corner, we've still got a few games launching on Xbox over the next few days, with and today (November 17th) is no exception - although there's literally only two known new releases.

The highlight is Ranger's Path: National Park Simulator, which is an Xbox Play Anywhere title from the publisher behind the likes of Police Simulator: Patrol Officers and Firefighting Simulator: Ignite. It's been on PC since March and has a "Mostly Positive" rating on Steam, but today marks its debut across Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S.

It's also worth mentioning that we've got two Xbox Game Pass additions today, with PAW Patrol: Dino World arriving for XGP Ultimate and Premium, and Aphelion jumping from Ultimate to Premium for the first time. Neither of these are brand-new releases though, so we're not counting them in the list of new Xbox launches.

So, here's what's new on Xbox today (November 17):

In Ranger’s Path: National Park Simulator, you take on the everyday responsibilities of a real park ranger in the stunning Faremont National Park. Restore and maintain scenic trails, assist visitors, and document wildlife in a living, breathing ecosystem.

Step into the magical world of Unicorn Academy and begin your very own adventure! Create your own first-year student at the academy and call upon the help of Unicorn Academy’s favorite characters. Groom, race and bond with your unicorn. Embark on missions to stop Ravenzella’s evil plot.

ROARR! Sounds like trouble. Did that come from Dino Island? It looks like Rex needs the PAW Patrol to save the day! Mayor Humdinger’s causing chaos. Dinosaurs are scared and running wild. Help them get back to their families safely.

Aphelion is a cinematic third-person action-adventure set on the planet Persephone, an uncharted, frozen world. Stranded and isolated, you must survive the truly unknown, find each other, and complete the mission. Traverse stunning alien landscapes, avoid lurking threats, and discover the haunting secrets buried beneath the ice.
