"Honestly, one of the most underrated features in the Xbox ecosystem"

The long wait for Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally over, with players flocking to their Xbox consoles to boot up the latest crime-fuelled romp through Vice City.

GTA 6 launched today on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles, with no immediate plans to launch the game on PC. However, Xbox Remote Play appears to be getting heaps of praise for its flexibility, responsiveness and low latency when streaming GTA 6 through the Xbox app.

If you're unfamiliar with the streaming feature, Xbox Remote Play allows players to stream their console to other smart devices through the Xbox app, or the Xbox Play page on mobile. It's particularly useful when access to the TV is limited or when players are in another room away from their Xbox console.
