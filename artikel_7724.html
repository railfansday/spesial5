Illegal Alien From Mexico With Prior Deportations and Multiple Violent Felony Convictions Sentenced to Prison for Illegally Reentering the United States

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

LAS VEGAS – An illegal alien from Mexico was sentenced today by United States District Judge Cristina D. Silva to 18 months in prison to be followed by three years of supervised release for illegally reentering the United States after having been removed from the country on two prior occasions. The government recommended a sentence of 18 months in prison.
