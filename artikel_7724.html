Rivals race to rescue Weil Gotshal lawyers as firm heads toward closure

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Several leading law firms are looking to take on junior lawyers and trainees from Weil, Gotshal & Manges after its management committee recommended an orderly wind-down of the firm’s global operations. (Photo from Shutterstock)

Several leading law firms are looking to take on junior lawyers and trainees from Weil, Gotshal & Manges after its management committee recommended an orderly wind-down of the firm’s global operations.

Law.com reported Thursday that Sullivan & Cromwell and Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison are speaking with Weil Gotshal’s associates and trainees, and that other firms—including DLA Piper, Simpson Thacher & Bartlett and Latham & Watkins—have varying degrees of interest and engagement. According to Law.com, Weil Gotshal is working to find employment for as many people as possible.

Law.com further reported that priorities differ among the suitors. One source said Sullivan & Cromwell is currently favoring associates over partners, while another said Paul Weiss has already brought on several Weil Gotshal partners and is still courting junior lawyers. Meanwhile, another source told Law.com that some firms are offering places to prospective trainees who were due to join Weil Gotshal in September.

The wind-down still needs formal approval, with a partnership vote expected this week. Weil Gotshal’s leaders said the recommendation followed a thorough review of alternatives, and sources said partners have begun telling clients that the firm cannot continue. According to Law.com, the firms named were approached for comment.
