Six Sentenced to Combined 37 Years in Federal Prison for Drug Trafficking

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Six drug traffickers were sentenced today to a combined 37 years in federal prison for selling fentanyl, cocaine, and methamphetamine in the Eastern Panhandle of West Virginia, announced U.S. Attorney Matthew L. Harvey. The separate cases are a part of a collaborative effort by multiple states and jurisdictions to arrest offenders and remove deadly drugs from our communities.
