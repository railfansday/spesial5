Chipotle's Smoked Brisket Is Making A Comeback—Here's When You Can Get It

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It might be time to give your usual protein the day off.

Chipotle has been pretty generous with second chances this year, at least when it comes to its proteins. Chicken al Pastor made its comeback in January, followed by Chipotle Honey Chicken in April. And apparently, the chain isn't finished digging through its greatest hits just yet.

Starting November 5, Chipotle's Smoked Brisket is returning to restaurants across the U.S. and Canada for a limited time. And with football season in full swing, the timing couldn't be better for anyone whose game-day cooking plans begin and end with opening the Chipotle app.

"Bringing it back in the heart of football season gives fans one more reason to make Chipotle part of their game-day lineup," Stephanie Perdue, Chipotle's senior vice president of brand marketing, said in a statement.

But there's quite a bit more going on with this brisket than simply tossing another protein onto the menu.

Chipotle is pretty particular about the beef it buys. In fact, the chain estimates that only about 5 percent of the country's beef supply qualifies under its sourcing requirements. To make the cut, cattle must be raised on a vegetarian diet without added growth hormones or routine antibiotics, and Chipotle must be able to trace the beef back to the ranch it came from.

And the smoky part of Smoked Brisket isn't just a name. According to Chipotle, the meat spends hours over mesquite wood after being seasoned with a pepper-heavy spice blend featuring serrano and chipotle. The work continues in restaurants, where employees sear it on the plancha, add another round of seasoning and a chili-pepper sauce, and chop it into smaller portions throughout the day.

Chipotle's senior vice president of culinary, Nevielle Panthaky, says the preparation is designed to give the brisket its rich, smoky flavor while letting the sauce bring some tang and heat into the mix.

And if you're already mentally assembling your next burrito bowl, Panthaky has a recommendation. His ideal order starts with white rice, pinto beans, and Smoked Brisket, then adds fresh tomato salsa, a little tomatillo-red chili salsa, cheese, and Queso Blanco. According to Chipotle, the combination packs 45 grams of protein and 11 grams of fiber.

There's also a little incentive to stay firmly planted on the couch. From November 5 through November 18, Chipotle is offering $0 delivery fees on Smoked Brisket orders placed through its app or website in the U.S. and Canada.

As for how long the brisket will stick around, Chipotle hasn't announced an end date. So if you missed your chance during its last appearance, you might not want to put off this reunion for too long.
