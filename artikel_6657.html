Flambéed French Onion Crêpes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

“As much as I appreciate a classic like crêpes Suzette, I also love a cheeky little twist!” says New York City pastry chef and cookbook author Caroline Schiff. Here, she takes the traditional dessert on a 180-degree ride to the savory side and nods to another classic along the way: French onion soup. She ditches the citrusy butter sauce in favor of golden caramelized onions and gloriously gooey gruyère, but keeps the drama by adding a splash of sherry and igniting it before serving.

Featured in “Where’d You Go, Crêpes Suzette?” by Ellery Weil.
