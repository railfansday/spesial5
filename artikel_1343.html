Chivu: ‘Inter must pick up where we left off, important to find balance’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Cristian Chivu outlines his Inter changes for the trip to Atalanta, warning they must ‘pick up where we left off’ with the victory over Como.

It kicks off at the New Balance Arena at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The Nerazzurri are feeling the pressure with this schedule, knowing that the other teams fighting for the top spots already secured victories this weekend.

“We all know how Maurizio Sarri plays, his organisation is something he is trying to bring to this team, but has only been here for three or four months, and had a lot of problems with injuries, so was unable to use his ideal defence,” Chivu told DAZN Italia.

“They’ve had a little more time on the break for international duty, even if like us they had a lot of players around the world.

“It is never an easy game here, as both teams have high expectations, so we must build on what we did two weeks ago.”

Inter went into the break for international duty with a victory over Como and Cesc Fabregas, consolidating second place behind surprise outfit Lazio.

Curtis Jones has been out since his injury against Roma on September 19, while Lautaro Martinez and John Stones also return for a spot on the bench.

However, Alessandro Bastoni is picked in the centre of a three-man defence, a role he has sometimes struggled with. Is this an experiment for Chivu?

“I do not perform experiments. I deal with a team and the problems that emerge on a daily basis. Benjamin Pavard has been training well and recovering, Jones is returning slowly, so this is my choice for tonight,” explained the Inter coach.

“We have great players at our disposal, and so many deserve to start, but nowadays the substitutes give a side so much too.”

Ange-Yoan Bonny and Aleksandar Stankovic are still on the absentee list, after missing international duty.

“We’ve got to pick up where we left off against Como, to show our form, fitness, and strategy, because we often talk about names, but we must also see the characteristics, if someone is more attacking or defensive, and provide solutions that also give us balance.”

Inter have a big Champions League fixture coming up against VfB Stuttgart on Wednesday, and absolute must-win after a difficult start to the tournament.
