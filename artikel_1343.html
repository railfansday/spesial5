Ex-Napoli striker takes aim at Allegri over Hojlund with cryptic social media post

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Napoli striker Roberto Sosa took a cryptic dig at Massimiliano Allegri, asking why Rasmus Hojlund keeps scoring for Denmark while struggling to match those numbers for Napoli.

Hojlund has just started his second season at Napoli, but has only managed three goals in 14 appearances across all competitions under new coach Allegri.

His numbers with the Danish national team are very different, as the former Manchester United striker has netted six goals in his last six international appearances.

Sosa, a former Napoli striker, seems convinced that Hojlund’s poor form is tied to Allegri’s tactics.

“After dinner, after a nice digestif, don’t the coach’s friends ever ask themselves why the striker always scores for his national team but doesn’t score nearly as many goals (at least so far) for his club? Enjoy your meal,” Sosa wrote on X.

Sosa’s posts drew mixed reactions from Napoli fans, with some urging him to be less critical of the team and others backing his views.

Therefore, the former Argentine striker shared another post, this time a video from the seaside.

“After you’ve watched this video, you are free to write to me: ‘Pampa, I’m going to block you.’ And I’ll react with a heart,” he said.

“I watch every Napoli game, every Serie A, Premier League and European competition. If a team plays badly, I can’t say otherwise; I have to examine ball possession, counter-pressing, how they track back, and how they try to win those games. Then, I might note injuries, whether certain teams didn’t buy any players. But to me, we shouldn’t start backward, as some do, creating excuses or alibis. Well, those are the famous like-chasers. I wish you a good day, a good lunch and a good dinner!”

Sosa scored 28 goals in 116 Napoli appearances from 2004 to 2008.

Napoli currently sit 10th in the Serie A table with 15 points in ten matches and just one win from their last five league games.
