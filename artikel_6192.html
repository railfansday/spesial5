10 Vacation Rentals Across North America For Your Next Family Reunion

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Family reunions can be stressful. There’s much to consider, from dealing with a motley of personalities to making sure the kids are looked after. But things get much easier when you land on the perfect vacation rental, one in a crowd-pleasing location, with a spacious layout and plenty of amenities that will keep everyone happy. Whether you’re bringing the gang together for an outdoor adventure at Yosemite National Park, or seeking a laid-back sojourn in the Caribbean, the rentals on this list satisfy every kind of trip. Each featuring four bedrooms or more, these properties are sure to fit the entire clan with luxe amenities worth traveling for: think saltwater pools, golf simulators, and private beach access. What’s more, many are within the top 1% of guest reviews in their area, which means they’re loved by the people who have visited (and are often booked for return trips). Below, the best vacation rentals for family reunions across North America.

We've selected these listings based on ratings, amenities, location, decor, editor stays, and previous guest reviews.

Just steps away from Alys Beach, located on Scenic Highway 30A, this sunny and spacious six-bedroom home was designed for families: four king bedrooms with en suite bathrooms (one of which houses two extra twin beds); a bunk room with a double and four additional twin beds; and a queen with a trundle. There’s a game room, complete with ping-pong and Foosball tables, plus large-screen TVs. The little ones can enjoy amenities too, like mini cribs and pack-and-plays. If the family would rather swim at home, there’s a gorgeous heated pool for everyone to wade in, with sun loungers, an adjacent barbecue, and even a little tanning ledge for kids to take their first steps in the water.

Location: Inlet Beach, FloridaThings to do nearby: Seacrest Beach, Deer Lake State Park, Alys Beach

Nantucket, with its pristine beaches and vibrant historic town, makes for an excellent family gathering. This classic, cedar-shingled home features an abundance of windows that bring in natural light, spotless interiors, and plainly chic furnishings. There are five bedrooms with high ceilings (some offer glimpses of the harbor), four full bathrooms, and a spacious kitchen. On the first floor, French doors open out to an outdoor entertainment area, with sectional seating, a dining table that seats 10, a gas grill, and an outdoor shower, all enclosed by a landscaped lawn—the perfect place for the group to kick back and relax.

Location: Nantucket, MassachusettsThings to do nearby: Nantucket Atheneum, Quaker Meeting House, Children's Beach

Number of guests: 20Bed and bath: 7 bedrooms, 6 bathsWhy we love it: Hot tub, proximity to ski resorts, golf simulator

This dreamy A-frame home in Pennsylvania’s Laurel Highlands offers adventurous family members proximity to the slopes at Hidden Valley and Seven Springs resorts. With seven bedrooms spread across three floors, there’s enough space for 20 guests to spread out, plus a fully equipped kitchen, dedicated theater area, game room, and even a golf simulator. After a long day of activities, there’s no better place to relax tired muscles than the hot tub outside, fit for six, or the firepit, surrounded by cozy loungers. Keep in mind the area does get icy over the winter, so 4WD is recommended.

Located in the heart of Ohio’s Amish country, Hillside Villa is a bucolic escape for the extended family. While the entire villa can be booked (check for availability on the “Premier House” option), you can also opt for individual units, from a deluxe room for two with a jetted hot tub to a junior room that sleeps up to four. Each comes with scenic views of the hills, along with shared kitchen access (coffee and tea maker provided). There are 55-inch flat-screen TVs too, along with features like fireplaces and porches. In the backyard, you’ll find a gazebo with a firepit, perfect for watching the sunset descend over the farm-filled valley.

Offering 200 feet of private lakefront in Austin, this modern four-bedroom is a rare retreat. Ideal for a multigenerational family, it’s composed of a main house with two king bedrooms and a guesthouse with a king bedroom, plus a large bunk room with eight twin beds. Spend your days gliding along the lake on a kayak or paddleboard, floating on the 16-foot lily pad, or taking a hike through the surrounding Balcones Canyonland Preserve. End the fun on the two-story party dock, where you can enjoy a meal outside, with s’mores for dessert around the firepit.
