The Iran War, Harder This Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Don’t believe the memorandums of misunderstanding.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth looks on as President Donald Trump speaks during a Cabinet meeting at the White House in Washington, D.C., April 30, 2025.

The Iran war is a triumph, one that we inexplicably have failed to realize. So they say.

Just finish the job! It only takes a little more willpower and foresight to fix the Iran war. If only for this one thing, all our goals will be realized, none of the downsides. Just a few more weeks of bombing. Or just stuff JD Vance’s MOU into the memory hole. Or just arm someone on the ground, maybe the Kurds, maybe the protesters. Just do the magic thing — whatever that is — and soon the mullahs will go away, and Iranians will reoutfit their Shahed drones with fragrant damask roses, to be laid in gratitude at the doors of Washington think tanks!

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth looks on as President Donald Trump speaks during a Cabinet meeting at the White House in Washington, D.C., April 30, 2025.
