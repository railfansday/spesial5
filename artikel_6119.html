Norse Atlantic Cancels All Flights, Will File For Bankruptcy

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Just days after warning that its financial situation was “critical,” Norse Atlantic Airways appears to have reached the end of the line.

The low-cost carrier said Monday that its main Norwegian operating unit will file for bankruptcy. All flights were canceled, and ticket sales were discontinued.

Despite “extensive efforts,” the company was unable to secure financing to meet its short-term liquidity needs, officials said. The board of directors “concluded that there is no longer a basis for continued operations” for the Norwegian business.

A bankruptcy petition is expected to be filed Monday with Agder District Court in Norway.

Other parts of the company, including Norse Atlantic UK, could be restructured and continue operating, but no firm plans have been announced.

“We are deeply sorry for the impact this has on our passengers, employees, and partners,” CEO Eivind Roald said in a statement. “The board, management, and advisers of the listed entity Norse Atlantic ASA and the U.K. subsidiary are working to find a solution for the remaining part of the business to continue operations.”

Norse Atlantic operated only long-haul international flights, connecting cities such as Oslo, New York, Stockholm, Rome, Cape Town, Orlando, Florida, and Phuket, Thailand. That business model became unsustainable as the war in Iran pushed jet fuel prices higher.

The carrier said earlier this year that it was contemplating a sale, but no partner was ever announced. On Wednesday, it warned that its financial situation was “critical,” and said short-term financing was needed immediately to keep operations going.
