United Airlines Cancels 7 Routes From 3rd Busiest Hub: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

United Airlines is the world's third-largest airline for flights and seats for sale. However, due to the Star Alliance member's strong long-haul operation — it has more long-haul flights than any other operator — it ranks first globally for available seat miles.

United has seven hubs in the mainland US: Chicago O'Hare, Denver, Houston Intercontinental, Los Angeles, Newark, San Francisco, and Washington Dulles. With 948 daily movements, influenced by much regional jet activity, OAG data shows that Houston Intercontinental has the third-highest number of departures.

United's schedule from Houston for January 2026 to March 2027 has been compared to what is planned to operate from next April. This process shows that eight routes have ended or will cease soon, two of which are international. No future flights are scheduled, but this could change. It reflects what is known as of November 17.

All the airline's routes to Tulum are ending. US Department of Transportation (DOT) data shows that Houston had an overall load factor through August 2026 of just 65.6%. No wonder it is ending

The link to Vancouver was served on a year-round basis, often with a double daily operation in the summer and no more than a daily flight in the winter. As no services are scheduled beyond April next year, it is reasonable to assume that it is ending. The 737-800 and 737-900 will be deployed in the remaining months.

Fellow Star Alliance member Air Canada runs daily. The 137-seat A220-300 will operate this winter, with the 196-seat A321 next summer. Perhaps reflecting the absence of United, that'll be the airline's highest-capacity equipment to date.

Flights in what were three brand-new markets do not appear to be returning. Service to Portland, Maine, materialized in June last year, followed by Burlington, Vermont, and Spokane, Washington, in May 2026. In all cases, no nonstop service is scheduled next summer. They only had a Saturday-only operation.

Burlington exclusively saw Mesa's 76-seat Embraer E175s, while Spokane was in the hands of SkyWest's E175s. Portland was a bit different, as mainline A319s coexisted with Mesa E175s. The three routes were United Express's three longest regional jet links from Houston Intercontinental in 2026. At 1,473 nautical miles (2,728 km), Houston-Spokane was the carrier's second-longest RJ route network-wide, after San Francisco-St. Louis.

Covering 1,329 nautical miles (2,461 km), the link from Texas's second-busiest airport to Nevada's second-busiest airport has been served for many years. DOT data shows that Continental inaugurated flights in 1993, but it is possible that they existed before then. They returned in 1998 and operated until 2008, influenced by the Global Financial Crisis. Service resumed in 2011.

In the 12 months to August 2026, DOT data shows that only 10,667 round-trip passengers were transported. That tiny volume was because flights usually only existed on weekends. The load factor was 81.6%, with most passengers (58.1%) only flying between Houston and Reno. It appears that the final departure is set for February 27 on the A319. However, while the route is usually served on a year-round basis, it is possible, but unconfirmed, that it'll become winter-seasonal and return next winter.
