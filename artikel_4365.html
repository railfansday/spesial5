A cactus that changes gears: How the prickly pear makes the most of desert rain

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In the desert, water doesn't arrive steadily. It comes in short pulses: a winter storm, a few rainy days, then months of drought. A desert plant needs two skills to live there. It has to survive the long dry spells, and it has to make full use of the brief windows when water is plentiful. A new study from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev and the Volcani Institute shows that the prickly pear cactus has an unexpected way of doing both.
