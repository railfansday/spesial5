Designed for home backup, camping, and road trips, the Elite 200 V2 delivers 2,600W of continuous AC power and a 3,900W power-lifting mode for compatible high-demand appliances. It can run everything from refrigerators to coffee makers, while powering up to nine devices simultaneously.

There are several charging options, too. Turbo mode can take the battery from empty to 80% in one hour using 1,800W AC input, and dual AC and DC charging can achieve the same level in just 50 minutes. You can also choose between Turbo, Standard, and Silent charging modes through the Bluetti app. Plus, its LiFePO4 battery is rated for more than 6,000 charge cycles, with Bluetti saying that averages a lifespan of up to 17 years of everyday use.
