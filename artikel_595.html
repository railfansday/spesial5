‘Dancing With the Stars’ Season 35 Finale Attracts 9.2 Million Viewers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

“Dancing With the Stars” aired its Season 35 finale live coast-to-coast for the first time on Tuesday, and the results were big. A season-best 9.2 million total viewers watched Paralympic track and field athlete Ezra Frech and partner Daniella Karagach take home the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy, according to Nielsen live+same-day preliminary, panel-only program ratings.

According to ABC, the finale posted a 2.38 rating with adults 18-49, which made it the best number in the demo since Nov. 25, 2014 (coincidentally, the season when now-host Alfonso Ribeiro won the trophy with partner Witney Carson). That 2.38 rating makes it TV’s top entertainment telecast with adults 18-49 since the Oscars in March and the highest-rated regularly scheduled entertainment telecast in 18-49 since the April 1, 2020, episode of Fox’s “The Masked Singer.”

In total viewers, the 9.2 million viewers matched last year’s finale.

In other demos, the finale averaged a 2.86 rating with adults 18-34 — the best performance since the Season 12 finale on May 24, 2011. With women 18-34, “Dancing With the Stars” posted a 4.46 rating, which is the best series telecast number in the demo for any network broadcast since the Season 3 premiere of Fox’s “Empire” on Sept. 21, 2016. And the show posted the best teens 12-17 rating (1.18) since “The Masked Singer” on Feb. 27, 2019.

ABC said the finale attracted more than 86 million votes, a new high (up 19% from 72 million in 2025) — the most ever for a single episode. The finale was the No. 1 most social program on television across broadcast and cable on the night and the No. 1 most social episode of any broadcast primetime series, year-to-date.

“Dancing with the Stars” aired live in every U.S. time zone for its special three-hour finale on Tuesday; the finale also re-aired in primetime in the Pacific time zone and started streaming the next day on Hulu.

The five final couples also included Amber Glenn and partner Pasha Pashkov, Maura Higgins and partner Mark Ballas, Connor Wood and partner Rylee Arnold and Tatyana Ali and partner Jan Ravnik.

The three-hour event included an instant dance challenge, where the couples were tested on their ability to improvise; a “judge’s choice” round and a freestyle round. The finale kicked off with an opening number to “One More Time” by Daft Punk. The “Dancing with the Stars: Live!” 2027 tour pros performed a routine to “It’s Not Right but It’s OK (Thunderpuss Mix)” by Whitney Houston, and then all Season 35 couples returned for multiple performances. Season 34 champs Robert Irwin and Witney Carson danced to “Free Bird” by Lynyrd Skynyrd.

“Dancing with the Stars” is co-hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough. Ballroom judges are Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough. BBC Studios produces.

“Dancing With the Stars” Season 35 had been building to its finale with some of its strongest ratings in years. For its penultimate episode on Nov. 17, the show hit season highs with 7.64 million total viewers and a 1.68 rating in adults 18-49, based on preliminary Live + Same Day Panel Only data. It was the best ratings for a semifinal telecast in nine years, since Season 25 on Nov. 20, 2017 and Season 24 on May 22, 2017.

Season to date, “Dancing With the Stars” is primetime’s No. 1 entertainment series in adults 18-49 (1.59 rating in live+same day) for the third year in a row. It’s 86% above the No. 2 show, CBS’ “Survivor” (0.86), which ABC said is the largest advantage in Nielsen electronic measurement history.
