‘A Paw Patrol Christmas’: When and Where to Stream the Animated Holiday Special Online Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The pups are returning to primetime for a special Christmas event.

‘Tis the season for A Paw Patrol Christmas. Rubble, the crew’s construction Bulldog, star alongside the rest of the pups — Ryder, Chase, Marshall, Skye, Rocky and Zuma — in a Paw Patrol original holiday special, returning to TV this weekend.

The hour-long event, filled with both original and classic holiday songs, first premiered last year, but CBS is bringing it back to the airwaves this Saturday, Nov. 28 at 8 p.m. PT/ET. You can watch A Paw Patrol Christmas on TV through your local CBS affiliate.

It will take all the PAW Patrol and Rubble making a sacrifice to stop Mayor Humdinger from ruining Christmas and learning the true meaning of the season.”

CBS is included in any of DIRECTV’s Streaming Packages: ENTERTAINMENT (90+ channels), CHOICE (125+ channels), ULTIMATE (160+ channels) and PREMIER (185+ channels). Each of these four plans offers a free five-day trial.

DIRECTV’s Streaming Packages start at $49.99 per month for the first month after your free trial is up.

You can also watch A Paw Patrol Christmas online without cable using Fubo, another live TV streaming service. Fubo also carries a live CBS feed on all its packages, so you can use it to watch the Paw Patrol special in addition to more than 200 other live television channels.

Like DIRECTV, Fubo also offers a five-day free trial that will let you stream A Paw Patrol Christmas online for free. Fubo’s free trial includes free DVR so you can record the live airing on CBS and watch it back on-demand later.

All three streaming services above let you watch the Paw Patrol Christmas episode on your phone, computer, tablet or smart TV.
