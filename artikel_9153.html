Guadalupe Maravilla to Offer Midnight Mass Sound Bath at St. John the Divine Cathedral

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As part of his forthcoming Creative Time commission at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine, artist Guadalupe Maravilla will offer a bevy of programming beginning December 18.

Titled Consejos de las Estrellas and presented in collaboration with the Clemente Soto Vélez Cultural and Educational Center, the project comprises a sculptural installation that will “serve as a healing instrument and a place to find warmth and belonging when the holidays deepen the feeling of absence,” as Maravilla told ARTnews when the project was announced in October. Core to Maravilla’s sculptures is their ability to be activated for sound baths, and Consejos de las Estrellas will offer eight sound baths during its run. Two of them will be staged in tandem with two winter holidays: a Christmas Eve Midnight Mass sound bath, beginning at 11:59 p.m. on December 24, and New Year Cleansing sound bath on January 9. The latter event will be proceeded by an herbal cleansing workshop by Herban Cura, which describes itself as a “healing justice herbalism and ecology school,” per its website.

A cancer survivor himself, Maravilla has long held sound baths specifically for the cancer community and he will offer two at St. John the Divine that will bookend this part of the programming. The first of these, on December 18, will be specifically for cancer survivors, healthcare workers, and caregivers and realized in partnership with the Mount Sinai Tisch Cancer Center. The final one will be staged on February 4, which is also World Cancer Day.

Consejos de las Estrellas will also feature performances by artist-collaborators who will activate Maravilla’s installation. They are Holland Andrews (January 22), Tesora Garcia and Camilo Godoy (January 29), and Jesús Hilario-Reyes (February 6). Maravilla will hold an artist conversation and Tarot reading on January 14.

Additionally, there will be a Three Kings Eve celebration and procession on the afternoon of January 5 with puppeteers from Teatro Sea. The project also will serve as a mutual aid hub, collecting food, clothing, and toy donations during its open hours, Wednesday to Sunday.

Consejos de las Estrellas will be open at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine in New York’s Morningside Heights neighborhood from December 17 to February 7. Opening hours will be Wednesdays to Fridays from 2 p.m. to 8 p.m. and Saturdays and Sundays from 12 p.m. to 7 p.m.; the project will be closed from December 31–January 2. Full details for the accompanying programming, which is free and open to the public, can be found on Creative Time’s website.
