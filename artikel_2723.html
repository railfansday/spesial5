Florida high school football rankings: USA TODAY Sports Super 10, Week 15

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Open Division play in the Sunshine State has produced more blowouts than instant classics over the last two weeks, but the two classics won't be forgotten anytime soon.

No team in the state has more impressive wins than Lakeland during that span, as the Dreadnaughts beat St. Thomas Aquinas in overtime and then rallied to beat Chaminade-Madonna.

As for pure dominance, Columbus and West Boca claim that title with back-to-back double-digit wins. The stage has been set for massive matchups coming down the pipeline.

Following the third week of postseason play, here is the latest USA TODAY Sports Super 10 Florida football rankings...
