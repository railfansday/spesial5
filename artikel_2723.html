Watch Bears HC Ben Johnson’s electric locker room speech after coming back to beat Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chicago Bears head coach Ben Johnson praised his team's preparation and belief in the locker room after Thursday's 39-35 comeback win over the Detroit Lions at Ford Field. Rome Odunze, who caught the winning touchdown with three seconds left, was among those who received a game ball. The wide receiver also presented one to the Bears head coach after the Thanksgiving win.

Johnson spent three seasons as the Lions' offensive coordinator before taking over as Bears head coach in 2025. The Lions won both matchups in his first season with the Bears, including a 52-21 rout at Ford Field.

The Bears shared Johnson's postgame locker room speech on their official account on X, formerly Twitter. Johnson opened his remarks by reminding players what they had discussed before facing the Lions.

"The professionalism that you had all week long, the hard work that you put in, and then the belief that you had that entire game, guys. That's what good teams are made of, right there."

Jahmyr Gibbs' 70-yard touchdown run put the Lions ahead 35-24 in the fourth quarter. The Bears cut the deficit to three, then stopped Detroit on fourth-and-4 to get the ball back with 2:17 left.

Caleb Williams led a 13-play drive and found Odunze in the corner of the end zone for a 6-yard touchdown. The toe-tap catch held up after review and put Chicago ahead for the first time of the game. Odunze finished with six catches for 87 yards and two touchdowns.

The Bears improved to 9-2 and took sole possession of first place in the NFC North. The Lions fell to 8-3, ending their six-game winning streak.
