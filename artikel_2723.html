Rams list Myles Garrett as questionable, but Sean McVay expects him to play

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Rams are listing Myles Garrett as questionable for Sunday’s rematch with the Cardinals, but the club is still expecting him to play.

Garrett will not practice on Friday, head coach Sean McVay said in his press conference, as Los Angeles is trying to keep him at his best.

“He’s feeling good,” McVay said, via Gary Klein of the L.A. Times. “It’s more of getting him feeling as good as possible. He’s a had a really good week, played a bunch of snaps, and expect him to be able to build on that, but we’re being smart with him.”

Garrett was plenty impactful last week in his return from a knee procedure, picking up 3.0 sacks and a forced fumble.

Additionally, cornerback Trent McDuffie (back) and safety Kam Curl (toe) will be listed as questionable for the game after being limited in practice on Friday.

Guard Steve Avila (hamstring) and tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle/hamstring) have been ruled out.
