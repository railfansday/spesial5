Penguins Recall Atley Calvert, NHL Debut?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

On paper, the Pittsburgh Penguins did not need to recall Atley Calvery Friday, as the team already had 13 forwards, but did so anyway. The 23-year-old grinder who has worked his way up from the ECHL could make his NHL debut when the Penguins visit the Buffalo Sabres Friday at KeyBank Center.

Whether the team has additional injuries or is simply rewarding a player who has earned his way to the NHL will be revealed following the Penguins' morning skate.

Calvert was an undrafted free agent who began his professional career with the Penguins' ECHL affiliate in Wheeling in 2024-25. He graduated to the Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins the same season and has shown a steady progression since.

Last season, the Penguins upgraded Calvert's deal to an NHL contract.

Calvert is 6-foot-1, 203 pounds, and is known for a tenacious, gritty game. This season, he has 15 points (5-10-15) in 20 games, while last season he scored 16 goals and 30 points in 78 games.

He spent five seasons with the Moose Jaw Warriors, where he was a teammate of former Penguins first-round pick Brayden Yager.
