Garrett Wilson unable to practice Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

New York Jets WR Garrett Wilson (concussion) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 4.

Why It Matters: Wilson will have to pass his concussion tests before he can play, so fantasy players should check on his status later in the week. He is the main target in the passing game and should be a No. 2 receiver if he is active.
