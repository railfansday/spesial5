Updating Eagles' 2027 draft pick after Week 10 bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Updating Eagles' 2027 draft pick after Week 10 bye week originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles (2-7) had their bye week in Week 10 but didn't get much help.

They are still sitting in the No. 5 draft slot in 2027.

The Chargers won their first game of the season on Monday night but still have just one win. The Colts were able to able to beat the Dolphins but the Jets lost their eighth game. So the Colts pick and the Jets picks swapped but there are still four teams ahead of the Eagles.

After the Chargers won a game, the Eagles now have the longest losing streak in the NFL at seven games.

As a reminder, the Eagles did pick up an additional first-round pick from the Jaguars in a trade for Jordan Davis. But that will likely be toward the end of the first round.

Here's the remaining strength of schedule for those teams in the top 10 right now. The Eagles have the third most difficult schedule among them:

1. Titans: .5002. Chargers: .4933. Dolphins: .5874. Jets: .4925. Eagles: .5076. Colts (Jets): .4927. Cardinals: .4548. Falcons: .5239. Texans: .43310. Buccaneers: .413

The Eagles have drafted in the top five just six times since 1970: DE Richard Harris (No. 5 in 1971), OT Jerry Sisemore (No. 3 in 1984), WR Kenny Jackson (No. 4 in 1984), QB Donovan McNabb (No. 2 in 1999), OT Lane Johnson (No. 4 in 2013), QB Carson Wentz (No. 2 in 2016).

Since becoming Eagles GM in 2010, Howie Roseman has made just four picks in the top 10: Lane Johnson (No. 4 in 2013), Carson Wentz (No. 2 in 2016), DeVonta Smith (No. 10 in 2021) and Jalen Carter (No. 9 in 2023).

The Eagles during their bye week did pick up an additional first-round pick in a trade for Jordan Davis. The Jaguars are 7-2 and atop the AFC South so that pick will likely be in the 20s or worse.

In addition to those two first-round picks in 2027, the Eagles also have a second-round pick, a projected third-round compensatory pick, a fourth-round pick, three fifth-round picks and a seventh-round pick.
