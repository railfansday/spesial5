Packers’ Evan Williams reveals surprising family connection to Tom Brady

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Tom Brady had an unexpected personal connection to the Green Bay Packers during Fox's coverage of their 45-27 win over New England. The former Patriots quarterback was part of the broadcast team when an interesting family detail about Packers safety Evan Williams came to light. Williams' mother, Dani, was watching from the stands and had once looked after Brady as a babysitter when he was about five years old.

Williams later shared the story behind the unusual connection when asked about it after the game. His mother grew up in the Bay Area, just like Brady, and their families apparently knew each other through his grandfather, who had played with Brady's father. Williams also remembered how casually his mother mentioned that she had once babysat the quarterback, now widely regarded as the GOAT.

"I remember her telling me when I was growing up, I was probably starting high school or something, and she just tossed it out, 'Oh yeah, I babysat Tom Brady, by the way,' and then continues another conversation. 'Like woah, woah, woah! We gotta go back!' I never was really too sure, but apparently it's true," remarked the Packers safety.

Williams added another highlight to an already memorable afternoon by getting his hands on a pass from Maye. His interception was one of three recorded by Green Bay against New England and marked his third of the 2026 campaign. That total puts him alongside McKinney at the top of the Packers' interception chart.

Green Bay's defense has developed a knack for creating takeaways under new coordinator Jonathan Gannon. The Packers have already recorded 13 interceptions this season, more than any other team in the NFL. Pairing that kind of turnover production with Love's MVP-caliber play gives Green Bay a formidable combination as the season progresses.
