NHL World Reacts to Montreal Canadiens Acquiring Dylan Larkin

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Montreal Canadiens have now acquired 30-year-old Dylan Larkin from the Detroit Red Wings.

After a long standoff with the Red Wings, he is heading to Montreal in exchange for a significant haul: Kirby Dach, Samuel Montembeault, the rights to forward Michael Hage, a first-round pick in 2027, a second-round pick in 2027, a fourth-round pick in 2028 and a first-round pick in 2029.

Following the announcement, social media erupted.
