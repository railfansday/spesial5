Commanders back in front of Giants, 17-14

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Commanders answered the Giants with a six-play, 77-yard drive.

Rookie Antonio Williams scored his second career touchdown on a 31-yard catch. David Blough's third-and-1 play call has Washington back in front, 17-14.

The Commanders started the drive with a 24-yard pass from Jayden Daniels to Ben Sinnott, who has four catches for 42 yards and a touchdown.

The Commanders have 208 yards, and Daniels is 15-of-19 for 177 yards and two touchdowns.
