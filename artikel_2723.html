Jets hold a pair of top five picks in the 2027 NFL Draft after Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Jets beat the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 11 action. The victory snapped a five game losing streak.

At this point during a lost season, one of the biggest debates in the fanbase tends to be whether the team is better off winning games or losing for Draft positioning.

Fans of tanking might have been disappointed in the outcome Sunday, but the Jets remain in strong position in the 2027 NFL Draft. If the season ended today, the Jets would own a pair of picks in the top five along with three in the top fifteen.

The Jets have their own first round pick. They also have Indianapolis' from the Sauce Gardner trade and whichever first round pick between Dallas' and Green Bay's is higher from the Micah Parsons trade.

Picks 19 through 32 are determined by the results of games in the NFL Playoffs.
