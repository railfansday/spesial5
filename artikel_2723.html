Saints Safety Justin Reid to Host First Annual ‘Tee’d Up with JReid’ Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

New Orleans Saints safety Justin Reid will host the inaugural Tee'd Up with JReid on Monday, November 16, 2026, at Five O Fore Golf in New Orleans.

Join Justin and his Saints teammates for a great event supporting an even greater cause!

Every dollar raised at the event will benefit the JReid InDeed Foundation and its mission to support youth and communities through interactive programs, community engagement, and access to technology in New Orleans, Houston, Baton Rouge, and Kansas City.

General admission tickets are $125 each, and guests will enjoy a private venue takeover featuring:

Live and silent auctions featuring signed memorabilia and one-of-a-kind experiences

Photo opportunities, interactive stations, and opportunities to mingle with Justin Reid and his teammates

Come swing with purpose and help make the first annual Tee'd Up with JReid an unforgettable night!

For more information, to purchase tickets or sponsorships for the event, please visit Tee'd Up with JReid | New Orleans Charity Golf Event

To learn more about Reid's Foundation, please visit JReid Indeed
