Woody Marks ruled out of Texans-Browns with a concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

If the Texans are going to beat the Browns, they’ll have to do it without one of their key offensive players.

Running back Woody Marks is out with a concussion, Houston announced.

Marks went down midway through the third quarter on a short run. He was crunched between two defenders and stayed down before getting up under his own power to be examined in the sideline medical tent.

Marks had 50 yards on 12 carries plus a 15-yard catch before exiting the contest.

Down 20-17 early in the fourth quarter, the Texans have not registered a first down in the second half.
