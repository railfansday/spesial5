Carson Beck on his first start: It was cool, but losing is awful

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Carson Beck started his first NFL game Sunday. It didn’t go as he had hoped, but the lights weren’t too bright for the Cardinals’ rookie quarterback.

“The ebbs and flows of football, I’ve been through it,” Beck said, via Darren Urban of the team website. “Not at this level. But it’s still the same game that we play. I’ve been through the good, the bad, the ugly, the uglier.

“How are you able to respond when things go bad? Obviously, we didn’t respond well today, and that starts with me. . . . But having the opportunity to play, that’s what allows us to stay focused. To be out there is fun. Football is fun.”

Beck went 11-of-18 for 108 yards and two interceptions in the 34-6 loss to the Rams. The Cardinals had only 123 yards and six first downs.

Coach Mike LaFleur said Beck did everything they asked him to do but conceded it was “a rough day” for the offense.

“It was cool,” Beck said. “Losing is awful. Don’t get it twisted. Losing is the worst thing ever. I hate losing. I haven’t done a lot of it. But it’s not about me. It’s about the team, and what we can do to fix the things to win games like this.

“But it was awesome. Going out on the first drive, looking across the field at guys like Aaron Donald and Myles Garrett. I just smiled for a second and was like, ‘This is sick.’ This is the thing you dream of when you start playing football at 6, 7 years old.”
