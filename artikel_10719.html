Umamusume horse girls are taking over a Denver Broncos NFL game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Umamusume: Pretty Derby is teaming up with the Denver Broncos for their clash against the Miami Dolphins, bringing Gold Ship and Mejiro McQueen to the NFL in specially designed uniforms.

From appearing at the Grand Prix de Paris to being plastered across a BMW in Japan’s Super GT championship, Umamusume’s anime horse girls have been sighted at a lot of sports events this year.

While the Seattle Seahawks previously shared Umamusume-inspired fan art on social media, the Denver Broncos are taking the crossover further with an official collaboration that brings both Gold Ship and Mejiro McQueen to their stadium for the December 6 game.

During the game at Empower Field at Mile High, Umamusume will appear on the stadium’s screens and horseshoe-shaped LED ribbon.

Fans attending the game could also find themselves on an Umamusume-themed fan cam, you know, just in case you were worried there weren’t enough horse girls in your soccer game.

There’s even going to be life-size cutouts of Gold Ship and Mejiro McQueen available for photos at a booth near Gate 8. Visitors can collect a postcard featuring the pair and a rally flag showing Gold Ship’s touchdown, with both illustrations specifically created for the event.

It’s important to note that you don’t need a game ticket to visit the Umamusume booth or receive the giveaways, but you will need one to watch the Denver Broncos take on the Miami Dolphins inside the stadium.

The booth opens at 11:05 a.m. MST on December 6 and closes at 2:05 p.m., when the Broncos’ Week 13 game kicks off.
