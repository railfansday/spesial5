Viral steak pop-up shut down after beef with neighbor over food lowered from apartment window

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A viral Brooklyn steak pop-up has been shut down after complaints over its unusual setup, which saw boxed meals lowered from a second-floor apartment to customers on the sidewalk.

Some Guy’s House was launched by 24-year-old chef Chianli Hang and five friends as a limited food experiment and social media project. Instead of operating from a traditional storefront, the group used a homemade pulley system to lower steak dinners from their apartment window at 119 McKibbin Street.

The concept quickly gained attention online. Customers paid $25 for a sticker that came with a steak dinner, with the meal including steak, potatoes and spinach. The pop-up operated on Thursday and Friday evenings, with customers booking spots online before collecting their food from the street.

A post shared by Brooklyn Magazine (@brooklynmagazine)

The pop-up’s official website now says that Some Guy’s House is “no longer operating” and that orders booked for the current and following week will be refunded.

A follow-up video from Brooklyn Magazine said the business was shut down after roughly two weeks of service, with the team describing the closure as “inevitable.” The outlet also interviewed the group about the short-lived project and its sudden end.

According to The New York Post, Hang’s landlord told him that one person had filed multiple complaints with New York City’s 311 system. Six complaints were reportedly filed over the pop-up, and two resulted in written warnings from the Department of Health being sent to the landlord.

“We were really excited for the send-off. But then we got shut down,” Hang said.

A post shared by Some guy’s house (@someguyshouse119)

The group had planned to operate for another week, fulfill around 100 pre-orders and potentially hold a DJ-backed send-off. However, Hang said the landlord believed the apartment might be operating as a full-scale commercial kitchen.
