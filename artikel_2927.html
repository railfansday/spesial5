Matthew Stafford’s wife Kelly blasts NFL over Thanksgiving Eve game

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

NFL teams' bye weeks typically last 13 days so players can take time off and retool their bodies. The Rams will be given 10 this season.

After their bye week, the Rams will play in the first NFL Thanksgiving Eve game against the Packers, and quarterback Matthew Stafford's wife, Kelly, isn't too happy about it.

On Tuesday, Kelly made a post on her Instagram Story, tagging the NFL and calling it out over the league's choice for the 10-day break.

Kelly Stafford blasts the NFL's choice to schedule the Rams on Thanksgiving Eve. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"Just curious to hear the thought process behind making the Rams the only team in the NFL without a true bye week," Kelly Stafford wrote. "Because damn, after 18 years around this league, even I need a week off during the season … and I have never played a single snap."

"I'm tired just watching from the stands. Can't imagine how the guys feel."

Kelly added, "Anyway, feel free to leave that explanation right here…"

Ironically, while the Rams will have a shortened bye week, the Packers will receive some extended time off. Green Bay will go straight into its bye week after the Thanksgiving Eve game and won't take the field until Dec. 6.

According to Jim Taylor, who has a Ph.D. in psychology of sport and parenting, "Routines are one of the most important aspects of sports that athletes can develop to improve their training and competitive performances. The fundamental value of routines is that they ensure total preparation in athletes' efforts. Routines enable athletes to be completely physically, technically, tactically, and mentally ready to perform their best."

When the season ends, the Rams will be the second-most-traveled team (34,847 miles), behind their NFC West rivals, the 49ers, who will travel 38,105 miles this season.

Part of why the Rams and 49ers will finish as the NFL's most traveled teams is because they opened the season on Sept. 10 in Melbourne, Australia.

The Rams then had to wait until Sept. 21 for their next game, when they hosted the Giants.

Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford kisses his wife ahead of an NFL game against the Philadelphia Eagles. AP Photo/Matt Slocum

They then had to play back-to-back road games against the Broncos and Eagles, and then had another home Monday night game against the Bills on Oct. 12.

The 2026 NFL season hasn't gotten off to the start the Rams (4-4) envisioned, as they've been dealing with injuries across the roster and just got their record back to .500 following Sunday's win over the Chargers.

While they are at the halfway point of the season, the inconsistency of their schedule remains.

After their game against the Packers, they will have eight days off and will have to play the Chiefs on Thursday, Dec. 3.

Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford before they played the Chargers. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

They then have back-to-back Sunday games against the 49ers and Cowboys, but then will go on the road to play against the Seahawks.

After Seattle, the Rams will travel to Florida to play the Buccaneers and then finish the season at home against the Seahawks.
