Caleb Williams on Rome Odunze: When big moments come up, you go to your guys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bears wide receiver Rome Odunze had one catch for nine yards at halftime in Detroit on Thanksgiving, but there was a lot more to come in the final 30 minutes.

Odunze had five catches for 78 yards after the break, including a pair of touchdowns in the fourth quarter. The first was a 41-yard pass from Caleb Williams that made it a three-point game with 7:20 left to play and the second was a toe-tapping catch in the corner of the end zone with three seconds left on the clock.

It was also the game-winning play and it was a play the Bears had to have, which is why Williams said he went in the wideout's direction. Williams and Odunze both joined the Bears as 2024 first-round picks and Williams said after the win that "you go to your guys" in the biggest moments.

"He was brought in with me," Williams said. "We've been through ups, we've been through downs, we've been all of the above. When those moments come up and he runs great routes, I give him the ball, he catches it. He tends to show up in those big moments. And I know he's working his tail off to be as consistent as possible each week to be able to get the ball in his hands as much as possible. We're working on that, Coach and I. When these big moments come up, it just tends to go to Rome."

Odunze didn't do much early in Thursday's game, but his contributions proved to be worth the wait for the first-place team in the NFC North.
