People Business Partner, GTM at LiveKit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

LiveKit is building the infrastructure layer for the voice-driven era of computing. Our platform gives developers everything they need to build, test, deploy, scale, and observe agents in production. Founded in 2021, LiveKit powers voice AI applications for OpenAI, xAI, Salesforce, Coursera, Spotify, and thousands of others, collectively facilitating billions of calls each year.

We're looking for a Senior People Business Partner for our GTM org. You don't need to be the deep expert in every corner, but you're dangerous enough across all of it to move fast and recognize when something deserves more attention than you can give it right now. You also know when to slow down and when to raise the harder question early.

You build the kind of relationships where leaders and individual contributors bring you the hard stuff while there's still time to proactively do something about it. You're approachable, direct, and willing to push back when something doesn't make sense. You don't need every answer, but you know how to ask good questions, work through messy situations, and help leaders make better decisions.

You'll partner with leaders across Sales, Marketing, and the rest of our revenue organization as they build and grow their teams, and you'll work closely with our VP of People and the broader People team to build scalable, intentional People programs that enable folks to do their best work.

You'll thrive as a People Business Partner if you:

Take the time to understand the business just as much as the people side

Have worked in enough different environments to have a real sense of what good looks like, and hold yourself to it

Stay steady when a sales org is moving fast: quota changes, reorgs, and rapid hiring don't rattle you

Are direct with people, including when it's uncomfortable—feedback doesn't get softened into confusion, and people still trust you after

Assess risk quickly—not just what could go wrong, but what's actually likely to and how much it matters

Know when to escalate and when to just decide; you bring a recommendation, not just a problem, and you know which things (comp exceptions, anything legal, changes to established policy) always get a second set of eyes

As a People Business Partner at LiveKit, you will:

Own performance management, manager coaching, org design, and headcount planning as the primary People partner for Sales, Marketing, and our broader Revenue organization, including pre- and post-sales teams

Navigate complex situations and help the org grow without breaking, including pushing back on adding layers or headcount before the case is made

Partner with our GTM Talent Partner on hiring plans, role scoping, and ramp so headcount decisions are made with the full picture

Work as one team with the other People Business Partners (EPD and G&A) so leaders get a consistent partnership experience

Work directly with the VP of People and larger People and Talent team to design, build, and operationalize People programs across all COEs

Own end-to-end delivery of programs and projects, from strategy to rollout to iteration

Make our work repeatable: documented, handoff-ready, and not dependent on anyone being a hero

Experience as a People Business Partner (or equivalent) supporting Sales and other revenue-generating teams: performance management against quota and ramp, comp plan changes, rev org reorgs, and coaching sales managers

Think from first principles and know when to ask for help

A clear, direct communicator who can challenge strong personalities while maintaining trust

Confident in what you know and what you don't—able to respond decisively even without full information

Comfortable in ambiguity, with strong relationship-building instincts and data-informed decision-making

Personable, approachable, and comfortable working with leaders and ICs at all levels

Understand how hiring, talent development, and organizational decisions impact a sales team's success

Experience with global HR, leaves of absence, or benefits administration

Direct experience building or significantly iterating programs across multiple People COEs

Experience using AI tools to improve how you work, with good judgment about when and how to use them

An opportunity to build something truly impactful to the world

Work alongside world-class people at one of the most important AI infrastructure companies being built right now
