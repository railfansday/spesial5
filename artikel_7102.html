GitLab: Executive Assistant, Office of the CFO

As an Executive Assistant in the Office of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO), you’ll support multiple senior leaders in GitLab’s Finance Group, helping them focus on their priorities through proactive calendar, travel, and expense management. Reporting to the Senior Executive Business Partner to the CFO, you’ll be part of an integrated model that gives Finance leadership balanced, consistent support. You’ll work across time zones, help coordinate team events and projects, and partner with colleagues to keep day-to-day operations running smoothly.

We value diverse backgrounds and transferable experience.

The Executive Business Administration function supports GitLab leaders by creating the operational foundation for effective work in an all-remote, asynchronous environment. The Office of the CFO brings the CFO's leadership team together under one operating model. Its Executive Business Administrators work as one team, using shared standards and templates, a common schedule of recurring activities, coordinated coverage, and a shared view of Finance leadership priorities. This approach helps the CFO and vice presidents receive consistent support as the organization grows.
