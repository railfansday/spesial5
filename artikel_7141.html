X Previews Labels That Tell Users Why Its AI System Flagged Their Posts

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

X’s AI-powered content moderation system once mistakenly labeled a post featuring an image of a SpaceX rocket launch as “adult content.” No one, including the author of the post, could see why.

That’s the problem Michael O’Herlihy, a Twitter veteran who recently rejoined X as head of safety, wants to fix.

In the future, X’s content moderation systems may show their decisioning process, he suggested at the Brand & AI Safety Summit in New York on Tuesday (Nov. 10). There, O’Herlihy previewed designs for labels displayed beneath content on X that explain what its AI detected in a post, detail how exactly the post’s reach was limited after being flagged, and allow users to appeal the decision.

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
