Luca Williams-Barnett reveals Roberto De Zerbi ‘prank’ ahead of first Tottenham goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Luca Williams-Barnett revealed that Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi played a prank on the teenager ahead of his maiden Premier League goal for the club.

The 18-year-old grabbed the opener against Ipswich Town at the weekend, before going on to mark his first full Premier League start with an assist for Dominic Solanke.

The winger had been involved in the first-team squad for the last four Premier League games prior to the Ipswich match, and had continued to train with De Zerbi’s squad in the week.

As such, being told that he was playing for the Under-21s would have been a shock for the young star.

He revealed De Zerbi’s practical joke in an interview with SpursPlay: "The day before, the gaffer obviously pulled me aside.

"He actually said, ‘You’re going to be playing with the ‘21s, 60 minutes to get your legs back,’ and I was like, ‘What?’ but obviously he told me the news and the whole day I was just buzzing. I couldn’t wait.

"Everything I’ve dreamed of. Obviously scoring and getting an assist, it’s like a dream come true basically."

Williams-Barnett admitted he thought he had spurned his chance for a dream debut, missing a similar opportunity to the move that led to his goal.

He said: “The first ball into the box I wasn’t really there and I was quite annoyed because it was open and I could have had the goal.

“Once I saw it go back to Pedro [Porro], it was an instinct. Once I saw he was able to cross the ball, I just had to get behind my man and put the ball in the goal.”
