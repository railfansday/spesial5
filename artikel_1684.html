Sarr injury still ‘touch and go’, reveals Palace boss Frank

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Frank revealed Ismaila Sarr's injury is still "touch and go" and ruled the winger out for Crystal Palace's Europa League encounter with Real Sociedad.

Sarr has missed each of Palace's last six games with a groin injury, last appearing in their 2-2 draw with Besiktas on October 22.

The 28-year-old scored 21 goals across 45 games in all competitions last season, marking the best scoring campaign of his career.

Since his move to Selhurst in August 2024, Sarr (41 – 33 goals, eight assists) has directly contributed to the joint-most goals among Palace players, along with Jean-Philippe Mateta.

However, Sarr will have to wait longer to appear under Frank, who took over from Pierre Sage during the international break.

"Sarr, that will not be a long, long one, but it will be weeks before he can help the team," said Frank.

"That [Sarr returning after the international break] was the hope. But it's a groin issue, so it's a bit touch and go. It's nothing big, it will just take time."

Palace's only previous European meeting with a Spanish side came in the 2025-26 Conference League final, when they won 1-0 against Rayo Vallecano.

Since losing 1-0 to AEK Larnaca in October 2025 in the Conference League, Palace are unbeaten in all eight major European games at Selhurst Park (W5 D3).

La Real have just one win in 11 European games against English teams (D3 L7), winning 1-0 away at Manchester United in September 2022.

"To be able to play the Europa League is fantastic," added Frank.

"It's a big praise to the former staff, the reason why we are here is because they won the Conference League.

"We're going to be very competitive tomorrow and will do everything we can to win the game."
