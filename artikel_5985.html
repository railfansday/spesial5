'You will regret it': Angry resident confronts Democratic mayor during chaotic council meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A Sacramento City Council meeting descended into chaos after a resident confronted Mayor Kevin McCarty, threatened his political career and attempted to publicly reveal his home address, prompting officials to cut her microphone and recess the meeting. (City of Sacramento via YouTube)

A Sacramento City Council meeting descended into chaos Tuesday after an angry resident threatened the Democratic mayor's political career and attempted to publicly reveal his home address, prompting officials to cut her microphone and temporarily recess the meeting.

Video from Tuesday's City Council livestream reviewed by Fox News Digital showed a woman confronting Sacramento Mayor Kevin McCarty during the public comment portion of the meeting, warning that protesters would continue disrupting city business if officials failed to address their concerns.

"If you do not vote in our interest again, you will regret it," the woman said. "We protest. We stop things. We shut things down, Kevin. And careers."

The woman then suggested that McCarty's political career had run its course.

"It's time for your f---ing career to end," she said, before raising the possibility of his retirement.

As council officials repeatedly attempted to restore order, someone in the audience also urged the angry woman to show decorum.

The confrontation escalated further when the woman threatened to disclose the mayor's home address.

Her microphone appeared to be cut as she began reciting what she claimed was the mayor's address, and a council official warned her against disclosing private information.

As shouting erupted in the council chambers, officials called a recess and council members left the dais.

Approximately 20 minutes later, council members returned and resumed public comment. No audience members were removed, The Sacramento Bee reported.

According to the newspaper, the confrontation with the woman, identified by the outlet as a local immigration activist, unfolded amid ongoing tensions over the city's immigration enforcement policies and its cooperation with federal authorities.

Earlier in the meeting, McCarty announced that the council would postpone consideration of a $3.7 million Department of Homeland Security grant agreement amid questions about potential data-sharing provisions.

Tuesday's incident marked the fifth time this year that Sacramento City Council members had recessed a meeting because of audience disruptions, according to The Sacramento Bee. The repeated confrontations have largely stemmed from activists' concerns about immigration enforcement and the city's sanctuary policies, the outlet reported.

The mayor's office declined to comment on the incident to Fox News Digital.

This week's outburst comes months after Fox News Digital reported that a live microphone captured McCarty and Vice Mayor Karina Talamantes discussing whether disruptive protesters could be barred from future council meetings.

"We should go over this disruptions thing, because if we want to ban somebody in the future, like to take them out, we have to point out specifically," Talamantes was heard saying during the July meeting.

"I know, but the city attorney said we can't do that," McCarty responded.

The woman who confronted McCarty on Tuesday also appeared during the July meeting, when she shouted at the mayor, "Kevin, listen to me. Stop being an a--hole, dude. Listen to your f---ing constituents."

Following the July incident, McCarty told Fox News Digital that council leaders were responsible for ensuring public meetings remained accessible and orderly.

Fox News Digital's Stepheny Price contributed to this report.
