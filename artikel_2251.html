Wow' - Wolves star Adam Armstrong drops reaction after big personal moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Wolverhampton Wanderers striker Adam Armstrong has taken to social media after recording his 500th career appearance in his side's 2-1 victory over Bristol City at Ashton Gate on Wednesday night.

The experienced striker has played for a plethora of clubs in the Premier League, Championship and League One, but for now is fully focused on aiding Wolves' aim of an instant return to the top-flight under Cesar Peixoto.

Armstrong played 85 of the 90 minutes in the West Country before being replaced by Tawanda Chirewa, just three minutes after his initial effort, which was saved by Brad Collins, was turned home on the rebound by Newcastle United loanee, Sean Neave, who went on to net the winner in the final minute.

It was a joyous occasion for all associated with the Black Country outfit, as a fourth win in five matches saw them climb into the second tier's automatic promotion places, just one point behind leaders Middlesbrough ahead of Saturday's trip to Wrexham.

Armstrong signed for his current employers in January from Southampton in a deal reportedly worth £7m plus £2m in add-ons.

The experienced forward has made 15 of his 500 appearances in the early weeks of the current campaign, during which he has scored four times, and will be hoping it ends in similar fashion to the 2023/24 season when he tasted promotion under Russell Martin at St Mary's.

Taking to Instagram after noting his personal milestone, the North East native said: "Wow what a night and what a win!!

"Who would have thought, 500. To the fans, teammates, staff at all the clubs. You played a huge part in me getting to this milestone.

"To my family. Thanks for all the support along the way. You are what drives me each day. Next goal promotion and 600 games," he added.

Among those in the replies paying tribute to the longevity of the striker's career were Ben Brereton Diaz, Will Smallbone, Harry Wilson, Bradley Dack and his current Molineux teammates, Mateus Mane and Tommy Doyle.

The Newcastle United academy graduate has enjoyed a fine first 500 games, with 102 of those coming in the Premier League, albeit he would have ultimately liked to have seen a better return than eight goals and nine assists in the top-flight.

However, Armstrong's clinical ability in front of goal hasn't ever been disputed in the Championship for the likes of Wolves, Southampton and Blackburn Rovers in particular.

289 of his outings have come in the division which he currently plies his trade in, and so have 94 of his goals and 37 of his assists, none bigger than his winner for Saints in the 2024 play-off final against Leeds United at Wembley Stadium.

Armstrong has started in nine of his 12 Championship outings so far this season, but it's fair to say the striker hadn't had the greatest of starts to the new season until recently.

He has since scored two in Wolves' past three games whilst having a big role in Neave's initial equaliser midweek, and this comes after receiving plenty of backing from Peixoto to start firing plenty of goals in.

“It is important for his confidence but he is an experienced player and I don’t believe he was losing confidence," said the Portuguese.

With Armstrong setting his dream out in terms of promotion, he will hope to start his next century of games with a positive result in North Wales this weekend.
