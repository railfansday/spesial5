Derby County dealt another injury blow with key forward set for six weeks out

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Derby County went another game without a win as they were torn apart by Norwich City 4-0 on Tuesday night. John Eustace is under serious pressure as DCFC host Bolton Wanderers, in what could be his last chance to turn things round at Pride Park.

The Rams have not been helped by injuries this season and they have been dealt yet another blow now. John Eustace has told the BBC that Sammie Szmodics is set for surgery on a knee injury that will see him out for six weeks.

The forward limped off in the defeat to Norwich City during the second-half. Szmodics had been playing through the injury but after aggravating it on Tuesday, surgery was the only option.

He has made a decent start to this season having returned to the club in the summer after an initial loan deal last term.

Losing Szmodics a significant blow for Derby County, particularly given their precarious position in the Championship. Eustace’s side sit 23rd with just seven points from 14 games, having recorded only one win all season.

Szmodics is joint-top scorer with three league goals, alongside Bobby Clark, and has also provided one assist. Eustace described the loss as “huge” after confirming Szmodics will undergo surgery and miss approximately six weeks, potentially ruling him out until mid-December.

DCFC already have several players unavailable. While six weeks might ordinarily be manageable, the Rams’ lack of goals, creativity and squad depth makes this a considerable setback as they attempt to escape the relegation zone.

Filip Bilbija looks the most likely direct replacement for Szmodics in the No.10 role, with Bobby Clark another strong option. Szmodics has predominantly featured as an attacking midfielder in Eustace’s preferred 4-2-3-1 formation, operating behind the striker with freedom to get forward.

Bilbija has already been tipped to fill that position against Bolton, allowing Clark to remain in the double pivot alongside Lewis Travis or Alex Mowatt. Clark is also joint-top scorer with three goals and has the versatility to play further forward if required.

Other alternatives include Rhian Brewster, who can operate centrally or out wide, while Mohammed Fuseini and Corey Blackett-Taylor offer further attacking options. Henrik Meister and Carlton Morris could also provide cover in a supporting role.

Ryan Hedges is another fitness doubt, adding to Derby’s injury concerns. Eustace has options, but none quite replicate Szmodics’ experience and goal threat in the No.10 position.
