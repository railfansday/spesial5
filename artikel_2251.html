Tom Brady eyes stake in Premier League giants after Birmingham City departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Birmingham City confirmed last week that Tom Brady’s involvement with the club had come to an end. The former NFL star joined as part of Knighthead’s investment group following its takeover in 2023 and subsequently held a position on the Blues’ advisory board.

Brady has since spoken out on the ‘special club’ that Birmingham City is since his departure. However, he is already in talks to join another English club, with talks underway with Chelsea over a minority stake in the club, according to The Guardian.

The talks are said to be positive and he already has a strong relationship with Behdad Eghbali, whose fund Clearlake Capital owns 86.5% of Chelsea after Todd Boehly and Mark Walter sold their shares in September.

They have also said that has decision to sell his stake in BCFC, was too pursue the deal with Chelsea. Brady has previous with Chelsea, where he was a guest for the club as they won the Club World Cup back in the summer of 2025 and he was seen celebrating with the players.

While Brady was never a prominent member of the ownership around the club, he was the most well-known by some margin. That brought a lot of commercial benefits and a lot of eyes on the club, alongside their Amazon Prime series.

His departure did feel abrupt but now the reason is out, things start to make more sense. At the end of the day though, Brady brought eyes to the club and that has helped them keep the positivity flowing following their relegation in 2024.

Now, with BCFC flying at the top end of the Championship, Brady’s was no longer required. Selling his stake to buy one in Chelsea makes sense, considering his lack of real involvement with Birmingham City.

For Birmingham City, outside noise may not be what they need right now. Brady moving on will take a lot of that away, especially from outside of the UK.

However, their sole focus should be on what is happening on the pitch, not anything off the field. Chris Davies’ side return from the international break with a massive top of the table clash away at Stoke City.

BCFC know that a defeat to Stoke will mean they leapfrog them in the table. With the Blues currently top of the table, that is not what they would want, especially given Middlesbrough and Wolves could also go above them with wins this weekend.
