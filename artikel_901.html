Wales beaten by 10-man Portugal as Neto scores but is sent off

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wales vs Portugal. UEFA Nations League Group A4.

Match report as Wales lose final Nations League Group A4 game to 10-man Portugal in a 3-2 home defeat; Man Utd's Bruno Fernandes hit opener; Chelsea's Pedro Neto scored Portugal's second but was sent off in second half for two bookings in two minutes; Portugal finish top with Wales bottom

Wales ended their Nations League group campaign with a 3-2 defeat against 10-man Portugal in Cardiff after Chelsea's Pedro Neto was sent off.

Although there was little riding on the contest, with Wales guaranteed a League A relegation/promotion play-off in March and Portugal having qualified for the quarter-finals, the two sides produced an entertaining spectacle.

Portugal skipper Bruno Fernandes blasted the opener after 11 minutes, but that was immediately wiped out by David Brooks' ninth goal for Wales.

Sorba Thomas broke free to put Wales ahead before the break, but again there was a quickfire response as Neto pounced to restore parity.

Substitute Carlos Forbs stroked Portugal ahead in the final quarter, and the visitors had to negotiate the final 16 minutes a player short after Neto was sent off after receiving two yellow cards in the space of two minutes.

Wales finish bottom of League A4 with one win and five defeats, and end a disappointing 2026 with a solitary victory, three draws and six losses from 10 games.

Portugal, meanwhile, finish the campaign as group winners as they earned their first win since Cristiano Ronaldo's international retirement.

Craig Bellamy's side had lost 1-0 in Lisbon in September in a game that was to prove Ronaldo's last for Portugal.

Ronaldo's subsequent retirement from international football probably had an impact on the attendance at Cardiff City Stadium, with some seats unfilled, despite the game being officially declared a sell-out.

There would be no clean sheet for Danny Ward on his 50th appearance as Joao Neves robbed Josh Sheehan and Fernandes lashed home from the edge of the box.

Wales responded immediately with Rubin Colwill twice involved, first setting up Harry Wilson for a shot Diogo Costa blocked with his legs and then Brooks, who swept home his ninth international goal, and second in two games.

Wales hit the front five minutes before the break after Brooks worked a one-two with Wilson before releasing Thomas, who advanced from his own half to slot past Costa.

The lead lasted three minutes as Neto lost Jay DaSilva from Trincao's cross to finish at the far post.

A breathless first half was not finished as Wilson hit the roof of the net from halfway with Costa retreating frantically, and the Leeds midfielder then forced a sprawling save from the Portugal goalkeeper.

Portugal regained the lead 20 minutes from time when Club Brugge winger Forbs - wearing Portugal's new-styled number seven shirt in the post-Ronaldo era - collected Ben Cabango's poor header and stepped inside Ben Davies to find the bottom corner.

Neto clattered into Brennan Johnson just one minute and 59 seconds after being booked, but Portugal held on for a fifth Nations League win to seal top spot.

Wales head coach Craig Bellamy to BBC Sport:

"We knew in moments we were going to have to suffer due to the quality they have. The intensity wasn't there. We have a lot of players who don't play week in, week out a high-tempo game in such a short space of time. It's not ideal.

"We knew that at moments, when we win it, the spaces open. There were moments that worked really well. It summed up the group for us - really good moments for us; we make a mistake - at this level, these are normal things that happen.

"But also, that final moment. These teams have that profile to hurt you. At both ends, that last bit has caught us a bit in this group.

"Rubin Colwill is really good. There are times where he takes too many touches. You can't do that against this type of team. Five, six, seven - no! When he motors like he has, the ability to be a real top-end player. I'm very critical of him; I do love him.

"But I know there's more. That's why I wanted to play him in this A league. He's going to have to prove himself."

"For large parts, we played really well again. At times in the first half, we had to suffer. They're a great team full of world-class stars. At times in our shape, we were comfy.

"But look at our three goals. We're disappointed. But for large parts we were really, really good again.

"We knew from the start it was going to be tough. The first game in the group against Portugal, we showed we belong. Against Norway, we beat them. In large spells tonight, we were the better team.

"Going forward or defending the goal a bit better, that's what we've got to focus on. We're going toe-to-toe with these teams; our shape is good off the ball."
