In the past 24 hours, Bitcoin [BTC] and Ethereum [ETH] have shed 1.87% and 5.27%, respectively. Following weakness in stock markets, particularly tech and AI, as well as a broader risk-off market sentiment, crypto has been negatively affected in recent weeks.

More than $1 billion in leveraged positions across crypto have been wiped out in the past 24 hours due to a sharp sell-off in the stock market.

The S&P 500 Futures showed $300 billion in market cap wiped out a minute after the market opened, and just six minutes later, the losses were reversed.

Bitcoin slipped below the psychological $75k level as a result of the selling. It continued its bearish momentum, as did Ethereum, which was on the verge of sliding below the $2,000 support.

Over the past week, the memecoin sector was one of the worst performers, data from Glassnode showed. The sector’s market cap was down 7.3%, compared to Ethereum’s 6.3% loss or Bitcoin’s 4.1% drawdown.

Even Dogecoin [DOGE] was down 8.1% in a week. By contrast, Pepe [PEPE] was up 5.13% over the past week, although it has struggled to push itself convincingly beyond the $0.0000050-$0.0000052 supply zone.

In the past 24 hours, PEPE saw a 212% increase in daily trading volume and has bounced by 5.45%. The popular memecoin has been unable to climb above the $0.0000052 resistance.

In an earlier report, AMBCrypto pointed out that the Value Area High was an important resistance level. It has moved higher from $0.00000491 to $0.00000518 due to the price action and volume trends of the past ten days.

The memecoin was yet to break out past this supply zone and beyond the $0.00000536 swing high from September.

The OBV maintained its upward trajectory, showing steady buying pressure on PEPE.

The 4-hour chart showed a price compression in progress, while the wider price structure on this timeframe retained a bullish bias. In light of this compression, swing traders can expect a breakout to the upside in the coming days.
