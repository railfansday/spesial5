David Schwartz, CTO Emeritus at Ripple and one of the creators of the XRP Ledger, has once again commented on his XRP and Bitcoin sales. This time, the reason was a viral post on X that claimed he had sold almost all of his crypto over the last few months.

According to the post, Schwartz's portfolio reportedly went from 26 million XRP, 1,500 BTC and 40,000 ETH to just 3 million XRP, 1 BTC and 6 ETH. The author called it "an insane reduction" and also referred to Schwartz as the current CTO of Ripple, while he now holds the emeritus title.

Schwartz replied that the timeline in the post was wrong. "That wasn't in the last few months, that was in the last decade," he wrote. As proof, he shared his own post from April 21, 2019. In that post, Schwartz said he was still sticking to the "de-risking strategy" that he and his wife agreed on when they first decided to put their own money into crypto. He also admitted back then that selling during the bull run "still hurt."

At the same time, Schwartz did not dispute the numbers themselves. "But it's basically true. I hate risk. I can't help it," he wrote in a follow-up post. According to him, pretty much every big risk he has ever taken has worked out well, and rationally he should be taking more risk as he gets wealthier. "But I hate it. I just hate risk and can't do anything about it," Schwartz added.

That wasn't in the last few months, that was in the last decade. You can find me talking about this in many posts over the years, like this one:https://t.co/5MgcPZ5qyG

At press time, XRP was trading at around $0.939, Bitcoin at $61,477 and Ethereum at $1,722. At these prices, the remaining 3 million XRP, 1 BTC and 6 ETH are worth about $2.89 million, and XRP accounts for more than 97% of this sum.

For comparison, at current prices, the peak holdings mentioned in the post would be worth over $185 million, with Bitcoin alone accounting for about half of it.

Schwartz has already explained his sales several times this year. In May, he said he did not have much XRP left and had tried to move most of his assets, other than Ripple stock, away from crypto exposure. In July, he said he regrets selling XRP at $0.10 and ETH at around $1, but noted that he and his wife had agreed to sell at every new all-time high.

Earlier in November, Schwartz disclosed that he sold about 2.5 million XRP in 2025. Per Schwartz, most of these tokens were received in 2020 and 2023, and he paid approximately $1.7 million in capital gains tax on those sales. He shared this in response to a user who called XRP a "terrible investment."

Among other things, Schwartz has previously said that his main exposure to the crypto sector now comes through Ripple stock, not XRP. In March, Ripple launched a share buyback of up to $750 million that valued the company at $50 billion.
