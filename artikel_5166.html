XRP Certificate Logs Banks Freshly Joined Infra

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Home » Ripple (XRP) » XRP Certificate Logs Banks Freshly Joined Infra

Ripple’s ties to the corporate corridors are evident in the surfaced logs, disclosing four colossal names.

Public certificate transparency records have drawn attention to technical setups connecting Ripple systems with multiple banks.

The logs, visible on sites such as crt.sh, list domains under bank names that include “ripple” or related identifiers, along with certificates issued or renewed in recent years.

One record points to a Bank of America address containing the word “prod,” indicating a production environment.

The certificate is issued to Ripple Labs Inc. It was first issued in late October 2023, renewed in 2024, and renewed again on October 27, 2025. The domain continues to resolve.

Bank of America has been listed as a RippleNet member for years, though membership in the messaging network does not by itself confirm use of XRP for settlements.

Certificate transparency logs record the existence of TLS certificates for specific domains. Renewals over multiple years further indicate that the setups have not been abandoned.

No recent public statements from the banks have confirmed the scale or nature of any current activity tied to these certificates.

The records provide a technical signal that several banks have maintained environments linked to Ripple systems.

For institutions already known to have explored or joined RippleNet in prior years, the continued presence of these certificates is unsurprising.

Determining whether the setups support testing, limited production use, or broader settlement activity requires disclosures beyond public certificate logs.
