'Bitter' Romelu Lukaku set to 'drop a bombshell' on Belgian football following his shock retirement, claims former team-mate after one-hour phone call

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 04:37 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 04:38 EST, 24 November 2026

Radja Nainggolan has claimed his former team-mate Romelu Lukaku is planning a 'bombshell' statement on Belgian football following his shock retirement from the national team.

Lukaku, 33, unexpectedly announced his retirement from international football following Belgium's Nations League matches against Turkey and Italy earlier this month.

Belgium's all-time record goalscorer had been left on the bench against Italy, while he had not been in the squad three days earlier for the match against Turkey.

Lukaku's shock announcement caught Belgian media by surprise, while his former international team-mate Nainggolan has claimed more news will follow from the star.

Nainggolan, who won 30 caps for the national team between 2009 and 2018, revealed on the Ongefilterd Voetbal podcast he had a one-hour phone call with Lukaku following his decision.

The former Inter Milan and Roma midfielder stated that Lukaku told him: 'You are only allowed to say that a bombshell is about to explode.'

'So something is still coming,' Nainggolan said.

'What? I don't know. There were some internal problems between him and a certain person. But I'm not going to say much about that.

'Romelu will bring that up himself. He was a bit bitter, though. He was disappointed, frustrated.

'We will hear from him soon what will happen.

'About the real problems that were at play, he will be able to explain that better than I.'

Lukaku's retirement had marked a stunning reversal of his plans, with the former Chelsea and Manchester United forward having vowed in January that the 2026 World Cup would not be his last major tournament with the Belgium national team.

Describing his international career as a 'great ride', Lukaku had confirmed his international retirement on social media.

'16 years old', his Instagram statement began. 'For 16 years, I gave everything for the national team. I experienced every possible emotion. I played with fantastic teammates for whom I will always be grateful.

The Belgian forward has fallen down the pecking order and spent a lot of time on the bench

'I want to thank them all. I also want to thank all the coaches for pushing me and giving me the confidence to help the team. It was a great ride, and I enjoyed every moment.

'To the Belgian fans, thank you for the support you have given our team. I am proud to have represented my country at four World Cups and three European Championships. I will never forget those moments. I am at peace with my decision to stop here. It was amazing, it was a joy. I am grateful. Thank you.'

The Belgian veteran accompanied his post with a gallery of photographs from his storied international career, from his debut against Croatia under Dick Advocaat in 2010 to his final chapter wearing the armband.

Lukaku has captained Belgium on a handful of occasions since 2020, alongside the likes of Kevin De Bruyne and Youri Tielemans.

The 33-year-old forward, who became one of the hottest properties in world football after breaking onto the scene at Chelsea, has been a regular fixture in the international set-up for almost two decades.

Spearheading Belgium's golden generation, which included star-studded talents such as De Bruyne, Thibaut Courtois and Eden Hazard, Lukaku chalked up an impressive 135 caps for the international team.

He also retired as Belgium's all-time top scorer on 94 goals, just six shy of a century.

After suffering multiple injury setbacks, he featured at the 2026 World Cup under Rudi Garcia and continued to play a crucial role, scoring three times as a substitute to guide Belgium to the quarter-finals where they were ultimately stopped by eventual champions Spain.

Since Mark van Bommel's appointment in August, following Garcia's departure at the end of his contract, Lukaku has slipped further down the pecking order, with Charles De Ketelaere preferred to lead the line.

During Belgium's resounding 4-1 victory over Italy on Sunday, Lukaku warmed the bench as an unused substitute – and wasn't even in the squad to face Turkey three days before.

Lukaku, pictured with Kevin De Bruyne, was an unused substitute as Belgium beat Italy 4-1

While Belgium were winning in the second half against the Italians, Lukaku was seen warming up on the sidelines in the hope of featuring for one last time, but unfortunately was not given the opportunity.

VTM NIEUWS anchor Maarten Breckx claimed De Bruyne looked 'concerned' while speaking to Lukaku on the bench, suggesting the former Man City star was unhappy to see his team-mate overlooked.

'When it became clear that he wouldn't be allowed to come on, De Bruyne was talking to him. You could see the concern in his eyes,' he said.

'You could tell, especially from Romelu's attitude, that he was dissatisfied and displeased. He had probably counted on getting some minutes...'

With only Jan Vertonghen and Axel Witsel registering more international caps than Lukaku over their careers, boasting 157 and 140 appearances respectively, Belgium are expected to give the 33-year-old forward a send-off he deserves in the near future.

Witsel, who also called time on his Belgium career this year, was one of the team-mates to celebrate Lukaku's retirement on Instagram.

'Thanks for everything, legend. Thank you, what a journey we've had together,' he wrote.
