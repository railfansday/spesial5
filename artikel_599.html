Gayle King to Miss Week of ‘CBS Mornings’ Shows Amid Death of Her Son-in-Law Virgil Miller

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Executive producer Jon Tower wrote in a memo to staff obtained by People that the host will be back the week of Dec. 7.

Gayle King‘s son-in-law Virgil Miller has died following a battle with cancer at 45, and the CBS Mornings host will miss next week’s shows “to help with family matters,” according to People.

A memo sent to staff from executive producer Jon Tower and obtained by People announced Miller’s death. “Mornings Family — I’m sorry to be delivering this sad news,” the message began, as he wrote that “over the Thanksgiving holiday,” Virgil died after a “courageous battle with cancer.”

King’s daughter Kirby Bumpus and Miller got married in December 2020 at Oprah Winfrey’s home in Santa Barbara in a small ceremony. They share a son and daughter. The Emmy winner commended their nuptials on CBS Mornings in February 2021, and also in a heartfelt Instagram post.

Miller attended Florida A&M University, graduating with a bachelor’s degree in biology in 2003 and a master’s degree in public health in 2005. There, he was a drum major as part of FAMU’s Marching 100.

He went on to serve nearly 13 years in the U.S. House of Representatives, and joined Akin in 2019 as a senior policy and strategy advisor before becoming a senior advisor.

The Hollywood Reporter has reached out to CBS, but did not hear back.
