Bayern Munich’s Max Eberl has bigger fish to fry than Jesse Marsch

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Max Eberl had to wonder what he did to deserve Friday's headache.

Between former FC Bayern CEO Karl-Heinz Rummenigge criticizing Jamal Musiala's contract and Canada coach Jesse Marsch taking aim at the club, it was a lot to handle.

Still, Eberl has no interest in turning Marsch's remarks into a public back-and-forth.

Asked about the Canada boss's comments, the Bayern Munich board member for sport made it clear the club will not engage in a war of words. His reply was brief, pointed, and deliberately dismissive.

"I heard what he said, but we will not respond to it. Sometimes silence is better," Eberl told Sport1 (as captured by @iMiaSanMia).

That is about as close to shutting the door on a topic as one gets without leaving the microphone. Eberl acknowledged the criticism, but Bayern clearly sees no value in a public response.

Whether that is the right call depends on how Marsch's comments are viewed, yet Bayern's leadership is content to let them sit without escalating things. In a noisy football cycle, refusing to feed a controversy can make a statement of its own — and Eberl is not giving Marsch any extra airtime.
