CrowdStrike (CRWD +1.25%) announced a definitive agreement to acquire Zscaler for approximately $43.9 billion, a strategic transaction that incorporates network security into the Falcon platform to establish a unified control plane. The company reported record net new annual recurring revenue and accelerated growth across its primary financial metrics, driven by high demand for AI-driven security modernization. Management stated that the combination of the two cloud-native leaders will cover devices, workloads, identities, data, and the network on a single platform. The quarter was characterized by significant adoption of the Falcon Flex commercial model and the rapid growth of Falcon Guardian, which secures autonomous AI agents. The combined entity is expected to begin with more than $10 billion in ending annual recurring revenue once the transaction closes in the second half of fiscal year 2028.
