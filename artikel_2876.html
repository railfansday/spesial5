Are the Browns playing today? NFL Week 11 schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Cleveland Browns have had an up and down season so far.

Deshaun Watson, in the last year of his monster contract, has thrown for 2,118 yards through ten games along with seven touchdowns and four interceptions. He has added 266 yards on the ground with two rushing scores.

The quarterback was put under concussion protocol back in the Week 8 rivalry game against the Pittsburgh Steelers after a helmet-to-helmet hit. He sat out the next week's matchup and Shedeur Sanders took the reins in an overtime loss to the New Orleans Saints. Watson then returned in Week 10 for a win over the Houston Texans.

On the defense, Denzel Ward has three interceptions and Mason Graham and Isaiah McGuire each have four sacks.

The Browns are trying to return to the playoffs for the first time since 2023.
