The Lakers finally slowed down Luka Doncic — for one night

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After a seemingly endless flurry of efficient 40- and 50-point masterpieces, fatigue finally caught up to him. His legs felt the weight of carrying the team without Austin Reaves, who has been sidelined for three straight games. His shot was finally off.

It took him playing on the second leg of a back-to-back following a game that began at 8 p.m. and went into overtime the night before.

But Doncic was finally not quite himself Wednesday.

The Slovenian superstar finished with 29 points (2-for-12 shooting from beyond the arc), five rebounds, seven assists, two steals and nine turnovers in the Lakers' 109-97 loss to the Jazz, which snapped their nine-game winning streak.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers handles the ball against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"Yeah, I mean, honestly I just got to do way better than this," Doncic said. "This is unacceptable for my part. I got to lead the team in better ways. So just, like I said, got to look at this game, got to move on and learn from this."

Doncic proceeded to pick apart his performance.

"Well, just a little bit of everything," he said. "Obviously the shots, the turnovers. Those [are] two [of the] specifics. I think defensively, we're in a good space. Just offensively, I got to lead the team better than this."

It was an exceedingly rare relative off night for the 27-year-old amid an MVP-caliber start to the season. He entered Wednesday's game leading the league in scoring with 38.8 points per game on a career-high 39.8% shooting from beyond the arc — while carrying the Lakers to the best offensive rating in the league.

But alas, after having the best start in modern NBA history, he finally sputtered a bit.

It happened after Doncic had a 36-point, seven-rebound and seven-assist performance less than 24 hours earlier as the Lakers clawed their way back from an 18-point deficit to beat the Hornets in overtime on Tuesday, 118-114.

Lakers coach JJ Redick acknowledged that the load Doncic has been carrying without Reaves is astronomical.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers handles the ball against the Utah Jazz on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

"The shot creation that is required from him, I think it takes its toll," Redick said. "…So you know that's the luxury of having two really elite creators, and obviously when they're both in the lineup, it makes things a little bit easier."

Doncic has the highest career usage rate in NBA history and he's nipping at the heels of the highest single-season usage rate ever. It's a load that has been exacerbated without Reaves, who averages 24.7 points per game, and is considered day-to-day after sustaining a left heel bruise against the Bucks on Nov. 11.

Quentin Grimes compensated for Reaves' absence Tuesday by exploding for a career-high 47 points.

But Wednesday, Doncic didn't get enough offensive assistance to offset his less-than-brilliant performance. Grimes, who was the team's second-leading scorer, finished with 20 points, going 1-for-6 from the field in the fourth quarter. Walker Kessler had 10 points on 3-for-7 shooting and 11 rebounds.

When Jake LaRavia was told Doncic took responsibility for the Lakers' loss, he balked.

Luka Doncic handles the ball during a game against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"Yeah, of course he's going to say that," said LaRavia, who had four points, six rebounds and two assists. "It's obviously not on him. It's a team game. Yeah, I mean he's obviously been carrying the offensive load, so if there's ever bad shooting nights, it's on the rest of the team to kind of help be better on offense and help make shots and stay in rhythm offensively."

And when Doncic's battery dipped in his third game in four nights following a brutal turnaround at Crypto.com Arena, the 3-13 Jazz were able to steal a game against the 13-3 Lakers.

Doncic had been playing so stunningly well that a 29-point performance could come across as a relative stinker.

But a bit of exhaustion finally took a toll on the epicness of Luka magic for one night.

"Yeah, that can't be an excuse," Dončić said. "All NBA teams play 82 games, so that can't be an excuse. I got to do way better. I feel that we had some open shots we didn't make, but it's one game. We got to move on."
