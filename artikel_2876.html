Kickoff time set for Gamecocks-Georgia battle on Nov. 21

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Nov. 9—COLUMBIA — The Southeastern Conference on Monday set a kickoff time and network for South Carolina's final home game.

The No. 21 Gamecocks' Nov. 21 game hosting No. 2 Georgia will kick off at 7:30 p.m. and be broadcast on ABC. It is USC's final home game of the season ... perhaps.

USC (6-3, 4-3 SEC) is charging toward the College Football Playoff, and if the Gamecocks win out, they could get another home game in the CFP. But the Bulldogs (8-1, 6-1), fully expected to be No. 1 when the new CFP rankings are released on Tuesday, will be a major hurdle in front of USC's dream.

The Bulldogs lead the series 55-19-2, but the two haven't played since 2023, a 24-14 USC loss in Athens which the Gamecocks led 14-0. Georgia is the opponent that USC has played the second-most times behind archrival Clemson.

The Gamecocks are at Arkansas this weekend. Georgia hosts Missouri.
