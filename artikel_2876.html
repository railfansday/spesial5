Recapping Colts trade deadline deals and 2027 NFL draft picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With the trade deadline behind us, let's recap each of the moves made by the Indianapolis Colts and the draft capital the team was able to accumulate along the way.

Although just a few weeks ago, it was assumed that the Colts would be having a fire sale, that isn't exactly how things played out. For the rest of the season, Daniel Jones, Jonathan Taylor, and Grover Stewart will remain with the Colts.

But on the flip side, the Colts did make four trades over the last few weeks, as they began building draft capital for the incoming rebuild.

Quenton Nelson was traded to the Seahawks, and in return, Indianapolis received a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

DeForest Buckner was traded to the 49ers for a 2027 fourth-round pick.

Keenan Allen was traded to the 49ers as well for a 2027 sixth-round pick.

Josh Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick were traded to the Chiefs for 2027 fourth- and fifth-round selections.
