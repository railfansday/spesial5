Here are our PS Plus monthly games for November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dead Island 2! Tomb Raider! Monster Train 2!

Sony has announced the monthly games coming to PlayStation Plus Premium and Extra next month, bringing a mix of triple-A bangers and remastered classics to the service on the 3rd November. All are, of course, coming at the start of what is almost certainly going to be the biggest month of the year releases-wise.

Perhaps the biggest game on offer is Dead Island 2, Dambuster Studios and Deep Silver's first person zombie action game. There's plenty of grisly goodness here, and ample loot to snatch up and use against the undead hordes. It's a decent enough game, which should offer plenty of entertainment to those looking for something chunky to dig their teeth into while the Halloween spirit still lingers.

The remastered Tomb Raider Darkness Trilogy is not one to be skipped, especially if you're looking for a sizable time sink from your PlayStation Plus monthly games. While not known as being exactly the best Lara Croft games out there, Aspyr did some fine work polishing them up for modern platforms.

Finally we've got Monster Train 2. A deckbuilder roguelike that has you operating and managing trains where the forces of heaven and hell reside, all to defeat titans that threaten the orderly state of things. As the sole indie release on this monthly selection of PlayStation Plus games, it may be worth a try for those who can't get enough from the Roguelike genre even now. It even features a smug looking mushroom fella, how can you go wrong with that.

The reveal of these games coming to PlayStation Plus means the clock is ticking to grab October's offerings, many of which would fit well on the console of those getting into the spooky season.
