10+ Xbox Games Are Releasing Today, Including A Dangerous Twist On Mario Party (November 26)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

"New twists on the party genre for sessions of fun, screaming, and horror"

December is just around the corner, yet we've still got plenty of November games to get through.

Today's lot might not look like much, but there are a few interesting games worth keeping tabs on, starting with Machine Party. Launching with Xbox Play Anywhere support, this dark and dangerous Mario Party-inspired title pits you against your friends in a series of murder-themed mini-games.

Also launching today is Security 51, another XPA title that's giving all sorts of Papers, Please vibes. If you don't know what I'm talking about, the game essentially has you working as a security guard, checking documents and questioning visitors to Area 51. Not everything is as it seems though, so keep your eyes peeled, yeah?

And finally, there's The Spectrum Retreat: Redux, a first-person puzzler from an award-winning game dev. Personally, I don't know much about this one, but on the surface, it looks pretty cool.

In this series of dangerous, and some would say highly illegal, party games you will have to use every trick in the book to survive. Play through classic party game formats and new twists on the genre for sessions of fun, screaming, and horror.

Security 51 is an atmospheric security guard simulator inspired by Papers, Please, and SCP paranoia. You’re on duty in the underground bunker of Area 51, where the most dangerous part begins at the elevator: the shaft descends for miles down to unknown levels.

The Spectrum Retreat: Redux is a first-person puzzle thriller created by Dan Smith, Winner of the 2016 BAFTA Young Game Designer Award.
