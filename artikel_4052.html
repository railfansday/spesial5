GTA 6 Won’t Let You Enter A Hostile Area The Same Way Twice

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ever since Grand Theft Auto 6 was released, countless players have been flooding social media with discoveries, comedic moments, and features that have managed to impress players who previously dismissed the series altogether. While most of these are entirely original to GTA 6 itself, some of the recently discovered mechanics are ones that seem more than a little familiar to stealth fans.

Although Rockstar has plenty of digs at other game studios and references to real-world franchises, one of the ways GTA 6 tackles repeat attempts at raiding a location bears a striking resemblance to those found in none other than Metal Gear Solid 5: The Phantom Pain of all places.

Added alongside the versatility of crime options scattered across Leonida comes in the form of criminal bases you can raid for goods like meth labs and other gang bases. While they seem straightforward at first, players like David Franklin on X were quick to discover that if you fail too many times going through a specific spot, the NPCs will react to it and prepare new defenses accordingly.

This isn't the only case either, as other players have reported similar instances like climbing over a spot of broken barbed wire and failing, only to come back and find a fresh piece tacked on, forcing them to find another way inside. It isn't just defenses either, as players have reported loot and enemy locations moving afterward too.

While you can find countless other examples of absurdly detailed mechanics and small features just by scrolling through the comments of posts like these, this specific mechanic is one that we've seen in more than a few games.

Most notably with Metal Gear Solid 5, which offered a similarly drastically expanded gameplay system compared to its predecessors, if you use a specific strategy like headshots or attacking at night too many times, enemies will actively react by starting to wear more helmets or equip additional night vision goggles.

People are spreading complete misinformation for a few likes when we should be celebrating how impressive the grand theft auto is in GTA 6.

Although far from the only game to have a similar system, with Hello Neighbor and Alien Isolation both having adaptive enemy AI reacting to your decisions, Metal Gear Solid definitely feels the most similar for the vibe of Grand Theft Auto, especially with plenty of comparisons to the classic franchise being made after GTA 6 significantly revamped its stealth mechanics.
