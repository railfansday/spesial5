‘Dancing With the Stars’ Cast Are Allegedly Upset With One Pro’s Choreography, Think She’s “Gunning for a Broadway Role” and Doing a “Disservice” to Her Celeb

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The pros are “stunned” and in “disbelief,” allegedly!

Another day, another (alleged!) drama on the set of Dancing With the Stars. According to sources who simply love to spill, the show’s talented team of pros think Jenna Johnson is “gunning for a Broadway role, 100%” and making it obvious in her choreography.

The Sun reports that the DWTS cast was “stunned” by Jenna’s 10/10 choreography for “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” with Harry Shum Jr. (which earned them the top scores of the night, for what it’s worth!)—mainly because she gave herself a mini-solo.

“She has become more and more focused on her getting a Broadway offer after this season and after the DWTS tour ends than she is highlighting Harry and it shows,” a source tells The Sun, claiming that pros were in “disbelief” over Jenna’s decision to give herself a small solo. “She put a solo of herself into the routine for Disney Night—which is basically unheard of for a pro to do. Jenna is doing a disservice to [Harry] by putting the emphasis on herself, and the other pros know it.”

Fans definitely took note of the solo moment (and some people questioned it), but the source insists that “Harry has been a great sport and isn’t complaining, he’s just going with the flow.”

And like it or not, Jenna’s choreo is definitely working! Thanks to having the highest scores during Disney night, she and Harry don’t have to compete in the dance-off during Grammys night and can instead focus on nailing their routine, a salsa to “Die With a Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars.
