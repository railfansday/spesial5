Meet the Real-Life Partners of the ‘Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping’ Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The new cast is stacked—and sadly, most of them are taken IRL.

At long last, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is finally (almost) here, revisiting the world of Panem 24 years before the events of the original Hunger Games trilogy (and 40 years after The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes). Centered around a young Haymitch Abernathy, Sunrise on the Reaping tells the story of the Second Quarter Quell (aka the 50th Hunger Games), during which the Capitol doubles the stakes by demanding twice the normal number of tributes.

Of course, that means the cast is twice as packed with familiar faces (including Cosmo’s latest cover stars Whitney Peak, Mckenna Grace, and Maya Hawke). Wondering who from the star-studded cast is taken IRL? Keep scrolling to meet the real-life partners of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping cast, from Joseph Zada’s lowkey girlfriend Polly Talarico to Mckenna Grace’s adorable showmance with Mason Thames.

Current relationship status: Dating Polly Talarico

While Joseph keeps his love life under the radar, he’s (reportedly) been dating “student and nonactor” Polly Talarico since meeting her “in a bar in Sydney earlier this year,” as he revealed during a Vanity Fair interview.

Current relationship status: Dating Mason Thames

Mckenna found love with fellow actor Mason Thames on the set of 2025’s Regretting You, and they’ve been loved up ever since.

Current relationship status: Dating Gus Wenner

Elle first sparked dating rumors with former Rolling Stone CEO Gus Wenner back in fall 2023, and by the 2024 Golden Globes, they were red carpet official. And nearly three years later, they’re still going strong!

Current relationship status: Married to Christian Lee Hutson

Maya was friends with singer-songwriter Christian Lee Hutson for years before they worked together on Maya’s 2023 album Moss...but then things turned romantic. They then made things official on Valentine’s Day 2026 by tying the knot.

Current relationship status: Married to Kirsten Dunst

Jesse first crossed paths with fellow actor Kirsten Dunst while filming Fargo season 2 back in 2015, where they quickly fell for each other. After two kids and a five-year engagement, they finally said “I do” in July 2022.

Current relationship status: Married to Jazz Charton

Kiernan met his now-wife Jazz Charton at a NYC bar back in 2012, and they eloped the following year. Now they have three kids together, the youngest of whom was born just over a year ago.

Currently relationship status: Dating Sophia Rose McDowell

Kelvin reportedly met fellow actor Sophia Rose McDowell on the set of 2020’s Gucci x Dazed: Unity Call as part of Gucci and Dazed’s Absolute Beginners series. And though they’ve never *actually* confirmed their relationship status, they’ve been showing up on red carpets together ever since.

Current relationship status: Dating Sam Nivola

Iris has been with fellow actor Sam Nivola since at least spring of 2024, when she visited him on the set of The White Lotus season 3.

Current relationship status: Married to Cooke Maroney

Jennifer was introduced to art dealer Cooke Maroney by a mutual friend in 2018, and they were so smitten that they tied the knot after just over a year of dating. The couple shares two children together.

Current relationship status: Dating Claudia Traisac

Josh started dating Spanish actor Clauda Traisac in 2013 after they met on the set of Escobar: Paradise Lost, and 13 years later, they’re reportedly still going strong (though they keep their romance very low-key).

Current relationship status: Married to Laura Louie

Perhaps one of the chillest relationships in Hollywood, Woody’s romance with Laura Louie began when she was hired as his personal assistant back in 1987, though it took him years to confess how he really felt. They welcomed three daughters together before finally saying “I do” in 2008.
