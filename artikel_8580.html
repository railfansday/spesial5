Skip the cover charge and a surge-priced ride home this New Year’s Eve, and throw the party at your place. Make the appetizers ahead, pour cava at $8 to $15 a bottle instead of Champagne at $40 or more, and line up free games that build to midnight.

Last New Year’s Eve, open-bar tickets at bars and lounges in major U.S. cities started at $79, and nightclubs ran to $130 or more, according to TickPick. The ride home can cost extra too, since Uber raises fares when many people in one area request rides at the same time.

For 12 guests, $79 tickets cost $948 for four to five hours of open bar, before food or rides. At your place, a bottle of cava for each guest at about $11 comes to $132, and the four make-ahead appetizers below need about $34 of beef, eggs, chicken, and strawberries.

Together that’s about $166, under $14 a guest, before sauce, chocolate, and seasonings from the pantry. The plan above is sized for about a dozen guests, and a big party on a small budget for 20 or more works differently.

Bake and freeze the meatballs the weekend before, and buy the cava then too. On Dec. 30, move the meatballs to the fridge to thaw and make the deviled eggs. That leaves only the chicken and the strawberries for New Year’s Eve.
