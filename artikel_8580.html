Weeks After R.J. Mitte's Death, Breaking Bad Star's Divorce Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dean Norris, known for playing Hank Schrader on Breaking Bad, is facing a difficult personal chapter: his wife Bridget filed for divorce in February 2025 after more than two decades of marriage, and the couple's Temecula restaurant, Swing Inn Cafe, has entered Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The news comes less than a month after the death of his co-star R.J. Mitte.
