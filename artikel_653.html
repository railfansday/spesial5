Taylor Swift Performs ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ and ‘Our Song’ Mashup at CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

“It’s been a while,” Taylor Swift sang during her performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You” at the Country Music Association Awards on Wednesday night — and it sure was, marking her first time performing at the show in 13 years.

The singer gave the first public performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You” about midway through the CMA Awards, bringing along a few famous friends for the ride. Swift began the performance backstage, singing the “Toy Story 5” song as she passed by country stars including Eric Church, Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Brooks & Dunn, Sugarland and Lainey Wilson, the latter of whom was hosting the show. Swift was then brought to the CMA stage by Bill Anderson, the longest-serving member of the Grand Ole Opry, and finished “I Knew It, I Knew You” alongside Keith Urban, who played the “ganjo,” or a banjo with six strings. Along with backup singers and a full band, Swift sang in front of a giant tree with a tire swing, weaving “Our Song” into “I Knew It, I Knew You” before finishing the song lying against the tree. (It’s worth noting that “Our Song” was her first No. 1 at country, and “I Knew It, I Knew You” is her latest, honoring the 20-year gap between milestones.)

It was a welcome return to the CMA stage for Swift, who performed at the ceremony back in 2013 when she received the Pinnacle Award and sang “Red” with Vince Gill and Alison Krauss. She last appeared at the CMAs in 2016 as a surprise presenter to give Entertainer of the Year to Garth Brooks.

Her cameo at the show comes 20 years after her first appearance at the CMAs. Swift recently made a foray back into country with her “Toy Story 5” single “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which was her first new song in over a decade to be marketed as a country song. She made a bold pivot to pop back in 2015 when she released “1989,” though she had experimented with pop and dubstep on her smash album “Red” on songs like “We Are Never Getting Back Together” and “22.”

Swift is nominated at the CMAs this year with “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which is up for single of the year. Earlier this week, she picked up five Grammy nominations, including album of the year for “The Life of a Showgirl”; song of the year and best song written for visual media for “I Knew It, I Knew You”; best pop duo/group performance for “The Life of a Showgirl” with Sabrina Carpenter; and record of the year for “The Fate of Ophelia.”
