‘The Comedy About Spies’ to Open on Broadway This Spring

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The spoof is from the same team as the slapstick comedy 'The Play That Goes Wrong.'

The Comedy About Spies, from the makers of The Play That Goes Wrong, is coming to Broadway this spring.

The play is set to play an 18-week engagement at the Music Box Theatre starting March 27, ahead of an April 14 opening night. The show comes to Broadway after premiering in the West End in 2025, and then returning there for a brief run this August.

The Comedy About Spies will star members of the original Mischief company, though exact members were not named. The play is written by Mischief co-founders Henry Lewis and Henry Shields and directed by Matt DiCarlo. As with their previous works, including The Play That Goes Wrong, which ran on Broadway from 2017 to 2019, the play involves a large degree of physical and slapstick comedy, with the set playing a large role.

“When a rogue British agent steals plans for a top-secret weapon, CIA and KGB spies descend on London’s Piccadilly Hotel in pursuit of the elusive file. Throw in a clueless young couple, a hapless actor angling for a part in a spy movie and enough double agents to confuse even the sharpest operative, and the result is a 1960s escapade of bungled missions, tangled identities and catastrophic miscommunication that spirals hilariously out of control,” the production description reads.

The play features set design by David Farley, costume design by Deborah Andrews, movement direction by Shelley Maxwell, lighting design by Johanna Town and sound design and composition by Jon Fiber for Jollygoodtunes.

The Comedy About Spies is produced on Broadway by Kevin McCollum (Six, Oh, Mary!), Kenny Wax and Stage Presence Ltd in association with Jo Danvers.
