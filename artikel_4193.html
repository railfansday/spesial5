Skyrim Celebrates 15 Years Of Adventure With Switch eShop Sale, Get 60% Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Forget Zelda and GTA for just a moment, as Bethesda needs to remind us about its epic fantasy RPG, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim.

If you somehow haven't played it before, there's now another opportunity to give it a go. The game is celebrating its 15th anniversary this week, and on the Nintendo front, there's an eShop sale. In the US, there's a 60% discount on the original release, reducing it to just $19.99 on the Switch.

Nintendo of America: "Starting Nov 11 at 9am PT until Dec 2 at 11:59pm PT, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim will be 60% off on Nintendo Switch!"

Unfortunately, at least for now, this sale does not seem to apply to the Anniversary Edition in this region, but if you do happen to own that, there's a free Switch 2 upgrade for this version of the game.

Bethesda has also released a special "Anniversary Creation Bundle" on Xbox, PC and PlayStation to celebrate this special occasion. You can find out more about this on Bethesda's website.

"To celebrate this momentous anniversary, the Skyrim Anniversary Bundle gathers five Creations from our Verified Creators, each offering a new reason to return. From finding a cause worth fighting for and meeting a new companion to exploring forgotten depths, wielding powerful weapons and discovering a new place to call home."
