LEGO City Undercover's Developer Is Teasing "Something"

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Update: It's a "massive" world map image

Update [Sat 21st Nov 2026, 12:30am]: Well, the big reveal is here, and admittedly, it hasn't gone down quite so well with fans of LEGO City Undercover.

The teaser was for a "massive world map image" TT Games has been hiding away for some time. To add to the upset, this download was actually released nine days ago on its official game site.

TT Games: "We said we have something for you regarding LEGO® CITY Undercover, and here it is. The massive world map image we've been hoarding for all this time. Now you too can have it."

Do you think we'll see LEGO City Undercover make a return in some way or form in the future? Let us know your thoughts in the comments.

Original Story: If you're itching to jump into a busy city filled with chaos, you might want to keep an eye on TT Games this week.

The developer behind multiple LEGO video games is apparently teasing "something" related to LEGO City Undercover. Here's its response to a recent Warner Bros. Games social media post highlighting some LEGO City Undercover gameplay:

TT Games: "Speaking of LEGO® CITY Undercover, we got something for you all tomorrow!!"

As noted, we'll apparently find out more about this tomorrow. LEGO City Undercover started life on the Wii U in 2013, and sees you take on the role of the undercover police officer Chase McCain. The title was most recently released on the Switch in 2017.

There's no other information about what exactly TT Games might be teasing here, but it's already led to plenty of requests - with fans calling for a Switch 2 version of the game, and even a sequel.

Notably, at a convention in Zurich last September, LEGO City Undercover was apparently spotted on a Nintendo Switch 2 banner alongside multiple other LEGO titles.
