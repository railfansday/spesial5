Man Utd plot move for La Liga star Mauricio Pochettino says can be ‘the best in the world’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United could push for Villarreal full-back Alex Freeman in January, a player Mauricio Pochettino has tipped to become ‘one of the best in the world’ in his position.

Pochettino was confirmed as Michael Carrick’s successor last week and will arrive in Manchester today ahead of his first game in charge away to Liverpool this weekend.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager will be backed in the January transfer window with a striker and a full-back thought to be priorities.

USA international Folarin Balogun is reportedly a target United are considering to bolster their options up top. The Daily Telegraph report another name has been discussed in Freeman, a player Pochettino is a huge admirer of.

The right-back joined Villarreal in January 2026, thriving under head coach Inigo Perez and emerging as an important member of the first-team. Having started out as a winger, he has established himself at right-back, having also played on the left.

Pochettino recognised the 22-year-old’s qualities while at the helm of the US men’s national team, giving him his senior debut and starting him in four of their five matches at the World Cup.

‘He is so humble, he has an amazing profile, he always listens,’ Pochettino said after Freeman scored in the USA’s World Cup win over Australia in June.

‘You really enjoy being with him, coaching him, and [he] is an amazing player. For me, [he has potential] to be one of the best players at his position in the world.’

United have also been linked with another USA international in Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson with former Premier League star Darren Bent believing he and teammate Calvin Bassey would be ‘very good additions’ to United’s backline.

‘In January, the first thing Pochettino needs to do as Manchester United manager is go and get defenders,’ Bent told Mr Q.

‘I would say three defenders. A full-back of some kind and two centre-halves, that’s where they need to start. That would be perfect.

‘I like the option of Antonee Robinson. I think he’s a very good defender. So that would be a good start because he’s better than what they’ve got right now at Manchester United.’
