Thomas Frank confirms Chelsea man ‘very close’ to becoming his first Crystal Palace signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Thomas Frank has confirmed Crystal Palace are working to appoint Chelsea set-piece coach Bernardo Cueva, with a deal “very close.”

Frank was appointed the Eagles’ head coach this week, replacing the Frenchman Pierre Sage, who lasted just 16 matches in the job and left the club dead last in the Premier League.

As he looks to establish his coaching staff, though, Frank is looking into a reunion with Cueva. The pair were colleagues at Brentford between 2020 and 2024, when Cueva was poached by the Blues, who reportedly spent £1m to secure his services.

But Chelsea have upgraded on Cueva this season, signing Aston Villa’s Austin MacPhee in a similarly pricey move.

Palace have reportedly been in talks with Chelsea over Cueva’s transfer for some time, and Frank, speaking in his first press conference since taking over, has now confirmed the widespread reports that a deal is imminent.

He said Cueva is “very close” to joining his staff. He is expected to serve as a general assistant coach, rather than as a dedicated set-piece coach.

Frank’s backroom staff is currently comprised of Paddy McCarty and Andy Quy, with BBC Sport reporting Palace are working to add Justin Cochrane and Chris Haslam, both of whom have worked with the Dane in previous posts.

Top of Frank’s to-do list in south-east London is to rectify Palace’s form and drag them out of the relegation zone, with a trip to Coventry on the cards this weekend.

"I know it'll be difficult,” said Frank of Saturday’s match. "But I also trust the boys, I know we'll perform on Saturday. We just need a little margin to go in our favour."

The quality of Palace’s summer recruitment has been criticised this season and cited by some as a reason for Sage’s struggles.

But Frank insisted he has plenty to work with as he looks to get the club back on track: "I'm looking forward to come in to do everything I can to help the team, the club.
