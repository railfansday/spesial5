How Jell-O’s new creative platform looks to shift consumer perceptions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The campaign, the first by Johannes Leonardo for the Kraft Heinz brand, represents its largest marketing investment in more than a decade.

Jell-O on Monday launched a new creative platform, “Oh Hello, Jell-O,” that seeks to shift consumer perceptions about how the product can be enjoyed beyond traditional use cases. The effort represents the Kraft Heinz brand’s largest marketing investment in more than a decade, per details shared with Marketing Dive.

“As a nearly 130-year-old brand, we recognized a disconnect between how familiar consumers are with Jell-O and how often they think to purchase it,” Kathryn O’Brien, head of marketing for desserts at Kraft Heinz, said in emailed comments. “We want Jell-O to be more than something that comes to mind for a bowl of gelatin or pudding, but rather a part of the creative process in the kitchen.”

Central to the campaign are two 15-second spots that show home cooks in monochrome kitchens preparing desserts with Jell-O before a social-inspired flip transition pivots the scene to others marveling over the finished product.

The campaign was created by Johannes Leonardo and is the first campaign for the brand by the agency, which has held creative duties on select Kraft Heinz brands since 2020. The spots were directed by Drew Kirsch, a veteran of music videos who has worked with Taylor Swift, and feature an original electronic-pop song that uses “oh hello, Jell-O” as a vocal hook.

“Rather than borrowing an existing song, we saw an opportunity to build an ownable sonic identity around the platform,” O’Brien said. “The original track is a recognizable brand asset that can extend across social, content and future campaign moments.”

As part of the launch, Jell-O will take over the Las Vegas Sphere this week, transforming the orb into a gelatin mold, a “dirt cup” dessert and banana cream pudding. “Oh Hello, Jell-O” will run across streaming, online video, social and digital throughout 2027, with investment pulsing around key seasonal moments. The significant media investment comes at a pivotal moment for the brand, O’Brien explained.

“Our first-ever CTV buy allows us to bring the campaign's highly visual, music-driven creative to life on the big screen, showcasing the playfulness, vibrant colors and unexpected transformations in a high-attention environment,” the executive said. “We're taking an integrated approach throughout 2027, combining streaming and online video with social, digital and e-commerce touchpoints.”

The creative platform builds on a 2023 rebrand, crafted with agency Brand Opus, that updated Jell-O’s visual identity with a more modern look and feel. The rebrand, which at the time was its first such effort in 10 years, arrived amid an industry-wide move to help CPG brands engage with new consumer groups.

Kraft Heinz in February stopped plans to split its condiment and grocery staples businesses, with CEO Steve Cahillane saying many of the CPG company’s challenges are fixable. The company is pouring $700 million into a turnaround strategy that includes significant investments in marketing, which has been increased to at least 6% of net sales.

While Kraft Heinz saw organic net sales decrease 1.1% in Q3 2026, the company’s market share, sales and volume trends improved in every region, per an earnings report. Jell-O was cited on an earnings call as a brand with which the company is looking to win share by investing selectively.
