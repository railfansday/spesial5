Arknights: Endfield version update ‘Meeting You in the World o’Shrooms’ launches November 25

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Publisher GRYPHLINE and developer Mountain Contour will release the version update “Meeting You in the World o’Shrooms” for free-to-play 3D RPG Arknights: Endfield on November 25, the companies announced. The game’s latest live stream also introduced the upcoming version 2.0 update, which takes players to Gypsosia and aboard the Rhodes Island landship, and teased 10 characters who will join the journey across upcoming content updates.

“With Wuling’s story wrapping up, and the main story taking us through all the heavy moments, we want to give you a change of pace with a lighthearted, upbeat update,” said Arknights: Endfield producer Light Zhong in a press release.

Here is an overview of the update, via GRYPHLINE:

Version Update “Meeting You in the World o’Shrooms”

Strange mushrooms have overrun an abandoned dam outside Wuling, where Landbreakers worship a giant mushroom and visitors keep slipping into hallucinations. There, the Endministrator meets Kay, a Rhodes Island Operator who has been adventuring alone across Talos-II in search of them. Kay happily shares everything she treasures, including a towering stack of honey waffles. But which would she choose: a mountain of waffles or a dear friend?

A Nature Vanguard with a carefree fighting style, Kay throws weapons that scatter across the battlefield, then gathers them up for a heavy hammer slam. Along the way, she drops Picnic Snacks that heal her teammates.

The Old Dam, the update’s new area, has grown into a fairytale-like mushroom kingdom. Players fire Kay’s honey waffles at mushrooms to open doors and spin fungal bridges, and must avoid Loquacious Shrooms that scramble their sense of direction. Mushroom foes like the Bolete Prince guard the dam, and a mushroom as tall as a mountain waits at its heart.

The update’s music leans into its whimsical tone, with Ori composer Gareth Coker and ambient producer Owsey returning to score Kay’s honey waffle adventure. It caps a first year in which the soundtrack has followed the story, from the sweeping sci-fi orchestral sound of Valley IV to the choir-driven battle against Alleikhreos, Typhoeus’s JRPG-inspired winter journey and the modern electronic take on Eastern music in Si’s painted world.

Later in the update, every player who logs in during the Workers’ Guards: Roll Call event will receive the Operator Mimino and her signature weapon, Star of Tomorrow, for free, with event missions offering extra copies to reach full Potential. Operators Gilberta and Rossi also return in successive reruns.

For factory builders, Exclusion Zone Rebuilding is a new permanent mode in which players supply materials across four phases to restore the North Wuling Exclusion Zone, using the new Forge of Luminance to transform products on their conveyor belts. For a lighter break, Lucky Carrot’s Games of Wonder adds two multiplayer card games, Witch’s Darkjack and Band Uprising, that players can enjoy with friends or through matchmaking.

A First Look at Version 2.0 and Rhodes Island

The livestream also previewed the next chapter of Arknights: Endfield, arriving in the new year. Exiles have launched attacks across the Civilization Band, and their real target is Rhodes Island. The Endministrator sets out with Perlica into Gypsosia, the Cretaceous Frontiers, where players will be able to board and explore Rhodes Island itself, a massive landship. A 10-character teaser offered a glimpse of companions for the journey, including Warfarin, appearing as the leader of Rhodes Island, and M3, who returns looking completely different.

The chapter’s first update will add four new maps at once, including Rhodes Island. Main Mission Early Unlock: Gypsosia will let players who have completed the main mission Remediation Progress skip ahead to the new story while still earning all beginner-event rewards. New modes are also in development for future updates, including a management sim with the Golden Branch Tribe and the Shattered Protocol combat mode.

Arknights: Endfield is now Steam Deck Verified, with default controller support and every feature available on Valve’s handheld. The update also brings Despot Weaponized, a new high-difficulty challenge series in Umbral Monument; the return of RISE! MEGA ARENA!, a two-Operator arena event; and new seasons of the Echoes of War combat mode.

Arknights: Endfield is available now for PlayStation 5, PC via the official launcher, Steam, and Epic Games Store, iOS via App Store, and Android via Google Play.
