Bitmap Brothers' iconic, robotic RTS game Z is unofficially back thanks to an open source, 3D remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There can be such a wonderful charm that comes from fan games because the people that make them really are just in it for the love of the (literal) game. They ain't gonna make any money from this thing! So I'm quite tickled by OpenZ, a 3D remake of the 1996 RTS game Z from The Bitmap Brothers that's completely open source (and, thankfully, hand made).

As it currently stands, OpenZ is a one-person operation who's remaking the game to celebrate its 30th anniversary. "This is the faithful 3D remake we always wanted but never got. Every single detail has been meticulously recreated and restored with an almost ridiculous level of attention," explains the game's itch.io page. "OpenZ is completely free and open source. It will never be sold: no donations, no commercial use, ever. All assets are 100% original, and no copyrighted material from the original game is used."

At the moment there's only two levels, though it's sounding like it's the full run of them, described as having been "meticulously restored and polished to an extreme level of detail," with some added improvements thanks to "30 years of RTS game design experience." There's a few new bits to find in this remake too, like new mech and electrobot units.

It sounds like there are bigger plans for the remake overall, presumably for the full game too: "This is only the beginning. Together as a community we can bring Z back to its former glory: more restored classic levels, completely original new levels, new units and maybe even multiplayer." As mentioned, it's completely free to download and play too, so where's the harm in giving it a go!
