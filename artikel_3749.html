Nvidia's latest graphics card driver appears to be unnecessarily writing to PC SSDs, something that's dumb, annoying and definitely warrants reverting

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ooh, an Nvidia-related story that isn't about their now former CEO allegedly getting amorous with someone. I wonder what it's abou-oh. Oh no. It looks like Nvidia's latest GeForce driver, 621.24, is needlessly writing vast amounts of data to PC SSDs.

As noted by PC Gamer, reports of driver 621.24 writing more than it should to SSDs have popped up across various forums and Reddit.

Some instances of the bug, which doesn't appear to be affecting everyone, have the driver writing up to 30GB every hour. For context, it'd take a bugged driver writing that much thousands of hours to break a gaming SSD with a typical endurance rating in the hundreds of terabytes. However, that doesn't mean this is anything less than a pretty bad bug. As such, reverting to the previous driver, 621.01, is a good idea if you're on 621.24 and suspect it of writing chunks of gigabytes to your SSD.

In order to check if that's going on, open task manager, head to the 'details' tab, right click on the column header, click 'select columns', select 'I/O write bytes' from the menu of check boxes that brings up, then look for 'NVDisplay.Container.exe'. The number next that exe is in bytes, so you'll need to do a bit of maths to work out if it's writing mere MB or globs of GB.

Bizarrely, the affected 621.24 driver still hasn't been pulled by Nvidia as of writing, meaning it can still be downloaded by people who might not be aware of any issues and think 'ooh, haven't checked I'm on the latest driver in a while.' The driver was released on November 12th, so it's not a brand new arrival that the company just haven't had a chance to look into yet.

Here's hoping that it is fixed or replaced ASAP, if it hasn't been by the time you read this. AMD look to have dealt with a similar issue recently, and I'd like to think driver updates across all cards aren't going to be some form of permanent Russian roulette going forwards.
