Why Andrew Lincoln Left The Walking Dead During Season 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After leading The Walking Dead for 9 seasons, the departure of Andrew Lincoln's Rick Grimes set up much of the rest of the series and beyond. As the closest thing to the show's main character, it seemed inevitable that Rick would be one of the few original Walking Dead characters to survive to the end. However, Rick leaving the show in season 9 actually set up an even bigger future for the franchise.

Even for a show like The Walking Dead, with its shocking character deaths and ever-changing cast list, Lincoln's season 9 exit was a huge shock to viewers. One of the reasons The Walking Dead gained such a big following was the frequency with which major characters met a sudden, unexpected end.

Yet, when it came to Rick, Andrew Lincoln's exit from The Walking Dead actually didn't involve his character's death. In fact, it was rather a decision by the actor that left room for the character to return.

By the time season 9 rolled around, Rick Grimes and Daryl Dixon (Norman Reedus) seemed to be the Walking Dead mainstays who would last until the end. Rick was there until the end of the story of The Walking Dead comics, so viewers of the AMC show familiar with the source material expected Andrew Lincoln to be a permanent TWD cast member.

However, season 9 kept comic book fans on their toes with a decision that deviated from the show's source material. After seemingly sacrificing himself by blowing up a bridge to stop an impending horde of walkers, Rick's group believed him to be dead. However, season 9, episode 5, "What Comes After" clued viewers into his survival.

While audiences were aware that Rick hadn't died, the character wasn't seen again until The Walking Dead series finale.

As for why Rick Grimes was written out of the show, Andrew Lincoln left The Walking Dead out of a desire to be close to his family. Andrew Lincoln is the father of two children, and they live in the UK. Since The Walking Dead's production took place primarily in Atlanta and lasted several months, Lincoln realized that the show prevented him from spending as much time at home as he'd like (via EW):

"I have two young children, and I live in a different country, and they become less portable as they get older... It was that simple. It was time for me to come home."

Regardless of why Rick left The Walking Dead, it proved to be a turning point for the series. Without its biggest character, the show had to turn its focus elsewhere and reinvent the story. The Walking Dead season 9's six-year time jump coincided with Lincoln's exit, which allowed the series to show what happened to the core group of characters without Rick around.

For the creators, writers, and viewers, Rick Grimes' departure seemed to be a blessing in disguise. The Walking Dead was well-received afterward and managed to carry itself through to the season 11 finale without the group's leader.

Rick's exit from the series was a reminder of the hero he had always been on The Walking Dead. Though he ultimately survived the events of "What Comes After," the bridge explosion was still a dangerous, selfless act that separated him from the people he loved for years. Rick was prepared to make the ultimate sacrifice; he just didn't die.

Instead, Jadis showed up in the CRM helicopter, taking Rick away, not to be seen again for quite some time. That made Rick's reappearance in The Walking Dead series finale so shocking.

In the series' final moments, Rick wrote a letter to Michonne about both the dead and the living. Rick could be seen on a beach, packing a backpack. He put his message in a bottle and then threw it into the lake when a helicopter can be heard from off-screen.

He quickly threw the backpack into a nearby boat when a CRM helicopter said via loudspeaker, "Consignee Grimes, you've been located and are instructed to surrender." Rick ended up putting his hands in the air, smiling as the helicopter descended. Michonne was also alive, racing on horseback to search for Rick after finding his backpack.

It all tied together nicely, and that final scene in The Walking Dead set up the Rick and Michonne six-episode miniseries, The Walking Dead: The Ones Who Live. The show premiered in February 2024 and continued the love story between Rick and Michonne as they fought to reunite.

Rick Grimes is an iconic character in television history, so it can be assumed that Andrew Lincoln had a number of acting opportunities after leaving the series. However, the actor didn't have any major roles outside of The Walking Dead universe for a number of years.

Aside from returning as Rick in The Walking Dead: The Ones Who Live, Lincoln's latest role since leaving the show was narrating the nature docuseries Force of Nature.

Guillermo del Toro's Cabinet of Curiosities

In 2020, Lincoln made his first movie in a decade with the Australian drama Penguin Bloom, starring Naomi Watts. In the same year, Lincoln also made his return to the theater, starring as Ebeneezer Scrooge in an Old Vic production of A Christmas Carol.

In 2025, Lincoln returned to television with the British series Cold Water, in which he played an unhappy man in a small Scottish village who strikes up a friendship with his odd neighbor.

In the wake of The Walking Dead, Lincoln seems to be pursuing more personal and local passion projects, and, hopefully, beginning of an exciting new phase in his career. Of course, even though The Walking Dead: The Ones Who Live was always intended as a miniseries, The Walking Dead's sprawling universe makes Rick's return a constant possibility.
