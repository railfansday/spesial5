Carrot is looking for a Fertility Medical Expert (RN, NP, or PA) to join our Medical Experts team on a contract basis for approximately a 3-month engagement, working a minimum 15 hours per week in a fully remote role. In this role, you will serve as a clinical resource for members navigating fertility and family-building, providing evidence-informed information and guidance that helps people understand their options and make confident, informed choices.

Day to day, you will meet with members by video to talk through their fertility questions and goals, offering clear, practical information without diagnosing, prescribing, or directing care..

This is a chance to put your fertility experience to work in a fast-growing digital health environment, with the flexibility of a contract schedule. You will work alongside a diverse team of medical experts across fertility, pregnancy, urology, mental health, and nutrition who share a passion for continuous learning and an evidence-based approach.
