Bitmine adds 7,819 ETH as Tom Lee makes the ‘hard money’ case

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Ethereum treasury company added 7,819 ETH last week, bringing its holdings to 6.09 million tokens worth approximately $10.4 billion.

Bitmine Immersion Technologies Bitmine Immersion Technologies, Inc. (BMNR) increased its Ethereum holdings to 6,085,564 ETH after acquiring 7,819 ETH over the past week, the company said in a statement on Monday.

The holdings represent 4.98% of Ethereum's total supply of 122.2 million tokens and were worth approximately $10.4 billion as of Nov. 22, according to the Norwalk, Connecticut-based company.

Bitmine said it has staked 5,227,037 ETH worth $9 billion. The company projects $245 million in annualized staking revenue at current levels and up to $286 million once fully staked at its 2.74% seven-day yield.

The company reported combined crypto, cash, marketable securities and "moonshots" holdings of $11.1 billion, including $384 million in cash and marketable securities, 216 BTC, a $180 million stake in Beast Industries and a $54 million stake in Eightco Holdings.

Bitmine Chairman Tom Lee reiterated the company's conviction in ether, pointing to the asset's issuance metrics to argue that it functions as a store of value alongside Bitcoin.

Lee said ETH supply had grown 1.26% since June 30, 2025, when Bitmine adopted its Ethereum treasury strategy. He compared that figure with 0.82% annualized growth for bitcoin, while Solana (SOL), XRP and Cardano (ADA) recorded annualized rates of 7.6%, 5.4% and 2.9%.

"In other words, ETH and BTC have essentially the same inflation rate and both are 'hard money' measured in supply growth," Lee said.

ETH traded at $1,717.20 on Monday, up 0.89% over 24 hours but down 27.97% over the past month.

Bitmine shares gained 8.94% in pre-market trading after closing at $12.98 on Friday. The stock is down 44.43% over the past month and 53.64% year to date.
