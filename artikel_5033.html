Midnight [NIGHT] surges 13%: THIS barrier stands in way of $0.053

The crypto market found some upward momentum over the past 24 hours, with total market capitalization rising 2.96% on the 23rd of November.

Notably, Bitcoin [BTC] climbed 3.56%, trading at $61,123 at the time of writing, but Midnight [NIGHT] took the cake as it had a considerably stronger run, gaining 13.25%.

While the wider market recovery may have helped NIGHT, the token’s 13.25% gain suggests a more isolated type of buying interest. Whether the token is able to maintain this surge and potentially break $0.053 really depends on buyers’ continued demand and how well Bitcoin holds its recent recovery.

NIGHT crypto has reclaimed the 23.6% Fibonacci level after briefly losing it on the 17th of November. At the time of writing, the token traded at $0.05.

The token has largely traded within a $0.035-$0.053 range since October, suggesting an established consolidation in which buyers have repeatedly defended lower prices. This range has also limited upward advances, with the $0.053 area acting as a ceiling for the past two months.

The latest recovery has brought NIGHT towards its upper boundary once again. If the move above this range is sustained, it could signal a shift in market structure and open the door to further gains.

The RSI has recovered to 62.09, hinting at strengthening bullish momentum, and the OBV’s recent uptrend suggests that buying volume is supporting the token’s recovery.

Looking at the H4 chart, NIGHT fell to $0.03896 on November 21 but made a quick recovery over the following two days. It has since reclaimed the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement at $0.04863, recovering most of its previous decline, bringing its previous swing high back into focus.

The shift in momentum is also reflecting on the MACD as its moved above both its signal and zero lines. Furthermore, the OBV continued its upward trend, suggesting that buyers are slowly starting to re-enter the market, helping sustain the move higher.

NIGHT is now approaching its previous H4 swing high at $0.05126. Breaking above this level gives the bulls another shot at pushing out of the token’s two-month consolidation as it puts the daily range resistance at $0.05315 within reach.

However, another rejection would send NIGHT lower, with the daily 50% Fibonacci level at $0.03795 becoming the next support.
