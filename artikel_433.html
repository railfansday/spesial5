AirTags are Apple's handy little Bluetooth trackers, designed to help you find your belongings using the Find My app on your iPhone or iPad. Pop one in your bag, attach it to your keys, or slip it into your luggage when flying, and you'll be able to check its location whenever you need to. And if every traveler's worst nightmare does happen and your bags go missing, there's the option to share their location with participating airlines to try and help locate them.

It also has a small built-in speaker that helps you hear where your lost item is. And for items that have been misplaced further afield, Apple's Find My network can help pinpoint their whereabouts, and Lost Mode alerts you when your missing AirTag is detected.
