Thanksgiving Leftovers Nachos

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Recipe by Lauren Habermehl, Pewaukee, Wisconsin

These Thanksgiving leftovers nachos are piled high with turkey, Brie, stuffing and squash before being finished with gravy, cranberry sauce and French-fried onions.

Scouring the internet in search of ideas for what to make with your Thanksgiving leftovers? While turkey potpie and the infamous Moist Maker sandwich from Friends are always solid choices, I implore you to try Thanksgiving leftovers nachos this year.

If you spend any time on social media, this likely isn’t the first recipe for Thanksgiving leftovers nachos you’ve seen. But I promise it’s the best. Our rendition begins with a pile of corn tortilla chips layered with leftover roast turkey and a secret ingredient: creamy Brie cheese. Next, leftover stuffing and roasted butternut squash (or candied sweet potatoes) are piled on before the nachos bake to crispy, cheesy perfection. Once finished with turkey gravy, cranberry sauce, and a shower of French-fried onions and herbs, this mountain of Thanksgiving glory is a feast for the senses.

Serve these Thanksgiving leftovers nachos after a day of Black Friday shopping, or invite friends over for the big game on Sunday afternoon. Absolutely no one will complain about a second Thanksgiving in nacho form.

Preheat the oven to 400°F. In separate microwave-safe containers, reheat the leftover turkey, squash and stuffing until they’re warmed through.

On a 15x10x1-inch baking sheet, arrange a layer of tortilla chips. Top them with half of the cubed Brie and shredded turkey. Repeat with another layer of chips, Brie and turkey.

Crumble the stuffing over the nachos, then top with the roasted butternut squash. Bake the nachos until the cheese is melted and the toppings are heated through, about 10 to 12 minutes.

While the nachos bake, warm the gravy in the microwave or on the stovetop until it’s heated through.

Drizzle the nachos with warm gravy, dollop small spoonfuls of cranberry sauce on top, and sprinkle them with fresh herbs and French-fried onions. Serve the nachos with additional cranberry sauce and gravy on the side.

Thanksgiving leftovers nachos are best enjoyed immediately. Since they’re piled high with toppings and warm gravy, the chips will get soggy when stored. For best results, make only what you intend to eat in one sitting.
