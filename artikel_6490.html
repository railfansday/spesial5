All The <i><em>Avengers: Doomsday </em></i>Food Collabs You Can Get Your Hands On Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

My bank account might not survive this movie.

Doctor Doom hasn't even arrived in theaters yet, and Avengers: Doomsday is already taking over just about everything I eat and drink.

I've talked about being a die-hard Marvel fan before, but this is one movie where you can pretty much guarantee I'll be ordering every themed drink, trying every snack, and buying every collectible I can get my hands on. And if some of those purchases happen to find their way into the movie theater with me on December 17, well, I won't tell if you won't. (Sorry, AMC.)

With the film officially opening next month, restaurants and food brands have been busy giving us plenty of ways to spend our money before we've even bought a ticket. So whether you're a fellow Marvel obsessive or just someone who could use an excuse to order more donuts, here's a look at the Avengers: Doomsday food and drink collaborations you can find stateside.

Dunkin's massive Marvel collaboration launched November 4 with six themed drinks, including the Iced Captain'ccino, Doom Cloud Latte, and Wakandan Daydream Refresher, along with special donuts and MUNCHKINS. As if the drinks weren't enough to drain your wallet, collectible tumblers, mugs, cup sleeves, and even a Doctor Doom MUNCHKINS tin are also available to add to your collection. And if you happen to live in New York, Boston, Chicago, or Los Angeles, four Dunkin' restaurants are getting full Marvel makeovers beginning November 17.

Jack in the Box is feeding the entire team with its Doomsday Dipping Box, which packs popcorn chicken, mozzarella sticks, tacos, curly fries, a drink, and three dipping sauces—including the mysterious Assemble Sauce—into one meal. Available since November 5, the meal also comes with one of eight surprise magnets that fit together like puzzle pieces. If you're determined to collect the entire set, I hope you really like curly fries.

Dave & Buster's is committed to the superhero bit, going so far as to temporarily rename itself Doom & Buster's when the nationwide takeover officially begins November 16. It’ll bring Marvel-themed arcade experiences, prizes, collectible Power Cards, and five specialty drinks, including the Panther's Potion and Captain Punch mocktails. There's also DOOMHOUR, when rewards members can earn double tickets on eligible games during surprise one-hour windows, with the promotion running through January 31, 2027.

Considering Robert Downey Jr. co-founded happy coffee and is playing Doctor Doom, this partnership practically wrote itself. The brand has released eight collectible Marvel cans across four drinks: Classic Cold Brew, Tahitian Vanilla Cold Brew, Vanilla Latte, and Chocolatey Chip Latte. Each flavor features two character designs, including Doctor Doom, Thor, Gambit, and Mystique, and they're available online and at select retailers nationwide.

Even the M&M's characters are getting suited up. The candy brand has released special Marvel packages featuring combinations like Red as Deadpool, Blue as Daredevil, and Green as She-Hulk, plus a Walmart-exclusive design starring Yellow dressed as Doctor Doom. There's no new candy flavor involved, but you can enter a sweepstakes for prizes including a Disney Cruise Line vacation, and there's even a Marvel crossover comic book.

No new popcorn flavor here, but Pop Secret is giving several of its existing varieties special Avengers: Doomsday packaging with a pretty convenient offer. If you buy three qualifying Pop Secret products in one purchase and submit your receipt, you can get a Fandango code worth $8 off admission. There's also a sweepstakes offering a trip to New York City and a few other prizes, so if you’re already assembling this many movie snacks, you might as well save a few bucks on the ticket.
