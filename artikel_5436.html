Texas is resuming data center environmental permits, handing SpaceX an approval

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The state regulator authorized emissions tied to 43 SpaceX generators at Starbase after Gov. Greg Abbott's pause on permits ended

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality has resumed reviewing data center permit applications, ending a pause that Gov. Greg Abbott $ABT ordered in September, the agency said.

After restarting the review, the regulator signed off on air pollution from the 43 generators SpaceX wants to run at Starbase, according to Bloomberg, citing a Nov. 16 letter from the agency. SpaceX, led by CEO Elon Musk, intends to construct, own and run a gas-fired power station along with a supercomputing facility at the site.

TCEQ spokesperson Laura Lopez said the agency “has resumed its normal permit review process and will evaluate applications under all applicable state and federal laws and regulations,” according to The Texas Tribune.

Abbott's office told TCEQ on Nov. 13 that the Electric Reliability Council of Texas, the state's grid operator, had compiled details on how proposed data center projects could affect nearby communities, the agency said. The information was needed for an audit that was mandatory, according to Bloomberg.

ERCOT plans to report its full findings on Dec. 10, then take them up on Dec. 17 during an open meeting of the Public Utility Commission of Texas, according to Bloomberg.

Public Citizen, a consumer advocacy group, called on TCEQ commissioners to resign. "Agency leadership has begun unilaterally issuing data center permits" while keeping communities in the dark about what ERCOT found and how those conclusions will shape its decisions, Kathryn Guerra, the group's TCEQ watchdog campaign director, said in a statement, according to The Texas Tribune.

Abbott directed TCEQ on Sept. 21 to stop issuing data center permits until audits of the industry's grid and water use were complete. The order broadened an August freeze on new data center connections to the grid. It also covered developers that generate their own power and never seek a grid connection.

SpaceX has proposed a natural gas power station and supercomputing site in Starbase. The initial phases would carry an estimated $24 billion in capital investment, according to a City of Starbase hearing notice. The Starbase City Commission is scheduled to consider a property tax abatement agreement and a related economic development agreement for the project on Dec. 9.

Abbott was defeated on Nov. 3 while seeking a fourth term, losing to Democrat Gina Hinojosa. Gov.-elect Hinojosa, who takes office in January, campaigned on a freeze on building additional data centers, according to Bloomberg.
