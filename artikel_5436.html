Hyperliquid is trading near $65–$68 in mid-November 2026 after pulling back with the broader market. In an over-the-top bullish scenario, Microsoft Copilot AI predicts that HYPE could still deliver one of the more explosive moves among major DeFi assets by January 1, 2027.

The landmark catalyst driving this extreme upside is a highly unlikely but not impossible regulatory and institutional breakthrough.

In this hypothetical, Hyperliquid successfully obtains a major U.S. regulatory designation that allows its perpetual futures platform (or a closely affiliated entity) to operate as a fully compliant, onshore derivatives venue accessible to a wide range of traditional finance participants.

Combined with a surge in institutional volume migrating onto the chain and aggressive token utility expansions (such as enhanced staking, revenue sharing, or treasury mechanisms), HYPE experiences a structural demand shock that re-rates the entire project far beyond current valuations.

HYPE has corrected sharply from its late-October highs near $95–$96 and is now consolidating in the mid-$60s after testing support around $62–$64. The recent decline has formed a series of lower highs, with immediate resistance clustered between $72 and $78 and a more significant supply zone near the prior peak.

On the downside, the $60–$62 area represents the first major demand zone; a sustained break below it would open the path toward the $50 region. Momentum indicators have cooled considerably after the sell-off, leaving the asset oversold on intermediate timeframes and creating room for a sharp reversal if buying pressure returns.

In a standard recovery, a weekly close back above $75–$80 would signal that the corrective phase is ending. Under the extreme regulatory and institutional catalyst outlined above, those intermediate levels would be cleared rapidly, allowing measured-move extensions and multi-cycle projections from the current base to support a far more aggressive upside trajectory.

While present price action reflects broader risk-off conditions and recent liquidations, Hyperliquid’s position as a high-volume perpetual exchange continues to give it a distinct profile among large-cap tokens.

The combination of its existing product-market fit and the theoretical potential for a transformative regulatory green light creates a setup from which a parabolic move remains possible.

Microsoft Copilot AI predicts that if everything aligns, HYPE could reach somewhere between $280 and $380 by January 1, 2027, with a stretch target above $450 in the most aggressive outcome.

This remains pure speculation and entertainment, not a realistic base-case outlook. The catalyst would require extraordinary regulatory progress and execution for Hyperliquid at a scale that is currently highly unlikely.
