Bruno Fernandes reveals talks over new Manchester United contract are on hold

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Club captain confirms he has been offered a new deal

‘Not the right moment to be talking about negotiations’

Bruno Fernandes has revealed that Manchester United have offered him a new contract, but discussions have been ⁠put on hold as the club focus on turning around their terrible start to the season.

Fernandes’s current deal expires at the end of this campaign, although United ⁠hold an ​option to extend their captain’s contract by a further year until June 2028, giving the club more time to reach ⁠an agreement on fresh terms.

“The club offered me a new contract so I think that, from their side, shows that they ⁠want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on,” ​Fernandes told Sky Sports in an ‌interview published on Sunday.

“We ‌spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in at ‌the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but because of the context of the team and the club, I just put that to one side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations,” Fernandes added. “The most important thing now is that ‌the club gets back to winning games.“

The 32-year-old’s future is still up in the air after it was revealed he turned down a second contract offer from the club. Fernandes’s form has dropped off this season, his goal and assist in the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa providing his first goal involvements in the league since scoring a hat-trick against Ipswich in August.

The Portugal international also hit back at rumours he had demanded more money and a longer deal, ​saying his priority was to remain at Old Trafford and help restore the club’s fortunes.

“I’ve ⁠seen people saying that he’s asked for more money, he’s asked ​for more years. If ​I wanted more money, I wouldn’t ​be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more ​money than being ‌at Manchester United,” ​Fernandes said. “I could ​have been in a place that’s much warmer than Manchester.”

United are second-bottom of the Premier League standings having won only one of their opening 10 league matches – their worst start to a season in 40 years. Their new head coach, Mauricio Pochettino, will take charge of his first game when United face fifth-placed Liverpool on Sunday. The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager was appointed after Michael Carrick was sacked this month.
