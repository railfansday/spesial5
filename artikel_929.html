Friday nights in Amsterdam usually get pretty lively, and it should be no different at the Johan Cruyff Arena. Netherlands host Greece in a clash of the top two in Nations League Group A2, with the winners guaranteed a place in the quarter-finals.

Barcelona legend Xavi has made a promising start to life as Oranje head coach, claiming two wins over rock-bottom Serbia and twice seeing his side fight back to draw, against Germany and Greece. The Netherlands lead Group A2 on goals scored and a win would mean they can finish no lower than second, by virtue of having a superior head-to-head record over the Greeks.

The bad news for Dutch fans is that they are missing several key players, with captain Virgil van Dijk, Brian Brobbey, Ryan Gravenberch and Kenneth Taylor all injured. However, in-form Roma striker Donyell Malen returns to the squad to add quality in attack.

Greece are aiming to claim a first-ever competitive victory over the Netherlands, having been forced to settle for a 2-2 draw in Thessaloniki last month after taking a two-goal lead.

Ivan Jovanovic's side arrive in Amsterdam knowing a win would see them finish top of the group, beating both the Netherlands and Germany on head-to-head, while a draw would also put them in a strong position to qualify ahead of Sunday's finale at home to Serbia.

Midfielder Christos Zafeiris and defender Manolis Saliakas are both suspended, but Greece have plenty of quality in their forward line, including Arsenal winger Christos Tzolis and Brighton & Hove Albion striker Charalampos Kostoulas.

The Greeks have proved tough to beat and we are backing them to take a point in Amsterdam.
