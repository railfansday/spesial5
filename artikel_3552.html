Kristen Bell, Andie MacDowell, Eva Longoria, and More Honor Community Builders at L’Oréal Paris’s Women of Worth Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

L’Oréal Paris welcomed its latest Women of Worth honorees with an intimate, elegant gala in Los Angeles on Wednesday night. For the annual celebration, which honors unsung nonprofit leaders, guests gathered at The Ebell of Los Angeles, a venue with a rich history of its own.

The evening began with an outdoor cocktail hour, where guests enjoyed passed apps and specialty drinks like the “Parisian Refresher” and “Worth It Spritz” under sparkling bistro lights. As honorees and attendees mixed and mingled, they also had the opportunity to pose for a photographer and peruse a beauty bar stocked with L’Oréal Paris-brand lipsticks, lip oils, and glosses, which were available for guests to take home in a plush velvet bag with “I’m Worth It” embroidered on it.

As the gala officially began inside The Ebell’s picturesque ballroom, longtime brand ambassador Eva Longoria gave the introductory remarks, noting that “this night always gives me goosebumps. I’m always crying.” She saluted her 20-plus-year partnership with the brand and joked that it made her “112 years old,” though she doesn’t look like it, the actor-director said, “because I use Revitalift.” She gave a shout-out to the many beauty influencers in attendance, including Laura Lee and Desi Perkins. “You guys really changed the game of beauty, and I just applaud you,” she said. “Put me in your TikToks.”

L’Oréal Paris USA president Laura Branik followed Longoria, emphasizing the brand’s commitment to and pride in its Women of Worth program, which continues to celebrate “the power of women who know their worth and use it,” she said. She then welcomed the company’s newest global ambassador, Kristen Bell, to the stage. Bell, who was attending her “first Women of Worth ever,” said she was “very happy to be here” and introduced moving presentations for the first two honorees: Vy Le of WhereWear, which transforms clothing donations into targeted support for women’s and housing-insecure shelters and the people they serve, and Amanda Rice of The Chick Mission, a nonprofit dedicated to ensuring that every young woman newly diagnosed with cancer has the option to preserve her fertility.

Actor and brand ambassador Aja Naomi King was up next, taking note of the room full of glamorous gowns and festive energy. “Everyone showed up and showed out,” she said, “and we appreciate that because these women deserve to be celebrated properly. I had the immense honor of helping select this year’s Women of Worth, and I keep thinking about how much it matters when someone believes in your potential.” King introduced two honorees: Maya Bird-Murphy of Mobile Makers, an award-winning nonprofit that expands access to design education through workshops, community engagement, public installations, and pop-ups, and Christy Vogel of Sounding Joy, a nonprofit dedicated to providing hearing aids to children whose families cannot afford them.

Longoria then returned to present another pair of honorees: Marisa Hamamoto of Infinite Flow Dance, an award-winning disability-led nonprofit and professional dance company with a mission to reimagine possibilities, one dance at a time, and Chanel Power of Mentor Me Collective, a technical institute transforming how emerging technologists are identified, developed, and positioned to lead.

During a break in the program, guests enjoyed dinner from a menu that included roast chicken, citrus-soy-and-apple-cider-braised short ribs, and vegan pan-seared “scallops.” Another highlight? The venue’s bathrooms were stocked with more coveted L’Oréal Paris goods like lip oil, mascara, setting spray, and velour puff makeup applicators.

Following the break, ambassador, actor, and model Cara Delevingne led the room in a cheerful group proclamation of “We’re worth it!” before introducing her honorees: Emily Cho of Juvie for STEM (JSTEM), an international nonprofit dedicated to expanding access to high-quality STEM education for justice-involved youth, and Arielle Galinsky of The Legacy Project, Inc. (TLP), a national nonprofit connecting younger and older adults for multigenerational friendship and storytelling.

Legacy L’Oréal Paris ambassador Andie MacDowell had the pleasure of introducing the event’s final honorees. “For 40 years I have been a part of the L’Oréal Paris family,” she said, with the milestone receiving enthusiastic applause from the audience. “After all these years, the thing that stays with me [about the Women of Worth honorees] is the determination to make life better for someone else. That kind of care is powerful.” MacDowell introduced Jordan Pine of Greenlight Operation, a Pennsylvania nonprofit that strives to strategically combat human trafficking and serve sex trafficked women through a continuum of restorative care, and Dr. Christie Brungardt, cofounder of Jana’s Campaign, a Kansas-based nonprofit dedicated to preventing gender and relationship violence through education.

Proud “newbie” Bell returned to the stage to offer closing remarks and thank all the honorees for their tireless efforts to make life better for others. “I will be thinking of all 10 of these stories and the women we met tonight for quite some time,” she said. “Even though their stories couldn’t be more different, they seem to have one thing in common: that they all saw what needed to change and they did it. They didn’t wait for someone else to do something; they stepped forward and they did it themselves. And honestly, I think that’s gonna be my biggest takeaway from my first Women of Worth celebration ever: Never underestimate what one woman can set in motion.”

Each of the 10 Women of Worth 2026 honorees received a $25,000 grant, mentorship, and national visibility. As the program concluded, honorees, their proud guests, and other attendees continued to soak up the special evening and shared in a few more hugs and photo ops. Before departing for the evening, guests were invited to enjoy a final “sweet moment” in a separate room where a colorful candy bar and glasses of Champagne awaited.
