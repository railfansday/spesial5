Ezra Frech Expertly Recovers After Prosthetic Mishap During 'DWTS' Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dancing With the Stars’ Ezra Frech rose above a mishap with his prosthetic during the season 35 finale, with the judges praising him for making a statement despite the unexpected incident.

During the freestyle round of the Tuesday, November 24, finale, Frech, 21, intentionally began the number without his blade attached. After he was taken behind a barrier, he returned with the prosthetic on. However, an unexpected glitch took place when the prosthetic came off during his dance with partner Daniella Karagach.

Despite the live snafu, Frech recovered and was able to get his prosthetic back on with the help of Karagach, 33, and the other performers. When the dance was over, they were met with a standing ovation from the judges.

Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough all praised Frech for his recovery.

“Ezra, you are a hero. This performance will have a ripple effect far beyond this show. What you did tonight … for forever, kids will look up to you and will see what’s possible. The fact that the worst case scenario might have happened and you wouldn’t even have known, the way you rose in that moment,” Hough, 41, said. “Ezra, you are incredible, man!”

Tonioli, 71, added, “What we witnessed tonight goes beyond dancing. This is a powerful, beautiful, life affirming statement that will inspire the nation and the world, my darling.”

Inaba, 58, also celebrated Frech’s performances throughout the season, pointing out that he’s only improved each week. “You have been the most amazing ambassador for people with disabilities, but also, now you’re an ambassador for dance,” Inaba, 58, said.

After the judges gave their comments, Frech reflected on the routine before receiving his final scores.

“It’s crazy. You do something so many times and I got suctions and it came off in the middle of the dance,” Frech told host Julianne Hough. “We put our heart, our blood, our sweat, our tears into this. If one person with a disability feels more confident, holds their head up high because of this show, then I’ve done my job.”

Frech and Karagach earned all 10s from the judges to give them a perfect score for the final round — their third of the night. By the end of the episode, the pair were crowned winners of the entire season.

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
