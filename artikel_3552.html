GTA 6 Gym Workouts Have A Useful Trick That Saves You A Lot Of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

You won't get muscles in real life without putting in some work, but fortunately, leveling up your pectorals in Grand Theft Auto 6 is much less strenuous. The only cardio you'll need to do is a series of finger movements on your controller, but admittedly, getting either of the game's protagonists into shape can be time-consuming. Fortunately, there's a trick that can help you cut workout times in half, which I wish I could apply to real life as well.

Essentially, all you need to do in GTA 6 is make sure you're going to the gym as a couple. If you only focus on Jason or Lucia for a workout, then they'll gradually gain muscle and tone up on their own. But if you go as a pair, it doesn't matter who you work out with; both characters will gain and progress on their path toward becoming absolute units.

That makes for a quick way to bulk up, though we still need to talk about Jason's gym etiquette.

This isn't the first time GTA has introduced gym gameplay mechanics to keep your protagonist in shape; back in GTA: San Andreas, players could frequent locations across the map and work on CJ's appearance. Not only could he increase his muscle mass and stamina by pumping iron, but CJ could also learn new fighting styles from trainers along the way. Working out in GTA 6 also has benefits, as lifting weights can improve overall health, while cardio activities like running and biking improve stamina.

You can even track Jason and Lucia's workouts via a smartphone app, and there's an optimal time for them to train to maximize their efforts. You can do that either at a proper gym in the game world or, if you're on a budget, use the set of weights outside Jason's house.

With GTA 6 out in the wild, players are quickly discovering just how much there is to do in Rockstar's massive open-world game. If you're one of them and looking for tips and tricks, check out GameSpot's GTA 6 guides hub for walkthroughs to help you out.
