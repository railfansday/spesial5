Dane Cook and Wife Kelsi's Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dane Cook knows his relationship with a woman 26 years his junior raises eyebrows, but he’s shown a sense of humor about falling in love with Kelsi Cook.

“Sometimes I actually find myself saying, ‘Where has she been all my life?’” Dane mused during a 2018 appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live! “And then I realize she wasn’t alive for the first 26 years of it.”

He continued: “People are like, ‘You’re robbing the cradle.’ I was like, ‘She hasn’t slept in the cradle for, like, nine years. Relax.’”

All jokes aside, Dane praised the singer-songwriter, whom he first started dating in 2017. “Here’s the thing: I love her,” he told Jimmy Kimmel. “She is the kindest, sweetest, just my favorite — my best friend.”

When an Instagram user asked the comic about his and Kelsi’s favorite aspects of one another, he replied, “Kelsi is smart, kind, creative, loyal & honest. As for me, I think Kelsi would [say] I’m tolerable. LOL.”
