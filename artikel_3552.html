All Gold Skulltula locations in Ocarina of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

How to save the cursed family in Kakariko Village

Gold Skulltulas act as a collectible in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, but they're also needed to cure a cursed family in Kakariko Village.

The poor family have been turned into Skulltulas, and the only way to cleanse them of their curse is to hunt down the Gold Skulltulas scattered across Hyrule. However, that's easier said than done, as there are 100 of them in total, and they're extremely difficult to find.

Here's where to find every Gold Skulltula in Ocarina of Time remake, and the rewards for curing the family in Kakariko Village.

[Ed. note: This guide contains spoilers for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, so continue at your own risk! Feel free to follow along or bookmark this page and come back later.]

As you collect Gold Skulltula tokens, you can turn them in to the cursed family in Kakariko Village for rewards. Every 10 Gold Skulltulas you collect from 10 to 50 will lead to a reward, but then you won't receive anything until you turn in all 100 Gold Skulltulas. After you collect 20 Gold Skulltulas, make sure to get your reward, as the Echo Gem is extremely helpful for finding the rest of them.

Below, we've listed all the Gold Skulltula rewards, and how many you need to turn in to receive them.

This big wallet, made for grown-ups, is able to hold up to 500 rupees.

Equipping this gem will help you detect nearby Gold Skulltulas through a combination of light and sound.

This enormous wallet is able to hold up to 1,000 rupees.

Once placed on the ground, this wall-climbing bomb zooms off on its own before exploding after a certain distance.

Collect four Pieces of Heart to gain a heart.

Every time you talk to the Restored Rich Man, he'll give you a gold rupee.

There are 100 Gold Skulltula tokens in Ocarina of Time. We've listed where to find every Gold Skulltula separated by zone. In each section, the Gold Skulltulas are listed in chronological order.

If you're looking for a specific area, make sure to use the Ctrl + F shortcut and search for its name.
