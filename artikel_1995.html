West Ham United v Preston North End | Scott Parker delivers shock line up

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Preston North End at home – and a traditional 3pm kick off on a Saturday afternoon is Scott Parker’s home debut as West Ham United manager. The expectation is high after many fine words from fan favourites Parker and his assistant Mark Noble – and of course the two week break from football has only heightened the anticipation as bottom club Preston come to visit.

After beginning with a draw and a loss within his first three days at the helm, Parker gets to show us ‘his’ West Ham line up and set up this afternoon. Having had a two week break to work with the bulk of his West Ham side, we’ll get to see a Scott Parker XI now along with the manner in which he plans to set his team up.

The first line up of the Scott Parker era has been announced: And there’s a couple of big shocks with third choice keeper Fin Herrick selected in place of Mads Hermansen AND Edson Alvarez getting a recall!

Herrick | Veltman, Mavropanos, Morato, Mayers | Engels Alvarez Mukasa | Bowen, Piroe, Austbo
