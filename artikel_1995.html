Why West Ham’s Fulham Defeat Gives Scott Parker Plenty To Be Pleased About

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

West Ham United were beaten 1-0 by Fulham in a behind-closed-doors friendly on Thursday, but I don’t think the result matters at all. If anything, losing by just one goal to Premier League opposition when we’ve got ten players missing on international duty is no shame whatsoever.

According to ExWHUEmployee, reporting through The West Ham Way, the match was arranged to give Scott Parker an opportunity to assess some of his players and help others build up their match fitness. No team sheet has been made public, so we don’t know exactly who featured.

What I’m particularly impressed by is how quickly Parker has organised and sorted this out. He’s barely been in the job five minutes, yet he’s already managed to arrange a friendly against Premier League opposition, presumably using his Fulham connections to get it done. That’s exactly the sort of proactive approach needed to drag the club back up the league table.

The bit I found particularly interesting was the emphasis on getting players up to fitness.

From that point of view, I’d have thought Soungoutou Magassa and Edson Álvarez would be prime candidates to have participated in the game. Alphonse Areola would also be an obvious beneficiary if he’s recovered sufficiently from his recent injury.

It’s also important for Parker to have a look at some of the other academy goalkeepers, particularly given the concerns over Fin Herrick’s fitness and the recent poor form of Mads Hermansen.

I’d rather hope Lewis Orford played some part in the game as well. He’s not getting nearly enough first-team football, and this would have been an ideal opportunity to show the new manager what he can do.

And, as I wrote earlier this week, the international break is going to afford Pablo the opportunity to try to impress the new Hammers manager.

Parker omitted the Brazilian striker from his first two matchday squads altogether, so a friendly like this could have been particularly useful for him.

We already knew that Parker’s preparations would be disrupted by the international call-ups, but he’s clearly not prepared to sit around waiting for everybody to return.

He’s taken a situation where ten players are unavailable and turned it into an opportunity to assess the rest of his squad against decent opposition.

I wouldn’t be remotely concerned about the 1-0 defeat. We don’t know who played, how many substitutions were made or even whether Parker was particularly interested in winning the match. Hopefully those details will be revealed by Ex later today?

For all we know, he may have learnt more from this game than he would have from a comfortable victory against weaker opposition.

It’s a thumbs up from me for Parker being proactive, particularly so early in his West Ham managerial career.

The only question is whether he’ll arrange another friendly next week before the Championship resumes against Preston.
