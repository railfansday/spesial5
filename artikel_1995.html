Keith Wyness shares Jack Grealish verdict after Everton go public with what happened

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Everton have handled the disciplinary situation surrounding winger Jack Grealish perfectly so far.

That is according to former Toffees chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that no further punishment is needed for the 31-year-old.

The wideman has been suspended by the Merseysiders for a breach of club conduct and has missed the last two games as a result.

However, Wyness believes that David Moyes is the perfect manager to handle the situation, and does not expect Grealish to be out of action for much longer.

The Manchester City loanee reportedly spent two nights out drinking, and failed to arrive at training at the start of last week.

However, Wyness believes that Grealish has now served his time for his misdemeanours and should now be reintegrated into the group.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that his former club deserve praise for the way that they have handled Grealish’s absence.

The club have disclosed limited information surrounding the situation through club channels, instead allowing Moyes and his staff to deal with the issues in-house.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that he expects everything to return to normality sooner rather than later.

“Well, I think they've handled it really well. Moyes came out early, said that he's been dropped,” he said.

“They said that it was a breach of club discipline. He didn't travel with the team for the game on Monday night, so it's being handled, and I'm sure that he will be reintegrated and we'll all move on.

“But I think Moyes is probably the right manager to handle a situation like this, and I think the club have handled it really well.”

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider earlier this week that Everton’s squad will be hugely unhappy with Grealish and his actions.

The ex-recruitment man suggested that Moyes’ group are likely to feel a little betrayed by the star, given that they are all continuing to work hard in training day in, day out.

Brown questioned whether Moyes and his players will ever truly be able to trust Grealish again, even if he returns to the fold in the coming weeks.

Everton understandably feel let down by Grealish’s actions, especially given the importance that he carries for them out on the pitch.
