Crawley Town player ratings: Jennings and Aderoju shine as Reds see off Fleetwood

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A late Will Davies goal on 79 minutes gave the Reds fans a nervy final 10 minutes, but they held on for the win.

The Reds made three changes from their last league game against Oldham at the end of October.

Josh Flint, Ben Heneghan and Pemi Aderoju came in for the injured Priestley Farquharson, Lewis Richards and Stephen Duke-McKenna who both drop to the bench.

Crawley remain bottom but are now only a point behind Rotherham and three points from safety with three games in hand over Rochdale in 22nd.

Vito Mannone – 7 - Despite the goal, he made a couple of saves but was not really troubled that much. Made a fantastic save in injury time to secure the win for the Reds.

Kellan Gordon – 7 – Was great going forward as a wingback, was the main outlet for the Reds in both halves.

Ben Heneghan – 7.5 – Calm and assured at the back throughout the game, marshalled the defence well and showed his experience.

Josh Flint – 7.5 – Showed what Crawley have been missing at the back this season, with his experience and calmness.

Jonny Russell – 7 – Looked good in defence with Flint and Heneghan in the middle giving him confidence. Replaced by Lewis Richards on 76 minutes.

Charlie Barker – 7 – Was playing in the midfield, just in front of the back four, and was really good in the role.

Jude Arthurs – 7 - Booked on 70 minutes but led the team well both in defence and attack.

Dylan Morgan – 7 - Had a quieter game than usual but still caused problems for the Fleetwood defence. Replaced by Stephen Duke-McKenna on 73 minutes.

Kai Jennings – 8.5 - Scored his first goal in a Reds shirt on 5 minutes and was excellent in midfield.

Pemi Aderoju – 8.5 - Scored an excellent goal at the start of the second half and looked a threat going forward. However, Fleetwood’s goal slipped through his legs.

Danilo Orsi – 7 - Has looked a different player, playing off of Aderoju, booked on 77 minutes when being fouled. Replaced by Ade Adeyemo on 80 minutes.

Stephen Duke-McKenna – 6.5 - Replaced Dylan Morgan on 73 minutes. Did ok when he came on and had an effort superbly saved in injury time.

Lewis Richards – 6.5 - Replaced Jonny Russell on 76 minutes. Did ok when he came on.

Ade Adeyemo – N/A - Replaced Danilo Orsi on 80 minutes. Did ok when he came on, but wasn’t marked.
