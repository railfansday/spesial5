Hearts set for six-man injury boost as Wouter Vrancken bullish over revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Wouter Vrancken is confident the impending return of no less than half a dozen key players from injury after the international break will help spark an upturn in fortunes for embattled Hearts.

The Jambos had 11 absentees for Sunday’s dismal 5-2 defeat at home to Falkirk and then lost key defender Malachi Fagan-Walcott in the second half.

Vrancken was optimistic that the centre-back had not sustained any serious damage and he is expecting his injury list to ease when his team return to action away to Dundee a week on Saturday.

The head coach reported that Eduardo Ageu, Tomas Magnusson, Rogers Mato, Blair Spittal, Oisin McEntee and Christian Borchgrevink are all in the frame to come back into contention, if not for Dens Park then certainly soon afterwards.

“I think Eddie would be back, because he’s already training in the team,” said the head coach, offering an update on the injuries. “Tomas, for sure, because he has his protocol with the concussion.

“I think Spitts and Osh will be 50-50 when we come back. Mato, hopefully, if everything goes well.

“Christian is also maybe one who will be on the edge of coming back at this moment. I think these are the players (that are close).

“Stephen (Kingsley), we have to wait for the scan.”

Hearts have won just two of their last 11 matches amid a gruelling domestic and European schedule, leaving them fourth in the William Hill Premiership, out of the Premier Sports Cup and struggling to qualify for the Conference League knockout phase.

Vrancken is adamant that having a greater array of options available to select from will help them get back on track, however.

“I’m pretty confident in this, especially when we get numbers back, because these guys showed already what qualities and what extras they can bring on the pitch,” said the Belgian, whose side were on a nine-game unbeaten run before injuries started to undermine their campaign.
