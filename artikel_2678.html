Eagles sign Jaydon Blue to active roster, add NT to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Eagles sign Jaydon Blue to active roster, add NT to practice squad originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles on Tuesday made a couple of roster moves as they get ready to return to practice after the bye week.

They signed RB Jaydon Blue to the 53-man roster and NT Leki Fotu and WR Quentin Skinner to the practice squad.

Blue, 22, first came to the Eagles in mid-October when they signed him off the Cowboys' practice squad. He has since split his time between the 53-man roster and practice squad.

Blue played against the Commanders and the Giants before the bye week and saw eight offensive snaps and 13 special teams snaps. He has returned punts when he's been up.

Before this promotion, Saquon Barkley was the only running back on the 53-man roster. But the Eagles have also activated the 21-day practice window for Will Shipley (foot), who has been on IR since Oct. 16.

Fotu, 28, was a fourth-round pick back in 2020 out of Utah. He was drafted to Arizona and played four seasons with the Cardinals. He has also played for the Jets, Raiders and Texans. In total, Fotu has played 66 games in the NFL with 26 starts.

After trading away Jordan Davis to the Jaguars, the Eagles were without a prototypical nose tackle. Fotu (6-5, 330) fits that requirement but would need to be elevated from the practice squad in order to play.
