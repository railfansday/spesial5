Lakers survive Suns’ furious rally behind Luka Doncic’s 43-point night

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

PHOENIX — The 21-page presentation toward the beginning of their three-day break from games came with a simple message: Even though the Lakers were tied for the best record and had the league's best offense, they could be even better.

Despite being without Austin Reaves as he allows the bruise in his left heel to recover, the Lakers showed for most of their 131-123 victory over the Suns Sunday afternoon what their better could look like.

Luka Dončić #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts during an NBA game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

They also showed more progress needs to be made after they nearly blew a 31-point second-half lead.

The Lakers rode the momentum of another hot start from Luka Dončić (43 points, nine assists) and precise execution to their eighth straight victory to put them at a league-best 12-2 on the season.

Luka Dončić (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts during an NBA game against the Phoenix Suns on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Phoenix. Getty Images

Dončić had 20 points of his scoring total in the first quarter, which was not only his second 20-point opening quarter of the season but also made him the only player in the league with multiple 20-point first quarters.

And after the non-Dončić minutes had been a sore spot for the Lakers for most of the season, they flipped the script on Sunday.

They won the minutes Dončić was on the bench in the second quarter, helping them take a 64-54 lead going into halftime, 101-70 lead late in the third and 104-80 lead going into the fourth.

But the Lakers didn't find the same success when Dončić wasn't in the game to start the fourth.

By the time the Slovenian star subbed back in with 6:48 remaining, the Suns had the momentum after trimming their deficit to 14 points, and eventually six points multiple times late in the fourth.

Luka Dončić #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers controls the ball against Jordan Goodwin #23 of the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Dončić's late-game offensive heroics, in addition to the Suns missing three free throws in the final two minutes, ensured the Lakers didn't relinquish the lead and held on for the wire-to-wire victory.

Quentin Grimes (22 points) came up with an offensive rebound and banked in a 3-pointer with 34 seconds left to ice the game for the Lakers.

Walker Kessler (14 points, 12 rebounds), Matisse Thybulle (12 points) and Jake LaRavia (12 points) all scored at least 12 points, putting all of the Lakers' starters in double figures in scoring.

Collin Sexton added 16 points and three assists off the bench.
