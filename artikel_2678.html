Brooks Koepka Admits Missing Ryder Cup and Presidents Cup Frustrates Him

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brooks Koepka has admitted that missing major team events such as the Ryder Cup and Presidents Cup frustrates him. The five-time major champion has made it clear that he wants to return to the U.S. team and has set his sights on earning a place at the 2027 Ryder Cup at Adare Manor.

Koepka has not featured in recent team competitions and says watching from the sidelines is not something he enjoys. Despite having played in only one Presidents Cup, the American believes representing his country and spending time with fellow players are important parts of the game.

The 35-year-old also acknowledged that his recent season has been disappointing and that he needs to improve if he wants to achieve his goal of making the 2027 U.S. Ryder Cup team.

Speaking about his ambitions, Koepka identified Adare Manor as a major target for him. He also explained how difficult it has been to watch team competitions without being involved.

"Yeah, I think Adare Manor is a big goal of mine. But this year, I guess you can call it this year has just been a little bit of a disappointment, so I know I need to pick it up a little bit," Koepka said.

Koepka has not featured in recent team competitions and says watching from the sidelines is not something he enjoys. Although he has played in four Ryder Cups, he has appeared in only one Presidents Cup, in 2017. The American believes representing his country and spending time with fellow players are important parts of the game.

Koepka also spoke about the experience of representing his country and being around other players during those competitions.

"It's something I don't enjoy, sitting back and watching the Presidents Cup. I know I've only played in one, a lot of those are injuries, but those team events are a lot of fun, I really enjoy those. Getting to be around the guys and representing your country's pretty cool," he added.

He then made his frustration clear, explaining how difficult it is to miss out when he has the opportunity to compete.

"So anytime you can do that or you have the opportunity to and you're not able to go play, it kind of infuriates me sitting on the sidelines."

Koepka's comments make his ambition for the 2027 Ryder Cup clear. The American wants to be part of the U.S. team when the competition heads to Adare Manor in Ireland.

However, he knows that he needs to improve after describing his recent season as disappointing. His performances will be important if he wants to put himself in contention for a place on the team.

The five-time major champion has previously experienced the atmosphere of team competitions and clearly wants another opportunity to represent the United States. With Adare Manor now a major goal, Koepka will be looking to get his game back on track ahead of the 2027 Ryder Cup.
