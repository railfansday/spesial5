Deebo Samuel cleared for Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

San Francisco 49ers WR Deebo Samuel (hamstring) practiced fully on Friday, Nov. 13, and no longer appears on the injury report for Week 10 against the Dallas Cowboys.

Why It Matters: Samuel being in the 49ers' lineup could make him the WR1 in the offense in Week 10. Fantasy players could have him in their flex spot against a soft Cowboys defense.
