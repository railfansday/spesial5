Browns, Texans Week 10 has huge AFC playoff odds impact

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Cleveland Browns are looking to turn the tables after two straight losses, returning home, where they are 3-0 this season, with QB Deshaun Watson back in place as the team's starting quarterback. Week 10 brings the Houston Texans to town, with injuries to both the hosts and visitors being part of the story, as always in the NFL.

The Browns opened as underdogs to start their four-game home stretch, with the Texans hoping to continue their turnaround from a poor start to the 2026 NFL season.

Despite the two straight losses, Cleveland enters Week 10 in a tie for the last playoff spot, missing out due to a tiebreaker, while Houston is a game and a half back and in the 11th seed in the AFC. Browns versus Texans is the biggest swing game for the AFC playoff race in Week 10:

It is interesting, according to the above chart, that the outcome would have a bigger impact on Houston and that they would have a higher chance of making the playoffs on both sides of the equation: 17% vs. 13% for losing, 51% vs. 42% for winning.

According to FanDuel, the Texans (+136) already have better odds to make the playoffs than Cleveland (+270), while The Athletic gives the Browns a slightly better chance to make the postseason (45%) with a win this week.
