Sean Payton pleased with the offensive line play vs. Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Denver Broncos found one thing against the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday that they haven't had since early last year. A potent rushing attack.

Sure they had more total rushing yards against the Arizona Cardinals, but that was due to Bo Nix having that 60+ yard rush. Nix had 6 carries for 5 yards on Sunday. No, this 133 yard rushing total was behind the legs of Jonah Coleman and a revitalized offensive line.

When asked about the line's improvement, head coach Sean Payton was quite pleased overall.

"If the offensive line improved, then you're going to run the ball better," Payton said on Monday. |So I think you're asking was the offensive line improved or better. I think we played really good in the front. Obviously we played more players. I thought from a protection standpoint and from a run game standpoint, there were a lot of good things to be excited about."

That last part is the most interesting. If Payton saw a lot of good things to be excited about, then we should also feel a lot better about where they might be heading into the part of the year when a strong rushing attack is how you win games in the NFL.
