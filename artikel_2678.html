WR George Pickens, OG Tyler Smith were limited in Wednesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Cowboys' injury report has 16 names, with five players sitting out Wednesday's practice with injuries.

Defensive back Caleb Downs did not practice with a concussion and an ankle injury. He left Sunday's game with a head injury.

Downs, a first-round rookie, has played 509 defensive snaps — 92 percent — and 45 on special teams. He has totaled 52 tackles, a sack, a pass defensed and two forced fumbles.

Tight end Jake Ferguson (ankle), offensive tackle Broderick Jones (personal), outside linebacker Malachi Lawrence (ankle), safety P.J. Locke (concussion), outside linebacker Von Miller (rest) and wide receiver KaVontae Turpin (ribs) did not practice.

Wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring), tight end Luke Schoonmaker (groin), left guard Tyler Smith (ankle) and linebacker Dee Winters (calf) were limited.

Defensive tackle Jonathan Bullard (calf), defensive tackle Kenny Clark (back), outside linebacker Marist Liufau (forearm), cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) and offensive tackle Drew Shelton (hamstring) were full participants.
