Jack Campbell injury updates: Lions LB remains in hospital, but 'indications are positive'

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Detroit Lions linebacker Jack Campbell was strapped to a backboard and carted off the field after appearing to suffer a serious injury during the team's Week 8 matchup against the Minnesota Vikings.

Following the 19-15 victory, Lions coach Dan Campbell told reporters he had no further information on Jack's status, other than that he was receiving medical care. He stayed in the hospital overnight for evaluation, and the team announced it would provide more information when it had it available. Monday afternoon, the team provided the following statement:

Jack Campbell continues to rest and recover at a local hospital. Preliminary indications are positive and out of respect for Jack and his family we will not share further information at this time.

Shortly after that update, Dan Campbell met with the media, and though he did not share any further updates, he did ask for continued prayers and say he had a chance to visit Jack in the hospital Sunday night and talk with his family.

"It was good, it was positive," Dan Campbell said. "Got to see his parents, talk to his wife, and everybody's in good spirits. It's about the recovery, and it's a small setback. That's the family. They're very positive. So that's good in that regard."

Campbell also said "a lot of people went over there" when asked if teammates were able to visit Jack, though he acknowledged there were lots of tests being run, so not everyone could visit with the linebacker.

On the first play of the fourth quarter, Jack Campbell and teammate Eric O'Neill combined to tackle Vikings tight end T.J. Hockenson. Campbell then went limp. While lying prone, Campbell was not seen moving before trainers reached him. After about 10 minutes, Campbell was strapped to a backboard and carted off the field.

The Lions announced that Campbell was transported to a local hospital to be evaluated for a neck injury.

It was an emotional scene at Ford Field. The entire Lions team went over to see their teammate off, while the defense gathered for a moment to rally after seeing one of their leaders go down.

In seven games this season, Campbell recorded 57 tackles, two QB hits and a fumble recovery. Just six months ago, he signed a four-year, $81 million extension that made him one of the highest-paid off-ball linebackers in the NFL.

Campbell was selected with the No. 18 overall pick in the 2023 NFL Draft out of Iowa, just six selections after the Lions drafted running back Jahmyr Gibbs. The 26-year-old had a career year in 2025, recording 176 tackles, nine tackles for loss, five sacks, three forced fumbles and four passes defended. He was named a First Team All-Pro and earned his first Pro Bowl bid.
