Report: Mike McCarthy informed team of Aaron Rodgers benching on Wednesday morning

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The surprise, sudden benching of Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers raises more questions than it answers. Coach Mike McCarthy may be starting to get some answers regarding the question of how the decision will go over in the locker room.

Via Mike DeFabo of The Athletic, “McCarthy talked to his players about the QB change for the first time during their regularly scheduled 8 a.m. meeting” on Wednesday. Also from DeFabo: “It sounds like this was not a popular decision in the locker room.”

The reaction within the locker room becomes a major factor, especially with a game coming in only two days. And with Rodgers presumably still on the team, even if there’s a chance that’s temporary.

If nothing else, more than few players will surely wonder why the Steelers announced the decision on Twitter late last night in lieu of telling the players first.

As Wednesday unfolds, will Rodgers have something to say? Will McCarthy meet with reporters to explain the decision — especially after he said on Monday, “Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback”?

Through it all, the 8-2 Broncos are coming to town for a game that begins in less than 54 hours. Good luck getting the players properly refocused on the task at hand, which happens to be coming against one of the best teams in the league.
