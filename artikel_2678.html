Will Rams offensive lineman recover for Week 12?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Los Angeles Rams beat the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday, but they lost their starting center Coleman Shelton and back-up center Dylan McMahon. It was an unbelievable occurrence, as the injuries mount up for L.A., but they were able to win and now get the bye week to rest.

For the Rams, they have to hope they are healthier for their Week 12 matchup against the Green Bay Packers. If the Rams centers are ready by then, they may have to sign some new faces.

The injuries keep piling up for the Rams, but they are on a four game win streak and will spend the next week hoping to get some guys back as they prepare for an end of season run. These next few weeks will likely determine if the Rams make the playoffs or not. Having their centers back could make push easier, but they'll play with the guys they have.

"Starting center Coleman Shelton exited early in the first half with a hamstring injury. His replacement, Dylan McMahon, suffered a knee injury shortly before halftime, forcing the Rams into another round of musical chairs.

Beaux Limmer, already starting at left guard for the injured Steve Avila (hamstring), shifted to center while Bill Murray took over at left guard."

"There truly wasn't much in the way of positives to cling to in this one, which is why CBS Sports' John Breech gave the Cardinals a big fat "F" for their most recent performance.

"Carson Beck's first career start was a total disaster. When you're a rookie QB, it helps when you have a ground game that can take some pressure off you and/or an offensive line that can protect you, but Beck had neither of those. Beck got sacked five times and he threw two interceptions, including one that Devin Duvernay practically handed to the Rams. The Cardinals had a big first quarter with 71 yards, but they never really moved the ball after that, totaling just 52 yards for the rest of the game. If this is what the Cardinals are going to look like for the second half of the year, don't expect many fireworks from this team for the rest of the season."

"But the Rams continued to show their depth and resourcefulness Sunday in a 34-6 road victory over the Arizona Cardinals, picking up their fourth straight win heading into a much-needed bye week.

"Luckily, we've got a lot of good football players at different positions," Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford said. "Really proud of those guys, just their composure, their effort, and their ability to not get a lot of reps in practice and still go in there and execute in the game. It's awesome."

Stafford threw for 249 yards and three touchdowns behind third-string center Limmer for most of the game, Blake Corum gained 100 yards on the ground and the Rams overcame a slow start by scoring 31 straight points to close the game."

"The Arizona Cardinals hit another low point in a lopsided loss to the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday, and head coach Mike LaFleur did not pretend that help was on the way.

Arizona fell 34-6 at State Farm Stadium, dropping to 3-7 on the season. After the game, LaFleur revealed the blunt message he delivered to his players.

"I said, I owe it to you guys to tell you the truth. There's no magic savior coming right now. Like, this is what we got. And we can make plays, like you guys believe in yourself. Like, you can make these plays, you can help everyone. You know, we can collectively play better than that. And I really believe that. And I know some people might not, but I do," LaFleur said, via PHNX Cardinals."

"GLENDALE, Ariz. — The Rams get their revenge on the Cardinals with a 34-6 win in Arizona, extending their win streak to four games and heading into the bye week with a 6-4 record. The defense was highlighted by five sacks and two interceptions, as the offense shared the wealth in the passing game for 249 yards and was led by a rushing attack that accounted for 202 yards.

Head coach Sean McVay, quarterback Matthew Stafford and running back Blake Corum talked about the offense's creativity and complementary football from the podium."

"Without Nacua, Adams and Ferguson, questions all week were centered around who could be the individual player that would step up for the Rams.

Instead, Stafford elected to share the wealth in the passing game to get everyone involved.

"Luckily we got a lot of good football players at different positions," Stafford said. "Obviously, some young guys got some more opportunities today in the receiver room. I thought our tight ends played fantastic. Both our backs played great."

Against Arizona, Stafford connected with nine different receivers, tight ends and running backs. And no one pass catcher had more than three receptions, while three different players had a receiving touchdown.

"(We were) just trying to spread the ball around," Stafford said. "Obviously, wasn't perfect, but proud of those guys. Each guy kind of got their opportunity and made the most of it, which I was happy for."

Stafford was 18-for-26 for 249 yards, three touchdowns and an interception, averaging 13.8 yards per reception. Multiple catches went for big gains of 20-or-more yards to boost that yards-per-reception number — including a 31-yard catch from Tutu Atwell, a 22-yard catch for CJ Daniels Jr., 27-yard and 44-yard receptions from Konata Mumpfield and a 22-yard touchdown reception from Xavier Smith."
