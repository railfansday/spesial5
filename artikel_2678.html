Breece Hall produces 87 total yards in return from five-game absence

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New York Jets RB Breece Hall carried 15 times for 61 yards and caught three of four targets for 26 yards in Week 9 versus the Kansas City Chiefs.

Why It Matters: Hall returned after missing five games with a quad injury, got the start and operated as the clear lead back, although the Jets liberally worked Braelon Allen into the game throughout. There is trade interest in Hall around the NFL, but several reports suggest that the Jets do not intend to trade him. Hall figures to remain a serviceable fantasy RB2 for the time being and is worth a start again in Week 10 against the Bills.
