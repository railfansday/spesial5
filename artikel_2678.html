Jahmyr Gibbs has 52 career rushing TDs, ties Barry Sanders for most before turning 25

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Two Lions running backs now share the NFL record for the most rushing touchdowns before turning 25 years old.

Jahmyr Gibbs scored his 51st and 52nd career rushing touchdowns on Sunday against the Buccaneers, which ties him with Barry Sanders for the most rushing touchdowns by a player under the age of 25 in NFL history.

Gibbs doesn’t turn 25 until after this season, so if he scores just one more rushing touchdown this year, he’ll move ahead of Sanders and have the record to himself. It’s safe to say Gibbs will score at least one more in the Lions’ seven remaining games. Sunday was Gibbs’ 18th game with multiple touchdowns, and he now has 62 total touchdowns in 59 career games.

Gibbs is the betting favorite to win the NFL’s Offensive Player of the Year award. He has 1,502 scrimmage yards and 15 touchdowns this season, both leading the league.
