Magic The Gatherings Star Trek Federation Fleet Commander Deck is close to an all-time low in Amazons Black Friday sale — save $20

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Star Trek Federation Fleet Commander Deck is on sale for $54 at Amazon, down from its $74.99 list price.

Fans of Star Trek looking to bring Captain Picard and his crew to their next Magic: The Gathering game, and even honor the late and great Patrick Stewart, can now do so for considerably less —  with Amazon's Black Friday discounts extending to the trading card crossover's preconstructed Commander decks.

That's only 12 cents off its all-time low of $53.88. This Magic: The Gathering Commander deck is also only $7.92 above its $46.08 market price on TCGplayer.

With Jean-Luc Picard at the helm, Federation Fleet's blue-red-white lineup revolves around drawing cards, strengthening your crew, and getting your starships operational. Characters like Data, Geordi La Forge, and Captain Janeway are all accounted for, alongside 10 double-sided tokens, a storage box, a strategy guide, and a reference card.
