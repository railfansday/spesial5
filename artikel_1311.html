Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has reportedly decided that Malo Gusto is not in his first-team plans amid interest from Liverpool.

The France international has mostly shone during his time at Stamford Bridge, but so far this season he’s seen a reduced role under new manager Alonso.

The Spanish tactician took over at Chelsea in the summer and his use of wing-backs might not leave much room for Gusto, who has previously been linked with Liverpool by Simon Phillips.

Now a new report from Phillips’ Substack today states that Alonso has advised Gusto to find a new club as he is not in his plans as a regular starter.

It remains to be seen if this will definitely lead to Liverpool making a move for the 23-year-old, but it could be a move that makes sense for all parties.

Gusto might not be every Liverpool fan’s dream signing, but the Merseyside giants could do with making a change in the right-back position.

That area of their squad has been an issue since Trent Alexander-Arnold left, with Jeremie Frimpong struggling since joining from Bayer Leverkusen.

Ronald Araujo, normally a centre-back, is now filling in pretty well on the right since joining Liverpool on loan from Barcelona, but that probably isn’t the best long-term solution.

Gusto could therefore be a good option for LFC to consider if he is indeed set to be available.

While this perhaps seems unlikely now, could Chelsea boss Alonso push for a swap deal involving Gusto and Frimpong?

Frimpong was superb in Alonso’s system at Leverkusen, so it could be a good fit, even if he’s struggled in English football so far.

Perhaps Liverpool wasn’t the right move for him, and a return to a system like Alonso’s could get his career back on track.
