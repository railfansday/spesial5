Video: McKennie joins Juventus group training before AZ Alkmaar clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie trained with his teammates at Continassa this morning, on the eve of a Europa League game against AZ Alkmaar.

Juventus are preparing for Thursday’s away Europa League game against Dutch side AZ.

The Bianconeri’s midfielder McKennie was spotted on the pitch alongside his teammates this morning. He warmed up separately with Juan Cabal, then joined the rest of the team for a training game.

There were doubts about McKennie’s availability for the game against AZ after the USMNT international was subbed off in the previous match against Napoli.

Zeki Celik continued training separately today, while Jeff Ekhator will be called up for his first European match.
