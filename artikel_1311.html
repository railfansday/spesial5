Torino vs. Lecce delayed by 16 minutes by wonky goal-line problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Serie A match between Torino and Lecce saw kick-off delayed by 16 minutes after officials realised the lines around the pitch were wonky, causing problems with the technology.

It was scheduled to kick off at the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino in Turin at 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games on the Liveblog.

After the minute’s silence, which is before all Serie A and B matches this weekend for Spanish player Jesus de Miguel Alameda, the officials realised something wasn’t right with the goal-line near the Curva Maratona end of the stadium.

The goal-line technology was having difficulty reading it, and some checks showed this was because the line was not completely straight.

There was a delay with players continuing warm-up exercises while the stadium staff drew the line on again, and the checks with help from VAR officials confirmed the issue was fixed.

It was the kind of error that is rarely seen in modern football, certainly in the top flight of a major five European League.
