Video: Newcastle loanee Woltemade scores first Serie A goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Nick Woltemade scored his first Serie A goal for Juventus on Sunday, helping secure a 2-2 away draw against Fiorentina.

The German international levelled the score in the first half at the Stadio Franchi.

Randal Kolo Muani dribbled past Fiorentina defender Viery, and his pass into the box found Woltemade alone, ready to score with his right foot.

Just a few minutes later, the German had a chance to score his second goal in similar circumstances, but David De Gea denied him.

Woltemade had already scored three goals in four Europa League matches for Juve, but this was his first Serie A goal this season.

Woltemade is on loan at Juventus from Newcastle for the 2026-27 season, and the Old Lady has no option to make the move permanent in the summer.
