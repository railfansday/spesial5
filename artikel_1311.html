Pagliuca analyses Belgium’s ‘deserved’ victory over Italy and advises Donnarumma

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Former Italy goalkeeper Gianluca Pagliuca has delivered his verdict after Sunday’s 4-1 Nations League defeat against Belgium, feels that it is only natural that the Azzurri encounter a few difficulties during a rebuild phase and has explained where Gianluigi Donnarumma went wrong with his error against France on Thursday night.

Italy’s Nations League campaign came to a premature end after a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday evening. The Azzurri had started the night in first place in their group, but were overtaken by both their opponents on the night and by France, whom they had beaten 3-1 earlier in the week.

Italy had started brightly against Belgium, taking the lead through the in-form Pio Esposito within the first 11 minutes of the game.

Pagliuca, speaking to Radio Anch’io Sport on Monday morning, said that he thought that Italy were going to pick up where they had left off against France.

“At first I thought it was going to be a continuation of the performance from the France game, but then Belgium’s comeback began,” said the former Azzurri goalkeeper. “We suffered a lot in the second half as well. Belgium deserved it, they are more ready than us at the moment.”

Pagliuca feels that it is only normal that Italy aren’t quite up to the level just yet at this stage of Mancini’s rebuild: “I think it’s normal for a national team that is still being rebuilt to not have players at the same level of a team like France.”

He feels that Italy struggled defensively against Belgium, but, again, that this is only normal.

“We suffered against Belgium’s fast attackers. Calafiori and Bastoni, who were perfect against France, suffered yesterday as well. However, I don’t think there’s a big defensive problem. The game is a lot quicker now and more planned in attack, which means you are going to suffer a bit more defensively, but that is the same for everyone.”

Mancini’s inclusion of several youngsters over the last few weeks has gone down positively among supporters and pundits. Only three players of Italy’s starting XI on Sunday night were born before the year 2000 and five of them were aged 22 or under.

“There are lots of good young players, there’s not just one in particular,” said Pagliuca. “I think the coach, who wants to aim for the EUROs and World Cup, needs a period to test a few things out. I think it’s right to call up players even if they are not starters at club level.

“Mancini is experimenting. He will also call up some of the more experienced players for the qualifying matches. For now, he wants to test young players and see what they’re made of. The Nations League does have value, but the important thing is to qualify for the EUROs and World Cup.”

Pagliuca was asked for his thoughts on Donnarumma’s recent performances and the error that he made that led to France’s only goal in Thursday’s 3-1 win in San Siro.

“Sometimes, goalkeepers want to overdo it,” he explained. “Gigio made a mistake against France on Thursday because he was overdoing it. A goalkeeper has to do the simple things correctly. But, he made up for it by making great saves, yesterday as well.”

Lastly, on Pio Esposito, Pagliuca added: “He has also played a lot of games and is improving with every appearance. He’s cool in front of goal and is having a great season.

“I think Cristian Chivu will stick to playing with two strikers for now, but maybe we’ll start to see all three of them at some point in most games. Inter are the clear favourites for the Scudetto and I think they will win the league.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
