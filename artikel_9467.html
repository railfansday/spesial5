Honey almond snack cake!? You know you want to jump in on the fun! This GORGEOUS loaf is perfectly moist with a lightly floral flavor and beautiful sponge texture. Bonus? It’s made without gluten or dairy, but no one will know.

After mixing together your wet ingredients, then adding your dry ingredients, it’s time to pour the batter into a parchment-lined loaf pan. The parchment paper will prevent sticking and make it super easy to remove the cake from the pan.

We also think this cake would work beautifully in an 8-inch round cake pan, but it will bake much faster!

Top with sliced almonds before baking, then pop that beauty in the oven! After baking and letting the cake cool slightly, we love to add an extra drizzle of honey. Deliciousness awaits, friends!
