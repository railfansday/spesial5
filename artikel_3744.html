Dawn of War 4's newest trailer teases the appearance of a fan-favourite character in the game's post-launch DLC

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As Dawn of War 4's December release date inches ever closer, a new trailer appears to have teased a fan-favourite character's inclusion in the RTS's first post-launch DLC.

The trailer, which covers the Commander Edition of Dawn of War 4, includes a breakdown of all the extra stuff packed in alongside this version of the game. This includes the digital soundtrack, three-days of early-access to the game, and the story prologue. It'll also include the Aftermath post-launch DLC too, with its dedicated campaign and new playable faction.

Now, ever since the Aftermath DLC was announced, people have speculated it would be chaos-focused. The design of the DLC logo is black with a gold trim, an often-used colour scheme for the faction. However, which sort of Chaos was left up to debate. That is, until this trailer, where the answer is seemingly given away with a cheeky tease.

In its final few seconds, the trailer shows a pair of glowing yellow mechanical eyes peering out from a gloomy background. This was quickly identified as almost-certainly Vashtorr the Arkifane, a relatively new addition to Warhammer 40,000 and a fan-favourite baddie all around.

Vashtorr as a character has been getting a lot of love ever since his introduction in 2023. It was then, during the Arks of Omen campaign for the tabletop game, that he played a central part in the narrative event bridging the game's 10th and 11th edition. Since then he was the main antagonist of Boltgun 2, and was central in the 2025 novel Achmagos.

What this means for Dawn of War 4 is that, if he is a commander of Chaos forces, expect a lot of machinery and demon engines on his side. That's kind of his whole deal. Whether that means he's paired with tech-focused Dark Mechanicum as a sort of mirror to the Adeptus Mechanicus faction already in the game, or if he'll come alongside Chaos Space Marines to better reflect the Dark Angels / Blood Ravens remains to be seen. It could also be a mix of both! We'll have to wait and see.
