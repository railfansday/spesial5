The Dark Souls Devs Are Masters At Looney Tunes–Style Physical Comedy

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I used to think that Dark Souls was a very serious game. A serious, serious game for serious, hardcore gamers. Grimdark, full of esoteric mysteries and depressing, philosophical themes. Tragedy in every sense. No hope for its characters. Any shred of light snuffed out by the encroaching shadow.

I have been delighted to learn in more recent years that this is actually wrong, and Dark Souls (and its sequels, both real and spiritual) are actually comedy games. Dark Souls is video-game Looney Tunes.

What the fuck is that?! A skeleton attached to a wheel of bones? A bonewheel? I guess?!

A lot of games try really hard to be funny via attempts at witty writing or quippy characters. Some succeed, some fail. But with Dark Souls (and Elden Ring, which I played a few years ago), FromSoftware proves it is is superb at something I imagine must be incredibly difficult to do in video games: physical comedy. You have to take into account player movement, enemy AI, objects getting caught on one anotherâthe delicate dance required to make a physical gag happen in a game like this takes such an expert hand. And yet, FromSoftware manages to Wile E. Coyote the player into incredible slapstick moments over and over, through clever use of environmental hazards and very smart enemy placement within them. Yes, many (most) of these scenarios result in player death, which can be frustrating, but how can I be mad at dying if my death was absolutely hilarious? How can anyone at all be mad when your death animation looks this stupid?

It’s even better when it’s self-inflicted, which it so often is. Dark Souls is full of such clunky, clumsy movement that often, one wrong step will send you into the abyss, and it will be all your fault. Two nights ago, I lost over 30,000 souls because I accidentally dodge-rolled off an easy-to-traverse cliff on a corpse run.
