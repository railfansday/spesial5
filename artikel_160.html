NFL’s Jaylen Harrell reportedly assaulted driver before crash that left player seriously injured

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Incident occurred in Tampa on Friday morning

Crash leaves linebacker with serious injuries

Tampa police are investigating whether Tennessee Titans linebacker Jaylen Harrell assaulted a rideshare driver before a crash that left the NFL player in hospital.

The Tampa ⁠Police Department, in ⁠a preliminary report, said Harrell was in the back seat of the car around 8.45am on Friday in the backseat when “he reportedly assaulted the driver while ⁠the vehicle was in motion.” The driver then swerved, struck another car and collided ⁠with a utility pole, per the police report.

Harrell was thrown from the backseat, hit the windshield ‌and lost consciousness, according to ‌the police report. Harrell remains in hospital with serious injuries, while the rideshare ‌driver, a man in his 30s, sustained “minor injuries” and was treated at the scene, according to police. The driver of the other vehicle, a woman in her 50s, was not injured, the police said.

The Titans issued a statement on Saturday ‌morning but did not disclose Harrell’s condition or the injuries he suffered, nor did they mention the assault accusation. The 24-year-old has not played this season after tearing an ACL in the preseason.

The accident occurred in Tampa, Harrell’s hometown.

Harrell played college football at Michigan before the Titans picked him in the seventh round of the 2024 draft. He played in every game in his first two seasons and recorded 4.5 sacks. His father, James, played eight seasons in the NFL before becoming a successful high school coach.
