National CineMedia, Inc. (NCMI -2.41%) reported third-quarter results impacted by a misalignment between theater attendance peaks and advertiser demand cycles. Management announced the retirement of Chief Executive Officer Tom Lesinski, effective December 31, while transitioning leadership focus toward the integration of the recently acquired Captivate network. The company reported that national advertising budgets shifted toward major sporting events during the period, although core local sales performance remained positive. Strategic efforts are now centered on monetizing increased cinema attendance through more flexible inventory packaging and expanding the digital out-of-home footprint across office and residential locations.
