Anthropic IPO Investors Weigh Valuation as Low as $1.2 Trillion

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Anthropic's IPO plans are running into a punishing post-election selloff, prompting a rethink among prospective investors of the company's expected valuation, which could now be as low as $1.2 trillion, according to sources. Bloomberg's Seth Fiegerman joins Tim Stenovec on “Bloomberg Tech.” (Source: Bloomberg)
