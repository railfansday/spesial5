Jeffries faces online fury over cryptic answer when asked if Dems would risk gov't shutdown: 'Political games'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In a recent interview with The New York Times’ Lulu Garcia-Navarro, Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., refused to say "one way or the other" whether Democrats would pursue a government shutdown to advance their agenda.

Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., triggered online backlash after declining to say "one way or the other" whether the Democratic Party would pursue a government shutdown to advance its agenda during an interview with The New York Times.

When asked by The New York Times' Lulu Garcia-Navarro if he would be willing to risk shutting down the government to achieve Democrats' goals, Jeffries declined to rule out the possibility.

"I'm not going to say that one way or the other because we got to figure out right now what's possible in terms of the appropriations process," he said.

Jeffries said Democrats are now weighing possible restrictions on funding for the Iran war "to make clear that this [war] has to end" ahead of the Dec. 11, 2026, federal funding deadline.

"It needs to be over," he said. "In fact, Donald Trump can end it right now. That doesn't mean there's not going to be a lot of work ahead, because Iran is in a stronger position now than they were before this reckless and costly war of choice started."

He added the 43-day government shutdown that began in October 2025 was an example of Democrats "not going to go along simply to get along."

"That was the longest complete shutdown in American history, and at the end of it, we actually were successful in forcing a vote to extend the Affordable Care Act tax credits," he said.

Social media users were quick to criticize Jeffries' open-ended answer in the viral exchange on Saturday.

"Bookmark this video," Shane Schaetzel, a religious influencer, posted on X. "The Democrats said it first and in advance. They're going to shut down the government. Now when they do this, they always blame the Republicans, and the Legacy Media always dutifully backs them on this. Yet here it is, they're announcing they THEY (the Democrats) are going to do it."

In another post on X, conservative influencer Josh Fulfer, who runs the social media account Oreo Express, wrote, "Democrats are responsible for all the shutdowns that have cost our economy billions of dollars, and left people homeless because they were not paid. They are planning to do it again."

"Federal funding set to expire on December 11 and we know that they are willing to do it to get their way," conservative account Defiant L's posted on X. "People shouldn't suffer because of political games."

"Hakeem Jeffries won't rule out shutting down the government to enact Democrats' agenda," RNC Research posted on X.

Conservative commentator Paul Szypula also posted on X, writing, "Everyone saw this coming. Democrats already forced shutdowns while in the minority. With a majority they'll have an easier time doing it."

Democrats secured several midterm victories this month, taking back control of both the Senate and the House beginning in January. Some Republicans and conservatives worry the shift could lead to future shutdowns and bitter standoffs.
