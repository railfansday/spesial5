Volkswagen ID Buzz Made For Sleeping In

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Volkswagen ID Buzz has not been a big hit in the US (where it’s just too expensive and doesn’t even provide a lot of range), but it’s still one of my favorite vehicles. I would certainly have one in my driveway if I had enough money for one. Part of what makes the ID Buzz so appealing is how it reminds one of the VW Hippie Bus of yesteryear, but that vehicle was super low cost as well as versatile and useful. The ID Buzz is sort of a deceiving imitation of that model, as it’s quite expensive (especially in the US) and doesn’t really provide a lot of extra utility compared to what’s on the market today.

However, the company has just rolled out a new version of the ID Buzz that is designed to allow for easy sleeping within the van. The “camper” version of the ID Buzz has a few special features for this. Also, just to reiterate, it’s not cheap. So it’s more a little luxury camper van for people with money than a way to get around and live freely for young people scraping by and vagabonds.
