Pope Leo hit by flying rosary while waving to crowds in Peru

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Pope Leo XIV was struck in the face by what is thought to be rosary prayer beads while travelling in the popemobile to deliver a farewell Mass in Peru.

Photos and video show the pope rubbing his eye shortly after the object was tossed toward him. He sustained no injuries and the Mass went ahead.
