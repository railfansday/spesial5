Pope concludes South America homecoming, reassures faithful after cancer scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Pope Leo XIV finished Monday his 12-day tour of Uruguay, Argentina and Peru reiterating his advocacy for social justice, environmental protection and the importance of community. Just a month after undergoing lung cancer surgery, the 71-year-old pontiff drew millions of faithful across South America, where he spent nearly two decades as a missionary.
