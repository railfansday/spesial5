All About Jim Curtis, Jennifer Aniston’s Boyfriend

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Jennifer Aniston has kept her love life largely out of the public eye since her 2018 split from Justin Theroux, but that appears to be changing. In July 2025, the Friends star was photographed vacationing in Mallorca, Spain, with Jim Curtis, a wellness expert whose résumé spans everything from hypnotherapy to roles at major health platforms. Days later, People and Us Weekly confirmed that Aniston and Curtis were dating.

Things became more serious, and on Nov. 2, 2025, Aniston went Instagram official with her partner. She later spoke about him in her ELLE Women in Hollywood interview, calling him “quite extraordinary.” Below, everything to know about Curtis.

Curtis refers to himself as a “wellness pioneer, author, and coach,” on his official website. His approach blends subconscious work, mindfulness, and spiritual coaching to help people “break free from their past to create an entirely new, empowered reality.”

Curtis’s background includes roles at WebMD and Everyday Health. He now works as a transformational coach and speaker, offering services he calls “hypno-realizations.”

Curtis’s focus on healing is personal. At 22 years old, he was diagnosed with lesions on his spinal cord, which led to years of chronic pain, muscle spasms, and mobility issues. “It was unknown how they started, but I very rapidly became ill and soon had trouble walking,” he said in a 2018 interview with Fast Company.

“My own illness and disability forced me to confront the limited beliefs, disconnection, and trauma that were keeping me sad, sick, and alone,” Curtis writes on his website. “Through many powerful tools, including hypno-realization, I not only transformed my physical health, depression, and relationships, but I’ve also helped thousands of others break free to create an entirely new reality full of connection, community, and love.”

Curtis has written two books: The Stimulati Experience (2017), which outlines nine principles for overcoming pain and setbacks, and Shift: Quantum Manifestation Guide (2024), coauthored with Sara Hadir.

In May 2025, Aniston shared a photo of one of Curtis’s books in an Instagram carousel, tagging him in the post.

Curtis has cultivated a strong online presence, posting manifestation exercises and motivational videos for over 550,000 followers. His content regularly covers themes like emotional resilience, moving on from heartbreak, and building self-worth. Some of his past clients include Miranda Kerr and Julianne Hough.

Beyond coaching, Curtis is the co-founder of UFACTORY, a branding firm that helps creators and entrepreneurs grow their digital identities. The company’s mission, per its website, is to build “creator, entrepreneur, and CEO personas that attract loyal fandoms and supercharge revenue opportunities.”

Curtis and Aniston were first spotted together at the luxury resort Ventana Big Sur in California.

In July, they were photographed again in Mallorca, alongside Aniston’s longtime friends Jason Bateman and his wife, Amanda Anka. Aniston appeared to be introducing Curtis to the couple before they boarded a yacht.

More photos published a few days later showed Aniston and Curtis getting affectionate aboard the vessel, holding hands and leaning in toward one another. In one image, Curtis touches the actress’s hair in a sweet gesture.

While the Mallorca trip brought their rumored romance into the public eye, Aniston and Curtis connected before. Aniston reportedly followed Curtis on Instagram for nearly two years and had liked numerous posts on topics such as healing, affirmations, and relationships.

In April, she told Travel + Leisure that she’d recently begun using hypnosis to overcome her fear of flying, though she didn’t mention Curtis by name. “But I have been doing some hypnosis lately...it’s shockingly good!” she said.

On July 13, 2025, a source told People that Curtis and Aniston were set up by a friend. “They’ve been seeing each other for a few months now,” the source said. “They were introduced by a friend and started out as friends. Jen had read his book and was familiar with his work. She’s really into self-help and wellness. They are dating, but it’s still casual.”

In mid-July, shortly after a vacation in Mallorca with Aniston, Curtis wrote in his newsletter, per Page Six, “I am back from vacation and what an experience! When I was sick, sad, stuck, and in pain, I never thought I would have the abundance, joy, and love I now experience on a daily basis.”

On August 4, Aniston and Curtis were seen in New York City enjoying a dinner with Jason Bateman and Amanda Anka, days after returning from their trip in Spain.

On August 18, the couple was spotted in Malibu with Courteney Cox and Johnny McDaid, with the group being photographed leaving Nobu together. Aniston opted for a laid-back look with a blue jacket layered over a white shirt, paired with relaxed blue jeans and thin-framed glasses.

Curtis kept his look casual in a dark denim button-down worn open over a gray T-shirt, paired with green trousers and white sneakers.

Aniston and Curtis were photographed back in New York City on Friday, September 5. Aniston wore a white tank top, a gauzy ivory-colored jacket, and a pair of blue jeans with gray patch details. She carried a black purse over one shoulder and wore a pair of black thong sandals. Curtis followed behind in a black jacket, matching pants, and a white T-shirt.

On September 7, Aniston shared an Instagram recapping her summer. The second-to-last slide was a photo showing the back of Curtis’s head as he looked out at a sunset over the ocean. She captioned the post, “Thank you summer ☀️❤️🙏.”

Other slides featured pics of her spending time with friends, including Courteney Cox, Sandra Bullock, Sean Hayes, and Adam Sandler.

On September 10, Curtis stepped out with Aniston at the season 4 premiere of The Morning Show. Aniston walked the carpet on her own in a simple black gown, with Curtis standing nearby as she posed for photos.

During a Q&A on Instagram, Curtis was asked, “How to find love at 42?”

He answered, “That’s a great question, same as you do at 22 and 32 but with more confidence, more experience and more authenticity. You’re not old. First love yourself and recognize that you are the perfect age. And that life is not over at 42. When you are 62 and 72, you will look back at 42 and wish that you were that age.”

He continued, “So start now. Go out, open yourself to love. Make eye contact and smile. Connect with people, and most importantly, love yourself. When you love yourself, you will magnetize more love to you.”

On November 2, Aniston celebrated Curtis’s birthday by posting a black-and-white image of them on Instagram with the caption, “Happy birthday my love. Cherished ❤️”

On November 9, Entertainment Tonight reported that Aniston and Curtis’s relationship is heading in a good direction. A source told the outlet that Aniston is “so happy,” adding, “They just get each other. They’re both funny, spiritual, and it just works.”

The insider noted, “Jim is successful and is used to being in the celebrity world through his work as a life coach, so he feels totally comfortable with Jen and her friends.”

The source also revealed how the couple shares a mutual respect, saying, “They’re very supportive of each other and let each other shine.”

Aniston made her first remarks about Curtis in her November 2025 ELLE Women in Hollywood interview, calling out the incredible work the life coach is doing.

“Hypnotism is one of the many things that he does,” she began. “He’s quite extraordinary, and helps many, many people. He’s very special, very normal, and very kind, and wants to help people heal, move through their trauma and stagnation into clarity. It’s a beautiful thing to commit your life to.”

“Their relationship is different,” said the insider. “She’s very excited about it.”

They continued, “It’s obvious that she admires him. She talks about how he has amazing energy, and she loves what he does for living, because it truly helps people, including herself.”

“She acts like she feels very safe with him. She’s really in a great place,” said the source. “They both liked little rituals and moments that are grounding. They are both into symbolism.”

The couple stepped out on November 14 in the Hollywood Hills for dinner, wearing matching black ensembles. They headed to dinner at the Italian restaurant Pace with friends Jamie and Nico Mizrahi.

On Nov. 25, 2025, Aniston posted a video showing fans how to use products from her hair care line, Lolavie, to tame frizzy hair while keeping its waves. In the clip, she blends her brand’s hair paste and oil before applying it to Curtis’s locks as the hypnotist smiles into the camera. She ends the clip, calling Curtis’s primped look “cute.”

A source speaking with People says that the Friends star was “cautious” when she first started speaking to Curtis, but then she “just went for it.”

The pair spent a lot of time “chatting and getting to know each other” first.

“Jen really valued their friendship in the beginning of the year,” the insider shared. “When it all took a turn to something more romantic, she was cautious at first. Now, she’s excited that she just went for it.”

The source said it “feels like kismet” that they have met and The Morning Show star “truly loves” the influencer.

“He’s brought so many good things into her life,” they added.

Aniston was seen with Curtis on Jan. 3, 2026, at his book event at Godmother’s bookstore in Summerland, California. Curtis was hosting A Deep Reset for the New Year, and the actress dressed casually for the afternoon in a green sweater and jeans.

During a January 26 appearance on the Todayshow, Curtis addressed his relationship with Aniston, revealing how they met and when they started dating.

He told the hosts, “We were just introduced by friends. Yeah, that’s it. We found out that we had mutual friends, and we started to just chat.” Their connection developed gradually, he explained, saying, “It took a long time. We chatted for a long time, and we became close.”

When Craig Melvin asked how long Curtis and Aniston had been together, the life coach replied, “A long time—months now, almost close to a year.”

On February 11, Curtis shared new photos with Aniston to celebrate her birthday. In the first slide, the couple is laughing together on what looks like the deck of a boat. Aniston is bent over giggling and has her hand at Curtis’s waist while he reaches to grab her shoulder. In the second picture, they share a kiss.

In the caption, Curtis wrote, “HBD MY ❤️.”

On Valentine’s Day, Aniston posted a photo in her Instagram Stories. The image was focused on a box of Sweethearts candies with the words “Cutie Pie” written in a big yellow heart on the front. In the background, Curtis can be seen just out of focus wearing a dark T-shirt and looking down at something on the table in front of him.

On February 12, Curtis opened up about his relationship with Aniston during an appearance on the Ced with Intention podcast. Speaking candidly about how they handle conflict, he said, “I spend a lot of time with my girlfriend. We spend a lot of time in the house together. Sometimes, we can have little things that flare up. We have the opportunity to be silent and be angry, [we] could leave the house, think about it, and meditate on how to change it.”

He continued, “Or we can say, ‘Hey, this is what happened, I’m sorry,’ and do the repair, and then go and really work on making sure it happens less or doesn’t happen again. Because once you make a repair, and it just happens five more times, no one trusts it.”

Curtis also emphasized the importance of mutual effort, explaining, “In this relationship, you can’t be emotionally closed off while I’m doing all the work. We both have to work on ourselves. I can’t fix you; you can’t fix me. But we have to at least be in agreement, ‘This is gonna happen when we have this discussion.’”

He added that they’ve discussed how to approach disagreements in advance: “We’ve had discussions where, before anything bad happens, ‘In this situation, do you need time? Do you need 10 minutes to yourself to think about it, or do you want to get into it immediately? Is it OK if we sleep overnight, or are you the type of person who, if you go to bed angry, it’s really painful for you?’ I think there needs to be, especially in a romantic relationship, you need to pregame the rules to this thing that you’re doing.”

On February 19, a source told Entertainment Tonight that Aniston and Curtis’s relationship is “the real deal.” The insider added, “Their relationship is serious. Her close friends love him and think this is it for her.”

The source also said the two “started out as friends,” explaining, “She wasn’t a client of his, but he saw other people in her life, and they became close. They are very comfortable with each other.”

On Easter Sunday, Aniston shared a new carousel of photos as part of her “Sunday dump day.” Curtis appeared in two shots:

She shared one photo of them embracing and gazing seriously at the camera and another of just Curtis, relaxed in a chair with a puppy in his lap.

Curtis shared a new look at his relationship with Aniston on Instagram in May 2026. In a carousel captioned, “Life lately. Grateful,” Curtis included a close-up selfie of Aniston kissing his cheek.

The update came shortly after People reported that the couple recently celebrated their one-year anniversary. A source told the outlet, “It’s a great partnership and makes Jen very happy.”

The insider added, “Jim’s a special guy. Everyone loves his energy...Jen’s very grateful for her life, and Jim came into it at a really good time. Their initial friendship made their relationship feel much more genuine from the start.”

On the first weekend of August, Curtis and Aniston were seen in Majorca, Spain, with their friends Jason Bateman, Christian Hogue, and Jamie Mizrahi. The couple was photographed sharing a sweet kiss and embrace aboard their yacht before heading out for a paddleboard.

Ahead of the release of his new book, The Book of Possibility: Release. Align. Become., Curtis spoke with The Sunday Times in an interview published in September 2026 and shared that his relationship with Aniston is “more natural” than those in his past. “The drive was...more organic, less needy,” he said. “In the past I’ve felt this idea of, ‘I need you to be in love with me.’ I wasn’t searching for love anymore.”

They had “a many-month friendship first,” he shared. That foundation then led to a relationship “with someone unlike anyone I had ever been in a relationship with.”

“What do I feel now?” he asked himself. “I feel real joy, real presence, real calmness, real partnership.”

On November 25, Curtis gave Aniston a sweet shout-out on Instagram Stories. The hypnotherapist shared a photo of a café window with the words “I love you so matcha!” written across it, tagging Aniston underneath the message. The gesture comes as the actress spends time in London.
