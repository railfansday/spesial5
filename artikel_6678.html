These Cashmere Perfumes Are the Equivalent of Quiet Luxury in a Bottle

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Like wrapping yourself in a $400 cashmere sweater.

Not much compares to the snuggly feeling of a cashmere sweater against your skin, especially in cold weather. But if you don’t want to drop the $$$ on a whole cashmere wardrobe (if you do, make sure it’s Quince’s affordable cashmere), the next-best thing is a cashmere perfume.

Obviously, cashmere itself doesn’t have a smell. It’s a fabric, after all! But perfumers have found a way to capture that fluffy, cozy feeling through an accord called “cashmeran”—a blend of fragrance notes designed to evoke the soft, warm sensation of cashmere. Usually, that means it’s aldehydic (like fresh linens and cotton), warm, and a little bit powdery. But perfumes can still fall into the “cashmere” bucket even without a specific accord, usually using notes like creamy sandalwood and vanilla, warm amber and tonka bean, and even cloud-like musk and jasmine.

Smelling like cashmere has been my preferred way to embrace the homey feeling heading into winter. Nestling beside a fire, drinking a hot beverage, and wearing—you guessed it—a cashmere sweater has never been easier, so long as I have one of the below scents sprayed on. Keep reading for Cosmo editors’ top seven picks from brands like Byredo, Orebella, Phlur, and NOYZ.

"I always thought powdery perfumes would make me smell like a grandma, but either I'm inching toward grandma status or Angel Dust by Fugazzi is built different. It's powdery but not from heavy florals. Instead, the scent combines a mix of skin-like cashmere, musk, and amber. It's soft and reminiscent of the inside of a knitwear shop.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

“It’s hard to find a fragrance that straddles freshness and coziness, but this one does exactly that. Its moniker comes from a Brazilian Portuguese expression that describes the sensation of good vibes, happiness, and optimism, and it’s fair to say this perfume captures that sentiment perfectly. It oozes Brazilian warmth and sunniness and makes me want to book a trip to Rio.”—CELIA SHATZMAN, CONTRIBUTING WRITER

“I rely on body mists immediately after I get out of the shower to lightly fragrance my skin without feeling like too much. This one is my go-to right now (and the inspiration behind my current cashmere fragrance obsession). Even with nothing on (spicy!), my skin smells so toasty. It makes me want to immediately nuzzle into a plush robe.”—BG

“Unmute has a little bit of everything—juiciness from the plum note, spice from the cinnamon, musk from the amber, warmth from the sage, and sweetness from the pistachio—all wrapped in a cozy, comforting vanilla base. There’s something sexy and self-assured about the scent, without feeling too put-on or artificial.”—DANIELLE HARDMAN, CONTRIBUTING WRITER

“Milky perfumes are my favorite category of fragrance lately, and this does the lactonic, creamy fragrance so well. It’s not too sweet or cloying; instead, it leans on resinous amber, oak, and tonka bean to balance the scent. It’s like sitting by a fire, wrapped in the softest knits.”—BG

"I usually associate floral perfumes with spring and summer, but this one is undeniably perfect for cooler weather. Odéon is a smooth, rich rose scent, with a hefty dose of amber and cashmeran that gives it depth. It's inspired by Parisian cafe culture—and since I studied abroad there (hold the eyerolls), I feel qualified to say that this scent embodies the city’s romantic allure. It smells like window shopping in boutiques I can't afford and moonlit strolls along the Seine."—LAUREN GRUBER, COMMERCE EDITOR

“It’s an icon for a reason! Cashmere Mist is the OG of cashmere scents—it’s musky, powdery, and creamy, with soft woods and airy florals. My aunt was obsessed with this perfume in my youth, and I remember sneaking sprays of it whenever I wanted to feel like a ~sophisticated lady~”—BG
