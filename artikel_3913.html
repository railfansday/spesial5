Slay the Spire 2 is one of this year's best selling games despite only being available on PC, and its players are "obsessed"

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

One million copies for each month on sale, it seems. Slay the Spire 2, Mega Crit's roguelike deckbuilder, is estimated to have sold 7.9m copies since its release in Steam Early Access in March.

That's according to estimates by Alinea Analytics industry analyst Rhys Elliott, who revealed in a LinkedIn post that those sales translate to $157.6m (~£119.6m) in revenue for the small studio.

Slay the Spire 2 is now the sixth best-selling 2026 release across Steam, PlayStation and Xbox. That is notable, as the game is only out on Steam, meaning it has sold enough copies to outpace games on platforms it's not even available on.

We already knew Slay the Spire 2 was one of the year's biggest releases. In its first week on sale, the game sold over 3m copies. It quickly became March's top-selling game on Steam, beating blockbuster competition in Crimson Desert and Resident Evil Requiem. Slay the Spire 2, alongside other indie titles such as Meccha Chameleon, have helped Steam deliver another record year of revenue.

It was such a hit, even, that other indie developers felt it negatively impacted sales of their own games. Mewgenics maker Edmund McMillen recently told Eurogamer "it sucked everybody out". "The same players who liked Mewgenics, they were Slay The Spire players. So they all went woosh and right over," McMillen said.

Slay the Spire 2 has been consistently popular with players, too, and that popularity has seemingly continued. Elliott noted the game never fell below 2.5 million active players each month since March.

Slay the Spire 2's loyal following also meant that the recently released beta patch, which added a new character, was enough to bring back millions of players. Despite only being available for players who opt into the beta branch of the game, Slay the Spire 2 more than doubled its daily active users to reach its highest daily player count in six months.

"Simply put: Slay the Spire 2 players are obsessed," wrote Elliott. "And it's not just a niche group of dedicated players, either. The average play time is nearly 84 hours, sure. But zooming out, 1.9 million folks have played for over 100 hours."

Incidentally, nearly 100,000 players bought the game but never played it, which Elliott added could explain the discrepancy between player numbers and copies sold.

Looking ahead, Slay the Spire 2's road to version 1.0 is expected to be long, with several features and gameplay additions still in the works. Then there are the console ports, which have yet to be announced, but are very much expected.

In Eurogamer's Slay the Spire 2 early access review, Bertie Purchese called it "a deceptively familiar sequel" whose confident design and smart new additions build on what made the original work so well.
