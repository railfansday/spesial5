After Grand Theft Auto 6's launch, players are in love with wacky criminality, in-game social media, and animal fatalities

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With Grand Theft Auto 6 launching late last night (unless you're in New Zealand, in-person or otherwise), players have been flocking to online spaces to share their favourite moments. So far, slapstick violence, animal fatalities, and the in-game social media have won the hearts of many.

The game, a vast open world in which crime and violence may in fact pay, obviously allows players to get into brawls with the denizens of Vice City. While this can of course be brutal, many of these scuffles have a capacity for grand comedy.
