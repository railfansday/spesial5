NASA's Voyager 1 phones home from record one light-day away, 49 years after launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Voyager 1 probe launched by NASA nearly a half century ago has reached a distance from Earth of one light day (more than 26 billion kilometres), the farthest any human artifact has ever ventured from our planet. The space agency received a signal from the spacecraft Thursday, exactly 24 hours after it was sent.
