'Out of control': ICE agents make arrests at Seattle airport in 'horrific' scene

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The governor of Washington state called US federal immigration agents "out of control" after they arrested two people at Seattle's main airport on Saturday. ICE officers have conducted raids across the United States, part of President Donald Trump's immigration crackdown which has forged ahead despite a rebuke by voters at this month's midterms.
