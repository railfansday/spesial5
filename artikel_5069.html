Chainflip identifies who lost money in $12.6 million hack as recovery plan takes shape

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Chainflip finalized its user loss assessment following a $12.6 million exploit, laying the groundwork for a recovery fund.

Chainflip said on Nov. 27 that it had finished assessing how the theft from its Bitcoin and Ethereum vaults affected user balances.

The cross-chain swap protocol said the accounting gives it a basis to develop a recovery plan for people who were swapping at the time, regular account holders, and liquidity providers who supply assets for trades.

In its second incident report, Chainflip said it had finalized the list of affected users and the method for calculating the impact on each account. It said it will settle outstanding loans at prices from the time of the Nov. 21 attack to finalize affected balances.

The team said it could use the list to record the losses on-chain and set up a recovery fund managed by the protocol.

Chainflip’s Nov. 27 update described how it identified affected users and what work remained.

The report gave no individual balances, funding terms, proposed payment amounts or payout timetable.

The new accounting looks at the impact on individual accounts, which is what Chainflip says will inform its recovery proposal.

Chainflip began returning free balances to liquidity providers on five unaffected networks while it assessed losses. The first report named Solana, Arbitrum, Tron, BNB Chain and Polkadot Asset Hub. Governance used designated refund addresses to send back those available funds.

The first report said assets in open loans, strategies, or range orders could still be held in Chainflip accounts, so a provider could receive a precautionary refund and still have assets in an open position.

Chainflip said the protocol remains paused pending security review and fixes. It said major active liquidity providers were willing to return if that work met a high standard and a plan to replenish their capital was put into effect.

Chainflip and the Crosschain Association are also working to add SwapKit to the existing swap site. That temporary service would route new trades through other providers while Chainflip works on its own restart.
