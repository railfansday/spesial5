Why CleanSpark Stock Was a Winner on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The company published its final earnings report for fiscal 2026.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 25, 2026 at 7:10PM EST

10 stocks we like better than CleanSpark ›

CleanSpark's (CLSK +1.27%) final earnings release of its current fiscal year was published after market hours on Tuesday. The following day, investors showed their somewhat cautious appreciation for the company's performance by trading its shares up by almost 1.3%. While not every metric in CleanSpark's fourth quarter was impressive, the company improved several important line items.

We can't say that for net revenue, as it declined to $120.9 million from $223.7 million in the fourth quarter of 2025. That wasn't a shock or surprise, however, as the company's legacy business of Bitcoin (BTC +2.15%) mining was affected by a drop in the crypto's average price while it was building out its artificial intelligence (AI) data center business.

The dynamic was much different on the bottom line, where CleanSpark flipped to a net profit of $9.8 million ($0.04 per share) from the year-ago loss of $925,000. Much of this, however, was tied to its legacy business -- it booked a nearly $306 million accounting gain on the value of its Bitcoin, despite the digital currency's recent softening.

This meant a mixed quarter for CleanSpark, as it whiffed on the analyst consensus estimate of $134.3 million. Yet it trounced the average pundit expectation of a $0.33 per share net loss.

The company's results are choppy due to the nature of the Bitcoin mining business, which, for obvious reasons, depends on the cryptocurrency's price. CleanSpark has now entered a pivotal fiscal year for its transformation, as it expects to begin drawing revenue from high-profile clients for AI data center space.

Given that, I'd say that investors were buying into CleanSpark based on that high-potential future rather than the unexpected flip into profitability. I'd be even more cautious than those folks. At the same time, though, AI data center operation is almost certainly a durable and lucrative business; this company isn't the only legacy crypto miner making the transition, and it hasn't yet proven it can soar above such peers.
