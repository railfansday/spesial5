Cowboys lead Colts 20-17 headed to fourth quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Cowboys have a 20-17 lead on the Colts headed to the fourth quarter.

Dallas took a two-score lead on Indianapolis on Brandon Aubrey's 28-yard field goal with 2:10 left in the third quarter. Three plays earlier, CeeDee Lamb scored an 11-yard touchdown, but replay showed he had only one foot down in the end zone before going out of bounds.

However, only 53 seconds later, the Colts made it a one-score game again.

Ashton Dulin went 55 yards to the end zone with a Daniel Jones pass, the team's first play over 50 yards this season. Their longest play Sunday was 16 yards before Dulin's catch and run.

Both teams have had a number of injuries.

Cowboys tight end Jake Ferguson appears ready to come back in after an apparent left knee injury, and running back Tyler Goodson has returned despite a knee injury.

Cowboys linebacker Dee Winters is being evaluated for a concussion.

The Colts have ruled out right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) and linebacker Jaylon Carlies (neck).

Colts cornerback Charvarius Ward is being treated for cramps and is questionable to return.
