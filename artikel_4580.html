These Are Our Top-Rated Car Brands, Ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We averaged the individual ratings of the hundreds of models on sale to determine which brands are top-rated overall in our evaluations.

Which brands make the best cars? It's an age-old question that's constantly changing as car companies compete with each other to produce the latest and greatest products. But it's a question that we set out to answer with our comprehensive testing of every new vehicle we can get our hands on. We rate vehicle models on a one-to-10 scale—find more about how we determine our ratings here—and now we've averaged all our ratings by brand to determine which ones earn the highest ratings in Car and Driver's testing. We included the top 10 brands in this list.

Because we rate vehicles within their relative segments, this ranking of car brands shows which makes produce the most competitive products across the industry. These ratings come from a combination of objective instrumented testing and subjective real-world driving experience. And note that our ratings are constantly changing as we evaluate new products, so these average scores merely represent a snapshot of where these brands stand in today's evaluations.

Lucid is a new electric automaker that only started selling cars a few years ago, but it's off to a strong start. Both the Lucid Air electric luxury sedan and Lucid Gravity electric luxury SUV are previous winners of our 10Best award, so it's no surprise they rate highly in our book.

Porsche's lineup used to consist of only sports cars, but it has expanded in the past few decades to include SUVs, sedans, and several electric models. With the 911 sports car serving as the cornerstone of Porsche's excellent driving dynamics, the brand does a good job infusing the same precise feel into its other products, so nearly all Porsche models carry high C/D ratings.

Rivian, like Lucid, is a new EV automaker that has only a few models in its stable. But they're all good at what they do, so it's one of our top-rated brands. The R1S SUV and R1T pickup truck are Rivian's larger offerings, and the R2 compact SUV arrived recently to bring the brand's entry price point down to a more accessible level.

Another relatively new brand that quickly rose to the upper echlons of the automotive world is Genesis, Hyundai's luxury spinoff. It's been around since 2015, and its products have improved rapidly. The GV70 and GV80 luxury SUVs both sit near the top of their class, while the G70, G80, and G90 sedans are also strong offerings, bolstering Genesis' average score above its more well-established luxury competitors in our ratings.

Honda models regularly earn multiple spots on our annual list of 10Best award winners, so it's no surprise that this brand places highly here. The mainstream Accord, Civic, and CR-V comprise the core of the Honda lineup in terms of sales, but there's excellence to be found elsewhere, such as the Passport two-row SUV and the high-performance Si and Type R variants of the Civic.

Volkswagen may have shifted its focus towards SUVs in the U.S., but it still offers enthusiast favorites including the Golf GTI and Golf R that help bolster its reputation among car lovers. Plus, its SUV offerings are competitive, especially the Tiguan which places highly in our compact-SUV rankings.

BMW has a broad lineup ranging from small sedans to full-size, three-row SUVs. And there's a clear throughline within this portfolio, as most BMW models are competitive in their segments and offer the satisfying driving dynamics and refined powertrains we've come to expect from this German luxury marque.

Familiar Ford nameplates such as the Focus and Fusion have been gone for years now, as the Blue Oval now builds almost exclusively SUVs and trucks. But its SUVs and trucks are largely competitive, and there's plenty of fun to be had within the Bronco side of the corral. And of course, the Mustang pony car is still going strong.

Hyundai has an impressively full lineup of sedans, SUVs, and even a pickup truck, and it's taken the fight to stalwarts such as Honda and Toyota by offering many hybrid and battery-electric models in competitive segments. The Palisade Hybrid SUV and Ioniq 5 electric SUV are among the brand's current standout models.

Mazda earned its way into our hearts by building the MX-5 Miata sports car—and then by imbuing its more mainstream sedans and SUVs with the same fun-to-drive spirit. Some newer Mazdas have prioritized technology and size over driving enjoyment, so a few models have slipped in our ratings, but the brand still offers several models sitting at or near the top of their segments.

Despite being raised on a steady diet of base-model Hondas and Toyotas—or perhaps because of it—Joey Capparella cultivated an obsession for the automotive industry throughout his childhood in Nashville, Tennessee. He found a way to write about cars for the school newspaper during his college years at Rice University, which eventually led him to move to Ann Arbor, Michigan, for his first professional auto-writing gig at Automobile Magazine. He has been part of the Hearst Autos team since 2016 and now lives in New York City.
