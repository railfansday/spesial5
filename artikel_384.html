Louis Therouxs Instagram post with Nathan Fielder has the internet abuzz

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Nathan Fielder and Louis Theroux in the same room?

For fans of deadpan interviews that can verge on the awkward and surreal, it's a great day.

Theroux, whose recent documentary Inside the Manosphere made a splash on Netflix, shared an image of himself having coffee with Fielder on Instagram.

"Catching up with this guy. Apparently he has a new documentary out," wrote Theroux, referring to Fielder's much-hyped Elizabeth Holmes documentary You Can See Everything. "Has anyone heard anything about it?"

The excitement in the comment section was palpable.

So, what does it mean? Are the two working on something together?

Well, if they are, then the most likely format would probably be Theroux's weekly podcast. But what's just as likely is the two could simply be meeting up for chat — Fielder and Theroux have in fact been in conversation before for an article for Interview Magazine, and it's clear that they're fans of each other's work.

Here's hoping we see the pair sharing the same screen very soon.
