The best Black Friday 3D printer deals start at $159.99 at Amazon

Black Friday 3D printer deals at Amazon and Bambu Lab cover multi-color filament machines, enclosed CoreXY printers, and high-resolution resin models from Creality, Flashforge, Elegoo, and Anycubic. Every link below goes to an individual product page.

If you’re shopping for a first machine, look for automatic bed leveling so you spend weekends printing instead of turning thumbscrews. CoreXY printers move the print head along X and Y while the bed only travels up and down, which keeps prints steady at high speeds. MSLA resin printers cure liquid photopolymer layer by layer with UV light.

Amazon has the Creality K2 Combo at $449, which is $197 under its $645.99 list price. The bundle pairs an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 260 x 260 x 260 mm build volume and one four-spool CFS unit, and Creality rates it for speeds up to 600 mm/s. The 16-color figure in Creality’s marketing takes four CFS units, so plan on four colors per print unless you buy three more.

The Adventurer 5M Pro is the pick here for someone who wants an enclosed printer for ABS or ASA and doesn’t need multi-color. It’s $359 at Amazon, down from a $479 list price. Flashforge lists a 220 x 220 x 220 mm build volume, a 280-degree Celsius quick-swap nozzle, a built-in camera, and a HEPA and carbon filter, and it rates quiet mode at 50 decibels. That build volume is smaller than the 260 mm cube on the K2 Combo above, so check the size of what you plan to print.

Resin is the way to go for miniatures and other small, detailed prints, and the Saturn 4 Ultra 12K is $399.99 at Amazon, a $194 cut from its $593.99 list price. It has a 10-inch 12K monochrome LCD and a tilting vat that peels each cured layer off the release film at an angle. Elegoo rates print speed at up to 150 mm/h. Budget for nitrile gloves, isopropyl alcohol, a wash and cure station, and a ventilated place to work, because resin printing needs all four. Elegoo also sells a 16K version with a sharper screen, so make sure you’re comparing the right model.

The other multi-color machines in this post top out at four colors without add-ons. The Kobra X Combo prints seven out of the box with Anycubic’s ACE 2 Pro filament box, and it’s $449.99 at Amazon (was $569.99). Its build volume is 260 x 260 x 260 mm, and Anycubic says the ACE 2 Pro dries filament at up to 65 degrees Celsius during a print. Reaching the advertised 19 colors takes four ACE 2 Pro units, and Anycubic warns against using cardboard spools in the box.

Bambu Lab’s own sale ends Dec. 1 at 2 a.m. Eastern and includes the dual-nozzle X2D, which succeeds the retired X1-Carbon.

These Amazon deals range from a $169 beginner bed slinger to a four-toolhead machine built to skip purge blocks.

For tabletop miniatures and display figures, MSLA resin printers still beat filament on fine detail.

Damp filament causes stringing and weak layer adhesion, and resin prints need an alcohol wash and UV cure before handling.
