Anthropic Will Pay SpaceX Up to $120.5 Billion for Compute Through 2029. That's More Than SpaceX Raised in the Biggest IPO in History.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SpaceX and Anthropic have formed a massive AI compute partnership, but there's a catch.

By Keith Noonan – Nov 18, 2026 at 8:45AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Space Exploration Technologies ›

Anthropic's Claude models are at the forefront of the artificial intelligence (AI) software space and have helped the business achieve stellar growth. The company's revenue increased 47% sequentially in the third quarter to $11.7 billion, and revenue in the period was up 1,127% year over year.

Along with the rapid scaling of its AI software services, Anthropic is making massive compute purchases -- and Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +0.45%) has emerged as one of the company's most important partners. Read on for a breakdown of Anthropic's $120.5 billion contract with SpaceX and what it could mean for the two companies.

The contract size is notably larger than the $85.7 billion that SpaceX raised in its June initial public offering (IPO), underscoring how quickly Anthropic has become one of the company's most important customers. On the other hand, whether the full $120.5 billion deal is actually realized isn't set in stone.

The contract has lock-in clauses that extend through the first quarter of 2027, but after that, either party is free to back out with 90 days' notice. That creates a dynamic in which maintaining Anthropic as a customer takes on significant importance for SpaceX and its shareholders.

For example, SpaceX guided for its first-quarter sales to come in between $33 billion and $37 billion -- and its compute sales to Anthropic should contribute $9.3 billion in revenue for the period. Further out, there are some significant causes for uncertainty.

If compute prices come down, Anthropic would have plenty of incentive to back out of the deal. Anthropic is also building its own data centers, and it may reduce reliance on SpaceX as it brings more of its own compute capacity online. Alternatively, it's also possible that compute prices could actually increase -- giving SpaceX cause to seek higher rates.

Exactly how the contract's realization shakes out remains to be seen, but the deal sets up a dynamic in which SpaceX and Anthropic's relationship will be very important to each company's stock performance in the coming years.
