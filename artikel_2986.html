Stock Market Today, Nov. 23: Nvidia Surges 31%, Adding Nearly $1 Trillion in Market Value as Jensen Huang Reclaims CEO Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nvidia's historic rebound has helped reverse some of Wall Street's recent losses, but questions surrounding regulatory scrutiny, export restrictions, and the sustainability of AI spending remain unresolved.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 23, 2026 at 4:40PM EST

Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%) staged a historic comeback on Monday, November 23, surging 31.48% to close at $165.05 following the board's decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO and president. The announcement helped restore confidence in the semiconductor giant's leadership, adding nearly $1 trillion to its market capitalization and fueling a broader recovery across Wall Street.

The rally marked Nvidia's largest single-day percentage gain in more than two decades, reversing a substantial portion of the losses suffered during last week's semiconductor sell-off. Trading volume surged to 772.5 million shares, approximately 330% above its three-month daily average of 179.8 million, reflecting extraordinary investor interest.

Wall Street staged a remarkable recovery as technology stocks led a broad market rebound following last week's steep sell-off. The S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.73%) surged 4.74% to close at 7,238, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.51%) jumped 6.51% to 24,090, reflecting a sharp reversal in investor sentiment.

Semiconductor stocks were among the biggest beneficiaries of the rally, with Nvidia's leadership announcement helping restore confidence across the industry. Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +6.85%) climbed 6.85% to $453.46, while Broadcom (AVGO +3.00%) gained 3.00% to $313.55.

Nvidia's historic rally marks a significant reversal in investor sentiment following last week's steep sell-off. Jensen Huang's reinstatement removes a major source of leadership uncertainty, potentially restoring confidence in the company's strategic direction and relationships with key customers. However, the magnitude of Monday's rebound also raises questions about whether investors are responding to an improvement in Nvidia's underlying outlook or simply reversing the excessive pessimism that drove its recent decline.

Fundamentally, Nvidia's business remains exceptionally strong. Third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue surged 103% year over year to $115.8 billion, while management raised its fourth-quarter outlook above Wall Street's expectations. These results demonstrate that demand for AI infrastructure remains substantial, even as investors increasingly scrutinize the sustainability of technology spending.

Looking ahead, Nvidia's reported SEC accounting investigation and export controls on semiconductors to China remain important developments to monitor. Additional regulatory disclosures or changes to export policies could materially influence the company's growth prospects and valuation. While Monday's rebound is encouraging, more progress is needed before a sustained recovery can be confirmed.
