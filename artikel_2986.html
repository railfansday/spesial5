StubHub: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rapid deleveraging cut net leverage from 4.5 to 2.5 times EBITDA, but the open distribution push is still unproven at scale.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, StubHub (STUB -3.42%) announced an increase in its full-year gross merchandise sales, representing the total dollar value of tickets sold on its platform, guidance to 11% to 13% growth. Operating efficiency drove adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) margin up 10 percentage points year over year to 25%. Debt repayments of $1.1 billion since its initial public offering have lowered borrowing costs and strengthened its balance sheet. Three takeaways from management's comments stand out.

Sales and marketing costs fell nine percentage points year over year to 45% of revenue in the third quarter. Management attributes this efficiency to established market leadership, allowing it to capture repeat customer traffic without spending heavily on acquiring buyers.

Net leverage dropped from 4.5 times adjusted EBITDA at the end of 2025 to 2.5 times at the end of the third quarter of 2026. The company paid down $100 million of debt in July and extended term loan maturities to October 2033, creating balance sheet flexibility for strategic investments.

Management is expanding beyond traditional resale through open distribution, which allows primary ticket sellers and content owners to list inventory directly on the platform without exclusivity. Concurrently, the platform is testing sponsored listings, allowing sellers to pay for elevated visibility as event dates approach.

Live event demand remains steady across sports and music categories, and international concert travel continues to expand the customer base. StubHub benefits from network effects and a long operational history handling high-demand ticket fulfillment, while management watches the timing of major 2027 concert ticket releases and updates to search engine algorithms.

With leverage down and term loan maturities pushed out to October 2033, the company is shifting focus toward driving free cash flow per share over the long term.
