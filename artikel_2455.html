Roma could revive Leweling interest in January as Stuttgart lower asking price

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Last summer, Jamie Leweling was linked with a move away from VfB Stuttgart, but he ultimately stayed put after the German side rejected a €35 million offer plus bonuses from AS Roma shortly before the transfer window closed.

Stuttgart invested €55 million in new signings, and the club's supervisory board has now decided that player sales will be necessary to balance the books.

According to Bild, Leweling is among the players who could be sold – and potentially as early as January.

The German tabloid reports that Stuttgart are now willing to let the winger leave for a fee of around €30 million, having lowered their asking price.

Reports in Italy suggest that Roma are still looking to strengthen their options out wide, so it would not be surprising if the Serie A club's decision-makers once again considered a move for the 25-year-old.
