‘I wanted to score more!’ – Vinicius explains Brazil sub frustration

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Vinicius Junior's frustration at being substituted in Brazil's 7-0 demolition of Singapore was simply because he wanted to add to his hat-trick.

With three goals in the opening 21 minutes in the friendly at Singapore National Stadium, Vinicius scored his first senior international hat-trick in his 59th cap.

The Real Madrid winger fired home from an excellent Bruno Guimaraes pass in the ninth minute before Endrick and Estevao granted him simple finishes to put Brazil in control.

Vinicius had a goal ruled out before being replaced by Allan on the hour mark, and he was visibly irritated by the decision.

"It was agreed that I would play 15 or 20 minutes more. I always want to score more goals," said Vinicius.

"I understand that more people needed to play, like Allan. I could have scored more, but I'm happy for the victory."

Vinicius made his Brazil debut in 2019 and had previously bagged three braces for the Selecao, the latest of which came during Saturday's 3-2 loss to Japan.

"May it be the first [hat-trick] of many," said Vinicius.

"We were focused, knowing we were up against an opponent of a much lower level, but we had to make the game easy for ourselves.

"This period is for preparation, for being tested, and the coach did that by playing everyone.

"Now we have to finalise the squad because the qualifiers are approaching."

Endrick, Estevao and Rayan were also on target for Brazil, before Raoul Suhaimi's own goal rounded off the scoring in the 74th minute.
