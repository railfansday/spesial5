Prediction: Norway vs Wales

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

In what is a must-win Nations League showdown for both camps on Saturday evening, Norway will welcome Wales to Oslo.

With a stacked schedule from across the continent this weekend, there are plenty of attractive Nations League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Saturday’s League A showdown from Oslo, you can find all of the key Norway vs Wales H2H stats here.

Verdict: Martin Odegaard over 0.5 shots on target

Starting with the hosts, punching a World Cup quarter-final ticket over in North America during the summer, Norway rightfully received plenty of plaudits from across the globe. However, despite opening their Nations League adventure with an action-packed 3-2 victory at home against historic rivals Denmark on September 24th, Stale Solbakken’s men have come crashing back down to earth in a concerning fashion. Last seen signing off September’s extended international break with a 2-1 loss at the hands of European giants Portugal, Norway have now lost each of their previous three consecutive League A showdowns and they suddenly have a real battle in the race to keep hold of their top-flight status. Opening October with a 2-1 defeat against Wales, Saturday’s hosts have have plenty of pressure on their shoulders when they return to Oslo this weekend.

Getting back to his best with reigning Premier League champions Arsenal this season, skipper Martin Odegaard will lead Norway out in Oslo and earn a 78th international cap on Saturday evening. Notching nine top-flight strikes for Manchester City since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, striking superstar Erling Haaland will spearhead Solbakken’s charge in the final third.

Already registering 11 direct Bundesliga goal contributions for RB Leipzig (four goals, seven assists), the red-hot Antonio Nusa will join Haaland in attack. Completing a season-long loan switch to French outfit Brest, Egil Selvik should keep his spot between the sticks.

As for the visitors, while Wales might have claimed that stellar 2-1 victory at home against Norway last month, it appears that was just a minor boost for Craig Bellamy’s squad. Immediately crashing back down to earth as they signed off September’s extended international break with a toothless 1-0 defeat against Denmark, the former Burnley coach has seen his squad lose three of their four Nations League appearances and they find just their goal difference ahead of Saturday’s opponents. Suffering World Cup qualifying heartbreak back in March, Wales are also searching for an essential clinical edge in the final third. Getting their Nations League charge underway with another 1-0 loss against Portugal on September 24th, Bellamy’s men have recorded more than a single strike in just one of their previous eight matchups across all competitions.

In what is a real blow for Wales, Wrexham striker Kieffer Moore has been forced to withdraw from Bellamy’s squad due to injury. However, the Dragons have welcomed back the Leeds United duo of Joe Rodon and Daniel James this month.

Picking up a costly Nations League booking against Portugal last month, Swansea City defender Benjamin Cabango will miss out through suspension. Despite still waiting to open his Premier League account after completing a big-money summer switch to Everton, Brennan Johnson could play a leading role in the final third.

While Wales might have clinched a shock 2-1 win when they welcomed Norway to Cardiff for their reverse Nations League showdown last month, the Dragons travel to Oslo on Saturday evening pencilled in as a landslide underdog. Two camps who are facing a real battle in the race to keep hold of their League A status, we look set for what is a must-win battle this weekend. With Norway remarkably losing all of their previous three consecutive Nations League showdowns, Saturday’s hosts will be calling on their all-star attack to spearhead their charge on home soil.

Verdict: Martin Odegaard over 0.5 shots on target
