Why the Women’s World Cup is coming to Scotland at exactly the right time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hot on the heels of the launch of the Scottish government’s ‘Project 2035’ drive to make Scotland a leading force in women’s football comes the news that Scotland will host Women’s World Cup games during that same year.

The tournament, to be jointly staged with England, Northern Ireland and Wales, will see games played at Hampden Park and Easter Road in Edinburgh. So girls dreaming of footballing glory can now add the spine-tingling Hampden roar to imagined wondergoals.

The gap between such reveries and reality usually requires a considerable amount of hard work to bridge. However, in order to achieve a dream, one first needs to have it.

If large numbers of girls take up the sport, it will not only benefit the Scottish national side, but society as a whole. Everyone, no matter their age or gender, should be physically active – humans are simply not designed to exist in the seated position.

If we embrace the idea of being a ‘sporting nation’, we will be both happier and healthier. And that, as much as World Cup glory, is a dream we should all share.
