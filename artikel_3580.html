Grimes Addresses Elon Musk Criticism Over Alleged 'Fixation on Fake Women'

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Grimes is speaking out after publicly opposing her former partner Elon Musk’s well-documented interest in artificial intelligence.

“Super intelligence will hack your limbic system,” Musk, 55, wrote via his X platform on Tuesday, November 17, resharing an announcement about the first AI-generated influencer.

Grimes, 38, responded in the comments section, tweeting, “The fixation on fake women feels like cope.  I’m a fan of Claudia, but she doesn’t have a birkin deal and she isn’t a she, she’s an alien creature who’s both blessed and haunted by the entirety of ancestral memory.“

She continued, “Secualizing [sic] a species that cannot have sex is the ultimate definition of Nietzsche’s last man, and we can do better than reducing ‘it’ to a sex slave.” (For the uninitiated, philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche introduced the concept of “the last man” in 1883, offering a cautionary tale of lazy nihilism in which humans give up on creativity, creation, and the experience of struggle for comfort and and safety.)

While Musk has not publicly addressed Grimes’ public criticism, the singer’s rep offered further insight into her decision to speak out in opposition to the father of her children. (Grimes and Musk share sons X Æ A-Xii, 6, and Techno Mechanicus, 4, as well as daughter Exa Dark Sideræl, 4. (Musk also shares 11 additional kids with three other women.)

“Grimes opposes unelected power, and that includes Mr. Musk’s unelected power,” Grimes’ spokesperson Mitchell Jackson told Us in a statement. “Given Mr. Musk’s power, Grimes believes it’s ridiculous that Mr. Musk behaves this way and spends his free time promoting AI slop while needlessly lowering morale and alienating the people.”

Jackson added, “Grimes is disappointed in the lack of maturity and leadership from the tech giants. She will continue to speak out on this important topic and welcomes the discourse surrounding these tweets. Grimes believes we cannot afford any more of this aggressive nihilism from those in power.  She will continue to speak out about these important issues.”

Grimes is set to further address her tech safety concerns in her forthcoming record, including the newly released single “DeepSeek.”

“DeepSeek’ is about machine welfare,” Jackson noted in his statement. “It’s clear tech leaders do not care about AI safety for humans or machines.”

Grimes and Musk were in a relationship from 2018 to 2022, since going back and forth about coparenting arrangements and social media guidelines for their three children.

“I, unfortunately, do not control the internet and cannot wipe this from the internet. I have tried begging the public and my kids’ dad to keep them offline,” Grimes tweeted in March 2025, claiming she asked Musk not to show their kids’ faces publicly. “The state of my children’s lives being public is of grave concern to me and I think about how to solve this every day. It’s insane to me that there’s no way to deal with this.”

Musk has not addressed Grimes’ concerns at the time of publication.
