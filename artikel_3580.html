Kim Kardashian, Alix Earle and More Stars Stun at 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Kim Kardashian, Alix Earle, Kylie Jenner and more of Hollywood’s biggest stars turned out to support the visual arts at the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala.

The annual event took place at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art on Saturday, November 7, to fundraise and promote the growth of film representation at the museum, which opened its doors in 1965.

The 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala was cochaired by Oscar winner Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow for the 15th consecutive year. Sponsorship was provided by Gucci.

Esteemed visual artist Vija Celmins was honored for her “detailed artworks exploring the beauty and complexity of the natural world” while Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve was celebrated for becoming “one of the most influential directors working today,” per LACMA.
