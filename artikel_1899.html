Rotherham United chief's welcome 'headache' ahead of League Two trip to Grimsby - plus update on duo

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The fact that top-scorer Paul Mullin and defender Dominic Iorfa have presented him with one ahead of this weekend's trip to Grimsby Town is not lost upon him.

The pair were absent in the Millers' last competitive fixture, a much-needed 2-0 FA Cup first-round success at third-tier Bromley on November 7.

The visitors performed admirably in their absence, winning for the first time in 10 matches.

Mullin is back in contention following a bout of flu, while ex-Sheffield Wednesday man Iorfa has served a suspension.

Bruce said: "It's a good headache to have.

"Both players, obviously, give us something."

The Millers treatment room is showing welcome signs of being slightly less congested, with senior striker Tom Bradshaw and Jamaican midfielder Brandon Cover rated as 'touch and go' ahead of the game at Blundell Park, against a Mariners side managed by ex-United defender Dave Artell.

Cover trained on Thursday with Bradshaw due to return to training at Roundwood on Friday.

The pair had been suffering from groin issues, with the problem ensuring that Cover could not link up with Jamaica for their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final double-header with Mexico.

Bruce added: "Brandon nicked his groin a little bit in the game against Bromley. He managed to finish the match. We made the decision - he made the decision as well - that we needed to get him right.

"Tom has also nicked his groin a little bit. He's in the same boat as Brandon. We're hoping that both are going to be all right for the weekend.

Meanwhile, attacker Marvin Kaleta is in contention after missing the last seven games with a hamstring problem.

"Marvin joined in with the group on Monday for the first time," Bruce said.

Bruce's second-from-bottom side are hoping to end a seven-match winless run at league level on Saturday - stretching back to September 19.
