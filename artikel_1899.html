Newcastle make their Lewis Hall stance crystal clear

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Newcastle have made it abundantly clear that they will not allow England international Lewis Hall to leave the club during the January transfer window, according to a report from The Times.

Featuring for England over the past fortnight and appearing to have cemented his spot as Thomas Tuchel’s number one choice at left-back, Hall finds his stock at a red-hot high.

While the Magpies might be searching for some essential Premier League stability on their return from November’s international break this weekend, the 22-year-old has been a standout performer on Tyneside.

Although it might be suggested that Manchester United are keen on reigniting their summer interest when the transfer window reopens, the hierarchy at St. James’ Park have made their stance crystal clear.

The Times reported this week that while Newcastle might be bracing themselves for another pursuit from Man United in January, they will not allow the one-time Chelsea youngster to leave the club under any circumstances.

While Newcastle might have watched the likes of now-former skipper Bruno Guimaraes complete a £75million move to reigning Premier League champions Arsenal over the summer, they have no intention of parting ways with their defensive talisman.

Pledging his future to Newcastle back in September, Hall put pen to paper on a bumper new deal at St. James’ Park that is set to run until 2031.
