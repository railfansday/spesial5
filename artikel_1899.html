Tottenham XI vs Ipswich: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup, injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham are set to welcome back a host of players ahead of their Premier League clash at home to Ipswich this weekend.

Destiny Udogie, Savio, James Maddison, and Lucas Bergvall were all pictured in training at Hotspur Way this week and could now be in contention for minutes on Saturday.

Udogie was left out of Roberto De Zerbi’s squad as Spurs lost 1-0 to Leeds before the international break, though the Italian left-back took to social media to suggest he was not injured.

De Zerbi has previously said that Udogie’s injuries were becoming “problematic”, with the 23-year-old starting just two league games this season.

Savio, meanwhile, is in line to return to action for the first time since October’s 1-1 draw with Manchester United. The Brazilian winger has missed Spurs’ last four league games with a muscular issue.

Spurs sorely lacked a creative threat against Leeds. But despite naming Maddison on the bench at Elland Road, De Zerbi didn't bring him on in search of a goal, raising concerns about his fitness.

Maddison has struggled to train consistently since the start of the season and came off midway through Spurs’ 2-0 win over Chelsea with a suspected groin issue.

The 29-year-old has not featured since that historic victory at Stamford Bridge, despite being available for selection. Still, it is hoped that during the international break he has had time to work on building his fitness.

Bergvall is also expected to be in contention for minutes against Ipswich, having returned from a knee injury.

The Swedish midfielder was not called up for international duty by Graham Potter, but could make his first appearance since the start of October after returning to Spurs training.

Mathys Tel is expected to be out for a significant period with an Achilles injury sustained following a heavy tackle from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle. It is also unclear whether reserve goalkeeper Martin Dubravka has recovered from a knee injury.

Long-term absentee Xavi Simons is ahead of schedule in his recovery from an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury, but remains some time away from a return to match action.

Wilson Odobert also remains out with an ACL injury, but Chelsea loanee Mykhailo Mudryk could return from an ankle ligament injury that has kept him out since September.
