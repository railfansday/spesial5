Roberto De Zerbi speaks out on ‘unlucky’ Savio injury in Tottenham’s Ipswich win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tottenham winger Savio returned from injury on Saturday and almost immediately suffered the same injury that had just kept him out for six weeks.

Spurs beat Ipswich Town 2-0 in north London, thanks to goals from teenager Luca Williams-Barnett on his first Premier League start and Dominic Solanke.

Tottenham climbed to ninth with the win, but there was disappointing news for them as Savio came off the bench in the second half but did not make the final whistle.

The summer-signing from Manchester City had not played since the draw with Manchester United on October 10 and may now face a similar period on the sidelines after a recurrence of the same hamstring problem.

Spurs boss Roberto De Zerbi said: ‘I’m sorry for Savio’s injury, because it was the same injury as last time, and we need him.

‘He’s a crucial player for us. He’s unlucky in this period. We have to be close with him to help him to become the top player we bought at the beginning of this season.’

The 22-year-old joined from Manchester City for an initial £75 million and scored on his debut in a League Cup win over Charlton Athletic, but has not done much of note since then, hampered by injury.

The emergence of Williams-Barnett is much better news for De Zerbi, who has not been shy in talking up the talents of the 18-year-old who has come through the youth ranks at Spurs.

‘Luca is a special player,’ said the Italian. ‘I’m not surprised because his talent is bigger than you think. He played very well. He’s lucky because he’s playing in a top club with top team-mates.

‘I decided to play today with Luca from 10 days and in my plan, it was [James] Maddison and Luca very close to speak the same language, to play together and the experience of Maddison I thought could be important.

‘The behaviour of Maddison could help him to play with freedom. And Thursday, I spoke with Luca and I asked him two things. Just two things. To play like he plays on the park with his friends, with the same freedom.

‘And to play and to work hard without the ball, like [Ousmane] Dembele, like Raphinha, like [Lamine] Yamal, because football is going in a different way and the first defenders are the attackers.

‘Even if they are Ballon d’Or winners, or any way the players with talent, they have to work on the pitch.’

De Zerbi, who was serving a touchline suspension against Ipswich, was asked if Williams-Barnett could reach the levels of the superstars he mentioned and said: ‘Yes, I think so.

‘And one of the best things in my career… I’ve seen today in the bus from the training ground to the stadium, because he was very close with me, while we were coming in the stadium, he was watching on YouTube Ronaldinho, Hazard, Neymar.

‘I thought he was a special guy, because if one hour and thirty minutes before you are debuting in the Premier League, you are watching Ronaldinho, Hazard, Neymar, all these types of talent. The football is magic, no?’

Spurs have now won four of their last five Premier League games and ended Saturday just three points adrift of Bournemouth in fourth.
