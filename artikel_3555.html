The 75-inch Hisense U6SF Pro has dropped to $747.97 from $1,399.99 ahead of Amazon’s Black Friday sale—bringing it to its lowest price to date, according to price trackers. The rest of the U6 Pro lineup is discounted as well, so you can pick up the same TV in sizes from 55 inches to a massive 100 inches, for anyone with the room for something closer to a home-theater-sized screen. Whichever size you choose, you’ll get free delivery to the room of choice—a service that normally costs $19.99. As for the TV itself, it’s a 2026 model that sits below the U7SG (also on sale for $1,297.99, from $1,999.99) in Hisense's lineup as the brand's entry point into Mini LED, pairing a 4K panel and local dimming with a matte, anti-reflective coating meant to cut down on glare, notes this PCMag review.

For a budget set, the U6 Pro gets a lot right when it comes to picture quality—its high peak brightness helps it hold up during a sunny afternoon in a living room with big windows, so a daytime football game or cooking show won’t look washed out, while its excellent contrast and effective local dimming keep dark scenes looking deep when you settle in for a movie at night. It also does a good job cleaning up lower-quality content, which is useful for older cable broadcasts or compressed YouTube videos—and its Fire TV interface makes it easy to jump between those kinds of content and the major streaming services without needing a separate streaming device.

The U6 Pro also brings plenty to the table for gaming, with HDMI 2.1, VRR, ALLM, and a 144Hz refresh rate that PC gamers can push to 240Hz at a lower 1080p resolution. That said, the panel’s slow response time and poor motion performance can reportedly make fast-moving scenes look noticeably blurry. Viewing angles are limited too, with the picture losing quality fairly quickly when you move off-center—and its built-in speakers aren’t particularly impressive either, so a soundbar could be worth budgeting for.
