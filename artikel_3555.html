2 Texas Men Dead After Hostage Situation Ends in Apparent Murder-Suicide

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Two men were found dead inside of a home in Gillespie County, Texas, after a hostage situation resulted in an apparent murder-suicide, according to authorities.

The Gillespie County Sheriff’s Office said in a press release shared via Facebook that deputies responded to a home on Cemetery Road in Stonewall, Texas, shortly after 5 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18, after they received reports of a disturbance that involved a man with a gun.

Deputies began communicating with the victim inside the residence via phone during the incident, and he told deputies that an armed man would not allow him to leave the home.

Because the incident was treated as a hostage situation, deputies made multiple attempts to communicate with both the suspect and victim so that the conflict could hopefully end in a peaceful resolution.

Authorities said they lost communication with the men for several hours. Law enforcement then entered the home around 3:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 19, and they found both men dead in a room.

Investigators ultimately determined that the incident was a murder-suicide involving a 35-year-old suspect and a 49-year-old victim.

The Gillespie County Sheriff’s Office said that they worked on the case with the help of the Kerr County Sheriff’s Office Special Operations Unit, Texas Department of Public Safety Special Operations Unit and a Texas Ranger.

Authorities have not yet released the men’s identities as they await to notify next of kin. It’s not currently clear how the two men knew each other or if authorities believe there was a motive in the case.

“More information will be released as the investigation is completed. We will not release any names pending notification of next of kin,” the sheriff’s office concluded in the press release. “Please keep the families in your thoughts and prayers during this very difficult time.”

The Gillespie County Sheriff’s Office had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.

After the sheriff’s office shared the update, several Facebook users took to the comments section to react to the tragedy. “Oh my gosh, sending prayers for your officers that were involved [it] must feel bad that they weren’t able to save this person. And for all the families affected, and the neighbors will have to continue to remember,” one person commented, and another chimed in, “Such a tragedy. Thankful for all the law enforcement personnel and their attempts at a more positive outcome.”
