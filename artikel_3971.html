Xbox Fans Urge Caution Over New Zealand Trick For GTA 6, While Others Say It's A Non-Issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

"They literally told me this in person in Seattle at Xbox FanFest last week"

Xbox's Aaron Greenberg appears to be joking about this now too.

Original Story: We've already talked a little bit about the New Zealand trick and how it's pretty much the number one topic on social media when it comes to Xbox and GTA 6 right now, so it's naive to think nobody's going to try it this Wednesday.

If you're not in the loop, this refers to people changing their region on Xbox to access GTA 6 at local midnight time in New Zealand, which equates to 6am EST / 11am GMT on Wednesday, November 18th.

News Here's What Xbox Owners Need To Know About 'Moving To New Zealand' For GTA 6

Over the past couple of days, there's been a ton of debate online about whether it's "OK" or "safe" to switch regions in order to play GTA 6 early on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, and if you listen to what the majority say, the answer is that Xbox "don't care" if you do it, and that it's happened on many occasions without bans in the past.

However, it's true that Xbox specifically states in its TOCs that you shouldn't change regions unless you've actually moved to the country in question, and over on X/Twitter today, Windows Central editor Jez Corden and YouTuber Colt Eastwood revealed that they talked about this exact topic during a recent visit to Xbox's headquarters in Seattle.

A lot of people still believe that the risk is extremely low though, with some even arguing that it's "fear mongering" to suggest that Xbox will start suspending or banning people for wanting to play GTA 6 a few hours early.

There are others in the industry, such as The Verge editor Tom Warren, who also believe it's a non-issue:

The reality is that nobody knows for sure whether Xbox will take action against people switching their consoles to New Zealand to play GTA 6 early this Wednesday, but based on historical evidence, it seems pretty unlikely.

Is it worth taking the risk though? That's the bit we'll leave up to you, and speaking personally, I would never want to recommend doing something like this in case Microsoft suddenly decides to throw the ban hammer around.
