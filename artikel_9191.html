As the iPhone Duo faces cracks in the facade, is the Galaxy Z Fold still king?

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

With cracks starting to show with Apple's newest iPhone Duo release, Samsung has swooped in to remind us who got there first in the world of foldable phones. With its Galaxy Z Fold ruling since 2020, it seems Samsung isn't giving up the crown without a fight, and its recent shady ad is proof.

It's no secret that Apple and Samsung are among the brands that fight the most online, so the recent stab at Apple shouldn't exactly come as a surprise. While Apple purists may defend the Duo's screen-cracking debacle, Samsung fans are sitting smugly, knowing the Z Fold has nailed foldable design – but is it enough for Apple fans to convert?

We want to hear your opinion. Is the iPhone Duo here to take over, or has Samsung's legacy secured its place as the foldable phone king? Are you team Apple, team Samsung, or is there another rival brand that deserves the crown? Have your say in the comments below.
