Two of Lakers’ ‘Big 3’ sitting for home game vs. Hornets

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Lakers will be without two of their top three players for Tuesday's home game against the Hornets.

The team's Monday evening injury report revealed that Walker Kessler (sore lower right leg) and Austin Reaves (bruised left heel) will be sidelined for the start of the Lakers' four-game homestand.

Walker Kessler of the Los Angeles Lakers plays against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Kessler's absence will be his first of the season after he played in each of the Lakers' first 14 games, with the team going an NBA-best 12-2 to start the season.

The starting center is averaging a career-high 14.5 points (68% shooting), 10.9 rebounds and 2.2 blocked shots after signing a four-year, $130 million contract with the Lakers in July.

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves shrugs during the first half of an NBA basketball game against the Milwaukee Bucks, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers look on during a game against the Toronto Raptors on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

Tuesday will be Reaves' second absence because of the heel injury he suffered during the Nov. 11 win over the Bucks.

The Lakers will host the Jazz (Wednesday), Kings (Friday) and Jazz (Nov. 23) again as part of their homestand entering Thanksgiving.
