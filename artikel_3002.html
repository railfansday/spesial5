JD.com: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Acquiring CECONOMY gives JD an established European store network to pair with its automated warehousing, a growth path the margin story does not yet show.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 3:22PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 17, 2026, JD.com (JD +2.53%) announced that total revenue returned to positive growth, rising 3.5% year over year to RMB 310 billion (roughly $43 billion). Expanding gross margins and disciplined spending on new initiatives allowed non-GAAP (adjusted non-generally accepted accounting principles) net income to jump 43.7% year over year. The operational shift demonstrates how supply chain scale and marketplace advertising revenue can defend profitability even as domestic consumption slows.

Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term shareholders.

Retail gross margin surpassed 20% for the first time, driven by high-margin third-party marketplace advertising and general merchandise, which grew faster than direct sales. Management reinvested part of this profit surplus into a 45.4% surge in research and development (R&D) expenses for AI-related applications within business operations.

Operating losses in new business initiatives narrowed by 38.7% year over year as management shifted from early-stage volume subsidies to strict unit economics. Food delivery led this disciplined pullback, turning away from heavy promotional spending while maintaining sequential growth in order volume.

Global expansion provides a secondary growth path through Joybuy and cross-border logistics. Acquiring CECONOMY, a European consumer electronics retailer, adds an established offline distribution infrastructure to pair with automated warehousing technology.

Total revenues regained positive momentum across major retail lines while the company deployed 11 new robots across nine production lines. The company relies on its proprietary supply chain capabilities and physical robotics network to defend market share as macro conditions evolve. Management tracks advertising growth and return on investment across artificial intelligence expenditures.

Free cash flow reached RMB 19 billion over the trailing 12 months, providing balance sheet strength for measured overseas expansion. Improving unit economics across food delivery and digital platform services positions the business for durable compound earnings.
