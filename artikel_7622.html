TikTok Shop now has a standalone app in four Southeast Asian markets

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

According to one estimate, the ecommerce market in Southeast Asia will generate $226 billion of gross merchandise value in 2026. TikTok Shop already accounts for a sizable chunk of that GMV, but ByteDance's ecommerce hub is looking to increase the size of its market share. With the launch of a standalone Shop app, TikTok will position itself to compete more directly with retailers like Shein and Temu.

Android and iOS versions of the standalone TikTok Shop app have arrived in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and The Philippines. The app was launched with little fanfare, but was spotted by tech consultant Matt Navarra.
