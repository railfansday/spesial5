Chivu: ‘Embarrassed to be compared to Mourinho, he’s a legend, I’ve only just begun’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cristian Chivu says that it is ‘impossible to not feel moved’ after winning the Panchina d’Oro for the 2025-26 season and says that he is ‘embarrassed’ to be compared to his former boss Jose Mourinho, the last non-Italian to win the award.

Chivu was awarded the Panchina d’Oro at Coverciano on Monday afternoon, a prize that has been handed to the best coach in Serie A every year since the 1990-91 season.

Chivu was announced as the winner for the 2025-26 season on Monday, becoming the third non-Italian coach to win the prize and the first non-Italian to win it since Jose Mourinho in 2009-10.

“Recognition from my peers means twice as much, it’s impossible to not feel moved by it,” said Chivu at the ceremony at Coverciano, which was attended by Football Italia.

“I’d like to thank everyone for their kind words and the value that they have put in our championship-winning season. The credit must also go to the key players: Those who step out onto the pitch and turn our ideas into results, a lot of credit must be given to the players.”

Chivu won the Serie A title and the Coppa Italia in what was his first full season as a head coach of a senior men’s side.

“I think about the opportunity I was given and a club that put its trust in me and gave me a lot of responsibility,” he said. “I think about the excellent work that the lads have put in over the last few years. I strive to maintain a high standard, not just for our club, but for the whole of Italian football.”

Chivu’s old treble-winning head coach Mourinho was the last non-Italian to win the Panchina d’Oro, but the current Nerazzurri head coach does not feel like the comparison is justified just yet.

“Being compared to Mourinho is too much, you’re making me embarrassed! He’s a legend and I have only just begun. This is only my second year in the professional ranks. If I could achieve even a fraction of what he has won, I would be a very happy man, even happier than I am today.”

Inter have been labelled the favourites to go on and win the Serie A title again in 2026-27, but Chivu would like to avoid that pressure if possible.

“I’m not a fan of labels like that. It’s far too easy to talk about Inter as the clear favourite that ‘must’ win every game. We simply need to continue what we have been doing over the last few years: Working hard, delivering results and maintaining consistency.

“It’s a long road, the margin for error is razor-thin. We all know how it goes, a single defeat can feel like a tragedy, but a week with two wins can spark immediate euphoria. We need balance from everyone.”

Inter will next be in action against Atalanta on Sunday, kicking off at 19.45 GMT. So, what does Chivu want to see from his players once domestic football returns?

“I expect us to pick up where we left off: Keeping the right balance, securing results and keeping clean sheets. We also want to show a certain level of aggression and quality on the pitch, making the right decisions in the right situations.

“I expect to see a team that will do everything in its power to get through the demanding run of fixtures between this weekend and December 22.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
