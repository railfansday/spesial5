Chocolate brown has been quite literally everywhere this season. We totally get it; the shade is a great neutral that matches well with tons of items in your closet. But suddenly, we started noticing a new deep-hued color getting some attention, and it gives a pop to wardrobes in a way we didn’t expect. May we introduce you to royal purple.

Deep purple is the new fall 2026 trend that’s making waves in the fashion world. The deep plum shade oozes understated sophistication that makes a nice ‘neutral’ without being too plain Jane. The rich color graced fall/winter 2026 runways over and over, making it hard not to notice. And if the color can make an impact in a fashion show, just imagine what it can do for your wardrobe.
