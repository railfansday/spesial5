Kourtney Kardashian Slams ‘Fabricated’ Rumors That She Doesn’t Live With Travis Barker and Shares ‘Really Vulnerable’ Renovation of Calabasas Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Kourtney Kardashian is setting the record straight on her living arrangements with her husband, Travis Barker.

On the Nov. 5 episode of "The Kardashians," the Poosh founder is seen driving around town with her close friend Simon Huck. She mentions that she received a phone call from her publicist, asking her to comment on previous speculation that Kardashian and the Blink-182 drummer were living separately and planning to split.

Kardashian called the claims "speculated rumors."

"I go, 'Tell them to print it. See what happens,'" she recalled.

"I was just like, 'They know it's not true. They know they saw a dumb TikTok of someone who makes up fabricated stories,'" Kardashian continued.

Huck agreed, noting he often sees "crazy, weird conspiracy theories" about Kardashian and her famous family.

"People believe it," Kardashian said of the gossip, which is sometimes fueled by AI-generated clips. It is "so bizarre."

Moments later, Kardashian, Huck, and her stepson, Landon Barker, pulled up to her longtime Calabasas home, which is undergoing a major renovation.

In the scene, Kardashian said she expected they were about six months away from being able to move back in. But she was beginning to second-guess that timeline after seeing how few changes had been made since her last visit.

"It's a really vulnerable feeling to have all of the walls of your home ripped apart. There's no electrical. There's nothing," she said in a confessional interview during the episode. "It's like a very vulnerable feeling."

While the renovation was in too early a stage to gather much about Kardashian's design plans, she did bring the cameras through a guest room that she said was going to be for Landon, before he moved into a place of his own.

The 23-year-old rapper was quick to pipe up and claim the space, however, saying that he'd love to have his own space because his current residence is a rental.

Barker and Kardashian have a large blended family of seven children. In November 2023, they welcomed a son, Rocky Thirteen Barker. Before their marriage in 2022, Kardashian had three children with her ex Scott Disick: Mason Disick, 16, Penelope Disick, 14, and Reign Disick, 11.

In addition to Landon, Barker and his ex Shanna Moakler are parents to a 20-year-old daughter, Alabama Barker. The musician also has a stepdaughter—Atiana De La Hoya, 27, whom Moakler had with boxer Oscar De La Hoya.

When asked if she was planning to house the couple's entire family under one roof, Kardashian appeared to have mixed feelings.

"I love it in theory, but I think it's really hard with everybody of all different ages," she said. "It's a much different balance."

Although all of the children may not end up living in the home full time, Kardashian confirmed there will be plenty of space for everyone to make memories in one way or another.

"We're working toward something amazing," she said. "I can't wait to cook and have movie nights, barbecues. I want to make it feel magical for everybody. It's so fun."

Kardashian and Barker have previously opened up about the arrangements they've made to suit their large family. In a 2024 episode of "The Kardashians," which was filmed when she was pregnant with Rocky in 2023, she discussed their plans to move into Barker's nearby Calabasas estate.

"We're going to move into his house, which is a block away, and then redo my house at the same time so that we can all be together, living under one roof with the baby," she said.

Barker opened the doors of that very house to Architectural Digest in May 2022, revealing a surprisingly laid-back aesthetic for a rocker whose vibe typically leans much edgier.

"I've had homes with lots of flashy cars, murals, and bikes hanging from the ceiling. But with three children of my own, plus Kourtney's kids, this place felt right for this moment in my life," he said. "I wanted a house where I can rest and enjoy my family, a place where we can create memories."
