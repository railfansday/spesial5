Omar Marmoush hails ‘unbelievable’ Tottenham star as new long-term contract signed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Omar Marmoush has hailed Luca Williams-Barnett as “unbelievable” and believes the teen sensation has a “very promising future ahead of him”.

The 18-year-old scored one, and set up another, on his full debut for Tottenham in their 2-0 home win against Ipswich last weekend.

Williams-Barnett has since signed a new deal to commit his future to Spurs until 2031.

“He is unbelievable,” said Marmoush of the Tottenham academy graduate.

“He is a great, great talent and what I like most about him, of course his talent is something that everyone sees, is that he is very brave, too.

“When he came on in his first game, he just took the ball and tried things. He is very direct, tries to beat his opponent and he has a very, very promising future ahead of him.”

Speaking to Sky Sports, Marmoush continued: “He has got it, and you can see that. One of the big traits of a big talent, and a big player, is the personality and the character and that is what stands out most for me, his courage and to be brave.

“As a young lad to come into Spurs’ first team and perform like that is not easy, and just to believe in yourself, and do the actions on your pitch is great. I hope he is enjoying it, and he will have a very, very big future, that’s for sure.”

Marmoush joined Tottenham on a season-long loan from Manchester City at the close of the summer transfer window, to follow Savio, who completed a permanent £85million transfer, out of the Etihad Stadium.

Reflecting on his move to north London, Marmoush, who has played 11 times so far for Roberto De Zerbi’s improving side, continued: “Spurs are one of the biggest clubs in the world and in England.

“They have a big project and lots of ambitions and they are clear in what direction they want to go, and they are building towards that, and both me and Savio have the same in us – a lot of goals that we want to reach and ambitions, too, so it suits us so well at this club.

“We were convinced by the project and we are very happy to be here.

“The boys are very happy. We are beginning to see the work we have put in come to life. The team spirit is very good right now, and we keep looking forward, and keep working.”

Spurs will be aiming to build on their fourth win in five games – which has lifted them to 10th in the Premier League table – when they take on Sunderland at the Stadium of Light on Sunday.
