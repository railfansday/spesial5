This Pennsylvania Real Estate Brokerage Is Doubling Down for the Changing Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A paper house, money, and hands on a laptop. The concept of buying a home on credit or for cash.

Rather than wait for the market to change, one Pennsylvania brokerage owner is taking steps to compete in the market it has today. Broker/Owner Frank Ramos runs United | Strive 212 in Wyomissing, Pennsylvania & United | Strive 212 Lehigh Valley in Allentown, Pennsylvania. United® Real Estate shares he recently renewed his brokerage affiliation agreement with the firm, continuing the decade-long collaboration to compete within his expanding footprint. Partnering is an especially important competitive strategy in a market where home sales are roughly 25% below 2019’s pre-pandemic level, nationally. One of the nation’s 10 largest brokerages in number of homes sold in 2025, United brings national-scale tools and technology to Ramos and his agents–resources afforded through United’s size and purchasing power, the company says. However, while the scale of a brokerage partner is important, for Ramos, the decision comes down to having the support, relationships and continued innovation to compete through changing market cycles amid skyrocketing prices and a home sales slump nationwide, United notes. That partnership means he can do business without raising his overhead while keeping business costs affordable for his firm’s agents. For brokerage owners across America, the current housing conditions put an even greater premium on efficiency, adaptability and support, United explains. “United gives you the support and resources of a big company, but it still feels personal and customized to your needs,” said Ramos. “You feel valued and appreciated, and you know there are people there who are ready to help you succeed. I like the direction United is going. They keep growing, innovating and rolling out new resources that support my brokerage and my agents.” United Real Estate President Rick Haase says that exchange makes the larger organization stronger. “Markets change. Great brokerage owners adapt, lead and keep finding opportunities for their people,” said Haase. “That’s what we value about Frank and his team. They know their communities, understand what their agents need and generously share what they learn with others in our network. We’re grateful Frank has chosen to continue this partnership and optimistic about what we can accomplish together.” United provides its affiliated brokerages with technology, training, marketing, recruiting resources and brokerage support designed to help owners operate efficiently and profitably through different market cycles. Ramos says the ability to learn from other broker/owners is equally valuable. “One of the best parts of United is the network of other Broker/Owners and Managers,” said Ramos. “At United’s Leadership Summit and throughout the year, I’m able to connect with people who understand the challenges we face, exchange ideas and learn what’s working in their businesses. It’s a collaborative community where people truly want to help each other succeed.” For Ramos, United says renewal ultimately comes down to preparing his brokerage for whatever comes next. “United keeps growing and offering new resources that support my brokerage and my Agents. I want to be with a company that keeps looking ahead and finding new ways to help us succeed.” For more information, visit JoinUnitedRealEstate.com.
