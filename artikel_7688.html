Court will hear arguments in January on six-person juries, religious zoning dispute

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

The Supreme Court will hear oral arguments on Jan. 13 in a Florida man’s challenge to the state’s use of six-person juries; one week later, it will hear a religious zoning dispute between a Catholic church in northern Kentucky and a couple who live across the street and oppose the church’s plans to build a shrine to the Virgin Mary. Those cases, Kian v. Florida and Missionaries of Saint John the Baptist v. Frederic, are among the highlights of the court’s January argument calendar, which was released on Monday afternoon. The justices will hear six arguments over five days, from Jan. 11-13 and again from Jan. 19-20.

Amy Howe is the co-founder of SCOTUSblog and its primary reporter.
