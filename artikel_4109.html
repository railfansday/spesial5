The model will apparently be "hatching soon"

A new Lego Yoshi set is on the way, and based on a few clues, it seems as though it'll be a big one.

Teased via the Nintendo Today app (which provides personalized updates for you based on the games you like), the Lego Yoshi set appears in an extreme close up with the promise that it's "hatching soon." Because this is a for-adults model, it'll be a display piece - presumably a full figure - rather than a playset like the upcoming 2027 range. Some of the best Lego sets so far have been Super Mario collector's items, so the bar is pretty high here.

All we can see of the new kit is its face, with eyes and part of a nose poking out the side of the screen. However, it's possible to guess how large Yoshi will be by the size of the pieces on display... and from what I can tell, it'll be to scale with the Mario and Standard Kart set (which may tell us something about price, because that one's currently $150 on Amazon). The curved pieces with a single stud on the nose are around an inch across at the widest point, for example, so Yoshi's head looks as though it'll be roughly four inches tall. You can then start to work backwards and figure out how big the rest of the model will be.

Using that guestimate and assuming he's standing, I suspect he'll be around a foot or so as a result - e.g. a similar size or just taller than the Lego Piranha Plant set (which used those same curved pieces for its head), but smaller than Lego The Mighty Bowser.

Naturally, all this assumes he isn't on a vehicle like Lego Luigi & Mach 8. If he is, that'll obviously change things because he's sat rather than standing proud. I'm not sure what they'd put him on, but he's often seen on bikes in Mario Kart 8 or the Wild Wiggler kart. Because we've seen a couple of karts already courtesy of Mario and Luigi, I'd quite like to see the franchise's bikes get some love this time around.

First discovered by Vooks, this is a slightly unusual way for Lego to unveil a new set; at the time of writing, you can only find it on the Nintendo Today app. Still, that tease does give us some handy information nonetheless. Judging by the 2027 trademark we know for a fact Lego Yoshi won't be launching this year, for instance.

Even if he doesn't cross the finish line until later, it's still a good time to be a fan of Lego dinosaurs right now; alongside Yoshi, a huge Lego Godzilla kit was revealed last week. While it's changed a lot since the original fan design was submitted, it's still tremendously impressive. Hopefully Yoshi will live up to that.
