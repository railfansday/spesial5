Multiple Games Are Available Today On Xbox, Including One With GTA & Zelda Inspirations (November 18)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Just in case you wanted a break from the GTA news, we thought we'd remind you that we've got a few games either releasing or being added to Xbox Game Pass today, and there's some interesting stuff in the mix here.

The one that catches the eye is Vice: Magic City Mayhem, which we mentioned recently in our article about two Vice City games releasing in mid-November. While the cover art is very GTA-inspired, and it's set in a world with a "Vice City theme", it actually appears to have more in common with classic Zelda in terms of the gameplay style.

It's also a very temporary Xbox and and Epic Games Store exclusive, as the Steam, PS5 and Nintendo Switch versions have been slightly delayed for whatever reason.

News Xbox Suddenly Has Two Vice City Games Releasing In Mid-November

This one's based on Zelda with GTA influences!

Beyond this, three games are being added to the Xbox Game Pass Premium service today, with High on Life 2 arguably being the highlight (if you enjoy the kind of humour it throws at you). The other two are Call of the Elder Gods and Roadside Research, which are both worth a play as well.

GTA 6 is technically already out in some countries as well (such as New Zealand), but its official release date is November 19th rather than the 18th, so we'll highlight it in tomorrow's article instead.

We'll throw all of today's new Xbox releases and Xbox Game Pass additions down below.

Vice: Magic City Mayhem is a top-down 8-bit retro action/adventure game inspired by Zelda with a Vice City theme. Step into the shoes of Dutch, one of Magic City’s most feared enforcers, on a thrilling and dangerous quest to rescue your one true love, Sophie. Navigate through the chaos of wild nightclubs, scenic beaches, and raptor-infested yachts in this raunchy, high-stakes adventure.

Turn-based management roguelike. Command the Inquisition and rule an industrial city on the empire’s edge. Use your train to meet prisoner shipment quotas by hunting heretics and slaying magical beasts. Produce supplies, contain revolt, and sacrifice prisoners to stop the madness.

An intergalactic conspiracy threatens the fate of humanity! Team up with a wide cast of talking alien guns as you shoot, stab, and skate your way through the exotic locales to take down the bad guys and save your favorite species (humans)!

Journey to the far corners of the Earth and unearth ancient horrors in this Lovecraftian narrative puzzle adventure and sequel to 2020’s critically acclaimed Call of the Sea.

Roadside Research is a 1 to 4 player co-op gas station simulator. Except you’re aliens. And undercover. Do very human things like restocking shelves while preparing for the invasion. Just don’t get caught.
