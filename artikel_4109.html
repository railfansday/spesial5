GTA 6: The Festival Is a One-Off Live Music Event, Partly Presented by PlayStation

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Rockstar Games has announced GTA 6: The Festival, a special live music event being held to celebrate the launch of the open world action game.

Presented in partnership with PlayStation, the one-off event takes place in Miami, Florida on 21st November, 2026.

"Experience the music of Vice City and Leonida at Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack."

They will play "nonstop music" across two stages.

If you're interested, you can sign up for the ticket lottery on the official website. The lottery closes at 4pm ET on 10th November, so you don't have long to put your name in the hat.

Ticket prices have not been revealed as yet.

This is just the latest major marketing beat as we lead up to the launch of GTA 6. Most of the bigger events are happening within Miami and Florida, which is of course the real life inspiration for Vice City and Leonida, where the game takes place.
