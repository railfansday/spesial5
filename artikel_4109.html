The Witcher 3 Remastered Ver. 5.02 Update Is Now Live, Here Are The Full Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

CD Projekt Red is still sending out the patches for The Witcher III: Wild Hunt - Remastered, and the latest has gone live today.

The ver. 5.02 update is now available, fixing all manner of issues across gameplay, UI, quests and more. A few of these fixes are specific to issues found on Switch 2 — including increasing the allowed mod size — which is always nice to see.

The full patch notes were shared on the official Witcher website, and we have gathered them together for you to check out below.
