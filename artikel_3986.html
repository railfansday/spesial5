Where To Play Deltarune Artist’s Free New Game ‘Of The World’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While Undertale has already been a viral sensation for years, its sequel Deltarune has somehow been even more viral throughout its numerous chapter releases, with hundreds of thousands of players lining up for each update. While we're still waiting to hear about when Deltarune chapter 6 will release, its creators have just released an entirely new game that you can download and play completely for free right now.

While not associated with Toby Fox's previous work, the new game was created by Deltarune/Undertale artist Temmie Chang, and features music by several other creatives that include none other than Toby Fox himself.

As announced by Temmie herself on X, the free retro indie RPG 'Of The World' features a mix of hand-drawn 2D animated cutscenes and charming top-down spritework for its gameplay and combat, which has already been blowing up massively online since it released with over 100K likes on X alone, and countless glowing reviews.

The best part about the release is you can either play or download it entirely for free from Temmie's official Itch.io page, although you also have the option to pay extra to support her development of future projects and game development outside of Undertale's sequels.

It's recommended to download the game rather than play in the browser, as future updates could delete your cloud saves.

Although Of The World can be enjoyed as a standalone experience, it does take place in the same world as her previous gaming releases, which are similarly free and take only a few hours to complete if you feel like catching up before diving into her newest release.

While Temmie might not get as much attention as Toby Fox himself for her work on the games, a massive chunk of Deltarune and Undertale wouldn't exist without her efforts that have translated excellently into her original releases and stories, which even bear a few resemblances to her previous work with Toby Fox.
