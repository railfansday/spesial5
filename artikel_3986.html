Today Is Your Last Chance to Play Destruction AllStars on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Forgotten car combat game Destruction AllStars has been in some stage of extinction for years now, but if you do have the PS5 game in your library, then today truly is the last day you can play it in any meaningful manner.

From tomorrow, 25th November 2026, the last of its game services will be taken offline.

The multiplayer servers were already permanently pulled in May 2026, and within the next 24 hours, "all remaining game services will be taken offline", per communication from Sony.

Single player modes had still been available to play, but those will be removed too when their servers are taken offline.

Quite literally the only content left in Destruction AllStars after tomorrow is an arcade mode and challenges. Even then, though, Sony warns the "functionality and player experience" tied to that content could be impacted.

Basically, if you want to experience even a sliver of the Destruction AllStars experience, you've only got today left to do it. It's already been delisted from the PS Store, so if you don't have a copy now, you're completely out of luck.

It's not like you'd be missing out on much, to be fair.

Destruction AllStars never made a splash in terms of quality or popularity. While it did have a small playerbase during its days with active servers, the game quickly died out after a February 2021 release.
