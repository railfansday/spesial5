Poll: Is GTA 6 Being Stuck at 30fps on PS5 a Dealbreaker?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Your (potential) nightmare has become a reality: GTA 6 runs at just 30 frames-per-second on PS5 and PS5 Pro.

The target frame rate was confirmed earlier today by Rockstar Games itself, prompting an outpouring of relative disappointment from those who were banking on a silky 60fps experience.

Of course, 30fps isn't the end of the world — as long as it's nice and stable — but there's no getting away from the fact that the vast majority of games on PS5 offer some form of higher frame rate.

Most AAA releases, for example, provide a choice between a 30fps 'quality' mode, which trades frames for increased visual clarity, and a 60fps 'performance' mode, which usually sacrifices resolution for smoother gameplay.

GTA 6 will offer no such compromise, presumably because it's so beefed up in the graphical department.

Indeed, early tech analyses have pointed out that this could be one of the most graphically intensive games ever made; an open world effort that goes beyond anything the genre's seen up until now.

The bottom line here is that you'd like to think Rockstar knows what it's doing. If it had to keep the frame rate capped at 30 to deliver on its vision of a modern Vice City, then that's the long and short of it.

Although having said that, I'm sure there are plenty of people out there who would have happily taken a graphical hit to have GTA 6 run at 60fps. I might be one of them!

But is GTA 6's lack of 60fps a dealbreaker for you? Might you actually avoid playing it now that only 30fps is confirmed? Vote in our polls, and then move at a higher frame rate in the comments section below.
