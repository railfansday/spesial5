Martin O’Neill answers Celtic ‘chucked it’ claims after major team gamble

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Chris Sutton has praised Martin O’Neill for making his critics “eat their words” after Celtic breathed new life into their Europa League campaign.

There was widespread surprise when O’Neill named a much-changed side to face Besiktas, with Camilo Duran and Callum Osmand among those left on the bench.

Writing in his Daily Record column, Sutton admitted he shared some of those concerns but held fire on criticising the Celtic boss. He said: “Martin O’Neill has chucked it. The Celtic team sheet is more like a white flag. He’s given up the ghost in Europe.
