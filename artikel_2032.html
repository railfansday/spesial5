“A better manager now” – Scott Parker verdict from one who knows him best | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With a reputation as being the manager with the specialist knowledge to get West Ham promoted, Scott Parker’s credentials for the West Ham job couldn’t have been better. Three previous successful promotion campaigns with Fulham, Burnley and Bournemouth make him one of the most successful ever, and as a former Hammers’ skipper and HOTY he comes with unique insight and history with West Ham fans of a certain age.

The suggestion abounds, however, from fans of teams he’s successfully steered to promotion, that his is not the most expansive style and that supporters will be bored into submission by his defensive, conservative football.

Parker and Cole actually house – shared as teenagers: Cole is adamant that Parker is the right man for the job

Joe Cole, who knows Parker better than most, has been quick to leap to Parker’s defence on the whufc web site, pointing out that the Head Coach has grown in ability as his managerial career has blossomed:

“I think he’s probably a better manager now than when he first started seven or eight years ago. I think he’s a perfect choice….

“Everything that he stands for as a person is what the Club needs at the moment. You need to get the fans on board. You’re not going to keep everyone happy and there will be people that didn’t want Scotty or people who wanted someone else. That’s completely normal. You don’t have to all agree.

Pointing out that Parker may yet surprise a few with the style of football – and that there’s much more to come, Cole concluded:

“What fans want is to turn up and they know they’ve got a team they can get behind. The first two results weren’t what we wanted, but there have been signs that speak to West Ham fans of the type of football that Scott wants to be played.

“He’s the right man and I’m excited now. I feel like West Ham fans feel excited now that the future looks bright.”
