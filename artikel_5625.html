NEW YORK, November 16, 2026 – CB Insights today named hellocare.ai to its eighth annual Digital Health 50, showcasing the 50 most promising Digital Health startups in the world.

“AI is beginning to change not just how healthcare organizations operate, but how they actually deliver care,” said Ellen Knapp, Lead Healthcare Analyst at CB Insights. “This year’s Digital Health 50 winners are finding new ways to extend clinicians’ reach, simplify how patients access treatment, and deliver more continuous care for complex conditions.”

“Healthcare is entering a new era where AI, virtual care, and connected hospital room technology can work together to support clinicians and improve the patient experience,” said Labinot Bytyqi, Founder and CEO of hellocare.ai. “Being recognized by CB Insights is an important validation of our vision to make intelligent care delivery available across every hospital room and beyond the hospital walls. We are proud of the health systems trusting us to help bring that vision to life.”

The list primarily includes companies using AI to reshape care delivery, streamline healthcare operations, and improve patients’ access to treatment. Our research team picked winning companies based on CB Insights datasets, including deal activity, industry partnerships, team strength, investor strength, employee headcount, and proprietary metrics.

hellocare.ai provides an integrated intelligent hospital room and virtual care delivery platform designed to help health systems modernize care delivery at scale. Its technology brings AI assisted virtual nursing and rounding, telehealth, virtual patient observation, patient engagement, digital whiteboards, digital door signs, and hospital at home capabilities into a unified ecosystem. By combining bedside technology with AI driven clinical workflows and integration into existing hospital systems, hellocare.ai helps care teams extend their reach, enhance patient safety, and improve operational efficiency. The company works with more than 120 health systems and has supported major enterprise deployments, including a rollout spanning more than 13,000 patient rooms at AdventHealth.
