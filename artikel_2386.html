RB Leipzig's Antonio Nusa named among four wingers on Arsenal's January shortlist

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A new report from The Independent has added weight to speculation linking Arsenal with RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa.

According to the report, the Norwegian international features on a four-man shortlist of wingers Arsenal could target in January, alongside Atletico Madrid's Alex Baena, Real Madrid's Endrick and Bournemouth's Junior Kroupi.

A potential sale of Martin Zubimendi could help fund a move for an attacking player. The midfielder has struggled to secure regular minutes in central midfield and has been linked with clubs such as Real Madrid, Atletico Madrid and Chelsea.

However, reports in Germany have suggested that Nusa is highly unlikely to leave RB Leipzig in January, with a move next summer looking far more realistic.

A price tag of around €80 million has recently been reported for the Norwegian international, who has been in blistering form this season, scoring six goals and providing eight assists in 15 appearances so far.
