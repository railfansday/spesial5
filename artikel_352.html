Our Top Tested Picks From Tineco’s Black Friday Sale (2026)

Black Friday is associated with deals on high-end electronics, but it's also one of the best times of the year to pick up some new home essentials. Tineco makes some fantastic cleaning gadgets, and right now, many of our WIRED-tested favorites are on sale for up to 60 percent off. I've rounded up the highlights below, and you can check out the full sale here.

We're covering tons of early Black Friday deals and will be ramping up our deals coverage throughout Cyber Monday.

The Tineco Pure One Station 5 is a very solid cordless vacuum. We like its self-emptying docking station that's easy to set up (no drilling or fancy installation required). There's a handy headlight that makes it easier to spot dirt and debris, and we like the large canister capacity. It performs better on hardwood than on carpet, though. This is the best price we've seen for it.

This carpet cleaner is on the heavy side, but it's one of our favorites, especially with its easy-to-rinse tanks for the dirty and clean water. There's a large LED screen that shows which cleaning mode you're in, and the drying mode gets everything dried off in a flash. It'll pull old stains from your carpet and even comes with an accessory for upholstery.

I haven't tested the newer version of this steam mop yet, but I've been testing models from other brands for an update to that buying guide, and this older Floor One S7 still holds up. It looks great on its docking station, and it cleans my floors efficiently without leaving behind too much water. It does struggle along baseboards, but I truthfully have yet to meet a steam mop that doesn't. It is easy to maneuver with its self-propulsion and also has a handy light that'll show you cleaning progress as you pass over an area. I like the built-in suction too, which means I don't have to use two gadgets to clean one floor.
