The Lego Pokémon Arcanine has hit its lowest-ever price before Black Friday — save $22

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

SAVE OVER $20: As of Nov. 17, the Lego Pokémon Arcanine has hit a new low price of $87.99 at Amazon. That's $22 off its full price of $109.99.

We're just a couple of days away now from Amazon's Black Friday sale, but why wait until then to shop for Lego?

There are already quite a few sets on sale that have stolen our attention, including the adorable Lego Arcanine. As of Nov. 17, Pokémon fans can snag this build for $87.99, the lowest price we've seen it go for at Amazon, according to our research.
