Prediction: Dinamo Zagreb vs Bayer Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Competition: UEFA Europa League Date: Thursday, November 26, 2026 Kickoff: 17:45 GMT / 18:45 CET Venue: Stadion Maksimir, Zagreb

Verdict: Bayer Leverkusen to win Best odds: 73/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet

Jerko Leko has enjoyed a perfect start in the Dinamo Zagreb dugout, but Thursday brings a demanding examination of that early progress. Bayer Leverkusen visits Maksimir after a convincing Bundesliga win, with just two points separating the clubs in the Europa League standings ahead of their first competitive meeting.

Three games, three wins and eight goals have changed the mood around Dinamo. Leko began with a 2-0 victory at Sunderland, followed it with a 3-1 success at Varazdin and then marked his home debut with Sunday’s 3-2 win over Istra 1961.

The club’s report of the Istra match described a performance that could have produced a wider winning margin. Luka Stojkovic, Fran Topic and Dani Rodriguez shared the goals, giving Dinamo several reasons to feel encouraged about its attacking play.

Dion Beljo remains the central threat. The striker has scored 13 league goals and four in the Europa League this season, making him an obvious concern for the visiting defense. Dinamo also has three clean sheets from four European games, although the 4-2 home defeat to NEC Nijmegen showed how quickly that defensive record can unravel.

One detail from Leverkusen’s opponent analysis deserves attention: Dinamo has allowed 57 opposition attempts in the competition, compared with the German side’s 29. Keeping the visitor away from shooting positions will require a considerable improvement on that underlying record.

Scott McKenna and Robert Mudrazija remain unavailable. Niko Galesic and Sergi Dominguez are options in central defense, while Beljo is expected to lead the attack. Stojkovic’s productive weekend strengthens his case for another prominent role behind the striker.

Leverkusen’s 4-0 victory over Paderborn supplied a welcome reminder of its attacking quality. Afonso Moreira scored twice, Patrik Schick and Ezequiel Fernandez also found the net, and Nathan Tella marked his return from injury with two assists.

Schick has now scored nine times in the opening ten Bundesliga matchdays. Moreira’s contribution adds another threat around him, while Malik Tillman also helped create the opening goal against Paderborn. For Carles Martinez Novell, the challenge is to reproduce that fluency against an opponent with far more confidence than it had a month ago.

The Europa League campaign began with three victories and three clean sheets, including a 4-0 win at Lech Poznan. A 1-0 home defeat to Marseille interrupted that sequence, leaving Leverkusen on nine points. A win in Zagreb would restore momentum and create a useful cushion over a nearby rival.

Martin Terrier and Kennet Eichhorn remain absent. Victor Boniface and Tim Oermann are not registered for the Europa League, despite Oermann returning to domestic action against Paderborn. Tella’s successful comeback gives Martinez another attacking option, with Schick expected to start up front.

Dinamo’s revival and Beljo’s form should make this a difficult away assignment. Our preference is still Leverkusen, whose improving attacking options look capable of exploiting the chances the host has allowed in Europe. We expect Dinamo to score, with the visitor edging an entertaining contest 2-1.
