Manchester United: Bruno Fernandes fumes in unseen interview amid new Leny Yoro red card verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bruno Fernandes laid into the standard of Premier League referees after Manchester United were beaten by Liverpool at the weekend.

The Red Devils played most of the match with ten men after Leny Yoro was dismissed for tripping Alexander Isak, who was through on goal, and Dominik Szoboszlai powered home the opener from the resultant free kick.

Further goals from Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz consigned them to a heavy defeat on Mauricio Pochettino’s return to the Premier League, with his new club still mired in the relegation zone.

Fernandes, whose future remains up in the air with his current contract due to expire in the summer, played the full duration at Anfield and cut a frustrated figure at the full-time whistle.

Speaking post-match, he questioned referee Sam Barrott’s decision to dismiss Yoro. Several pundits have questioned whether Isak’s heavy touch had killed his chances of scoring from the one-on-one, with debate raging for days post-match.

“I’m not complaining,” Fernandes caveated, speaking in Portuguese to ESPN Brasil. “The situation is clear from what they explain to us at the start of the season.

“I think we have far too many meetings with referees for them to explain the rules, only for the referees themselves not to know them.

“We have four officials at the game, plus another two on VAR. I understand it’s a difficult situation, but VAR can watch it again and again, with replays, slow motion, everything.

“Isak puts his leg in front of Leny simply to try to win a foul. The ball is knocked forward, towards Senne’s hands. He has absolutely no control of the ball.

“For me, fine, it’s a foul. But you can’t send him off because there’s no control of the ball. It’s not a situation where Isak is going one-on-one with the goalkeeper. So I think the punishment was a bit harsh on Leny.”
