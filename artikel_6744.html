ELLE’s Guide to the Best Black Friday Fashion Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Save on designer bags, holiday party looks, jewelry gifts, and cold-weather staples.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

It’s a great time to love fashion. Hundreds of Black Friday sales have already begun—but with so many places to shop, it can be hard to know where to start (and when to stop). To help you maximize your budget, we’ve detailed the places ELLE editors are shopping this year. From major department stores to designer labels, these are the 20 best sites to browse for Black Friday deals ahead of the holiday weekend, organized alphabetically for your convenience.

Aligne is one of ELLE editors’ go-to fashion labels, so we’ve been monitoring the brand’s site for a Black Friday sale. A handful of early markdowns are already live, but we’re more impressed by the Aligne deals we’re seeing at Nordstrom.

We can confirm Alo will be hosting a Black Friday sale. In years past, the brand has offered a sitewide discount, excluding cashmere and Atelier items. It’s worth browsing Alo’s site now to prepare your cart, as popular workout leggings, sports bras, and jackets will sell out fast.

Amazon’s massive Black Friday sale includes thousands of fashion items, including modern cult classics like Ugg boots, Rag & Bone sweatpant jeans, and Ray-Ban sunglasses. The retailer typically matches prices with department stores and DTC websites, so you can do all your sale shopping in one fell swoop.

Banana Republic shoppers can take an extra 20 percent off existing sale items now through November 22. Hold off on purchasing full-priced products until next week—we expect the brand to offer additional deals closer to the holiday.

Coach has markdowns on all kinds of handbags to gift—and boots, loafers, and leather jackets worth keeping for yourself. Beyond the label’s website, expect Nordstrom, Revolve, and Amazon to have Coach deals, too.

ELLE readers can use the promo code ELLE20 to score 20 percent off all DeMellier bags through November 30. Before you shop, check out our guide to celebrity-worn styles included in the sale.

Dôen fans will have to wait for a Black Friday sale on the celebrity-worn label’s site, but we can confirm the brand will be having one. Until then, you can shop markdowns at Net-a-Porter and Saks Fifth Avenue.

J.Crew has hundreds of markdowns on its site, but we wouldn’t be surprised if the brand offered a sale-on-sale promotion closer to Black Friday. Browse now to get ideas, but wait until after the holiday to finalize your cart.

Lululemon already kicked off its Black Friday event—but the brand rarely hosts sales, so deals will sell out fast (especially on neutral colors like black). Expect new markdowns to be added during the holiday weekend.

Lululemon Wunder Train High-Rise Tight 28”

Macy’s shoppers don’t need to wait. The department store started its Black Friday sale early. Watch for daily deals with even bigger savings on holiday gifts and cold-weather fashion staples.

The editor-loved, celebrity-approved footwear brand Margaux is hosting its only sitewide sale of the year. We’ll be shopping boots and flats while they’re 20 percent off through November 30.

Those looking for jewelry gifts should take advantage of Mejuri’s sitewide Black Friday sale, which includes natural and lab-grown white sapphires and diamonds for up to 30 percent off. Items return to full price on November 30.

Mejuri Lab-Grown White Sapphire Tennis Bracelet

Mejuri Multi-Station Natural Diamond Necklace

If you have a wedding or special event on the calendar, Meshki’s sitewide Black Friday sale is a great place to shop. You should also look for holiday party and New Year’s Eve outfits while everything from the Australian label is 30 percent off.

Mytheresa is one of the best destinations for luxury fashion deals. Entering Black Friday and Cyber Monday, the retailer has markdowns on Toteme, Victoria Beckham, Gucci, Fendi, Valentino Garavani, and other sought-after designer labels. For a limited time, enjoy 20 percent off purchases over $350.

Valentino Garavani Vain Small V-Logo Shoulder Bag

Now is a great time to shop on Net-a-Porter. The luxury retailer has thousands of markdowns on designer bags, shoes, and clothing to browse during your free time. Chloé, The Row, Tom Ford, and other trending labels are up to 60 percent off, but stock is extremely limited, so you’ll need to hurry.

Willy Chavarria Checked Belted Trench Coat

Nordstrom’s Black Friday sale is one editors never miss. This year, the retailer kicked things off early by offering over 130,000 early fashion markdowns. You can already save hundreds on covetable designer sunglasses and handbags that make for easy holiday gifts.

Favorite Daughter Metropolitan Wool & Cashmere Coat

Our inside sources at Reformation confirm the brand’s Black Friday sale will be massive this year. If you’re itching to shop Reformation deals now, head to Nordstrom, Shopbop, and Saks Fifth Avenue.

Revolve might not have kicked off its official Black Friday sale, but the retailer already has close to 50,000 items in its sale section. No matter the time of year, the site is where editors go for discounts on cool-girl labels like Verafied, Helsa, HOMMEgirls, 437, Guest In Residence, Cou Cou Intimates, and Frankies Bikinis.

Those who praise high-quality basics should peruse The Park’s seasonal sale. The minimalist label is offering 30 percent off tees, dresses, skirts, and tanks that make building a capsule wardrobe a breeze.

Amazon, Nordstrom, and other major retailers have deals on Tory Burch bags, shoes, and accessories, but the designer’s website is still the best place for markdowns on clothing. Right now, you can save 30 percent on all purchases over $250 by going straight to the source.
