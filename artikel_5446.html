Siemens Energy is targeting a 20% to 22% profit margin by 2030 after a record year

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The company proposed a €2.30 dividend and plans to buy back up to €9 billion more stock; its fiscal 2026 margin was 12.4%

Siemens Energy reported a record fiscal 2026 on Wednesday and set a target of a 20% to 22% profit margin before special items for fiscal 2030, the company said. It also aims to grow revenue by 12% to 14% a year through 2030.

Revenue grew 15.5% on a comparable basis to €44.2 billion ($49.5 billion) in the fiscal year that ended Sept. 30. Profit before special items reached €5.5 billion, and the margin rose to 12.4% from 6.0% a year earlier. Net income was over €4.1 billion, compared with €1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. In the fourth quarter, net income was €1.36 billion, up from €236 million a year earlier.

Wind unit Siemens Gamesa reached break-even for the year, with profit before special items of €8 million, compared with a loss of €1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Orders rose 18.8% on a comparable basis to €68.1 billion, and the order backlog reached a record €164 billion. Free cash flow before tax came in at €8.8 billion, above the company's target of around €8 billion.

"We delivered on our commitments: We met all raised guidance targets, significantly increased earnings and achieved break-even in our wind business," CEO Christian Bruch said in a statement. "Our 2030 targets set a clear direction and underline our level of ambition."

The company will propose a dividend of €2.30 per share for fiscal 2026, compared with €0.70 per share for fiscal 2025. It intends to devote as much as another €9 billion to buybacks of its own stock by the close of fiscal 2030. Shareholder returns for fiscal 2026, including buybacks, total €5 billion.

For fiscal 2027, Siemens Energy expects comparable revenue growth of 14% to 16% and a profit margin before special items of 14% to 16%. It forecasts net income of around €5 billion and free cash flow before tax of around €10 billion.

Siemens Energy stock rose 7.7% to €129 in European morning trade on Wednesday. Both the 2027 and 2030 guidance exceeded market expectations, according to The Wall Street Journal.
