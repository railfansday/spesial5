NatGasHub.com Ranked Number 182 Fastest-Growing Company in North America on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Attributes 391% Revenue Growth to widespread adoption of its natural gas multi-pipeline software platform.

HOUSTON, Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NatGasHub.com today announced that it ranked number 182 on the Deloitte Technology Fast 500â¢, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing tech companies in North America, now in its 32nd year. NatGasHub.com grew 391% during 2022 to 2025.

NatGasHub.com's chief executive officer, Jay Bhatty, credits demand for natural gas to power AI data centers and increased natural gas consumption in North America with the company's 391% revenue growth. He said, "Natural gas is powering the AI revolution, and our industry needs technology that can keep pace. We're proud to automate manual workflows, and grateful to our clients and team for driving this growth."

"Nearly 70% of this year's award winners returned as repeat honorees, reflecting the power of disciplined and sustained execution. More than 30% made the list for the first time, illustrating how quickly new players are shaping their respective sectors. Furthermore, private companies represented almost 80% of the list, a clear indication that the agility and strategic focus of private enterprises remain essential to scaling in competitive markets."

Technology Fast 500 award winners are selected based on percentage fiscal year revenue growth from 2022 to 2025.

In order to be eligible for Technology Fast 500 recognition, companies must own proprietary intellectual property or proprietary technology that significantly contributes to the company's operating revenues. Companies must have base-year (fiscal year 2022) operating revenues of at least US$50,000, and current-year (fiscal year 2025) operating revenues of at least US$5 million, with a growth rate of 50% or greater. Additionally, companies must be in business for a minimum of four years and be headquartered within North America (United States and Canada).
