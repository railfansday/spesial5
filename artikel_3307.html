Apple has renewed Slow Horses for seasons 7, 8, 9, and 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Slow Horses isn’t going away anytime soon, as Apple TV confirmed today that the hit series has been renewed through season 10—meaning at least four more seasons are coming.

The era of streaming TV has often been marked by shows being canceled too soon, and taking long breaks between seasons.

Slow Horses is a stark exception on both fronts. The British spy series premiered in 2022, and Apple TV recently finished airing its sixth season.

Apple has made a practice of ordering more Slow Horses seasons far sooner than most shows get renewed. Viewers now expect that at the end of one season, a trailer for the next season will debut.

But today Apple has extended Slow Horses far beyond what’s typical. Via Deadline, the company confirmed that Slow Horses has been renewed for seasons 9 and 10.

The latest approvals join the already-approved seasons 7 and 8.

It’s very rare for any show to run for 10 or more seasons. But it’s perhaps even harder to find a series that’s picked up for four additional seasons in advance.

Slow Horses season 6 wrapped up last month, and now fans know that there are at least four more seasons coming—likely with the same annual premiere cadence.
