‘GTA VI’ Is Finally Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Buckle up, and try not to get carjacked. After 13 years and back-to-back delays, the excruciating wait for Grand Theft Auto VI has finally come to an end. As the clock struck midnight on Nov. 19, the most anticipated video game of all time officially launched, opening up the neon-soaked streets of Vice City for millions of players on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

By the way, that “millions” isn’t an understatement. With pre-orders live since June, and the ability to pre-download the game on Nov. 10 driving a surge in early purchases, it’s estimated that over 17 million copies sold prior to launch day. Potentially raking in over $1.6 billion before it’s even available to play, that puts GTA VI over halfway to the lifetime grosses of blockbuster movies like Avengers: Endgame and Avatar (both over $2.9 billion) — well on track to fulfill the expectation of being the largest single media launch ever.

And although it’s already raking in cash for the game’s publisher, Take-Two Interactive, the launch of GTA VI and the subsequent days to follow could potentially be some of the least productive for companies outside of major holidays. A recent IGN poll shows thousands of fans are planning to take paid time-off or sick days to play the game, something Take-Two CEO, Strauss Zelnick, joked about back in April. But it’s more than just a meme on social media; major corporations like Logitech have actually declared the release date a company-wide holiday.

But if you’re not one of the early adopters, or are on the fence about diving into the zeitgeist, there’s plenty of information already available about what to expect from gaming’s once-white whale.

Like its predecessors, GTA VI is an open-world crime odyssey where players take on the role of fledgling gangsters looking to make their mark on the underworld. As with most mainline entries, the characters and plot are mostly independent from previous installments, but it’s still set in the same satirical take on the U.S.. This time around, the primary setting is in the fictional state of Leonida — a stand-in for Florida — and includes a modern version of the Miami-inspired locale of Vice City. This new installment is unique in that it follows two protagonists, the Bonnie and Clyde-like duo of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. The latter is a big deal for the franchise, marking the first female protagonist for the series. The story begins with Lucia being newly released from prison, reuniting with her paramour to stumble back into a life of crime. Early details indicate that Jason and Lucia’s relationship is the beating heart of GTA VI, and players’ decisions can heavily impact how the two interact. Maintaining a good standing could be critical to surviving the mean streets of Vice City over the course of the game’s runtime, reportedly spanning anywhere from 70 to over 100 hours.

Right now! The game has been available to download for pre-orders since Nov 10., and has been fully available since midnight eastern time in North America. The North America part is key, too. Instead of having a unified global release, GTA VI’s rolling launch granted early access to players worldwide at 12 a.m. local time by region, meaning users in Oceania and Asia got nearly a full day’s headstart ahead of their U.S. counterparts. To get in completely blind, it’s best to avoid social media. Spoilers are already circulating online.

Like its 2013 predecessor, the new GTA is starting its life cycle as a timed console exclusive, currently available for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. As expected, the divide between PlayStation and Xbox players leans heavily toward the former, with approximately 75 percent of users being on PS5. PC gamers have once been left out in the cold, with no release date currently set outside of consoles. Sadly, Nintendo’s Switch 2 has also been snubbed, despite its rapidly expanding audience driving mature games like Call of Duty back to the platform after a yearslong hiatus. The Standard Edition of GTA VI costs $79.99, which is technically the base game, plus some extra cosmetic goodies if you’ve pre-ordered. But there’s a catch. Unlike most standard editions which can be upgraded to premium for extra content down the line, the core version simply cuts off access to in-game stores and accessories in the main game. Virtual locations like Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor and One-Eyed Willie’s Mod Shop still exist, but their doors are closed without purchasing the Ultimate Edition ($99.99) — which really makes a hundred bucks the real cost to play GTA VI in its entirety. In fact, the Ultimate Edition is the one immediately offered on digital storefronts, with players needing to toggle to the cheaper one to find it.

On that note, a digital storefront on PlayStation or Xbox will be an essential part of accessing GTA VI. Although you can buy a “physical” box in stores, the purchase doesn’t come with a disc. Instead, in-store versions come with vouchers for a digital download, meaning it’s indistinguishable from buying it on your console. It also means you’ll need to set aside both over 210GB of space on your device to download it, as well as the time to do it. If your internet connection isn’t up to snuff, you could be waiting a few hours after buying the game before you can play it.

There’s no overwhelming benefit to playing on any specific platform, but there are some differences depending on which console and model you own. On both PS5 and Xbox Series X, the game’s visuals target 30 frames-per-second, which is the baseline for modern AAA releases. Although many newer releases get heavily upgraded fidelity or performance on the more expensive PS5 Pro, GTA VI offers slightly enhanced graphics, including better lighting effects and exclusive ray-tracing shadows, with the device’s PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) improving overall image quality. Both base PS5 and Pro consoles will include haptic feedback integration on DualSense controllers, and spatial audio via Sony’s Tempest 3D technology.

The Xbox Series X appears to run the game just as well as the base PS5, although GTA VI will also be accessible to stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming, meaning it can even be played on the last-gen Xbox One. The caveat here is that Xbox Cloud Gaming caps streaming time based on your subscription plan (15 hours per month at the highest tier), meaning you won’t get much time in-game if it’s not downloaded to your device. Rockstar also says that the cloud version is based on the specs of the Series S, implying the more affordable of Xbox’s two current consoles has weaker performance.

One thing you won’t be doing is playing GTA VI online with friends. At launch, the new game is strictly a single-player only experience, with a multiplayer update expected to come down the line. According to reviews, yes. The buzz thus far has been enormous, with Rockstar Games’ record for acclaimed games fully intact. GTA VI currently has a Metacritic score of 99, making it the highest-rated video game of all time. On top of that, Grand Theft Auto VI has already received eight nominations at The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year — announced two full days before 99.9 percent of people have even played it.

So, if all of this sounds like your jam, it’s time to put life on pause and dive into the criminal underworld, lest you be left behind.
