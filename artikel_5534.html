Kraft Heinz is raising its annual sales forecast after beating earnings expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Kraft Heinz expects 2026 organic net sales to fall 0.5% to 1.25%, narrowing a prior forecast of a 0.5% to 2% decline

Kraft Heinz $KHC raised the low end of its 2026 organic net sales forecast on Wednesday as it reported third-quarter results with lower sales and profit.

The company now projects a full-year organic net sales decline of 0.5% to 1.25%, an improvement on the earlier outlook of a 0.5% to 2% drop. The outlook includes an approximate 100 basis point impact from incremental SNAP headwinds, the company said.

Kraft Heinz raised its adjusted earnings per share forecast to a range of $2.07 to $2.13, from $2.03 to $2.09. It lowered its expected effective tax rate on adjusted earnings to about 23.5% from about 24.5%. Its forecast for constant currency adjusted operating income, a decline of 16% to 18%, was unchanged.

Net sales fell 2.0% to $6.1 billion in the third quarter, and organic net sales fell 1.1%. Net income attributable to common shareholders was $544 million, or 46 cents a diluted share, compared with $615 million, or 52 cents, a year earlier. Adjusted EPS was 53 cents, down from 61 cents. Operating income fell 14.6% to $0.9 billion.

Analysts expected adjusted earnings of 42 cents a share, according to The Wall Street Journal. Eight analysts surveyed by Zacks Investment Research expected 43 cents, according to The Associated Press. Analysts expected revenue of $6.09 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Kraft Heinz stock rose 3.1% in premarket trading.

By region, North America net sales fell 3.4% and Europe and Pacific Developed Markets net sales fell 5.6%. Emerging Markets net sales rose 13.8%.

Volume and mix fell 1.1 percentage points. The company cited declines in North America across meats, sauces and refreshment beverages, along with an approximate 80 basis point headwind from inventory pull forward into the second quarter. Price was flat.

Higher procurement and logistics costs, higher variable compensation expense and increased advertising expenses weighed on operating income, the company said.

"Our 2026 operating plan to turn around the U.S. business and accelerate growth internationally is on track," CEO Steve Cahillane said in a statement. "Relative to last year, our market share, sales, and volume trends are improving in every region."

Cahillane said the company is raising the low end of its organic net sales outlook to reflect the momentum it has built.

Kraft Heinz is holding a live question-and-answer session at 9 a.m. ET.
