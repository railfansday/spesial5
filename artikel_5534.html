Crypto and Midterm Election: 3 Risks From Trump’s $5,000 Stimulus to Iran

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Crypto is facing a major political test as Americans head to the polls on November 3, with the US midterm election potentially reshaping regulation, stimulus expectations, and investor risk appetite.

Republicans are fighting to retain control of Congress, while Democrats are seeking to regain power in at least one chamber. The outcome could determine if President Donald Trump advances his economic agenda or faces a tougher legislative road ahead.

For crypto, the midterm election comes with three major risks: Trump’s proposed $5,000 stimulus dividend, the future of stalled crypto legislation, and escalating tensions with Iran.

The election itself may not immediately change Crypto’s fundamentals. However, it could shift expectations around liquidity, regulation, and the financial markets.

Trump has promised a $5,000 dividend payment to adult US citizens if Republicans retain control of both the House and Senate. The proposal would cover 240 million adults, potentially costing around $1.2 trillion, and Trump has also suggested that tariff revenue could help fund the payments.

A Republican victory could then strengthen expectations for additional household spending and potentially inject fresh liquidity into financial markets. We, in the crypto market, have historical reasons to watch stimulus closely.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, US stimulus checks coincided with extraordinary monetary easing and a powerful crypto rally. A Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland study found that the 2020 payments increased Bitcoin buy volume by 3.8%, while the associated price effect was modest.

The 2020–2021 rally also benefited from low interest rates, institutional adoption, and abundant liquidity. Hence, a new stimulus program could encourage retail investors to allocate more money toward Bitcoin and other risk assets.

However, the proposed dividend is not approved, and higher inflation expectations or Treasury yields could offset some of the bullish impact. If Democrats win control of Congress, Trump’s proposal could face a much tougher path toward approval.

The Midterm election could also determine whether lawmakers make progress on the most-awaited crypto regulation, the Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act. It aims to establish clearer rules governing digital assets and the division of responsibilities between US regulators.

The bill remains stalled after failing to advance through a Senate procedural vote in September. A Republican victory could improve its chances, although bipartisan support would still be necessary to overcome legislative hurdles.

A Democratic victory, particularly if the party takes control of the House, could bring increased scrutiny of Trump’s crypto-related ventures and campaign connections. That could delay legislation as lawmakers focus on investigations and oversight.

Still, a Democratic win would not automatically mean tougher crypto regulation. Bipartisan support for clearer digital asset rules remains possible, although the final legislation could look different from the current proposal.

The Midterm election presents a mixed outlook for crypto. A Republican victory combined with stimulus optimism, progress on crypto legislation, and Iran de-escalation would create a potentially bullish environment.

A Democratic victory could introduce regulatory uncertainty, while renewed conflict in the Middle East could pressure crypto regardless of the midterm election result. For us, the key question is whether political optimism can outweigh geopolitical risk.

Trump’s stimulus promise may offer a powerful bullish narrative, but crypto’s next major move could ultimately depend on improving liquidity or another inflationary shock.

The biggest immediate threat to crypto is not the midterm election, and it may come from outside Washington. The US carried out limited strikes on Iran’s Qeshm islands near the Strait of Hormuz. This revived concerns about escalation, even as diplomatic contacts continued.
