New Jersey-based Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group Select Oracle Health's New AI-Powered EHR to Create a More Connected Experience for Patients and Care Teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Technology will unify Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group to streamline care, improve communication, and enhance the patient experience

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group (Shore) is expanding its longstanding relationship with Oracle Health. The healthcare organization will adopt a unified, AI-powered electronic health record (EHR) system designed to create a more connected healthcare experience for patients, providers, and staff across Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group. By implementing one integrated Oracle Health system across hospital, outpatient rehabilitation, urgent care, primary care, and specialty practices, Shore aims to enhance care coordination, simplify workflows for clinicians, and create a foundation that makes it easier for the health system to adopt future technology and innovations. In addition, Shore will support patients with one convenient portal to access their health information and connect with their care teams.

Shore Medical Center has been an independent, nonprofit community hospital serving residents of South Jersey and visitors to the Jersey shore for more than 85 years. After many years of using Oracle Health technology within its hospital operations, including Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications, the healthcare organization will now expand and standardize Oracle Health clinical solutions across Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group. This will create a seamless experience across all settings of care, access to more complete documentation to aid in clinical decision-making, and support more efficient clinical and financial operations. Adopting AI-powered solutions will enable Shore to support clinicians in reducing cognitive and administrative burdens, allowing them to be more present during patient visits. Shore anticipates the implementation project will be completed by December 2027.

"We are making this significant investment in technology to enable our patients to continue to have the very best experience possible right here in our community," said David R. Hughes, Chief Executive Officer and President of Shore Medical Center. "By bringing Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group together on one integrated Oracle Health platform, we will create a seamless experience for patients with one patient portal and improved communication across every point of care."

Hughes added, "This technology will also help support our physicians, nurses, and staff by giving them easier access to the information they need so they can stay focused on delivering exceptional care. We are proud to continue our strong collaboration with Oracle Health and excited about the opportunities this innovation will bring to our community."

Shore plans to adopt the new AI-powered Oracle Health EHR to help streamline clinical workflows and decision support across the organization. Oracle Health's voice-first EHR employs native AI to surface information at the point of care to help alleviate administrative burdens for clinicians, enhance patient care, and improve efficiencies. Instead of navigating multiple screens and workflows, Shore's clinicians will be able to use voice commands to quickly access important patient information, such as recent lab results and current medications. By bringing relevant information into the flow of the visit, Oracle Health EHR helps clinicians make timely, well-informed decisions while remaining focused on their patients. Personalized workflows tailored to individual clinician preferences will also help Shore's care teams work more efficiently and effectively.

In addition, Shore will expand its use of Oracle Health AI Agents across more providers to help significantly reduce manual documentation time by automatically creating draft notes based on patient-clinician conversations. By using this solution during appointments, clinical notes can be drafted and available for the clinician to review within seconds after the visit. Shore will also adopt AI-powered order creation capabilities and anticipates integrating additional AI tools into workflows as they become available.

Shore also plans to implement the new Oracle Health Patient Portal to create a more convenient and connected experience for patients across Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group. Instead of navigating multiple systems, patients will have one portal where they can access medical records, communicate with providers, review test results, manage appointments, and stay informed about their care. The portal's built-in AI capabilities will also help explain medical information in plain language, making it easier for patients and families to better understand diagnoses, treatment options, and next steps in care.

Finally, by adding Oracle Health Patient Accounting to modernize financial workflows, Shore can create a more seamless patient financial experience. The solution uses AI and automation capabilities to help streamline workflows, strengthen operational insights, and support financial and operational strategies across the organization.

"Shore Medical Center and Shore Physicians Group are raising the bar for how health systems can use AI to improve care delivery," said Seema Verma, executive vice president and general manager, Oracle Health and Life Sciences. "By standardizing on Oracle Health and expanding AI across its clinical and operational workflows, Shore will reduce complexity, help ease administrative burdens for clinicians, and deliver a connected, modern experience for patients and providers."

Shore signed this agreement in February 2026.

Learn more about how Oracle is helping customers build open and connected health systems at www.oracle.com/health.
