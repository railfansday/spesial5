All Guardian Boss Lairs and rewards in The Division 2 Echoes of Central Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Echoes of Central Park DLC has modified the endgame experience of The Division 2 with the introduction of the Guardian bosses. Once you’re done with the campaign, you’ll find the mention of five bosses who are located all around the new map.

Once you reach the conclusion of the main story of Central Park, you’ll get to learn more about the Guardians. All five of them used to be SHD Agents, but consumption of something nefarious turned them into monstrosities.

The locations of all five Guardians are highlighted on the image above. You can always find them on the map (as long as you have completed the story).

The table below has information about all the Signature Skills and abilities of all five Guardians.

The Echoes of Central Park DLC offers exclusive skills that will drop when you defeat these Guardians. There are six new variants to obtain by defeating them.

Additionally, you can also get the new Apex Protocol gear pieces. The pieces drop randomly, so you’ll have to kill the Guardians a fair number of times to complete the full set.
