The Best Black Friday Meal Kit Deals Offer Deep Discounts

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It’s no surprise that BA editors love to cook, and considering you’re on our site right now, we’re willing to guess you do too. But life can get hectic, especially in the lead-up to the end of the year. That’s where meal kits come in: Think of them as a dinnertime helping hand—everything you need to get a hot meal on the table is delivered in a single box. And during the Black Friday sales bonanza, many of these services are available at a discount.

Some of the best meal kits according to our years of extensive testing—including the best affordable meal kits and some of our favorite meal kits for families—have Cyber Week offers running. Each of these services offers a weekly menu (so you can take a break from meal-planning), and includes options for a range of dietary needs, whether you're following a low-carb, gluten-free, dairy-free, Paleo, or pescatarian eating style. And if fully-prepared heat-and-eat meals are more your speed, we found deals on those, too.

Want excellent, chef-designed prepared meals delivered to your door? One of our absolute favorite meal delivery services is CookUnity. It specializes in pre-cooked meals that you heat in the oven or microwave when you're ready to eat. But these aren't your average re-heatable dinners. All the dishes are unique and made by chefs across the country—think recipes like Desi Butter Chicken with Cauliflower Mash and Roasted Peri-Peri Chicken with Harissa Brussels Sprouts.

If you're already salivating, trust us—we're right there with you. You can satiate the craving at a discount this Black Friday with a whole 50% off your first order. Just use code BONAPPCU50.

HelloFresh offers great portion sizes, easy-to-cook meals, and over 100 weekly menu and market items to choose from. It's great for single- or dual-person households, and people who can't commit to using a whole bulb of garlic with pre-portioned ingredients and super clear steps. Through Cyber Monday, you can get 10 free meals and a free Staub ceramic baking dish (which will come with your third box) to help make dinner prep even easier.

Green Chef sets itself apart by offering organic ingredients and sticking to a sustainability pledge that has the brand offsetting its delivery emissions and plastic use. For Black Friday/Cyber Monday, they’re offering 50% off your first box and 20% off your next two months of orders.

You can’t go wrong with a meal kit like Blue Apron, one of the OGs in the space. The promise is similar to HelloFresh: pre-portioned ingredients and straightforward instructions that'll have you making healthy and delicious meals at home with ease.

Purple Carrot is a fully plant-based meal kit delivery service that offers both premade meals and meal kits for you to prep. Multiple BA staffers and contributors have tried it many times throughout the years, and it's consistently fared well in our product tests. Proof? This review from contributor Carina Finn: “I never missed the meat in a Purple Carrot meal. I even found myself getting legitimately excited about my “healthy dinner at home” nights—especially since I didn’t have to grocery shop.”

For Black Friday and Cyber Monday, they’re offering new customers 50% off their order plus free shipping. Use code 50FORME to redeem the discount, which is valid on all orders over $65.

If you love cooking but don't have time for all of the planning, prep, and shopping required to put together satisfying home-cooked meals, try Marley Spoon. The service uses recipes from Martha Stewart herself and still requires you to do at least some of the chopping and dicing to making your meal. The recipes even recommend a few cooking tricks to use in your everyday life.

Parents and caregivers who want to skimp on shopping and prep without skimping on nutrition will love Little Spoon—a meal kit specifically for feeding babies, toddlers, and little kids. The service will ship ready-to-heat-and-eat meals (and snacks!) that are chef- and dietician-approved. For Black Friday, save 60% on your first order with the code EARLYBIRD60 through 11/26, and 65% on your first order with code SAVEBIG65 from 11/27–12/01.
