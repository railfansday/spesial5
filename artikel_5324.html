Disney is folding Hulu into Disney+ for 4 reasons, and the app's end is unconfirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Disney is folding Hulu into Disney+ in December 2026. Four reasons explain the move, and the standalone Hulu app's fate is unconfirmed.

Anyone who pays for more than one streaming service knows the routine of opening one app, scrolling its menus and then opening another to find a show. The companies behind those apps are now combining them. Disney $DIS wants viewers to browse Hulu and Disney+ programming without switching apps, and its rivals are doing the same: HBO Max and Paramount $PARA+ plan a joint discounted bundle for early 2027, according to LEVEL UP. Media companies once competed by launching more services, and they are now competing to keep viewers inside fewer of them. That change raises a practical question for anyone with a Hulu login, who wants to know what it means for the service they already pay for.

Hulu's shows are not going away, only the separate place to watch them. So far the merger changes the screen, not the bill. The premium bundle of Disney+ and Hulu currently costs $21.99 a month, according to LEVEL UP, and Disney has announced no price increases tied to the integration.

Folding Hulu into Disney $DIS+ matters to viewers only if they can find more in one app than they could in two, so discovery is the test of the whole merger. Disney said in its Nov. 17 earnings update that its goal is "creating a more seamless way to discover and engage with the breadth of content across our brands and franchises." For a customer, that means looking for a show from any Disney brand or franchise in one place. The rollout is taking place in the United States, according to LEVEL UP, and the people who gain the most are U.S. viewers who already pay for both services and switch between two apps. For someone who pays for neither, it offers little new reason to sign up.

Disney $DIS describes its absorption of Hulu as almost finished, and the last features to move are live TV and premium add-ons, the services that most set Hulu apart. "Our app unification of Disney+ and Hulu is nearly complete," Disney said in its Nov. 17 earnings update. Live TV and add-ons join a fully unified Disney+ experience in December 2026, the final stage of a rollout Disney announced in 2023, according to Parade. A single search is only as useful as the catalog it covers, and the Disney+ catalog does not yet include all of Hulu. As long as those gaps remain, Hulu's separate app still has a use, and finding everything in one place is a goal rather than a description of the product Disney sells today.

Disney presents that result as a gradual expectation, not as something it has already achieved. "We believe our continued focus on product and technology will yield further benefits in engagement and churn over time," Disney said in its Nov. 17 earnings update. Disney has attached no target to that gain, so retention, the reason it most needs to justify the merger, is also the one it cannot yet demonstrate.

Their answer is what Disney is betting on, and the company has not seen it yet.
