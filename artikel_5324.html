Tuya Reports Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results and Declaration of Cash Dividend

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

SANTA CLARA, Calif., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Tuya Inc. ("Tuya" or the "Company") (NYSE: TUYA; HKEX: 2391), a global leading AI cloud platform service provider, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and the declaration of a cash dividend.

For further information on the non-GAAP financial measures presented above, see the section headed "Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures."

Mr. Xueji (Jerry) Wang, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Tuya, commented, "In the third quarter, opportunities and challenges coexisted in the global consumer electronics industry. On the one hand, AI-native hardware continued to move from concept toward deployment at scale. New categories such as companion robots and AI toys rose rapidly, becoming a new engine for industry growth, which fully demonstrated that users' new demands for hardware in the AI era have begun to emerge. On the other hand, upstream component costs such as memory chips were still rising, and demand in traditional categories also faced pressure. Against this backdrop, we still maintained solid growth, with total revenue increasing by 19.5% year over year to US$98.6 million. The growth rate further accelerated compared to the second quarter, and we achieved year-over-year growth for the 13th consecutive quarter. In particular, PaaS revenue grew by 18.3% year over year, reflecting the continued increase in penetration of smart products across different regions and categories.

We continued to fully embrace AI and accelerated the promotion of ecosystem deployment. During the quarter, shipments of new AI-native categories grew by over 400% year over year. We debuted the AI home companion robot Doova at IFA in Berlin and the Global Digital Trade Expo. With the AI life assistant Hey Tuya as a core entry point, we integrated the three major application ecosystems of AI Home, AI Robot, and AI Energy, as well as the full-stack Matter combo solution. At the same time, subscription businesses such as door lock value-added services and pet cloud storage developed steadily, driving a continuous increase in the proportion of recurring revenue. Regarding developer tools, following Tuya Cobuilder, we launched the AI-native no-code application development platform Tuya AI Coding this quarter, extending developer tools from hardware development to application development. Looking ahead, we will focus on the three major scenarios mentioned above to promote large-scale shipments and the commercialization of AI-native hardware such as companion robots, pet care devices, and home energy management devices, and continue to improve the developer toolchain, Agent orchestration, and commercialization capabilities."

Mr. Yi (Alex) Yang, Director and Chief Financial Officer of Tuya, added, "In the third quarter, all three business segments delivered year-over-year revenue growth, with total revenue increasing by 19.5% year over year to US$98.6 million. As a foundation for our growth, the PaaS business saw its revenue increase by 18.3% year over year to US$70.1 million; Premium PaaS customers contributed approximately 89.6% of the PaaS revenue, and the core customer base remained stable. Smart home & robot product revenue grew by 26.7% year over year to US$15.0 million, primarily benefiting from growth in demand for categories such as AI audio and video, AI companion toys, and energy. AI application & others revenue increased by 18.6% year over year to US$13.6 million, primarily driven by the growth in cloud services, AI video cloud storage, and AI value-added services."

Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

Total revenue in the third quarter of 2026 increased by 19.5% to US$98.6 million from US$82.5 million in the same period of 2025.

Total gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 increased by 13.6% to US$45.2 million from US$39.8 million in the same period of 2025. The gross margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 45.8%, compared to 48.3% in the same period of 2025.

Gross margin fluctuated primarily due to changes in product and solution mix, as well as fluctuations in semiconductor supply-chain pricing. As an AI developer platform with a rich ecosystem of smart devices and applications, the Company remains focused on AI offering with compelling value propositions while maintaining economic efficiency.

Operating expenses increased by 0.8% to US$36.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 from US$36.0 million in the same period of 2025. Non-GAAP operating expenses increased by 15.9% to US$35.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 from US$30.9 million in the same period of 2025. For further information on the non-GAAP financial measures presented above, see the section headed "Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures."

PROFIT FROM OPERATIONS AND OPERATING MARGIN

Profit from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was US$8.9 million, compared to US$3.8 million in the same period of 2025. The Company had a non-GAAP profit from operations of US$9.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$8.9 million in the same period of 2025, demonstrating consistent operating profitability and leverage.

Operating margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 9.0%, up 4.4 percentage points from 4.6% in the same period of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 9.5%, down 1.3 percentage points from 10.8% in the same period of 2025.

Net profit in the third quarter of 2026 was US$18.5 million, an increase of 23.8% from US$15.0 million in the same period of 2025. Non-GAAP net profit in the third quarter of 2026 was US$19.1 million, compared to US$20.1 million in the same period of 2025.

Net margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 18.8%, an improvement of 0.6 percentage points from 18.2% in the same period of 2025. Non-GAAP net margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 19.3%, compared to 24.4% in the same period of 2025.

Basic and diluted net profit per ADS was US$0.03 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$0.02 in the same period of 2025. Each ADS represents one Class A ordinary share.

Non-GAAP basic and diluted net profit per ADS was US$0.03 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$0.03 in the same period of 2025.

CASH AND CASH EQUIVALENTS, TIME DEPOSITS AND TREASURY SECURITIES RECORDED AS SHORT-TERM AND LONG-TERM INVESTMENTS

Cash and cash equivalents, time deposits and treasury securities recorded as short-term and long-term investments were US$971.9 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to US$1,017.3 million as of December 31, 2025. The Company believes its current cash position is sufficient to meet its current liquidity and working capital needs.

NET CASH GENERATED FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES

Net cash generated from operating activities in the third quarter of 2026 was US$12.7 million, compared to US$30.0 million in the same period of 2025. The net cash generated from operating activities for the third quarter of 2026 was mainly attributable to working capital changes in the ordinary course of business.

For further information on non-GAAP financial measures presented above, see the section headed "Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures."

Currently, innovative AI hardware is in a rapid ramp-up phase, but the overall macroeconomic environment remains complex. On the one hand, AI-native hardware is moving from concept toward deployment at scale, with new categories such as companion robots and AI toys rising rapidly, becoming a new engine for industry growth. On the other hand, upstream component costs such as memory chips continue to rise, demand recovery in certain traditional categories is relatively slow, and uncertainties relating to trade policies and supply chains remain. Performance varies across regions, categories and markets. With the accelerated integration of AI and consumer hardware, Physical AI scenarios are ushering in a broader space for development.

Against this backdrop, we will continue to advance our AI-driven strategy. At the product level, we will focus on three high-potential scenarios, namely AI Home, AI Energy and AI Robot, to promote large-scale shipments and the commercialization of AI-native hardware such as companion robots, home energy management devices, and pet care devices. At the developer platform level, we will continue to improve Tuya Cobuilder, Tuya AI Coding and Agent orchestration capabilities, further lowering the development threshold, and enabling more customers and brands to quickly launch their own AI applications based on the Tuya platform. At the user application level, we will continue to enhance the response efficiency and reliability of Hey Tuya, and advance subscription and value-added services such as AI-powered protection, AI energy saving, pet care, and video understanding, to enhance our renewal and ongoing service capabilities.

Looking ahead, we will maintain disciplined execution, and continuously invest in AI R&D and platform capabilities. We will focus on AI-native applications, Physical AI scenarios, and developer platform construction, leveraging the advantages of our platform, device ecosystem and global developer base to transform AI technology from a tool capability into real and scalable commercial value. At the same time, we also recognize that our future performance may be affected by changes in consumer demand, regional economic conditions, inventory cycles, component and logistics costs, foreign exchange and interest-rate volatility, tariffs and trade-policy adjustments, and broader geopolitical uncertainties.

Declaration of Cash Dividend and Record Date

On November 23, 2026, the Board approved the declaration and distribution of a cash dividend (the "Cash Dividend") of US$0.0570 per ordinary share, or US$0.0570 per ADS, to such holders as at the close of business on January 6, 2027, Hong Kong Time and New York Time, respectively. The aggregate amount of the Cash Dividend will be approximately US$35 million, which is payable in U.S. dollars and in cash, and will be funded by surplus cash and to be paid out from the share premium account of the Company. The determination to make distributions and the amount of such distributions will be made at the discretion of its Board and will be based upon the Company's operations and earnings, including, but not limited to, considerations of the Company's GAAP and Non-GAAP net profits, cash flows, financial conditions and other relevant factors.

In order to qualify for the Cash Dividend, with respect to ordinary shares registered on the Company's Hong Kong share register, all valid documents for the transfers of shares accompanied by the relevant share certificates must be lodged with the Company's Hong Kong share registrar, Computershare Hong Kong Investor Services Limited, at Shops 1712-1716, 17th Floor, Hopewell Centre, 183 Queen's Road East, Wan Chai, Hong Kong, no later than 4:30 p.m. on Wednesday, January 6, 2027, Hong Kong time; and with respect to the ordinary shares registered on the Company's principal share register in the Cayman Islands, all valid documents for the transfers of shares accompanied by the relevant share certificates must be lodged with the Company's principal share registrar, Maples Fund Services (Cayman) Limited, at PO Box 1093, Boundary Hall, Cricket Square, Grand Cayman, KY1-1102, Cayman Islands, no later than 3:30 p.m. on Tuesday, January 5, 2027, Cayman Islands time (due to the time difference between Cayman Islands and Hong Kong). Cash Dividend to be paid to the holders of ADSs issued by the depositary of the ADSs will be subject to the terms of the deposit agreement. The payment date is expected to be on or around January 20, 2027 for holders of ordinary shares and on or around January 27, 2027 for holders of ADSs.

Tuya Inc. (NYSE: TUYA; HKEX: 2391) is a global leading AI cloud platform service provider with a mission to build an AI developer ecosystem and enable everything to be smart. Tuya has pioneered a purpose-built AI cloud platform with cloud and generative AI capabilities that delivers a full suite of offerings, including Platform-as-a-Service, or PaaS, AI application & others and Smart home & robot products for developers of smart device, commercial applications, and industries. In evaluating the business, the Company considers and uses non-GAAP financial measures, such as non-GAAP operating expenses, non-GAAP profit from operations (including non-GAAP operating margin), non-GAAP net profit (including non-GAAP net margin), and non-GAAP basic and diluted net profit per ADS, as supplemental measures to review and assess its operating performance. The presentation of non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America ("U.S. GAAP"). The Company defines non-GAAP financial measures by excluding the impact of share-based compensation expenses and credit-related impairment/(reversal) of long-term investments from the respective GAAP financial measures. The Company presents the non-GAAP financial measures because they are used by the management to evaluate its operating performance and formulate business plans. The Company also believes that the use of the non-GAAP financial measures facilitates investors' assessment of its operating performance.

Non-GAAP financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. One of the key limitations of using the aforementioned non-GAAP financial measures is that they do not reflect all items of expenses that affect the Company's operations. Share-based compensation expenses and credit-related impairment/(reversal) of long-term investments have been and may continue to be incurred in the business and are not reflected in the presentation of non-GAAP measures. Further, the non-GAAP financial measures may differ from the non-GAAP information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. The Company compensates for these limitations by reconciling the non-GAAP measures to the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures, all of which should be considered when evaluating the Company's performance. The Company encourages you to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.
