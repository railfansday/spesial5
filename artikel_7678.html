Also on Friday, in an apparent response to the lawsuit, the Mexican government issued a vow to protect the vaquita with the help of surveillance, fisheries management and combatting illegal trafficking of species with economic alternatives for coastal communities.

Though fishermen do not fish for vaquita, the small mammals are vulnerable to being caught in gillnets used by fishermen in Baja used to catch shrimp and illegally fish for wild totoaba.
