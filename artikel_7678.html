Professionals in any industry need tools and supplies they can rely on to get the job done. If you have a pro in your household, family, or friend group, then they are sure to appreciate pro-grade gifts for birthdays, holidays, and any other special occasions on the horizon. Whether you are looking for tools, like the best paint sprayers, or products, like gutter sealant and stain, you will find a variety of the best gifts for the pros in your life below.

Anyone who hauls tools from the truck to the jobsite will appreciate a box that’s built to take the abuse. The Milwaukee Packout Large Tool Box earned our Best Weather-Rated pick in our guide to the best tool boxes for its rugged, watertight build and latches that open with one hand. It also stacks, rolls, and locks together with other Packout boxes, so it’s an easy gift for a pro who already uses the system or wants to start one.

What our tester says: “Although the box features steel latches that hold the top down securely, we were able to open and latch them with one hand. The Milwaukee tool box comes with a 100-pound weight limit and we have no reason to doubt that. We loaded it with heavy tools and sockets and even overloaded it a bit so we had to push the lid to get it to close. The latches (and hinges) held tight.” —Mark Wolfe, Product Reviews tester and writer

Get the Milwaukee tool box at Amazon, The Home Depot, or Ace Hardware.
