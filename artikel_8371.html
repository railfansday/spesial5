Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Best Site for &gt; 20 GB Free Cloud Storage

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

What I've seen is a request for "Free" Cloud Storage for non-confidential PDF's and Photos....

If you truly don't care about personal privacy, use something like Amazon Photos, Google Drive, or Microsoft OneDrive. They probably have the biggest free allocation before you start paying for it.

But for me, I don't need to worry that my photos or random PDFs get viewed by some server side AI agent. I prefer a fully end-to-end encrypted option.

That would be Apple iCloud with Advanced Data Protection enabled. Or another service that offers true zero knowledge, end-to-end encryption.

I use two. The primary is Tresorit. It's not free. But it's about $150 a year for 1TB. It's fully end-to-end encrypted (meaning no one at that company has any ability to see my files). I can keep my tax returns, photos, and other documents stored in there and not worry about anything. I also utilize iCloud with Advanced Data Protection enabled. By enabling this, Apple can't see my files.

I'm not paranoid, I've got nothing to hide, but I'd rather not worry about one of my tax returns, PDFs, or family photos being discovered through some data breach. End-to-end encryption helps protect me and my family from that.
