How To Get The Goldblaze In Phantom Blade Zero

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Pangzhen is massive, but if you know where to look, you can still fulfill Quick Hand Zhang's request.

Quick Hand Zhang is one of the more interesting mysteries in Phantom Blade Zero, and he offers some of the finest rewards for your efforts. Four main areas on the world map contain treasures for him to find, and because Pangzhen is the largest, it can be tough to spot them all.

Luckily, those glowing green chests aren't hidden too deep in any corners, but they aren't always where you might check. Whether they're hidden after a boss or locked behind a traversal Phantom Edge, you can spot all of Quick Hand Zhang's Pangzhen treasures in no time with a little help.

To get to Quick Hand Zhang's Pangzhen hideout, make your way through Myst's Tavern and cross the bridge to the Backstreet Archway bell. Head up the stairs, and you'll find a large courtyard where you'll have to fight Wu Xuan, The Fallen General.

After defeating him, turn right from where you came in; that's north on the map. A path to the left leads up a large staircase, but go right and follow the path through a building surrounded by jars. Through that building, you'll find wooden boards you can climb, and you'll notice the glint of a throwing knife with coins hanging from it.

At the top, head straight into the building with another throwing knife, and you'll find the bell for Zhang's Hideout (Pangzhen). Head down that ladder, and you'll find Zhang waiting for you amid his many treasures.

The places some people will hide their treasure...

To get to the Tiger Paperweight, you've got to complete a few steps. First, start at the bell at Huang Manor Crossroads and run west across the courtyard to the bell at Myst's Tavern. The first time you come through here, you'll face a challenging fight before you can explore the area.

A locked door leads to a building called Tianxiang Pavilion. To get the key, go into Myst's Tavern and speak with the Leading Opera Actress puppet. She wants her fan back, which is located in the Tianxiang Pavilion.

She will tell you about someone who always hung around the Pavilion and loved the scent of the performers. He will have the key, but you have to draw him out. The actress puppet will give you some of her makeup and tell you to burn it in the incense burner in the alley outside the tavern. When you do, a few enemies will attack you, and after you beat them, you'll get the key.

From there, head back to the locked door and open it, where you'll face a lot of enemies. Clear out the first floor, and on the second floor, you'll find a glowing green chest next to some decorative panels. Open it up to find the Tiger Paperweight.

After you defeat Wu Xuan, The Fallen General, keep heading west, and you'll find a building to the left or a stone archway straight ahead. Either path leads to an open courtyard where you'll have to fight a few enemies.

At the back of this courtyard is a building with red lanterns hanging outside the door, and inside you'll see the chest that contains the Ink Stick for Quick Hand Zhang.

Once you've reached the Narrow Alley Residence 2nd Floor, head out of the building and take the zipline all the way down. Take out the enemies in the area, then climb the small staircase through a wooden archway directly ahead of where you land from the zipline.

You can go through that building; inside is a memory flower and the chest containing the Carved-Edge Inkstone. If you keep going through that building, you'll also find the remains of the dog for the tale, Dahuang, after fighting a few frenzied hounds.

To get the Jade Brush Rest, you'll need to get your hands on You Loong's Deluge. Then go to the bell at the Marketplace in Pangzhen, head down the stairs, and make your way to the flaming brambles blocking the path straight ahead. This is the first spot in the city where you can use the Deluge.

Once you've gone through the opening, take out all the enemies that are waiting for you. Then, turn towards the low rooftops just to the left of where you came in and use the Hunter's Chain Sickle to get onto the roof. Climb the wooden box with scratch marks and make a left at the top.

Keep to the right by the stone wall and follow it until you reach a landing. An enemy will be there that you can easily assassinate, and the chest with the Jade Brush Rest will be just to their left.

Once you've returned all four items to Quick Hand Zhang, he will reward you with the Goldblaze rifle. This ranged Phantom Edge lets you knock down hanging items throughout the world, in place of the Feather Bow.

In combat, you fire the Goldblaze the same way you would the bow, by activating your Phantom Edge while it's equipped. Pulling the trigger once fires one round, but you can pull it twice to continue the firing sequence, which uses one full Remnant Spark.

A full firing sequence uses the same amount of Remnant Spark as firing once, so it often pays to fire twice anyway. However, the Goldblaze does the most damage against enemies that haven't taken any damage in combat yet.

Once you've found something you like, you've got to make sure it stays worth using.
