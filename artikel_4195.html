8 Best Connections to GTA 5 in GTA 6

Rockstar spent a long time working on Grand Theft Auto 6, and it shows through the games many small details. There are tons of hidden easter eggs and secrets scattered across Leonida, and some of them connect to previous GTA titles.

More specifically, there are a decent number that connect back to GTA 5. Have you ever wondered what happened to Michael’s boat? Who took the blame for the murder of Floyd and Debora?

Thanks to GTA 6, we now the answers to these questions, and there are many more connections to GTA 5 that you might not have discovered. Everything is connected after all.

If you played GTA 5, odds are you spent a great deal of time exploring the map and going from town to town. It was quite a big map, but you wouldn’t have spent your entire time in the city, so you probably recognize Grapeseed and the Grand Senora Desert.

Grapeseed is a small town in the northern part of the GTA 5 map, with it residing in Blaine County. The Grand Senora Desert is a much larger area, but it also resides in Blaine County.

While exploring the many buildings that you can enter in GTA 6, you can wander into a few trailers, and some of them have postcards that link Leonida to Los Santos. You can find postcards specifically for Grapeseed and Grand Senora Desert.

You get a lot of phone calls in GTA 5. Many missions start with you getting a call, and then proceeding to head to some location. Because of this, if you have played the story, you will have heard Michael’s ringtone quite a bit.

Well, if you feel so inclined, and want to stay true to the spirit of GTA 5, you can have Michael’s ringtone in GTA 6. All you need to do is go into your phone’s settings, choose the ringtone option, and then select the one called Wind Chimes.

This ringtone is exactly the same as Michael’s from GTA 5, giving you that classic GTA feeling from 13 years ago when you played the campaign for GTA 5.

With the release of Grand Theft Auto 6, it is time to look back and remind ourselves why Grand Theft Auto 5 will be remember for years to come.

One of the best easter eggs in GTA 6 is that you can find Michael’s boat from GTA 5, and you don’t really need to go all that far. In fact, you have to stick rather close to home, because it is sitting right outside Jason’s house at the start of the game.

Michael ends up having his boat stolen in GTA 5, and you never learn what happens to it. Well, it somehow found its way to Leonida and ended up in Jason’s yard.

If you decide to climb up on the boat, you can see that a screen is still active on it. This screen shows the shores of Los Santos from GTA 5, seemingly proving that this is indeed the same boat that was stolen from Michael all those years ago.

Do you remember Fabien? He teaches Michael how to do yoga in GTA 5, and is known for having some rather… suggestive yoga poses. Throughout GTA 5, you can perform yoga as Michael either at his house or at the peak of Mount Gordo.

Fabien has been doing well since the events of GTA 5, and you can see what he has been up to thanks to his article in one of the game’s magazines. This is also an easter egg that you can find outside of Jason’s house.

Go out onto the porch, and open the magazine that is on the table near the workout equipment. Flip through the pages, and you will find that Fabien has been doing well. He has been on an “epic pilgrimage of enlightenment.”

It is a little ambiguous as to if the Mount Chiliad Mystery in GTA 5 was ever solved. While there is a means of seeing a UFO from it, it didn’t have a narrative conclusion, so it is debated on whether or not it was ever completed.

However, this mystery is mentioned in GTA 6, and it is on a vehicle. You can find an RV in Leonida that is covered in phrases like, “Don’t be fooled,” and “There’s no driving away.”

What connects this to Mount Chiliad is the eye symbol. It is the same eye symbol that is found at the top of the Mount Chiliad mural, making it seem like the mystery was never fully solved, or maybe that is just what Rockstar wants us to think.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a nominee for Game of the Year. It was selected at the last minute, but there are plenty of reasons it deserves the award.

There are some small nods to GTA 5 hidden throughout GTA 6, and one that is easy to overlook is a small book review for a novel sitting in Jason’s house. The book in question is titled Lethal By Design: A Liberty City Memoir, written by Tom Everett.

This book connects to the events of GTA 4 and Niko Bellic - a fan-favorite protagonist. With that being said, one of the reviews is actually from a resident of Los Santos.

The review reads, “Illuminating, revelatory, heartbreaking, and pulse-quickening - and that’s before you’ve even finished the editor’s forward.” This review came from Alison Taylor from the Los Santos Book Review.

There is an obscure reference to GTA 5 in GTA 6, and it connects back to some of the promotional material for GTA 5. One of the trailers for GTA 5 featured a man with a newspaper on his desk.

If you are paying attention, you can see that the newspaper’s headline reads, “Aliens ate my prize [blank]. Of course, I have to bleep out the final word due to vulgarity, but it connected to GTA 4, and it also played into the insane satirical nature of GTA 5.

This same newspaper has returned, and it is in GTA 6. In a shack in the Grassrivers, you can spot the same newspaper sitting on a table. It is amazing that Rockstar remembered to bring back this strange newspaper.

Floyd and Debora Hebert were an unhappy couple living in Vespucci Beach in GTA 5. Their house is used as a safehouse by Trevor and their cousin Wade. However, they don’t survive the events of GTA 5, as Trevor murders them in a brutal off-screen killing that leaves him covered in blood.

It is never known exactly what happened after that event, but you can learn the truth of what transpired after the events of GTA 5 by listening to the radio in GTA 6.

If you listen to the crime thriller podcast channel on the radio, you will hear them talk about Floyd and Debora. It turns out that Wade got blamed for their deaths.

Like many games, Grand Theft Auto 6 is full of easter eggs for you to discover. These are some of the best Leonida has to offer.
