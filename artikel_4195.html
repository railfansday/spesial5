Late Submissions Dominate Game of the Year Nominees

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

The Game Awards 2026 is almost here, and the nominees for the coveted Game of the Year prize have been revealed, with most of the entries launching near the end of the year, and one of them isn’t even out yet.

While not as prestigious as the Academy Awards, The Game Awards is the industry’s best way to reward beloved titles, as well as shine a light on games that might not have been played as much, all with a heavy dose of commercials and reveals for upcoming releases.

XBOX is collaborating with DJ Khaled to sign sick notes to get players a day off work to play GTA 6, although the game isn't explicitly mentioned.

The official The Game Awards Twitter/X account has announced the six titles up for nomination for Game of the Year. Curiously, most of the titles that made the shortlist only came out recently, with one that is not available to the general public for another two days.

Here are your six nominees for Game of the Year. #TheGameAwards streaming live on Thursday, December 10.

The six nominees for Game of the Year are 007 First Light by IO Interactive, Control Resonant by Remedy/Annapurna, Grand Theft Auto 6 by Rockstar Games, Phantom Blade Zero by G-Game, Resident Evil Requiem by Capcom, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time by Nintendo. Most of those titles came out very recently, and many players haven't even had a chance to finish them.

Of the six nominees, only two came out in the first half of the year. 007 First Light came out on May 27, and Resident Evil Requiem launched on February 27. Meanwhile, Control Resonant launched in late September, Phantom Blade Zero was released in October, while the others came out in November.

The six nominees for Game of the Year are 007 First Light by IO Interactive, Control Resonant by Remedy/Annapurna, Grand Theft Auto 6 by Rockstar Games, Phantom Blade Zero by G-Game, Resident Evil Requiem by Capcom, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time by Nintendo.

Many fans are surprised that Grand Theft Auto VI is included in the nominations, but that, The Game Awards takes nominations from gaming outlets (including DualShockers), so being available to the general public is not part of the equation.

It’s not unheard of for the Game of the Year award to be dominated by late releases. After all, companies prefer to release titles near the end of the year so they can be purchased for the holiday season. There will certainly be a lot of people who won’t be returning to Vice City or Hyrule until December 25.

Clearly, Geoff Keighley and the people who nominated Grand Theft Auto 6 are confident that it has what it takes to win the Game of the Year award, and, to be fair, it’s a pretty safe bet, based on Rockstar’s history and reputation. The numerous delays for Grand Theft Auto 6 proved that Rockstar and Take-Two wanted to make sure the game was just right before releasing it, and fans are confident it will pay off.

Even if Control Resonant takes a surprise win, Rockstar and Take-Two are the victors, due to the sheer amount of money Grand Theft Auto 6 has brought in so far. The award is nice, but the millions of dollars are a little nicer.

We're not just talking about Grand Theft Auto VI and Ocarina of Time, but up to seven video games that could sweep The Game Awards.
