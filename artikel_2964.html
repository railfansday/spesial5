Dalton Kincaid doubtful for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Buffalo Bills TE Dalton Kincaid (thumb, ankle) did not practice all week and has been listed as doubtful for Week 11 against the Miami Dolphins.

Why It Matters: If Kincaid cannot play in Week 11, Spencer Hawes could be in line to start and play meaningful snaps. Hawes will have to have his status monitored by fantasy players on Sunday.
