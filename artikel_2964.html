Pistons vs. Raptors final score: Cade vs. Scottie battle goes to Raps

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Two former teammates and two Eastern Conference teams traded body blows all night. A Cade Cunningham 3-pointer to put the Pistons up 102-101 with 20 seconds left was answered by a Scottie Barnes driving layup over two defenders to give the Raptors the lead they would not relinquish. When a Kevin Huerter three came up short as the buzzer sounded, it was decided. Raptors 105-102.

Cunningham led Detroit with 23 points and nine assists, but he was outshined on this night by his former Montverde teammate Barnes, who led all scorers with 29 points and hit his first nine shots of the night.

While it was appointment television watching the two top players on each team matched up with each other, the rest of the game was pretty much a horror show for the Pistons. Distilling why the Pistons dropped this game will sound very familiar to Pistons fans — not owning the paint.

Detroit surrendered 54 points inside to Toronto and gave up crucial offensive rebounds late. It's one thing to see Barnes put a great game on you, but Detroit was owned inside by rookie Allen Graves making his first start of the season. He scored 20 points, had 10 rebounds, including four on the offensive end. Detroit simply had no answer for him. Certainly not John Collins.

The overmatched emergency starting center struggled again in just under 20 minutes of action. He was just 1-of-4 from the floor for three points and six rebounds. The return of Isaiah Joe from injury isn't looking very different from when he was playing early in the season — he's still struggling to find his shot. Joe was 1-of-6 from deep tonight. Getting those kinds of performances from your two highest-profile offseason acquisitions is not the recipe for winning basketball.

The other major issue tonight is that you were facing one of the worst-shooting offenses in the NBA and on this night, they could not miss. Some of that had to do with Detroit's occasionally passive defense. But tonight, the Raptors players were just collectively on one. We already covered how hot Barnes was, especially early, but the sweet shooting was up and down the roster.

The rookie Graves was 6-of-9, Jakob Poeltl was 5-of-7, and Immanuel Quickly was 6-of-10. Collectively, Toronto shot 67% inside the arc tonight. Again, I'm going to point to the big black hole in the middle of Detroit's lineup. A combination of John Collins and Paul Reed as your only big men is just not going to get it done in the NBA. The Pistons need Jalen Duren to come back desperately.

The loss to the Raptors drops the Pistons to 1-1 in NBA Cup play, but then again, I refuse to care about the NBA Cup. Detroit now goes on a mini road trip to Atlanta on Sunday and Orlando on Tuesday before enjoying seven of the next eight games at home.
