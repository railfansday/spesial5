Anthropic IPO: Here’s the 1 Thing That's Keeping Me From Buying

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthropic is about to hit the Nasdaq, but I don't see a lasting moat that keeps customers from leaving.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 19, 2026 at 8:42AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Anthropic (ANT +0.00%) is set to start trading today on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The IPO offering hopes to raise $81.3 billion publicly along with a $26 billion private placement for major tech investors. That makes it the biggest IPO of all time as measured by funds raised, and it will likely put the company’s valuation at $1.4 trillion.

Sounds like a potentially great investment opportunity in artificial intelligence, right? The problem is, I don't see Anthropic having a real, lasting business advantage that keeps competitors from taking its AI customers. Among investment analysts, this is referred to as a durable moat, and it’s all-important for long-term growth.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei. Image source: Getty Images.

Right now, the company's main advantage is that its Claude AI models are consistently among the best available. Customers are clearly excited: Third-quarter revenue jumped 1,127% year over year -- incredible. But this is not a durable moat to me, and I am skeptical that the pace of growth will hold much longer.

Over time, I think a lot of its customers will find that most of their AI needs can be met by models that aren't on the cutting edge, models that are good enough and can be attained for a fraction of the price. If that is the case, there's very little to keep these customers from leaving.

Most of the company's lucrative enterprise clients use Claude through an application programming interface (API) -- basically a backend connection that lets a company's systems send work to Claude and get results back. That makes switching to another model provider pretty dang easy.

Frankly, a customer doesn't even have to leave entirely. It could send the routine work to a cheaper competitor and keep Claude reserved for the hardest jobs. The question is, will there be enough of these "hardest jobs" to keep the revenue line going up?

It’s something long-term investors need to consider as Anthropic launches its IPO.
