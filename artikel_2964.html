Luka Doncic Leads Race in 1st Kia MVP Ladder Ranking Amid Historic Start

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Support Local Media - Click to Add LA Sports Report as a Preferred Source on Google

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic sat atop the 1st Kia MVP Ladder ranking of the 2026-27 season as he continues dominating the league.

Doncic is averaging a league-leading 38.5 points, 8.1 rebounds and 8.0 assists per game, and has been untouchable over the past handful of games. He has 40 or more points in each of his last three contests, and has six or more 3-pointers in his last six. Doncic only ranked fourth in MVP voting last year despite leading the NBA in scoring, and is finally getting the recognition he deserves in the first ranking of the year.

"Dončić is on a tear, but what else is new for a two-time scoring champ and six-time member of the All-NBA First Team?" NBA.com's Shaun Powell wrote. "He's the first player in history to record six straight games of 35-plus points, five-plus rebounds, five-plus assists and five-plus 3-pointers. He has scored at least 40 in five of his last six games and at least 20 first-half points in six straight games. He had 52 against Utah. That led to Giannis Antetokounmpo calling Dončić one of the greatest scorers of all time, high praise indeed.

"Dončić seems committed to being a better all-around player, a lofty bar to clear for sure. His defense seems to be on the up-and-up and his passing remains sharp. Dončić is once again among the assist leaders. As long as the Lakers keep winning, Dončić will enjoy the view from his place on the Ladder."

While Doncic currently leads the charge for MVP, plenty of other players are close behind him despite his incredible start.

Ranking just behind Doncic is Victor Wembanyama, fresh off of his best NBA performance. Wembanyama scored 52 points and secured 24 rebounds in the San Antonio Spurs' win over the Dallas Mavericks on Wednesday, and was a clear favorite for the award heading into the season.

He's averaging 29.0 points and 12.4 rebounds per game, and his defensive prowess is sure to swing some votes in his favor as well.

Back-to-back MVP winner Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is also in the running, with players like Tyrese Maxey and Jayson Tatum also in the mix. With such stiff competition, Doncic will have to sustain his brilliant run of form if he wants to take home his first MVP this season.
