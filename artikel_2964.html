Dell Ups Annual Sales Forecast Again on Rising AI Server Demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dell Technologies boosted its fiscal year revenue sales outlook for the third time as the rapid build-out of AI data centers fuels surging server sales.  Revenue in the fiscal year ending in January will be about $202 billion, including $78 billion in AI server sales, compared with a September forecast of $192 billion, the Texas-based company said Tuesday in a statement. Analysts polled by Bloomberg had expected sales of $193.5 billion.  Dell said its board has also boosted the company’s share buyback plan by $25 billion. Woo Jin Ho, Bloomberg Intelligence Senior Analyst for Hardware and Networking expects to see the impact of the midterm election on Dell's data centers in mid -2027. (Source: Bloomberg)
