EU nations unveil Rwanda deal for migrant return hub

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Five EU countries including Germany and Denmark on Monday unveiled a deal to establish a migrant deportation centre in Rwanda -- the first such facility outside the bloc. The group, also comprising Austria, Greece and the Netherlands, has been gunning to open a "return-hub" despite rights groups' concerns, after the European Union cleared the way with a recent toughening of migration rules. FRANCE 24's Dave Keating reports from Brussels.
