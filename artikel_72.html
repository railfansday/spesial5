Xi demonstrating China's 'global status', occupying 'central position' in relation to Trump & Putin

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sharon Gaffney is pleased to welcome Rex Li, professor of international relations at King's College London. For Professor Li, the meeting between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in China is less about "reaching concrete agreements and a common position." Rather, he argues, it is about demonstrating Xi's ability to bring the American and Russian leaders together to discuss major geopolitical issues. Li believes that Xi Jinping has the most to gain from the meeting, as it allows him to demonstrate China's global status and its role as a diplomatic convener. Putin may also benefit from the opportunity to meet the two leaders despite Russia's international isolation following its invasion of Ukraine.
