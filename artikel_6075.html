Trump wags finger at 'really rude' reporter, tells her she belongs in the 'back row' during press exchange

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

President Donald Trump dismissed a female journalist as "really a rude person" and waved his finger at her while wrapping up Friday’s event.

President Donald Trump rebuked a female reporter at the end of a White House news conference Friday, wagging his finger at her, calling her "really a rude person" and telling her she belonged in the "back row."

The exchange unfolded as Trump wrapped up a lengthy news conference following his return from an Asia trip, just after inviting White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller to the podium and praising Miller's work on immigration and deregulation.

Before addressing the press corps, Trump defended Miller's temperament, noting that critics often view him as overly strict on immigration.

"He’s really a talent, and he’s got a big heart. A lot of people think he doesn’t. They think he’s hard on immigration, harder on this. He’s got a tremendous heart. In many ways. They think he’s got too big a heart. But we’ll talk about that," Trump said before breaking off to address the journalist.

"Who are you with. Who are you with?" Trump asked while waving his finger in her direction.

After the reporter replied, "Who are you?" Trump sharply criticized both her demeanor and her seating placement in the room.

"And you’re really a rude person," Trump said.

When the reporter tried to speak again, beginning, "So if you’re talking about it," Trump cut her off and told her she should not be seated near the front of the press area.

"I’m not talking to you. I’m not telling you. You shouldn’t be in the front row. You should be in the back row," Trump said.

Trump then pivoted back to his closing remarks, inviting attendees to an afternoon ceremony and concert on the White House helipad with UFC CEO Dana White to honor and award money to U.S. military musicians.

"They’re unbelievably talented people, and we’re going to honor them in just a little while. And thank you for your fairness. Thank you for your questions. And Steve, thank you, J. D. Thank you. And Marco, thank you very much. Thank you everybody," Trump said as he concluded the appearance alongside Miller, Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio.
