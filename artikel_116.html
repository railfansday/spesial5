Laptop prices have been a hot-button topic all year long thanks to RAMageddon. We’ve seen existing models get more expensive and new models arrive at higher prices than last-gen — sometimes with lower specs, too. 2026 in general has been a bad time to buy a computer. But thankfully, Black Friday can offer a tiny bit of respite.

If the computer you currently use is working well for you, good. Happy for you. But if you’re in the market for something more powerful, bigger, smaller, thinner, etc. — and you prefer new over used — you can currently save some decent dough on MacBooks, Windows notebooks, and gaming laptops.

The big, multi-hundred-dollar savings are typically reserved for higher-end models with bigger specs, like Windows models running Nvidia’s new Arm-based RTX Spark platform — which are getting their first major discounts. More budget-friendly laptops are also receiving decent discounts right now, though, including the MacBook Neo. The current $100 discount on Apple’s budget laptop might seem like small potatoes considering it’s just a return to its original price, but it’s the best you can do for now (if you can’t hold out for a rumored new one in 2027).

As always, the deals below are for laptops we’ve reviewed or tested firsthand in some way at The Verge — either that exact model or a similar one we expect to be equally good. There are many laptops out there and myriad deals running now through Cyber Monday, but if any of these fit your needs, you can’t go wrong.

Apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review.

Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review.

Apple gave its base model MacBook Pro an upgrade to a 12-core M6 chip for 2026, and not a whole lot else. Read our review.

The non-OLED M5 Pro and M5 Max versions of the MacBook Pro deliver exceptional power and speed, making them ideal for creative work while offering best-in-class battery life. Read our review.

Apple’s latest (and priciest) MacBook Pro is its first with a touchscreen. The new PROLED models pack familiar M5 Pro and M5 Max chips into a thinner and lighter chassis. Read our review.

The best XPS 13 in years. It doesn’t have a headphone jack and starts with only 8GB of RAM but it’s an excellent thin-and-light with a great screen, a solid Intel chip, and speedy storage.

An incredibly nice feeling laptop with great battery life and a love-it-or-hate-it latticeless keyboard. Read our review.

The latest 13-inch Surface Laptop has lower starting specs than the 2025 model, with 8GB of RAM, but it’s still a well put together laptop that now feels better to use after some notable Windows 11 updates. Read our review.

The IdeaPad Slim 3x offers a 15.3-inch touchscreen and a comfortable keyboard. It runs a base Snapdragon X chip, making it well-suited for the basics. The real highlights are battery life and port selection. The speakers, however, are terrible. Read our review.

One of Lenovo’s new alternatives to the MacBook Neo, with an Intel Wildcat Lake chip, 8GB of RAM, and plenty of ports.

A great convertible Chromebook with fantastic battery life. Read our review.

A 16-inch laptop that’s as light as a 13-inch MacBook Air, packing a powerful Snapdragon X2 Elite Extreme chip and 48GB of RAM.

Asus’s Zenbook A14 is a bit of a MacBook Air wannabe, but it’s a compelling alternative if you value a superlight Windows laptop with long battery life. The 2025 model sports an Arm-based Qualcomm Snapdragon X Plus chip, a nice 14-inch OLED display, and a sleek textured beige finish.

An expensive but excellent dual-screen laptop with a powerful Intel Panther Lake chip. The only thing better than a lovely OLED display is having two of them. Read our review.

A good Intel Panther Lake chip, lots of RAM, and an OLED display combined in a 2-in-1 chassis.
