Putin Is Open to Meeting With North Korea’s Leader in China, Kremlin Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia could meet with Kim Jong-un if he travels to Shenzhen for the APEC summit this week. North Korea has not confirmed whether Mr. Kim will attend.

President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia could meet with Kim Jong-un if the North Korean leader travels to China for the APEC summit this week, the Kremlin said, reflecting the deepening of ties between their countries in recent years.

Asked about the possibility of a meeting, Dmitri S. Peskov, the Kremlin spokesman, said on Monday that it would “of course” happen if Mr. Kim went to Shenzhen, the Chinese economic hub and host city, according to Russian state news agencies. North Korea has not confirmed whether Mr. Kim plans to attend the summit.

Mr. Putin and Mr. Kim have publicly met three times since 2022, and North Korea has been a key supporter of Russia’s military actions in Ukraine.

As the war in Ukraine nears its fifth anniversary, with no end in sight, the Kremlin is expected to use Mr. Putin’s many meetings at the APEC summit to showcase how Russia is regarded as one of the major international powers alongside the United States and China.

A visit by Mr. Kim would be a rare foray outside North Korea for the leader, and it would further raise the prospect that he could meet with President Trump in China. On the first day of his 48-hour trip to Asia, Mr. Trump told reporters that Mr. Kim “would like” to come to the APEC summit. The president has repeatedly said he would like to meet with Mr. Kim again.

On Wednesday, Mr. Putin is scheduled to meet with Xi Jinping, China’s leader. On Thursday, he is set to take part in a trilateral summit with Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump, the first meeting of its kind. The Kremlin said on Monday that it was working to arrange a bilateral meeting between Mr. Putin and Mr. Trump.

Before his departure for China, Mr. Putin met on Monday in the Kremlin with Jo Yong-won, a secretary of North Korea’s ruling Workers’ Party. During the meeting, Mr. Putin said that North Korea’s help in driving Ukrainian forces from the Kursk region of Russia last year “marked a new chapter in the history of military cooperation between our countries.”

Moscow planned to unveil a monument in Kursk by the end of the year to honor the North Korean troops, Mr. Putin said.
