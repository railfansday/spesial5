Ecclestone ruled out of WBBL

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Adelaide Strikers announced on Tuesday that England spinner Sophie Ecclestone would be ruled out for the remainder of the WBBL season; it comes as Issy Wong has been withdrawn from England A's squad to take on India A in a multi-format series

Sophie Ecclestone has been ruled out of the remainder of the WBBL season due to a back injury.

Adelaide Strikers revealed the England spinner picked the injury prior to their match against the Perth Scorchers.

After undergoing scans, the England and Wales Cricket Board and the Strikers determined in consultation that Ecclestone would "require a period of rehabilitation".

Ecclestone, who has taken four wickets in four matches for the Strikers in the WBBL this season with an economy of 7.62, said she was devastated her season has come to an end prematurely.

"I've loved my time in Adelaide and with the Strikers, and I'll be supporting the team every step of the way for the remainder of the season," she said.

"Our squad has produced some incredible performances this year, and I'll be cheering the girls on as they push for finals."

It comes as a huge blow for the Strikers, who are already without fast bowler Darcie Brown, who has also been sidelined with a back injury.

Strikers coach Luke Williams said: "Sophie has been an incredible addition to the Strikers, not only with her batting and bowling, but also through her leadership and experience.

"She is one of the world's premier bowlers and, while we're disappointed to lose her, we're incredibly grateful for everything she has contributed during her time at the club.

"We wish Sophie all the very best with her recovery and look forward to welcoming her back to Adelaide in the future."

Wong, Lamb withdrawn from England A squads

Issy Wong has been withdrawn from England A's multi-format tour of India due to a stress fracture in her right foot.

She'll be replaced by Alexa Stonehouse for the four-day squad, while no replacement for Wong has been called up for the white-ball matches.

Lancashire's Emma Lamb has undergone surgery on a back injury and has subsequently been withdrawn from the 50-over and four-day squads.

She is replaced by 19-year-old Durham batting all-rounder Grace Thompson.
