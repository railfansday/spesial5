Eckert signs new Saints deal despite 'Spygate' controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Southampton were expelled from the Championship play-offs in May after admitting to "multiple breaches" of unauthorised filming of their opponents' training; club stuck by their manager Tonda Eckert and now have given him a new contract

Southampton boss Tonda Eckert has been handed a contract extension through to 2029.

Saints were kicked out of the Championship play-off final and docked four points this season having admitted three breaches of EFL regulations after being caught spying on semi-final opponents Middlesbrough and two other teams.

Eckert accepted full responsibility and was charged by the FA with three breaches of Rule E3.1 in relation to "misconduct", which hung over Southampton and their manager heading into the new campaign.

However, the club has stuck by their man with Southampton owner Dragan Solak saying at the time of the punishment: "We are confident the manager has learned from this matter, and a suspended sentence allows him to continue in his role."

Despite a four point deduction, Southampton sit five points off top spot in the Sky Bet Championship. Saints are also unbeaten in their last 17 home league matches, with Eckert suffering only one Championship defeat at St Mary's throughout his tenure to date.

Group Sporting Director Johannes Spors said: "I think it's a great commitment from both sides; from the club to Tonda, and from Tonda to the club. I'm very happy that we have achieved this agreement before the end of the international window, allowing us to go into the busy schedule that is ahead of us with clarity.

"Tonda is very talented and very precise with a great analytical brain, but also has the personality and authority that you need, even at a young age. It is very clear that this is the right place for him, and I'm very happy that he feels that too."

Eckert said: "We've had some conversations that started a couple of weeks ago, and the goal was always clear: to show the same commitment that we have had for the last 12 months. I'm very happy that we have come to the point that we can make it official now.

"There is a lot that has happened in the last 12 months, but the commitment from the club, from Dragan, Johannes and Phil, and the supporters, means everything to me. I am very grateful for that. We have started a journey that is not even halfway through - there is a lot more to come."

The play-off semi-final second leg is played at St Mary's. Shea Charles' extra-time winner earns Southampton a 2-1 victory and a place in the Championship play-off final.

May 18: Middlesbrough players return to training as the outcome of the independent disciplinary hearing nears.

May 19: EFL announces Southampton expelled from play-off final after hearing.
