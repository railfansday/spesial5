Ireland's Prendergast cleared to play Springboks as citing dismissed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ireland fly-half Sam Prendergast free to face South Africa on Saturday in Nations Championship (4.40pm) after hip-drop tackle citing was dismissed; Springboks coach Rassie Erasmus had predicted he would escape ban before alleging ‘match-fixing’ and being given six-Test ban

Ireland fly-half Sam Prendergast is free to face South Africa in the Nations Championship on Saturday, after a citing against him for a hip-drop tackle was dismissed.

Prendergast was cited for his tackle on Fiji captain Peceli Yato during last week's Dublin clash, which left the centre limping off injured. He attended a hearing on Tuesday evening alongside Ireland head coach Andy Farrell and lawyer Derek Hegarty.

The three-man citing committee, comprising Mike Hamlin and Becky Essex of England and Australia's Wayne Erickson, unanimously dismissed the charge on Wednesday morning.

A Nations Championship statement read: "The committee unanimously determined that the player had, to the required standard, proved that the citing should be dismissed."

"Detailed findings of facts and reasons for the committee's decision will be set out in its written judgment."

Prendergast, who lost his place in Ireland's matchday squad of 23 during the Six Nations earlier this year, returned to the team in July with Jack Crowley ruled out with a long-term leg injury.

The 23-year-old started both of Ireland's Nations Championship Tests in Dublin this month against Argentina and Fiji, but struggled off the kicking tee in the latter.

On Monday, South Africa head coach Rassie Erasmus stoked flames when he predicted Prendergast would play this weekend and would get off "on a technicality."

Erasmus went on to make extraordinary claims, saying "match-fixing" and "manipulation" was occurring in rugby following his side's defeat to France in Paris last week.

The Springboks coach was given a six-match ban by World Rugby for such comments, but SA Rugby have since requested a formal hearing.
