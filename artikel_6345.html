Black Friday weekend has historically been the best time to get a Nintendo Switch at a discount. Last year was an exception, considering it was a brand-new (and highly sought-after) system. Candidly, the sales were pretty weak, but this year is an entirely different ballgame.

Nintendo’s 2026 Black Friday deals include up to 75 percent off on games, accessories, and select Switch 2 bundles. The Mario Kart World Bundle is currently $50 off, and plenty of games are also on sale. Plus, Belkin, one of our favorite third-party accessory brands, is running a pretty awesome sale, too. Don’t just take it from me, though—browse our favorite deals below.

Despite the bundle being the worst deal of the bunch, at least percentage-wise, it’s probably the star of the show. It’s now back to its original retail value before Nintendo hiked the price.

The game deals are a close second. Donkey Kong Bananza is now $50, which is the lowest price I’ve seen since its inception. It’s also probably the best game Nintendo has made for the Switch 2 so far (unless you want to count the Ocarina of Time remake). Super Mario Party Jamboree is also now 25 percent off, and Kirby and Air Riders is nearly 30 percent off. $50 for relatively new Switch 2 titles is an absolute steal.

Belkin makes some of the best (and most convenient) Switch 2 accessories out there, and some of its top sellers are on rare sale. Save 21 percent on its fast-charging Nintendo Switch 2 Travel Case, which features a powerful integrated 10,000 mAh charger. Its standard carrying case is also almost 30 percent off, while its ultra-convenient sling bag carrying case is a solid 20 percent off and just $40. We’re looking at a Black Friday miracle here, folks.
