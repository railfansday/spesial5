Christian Watson injury update: Latest news on Packers WR’s status in Week 12 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Green Bay Packers have an injury concern with wide receiver Christian Watson in the first half of the Week 12 game against the Los Angeles Rams.

Watson got injured on the very first drive of the game and was spotted getting his leg checked out on the sideline.

"Christian Watson is getting his left leg checked out on the sideline," ESPN's Rob Demovsky reported.

Prior to his injury, Watson recorded one catch for six yards.

UPDATE: The Packers announced that Watson is officially questionable to return with a thigh injury.

"Packers WR Christian Watson is questionable to return tonight due to a thigh injury," ESPN's Adam Schefter wrote.

As previously noted, Watson was getting his knee checked out by trainers.

We're still waiting for an update from the team, but we'll put it right here once that becomes available.

Watson is one half of the league's best wide receiver duo, along with Matthew Golden, so losing him would be a significant blow to the Packers' offense.

If he can't continue, the Packers will need more out of Golden, along with Savion Williams and Bo Melton.
