Frustrated Stefon Diggs explodes on sidelines during Commanders game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former New England Patriots wide receiver Stefon Diggs was caught screaming on the sidelines in frustration during Monday's game between the Washington Commanders and Cincinnati Bengals.

The explosion from Diggs came on a play where it looked like Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels missed him coming across the middle of the field. Daniels hesitated during the play, held onto the ball for too long and attempted to escape from the pocket with his feet.

However, the third-year quarterback was sacked from behind.

Diggs' words weren't audible, but the best lip-reading guess would be that he said, "Throw the [expletive] ball." You be the judge.

Diggs is obviously competitive, and the season with the Commanders hasn't gone according to plan.

The former two-time All-Pro receiver had 85 catches for 1,013 yards and four touchdowns when catching passes from Drake Maye in New England last year. But so far this season, the veteran wideout only has 36 catches for 386 yards and six touchdowns.

Diggs is currently averaging 48.3 yards per game, which is the worst in his NFL career. The Patriots certainly could have used Diggs when Maye was healthy this season, and Diggs would likely have performed better in New England than he has in Washington.

That one-and-done split hasn't gone well for either side.
