Atomic Heart: The CUBE teaser trailer ‘Archive File #1’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Mundfish has released a new teaser trailer for June 2025-announced multiplayer RPG Atomic Heart: The CUBE, formerly just The CUBE, dubbed “Archive File #1.”

The accompanying text reads, “Observation Assessment: The magnitude of the threat posed by the giant rotating Cube was impossible to predict.”

Here is an overview of the game, via Mundfish:

Atomic Heart: The CUBE is a multiplayer RPG shooter that blends unique world-building, an intriguing narrative, and exciting quests into one game. A living world full of memorable characters, diverse biomes, and tons of enemies, including powerful bosses, awaits you.

Atomic Heart: The CUBE will be available for unspecified consoles and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store.
