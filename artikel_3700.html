Consoles cost more than ever, and even if you can afford to buy one you don't have as much left over to buy games for it as you used to. That's made 2026 an underwhelming year for console gaming sales, but according to the latest forecast from videogame market analysts Newzoo, the imminent launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 will turn that around.

"As we explored in the Global Games Market Report 2026," said Newzoo's director of market intelligence, Emmanuel Rosier, "without GTA VI the console market would likely be down year-over-year in 2026. Instead, the game's launch is expected to generate billions of dollars in revenue and give console spending a significant boost."

Rosier is the analyst who, back in August, was skeptical about the idea of BioWare surviving long enough to make another Mass Effect game. I wish he'd been wrong about that, but it turned out he was right on the money with that prediction.

"Western console has been losing ground," he went on to say. "Its share of global games revenue fell from 21.5% in 2015 to 17.1% in 2025, and its revenue has been broadly flat since 2021. Console adoption shows the same pattern. In Western markets, the console player base has been flat since 2022. Most people who want to play on console already do."

Newzoo's prediction is that GTA 6 "will lift console full-game spending by 17.5% this year". But that's just the start of the story. "So while GTA VI is undoubtedly one of the most important releases of the decade," Rosier says, "its initial impact will be concentrated in Western console. That’s still a big market, which we forecast will be worth $36.3 billion in 2026, but it's only part of the industry. The longer-term global story will depend on what happens next, particularly on PC and online."

Though tradition has plenty of people expecting a delay of roughly a year and a half between GTA 6's console and PC releases, general console malaise and the value of GTA Online—likely to be bigger on PC, and perhaps launch alongside the PC port—is a reason to think maybe the wait won't be so long. A recent GamesBeat report claims it'll be out in early 2027.

"PC is far bigger than console in several major markets, including China," Rosier says, "and a PC release could expose the game to a much broader international audience.

"The online mode is another key unknown. Depending on how it's positioned and monetized, it could significantly expand the game's audience. If Rockstar adopts a model that lowers barriers to entry, the long-term opportunity outside the traditional Western console audience could be much larger than it appears today."

GTA Online was a huge part of GTA 5's long-tail success. While Newzoo's data says at least 37% of players who preordered GTA 6 have spent money on GTA Online, only 15% did so in the last 12 months. It's in need of a refresh, and though Rockstar haven't confirmed anything, Twitch's CEO also says GTA 6's multiplayer component will be out in 2027.
