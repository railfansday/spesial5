Buy Electronics & Toys on Black Friday – Wait Until January for Treadmills & Sheets

Buy TVs, laptops, tablets, small kitchen appliances, smart-home gear, and toys over Black Friday and Cyber Monday, when their deepest discounts land. Wait for January’s sales on treadmills and bedding, and check the price history of anything else on your list before you pay.

A Black Friday discount is worth taking only on something that won’t sell for less later in the year.

Price data from Consumer Reports (CR) shows that 46 kinds of products it tests reach their lowest prices in November, among them TVs, laptops, tablets, air fryers, coffee makers, blenders, smart speakers, thermostats, and security cameras. CR expects Black Friday and Cyber Monday to bring the best deals of the year.

Adobe, which analyzes online sales at U.S. retailers, expects this holiday season’s best deals on TVs, toys, and appliances to land on Black Friday, and the deepest discounts on computers on Cyber Monday. It forecasts discounts of up to 30% off list price on electronics and 29% on toys. Sporting goods should see their best deals a day earlier, on Thanksgiving.

Fitness equipment and bedding are January buys instead. January’s sales are sparser overall, CR notes, but they tend to bring good prices on treadmills and other fitness equipment. Many stores also run white sales then, bedding promotions with big markdowns on sheets and pillows. So a laptop on your holiday list is worth buying on Cyber Monday, while a treadmill on the same list can wait for January.

For anything else on your list, such as a sofa, look up its price history, which charts what the item has cost over recent months. Buy if the price sits at or near its recent low, and otherwise wait for the item’s own yearly sale season.

Before Thanksgiving, mark each item on your list as a Black Friday buy or a January buy, and set a price alert on the rest.
