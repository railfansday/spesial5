With fans and competitors now looking to the 2027 Arnold Classic for the next big battle of the bodybuilding titans, last year’s winner could take to the stage with an even more impressive back. Recently, Chinedu Andrew Obiekea, who is better known to flex fans as “Andrew Jacked,” shared his training process—and it was boss level.

Undoubtedly, 2026 was a breakout year for the Nigerian French competitor. First, Jacked won the biggest prize in bodybuilding history, picking up a check for $750,000 for winning the Arnold Classic in Columbus, Ohio. Then days later, the big man won the Arnold UK, and picked up another $250,000 for his efforts. But with current and former Mr. Olympia’s like Nick Walker, Hadi Choopan, and potentially Samson Dauda hot on his heels, AJ is now in the process of jacking up his back to maintain his top position with the judges.

AJ jolts his back to adapt with this simple but seriously heavy workout.

“Yesszziirrr!!!,” came the trademark quote from AJ, as he shared his herculean efforts for more than a million followers on Instagram. Here’s how to try it for yourself:

AJ began with an unorthodox but rewarding version of the bent over row. Rather than use a dumbbell or barbell, the “Mona Lisa of bodybuilding,” stood behind the chest support on a T-bar row machine, gripping the frame for support, while taxing his working arm independently. By following the strict path of the machine, AJ maximized the load and strengthened his middle and lower trapezius (below his neck), while also building thickness in the side of his back working the lats and teres minor and major.

With his back now suitably blasted, Jacked continued the onslaught with the traditional standing barbell bent over row. Using a pronated (overhand) grip, AJ once again targeted the lats while also building his mid and upper back. Next up was the chest supported mid row, zeroing in on the upper back, including the traps and rhomboids (located between the shoulder blades and spine) Notice, in all of Andrew Jacked’s movements, how he likes to extend his arms to the very bottom of each lift, in order to find a maximum stretch before contracting his gigantic muscles. The lats then took another licking with cable lat pulldowns, while seated cable rows hit all the major muscle groups in the back.

To strengthen his posterior chain, Jacked performs back extensions at a 45-degree angle, building the entire back, glutes, and even his hamstrings. This exercise is particularly savvy, because it supports lumbar stability, improves hip mobility, and means that his other lifts may benefit while maintaining longevity to boot. To work his back even harder, the 2026 Arnold Classic champ pics up some weight plates to take with him on each extension.

When you attack the back from all angles, you will also recruit additional muscles, so to round out his session, Jacked focuses on his already stimulated biceps, and reps out some preacher curls to isolate his arms into exhaustion.

To try this workout for yourself, aim for 8 to 12 reps and perform 2 to 3 sets of each exercise. For the back extensions, start out without the extra weight, and bring it on when you feel comfortable. Yesszziirrr!
