Partners Behavioral Health Management: Maternal Health Care Management RN Supervisor (Remote NC)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

LOCATION: Remote – this is a home based, virtual position. The person in this position is required to maintain residency in North Carolina or within 40 miles of the NC border.

The Maternal Health Care Management RN (Maternal Health CM RN) Supervisor reports to the Care Management RN Director. This position provides clinical leadership and direct oversight of Maternal Health CM RN staff and ensures achievement of individual and team performance and productivity standards. The Maternal Health CM RN Supervisor implements process improvement interventions and develops staff in an environment that is motivating. This position promotes effective cross-organization communication and maintains collaborative relationships within the organization to achieve division objectives. The Maternal Health CM RN Supervisor maintains skills in care management, care coordination, disease management and patient transition management to allow for coverage during staffing shortages in the department. Performs other duties as assigned. Work is performed under the supervision of the CM RN Director with clinical oversight provided by the Care Management RN Director, Sr. Director of Integrated Care Management, and the Medical Operations team.
