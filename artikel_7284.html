Dun & Bradstreet: Pursuit & Channel Operations Manager (R-20201)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Pursuit & Channel Operations Manager, Public Sector maintains partners and contracting infrastructure and helps Account Executives use it effectively to secure wins. The role provides specialized execution capacity across longer-term opportunity identification, research and development, prime and partner outreach, teaming development, and day-to-day partner-channel operations.

The role combines public-sector pursuit support with disciplined channel administration. It develops actionable intelligence, recommends contracting and teaming approaches, coordinates required agreements and documentation, and identifies opportunities 12–24 months ahead so Account Executives can focus on mature, near-term pursuits.

Support larger pursuits as part of Account Executive-led deal teams by challenging assumptions, bringing relevant intelligence forward, identifying competitive vulnerabilities, and recommending actions.

Research contracts and acquisition approaches; assess incumbents, contract vehicles, timing, and buying patterns; and recommend vehicle, prime/subcontractor, and teaming options for specific pursuits.

Identify and engage prime contractors and partners for deal-specific teaming, and contact contracting officers and agency contacts to gather information and advance pursuits.

Prepare, route, and track nondisclosure agreements, contractor teaming arrangements, teaming agreements, and related documentation; coordinate with value-added resellers, resellers, and distributors supporting Account Executives.

Manage day-to-day channel and partner operations, including partner nondisclosure agreements, market-development-fund contracts, onboarding, and distribution documentation; maintain a current roster of partner contacts and regular working relationships.

Keep partners carrying D&B General Services Administration SKUs current on offerings, pricing, and pending modifications.

Partner with the Alliance Manager, Legal, Contracts, Pricing, and Sales to keep channel activity moving and escalate issues early.

Use government contract-data and market-intelligence tools to identify and qualify opportunities beyond published solicitations, including opportunities 12–24 months from anticipated procurement.

Respond to research requests from Sales teams by delivering concise, actionable findings and recommendations.

Enter and maintain longer-term opportunities on behalf of the team, then transition ownership to the appropriate Account Executive as the pursuit matures.

Bachelor’s degree in Business or a related field.

At least 5 years of relevant experience in public-sector business development, pursuit support, channel operations, government contracting, capture support, or sales operations.

Working knowledge of government contracting and federal procurement processes.

Familiarity with federal contract vehicles, teaming arrangements, and third-party government contracting tools and resources.

Experience supporting capture, business development, pursuit, or government contracting activities.

Strong partner and stakeholder communication skills, including comfort engaging with teaming artners, prime contractors, contracting officers, and agency contacts.

Strong organizational and administrative skills, with attention to detail in trackers, contracting documentation, partner records, and follow-through.

Ability to conduct structured research, synthesize information from multiple sources, and develop concise, actionable recommendations.

Ability to contribute informed perspectives and recommendations to capture and deal teams.

Strong prioritization and time-management skills, with the ability to manage multiple competing requests and deadlines.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite skills.

Show an ownership mindset, be a problem solver, be curious and be inspired to take action, be proactive, seek ways to collaborate and connect with people and teams in support of driving success.

Continuous growth mindset, keep learning through social experiences and relationships with stakeholders, experts, colleagues and mentors as well as widen and broaden your competencies through structural courses and programs.

Fluency in English and languages relevant to the working market.

Ability to sit, speak and operate telephone and/or computer for long periods of time

Ability to handle pressure, stressful conditions, and conflict resolution

Ability to work day, evening and/or weekend hours as needed

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
