The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel Gets Official Update For Skyrim's Anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

After eight extremely long years since its initial announcement, The Elder Scrolls VI finally broke cover the other day to give the waiting masses a title for the game they've been so desperate to see more of. Sentinel, as it is now known, is likely taking us back to Hammerfell, and it will be really interesting to see the important region in a modern game from the series.

Outside of that, though, we still don't really know anything about the game, or what it will look and play like. That's because it is almost certainly still a couple of years away from being in our hands at a minimum, and the announcement of the title was likely just to placate us for as long as they can, for the most part.

Unfortunately, it seems as though we have now had confirmation that nothing is coming for a while, and it comes from the developer themselves.

As first reported by ComicBook.com, a post on Reddit is drawing attention right now as it has attracted Bethesda themselves, who stopped by to put pay to a particular rumor that's circulating. Specifically, several members of the community felt that a full reveal could be on the way to mark the 15th anniversary of the release of Skyrim, which actually dropped today (at the time of writing), November 11, 2011.

Unfortunately, that's absolutely not going to be the case, as Bethesda's official Reddit profile shared the following response to quash that burgeoning speculation:

"Hey everyone, wanted to pop in to say that we aren’t going to have any big announcements tomorrow. We’re excited you’re excited but we will not be sharing anything new on Sentinel for some time," the comment read.

As you might expect, that led to humorous conspiratorial responses from the community, who felt as though this was simply Bethesda obfuscating their plans.

"This sub getting so unhinged the last few days that Bethesda has had to literally step in has made me so proud lmao." one comment read.

While another added, "I'm reading this as 'Wanted to let you know we are launching tomorrow. We are excited to keep you busy in Sentinel for some time'".

The recent teaser for the game gave us a look at a titular Elder Scroll being revealed from underneath sand which, while it doesn't reveal much on the face of it, could provide certain clues. One leading theory is that it symbolizes the Alik'r Desert, which stretches across the northwestern part of the province of Hammerfell, and could easily serve as a hidden location for the scroll.

That said, there is lots of existing lore around the Elder Scrolls that should mean they aren't able to be simply wielded by the player, or anyone else for that matter. It will be interesting to see how Bethesda puts them at the center of the narrative in Sentinel, as it relates to our character, without overriding much of the lore that made them compelling in the first place.
