The Best Fubo Deals, Including Free ESPN Unlimited, Hulu, FOX One, and Disney+

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Plenty of content has been migrating to streaming services for years now, but some of the most popular programming is still only available on traditional TV. From sports like NFL, college football, and NBA games to awards shows, some content is still most easily accessible via a traditional channel guide.

Luckily, if you don’t want a traditional cable or satellite package, there are now a handful of standout live TV streaming services that beat those dying cable boxes with better pricing, more streamlined packages, and viewing flexibility.

One of those standout live TV streaming services is Fubo. The popular service offers hundreds of channels in a selection of well-priced packages. If you’re looking to ditch cable — or find a way to access channels you lost by cutting the cord — it’s a great option.

Fubo offers more than 250 channels in its top-tier subscription. These channels include network stations ABC, CBS, and FOX, plus all of the most popular cable channels and regional sports networks (in certain packages). Cable channels include standouts like ESPN, FS1, Fox News, MSNBC, HGTV, CNN, Bravo, and Hallmark Channel. The only notable absence on Fubo is TNT. See Fubo’s full channel lineup here.

Fubo plans start at $64.99 a month, and the service’s most expensive package is $98.99 a month. Similar top-tier packages from other streamers can easily surpass $100 a month, making Fubo’s pricing very competitive.

Here’s a quick breakdown of Fubo’s (English-language) package offerings:

Sports + News: Fubo’s Sports + News plan is a streamlined, affordable option that costs $64.99 a month. You get 30+ channels, including local stations (ABC, CBS, NBC, and FOX), Fox News, and sports channels like ESPN, FS1, and NFL Network.

Pro: Fubo’s Pro plan costs $88.99 a month and offers a great balance of price and channel availability. It includes local channels, regional sports networks, cable sports channels, news stations, and a huge selection of entertainment channels.

Elite: Fubo’s largest package is Elite, which offers over 250 channels and — unlike the options above — 4K UHD high-resolution streaming for certain content. It costs $98.99 a month.

Another important note: all Fubo packages offer unlimited DVR recording and streaming on up to 10 screens at a time. That’s a better deal than any other live TV streaming service, and frankly, it’s shocking that both perks are available in all packages.

Fubo is currently offering deals on all of its plans. New users can get a discount of up to $30 off their first month, depending on the plan they select. You can get $25 off your first month with the Pro plan and $30 off your first month with the Elite plan.
