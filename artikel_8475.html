Personal Consumer Issues • Re: How to get Total Return in Google Sheets?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I calculate the total return in Google Sheets by grabbing the adjusted close price between two dates.

I use the Tiingo financial markets API to retrieve the adjusted close price. Register for an account here https://www.tiingo.com/. The free account is limited to 50 API requests per hour, which is plenty for my needs.

Then I use this formula in Google Sheets to calculate the total return for a symbol (cell B1) between two dates (cells B2 & B3). You'll need to put your Tiingo API token into the formula for it to work.

Code: 

=LET(  data, IMPORTDATA("https://api.tiingo.com/tiingo/daily/" & B1 & "/prices?startDate=" & TEXT(B2,"yyyy-mm-dd") & "&endDate=" & TEXT(B3,"yyyy-mm-dd") & "&format=csv&token=YOUR_TIINGO_API_TOKEN"),  adj, INDEX(data,0,7),  n, ROWS(adj),  INDEX(adj,n) / INDEX(adj,2) - 1)
