Carson Beck injury: Arizona Cardinals downgrade rookie QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Arizona Cardinals rookie quarterback Carson Beck will not get a second consecutive start. After being ruled questionable with a back injury on Friday on the team's final injury report of Week 11, the team announced Beck was downgraded to out on Saturday.

Because the Cardinals had to travel to Kansas City to face the Chiefs on Sunday, the announcement likely means that Beck didn't even make the trip with the team.

Beck suffered back spasms this week. He not on the injury report on Wednesday, but he was added on Thursday when he was limited due to the back spasms. He did not practice on Friday, and head coach Mike LaFleur announced that they were moving forward with the assumption that 40-year-old Josh Johnson would start for the Cardinals against the Chiefs. On Friday, he didn't rule out the possibility that Beck could play, but the door officially shut on Saturday.

Beck passed for 118 yards and two interceptions last week in his NFL debut against the Los Angeles Rams, a 34-6 loss.

Johnson, signed on Nov. 10, will make his Cardinals debut. It will be his 12th career start since he entered the NFL in 2008.

Joey Aguilar will be his backup, and receiver Devin Duvernay will be the Cardinals' emergency quarterback.
