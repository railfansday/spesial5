Celtics could be without two starters against Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

SACRAMENTO — The Celtics could be without Jayson Tatum and Derrick White when they face the Sacramento Kings on Saturday night.

Tatum is listed as questionable due to rest, while White is listed as questionable with a left AC joint contusion (the acromioclavicular joint connects the collarbone to the top of the shoulder blade).

The Celtics are playing on the first night of a back-to-back; they face the Kings on Saturday at 10pm ET, and then the Golden State Warriors on Sunday at 8:30pm ET.

White has yet to miss any games this season; he's averaging 12.7 points, 5 assists, and 3 rebounds per game, while shooting 42.5% from the field and 30.6% from three.

Tatum has missed one game to rest; he's averaging 26.4 points, 6.8 assists, and 6.6 rebounds, shooting 42.8% from the field and 33.3% from three.

Baylor Scheierman has started two games this season and would be a likely candidate to start in the event that either Tatum or White is sidelined. Scheierman is averaging 5.7 points, 3.7 rebounds, and 1.5 assists in 19.4 minutes per game.

Payton Pritchard, who has yet to start a game this season, could also potentially start. Pritchard is averaging 13.7 points, 3.8 assists, and 3.1 rebounds in 27.3 minutes per game, the fourth-most on the team.

It's also possible that Jordan Walsh will see a reduced role on Saturday; Walsh, who has started 10 of 15 games this year, played less than 5 minutes in Wednesday's loss to the Nuggets.

In that event, Sam Hauser or Hugo Gonzalez could see increased opportunities; Hauser is averaging 8.4 points and 3.1 rebounds in 20.3 minutes per game, while Gonzalez is averaging 3.9 points and 2.5 rebounds in 12.9 minutes per game. Hauser has been lights out from three so far this season, shooting 46.8% from beyond the arc.

When they face the Celtics, the Kings will be playing on the second night of a back-to-back; they face the Los Angeles Lakers on Friday night. The Kings have the second-worst record in the NBA at 1-13.

Celtics-Kings tips off at 10pm ET on Saturday night.
