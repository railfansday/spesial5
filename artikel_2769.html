Barlow looks set for season debut with Sixers after long days, ‘miserable' weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Barlow looks set for season debut with Sixers after long days, 'miserable' weeks originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Dominick Barlow is on the verge of his season debut with the Sixers.

The team listed Barlow as probable for its Saturday afternoon matchup with the Bucks at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

Barlow practiced Friday and has been making clear progress in his recent on-court work recovering from an injury formally classified as a "lower back stress reaction."

He was a reliable rotation fixture last season, playing 71 regular-season games and starting 59. Barlow posted 7.7 points, 4.8 rebounds, 1.2 assists and 0.9 steals per contest in 23.8 minutes per game. The Sixers then made a common-sense call to exercise his $3.4 million option for the 2026-27 season.

The 23-year-old described his back injury as "an overuse thing."

"I guess there was an area or maybe I started my (offseason) workouts too early," he said. "I don't know what it was fully in terms of what caused it. Once it happened, it happened. Nothing I can do about it besides try to correct it and get my body feeling as good as possible."

He called the past few weeks "miserable" as he rehabbed and waited on the green light by the Sixers' training staff.

"The days just felt longer," Barlow said. "Trying to be engaged mentally, which I think I've done a good job with, but the physical aspect wasn't there. I'm somebody that's prided himself on competing, being available. Not being able to do that was hard, but I'm excited to kind of put this past me."

With LeBron James and Jaylen Brown on the team now, Barlow won't start at forward when the Sixers are at full strength.

He's still a multi-dimensional frontcourt piece for Sixers head coach Nick Nurse who may very well get substantial opportunities at backup center.

"Just cutting, playing hard, being ready to make the open shot," Barlow said. "And then I don't think you can ever have too much defensive versatility. I think that's something I can bring, try to help us be more versatile."

When Barlow checks into his first game of the season, he'll appreciate the moment.

"You don't really realize what you have until it's not there (anymore)," he said. "I'm just excited to play. …. Just playing the first stint … that's going to be the main thing I'm looking forward to."
