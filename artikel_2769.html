Observations after Sixers come back for strong shorthanded victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Observations after Sixers come back for strong shorthanded victory originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Sixers stretched their winning streak to four games and snagged a strong road win Wednesday night over the Magic.

They earned a 121-116 victory in Orlando to improve to 13-3 on the season. The Magic dropped to 6-8.

Joel Embiid had 26 points, seven assists, six rebounds, three blocks and two steals.

VJ Edgecombe posted a season-high 24 points, eight boards and six assists.

LeBron James scored 22 points and Anfernee Simons added 18.

Magic star Franz Wagner had 27 points, 11 assists and eight rebounds.

The Sixers had a long injury list in Orlando. Tyrese Maxey (right foot soreness), Jaylen Brown (left hamstring strain) and Dean Wade (left hamstring soreness/tightness) were among their players out. Sixers head coach Nick Nurse told reporters in Orlando that Brown will be re-evaluated when the team returns to Philadelphia.

Orlando was without star forward Paolo Banchero (right high ankle sprain).

The Sixers will start a three-game homestand when they face the Bucks on Saturday afternoon. Here are observations on their win over the Magic:

The Sixers' undermanned starting lineup was Edgecombe, Simons, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, James and Embiid.

After scoring 37 points in the Sixers' win over the Nets, Embiid had a subpar start. He began 1 for 6 from the floor and committed a turnover that led to an Anthony Black dunk.

The Sixers' lineup looked exactly like a unit that hadn't built any chemistry yet. The team was not sharp early on either end of the floor.

Desmond Bane drained a long three-pointer to give Orlando a 27-12 advantage. Orlando made 11 of its first 14 field goals and led by as many as 17 points in the first quarter.

Labaron Philon Jr. and Justin Edwards joined the Sixers' rotation. So did two-way contract players Tacko Fall and Caleb Love.

Philon impressed in the first quarter, converting two floaters and a corner three. The rookie passed his previous NBA scoring high of five points and finished the night with 10 on 4-for-5 shooting.

Fall played 10 minutes and posted two points, three rebounds and three blocks. He slammed in a dunk and rejected two shots early in the second quarter. Credit to Fall for giving the Sixers a good backup center performance and doing his job as a rim-protecting ultra-big man.

Love entered in the second quarter and was ready to fire, hitting a catch-and-shoot three-pointer on his first touch.

The Sixers' zone defense also helped them eat into the Magic's lead. The zone seemed to disrupt Orlando's offensive rhythm and it was active enough to prevent the Magic from consistently creating high-quality looks.

Edgecombe's buzzer-beating mid-range jumper cut the Sixers' deficit to 69-64 after two quarters.

The Sixers pulled within a single point on multiple occasions in the third quarter, including on a Philon three late in the period. They trailed by five points after the third.

Fall continued to play well early in the fourth quarter. He deterred the Magic's drivers from getting all the way to the rim as the very large back line of the Sixers' zone defense and made eye-catching hustle plays on both sides of the ball.

The Sixers outscored Orlando by seven points on the night with Fall on the court. Soon after Embiid subbed back in, he evened the game at 104-all with two free throws. Jabari Walker swished a three to finally push the Sixers in front.

The Magic regained a late lead with a Jalen Suggs three. James then drove and kicked the ball out to Edgecombe, who nailed one of his customary clutch jumpers to put the Sixers back on top.

No matter the night-to-night personnel, the Sixers have been fantastic at the tail end of close games. They're now 11-0 in clutch situations, which the NBA defines as games within a five-point differential in the final five minutes.
