Jalen Brunson ‘can’t believe’ he has to try to end Taylor Swift-Knicks controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jalen Brunson is looking to put the Taylor Swift-Knicks fan narrative to bed.

Five months after the mega-pop star's Game 4 NBA Finals appearance and weeks following Tyler Kolek's jab, the Knicks star and Finals MVP told Us Magazine that Swift is "obviously" welcome at Madison Square Garden.

"I can't believe I have to say this," he said. "But I didn't say what was said. Being one of the leaders of the team — the captain of the team — I guess I now have to help somebody out. Yes, she's a member of the Knicks fandom. Yes, she is."

Singer Taylor Swift celebrates with friends during Game 4 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden in New York, Wednesday, June 10, 2026. Jason Szenes for the New York Post

Debate over Swift's NBA loyalty began when she was spotted at Game 3 of the Eastern Conference finals in Cleveland alongside her Ohio-born husband, Travis Kelce.

She was not wearing Knicks gear at the game as Kelce cheered on the Cavaliers — on their way to getting swept by the Knicks.

Weeks later Swift made an appearance at Game 4 of the Finals, witnessing an historic 29-point comeback and OG Anunoby's game-winning tip-in, while wearing a blue "Stevie Knicks" shirt alongside Mariska Hargitay and Haim sisters. Alana and Este.

The 14-time Grammy Award-winner cheered wildly with several viral reactions throughout the thrilling night.

Months later — and after Swift and Kelce's wedding at Madison Square Garden on July 3 — Kolek brought life to the debate again when he answered that Swift was the "fakest" celebrity fan of the newly crowned champions.

When asked the question during an NBA 2K TikTok livestream, the backup guard said: "At the Knicks games, I would say it's very performative because they just want to be seen at the game. They're not necessarily fans."

Singer Taylor Swift walks to her seat before Game 4 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden in New York, Wednesday, June 10, 2026. Charles Wenzelberg / New York Post

"Maybe Taylor Swift? I don't know if I've seen her at any other games," he added. "I guess she has a lot going on so she can't come all the time. But it's a little performative."

Following the comment, Swifties called out Kolek's playing time — or lack thereof — during the Finals.

"Tyler Kolek and Taylor Swift played the same amount of minutes in the 2026 NBA Finals," one fan wrote on X.

"If there's one thing Taylor and tyler have in common is that they were both sitting court side during the finals," another said.

New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson (11) reacts after hitting a three-point shot against the Indiana Pacers on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, at MetLife Stadium. Jason Szenes for NY Post

A Knicks spokesperson released a statement in support of Swift's fandom.

"Taylor Swift has been a Knicks fan for many years, has a long history with Madison Square Garden, and we are incredibly appreciative of her support of the organization," the statement read.

Days later, Kolek apologized for the comment and admitted he is a fan.

"It's just a question that was asked for me, that maybe I answered in the wrong way. Take that on the chin," Kolek said. "I love her music. We were just playing it five minutes ago, and I'm singing every word to every song. Nothing against her at all.

The Knicks (11-6) host the Cavaliers on Wednesday night followed by the Heat and Hawks throughout the Thanksgiving weekend.

The defending champs are riding a five-game winning streak after taking down the Pacers, 113-106, on Sunday.
