Josh McDaniels draws on past experience as Patriots turn to Tommy DeVito

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This is not Josh McDaniels' first rodeo with a backup quarterback.

Throughout his coaching career, McDaniels has called plenty of games with a backup under center. Perhaps most notably, he was the offensive coordinator in 2008 when Matt Cassel took over for Tom Brady after the quarterback tore his ACL.

Now, McDaniels is leaning on those experiences as his offense prepares to hand the reins to Tommy DeVito.

"There's always a way to win, and you have to," McDaniels said Wednesday. "Right now, the challenge is the formula may be slightly different when you change quarterbacks. But that's our job, is to try to find the right formula for this group, given our current situation, which we have a lot of confidence in."

McDaniels said the first two days of practice have gone well with DeVito filling in for the injured Drake Maye. The 28-year-old has brought energy and confidence to the position, while McDaniels and the offensive coaching staff have worked to build a game plan around what the new starter is most comfortable executing.

"I think the biggest thing you can do is focus on the person that's going to play now and not worry about the things that maybe you've been doing or don't have as much repetition in. What you have to do is now put a plan together that you have confidence in and he has confidence in, most importantly," McDaniels said.

"Being fluid is important, and making good decisions along the way in the process of each week is going to be an important part of our operation and making sure when we get to Sunday, whatever it is we're calling, whatever it is we're trying to execute, we know he has full faith in doing it and the team does, too."

After Maye suffered a fractured fibula in the Patriots' Week 10 loss to the Detroit Lions, DeVito struggled in relief. He completed just 12-of-24 passes for 81 yards with one touchdown and one interception.

Head coach Mike Vrabel and DeVito have expressed confidence that the quarterback will be better with an opportunity to prepare as the starter. McDaniels shares that optimism, particularly now that he has experience calling a game with DeVito and additional time to prepare coming out of the bye week.

"There's a lot of things Tommy does well," McDaniels said. "We have a week or two weeks to plan. We have repetitions. We have things that we can correct in practice. We have things that we like. We have things that maybe we don't like as much. We'll hold that for next week. So it's a totally different process now that you're going to go into the game this way.

"But he's delivered the ball well all year. He's thrown the ball well in the spring. He threw it well in training camp. Arm strength is not a problem, as you know and the guys know. He's made plays with his legs. He's a smart guy, and he's a competitive guy. So we like all those things about him."

The Patriots face an uphill climb moving forward after back-to-back losses in which their defense surrendered 80 combined points, compounded by the injury to Maye.

Still, with seven games remaining, New England sits just one game out of a Wild Card spot. The opportunity to make a playoff push remains in front of the Patriots, but doing so will require DeVito and the rest of the roster to elevate their play.

"I know this, the energy has been fantastic and it should be," McDaniels said. "It's just the 11th game of the season. We have a lot to look forward to, a lot to play for, and we have a lot of confidence in the guys that are in the huddle."
