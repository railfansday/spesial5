Ilia Malinin wins Saatva Skate America for a fourth time to start post-Olympic season

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

EVERETT, Washington — To start the previous Olympic cycle, Ilia Malinin announced his presence on the senior international stage by winning 2022 Skate America with the first quadruple Axel in Grand Prix figure skating history.

To start this new Olympic cycle, he won Skate America for a fourth time, but this time without the quad Axel as he challenges himself artistically more than ever before.

Malinin, the three-time world champion who was eighth at the Olympics, topped Friday's short program and was second in Saturday's free skate to win in his first event of the season.

He totaled 284.31 points, winning by 9.58 over Daniel Grassl of Italy. Grassl edged Malinin by 68 hundredths in the free skate.

Tamir Kuperman of Israel was third, making the podium in his senior Grand Prix debut.

Two-time U.S. Olympian Jason Brown was fourth in his first competition since placing eighth at last January's nationals. It matches Brown's best Grand Prix result in five years.

Donovan Carrillo was fifth in one of if not the best result for Mexico in any top-level international competition in a Winter Olympic sport.

Malinin competes again in two weeks at NHK Trophy in Japan, where a finish anywhere on the podium will likely send him to the biggest competition of the fall — December's six-skater Grand Prix Final in China, which Malinin won each of the last three years.

He has work to do. His total score from Skate America ranks him fourth in the world this season.

While Malinin's 14-event win streak was snapped at the Olympics, he has won eight Grand Prix events in a row dating to 2023.

Skate America finishes Sunday with the free dance and women's free skate from 2-6 p.m. ET, live on NBC and Peacock.
