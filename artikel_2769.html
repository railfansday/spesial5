AFC playoff picture: Projecting the 3 wild card spots, including the Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The AFC playoff picture is beginning to take shape as the season progresses. Several of the division leaders are starting to separate themselves, like the Buffalo Bills in the AFC East. Beyond those division contenders, the competition for the wild card spots is getting particularly interesting.

If the playoffs started today, the division winners would be the Buffalo Bills, Kansas City Chiefs, Jacksonville Jaguars, and Cincinnati Bengals. The wild card spots would go to the Denver Broncos, Pittsburgh Steelers, and Cleveland Browns. The next teams on the bubble would be the New England Patriots, Las Vegas Raiders, and Baltimore Ravens.

Three of the four AFC North teams would currently be in the playoffs, with just the Ravens on the outside looking in. With several games remaining between AFC North teams, a lot can still change in the division. At this point, it seems likely that the Broncos, Steelers, and Browns will claim the final three spots, but the Patriots could still sneak in. They have some challenging opponents remaining on their schedule, such as the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs, but they also have some very winnable games against teams like the Los Angeles Chargers and Miami Dolphins.
