See all Gadsden-area AHSAA Round 3 games on the Alabama high school football schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

High school football is rolling along in the Gadsden area as Round 3 of the playoffs take center stage.

Westbrook Christian will head down to Tuscaloosa to face Tuscaloosa Academy. The winner of that matchup will travel to Mobile on Dec. 2 for the Private A Championship. The Warriors are in the semifinals for the second time and trying to win the program's first AHSAA state title.

The regular season ran through October before the playoffs start in November. The AHSAA football championships are Dec. 1-5 in Mobile, one of many changes this season. The biggest shakeup was the public-private split and ensuing fallout that created two classes and got rid of 7A.

Most of the Gadsden-area football programs are available to stream on the NFHS Network. It is a subscription service that cost $11.99 but gives access to all content on the site across all sports, so you can keep up with volleyball as well this fall.

It's up to the schools to provide the stream, so it's not a guarantee all games in all sports are available, but most area schools look to have at least some football games on the site.
