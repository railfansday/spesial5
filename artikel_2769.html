ESPN’s College GameDay heads to Winston-Salem for Wake Forest vs. Duke

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ESPN's College GameDay is heading back to Winston-Salem as the Wake Forest Demon Deacons host the Duke Blue Devils in the regular-season finale.

GameDay has made the trip to Wake Forest one other time. That came in 2020, when Wake Forest opened the season against No. 1 Clemson. Because of COVID-19 restrictions, in-person fan attendance at the broadcast was limited.

Currently, the Deacons are 10-1 this season and heading to the ACC championship game. Duke sits at 7-4 after a loss to Clemson Friday night.

Kickoff for Saturday's game at Allegacy Financial Field is set for either 3 or 3:30 p.m. on ESPN.
