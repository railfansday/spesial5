Jaden Dugger pick-six puts 49ers up 28-10 early in third quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Things are starting to get out of hand in Santa Clara.

On the first play from scrimmage in the second half, Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins fired a pick-six to 49ers linebacker Jaden Dugger.

That made the score 28-10, San Francisco.

Cousins dropped the snap before getting back up, looking to his right, and trying to fire the ball to Tre’ Tucker. But Dugger stepped in front of the ball and had an easy pick.

He then didn’t have anyone in his path as he made his way to the end zone, pushing the 49ers’ lead to 18 points.

Cousins didn’t have all that many opportunities in the first half. But he’ll have to be better in the second to put the Raiders back in a position to make the game competitive.
