This is not meant to sound like a brag, but it inevitably will. Tons of luxurious products come across our desks; as such, I’ve developed especially discerning taste. I’ve tested more than my fair share of $500 colognes, $700 headphones, and $1,000 espresso machines over the years. Not that luxury is synonymous with price, but you know. While these occupational hazards might make me a challenge to shop for, they also give me a discerning eye for gifts that will actually impress my fellow snobs.

If they’re a coffee connoisseur who only drinks single-origin, always espresso, consider upgrading their at-home setup with Meraki’s dual-boiler espresso machine. For the audiophile whose vinyl collection rivals that of an actual record store, a Fluance turntable will do their records justice. And if their vanity would put a Saks to shame, an under-the-radar Creed fragrance and an award-winning red light mask are worthy additions.

The last thing they want is a bad interface or those ugly Alexa models. Thankfully, 2026 is the year Apple entered the home display market.

For a gift that will impress even the most particular of coffee connoisseurs, go with Meraki’s second-gen espresso machine. Featuring a built-in grinder, scales, and a dual-boiler system, it’s one of the most premium machines money can buy.

The ultimate snob’s wine opener. A needle and a bit of fancy pump work allow you to pour a glass without ever uncorking the bottle. Great for by-the-glass or sparing yourself any cork emergencies with older vintages.

Dubbed our top pick for audiophiles, this turntable has it all: consistently great audio quality, a built-in speed control mechanism, and an attractive wood finish.

Aesthetics and audio quality don’t always go hand-in-hand for headphones, which is precisely why this Bang & Olufsen pair is a standout. Our editors named it the best pair of noise-canceling headphones for sound quality, and it’s easily the most attractive model on the list.

If they’ve ever slept at one of the world’s best hotels, they have slept on Frette sheets and worn a Frette robe. Give them that luxury at home.

Ah, the status hand soap. A gift that works for virtually anyone, especially those with discerning taste. This pick from Nécessaire somehow feels less sceney than other options on the market, and it’s infused with skin-softening ingredients like vitamin B3 and linoleic acid.
