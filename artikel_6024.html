Trump signs proclamation to prevent changes to Oval Office, other White House rooms

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

President Donald Trump offers an exclusive look at the White House ballroom project, discussing its innovative design and its role as a secure venue for formal events.

President Donald Trump signed a proclamation Thursday designating the Oval Office, the Outer Oval Office and the Cabinet Room as a national monument and federal landmark, a move intended to preserve the spaces and prevent future administrations from altering them.

"It won't be able to be taken down, destroyed, touched or anything else," Trump told reporters from the Oval Office. "It’s actually called a national monument. It’s a federal landmark. When completed … it'll make it impossible for anybody ever to destroy this incredible space. "

Fox News Digital has reached out to the White House.

The proclamation is part of a broader series of post-election public appearances highlighting White House construction initiatives.

On Monday, Trump detailed planned updates during remarks in front of the Reflecting Pool. On Wednesday, he held a press briefing at the construction site of a new White House ballroom.

Since returning to office in 2025, Trump has prioritized several high-profile architectural and cultural projects, including a proposed 250-foot triumphal arch near Arlington National Cemetery and proposals to rename and restructure the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts.
