Michael Carrick facing Manchester United axe amid review by Jason Wilcox

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Director of football in discussions with Omar Berrada

Pochettino and Howe on radar as possible replacements

Manchester United are moving towards sacking Michael Carrick, though no final decision has been made by Jason Wilcox, the director of football, who is leading the review after the head coach was given the Chelsea, Roma and Aston Villa games to turn results around.

Wilcox has been consulting with Omar Berrada, the chief executive, before a recommendation regarding Carrick is made to Sir Jim Ratcliffe, who controls sporting policy.

If Carrick is removed, Mauricio Pochettino has emerged as a potential candidate as it is understood the USA head coach is open to the position, while sources indicate that Eddie Howe also remains on the radar of Wilcox too.

The former Newcastle manager is taking a break from the game but would consider the job if offered it. United are in the Premier League relegation zone and have three victories in 15 games in all competitions. Carrick came close to being removed after the 3-0 defeat by Bournemouth on 25 October. But after Wilcox and Berrada met the following day at the training ground to discuss his position, Carrick was given the three games until the international break to prove he and the players could reverse a dismal start.

A defeat, a win and a draw in those matches plus the team’s displays in each compounded with the overall run of form has moved Wilcox and Berrada towards a position where they are minded to remove Carrick. There is a growing expectation within the club that he will lose his job.

The final decision is yet to be made, though. When contacted on Monday night by the Guardian the club declined to comment.

In what may prove Carrick’s final three games in charge, United lost 2-0 at Chelsea, beat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford and drew 4-4 at home against Villa on Sunday. In each game United were uneven. At Chelsea, Carrick’s side impressed in the first half but failed to convert chances before conceding twice after the interval, the second from a penalty after Lisandro Martínez was sent off.

In the Roma victory they were 2-0 behind in a poor opening 45 minutes before three goals in a rousing second one. On Sunday United twice trailed by two goals and entered added time 4-2 down, strikes from Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu rescuing a point.

After the Roma game Carrick urged his team to build momentum but within four minutes against Villa they were 2-0 down, continuing a pattern of conceding very early.
