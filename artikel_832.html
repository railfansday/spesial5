Kate Middleton Reveals Theme of Her Annual Christmas Carol Concert

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

As Kate Middleton and Prince William prepare for the holidays, the theme for her annual “Together at Christmas” carol service at Westminster Abbey has been revealed.

Kate Middleton is already preparing for the most wonderful time of the year.

This December will mark the sixth time that the Princess of Wales has held her annual “Together at Christmas” service at Westminster Abbey, marking the holidays with an evening of carols and giving back.

Over 1,500 people attend the service every Christmas at the same location where Kate and Prince William tied the knot back in 2011. And the Abbey has continued to serve as an important landmark in the couple’s journey as a family, with their children Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 8, often joining their parents for the annual holiday celebration.

In addition to being an evening of carols, however, every year “Together at Christmas” also honors individuals around the United Kingdom who have gone above and beyond to help support those in their local communities and make them “feel supported, connected and less alone.”

This year’s event, according to an announcement from Kate and William’s The Royal Foundation, will “celebrate the power of love and kindness to bring people together, creating hope, connection and a shared sense of community during the Christmas season.”

“At the heart of this year’s services is the belief that small acts of care can have an extraordinary impact,” the announcement continued. “Whether through friendship, volunteering, neighbourliness or simply being there to listen, the services will recognise the many ways people help create environments where others feel safe, valued and able to reach out when they need support.”

In addition to the event at Westminster Abbey, there will be 15 carol services happening concurrently in local communities across the U.K., ensuring individuals who have gone above and beyond across all four nations are recognized for their efforts.

The announcement confirmed the “cherished” tradition will air in the U.K. on ITV1 and ITVX on Christmas Eve.”

The annual celebration marks a rare instance in which William and Kate step out as a family of five, with all three of their children. And not only does it provide a sweet look at their family bond as George, Charlotte and Louis grow older, but the event also allows for the couple’s children to show off their special talents.

In fact, last year’s broadcast saw a special duet from Charlotte and Kate, the mother-daughter duo sitting side by side at the piano to perform "Holm Sound" by Erland Cooper.

Alongside the piece of music, narration from Kate shared an emotional message about the spirit of the holidays.

"At its heart, Christmas speaks of love taking form in the simplest, most human ways," she said. "Not in sentimental or grand gestures, but gentle ones: a moment of listening, a word of comfort, a friendly conversation, a helping hand, presence. These simple acts of care might seem small, but they contribute to the beautiful tapestry of life to which we all belong."
