Inside Francesca Allen’s New Publication, Hair

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Last year, Francesca Allen brought Plaukai (Lithuanian for “hair”) to PhotoVogue Festival’s Women by Women, taking us into the world of a traditional long-hair competition in Lithuania. Now, she returns to the subject, continuing her exploration of the many ways women define themselves through hair.

For her new publication, Hair, Allen travelled to Las Vegas, where women from around the world gathered to compete for the inaugural title of Miss Rapunzel. She followed the contestants beyond the competition itself, from hotel rooms to casino floors and, finally, the stage where their hair is measured. Her photographs capture the curious rituals and unexpected intimacies of a pageant devoted entirely to hair.

Designed by Adam Ridgeway, Hair features a text by Claire Marie Healy and will be released as a limited edition in Paris on 12 November, during Paris Photo.

Francesca Allen’s practice spans photography and film, often centering on intimate depictions of girlhood and the complexities of coming of age. Her work has appeared in publications including AnOther, Dazed, i-D, M Le Monde, Vogue and ZEITmagazin, and has been exhibited at institutions such as the Grand Palais in Paris, BOZAR in Brussels, Somerset House and The Photographers’ Gallery in London. She is also the author of I’d Like to Get to Know You (2022) and Aya (2018). She lives and works in London.
