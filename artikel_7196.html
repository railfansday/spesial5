Collibra: Commercial Account Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Our customers are under pressure to deliver more value from their data while building a strong foundation for AI. As an Account Manager, you’ll help them connect those priorities to their investment in Collibra.

You’ll own the commercial relationship with a portfolio of existing customers, with responsibility for renewals and expansion. You’ll build relationships across each account, understand what customers need to achieve, and develop a clear plan to retain and grow their business.

Working closely with Customer Success, Customer Engineering and other teams across Collibra, you’ll connect customer outcomes with commercial opportunities. That includes strengthening adoption of our data governance products and identifying where our AI products can address additional business needs.
