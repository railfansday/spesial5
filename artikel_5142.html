Hoskinson Credits Cardano’s Staking Model After Binance Hack Leaves Network Unaffected

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Charles Hoskinson has credited the underlying architecture for keeping Cardano (ADA) and Midnight (NIGHT) unaffected by the recent Binance hack, arguing that years of deliberate design decisions are now paying off.

The Cardano founder acknowledged that the network’s early focus on emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, liquid non-custodial staking, and the need for Layer-2 (L2) solutions built around its Extended UTXO (EUTXO) model, came in handy during the hacking incident.

Hoskinson went further, saying these were not accidental advantages but deliberate choices based on anticipating the blockchain industry’s evolving needs. Furthermore, he suggested that the recent hacking demonstrated the importance of building networks with security and resilience at their core.

Cardano’s staking model continues to grow, with the Cardano Foundation reporting that 57.6% of ADA’s circulating supply is staked.

Notably, every delegated coin backs a stake pool that helps produce blocks and secure the network.

Unlike traditional staking models that lock up assets, Cardano’s liquid staking allows holders to delegate their ADA while retaining full control. As a result, they can spend, transfer, or move their funds without first having to unstake them.

Therefore, this approach is unique because it combines network participation with financial flexibility while supporting a decentralized pool ecosystem. Additionally, it reduces reliance on centralized custodians, boosting the network’s broader resilience.

Meanwhile, Cardano is expanding its real-world utility since police in Uttar Pradesh, India’s most populous state, recently began using its blockchain technology to track forensic evidence in active criminal investigations.

More importantly, the Cardano Foundation said evidence scans and laboratory results will be recorded on-chain, creating a tamper-resistant audit trail designed to strengthen the integrity of investigations.
