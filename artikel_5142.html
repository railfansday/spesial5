Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz has disclosed that he sold about 2.5 million XRP in 2025, most of which he received in 2020 and 2023. Responding to a user on X, Schwartz said he paid roughly $1.7 million in capital gains tax on those sales. The admission sparked debate among XRP holders. Some argued that if Schwartz truly believed in XRP, he would not have sold. One critic claimed he once held 25 million tokens and questioned why he would sell near $3.84 if XRP was meant to reach $1,000 or more, while another user claimed he still holds about 3 million XRP. Neither figure has been confirmed. Schwartz responded by noting that XRP at 10 cents, Ether at $1 and Bitcoin at $200 all once seemed ridiculous too. His comments come as XRP trades below $1 after a 17% drop over the past week.
