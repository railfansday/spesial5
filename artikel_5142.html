Yearn Finance (YFI) is a pioneering decentralised finance (DeFi) yield aggregator built on the Ethereum blockchain. It automatically shifts user funds across various DeFi lending protocols like Aave and Compound to maximise interest and auto-compound returns.

Its native token, YFI, serves primarily as a governance token, giving holders voting rights over protocol decisions, vault strategies, and fee structures.

With past TVL peaking at $496.36 million and influence in shaping ERC-4626 vaults, Yearn’s steady progress continues to attract attention.

Yearn Finance is undergoing several important changes in 2026, with a clear focus on making YFI more valuable to holders. The biggest change is a governance overhaul that could redirect more protocol revenue back to the community.

At the same time, Yearn is expanding beyond Ethereum through a modular vault system that makes it easier to deploy yield strategies across multiple blockchains.

Another important development is the integration with Trueo, which enables external applications to use Yearn vaults. As more protocols build on top of Yearn, the platform could see higher activity and stronger fee generation.
