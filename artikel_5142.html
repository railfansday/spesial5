Fidelity filed an S-1 for a proposed spot LINK fund expected to trade on Nasdaq under the provisional ticker FLNK.

The fund would hold LINK directly but would not stake tokens or distribute staking rewards.

Fidelity's proposed sponsor fee remains blank, and the filing gives no confirmed launch date.

Fidelity is moving further into the altcoin market, and Chainlink is getting a new route to traditional investors. The Fidelity Chainlink ETF registration proposes a Nasdaq-listed fund holding LINK directly, with a provisional ticker of FLNK. But while LINK trades near multi-month lows, investors shouldn’t mistake a preliminary filing for a launch announcement.

Fidelity filed its Form S-1 with the SEC on November 25. The proposed Fidelity Chainlink Fund would track a dollar-denominated reference rate for LINK, adjusted for expenses and liabilities. FD Funds Management would act as sponsor. Anchorage Digital Bank, BitGo Bank & Trust and Coinbase Custody are named as custodians.

The prospectus also makes one detail explicit: the fund will not stake its LINK. Shareholders would get price exposure, but no staking rewards through the product. Authorized participants could create and redeem baskets using LINK or cash. The proposed FLNK ticker remains bracketed in the filing.

The ETF filing followed Fidelity’s announcement that customers could trade Chainlink, Aave and Uniswap through Fidelity Crypto. The company’s announcement also describes LINK as a decentralized oracle and interoperability protocol.

That gives customers two potential routes: direct token trading through Fidelity Crypto, or exchange-traded shares if the fund becomes effective.

Fidelity isn’t entering an empty market. Grayscale’s GLNK and Bitwise’s CLNK already provide exchange-traded LINK exposure on NYSE Arca.

Fidelity’s proposed annual sponsor fee remains blank, and there is also no confirmed launch date. The SEC filing says shares cannot be sold until registration becomes effective. For now, the Fidelity Chainlink ETF is a proposal-though its arrival coincides with a key technical setup on LINK’s chart.

One market analyst sets an initial upside target for LINK price at $19.23, with $30.95 acting as the key breakout barrier and $7.006 serving as invalidation.
