As you age, your face shows signs of wrinkles, sun damage, and dryness. Getting older is totally normal and, frankly, should be celebrated. Your beautiful expression lines are earned and represent wisdom and happy memories over the years. Dermatologist Brendan Camp, MD, says a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer for mature skin, and sunscreen can keep your skin looking healthy with age.

With fall here, it’s essential to slather on a moisturizer tailored to your skin type and concerns, like perimenopausal acne, a common beauty change for women in their 40s. Face creams and lotions relieve dryness, maintain hydration, and support your skin barrier, says Dr. Camp.

These dermatologist-recommended moisturizers also help prevent your makeup from settling, flaking, and pilling. In just a few weeks, you might need less foundation and concealer because your natural skin becomes a smoother, glowing base. Talk about skincare goals.

Are you looking to plump up fine lines and wrinkles? Retinoids, like retinol and retinal, are vitamin A derivatives that promote skin cell turnover, boost collagen and elastin, and minimize discoloration, dermatologist Hadley King, MD, recently told WH in our guide to wrinkle creams. Plus, retinol helps improve the appearance of crepey skin by boosting collagen production and improving the firmness of skin, says Dr. Camp.

Speaking of collagen, your skin naturally loses it and elastin as you age, so slathering on a collagen-supporting moisturizer is helpful. With regular use, you’ll notice a firmer complexion.

If age spots and hyperpigmentation have you down, then choose moisturizers made with brightening ingredients, like vitamin C, niacinamide, and azelaic acid.
