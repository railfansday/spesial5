Back to King Arthur products for a minute. Have any of you used their chocolate chips? Are they much tastier/higher quality than say ghiridelli's or hershey's or other "common" chips? I've been trying to get the info about them from the company. Maybe I'm not asking theright question, but I'm not getting an answer to the question I think I'm asking. Is it worth the money to buy KA's chips plus shipping--is the taste/quality that much better--than picking up Ghiridelli's at the local grocery?
I've never used KA chocolate chips, but from their website it seems they're either Guittard or Callebaut. You can buy Guittard chocolate chips in some supermarkets, and can find Callebaut in specialty baking or restaurant supply shops (probably also at places like Williams Sonoma or Sur La Table) or on Amazon. I tried Guittard once and didn't find them particularly better than my favorite widely available chocolate chips, which are Ghirardelli 60% bittersweet chips.

I think for the sorts of things chocolate chips are used for (cookies, tossing into brownies, muffins, etc), there's a difference between higher quality chips (Ghirardelli, Guittard, etc) and lower quality chips (supermarket brand), but within the higher quality chips there isn't much difference. The more important differences are among the style of chip: bittersweet, semisweet, milk; and chips vs chunks vs wafers (the latter being more for melting than for baking with).
