GTA 6 Players Discover Easter Egg For Showtime’s Dexter TV Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto 6 has barely been out for a day at this point, but thanks to its record-breaking sales figures, there are millions of players out there already discovering everything the game has to offer. While most fans are likely taking their time leisurely exploring Leonida, some eagle-eyed GTA 6 players have already been finding numerous secrets and other Easter eggs across the map.

Even though references to past Rockstar games felt pretty much inevitable, a fan-favorite Easter egg theory surrounding the hit serial killer TV series Dexter from all the way back when GTA 6 was first teased has finally been proven right thanks to a recent discovery.

As spotted by Detonated on X, in the Northeast of Vice-Dale County (specifically on Washington Island), you'll be able to come across none other than Dexter's apartment that fans spotted during the initial teaser trailers, which has now been renamed to "Trinity Apartments" in reference to one of the series' most influential villains, the Trinity Killer.

Players can also scale the roof of the apartment to find several tools seemingly used by Dexter himself, including some rope, toolboxes, tape, gloves, and the iconic syringe he uses to incapacitate his victims, or head down to the dock to find a striking replica of his boat, the 'Slice of Life' used to drop off bodies in the ocean.

Despite not being shown in the video, players searching the area have also posted images of the trash bags Dexter uses to dispose of his victims on the sea floor near the apartment found while scuba diving.

While many players are still caught up with the main offerings of GTA 6 like the vehicles, map, and how realistic it all is, the Dexter Easter egg is far from the only cheeky reference Rockstar threw into the game.
