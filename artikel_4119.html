The Mysterious Stones in OoT are Gossip Stones, which you need the Mask of Truth to use

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Gossip Stones in the Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake are mysterious stones with faces painted on that appear frequently throughout the game, but don't seem to do anything when you interact with them. In fact, it's possible to go throughout the entirety of OoT without working out what Gossip Stones are for, but fortunately I can explain how to use them and how to get the item you need to get them to work below.

The Gossip Stones have several functions, but the main one is locked off until you get the Mask of Truth, a reward for completing the Zelda Ocarina of Time Happy Mask Shop side quest, which begins in Hyrule Castle Town as a child (our guide will show you how to do that).

Once you have the Mask of Truth, all you need to do is equip it via the item menu (or up on the D-Pad) and interact with Gossip Stones.

At this point, they'll give you a clue or piece of advice relating to the area you're in. It might help you complete a puzzle, find a secret, or something else.
