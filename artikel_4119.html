Where To Find The Marbelle Hotel Safe Code In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Here is how to find the Silent Hill easter egg and Marbelle Hotel safe code in GTA 6.

There are a lot of easter eggs and references throughout Grand Theft Auto 6. While some are more well-hidden than others, a few can be easily stumbled across even if you're not looking for them.

One of these is the Marbelle Hotel in Vice City, which is an unremarkable-looking building that's hiding a pretty spooky experience inside. Here is where to find it, how to trigger the easter egg, where the Marbella Hotel Safe code is, and if you get anything for your troubles.

If you're looking to find this easter egg, you will need to first make your way to Vice City in the center of the map. Once you arrive, head to the Crosstown district and go south towards the Pawn & Gun — Crosstown store. Just east of this store, about a block away, will be the Marbelle Hotel, which is a small three-story building that has a pretty worn and old facade. The front door will be locked, so head around the back and use the unlocked door.

Gotta make the place look nice for the big homecoming.

Once inside, you will see a safe on your right, but we don't have the code right now, so instead head up the stairs. When you reach the third floor, the door will open on its own (I'm sure it's fine), but don't go inside right away.

Instead, loop around the railing by the stairs to find a Ratface card on the old dresser in the corner. When you're ready, head through the door that opened and attempt to go up to the roof via the small staircase. Unsurprisingly, the door behind you will shut and lock, trapping you in this stairwell.

A bunch of spooky symbols and drawings will then appear all over the walls around you, and a heavy fog will flood the hotel and surrounding area. After a few seconds, the doors will unlock, but your visibility will be heavily limited due to the fog. Don't panic; there are actually no enemies or monsters you need to deal with.

Once the doors open, go onto the roof of the hotel and walk forward when you exit. On the ground, there will be a notepad that will give you a lot of lore about the Marbelle Hotel, along with the safe code, which is circled near the bottom of the notepad. You can also see the code briefly when all the symbols appear on the wall!

Now, head back downstairs and go to the safe and enter the code 955214. Doing so will unlock the safe, which contains some cash along with the legendary Cash and Crowex Ambassador Breakfast watch that you can use on either Jason or Lucia.

You will now be free to leave, as there are no more secrets outside of a pipe you can use as a melee weapon, which is another reference to Silent Hill, since it's a classic weapon throughout the franchise. Additionally, this will unlock the "The Truth Is Out There" trophy, which requires you to encounter one of a few specific supernatural occurrences.
