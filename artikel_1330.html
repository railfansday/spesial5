Report Balde offered to Roma, but Barcelona talent not a priority

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Roma have reportedly been offered the opportunity to sign Alejandro Balde from Barcelona, but would only be prepared to consider a loan deal, so Juventus remain favourites.

According to Retesport, the agents representing Balde have approached several clubs to propose his services in the January transfer window, including Roma.

Others believed to be interested include Juventus, Paris Saint-Germain, Manchester United and AS Monaco.

Although the left-back and winger is one of the names mentioned for Roma in the past, the cost of the operation makes this an implausible transfer target.

Not only were Barcelona seeking €60m for a sale just a few months ago, but his wages at €5m per season would also break the current limit at the Stadio Olimpico.

Agent Jorge Mendes is expected to meet with Barcelona next week to discuss his future, considering Balde has made only five appearances this season, for a grand total of 124 minutes of football.

Roma are believed to have other priorities, so would only be interested in Balde if this was a low-cost operation on loan.
