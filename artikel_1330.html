Gasperini: ‘A blessing to have Dybala and Soule available, too early to talk transfers’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Gian Piero Gasperini says that he feels blessed to have both Paulo Dybala and Matias Soule available for selection in attack following the international break, but says that it is still too early to be having serious discussions about potential January transfers: ‘Anything can happen’.

Roma return to Serie A action at 17.00 GMT on Saturday as they visit Parma in Serie A after a two-week pause for international fixtures.

The Giallorossi are one of two sides still unbeaten at the top of the Serie A table, and they went into the break with a run of three consecutive victories in the league, against Sassuolo, Udinese and Cagliari.

They will go up against Alberto Gilardino’s Parma, who find themselves in 16th place on eight points after the first 11 matches of the season.

Gasperini sat down for his customary pre-match press conference on Friday.

The positive news for Roma is that all three of Mile Svila, Gianluca Mancini and Paulo Dybala are available for selection: “Yes, all three have recovered,” Gasperini confirmed (via TMW).

Gasperini was quick to shut down questions relating to potential transfer targets, insisting that there is still a long way to go before the January window opens.

“There have been no discussions of that kind. As far as the transfer market goes, I think it’s appropriate that the CEO talks about it first and that they then say what Roma may or may not do in the January window, which is always a complicated market.”

When asked for further transfer updates later on in his press conference, Gasperini added: “It’s November 20, so any situation today doesn’t count for much. Obviously every team will be looking at each other, including Roma, but I don’t think there are any situations that we can nail down today.

“There are still a lot of league games to play where anything can happen and some players might play well, others maybe won’t. At the moment, my only focus is on the league. Of course, Tony D’Amico and I have constant conversations. But, in terms of the transfer market, there have been no discussions so far, except for the ones between me and D’Amico.”

Despite having Soule and Dybala available for selection, it remains to be seen whether the former will be thrown straight into the starting line-up after a long journey back from international duty in South America earlier this week.

“There are some players who arrived yesterday morning, so they have done some training, but there’s the travel and the time zones. We’ll have another training session this afternoon, even if it’s a refresher session ahead of tomorrow’s match, so we’ll see how they’ve recovered. It also applies to Balredi and to Castro. But obviously Soule was in extraordinary condition when we left off.

“We have a game tomorrow, we’re all still recovering a bit, and on Wednesday we will have another important one. Then another one again on the Saturday or Sunday afterwards. So there will be chances for Soule. The important thing is that there are no injuries.”

Gasperini was asked if this upcoming run of fixtures before Christmas is an opportunity for Roma to ‘kick on’ in the league.

“The season is much longer than this upcoming period, even if every period is important, this one will not be decisive,” he replied. “The important thing for us is to stay where we are and keep going on this current pathway.”

Asked whether Dybala is ready to start consistently for Roma after his recent injury lay-off, Gasperini explained: “We can only check that by letting him play. Of course he hasn’t played for a month, but sooner or later, he will have to start playing.

“Evaluations are made during games, checking to see how he reacts. But right now, he has recovered physically, he has no particular issues.”

Gasperini insists that it is not a ‘problem’ to try and fit Dybala and Soule into the same team, even if they prefer to play in the same position.

“It’s not a problem, it’s a blessing to have both of them available. Soule put in some really high-level performances during the period when Soule was injured. I think he also did well with Argentina, he earned a call-up and hadn’t had one before. Like Mora, who was also called up by Portugal.

“These are players who seem to be growing and the important thing is that we have them available. It’s a false problem that we can’t play them together. In my opinion, Dybala and Soule go perfectly together.”

Assessing Saturday’s opponents, Parma, Gasperini said: “Gilardino did very well with Genoa, taking over a team in Serie B and earning promotion back to Serie A that season. He’s done well and has had some difficulties since then, but his teams have always been very difficult to face, very compact, very organised. It will be the same tomorrow.

“Two games ago he won 3-0 in Bergamo and lost one against Bologna, but he was playing with 10 men after just a few minutes. So, I don’t trust him or Parma. I know the league well, there are traps all over the place and after an international break, we don’t have that rhythm. So tomorrow’s is a very important game in that respect.”

Lastly, Gasperini was asked where he feels Roma can improve.

He answered: “I believe that we are organised and have a way of approaching the game that is superior to what we had done last year or in recent seasons. That is important.

“Beyond that, there are endless ways to improve. Of course, you want to focus more on the details, the small things, but these are often the things that make the difference. The details are the difference between scoring or not, conceding or keeping a clean sheet. The details make the difference.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
