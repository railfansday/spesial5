Good news for Roma with Dybala on target, others ready for return

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There is some good news for Roma, as Paulo Dybala, Mile Svilar and Gianluca Mancini partially returned to full training today, and La Joya even scored a goal.

The Giallorossi have been stretched pretty thin in recent weeks by their Serie A and Champions League commitments when decimated by injuries.

Coach Gian Piero Gasperini has a limited group available to work with during the break for international duty, but there are some positive signs.

Dybala, goalkeeper Svilar and defender Mancini all participated in most of the training session today.

There was even something to celebrate for the striker, who scored a goal during the game between ranks.

Pierluigi Gollini continued to work separately from the rest of the group after the shoulder injury that ruled him out of the final two games, including the 3-2 Champions League defeat at Manchester United.

Roma are back on the field in Serie A against Parma on Saturday November 21, and hope to have most if not all those players available again after more than a month out.
