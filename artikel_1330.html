David Ornstein: Mauricio Pochettino Feels 'Different' After Man Utd Approach

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Manchester United are attempting to finalise an agreement to make Mauricio Pochettino their next manager if they decide to sack Michael Carrick, and Athletic journalist David Ornstein says he feels 'different' after their approach.

Man United are still yet to reach a final conclusion on the future of the 45-year-old, even though the expectation is that he will be dismissed following talks between Omar Berrada, Jason Wilcox and Sir Jim Ratcliffe in Monaco this week.

GIVEMESPORT sources revealed previously that three out-of-work managers in Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann and Antonio Conte were candidates to replace Michael Carrick and had their admirers on the United board, before we revealed on Monday night that Pochettino was a leading candidate with the expectation that Carrick would be sacked.

That decision is still expected, and United intend to move forward with appointing Pochettino, who GMS understands has a release clause worth $10m written into his contract with the USMNT, plus an extra fee for his staff.

The indications have been that the former Tottenham boss is keen to replace Carrick at Old Trafford, even though he only recently extended his stay with the United States following a promising 2026 World Cup campaign.

Ornstein has been speaking about the potential appointment of the 54-year-old on the Talk of the Devils podcast on Wednesday morning, and he says that Pochettino feels different to what he did before.

I’m hearing now that he is reinvigorated to an extent that he’s not been at for a long time, that you will see a different version of him now, that he has his energy restored.

GMS senior correspondent Ben Jacobs revealed on Wednesday morning that there is now an agreement in principle between United and Pochettino, as the appointment moves closer.

Pochettino has been out of club football for two-and-a-half years, and there will be concerns about not only that, but also that his previous spell at Chelsea didn't work out amazingly well.

However, the Argentine has shown how big his personality is by taking on the American job and leading them as a host nation at a World Cup, and he has performed positively, with a 60% win rate.

United fans will look at what the 54-year-old achieved during a five-year spell at Tottenham Hotspur and pray that he can do the same at Old Trafford.

Pochettino made Tottenham exciting to watch and a team who played pressing football from the front, with the likes of Harry Kane, Son Heung-Min and Dele Alli excelling under his leadership.

He may have lost some of that energy in himself during his previous club role at Stamford Bridge, but Ornstein's information that English football will see a different version of him now should be exciting for the Old Trafford faithful.

If all goes well, United will hope to have their man in the dugout for the big Premier League match against Liverpool at Anfield a week on Sunday.

Pochettino will be determined to make a positive start against one of the club's biggest rivals, and they need at least a point to have any chance of moving out of the relegation zone.
