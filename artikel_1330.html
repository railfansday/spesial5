Man United 4-4 Aston Villa PLAYER RATINGS: Who looks like the only ray of light at Old Trafford? Whose display was a mess? And which 3/10 star was absolutely hopeless?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Published: 13:52 EST, 8 November 2026 | Updated: 15:31 EST, 8 November 2026

Manchester United will rot 18th in the Premier League table during the international break even after scrambling an unlikely 4-4 draw against Aston Villa.

Michael Carrick's side fought back from a two-goal deficit, inflicted in just four first-half minutes, and went into the interval level after goals from Bruno Fernandes and Youri Tielemans.

They had to pull off the trick a second time after John McGinn's penalty and Leon Goretzka's back-post tap-in made it 4-2 to Unai Emery's visitors.

But Carrick's side refused to throw in the towel despite how hopeless they were for the majority of this game and his substitutes paid off handsomely.

Joshua Zirkzee made it 4-3 in the 92nd minute, teed up by fellow sub Patrick Dorgu, and Dorgu completed the comeback with a 96th-minute finish of his own to send Old Trafford into raptures.

CEO Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox celebrated in the directors' box, all while Emery stormed off down the tunnel before the final whistle.
