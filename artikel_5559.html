BJ's Wholesale Club is raising its full-year earnings forecast after a strong quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The warehouse club operator lifted its adjusted EPS range to $5.10 to $5.30 as third-quarter comparable club sales rose 13.5%

BJ's Wholesale Club raised its full-year adjusted earnings forecast on Friday, setting a new range of $5.10 to $5.30 per share versus $4.60 to $4.80 previously. The company moved the bottom of its guidance for comparable club sales growth, not counting gasoline, up to 2.5% from 2%. The high end stays at 3%.

Adjusted earnings for the third quarter ended Oct. 31 were $1.73 a share, compared with $1.16 a year earlier. Net income rose to $219.5 million from $152.1 million, and total revenues rose 17.5% to $6.28 billion.

Analysts were forecasting a quarterly adjusted profit of $1.26 per share on revenue of $5.97 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Comparable club sales rose 13.5% in the quarter, or 4.1% excluding gasoline sales. Membership fee income increased 9.5% to $138.4 million. Digitally enabled comparable sales grew 38%.

"We delivered a strong third quarter driven by robust traffic growth and record membership," Chairman and CEO Bob Eddy said in a statement. Chief Financial Officer Laura Felice said, "we are raising our full-year guidance for both comparable sales and adjusted EPS."

Merchandise gross margin rate, which excludes gasoline sales and membership fee income, fell about 170 basis points from a year earlier. The company attributed the decline to promotions tied to its 42nd birthday celebration event.

Selling, general and administrative expenses dropped to $696.1 million from $788.2 million a year earlier. They included a benefit of about $240 million, net of legal fees, from a settlement of litigation over credit card interchange fees in which BJ's was the plaintiff. The company recognized an estimated $95 million in expenses tied to the use of the settlement proceeds, which partly offset the benefit. Gains from sale-leaseback transactions also lowered expenses, while labor, occupancy and operating costs rose with new club and gas station openings.

Operating income rose 43.7% to $313.8 million.

For the fiscal year ending Jan. 30, 2027, BJ's expects capital expenditures of about $800 million. The spending supports new club openings and upgrades across its distribution network, including the ambient distribution center.

BJ's opened two new clubs and five gas stations in the quarter. It operates 271 clubs and 211 gas locations in 22 states.

The company scheduled a conference call on the results for 8:30 a.m. ET on Friday.
