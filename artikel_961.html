Jack Grealish plays for Everton for the first time since being axed for skipping training without permission in 'breach of club discipline' - as winger comes off the bench during loss at Brentford

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Published: 14:08 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 17:56 EST, 23 November 2026

Jack Grealish returned to the Everton squad for the first time since being made unavailable for selection due to a breach of club discipline, and came off the bench at half-time against Brentford on Monday.

The Manchester City loanee was punished by the club for failing to report to training sessions and missed several matches. He had not featured for four games: one because of illness, then a 'breach of club discipline' and a lack of fitness thereafter.

He last featured on October 17, over a month ago, but returned to the match day squad to take on Brentford on Monday night – named on the substitutes' bench. He was introduced at half-time for Tyrique George, with the Toffees 2-0 down and down to 10 men after James Tarkowski's red card. They would ultimately lose 4-0.

Manager David Moyes said ahead of the game: 'He has served his punishment and we will treat him as we were treating him before.

'We will get on with things. The team is going really well at the moment and what I don't want to do is turn it into a Jack story because it shouldn't be.'

Pushed if that suggests he will not play on Monday night, Moyes replied: 'He could easily. He is back in the squad, he is a good player. Who knows?'

He was named on the bench against Brentford, coming on for Tyrique George at half-time

Everton had won their last three games and went into Monday's match sixth in the table. The result, though, was a blow as they dropped to ninth.

Asked what Grealish can offer, Moyes added: 'Jack can offer us a lot. I want more from him, I want him to show more than he has done. I want him to give me more assists, goals.

'But there's lots of things about him which we need and he's good at retaining the ball, giving us good possession, carrying. So we hope that he can get back to those levels and give us what we need.

'I think this time last year he was probably sitting on five or six assists so we're hoping to get that.'

Grealish's social media apology came after he featured in a behind-closed-doors match during the international break.

'Nice to be back out there with the boys today after a few good days training!' Grealish wrote. 'I want to apologise to the fans (and) everyone at the club for not being able to help the team the last few weeks.

'I love playing for this club (and) can't wait to be back playing in front of you guys in an Everton shirt.
