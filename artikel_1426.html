Spain 2-3 England PLAYER RATINGS: Which star was the man of the match for Thomas Tuchel? Who was key to their energy and adventure? And who now deserves a run in the side?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Published: 17:06 EST, 15 November 2026 | Updated: 17:47 EST, 15 November 2026

England secured a historic victory over Spain in the Nations League to secure top spot in their group ahead of their quarter-final matchup next year.

Thomas Tuchel's side raced out the blocks to cause the world champions trouble early on, but Unai Simon ensured Spain held firm and weathered the early storm.

England did get their reward, though, just before half-time when Harry Kane won and scored a penalty to give his side a deserved lead.

After the break, they went up a gear, with Arsenal defenders Ezri Konsa and Ben White netting to leave England in dreamland.

That was until Elliot Anderson was sent off – conceding a penalty against Lamine Yamal in the process – and Spain were back in it through Mikel Oyarzabal. Nico Williams then ensured it would be a nervy last 15 minutes for England, but they held on and secured the 3-2 victory.

Daily Mail Sport's CRAIG HOPE was in Madrid to rate the Three Lions players.
