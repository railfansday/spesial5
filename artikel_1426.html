Dejan Kulusevski tackles Tottenham after 526 days of absence

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Long injured on the Tottenham side, the Swedish winger will have experienced 526 days of absence between his knee injury contracted against Crystal Palace on May 11, 2025 and his return against Coventry (3-1) on October 19. A long ordeal for him who notably experienced a blank 2025/2026 financial year.

Thinking for a moment that his return from injury was going to be impossible, Dejan Kulusevski experienced a crossing of the desert and did not spare his club Tottenham since the Mirror says the former Juventus player was “disappointed by some people”.
