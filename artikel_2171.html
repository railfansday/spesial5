‘Definitely’: EFL expert has say on Birmingham City’s top two chances

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City are sat top of the Championship table after their first 15 fixtures of the season. BCFC are above Middlesbrough and Stoke City on goal difference, with the likes of Wolves, West Brom and Swansea City also chasing them down.

The Blues are back in action after the international break with an away trip to the Bet365 Stadium to take on Stoke City as they aim to keep their momentum going. They beat Swansea City 2-1 at home last time out after a brace by Jay Stansfield.

Elek reckons the Midlands outfit can keep it up and has said on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “They can definitely stay there. There is no reason why they can’t. We’re not talking about a Championship minnow here. We’re talking about a club who have invested a lot of money in their playing squad. They were the best League One squad vs the level we’ve seen in a long time.”

He added: “The ascent Ipswich and Sunderland have shown, and to an extent Coventry although that took a few more seasons, the pathway is already there.”

Birmingham City came up from League One back in 2025 after they stormed to the title. BCFC then spent the last campaign adapting to life in the Championship and fell short of the play-offs.

The Blues stuck with head coach Chris Davies in the summer though and that decision has worked out well for them. They are rock solid at the back and they have the best defensive record in the division at the moment ahead of West Brom and Southampton.

Christoph Klarer and Phil Neumann have both been excellent at the heart of defence, with Alex Cochrane to the left of them. Dael Fry, who joined in the summer after the end of his contract at Middlesbrough, then provides useful back-up from the bench.

Further up the pitch, Jhon Solis, Paik Seung-ho and Tomoki Iwata inject quality into their midfield department. Stansfield, Liam Millar, Patrick Roberts, Demarai Gray and August Priske are all dangerous attackers and can cause serious issues to opposition defenders.

Birmingham City could do with another striker in the January transfer window. BCFC need some competition for Priske with Luis Vazquez struggling to get to grips with English football.

The Blues would also benefit from another attacking midfielder or winger ahead of the second-half of the season. Their pursuit of Joe Gelhardt in the summer was well documented and someone like him would be ideal, although he won’t leave Hull City.

You could also argue that Davies should bring in another central defender this winter too. If Klarer, Neumann or Fry were to get injured, they would be left pretty short of depth in that area.

Birmingham City are on a roll and will be full of confidence. Coventry City and Hull City showed last season that you don’t need parachute payments to go up.

The Blues need to stay grounded though and can’t get too carried away. Their away defeat at Cardiff City last month proved every single match is tough.
