Ex-chief scout tells Rio Ngumoha what areas he must develop to become a 'world-class winger' for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Liverpool sensation Rio Ngumoha has been told the key areas he must develop his game if he wants to be an England regular.

The 18-year-old made his fourth senior appearance for the Three Lions during their 2-1 win against Croatia on Thursday night, where he came off the bench late in the game.

He has yet to be given a serious change to impress in and England shirt, but it is clear that Thomas Tuchel has faith in his long-term development to become a key player.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Ngumoha still has room to develop before he can be considered a leading option for the national team.

Despite his quick feet, speed and ability to beat a man, the winger must still add goals and assists to his game as he is being primed to make a difference in the attacking third.

There are also concerns about his physicality, although his young age is a valid excuse in that respect, with high hopes for his progress to one day be a crucial asset.

Football Insider reported back in June that Ngumoha has impressed his coaches both with England and Liverpool, following his senior international debut in which he impressed.

His continued involvement with the senior squad has been a huge show of faith from Tuchel, even when he hasn't been starting games regularly for Liverpool this season.

While there is an acceptance that he may not yet be ready for a regular starting role, especially given the level of competition, he is being backed to reach those heights before long.

"Rio Ngumoha is certainly going to be one to watch in this international break," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's a very promising player and it's clear he has the support and trust of Thomas Tuchel, because he's being named in every squad at the moment and is often called upon.

"It's clear he has a bright future, he's done very well for Liverpool and he's still so young, but there are assets of his game he needs to improve.

"He's got very quick feet, lots of pace, so he can beat his man easily nine times out of ten, but it's about what he does once he beats the man and gets into a forward position.

"He needs to deliver that perfect cross, or get into a position where he can shoot and score himself, that's the next piece of the puzzle. I don't think he does it enough at the moment.

"It's the difference between a good winger and a world-class one, the work they do in front of goal to provide opportunities and score them.

"I think he could do with being a bit stronger as well, he's quite lightweight when he's on the ball and gets brushed off it too easily at times.

"But all of these things can be excused by the fact he's only 18, he has plenty of time to develop his game and to improve before he becomes a regular, and I'm sure he will do that."

Discussions about Ngumoha's exciting future with England have come against a backdrop of question marks about his long-term future at club level with Liverpool.

Bayern Munich have been long-term admirers of the teenager, and are once again being linked with a move to prise him away from Anfield in the upcoming transfer window.

However, former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider that Liverpool are set to ease any concerns by agreeing a contract extension with Ngumoha before too long.

The 18-year-old's current deal is set to expire in 2028 and there has been fears that he would seek a move elsewhere amid growing interest from European giants.

However, he is now considered likely to commit his future to Anfield, where talks have been ongoing for months as they aim to get him tied down.
