Chelsea's Morgan Rogers is just one of many footballers showing support and love for Harry Kane after his new long-term injury.

England's record-breaker was forced off against Cologne on Friday, 20 November, having initially tried to carry on in Bayern Munich's 1-1 draw.

It has now been confirmed that the centre-forward has torn ligaments in his left ankle, a blow which is likely to sideline him for the "next few months".

Of course, this is a tremendous blow for the Bundesliga giants, but also for all of his international teammates back in the Premier League, such as Rogers.

The Three Lions are set to face Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals in March, and it is unknown whether he'll be fit in time for that.

Knowing Kane, he will try his absolute best to return to the pitch as quickly as possible, but this isn't an injury that he can rush back from.

Kane is rarely one for long spells on the sidelines, but at 33 years of age, this could become a more common thing in his career, sadly.

The Bayern striker sent out of battle cry via Instagram on Saturday, 21 November, promising to come back stronger than ever from his hospital room.

He wrote: "The highs and lows of life! Obviously, very disappointed to get ths injury, but as always, it's an opportunity to show resilience and improvements going into 2027.

"I'll be back fresh and ready to go fo the business end of the season! 'Tough times don't last, tough people do' - my guy Julian Edelman told me this one!

Thank you for all the messages! Alles gut!" to which Chelsea's No.17 responded with a heart emoji, as messages of support flooded in.

What is abundantly clear is just how loved Kane is, with likes from Reece James, James Garner, James Maddison, Morgan Gibbs-White and Jarrod Bowen, to name just a few.

Former teammate Lucas Moura has commented, writing: "God bless your recovery, H! You will come back stronger as always."

Robbie Keane also responded, saying: "Wishing you a speedy recovery pal," with praying emojis, and messages will only continue to flood in.

Kane is clearly one of the good guys in football, and all fans will be ready to clap him back onto the pitch when he finally recovers.
