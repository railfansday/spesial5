Taylor Swift Jokes About Being '0 for 8' for Grammys Song of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Taylor Swift is finding humor in never winning the Grammys’ Song of the Year category.

The Recording Academy tapped Swift, 36, to help announce some of the nominations for the 2027 Grammys on Monday, November 16, which resulted in the pop star reflecting on her own history with the awards show.

“I’m very honored that the Grammys have asked me to be here to help reveal the last two categories for the Grammy nominations,” Swift, 36, said during Monday’s livestream.

She continued, “But I first wanted to start off by saying congratulations to anyone who is nominated for a Grammy this year in any category, because what this means is that your peers, your fellow musicians, producers, engineers, artists, writers, they love what you made.”

From Swift’s perspective, “that’s the coolest feeling in the world.” She noted in her message that she hopes the nominees “have a really wonderful day.”

“The last two categories to be announced, one of them I’ve had a lot of luck in. The other one I’m 0 for 8,” she said. “Here are the nominees for Album and Song of the Year.”

Swift earned her first Song of the Year nomination at the Grammys in 2010 for “You Belong With Me.” She has since received several additional nods with 2015’s “Shake It Off,” 2016’s “Blank Space,” 2020’s “Lover,” 2021’s “Cardigan,” 2023’s “All Too Well (10 Minute Version) (The Short Film),” 2024’s “Anti-Hero” and 2025’s “Fortnight.” She also received a nomination for the upcoming 2027 Grammys with “I Knew It, I Knew You,” a track off the Toy Story 5 soundtrack.

In the Album of the Year category, has been nominated seven times, winning for Fearless in 2010, 1989 in 2016, Folklore in 2021 and Midnights in 2024.

This year, Swift is also up for a trophy for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance with “The Life of a Showgirl” featuring Sabrina Carpenter, Record of the Year with “The Fate of Ophelia,” Album of the Year with The Life of a Showgirl and Best Song Written for Visual Media with “I Knew It, I Knew You.”

The 69th annual Grammys will be presented on February 7, 2027, at Los Angeles’ Crypto.com Arena, airing live on ABC. The broadcast will also be streamed on Disney+ and Hulu.

Ahead of the 2027 Grammys, Swift is set to appear at another awards show: the 2026 CMAs. Swift, who received a nomination for Single of the Year with “I Knew It, I Knew You,” confirmed her plans to attend the ceremony and perform. Prior to this, Swift had not performed at the CMAs for 13 years — which is her lucky number.

“Looks like I haven’t yee’d my last haw,” Swift wrote via her Instagram Stories after the nominations were announced. “See you there Nov 18.”
