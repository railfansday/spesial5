Miley Cyrus Breaks Silence After Intimate Wedding to Maxx Morando

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus is officially a married woman, giving her wedding to Maxx Morando its flowers via social media.

“M + M 🤍 11.21.26,” Cyrus, 33, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, set to her appropriately titled song “Let’s Get Married.”

Cyrus shared the first glimpse of the couple’s big day in California, in which she stunned in a white long-sleeve bridal gown with a dramatic open back and Chopard jewelry. Morando, 27, looked dapper in a classic black tuxedo.

The couple were joined by their loved ones, including Miley’s parents, Tish and Billy Ray Cyrus, and their respective new partners. (Tish is married to Dominic Purcell, while Billy Ray has been dating Elizabeth Hurley since 2025.)

Miley and Morando have been together since 2021, getting engaged in December 2025.

“We were in Kyoto, Japan. For some reason, you’ll find this as crazy as I did, she’d never been to Japan before. Neither had I, and we’d both wanted to go for a really long time,” Morando recalled to Wonderland magazine in July of his proposal. “There were talks before we went about me popping the question, but I was trying to keep it secret. She was not trying to keep it a secret, if you know what I mean.”

He added at the time, “I had to be really sneaky about it. We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could.”

To throw Miley off the trail, the drummer coyly asked to take a seemingly innocent walk around the hotel grounds.

“There was this little outer garden that you had to cross a plank to get over. There was a sign that said you couldn’t cross. I was like, ‘That’s crazy, let’s go over there,’” he quipped to the outlet. “She’s like, ‘No, it’s blocked.’ Eventually, I persuaded her. We walked in, and it was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”

Miley was admittedly taken aback by Morando’s proposal.

“I am not easy to surprise because I love to control every situation, and I had completely surrendered,” Miley told Good Morning America late last year. “I’m telling you, I was so, so surprised.”

Miley, who was previously married to her The Last Song costar Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020, initially wasn’t sure if another marriage was in the cards following her public divorce.

“Miley never thought she would get married again, and Maxx changed everything for her,” a source exclusively told Us in December 2025. “They have a really healthy and private relationship.”
