Tanggal: 2026-11-19

K-12 Dive’s three-part series, Scrambling for Students, explores how districts are trying to stabilize and improve enrollment as the competition for students heats up.

Private school choice policies in states — and, soon, at the federal level — have ramped up in recent years as the pool of students enrolling in public schools continues to shrink.

While declining birth rates are driving most of these enrollment declines and leading to more public school closures, K-12 Dive sought to explore the growing role private school choice plays in these ongoing challenges for public schools through our three-part series — Scrambling for Students.

The three-part series also examines how districts are trying to stabilize and boost enrollment as they face growing competition for students.
