Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Break Even Point on Receiving Amount in Social Security Benefits that one Paid-in Through Taxes

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

IMO, you'd need to consider a range of other factors:

- Inflation erosion each year. For example, a $1 in benefits today is only worth 39 cents compared to it's value 35 years ago.

- Taxes on current benefits. The money paid in was already taxed, now many recipients are paying taxes again on a portion of the benefit.

- Opportunity cost. What if you had put that money into the S&P 500 each year? Or a CD? Or maybe you would have spent it or a bought a series of stocks that went bankrupt.

I've always viewed S.S. as insurance, not an investment with a return.
I agree.

And $1 paid into Social Security 35 years ago was worth a lot more than $1 contributed today. The inflation issue goes both ways.
