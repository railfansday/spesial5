Brock Bowers’ injury revealed, yet more tests need to be done in concerning twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Las Vegas Raiders may have avoided a significant injury to their biggest offensive weapon. Tight end Brock Bowers exited Sunday's 21-13 loss in NFL Week 10 to the Seattle Seahawks with an ankle injury, but the initial outlook is reportedly encouraging.

According to NFL Network insider Ian Rapoport, Bowers is not believed to have suffered a major injury and is currently considered day-to-day. However, additional testing is scheduled for Monday to determine the full severity of the issue.

Bowers suffered the injury during the opening drive against Seattle and attempted to play through it. He briefly returned to the field during the first half, including making a 12-yard reception, but eventually went back to the locker room and was ruled out for the remainder of the game.

The tight end finished with two receptions for 18 yards. His early exit was a significant loss for an offense making its first start with Fernando Mendoza at quarterback.

The initial report that Bowers avoided a major injury is encouraging for Las Vegas, especially considering how important he has been to the passing attack this season. He entered Week 10 with 48 receptions for 658 yards and six touchdowns through eight appearances.

Although the initial news is positive, Bowers' status for Week 11 remains uncertain. Rapoport reported that the tight end is considered day-to-day, but additional tests will help determine the extent of the ankle injury and whether he will be available against the Denver Broncos.

Head coach Klint Kubiak said after Sunday's loss that Bowers would be reevaluated Monday and that the team did not yet know the severity of the injury.

The Raiders will have to be careful with their star tight end, particularly given his importance to the offense. If Bowers is unable to play against Denver, Michael Mayer could see an expanded role in the passing game.

For now, the initial indication that Bowers avoided a major injury is encouraging. The results of Monday's testing should provide a clearer picture of his availability moving forward.
