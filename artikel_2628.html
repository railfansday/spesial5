DK Metcalf will not be suspended for actions during Steelers-Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Steelers receiver DK Metcalf will not be suspended for his actions during Sunday’s loss to the Eagles, according to multiple Monday reports.

Metcalf was ejected for committing two unsportsmanlike conduct fouls over the course of the game.

Metcalf then left the locker room without speaking to reporters. But spoke to them on Monday, denying the accusation that he was trying to poke Cooper DeJean in the eye.

It’s been a trying year for Metcalf, who has just 36 catches for 434 yards with two touchdowns in 10 games. That the receiver will not be suspended is a positive development for him. But Pittsburgh would surely like to see Metcalf increase his production and reduce the on-field antics that do not help the team.
