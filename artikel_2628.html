CHN Daily: Kantserov’s Hat Trick, Blackhawks Claim Glendening; Larkin Blockbuster

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

With two wins in as many days over the weekend, the Chicago Blackhawks get a well-earned day off to begin the week.

The Hawks return to the ice in Seattle on Tuesday for practice, then take on the Kraken at Climate Pledge Arena on Wednesday night to cap the three-game road trip.

On Sunday night, the Blackhawks won another bottom-feeder matchup, defeating the 32nd-ranked Vancouver Canucks 6-3 at Rogers Arena. Russian rookie Roman Kantserov recorded his first career NHL hat trick and four-point game. Patrick Kane and Tyler Bertuzzi continued their recent runs with multi-point efforts, and the red-hot power play converted three times.

More in the links for highlights and the key takeaways from the Hawks second straight win, moving to 4-1-1 in their last six games.

Also in Blackhawks news, the NHL Department of Player Safety dished out another harsh suspension, and a 37-year-old veteran was claimed off waivers to give the team some help at the faceoff circle.

Around the league, the Dylan Larkin Saga has finally ended, Jake Oettinger alreayd has five shutouts this season, and Alex Ovechkin's career-worst slump.

Chicago Hockey Now: Saturday was the Sam Rinzel game, and Sunday was the Roman Kantserov game. The Blackhawks won both games of their weekend back-to-back, capped off with a 6-3 win over Vancouver. Kantserov tallied a career-best four points, including his first NHL hat trick. Don't look now, but the Hawks have won four of their last six games. Hit the link for highlights from Sunday's victory.

More CHN: After Sunday's four-point performance, Roman Kantserov now leads the Blackhawks with 13 goals and 18 points in 24 games this season. He's now fully emersed in the Calder Trophy race. Meanwhile, key veterans like Patrick Kane and Tyler Bertuzzi have picked up the slack, and the power play has seen a dramatic turnaround. What a 6-3 win for the Blackhawks!

More CHN: The Blackhawks have been the NHL's worst faceoff team all season, and they're banged up down the middle without Connor Bedard and Anton Frondell. After recalling Dominic Toninato on Sunday, the Hawks made another addition, claiming Luke Glendening off waivers for New York. The 37-year-old might not have much left in the tank, but he can help at the faceoff circle.

Glending has a career 55.7 faceoff-win percentage in 938 career games. Blackhawks head coach Jeff Blashill is plenty familiar with Glendening, having coached him from 2015 to 2021 with the Detroit Red Wings and from 2023 to 2025 as an assist in Tampa Bay. He'll wear No. 23 for the Blackhawks and is set to join the team in Seattle on Tuesday.

More CHN: For the second time already this season, the NHL Department of Player Safety has cracked down on a Blackhawks skater who's without any previous fine or suspension history. Yes, Wyatt Kaiser deserved to be suspended for his illegal hit to the head on Calgary Flames forward Martin Pospisil. But as a first-time offender, Kaiser's three-game suspension seems harsh.

Without Kaiser for three games, the Blackhawks have no choice to play Kevin Korchinski, who dressed in Vancouver on Sunday for only the second time since Halloween. Unfortunately, Korchinski struggled in limited ice time against the Canucks. He was minus-two with a penalty in just… 5:56 of ice time. He received one shift in the third period.

Before Sunday's game, Frank Seravalli joined the Chicago Sports Network pregame show and said that captain Connor Bedard, who will undergo right shoulder surgery today, never really considered rehabbing his right shoulder after suffering a second injury in 11 months. It was always going to be surgery. The 21-year-old has made the wise decision, as tough as it must have been.

The Athletic: Sam Rinzel had his remarkable five-game point streak end in Sunday's win, but he still played a terrific game on the top pairing with Alex Vlasic. Mark Lazerus wrote about Rinzel's historic run revitalizing the Blackhawks' offense and power play, and perhaps their entire season. Only four defensemen in NHL history accomplished what Rinzel did over the past week and a half.

Detroit Hockey Now: The major news from Sunday, the Dylan Larkin Saga in Detroit has finally ended. The Red Wings traded their former captain to the Montreal Canadiens in exchange for a haul, including two conditional first-round picks, two other draft picks, top prospect Michael Hage, Kirby Dach, and Sam Montembault. What a blockbuster between two Original Six franchises!

More Detroit Hockey Now: Max Smith analyzes the trade and offers a grade for the Red Wings return. Detroit didn't get a ton of current NHL roster help, but adding Hage, plus two first-round picks, is about as good as interim GM Shawn Horcoff could have done.

NHLtraderumors.me: Quietly, Alexander Ovechkin is currently going through something he's never dealt with before. The Great 8 isn't even The Good 8 right now for the Washington Capitals. Ovechkin has now gone 16 straight games without a goal, marking the longest goal drought of his Hall of Fame career.

NHL.com: Jake Oettinger already has league-leading five shutouts this season after blanking the Pittsburgh Penguins 1-0 on Sunday. With four straight losses, the Penguins are now below the Blackhawks in the league-wide standings.

Sportsnet: Former Blackhawks Jason Dickinson and Colton Dach connected for a goal early in the third period, putting the Edmonton Oilers ahead 4-1. But the St. Louis Blues rallied for three late goals to force overtime, then Mason McTavish won the game in a shootout. St. Louis is now 8-3-1 at home this season.
