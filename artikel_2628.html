New NFL Trend Should Shape Your Week 10 NFL Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Something interesting is happening in the NFL and should shape your opinion on your picks moving forward.

You can do analysis of every NFL football stat known to man, but to me there are only a few that matter: Who wins and by how much.

In the last three weeks, the average margin of victory in the NFL has gone from 11.1 (in Week 7) to 11.5 (in Week 8) to 12.87 points last week.

I still think it's a take-the-points league, but what good is a philosophy if it doesn't adapt and change with the times? We have to start identifying games that will be blowouts (by my definition, anything more than 10 points) and react accordingly.

KANSAS CITY, MISSOURI - SEPTEMBER 20: Patrick Mahomes #15 of the Kansas City Chiefs reacts prior to the game against the Indianapolis Colts at Arrowhead Stadium on September 20, 2026 in Kansas City, Missouri. (Photo by Jamie Squire/Getty Images)

Inside the Odds: The Chiefs are 4-4 against the spread and 2-2 on the road this season. KC has won every game straight up they have been favored in, lost both they were dogs in. Atlanta has been underdogs four straight and has lost four out of five straight up. However, they are 7-1 in last game games against the spread as underdogs.

The pick: I think this is a spot where KC is undervalued and this game is a blowout for the Chiefs. Mahomes is dealing with a sore elbow – we will probably find out after the season he tore a tendon or something, but he's playing. Kenneth Walker III is dealing with a groin injury too. The Chiefs added Josh Downs in a trade. Atlanta's offense has melted the last two weeks. I like KC big.

SANTA CLARA, CALIFORNIA - NOVEMBER 08: Kirk Cousins #8 of the Las Vegas Raiders throws a pass against the San Francisco 49ers during the second quarter at Levi's Stadium on November 08, 2026 in Santa Clara, California. (Photo by Ezra Shaw/Getty Images)

Inside the Odds: Maybe the best game of the weekend. Minnesota is 4-4 against the spread and 3-1 on the road. Green Bay has won five of six straight up and its last five games against the spread. Minnesota won the game in Week 1.

The pick: Kirk Cousins is riding to the rescue for the Vikings. He will start for Minnesota after a trade with the Raiders brought him back to Minnesota. Are the Vikings in desperation mode already? They do have a championship defense. But I'm just not sold on a 38-year-old QB who has been around the NFL for making a great impact. You can't go home again. Green Bay is motivated and I believe will cover the spread.

CHARLOTTE, NORTH CAROLINA - OCTOBER 04: Tetairoa McMillan #4 of the Carolina Panthers celebrates a second quarter touchdown against the Detroit Lions at Bank of America Stadium on October 04, 2026 in Charlotte, North Carolina. (Photo by David Jensen/Getty Images)

Inside the Odds: Both teams are 3-5, and uh, in first place in the NFC South. Both teams are also 3-5 against the spread. Carolina is 4-4 over/under while the Saints are 5-3. The Saints have won five of the last six meetings and are 4-1-1 against the spread in those games.

The pick: Someone has to win the NFC South, and I don't make the rules pal, but the division champ gets a home game in the first round of the playoffs. Those are the rules. The Saints have weirdly dominated the Panthers the last few years and are coming off an OT win over the Browns. The Saints pass rush with Chase Young is pretty good. But Carolina is more balanced. I'm taking the Panthers to cover the spread, maybe even blow the Saints out.

SEATTLE, WASHINGTON - NOVEMBER 08: Drew Lock #2 of the Seattle Seahawks looks to pass against the Arizona Cardinals during the first quarter at Lumen Field on November 08, 2026 in Seattle, Washington. (Photo by Olivia Vanni/Getty Images)

Inside the Odds: Vegas is 6-3 against the spread this season but 4-5 straight on. They're also 7-2 over/under (they give up a lot of points). Seattle is 5-4 and 4-5 against the spread. They've been taking every team's best shot as defending Super Bowl champs.

The pick: So it's Cousins out and Mendoza in for Las Vegas. At least he makes his first start at home, but the Seattle defense will not be kind. The Seahawks defense is tied for fourth in points allowed and gives up 244 yards a game. Vegas has lost five of its last six. Drew Lock gets the start again for Seattle at QB. The Seahawks hope to get everyone back after a bye next week. The NFL isn't kind to rookie QBs. I like Seattle comfortably.

INDIANAPOLIS, INDIANA - NOVEMBER 08: Caleb Downs #13 of the Dallas Cowboys celebrates with teammates after a game-winning interception against the Indianapolis Colts during the fourth quarter at Lucas Oil Stadium on November 08, 2026 in Indianapolis, Indiana. (Photo by Michael Hickey/Getty Images)

Inside the Odds: San Francisco has won the last four meetings and covered the spread in all four. The Niners are 5-3 against the spread and 6-1 ATS in their last seven games as road favorites. Dallas is 4-5 for real and 4-5 against the spread, but Dallas is 5-0-1 in their last six games as a home underdog.

The pick: I think Vegas is giving Dallas a bump for some reason. The Cowboys are coming off a tough win over the (checks notes) Colts. San Francisco looks like the best team in the NFL. I've seen too much of Dallas' defense to think this is close. They couldn't stop (checks notes) Arizona. This one looks like a Niners romp.
