Lakers become latest franchise to land partnership with prediction market behemoth

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lakers announced a new partnership with Kalshi on Tuesday, which will serve as the team's official prediction market sponsor.

The NBA announced partnerships with Kalshi and Polymarket last month.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw against the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

"We're excited to partner with Kalshi to explore innovative ways to engage fans and strengthen their connection to the game," Lakers president and chief business officer Lon Rosen said Tuesday.

The Kalshi partnership came a day after new owners Josh Kushner and Bob Iger attended their first Lakers game since purchasing the team at a record $12.5 billion. Kushner and Iger were spotted sitting with Lakers governor Jeanie Buss.

Austin Reaves hit a variety of big shots in the Lakers' win over the Pistons. AP Photo/Duane Burleson

"Expanding our footprint in the NBA alongside a legendary franchise like the Los Angeles Lakers is an incredible milestone for Kalshi," said Adam Barrick, Kalshi head of sports partnerships.

"The Lakers represent a global standard of excellence with a dedicated and loyal fan base. We are thrilled to collaborate on bringing the Lakers fans new ways to engage with their favorite team."

Kalshi and Polymarket logos in this illustration taken on October 24, 2026. REUTERS

The Lakers aren't the only sports franchise in Los Angeles to have partnered with Kalshi. The Dodgers announced a multiyear brand deal in August as one of five MLB teams to make Kalshi their exclusive prediction market partner.
