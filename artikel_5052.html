BlackRock Bitcoin ETF outflows hit $2B over nine sessions

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

US spot Bitcoin ETF outflows rose to nearly $300 million on Wednesday, up from $95 million a day earlier, as five-session withdrawals reached $1.44 billion.

BlackRock’s spot Bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF), the iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT), recorded more than $2 billion in net outflows over nine consecutive trading sessions through Wednesday.

Investors pulled a net $269.7 million from IBIT on Wednesday, accounting for about 90% of the day’s $300 million in net withdrawals across US spot Bitcoin ETFs, according to Farside Investors data.

Grayscale’s Bitcoin Trust ETF (GBTC) recorded $39.1 million in net withdrawals, while the Morgan Stanley Bitcoin Trust ETF (MSBT) drew $9 million in net inflows.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs posted nearly $1.44 billion in net withdrawals over five straight sessions. Wednesday’s increase followed three sessions of easing outflows.

Crypto exchange Bitfinex said on Wednesday that slowing ETF outflows could be an early step toward a market floor, but were not enough on their own to establish a recovery.

Bitfinex found that in the ETF outflow streaks it studied earlier this year, Bitcoin gained a median 6.4% over 10 trading sessions when the streaks ended with at least three consecutive sessions of net inflows.

Bitcoin traded near $73,000 at the time of writing, down 8.5% over the past week, according to CoinGecko.

In a Wednesday note shared with Cointelegraph, Laser Digital’s derivatives trading desk pointed to weak spot demand, a persistently negative Coinbase premium and large liquidations of leveraged long positions.

The desk said Bitcoin barely rose during Monday’s rebound in the Nasdaq, which gained almost 3%.
