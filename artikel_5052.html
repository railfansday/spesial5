Ripple Custody Partners With Major Private Bank in Türkiye

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ripple has once again landed a major partnership in Türkiye, but this time around with one of the country’s largest private banks, putting XRP in the spotlight.

While this comes as part of Ripple’s growing expansion into the traditional financial space, the blockchain firm announced it has partnered with Yapı Kredi, a major Turkish private bank, to support its cryptocurrency business.

As part of the partnership agreement, Ripple Custody will help the bank to securely manage its digital assets and provide custody services for its crypto trading platform dubbed Yapı Kredi Kripto.

While the partnership demands the full integration of the Ripple custodial infrastructure, the firm revealed that Ripple Custody will be installed on the bank's own premises.

This way, Yapı Kredi will have access to adequate security and control it needs to manage all digital assets on behalf of its customers, boosting its security.

The development is significant for the bank and the broader Turkish crypto market; it also marks a major milestone for Ripple as the firm claims to be the only custody service provider currently supporting leading Turkish banks licensed by the CMB as crypto asset custodians.

It is important to note that Türkiye currently stands as one of the world's top 10 retail crypto markets, with XRP recorded as one of its most widely used cryptocurrencies.

With Yapı Kredi now fortifying its position as a major player in Türkiye’s fast-growing crypto market, its partnership with the blockchain firm associated with XRP is likely to open new opportunities for XRP while positioning it for bigger adoption in the country.
