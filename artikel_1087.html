Interim Blackburn boss reveals Tony Mowbray message to players before derby

Blackburn came back twice to draw against rivals Burnley in their first match since Mowbray stood down

Blackburn’s players followed Tony Mowbray’s pre-match instructions to ensure they earned a 2-2 Championship draw at neighbours Burnley, assistant head coach Damien Johnson said.

Johnson and fellow coach Mike Dodds have taken charge of the team after Mowbray announced he was stepping back from his duties on Tuesday because of health issues.

Mowbray, appointed head coach in June, has been in contact with club staff since the announcement.

He missed the trip to Turf Moor, on his 63rd birthday, for what would have been his 900th match in management.

Without him, Blackburn came back from behind twice – with Andri Gudjohnsen’s 12th goal of the season and Todd Cantwell’s 74th-minute penalty cancelling out goals from Largie Ramazani and Jaydon Banel.

Johnson said it had been a huge surprise when Mowbray had announced the need to take a step back.

He said: “Tony’s had a huge impact on the club. And that’s with the staff, the players, everyone. He had a close connection with us all really. And so it was a shock and a concern.

“He felt he had to take a step back and so we respect that and we want what’s best for him. And he sent some messages and had been in contact and he wanted us just to knuckle down, prepare for the game properly. And I felt the players did that.

“They’ve been terrific all week and I thought they took that into the game today in what was in these circumstances.”

Blackburn warmed up in T-shirts bearing the message ‘We’re with you, Tony’, with Gudjohnsen grabbing one from a pitchside photographer to hold up for the cameras after his 31st-minute goal.

They had a testing afternoon on the field, having to deal with the early loss of defender Tom Atcheson, who was concussed after receiving a boot to the face.

“Taking the week into consideration, it’s not been an easy one, but the reaction from the players after the initial announcement was terrific and we knew this would be a difficult game,” Johnson added.

“All in all, I’m really proud of the players and the effort and the character they’ve shown this week and in the game this afternoon. And so while we’d have loved to have won it, I think, all in all, we’ll take a point.”
