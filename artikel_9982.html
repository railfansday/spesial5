Frontier Design Benchmark

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Exploring the frontier of AI design capabilities through real client briefs from Contra’s marketplace, golden designs and model comparisons.

How frontier models rank in blind head-to-head votes on real client briefs from Contra’s marketplace.

Frontier Design Benchmark is the first benchmark built on real world, high value, and complex design projects. It is judged by professional designers in the Contra Labs network, ensuring the quality of evaluation.

In the benchmark, we also built a design critique rubric and a list of AI slop patterns. The rubric scores are highly correlated with the designers’ ratings of the models (r = 0.94). These additional metrics provide explainable design quality judgement on top of pairwise preferences.

Real client briefs, each with its golden: the design set beside the models’ outputs as the bar.

Two model outputs for the same brief, live and side by side, without the models’ names. Pick the better one, or call a tie.
