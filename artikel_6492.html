One-Pan Creamy Spinach-Artichoke Gnocchi

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Out of all the party dips, spinach and artichoke reigns supreme in our book. While we’re all for a chip-and-dip dinner, that’s not the most family-friendly weeknight meal, so we turned it into this decadent pasta dish. We combined everything we love about our favorite dip—spinach, artichokes, cream & cheese—with quick-cooking gnocchi, turning it into an easy meatless dinner that’s loaded with flavor. It’s easy enough for when you’re just craving comfort food (just one pan!), but fancy enough to serve to guests too.

Top tips & variations:We added briny capers here to help cut through all the richness, but if you’re not a fan, feel free to skip. While the gnocchi simmer, stir to prevent sticking, and try to keep them in an even layer so they stay submerged and cook evenly. The recipe calls for marinated artichokes because they’re already flavored with ingredients like garlic and red pepper, but canned or frozen will work in a pinch too.

It’s up to you if you’d like to only bake it until melted or take it one step further by letting your gnocchi sit under the broiler for a minute to get some attractive browned spots. Whichever you choose, we definitely recommend the final basil garnish to add flavor and freshness to your final dish.

Storage:Have leftovers? Store them in an airtight container in the fridge for 3-4 days.
