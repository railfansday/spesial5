300 Beautiful Muslim Baby Names And Their Meanings

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

150 boy names and 150 girl names, filled with history, beauty, and qualities a child can grow into.

Choosing a baby name can begin with sound, family tradition, or a meaning that catches you at exactly the right moment. Some names feel strong and grounded; others bring to mind light, tenderness, wisdom, or the beauty of the natural world. Then there are the ones that simply sound right when spoken aloud.

Muslim baby names draw from a rich range of languages, histories, and naming traditions. Arabic and Quranic choices appear here alongside other names found within Muslim families and communities. Some are familiar favorites, while others may offer something less commonly heard.

Meanings and English spellings can vary between languages, regions, and transliteration styles, so it is worth checking the pronunciation and cultural context before making a final choice. Ahead, find 300 Muslim baby names and the meanings connected to them.

Ihsan – Excellence; kindness; beautiful conduct

Tahsin – Improvement; enrichment; beautification

Delara – Beloved; one who delights the heart

Layan – Softness; gentleness; comfortable living

Rayhana – Fragrant basil; sweet-scented plant

A beautiful meaning is an important beginning, but a name also lives through pronunciation, family history, and the culture in which a child will grow. Say your favorites with the family name, write them down, and ask relatives how they sound in the languages your child may hear at home.

Often, the right choice is the one that feels natural when spoken and holds something your family hopes to pass on. It may be waiting somewhere in this list, or one name may lead you back to a family favorite you had not considered before. Either way, that moment of recognition is worth waiting for.
