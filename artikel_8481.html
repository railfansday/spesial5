However, my personal take is:

a) Financial risks, from the credit issues of Coreweave, to Oracle's debt, to the S1 filings of Anthropic, were/are / have been priced in incrementally as the market adjusts to new information

So now the interesting derivative will be how much increasing information about b) actually ends up impacting a).

Or put differently, will an increase in potential increase of regulatory scrutiny dampen access to capital for a sector that was already facing credit quality downgrades?
