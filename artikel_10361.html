Oppo may finally be fixing broken 3rd-party launchers with ColorOS 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Most Chinese-brand Android phones don’t like it when you use a 3rd-party launcher, but the ColorOS 17 update may be changing that.

If I hop on a phone from Oppo, Vivo, Honor, or most other Chinese brands and try to use a 3rd-party launcher, I’m definitely not going to like how the phone operates. Often, there’s a weird delay when navigating back home or opening the Overview multitasking screen. Sometimes, Overview doesn’t work at all, essentially forcing you back to the default launcher that shipped with the phone.

But one Reddit user says Oppo is doing things differently in the ColorOS 17 update. According to their experience, the delay and other issues with 3rd-party launchers are completely solved. OnePlus’s OxygenOS doesn’t have the same issues, so it’s good to see that OnePlus users who are still set to receive ColorOS 17 in the near future won’t feel like their phones are losing a valuable feature.

Chinese Android brands like Oppo, Vivo, Xiaomi, Realme, and several others often outshine Western brands in many ways, but they’ve always had one annoying weakness: software. Whether it’s the number of updates a phone receives, overall quality, or another specific issue, Chinese brands often build very different visions of Android than Google, Samsung, Motorola, or Nothing.

The value and hardware prowess these phones usually deliver help outshine software weaknesses, and these companies have been steadily improving software over the past year or two to meet the demands of a more Westernized audience. Companies like Honor now offer 7 years of software updates, and ColorOS is widely regarded as the best Android skin next to Samsung’s One UI in many circles. That’s great news for everyone, especially in an age where fewer smartphone companies compete for your hard-earned dollars.
