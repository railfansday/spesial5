With excellent credit, pick a card for how you spend, not for how exclusive it is. For most people, that’s a no-fee 2% card like the Wells Fargo Active Cash. Pay for a premium travel card like the Capital One Venture X only if you’ll use its yearly travel credit.

The first decision is whether to pay an annual fee. A premium travel card earns it back through statement credits, lounge visits, and points, but only on trips and spending you’d have anyway. Without those, a no-fee card that pays 2% on everything, or up to 5% in bonus categories, comes out ahead.

Each card below is a top pick for one way of spending. If most of your card spending goes to restaurants or supermarkets, a dining card or a grocery card pays more there than a flat 2%.
