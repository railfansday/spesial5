Strength training can do more than build muscle. Research has linked it to better overall health and physical function, making it a worthwhile part of a regular fitness routine. But finding the time, space, and equipment to train consistently can be a challenge.

Home gyms appeal to so many lifters. You can work out on your own schedule, skip the trip to the gym, and choose equipment that fits your space. But if you’re starting from scratch, what do you really need?

Adjustable dumbbells are worth considering, especially if you don’t have room for a full rack of weights. Instead of buying a separate pair for every weight, you can change the resistance on a single set. They take up less room while supporting many of the same exercises you’d do with traditional dumbbells.

You can use them for presses, rows, curls, lunges, and more. Keep in mind, though, that adjustable dumbbells can feel different from traditional ones. Their shape, grip, and adjustment system vary by model, so consider which available weight range suits your training needs.

5 Ways to Get Started With Adjustable Dumbbells

Adjustable dumbbells have been around for decades, and manufacturers have developed different ways to make changing weights more convenient. One company in this space is PowerBlock, founded by Carl Towley in Owatonna, Minnesota, in 1993.

PowerBlock’s design uses a nested weight stack and a magnetic selector pin to change the load without requiring a separate pair of dumbbells for every weight. The system is designed to keep multiple resistance options in a compact footprint, which can be useful for lifters working with limited space.

PowerBlock offers dumbbells that are non-expandable, meaning they’re fixed in their range, and dumbbells that are expandable, meaning that you can purchase up to the weight you know you’re able to move, and as you get stronger, your dumbbells get heavier with expansion kits for the specific model.

Two models in the lineup offer different weight ranges and expansion options to accommodate lifters as their strength improves.

The Pro 100 EXP starts at 5 pounds per dumbbell and can expand to 100 pounds per dumbbell with additional weight kits. Depending on the configuration, weight increments range from approximately 2.5 to 5 pounds. Urethane-coated weight plates and grip options add versatility, while the expandable design allows users to increase the weight capacity as their strength grows. The set also helps save valuable home-gym space by replacing 30 pairs of traditional dumbbells.

The Elite USA 90 offers a similar space-saving design, with a capacity of up to 90 pounds per dumbbell. Its steel weight plates (Made in the USA) and 2.5- to 5-pound increments provide options for progressively increasing resistance. According to PowerBlock, the set replaces 28 individual dumbbells and 825 pounds of free weights in a compact footprint.

With more than 30 years in the adjustable-dumbbell market, PowerBlock says its mission is to make versatile fitness equipment accessible to people at every stage of training.

“Our focus is to provide high-quality, versatile fitness equipment for people at every stage of their fitness journey, whether they’re just getting started or continuing to pursue their goals,” says Josh Leake at PowerBlock. “Looking to the future, our goals are to keep providing premium home fitness equipment and continue to innovate to serve our customers best.”

Adjustable dumbbells can be a good fit if space is limited or you want several weight options without buying a full rack.

Before buying, compare the weight range, how the adjustment system works, the overall dimensions, expansion options, and price. Think about the exercises you do most often and whether the dumbbells will still meet your needs as you get stronger.

A useful home gym doesn’t have to be complicated. Start with equipment that fits your space, budget, and goals, then build your routine from there.
