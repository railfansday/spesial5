5 Early Cataract Warning Signs People in Their 40s and 50s Often Miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Many assume cataracts are an older person's problem, but changes behind the condition can begin in your 40s and 50s—producing subtle symptoms easily mistaken for normal aging. Eye specialists explain five early warning signs, from night-driving glare to fading colors, plus when to get checked and how surgery helps roughly nine in 10 patients.
