A basic pair of single-vision glasses, lenses included, starts under $10 at some online sellers and near $100 at others. Price the exact lenses your prescription needs at two or three sellers, then pick the one whose return terms and insurance billing fit you.

A basic pair of single-vision glasses starts at $6.95 at Zenni Optical, with the lenses, a scratch-resistant coating, and UV protection included. At Warby Parker a basic pair starts at $95, a price that also covers an anti-reflective coating.

Both are starting prices for the simplest lenses. A stronger prescription, progressive lenses, or thinner lenses add charges that differ from one seller to the next, so a frame’s price says little until your own lenses are in the cart. Where you fill the prescription can move the total more than the frame you pick.

Before you shop, have a copy of your prescription and your pupillary distance, the space between your pupils, which online orders ask for. Then price the same lens package at two or three sellers. Check how each lets you judge the fit, whether by webcam, frames mailed home, or an optician in a store, and how long you have to return or exchange a pair.
