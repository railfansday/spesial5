Take-Two CEO is shutting down questions about GTA 7 just days after GTA 6’s launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick is already being asked about GTA 7, despite GTA 6 only being days old.

Rockstar released GTA 6 on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, ending a 13-year wait for a new numbered Grand Theft Auto.

But while players are only just getting their first look at Vice City and the rest of Leonida, some are already looking ahead to the next entry in the series.

On launch day, Zelnick joined the TBPN podcast to talk about the release, and was asked early on about when GTA 7 could drop, to which he answered: “Once you get through several hundred hours of the most incredible fun you’ve ever heard, ask the question again.”

Later in the interview, he was asked about the sequel again and how big the map could be, given that GTA 6 covers a huge city and a surrounding state.

“Theoretically, GTA 7 could just be a live version of the entire world, right?” one of the hosts asked, with Zelnick clearly amused by the question.

“We at Take-Two just went live with GTA 6 on November 19,” he jokingly replied. “Let me check my watch. Oh, it’s still November 19. And you’re asking a question about GTA 7. I mean guys, really?”

He then mentioned a quote his father used to say about past achievements being “yesterday’s headlines,” but then quipped: “It’s not even yesterday.”

With GTA 6 only just arriving, it will likely be years before we get any concrete information about its potential follow-up. However, Zelnick has hinted that Rockstar’s future games won’t take as long to make.

Plus, we still have GTA 6 Online to look forward to, which should tide players over until GTA 7.
