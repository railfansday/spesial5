Bolton Wanderers team news and predicted XI vs Portsmouth with defensive reshuffle needed to improve leaky backline

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bolton Wanderers return from the international break looking to pick themselves up from a demoralising defeat to Derby County two weeks ago. Steven Schumacher’s team sit seven points above the bottom three but risk sliding at pace if they can’t begin to fix their defensive woes, having conceded 29 goals so far.

The biggest issue for the Trotters is their home form. BWFC have lost all but two of seven games at the Toughsheet Community Stadium, conceding 15 of their 29 goals at the ground. Wanderers have to find form at home, and will hope Saturday’s game can be the start.

Whilst Bolton Wanderers have been trending down, it is the opposite for Portsmouth. Despite losing 2-1 to Stoke City before the break, Pompey have won three of their last six, sitting just two points off the top eight.

John Mousinho’s side head to Bolton with a good record against their opponents and will be hopeful of returning to winning ways at 15:00 on Saturday.

Bolton Wanderers have welcomed back all four internationals without any injury concern.

Thierry Gale and Chris Forino, who faced each other whilst playing for Barbados and Saint Lucia, both came back unscathed. The same can be said for Gaizka Larrazabal and Josh Sheehan, who both played one game for their respective nations.

Eoin Toal and Gavin Bazunu could be available on Saturday after training this week.

Bazunu in particular could be a big boost for BWFC. Whilst Jack Bonham has proven to be a solid deputy, the Irishman has been an astute signing from Southampton, preventing 1.45 expected goals and claiming two clean sheets this season.

Elsewhere John McAtee, Ryan Hardie, Jayden Nelson and Ethan Erhahon all remain out injured.

Bonham; Stephenson, Forino, Davies, Famewo; Sheehan, Simons; Larrazabal, Rodrigues, Gale; Dalby

With Bazunu’s full status as of yet unconfirmed, Bonham keeps his place, but the defence is reshuffled. Akin Famewo moves to left-back in place of Max Conway, with Ben Davies taking his position in central defence.

Bolton Wanderers have need to stop conceding easy goals and they have performed better playing one central defender on either side of the back four. Famewo has done so before and played well. On Saturday, he will have the task of handling Adrian Segecic and Terry Devlin on the Pompey right.

Gaizka Larrazabal continues at right-wing having been one of Wanderers’ better players at Pride Park, whilst Gale also keeps his place, simply because he is one of the only wide men who often tracks back, something BWFC need to stop their defensive issues.

Bolton Wanderers’ clash with Portsmouth kicks off at 15:00 on Saturday afternoon.
