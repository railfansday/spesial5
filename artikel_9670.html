Team8 Expands Into AI Infrastructure With Infra Village Launch at the NYSE, Names 2027's AI Leaders

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

For large organizations, the conversation around artificial intelligence (AI) is moving past whether to adopt it and toward what it takes to run it at scale. Team8, a venture fund with more than $2 billion in assets under management, is organizing its next phase of investing around that shift. The firm has launched the Infra Village on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), widening its company-building and investment activities to cover AI and software infrastructure.

The initiative was showcased at "The Floor is Yours," an NYSE industry event, where Team8 also unveiled its Next AI Leaders of 2027, a recognition program created in collaboration with Amazon Web Services (AWS), Anthropic and OpenAI. The program recognizes the next generation of influential technology executives shaping how AI is built, secured and deployed.

Team8 ties the launch to a broader shift in the AI market. As organizations move past experimentation and deploy AI across their operations, the firm says, the challenge is increasingly about the infrastructure required to run these systems with control, reliability and predictable costs.

Liran Grinberg, Co-founder and Managing Partner at Team8, framed the expansion in historical terms. "Every major technology revolution eventually becomes an infrastructure revolution. AI is no exception. As enterprises move from experimentation to large-scale adoption, the companies that will define this next chapter are those solving the fundamental challenges of running AI reliably, securely, and economically," he said.

The firm is not entering the category from a standing start. Team8 has already created and backed several infrastructure companies, including Port, Finout and Classiq, which it counts among the fastest-growing companies in its portfolio.

Port raised $100 million at a valuation approaching $1 billion and was named to Forbes Israel's 2026 Next Billion Dollar Startups list. Classiq drew significant attention of its own, with Intel acquiring the company for $2.3 billion, described as the largest deal to date in quantum computing. Team8 presents these developments as an early indication of the opportunity in infrastructure, and the Infra Village is designed to extend that pattern across a broader range of AI and software infrastructure companies.

Grinberg tied the new effort directly to the firm's resources and relationships. "We see an opportunity to apply our company-building experience, capital, and enterprise network to help create the next generation of infrastructure companies," he said.

The Infra Village combines three elements: a dedicated venture-creation effort, additional investments and a new executive-village community. Together they are meant to help build the infrastructure layer that the next generation of enterprise AI will rely on, a foundation that Team8 describes as spanning compute, inference, data, developer platforms, observability and security.

The community component follows a format Team8 has used before. Israel became a global cybersecurity powerhouse by combining deep technical talent with early, direct access to the enterprises defining the industry's most pressing challenges, and Team8 helped develop that model through its CISO Village, which connected cybersecurity founders with enterprise security leaders, including chief information security officers (CISOs). The Infra Village extends the same Village model to AI and software infrastructure. Chief information officers (CIOs), chief technology officers (CTOs) and AI and infrastructure executives from major enterprises gain a vendor-neutral setting to discuss what works in production, how infrastructure costs are developing and which technology investments deserve attention. The same community gives founders direct connections to those executives.

Aviad Harell, Managing Partner at Team8, explained the thinking behind the community. "Building successful infrastructure companies starts with understanding the problems enterprises are facing in production. The Infra Village brings the executives responsible for these decisions into direct conversation with the founders developing the solutions," he said. "By creating a vendor-neutral environment for sharing knowledge and identifying emerging needs, we can help founders build companies around real enterprise challenges and accelerate the development of the AI infrastructure ecosystem."

Team8 also points to the pace of change. Decisions that once took years are now being made within a single quarter, according to the firm, and responsibility for AI infrastructure is spread across CIOs, CTOs and data leaders rather than sitting with one owner. The vendor landscape is also changing faster than traditional analyst cycles. At the same time, the capabilities behind enterprise AI must support workloads that did not exist just three years ago, while helping organizations manage environments that are becoming more complex and more expensive.

Team8 sees this transition as an opportunity to apply its established company-building model to a new category of enterprise technology. The Infra Village brings the CIOs, CTOs and infrastructure executives responsible for these decisions into direct contact with the founders building solutions.

The Next AI Leaders of 2027 program was created in collaboration with AWS, HumanX, NYSE, Kiteworks, Goodwin, Meitar and Anthropic, according to Team8. Judges include senior industry experts such as Erwan Menard, SVP Product Management, Crusoe; Tasso Argyros, VP of Engineering, Databricks; Apoorv Jha, Applied AI, OpenAI; Andrew Blum, Co-Founder & COO, HumanX; Rachael Wang, Head of Agentic AI Professional Services, AWS; and Andrew Reiskind, Chief Data Officer, Mastercard. Selected from hundreds of nominations, the 20 honorees include CIOs, CTOs and senior technology executives, many of whom already lead AI infrastructure initiatives at major organizations. Their work spans AI infrastructure and compute, inference, developer platforms, data and applied AI, at organizations including Microsoft, Meta, Adobe, Anthropic, Salesforce, Cloudflare, CoreWeave, Cerebras, Mistral AI and Wiz, among others.

Team8 positions the Infra Village as the next step in carrying its venture creation and investment models, its network of industry leaders and its company validation process into the infrastructure that powers enterprise AI. The firm's stated aim is to position founders to address some of the world's most significant technology challenges, starting with the systems enterprises need to run AI at scale.

Full honoree profiles are available on Team8's Next AI Leaders of 2027 page.
