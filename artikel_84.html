French far right on track to secure €10 million loan for Le Pen's presidential campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

France's far-right National Rally party on Tuesday reached a preliminary agreement with two European banks for a loan to help fund Marine Le Pen's presidential campaign. The lenders are expected to finalise the €10 million loan by the end of the month.
