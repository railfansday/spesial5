The Hot List: 20 Must-Have Pieces, According to Instagram’s Favorite Fashion Sourcer

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Welcome to the Hot List, a monthly column from fashion sourcer extraordinaire Gab Waller. As a professional personal shopper to some of the best-dressed girls—she gets about 100 requests a day to track down hard-to-find wares!—Waller spends her days sourcing the most in-demand fashion and accessories. Each month, and exclusively for Vogue, she’ll deliver a cheat sheet of all the pieces she recommends you scoop up before it’s too late and the most dreaded three words in online shopping appear: “out of stock.” You can shop her personal favorites on FOUND By Gab. “This is a world in which I live and breathe,” Waller says. “I get to be a fashion detective day in and day out, and I have eyes and ears all over the globe.” Let the fashion snooping begin!

November, already!? I’m sure I’m not alone in wondering how we’re possibly already in November. This year has flown by! With Thanksgiving right around the corner, I’m starting to feel the holiday energy and can’t wait for all the festivities over the next two months to close out 2026. As parties fill up calendars, so do my DMs with requests for what to wear to each. I wanted to focus this month’s column on just that. Think velvet, sequins, extra fun colors, embellishments of all kinds, and more. Let the celebrations—and the holiday dressing—begin!

We all collectively need a go-to LBD for the holidays, and this newest style from Khaite ticks all the right boxes. I LOVE that they described it as having a doll-like silhouette. I’ll take it!
