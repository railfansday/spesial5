Copycat Coopers Hawk Red Wine–Braised Short Ribs

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This copycat Cooper's Hawk red wine-braised short ribs recipe delivers fall-apart-tender short ribs bathed in a luxurious sauce. It's like dining out in your kitchen.

One taste of the red wine–braised short ribs at Cooper’s Hawk is enough to understand why they’ve remained a bestseller. A generous portion of melt-in-your-mouth braised short ribs is stacked over a mound of buttery whipped potatoes, then drizzled with umami-rich braising liquid. When served with roasted vegetables and garnished with crispy fried onion strings, it’s a plate of comfort food that’s just as much a feast for the eyes as it is for the taste buds.

Whether you’ve been to Cooper’s Hawk and want to recreate the magic at home, or you don’t live near a Cooper’s Hawk location and want to know what all the fuss is about, this copycat recipe belongs on your must-try list. It’s the perfect recipe for chilly days when you want a meal that feels like a warm hug. It’s also impressive-looking and great for feeding a crowd, so keep it in mind as the holiday season approaches.

Preheat the oven to 425°F. Sprinkle the short ribs with salt and pepper. In an ovenproof Dutch oven over medium-high heat, brown the ribs in oil in batches. Remove the meat and set it aside.

Add the celery, carrot and onion to the drippings, and cook them until they’re tender. Add the garlic, and cook for one minute longer. Add the wine and broth, stirring to loosen the browned bits from the pan.

Return the ribs to the pot. Add the parsley and thyme. Cover the pot and transfer it to the preheated oven. Cook the ribs for 2 hours to 2 hours and 30 minutes or until the meat is tender.

While the ribs are braising, in a small bowl, whisk all the butter ingredients until they’re blended. Transfer the mixture to a sheet of storage wrap, and roll it into a log. Refrigerate the butter until it’s chilled. Unwrap and slice it.

Place the hot mashed potatoes on a serving plate. Top them with the short ribs. Immediately top the ribs with a slice of Dijon butter and french-fried onions. Garnish the dish with additional fresh parsley, if desired.

Store any leftover short ribs in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Enjoy them within four days. Leftovers can be microwaved in a microwave-safe container, or they can be transferred to a Dutch oven and warmed in a 350° oven until they’re heated through.

Braised short ribs freeze like a dream. Store them in a freezer-safe airtight container and freeze them for three to four months. Defrost them completely in the refrigerator before warming them in the microwave or in the oven.

Short ribs are a heavily marbled cut of beef from the lower portion of the rib cage (the same area as the chuck and brisket). They have a robust, beefy flavor but contain a lot of connective tissue, so you need to cook them slowly with low heat for the meat to become tender.

Braising is one of the best (and most popular) ways to prepare short ribs. That said, you can make bone-in short ribs in a slow cooker or on a wood, pellet or charcoal smoker. To speed up the process, you can also make tender bone-in short ribs in a pressure cooker.

Cooper’s Hawk serves their short ribs with mashed potatoes and roasted vegetables, but that is far from the only pairing option.
