Marshalls and Lindsay Lohan Launch "Unwrap the Internet," a Collection of Holiday Gift Guides Inspired by Today's Most Engaged Online Communities

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Featuring seven influential personalities and creators across reality TV, wellness, travel and more, the campaign translates the interests and recommendations shaping online communities into shoppable guides filled with high-quality gifts, unexpected finds, and premium brands at amazing prices.

FRAMINGHAM, Mass., Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- This holiday season, Marshalls (NYSE: TJX) is teaming up with Lindsay Lohan to launch "Unwrap the Internet," a collection of holiday gift guides inspired by some of the internet's most engaged communities to help shoppers uncover the gifts people actually want. Spanning wellness, travel, parenting and more, the collection brings together seven influential personalities and creators who tapped into their audiences to help curate each category with high-quality gifts. Uniquely built to turn what people across the internet are talking about into shoppable guides, "Unwrap the Internet" offers gifting inspiration for every budget and personality.

People aren't just into things anymore; they're deeply invested in the communities and cultural conversations that shape who they are. Yet when it comes to holiday gifting, people are often reduced to broad categories, like "gifts for mom" or "gifts for a co-worker," missing the thoughtful finds that show you truly understand them. For a campaign inspired by the corners of the internet people care deeply about, Marshalls found a natural partner in Lohan, whose work has inspired passionate fandom of its own. As the face of the campaign, she steps into these corners of the internet in a four-part video series that brings together all seven personalities and creators as unexpected worlds collide, uncovering shared fandoms and incredible Marshalls finds along the way.

Because Marshalls is built for delivering unexpected discoveries across its expansive mix of merchandise, coveted brands, and accessible price points, shoppers can find thoughtful gifts that speak to even the most specific personalities, passions, and interests on their lists. Available to shop throughout the holiday season, the guides leverage insider perspectives and community recommendations from across the internet. "The holidays can be hard to shop for, but there's nothing more meaningful than finding a gift that shows you really understand someone," said Lohan. "I love how this campaign brings together experts from every corner of the internet, tapping into their unique interests and passions to make gifting feel easier and more personal. Through this campaign, I hope to help holiday shoppers feel inspired to discover thoughtful, unexpected gifts that reflect what makes everyone on their list special."

Now live, the four-part video series stars Lohan, who steps into different corners of the internet and brings together all seven personalities and creators to unwrap shared fandoms, surprising connections and incredible Marshalls finds. As unexpected worlds collide, she helps show how even the most specific passions can lead to a gift that says, "I know you."

"With inspiration everywhere, from the communities we follow to the conversations we're having online, 'Unwrap the Internet' brings those interests to life and helps people discover gifts they'll genuinely be excited to give and receive," said Emily Trent, Senior Vice President, Head of Marketing at Marshalls. "The way we buy means there is always something new to find at Marshalls, with an ever-changing assortment of high-quality finds that spans interests and price points. That breadth allows us to move beyond one-size-fits-all gifting and offer something for every kind of shopper, whether they're shopping early or right down to the wire. Just as importantly, we wanted the campaign to be fun, with entertaining content that celebrates the many interests that bring people together and the joy of finding something unexpected."

Catch all the festive fun featuring Lindsay Lohan and unexpected community crossovers on @Marshalls across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. Discover something for every personality on your list, at a range of price points, in stores and at Marshalls.com.
