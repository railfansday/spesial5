RealTrends Verified brokerage ERA Live Moore acquisition and other brokerage moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Several brokerages announced acquisitions and affiliations across the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic. Highlights include ERA Live Moore’s Blue Door Group deal, SERHANT.’s Pennsylvania leadership hire, and expansions by Corcoran Reverie and Century 21.  AI Summary

ERA Live Moore, one of the Carolinas’ premier real estate brokerages, today announced the acquisition of Blue Door Group Real Estate, a respected and established local firm serving the Greater Winston Salem market. Moving forward, the brokerage will operate as Blue Door Group Real Estate, an ERA Live Moore Company, preserving its trusted local identity while connecting agents and clients to the expanded resources of the ERA Live Moore network.

Blue Door Group Real Estate was founded in 2020 by Jodi Tate, who is no stranger to ERA Live Moore: in 2016, ERA Live Moore owner Eb hand-picked her to launch their Winston Salem office, knowing that one day she wanted to establish her own company. After significantly expanding the ERA office’s agent base, she started Blue Door group in 2020 with Eb and Tania’s full support.

RealTrends Verified brokerage SERHANT. announced the appointment of Mishel Taylor as Managing Director and Principal Broker of SERHANT. Pennsylvania. A licensed broker in Pennsylvania and New Jersey, Taylor joins from eXp Realty, LLC and brings more than two decades of experience spanning residential finance, multi-office brokerage management, agent development, and organizational growth across the New Jersey and Greater Philadelphia region.

In her new role, Taylor will oversee brokerage operations, compliance, recruiting and agent performance for SERHANT. Pennsylvania. She will focus on building scalable systems that help agents grow their businesses across Philadelphia’s city neighborhoods and the Main Line communities, from Center City to Bryn Mawr, Villanova and Wayne.

Corcoran Reverie, a RealTrends Verified brokerage, announced its acquisition of NextHome Music City Realty, expanding the firm’s presence across Tennessee. The Murfreesboro-based brokerage will now operate under the Corcoran Reverie banner. Co-owners Tad Ritchison and Todd Grubaugh will continue to lead their team of agents and serve clients throughout Nashville, Franklin, Murfreesboro and the surrounding communities.

Century 21 Real Estate LLC, which has franchises recognized on the RealTrends Verified top brokerage list, is expanding its brand presence along Florida’s Atlantic coast with the latest affiliation of Realty Exchange LLC, a 20-year-old brokerage with nearly 100 independent sales professionals based in Palm Coast.

Now doing business as CENTURY 21 Realty Exchange, the brokerage was founded in 2006 by Julie Sabine, who continues to lead the business alongside co-owner Dave Petkovsek. Sabine, a full-time real estate professional since 2004, has helped close over 1,600 transactions across her 22-year career.

RealTrends Verified brokerage Michael Saunders & Company, announced that the commercial real estate team and business operations of Florida ROI Commercial Property Brokerage, a Tampa-based firm established in 2008, have transitioned to its commercial division, MSC Commercial. As part of the transition, the property portfolio previously managed by Florida Investment Real Estate (FIRE) will also move to MSC Commercial, extending its presence into the greater Tampa Bay market.

The move builds on Michael Saunders & Company’s recent expansion into Sanibel Island and Pinellas County/St. Petersburg and marks another significant step in the company’s continued growth up and down Florida’s Gulf Coast, where the firm’s offices, listings, and market experience now extend from Tampa Bay to Fort Myers.
