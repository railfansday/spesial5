The Trump administration is pulling the nomination of Heidi Overton to lead the U.S. Food and Drug Administration over concerns that she lacked support to get confirmed by the Senate, STAT notes. The Senate’s health panel had been expected to vote on her nomination Wednesday, and the panel’s leader, Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-La.), said on Tuesday that Overton had enough support to clear his committee and win confirmation. But some GOP lawmakers had reservations about Overton, a preventive care doctor who served in the Trump White House, due to her support for scaling back vaccine recommendations, her role in loosening restrictions on vapes, and her stance on abortion policy.

Merck plans to stop development of its experimental blood cancer drug, nemtabrutinib, after a ​review found the benefits may not outweigh the ‌risks, Reuters says. An independent monitoring committee reached that conclusion after reviewing results from a late-stage study and Merck said its review of the full ​data confirmed the finding. The decision ends a broader development program ​for treating chronic lymphocytic leukemia and small lymphocytic lymphoma, ​including two other late-stage trials. Nemtabrutinib is an oral drug designed to block BTK, a ​protein that helps ​cancerous B ⁠cells grow.
