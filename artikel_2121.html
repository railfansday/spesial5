Dado Prso and the Rangers love affair that gave him the 'best memories of my life'

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dado Prso has opened up on the “best memories” of his life at Rangers as the Ibrox cult hero reflected on an unforgettable three years in Glasgow.

The Croatian striker arrived from Monaco in the summer of 2004 and quickly established himself as a fan favourite, helping Rangers claim a dramatic league and League Cup double during his debut campaign in 2004/05.

Speaking to RFPBC, he said: “I can be happy, I played my best football and had the best memories of my life with Monaco and Rangers and when you are here you know what a massive club Rangers are.

“I remember when I was here with Monaco, I was surprised at the noise. I was on the bench with Julien Rodriguez and we had goosebumps.

Prso scored 18 goals in 44 competitive appearances during his first season at Rangers and forged a formidable partnership with Nacho Novo.

That campaign culminated in the extraordinary final-day title triumph at Easter Road, with Rangers snatching the championship after Celtic slipped up at Motherwell.

Prso said: “Easter Road was unforgettable for everybody. We had to win our game and wait to see what happened with Celtic.

“We were winning and heard the noise from the stands and it was unforgettable.

“We heard another roar and we all jumped up. It really was a special moment. It was incredible.”

For Prso, though, the title triumph was anything but fortunate.

He added: “It was not lucky, we absolutely deserved this title, even though it was the last day in the last minute.

“We trained hard, played well and we never stopped. We stuck together and even in the games we didn’t play great football we still gave everything.”

Prso was also central to Rangers becoming the first Scottish club to reach the Champions League last 16 the following season.

He vividly remembers their 3-2 victory over Porto at Ibrox, insisting Rangers’ aggression and determination overwhelmed their technically gifted opponents.

He said: “They had a very technical side and were better but we had passion and didn’t let them play.

“That’s how we won the game. They were surprised to see us like lions on the pitch and they were not ready to fight with us.”

Injuries eventually took their toll and Prso brought the curtain down on his time at Rangers in 2007.

His final farewell at Ibrox remains particularly emotional.

“I don’t like to say goodbye, it was a really sad moment and I could feel a lump in my throat in the tunnel,” he said.

“As I said, I played my best football and had the best memories of my life and when you are here you know what a massive club Rangers are.”
