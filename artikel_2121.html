Derek McInnes makes Rangers transfer stance crystal clear over in-form star

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Derek McInnes hopes Rangers can turn James Penrice’s loan move into a permanent deal after making his feelings clear to the Ibrox hierarchy.

The 27-year-old is currently on loan at Rangers from Greek side AEK Athens and has impressed since making the move to Glasgow.

Penrice’s form has now been rewarded with his first Scotland call-up, while McInnes has made no secret of his desire to keep the defender beyond his current agreement.

Asked whether discussions had taken place over making the move permanent, McInnes said: “No, I think, well, certainly I've made my feelings clear on it. Ideally, I would like to have done it as a permanent, but the best way forward at the time was a loan.

“But I think we're all aware of what it needs to be to make it permanent. But I think there's a lot of goodwill all round to try and that could be something that we'd like to do.

“The conversations I've had with Jim and the board have been along those lines, and hopefully we can make that permanent.”

Penrice has quickly established himself as an important figure under McInnes, with the Rangers boss impressed by the quality and energy he has brought to the side.

He continued: “Because I think what he's done since the first minute is just play with so much enthusiasm, clearly wants to be here.

“Plays properly and he brings quality, but he also brings energy, the fans take to him because of how he performs and how much he wants to win.

“So it's just for me, those types of boys, that age is actually quite ironic. He's one of the older ones at 27, I think. And a lot of these things are happening for him now, the first Scotland call-up and all the rest of it.

“But he's a player that I trust and I think the supporters trust him.

“So hopefully we can make it a longer-term thing going forward. But that's for the people upstairs to try and sort out.”
