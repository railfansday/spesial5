Martyn Waghorn makes controversial Richard Keogh claim that some Derby County fans aren't buying

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Martyn Waghorn believes the Derby County senior leaders could have done more to prevent the car crash that left captain Richard Keogh injured during his time at the club.

The incident occurred in September 2019, following a Derby County team-bonding evening which began with bowling and continued at the Joiners Arms pub. A small group, including captain Richard Keogh, stayed drinking into the night, per The Guardian.

Tom Lawrence subsequently crashed his Range Rover into the back of Mason Bennett's Mercedes before hitting a lamppost, with Keogh unconscious in the back. Both Lawrence and Bennett were over the drink-drive limit and initially left the scene. Keogh suffered serious knee ligament damage, facing around 15 months out.

According to the report in The Guardian, the controversy deepened when Derby sacked Keogh for gross misconduct, although he later won £2.3 million in compensation.

However, Martyn Waghorn believes more could have been done to prevent the incident altogether during his interview with BBC Sport Derby, stating: "We can't get away from the fact that it's completely unacceptable.

"I don't know the full ins and outs, [but he (Richard Keogh) was scapegoated, wasn't he?] Yeah, he was.This is really difficult for me.

"He shouldn't have been sacked the way he was so abruptly, but I understand the decision. In the long-term, as a senior leader, I think he could have had a bit more of a say in terms of no, let's not do this.

"You're the captain of the football club. Taxis are not far away. We can easily sort something out. I just feel some senior figures could have done more to prevent the situation and it was easily preventable."

Waghorn's statement won't impress everyone but he will mostly be fondly remembered at the club, having enjoyed two spells at Derby, scoring 37 goals in 150 appearances.

He initially joined from Ipswich Town in 2018 after an impressive season, having previously enjoyed a prolific spell at Rangers, where he scored 44 goals in 78 appearances and won the Scottish Championship.

Waghorn was part of Frank Lampard's side that reached the 2019 play-off final and later scored a crucial penalty against Sheffield Wednesday in May 2021 to help Derby avoid relegation.

He returned in 2023, scoring seven goals as Paul Warne's side won promotion from League One. Having done that, he retired from professional football in 2025 after spells at Northampton and Coventry City.

Not everyone connected with Derby County has reacted well to the statement, with one Derby supporter on X stating: "Just another grown man not taking accountability for his actions, nothing new."
