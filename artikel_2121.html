'He needs to do better' - £7m Middlesbrough ace sent transfer warning that will interest Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Middlesbrough have made a strong start to the season through this period up to the November international break.

The Teesside outfit lost in the play-offs last year, and are hoping to go one step further this campaign and earn their place back in the Premier League.

By the end of the term, Boro will have reached 10 years without top flight football, with their last stint at that level coming to an end in 2017.

However, Kim Hellberg’s side have gone into the break off the back of a disappointing 2-0 loss to Wrexham and a 0-0 draw against Watford.

These results have seen them lose their lead at the top of the table, falling behind Birmingham City due to an inferior goal difference.

And Radek Vitek’s mistake in the defeat to the Red Dragons has led to increased scrutiny on the goalkeeper after he joined the club from Manchester United in the summer.

When asked about Vitek’s mistake, and his general performance levels, FLW’s Middlesbrough fan pundit Liam Day claimed that he does have to improve, as he hasn’t been as good for the team as expected.

He is confused why the Czech shot-stopper is performing worse in a better team compared to his loan stint with Bristol City last season, where he was a standout performer.

“Vitek’s obviously had a very, very tough start to his Boro career, and it is quite obvious that he’s not been worth the money that we’ve paid so far,” Day told Football League World.

Middlesbrough head coach Kim Hellberg has reacted to reports linking him with the job at Premier League outfit Crystal Palace

“Sol Brynn really disappointed me last season.

“Obviously, I would say one of the main causes of us not being in the Premier League, to be completely honest with you.

“So, goalkeeper was a main priority and I can see why we went for Vitek.

“You look at last season, and you look at what he’s doing for us, and I’m really confused why the fall-off has happened.

“He’s playing in a better team, we concede less chances, and yet he’s struggling to do anything with the ones he is conceding.

“He’s not made many saves, and it is really just quite confusing.

“And that Wrexham one midweek was a really weird one, just went straight through him.

Vitek spent last season on loan at Bristol City, where he kept 12 clean sheets from 41 appearances in the Championship, conceding on 54 occasions.

Middlesbrough agreed a deal with Man United that involved an initial £7 million fee, with add-ons and a sell-on clause also involved in the arrangement.

The goalkeeper signed a five-year deal, keeping him at the Riverside until June 2031.

Kieffer Moore’s tame shot from outside the box was spilled by Vitek, which allowed George Thomason to pounce to open the scoring against Boro from a tight angle last week.

And it went from bad to worse for the 23-year-old, who saw Ollie Rathbone’s 84th minute strike go through his legs to make it 2-0 to the Red Dragons.

Vitek has a goals prevented per 90 of -0.20 so far this season, suggesting he is letting in more goals than he should based on the quality of chances conceded (all stats from Fotmob).

Next up for Boro is a home clash against the goalkeeper’s former club Bristol City on 21 November in a 3pm kick-off.

It’s still early days for Vitek’s time at Middlesbrough, as he is being judged off of 15 games on a five-year contract.

However, it’s clear after the Wrexham defeat that he does need to improve his standard if he is to retain his position in the team long-term.
