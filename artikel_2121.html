Why Gary Neville will have a keen eye on Sheffield Wednesday now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sheffield Wednesday's newest era has already begun, with Dave Challinor appointed as Henrik Pedersen's successor as head coach at Hillsborough.

Given the fact that ex-Stockport County chief Simon Wilson was appointed as Owls sporting director over the summer, many expected Challinor to follow him if things ended up getting tricky for Pedersen in League One - and that's exactly what has happened.

Challinor has already managed two matches for Wednesday, albeit they have come in the FA Cup away at Oldham Athletic and an EFL Trophy clash with Accrington Stanley, but his big welcome will come on home soil this coming weekend when Plymouth Argyle make the trip to the Steel City.

But there's one man that may be looking on closely at the future developments at Hillsborough - and that is none other than Man United icon Gary Neville.

Neville, besides being a one-club footballer with the Red Devils and then turning into a prominent pundit and commentator, has also been a part-owner of Salford City since 2014 alongside his former team-mates Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, Nicky Butt, David Beckham (who joined later in 2019) and his brother Phil Neville.

Under the Class of 92, the Ammies have risen from the eighth tier of English football to League Two, winning four promotions in five seasons between 2015 and 2019, but their progress has somewhat stalled since 2020.

Karl Robinson departed as manager after two-and-a-half years in the role this past summer, having overseen a play-off final drubbing at the hands of Notts County, and the immediate link to the vacancy was none other than the aforementioned Dave Challinor.

As per The Sun, Neville and co wanted to appoint Challinor after he left Stockport around the same time as Robinson departed Moor Lane, similarly after he was on the wrong end of a play-off final beating at League One level.

However, it was claimed that Salford 'missed out' on the Chester-born man, because he was heading to Blackburn Rovers of the Championship instead - that however never ended up happening, as he remained a free agent until the start of November, when he landed the vacant Wednesday job.

So, which way did Salford turn instead after not landing Challinor's services?

Someone with League Two know-how is the route you'd assume the City hierarchy would go down, but they did the complete opposite - by hiring someone who'd never worked in English football before.

In came Australian coach Peter Cklamovski, who aside from managing Japanese trio FC Tokyo, Montedio Yamagata, Shimizu S-Pulse and the Malaysia national team, has also been a sidekick of Ange Postecoglou, both when he managed Australia's senior side and also Yokohama F Marinos.

There were understandably concerns among the Salford fanbase when they lost their first three league matches, but the form since then has been almost impeccable, winning 10 of their last 11 League Two outings to top the table.

This could finally be the year that they make it into League One, one step away from the initial goal of 2014 that was reaching the Championship, although it has taken a small fortune and extra investors coming on board to get to this point.
