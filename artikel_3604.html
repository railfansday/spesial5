Amber Glenn Reacts to 'DWTS' Fans Defending Her After Being Underscored

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Amber Glenn is grateful for fans’ support amid her journey on Dancing With the Stars.

“I mean, fans, they’ve got my back,” the Olympic figure skater, 27, exclusively told Us on Tuesday, November 17, after landing in the season 35 finale with pro dance partner Pasha Pashkov.

For weeks, some fans have argued that Glenn and Pashkov, 40, have been underscored by the judges.

“I love you guys. You are ready to fight for me, and I appreciate it,” the gold medalist told her supporters. “I’m your scout leader. We’re gonna go, we’re gonna fight, and you’re gonna fight for me to win because I want us to get that trophy at any cost. Thank you, guys, and we’re going to try our best.”

During the semifinals on Tuesday night, Glenn and Pashkov danced a salsa to “Bemba Colorá” by Celia Cruz and a Viennese waltz to “Dangerous Woman” by Ariana Grande. The pair received all 10s from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli after both performances, resulting in a total of 60 out of 60 points for the night.

Tuesday’s episode marked the first time that Glenn and Pashkov received perfect scores, which many viewers believe was a long time coming due to the pair’s improvement throughout the season.

“It’s really amazing to have the hard work and dedication acknowledged by the judges,” Glenn told Us on Tuesday. “Their comments really hit home for me. I have been putting in so much work, and Pasha always puts in his 100 percent. So to be able to get that kind of feedback and that result has been incredible.”

The athlete encouraged fans at home to continue voting for her in the finale next week.

“The fans at home [are] keeping things going, keeping things alive, voting. Thank you so much. We’re almost there. Please take us all the way,” she said.

Pashkov gushed over his partner, adding, “She’s the most deserving. She put herself out there, fully, 100 percent vulnerable, every week, diving into every single style, trying to understand its essence through thick and thin. … Fighting through any pain or anything, any doubts, and leaving your heart on the floor every single week. So thank you for doing that, and that is why [fans should vote for her].”

Glenn also reflected on Pashkov’s leadership and revealed what she’s learned from him this season.

“I think something that I noticed, especially from day one, is how you put yourself into something, like, 100 percent,” she told him. “And I think for a long time, I’ve always been scared to because I have a fear of failure. Shocker. And for a long time, that was kind of something I struggled with, was really just putting my all into something out of fear of messing up or failure or something. And he does every — literally every single run, like, gives everything. I’m kind of like, ‘Whoa, what? Like, we’re not performing now. Like, chill out.’ And I’m like, ‘Damn.’”

Glenn continued, “But that’s what makes you such a great competitor and such a great performer, is having that love and dedication and just, like, passion. And that’s something I want to bring into my skating in the future because I felt like I did that tonight. I felt like I was able to give 100 percent, and everything went away, and there wasn’t any fear. There was just love, and there was just emotion and excitement.”

The Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale airs Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+.
