Stuttgart star parts ways with his agency amid ongoing transfer speculation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

VfB Stuttgart midfielder Angelo Stiller has parted ways with his agency, Sports360, by mutual agreement, according to Sky Germany.

The split is understood to have been caused by differing opinions over how Stiller's future should be handled.

Stiller is under contract with Stuttgart until 2028, and with an extension seemingly not on the horizon, speculation surrounding his future has continued to grow.

His departure from Sports360 only adds further uncertainty, with a new agency yet to be appointed and likely to play an important role in planning the next stage of his career.

According to Sky, Stiller is open to a move away from Stuttgart, with potential destinations extending beyond Europe's top leagues to include Saudi Arabia, the United States and Turkey.

Stiller, who is currently sidelined with an adductor injury, joined Stuttgart from Hoffenheim in 2023. He has since made 145 appearances for the club, scoring seven goals and providing 31 assists.
