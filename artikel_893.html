Miami Magic fancied for Newbury gold as ante-post selections revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With the National Hunt season clicking into gear, the Weekend Winners team nominate their ante-post fancies for the coming months; watch the episode in full on the At The Races YouTube channel

On the latest episode of Weekend Winners, the panel reveal their top ante-post bets for the upcoming winter months.

Host Kate Tracey looks to next week's Coral Gold Cup at Newbury for her selection, with panellists Declan Rix and Sam Boswell setting their sights on races on the horizon.

"I like an old favourite of the show who has returned in good order this season in King Turgeon for the Becher Chase. He is in both the Grand Sefton and the Becher, but he must run in the Becher. He is such a good jumper, and he has won a Grand Sefton before.

"He is a horse that is so, so progressive and last season was a disaster. He has had a wind operation, and he looked like he took a blow at Aintree. We saw signs of life in the stable of David Pipe; he will be for me at a good price."

"I'm aiming for the Coral Gold Cup next week and I like Miami Magic for that. His chasing debuts weren't anything special when getting beat at short prices but he got his act together later on in the season.

"He ran well over hurdles in a Pertemps qualifier last month which gave him a perfect prep. He is a second-season chaser, which means he fits the perfect profile for the race and he's a good price for this race. Hopefully he can go well."

"With plenty of the possible King George VI field not looking likely to turn up, it's more and more obvious to just back Kitzbuhel for the race on Boxing Day. He's Kempton proven, the ground he is proven on - I think the race will decimate and I think he is the one that we will see this weekend for a prep and then kick on to here."
