Arsenal 1-1 Borussia Dortmund: The Gunners remain unbeaten in the Champions League but failed to capitalise at the Emirates

Arsenal were held to a 1-1 draw in their Champions League clash against Borussia Dortmund but remain on course to qualify in one of the automatic knockout spots.

The Gunners started the clash at the Emirates Stadium in quick fashion and controlled possession with short, slick passes and forward runs. Noni Madueke impressed on the right and set up Viktor Gyokeres to score the opening goal from outside the penalty area.

However, Mikel Arteta’s team slackened off in discipline as the half went on which allowed Dortmund to gain a foothold. The Bundesliga leaders then came out from half-time with renewed vigour and secured an equaliser through Maximilian Beier.

With the scores level both teams created further chances, Declan Rice hit the post and Jobe Bellingham had a goal disallowed, but neither could find the back of the net again.

The points were shared leaving Arsenal fifth in the league phase table with Dortmund one point below them in seventh.

It isn’t yet a real problem for Arsenal, but it may just breathe a bit of life into the number of teams looking up at them: a sudden inability to kill games.

And while that usually refers to finishing - in the sense of scoring - it is here as applicable to their ability to strangle matches.

"That's what we want [to finish in the top eight],” Arteta told Amazon Prime Video.

“We have a big game against Manchester City at home on Sunday so we need to recover really well, we have four days to do that and sustain that momentum the way we are playing and the win will come."

The Arsenal manager also spoke about Noni Madueke and his reaction to the result in the dressing room.

Arteta revealed: "He [Madueke] was really good but you go in the dressing room and he is not satisfied because we had so many chances where we can put the ball in the net and finish the action."

Of his Swedish striker, Mikel Arteta added: "He was great, not only the goal but he connected with the team.

“We found him in various ways in many situations and it was always a threat."

The Arsenal manager said: "The performance was superb and there is no question about that from the start to the end.

“We played against a really good side and the domination and threat was really impressive. The general feeling inside the dressing room is that we should reward ourselves with more.

"I love the courage, the fluidity and the understanding with the players with and without the ball.”

“We are very confident. We will go for it."

Arsenal’s goalscorer Viktor Gyokeres spoke after the game.

He said: "We could have scored in the second half. We could have won the game in many different ways.

“We played well and created a lot of chances, we deserved to win the game but sometimes it is like that.

"The most important thing is that we create those chances and get shots on goal. For sure we will score goals when games are coming up."

Manchester City were also held to a draw tonight as they finished their encounter with Napoli 2-2.

Meanwhile, Jude Bellingham scored a screamer in a 5-1 victory for Real Madrid over PSV Eindhoven.

Arsenal try their best to secure an equaliser but it’s not enough and the points are shared at the Emirates Stadium.
