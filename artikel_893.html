Ratcliffe's Ineos Group settles £10m-plus claim with Ainslie's firm over yacht

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ineos Racing sued Athena Racing earlier this year, alleging the latter breached a deal between the two companies by refusing to return multimillion-pound yacht; Sir Ben Ainslie's GB1 team is the challenger of record for the next America's Cup in Naples, Italy in 2027

A company connected to Sir Jim Ratcliffe's Ineos Group said it has settled a High Court claim against a firm founded by Sir Ben Ainslie.

Ineos Racing sued Athena Racing earlier this year, alleging the latter breached a deal between the two companies by refusing to return the multimillion-pound yacht Ineos Britannia, which Ainslie captained at the 2024 America's Cup in a 7-2 defeat to Emirates Team New Zealand in Barcelona.

Lawyers for Ineos said in court documents filed earlier this year that the claim was valued at more than £10 million.

Athena Racing was defending the claim, arguing that Ineos was not entitled to have the yacht back.

In a statement on Monday, Ineos said: "Ineos has reached a settlement with Athena Racing and considers the matter now closed."

No further details, including whether the yacht has been returned, have been provided.

Four-time Olympic champion Ainslie hit out at Ineos Britannia in April 2025 after they confirmed their challenge for the 38th America's Cup in Naples in 2027 would not involve him.

Ainslie's GB1 team is the challenger of record for the event in Italy next year.

No British team has ever won the America's Cup since it was first contested in 1850.
