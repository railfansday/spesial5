Women's TGL: Latest results, schedule as Hull, Woad feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Five teams feature in the inaugural women's TGL season, with every match live on Sky Sports; Charley Hull plays for Atlanta Drive GC and Lottie Woad is part of Boston Common Golf's roster; WTGL season runs starts on November 8 and runs until December 23

A look at the fixtures and full schedule for the inaugural women's TGL season, featuring Charley Hull and Lottie Woad, with every match exclusively live on Sky Sports.

The tech-infused golf league sees LPGA Tour stars feature in a unique team format, following on from the success of the men's competition, with all players mic'd up and every match being played at SoFi Center - a purpose-built indoor venue in Florida.

Five teams feature in the inaugural season, each having a five-team roster, with Atlanta Drive GC, Boston Common Golf, Los Angeles Golf Club, Motor City Golf Club and New York Golf Club all involved.

Teams play 15 holes of golf each match, divided into two sessions, with nine holes of triples - three against three playing alternate shots - and six holes of head-to-head singles golf.

Each team players four matches in the regular season, with the top four teams in the standings then progressing to the semi-finals. The best-of-three finals then take place on the week beginning December 21, all live on Sky Sports.
