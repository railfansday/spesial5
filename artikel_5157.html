🐂 Tom Lee Calls the Binance Hack Bullish for Ethereum

Tom Lee looked at the largest crypto theft ever recorded and called the market’s response “constructive”. His company, BitMine Immersion, bought another 8,573 ETH last week, roughly $19.6 million worth, lifting the treasury to 6,060,506 ETH and about $13.7 billion. That’s 4.9% of the total circulating ETH supply, which makes Lee the most ETH-exposed executive on the planet.

His case comes down to one comparison. ETH fell about 4% after the $6.3 billion Binance hack. After the Bybit hack in February 2025, it dropped 8% on day one and 20% over the following week. Lee called that “relatively muted crypto market reaction very constructive” and read it as proof the industry can take a hit this size without cracking.

The price read holds up, but there are bigger questions for ETH’s future from a trading perspective. October was the worst month in the history of U.S. spot ETH ETFs, with $1.61 billion in net outflows. ETH is down 19% from its local peak, opposed to 11& for Bitcoin.

A potential driver of ETH’s weakness is Tom Lee himself. BitMine is now 99% of the way to the 5% supply cap Lee has said the company will stop at, about 49,500 ETH short, roughly six weeks at last week’s pace. ETH has had a large, price-insensitive, weekly corporate buyer for most of a year, and that buyer is nearly finished.

Now we wait to see what the bid looks like without it. And if Tom Lee’s bullish read is the correct one…
