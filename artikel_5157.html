XRP’s $18 Chase Hinges On Clearing Multiple Resistance Layers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Home » Ripple (XRP) » XRP’s $18 Chase Hinges On Clearing Multiple Resistance Layers

Seasoned market professionals are trying to establish the bottom as double-digit targets resurface.

The global crypto market saw multiple multi-billion dollar liquidation events over the past weekend, scaring investors as major-cap currencies dip below their thresholds. XRP’s price is now trading at $0.95, hitting a 3-month low.

On Monday afternoon, vet analyst Ali Martinez spotted a multi-year ascending triangle on XRP’s weekly chart since 2017, with the current 43% price drop from the October $1.55 high potentially testing the rising lower trend-line near $0.78 as key support.

Holding the $0.78 major demand zone can spark recovery toward the triangle’s $3.50 horizontal resistance, where a weekly breakout and successful retest might validate the pattern and target Ali’s major upside move to roughly $18 per one XRP coin.

Other analysts apply a more cautious approach. For instance, More Crypto Online team’s analysis suggests that XRP’s recent price action was showing a weak reaction without a confirmed local low, with the key zone at $0.98 that price must surpass to signal a bottom.

$XRP is showing only a weak reaction so far, and there is no signal for a low yet. The first resistance is still the zone between $0.981 and $1.09. The price needs to rise above this first resistance to signal that a local low is in. pic.twitter.com/Qdu8CfaZg6

The 4-hour chart uses classic Elliott Wave theory labeling, positioning XRP price at two plausible bottoms. That’s if the OG altcoin’s price doesn’t restore the lower-boundary of the marked first resistance zone at $0.98.

On the other hand, French crypto analyst Egrag was highlighting a repeating XRP macro fractal across three major cycles where price tests descend trendlines and the 21 EMA. Similarly to Ali Martinez, Egrag presents a couple of double-digit targets at $10 & $16 following the shake-out.

XRP’s current price is trading below all four moving averages on the MA Ribbon, judging from TradingView’s data. In October, the 200 EMA acted as a strong resistance that XRP was unable to break. Now, the confluent resistance grew a second layer, adding a bearish tone below $1.15.

But the bigger tell is the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF). This crucial indicator, measuring the crypto whale sentiment, has posted extremely negative figures on Monday. Reading below -0.17, this testifies to a hefty selling sentiment among largest crypto investors while XRP dwells below $1.

Right now, XRP’s ultra high price correlation with Bitcoin (BTC) & Ethereum (ETH) is casting a bearish shadow with sell-offs deepening across the digital sector, still soaking up the Nvidia scandal from last week. Without fresh buying power, the 1.38 Fib at $0.79 could be on the cards.
