Ripple CTO Emeritus: Ridiculous XRP Prices Became The Norm

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Ripple CTO Emeritus: Ridiculous XRP Prices Became The Norm

David Schwartz explains where Bitcoin & XRP’s prices stopped making sense for him on a fiery crypto X thread.

David Schwartz, XRP Ledger co-architect and Ripple’s CTO Emeritus, replied to criticism of his sales with a short reminder:

“When XRP hit 10 cents, that seemed ridiculous. When ETH hit $1, that seemed ridiculous. When BTC hit $200, that seemed ridiculous.”

The remark followed days of disclosure. Schwartz had already stated he cut his XRP from a peak of roughly 26 million tokens to about 3 million, his Bitcoin from around 1,500 BTC to one, and his Ether from roughly 40,000 ETH to six.

JoelKatz has long said he and his wife agreed he would sell at every new all-time high because he strongly dislikes risk. Ripple CTO Emeritus still holds substantial Ripple equity, which he has described as already more risk than he wants.

David Schwartz has recounted the same pattern for years. Ten cents once felt like a complete victory for XRP. Bitcoin at $200 or $1,000 once sounded impossible. He began selling XRP once it reached 10 cents because the valuation seemed “insane” at the time.

When XRP hit 10 cents, that seemed ridiculous. When ETH hit $1, that seemed ridiculous. What BTC hit $200, that seemed ridiculous.

The latest JoelKatz’s post simply notes that the market has repeatedly moved past levels the people closest to the technology once treated as absurd.

Crypto critics argued that selling around a few dollars makes little sense if the network is built for large institutional volume. Schwartz’s record shows a more ordinary outcome: early conviction followed by deliberate de-risking once the stakes became large.

I haven't bought XRP in a long time. I am very uncomfortable with risk and have sold nearly all of the digital assets I've ever held. I still hold quite a bit of Ripple stock, and that alone is more risk than I wish I had.

He has said he has not bought XRP in a long time and has sold nearly all the digital assets he has ever held.

XRP currently trades near $0.95, well below its July 2025 high near $3.65. The distance between today’s price and the levels that once seemed ridiculous is already large.

Whether the pattern continues remains open, one that even an original architect of the ledger has never claimed he can answer in advance.
