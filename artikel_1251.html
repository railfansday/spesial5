What Brooklyn Beckham’s Life Looks Like After David & Victoria Fallout

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It's been ten months since Brooklyn Beckham launched an explosive attack on his parents, and despite this very public feud, the son of the Manchester United legend appears to be doing very well.

The 27-year-old, who was born in London whilst his father, David, was playing for the Red Devils, has been estranged from his famous family for almost a year now. Accusing his mother, Victoria, of 'sabotaging' his wedding to his spouse Nicola Peltz, Brooklyn also criticised the artificial nature of the 'Beckham Brand'.

Distancing himself from his loved ones, Beckham's eldest child has gone in a completely different direction, settling with Peltz in Beverly Hills, Los Angeles.

Despite no longer holding ties to his family, Brooklyn is seemingly still living a lush lifestyle with his wife and children.

Brooklyn spent this year's Thanksgiving with his beloved wife, Peltz, and six other guests at the couple's home in Beverly Hills.

The aspiring chef forked out a reported £130 on a piece of cheese from 'the Cheese Store of Beverly Hills' (via the Mirror). The store shared a video on their social media pages, which revealed how Brooklyn splashed the cash.

In the clip, the model was captured informing the Cheese store's owner how much he enjoys watching their cheese videos in bed whilst his wife watches make-up tutorials.

Brooklyn's love for cooking is well-documented, and he admitted earlier this week that he was apprehensive about preparing a big Thanksgiving spread.

Victoria is said to be "devastated" by the claims made by her eldest son as sources recount what happened on Brooklyn's well-documented wedding day.

In the cheese store, the former Arsenal academy star revealed that he would be brining his turkey in Guinness, whilst he also detailed his plans to wake up early and slow-cook the meat in the oven, before presenting it alongside a dish of sweet potato and marshmallows.

"It's my first time cooking Thanksgiving and I'm a little bit nervous," he said.

This was his first Thanksgiving away from his parents and siblings, instead spending the American holiday with his American wife and children, who celebrate the festivity annually.

Brooklyn has also formed a 'bromance' with Hollywood star Mark Wahlberg, having recently attended Bellagio Fountain Club’s culinary experience, joined by other chefs ahead of the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix. They gave joint interviews to US media at the event, and the American actor is said to be almost becoming a 'father figure' to him.

A source who was present at the Las Vegas Grand Prix told The Sun:

"It must be like a dagger to the heart for David, who is so desperate to see his son. Watching Brooklyn beam alongside a man David fell out with so publicly, treating him like a mentor, would be enough to crush any father."

Back in January, Brooklyn released an explosive statement via social media, detailing his relationship with his family. In the statement, he explained that he had no plans to reconcile with his parents after they'd 'controlled narratives' in the press throughout his life.

"I have been silent for years and made every effort to keep these matters private," the statement read.

"Unfortunately, my parents and their team have continued to go to the press, leaving me with no choice but to speak for myself and tell the truth about only some of the lies that have been printed.

"I do not want to reconcile with my family. I'm not being controlled, I'm standing up for myself for the first time in my life. For my entire life, my parents have controlled narratives in the press about our family. The performative social media posts, family events and inauthentic relationships have been a fixture of the life I was born into."

"Recently, I have seen with my own eyes the lengths that they'll go through to place countless lies in the media, mostly at the expense of innocent people, to preserve their own facade. But I believe the truth always comes out. My parents have been trying endlessly to ruin my relationship since before my wedding, and it hasn't stopped.

"My mum cancelled making Nicola's dress in the eleventh hour despite how excited she was to wear her design, forcing her to urgently find a new dress. Weeks before our big day, my parents repeatedly pressured and attempted to bribe me into signing away the rights to my name, which would have affected me, my wife, and our future children.

"They were adamant on me signing before my wedding date because then the terms of the deal would be initiated. My holdout affected the payday, and they have never treated me the same since.

"During the wedding planning, my mum went so far as to call me 'evil' because Nicola and I chose to include my Nanny Sandra, and Nicola's Naunni, at our table, because they both didn't have their husbands. Both of our parents had their own tables equally adjacent to ours. The night before our wedding, members of my family told me that Nicola was 'not blood' and 'not family.'

"Since the moment I started standing up for myself with my family, I've received endless attacks from my parents, both privately and publicly, that were sent to the press on their orders. Even my brothers were sent to attack me on social media, before they ultimately blocked me out of nowhere this last summer.

"My mum hijacked my first dance with my wife, which had been planned weeks in advance to a romantic love song. In front of our 500 wedding guests, Marc Anthony called me to the stage, where in the schedule was planned to be my romantic dance with my wife, but instead my mum was waiting to dance with me instead. She danced very inappropriately on me in front of everyone.

A guest in attendance has now spoken out amid allegations over Victoria Beckham's dancing.

"I've never felt more uncomfortable or humiliated in my entire life. We wanted to renew our vows so we could create new memories of our wedding day that bring us joy and happiness, not anxiety and embarrassment.

"My wife has been consistently disrespected by my family, no matter how hard we've tried to come together as one. My mum has repeatedly invited women from my past into our lives in ways that were clearly intended to make us both uncomfortable."

Brooklyn has since been absent from David's knighthood and missed the ex-footballer's 50th birthday in 2025, whilst he also sent his parents a legal notice, telling them that they could only contact him through lawyers.

There are currently no signs that the parents will be reunited with their son in the foreseeable future.
