Man Utd to Make PL Complaint Despite Leny Yoro Red Overturn

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Despite having Leny Yoro's red card overturned, following the release of VAR audio which revealed that the defender was wrongly sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity just eight minutes into Manchester United's 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday, the Red Devils are still set to contact the Premier League and ProRef over the controversial decision.

The Red Devils were condemned to their heaviest defeat under a new manager in 100 years on Sunday afternoon, with goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz ensuring the Reds made full use of their numerical advantage. It also handed Mauricio Pochettino his first real taste of just how difficult his new job is likely to be.

His adopted side now sit 19th in the Premier League table, just one point above bottom-placed Crystal Palace, having managed just one win in their opening 11 league outings.

However, United will feel particularly hard done by after believing their latest fixture was effectively taken out of their hands by referee Sam Barrott and his VAR team, with just eight minutes on the clock.

Despite seeing retrospective action taken to ensure Yoro doesn't serve a suspension, United still think ProRef''s interpretation of the incident is 'totally wrong'.

They view it as both a process error and a failure to interpret the rules correctly. There is disappointment the VAR didn’t challenge the on-field decision, and they don’t see the incident as subjective.

Mauricio Pochettino watched his side go down to ten men after just eight minutes.

Data can be used to show if Isak had maintained his top-recorded speed, he still couldn’t have reached the ball before Senne Lammens. The club plans to raise this with both the Premier League and ProRef directly.

On the VAR audio itself, club insiders - and the top brass - feel there is a quick look and consensus to support the referee with no meaningful challenge or consideration.

Yoro was set to serve a one-match suspension, but it has now been removed after United successfully lodged an appeal for wrongful dismissal. The 21-year-old will now be available to face Brentford on Saturday at Old Trafford.

Debate on whether the foul was a clear denial of a goalscoring opportunity centred on whether Isak would have kept possession of ball as he approached the penalty area. Under the laws of the game, one of the considerations regarding a red card for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity (DOGSO) is the likelihood of the attacking player "keeping or gaining control of the ball" - which is why the FA deemed Sam Barrott's decision to be wrong.

Yet, in the latest edition of Match Officials Mic'D Up, Pro Ref chief refereeing officer Howard Webb still backed the process of his officials although was non-committal when sharing his own verdict on the incident.

"I think by and large most people would see that as a correct decision with Isak moving quickly on towards that ball," said Webb. "The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but I think it's a little bit debatable, I guess. Certainly not clearly wrong."
