Japanese Free Agent Linked to Red Sox in Boston's Rotation Pursuit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Japanese Free Agent Linked to Red Sox in Boston's Rotation Pursuit originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Red Sox and other teams in the Northeast definitely have a harder time landing Japanese free agents, and that's not likely to change anytime soon.

However, the Red Sox are still the Red Sox, and the brand is going to speak for itself.

With Boston having a very good team on paper right now and having had a nice year last season despite some injuries, there's a scenario in which the Red Sox could be a favorite to land some of these Japanese players who are still available.

Jen McCaffrey of The Athletic recently discussed Hiromi Itoh, the 29-year-old from Japan, as a legitimate option.

"Itoh, 29, is not as dominant a starter as some other Japanese free agents pitchers in recent years, but he still has a strong track record in Nippon Professional Baseball, including winning the 2025 Sawamura Award, Japan's equivalent of the Cy Young.

"He doesn't throw particularly hard, sitting at 93 mph, but has a deep pitch mix that's helped him be effective with a low walk rate (4.6 percent) despite a low strikeout rate (21.7 percent). Last season, he posted a 3.18 ERA in 26 starts, and he's thrown 170 or more innings in each of the last three years. As a right-hander, he'd offer some balance to the rotation. MLB Trade Rumors projects a two-year, $20 million deal for Itoh," McCaffrey of The Athletic wrote.

Itoh was very good in the NPB a year ago, posting a 3.18 ERA and striking out 159 hitters in 172.1 innings.

His strikeout numbers have been somewhat up and down throughout his career, but he's been an efficient arm who can get outs at a high level. He might not be the guaranteed star coming over from Japan like others who have signed in the past, but he has above-average stuff that could play in Boston.
