2-time Indy 500 winner Josef Newgarden agrees to multi-year contract extension with Team Penske

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

MOORESVILLE, N.C. (AP) — Two-time Indianapolis 500 winner Josef Newgarden has agreed to a multi-year contract extension to continue driving for Team Penske.

Team officials made the announcement Wednesday, but did not provide details of the new deal that locks up one of IndyCar's top drivers for "years to come."

The Tennessean just completed his 10th season with the powerhouse team. He's been one of the most successful drivers in Penske history, winning the Indianapolis 500 in 2023 and 2024 and series crowns in 2017 and 2019, and he owns 31 wins over the past decade.

"Josef Newgarden is one of the best drivers of his generation and has delivered in every aspect for our team for over a decade," IndyCar team president Jonathan Diuguid said in a statement. "He is a two-time Indianapolis 500 winner and two-time IndyCar Series champion who wins for Team Penske and our partners on and off the track. We are thrilled to announce his extension and look forward to continuing that winning tradition for many years."

Diuguid, the race strategist for Newgarden's No. 2 car, also said Penke's three-driver lineup — Newgarden, Scott McLaughlin and David Malukas — will keep the team in position to win races and series titles into the future.

The 35-year-old Newgarden won twice in 2026, extending his streak to 12 consecutive years with at least one trip to victory lane. He finished eighth in the standings this season and his 34 career wins rank ninth all-time. Newgarden now has more wins than other American driver in the series.

He has also been swept up in controversy each of the past two years, getting penalized for making illegal modifications to his car and later allegedly misusing his push-to-pass button. It didn't impact either side when it came to decision time, though.

"I've had the great privilege of driving for Team Penske for over a decade now," Newgarden said. "This is an exciting time for the IndyCar Series as we build towards a robust future for our sport with our partners and fans. My greatest enjoyment has been working with the men and women on this team, our partners, and pushing each other forward each day in an effort to produce results."
