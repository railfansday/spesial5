Chiefs worked out K Matt Prater on Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Chiefs worked out free agent kicker Matt Prater on Tuesday, according to the NFL's personnel notice.

The team already has two veteran kickers, with Harrison Butker on the active roster and Justin Tucker on the practice squad. Butker is only 7-of-19 on field goal attempts this season.

His struggles prompted the Chiefs to sign Tucker on Oct. 28.

Butker had no field goal attempts in Sunday's 35-12 win over the Falcons, but he did make all five extra points.

Prater, 42, has kicked for the Falcons (2007), Broncos (2007-13), Lions (2014-20), Cardinals (2021-24) and Bills (2025) in his 19-year career.

Last season for the Bills, Prater made 23-of-25 field goals and 46-of-49 extra points, including the postseason.

The Chiefs also worked out tight end John FitzPatrick, defensive tackle Aaron Graves, defensive tackle Eric Johnson and defensive tackle J.R. Singleton. FitzPatrick signed to the practice squad.
