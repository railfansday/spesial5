Amon-Ra St. Brown active for today's Lions game vs Patriots in Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Editor's note: Join our live coverage of the Lions-Patriots game in Germany. And right after the game, join our live reaction and analysis conversation on our YouTube page.

The Detroit Lions will be down a few starters in Germany.

The Lions take on the New England Patriots at Allianz Arena in Munich, Germany, on Sunday, Nov. 15. Three starters didn't make the trip with injuries, however, with safety Kerby Joseph, center Cade Mays and cornerback D.J. Reed all out for today's game.

Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown was also listed as questionable with a rib injury on Friday but is expected to play today, as first reported by ESPN's Ian Rapoport.

Patriots get the return of Pro Bowl receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee), and in a surprise, starting running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) is also active after getting hurt against Green Bay.

The Lions listed six inactive players for today's game.
