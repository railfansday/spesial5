strategies for preventing high capital gains distributions from blowing up your taxes?

there is no magic formula, i had a similar issue in a brokerage fund from VPMAX shares, due to size of holdings i was getting hit with $30k to $40k of LTCG distributions per year but was hesitant to flip the shares and take the capital gains harvesting hit since they had appreciated 80% over 20 years or so.

finally had enough so i did sell a bunch this year, also took $45k worth for charity and created a DAF to offset some of the AGI hit. I kept some of the proceeds in cash for living expense and purchased indexed mutuals like VTSAX or VTIAX with the rest.

to keep a interest in VPMAX due to FOMO i exchanged some funds in TIRA and/or RIRA.
