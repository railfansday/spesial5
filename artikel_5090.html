Evernorth’s $35 million fundraising could buy 35 million XRP, but there’s a catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The potential cryptocurrency purchase would expand its treasury, yet the issuance of 3.07 million shares means existing investors may not see a proportional increase in XRP backing.

Evernorth raised $35.3 million through warrant exercises, adding capital to its XRP treasury strategy while expanding its share count.

The Nasdaq-listed company disclosed in its Nov. 13 regulatory filing that investors exercised 3,073,366 warrants between Oct. 12 and Nov. 9, paying $11.50 per share. The transactions generated $35.3 million in gross proceeds and resulted in the issuance of an equivalent number of new Class A shares.

The fundraising gives the Ripple-backed company additional financial resources to expand its cryptocurrency holdings. However, it also introduces a challenge for shareholders as Evernorth attempts to increase its XRP treasury without diluting the amount of cryptocurrency attributable to each share.

At XRP's current price of about $0.99, the newly raised funds could theoretically buy 35.7 million tokens, although Evernorth has not disclosed any acquisitions financed by the proceeds.

In October, Evernorth announced that it controlled a treasury of approximately 473 million XRP, positioning the company among the largest publicly traded institutional holders of the digital asset.

The firm has outlined plans to accumulate additional tokens while deploying portions of its holdings through institutional investment and yield-generating strategies involving Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo.

However, the latest financing highlights a potential complication in that strategy.

Evernorth measures its performance partly through XRP per share, calculated by dividing the tokens attributable to the publicly traded parent company by its outstanding Class A and Class C shares.

This means that increasing its overall XRP holdings does not necessarily translate into higher cryptocurrency exposure for existing shareholders.

The latest warrant exercises added more than 3 million shares to Evernorth's capital structure. As of Nov. 9, the company reported about 27 million Class A shares and 31.4 million Class C shares outstanding, for a combined total of 58.4 million.

Consequently, future XRP purchases must generate enough additional token holdings to offset the expanded share base if management is to deliver growth in XRP per share.

That distinction is particularly important because Evernorth has several avenues for raising more capital.

The filing shows that approximately 8.67 million warrants remain outstanding. Exercising all of them for cash at $11.50 would generate another $99.7 million in gross proceeds while creating an equivalent number of additional shares.

Separately, Evernorth secured an equity financing arrangement with Cantor Fitzgerald in October, allowing it to sell up to 5.22 million Class A shares at prices generally equivalent to 97% of the applicable volume-weighted average price.

The facility offers another source of funding for XRP acquisitions and corporate operations, although utilizing it would further increase the company's outstanding equity.

For shareholders, the outcome depends on how effectively Evernorth converts its financing into additional XRP reserves.

Deploying the capital into cryptocurrency purchases could increase XRP per share if the resulting treasury growth outpaces dilution. Conversely, retaining funds for operational expenses or issuing additional shares without sufficient token accumulation could weaken that measure.
