Gate Adds GBP and EUR Banking Support for Gate Money via OpenPayd

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Home » Exchanges » Gate Adds GBP and EUR Banking Support for Gate Money via OpenPayd

The expanded integration adds virtual pound accounts and euro SEPA rails across Gate’s unified financial platform.

Cryptocurrency and financial services platform Gate has expanded its multi-year partnership with financial infrastructure provider OpenPayd to support the international rollout of Gate Money, its unified fiat, crypto, and equities account.

Under the expanded agreement, Gate is integrating OpenPayd’s British pound (GBP) account infrastructure and euro (EUR) Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA) payment rails across additional corporate entities.

The expansion builds on a partnership established in 2022, when Gate began using OpenPayd’s virtual IBANs to manage and reconcile European retail and corporate payment flows.

According to the companies, the additional GBP and EUR capabilities will support Gate Money while remaining available across Gate’s broader platform, which serves more than 62 million users worldwide.

The infrastructure expansion follows the October 7, 2026 launch of Gate Money. The product brings digital assets, equities, payments and asset management together within a single account.

Users can hold, receive, transfer, pay and exchange GBP and EUR alongside digital assets, consolidating multiple financial activities within one platform.

The launch represents Gate’s expansion beyond cryptocurrency trading into broader financial services, with fiat payment infrastructure playing a central role in connecting traditional currencies and digital assets.

“Bringing assets, accounts and payments together requires fiat infrastructure that can operate across products and markets,” OpenPayd Chief Commercial Officer Lux Thiagarajah said in a statement.

Gate Founder and CEO Dr. Han said the expanded partnership provides the financial infrastructure needed to integrate Gate’s services and simplify everyday fund management and payments for its global user base.

Gate’s expansion reflects a broader push by crypto exchanges to become all-in-one financial platforms.

Coinbase opened stock and ETF trading to all US users in February, while Kraken and Bybit have expanded into tokenized equities.

Gate announced a crypto-linked Visa card in early October, that is expected to reach more than 40 markets, allowing users to spend their Gate Money balances at merchants. Its broader platform also provides access to metals, indices, forex and commodities, alongside more than 5,400 digital assets and 12,800 stocks and ETFs for trading.

For OpenPayd, the expanded agreement deepens its role in Gate’s financial services operations, strengthening its position as a provider connecting traditional banking with digital assets.

Founded in 2018, OpenPayd offers businesses a range of financial services through a single API, including embedded accounts, foreign exchange, domestic and international payments, Open Banking and stablecoin on- and off-ramps.

The company says it serves more than 1,200 businesses, including eToro, Kraken, OKX and B2C2, and processes over $360 billion in annual transaction volumes.

For London-based OpenPayd, the expanded mandate adds to a growing roster of digital asset clients—including Kraken, OKX, Bybit, eToro, and B2C2—that rely on its API for fiat on/off-ramps and stablecoin settlement.

Seamless fiat banking rails are the missing link for crypto exchanges trying to compete with traditional neobanks as everyday financial hubs. By wiring direct fiat infrastructure into their ecosystem, crypto platforms reduce reliance on third-party workarounds and make it easier for users to hold, convert, and spend traditional currencies alongside crypto and equities.
