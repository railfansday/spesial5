Ripple Lands Major Turkish Bank Deal – So Why is XRP/Lira Trading Down 70%?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Yapı Kredi adopts Ripple Custody to expand its crypto services in Türkiye as XRP trading volume on Binance falls 70% year over year.

In today’s Ripple news, Yapı Kredi, one of Türkiye’s largest private banks, will use Ripple Custody to run its digital asset business. The bank will hold crypto for Yapı Kredi Kripto, the group’s trading platform, using Ripple’s technology on its own premises.

More institutional momentum in Türkiye. 🇹🇷@YapiKredi, one of the country's largest private banks, is leveraging Ripple Custody to power secure digital asset services in the largest crypto retail market in the Middle East, where XRP is among the most widely held digital assets.…

Ripple called Türkiye the largest retail crypto market in the Middle East and Africa, and said XRP is “among the country’s most widely held digital assets.”

The deal lands as Turkish trading in XRP cools. Our Binance data show that monthly XRP trading against the Turkish lira is on pace to finish November about 70% below its level a year ago.

XRP itself trades near $0.92, down 58% in a year.

Those rules matter. Turkish trading platforms must keep at least 95% of customer crypto with an authorized custodian, so licensed banks like Yapı Kredi sit at the center of the market.

For Ripple news watchers, the bigger claim is this: Ripple says its technology is behind every leading Turkish bank licensed as a crypto custodian so far. Reece Merrick, Ripple’s managing director for the Middle East and Africa, said Ripple Custody “gives financial institutions the security and control they need to build digital asset services.”

Ripple news often stresses XRP’s popularity in Türkiye. Trading tells a quieter story. We added up XRP’s trading against the Turkish lira on Binance, month by month, and converted it to dollars.

XRP is also a smaller slice of Turkish trading than of global trading. Over the past 30 days, it made up 1.7% of Binance’s lira volume, ranked 11th, against 3.3% and fifth place in dollar-stablecoin pairs. On local exchange BtcTurk, it ranked 11th by lira volume on Tuesday. Turkish traders are chasing small-cap tokens instead.

The lira has softened the blow for local holders. XRP is down 50% in lira terms over the past year, against 58% in dollars, because the lira lost 16% of its value against the dollar.
