California Ski Resorts Are Getting a Midwinter Dump Before Thanksgiving—and Are Opening Early

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

California’s ski season is getting an early-season boost in a huge way. A powerful storm system is dumping significant snowfall across the Sierra Nevada, with Lake Tahoe-area ski resorts among the biggest winners.

The storm has delivered some eye-popping totals already. According to resort snow reports from Thursday, Nov. 19, Boreal Mountain picked up 52 inches in the previous 24 hours, while Mt. Rose reported 32 to 36 inches. Sugar Bowl recorded 34 inches, Palisades Tahoe 25 inches, and Kirkwood 27 inches. Northstar and Bear Valley each reported 22 inches.

The snowfall extends beyond Tahoe, too. Mammoth Mountain reported 20 to 32 inches at Main Lodge in the 24 hours leading into Thursday morning, adding to an already promising start to the season.

We’re not jealous here in Colorado. Not at all.

The timing couldn’t be better for resorts gearing up for Thanksgiving week. Several mountains have moved opening dates forward or are preparing to welcome skiers as early as this weekend. Palisades Tahoe, originally scheduled to open Nov. 25, will now spin lifts Saturday, Nov. 21. Northstar, Heavenly, and Boreal are also scheduled to open today (Friday, Nov. 20). Kirkwood just announced they’re opening nearly two weeks early, on Monday, Nov. 23.

But wait, there’s more: Another storm system is expected to move into the Sierra early next week, potentially bringing additional heavy snowfall just in time for Thanksgiving. Forecasts are calling for several more feet across the mountains.

There is one important caveat, fellow skiers: Getting to the mountains could be a challenge. Heavy snowfall has already caused travel disruptions along Interstate 80, and the Sierra Avalanche Center issued a high avalanche danger warning for the Tahoe backcountry. Skiers should check road conditions and resort operations before heading out, and stay out of the backcountry until conditions improve.

This is like an early Christas gift after a disappointing snow season last winter. And with Thanksgiving still several days away, winter is just getting warmed up.
