Video Shows Dramatic Rescue of Hiker Who Plunged Hundreds of Feet off Utah Summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Updated footage released last week shows the dramatic aerial rescue of a female hiker who slipped off a trail in Utah and slid/fell hundreds of feet before stopping on a narrow ledge on October 26. The hiker had been navigating a steep descent on Mount Timpanogos, an 11,700-foot peak near Salt Lake City, when she lost her footing and fell.

Search and rescue personnel spotted her from an airplane. She was then hoisted to safety by a helicopter and transported to a waiting ambulance. The hiker was reportedly badly injured during the fall but is “recovering well from her injuries,” according to an updated Utah County Sheriff’s Office (UCSO) press release.

The updated footage shows the mountain’s snow-covered peak and pinpoints where the hiker fell from. The hiker can be seen lying precariously on the edge of a narrow, exposed cliff as a rescuer is lowered down to her. “They said she was conscious and breathing … when they were talking to her,” one of the rescuers can be heard saying in the video. “She is alive,” confirms the rescuer on the hoist upon reaching the ledge, later reporting that the hiker was able to sit up as they worked to secure her for the airlift.

“Thank you to all those agencies that rushed to the rescue,” said UCSO in its press release. “This incident is a stark reminder of how fast a fun outing can turn into a life-or-death situation. We here at the UCSO are grateful for the outcome of that rescue.”
