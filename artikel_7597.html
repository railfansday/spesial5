Law firms face increasing AI-fueled cyberattacks, new report says

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Researchers are warning that cyberattacks using agentic artificial intelligence on law firms and other groups in the legal profession are on the rise. (Image from Shutterstock)

Researchers are warning that cyberattacks using agentic artificial intelligence on law firms and other groups in the legal profession are on the rise.

These attacks have doubled since 2023, according to cybersecurity company CrowdStrike’s 2026 Legal Threat Landscape Report, which was released Wednesday.

The report shows that between August 2025 and August 2026, cyber adversaries named 455 legal entities as hacking targets, an increase of 39% from the prior year.

While CrowdStrike did not name specific firms or other legal groups as the victims of attacks, Reuters has reported that Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan, Wilmer Cutler Pickering Hale and Dorr and Seyfarth Shaw are among those that have experienced cyber breaches in recent months.

“What we’re seeing across the broader threat landscape, more and more threat actors are using agentic technology ⁠to democratize hacking,” said Adam Meyers, CrowdStrike’s senior vice president for counter adversary operations. “It’s going to get a lot worse before it gets better.”
