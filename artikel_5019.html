Tokenized Stocks Shrug Off the Crypto Selloff

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bitcoin fell as low as $62k late Saturday, its sixth straight weekly decline. Meanwhile, tokenized stocks crossed $4 billion in onchain value for the first time on Nov 11. Tokenized stocks are now roughly five times their value on January 1.

Tokenized stocks track equity prices, not crypto, so a falling BTC doesn’t directly pull their prices down. What stands out, however, is that crypto users appear to keep buying them through the selloff. Demand for 24/7 trading and onchain access to US equities appears to have found strong product-market fit as they are holding up even as the rest of the crypto market weakens.

In September, rising share prices drove most of the growth in value of tokenized stocks. Since mid-October, new minting has driven the growth in value even as share prices dropped. Over the eleven weeks to Nov 13, minting added $1.12 billion while price moves took away $339 million. Binance’s bStocks accounted for about four fifths of that minting, led by NVIDIA, SpaceX and Circle.

Market value has stalled over the past two weeks. We expect minting to pick back up again with the launch of OKXICE on Nov 23 opening large-cap tokenized stocks to US retail.

Securitize reported $11.5 million in tokenization revenue in Q3, its highest on record and 28% more than Q3 2025. Securitize's average tokenized assets reached a record $5 billion as of Q3 as BlackRock, Ark Invest and Neuberger brought new products onchain.

Tokenization revenue is more variable because it rises and falls with issuance, while asset servicing has settled around $7 million a quarter. Securitize's acquisition of River One could help smooth out those revenue swings and diversify its top line. Lending against tokenized assets adds recurring income on top of issuance. We expect credit to show up as Securitize's third revenue line within a year of the deal closing.

Most of the roughly $4 billion of tokenized stock sits in wrappers that US investors can't buy. Only about 10% is open to American investors, and almost all of that is crypto companies including Securitize, Figure, Exodus and Forward Industries. NVIDIA, SpaceX and the other large names are effectively offshore only.

That could change on Nov 23, when OKXICE opens to US customers who have onboarded through its CeDeFi Wallet. It’s the first venue built for American retail at scale, so the onshore share should grow post-launch. We expect issuer-backed listings to drive it, with 67 companies having opted out.
