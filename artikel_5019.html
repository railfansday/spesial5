Cathie Wood Bets $91M on Anthropic IPO While Paring Crypto Exposure

Cathie Wood’s ARK Invest jumped in on the Anthropic IPO on the AI company’s first day of public trading, buying roughly $91 million in shares while quietly paring back a sliver of its crypto exposure.

The firm bought 934,968 shares of Anthropic, worth about $90.9 million at Thursday’s closing price, spread across six of its actively managed exchange-traded funds on the Claude maker’s Nasdaq debut.

Roughly 60% of the purchase landed in ARK’s flagship Innovation ETF, known as ARKK, where the shares make up about 0.4% of total holdings.

During the same session, ARK trimmed two crypto-linked positions, selling about $2.9 million of the 3iQ Ethereum staking ETF and $1.2 million of the 3iQ Solana staking ETF, a combined $4.1 million, from its ARKW and ARKF funds.

The reductions are modest next to the Anthropic buy, and market observers framed them as routine portfolio rebalancing rather than a change in ARK’s stance on either token.

Wood’s firm frequently repositions its exposure, and earlier this week ARK sold a combined $85 million in Nvidia shares amid the leadership turmoil that saw Jensen Huang resign. At the time, the firm also bought crypto-linked stocks including Coinbase, Circle and Block.

Taken together, the trades show ARK leaning harder into AI equities at a moment when its crypto-adjacent bets have been battered by a market downturn.

Anthropic’s debut was one of the year’s most anticipated listings. The company priced its IPO at $83 per share, and the stock surged before cooling, closing its first day at $97.17, up 17% from the offering price but down sharply from its intraday high, with nearly 850 million shares changing hands.

It slipped again in premarket trading the next day. Even after the purchase, Anthropic sits outside ARK’s top 10 holdings, which are led by Tesla, SpaceX, Tempus AI and Amazon; ARK’s SpaceX stake alone is valued around $1.69 billion.

The timing underscores the crosscurrents buffeting both sectors. ARK’s crypto trims landed as digital assets slid, with Ethereum down about 8% on the day near $1,620, part of a broader selloff that has pressured crypto funds.

For Wood, long one of crypto’s most prominent institutional champions, the week’s trades suggest a tactical tilt toward the AI boom even as she keeps a foot firmly in digital assets, buying crypto stocks one day and trimming crypto ETFs the next.
