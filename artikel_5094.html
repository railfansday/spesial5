JPMorgan and DBS offer 24/7 dollar transfers… without stablecoins

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Institutional clients can move dollars between the US and Singapore around the clock. One JPMorgan conversion step still pauses for three hours on Saturdays.

Singapore bank DBS and JPMorgan's blockchain business Kinexys have connected their blockchain-based dollar accounts in Singapore and the US, allowing institutional clients to transfer funds instantly around the clock as an alternative to stablecoins, according to DBS's Nov. 16 announcement.

The service gives corporate treasurers another way to move bank deposits beyond traditional banking hours.

Ant International is the first client of the commercially ready connection, which uses Partior to coordinate settlement between the banks' permissioned networks. The launch puts a practical service behind the interoperability framework the banks announced in November 2025.

The competitive opportunity for banks is to make deposits easier to move while preserving the customer relationship attached to them.

Stablecoins offer another way to transfer dollars digitally, but whether companies favor one route depends on where they can send the money, how they recover cash, and what the whole transaction costs.

A tokenized deposit represents a bank deposit, while USDC is issued by Circle affiliates and backed by cash and cash-equivalent reserves. Moving either on a blockchain does not make the customer's counterparty or redemption arrangements identical.

DBS says transfers within Kinexys's Blockchain Deposit Account network operate 24/7/365. However, moving money between those accounts and traditional demand-deposit accounts has a maintenance interruption every Saturday from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time.

The banks did not disclose fees or how much cash Ant needs to keep on hand, so companies have no like-for-like cost comparison with stablecoin transfers.

Circle says standard banking-rail USDC redemptions can be free and typically settle in up to two business days, while qualifying Circle Platform and Circle Payments Network customers can obtain free near-real-time redemption.

Circle attributes fast redemption to cash pre-positioned at bank partners, which earns less than Treasuries, and its Mint product terms also make instant settlement conditional on participating banks offering it.

Direct Mint access is restricted to eligible institutions, while individuals obtain USDC through exchanges and wallets.

A Bank for International Settlements (BIS) working paper published Oct. 13 uses weekly US data from January 2019 through June 2025. Researchers Rashad Ahmed and Iñaki Aldasoro found that higher deposit rates were followed by slower stablecoin growth, and that the relationship was stronger for USDC than USDT.

The paper links a one-standard-deviation increase in deposit repricing to about 2.4 percentage points slower stablecoin growth over ten weeks. Stablecoin growth averaged 15% over a ten-week period in the sample.

The findings suggest that deposit pricing helps shape demand for stablecoins, but the authors say their analysis does not establish that stablecoin growth necessarily reduces total bank deposits or lending.

The DBS link is part of a wider effort to make bank money usable across more systems, though the products are distinct.

On Oct. 19, JPMorgan brought its JPM Coin deposit token to Ethereum, alongside Base, for institutional clients. That public-chain token is separate from the permissioned Blockchain Deposit Account network connected to DBS.

Using its internal data, JPMorgan reported more than $4 trillion in cumulative transactions and $7 billion in average daily volume across the broader Kinexys platform.

In September, DBS said it and Citi's New York office had completed a dollar payment via SWIFT Digital Ledger in minutes over a weekend.

Separately, The Clearing House selected Quant to support a tokenized-deposit network with connections to RTP and CHIPS. Participating institutions are expected to gain access in the first half of 2027.

A faster transfer inside one network only helps if the receiving bank can accept it.

The competition is consequently about more than whose blockchain runs all weekend. The decisive advantage for corporate cash would be money available where it is needed, with fewer costly balances waiting elsewhere.
