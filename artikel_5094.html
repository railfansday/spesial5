$36B Wealth Firm Strategist: Bitcoin’s Supercycle Is “Still Intact”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

James Thorne, Chief Market Strategist at Wellington-Altus, tells BMTV that Bitcoin's super cycle is still intact after the drop from about 83,000 in mid-October to around 64,000.

James Thorne, Chief Market Strategist at Wellington-Altus, tells BMTV that Bitcoin’s super cycle is still intact after the drop from about 83,000 in mid-October to around 64,000. He reads the chart as a head and shoulders bottom as long as it holds above 57,000, and says a thin-weekend sell-off does not change the long-term fundamentals. In his view, Bitcoin is a highly volatile asset, and the energy shock fading raises the probability of a significant rate cut.
