The USDe supply on Avalanche has crossed $100 million, the Ethena [ENA] Ecosystem social media handle announced on X.

Increased adoption and ecosystem integration via EthenaPay on the Avalanche blockchain drove the synthetic dollar growth.

The USDe supply was just $20 million towards the end of October. Ethena has been building toward this Avalanche push for months, according to reports.

Ethena Pay, the self-custody mobile and money payments app, can automatically earn daily yield, tiered cashback in AVAX based on monthly spending, and allows free, instant transfers to other Ethena Pay users.

These features helped explain the USDe growth, but they might not contribute much to ENA token price growth.

Popular onchain tracking platform Lookonchain posted that a whale had deposited 20 million ENA tokens, worth $2.33 million, onto Binance within the past 24 hours.

This whale had bought 37.96 million ENA, worth $10.29 million at around $0.27 a month ago.

Deposits onto exchanges are not proof of selling, but they do highlight increased available supply ready to be sold. It can be taken as a sign of partial capitulation of the whale’s initial position, following ENA’s steady downtrend over the past month.

The ENA 1-day chart has flipped from bullish to bearish in its swing structure. The past month’s steady selling drove a price move below $0.1348, the previous swing low.

The descending trendline resistance was unbroken. To add to the bearish pressure, the former local support zone at $0.18 had also been decisively breached. This helped accelerate the selling pressure on ENA that led to the bearish trend shift.

On the 1-hour timeframe, ENA was trading within a range that reached from $0.110 to $0.122. The technical indicators on this timeframe were neutral, further highlighting the short-term consolidation type price action.

In the coming days, a move past the range high would likely result in a bounce to $0.131. This level was a higher timeframe supply zone, and ENA might struggle to climb any further.
