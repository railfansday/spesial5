Anthropic Stock Opens 33% Above IPO Price as AI Stocks Rally

Anthropic shares opened at $111.50 on Thursday, approximately 33% above their $83 initial public offering price, as investors piled into the Claude developer on its highly anticipated Nasdaq debut.

Initial indications on the IPO open climbed from $108 earlier in the session, with successive price estimates reportedly moving closer together, Yahoo reported.

During the opening process, market makers and underwriters monitor incoming buy and sell orders to determine a price at which supply and demand can meet. Indications quickly climbed as Nasdaq established its official opening price.

At $111, Anthropic commands a fully diluted valuation approaching $2 trillion, up from around $1.5 trillion at its IPO price. That represents nearly $500 billion in additional implied market value before its first public trade.

The excitement is spreading beyond Anthropic itself. U.S. stocks climbed Thursday, with semiconductor companies leading a broader rally in AI-related shares. Optical equipment makers, laser technology firms and neocloud providers also advanced, helping lift the technology sector as investors awaited Anthropic's first trade.

At the Nasdaq ceremony, Anthropic co-founders Dario and Daniela Amodei rang the opening bell, marking a milestone for one of the world's most prominent AI developers.

Daniela, who serves as the company's president, used the occasion to address people concerned about AI's growing influence.

"This technology needs you. Not your trust, not your use, but your voice," she said, urging the public to participate in shaping the technology's future.

"The end of the story isn't written yet. Intelligence is now something humans can build. What we choose to build with it is up to all of us," she added.

Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83, raising $81.34 billion—more than SpaceX's initial $75 billion IPO, although still below its expanded final offering.

The debut follows a remarkable turnaround in sentiment. Just two weeks ago, some major investors were questioning whether Anthropic could sustain a $1.5 trillion valuation, with one suggesting it might need to accept something closer to $1 trillion.

For investors allocated shares at $83, a $110 opening translates into an immediate paper gain of $27 per share.

The bigger question is whether Anthropic's strong debut marks renewed confidence in the AI boom—or simply another example of investors paying increasingly extraordinary prices for its biggest names.
