Where To Find The Aquarium In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

One of Vice City's most unique spots can be pretty tough to find. Here's where you need to go.

Grand Theft Auto 6's Leonida isn't just the biggest map that Rockstar Games has ever made, as it's also full to the brim with unique and detailed locations for the player to stumble upon.

One of the memorable optional locations in Vice City is one that fans have been talking about ever since it was originally spotted - the Vice City Oceanarium. Here's where you can find it.

Grand Theft Auto 6's take on one of Florida's many aquariums (most likely Orlando's SeaWorld, let's be real) can be found on Barentine Drive, to the East of the Catalan Key. You'll know you're in the right place when you see signage for the aquarium, as well as its signature domelike structures that can be easily spotted throughout Vice City and by looking at the map.

As you would hope, the Oceanarium is home to some unique aquatic life, including flamingos, manatees, and an orca. Those last two are noteworthy, as my many hours in Leonida suggest that they can actually only be found in the Oceanarium, making it an essential visit if you're trying to scan every animal.

If you visit at night, you can even get into the tank and swim around with the Oceanarium's inhabitants. I never tried it during the day, but I can't imagine it goes down well with the staff.

Beyond being one of the most impressively detailed locations in Grand Theft Auto 6, the Oceanarium actually has a point to it, as visiting the entrance at night reveals a whole sidequest centred around protestors. As far as I can tell, there's nothing that points you to the Oceanarium and its side quest, making it one of the game's more easily missable missions.
