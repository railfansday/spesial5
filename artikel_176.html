Black Friday isn’t just a good time to save on a new TV but also a chance to spend less on what you watch on it. The shopping holiday often brings steep streaming discounts, and this year is no exception, with some streamers offering more than 50 percent off. HBO Max, Apple TV, and Hulu are all offering deals, with several plans going for just a few dollars a month. Some discounts even apply to ad-free plans, though there are fewer than last year and prices are generally higher.

Still, you can save big if you don’t mind a few commercials. Right now, for example, you can get a year of the ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month, saving you $96, or sign up for the most basic HBO Max plan for $5.49 a month for 12 months, saving you $66. We’ve rounded up these deals and more below, including what each plan offers and how long you can take advantage of the discounted rate.

With everything from Disney classics and Marvel movies to FX dramas like The Bear and Shōgun, the Disney Plus and Hulu bundle offers plenty for the whole family to watch. For Black Friday, you can get both services with ads for just $4.99 per month ($8 off) for a year via Hulu or Disney Plus, saving you $96 in total. The ad-free bundle is also on sale for $14.99 per month ($7 off) for 12 months, saving you $84. Both discounts are available through 11:59PM PT on November 30th.

Whichever plan you choose, you’ll get access to both streaming libraries. Hulu offers dramas like Furious, Alien: Earth, The Lowdown, and The Handmaid’s Tale, as well as comedies like Bob’s Burgers, Futurama, and It’s Always Sunny in Philadelphia. Disney Plus, meanwhile, is home to Disney and Pixar favorites, The Simpsons, and a wide selection of Star Wars and Marvel movies and shows. It’s also a good option for holiday movie nights, with classics like Home Alone and The Santa Clause.

Although the plan doesn’t include live sports, it supports simultaneous streaming on two devices and gives you access to everything from award-winning films like Sinners and Marty Supreme to Wuthering Heights. There’s arguably even more to watch on the TV front, from dramas like The Gilded Age, Lanterns, Euphoria, and Heated Rivalry to comedies such as Hacks and The Righteous Gemstones. Fantasy fans, meanwhile, can immerse themselves in the world of Game of Thrones and its spinoffs, including House of the Dragon and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, or check out Dune: Prophecy. You’ll also be able to stream new releases, including HBO’s upcoming Harry Potter series.

The cheaper Select tier is a particularly good value if you mainly watch shows from networks like NBC, Bravo, and Telemundo, including Saturday Night Live, The Office, and Below Deck. Movie buffs and sports fans, however, will prefer Premium, which offers access to a much wider selection of entertainment. Peacock’s catalog includes films like The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, Disclosure Day, and Minions & Monsters, along with shows like The Good Daughter, Dig, and The Paper. A Premium subscription also lets you watch live sporting events, including Sunday Night Football, as well as Peacock originals, such as the various Love Island spinoffs.

Paramount Plus includes CBS shows like NCIS and Everybody Loves Raymond to Tulsa King and the various Yellowstone spinoffs, including both 1883 and 1923. Movie fans can also browse Paramount’s film library, which includes titles like Arrival, Top Gun, and Interstellar, while sports fans can watch select NFL games, UFC events, UEFA Champions League matches, and March Madness games. Premium also grants you access to Showtime originals and a live feed of your local CBS station, along with ad-free on-demand viewing, though live programming still includes commercials.

Six months should give you plenty of time to work through Apple’s other originals, including Severance, Pachinko, The Morning Show, and Pluribus, or settle in for a movie like Arrival or Anchorman. The service also includes live sports, specifically Formula 1 races and Major League Soccer matches.

All three subscriptions offer access to Crunchyroll’s full anime library without ads, including new episodes shortly after they air in Japan. The Fan tier lets you watch on one device at a time, while Mega Fan increases that limit to four devices and adds offline viewing and access to Crunchyroll’s Game Vault. Ultimate Fan, meanwhile, supports six simultaneous streams and includes Crunchyroll Manga, along with additional store perks.

Amazon is also discounting a wide range of subscriptions through Prime Video, including NBA League Pass, AMC Plus, MGM Plus, and several bundles. Most of the deals below are available through November 30th.
