From a kid-safe saw (yes, really) to a screen-free telephone “club,” these are the picks blowing up our mom group chats.

Our editors pooled our mom group chats to parse through all the convos and conjecture about what our kids want this year, and we came up with a list of kids’ gifts real moms are actually buying (or have already procured and stuffed in a secret hiding place).
