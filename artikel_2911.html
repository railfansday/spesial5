10 things you need to know about new Eagles quarterback Joe Milton

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

10 things you need to know about new Eagles quarterback Joe Milton originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Joe Milton on Sunday will become the Eagles' third starting quarterback this year and the first former Cowboy to start at quarterback for the Eagles in more than 30 years.

What fruit is Milton famous for throwing the length of a football field? What long-time Eagles safety went to the same high school as Milton? Who's the only other quarterback drafted by the Patriots to play for the Eagles?

You'll find answers to these questions and several other fascinating nuggets in our 10 Things you Need to Know about Joe Milton.

Guaranteed it's more interesting than 10 Things you Need to Know about Andy Dalton.

1. Milton's cousin is Anquan Bolden, the long-time Arizona Cardinals wide receiver, who caught 1,076 passes for 13,779 yards in his 14-year career. Both went to Pahokee, Fla., High School, also the alma mater of long-time Eagles safety Andre Waters. Another cousin of Milton's is Kevin Bouie, the Eagles' 7th-round pick out of Mississippi State. Bouie, who also attended Pahokee High School, never got into a regular-season game for the Eagles but played for the Chargers in 1996 and Cards in 1997.

2. Milton spent the first two years of his college career at Michigan, backing up Shea Patterson. During the 2020 COVID season, Milton started three games but gradually lost playing time to Cade McNamara and then transferred to Tennessee before the 2021 season. Milton was hurt most of 2021 and backed up Hendon Hooker for most of 2022, although he did win two late-season starts after Hooker suffered a torn ACL in a game against South Carolina. Milton went 8-4 in 2023 with 20 touchdown passes and five interceptions, passing for 2,769 yards and rushing for 292 with six more TDs.

3. Milton was originally the Patriots' 6th-round pick in 2024. He played in one game in New England, going 22-for-29 for 241 yards and a 48-yard touchdown pass to Kayshon Boutte in a 23-16 win over the Bills on the final day of the 2024 season after Drake Maye played one series. Milton also ran for a touchdown. Patriots head coach Jerod Mayo was fired soon after the game ended. Milton's passer rating of 111.4 was highest by a Patriots rookie since Bailey Zappe had a 118.4 in a game against the Browns in 2022

4. Although Milton has thrown about a quarter as many career passes as Tanner McKee – 202 to 53 – the strong-armed Milton has two more completions of at least 35 yards than McKee. Milton threw a 48-yard TD to Boutte in 2024 and a 35-yarder to Jalen Tolbert for the Cowboys in a 44-23 loss to the Broncos last October. McKee's longest career completion is the 34-yarder to Eli Stowers on the final play of the loss to the Commanders Sunday. In his career, Milton has averaged 8.0 yards per attempt and 11.5 yards per completion, compared to McKee's 6.1 yards per attempt and 9.8 yards per completion. Among 79 active quarterbacks who've thrown at least 50 passes, Milton's 8.0 yards per attempt is 3rd-highest behind Brock Purdy and Maye and his 11.5 yards per completion ranks 22nd.

5. Milton will become the Eagles' first quarterback to start a game after joining the team after opening day in 33 years. In 1993, the Eagles signed Ken O'Brien on Oct. 4, and he started four games, going 0-4, the last four starts of his career. O'Brien, who had made two Pro Bowls in nine seasons with the Jets, made his first start as an Eagle against the Cowboys on Oct. 31, 27 days after he signed. Milton will top that, starting against the Giants on Nov. 8, only 23 days after the Eagles acquired him on Oct. 16.

6. Milton will become the first quarterback from the University of Tennessee to throw a pass for the Eagles since Pat Ryan played in four games at the end of 1991. Ryan threw no touchdowns and four interceptions as an Eagle and had a passer rating of 10.3, worst by an Eagles quarterback who threw at least 25 passes since 1943. The Eagles' only other Tennessee quarterback was Johnny Butler, who threw 13 passes in 1943 and lost his only start, 13-7, to the Brooklyn Dodgers at Ebbets Field in 1943. Previous quarterbacks who've played for both the Eagles and Cowboys include Cunningham, Rodney Peete and Mark Sanchez. Only Peete played for the Eagles after the Cowboys.

7. Milton will be the second quarterback (and third player) drafted by the Patriots to throw a regular-season pass for the Eagles. Matt Cavanaugh, the Patriots' 2nd-round pick out of Pitt in 1978, spent 1986 through 1989 with the Eagles backing up Ron Jaworski and Randall Cunningham. He went 1-1 in two starts for the Eagles, both in 1986. Cavanaugh spent 13 years in the NFL – four with the Patriots, three with the 49ers, four with the Eagles and two with the Giants. He was 8-11 in 19 career starts with 28 touchdowns and 30 interceptions. After retiring from the NFL, Cavanaugh coached with the Cards under Buddy Ryan, the 49ers under George Seifert, the Bears under Dave Wannstedt, the Ravens under Brian Billick (where he was Cunningham's final NFL offensive coordinator), the Jets under Rex Ryan, the Bears again under Marc Trestman, Washington under Jay Gruden, the Jets again under Robert Saleh and he's currently a senior offensive with the Commanders under Dan Quinn. The only other player drafted by the Patriots to throw a pass for the Eagles was safety Joe Scarpati, the Patriots' 13th-round pick in 1964. He was 1-for-2 with a three-yard completion to Sam Baker in a 1968 win over the Lions at Tiger Stadium.

8. Following Jalen Hurts, Andy Dalton and McKee, Milton will become the fourth Eagles quarterback to throw a pass this year. Last time four quarterbacks threw a pass for the Eagles in the same season was 1991 when five did – Randall Cunningham, Jim McMahon, Brad Goebel, Jeff Kemp and Pat Ryan.

9. How strong is Milton's arm? You can find the video online, but while he was at Tennessee he threw an orange an estimated 100 yards. Milton, during a practice at the Vols' 80-yard practice facility, hurled the orange from his own end zone across the field and into the facility's back wall. He is said to have thrown a football 100 yards as well and a baseball 95 miles-per-hour.

10. Tradition in Pahokee is for kids to chase and catch rabbits that scatter when local farmers burn the sugarcane fields in the fall. According to local tradition, the faster you are and the more rabbits you catch, the better football career you'll have. Milton even has a tattoo of a rabbit on his arm to honor the tradition. Another Pahokee native, Janoris Jenkins, a Pro Bowl cornerback with the Giants, actually went by Jackrabbit Jenkins during his 11-year NFL career to commemorate his hometown tradition.
