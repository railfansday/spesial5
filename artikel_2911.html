Travis Kelce continues to make history while helping the Chiefs win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Kansas City Chiefs' offense continued its tear through the middle of the schedule with another 30-plus-point performance, their sixth of the season, against the Arizona Cardinals in Week 11.

The Chiefs got off to a hot start, and so did tight end Travis Kelce, who caught two touchdowns in the first half. He finished with a team-high seven targets and six catches for 74 yards.

They were the 109th and 110th scores of Kelce's career, both in his 100th game at Arrowhead Stadium (he became the fourth Chiefs player ever to do so). The touchdowns pushed him up the list with the all-time greats, surpassing former New England Patriots legend Rob Gronkowski (108) to rank third in history, behind Tony Gonzalez (115) and Antonio Gates (118).

Kelce's fifth touchdown of the season matched or exceeded his year-long totals from each of the last three years

Kelce also became the second-oldest tight end ever to score two touchdowns in a game (since 1970), less than 200 days younger than Gonzalez when he topped the list in 2013.

He was also just the fourth player in NFL history with two or more scrimmage touchdowns in a half at age 37 or older, according to the CBS broadcast. He joined Jerry Rice, Steve Young and Jim Thorpe.
