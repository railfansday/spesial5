The Tesla Model Y Performance takes the bestselling electric vehicle in the United States and turns it into a zippy, agile sports SUV.

The Model Y Performance shares its egg-like shape with other Teslas but looks sharper than the anonymous base Model Y thanks to black trim, a redesigned front bumper, a small lip spoiler, and this Ultra Red paint.

The Model Y Performance is powered by a pair of electric motors, one mounted at each axle, producing a total of 510 horsepower.

Nail the go-pedal, and the Model Y Performance will scoot to 60 mph in 3.3 seconds and run through the quarter-mile in 11.5 seconds at 122 mph.

The Model Y Performance has 14.0-in vented front brake discs and 13.2-inch vented rear discs; it can come to a stop from 70 mph in 179 feet.

The Model Y Performance has an EPA-estimated range of 306 miles, and in our 75-mph highway test, it managed to travel 270 miles.

The 21-inch wheels are wrapped in Hankook Ion Evo AS SUV tires, with the Model Y Performance putting up a respectable 0.88 g on the skidpad.
