FitLife Brands, Inc. (FTLF -0.73%) reported year-over-year revenue growth driven by the integration of the Irwin brand and expansion in digital sales channels. Management completed a restructuring program involving headcount reductions and facility consolidation to lower the company's annual cost structure. The company increased working capital to fund strategic forward purchases of key commodities, aiming to protect margins against rising global costs for protein and fish oil. Performance in the online segment reached record levels for specific brands, offsetting declines in the specialty retail channel and legacy wholesale business. Management indicated that the business has maintained relatively stable revenue and adjusted EBITDA levels over the preceding four quarters.
