BorgWarner: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Margin expansion in a softer auto market signals the company's ability to offset production headwinds through operational discipline and new revenue streams.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, BorgWarner (BWA -0.20%) raised its full-year earnings guidance. The supplier is expanding its power technology into data center markets. Management is using cash flow to buy back shares.

Three takeaways from the call highlight how management is positioning the business for growth.

Sales of eProducts, or electric and hybrid vehicle components, jumped in Europe even as global vehicle assembly slowed. Management expects this division to keep gaining market share as automakers adopt advanced hybrid technologies.

BorgWarner is adapting its automotive electronic technology for data center energy infrastructure, including microgrid inverters, which convert direct current electricity to alternating current for local power networks. Accelerated quoting activity with major tech customers shows promising traction beyond traditional vehicles.

Heavy share repurchases continue to lift adjusted EPS, or adjusted earnings per share, which strips out one-time items. Management is directing cash flow toward stock buybacks while keeping leverage low.

Vehicle production volumes face ongoing pressure from high interest rates, but BorgWarner is protecting profitability through strict operational cost controls. Its strong customer relationships, proprietary power electronics designs, and flexible manufacturing plants give it a structural edge as it monitors supplier inflation.

By pairing its core automotive business with new industrial growth opportunities, BorgWarner aims to maintain long-term earnings expansion.
