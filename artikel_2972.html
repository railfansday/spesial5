Why Nvidia Stock Continues to Fall After the Jensen Huang Pop

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There are many moving parts to the Nvidia stock story. Here's how investors should think about it.

By Howard Smith – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:42AM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -8.64%) stock soared when the company's board announced Jensen Huang's return as CEO earlier this week. That now looks more like a dead cat bounce, though.

After rocketing over 30% on Monday, Nvidia stock has given back 15.5%, including a 7.3% decline today, as of 10:38 a.m. ET.

Here's how investors can take advantage of the wild moves.

First, investors should think about the reason for the stock's reversal after the euphoria over Jensen Huang's return wore off. Recall that the company hired an independent firm to investigate Huang's actions, including an admitted affair with a customer who is suspected of being a Chinese spy. That investigation is ongoing.

The board reinstated Huang on the U.S. government's assurances that he has not put national security at risk. But there are plenty of elected officials on Capitol Hill who will continue to probe Huang. He will be testifying multiple times to congressional leaders in the coming weeks and even months. That will continue to cause volatility, but that can also be a real opportunity for retail investors.

The underlying business remains sound, even as fears grow about artificial intelligence (AI) capital spending. Nvidia still expects 75% revenue growth next year, and the stock is trading near its lowest price-to-earnings (P/E) valuation in a decade based on next year's expected earnings. The forward P/E is below 10 based on some analyst estimates.

Investors can use dollar-cost averaging or scale into Nvidia stock over time to take advantage of dips. It may take patience and a strong stomach, but it could be the way to own a premier, innovative company that still appears to have a long growth path ahead.
