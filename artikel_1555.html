Joao Pedro double and Caicedo stunner give Chelsea win over AC Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Chelsea cruised to victory over Ruben Amorim’s AC Milan in Jakarta, Indonesia, on Saturday. Goals from Moises Caicedo and a double from João Pedro either side of half-time led Xabi Alonso’s side to their second win on their Far East tour.

The game was fairly even in the first half and looked set to head into the break level at 0-0 but Pedro’s header from Moises Caicedo’s corner put Chelsea in front.

The west London side then doubled their advantage seconds into the second half as Pedro scored his second, assisted by Pedro Neto.

Just minutes later, Chelsea had their third courtesy of a well-worked corner routine. Romeo Lavia’s delivery was met by Caicedo on the volley, with the Ecuadorian’s finish with the outside of the boot finding the bottom corner.

Chelsea had further chances to extend their lead in a dominant second-half performance but were unable to add to their three goals.

Chelsea conclude their tour of the Far East with a game against Johor Darul Ta’zim on Sunday in Malaysia before returning home.

Their final pre-season game will be against Real Sociedad at Stamford Bridge before beginning their Premier League campaign at Fulham.

AC Milan take on Amorim’s former club Manchester United next Saturday before they kick off their Serie A campaign at Torino on August 23.

Chelsea managed to lure Austin MacPhee to take care of their set-pieces and today Chelsea scored two goals from corners. Here is a snippet of Mike McGrath’s piece on MacPhee’s arrival at Chelsea:

He is among the new breed of coaches who are rock stars in their own right, taking centre-stage since rules were changed so that only one person is allowed in the technical area at a time. For MacPhee to get closer to the players for a set-piece, it will mean Xabi Alonso taking a step back onto the bench.

It has been an incredible rise for MacPhee, 46, who was working on Michael O’Neill’s staff with Northern Ireland 10 years ago this summer, helping them reach the knockout stages of the European Championship.

Chelsea take on Johor Darul Ta’zim in Malaysia tomorrow before returning to the UK. Their final pre-season game will be against Real Sociedad before taking on Fulham on the opening weekend of the new season.

AC Milan will play Manchester United next weekend and then kick off their Serie A campaign against Torino.

Chelsea’s pre-season continues out in the Far East as they take on AC Milan in the Gelora Bung Karno Stadium in Jakarta, Indonesia. Both clubs now have new managers, with Xabi Alonso taking over at Chelsea and former Manchester United boss Ruben Amorim now at the helm at AC Milan. These sides met in pre-season last year, with Chelsea winning 4-1 at Stamford Bridge. At that time, now-Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca was in charge whilst Max Allegri, who is now at Napoli, was leading AC Milan. The last time they met competitively was during the 2022/23 Champions League season, with Chelsea winning both games.

Chelsea lost 1-0 to AC Milan’s Serie A rivals Juventus on Wednesday in Hong Kong, in which Danny Welbeck made his first Chelsea appearance after joining from Brighton in the last week.

“We want to have a great team spirit and that is built on a daily basis with a great culture in the building,” Alonso said in his pre-match press conference. “That helps in the moment you have to compete. You feel better having that togetherness, trust, and the bonds between the players are so important to ultimately building a winning mentality, and a team that can compete anywhere. So the football side is so important, but the mental side is as important. And the strength of the team is the most important. We have been able to train and prepare [during pre-season] and I am very happy with the attitude and personality, we know that it is a very important part of this tour.”

It has been another busy week on the transfer front for Chelsea; not only has Welbeck arrived over the last week but so too have Jordan Henderson and Valentin Barco. Alonso’s men have two more games after this against Johor Darul Ta’zim and Real Sociedad before getting their Premier League campaign under way with a derby against Fulham at Craven Cottage.

AC Milan finished fifth in Serie A last season to earn a spot in the Europa League last season. Their big-money acquisition this summer as been Portuguese striker Goncalo Ramos from PSG. They have played two pre-season friendlies so far, which have both ended in draws; 2-2 against Celtic and then 1-1 against city rivals Inter. They kick off their Serie A campaign at Torino on August 23.
