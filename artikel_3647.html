GTA 6 File Size Revealed, And It’s Truly Massive–See How It Compares To Other Huge Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ahead of the game's release on November 19, GTA 6's file size has been revealed, and it is massive. Rockstar Games has confirmed that people will need about 212GB of space to install the game.

As announced previously, preloading for GTA 6 begins on November 10, which is good news considering the game is massive in terms of its file size. People will no doubt have to delete other content to fit the game on the PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. This is double the file size of Rockstar's last game, Red Dead Redemption II.

How does GTA 6 file size compare to other games?

Content creator NikTek put together a graphic that shows how GTA 6's file size, 212GB, compares to other big games--and it's the biggest.

As you can see, the next closest is Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War (175GB), followed by Assassin's Creed Valhalla (160GB), Stalker 2 (160GB), and Forza Horizon 6 (156GB).

Everyone expected GTA 6's file size to be gigantic, but now there is a number behind it, and it's one that makes it among the largest games ever in terms of file size. GTA 6's map is three times bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2's, and the game is filled with lots of detail in the vein of that game's horse testicles.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it's expected to be the biggest game of all time. One report said the game could hit 25 million preorders by launch, generating $2 billion in revenue.

GTA 6 is a strictly single-player game, but it's safe to assume that Rockstar will eventually release a new version of GTA Online. A PC edition, meanwhile, is expected in early 2027.
