Posties have a new reason to keep their eyes on Thrilljoy. The collectible brand has announced a PIX collaboration with Post Malone, marking its first-ever celebrity partnership for the stylized figure line. The limited-edition release will feature 9,999 pieces of the “Circles” singer in his signature look, complete with his iconic tattoos, beard, jewelry, “Dallas” hat and microphone. But there’s more to this release than the standard figure. The collaboration also introduces a chase variant and an exceptionally rare one-of-one chrome edition. Founded in 2024 and comparable to the popular Funko Pops, the fast-growing brand is making waves in the collectible market with its PIX figures, which bring recognizable faces from pop culture into the world of vinyl collecting, like Wednesday Addams, House of the Dragon‘s Alicent Hightower and SpongeBob SquarePants. The newly minted Post Malone edition will go on sale at 8:00 a.m. PST on Friday, Nov. 13.

Thrilljoy’s first celebrity collaboration features three distinct variations, including an ultra-rare, one-of-one chrome figure.

Each of the Post Malone PIX figures offers something different for collectors. The Hero PIX is the standard edition, limited to 9,999 pieces, depicting the musician holding a microphone in his signature look. The chase variant takes a more uncensored approach, featuring the rapper holding a beer and cigarette. However, the biggest standout is the one-of-one PIX, a fully chromed-out version of the musician with only one figure in existence. With its extremely limited availability, the chrome edition is undoubtedly the crown jewel of the collaboration and a particularly exciting addition for serious collectors.

Founded by former Funko CEO Brian Mariotti, Thrilljoy is an officially licensed collectibles company focused on transforming memorable moments and recognizable figures from pop culture into unique keepsakes. Its offerings combine detailed character designs with creative packaging and limited-edition releases that appeal to fans and collectors alike. Among its signature offerings are Thrilljoy PIX, a line of four-inch stylized vinyl figures that celebrates beloved names and moments across entertainment and pop culture, such as Dale Doback, Hermione Granger, Beetlejuice and Batman.
