European football: Barcelona roar back from two goals down to sink Villarreal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Barça prevail 3-2 despite Lamine Yamal’s missed penalty

Atalanta snatch point in thrilling 4-4 draw with Inter

Barcelona came back from two goals behind to beat Villarreal 3-2 at home on Sunday and restore their three-point lead at the top of La Liga.

Two goals inside seven minutes from Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi gave Villarreal a commanding lead at Camp Nou and things got worse for Barça when winger Lamine Yamal missed a first-half penalty.

The Catalan giants fought back in style, striking three times in an 11-minute second-half spell to claim victory. Gerard Martín and Fermín López pulled Barça level before Pau Navarro’s own goal gave them victory.

Barça have now won four consecutive matches across all competitions since their Clàsico defeat by Real Madrid in October, and restored their three-point cushion over José Mourinho’s side, who had moved level with Saturday’s victory over Celta Vigo.

Buchanan fired Villarreal ahead in the fifth minute, producing a perfect sliced effort from distance after López gave the ball away. Two minutes later, the visitors doubled their lead through another Canadian, as Oluwaseyi broke down the left flank and smashed an effort into the roof of the net.

Hansi Flick’s side should have pulled one back when Oluwaseyi felled Lamine Yamal in the area and a penalty was given after a VAR review. The teenager took it himself but dragged his spot-kick against the post and wide.

Barcelona came out invigorated for the second half and Peter Gulacsi saved López’s low effort before Martín headed home in the 57th minute from Pedri’s cross.

Four minutes later, López slammed home the second after being teed up by Lamine Yamal and the third was quick to follow. Lamine Yamal’s clever backheel pass fed Eric García, whose cross was bundled into the back of his own net by Navarro, who was later sent off for a second yellow card to complete a miserable afternoon.

Atlético Madrid stayed on the tail of Spain’s top two with a comfortable 3-0 derby win over Getafe.

Diego Simeone’s side are five points behind leaders Barcelona and two off Real Madrid. Álex Baena and Johnny Cardoso’s first-half goals, both set up by Julián Alvarez, got the job done away at the Coliseum stadium in the suburbs south of the Spanish capital.

Álex Grimaldo wrapped up the victory in stoppage time with a penalty, after Getafe’s Djene Dakonam was sent off for fouling Alexander Sørloth.

In Serie A, Atalanta rescued an extraordinary late 4-4 draw against Inter on Sunday as the champions fell three points off the title pace despite Pio Esposito’s hat-trick.

Inter looked comfortable in Bergamo when Petar Sucic’s penalty gave them a two-goal cushion in the 76th minute after Esposito’s goals, but Charles De Ketelaere’s second of the night and a stoppage-time Franck Kessié penalty salvaged a point.

It marked only the third time Inter have dropped points in the league this season, but kept Cristian Chivu’s side in third place, one point behind Roma and three adrift of leaders Lazio.

Esposito struck twice in the first half, pouncing on a deflected ball in the box to sweep home the opener before doubling Inter’s lead by heading home from a corner. Eljif Elmas quickly got one back for the hosts to keep the game alive, but Esposito took his third in composed fashion early in the second half, marking his eighth goal in five games for club and country.

De Ketelaere proved the catalyst for Atalanta’s fightback, pulling a goal back with a header and firing home a powerful finish either side of Sucic’s spot-kick.

That set up a thrilling conclusion as Atalanta chased what had seemed an unlikely point, which they claimed when a penalty was awarded for handball that Kessié calmly converted.

Juventus moved into the top six with a thumping 4-0 victory over Venezia as their recent upturn in form continued. Luciano Spalletti’s side were booed off after a goalless first half, but found four second-half strikes as their unbeaten run stretched to four games in all competitions.

Nick Woltemade sparked the game into life after coming off the bench, pouncing on a loose ball to smash home his second league goal in two games before turning provider for Teun Koopmeiners’ whipped finish. Juan Cabal headed home Juve’s third before Kolo Muani won and converted a penalty to complete the rout, with all four goals coming between the 53rd and 71st minute.

“In the first half, we gave the ball away too much,” Spalletti told Dazn. “It was crucial the players recognised something was missing … in the second half, the players were decisive and more relaxed.”

Milan returned to the top four as Gonçalo Ramos ended his goal drought in a 5-1 thrashing of Frosinone.

Ruben Amorim’s side sealed back-to-back wins for the first time in over a month to go fourth, five points behind leaders Lazio, as club record signing Ramos broke a five-match goalless streak.

Milan got off to a flying start with a Gabriele Calvani own goal and a composed Ramos strike inside 19 minutes. Amorim accused his players of “not helping Ramos” before the game after the Portugal international scored just twice in his first 11 league games following his summer move from Paris Saint-Germain.

His third was met with a loud roar from the San Siro crowd after he was teed up by Adrien Rabiot, who became the first Milan midfielder since Kaká in 2004 to notch a goal or assist in seven consecutive league games.

The visitors soon hit back through Patrizio Masini, but Milan pulled clear in the second half with some lethal counterattacking as substitutes Ardon Jashari and Alphadjo Cisse struck before Christian Pulisic’s dinked finish completed the biggest victory of the Amorim era.

Milutin Osmajic scored twice in a 2-0 win for Genoa at Sassuolo, while Bologna were held to a 1-1 draw by Udinese, who climb off the bottom of the table at Venezia’s expense.

In Ligue 1, Paris FC climbed to second place behind local rivals Paris Saint-Germain with a 4-0 home win over lowly Angers. Patrick Zabi, Luca Koleosho, Maxime Lopez and Diego Coppola all struck in the second half for Liam Rosenior’s high-flying side.

Bayern Munich’s Jamal Musiala reportedly suffered another seizure in training this week, according to information received by an AFP subsidiary on Sunday.

Musiala collapsed twice on the pitch in pre-season with the 23-year-old later revealing he had a “neurological disorder” which can lead to “temporary but treatable seizures”. Last Monday’s incident was the second since he made the announcement; the player is reportedly adjusting his medication to properly treat the condition while continuing to play.

News of the incident comes as Bayern have faced criticism from footballers’ union Fifpro over what they claim is a “coordinated campaign” to pressure Musiala to renegotiate the contract he extended in 2025.

On Friday, Bayern’s supervisory board member and former chief executive Karl-Heinz Rummenigge suggested the Germany international may need to take a pay cut if he cannot recapture his old form.

“Based on what we know, it unfortunately suggests that it will be difficult for him to return to the level he so often demonstrated,” Rummenigge said. “If he doesn’t return to being the Jamal of old, then we need to have a conversation … because frankly, the current financial figures would no longer be justifiable.”
