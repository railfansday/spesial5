Violence against women in the digital age: When the internet becomes a weapon

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

November 25th is the United Nation's International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women. In the age of social media and artificial intelligence, there are now new spaces in which violence against women has appeared, with the internet now a growing weapon in the hands of abusers. 85% of women who spend time online have witnessed violence, and 38% have been the target of it.
