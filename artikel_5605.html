Inflation Remains Hot in October and Strengthens Case for Another Fed Rate Hike

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Inflation accelerated faster than anticipated in October, driven by surging costs for housing and food, which will bolster the case for another Federal Reserve interest rate hike next month.

On a monthly basis, overall prices rose 0.4%, according to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) data released released by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) on Tuesday. That's down from the 0.6% monthly increase recorded in September but higher than the 0.2% gain forecasted by economists.

CPI measures the average costs consumers pay for a range of goods, including food, energy, commodities, and services.

Core inflation, which strips out volatile food and energy categories, rose 0.3%, also exceeding the anticipated 0.2% increase.

The BLS did not publish an official annual inflation rate because last year's government shutdown prevented the agency from collecting survey data for October 2025.
