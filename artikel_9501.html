Sonic Is Bringing Back 10 Iconic ’80s &amp; ’90s Toys—And Yes, There’s A Furby

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Good luck convincing adults these are just for kids.

Tell the Happy Meal to move over—Sonic's Wacky Pack is here to steal its thunder. The kids' meal comes with a choice of entree, side, and drink, plus a toy. Past collections have included everything from Hello Kitty to Hot Wheels to Batman. Now, however, Sonic has teamed up with toy giant Hasbro to bring 10 throwback collectibles to its Wacky Packs.

The new collection is available now through the end of January 2027 at participating locations, while supplies last, and it's sure to get plenty of '80s and '90s kids excited.

And hold onto your scrunchies, because these toys might have you Skippin' It straight to Sonic the second they drop. The lineup includes pink and yellow Furbies with eyes that appear to blink, pink and yellow mini NERF Super Soakers, and My Little Pony figures featuring Applejack and Blossom. There are also Transformers figures of Megatron and Optimus Prime with glowing eyes, plus Littlest Pet Shop cat and dog leash spoons with removable figures.

Each Wacky Pack comes with one toy, and the design may vary depending on what's available.

Fans wasted no time reacting to the collection on Instagram. “OH MY F*****G GOD,” one person wrote, while another commented, “Guess I'm going to Sonic again.”

The Littlest Pet Shop spoons got plenty of attention, too. “Need 2 billion of those spoons please,” one user wrote, while another commented, “Give me the LPs spoons idc if u just called them retro.”

The love wasn't limited to one toy either, as another future customer said they “WILL be at sonic to get the ponies.”

If nostalgia is your jam, you'll want to get in line to get your hands on one—or all—of these new toys, whether you're a kid or not.
