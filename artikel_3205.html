This Paramount+ deal will only last until Nov. 30, so you’ll want to act fast to unlock the two-month savings. Head to Paramount+ here to get the deal.

The current Paramount+ deal lasts for two months, and then the price goes back up to its normal price — either $13.99 a month or $8.99 a month, depending on which plan you choose. You can also switch plans at any time.

The deal also comes at an ideal time for holiday and winter streaming. Paramount+ carries a huge library of hit movies and TV shows, and it offers livestreams of sports, including certain NFL games, all UFC fights, and soccer matches.

Paramount+ offers two subscription tiers: Essential and Premium. Essential normally costs $8.99 a month and includes ads on all content. This plan also offers access to select Showtime series and lets you watch some NFL and UEFA Champions League games online.

The Premium plan (normally $13.99 a month) removes ads from on-demand content and gets you access to all of Showtime. Plus, you can watch CBS live for tons of sports content, and you can download content for offline viewing. 4K UHD, Dolby Vision, or HDR10 are also supported with the Premium plan.

But the best part about Paramount+’s Black Friday deal is that both plans are discounted to the same price of $2.99 a month for the first two months.

Formerly known as CBS All-Access, Paramount+ has significantly expanded its content offerings over the last couple of years. One of the biggest additions is live sports, with coverage of UEFA Champions League matches and NFL games that air on CBS, plus world-class sports commentary thanks to CBS Sports HQ.

Perhaps the biggest draw is UFC events, which are all available to stream on Paramount+ at no additional cost (Paramount+ ditched the pay-per-view model that UFC used to have on ESPN+).

Paramount+ also delivers some excellent hit series like Marshals, Tracker, Watson, NCIS / NCIS: Origins / NCIS: Sydney, 1923, The Madison, Star Trek: Starfleet Academy, and School Spirits. Movies available to stream on the platform include blockbusters like Top Gun: Maverick, The Banshees of Inisherin, 21 Jump Street, 22 Jump Street, Rocketman, The Kite Runner, Chinatown, and Spencer.
