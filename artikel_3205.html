Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Management outlines AI tools, cost cuts and a 2027 growth push.

ACCESS Newswire Inc. (NYSE American:ACCS) reported that its third quarter results were impacted by a broader industry slowdown in press release volumes, though the company improved its margin profile and cash generation. Management completed a restructuring in October to reduce its cost structure and reorganized its sales and development teams to focus on subscription growth. The company is introducing new AI-driven product tools and entry-level pricing plans to target small and medium-sized businesses. Balbirnie indicated that the company expects to return to top-line growth in 2027 through its new product roadmap and outcome-based pricing strategy.

Brian Balbirnie: Thanks, Steve. So let me tell you about SPOT. SPOT stands for Smart PR Optimized Tools, is our newest advancement to our platform suite of AI agents that check a press release before it goes out. We built SPOT for one simple reason. We have been examining the PR and IR markets for a year and have noticed that our competitors are building traditional subscriptions or AI tools around the wrong stage of the story. Looked at simply, a story has 3 stages: before distribution, the actual distribution and after distribution. All newswires today focus on steps 2 and 3 and all other communication platforms also focus on steps 2 and 3.

Even the companies in the Magic Quadrant, according to Gartner's report, specialize in the second and the third. Those are the obvious places that we all sit, which makes the moat very difficult to defend. In the first step, we believe there is significant value to us. SPOT runs in the optimized step after the release is written and before it's submitted, while the release can still change. This is where we believe there are opportunities to help improve outcomes and engagement for our customers. Distribution comes second, and our editorial teams still review every single press release.

After distribution, we will be there as well with our ACCESS Insights & Analytics, which we launched May of this year, measuring how the story performs for each of our customers. This is why we released SPOT in the first step of our agents. There are several more coming over the next few months. These agents are included with every release, ACCESS Verified, Source Verification and Release Timing Advisor. Four more are available as add-ons: LLM Visibility Scoring, Readability Score, Earnings Audit and PR Headline Optimizer. These agents are built to be outcome-based and give customers the ability to buy up when their outcomes matter most, and we plan to introduce lower-tier subscriptions with outcome-based pricing built in.

These SPOT upgrades can move gross margins from mid-70s to high -- as high as 80% on a per release basis. So as we continue to hold our volume position in the market, we have built tools that give us some additional gross margin leverage. When industry-wide growth resumes, we will be uniquely positioned to be one of the most profitable newswires in the market. As we look at the industry, we do believe that growth will resume. Gartner recently published a report calling the public relations industry to double in the coming years with AI being a significant contributor.
