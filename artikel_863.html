Mikel Arteta confirms David Raya doesn’t need surgery in huge Arsenal injury boost

Mikel Arteta has provided an injury update on David Raya after Arsenal’s first-choice goalkeeper was forced off with a shoulder problem against Newcastle.

Raya was hurt in a seemingly innocuous collision with Magpies striker William Osula from a corner, departing the field clutching his left arm in visible agony. He was replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga in the 70th minute of the 2-2 draw at St James’ Park.

Arteta confirmed after the match that the 31-year-old would have an MRI scan on Sunday and said his withdrawal was “never good news”.

Amid fears that Raya had suffered a serious injury, there were rumblings that the goalkeeper had escaped with a knock on the nerve, which would rule him out for a significantly shorter period.

He was nevertheless missing from an open training session on Monday as Arsenal prepared for their Champions League clash with Borussia Dortmund.

And speaking ahead of that match, Arteta confirmed that the Spaniard will not be fit for the visit of Dortmund but his injury is not as bad as first thought.

"He is not fit for tomorrow unfortunately but it doesn't look too serious,” Arteta said.

“We need some more tests tomorrow but it is quite encouraging with the pain that he was showing immediately after the action - we were all quite worried but it seems to be better than what we expected."

He added that Raya will not require surgery that “would have kept him out for months”.

“We expected something much worse, as he could almost not handle the amount of pain that he had,” he said. “No need for surgery which was the main fear, so let's see.”

Raya was instrumental in Arsenal ending a 22-year wait for a Premier League title last season has carried his stellar form into the current campaign, boasting seven clean sheets in 10 matches prior to Arsenal’s trip to Newcastle.

His injury came on the day Arsenal welcomed William Saliba back to their Premier League matchday squad, with the Frenchman yet to play a minute this season due to a back injury sustained at the World Cup. He was an unused substitute.

The Gunners remain without talismanic winger Bukayo Saka due to a thigh problem suffered against Bayern Munich in October, with Arteta confirming before the Newcastle game that Saka is expected to be out for another four weeks.

Martin Zubimendi is also sidelined with a hamstring problem and is not expected to return until late December.
