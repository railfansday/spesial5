France reach Nations Championship final with bonus-point Argentina win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tries: Guillard (7), Mauvaka (34), Gourgues (47,70), Gailleton (64), Duguid (68), Bielle-Biarrey (78) Conversions: Gourgues (8,35,48,69,79)

Tries: Piccardo (17), Matera (72) Conversions: Albornoz (18,73) Penalties: Albornoz (24)

France vs Argentina Nations Championship 8:10pm Saturday 21st November

France reached the Nations Championship final after beating Argentina 45-17 with seven tries on Saturday.

England's 37-12 win against New Zealand left France needing a bonus-point victory to make the November 29 final against South Africa, who topped the southern hemisphere group despite losing to Ireland.

Already leading 21-10, Emilien Gailleton got France's fourth try for the bonus point in the 64th minute against 14 men, and moments later the yellow card for Pumas prop Mayco Vivas was upgraded to red.

France finished with three more tries. Lock Tyler Duguid charged through following an attacking lineout, centre Kalvin Gourgues showed stunning pace from near halfway to make the left corner, and Louis Bielle-Biarrey completed the rout with his 31st try in 30 tests.

In the first half lock Mickaël Guillard and try-scoring hooker Peato Mauvaka crossed for France. Returning fly-half Matthieu Jalibert cleverly set up silky centre Gourgues shortly after the break for the first of his two tries.

Argentina led briefly at 10-7 for 10 minutes thanks to a converted try from centre Justo Piccardo and a penalty from fly-half Tomas Albornoz.

Jalibert's faster than expected recovery from a torn thigh allowed captain Antoine Dupont to resume at scrum-half after wearing the No 10 jersey a week ago against South Africa.

Dupont showed his class at the end with a kick down the left flank for Bielle-Biarrey's try.

Gourdes nailed five conversions as a makeshift kicker on the night.

Tributes were held before the game for Federico Martín Aramburú, the former Argentina player who died four years ago after being fatally shot in Paris.
