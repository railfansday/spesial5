HanLP (36.5K stars) edges out spaCy (34.1K) as the most-starred open source named entity recognition project on GitHub, with Flair a distant third at 14.4K. Below are all 50 NER libraries, models, datasets and labeling tools with 1,000+ stars in the Text Mining directory, ranked.

The Text Mining directory tracks 7,044 open source text mining projects on GitHub. Its Named Entity Recognition filter tags 297 of them, holding about 367K stars between them. We kept repos with 1,000+ stars (67) and removed 17 that are tagged NER but aren't NER tools: general NLP courses and interview notes, paper lists, knowledge graph tutorials, pretraining frameworks and chatbot apps. That leaves 50. Star counts are from the directory's November 20, 2026 snapshot.
