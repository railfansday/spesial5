Securitize Serves as Tokenization Provider for OKXICE's Newly Launched 24/7 Securities Trading Venue

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Regulated tokenization infrastructure supports new venue for trading securities onchain, with 113 assets confirmed for phased rollout

MIAMI, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Securitize Corp. (NYSE: SECZ), the leading platform for tokenized assets, today announced that it is serving as the tokenization provider for the newly launched OKXICE Tokenized Securities Venue (TSV), bringing its regulated securities infrastructure to a new marketplace for 24/7 trading of tokenized U.S. securities.

OKXICE, a joint venture between OKX and Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), parent company of the New York Stock Exchange, launched its TSV today with 113 assets confirmed for a phased rollout, including stocks, exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and exchange-traded products (ETPs). Confirmed issuers and asset managers include Better.com, BitGo, Circle, Coinbase, Direxion, eToro, Fidelity Investments, Robinhood, Securitize, Strategy, Strive and Vida Global. Additional issuers and assets will be announced in the future.

Securitize (NYSE: SECZ) is serving as tokenization provider for newly launched OKXICE Tokenized Securities Venue (TSV)

As the venue's tokenization provider, Securitize brings its regulated securities infrastructure to support onchain trading. Each token represents a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying share, with applicable shareholder rights and economic benefits, including dividends, voting rights and corporate actions.

"Tokenization is moving beyond the issuance of securities onchain and into the infrastructure of how capital markets operate," said Carlos Domingo, Chairman and CEO of Securitize. "The launch of OKXICE TSV is another important step toward markets that can operate around the clock while preserving the rights and protections investors expect. Securitize is helping build the infrastructure that connects traditional securities with this next generation of capital markets."

"Today, we are launching the next revolution on Wall Street. With OKXICE TSV, we're breaking down the barriers of traditional trading and bringing real securities into a 24/7 marketplace â without sacrificing the shareholder rights and protections investors expect. America built the strongest, most trusted capital markets in the world. Now we're making them faster, more transparent and more accessible. We're not just talking about the future of Wall Street. We're building it," said Governor Andrew M. Cuomo, Co-Chair of OKXICE.

"With the OKXICE TSV, eligible investors will be able to access tokenized U.S. securities around the clock, under the framework of the SEC's Innovation Exemption. We're grateful to our first-mover issuers for helping us turn this vision into a live market, and we look forward to expanding access as the venue grows," said Trabue Bland, Co-Chair of OKXICE.

Trading on OKXICE TSV occurs through automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools on Uniswap v4, built on X Layer, OKX's Ethereum Layer-2 network. Smart contracts limit trading and liquidity provision to eligible wallets. OKXICE TSV does not hold customer assets or private keys.

OKXICE TSV operates pursuant to the SEC's Innovation Exemption. Access is subject to eligibility, onboarding and geographic requirements. Participants must complete KYC, activate a CeDeFi Wallet and complete OKXICE onboarding. The venue is currently available to eligible U.S.-based OKX Inc. customers only.
