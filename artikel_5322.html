Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, raising a record $81 billion in biggest offering ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Claude maker's stock is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the symbol ANT after pricing above its $70 to $78 range

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 a share on Wednesday, and shares of its Class A common stock were set to debut on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday with the symbol "ANT."

The offering totals $81 billion, edging past the $75 billion that SpaceX collected when it went public in Junel. SpaceX's total reached $85.7 billion after its underwriters exercised their overallotment option, according to CNBC. Anthropic has given its underwriters a 30-day option to buy up to 147,000,000 more of its Class A common stock.

The offering totals 980,000,000 Class A common stock, of which Anthropic is selling 956,351,354 and certain selling stockholders are selling 23,648,646, the company said. Anthropic receives no proceeds from the selling stockholders' portion. The offering is expected to close on Nov. 20, subject to customary closing conditions.

The price tops the $70 to $78 range Anthropic had guided for a planned offering of 825 million, according to The Wall Street Journal. Investors asked to buy several times as much stock as Anthropic put up for sale. The offering should put Anthropic's fully diluted valuation at roughly $1.5 trillion.

Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are the contacts listed for copies of the final prospectus, which the company said is the only means by which the offering is being made.

Amazon $AMZN and Google $GOOGL are set to own about 28% of Anthropic in aggregate after the IPO and a concurrent private placement of about $26 billion. Microsoft $MSFT and Google are among seven companies that agreed to buy non-voting Class N stock in that placement at the IPO price. The purchase depends on the IPO being completed.

New York City Comptroller Mark Levine has criticized Anthropic's governance plans for the offering and urged the company in an Oct. 14 letter to pause the IPO. Anthropic's filing lists a single share of Class F stock held by a founders' entity that carries 50.1% of total voting power. Anthropic will be a controlled company under Nasdaq rules, and its seven co-founders, together with an independent trust, will remain in charge after the listing.
