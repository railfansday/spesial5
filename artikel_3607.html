Xbox Game Pass Gets Even More Games In November

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The second half of November is usually quiet for several reasons when it comes to new game releases–and one other big reason–but Xbox Game Pass subscribers can look forward to several new titles to close off the month--and eight games will also be leaving Game Pass on November 30.

Microsoft has announced a handful of new additions to the Game Pass library, including a kid-friendly game to kick things off and a sneak peek at some sporty fun for the first week of December. These announcements typically come in two waves, with a sneak peek split between them, so this list isn't complete yet.

The first game on the way is Paw Patrol: Dino World. Ideal for younger members of the family who definitely shouldn't be playing GTA 6 right now, this family-friendly game revolves around the PAW Patrol working together to round up lost and scared dinosaurs.

Other highlights this month include Call of the Elder Gods for a Lovecraftian puzzle-adventure, High on Life 2 joining Game Pass Premium, and Roadside Research, which is all about aliens trying to run a gas station while poorly disguised as humans.

If you're looking for action, Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 is worth checking out. All the thrill of long-distance sniping, with a surprising amount of close-quarters stealth gameplay as well. Also recommended is Motorslice, where you'll get to parkour through the ruins of a brutalist megastructure, climb massive bosses in tense fights, and hunt down living construction equipment.

Starting in December, you can get Freestyle Football 2, which is an online multiplayer soccer game built around 5v5 matches. We already have that game in the form of Rematch from Sifu developer Sloclap, but if you want a different spin on that soccer idea, take a quick look.

November has been pretty good for new entries in the Game Pass library, with the start of the month bringing the dungeon-crawling roguelike deck-building game Dungeon Crawlers to more Game Pass tiers, while subscribers also got Void/Breaker, Warhammer Survivors, and John Carpenter’s Toxic Commando. Game Pass Essential saw a big influx of new games, with several Banjo-Kazooie titles joining People Can Fly’s Gears of War prequel and the beloved Viva Piñata games.
