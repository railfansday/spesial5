Inside Anna Sitar, Josh "Bru" Brubaker's Glamorous Engagement Party

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Anna Sitar and Josh "Bru" Brubaker gave E! News an exclusive look inside their Hollywood Glam-themed engagement party: “We’re still on cloud nine!”

Anna Sitar and Josh "Bru" Brubaker are celebrating the latest chapter in their love story.

Seven months after the influencer and radio host announced that they were engaged, they shared a look into their extravagant engagement party.

“We’re still on cloud nine!” Sitar and Brubaker said in an exclusive statement to E! News. “It was so special to celebrate this next season with all of our favorite people in one place.”

The couple invited 200 of their loved ones to celebrate the milestone at the The Maybourne Beverly Hills Nov. 7, where they brought the glitz to the Hollywood Glam-themed party.

“We feel incredibly lucky to be surrounded by so much love and couldn’t have imagined a more perfect way to kick off this next adventure together,” they added. “We can’t wait to get married!”

For the occasion, Sitar got a headstart in dressing for her bridal era as she donned a white floor-length dress that featured a corset bodice and dramatic scarf, per photos exclusively obtained by E! News.

Meanwhile, Brubaker proved he was on the same wavelength as he wore a black suit with a white button-down shirt underneath.

Brubaker (better known as Bru on the Radio) revealed that he’d popped the question to Sitar last April, writing over photos of the special moment on TikTok, “BREAKING: Anna and Bru are no longer dating. Because we’re engaged!!!”

Sharing even more photos from the beach proposal, Sitar wrote on TikTok at the time, “i’m so happy you’re here (forever).”

But even before Brubaker, 30, got down on one knee, it was clear that he and Sitar, 29, who began dating in 2021, were operating on the same frequency.

After all, she confessed that the engagement wasn’t a total surprise, posting a video of herself filmed in January saying, “I feel like something’s gonna happen this year. The girlies that get it, get it.”
