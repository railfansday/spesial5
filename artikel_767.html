Ella Langley Becomes First Act to Earn Top Two of TouchTunes Frontline Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

"Choosin' Texas" was the most-played song of the third quarter of 2026 on TouchTunes jukeboxes, while Langley's "Be Her" is No. 2 on the Frontline list.

For the first time since the inception of the TouchTunes Frontline and Catalog charts in 2024, the same artist occupies the top two positions of either ranking.

Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” remains at No. 1 on the Frontline Chart for the third quarter of 2026, and Langley’s “Be Her” zooms to No. 2. The TouchTunes charts for the third quarter of 2026 track the most played songs and artists on TouchTunes jukeboxes from July 1 to Sept. 30, 2026, with the Frontline ranking inclusive of music released in the last 18 months, followed by the Catalog tally for any music that was released more than 18 months ago. The TouchTunes Artists Chart tracks the same period, combining an artist’s plays across both rankings. TouchTunes has jukeboxes in more than 60,000 locations worldwide. Its data is not factored into other Billboard charts.

“Choosin’ Texas” has spent the entirety of 2026 at No. 1 on the Frontline Chart, after debuting at No. 2 for the fourth quarter of 2025. But while the rankings have seen similarly dominant leaders — think Shaboozey’s “A Bar Song (Tipsy)” and, on Catalog, Chris Stapleton’s “Tennessee Whiskey” — Q3 of 2026 marks the first time one of those acts was able to occupy the entire top two of one of the tallies, with “Be Her” up to No. 2 on Frontline after reaching No. 3 in the second quarter of the year.

In all, Langley is featured on the Frontline survey five times, four as a lead act (her Morgan Wallen duet “I Can’t Love You Anymore” at No. 11 and “Dandelion” at No. 23) and once as the featured performer on BigXthaPlug’s “Hell at Night” at No. 6. She also appears on Catalog at No. 7 via “You Look Like You Love Me,” a duet with Riley Green. Combined, that puts her at No. 2 on the TouchTunes Artists Chart for another quarter, behind perennial No. 1 Wallen.

Following Langley, there’s quite the turnover on Frontline for the latest quarter, with nine debuts among the 25-position chart. Yung Miami’s “Spend Dat” leads the pack at No. 3, followed by Stella Lefty’s “Boston” at No. 4.

“Tennessee Whiskey” remains top dog on Catalog, with Toby Keith’s “I Love This Bar” rising to No. 2. The chart features a top-10 debut in Wallen’s “I’m the Problem,” which bows at No. 8 after graduating to Catalog from Frontline, where it had been No. 2 for Q2.

The August death of Dolly Parton drives a pair of the country legend’s songs onto Catalog in “Jolene” at No. 6 and “9 to 5” at No. 15, while Parton herself bows at No. 7 on the Artists Chart.

Yet again, it’s rock that leads the overall genre ranking for catalog titles, accounting for 37% of plays in the third quarter, down 3% from the second. Country leads frontline-specific songs with 43% of all plays.
