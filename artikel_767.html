How Alix Earle Really Feels About Ashtin Earle Dating Landon Barker

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Alix Earle shared her candid reaction after sister Ashtin Earle Landon Barker confirmed they’re dating with coordinating Halloween costumes.

Alix Earle is fully on team Ashtin Earle and Landon Barker.

The Earle Meets World star reacted to her little sister’s burgeoning romance with the son of Travis Barker and Shanna Moakler shortly after the two confirmed their romance with coordinating Halloween costumes.

“I’m obsessed with them,” Alix raved to Entertainment Tonight in an interview posted to TikTok Nov. 3. “And she's just head over heels in love and they're just the cutest. And I think he's so, so sweet.”

And it’s not just Ashtin and Landon’s chemistry that Alix was thrilled with, but also their physical appearance, adding, “I think they're just pleasing to look at together.”

“I’m just like, ‘Wow, you guys make so much sense,’” the 25-year-old mused. “‘You guys look like a variant of the same person.’ They just really bring out the best in each other.”

The relationship has been especially heartwarming for Alix as she’s had the chance to watch her younger sister be “so so happy,” raving, “It’s all you could wish for.”

Of course, if the Hot Mess podcast host had not approved, she would have been very vocal with Ashtin, especially because she feels “very protective” of the 23-year-old.

“I’m just very brutally honest with her too,” Alix admitted. “So those two don't mesh well together if there isn't someone that is up to her standards.”

Luckily, Landon has proven himself to be ever the gentleman, with Alix quipping, “We haven't had any problems so far.”

Ashtin and Landon first sparked romance in July when the Earle sisters were featured in a video alongside the 23-year-old. They continued the fuel rumors when she joined him at the premiere of his dad’s documentary Louder than Fear in August, with Landon also accompanying Ashtin and her family to the premiere of Earle Meets World in September.

Ashtin also costarred in the music video for Landon’s song “If I Ever Talk to God,” in which he appears to try and break up Ashtin’s wedding, with the support of dad Travis and stepmom Kourtney Kardashian.

The two finally confirmed their romance over Halloween weekend, dressing up as Sid Vicious from the Sex Pistols, and his girlfriend Nancy Spungen.

“I’m Nancy," Ashtin posted on Instagram Nov. 2 alongside photos of herself and Landon, “and the other guy is Sid.”

Before his romance with Ashtin, Landon had dated model Skyla Sanders for a year and Charli D’Amelio for two years. Meanwhile, Ashtin had admitted that she needed a break from dating for a bit after her 2024 split from John Summit.

“I’m done dating,” she said in a May TikTok. “Done texting boys, done being nice and pretending to entertain them. Everyone’s a freak and a weirdo and a creep. I just don’t need that in my life. I’m so good not having freaks and weirdos in my orbit.”
