For free gift cards, start with Fetch, since almost any receipt you get earns points, and add Capital One Shopping if you shop online or Microsoft Rewards if you’ll search with Bing. Save survey and game apps for spare minutes, since they pay for your time rather than for what you’d do anyway.

Whichever you pick, a legit app never makes you pay a fee, buy a gift card, or share a card’s numbers to collect what you’ve earned. The Federal Trade Commission warns that no honest business asks you to buy a gift card as payment for a prize, and that anyone who gets a card’s number and PIN can spend the money on it.

Every app here pays out in gift cards, and some pay cash too, but expect pocket money rather than a wage from any of them. You can also buy gift cards below face value for stores you use, and sell any you won’t use.

Receipt scanning apps pay on purchases you’ve already made, so the work is getting your receipts into the app after each trip.

Almost any receipt counts, from groceries to online orders, so it’s the easiest start for most people, and receipts with the brands its offers feature earn the most points.

For more information, read our full Fetch Review.

Anyone who adds its offers before a trip gets the most from it, and it’s the receipt pick that can also send cash to a bank account or PayPal.

For more information, read our full Ibotta Review.

Online shoppers who’d rather not hunt for coupon codes get the most from it, since it tries them at checkout while an eligible order earns toward a gift card.

For more information, read our full Capital One Shopping Review.

Searches you’d make anyway earn its points once Bing is your search engine, so it suits anyone willing to switch, and the points buy gift cards to stores like Target and Walmart.

For more information, read our full Microsoft Rewards Review.

Daily walkers can earn without buying anything, since its coins come from steps, collected with a tap in the app, and it trades them for gift cards from stores such as Walmart and Starbucks.

For more information, read our full CashWalk Review.

Paid survey sites pay in full only for surveys you qualify for, so joining two or three keeps enough of them coming your way.

Shoppers who also take surveys now and then get the most from it, since its shopping cash back and survey rewards add to one balance you trade for gift cards.

For more information, read our full Swagbucks Review.

Short sessions suit it, since each survey shows its points and estimated time before you start, and one that turns you away still pays a few points.

For more information, read our full Survey Junkie Review.

Its own estimate runs from $5 to $100 a month, depending on how often you take surveys and how many are available, and its loyalty program adds weekly bonus points.

For more information, read our full Branded Surveys Review.

New mobile games you start through it earn units you trade for gift cards, so it fits players who enjoy trying new ones, not anyone counting on steady pay.

For more information, read our full Mistplay Review.

Free gift cards come easiest from what you already do, a few dollars at a time. Set up a receipt app and a shopping extension first, and redeem each time your balance reaches a card you’ll use.
