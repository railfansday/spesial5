Diriyah has canceled a $191 million hotel contract and won’t say why. It’s the first Saudi hotel project known to be pulled since the Houthi strikes on Riyadh, and it comes from one of the few giga-projects still spending.

Diriyah Company, the Saudi sovereign fund-backed developer building a giga-project on the edge of Riyadh, has canceled a SAR 717 million ($191 million) contract to build a 325-room hotel, nine months after awarding it.

The contract was awarded in February to a joint venture between BEC Arabia Contracting and the Saudi subsidiary of India’s Ashoka Buildcon for construction of the One Hotel. Diriyah Company, the Saudi sovereign fund-backed developer building a giga-project on the edge of Riyadh, canceled a SAR 717 million ($191 million) contract for a 325-room hotel nine months after awarding it. Ashoka Buildcon, whose Saudi subsidiary holds 49% of the joint venture that won the One Hotel contract in February, told the Bombay Stock Exchange on Nov. 19 that Diriyah said the project was "cancelled due to internal reasons attributable to the employer," with no further detail. Ashoka said it is approaching Diriyah to understand the reasons before deciding its next step. It isn't clear whether the decision is linked to the Houthi attacks on Riyadh. Those attacks have already hit demand: Riyadh occupancy fell 34.7 percentage points year over year in October to 34.3% from 69%, according to CoStar's STR, after a missile strike on King Khalid International Airport, and Houthi strikes have since damaged landmarks in the city's financial district twice in two weeks. Diriyah has awarded more than SAR 110 billion ($29.3 billion) in contracts to date and recently secured $640 million for hospitality projects in Wadi Safar, and it has been the giga-project still spending heavily as Saudi giga-project awards fell to $10.6 billion so far this year from $13.6 billion in 2025, according to MEED. Only one hotel, Bab Samhan, is operational in Diriyah.
