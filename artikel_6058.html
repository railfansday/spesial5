Delta Holding December 2 Seattle Event To Announce New Singapore Flight—And Pick A Fight With Its Former Partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Delta Air Lines is throwing an Asian night market in Seattle on December 2, where they’ll finally reveal non-stop Singapore service. That would put Delta into a roughly 17 hour nonstop fight with Singapore Airlines, giving its customers a way to reach one of Asia’s most important business centers without changing planes in Seoul.
