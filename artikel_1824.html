Rangers’ knack for winning ugly is becoming a habit but utility man wants fewer self-inflicted scares

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

James Penrice believes Rangers are acquiring the habit of winning ugly and grinding out results as their Premiership title challenge gathers momentum, but has warned they must address their tendency to surrender control after taking commanding positions in matches.

A gritty 3-2 victory over Aberdeen at Ibrox on Tuesday night brought Rangers to within a point of Celtic at the Premiership summit and extended their unbeaten sequence to 14 games. It was also another test of their resilience, with a disjointed first-half display giving way to an emphatic response after the interval, when Rangers struck three times in a blistering 12-minute spell.

The hosts had gone in at half-time trailing to Brad Lyons’ header, prompting McInnes to make a triple substitution and reshape his side. The changes had an immediate impact, with Ryan Naderi, Djeidi Gassama and Nico Raskin all finding the net during a devastating spell early in the second half. However, Findlay Marshall’s reply ensured Rangers were unable to enjoy a comfortable conclusion to the match.

Penrice, who operated in a midfield three alongside Raskin and Dan Neil, praised the character shown by his team-mates to secure another welcome three points, but was equally candid about the frailties which threatened to undermine the victory.

“Very mixed, I think,” he said when asked for his assessment of an absorbing contest. “We conceded in the first half, but came flying out of the traps after the break. I thought we were really good for 10 or 15 minutes. We scored the third and looked comfortable, but then we switched off. We made it difficult for ourselves again, but the main thing is that we walk away with three points.”

McInnes used the interval to make tactical changes, with Penrice crediting the substitutes for injecting the energy and intensity needed to wrestle control of the contest from Aberdeen.

“We made a couple of changes. I thought the boys that came on were brilliant, that real spark and energy,” he added. “We started the second half really well, but ultimately we need to score more. We could have done that and made it really easy for ourselves, but then again we switch off and we make it difficult.

“Ultimately, it's not a bad trait to be scoring late on sometimes and winning games, but as players we want to make it as easy as possible. Again, we’ve made it hard but we’re still winning games and going on a wee bit of a run now, so hopefully that continues.”

Penrice’s versatility has become an increasingly valuable asset for McInnes. Primarily a left-back, he has also been deployed in central and wide positions as the manager continues to adapt his tactical approach to suit each fixture.

“I'm not bothered where, I’ll play absolutely anywhere to help the team,” he admitted. “It’s a massive thing for me. We work on it [in training] but I've not really trained over the last couple of days, because I was feeling a wee strain so it was more just dealing with it. It's not alien to us, we've done it numerous times this season, guys playing in different positions.

“I've played in the middle, I've played out wide, so we should all be used to it now and we are. It just takes that adjustment period on the pitch but us as players we've got a responsibility when there's a plan in place and something's not going right we fix it ourselves. We don't need to go to the side and I think that's what the gaffer is looking for. He's looking for us to take responsibility on the pitch and we've got that now.”

Rangers have little time to dwell on their latest victory, with Falkirk due at Ibrox next on Saturday, a fixture that offers McInnes’ side the chance to leapfrog Celtic and move top of the Premiership table, having closed the gap on the leaders with their comeback win over Aberdeen.

“It's a massive incentive, but the league's not won in November,” Penrice declared. “It will be a difficult game. Falkirk are playing well and they've travelled well away from home. So we'll need to be on it. We can't go into the game on Saturday like we were tonight. We need to be better. We just need to concentrate on ourselves, it's another game to tick off, one at a time.”
