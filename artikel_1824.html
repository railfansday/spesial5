Mowbray steps back as Blackburn head coach due to health issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Tony Mowbray has stepped back as Blackburn Rovers head coach due to "health issues".

Mowbray returned as Blackburn head coach back in June, having previously spent five years at the club between 2017 and 2022.

Last season, Blackburn struggled for form and were dragged into the relegation battle, eventually finishing 20th to stay in the Championship.

Under Mowbray, they sit 13th in the second tier after 15 games, with 21 points to their name so far (W6 D3 L6).

The 62-year-old had previously resigned as Birmingham City head coach in May 2024, citing health reasons for the decision, and it was later confirmed he had been diagnosed with bowel cancer.

In a statement, Mowbray said: "Unfortunately, due to health issues, I have to step back from my role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers.

"I'm immensely proud of this group of players. Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I've loved working with them.

"Thank you to my coaching staff for all your hard work and for the support you've given me. It's been a pleasure working alongside you.

"To the fans, thank you for the way you've supported me and the team this season. I've always tried to give you a team that you can be proud of. Your support has meant a great deal to me.

"I'm grateful to the owners and Suhail [Shaikh, Blackburn's chief operating officer] for giving me the opportunity to lead this great club once again.

Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds will take responsibility of first-team duties, with Blackburn facing Burnley in the league on Saturday.

Some reports suggest that Mowbray has not stepped down from the role completely, meaning Blackburn are not thought to be looking for a successor.
