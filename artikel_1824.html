Farke: Leeds ‘have created history’ with Chelsea victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Daniel Farke believes Leeds United "have created history" with their 4-1 win over Chelsea on Saturday, marking the first time the Whites have won at Stamford Bridge since 1999.

Leeds were dominant from the off despite Emmanuel Emegha netting the opener in the 25th minute, as Noah Okafor struck twice, while Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronson also got on the scoresheet.

Okafor restored parity three minutes after Emegha's opener, while Calvert-Lewin struck early in the second half from the penalty spot to complete the turnaround.

Farke's side continued to press and were rewarded for their efforts when Okafor made it a brace in the 58th minute, before Aaronson added gloss to the scoreline in stoppage time.

"The last time this club was able to win here was last century," Farke told BBC Match of the Day. "We have created history but it doesn't feel like this.

"We fully deserved to win this game and it's great for everyone connected with Leeds.

"We came here to win the game. We spoke the whole week about this and wanted to make this our game where we play on the front foot. We didn't want to allow Chelsea to control the mood.

"We need to have good fitness levels and I have smart players who understand how we want to press and play.

"We have to be more concentrated to execute what we want to do. Desire is second to none and we are competitive in the best league in the world."

Indeed, Saturday's victory was Leeds' first away to Chelsea in any competition since December 1999, ending a 10-match winless run at Stamford Bridge (D2 L8).

Farke's side also scored at least four goals away to Chelsea for the first time since a 5-2 victory in January 1970.

The result brought an end to Leeds' struggles in London, as they claimed their first Premier League victory in the capital since a 2-1 win over Brentford in May 2022, ending a 15-match winless run (D4 L11).

It was also their biggest winning margin in London since a 6-1 triumph over Charlton Athletic in April 2003.

Having lost 6-3 at Stamford Bridge in the EFL Cup earlier this season, Leeds also became just the second team to score at least three goals in two away matches against Chelsea in the same campaign, after Manchester United did so in 2012-13.

"We know our qualities, we have trained a lot and it's a big compliment on the whole team," goalscorer Okafor told BBC Match of the Day.

"We have to keep going and working hard. Dom [Calvert-Lewin] had chances, normally he would score them, and I told him at half-time that he would score. We're really happy for him.

"The support from the fans was incredible. The support is unbelievable, it's an amazing club with amazing fans."

Okafor has become something of a thorn in Chelsea's side, having now scored more Premier League goals against the Blues (three) than against any other opponent.
