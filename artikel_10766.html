Wendy’s free RPG rewards players with real food for beating bosses and is 15 hours long

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Wendy’s has officially launched its free RPG, and somehow, the fast-food chain has created a game longer than some AAA blockbusters, complete with boss battles that reward players with actual food.

When WendyQuest: Legend of the Four Corner Stones was first announced, the biggest surprise was that Wendy’s was making a full-blown, Final Fantasy-style RPG.

As it turns out, the company wasn’t messing around.

According to Wendy’s official website, the free-to-play adventure clocks in at approximately 15 hours of gameplay, featuring over 1,000 lines of dialogue.

For comparison, that’s more than double the length of some AAA campaigns such as Wolverine, which can be finished in 13 hours.

That’s an absurd amount of content for a game about a redheaded burger mascot fighting the forces of frozen beef.

Wendy’s has confirmed that players can unlock real-world food offers by defeating five bosses throughout the adventure.

Each boss provides a chance to earn a reward code redeemable through the Wendy’s app, with prizes including free medium fries, a small Frosty, 10-piece chicken nuggets, a breakfast biscuit or croissant, and even a Dave’s Single burger.

There is, however, a catch. Every food reward requires a separate purchase, and codes are available on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last. Rewards are also restricted to US residents aged 18 and older.

Still, getting a burger for defeating an RPG boss is certainly a more delicious incentive than unlocking another achievement.

As for the game itself, WendyQuest features turn-based combat, puzzles, and five food-themed regions as players attempt to save the kingdom of Savoria from the villainous Dr. Mechborg.

Players can recruit allies including Frostyna, Sir Chicken, and the Baconator, who apparently wields a bacon sword. Naturally, there’s also a collection of enemies seemingly inspired by McDonald’s, including Golden Archers.

WendyQuest launched on November 4 and is available for free on Steam, iOS, and Android.

Whether it’s actually a good RPG remains to be seen, but at least Wendy’s has figured out how to make grinding bosses feel a little more rewarding.
