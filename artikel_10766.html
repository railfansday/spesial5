F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours of GTA 6 before winning Las Vegas grand prix

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Formula 1 world champion Max Verstappen revealed he played 40 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6 ahead of his win at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

On November 19, after 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto fans were finally able to jump into GTA 6. Rockstar Games’ long-awaited release disrupted the global economy, with millions of fans taking at least one day off to get stuck into the new game.

The release also disrupted the world of sports, too. Formula One legend Lewis Hamilton revealed he was hoping to take the day off to play it, while NBA star Steven Adams said that sports leagues would “plummet” in quality as a result of GTA 6.

In the case of Max Verstappen, the Dutch F1 star is a keen gamer and regularly streams on Twitch between races. He had spoken about wanting to play GTA 6 upon release beforehand, and has finally revealed how he went about it.

“I played a lot in the evening and in the morning in Vegas,” the four-time F1 world champion explained. “I mean, I can’t go out anyways and walk around.”

Verstappen added that he didn’t play on his flight back from Las Vegas, choosing to catch up on some sleep instead.

While he often dips in and out of games – including ARC Raiders and sim racing – he can’t juggle playing too many at once. “I’m so bad when I play too many games at the same time. I can’t, I just can’t,” he added.

The 40 hours of gameplay came as he went on to win the Vegas Grand Prix as well, securing his fourth victory of the season.
