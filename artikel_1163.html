Power couple break-up rocks A-League: Ex-Brisbane Roar goalkeeper Jamie Young and glamorous lawyer Abbey John in 'acrimonious' split

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

By KARLEIGH SMITH - SENIOR REPORTER, AUSTRALIA

Published: 06:38 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 06:46 EST, 27 November 2026

An A-League power couple has quietly split, with both parties engaging lawyers.

Championship-winning goalkeeper Jamie Young and his glamorous lawyer partner Abbey John separated in August 2025 after more than five years together.

Mr Young won the A-League championship in 2022 with now-defunct Melbourne club Western United. During this time, Ms John was a fixture on the sidelines along with their newborn.

The Daily Mail understands the split is acrimonious, with both sides engaging legal representation.

Ms John, 31, and Mr Young, 41, both confirmed their separation to this publication.

The couple were together for five years, before quietly splitting in August last year

Ms John was a fixture on the sidelines along with their newborn when Mr Young was playing for now-defunct Melbourne club Western United

Brisbane-born Ms John met Mr Young in a café in the Queensland capital while he was playing for the Brisbane Roar.

After he was dropped as their first-choice goalkeeper, the couple moved to Melbourne, where he played for United before moving to Melbourne City FC, retiring last year.

Mr Young, who was born in Brisbane and is of Scottish and Sri Lankan descent, also represented England as a youth international.

He was a fan favourite among A-League supporters.

It is understood both Mr Young and Ms John relocated to Brisbane after their split.

Ms John now runs Estates Now, a wills, estate planning and probate firm, but is looking to expand into family law.

Mr Young is a high-performance consultant who mentors young prospective footballers.

In the weeks after hoisting the A-League championship trophy in 2022, Mr Young took to LinkedIn to praise his then-partner.

Mr Young was a fan favourite at the club and now mentors young aspiring footballers

'When I had the opportunity to come to Western, I asked Abbey about it and we decided to go live life on our own terms in our own adventure,' he said.

'She put her career to the side to follow in mine and we both said that we are going to make the best of this no matter what.

'I am so proud that we got to do this together and what a year we’ve had with the birth of our daughter... #ganglife.'

After winning the Western United fans' player of the season accolade in that same season, Young said: 'This award is not just for me, but also for my partner Abbey – we celebrated the birth of our daughter this year.'
