Southampton in line for big injury boost ahead of Middlesbrough clash with key pair in contention to return

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Southampton have been without their key pair over recent times. They drew 1-1 away at Sheffield United on Tuesday night with winger Lewis Dobbin getting their goal in the first-half at Bramall Lane.

Jander, 23, has sat out of the Saints’ last five outings due to a muscle injury and he hasn’t played since West Ham away in late October. He has made only three appearances in the Championship so far this season.

As for Scienza, 28, he has been struggling with his fitness and hasn’t missed three out of their last four games. The winger has played 15 matches for the Hampshire outfit in all competitions in this campaign and he has chipped in with three goals and two assists.

Speaking after their clash against Sheffield United, head coach Eckert has said they could both feature in some capacity against Middlesbrough, as per a report by the Daily Echo: “There are good chances for both. We’ll see in the next days.”

Southampton are positioned in 6th place in the table after 17 fixtures. They are in the play-offs with Stoke City, Birmingham City, West Brom, QPR and Swansea City.

It has been a frustrating season for Jander and Southampton have missed him in midfield. The ex-Germany youth international has been a key player since his transfer from FC Nürnberg in 2025.

Jander, who was on the books at Borussia Dortmund and Schalke as a youngster, has adapted well to life in England. He has recently been linked with a move to Stuttgart so the Saints could face a battle to keep hold of him down the line.

Scienza is also one of Southampton’s most prized assets and was excellent last term. He hasn’t quite managed to reach the same heights this time around yet.

However, he still has plenty of time to turn things around and help Eckert’s side push for the Premier League again. He likes to play off the left and cut in on his right foot.

Jander is a quality player for Southampton to have at their disposal in the second tier. When fit again, he will battle it out with James Ward-Prowse, Cam Bragg and Flynn Downes for a place in their starting XI.

Scienza is one of the most talented individuals in the division on his day. Getting him back will be a massive boost for the Saints.

Eckert’s men will need to be at their best to get something against Middlesbrough. Boro are top of the league ahead of Wolves and Stoke City.
