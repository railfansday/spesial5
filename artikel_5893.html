Conservatives launch $10M anti-socialism blitz after Democrats dominate youth vote

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rep.-elect Michael Alfonso tells Fox News Digital that because the American education system fails to teach the truth about Marxism, young Americans are easily duped into believing that socialism is a feasible idea.

FIRST ON FOX: A conservative organization dedicated to mobilizing young Americans to push back against socialism has launched a nationwide campaign of more than $10 million that will target at least 30 Democratic strongholds to combat the growing popularity of socialism among young Americans ahead of the 2028 presidential election.

The Young America's Foundation (YAF), led by former Wisconsin Gov. Scott Walker, announced its "Freedom Not Socialism" initiative, which will bring prominent conservative leaders, including possible 2028 contenders, scholars and activists, to schools and communities across the country to challenge the influence of democratic socialism among younger voters.

"Our mission is to explain to people that their high cost of living is due to big government," Walker told Fox News Digital, explaining that the initiative will mobilize speakers to engage directly with young Americans at colleges, high schools and other institutions.

The announcement comes after Democrats captured the majority of the youth vote in Tuesday's midterm elections. The group launched its first event in Santa Barbara, Calif., over the weekend.

"YAF's initiative is vital for the future of our country because young Americans continue to get behind these false narratives of free this and free that, and they don't realize that the reason they are in the place where they cannot afford to live is because of big government," Rep.-elect Michael Alfonso, R-Wis., said.

During his interview with Fox News Digital, Alfonso, the 26-year-old grandson of immigrants who escaped communist Cuba, argued that the American education system had failed the next generation by not teaching the history of socialist regimes.

"The American education system has failed us," Alfonso said. "Young Americans do not know the truth about communist Cuba, communist Russia and communist Venezuela. All it takes is a simple look at these countries to realize how prosperous they were before communism came, and then what happened to them after."

"If we started teaching kids real history in the schools, maybe we wouldn't have so many people that come out hating this nation," said the congressman-elect, who will become the youngest member of Congress.

The group also announced plans to host a Freedom Conference in Dallas in early 2027 to prepare young conservatives to promote free-market principles and challenge socialist policies on college campuses.

"Young Americans are going to be the majority of voters in 2028, and they are increasingly being sold the promises of socialism without understanding the devastating consequences that follow," Walker, YAF's president, told Fox News Digital.

"As socialism gains ground in cities and states across the nation, we must teach the next generation what it truly is and act today," he continued. "The next generation cannot afford to sit on the sidelines while socialism infiltrates our communities and our schools."

The organization expects prominent Republican figures and potential 2028 presidential contenders to help carry its message, including Virginia Gov. Glenn Youngkin, Sen. Ted Cruz, R-Texas, and Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis.

"I can't think there's a more challenging place than college campuses to make this decision to embrace our foundational principles that not only laid the groundwork for the greatest nation ever, but were the foundational principles that guided us when we were lost," Youngkin said at the initiative's inaugural event on Sunday.

YAF argues that young Americans are increasingly exposed to socialist ideas in schools, universities and communities without learning about what the organization describes as socialism's historical failures.

Walker attributed the growing appeal of socialism among younger voters to a lack of understanding of its consequences, arguing that many are embracing the ideology without fully considering its implications.

"Freedom, not socialism, is about informing people of those ideas," Walker said.

Among the speakers expected to participate are Daniel Di Martino, a Columbia University graduate who fled socialist Venezuela, and Yeonmi Park, a North Korean defector who has spoken publicly about her experiences under the country's communist regime.

Walker said their firsthand accounts will help demonstrate why he believes socialist policies, including those championed by progressive leaders in Seattle and New York, "do not work."

The initiative is expected to expand throughout 2027 as conservatives seek to regain support among younger voters ahead of the 2028 presidential election.
