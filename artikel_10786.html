KPop Demon Hunters announces world tour bringing HUNTR/X to arenas in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

KPop Demon Hunters is heading on a world tour in 2027, bringing HUNTR/X, the Saja Boys, and the Netflix movie’s hit songs to arenas around the globe.

Fans who have spent months singing along to ‘Golden’ will soon get to join Rumi, Mira, and Zoey for a concert experience that puts the audience in the middle of their demon-hunting battle.

On November 13, Netflix announced the Golden World Tour, which combines animation with live performers, stunts, and audience participation to bring the film’s fictional K-pop group to the stage.

Alongside performances of the soundtrack and its choreography, fans will help HUNTR/X seal the Honmoon, the magical barrier that protects their world from demons. Rival group the Saja Boys and fan-favorite Derpy Tiger will also appear.

The tour begins in North America in June 2027 before reaching the UK and Europe that fall, while stops across Asia Pacific and Latin America will also be announced at a later date.

More than 150 performances are planned across nearly 50 cities throughout 2027 and 2028, giving fans plenty of opportunities to see the animated trio’s arena show.

While we currently don’t know how the performers will be displayed, HUNTR/X and their Saja Boy rivals will likely take inspiration from Vocaloid Hatsune Miku. The popular twin-tailed star is famously known to use screen projections and hologram technology to bring her to life alongside a live band.

“KPop Demon Hunters has transformed from a breakout hit into a generational phenomenon,” Netflix chief marketing officer Marian Lee said. “This fandom was never about a single moment, and the Golden World Tour is how we keep it going, bringing that ‘Takedown’ energy to arenas around the world, where fans can sing, dance, and help HUNTR/X seal the Honmoon.”

Fans hoping to attend can buy tickets from November 20, 2026, at 10am (local time), with Mastercard presale access opening November 18 at the same time. Those who register through the official tour website can also access the For the Fans presale on November 19 at 10am (local time), with the site listing the tour’s dates and locations.

Limited VIP packages will also be available, with potential extras including premium seats, onstage photo opportunities, preshow parties, and exclusive merchandise.
