Nocturnal hunter

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Orion the Hunter rises over a fog-filled valley, with ruddy Betelgeuse visible at left and the Orion Nebula (M42) just poking above the horizon. The latter, appearing as a reddish patch just beneath the Hunter’s belt, is stellar nursery about 1,270 light-years away.

15-second exposure at f/2.0 and ISO 3200 with a Canon 6D and a Sigma 50mm lens
