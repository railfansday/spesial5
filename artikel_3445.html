Every Show Premiering on ABC in Winter 2026–2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

September and October mark the end of summer, the return to school, and the premieres of network TV shows, either returning with new seasons or introducing their inaugural ones. But there's also a winter schedule that helps keep the momentum going after the holiday break. Some shows come back at the end of the year or beginning of the new year, through March.

Many popular series that came back during the 2026 fall season return in winter after their midseason holiday breaks. For ABC, this includes shows like Grey's Anatomy, 9-1-1, Abbott Elementary, R.J. Decker, and Scrubs. But there's plenty more to watch, too, with shows debuting their long-awaited new seasons and a couple of brand-new titles to add to your watch list.

Did you even know American Idol is still on the air? Yes, the reality singing competition is still going. Technically, American Idol did end in 2016, but it left a huge hole in the primetime TV lineup and came back in 2018, complete with Ryan Seacrest as host once again. There has been a rotating list of judges, but the latest, Carrie Underwood, Luke Bryan, and Lionel Richie, all return this season.

As usual, you'll see aspiring singers from all walks of life audition in hopes of making their dreams of becoming a star come true. It happened to people like Kelly Clarkson, Chris Daughtry, and Underwood herself, after all. It's the perfect show to watch with the kids or put on when "nothing's on" to enjoy in the background.

One of the most endearing characters in a police procedural of late, quirky accent and all, Will Trent (Ramón Rodríguez) of Will Trent has a special knack for solving cases as a GBI Special Agent in Atlanta. He can look at a crime scene and piece together what happened in his head. It's a byproduct of his traumatic upbringing as an orphan and foster child as well as his dyslexia.

Season 5 of the perfect mystery show picks up in the aftermath of an immense tragedy that rocked Will's friends and co-workers. There will likely be a lot of changes to the dynamic as well as new characters. Alexandra Metz is set to play the new GBI Deputy Director, Birdie Palmer. Fans can't wait to find out what's next for Will and others, like Angie (Erika Christensen), who have suffered tremendous loss.

The return of this crime comedy drama, about a high-intellect single mom who helps the local police force's homicide division solve crimes, is one of the most anticipated of the season given that High Potential has historically premiered in September. It was pushed forward this year to make room for Scrubs and allow the season to flow more nicely instead of going on hiatus through the holidays. But fans are on edge given that Season 2 ended with a massive revelation and cliffhanger.

While the story in one of the best network procedurals on TV right now continues to function as a case-of-the-week procedural, the overarching narrative of what happened to Morgan's (Kaitlin Olson) first husband, Roman, is coming together. And things are getting dangerous, potentially deadly. High Potential's return is so anticipated that ABC is premiering a special episode on February 14, 2027, immediately following the Super Bowl. Airing it outside its regular timeslot, and in such a coveted space, suggests ABC has high hopes that High Potential will return strong.

For lovers of true crime docuseries and news programming, ABC has a new investigative docuseries coming next year that sounds intriguing. Surviving a Scandal explores various situations when a famous (or not-so-famous) figure was marred by controversy that turned their lives and careers upside down. Thanks to the media and especially the tabloids, this can cause massive damage while also potentially revealing secrets and lies.

There's no shortage of news cases to cover for a show like this or individuals to highlight, from actors to musicians, politicians and other public figures. Already, we know a few people who will be covered in this series, including Milli Vanilli, Octomom, and Joey Buttafuoco. The idea is to rehash what happened during the biggest controversies in their lives and look at how these situations impacted their sense of self. I'll be tuning into this one for sure.

The Tim Allen and Kat Dennings sitcom returns for its third season to delight fans with its dark yet fun humor. In Shifting Gears, Matt Parker (Allen) has lost his wife and his estranged and now divorced daughter Riley (Dennings) moves in with her two daughters. None of this sits well with the gruff and surly Matt, but family is family, after all.

With this being its ninth season, The Rookie becomes the third-longest-running ABC series. The police procedural stars Nathan Fillion as John Nolan, a 40-year-old divorcee who decides that part of his new lease on life will involve making a big move from Pennsylvania to Los Angeles and becoming an LAPD officer. Being the oldest rookie on the force has its own challenges, but it's relatable to any industry, too.

Inspired by a true story, The Rookie, one of the best network procedurals, has a loyal following of fans who will undoubtedly be excited to tune in and cozy up every Wednesday night with new episodes. It's all about day-to-day police work, but you're seeing it from the lens of a man who, at times, feels out of place. But most importantly, he's determined to prove he can do this and handle anything the job throws at him, no matter his age.

You get a full two hours of The Rookie with the original series serving as the lead-in to this new spin-off, The Rookie: North. Set in Pierce County, it's a similar story with Alex Holland (Jay Ellis) being in a situation somewhat like John's. Following a violent home invasion, Alex decides that he's going to finally prove to himself and others that he can find purpose by joining the local police department.

The cases and scenery will be different given that the rural forest is not far away. Plus, being in such a secluded area, the officers can't easily call for back-up when something goes awry. We don't yet know much about this show, but Ellis will be joined by Chris Sullivan (This Is Us), Janet Montgomery (New Amsterdam), and Karen Fukuhara (The Boys), all veterans of the small screen.
