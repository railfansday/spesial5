The Best Tech Deals From the Sam's Club Early Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Retailers are churning out early Black Friday deals already, including the likes of Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and others. If you're waiting around for Friday, Nov. 27 to do all of your shopping, you might miss out on some deals and unnecessarily make the day more stressful than it needs to be. If you're a Sam's Club member, you have access to many early Black Friday deals worth checking out. I spent some time combing through their website and rounded up my favorite tech deals below.

The Bose SoundLink Flex SE is a portable Bluetooth speaker with an IP67 waterproof rating. The speaker even floats in the water, so you can bring it with you into the pool. While it doesn't offer full EQ customization in its companion app, the sound has rich bass depth and clear highs out of the box. It also features a speakerphone, allowing you to take calls and speak with it. It's light and portable, and the Bluetooth 5.3 supports multipoint connectivity and SBC, AAC, and aptX codecs. You can get the Bose 2nd Generation SoundLink Flex SE for $89 (originally $139).

Skullcandy makes fun speakers and headphones, although they're not designed for audiophiles (their sound signature is very bass-heavy). Their Skullcandy Stomp XL Max is their attempt at making a portable party speaker. It comes with two wireless mics, making it a great option for karaoke. It holds 12 hours of battery life (up to 20 hours with certain settings), has an IPX5 water resistance rating, and has LED lights that sync to the music. You can get it for $99 (originally $199).

Getting a soundbar can exponentially improve anyone's entertainment system, but a 5.1 system with rear speakers and a subwoofer takes it to the next level. The Samsung HW-Q65F 5.1 Channel Soundbar is $299 (originally $399), and it's a wireless setup that will keep the harmony of "too many cables" in the house. It supports Dolby Atmos and DTS Virtual:X to give you a 3D audio feel, and it has a dialogue feature that will let you hear the whispers.

Gaming monitors can get expensive quickly, but you can get decent options on a budget. A great example is the 31.5-inch MSi gaming monitor selling for $129 (originally $179). It has a 250Hz refresh rate and a 1ms response time to make gaming smoother. There's also AMD FreeSync Premium to get rid of tearing and stuttering.

These kinds of sales are always a good time to upgrade laptops. If you're looking for a powerful computer that is well-rounded, consider the 16-inch HP OmniBook 7 Flip going for $699 (originally $899). This laptop comes with an Intel Ultra 5 processor, 512GB of SSD, and 16GB of RAM (which, honestly, is the minimum anyone should be buying in 2026).

If you need a bigger screen, the 17.3-inch HP OmniBook 3 is a good choice for a bit less money. It comes with an Intel Core Ultra 7 processor, 512GB of SSD, and 12GB of RAM. Part of the bigger screen means a bigger keyboard, so if you deal with numbers a lot, you'd likely appreciate the numeric pad.

If you want your laptop strictly for gaming, the ASUS TUF F16 Gaming laptop is $1,149 (originally $1,399). With an Intel Core 7 processor and the RTX 5060 graphics card, you'll be well set up to tackle heavy games. It comes with a 165Hz refresh rate, Advanced Arc Flow fans and vents to keep your machine cool, and 16GB DDR5 RAM to keep your loads short.

Budget TVs get big discounts during Black Friday, and the 58-inch Vizio QLED TV is one of them. You can get one for $178 (originally $298), and it supports Dolby Vision HDR for better color and contrast. The operating system is the Vizio OS, but of course, you can always change it with a TV dongle if you prefer a different one.

If you prefer to splurge for a better QLED but still get a good deal, the 65-inch Samsung QN90H QLED TV at $1,197.99 (originally $1,497.99) is a great option. It upscales lower-resolution pictures to 4K with AI. It works for gaming with its 144Hz refresh rate, and because the operating system is Tizen OS, it comes with Samsung TV Plus, which brings over 2,700 free channels. The TV also comes with Dolby Atmos, so you can hear audio with a 3D effect as the audio follows objects around the display.

A 100-inch TV makes a statement: You take your entertainment system seriously. The 100-inch Vizio QLED TV is a budget-friendly option with a $300 discount, currently going for $1,198 (originally $1,498). This TV supports Dolby Vision HDR for more vivid images and has hundreds of free channels with the Vizio OS.

Nowadays, both large retailers and small businesses compete for Black Friday shoppers, so you can expect practically every store to run sales through Monday, November 30, 2026. The “best” sales depend on your needs, but in general, the biggest discounts tend to come from larger retailers who can afford lower prices: think places like Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and Home Depot. You can find all the best sales from major retailers on our Black Friday coverage.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, November 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday,  November 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early, and we expect sales to wind down by December 2, 2026.

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.
