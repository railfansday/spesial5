Fortnite Adds New Way to Get the Pirate Shop Token

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Increase your chances of unlocking the free Item Shop in Fortnite with this new method

The Pirate Shop Access token is the most sought-after item in Fortnite history, guaranteeing a total of four weeks of free Item Shop goodies. Nearly every player has been actively searching for it, opening as many chests as they can per match, even if it costs them their in-game lives.

We've officially reached the season's halfway point. Fortnite Treasure Hunters is coming to an end on Dec. 5, which means players have about two weeks left to find the Pirate Shop token before it's gone for good. Gamers have come up with dozens of routes to optimize their search, but it still takes a long time because Pirate Shop token drop rates are extremely low. However, they have gone up considerably because treasure has just been added to the loot pool in Respawn Rumble.

This change makes searching much easier for three reasons. As the name indicates, players respawn until the end of the match in Respawn Rumble, which means they don't have to repeatedly start a new match, lowering the stakes of dying to the storm and enemies. Also, this is a big team game mode, which means half of the players on the island cannot kill you, as they're part of your team. Lastly, there are a maximum of 16 players per match, so as long as you steer clear of areas with other players, you will be able to loot an area by yourself, greatly increasing your loot yield.

At the start of the season, some players were able to find treasure in game modes besides Battle Royale. However, Epic quickly noticed this mistake and fixed it. It is unknown whether being able to find the Pirate Shop token and other treasure in Respawn Rumble was an intentional change, so players should make the most of it just in case it's patched.

However, beware: while you're technically part of an 8-player team, your squad will consist of a maximum of four players. If anyone on your team outside your squad finds the token, you will not receive it — you or one of your squadmates must find it for the entire squad to get the reward.

To maximize your chances of finding the Pirate Shop token, enter a Respawn Rumble with a squad of four players (don't be afraid to play squad fills), land in a high-loot area (even if it's in the storm), open every chest container, and only grab healing items so you can survive longer in the storm. Rinse and repeat. Good luck.
