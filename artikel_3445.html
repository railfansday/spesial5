GTA 6 drops on November 19, and you can start pre-loading it on November 10. Rockstar’s upcoming open-world epic will have a huge 212GB install size, which could take a long time to download on a slow internet connection. However, Spectrum wants to give its customers the chance to download the game faster.

From November 9 through 30, Spectrum will give a free Gig Speed Lift to eligible customers. This boosts them to Spectrum Internet 1 Gig. Those who already have 1 Gig will get a bump up to 2 Gig. “With Spectrum Internet Gig, customers can start playing Grand Theft Auto VI in less than 30 minutes, so game day is for cruising, not downloading,” says Spectrum in a blog post.
