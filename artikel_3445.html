Chris Hemsworth’s ‘Heat’ Replacement Officially Steals a New Prime Video Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The last few years have been absurdly busy for Chris Hemsworth, who is now over three years removed from his last solo Thor movie. Hemsworth reunited with Thor: Ragnarök director Taika Waititi for Thor: Love and Thunder, but the film came out of the gates with poor reviews as many critics and fans felt it leaned too much into the silliness and less into the seriousness of Hemsworth’s character.

Hemsworth will return as Thor later this year in Avengers: Doomsday, but he is not yet confirmed for a role in the follow-up, Avengers: Secret Wars, which is coming to theaters in 2028. There is growing speculation that Hemsworth’s Thor will be one of the victims of Doctor Doom in the film, which would be a good way to establish the high stakes for the team-up movie.

Between this myriad of Marvel releases, though, Chris Hemsworth is still finding the time to star in new projects. Earlier this year, he reunited with his long-time Marvel co-star Mark Ruffalo for Crime 101, the new Heat replacement film also starring Halle Berry and Nick Nolte. The film left theaters after grossing only $72 million at the global box office, falling well short of its estimated break-even point calculated using its $90 million budget, but it has since gone on to become one of the most-watched movies of the entire year on Prime Video. Crime 101 is still hanging around in the top 10 in a few countries, months after its debut, and the film has now been in the top 10 for over 200 days on the platform. Barry Keoghan also has a small role in Crime 101.

Your nemesis does not want to beat you. He wants to know you completely, and he is patient enough to wait for it. The frightening part is how good the conversation is.

Your nemesis is not after money, territory or revenge. He is after a demonstration, and you are the person he has chosen to prove his point on.

Your nemesis respects you, which sounds flattering until you work out what it means. He chose you because nobody else was worth his time.

Your nemesis has no personal interest in you whatsoever, and that is precisely what makes him unsurvivable. You are not a rival. You are a stop on the way.

The most popular show in the world right now is the second season of The Terminal List, starring Chris Pratt. To take the #1 spot, The Terminal List had to dethrone Carrie, the new horror thriller show inspired by the novel of the same name by Stephen King. Both the flagship Reacher show and its first spin-off, Neagley, are also sitting handily in the Prime Video top 10 right now. One of the most-watched movies in the world on Prime Video at the time of writing is Love of Your Life, which stars Lanterns veteran Aaron Pierre and The Night Agent’s Gabriel Basso.
