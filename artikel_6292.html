Exclusive First Look: Beautiful New United Club In Washington Dulles E Concourse (Photos)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

I’ll say more about this tomorrow, but as a former DC resident and long-term United traveler, the United Clubs and the “temporary” terminal itself (that is older than I am…) have always been endearing, but absolutely uncompetitive in terms of facilities and amenities that make an airport or lounge great.

That’s changing with the opining tomorrow of the E Concourse expansion and a brand new United Club that is even more beautiful to me than the new Houston lounge.

I’ll let my photos do most of the talking, but here are the key facts about United Airlines’ latest lounge.

The entrance is my favorite part of the lounge: it feels like you are in a DC Metro station…such a thoughtful touch. Compared to the cramped, dark, dungeon-like lounges in the C and D Concourses, this really is like night and day.

This is a bight new chapter for United and for IAD. It has been an eventful day here and I can’t wait to share thoughts on the terminal expansion itself and conversations with some of the folks who made this possible.
