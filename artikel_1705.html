Report: Liverpool ready to sign one of the 'strongest' defenders in the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liverpool's defence still needs a decent amount of work doing to it.

Virgil van Dijk, Kostas Tsimikas and Joe Gomez could all end up exiting Anfield for nothing by the end of the season with all three players having their contracts expire in June.

Obviously, fans would rather keep Van Dijk around for as long as possible but, the other two are less likely to stick around.

Tsimikas has been given a more regular role this term but, since he's failed to meet expectations and has performed well below the standards expected of him, it doesn't really feel like there's any sort of future left for him at Liverpool, even as a back-up.

All this change could go one of two ways for Liverpool but, with the latest links to emerge from L4, it feels like these players leaving could be good for the Reds and their future.

Liverpool have re-entered the race to sign Alessandro Bastoni

Centre-backs are going to be a bit of a problem for Liverpool next season.

Right now, Van Dijk and Jeremy Jacquet are their preferred starting options with others such as Ronald Araujo and Gomez filling in when needed.

So far, this system has worked but, with the injury history of both back-ups taken into account, it's unclear how long that's going to last.

The fact that neither of their futures are guaranteed either is another worry that Andoni Iraola and co. will probably have.

This lack of knowing has led to questions being asked about the future of Liverpool's defence and now, it seems like their interest in one of their former targets has been reignited.

"Alessandro Bastoni’s representatives are carrying out due diligence on his options ahead of 2027," TEAMtalk wrote.

"Indeed, in addition to Chelsea, Liverpool have also been kept informed of the situation, although they are still weighing up their longer-term plans at centre-back and see the position as much less of a priority with Jeremy Jacquet arriving last summer and already showcasing his talents.

"The Reds also have Giovani Leoni and Ifeanyi Ndukwe coming through, with the teenage pair both considered hot prospects for the future.

"Manchester City and Manchester United have featured in conversations too around Bastoni, though for different reasons it remains unclear whether either club will be in a position to pursue a deal next summer."

Alessandro Bastoni is the first real sign that Virgil van Dijk could be leaving

One thing that Liverpool would lost if Van Dijk exited Anfield would be their main leader.

Ever since Jordan Henderson left, the towering Dutchman has been Liverpool's captain.

During that time, he's helped lead his men to another Premier League title and has developed his leadership skills massively.

Obviously, there is a long list of suitors waiting to sign him but, none of those rumours really had any standing.

That is until one of the "strongest central defenders around" became a target again.

The Italian may not be Inter Milan's captain or vice captain but, thanks to his experience and his ability to control the back line, he's one of the senior figures in the dressing room.

Recently, the Reds have been signing a lot of youngsters to prepare for their future but, with Bastoni, he's far more experienced and could plug the gap left by Van Dijk much better than someone such as Giovanni Leoni.

Right now, it's unclear what the future has in store for anyone but, if Van Dijk is going to leave, Bastoni is almost certainly going to be Liverpool's best option to explore.
