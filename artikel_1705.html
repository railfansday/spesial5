MN: Torriani ‘warming up’ as Milan await Maignan’s MRI results

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With Mike Maignan suffering an injury on Sunday night, AC Milan are having to prepare in case he’s out for this weekend’s game.

The international break was going really well for Milan, until the 45th minute of France’s game against Turkey. Maignan was forced to be substituted off, after a nasty collision, and he’s now set to have an MRI tomorrow.

Early suggestions are that he’s struggling with an issue with his hip or his thigh, so some more information is required there. Ultimately, though, the Rossoneri are wanting information on whether he’ll be facing some time on the sidelines.

As MilanNews writes, whilst fans – and Amorim – wait for the results of the MRI tests, plans have to be made in case the result is not positive. The goalkeeper is central to the Portuguese’s plans, so not having him available this weekend would be far from ideal.

However, he has already started to call upon Lorenzo Torriani, who is ‘warming up’. The Italian is the only alternative to Maignan currently, with Pietro Terracciano still unavailable and he’ll hope to come back with a stronger performance than his last.

He started in the 2-0 defeat to Ferencvaros and also played in the defeat to Bournemouth. So, he will be expected to improve on his record for the season.
