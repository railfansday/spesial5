GdS: Predicted XIs for Milan vs. Frosinone – Pavlovic and Cisse not included

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan will hold their penultimate training session today in view of Sunday’s return to Serie A action with a game against Frosinone.

La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) writes that the Milan squad will return to full strength with the arrival of the final players returning from international duty, allowing Ruben Amorim to begin detailed preparations for Sunday’s 18:00 CET clash against Frosinone at San Siro.

Mike Maignan is expected to start in goal. He took part in a portion of yesterday’s training session alongside the other goalkeepers. The Frenchman’s recovery is progressing well, and Pietro Terracciano – who has been out for about 40 days with a shoulder issue – could make the bench.

Strahinja Pavlovic also participated in part of the group training and will be included in the squad for the match against the Ciociari. However, the Serbian defender is not yet at 100%, so unless his condition improves quickly, he is likely to start on the bench.

Sankhoun Diawara would thus take the left centre-back spot, with either Davide Bartesaghi or Pervis Estupinan covering the left flank. The defence will be completed by Koni De Winter and Mario Gila, with the Spaniard operating on the right again.

In midfield, Yunus Musah, Luka Modric and Adrian Rabiot – who could play in a more advanced, attacking-midfield role – are expected to be the starting trio. In attack, Diego Moreira and Goncalo Ramos are guaranteed starters, with Christian Pulisic currently favoured over Alphadjo Cisse.

Predicted Milan XI (4-2-3-1): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi; Musah, Modric; Pulisic, Rabiot, Moreira; Ramos.

Predicted Frosinone XI (4-2-3-1): Palmisani; Oyono, Calvani, Monterisi, Terzic; Calo, Masini; Ghedjemis, Schmid, Kvernadze; Birligea.
