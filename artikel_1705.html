Starting with the hosts, while Nottingham Forest might have entered November’s international break with a sensational 3-1 victory at home against Manchester City, Oliver Glasner’s men came immediately crashing back down to earth last weekend. Marking their return from the fortnight interval with a deserved 2-0 defeat away at a high-flying Bournemouth, the former Crystal Palace boss has failed to offer any kind of stability since arriving at the City Ground. With just their goal difference keeping them outside the Premier League’s relegation zone, Nottingham Forest have won just a pair of their first 12 appearances across all competitions. Likewise, along with mustering just a sole clean sheet since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Glasner’s side have also drawn a blank in five of their first six domestic showdowns at the City Ground.

Missing out against Bournemouth last weekend after sustaining a recent hip injury, Nottingham Forest are once again sweating over the fitness of one-time Chelsea youngster Callum Hudson-Odoi. Likewise, yet to make a maiden appearance this season, Italian defender Nicolo Savona remains on the sidelines with a long-term knee injury.

Completing a big-money summer move from Stamford Bridge, Liam Delap will lead the line on Friday night and be looking to add to his three Premier League strikes this season. Enjoying what has been a real resurge at the City Ground this year, ex-Manchester City starlet James McAtee will start in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, while there might have been plenty of excitement around west London following Xabi Alonso’s arrival over the summer, Chelsea suddenly find themselves at the heart of a public struggle. Marking their return from November’s international break with a woeful performance at Stamford Bridge as they slumped to a 4-1 drumming at home against historic rivals Leeds, the former Real Madrid boss has some mounting pressure on his shoulders in SW6. Likewise, heading into the fortnight interval with a concerning 1-0 loss away at relegation scrappers Sunderland, Chelsea have now lost four of their previous five appearances across all competitions. Despite searching for a much-welcomed boost on Friday night, Alonso’s downbeat squad fell to a 3-1 defeat when they last met Nottingham Forest for a Premier League showdown back on May 4th.

While Moises Caicedo might have limped off in the first-half against Leeds last weekend after falling awkwardly, Chelsea are confident that the former Brighton star will be able to make an immediate return here. Yet to feature since the middle of October, the Blues are once again expected to be without leading marksman Joao Pedro on Friday night.

Sustaining another knee injury back in September, club captain Reece James remains on the sidelines. Registering seven direct goal contributions in his 11 Premier League appearances so far this season (five goals, two assists), big-money summer recruit Morgan Rogers could play a leading role in the final third.

Blown away at Stamford Bridge last weekend as they marked their return from November’s international break with a nightmare 4-1 drumming against historic foes Leeds, Chelsea suddenly find themselves at the heart of a real crisis.
