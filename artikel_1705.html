Kobbie Mainoo issues stark Manchester United relegation warning: ‘No one is coming to save us’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kobbie Mainoo has warned Manchester United that there is “no one coming to save us” after defeat by Liverpool left the club mired in the relegation zone.

The Red Devils found themselves on the back foot almost immediately at Anfield as Leny Yoro was dismissed in the eighth minute for taking out Alexander Isak. Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resultant free kick and United never recovered, going on to lose 4-0.

It was a wretched start to Mauricio Pochettino’s time as the club’s manager. His debut is the worst since Lal Hilditch lost by the same margin to Bolton in 1926.

United were already in the drop zone as they headed to Anfield, but have slipped from 18th to 19th with this defeat, having taken eight points from their first 11 matches. They are one of two clubs in England’s top four divisions to have won only one match this season.

Academy product Mainoo is fully aware of the club’s peril, and has issued a stark warning to his team-mates that they must take initiative if they are to dodge the drop.

“It’s all about character now,” he told MUTV.

“We’re at the lowest we’ve ever been and no one is coming to save us.

“There’s no helping hands coming, so we’ve got to work together and lean on each other and keep working and hopefully it’ll come good.”

Pochettino will hope to pick up a first win as United boss when they visit Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday evening, but he will be all too aware of the tricky run of Premier League fixtures he has waiting for him back in England. United host Brentford next, before visiting Newcastle.

Mainoo continued: “(Anfield) is a tough place to come, but I can’t fault the boys on their fight. We have to take the pain from the game and move on with it.

“The games are still coming thick and fast, so we don’t have time to dwell on it.

“We’re all committed. No one is going anywhere and we’re all leaning on each other.

“We all know what we have to do and we all know with the new manager, we need to get behind him, keep working in training. That’s all we can do.”
