GTA 6 Made 2 Million XBOX Players Lie About Their Nationality

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a game about criminals, which might explain why millions of XBOX players technically broke their terms of service and lied about their nationality, all just to play the game a little earlier.

There’s no denying that Grand Theft Auto 6 is the biggest game of 2026, selling millions of copies ahead of launch, and quickly becoming tied for the best-reviewed game of all time, matched only by the original version of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on the Nintendo 64. As such, millions of people were waiting at midnight on November 19, when the game became available in their region.

Rockstar's verified Douyin account is promoting GTA 6 to Chinese players despite the game lacking regulatory approval for official mainland China sales.

Or not. It turns out that millions of XBOX players jumped the queue and decided to lie to boot up Grand Theft Auto 6 early. This is thanks to a trick that’s much easier to pull off on XBOX than PlayStation 5, prompting lots of people to change their nationality.

A report by Ampere Analysis (with thanks to Eurogamer) has revealed that over two million XBOX players used the “New Zealand trick” to play Grand Theft Auto 6 early. 100,000 PlayStation 5 users did something similar, but that method was more difficult, which is why the number is much smaller.

The New Zealand trick has been used for many big releases on XBOX systems in the past. Because of time zone differences, most video games launch in New Zealand first. If a player changes their XBOX system settings to New Zealand, then they can access the country’s version of the Microsoft Store, and in the case of Grand Theft Auto 6, they were able to play it over twelve hours earlier.

The PlayStation 5 version of the trick involves creating a separate account with billing information, which is a massive pain in the neck, which explains why only 100,000 people bothered. The New Zealand XBOX trick literally takes a minute to pull off and is easy to revert afterward.

Some fans were concerned that Microsoft would ban accounts that did the New Zealand trick. After all, it breaks the Microsoft Store's terms of service. However, many official XBOX social media accounts playfully encouraged people to do it, as it was essentially a free win for the brand, at a time when it badly needed one. As it stands, there are reports of bans, even now that the trick isn’t necessary, so there's no need for anyone to worry about switching flags.

While the New Zealand trick is well-known among fans, and is something many gaming outlets will gleefully inform the public about ahead of a big release, it’s usually only done by a small audience. Grand Theft Auto 6 is such a massive game that more people than usual temporarily became Kiwis, giving them an early chance to play.

Now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is out, it’s unclear what the next big game will have a similar effect. 2027 has some massive hits, but it would be hard to claim any will be as important as Grand Theft Auto VI. If Persona 6 manages to launch in 2027, then the JRPG audience on XBOX might all take a journey to Mordor, but it’ll be unlikely that millions will do so.
