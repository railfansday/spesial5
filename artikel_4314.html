Two years after giving the Forester a proper hybrid system, Subaru has finally added the electrically assisted powertrain to the rugged Wilderness edition.

As with the rest of the Forester Hybrid lineup, the new Wilderness Hybrid is powered by a 2.5-liter boxer four-cylinder engine and an electric motor for a combined 194 horsepower.

A bit of software tinkering for the hybrid system means more torque from the electric motor with the off-road-oriented X-Mode engaged.

In addition to providing better fuel economy and smoother acceleration, we found the electric drive motor to be helpful off the beaten path.

As with the nonhybrid Wilderness, the hybrid version has 9.3 inches of ground clearance.

Turning on X-Mode automatically enables hill-descent control and turns on the 180-degree front-facing camera.

Pricing for the 2027 Wilderness Hybrid starts at $42,020, just $1000 above the nonhybrid.
