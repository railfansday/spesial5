GTA Definitive Edition Trilogy 67% Off On Steam

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

GTA 6 arrives in less than two weeks, and to celebrate, Rockstar is holding a huge sale on the one platform where you won't be able to buy it this month—Steam.

Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition is 67% off right now, meaning you can grab GTA 3, Vice City, and San Andreas for just $19.79.

GTA 6 is reportedly coming to PC within four months of launch.

The Definitive Edition is infamously the worst way to play the original trilogy, but unfortunately, the original trilogy has been delisted, so there's no alternative on Steam. Unless you have $80–200 to spend on keys.

Alternatively, you can pick up the best Grand Theft Auto game for $5.99 as GTA 4: The Complete Edition is 70% off until November 19, the day that GTA 6 launches.

Time to pre-order GTA 6. Here's everything you need to know before securing your copy ahead of release.

PC players might not be able to visit Leonida, but they can at least return to Liberty City and go bowling with Niko and his cousin. Bizarrely, however, GTA 5 is not included in the sale.

Everything still has that glossy, plastic sheen, with old characters feeling more like Fortnite models than their semi-realistic predecessors, but the game isn't nearly as bad as it was at launch.

The originals are still the best way to play these classics, but if you're hankering for a pre-GTA 6 marathon and have no other option, things could be a lot worse.
