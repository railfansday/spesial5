Casualties: Unknown release countdown — Exact date and time

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Casualties: Unknown is coming soon to PC players worldwide, and you won’t need to wait long to try out this psychological horror simulator developed by Orsoniks. The game is a mishmash of an exploration and survival title blended with a touch of horror.

Before we can begin our adventures, let’s find out when the game goes live.

Casualties: Unknown goes live on Monday, Nov. 9. The release date was announced earlier by the developers. With less than 24 hours left for the game to go live, I am not expecting any change. The following countdown will expire once the game goes live.

The countdown is based on the following times.

Casualties: Unknown introduces you to a harsh world where you’ll need to adapt and descend. While going down, injuries are unavoidable. The game follows a simulated limb and cardiovascular system, and you’ll have to bandage yourself, fight infections, and stabilize the blood loss.

To survive, you’ll need to use different tools that you craft with various resources. These tools will allow you to repair yourself and go deeper into the cave system. Each corner will have its own risk, and being careless can lead to your demise.

Your runs will also be affected by your character traits, and there are three different characters to pick from. With 11 different levels to walk through and over 300 items to find, there will be plenty to unearth as you go deeper into the alien planet’s core.
