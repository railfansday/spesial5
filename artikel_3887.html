Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Super Evil Megacorp has released the “Shell Squad Co-Op Update” for Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Splintered Fate, as well as the downloadable content “Trench Coat Pack.”

Trench Coat Pack (Premium Downloadable Content)

You all loved the Samurai Armor Pack, so we went straight into the lore vault for another legendary fan favorite. Trench coats for everyone, including our downloadable content characters!

Grab the pack today, go wipe out Shredder’s goons, and look stylish doing it!
