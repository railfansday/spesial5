Alexander Skarsgård’s Beloved Fantasy Horror Adaptation Is Officially Leaving Hulu After 4 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Alexander Skarsgård's hit horror fantasy TV show has been available on Hulu since January 2023, but if you haven't watched it yet, now's the time before it leaves the service. In the age of streaming, with shows at our fingertips, it feels like we have all the time in the world to watch what we want.

Gone are the days of waiting up to make sure we catch the latest episode of Lost, or lamenting the fact that we had some obligation the night of a season premiere. Now, when we want to watch a show, we can just watch it, at our own pace, and on our own time. Unfortunately, that's not always the case.

Shows and movies are not guaranteed to stay on one streaming platform, even if they've been there for years. Take True Blood, HBO's horror-fantasy series that landed on Hulu in early 2023 but is now set to leave at the end of the year. Hulu users may have thought they could watch the show whenever, but now they only have a few weeks.

True Blood premiered in 2008 on HBO, based on Charlaine Harris' The Southern Vampire Mysteries series. The series is set in a world where vampires are a known section of the population, and whose murderous impulses are kept in check by synthetic blood known as "Tru Blood".

The series involves vampires' attempts to assimilate into wider society, and the introduction of other creatures from fantasy and myth, including werewolves, fairies, witches, and more. A massive hit for the first few seasons, it started to fall off toward the end, which is why it's not always ranked among HBO's best originals.

Still, True Blood was one of the first major fantasy TV shows intended for mature audiences. Game of Thrones is usually considered the major turning point in fantasy television, proving that major budgets and mature storylines could find huge audiences, but HBO may not have had the same confidence in Game of Thrones as it did without the success of True Blood.

True Blood first came to Hulu in January 2023, but some sources say that the series will be leaving the platform on December 31, 2026 (via Instagram). All seven seasons and 80 episodes are available on the service, and with each episode being 45–64 minutes long, you'll probably want to start binge-watching now if you intend to finish.

Warner Bros. Discovery did begin licensing out HBO original shows once that merger happened, and with the recent Skydance acquisition, it's possible the title could be licensed out again. Though True Blood will be coming off Hulu, the new owners of HBO could be interested in making a similar deal soon.

Even if True Blood is coming off Hulu, it will still be available to stream on HBO Max through 2027, as would be expected of an HBO Original. However, there are a few cases of HBO Originals no longer being available on the service. That's what happened with Raised by Wolves and Mrs. Fletcher.

There's no guarantee that True Blood will always be made available for streaming, another case to be made for the value of physical media, so if you have any interest in the show, you had better try to catch it sooner rather than later.
