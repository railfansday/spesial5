'RHOA' Star Porsha Williams and Fiancee Sway's Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Real Housewives of Atlanta star Porsha Williams found love with Patrice “Sway” McKinney after a turbulent few years that included her divorce from Simon Guobadia.

Porsha was previously married to former NFL player Kordell Stewart from 2011 to 2013 and to Simon from 2022 to 2024 before moving on with Sway.

From a cryptic CultureCon mention in fall 2025 to a formal engagement one year later, Porsha and Sway took their romance from whispered rumors to Instagram official. Scroll below to see how their relationship unfolded:

Porsha first hinted she was dating a woman during an appearance at CultureCon in New York City, telling attendees that she had narrowed her romantic prospects down to two people.

“The people who I’m talking to now … it’s just two, I narrowed it down to two, and he is nice and she is nice,” she said at the time. “I really just wanted to make sure that these were normal people. And what I talked to my therapist the other day was, I’m having a hard time opening up because they’re not narcissists and guess what narcissists do? They … mirror you, and these people are not, they’re challenging you, they’re asking me questions, they’re not letting me walk over them.”

Months earlier, Porsha finalized her divorce from Simon in June 2025.

Porsha and Sway made their social media debut as a couple.

“🎉Happiest Birthday @sway_thepro Hope this year continues to bring you everything you want and more! 🥂🍾 Can’t wait to act up Saturday!” Porsha captioned the post, which included sweet PDA-filled images.

Sway responded in the comments section, writing, “Thank you P! I appreciate you.”

Fellow stars cheered on the public debut. Wild ‘N Out‘s Jessie Woo commented, “NOT THE HARD LAUNCH PORSHEEEERRRRR!!!!! 🔥,” while The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills star Bozoma Saint John added, “That’s a way to celebrate everythingggggggg!!!”

Porsha introduced Sway to the RHOA cast during a season 17 episode, referring to the hairstylist as her new “lady friend.” In a confessional, Porsha addressed speculation about her sexuality.

“People had a lot to say about me being with women in the past. It’s, like, literally the worst kept secret,” she said.

Costar Phaedra Parks publicly supported the relationship.

“I think Sway is so sweet. She is just such a gentle woman,” Phaedra gushed via Instagram, responding to media coverage of the couple’s whirlwind romance. “I just love it and, you know, she just adores Porsha and I think Porsha needs this type of love, someone that just loves her unconditionally. A lot of people aren’t courageous enough to walk in their truth.”

She added, “A lot of people have desires that they are afraid to act upon because of cultural norms, whatever the case may be, and so I’m just happy that she’s showing her truth. She’s walking in her truth and unapologetically allowing someone to love her. You know, they say ‘Love wins, honey,’ and it’s winning with Porsha and I love it.”

Porsha and Sway got engaged, announcing the news in a joint Instagram post.

“I said YES. Engaged 11/21/26 ❤️. I love you forever @sway_thepro,” the caption read.
