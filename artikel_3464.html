A Classic Justice League Hero Is Officially Replacing Darkseid In Two Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

DC reveals which unexpected character becomes Darkseid's replacement as the Justice League's next biggest villain. Darkseid wasn't originally devised as a Justice League villain, but his immense power and his obsession with destruction has made him one of the biggest threats to the DC Universe and therefore a recurring JLA antagonist. Besides, the ruler of Apokolips is a multiversal constant, present across all the DC multiverse as a permanent apocalypse looming over every reality. Even when Earth’s greatest heroes destroy his physical body, Darkseid still exists somewhere out there.

Several cosmic tyrants rival or even surpass Darkseid in destructive power, but they ultimately pose smaller threats to the DC Universe. Beings like the Anti-Monitor, Perpetua, Nekron, Barbatos, Mandrakk, and the Empty Hand have each come close to unmaking reality itself, while Brainiac and Lex Luthor match or exceed Darkseid’s intellect on Earth. However, most of these villains usually pop up once every decade for a crossover event before vanishing back into dormancy, while earthly geniuses like Luthor lack Darkseid’s divine army of Parademons. Darkseid strikes repeatedly across generations, constantly corrupting worlds, hunting the Anti-Life Equation, and even reshaping entire timelines like the Absolute Universe.

No other character embodies the total eradication of free will like Darkseid does, but some DC figures make remarkable attempts.

Justice League Unlimited #27 presents one of the most dramatic heel turns in JLA history. On the cover, Michael Holt sits triumphantly on a throne shaped like an upside-down Omega symbol while a shrunken Atom perches on his shoulder. Having previously lost his cherished sense of "fair play" to Charon in the underworld and watched the Atom grow increasingly disillusioned with the League’s villain-amnesty policies, Michael Holt finds an unlikely partner who shares his frustration with the status quo. The solicitation for Justice League Unlimited #27 confirms his new antagonistic role, teasing, "ONCE A MAN, NOW A NEW GOD. MR. TERRIFIC ASCENDS!"

ONCE A MAN, NOW A NEW GOD. MR. TERRIFIC ASCENDS! Mr. Terrific was once the third-smartest man on Earth. Now his amplified genius is about to span the cosmos as he prepares to ascend to godhood — and only the Justice League can hold him back!

Back in Justice League Unlimited #22, Mister Terrific absorbs the Justice League’s collective powers and arrives at the conclusion that he alone can fix a broken universe with his new combination of unmatched intellect and the Justice League's complete collection of superpowers, which he acquires when he gets his hands on the Power Bank holding them all within a single object. Feeling superior to the entire Justice League, Mister Terrific has started to understand Darkseid's ideology, and he now has everything he needs to prove himself the villain's equal and overpower all his former teammates.

Mister Terrific possesses one of the sharpest minds in the DC Universe, but once he absorbs the Justice League’s collective powers, pure logic convinces him that authoritarian control is the most efficient way to fix the universe. Without a selfless heart to temper his calculations, Michael Holt’s brilliance turns clinical and ruthless, echoing the exact same hubris that makes Darkseid impossible to bargain with. Batman, one of the only two DC humans brighter than Mister Terrific, tempers his obsessive strategic mind with compassion, which allows him to understand that efficiency without humanity inevitably becomes oppression.

Batman has proven this restraint virtually every time he has gained similar upgrades, and he has always used those temporary boosts strictly to save lives before he willingly gives them up. Bruce Wayne has sat upon the omniscient Mobius Chair, wielded a Yellow and Green Power Ring, and has temporarily acquired Kryptonian-like physiology, yet he has never let godhood seduce him into believing he should rule or reshape the world. The belief that "absolute power corrupts absolutely" is ultimately the difference between Batman's legendary heroism and Mister Terrific's tragic transformation into a Darkseid-like supervillain.
