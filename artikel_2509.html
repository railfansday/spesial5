Everyone on the internet loves to overreact, and that's especially true when we're talking about the NFL. Zach Pereles looked at several things that happened during Week 10, so decide for yourself whether we're all overreacting.

Statement: Patriots will miss the playoffs.Overreaction or reality: Reality. This game was a microcosm of the entire season. There were massive expectations around the Brown addition. He has fallen woefully short, first with a knee injury, then an ankle injury, and now a hamstring injury. With Romeo Doubs also on IR, the wide receiver corps looks nothing like New England envisioned. The defense also looks nothing like New England hoped. Simply put, this isn't a playoff team.

Statement: Kirk Cousins trade just saved the Vikings' season.Overreaction or reality: Reality. The Vikings' 30-10 destruction of the Packers showed, once again, just how good this defense is. Not just good - great. The type of defense you cannot waste. And the Vikings' trade for Kirk Cousins showed the Vikings won't waste it. Cousins went 21-for-31 for 207 yards, a pair of touchdowns, and an interception. It wasn't amazing by any stretch, and the Packers turning the ball over four times helped. But perhaps that's the point here. Minnesota doesn't need amazing quarterback play. It just needs not-awful play. All the Vikings needed was capable quarterback play. They got it from someone who only joined the team five days ago. Outstanding work all around.

Statement: Falcons should bench Michael Penix Jr. Overreaction or reality: Overreaction. Michael Penix Jr. looked awful in the Falcons' 35-13 loss to the Chiefs. He was all over the place, completing just 23 of his 43 pass attempts. He threw an awful pick late. He looked completely flustered when he got pressured - and even before he got pressured. Penix's play has taken a nosedive since his three-touchdown Week 7 against the 49ers. He threw four interceptions in monsoon-like conditions against the Buccaneers in Week 8. Last week, he threw another bad pick against the Bengals. This week was another brutal outing. Unfortunately, the Falcons don't have a better option. Tua Tagovailoa has looked terrible in the few moments he's actually been available, and his penchant for turnovers, poor arm strength, and lack of mobility are near non-starters. It's pretty bleak in Atlanta, which will certainly have a new quarterback next year.
