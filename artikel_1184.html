Napoli star De Bruyne to miss Sassuolo clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Napoli star Kevin De Bruyne will miss Sunday’s Serie A match against Sassuolo due to a muscle problem.

The Belgium international trained separately at the Napoli training centre today and won’t be available for the upcoming Serie A match against Sassuolo at the Mapei Stadium.

Sky says De Bruyne is not expected to be out of action for long, as he’s dealing with muscle fatigue that requires rest.

Napoli already have a long list of injured players, with Alex Meret, Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Benoit Badiashile, Alisson Santos, Stanislav Lobotka, Alessandro Buongiorno and Antonio Vergara unavailable for the upcoming league game.

Napoli have had a tough start to the season, sitting 8th in the Serie A table, nine points behind Milan in fourth.

They’ve won only one of their last five games across all competitions, with two draws and two losses.

De Bruyne, 35, is in his second season at Napoli and has four goals and two assists in 17 appearances across all competitions in 2026-27.
