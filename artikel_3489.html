The Witcher 3 Is About to Get a Big Change to the Series’ Formula

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Songs of the Past will have a very different vibe

Many phrases come to mind when thinking of The Witcher series: dark, unforgiving, morally ambiguous, Geralt in a bathtub. According to The Witcher 3’s new DLC Songs of the Past principal writer, Alexandra Motyka, this new chapter in Geralt's saga will add a few new adjectives to that list: “We wanted it to be paced differently from other adventures — a slow burn, sentimental, and somehow soothing.”

In an article from the 25th anniversary Xbox Magazine, CD Projekt Red staff shared new details about the long-awaited 2027 add-on to The Witcher 3. We already knew the story would take place in Letten, an unexplored region of The Witcher’s world and Dandelion’s ancestral home, but the interview gives more context on the specific vibe and atmosphere the team is going for with this new locale.

Letten is inspired by Polish romantic poetry, the English countryside, Tudor architecture, and some of the more verdant areas in the original Witcher game. It will be new territory for The Witcher series at large, as the author of the books, Andrzej Sapkowski, never explored the area in his works. “Uncovering a new region on the map of The Witcher world is just as thrilling for us as for the players,” Motyka said. “This time we could use our creative freedom while still remaining within the boundaries of the imaginary world we inherited.”

The Xbox Magazine article adds that while Letten is a much more outwardly idyllic place than most backdrops in the series, there will still be monsters to fight and dark secrets to uncover. However, one of the main reasons for this adventure is to return to beloved characters, like Dandelion.

“We could have chosen any reason to set off on a new journey — but all of us wanted it to be about Dandelion," CD Projekt Red narrative director Philipp Weber said. "He is a very much loved character and his bond with Geralt was always touching. We shove the characters into new, challenging situations, we make it tough for them, in order to shift the dynamics of their relationship and show its depth.”

The interview also confirms that Songs of the Past takes place after the events ot The Witcher 3: Blood and Wine. “We always said that Geralt deserved his rest after Blood and Wine, and we stick by that," Weber said. "However, that doesn’t mean that Geralt can never go on an adventure again, especially with his best friend…”

Beyond 2027’s Songs of the Past, CD Projekt Red has already announced that The Witcher 4 is planned for a 2028 release window. CD Projekt Red’s CEO, Michał Nowakowski, previously said that he wants the studio to release a full trilogy of Witcher games over six years. It’s quite an ambitious plan, one that subverts the extended timelines many AAA games face these days. If that wasn’t enough, CD Projekt Red has many other projects in development: a remake of the original Witcher game, a multiplayer Witcher title, a Cyberpunk 2077 sequel, and an unnamed new IP, Project Hadar.
