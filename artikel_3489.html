The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Makes Major Change to Broadway Lineup

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is typically a platform for Broadway shows to perform standalone medleys, with three to five shows included throughout the event. This year, however, the parade opted to replace these individual performances with one 10-minute “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” medley with performers from nine different shows.

The Broadway musicals included in the medley are The Book of Mormon, Chicago, Disney’s Aladdin, Hamilton, The Lost Boys, Rent, Richard O’Brien’s The Rocky Horror Show, Schmigadoon! and Wicked.

A coastal storm threatens to bring heavy rain and strong wind gusts of 30 to 45 mph to New York City on Thanksgiving Day, which has a “significant chance” of grounding the balloons, Dan DePodwin, AccuWeather vice president of forecasting, told The Post ahead of the parade.

New York City prohibits flying giant balloons if sustained winds exceed 23 mph or gusts surpass 34 mph.

However, the parade is not expected to be canceled altogether. Even if the balloons are not fully grounded, it’s possible that crews will fly them lower to the ground for safety, as done in 2019 due to weather conditions.

“We most definitely have our eyes on that. This is a cherished tradition in our city, and I know it’s one that Americans across the country look forward to,” NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani said at a November 23 news conference, noting that a decision about the balloons will be made on Thanksgiving Day.
