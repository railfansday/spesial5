Humble Bundle Undergoes A Vibecoded-Looking Redesign, Forgets To Implement Full Account Security

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Humble Bundle, the PC storefront behind various charity game bundles, has been updated with what appears to be an all-new vibe-coded redesign, and it seems to be extremely flawed. As a few people have noted, the update appears to have stripped the site of various security features, potentially putting user accounts and saved credit card information at risk.

Initially discovered by users on Reddit, the update has entirely removed the option for two-factor authentication; when logging into my own account, the choice to add or remove the feature is entirely missing under security settings. People are reporting myriad other issues, including errors preventing them from purchasing new bundles. Various standard security features are missing following the new update: there are no authentication checks when changing passwords or email addresses, potentially making it easier for anyone to gain control of an account without a user’s permission. If logged in, you can simply enter a new password without having to know the current one. Additionally, when attempting to change your email addresses, no verification is sent to the old email address, with information only being directed toward the newly inputted email account.
