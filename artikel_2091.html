Pocognoli names Scotland squad as McTominay returns and Celtic & Rangers stars added

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sebastien Pocognoli has named his Scotland squad for the Nations League matches against North Macedonia and Switzerland this month.

The Hampden boss has brought Scott McTominay straight back into the fold after the key midfielder was unavailable for the last camp due to needing a heart procedure.

Kieran Tierney is back in contention for Scotland after having to withdraw from the last camp, with Rangers' James Penrice included in the squad this time round; the Ibrox ace could win his first cap for the national team this month.

Che Adams is another face back in the Scotland squad, for his first camp under Pocognoli after missing out through injury last time.

Lennon Miller, Josh Doig, Scott McKenna and Colby Donovan drop out of the squad from last month.

Goalkeepers: Scott Bain, Angus Gunn, Liam Kelly, Jon McCracken.

Defenders: Luis Binks, Luke Graham, Jack Hendry, Aaron Hickey, Nathan Patterson, James Penrice, Andy Robertson, Kieran Tierney, Stephen Welsh.

Midfielders: Ryan Christie, Lewis Ferguson, Billy Gilmour, John McGinn, Josh McPake, Scott McTominay, Kristi Montgomery.

Forwards: Che Adams, Kieron Bowie, Findlay Curtis, Ben Gannon-Doak, Oli McBurnie, Robbie Ure.
