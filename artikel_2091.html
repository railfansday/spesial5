Middlesbrough repeating Ibrahim Osman transfer magic can land them £200m windfall

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Middlesbrough, unsurprisingly after an incredible summer transfer window, have emerged as serious contenders for automatic promotion this season, but may be looking to the January window to iron out some key areas.

As things stand after 15 games, Middlesbrough sit joint top of the Championship table on 29 points, sandwiched in between Birmingham City on top and Stoke City just beneath.

Despite seeing Hayden Hackney, Morgan Whittaker, and Matt Targett leave the club, Boro enjoyed what is widely regarded as one of the best summer transfer windows in English second-tier history, as the likes of Ashley Phillips, Will Lankshear, Radek Vitek, Sebastian Berhalter, and Amario Cozier-Duberry joined the club.

For all of their strength and depth, Kim Hellberg's side is still far from perfect, and with the January window right around the corner, the Swede could be on the lookout for improvements across the board.

Over the past few years, Boro have recruited incredibly well, and that has been reflected on the pitch by reaching the Championship play-off final last season and now being involved in the automatic promotion race this time around.

Boro have at least one ideal backup in almost every position, but taking a closer look at the final third, there remains an area for improvement, especially on the left flank.

Jeremy Sarmiento's loan move from Brighton and Hove Albion became permanent in the summer, but with just one goal in 12 league appearances thus far, he hasn't quite had the output Hellberg would've been hoping for, as Tommy Conway has deputised off the left on occasion.

With Cozier-Duberry on the right, Lankshear up top, and Riley McGree through the middle, the left flank could do with a bit of an upgrade, and Boro should be looking to get back in contact with the Seagulls ASAP.

Cozier-Duberry was one of the most highly coveted players wanted by Championship clubs in the summer following his excellent year on loan with Bolton Wanderers in League One, but it would be Boro who won the race for his signature, as with three goals and five assists in ten league games already, he has justified his demand.

Brighton have garnered a nous for finding and producing gems in recent years, and have become many second-tier clubs' first port of call when looking for loan talent, as Boro should head to the South Coast once again for Ghanaian winger Ibrahim Osman.

Osman, 21, came through the ranks of Danish outfit FC Nordsjaelland, the same club that has helped to produce the likes of Mohammed Kudus, Caleb Yirenkyi, Mikkel Damsgaard, and Sindre Walle Egeli, and joined Brighton in the summer of 2024 for around £16 million.

Since then, loans to Feyenoord, Auxerre, and Birmingham have followed, but with just over 200 minutes of first-team football across all competitions so far this season, he could do with a loan to the Championship, and Middlesbrough should oblige.

After six months in France during the first half of the 2025/26 campaign, Osman headed to St Andrew's @ Knighthead Park for the second half of the year, and in the Second City, displayed his class.

In 17 Championship appearances for Birmingham, Osman scored two and assisted two, playing predominantly off the left flank where he could cut in on his preferred right foot. Whilst his output wasn't anything other-worldly, his ability on the ball was evident, with brilliant close control and footwork to beat opposition with ease.

With Boro dominating possession more often than not, Osman will not be required to carry the ball up the pitch too often, but with the ability to take the edge of the game by driving his team forward, he could still certainly be a valuable asset for Hellberg's side.

As another option on the left flank, Osman could be a game-changer for Boro, and a player that could be the difference between automatic promotion and the play-offs, as they should be doing everything they can to lure the Ghanaian to Teesside this winter.
