Tonda Eckert reacts when asked if he wanted to sign Lankshear ahead of Southampton v Boro

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Southampton head coach Tonda Eckert has revealed the level of interest the South Coast side had in Will Lankshear, as his side prepare to take on Middlesbrough in a highly-anticipated clash on Friday night at St. Mary's.

The fixture represents the first meeting of Saints and Boro since May's Spygate debacle, which saw the Hampshire outfit expelled from the Championship play-off final against eventual promotion winners, Hull City, as well as being docked four points for the current campaign after breaching EFL regulations on three counts, having admitted to performing similar actions against Oxford United and Ipswich Town.

Lankshear, of course, spent last season on loan with Oxford, and his performances for the side who were still relegated to League One raised debates over whether he would remain as a regular at Tottenham Hotspur or find regular first-team minutes elsewhere.

Middlesbrough are certainly reaping the rewards of their agreement with the North London side right now as they top the Championship on goal difference, while Southampton could close the gap on their newest 'rivals' to just four points with victory against Kim Hellberg's men.

Lankshear netted 12 times for Oxford last term, and whilst Southampton denied the striker from adding to that tally in their two meetings, the forward clearly left an impression on Eckert.

Saints do boast the services of some impressive forwards, including Canadian international, Cyle Larin, who has 18 goals in his first 37 appearances for the club, having turned his initial loan from RCD Mallorca permanent for £3m.

But with Lankshear topping the divisional scoring charts with 14 strikes in his first 15 league games for Middlesbrough, Eckert was quizzed about whether there was interest from Southampton in the England youth international.

"He's a good striker. I remember when we faced him with Oxford," said the Saints boss. There is many strikers we had on the list, there was a couple of things we wanted to do.

Will Lankshear has sent a message to Middlesbrough fans ahead of Friday evening's hotly-anticipated clash with Southampton

"His numbers speak for themselves," Eckert stated.

Lankshear was linked with potential moves to Middlesbrough and Ipswich in January, although both failed to materialise.

Several clubs in the EFL and beyond, soon courted his signature in the summer, with one of those being Southampton, who, by the time of their interest, had already re-signed Larin.

It was reported that Saints, as well as newly-relegated Wolves, had submitted approaches including eight-figure packages involving permanent deals or an initial loan with an obligation-to-buy next summer, all of which were rejected by Tottenham.

Just one week later, Lankshear put pen-to-paper on a five-year deal with the Teesside outfit, who struck an initial £10m deal, although Spurs could land a further £10m through incentive-based add-ons.

Lankshear has been the talk of the town this season, and he was back on the goalscoring trail in midweek against Swansea City, having suffered a minor injury earlier in the month, before playing just 13 minutes against Bristol City last weekend.

The forward had previously scored 13 goals in as many league games for Middlesbrough, leading to talk of a potential senior England call-up, whilst it has been reported that the club have placed a £70m valuation on his services to deter potential January suitors.
