Kasper Hogh tipped for Celtic goal explosion after 19-game barren run

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Denmark star Albert Gronbaek has assured Celtic fans their £11 million striker Kasper Hogh will soon end his 1,018 minutes goal drought and turn into a prolific scorer.

The £11 million Hoops star has now gone 19 games without a goal, since netting a hat trick at Kilmarnock at the start of August, for club and country.

He was dropped by Denmark for the Nations League game following his lack of goals, after he came on as a sub in three games against Norway and twice against Wales in the previous international window.

Hamburg midfielder Gronbaek was a teammate of Hogh's at Bodo-Glimt, where the Hoops star scored 36 league goals in 49 starts for the Norwegian champions.And he reckons Hogh will come good.

Gronbaek said: "Kasper Hogh and I text daily. We check in with each other.

"And I know that as soon as he starts scoring again, the goals will come. He is incredibly tough and extremely talented.

"The way he can turn the negative into the positive is very unique. I also use him as inspiration a lot myself if I need to be lifted.

"At Bodo, it's so safe up there - that's just the way it is because it is one big family.

"The friendships and relationships you get up there, it's very special compared to when you go out to other clubs."
