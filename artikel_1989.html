Man City cannot ignore alarming 27.3% stat that proves Phil Foden is no longer the same player

Manchester City's Phil Foden is struggling to return to the same significant impact he once had at the Etihad.

The English forward has been poor this season for Manchester City, and Enzo Maresca seems to have recognised that in his team selection.

His influence in the final third has been diminished, with Foden failing to impact multiple games across this campaign.

The 26-year-old has the technical ability to dictate the game, and for a player who was once seemingly undroppable at points under Pep Guardiola, this fall-off is concerning.

His lack of consistency has become increasingly difficult to overlook, especially with him being the gemstone of City's academy.

Foden now faces a fight to re-establish himself, with his worrying stats from this Premier League season emerging.

Foden remains capable of producing top moments for Man City, but has so far shown limited signs that he can do so this season.

His talent and quality will not have disappeared overnight as he continues to struggle under Maresca.

As per FotMob, Foden is clearly a player lacking confidence, with him ranking in the 17.9th percentile for dribble success at 27.3 per cent.

Even just last season, Foden ended the season with 53.2 per cent dribble success.

Maresca is also seemingly not being too impressed, as Foden has only started four of his eight Premier League appearances this season.

In terms of duels, Foden is also losing more than he is winning, with a success rate of 47.9 per cent.

The Englishman does have three goal contributions in the league; however, two of his assists came in the same game as his goal in another.

While the numbers look good on the surface, you would rather those contributions be spread over a number of matches, rather than a select few good displays.

Man City are well stocked in the areas that Foden typically would start in.

Out wide, Iliman Ndiaye and Jeremy Doku seem to be the current first-choice options, while Rayan Cherki has been excellent as the number 10.

Foden's performances have made it difficult to even consider him as an option in any of those positions, and that will be even harder once Antoine Semenyo returns from injury.

This leaves the England international with a tough peak to climb at Man City, with the midfielder needing to prove in his performances that he is the player he once was under Guardiola.
