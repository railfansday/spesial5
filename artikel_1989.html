There has been a new update on the contract positions of a number of Newcastle United players.

A number of Newcastle United players see their current contracts set to end on 30 June 2027.

However, The Athletic reporting that with two of these United stars, the club have the unilateral option of triggering an extension.

Dan Burn is in the final year of his contract but the report states that Newcastle United have the option of retaining the England star until 30 June 2028. Burn turns 35 before the end of this season but triggering this extension looks a no-brainer.

Whilst with Joe Willock, The Athletic report that their information is that United also have the option of extending his contract to 30 June 2028.

Whilst I would be amazed if Dan Burn left Newcastle United before summer 2028, with Joe Willock I would definitely expect the contract extension to be triggered but then it would be in the balance whether United would do that purely to ensure they would receive a decent transfer fee next summer (2027), or whether the midfielder would continue with NUFC into the 2027-28 season.

With Fabian Schar and Nick Pope I think it is unlikely we will see then stay at St James’ Park beyond their current deals which end in the summer.

Back in September Lewis Hall signed a new long-term deal.

That was quickly followed by media reports of Jacob Murphy agreeing an extension on his existing deal, pushing the end back from June 2027 to June 2028. However, nothing said officially by the club.

The Athletic though talking about it as fact that Jacob Murphy signed an extension.

Like Joe Willock though, in the balance as to whether he will stay at NUFC beyond summer 2027.

This is where we appear to be currently in terms of Newcastle United contracts overall.
