Greek Prime Minister faces backlash for asking young people if they want GTA 6 for Christmas

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Greece’s Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis has come under fire after asking young people in a TikTok video whether they’d be asking their grandparents for GTA 6 this Christmas.

“Which of you will ask for Grand Theft Auto VI as a Christmas present from grandad and grandma?” Mitsotakis said in the video, posted on November 20, as the game’s logo briefly flashed up on screen.

The video was made to promote a €400 ($439) payment for pensioners from Mitsotakis’ government. “As of this afternoon, the permanent aid of 400 euros will be paid to over 2 million pensioners,” its caption reads.

Critics were quick to point out that Rockstar’s game is rated for adults, arguing it was inappropriate for a prime minister to pitch it to younger audiences on TikTok.

Greek newspapers on both sides of the spectrum weighed in, with left-leaning daily Efsyn calling the clip a “gaffe,” while conservative paper Dimokratia described it as a “tragic” error.

The timing has put Greece in the GTA 6 spotlight twice in one week, after Greek singer Alkistis Protopsalti’s song “Ola Afta Pou Fovamai” turned up on the game’s in-game radio at launch.

Mitsotakis’ office has yet to respond to the criticism, and the video remains on his TikTok account.
