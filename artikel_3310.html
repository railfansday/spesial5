Jeff Daniels Set For Ramy Youssef’s ‘Martha & Muhammad’ At A24

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Two-time Primetime Emmy winner Jeff Daniels is joining Ramy Youssef‘s A24 feature comedy Martha & Muhammad alongside previously announced Amanda Seyfried and Sharon Stone.

Plot and character details are being kept secret in what is Ramy alum Youssef’s feature directorial debut.

Daniels won a Drama Series Lead Actor Emmy for HBO’s The Newsroom in 2013 and Supporting Actor in Limited Series for Godless in 2018. He is starring in the upcoming Joseph Gordon-Levitt directed sci-fi thriller 2034 and Josh Wakely’s sci-fi thriller Starman. He recently starred as Ronald Reagan in The Brink of War and starred in A Man in Full, David E. Kelley’s Netflix miniseries based on the Tom Wolfe novel. Daniels is also a 5x Golden Globe nominee, 5x SAG Actors Award nominee and Tony Award nominee.

Three-time Emmy nominee and Golden Globe winner Youssef also wrote the screenplay. The multi-hyphenate’s Cairo Cowboy will produce alongside Eli Bush, Ronald Bronstein and Josh Safdie for Central, Christopher Storer and Tyson Bidner for American Light & Fixture, and A24, who will also finance. Daniels is represented by LBI, Martino Management and Yorn Levine.
