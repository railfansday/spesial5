Jonathan Spector’s Acclaimed ‘Birthright’ Sets Spring Broadway Opening

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Birthright, Jonathan Spector‘s critically acclaimed that played to sold-out houses at Off Broadway‘s MCC Theater last summer, will make its Broadway debut this spring, with Teddy Bergman again directing.

The play will begin a 20-week strictly limited engagement at Broadway’s Hudson Theatre on Tuesday, February 23, 2027, ahead of a Monday, March 15, 2027 opening night.

Casting for the play – written by the playwright of the Tony-winning Eureka Day – will be announced soon.

“Just over two years ago, I was racing to finish a first draft of Birthright before starting rehearsals for Eureka Day at MTC,” Spector said in a statement. “I could never have fathomed that such a short time later we’d be bringing it to Broadway, when the questions the play is wrestling with still feel so close to our present moment.” Related Stories News Raúl Esparza Still On Concussion-Caused Leave From Broadway's 'Galileo'; Michael Park Stepping In News Frida Kahlo Musical In Development With Diane Paulus Directing, Latin Grammy Winner Silvana Estrada Composing Score & Georgina Escobar Writing Book

He continued, “Writing this was the scariest thing I’ve ever attempted to do, and (perhaps not coincidentally), has become the most meaningful and rewarding. I’m beyond thrilled that a wider audience will have a chance to encounter the play and see the work of our brilliant artistic collaborators.” Watch on Deadline

The official synopsis: “Six friends, eighteen years, and all the questions/quarrels/hookups/hangups/group chats/overwhelming love that every close-knit group of people must face as they barrel into ‘real’ adulthood. Hilarious and hopeful, Birthright looks at what it means to be young-ish and Jewish in America, and how our friendships and identity can shape the core of who we become.”

“Working with Jonathan on his extraordinary play has been one of the greatest artistic experiences of my life,” said director Bergman. “The play brilliantly, honestly tells the story of 18 years of head-spinning change, refracted through seven completely alive characters. Deeply funny, loving, devastating, and blistering, Jonathan’s script makes real the difficult, essential work of trying to connect with our loved ones across our deepest divides. I couldn’t be more excited for this play to resonate with a wider audience this spring.”

The announcement of the Broadway staging was made today by producers Jenny Gersten, Sonia Friedman Productions, and Jana Bezdek & Jen Hoguet.

Following a development process and world premiere directed by Bergman at Miami New Drama in April 2025, Birthright opened to strong reviews at MCC Theater this past June (it was named a New York Times critic’s pick). The Off Broadway cast included Molly Ranson, Nate Mann, Liz Larsen, EliGelb, Molly Bernard, Zoë Winters and Hale Appleman.

Birthright features Scenic Design by Scott Pask, Costume Design by Clint Ramos, Lighting Design by Natasha Katz, Sound Design by Lee Kinney and Projection Design by David Bengali.
