Chris Long To Direct First Episode Of Fox Legal Drama ‘Katie Greaves’ Starring Edie Falco

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Two-time Emmy nominee Chris Long (The Americans) has been tapped to direct and executive produce the initial episode of Fox‘s upcoming legal drama Katie Greaves (working title), starring Edie Falco. The series, which will premiere during the 2027-28 season, comes from playwright and TV writer Sharr White (Apple TV’s Presumed Innocent), Scott Free Productions (FX’s Alien: Earth) and Fox Entertainment Studios.

The series centers on a whip-smart, fiercely committed attorney (Falco) who stepped away from the New Hampshire Public Defender’s office following a devastating personal tragedy. Now raising her two young granddaughters with her husband and trying to piece together a life she never expected, Katie is drawn back to the work that once defined her. Each case tests Katie’s instincts, wit, and deeply held belief that everyone deserves a second chance…and someone to fight for them. Katie Greaves (wt) is executive produced by White, Falco and Scott Fee’s Ridley Scott and David W. Zucker. Fox Entertainment Studios produces with worldwide distribution by Fox Entertainment Global. Watch on Deadline

Widely known for his work on The Americans, Long recently served as executive producer and primary director of FX’s limited series The Patient, starring Steve Carrell and Domhall Gleeson. On the film side, he most recently finished directing Chicago-based thriller The Up and Comer, based Howard Roughan’s novel. The film stars Nate Mann, Shay Mitchell, Andrew Burnap, Rebecca De Mornay, Noah Emmerich, Sarah Bolger, Kunal Nayyar, and is slated for release in 2027. Long is currently in development with Wolf Entertainment and long-time collaborators Ken Woodruff and Erin Donovan on an international crime drama set in Marseille. He is represented by WME and Rain Management.
