Judge who invoked role in garbage fine battle admonished

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A judicial misconduct watchdog said a Brooklyn, New York City, judge repeatedly invoked her judicial role and trial schedule while contesting New York City sanitation citations for garbage in front of her home. (Photo from Shutterstock)

A judicial misconduct watchdog said a Brooklyn, New York City, judge repeatedly invoked her judicial role and trial schedule while contesting New York City sanitation citations for garbage in front of her home.

The New York State Commission on Judicial Conduct admonished Judge Katherine A. Levine of the Kings County Supreme Court in New York, noting that she had served as a judge since 2008 without prior disciplinary issues. The commission determination, dated Oct. 2, was announced Monday.

The conduct took place between March 2024 through November 2025 and was connected to three citations. During this time, Levine repeatedly mentioned her judicial status and responsibilities when communicating about hearings and fines, according to the commission determination.

“Invoking judicial office to avoid paying a fine undermines public confidence in the integrity of the judiciary and suggests that judges are exempt from rules that apply to everyone else,” said commission administrator Robert H. Tembeckjian, according to a story by Law360. “The Office of Administrative Trials and Hearings was right not to give special treatment to a judge.”
