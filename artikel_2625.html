Gilbert Arenas claims ‘heartless’ Klay Thompson’s Megan Thee Stallion breakup led to resurgence

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Klay Thompson is finding himself delivering at a level many fans got accustomed to during his prime with the Golden State Warriors.

Earlier this year, Thompson ended his relationship with rapper Megan Thee Stallion, which became quite a pop culture controversy.

And given how well Thompson is playing since joining the Miami Heat, Gilbert Arenas believes the breakup has helped him find his form again.

Speaking on The Arena show, Gilbert Arenas spoke about Klay Thompson's recent return to form from a shooting standpoint.

Arenas suggests that the nature and fallout of his recent breakup with rapper Megan Thee Stallion has somehow given him the confidence he needs to once again become the elite shooter he once was.

"One of the greatest shooters of all time not having his shot, when that's what you do for a living… Sometimes, certain type of minds need certain things," Arenas said. "I think the Megan Thee Stallion (situation), just watching someone like, 'this is the worst break-up I've ever had,' made him sleep good at night. The old Klay is back!"

"Sometimes, in the movie, that killer has a soft spot, and he's more vulnerable… He just lost his touch. When he'd seen that, and she was all distraught, he probably sat back like, 'Yeah. This is me. When they're crying at the doorstep, my shot is back!'

"I just really believe that's all it took. Looking at the phone, talking about, 'I hate your guts,' and he's thinking, 'Yeah, I'm about to have 30 tonight!' Everything is back.

"Klay is back! The killer is back! Keep shooting the ball, baby, and keep breaking hearts! You've got to add that part to it. He shoots like he's heartless! Gotta keep breaking hearts!"

This season, Thompson is averaging 15.5 points, 2.1 assists, and 1.4 rebounds, while shooting 45.1% from the field and 43.4% from beyond the arc.

Thompson has hit 50+ three-pointers in just 13 games this season, the fastest he has reached this mark at any point in his NBA career.
