ESPN continues Tom Jackson tributes during 'Monday Night Football'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The tributes for Tom Jackson continued as ESPN aired the Nov. 23 edition of "Monday Night Football" between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders.

As play-by-play announcer Joe Buck opened the broadcast, the "NFL Primetime" music played in the background. Jackson, the former ESPN NFL studio analyst who co-hosted the weekly Sunday highlight show alongside Chris Berman for nearly three decades, died Friday at the age of 75. No cause of death has been provided.

"He was a wonderful being first and foremost," ESPN color commentator Troy Aikman said during the pregame show.

As a player, Aikman said, Jackson and Berman were must-see television – both in an entertainment sense but also because that was how fans saw what happened around the league that week.

The former Dallas Cowboys quarterback respected that they could be critical and opinionated without attacking the individual.

"I could throw three interceptions in a game, and we could lose, and I could watch Primetime and not wanna go hang myself at the end of the night," Aikman said. "They just had a respectful way of presenting the information and letting everybody know what happened, but doing it the right way, I thought."

During the beginning of the second quarter, Buck welcomed viewers over the "Primetime" theme once again.

"There are a lot of heavy hearts in our company as the NFL and ESPN family has lost a legend this past week," Buck said.

Aikman repeated his stance that "Primetime" was "must-watch" every Sunday.

"He was obviously a big part of the football season for many years," Aikman said.

Berman announced Jackson's death Nov. 22 during "Sunday NFL Countdown." To honor the former Denver Broncos linebacker who was part of the vaunted "Orange Crush" defense, the ESPN crew sported orange ties throughout the show.

ESPN also released a five-minute video of Jackson surprising Berman at the studio just a few weeks before his unexpected passing.

During halftime of Monday night's game, Berman hosted a special edition of his "Fastest 3 Minutes" segment solo. After once again eulogizing his best friend on air, Berman did the highlights of the Broncos' 38-24 victory over the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday. Then in typical "Boomer" and "Teej" fashion, the package shifted to highlights of Denver's 1977 AFC championship game victory, including postgame locker-room footage of Jackson, over the Raiders.

A special "TJ" logo with his Jackson's jersey number, 57, appeared in the upper right hand corner of the screen throughout halftime.
