Quinyon Mitchell listed as limited in Tuesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Eagles held their second walkthrough practice of the week on Tuesday and their injury report shows cornerback Quinyon Mitchell making progress in his return to the lineup.

Mitchell was listed as a limited participant with a hamstring injury after being listed as out of practice Monday. Defensive coordinator Vic Fangio said at his press conference that he expects Mitchell will play in Dallas on Thursday.

Linebacker Arnold Ebiketie (oblique) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (ankle) remained non-participants. Linebacker Smael Mondon (ankle) and running back Will Shipley (foot, illness) joined Mitchell in the limited category.

Running back Saquon Barkley (neck, illness), defensive tackle Jalen Carter (quad), defensive back Michael Carter (abdomen), wide receiver Britain Covey (knee), tight end Dallas Goedert (shin), linebacker Jonathan Greenard (ankle), quarterback Tanner McKee (rib), cornerback Kelee Ringo (neck), and wide receiver DeVonta Smith (hamstring) were all listed as full participants.
