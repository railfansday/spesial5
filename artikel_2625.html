Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Before UFC 334 kicks off later tonight (Sat., Nov. 14, 2026) featuring a heavyweight title fight between Ciryl Gane and Josh Hokit, Professional Fighters League (PFL) will stage its own 265-pound title fight at PFL Dubai from inside Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, UAE between Vadim Nemkov taking and Sergei Bilostenniy. Nemkov is 20-2-1 but he hasn't competed since defeating Renan Ferreira in Dec. 2025 to win the inaugural strap (see it again here).

Many readers check in before, during and after the fights to share their thoughts on all of the action, as the main card kicks off at 1 p.m. ET on the ESPN App and ESPN2. Feel free to leave a comment (or several) about the bouts and chat with all the other Maniacs during the show — it's always a lot of fun!

In the co-main event of the evening, surging welterweight contenders Patrick Habirora and Omar El Dafrawy collide, while Sergio Pettis takes Joji Goto in what is sure-to-be a thrilling bantamweight affair. Also in action, Adam Borics will battle it out against Khasan Magomedsharipov at featherweight.
