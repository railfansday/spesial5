The Morning After: The Steelers need to cut DK Metcalf

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It turns out that shopping for used cars and players on the trade block are similar experiences.

Both feature assets with some mileage to them, and a man at the top who will do anything he can to make you sign all the paperwork necessary to make the transaction official. Sometimes, both sides end up happy. Take the Buffalo Bills and Minnesota Vikings, for example. In 2020, the Bills sent their first-round pick to the Vikings in exchange for Stefon Diggs. The star receiver had multiple All-Pro seasons in Buffalo and helped Josh Allen become the superstar quarterback we see today, while Minnesota used that pick from the Bills to draft Justin Jefferson.

Other times, though, you have the great swindle that took place in March, 2025 when Seattle Seahawks GM John Schneider laughed all the way to the bank after selling a lemon to the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Pittsburgh traded a second-round pick for DK Metcalf, and then immediately gave him a four-year, $132 million extension to be their No. 1 wide receiver. Less than two months later, they shipped George Pickens away to Dallas for a third-round pick. What has happened in the year and a half since has made the Steelers look like one of the most foolish franchises in the NFL.

Metcalf has played in 25 games for the Steelers. In those 25 games, he has caught just 95 passes – less than four receptions per game – for 1,284 yards. Since the start of the 2025 season, there have been 127 receivers to run at least 250 routes – Metcalf ranks 93rd in target success rate.

And while there was hope this summer during a very productive training camp for Metcalf to take a jump in Year Two as a Steeler, he has, instead, regressed to a point that has made him borderline unplayable. After 11 weeks, Metcalf is 50th in the NFL in receptions (36), 48th in yards (434), and 59th in touchdowns (2). He is also 51st out of 55 receivers in target success rate (min. 250 routes run) while his EPA per target is 46th and his reception percentage is 50th. Since joining the Steelers, Metcalf has just three games of 100+ yards, compared to 12 games of 45 yards or fewer – all while being the seventh-highest paid wide receiver in the league.

This four-game stretch has been his most brutal to date. Over that span, Metcalf has caught six passes. In two of those games, he had fewer than five yards.

The Steelers brought in Metcalf and got rid of Pickens because they felt Pickens was too much of a head case to give a long-term deal to. Since the start of 2025, Pickens has 55 more receptions, 925 more yards, seven more touchdowns, and an All-Pro selection to his name, while having one (potentially soon to be two) fewer suspensions.

Which brings us to Week 11 in Philadelphia.

Metcalf was ejected from the game after two skirmishes that both resulted in unsportsmanlike conduct penalties, the latter of which he reached into the facemask of Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean. It's the second time in two seasons Metcalf has gotten into a physical altercation since joining the Steelers. Last season, he slapped a fan that was taunting him in Detroit during the team's win over the Lions in Week 16. He was subsequently suspended by the NFL for the final two games of the regular season. Given his history, the league will likely view him as a repeat offender and give him another suspension of some kind.

But what should the Steelers do? Should they get ahead of the league and suspend him internally for conduct detrimental to the team? I'll do you one better – they should cut him.

As Alan Saunders of Steelers Now points out, Metcalf doesn't have any guaranteed money remaining on his contract after a restructure earlier this year. Should he be released between now and any point before the 2027 season begins, the Steelers would eat a dead cap hit of $37.5 million – and that's what they should do.

It would be one thing to deal with Metcalf's temper if he was producing – he isn't. He offers more problems than production.. His presence on the field does more harm than good. And it was proven in real time that anyone else can fill his role – and do it better. Undrafted practice squad receiver Max Hurleman made his NFL debut on Sunday and caught six passes for 61 yards and a touchdown. He now has more games of five receptions and a touchdowns than Metcalf does this season.

Everyone recognizes that Metcalf has one of the worst contracts in the league. And based on how Deshaun Watson is playing for the Browns right now, Metcalf may have the worst contract in the NFL. Send a message to the rest of the team, and future Steelers, as well. It doesn't matter who you are or what you're making. If you are more hurtful to the team than anything else, you'll be shown the door. Admit your mistake, cut your losses, and get rid of the worst trade and contract the franchise has ever seen.
