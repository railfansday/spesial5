Gibbs, Irving lead Week 12 fantasy football running back rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

MIAMI, Nov. 25 (UPI) -- Jahmyr Gibbs, Bucky Irving and Bijan Robinson lead my Top-50 fantasy football running back rankings for Week 12.

Javonte Williams and Christian McCaffrey also are inside the Top 5 of my rankings, which are listed below. Chuba Hubbard, Emmett Johnson, James Cook, Saquon Barkley and Cam Skattebo join those rushers inside my Top 10.

No teams are on bye this week, giving fantasy football team owners options for starting lineups. Thanksgiving week games start Wednesday. Ensure your lineup is complete earlier than in a typical week.

My Week 12 waiver wire/free agent targets can be found here. My Week 12 wide receiver rankings also are available.
