Cowboys' Brian Schottenheimer rips 'unacceptable' defense, 's***' tackling in loss to 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Another week, another atrocious effort by the Dallas Cowboys defense.

This time, it took place in a 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers that featured defensive backs Caleb Downs and Cobie Durant getting trucked on the same 25-yard touchdown run by backup running back Kaelon Black.

It also featured 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy delivering downfield blocks on a 70-yard touchdown run in which Deebo Samuel went untouched.

It added up to a 29-point, 340-yard second half for the 49ers and a fundamental clinic by the Cowboys defense on what no to do that could be featured on any high-school teach tape.

The 49ers piled up 584 yards of offense on 9.4 yards per play. They rushed for 214 yards on 7.4 yards per carry. The 584 yards were the most allowed by the Cowboys in a home game in franchise history.

After the game Cowboys, head coach Brian Schottenheimer didn't mince words when speaking with reports about the Dallas defense. What choice did he have?

"I thought it was unacceptable," Schottenheimer said of the second-half defensive performance. "The tackling was atrocious. …

"At the end of the day, we tackled like shit in the second half."

While pointed, Schottenheimer's comments didn't reveal anything that anybody who watched the game didn't already know. And they're not going to fix the Dallas defense. At this point in a 4-6 Cowboys season, it appears as if nothing will.

In addition to Schottenheimer, critics have pointed to first-year defensive coordinator Christian Parker as a potential target for change. But head coach Jerry Jones has stood by both this season, leaning on his lengthy track record of declining to make personnel changes before it's too late.

On Sunday, Jones maintained his support of Parker while pointing to his experience overseeing 30-plus-year-old Super Bowl victories to back up his reasoning.

"Again, it's what it is," Jones said postgame. "He's a young coach in his first year of coordinating and calling the plays. So you have to say that you would hope you could win games like the last that we've lost.

"I'll weigh his early times. I've been with teams that had some pretty good coordinators on defense that ended up winning Super Bowls."

Again, the last time a Cowboys team won the Super Bowl was after the 1995 season. This team is not going to end that streak. Thanks to an abysmal NFC East, though, the Cowboys remain reasonably in the playoff hunt, 1.5 games behind a not-great Giants team.

But there's little from the players on the field, the coaches on the sideline or the owner in the luxury box that suggests the Cowboys have any hope of rallying to win the division.
