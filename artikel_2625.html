Starting with Patriots-Lions, Fox has three windows on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The last of the early-morning 2026 kickoffs happens today, with the Patriots playing the Lions in Germany. Unlike the others, which were televised by NFL Network, the Week 10 European game will be carried by Fox.

That gives Fox three windows in one day, with the morning game followed by the usual 1:00 p.m. ET window and the every-other-week 4:25 p.m. ET window.

Most of the country will see Vikings-Packers, featuring the return of Kirk Cousins, on Fox at 1:00 p.m. ET. The only Fox game at 4:25 p.m. ET will be 49ers at Cowboys.

With the Fox pregame show starting at 9:00 a.m. ET, it gives Fox more than 10 hours of continuous NFL broadcasting on Sunday. And with five of the six teams having winning records (and the sixth being the Cowboys), the numbers should start large and keep growing — culminating in a very large audience for the late-afternoon window, which has become the biggest of the NFL week.
