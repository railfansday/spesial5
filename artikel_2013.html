As a Newcastle United fan, have a look at these Premier League fixtures on Sunday and have a think about what you’d like to happen and what is most likely in terms of results.

My conclusions and predictions on these Sunday matches:

Brighton are top of the Premier League form table with five wins and one defeat. However, Brentford have only lost one of their last six, even if winning only two of the last half dozen.

Two teams that are good to watch and I think the fact Brighton had a midweek match might help Brentford get a draw.

Liverpool still don’t look convincing this season despite their huge spending in recent times. Losing to an average Fenerbahce team after having led twice, kind of sums them up.

They are though up against a desperate looking Palace who have lost their last three league matches whilst conceding nine goals and scoring none.

Palace did scratch out a 1-0 Carabao Cup win at Fulham recently and I am hoping they might come out battling here on home turf. I hope they might get a point…

Bournemouth have won their last seven in all competitions and will be big favourites today.

Ipswich though have won two of their four home EPL matches and drawn another. I think the Tractor Boys might get at least a point here, maybe even win.

Sunderland look on a one-way ticket to the Championship and I think flat-track bullies Chelsea will help them along their way back to the lower leagues.

Sunderland have picked up one point from their last six EPL matches and were humiliated at home by Dinamo Zagreb in midweek.

I would be very surprised if Chelsea don’t win this by at least a couple.

Man U are in the relegation zone for very good reason. Only a lucky win against Ipswich all season in the Premier League and if Villa turn up today then this will be an away win.

Man U fluked a midweek victory against an average Roma side in midweek but my prediction is that Carrick and his players will enter the international break on the back of another defeat. Whether Carrick is still in charge after the internationals…unlikely.
