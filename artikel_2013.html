Mykhailo Mudryk returns as Chelsea slip to Juventus defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Mykhailo Mudryk made his first Chelsea appearance since November 2024 as Xabi Alonso’s team succumbed to their second pre-season defeat.

Mudryk replaced Nicolas Jackson in the 82nd minute against Juventus, marking his return to competitive football after a long doping ban that was resolved this week thanks to changes in Wada regulations.

There were a couple of crosses delivered from the left flank by the Ukrainian winger, who could yet leave Chelsea on loan or on a permanent basis before the close of the transfer window.

Juventus were the victors though, thanks to a sumptuous 68th-minute goal by Kosovo winger Edon Zhegrova.

Alonso had 21 players on his bench, and made 12 second-half substitutions.

Levi Colwill and Cole Palmer featured in the defeat by 10-man Spurs on Saturday, but sat this game out as they continue to build fitness. Danny Welbeck did start though, reunited with former Brighton team-mate Joao Pedro up top.

Chelsea scored just one goal against Spurs and Juventus, but the thrust provided by right-back Marco Palestra in tandem with first Geovany Quenda and then Estevao was a bright spot for Alonso.

Chelsea continue their pre-season against AC Milan on Saturday, before they host Real Sociedad at Stamford Bridge on August 15.

They begin their Premier League campaign at Fulham in the Monday night slot on the opening weekend.

The wheels of commerce continue to turn for Chelsea in the transfer market but this afternoon Xabi Alonso’s team continue their preparations for the new season against Juventus in Hong Kong.

They have the chance to recover from the ignominy of losing to 10-man Tottenham Hotspur in Sydney on Saturday, but with 19 days until Chelsea’s Premier League opener against Fulham these matches are still a fitness building exercise. The positive from the Spurs game was Wesley Fofana, Levi Colwill, Cole Palmer completing 80 minutes – three players who endured injury problems last season.

Another new arrival is expected to follow Morgan Rogers, Marco Palesta, Maxence Lacroix, Jordan Henderson and Danny Welbeck to Stamford Bridge, with the club nearing a deal for Rayo Vallecano left-back Pep Chavarria.

It wouldn’t be Chelsea without talk of some eye-catching outgoings, with Spurs reportedly interested in Nicolas Jackson and Enzo Maresca eyeing a reunion with Pedro Neto at Manchester City.

Alonso’s team promise to be flexible in their tactical approach this season, but so far he has set up with a back four rather than the 3-4-2-1 system he used to such great effect at Bayer Leverkusen. That could change as availability increases closer to the start of the season, but Palestra’s bursts forward from right-back in tandem with Estevao have caught the eye.

Juventus finished sixth in Serie A last season under Luciano Spalletti, the former Roma and Napoli coach who usually guarantees expansive and attractive football. Turkey left-winger Kenan Yildiz was their top goalscorer last season, and it looks like they will make it through the summer without the left-winger being picked off by one of the Premier League’s elite. Yildiz scored 11 goals in all competitions, and Dusan Vlahovic was the only other player to reach double figures so scoring goals is a problem to address. Lois Openda and Randal Kolo Muani have been signed this summer to strengthen the forward line.
