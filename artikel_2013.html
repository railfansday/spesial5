Manchester United must back Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window if he is to have any impact at Old Trafford.

The 54-year-old will replace Michael Carrick in the coming days, with confirmation coming on Thursday that the former midfielder has been sacked.

The Red Devils have endured one of the worst starts to a season in their club history, winning just three of the opening 15 games in all competitions and winning only once in the Premier League.

It is hoped that Pochettino will be able to restore some stability at Man United, having transformed Tottenham in his five years in North London.

The Argentine went on to manage PSG and Chelsea before taking the role of manager of the United States national team, where he will leave for Old Trafford.

Here, Football Insider has picked out three players who Man United must target in January to back Pochettino.

We can confirm Michael Carrick has departed his role as men’s first-team head coach.

Son Heung-min was one of the standouts from Pochettino's time at Tottenham.

The winger arrived under the Argentine and exploded into a devastating inside forward, scoring 21 goals and providing seven assists in just his second season.

Man United are in desperate need of a left-winger amid Marcus Rashford's issues, and Kevin Schade could be just the man.

The 24-year-old will have just one year left on his deal next summer and has shown great potential to take a large step up.

Incredibly, Man United already had a solution to their left-back crisis on their books in 2024 - Alvaro Carreras.

The 23-year-old could not break into the senior team and left for Benfica on loan before immediately joining Real Madrid in a £42million deal.

But despite a strong start, the Spaniard now finds himself on the outskirts of Jose Mourinho's side, featuring just once in the last seven La Liga games.

If Man United can convince him to come back, Carreras could be an intelligent signing.

Full-back is an issue for Man United on both sides, and it is one that must be addressed immediately.

On the right, Givairo Read could be the ideal solution. The 20-year-old is teeming with talent and was heavily pursued by both Nottingham Forest and Roma in the summer.

Man United have a dark recent history for signing players from Feyenoord - Tyrell Malacia - but with five assists and 16 dribbles completed in 12 games this season, Read is the real deal.
