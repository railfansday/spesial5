Match in pictures - Bognor Regis Town lose unbeaten home record to Egham Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Connor Cullen’s goal just after the midway point of the second half settled the Isthmian South Central clash, with Rocks skipper Tommy Block sent off minutes later.

It means Danny Hollands’ side are now winless in three, although they remain second in the table, just a point off top spot.

They will look for better on Saturday, when they go to Windsor and Eton.
