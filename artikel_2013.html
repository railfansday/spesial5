Peter Beardsley was and still is my all time favourite toon player.

Around that time we also had Paul Gascoigne and Chris Waddle playing for Newcastle United.

Sweet baby Jesus and the orphans, imagine having all three of them playing for us today.

Instead of the heedless chicken Osula, Missa and Elanga.

So my view is, once you have seen the three mentioned 1980s icons walk out the front door, you are then kind of desensitised to coming and goings.

However, back in March 2022 I walked to St Mary’s stadium in Southampton (got soaked by the way) to watch Bruno Guimaraes make his debut.

After the FCB years I thought we had a player who bought into, not just Newcastle United, but our region, what it meant to be a Geordie,

A leader, his speech before the League Cup final and him getting it when we played the village of the damned away in the FA Cup cemented his Geordie legacy.

Turns out the first sign of things going messed up at Newcastle United, he couldn’t wait to get out.

So much for “My kids are Geordies and my family are Geordies” (His dad taking up a seat in the away end and next thing after Bruno signed for the Gunners we see him in an Arsenal top).

For me, Bruno Guimaraes is a bigger rat than Alexander Isak and we are going to win 1-0 today.
