As a VPN, Surfshark is very accomplished. It's fast – posting peak speeds of over 2,000 Mbps in our latest testing – and it's very effective at unblocking streaming sites, too.

The VPN also offers post-quantum encryption, RAM-only servers, MultiHop (its Double VPN feature), and VPN obfuscation through its Camouflage Mode.

However, Surfshark One is a step above. For a meagre $8 more over the course of 27 months, you'll get full Avira-backed antivirus. Then there's Surfshark's Scam Protection, which checks SMS messages for signs of fraud, uses AI to spot suspicious emails and phishing attempts, and blocks access to malicious or unwanted websites.
