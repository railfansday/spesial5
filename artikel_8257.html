9 Editor-Approved Steals from REI’s Holiday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Love it or hate it, the holiday season is apparently here. Sure, that means it’s time to panic-shop for gifts. But as trail runners, it’s also a once-in-a-year opportunity to score the biggest deals on the gear we need. (Yes, need.) That’s why I spent way too long pouring through REI’s Holiday Sale to find the best deals on the trail running shoes, apparel, and accoutrement that is genuinely used and loved by me and the Trail Runner team.

From November 13-23, REI is running its biggest sale of the season. Also during this window REI members get 20% off one full-price item—such as the men’s and women’s Arc’teryx Norvan Jacket, which for my money is simply the best trail running rain coat on the market, or the Salomon Adv Skin 12 Hydration Vest. (My favorite hydration vest.) REI members also get another 20% off one item at REI.com/outlet. (Use the code Holiday2026 for both.)

Below, you’ll find my hand-picked items from the thousands on site. You can also go wild and check out the full sale for yourself.

In my humble opinion these are hands-down the best trekking poles on the market. That’s thanks to the ingenious glove strap system that affixes to the pole with a magnet—no more faffing around trying to get your poles on and off with two hands. The gloves themselves provide excellent energy transfer, and the carbon poles are super light but also plenty sturdy. I used them for a lap of Mount Blanc this summer, no problem.

It’s a tried and true staple for a reason. My footwork has never felt more precise than when I’m wearing the La Sportiva Bushido. That’s thanks to a specially-designed midsole that hugs your foot, Ghillie lacing, and an ultra grippy outsole. The III has an extra breathable upper and side panels that increase airflow but keep the rocks out. If you’re running on a lot of technical trails, this shoe is hard to beat.

Are they for hiking? For fashion? Why not both! The Keen Jasper Zionic Sneakers came on my radar when Harry Styles and Zoe Kravitz were spotted hoofing it around New York City in matching pairs. I, too, am now a proud owner of a pair and they nail the combination of comfort and style. I would most certainly wear them on a hike, and I also wore them to a Wedding rehearsal dinner.

It’s cute, it’s functional, and it comes in tons of both subtle and fun colors. I love this sports bra-tank for warm runs, gym time, and even everyday wear. Plus, there’s a pocket in the back that’s big enough to hold a smart phone—if your shoulders are flexible enough to reach it. (Sadly mine are not … probably need to take this tank to a yoga class.)

I have worn the women’s version of these tights and can attest they keep your legs warm and free on the coldest winter runs. That’s thanks to strategically-placed fleece where you want to stay warm and stretch where you want to move. The waistband is, crucially, very comfortable and fully adjustable thanks to the drawstring.

Whether you’re running, ski mountaineering, or curling up on the couch, sometimes you just want to be cozy. The Mountain Hardwear Summit Grid Big Grid Hoody, while a mouthful to say, is the answer during those times. A cinch around the hood and the waist makes it practical for cold outings. (The hood actually stays up! Cold air doesn’t fly up your torso!) And that kangaroo pocket is just begging for your hands to keep warm.

Once you switch to wool, you can’t go back. And this merino wool base layer is la creme da la creme. It’s sturdy but soft, warm but breathable, doesn’t stink, has a UPF of 20, and transitions beautifully from the trails to daily life.

I live in these socks during winter, both on the run and in daily life. That’s because they are super warm but also super fine. In other words, I can wear my normal shoe size without squeezing my toes. Bonus points for fun patterns.

I’ve been using an older version of this pillow for years and can confidently say whether you’re fastpacking or simply want to sleep better when you’re traveling, this Sea to Summit Aeros Premium Pillow will revolutionize your life. While it’s inflatable and weighs next to nothing (3.5 oz for the regular size), it’s got a memory foam topper that will fool you otherwise. (Pssst, it would also make an excellent gift!)
