Rethinking Cybersecurity Awareness in the AI Era

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cybersecurity In-Depth: Feature articles on security strategy, latest trends, and people to know.

This installment of the Reporters' Notebook video series discusses some of the trends and topics from Cybersecurity Awareness Month, from using AI for better training to educating next-generation workforces on modern risks and emerging threats.

AI is changing the concept of cybersecurity awareness in more ways than one.

In the latest installment of our monthly Reporters' Notebook video series, Cybersecurity Dive's Eric Geller, TechTarget Cybersecurity's Sharon Shea, and Dark Reading's Rob Wright discuss some of the trends they've observed during Cybersecurity Awareness Month, which has shifted during the AI era with the emergence of news threats as well as revamped defense strategies.

As we’ve seen in years past, cybersecurity awareness training often becomes a compliance checkbox exercise rather than a vehicle to educate employees and users on the latest threats. And unfortunately, the threat landscape today is changing at a frenetic pace. Threat actors are taking advantage of AI tools and platforms to accelerate, enhance, and scale their attacks, whether it's social engineering schemes or wide-ranging supply chain compromises.

Thankfully, as the editors explain, some organizations are updating their programs to take more specific, line-of-business approaches for training while also accounting for the evolving threats related to AI; some programs are even using AI to create more convincing phishing simulations. Meanwhile, several US colleges and universities have increased their education offerings for AI security.
