Jojo Siwa Teases Baby Plans With Boyfriend Chris Hughes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jojo Siwa, who’s been dating boyfriend Chris Hughes for over a year, revealed her hope that her next major chapter includes becoming a mom and having a baby.

It takes two to tango, but Jojo Siwa is looking toward bigger dance numbers.

Over a year and a half after the Dance Moms alum found love with Love Island UK’s Chris Hughes, she revealed she’s ready to become a dance mom of her own.

As revealed during the Nov. 25 episode of the Petticoat Revolution podcast, her ideal next chapter would be to “have a baby. Be a mom. That’s my next thing.”

And the 23-year-old is more than ready to hit pause on her professional career in the meantime.

“Obviously that's not a career thing, but like a life thing,” she explained. “Like career, career is doing her thing. She’s always there. She's been there for 15 years. She'll keep going, she'll keep being there.”

But this is not the first time Siwa has envisioned a long future with Hughes, who she met while filming Celebrity Big Brother in the UK in the spring of 2025.

n fact, Siwa previously revealed she puts her daydreams into practice when it comes to Hughes one day getting down on one knee.

“He got me this ring for Christmas last year,” Siwa told E! News of the jewelry she wore on her right hand in an interview alongside her boyfriend in October. "I wear this every day, I’ve never taken it off—sometimes I'll switch it to my other hand just see what it feels like.”

And while the 33-year-old admitted that Siwa’s trial run with an engagement ring was news to him, the revelation brought a smile to his face.

“I didn't know she did that, but now I do,” he said. “It's kind of cute.”

But sometimes, Siwa revealed, she shimmies a bit too close to the engagement sun for someone who lives so squarely in the public eye.

“I'll forget that I've done it,” she said of wearing her ring on that finger. “And I'm like, ‘Oh crap, it's been there too long. Someone's going to get a photo or something.’”

Though the couple both confirmed there aren’t any immediate plans for wedding bells to be ringing, Hughes affirmed, “One day, of course.”

As Siwa put it, “We're in a very happy phase of life right now.”

The couple's whirlwind romance unfolded for the whole world to see during their appearance on Celebrity Big Brother.

A year later, Siwa—who split from ex Kath Ebbs following her time on the U.K. the series—released the song "Serendipity" in honor of the now-couple's chance encounter.

"The only word to explain what happened on this day a year ago… Serendipity," Siwa wrote in an Instagram post. "Today, I’m reminded of the moment that quietly changed everything."

Less than a month after they put their bond on display in the Big Brother house and emphasizing they were just friends, Hughes and Siwa confirmed they were dating in late May 2025.

At the time, Siwa cheekily nodded at the romance during a cover of "Bette Davis Eyes" at one of her shows, in which she instead sang "Chris Hughes' eyes."

The Love Island alum joined Siwa at several stops of her Infinity Heart Tour, which spanned across Europe, the U.K. and the United States. In a sweet glimpse at their backstage moments, Siwa added of the tour in an Instagram post, "The show represents who I want to be as a person and the artist that I wanna be in this lifetime, and it’s translated so well to the crowd."

Siwa and Hughes stepped out for their first red carpet as a couple at the Dancers Against Cancer's 2025 Gala of the Stars in Beverly Hills, California.

Siwa shared a glimpse into the couple's favorite bonding activity: golfing in a sweet Instagram photo dump of her summertime adventures.

In September 2026, Hughes gave his followers a slice of life moment by filming Siwa arriving to his place in England—and naturally they shared a sweet embrace to boot.
