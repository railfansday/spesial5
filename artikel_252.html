The Nape Pro was Keychron’s first trackball device that was introduced at CES 2026 with a slim and compact design allowing it to be positioned right up against a keyboard. The company’s new T1 HE instead looks more like other trackball mice already available from companies like Logitech and Kensington with a 34mm ball positioned on the side to improve ergonomics. But since hands and fingers come in various sizes, the T1 HE allows the position of its trackball to be fine-tuned with minor positional adjustments so it rests squarely under your thumb without any reaching.

The T1 HE is now available for preorder through Keychron’s online store for $59.99 in black, white, or translucent color options. Shipping is expected to start in early December. A pair of dials on the underside of the mouse are used to adjust the vertical and horizontal position of the trackball, which can also be extended or retracted. The adjustments are limited to just a few millimeters in either direction, but those minor improvements to ergonomics could go a long way to making the T1 HE more comfortable to use on a regular basis.

The sensitivity of the T1 HE’s optical sensor can be adjusted from 100 DPI to 2,400 DPI using a button that cycles through presets or the Keychron Launcher software that also allows for other customizations including button remapping and creating macros. The mouse features six customizable buttons in total, and a 600mAh battery good for up to 106 hours of use in Bluetooth mode, or 98 hours when using an included 2.4GHz wireless receiver. If you don’t want to worry about charging, the T1 HE Wireless Adjustable Trackball Mouse can also be used with a USB-C cable.
