Amazon agreed to buy AI startup Decart's technology for about $7.5 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Decart's software makes AI models run across different chips, as Amazon positions its custom AI chips as a rival to Nvidia's

Cole Burston / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Amazon $AMZN has agreed to acquire the technology behind AI startup Decart, along with its founding team, for about $7.5 billion, according to Bloomberg.

The purchase covers the Decart Optimization Stack, a software tool built to help AI models perform well on varied kinds of hardware, as well as Decart's researchers and engineers, according to Bloomberg. Amazon made the announcement on its company blog Monday. Amazon is not buying Decart's AI models.

Decart's website describes the stack as software that runs workloads on Google $GOOGL TPUs, Amazon's Trainium AI chips and Nvidia $NVDA GPUs. The company says the stack also powers its own models, as well as outside language models and agentic workloads.

Decart began collaborating with Amazon last year and has already put some of its models on Trainium, according to Bloomberg. The startup dates to 2023, when a trio of Israeli engineers — Dean Leitersdorf, Orian Leitersdorf and Moshe Shalev — launched it. It also makes AI models for editing video as it streams and for simulating real-world physics.

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy plans to start offering Trainium chips next year to other companies that build data centers, according to Bloomberg. Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud customers can already use the chips. Amazon has been trying to make Trainium simpler for customers to adopt, and it pitches the chip as a competitor to the Nvidia hardware behind most AI models.

Crypto Briefing put the deal at $7 billion to $7.5 billion. Crypto Briefing also says the price could be paid through a mix of cash, stock and deferred installments tied to performance, and that the founders have committed to remain at Amazon for five years. Crypto Briefing had reported that Amazon was expected to unveil the deal during AWS re:Invent on Nov. 30.

Decart announced a $300 million funding round in May, led by Radical Ventures. The round valued the company at about $4 billion, according to Crypto Briefing.

Decart held talks about a sale to Anthropic for about $6 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Amazon has been expanding its custom chip work on several fronts. In September, AWS agreed to license chip design technology from Synopsys $SNPS in a deal valued at more than $1 billion, the companies said. Jassy has raised the prospect of selling chip racks to outside customers. Annualized revenue from Amazon's Trainium, Graviton and Nitro chip lines is roughly $20 billion.
