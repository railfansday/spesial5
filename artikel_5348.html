Willog Listed as Representative Vendor in the 2026 Gartner® Market Guide for Logistics Tracking and Monitoring Systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NEW YORK and SINGAPORE and TOKYO, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Willog, an AIoT-based supply chain intelligence company, announced it was included as a Representative Vendor in the 2026 Gartner's Market Guide for Logistics Tracking and Monitoring Systems.

Gartner Market Guide for Tracking and Monitoring Market Report

The guide, published October 21, 2026, is Gartner's initial coverage of this market.

Gartner says "transportation and warehouse visibility remains a key priority for shippers, logistics companies and customers," and Gartner also states that, "This research provides information on 17 LT&M vendors that are perceived as relevant in the market." We are pleased that Willog is one of them.

In our view Global enterprises use Gartner reports to find and shortlist solutions. Listed with other global providers, we think Willog gains a chance to be noticed by global customers.

Willog Vision, Connecting Recording, Response, Proof and Compensation

Willog records cargo condition, responds to issues immediately, proves what happened and connects losses to insurance. Willog Vision, launched in September, unites this flow as an integrated supply chain solution linking devices, software, AI and insurance. Willog Safe: an in-house sensor device recording temperature, humidity, shock, tilt, light and location.

This Year's Global Milestones and Key-Base Strategy

In February, Willog unveiled Willog ControlTower 2.0 at Manifest in Las Vegas. This year it became the first Korean company selected for the 17th cohort of Lloyd's Lab, and in September joined the 2027 FreightTech 100. In October, it received the Prime Minister's Award at the 34th Korea Logistics Awards. With the Gartner listing, Willog plans to continue customer discussions centered on its key bases in the U.S., Singapore and Japan.

Ji-hyun Yun, Co-CEO of Willog, said, "This listing is the result of steady effort built around our key bases," and "We expect it to give global companies a reason to notice and consider Willog as they search for and shortlist solutions." Yun added, "We will keep creating customer results by proving with data how much customer losses have fallen."
