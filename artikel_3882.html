GTA 6 Causes PS5 Consoles to Sell Out in US Before Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sony’s struggling to meet demand for the PS5 following last week’s release of GTA 6.

Many major retailers in the US, including PS Direct, are currently completely sold out of the system – although you may find isolated instances of stock in stores.

The platform holder has no promotions on hardware this Black Friday, presumably because it can’t meet demand at full-price. Remember, the console is significantly more expensive than it was at launch right now, due to spiralling global memory costs.

Writing on Bluesky, Circana analyst Mat Piscatella said that PS5 hardware purchases increased 2.4x year-over-year in America through the week ending 14th November, just prior to the release of Rockstar’s open world.

There was a significant surge in US new video game hardware buying during the week ending Nov 7th in anticipation of Grand Theft Auto VI. PlayStation 5 hardware unit sales were >2.4x the same week a year ago, Xbox Series unit sales increased by >2.3x. Source: Circana Retail Tracking Service

Sony says GTA 6 is the biggest launch in its history, and it observed “the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays” on 19th November.

It added that for 70% of those newly connected consoles, GTA 6 was the first game played.

I imagine Sony is trying to get as many systems onto store shelves as it possibly can to meet this unprecedented demand, and I suspect demand will remain high through the holidays now.

In response to the situation, GameStop recently raised the price of used PS5 Pro consoles way beyond their already-sizeable MSRP.
