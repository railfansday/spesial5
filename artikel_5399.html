Insurance Agent Mara Geraghty Explains Hurricane Deductibles in Florida Homeowners Insurance in HelloNation

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mara Geraghty explains how hurricane deductibles affect insurance claims, what homeowners may owe after a storm, and why reviewing coverage matters.

DAVENPORT, Fla., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- How do hurricane deductibles work in Florida homeowners insurance? The answer is provided in a HelloNation article featuring insights from Mara Geraghty, Insurance Agent of Davenport, Florida. The article explains why understanding these costs matters before a hurricane causes damage.

How Is a Hurricane Deductible Calculated?

The HelloNation article explains that a hurricane deductible often differs from a standard deductible. Instead of a fixed amount, it is usually a percentage of the home's dwelling coverage.

For example, a home with $300,000 in dwelling coverage and a 2% hurricane deductible would have a $6,000 deductible. At 5%, that amount would increase to $15,000.

The article notes that these amounts depend on the insured value, not the repair bill or market price.

How Do Hurricane Deductibles Affect Insurance Claims?

The article explains that homeowners must pay their deductible before insurance covers the remaining eligible damage.

If a hurricane causes $10,000 in covered damage and the deductible is $6,000, the insurer may pay $4,000, subject to policy terms.

The article also notes that Florida has rules governing when hurricane deductibles apply and how covered hurricane losses are handled within one calendar year.

What Should Homeowners Review Before Hurricane Season?

The HelloNation article recommends checking the insurance declarations page for dwelling coverage and deductible details.

Homeowners should also understand that standard homeowners insurance policies generally exclude flooding. Separate flood insurance may be needed for storm surge or rising water.

According to the article, reviewing deductible options can help homeowners compare yearly premiums with possible costs after a storm.

What Is a Hurricane Deductible in Florida?

Mara Geraghty, Insurance Agent of Davenport, Florida, explains through insights featured in HelloNation that a hurricane deductible is the amount homeowners pay toward covered hurricane damage before insurance pays.

How Much Is a Typical Florida Hurricane Deductible?

According to insights from Mara Geraghty, Insurance Agent of Davenport, Florida, common options include 2%, 5%, and 10% of dwelling coverage. Available choices depend on the insurer and policy.

Does a Hurricane Deductible Apply to Every Windstorm?

The article featuring Mara Geraghty explains that Florida law sets specific rules for hurricane deductibles. Wind damage from other storms may fall under different deductible terms.

Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Hurricane Flooding?

Based on Mara Geraghty's insights, homeowners insurance generally excludes flood damage. Separate flood insurance may provide protection against covered flooding or storm surge.

Where Can Homeowners Find Their Hurricane Deductible?

Mara Geraghty, Insurance Agent of Davenport, Florida, points homeowners toward their insurance declarations page. It usually identifies dwelling coverage and the applicable hurricane deductible.
