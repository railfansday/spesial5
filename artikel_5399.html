ModelBest opening up access to AI technology on an international scale

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

LISBON, Portugal, Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- This is a report from China Daily:

The global race for artificial intelligence is often perceived as a competition over who controls the largest data centers. However, ModelBest offers a different perspective: how inclusive intelligence becomes depends on its accessibility and how widely it can be deployed.

This Chinese on-device AI unicorn has made efficiency the cornerstone of its overseas expansion strategy. By November, its self-developed MiniCPM series of models had recorded more than 70 million downloads worldwide.

"MiniCPM is not just one model. It is an entire family," Li Dahai, CEO of ModelBest, said at the Web Summit 2026 in Lisbon, Portugal, which took place from Nov 9 to 12.

Each model serves a different form of intelligence, but they share one goal: higher intelligence density, faster speed, and lower cost on devices, he added.

That efficiency-first approach has been put to work abroad. In April, ModelBest revealed the Lao national model, LaoAI, which marked a landmark case of a Chinese AI company helping another nation build its own national AI foundation.

Built on the company's MiniCPM architecture and high-efficiency training methods, the system is integrated into G-Chat v2, the national government communications platform, and is used across government agencies and key industries.

Sovereign AI has largely been dominated by chipmakers, for whom sovereignty implies acquiring advanced computing power and constructing national data centers. LaoAI offers another path: instead of relying on a national supercomputing cluster, the team made multiple field research trips to Laos in the months leading up to the launch, building the system on high-efficiency model architecture rather than massive computing power.

The team also incorporated Lao culture and values into training through field research. The Lao translation capability of their model outperforms mainstream systems on international benchmarks.

Crucially, the underlying model and application programming interfaces are open to local developers. The complete model weights have been delivered to the Lao side, giving them control over everything from the model to the data.

"Efficiency is the key to making large model technology available to everyone," Li said. "In the Lao national model project, an 8-billion-parameter system achieved world-leading Lao-language capability. This demonstrates that developing countries need not wait for top-tier computing power to benefit from AI," he added.

The significance is more developmental than commercial: countries of the Global South no longer need access to high-end computing capacity to step into the AI era.

The same principle applies to businesses. Making AI affordable for small and medium-sized enterprises is often touted but rarely realized. ModelBest's on-device approach changes that. When inference happens on the device, small businesses need neither cloud capacity nor data uploads to access usable AI.

The technical foundation is what the company calls the "Densing Law". The capability density of large models doubles roughly every 3.5 months, reducing the number of parameters needed for a given level of intelligence.

For such businesses, on-device AI is not merely cheaper: it is their first realistic chance to possess AI capability, with data on-premises, no per-inference fees, and no dependence on network coverage.

From sovereign models to on-device intelligence, ModelBest's international narrative aims to share the benefits of China's AI advancements with more countries, ensuring that each nation can develop its own AI capabilities in its own languages.

This direction echoes national policy: China's 2026 Government Work Report endorsed open-source AI ecosystems, and this year's World Artificial Intelligence Conference and the World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit both advanced initiatives for a fair, open and sustainable global AI ecosystem.

For ModelBest, the overseas initiatives represent a microcosm of this broader vision. By prioritizing efficiency and openness, AI can be extended to countries and businesses that have been waiting on the sidelines.

Beyond expanding access, ModelBest is also rethinking how AI itself is built. The next step is to use AI to build AI itself.

"This is not only a technological change; it is a fundamental shift in how intelligence is produced," Li said.
