Instagram launches iPhone Duo-native app

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The new version features functions specifically designed to take advantage of the device’s larger screen.

Instagram has launched a dedicated iPhone Duo version of its app that uses the device’s expanded display capacity to enable more on-screen functions.

As shown in this example image, the main Instagram feed will open to a wider presentation format, with more room on the screen.

For Reels, the Duo version of IG will display posts and comments in a side-by-side view, which will make it easier to view both elements.

As explained by Instagram: “When your phone is open, you can read comments beside a full-size Reel. The video keeps playing while you read along, so you can follow the conversation without shrinking what you’re watching.”

Instagram added that it’s moved the control buttons to the sidebar, which will leave more space for photos and videos.

“When your phone is folded, Instagram Stories and Reels keep their familiar vertical format,” Instagram said.

Messages and notifications will open into a two-column layout, with the inbox display on one side and highlighted exchanges on the other.

The custom presentation format could provide new opportunities for engagement and interaction in the app by making it easier for users to view content and see the related comments on a single screen.

That could also provide some interesting content considerations for creators, although this display format is restricted to Duo users only, which is a limiting factor.

Either way, this is a new view for Instagram, which aligns with Apple’s latest display approach.

Instagram also launched a Duo version of its Edits app last week.
