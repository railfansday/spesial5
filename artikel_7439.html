Custom Data Processing: Retail Support Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Custom Data Processing, Inc. (CDP) is a premier provider of data management systems and services for the public health community. Since 1979, we’ve delivered technology that makes a difference, from WIC services and electronic health records to environmental health and data analytics.

Built on family values, we operate with integrity. Our diversified mix of solutions gives us a deeper perspective; one we use to understand the whole picture and serve public health professionals across the country.

As a Retail Support Specialist, you’ll help grocery stores and other WIC retailers accept WIC benefits through our WIC Direct EBT system. You’ll work support tickets from retailers, state agencies and third party processors, help test and certify point-of-sale (POS) systems, and support special projects like WIC online shopping.

POS testing and certification/ validation

The salary range for this position is $60,000 – $72,000. Actual compensation within that range is based on factors unique to each candidate during the hiring process. CDP also offers a comprehensive benefits package and meaningful opportunities for growth and advancement within the company.

This is a remote position open and open immediately.

At our core, we value our relationships, both internally and externally. We respect everyone’s contributions: our staff and customers all have a seat at the table. We listen, we comment, and we decide our way forward based on what is best for all.

Our relationships are paramount because they determine our long-term success. When everyone feels empowered, everyone succeeds.
