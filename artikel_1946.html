Bradford City boss Michael Duff and Doncaster Rovers rival Grant McCann on second-half fracas

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The fuse for a hitherto pretty quiet derby was lit by an on-pitch fracas involving Kieran Morgan and George Broadbent, with both benches clashing - and Duff and McCann copping bookings in the process.

City were leading 1-0 at the time thanks to Nick Powell's 55th-minute goal. Rovers levelled courtesy of Jordan Thomas' fine overhead kick, but substitute Stephen Humphrys soon put the hosts back in front.

That looked to be that, only for Rovers defender Sean Grehan to head home a dramatic leveller in the fifth minute of stoppage time in a 2-2 draw.

Duff said: "We were having an argument because they were saying he (Morgan) punched him (Broadbent) and he hasn't at all.

"They've had a little wrestle and thrown him to the ground a little bit - that's where me and Grant had a chat about it.

"It's something and nothing. I've been doing that with Grant for 30 years. We used to have arguments on PlayStation games, when we were 20 years old and who's going to walk the dog? And we'd kick lumps out of each other in training.

"So that's just two blokes who want to win. I think that's why we're still in a job. We're both nearly 50 years old.

"You look back, it's pathetic, really. But in the moment, it's... I don't get who that person is at any one moment in time. It could be my missus.

"If she doesn't want me to win, then I'm against her. And then it was literally nothing. There was a disagreement in terms of the incident.

"They thought he punched him. I knew he hadn't. That's all it was."

McCann added: "I don't know what happened.

"I watched it back. I think it was a coming together in the box and then Morgan retaliated to George.

"To be honest, when I watch the back, it's probably the right decision from Darren Drysdale. I don't think there was anything in it. "Michael was just in my technical area and I just said 'get out of it', that was it.

"He should know better not to be in my area or else he'll get kicked out of it.

"He was talking about the incident, but I'd not seen the incident.

"They talk to me about iPads and Bradford, but Michael watched his iPad before I had an idea."

Asked if he would have a post-match beer with Duff, he said: "Absolutely.. If he lets me in..."
