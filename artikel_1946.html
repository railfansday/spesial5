Horsham FC edge past Torquay as penalty seals FA Trophy victory – the match in 48 pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The midfielder’s 21st minute spot-kick, converted after Charlie Hester-Cook had been fouled by the Gulls’ Caleb Roberts, proved to be the difference between the two National League South rivals at the Fusion Aviation Community Stadium on Saturday.

In an end-to-end Trophy tie, the Hornets hit the woodwork three times in the first half and had plenty of opportunities in the second.

Torquay saw a fine effort superbly saved by Horsham stopper Sam Howes before clattering the crossbar – but the hosts held on to advance to the third round.

The Hornets will host Isthmian North outfit Hashtag United in the next round, which will be played on Saturday, December 12.

Horsham visit Dover Athletic in National League South this Saturday before welcoming Truro City on Tuesday, November 24.
