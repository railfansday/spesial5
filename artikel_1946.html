When Football Felt Like Football: A Trip Back to Portsmouth v West Ham, 1992

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Tonight’s mid table clash, yep, 10th play 12th, brought back memories of a West Ham United game in Division 1 back in 1992/1993. Without wanting to sound like Paul Whitehouse’s legendary Fast Show character, Ron Manager, and his famous cry of “jumpers for goalposts”, my mind drifted back to the footballing world of the 1990s this morning.

I recall watching the game on ITV, on a programme called the London Match, hosted by Ian St John, and a quick search on YouTube found some footage, with Greavsies side kick being  joined by Glenn Hoddle. Jim Rosenthal was at Fratton Park as part of the commentary team.

Portsmouth featured in the FA Cup semi finals the previous season. West Ham drew 1-1 with Derby County the week before the game, Trevor Morley cancelling out a clearance by Mitchell Thomas which had cannoned off Ludo for an own goal earlier in the game, which kept the Hammers in the top six.

With Billy Bonds at the helm, West Ham were unbeaten in six games, yet faced a Pompey team that had only played twice at home, won both and not conceded. They boasted the prolific striker Guy Whittingham up front, and with Jim Smith in the manager’s seat, were stiff opposition for us. Yet as he said in the warm up the Hammers were one of the favourites to go up.

Pre game the big news was welcoming back Julian Dicks our skipper who came off a three match ban.

The Hammers took the lead just before half time with Clive Allen (main  pic) scoring, yet despite an onslaught of the Pompey goal in the second half they couldn’t add to their tally.

Trevor Morley was denied by the woodwork, while Martin Allen (who was never afraid to shoot from distance) also had a long effort saved.

Happy memories, as it was a time when the club had a greater connection to us, the fans.

I’d settle for a clean sheet and a win tonight, how football has changed or as Ron said “Isn’t it, wasn’t it, marvellous”.

I have been a season ticket holder since the late eighties, so experienced the highs and lows of being a West Ham supporter. I previously wrote for OLAS and have contributed to a number of football publications in the past.

Great days indeed. The London match was the hangover from ITV losing tv rights to Sky. It was pretty good and we seemed to feature quite allot as Itv regionalised live matches. The production was always good as it was made by the same people that had presented the match for first division football for many years. That also turned out in true hammers fashion to be a topsy turned season for us as we got it over the line on the very last match v Cambridge.

That was a season of heartache for Pompey. Missed out to the Hammers on goal difference for promotion and then to Liverpool in the FA Cup semis for a first Cup Final since 1939. That was a great, entertaining team put together by the Bald Eagle.

Pompey fan – I was at that game. Hope you hammers enjoy the old fashioned nature of Fratton Park tonight…but not too much eh?
