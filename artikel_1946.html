Struggling Crystal Palace confirm Frank as new manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Frank is back in management after Crystal Palace confirmed the Dane has been appointed as their new manager.

Frank returns to the Premier League after being dismissed by Tottenham last season, coming in to replace Pierre Sage, who was sacked after just 16 games in charge.

Palace parted ways with Sage last week after a dismal start to 2026-27, with the Eagles sitting bottom of the Premier League.

Sage won just five games across all competitions (D3 L8), with Palace shipping 30 goals during his tenure. Among teams from Europe's top five leagues, only his former club Lens (31) have conceded more goals this campaign.

Frank's arrival comes nine months after he was sacked by Spurs following an eight-game winless run in the Premier League that left them 16th in the standings.

The 53-year-old impressed during a seven-year stint with Brentford, winning 41.8% of his 317 games in charge of the Bees (W132 D77 L108).

However, he was unable to replicate that with Spurs, averaging just 1.12 points-per-game with the club in the Premier League (29 points in 26 games) and accumulating a win rate of just 34.2% (P38 W13 D11 L14).

Frank, who has penned a deal through to the end of the 2028-29 season, had been linked with the Palace job in the summer after Oliver Glasner left for Nottingham Forest, with Fulham also reportedly interested before they appointed Alvaro Arbeloa.

The likes of Frank Lampard, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira, Kim Hellberg, Vitor Matos, Tony Popovic, Jens Berthel Askou and Ole Werner were all viewed as potential candidates to replace Sage.

But Palace have chosen to go with Frank, who will be the third manager to take charge of three different London clubs in the Premier League after Alan Pardew (Charlton Athletic, Crystal Palace, West Ham) and Harry Redknapp (Queens Park Rangers, Spurs, West Ham).

Coventry City will be Frank's first opponents this Saturday, with his first home match coming five days later against Real Sociedad in the Europa League.
