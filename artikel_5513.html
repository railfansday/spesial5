Gates Introduces Compact ThermalCool™ 2100SS Pump to Expand Data Center Portfolio

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

4U-Compatible Design Delivers High Performance for AI and High-Density Computing Applications

DENVER, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Gates Industrial Corporation Ltd. (NYSE: GTES), a global manufacturer of highly engineered power transmission and fluid power solutions, today introduced Gates ThermalCoolâ¢ 2100SS Pump, expanding the company's portfolio of liquid-cooling solutions for AI and high-density data center applications. The new stainless steel pump expands Gates participation across the data center cooling loop, adding a new, purpose-built active coolant circulation solution alongside its fluid conveyance and connection technologies.

Higher rack densities are changing where data center cooling equipment is located and how much space it can occupy. Rack-mounted coolant distribution units (CDUs) integrate coolant circulation within the IT rack, which puts tight size and integration demands on every component. Gates developed the ThermalCool 2100SS Pump to meet those demands, combining substantial output with a 4U-compatible design while combining substantial coolant flow with integrated active cooling for the motor. Delivered in a platform configurable for different CDU architectures, these capabilities position Gates to serve a wider range of data center coolant circulation applications including those with high ambient temperature operating conditions.

"As data centers pack more computing power into every rack, CDU manufacturers are facing infrastructure challenges that existing pumps weren't designed for," said Rebekah Headley, general manager of data centers at Gates. "We developed the ThermalCool 2100SS Pump in response to requirements from a leading CDU manufacturer, working as an engineering partner to address an emerging system need. That application-driven approach resulted in a configurable platform that takes the solution beyond the original application and into the broader data center cooling market."

Key features of Gates ThermalCool 2100SS Pump:

The ThermalCool 2100SS Pump expands Gates' capabilities across the data center cooling loop, complementing its portfolio of hoses, UQDs, fittings and engineered assemblies. Together, these technologies strengthen Gates' ability to help customers engineer the fluid path from coolant circulation through rack-level connection and conveyance. The Gates ThermalCool 2100SS Pump is expected to be available globally in early 2027.
