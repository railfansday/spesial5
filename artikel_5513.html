Farrah Aldjufrie and Zach Goldsmith Welcome Their First Child—Inside the Umansky Family’s Plans for Baby’s First Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kyle Richards' daughter, Farrah Aldjufrie, has reportedly given birth to a baby boy—three months after revealing the identity of her unborn child's father as none other than her "Buying Beverly Hills" co-star and The Agency colleague, Zach Goldsmith.

The 37-year-old property mogul, who is the daughter of Richards and her ex-husband, Guraish Aldjufrie, shared the news in July that she was expecting her first child.

Then, one month later, she hard launched her relationship with Goldsmith while sharing a video of the two of them celebrating their gender reveal.

Now, a source has told Page Six that the TV alum has welcomed her son; however, Aldjufrie has yet to confirm the news herself.

While Richards was attending the Maison D’Or cocktail launch in Los Angeles over the weekend, she reportedly told the outlet, “I’m on baby watch right now. I can’t believe [I’m gonna be a grandma]. I hope it’s not tonight, because am I gonna go to the hospital like this?”

Then, according to the outlet, someone later asked Richards if Aldjufrie was in labor, to which she replied, "I think so."

Meanwhile, Richards' sister, Kathy Hilton, told Page Six that she arrived to the event late because she was on the phone with the hospital.

“I’ve been calling to make sure this is not a false alarm because I’m gonna call the hospital to make sure that she gets right in and has a room because, you know, we’re very close to the hospital,” Hilton told the outlet.

Just days earlier, Richards' other daughter, Alexia, whom she shares with ex Mauricio Umansky, spoke with Page Six's Virtual Reali-Tea to reveal the family's holidays plans as they add a new addition.

"Well, typically, we always go to Aspen for the holidays. We spend Christmas and New Year's there. Even at our most times of difficulty, we always made sure that the family was together for the holidays. So the holidays are a special time for us," she said.

"We will not be in Aspen this year because my sister is giving birth any day now. So we're not going to make her get on a plane and fly to Aspen," Alexia added.

Aldjufrie previously revealed that she was expecting a baby boy in an adorable video.

"Zach Goldsmith and I are having a..." she wrote in the caption of the clip, in which the happy couple were seen embracing each other as they discovered they are having a baby boy.

"Real Housewives of Beverly Hills" star Richards and her estranged spouse, The Agency founder Mauricio—who has helped to raise Farrah since she was a young girl—were also in attendance for the special occasion.

Aldjufrie's half-sisters Alexia Umansky, Portia Umansky, and Sophia Umansky could also be seen celebrating when their unborn nephew's gender was announced.

It is unclear when Aldjufrie and Goldsmith began dating; however, the two appeared on the Netflix series, which followed Umansky's real estate business, together from November 2022 to March 2024. Aldjufrie ended an engagement to her former partner, Alex Manos, in March 2024.

Aldjufrie and Goldsmith still work together as real estate agents for Umansky's firm, The Agency, with the company's website listing both as active members of the team.

According to Goldsmith's bio, he focuses his efforts primarily on luxury real estate in Beverly Hills, where he is hailed as boasting "an array of nuanced market knowledge."

"With a philosophy of building bridges (not burning them), he has become a nationally recognized figure and an industry trendsetter," the bio goes on.

The news of their baby's gender came after Farrah revealed she was expecting.

She shared the news on social media on July 3, posting a series of photos that showcased her growing baby bump alongside ultrasound images.

In the caption, Farrah described the baby news as her "most exciting launch yet," revealing that she is due to welcome her little one in late November.

"My most exciting launch yet. Coming soon late November 2026," she captioned the image.

Her announcement was met with great excitement from her mother, who will become a grandmother for the first time when Aldjufrie gives birth.

"I can’t wait for this new chapter!! I love you so much Fairy!" the reality TV star commented on her daughter's announcement.

Umansky also expressed his delight at the news—making clear his continued bond with his stepdaughter.

"You are gorgeous and I’m so happy for all of us and this baby is going to be so loved," Umansky wrote. "I can’t wait wooohooo."

Aldjufrie also shared a glimpse of the moment she revealed the news to her sisters, posting a clip from the FaceTime call during which she shared her pregnancy announcement, even receiving a notification from Realtor.com® in the process.

Upon hearing the news, the siblings burst into tears and were left speechless.

Richards, who joined Aldjufrie for the FaceTime call, was also seen opening up to her other daughters about how she learned of the pregnancy, while the expectant mother's positive tests could be seen scattered on the table in front of her.

"I was like, 'I need to call Farrah, I need to catch up with her, I need to get an update on her life, even though we just did a big update the other day. But I needed a new update,'" Richards explained.

"So then I was waiting, my phone rang a few times, whatever, I was about to call her, and then she sent me a text, and I was like, 'That's so weird.' And I even said to you, 'That's so weird, I was just going to call you.' And then she said, 'I'm pregnant.'"

The expectant mother captioned the clip, "Telling my sisters."

Aldjufrie, who has built her own career in luxury real estate and has appeared alongside her family on reality TV, has long worked within The Agency, the brokerage founded by Umansky.

The pregnancy marks a joyful personal milestone for the real estate mogul, whose professional achievements have often played out in front of TV audiences.

Aldjufrie has previously revealed how Umansky helped to cultivate her love of real estate, explaining in a 2016 interview with The Daily Dish that she used to drive around to different listed properties in L.A. with her stepfather as a child and was fascinated by everything that went into selling them.

“I always have been interested in real estate since I was a little kid. I’d drive around to open houses with my mom and Mauricio when I was 6, 7, 8 years old. I’d love to see all the houses,” she said.

“I know L.A. geographically really, really well, having grown up here. I was an only child for the first eight years, so I spent a lot of time with them and was very interested in everything Mauricio was doing. But I have my own love for property and being in L.A., there's some of the most amazing properties and lifestyles in the world.”

According to her profile on The Agency's website, Aldjufrie first became an agent in 2013—and has since helped the company to achieve "over $5 billion in real estate sales."

Interestingly, while she continues her professional and personal relationship with The Agency's founder, Umansky, her mother made clear just a few months ago that she believes she is entitled to a significant chunk of her estranged husband's empire—telling her Bravo co-stars that she will fight to claim her half of the business that she says she helped to build should the couple end up filing for divorce.

Umansky founded The Agency alongside Billy Rose and Blair Chang in 2011, 15 years after he wed Richards—and by May 2022, the business had facilitated more than $57 billion worth of real estate sales, according to Forbes.

According to The Agency's website, the company currently operates more than 145 offices in 14 countries, while Umansky's bio notes that he has "achieved nearly $5 billion in real estate sales and holds the distinction of selling the most homes in the country priced above $20 million.

"He has represented some of the world's most noteworthy properties, including the Playboy Mansion—the first house in L.A. to sell above the $100 million mark—the Walt Disney Estate, and residences owned by Michael Jackson, Michael Jordan and Prince."

Indeed, in the 15 years since it was founded, The Agency has built up a wealth of clients, working with the likes of Lady Gaga, LeBron James, and Ben Affleck.

While it's unclear whether Richards would seek to be involved in the day-to-day running of the business that she hopes to claim a 50% share in, it's likely that she could be in line for a hefty payout if Umansky wishes to keep The Agency to himself.

However, Richards noted that she isn't quite ready to pull the plug on their marriage once and for all.
