ESR x Craig & Karl Launch "Pocket Full of Joy": Art You Can Carry Every Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ESR, a global tech accessories brand trusted by more than 130 million users worldwide, announced its 'Pocket Full of Joy' lineup, a new collection created in collaboration with internationally recognized artist duo Craig & Karl. The collection transforms the duo's bold, playful artwork into everyday tech accessories, bringing color, character, and creativity to the objects people carry most.

Rooted in ESR's "Tech Made Easier" philosophy, the collaboration marks a new creative chapter for the brand, extending its commitment to intuitive, functional technology into the world of art and self-expression. Known for their vibrant visual language and playful approach to contemporary culture, Craig & Karl share ESR's belief that great design should be accessible and part of everyday life. Together, they bring a fresh perspective to familiar tech essentials, combining practical innovation with artistic expression.

The idea behind 'Pocket Full of Joy' is simple: art doesn't have to live only on a wall or inside a gallery. It can move with you. By placing Craig & Karl's artwork on the objects that travel from home to work, across cities, and through the small moments in between, ESR and Craig & Karl turn everyday carry into a new canvas for self-expression.

Each design captures a different side of Craig & Karl's visual world. The Collage design brings together sticker-like icons, vivid colors, and playful symbols inspired by everyday life. OK introduces a bold graphic expression of ease and optimism, while HI transforms a simple daily essential into a cheerful greeting that can be carried in a pocket.

The collection pairs Craig & Karl's artwork with practical features designed for everyday use. The protective phone cases feature ESR's versatile Flex Stand, offering dual functionality as both a protective case and a convenient hands-free stand, alongside MagSafe compatibility for seamless use with magnetic accessories. The MagSafe-compatible wallets attach magnetically to compatible phones, keeping essential cards conveniently within reach while adding a playful visual statement.

"Collaborating with ESR on 'Pocket Full of Joy' was a great opportunity to bring our playful, colorful artwork to everyday essentials in a new way," artists Craig and Karl share. "We love the idea that something people reach for dozens of times a day, like their phone, can spark a little moment of happiness, express their personality, or even start a conversation. It's exciting to see our artwork come to life on ESR's products and become part of people's everyday experiences."

"'Pocket Full of Joy' marks ESR's first collaboration with an artist, bringing a new dimension of creativity to our 'Tech Made Easier' philosophy," said Elsie Chu, Senior Brand Director at ESR. "Craig & Karl were a natural partner for this exploration, sharing our belief that everyday essentials can be both functional and expressive. Together, we've created a collection that combines intuitive technology with bold artistic design, giving consumers an easier, more joyful way to express their individuality."

The ESR x Craig & Karl 'Pocket Full of Joy' Collection includes five products and product configurations across the ESR Classic Hybrid Magnetic Case with Flex Stand, compatible with select iPhone models including iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, and iPhone 17 Pro; the ESR Classic Magnetic Wallet Stand (Grip); the ESR Classic Magnetic Wallet Stand; and the ESR Classic Magnetic Wallet, compatible with iPhone 12 through iPhone 18 series devices. The ESR x Craig & Karl 'Pocket Full of Joy' Collection is available on ESR's website and Amazon.
