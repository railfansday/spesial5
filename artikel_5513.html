Moderna raised its full-year sales outlook after a bigger-than-expected profit

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The drugmaker's 2026 revenue growth forecast is 15% to 25%, up from up to 10%; its year-end cash outlook rose to $8.1 billion to $8.3 billion

Moderna $MRNA raised its 2026 revenue growth outlook to 15% to 25% on Friday, up from the previous forecast of up to 10%. The company made the move after reporting a third-quarter profit of $162 million, a reversal from the $200 million loss it posted a year earlier.

Third-quarter revenue rose 29% to $1.3 billion from $1.0 billion in the same period of 2025. The company said stronger COVID vaccine demand abroad and shipments tied to multiyear government agreements drove the increase. Revenue totaled $780 million from U.S. sales and $533 million from sales abroad. Diluted earnings per share were $0.38, compared with a loss of $0.51 a year earlier.

Analysts had expected a loss of $1.25 per share and revenue of $885.1 million, according to CNBC. Moderna stock gained about 3% before the market opened on Friday.

"Building on this momentum, we increased our full-year revenue growth outlook to 15%-25%, while continuing to reduce costs and strengthen our balance sheet," Chief Executive Officer Stéphane Bancel said in a statement. Bancel also credited both higher sales and lower costs for the quarter. "It's the sales that are stronger and the costs that are also lower," he told CNBC.

Moderna also lowered its 2026 research and development expense outlook to about $2.7 billion from about $2.9 billion. It raised its year-end cash and investments outlook to $8.1 billion to $8.3 billion from $4.7 billion to $5.2 billion, citing improved operational execution and proceeds from a convertible notes financing. In September, the company completed a convertible senior notes offering of about $3.0 billion in principal, which raised about $2.6 billion in net proceeds after capped call purchases and fees.

The company continues to expect about half of its 2026 revenue to come from the U.S. and half from international markets. Moderna has scheduled an analyst day for Nov. 12.

Moderna stock had risen more than 600% this year by mid-October, as investors reacted to Phase 3 melanoma data and the company's addition to the Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 index. The stock has since surged past a 700% gain for the year.

In October, Moderna and Merck $MRK presented Phase 3 results at the European Society for Medical Oncology Congress for their personalized cancer vaccine intismeran autogene. In combination with Keytruda, it reduced the risk of recurrence or death by 42% compared with Keytruda alone in resected high-risk melanoma. Investors sold Moderna stock when a portion of the results surfaced on social media a few days ahead of the presentation, according to CNBC.
