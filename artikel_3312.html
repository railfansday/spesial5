Taylor Swift Spotted With Karlie Kloss At Chiefs Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Former Project Runway host Karlie Kloss and husband Joshua Kushner just made a deal to revive LIFE magazine and now, it seems, she’s living the life of a showgirl. Specifically, Kloss was spotted on Sunday seated next to Taylor Swift in the Life of a Showgirl superstar’s box at the Chiefs game.

Kloss and Swift are longtime collaborators. The supermodel appeared in the singer’s video for the 2015 tune “Bad Blood” as well as in video segments and onstage during Swift’s 1989 World Tour. That same year, the duo appeared together on the cover of Vogue.

Kloss likely had to go out of her way to attend the game. It was not in New York, where she and Kushner have a home, but at Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium. Assuming Kloss was rooting for the Chiefs and Swift’s husband, Travis Kelce, the journey was worth it; Kansas City won 35-12. Watch on Deadline

Kloss and Kushner, who is the brother of Trump Administration special advisor Jared Kushner, in 2023 acquired i-D magazine through their Bedford Media, for which she is chairwoman. In 2020, she was part of an investor group that bought W Media, which publishes the like-named oversized fashion magazine. Other investors included Jason Blum, Kaia Gerber and and Lewis Hamilton.

Kloss may soon be able to return the favor and invite Swift to sit in her box a Lakers’ game. Her husband recently announced that he was purchasing the fabled L.A. team — along with Bob Iger — for $12.5B.

Swift was later joined in the box by WNBA superstar Caitlyn Clark.
