Russell Brand’s “Appalling” Prison Message Being “Urgently” Investigated, Says 10 Downing Street

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A spokesperson for British Prime Minister Andy Burnham has decried the “appalling” message delivered by Russell Brand from behind bars.

On Sunday, Brand’s wife, Laura Brand, delivered some words on behalf of her husband on the Forgetting Sarah Marshall star’s YouTube channel.

Brand said he was “not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for” and confirmed that he was incarcerated at Littlehey Prison, a jail for men who have been convicted of sexual offenses.

Earlier this month, Brand was found guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. He was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for his crimes. On Monday, 10 Downing Street said: “This is clearly appalling and is being urgently investigated. We expect action to be taken if he’s found to have broken the rules.

“Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims, and speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances. Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.”

Reading from a notepad, Laura Brand said: “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”

She continued: “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders’ prison in Europe.

“The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind, and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.

“I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me. Let us all love, pray for, and forgive one another as God forgave us.”

An audibly emotional Laura Brand added: “Please support my treasured wife and family. I feel your prayers, your brother in Christ. Russell.”

Laura opened the YouTube video by saying that she was streaming to “continue the good work that was being established here in this Sunday Service community. Something very, very close to mine and Russell’s heart.”
