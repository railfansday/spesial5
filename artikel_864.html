Rooney revelations important - Bradford boss Duff

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Michael Duff was appointed Bradford City manager last month

Warning: This article contains references to suicide

Bradford City boss Michael Duff has praised former England and Manchester United captain Wayne Rooney for speaking out about the mental health struggles he had during his playing career.

Rooney, 41, told Gabby Logan on the Life at the Midpoint, external podcast that he was "contemplating suicide" and drinking excessively when his son Kai, now 17, was a baby.

Duff said that Rooney's comments were "important".

"People just see a superstar earning Â£300,000 a week and think 'it must be all right for him' - I can assure you none of our lads are earning that but they've all got their own lives," he told BBC Radio Leeds.

"Someone at that level to come out and say at the height of his fame and playing at the top level... the release is the football pitch. That's where they're happiest.

"I think it's really good what he's done, I think it's important."

During his own playing career, Duff played alongside Clarke Carlisle, who attempted to kill himself in 2014.

The former Northern Ireland international said players "weren't educated" to look for signs that team-mates were struggling at that time.

Duff said that social media brought added issues for modern players.

"Part of the job is you get stick and abuse and sometimes it goes too far," he said.

"Everyone has an opinion but you aren't allowed to have one back. They're human beings.

"I don't go on social media. If we win people will say good things, if we don't they won't.

"There's other pressure that people don't see. They see you putting on a shirt and playing in front of 20,000 which is amazing but there's loads of other pressure in that, whether it's money or providing for a family or caring for an ill parent or all these other things the outside world doesn't see."
