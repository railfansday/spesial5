Ethereum Foundation Sells ETH as Bitcoin (BTC) Price Drops to 2-Year Low

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Ethereum Foundation-linked wallet dumped another 2,500 $ETH ($3.88M), according to the Arkham database.

Wallet “0x5CBC” holds around $44.8 million in crypto, including $38.4 million of SUSDS and $6.41 million of Ethereum. Its latest transfer was on November 21, 2026.

Previously, another two wallets linked to the Ethereum Foundation transferred 4,393 ETH ($11.1 million at that time) to the Kraken exchange on October 12.

This transactions are not impulsive moves but part of a carefully planned 10-year strategy. Over the past decade, a total of 49,751 ETH has been sent from these addresses to exchanges.

The average selling price was a modest $658 by today's standards, although it’s worth noting that these coins were originally acquired in the project's early days, when ETH was worth less than a dollar.

Ethereum is currently going through one of its most challenging times in recent memory. Investor sentiment is still low, network activity has dispersed throughout Layer-2 solutions.

In addition to that, Ethereum has spent most of 2026 underperforming many rival ecosystems.

Earlier this year, Hsiao-Wei Wang has stepped down from the roles of co-executive director and board member of the Ethereum Foundation. In February, fellow co-Executive Director Tomasz Stańczak also stepped down.

Ethereum foundation has been selling ETH in 2026 during steep price movements, both up and down. For instance, BitMine acquired 10,000 ETH at an average price of $2,292 this June.

A similar sale one week earlier, where another 10,000 ETH was sold at $2,387 per coin.

On Friday, Bitcoin price dropped below $60,000. At some point, it fell 5.10% to $55,830, the lowest level since September 2024.

Liquidations on crypto exchanges reached $1.57 billion over 24 hours on Nov. 20. According to CoinGlass, 265,525 traders were liquidated over 24 hours, and long positions accounted for $1.43 billion of the total.

Bitcoin saw $554.28 million in liquidations, and 91.3% of that amount came from long positions.

Ethereum fell below $1,700 on Nov. 20, and ETH traders saw $331.08 million in liquidations.
