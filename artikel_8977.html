Text description provided by the architects. Almost 500 kilometers above the Arctic Circle, Båtsfjord lies at the edge of Norway, shaped by the sea, the climate and a strong fishing tradition. Known as Norway's fishing capital, the town is also home to a diverse international community. With harsh weather often limiting opportunities to gather outdoors, the municipality envisioned a new public place by the harbor. Their request was a sauna complex with meeting spaces to encourage people to connect and to enhance the sense of community.
