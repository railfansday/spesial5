How LeBron James' move from Lakers to 76ers unlocked both teams' superpowers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The NBA standings, heading into games scheduled for Wednesday, Nov. 18, tell a remarkable story involving LeBron James.

The Los Angeles Lakers, the team James left before the 2026-27 season, are 13-2 and off to their best start since 2008-09 (14-1 to start that season). That season, the Lakers won the NBA title.

The Philadelphia 76ers, the team James joined before this season, are 12-3 and off to their best start since 2000-01. That season, the 76ers reached the NBA Finals and lost to…the Lakers.

"You never know what… change can totally affect your team,'' said Randy Pfund, part of the Lakers coaching staff from 1985 to 1994 and general manager of the Miami Heat from 1995 to 2008.

Of course the oddity here is, four weeks into the season, the team James left and the team he joined both appear to have gotten better.

Analytics can help explain how the Lakers are thriving without James. Especially as presented by Vangelis Sarlis, a Greek postdoctoral researcher who has mined NBA data.

"I would describe it as role redistribution rather than addition by subtraction,'' Vangelis Sarlis told USA TODAY Sports. "…The more persuasive analytical explanation is that LeBron's departure clarified roles.''

With James no longer playing for the Lakers, Luka Dončić leads the league in scoring with 38.8 points per game and twice has scored more than 50 points.

"Luka Dončić is now the unquestioned primary creator and Austin Reaves the secondary creator, with personnel around them designed for spacing, screening and finishing,'' said Sarlis, whose research team includes George Papageorgiou and professor Christos Tjortjis at International Hellenic University in Thermi, Greece.

Their work includes the 2024 academic paper titled "Leveraging Sports Analytics and Association Rule Mining to Uncover Recovery and Economic Impacts in NBA Basketball."

Through 14 games, Sarlis pointed out that the Lakers' offensive rating had risen from 118.2 last season to about 123 points per 100 possessions, the best in the league. He also cited NBA tracking that shows the Lakers have experienced the league's biggest jump in transition scoring, from 22.6 to 26.2 points per game.

"That supports the observation that the offense looks quicker and more fluid,'' Sarlis said. He also noted Dončić's usage rate of 40.2%, which leads the league in adding to the Lakers' offense: "It's faster, but it is more concentrated, not less.''

Then there was James' $52 million salary with the Lakers.

The funds once spent on him allowed the Lakers to re-sign Reeves and overhaul the roster. Key additions include center Walker Kessler, who's averaging 14.5 points per game and 10.9 rebounds per game; guard Quentin Grimes, who is averaging 12.1 points per game and scored 47 points in the Lakers' 118-114 overtime victory against the Charlotte Hornets Tuesday, Nov. 17; and Sandro Mamukelashvili and Collin Sexton, both notable contributors.

"That personnel is…the most concrete link to LeBron's departure,'' Sarlis said.

LeBron James is not the same player who won four NBA titles, including one with the Lakers during the COVID-shortened season in 2020.

In 11 games played this season, James' shooting percentage (.443) is the worst since his rookie year and his 3-point shooting percentage (.225) is the worst of his career. But Bob Whitsitt, former general manager of the Seattle Supersonics and Portland Trail Blazers, suggested the early-season shooting trouble obscures James' contributions.

"He's always been a good passer, but maybe right now his passing is a little bit more of what's needed in Philly than the other things he's doing,'' Whitsitt said.

James leads the 76ers with 6.9 assists per game. No one seems to be overly concerned he's averaging only 15.1 points on a team with potent scorers like Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Joel Embiid.

"Rather than expecting LeBron to remain the primary scorer, the 76ers can utilize him as a fourth scoring option, playmaker and facilitator alongside Embiid, Maxey, and Brown,'' said Sarlis, the Greek researcher. "His experience and basketball intelligence could help optimize team rotations and strengthen Philadelphia's pursuit of another championship.''

Pfund won two NBA championship rings as a member of the Lakers' coaching staff from 1985 to 1994. He won a third NBA championship ring as the general manager of the Miami Heat from 1995 to 2008.

He also spent most of his NBA career working for legendary Pat Riley.

"Pat always believed you could never have too many veteran guys who would come through in the clutch,'' Pfund said.

Sarlis said his team's research shows James, 41, is among the most consistent historical performers in decisive moment.

Clutch veterans are most valuable in the playoffs, said Pfund, who cited two examples.

In February 1987, when Pfund was an assistant on the Lakers' coaching staff, Lakers traded for center Mychal Thompson. He was was an 8th-year NBA veteran. Not only did he prove to be a capable backup center behind Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, he also played a role in the Lakers' winning the 1987 NBA title.

Then in 2005, when Pfund was the general manager of the Heat, the team at Riley's behest traded for Antoine Walker. He was an 11th year veteran and three-time All Star with the Boston Celtics.

"He was pretty average all year,'' Pfund said. "And people were (saying), man, they spent too much money and whatever.''

But the veteran player delivered in the postseason., according Pfund.

"Antoine Walker had made a lot of big baskets,'' Pfund said. "He didn't hesitate at all.''

The Heat beat the Dallas Mavericks in the 2006 NBA title, winning the franchise's first NBA title.

It's still five weeks away from the NBA's slate of Christmas Day games, which this year will include -- you guessed it -- the 76ers vs. the Lakers at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.

But Whitsitt, the former NBA general manager, allowed himself to think well beyond Christmas.

"What kind of story would it be if the Lakers played the Sixers in the Finals?'' he said.

It's an enticing possibility as Whitsitt talked about what he expects to happen next. James will continue to blend in with his new teammates. The Lakers will eventually tighten up on defense. Hopefully, both teams will stay healthy as the season unfolds.

"They're both fun to watch,'' Whitsitt said.
