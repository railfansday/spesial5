Cam Jordan will become third defensive player to play 250 games with same team

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

When the Saints host the Browns on Sunday, New Orleans defensive end Cam Jordan will reach a rare milestone.

The Week 9 game will be Jordan’s 250th. Via NBC Sports research, Jordan will become only the third defensive player in NFL history to play 250 or more games with the same team.

The others are Darrell Green, who played 295 games for Washington from 1983 through 2002, and Jim Marshall, who played 270 games for the Vikings from 1961 through 1979.

Jordan, the son of former Vikings tight end Steve Jordan, was a first-round pick in 2011. His dad played in 176 games from 1982 through 1994.

Cam is an eight-time Pro Bowler, with 132.0 career sacks. He’s currently 22nd on the all-time list.
