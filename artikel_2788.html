Jeffery Simmons remains limited at Bears practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons remained a limited participant at Bears practice on Thursday.

Simmons landed on the injury report Wednesday with a knee issue, but it was an estimation because the team only did a walkthrough. While he remained in the same category at their actual practice on Thursday, it does not appear to be a major source of concern for the team.

Defensive coordinator Dennis Allen told reporters at a press conference that he doesn’t anticipate Simmons being at risk of missing Sunday’s game against the Saints.

Wide receiver Luther Burden (concussion), running back Kyle Monangai (toe), and defensive back Malik Muhammad (toe) were listed as full participants for the second day in a row. Cornerback Kyler Gordon (shoulder) remained out of practice.
