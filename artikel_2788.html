Spurs Dylan Harper slips, leaves game with apparent groin injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The San Antonio Spurs lost their 12-game win streak Tuesday night at the hands of the Pistons, but they may have lost someone more important to injury.

Second-year guard Dylan Harper went down with just more than a minute left in the game with an apparent groin injury after slipping on a wet spot on the floor. He stayed down while play continued on the other end, and when it finally stopped he had to be helped off the court and back to the locker room.

Coach Mitch Johnson had no update after the game, however, quotes from his teammates suggested Fox may miss some time (quote via Jared Weiss of The Athletic).

"At certain parts of [last] season where we didn't have certain guys, that's what made us good, was we were able to cover for guys when they were out," De'Aaron Fox said. "Right now, it looks like we might have to do that again."

Harper has taken a step forward in his second season, averaging 18.4 points on 50.5% shooting, plus 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists per game — he looks like he will be the second-best Spur in a year or two (if he's not already). He is a very good defender and when he is on the court the Spurs have. +22.3 net rating (and they are in the negative when he is off the court, although some of that is tied to heavy minutes next to Victor Wembanyama).

The Spurs have the depth to withstand Harper being out for a while, it will mean more Julian Champagnie and Keldon Johnson. However, for the postseason, the Spurs will need Harper, and groin injuries can be tricky and easy to re-injure, so expect San Antonio to be very cautious about bringing him back.
