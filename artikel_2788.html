Bijan Robinson having big game in Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Falcons running back Bijan Robinson is having a big game against the Bengals in Madrid.

With six minutes left in the third quarter, Robinson has 80 rushing yards and 50 receiving yards, and his one-yard touchdown run in the third quarter brought the Falcons to within one score as they trail the Bengals 24-17.

Bengals wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase has been the story of the game so far, but Robinson is also playing very well, as two of the league’s best offensive playmakers are putting on a show for an international audience.

As is so often the case with international games played on soccer fields, the footing has been a concern. Several Falcons reportedly changed their cleats to get better traction, and Chase and Joe burrow were seen before the game taking a close look at what appeared to be an uneven part of the playing surface.

But the footing hasn’t slowed down Robinson or Chase.
