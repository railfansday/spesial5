Sunday Night Football: Mike Evans active, Dre Greenlaw inactive for 49ers-Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Veteran 49ers receiver Mike Evans is officially back.

Evans is active for Sunday’s matchup against the Vikings in Mexico City. Evans has not played since San Francisco’s victory over Washington on Oct. 19.

In his first season with the 49ers, Evans has caught 27 passes for 420 yards with three TDs.

The 49ers, however, will not have linebacker Dre Greenlaw on Sunday, as he’s inactive with a neck injury.

The Vikings also have a significant contributor who won’t play, as cornerback Isaiah Rodgers is inactive with a quad injury. He was limited on Thursday and Friday’s injury reports and was listed as questionable for the contest.

Minnesota’s inactives are Rodgers, quarterback Kyler Murray, receiver Jordan Addison, quarterback Max Brosmer, and safety Jay Ward. Brosmer is the Vikings’ emergency third QB behind starter Kirk Cousins and backup Carson Wentz.

San Francisco’s inactives are Greenlaw, offensive lineman Enrique Cruz, defensive end Khalid Kareem, receiver Demarcus Robinson, and quarterback Kurtis Rourke. Rourke is the 49ers’ emergency third QB.
