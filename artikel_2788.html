Mike Evans remains out of practice, 49ers call him week-to-week

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It looks like the 49ers will be without wide receiver Mike Evans for at least one more game.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan said at his Wednesday press conference that Evans will remain out of practice on Wednesday. The wideout has been dealing with Achilles tendinitis.

Shanahan said that Evans’s timeline to return to action is week-to-week, which suggests that he won’t be in consideration to play against the Cowboys this weekend. The 49ers host the Vikings and Seahawks after their trip to Dallas.

The 49ers traded for Keenan Allen on Tuesday and they could get Deebo Samuel back in the lineup this week. Samuel is set to practice after missing three games with a hamstring injury.

Cornerbacks Renardo Green (back) and Deommodore Lenoir (foot) are also out of the team’s first practice of the week.
