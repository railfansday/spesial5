Alabama football TE Josh Ford donned helmet with crutches for a reason

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Alabama football TE Josh Ford has a reputation for being a hitter, and a season-ending knee injury didn't prevent him from living up to it.

Prior to the Crimson Tide's 47-0 win over Chattanooga on Saturday, Nov. 21, Ford, despite being on crutches, wore a football helmet on the sideline. It's an odd look for any player who knows he won't be playing, but for the transfer from Oklahoma State, his helmet had a purpose.

"He got his helmet on and was head-butting people with a messed up knee," said fellow tight end Kaleb Edwards. "That's the kind of guy he is; he's going to hyped up no matter what."

Ford was known to do just that before his injury, doing what he could to lift intensity among his teammates. Since getting hurt in the fourth quarter of UA's road win at LSU, however, helping from the sideline is all the impact Ford can make for the balance of the season.

Ford made only five receptions for 41 yards and two touchdowns for the season, but was highly valued as a blocker who could handle both linemen and linebackers alike. Edwards has been among multiple players to see more action in Ford's absence, and caught three passes for a team-high 63 yards against the Mocs.

Alabama plays host to archrival Auburn next Saturday.
