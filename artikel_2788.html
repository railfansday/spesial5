3 thoughts as the Mavericks get their doors blown off, 133-94, at the Minnesota Timberwolves

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Dallas Mavericks (7-5) embarked on a three-game road trip with the first stop in Minnesota to take on the Wolves Thursday night. Entering on a three-game winning streak, the Mavericks couldn't get to four-straight as they lost 133-94.

The game got off to a shaky start, with Daniel Gafford committing a foul seven seconds in and Cooper Flagg tweaking his ankle less than 30 seconds later when he came down on Rudy Gobert's foot after attempting a shot. The Mavs settled in behind Caleb Martin getting some early reps at point guard and doing a fine job keeping the offense flowing. Gafford's second foul resulted in Morez Johnson, Jr. checking in, looking no worse for wear after missing a game due to injury. It was a tight game throughout, but with two minutes remaining in the frame, Minnesota peeled off a 12-0 scoring run, good for an eight-point lead (34-26).

The Mavs locked in to begin the second quarter and had a 7-0 run to draw closer. Naji Marshall, Zaccharie Risacher and Santi Aldama were a big part of Dallas pulling to within a point, but the Wolves responded quickly and got out to their largest lead of the game (14) with just over two minutes remaining in the half. Kyrie Irving nailed a cold-blooded three that made you think a run was coming, but the Wolves had other ideas. Dallas was down 67-52 at halftime.

Dallas has been good at making halftime adjustments this season. They clearly had a plan to attack the rim, with Flagg getting to the line on the first possession. Free throws were a welcome sight after neither team attempted even a single one in the second quarter. The plan was foiled almost instantly, however, with the Wolves jumping the lead to 24 points within the first three minutes of the quarter, at one point having connected on 22 of their prior 30 shots. Another blink of the eyes and this one was all but over. The Wolves looked significantly fresher than the Mavs, with everything looking very easy for the former and very difficult for the latter. The Mavs spent much of the remainder of the third quarter down by 30 or more points before limping into the fourth quarter down 103-69.

The fourth quarter afforded the starters some rest and gave some of the players who see limited time a chance to showcase themselves. Unfortunately Max Chrisite couldn't capitalize on a chance to log some minutes. He tweaked his ankle, negating even the potential silver lining of him finding a shooting rhythm.

It was a tale of two rims on a number of occasions. Dallas had a few shots go in and out, while Minnesota had the opposite luck. In the first quarter alone, the Wolves had two three-pointers nearly spin out, only to drop through the net. The Wolves had hot shooting even without the favorable rolls, connecting on 61.4% in the first half – nearly 20 percentage points higher than the Mavs across the first 24-minutes. When all was said and done, the Wolves shot 57.8% and the Mavs shot 37.2% overall.

The one part of the game Dallas had a meaningful edge on was points off turnovers. Their advantage waned a bit as the game devolved into garbage time, but for much of the game they were plus-10 or better in this category. The Mavs did show effort in this sloppy outing, not hanging their heads or giving up regardless of the story told by the scoreboard. Even with the blowout and garbage time, Dallas managed to outscore Minnesota in this particular category 29-25.

The Mavericks hung in the three-point battle relatively well in the first half, despite LaMelo Ball nailing all manner of shot from the next town over as he is wont to do. Unfortunately, the Mavs shot worse from deep as the game wore on, making the battle of the long ball very one-sided. The longball opened up the entire offense for the Wolves while the Dallas misses allowed a good rebounding opponent to get out and run.

The Wolves were rested and made the most of their reserved energy. Throughout the night they were totally in a groove getting whatever they wanted and looking like a fully cohesive unit. Meanwhile, Dallas struggled to find the right lineup and fell behind big. The game lasted 48 minutes, but it was over long before then. With two more games on the road trip, the starters at least got some rest before the next one Saturday night in Cleveland.
