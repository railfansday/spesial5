Cowboys place CB DaRon Bland on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Cowboys cornerback DaRon Bland injured an ankle in Sunday’s loss to the 49ers. The team had already ruled out Bland for Sunday’s game against the Titans.

Bland will also miss the next three games, too.

The Cowboys announced Saturday they have placed Bland on injured reserve.

Bland has totaled 38 tackles and seven passes defensed.

The Cowboys also announced they elevated running back Jashaun Corbin and cornerback Derion Kendrick from the practice squad. Both are eligible to play against the Titans.

Cornerback Devin Moore (hip) and safety Jalen Thompson (hamstring) will remain on injured reserve and are not eligible to play on Sunday. Thompson returned to practice this week and Moore’s practice window opened Nov. 11.
