49ers had enough to worry about — then Vikings got Kirk Cousins

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The 49ers were already going to learn something about themselves Sunday night in Mexico City.

Then the Vikings went and got Kirk Cousins.

Minnesota Vikings quarterback Kirk Cousins (8) looks on during the fourth quarter against the Green Bay Packers in Green Bay, Wis., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

What had the makings of a classic confrontation between San Francisco's offense and Minnesota's defense suddenly became something considerably more revealing. The Vikings have a quarterback their head coach trusts, throwing to receivers he knows, with a defense capable of carrying a team deep into January.

For the 49ers, that makes this a timely examination of where they stand among the NFC's best.

They will arrive in Mexico City with an offense averaging a league-leading 399.8 yards and 31.2 points per game, second in the NFL. Minnesota counters by allowing a league-low 14 points per game and 273.3 yards, second fewest.

The deeper numbers reinforce the matchup. The 49ers lead the league in offensive points per possession at three. The Vikings allow a league-low 1.24 points per possession.

Kyle Shanahan has the personnel and imagination to make even a good defense look uncomfortable. Brock Purdy can distribute the ball to multiple threats, and Christian McCaffrey's ability to move between the backfield and the passing game forces opponents to account for him wherever he lines up.

Brian Flores has spent the season making quarterbacks uncomfortable before they even take the snap. His fronts invite questions about who is coming, who is dropping and how much time the quarterback will have to sort through it.

Watching those two work against each other would have justified the trip by itself.

But Cousins' return changes the demands on San Francisco's defense, too.

Minnesota acquired him from the Raiders last week and immediately put him back in an offense he operated under Kevin O'Connell in 2022 and 2023. Five days after the trade, Cousins threw for 207 yards and two touchdowns in a 30-10 win at Green Bay.

Brock Purdy (13) of the San Francisco 49ers looks to pass against the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas. Getty Images

There was an interception mixed in. There will be adjustments as he catches up with everything that has changed since he left.

Still, the Vikings suddenly look capable of making an opponent pay for devoting its entire week to figuring out their defense.

Cousins understands O'Connell's expectations. He knows how Justin Jefferson sees coverage and where Jordan Addison can hurt it. Those relationships give Minnesota a chance to accelerate a transition that normally requires considerably more time.

And they give the 49ers a second problem to solve.

San Francisco just scored 46 points against Dallas and needed nearly every one of them. Allowing 40 points in a victory leaves plenty to address, especially with O'Connell getting another week to prepare his reunited offense.

The 49ers can welcome that challenge. Their 8-1 record has earned them a place near the front of the conference. Beating a 7-2 Minnesota team with this defense and a familiar quarterback settling back in would provide another reason to believe they can stay there.

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan, left, and general manager John Lynch watch players warm up on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Arlington, Texas. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

Sunday offers the kind of opponent that can expose a weakness while there is still time to fix it.

Or confirm that the 49ers have fewer of them than almost everyone else.

Two coaches operating at the top of their games will spend Sunday trying to take away what makes the other so effective.

Shanahan wants Purdy playing with clarity, using motion, formations and the threat of the run to create favorable throws. Flores wants that picture changing, preferably after Purdy has committed to what he thinks he sees.

McCaffrey could determine who gets his way. Sending him into a route gives Purdy another dangerous option. Keeping him in protection helps account for the pressure Flores brings.

Shanahan's challenge is preserving enough receiving options to punish Minnesota's aggression. For Flores, it is forcing quick decisions without making the answers obvious.

Every protection adjustment and late defensive movement will have a purpose.

O'Connell and Morris helped the Rams win a Super Bowl following the 2021 season, with O'Connell coordinating the offense and Morris running the defense. Those practice field confrontations now become part of the preparation for a game with considerable NFC implications.

O'Connell has his quarterback back, and with Cousins comes the freedom to call plays knowing how the man executing them will respond. That familiarity makes Minnesota feel more complete.

Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O'Connell looks on after a game against the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wis., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Morris understands how O'Connell builds an offense and how its concepts fit together. He can use that knowledge to challenge Cousins' first read and force him to hold the ball.

O'Connell will anticipate those adjustments, of course.

That is what makes this compelling. Two former colleagues know how much the other knows, and both must decide when to break their tendencies.

Something feels right about Cousins finding his way back to Minnesota after 2 ½ seasons away, with his recovery from the Achilles injury that ended his 2023 season part of that journey. He returns to a more complete team, with a defense capable of giving him a chance to finish what he started. The 49ers will make Minnesota earn every bit of this. But with another week alongside O'Connell, Cousins makes enough timely throws, and Flores finds one late stop to give the reunion another memorable chapter.

The Vikings sacked Jordan Love eight times in Cousins' return last Sunday. Purdy's protection will face a considerably tougher test than the reunion storyline alone suggests.
