Saints vs. Bears Prediction: Frozen in the Windy City?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 3-6 New Orleans Saints are headed to the Windy City this weekend to take on the Chicago Bears at Soldier Field. It's a matchup in which few are giving the Saints a chance to win, but New Orleans is on a mission looking to silence the doubters and pull off a major upset on the road.

Chicago enters Sunday's contest with an impressive 7-2 record, riding a four-game winning streak and are coming off a bye week. The Bears have emerged as one of the NFC's stronger teams, averaging nearly 27 points per game. They have been dominant on defense, particularly on third down, making it difficult for opposing offenses to sustain drives.

For the Saints, coming off a disappointing home loss to the Carolina Panthers, the challenge will be getting points on the board early and finding consistency on both sides of the football while limiting costly mistakes against a strong Chicago team.

Unfortunately, the Bears have the bigger talent, momentum and home-field advantage which will likely freeze out the Saints of any chance of getting a victory in Chicago.
