The holiday gift season is not far off. For me, I’d rather spend December enjoying myself than panic-shopping for someone who claims they “don’t need anything.” Especially when that same person has been borrowing my tools all year long. If you’ve got a DIYer, weekend tinkerer, or devoted Milwaukee fan on your gift list, now’s a good time to get some shopping out of the way—and you don’t need a several-hundred-dollar power tool to make their day.

As a tool guy myself, I’d much rather unwrap something useful than another novelty gift destined for the attic. Give me a compact drill/driver that makes a repair easier, a utility knife that replaces the battered one in my pocket, or a magnetic tape measure that saves me from constantly asking, “Hey, can you hold this?” Shopping for someone who practically lives under a car? A cordless power ratchet might be just the thing to loosen those stubborn fittings. And if we’re talking about a holiday gift exchange, who doesn’t want to bring the present everyone wants to steal?

With all of that in mind, we’ve rounded up these Milwaukee gifts for less than $100. Whether you’re shopping for a loved one building their first tool kit or someone who’s garage is already bathed in the glow of red tools and white lightning bolts, there’s something here well worth the time and energy it takes to wrap it.

Few things are more annoying than hunting through your tool kit for the proper-sized drive bit. This kit really covers the fastening basics, as it includes various sizes of star bits, Phillips-head bits, and an assortment of standard slot-head sizes. I particularly like that Milwaukee included 1/4- and 5/16-inch hex drive bits—two I’m usually rooting around for—in the sturdy kit.

Milwaukee Fastback Folding Utility Knife with Multifunctionality

This sharp, serrated knife lives in that space between a razor, a utility knife, and a bare-bones multi-tool. The housing is sturdy and well-built, and the knife itself holds an edge for an exceptionally long time. Serrations are perfect for cutting heavy rope, which might come in handy for the camper or boat enthusiast in your life. And the swing-out screwdriver is more than just a gimmick in that it’s strong enough to handle prying with the slot head in place or extracting a stubborn Phillips head with that drive head locked in. And in the end, you can crack a beer with the built-in opener.

Milwaukee 12V Cordless 1/2-Inch Hammer Drill/Driver

For the Milwaukee fan who already has M12 batteries on hand, this compact hammer drill is a gift that’ll get put to work over and over. Its brushless motor handles everyday drilling and driving, and the hammer setting adds the ability to drill holes in brick and masonry with the right bit. The chuck accommodates bits up to 1/2-inch in diameter, but its all-metal construction grips smaller bits, drivers, and nut runners with equal strength. Many contractors—electricians among them—favor its compact size, which makes the drill easier to maneuver in tight spaces or between studs. Beyond that, this drill is an easy pull for everything from hanging shelves to mounting brackets on a brick wall. Just keep in mind that this is a bare tool, so a battery and charger need to be supplied separately.
