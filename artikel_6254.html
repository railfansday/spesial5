Atmos Rewards Business Card Changes: Higher(ish) Fee, New Benefits

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Atmos Rewards is the loyalty program of Alaska and Hawaiian, and that includes three co-branded credit cards. There’s only one business credit card in the portfolio, and that’s the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card.

Along those lines, there’s a major update, as the card has just undergone a full refresh. The bad news is that the card’s annual fee has gone up considerably (at least for most people), and the benefits have also changed. Let’s take a look, because as you’d expect, the changes are a mixed bag.

The good news is that there’s a huge incentive to apply for the card, given the massive limited-time welcome bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days.

Link: Learn more about the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card

The Atmos Rewards Business Card is undergoing some major updates. This includes a higher annual fee, new rewards structure, and new set of perks. However, these changes only apply to new card members, as existing card members will keep the old benefits (and fee).

Let me emphasize that the card has a huge limited time welcome bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days. So that’s a major incentive to apply for the card, if you’re eligible. I value Atmos Rewards points at 1.5 cents each, so to me, that’s worth $1,350.

With that out of the way, let’s go over the details…

The Atmos Rewards Business Card now has a flat $195 annual fee, which covers the primary card member and any employee cards. Previously, the card had an annual fee of $70 per company, plus $25 per employee, so the minimum annual fee you’d pay would be $95.

It goes without saying that this change represents a significant increase in the annual fee, unless you had more than four employee credit cards on your account.

What has changed here is the new 10x points for booking through Atmos for Business (great for those who can use it), as well as a full overhaul of the 2x points categories. Previously the card offered 2x points on eligible gas, EV charging stations, shipping, and local transit purchases (including rideshare), while now it just offers 2x points on dining. As before, the card offers one status point for every $3 spent, with no cap.

The Atmos Rewards Business Card is picking up quite a few new benefits that it didn’t previously offer:

The refreshed card also keeps a first checked bag free and preferred boarding on Alaska and Hawaiian flights for the card member and up to six guests on the same reservation when the trip is paid with the card, a 20% rebate on eligible inflight purchases, a 10% rewards bonus for cardholders with an eligible Bank of America small business account, and no foreign transaction fees.

Let me emphasize that the card will no longer offer a $99 companion fare, so that’s being replaced by the new 50% off vouchers that can be earned with spending.

As of November 18, 2026, customers who apply for a new Atmos Rewards Business Card will be receiving the refreshed card, with the new benefits and fee.

Applications submitted before November 18 will continue under the prior card offering. What’s interesting is that current cardholders will keep their existing benefits, and will not be automatically moved over. However, you can call up Bank of America if you’d like to see about the opportunity of switching.

As of now, it sounds like there are no plans to transition people to the new system, so existing card members shouldn’t be negatively impacted by this.

The Atmos Rewards Business Card changes are a mixed bag. First, I want to once again emphasize that since existing card members aren’t impacted by these changes, I don’t think there should be too much frustration here.

In the past, the Atmos Rewards Business Card basically felt like the business version of the $95 annual fee Atmos™ Rewards Ascent Visa Signature® credit card (learn more), while now the card feels more like a hybrid between that and the $395 annual fee Atmos™ Rewards Summit Visa Infinite® Credit Card (learn more), in terms of both cost and benefits.

The card is also getting a new set of benefits, including a new rewards structure, additional perks, and new incentives for spending. People will have varying takes as to whether these changes are positive or negative, depending on their consumer patterns.

My biggest takeaway is that the bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points upon completing minimum spending is excellent, and a great incentive to apply for the card.
