Winter is coming. And if you step outside here on the East Coast, you’d think it’s already here. We’re inching closer to Thanksgiving weekend, and inevitably Christmas, meaning it’s only getting colder from here on out. This is always bittersweet, but I’m more prepared than ever. Especially when we get into the dog days of winter (I think that’s a thing?) and the dreaded “s” word rears its ugly head. I mean snow, if you didn’t catch my “drift.”

Fortunately, we’re also in one of the best times of the year for in-season (or technically, pre-season) shopping: Black Friday weekend. We’re already seeing tons of standout deals on snow gear, including winter jackets, hats, gloves, and accessories. Layering isn’t optional for the inevitable heavy-duty snow removal, so you'd better be prepared. I highlighted some of the best deals from our favorite retailers, including Amazon, Huckberry, and REI. Take a look at what’s in store ahead of the big weekend.
