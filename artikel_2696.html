Giants vs. Commanders, Week 10: Giants lose to Washington Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The New York Giants fall to the Washington Commanders 24-21 on Thursday Night Football. The Giants fall to 5-3-1 on the season while the Commanders now have a 3-5-1 record

The Giants fail to capitalize on a number of big opportunities at the end of the game. But none was bigger than the chance to put a strangle-hold on the NFC East with a win over Washington. Now, the Giants have given the Commanders' life in the division as well as the head-to-head tiebreaker in the playoff race.

The Giants will face the trade-happy 6-2 Jacksonville Jaguars in Week 11.

EAST RUTHERFORD, N.J. — The New York Giants will try to solidify their NFC East lead Thursday against the Washington Commanders in miserable wet, chilly conditions at MetLife Stadium.

The Giants and Commanders tied 27-27 in Week 5. New York is 5-2-1, looking to extend their lead in the division. The 2-5-1 Commanders are looking for a victory that gives them hope entering the second half of the season.

A key for the Giants will be controlling the running of Washington quarterback Jayden Daniels, who scrambled for 38- and 26-yard touchdowns in Week 5.

The heaviest of the rain is expected to be over before the 8:15 p.m. kickoff. Still, the windy, wet, chilly conditions could be tough to navigate.

Running back Cam Skattebo will be back in the lineup for the Giants after missing Sunday's game against the Philadelphia Eagles with a hamstring injury. J.J. McCarthy will be making his first start at MetLife Stadium as Giants quarterback.
