Mike Macdonald says 'Sam's our quarterback' amid chatter that Drew Lock should start for Seahawks over Darnold

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Drew Lock threw for 240 yards with 2 touchdowns and no interceptions Sunday as the Seattle Seahawks secured a 21-13 win over the Las Vegas Raiders.

The Seahawks are now 4-0 this season with Lock, who's played objectively better than Sam Darnold in place of the injured Seattle starter in four appearances. This has prompted chatter amongst fans that maybe the Seahawks are better off with Lock as their full-time starting quarterback.

That chatter reached head coach Mike Macdonald on Monday. He was quick to shoot down the idea during an interview with Seattle Sports radio.

"Drew's been fantastic. He's been phenomenal," Macdonald said. "But Sam's our quarterback. … To have Drew as our backup and play the way he has has been awesome."

It's an interesting spot for the Seahawks. Darnold had the second-best season of his career in his first season with Seattle last year, and the Seahawks won a Super Bowl.

Seattle's defense and playmakers on offense in Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Kenneth Walker were the primary driving forces of that championship run. But Darnold played his role well on a championship team. And moving away from a Super Bowl-winning quarterback the very next season could certainly be seen as a drastic step.

Drew Lock has been objectively better than Sam Darnold this season.

But outside of last season and his 2024 campaign with the Minnesota Vikings, Darnold's body of work leaves a lot to be desired. A former No. 3 draft pick, Darnold was firmly in bust territory after bouncing around and failing to hold jobs with the Jets, Panthers and 49ers before landing in Minnesota.

That version of Darnold is the one who's showed up in 2026.

In seven starts this season including the season-opener in which he played just one possession due to injury, Darnold's completed 60.2% of his passes for 7 yards per attempt with 10 touchdowns and 9 interceptions. Those are generally not numbers fitting of an NFL starter. The Seahawks are 2-4 in Darnold's starts excluding the season-opener, and his turnovers are a significant reason why.

By comparison, Darnold has completed 73.1% of his passes for 9 yards per attempt with 7 touchdowns and no interceptions. It's a small sample size from a career backup against a softer slate (Patriots, Cardinals, Cardinals, Raiders) than Darnold's faced (Commanders, Chargers, 49ers, Broncos, Chiefs, Bears). With two lost fumbles against the Raiders, he's not turnover free. But the numbers in his favor are compelling.

MacDonald, meanwhile, had to stop himself short with his praise of Darnold Monday before shifting to attribute Darnold's struggles to injury.

"We saw what Sam's been capable of both last year and in the — the beginning of the year for Sam has been a tough road," Macdonald said. … "Sam's our starting quarterback. I can't wait for him to be fully healthy probably for the first time."

The Seahawks have a bye this week. They'll come out of the bye for a key division showdown against the NFC-best 8-1 San Francisco 49ers on the other side.

Macdonald told reporters Monday that the Seahawks are "optimistic" that Darnold will be ready to return from a quadriceps injury to face the 49ers.

If he's healthy, expect Darnold to play. If he struggles in a Seahawks loss, expect this conversation to be renewed.
