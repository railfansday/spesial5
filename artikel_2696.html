Jaguars rule out WR Parker Washington against Titans with a shoulder injury that's 'week to week'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. (AP) — Jacksonville Jaguars receiver Parker Washington won't play Sunday against Tennessee after injuring a shoulder in practice.

Coach Liam Coen ruled out Washington on Friday, a day after being hurt in practice, and said he is considered "week to week."

Washington leads the Jaguars (7-3) with 44 receptions for 748 yards and six touchdowns. He caught 58 passes for 847 yards and five scores in 2025, becoming Trevor Lawrence's go-to guy down the stretch.

A sixth-round draft pick out of Penn State in 2023, Washington is in the final year of his rookie contract and could be a candidate for the team's franchise tag if the sides can't agree to a long-term deal before March 3, 2027.

His absence could mean more snaps for rookie Josh Cameron, Brian Thomas Jr. and potentially two-way standout Travis Hunter. Jakobi Meyers is likely to become Jacksonville's No. 1 receiver against the Titans (1-9) and maybe longer.
