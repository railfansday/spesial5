Welcome to CricketNews.com's live coverage of the 1st Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium in Rawalpindi.

Sri Lanka won the toss and chose to field first. Pakistan will bat on Day 1.

Pakistan come into the two-match Test series after beating Sri Lanka 2-1 in the T20Is and winning the ODI tri-series at home, but their red-ball form has been poor. They lost 3-0 to England in their last Test series and have won only two of their last nine Tests.

Babar Azam leads a young Pakistan side without Shan Masood, Noman Ali and Sajid Khan, while Mohammad Rizwan missed out despite a double century in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy. Sri Lanka, under new captain Kusal Mendis, are expected to play an extra spinner in the top seven alongside a three-man pace attack.

Sri Lanka are seventh in the WTC standings with one win in six Tests, while defeat here would end Pakistan's hopes of reaching the final. Pakistan lead the head-to-head 23-17 from 59 Tests, and the pitch in Rawalpindi is expected to be flat, especially in the first two days.
