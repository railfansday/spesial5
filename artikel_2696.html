Game Preview: Knicks at Raptors, November 15, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Knicks (6-6) visit the Raptors (5-7) at Scotiabank Arena. It's the first of two straight in Toronto. After a dropping their first three games, the Knicks seemed to be turning the tide before being cut down by Cleveland the Indiana. Toronto is 3-7 and, awaiting the eventual return of Kawhi Leonard, has lost three in a row.

In their last regular-season meeting, on April 10, the Knicks won 112-95 behind 29 points from Jalen Brunson and 22 points and 10 boards from Karl-Anthony Towns. Studly Sandro Mamukelashvili led Toronto from the bench with 17 points and eight rebounds. New York scored 27 points off turnovers that night.

Toronto averages 114.2 points, with a 115.3 offensive rating and a 113.9 defensive rating. The Raps move the ball well, ranking third with 28.1 assists per game, and their rate of 10.6 steals per game recovers a little of the ground lost by their unimpressive shooting (33% from deep, 54% from the field).
