Bayern reveal squad for Atletico Madrid clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The day before their Champions League clash with Atletico Madrid at the Wanda Metropolitano, Bayern Munich have announced their squad for the game.

Manuel Neuer and Michael Olise have both been included despite recent injury concerns.

Neuer has missed Bayern's last three games due to a foot problem, while Olise was forced off towards the end of Bayern's 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund on Saturday after picking up a knock.

However, both players took part in Bayern's final training session ahead of the game, although Olise trained with his right knee bandaged.

Bayern will be without Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry, who both picked up more serious injuries against Dortmund.

Luis Diaz, Jamal Musiala and Bara Sapoko Ndiaye are also still unavailable.
