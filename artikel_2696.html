Badgers senior guard out for season with foot injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Wisconsin Badgers got some bad news on Monday, as the school announced that guard Jack Janicki would undergo surgery for a foot injury sustained against Murray State on Friday, which will cost him the rest of the 2026-27 season.

Janicki, a senior, had averaged 19.2 minutes per game this season before Friday's contest. This season, he was averaging 4.0 points, 1.5 rebounds, and 1.3 steals per game, while shooting 50 percent from the field and 30 percent from three.

Following Friday's game, head coach Greg Gard said the initial look for Janicki's injury was 'not good' and that diagnosis was confirmed with Monday's news that he'll now miss the entire season.

The injury comes at a tumultuous time for the Badgers, who lost two straight at the Baha Mar Championship and are struggling to figure things out early in the season. Janicki was expected to be Wisconsin's top reserve wing and had started all three of the Badgers' preseason games before Braeden Carrington took over in the starting lineup to start the season.

Janicki's presence as a defender who could be versatile and guard multiple positions was valuable to the team as a glue guy off the bench. His experience, especially in Wisconsin's system, was another plus, and the Badgers made sure to retain him each of the last two offseasons.

The Badgers did enter the season with a lot of perceived depth in the backcourt and on the wing. Behind starters Owen Foxwell, Braeden Carrington, and Eian Elmer, the Badgers have Zach Kinziger, Hayden Jones, Jackson Ball, and Trey Autry, who could all fight for minutes.

So far, Wisconsin has trusted the backup point guard role to Kinziger, who is averaging 12 minutes per game and shooting 30 percent from the field and 25 percent from three. Kinziger is averaging 2.5 points, 1.3 rebounds, and 1.2 assists per game.

Jackson Ball, a true freshman, is also seeing a lot of early playing time, with his aggressiveness standing out. Ball barely played in the Denver, Coastal Carolina, and Seton Hall games, but logged 13, 19, and 22 minutes in the other three, with the latter number coming against Murray State on Friday.

Ball had 12 points off the bench against the Racers, shooting 5 of 11 from the field and 2 of 5 from three in the 70-53 loss.

Hayden Jones and Trey Autry have played very sparingly this season. Autry was expected to be a part of the rotation and even fight for a starting spot, but he's slipped behind Carrington and Ball early on, averaging 6.2 minutes per game while recording a DNP.

Perhaps it'll be on one of those two to step up and play a rotational role going forward.

But Wisconsin was a team already dealing with early adversity. Now, they lose a key rotation piece for the rest of the season, leaving more questions for Greg Gard and the coaching staff to think about.
