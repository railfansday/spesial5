Dolphins sign Max Llewellyn from practice squad, sign Jack Browning to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Dolphins signed edge rusher Max Llewellyn to the active roster from the practice squad. the team announced Wednesday. The Dolphins also signed punter Jack Browning to the practice squad.

It appears the Dolphins will have a new kicker, after signing Daniel Carlson to the practice squad Tuesday and a new punter and holder in Browning.

Punter Bradley Pinion was not practicing Wednesday, but the Dolphins have not released their injury report yet.

Browning has appeared in five regular-season games, all with the Buccaneers in 2024. He averaged 42.5 yards on 10 punts, with a net of 34.6 yards.

Browning spent a week with the Cardinals in training camp this summer.

Llewellyn, a seventh-round pick out of Iowa, joined the practice squad after being waived out of the preseason. He has used up his three practice squad elevations, suiting up for games against the 49ers in Week 2, the Jets in Week 7 and the Patriots in Week 8.

He has totaled six snaps on defense and eight on special teams.
