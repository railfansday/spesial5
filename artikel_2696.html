At NC State, Philip Rivers will be one of the lowest-paid Power 4 coaches

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former NFL quarterback Philip Rivers earned $244 million during his playing career. He won’t be adding to it appreciably during the early years of his coaching career.

Via Kennington Smith III of Front Office Sports, Rivers will make $3.25 million in his first year as the head coach at NC State.

That puts him among the lowest-paid Power 4 coaches whose compensation is publicly known, and in the same range as Cincinnati’s Scott Satterfield ($3.8 million), Oklahoma State’s Eric Morris ($3.8 million), West Virginia’s Rich Rodriguez ($3.6 million), and Iowa State’s Jimmy Rogers ($3 million).

The less Rivers takes, the more that is available for players. And Rivers is confident that the investment will be made.

“It’s been very clear in our talks that they’re committed to doing everything that they can do to help the football program,” Rivers said at his introductory press conference, via Smith. “This thing is still about people. Having belief and confidence in people. So for me, we don’t need to pull out spreadsheets and go through every penny; but it’s a belief that we’re going to do everything we can to get North Carolina State to the top.”

Rivers also realizes that his lack of experience points to a lower entry-level rate, beyond the question of leaving money around for his staff and his players.

“You can’t negate the experience I’ve had in 25 years, but the truth is I am a first-time head coach in Power 4 football,” Rivers said. “So I think in some ways that makes sense in that reflection from a contract standpoint, but those other things are true too.”

If it works, he’ll surely get more from NC State. And doors will open elsewhere. Earlier this year, Rivers was interviewed for the head-coaching vacancy with the Bills. If Rivers turns the Wolfpack into a contender, other NFL teams inevitably will come calling.
