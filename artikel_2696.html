Colts sign OT Wanya Morris off Cowboys practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Colts are adding an offensive lineman.

Wanya Morris is signing with Indianapolis’ 53-man roster off Dallas’ practice squad, ESPN’s Adam Schefter reports.

Morris, 26, was traded to the Falcons from the Chiefs in June, but was waived by Atlanta at roster cuts in August. He then joined the Cowboys in early September.

He has not appeared in a regular-season game so far in 2026.

Originally a Kansas City third-round pick, Morris has appeared in 43 career games with 16 starts.
