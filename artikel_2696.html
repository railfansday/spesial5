Lakers' Austin Reaves set to return Friday for showdown against Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Behind Luka Doncic's continued MVP-level play, the Lakers went 4-1 while Austin Reaves was out with a bruised heel, but their top-three offense in the league fell to the middle of the pack without him.

Which is why it's good news that Reaves will return to the Lakers lineup Friday night for a big early-season showdown against San Antonio, he has been upgraded to available.

Reaves was off to a fast start and is living up to his hefty offseason contract, averaging a career-high 24.7 points per game while shooting 40.9% from 3, and adding 4.7 assists a night.

Reaves return for a high-profile showdown between two of the best teams in the NBA to start the season, and also a game that will have a big say in who advances in the NBA Cup West Group C (the loser is eliminated, but whichever team wins needs some other help to come out of the group). The Spurs could be without guards Dylan Harper and De'Aaron Fox, as both are officially questionable (and likely game-time decisions).
