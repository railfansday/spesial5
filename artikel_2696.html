NFL news, live updates, trade deadline rumors: Vikings trade for QB Kirk Cousins; Colts don't trade Jonathan Taylor, send Josh Downs to Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Week 9 of the 2026 NFL season is in the books, and we've got the NFL trade deadline on deck, which is set for Tuesday afternoon at 4 p.m. ET. Buckle up!

The biggest news of the day — the Minnesota Vikings have traded for Kirk Cousins in a reported deal with the Las Vegas Raiders, sending a third-round pick to Vegas in exchange for the vet. The move reunites Cousins with the Vikings, for whom he last played in 2023 before suffering a torn Achilles, and gives Minnesota some much-needed support at quarterback following Monday night's abysmal loss under Carson Wentz.

The Jacksonville Jaguars kicked off the NFL trade news cycle for the week with a splash move to bolster the secondary, sending a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick to the Atlanta Falcons in exchange for Pro Bowl safety Jessie Bates III. They continued adding on defense throughout the day, also acquiring Davon Godchaux from the New Orleans Saints and former first-round pick Jordan Davis in a blockbuster deal with the Philadelphia Eagles.

The Indianapolis Colts were another team to watch ahead of the trade deadline, with star running back Jonathan Taylor at the crux of it all. But as was first reported by NFL insider Tom Pelissero, the Colts did not trade Taylor ahead of Tuesday's deadline. The Colts did, however, trade wide receiver Josh Downs, who gets a fresh start with the Kansas City Chiefs and quarterback Patrick Mahomes.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 9.

After a great deal of speculation was made that Jonathan Taylor could be moved ahead of the NFL trade deadline, the Indianapolis Colts are reportedly standing pat.

The team will not trade him ahead of the deadline, NFL insider Tom Pelissero reported Tuesday afternoon.

Pelissero reported that the Colts had received multiple offers for Taylor of a second-round pick-plus, but instead, the team will move forward with him as a foundational piece within the locker room as they look to rebuild.

Acquiring Kirk Cousins wasn't the only notable Vikings roster move on Tuesday.

Shortly after Tuesday's 4 p.m. ET NFL trade deadline, the Vikings announced that they have released wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. Per NFL insider Tom Pelissero, Beckham will re-sign with Minnesota's practice squad.

The transaction clears a spot on the 53-man roster that can make room for Cousins.

Beckham joined the Vikings after being released by the Giants three games into his return to New York. Beckham played in just one game for Minneosta, Monday's disastrous loss to the Bills that ultimately prompted them to trade for Cousins on Tuesday. He did not catch a pass for the Vikings.

The Ravens and Falcons pulled off a buzzer-beater trade right at the deadline.

Baltimore sent G Andrew Vorhees and a 2028 6th-round pick to Atlanta for C Ryan Neuzil, per The Athletic's Jonathan Jones.

Vorhees was a backup guard for the Ravens, while Neuzil was the Falcons' starting center.

The Miami Dolphins placed quarterback Malik Willis on injured reserve on Tuesday, the team announced.

Willis has had a lingering groin injury that has been impacting his mobility. The quarterback had an MRI, and it showed that it is now a core muscle injury. Willis doesn't need surgery, but the amount of rest required to recover made it make sense for Miami to add him to IR.

With Willis sidelined for at least the next month, backup quarterback Kyle McCord is expected to take over as the starter this weekend against the Indianapolis Colts, per ESPN's Adam Schefter. Willis is not expected to miss the remainder of the season.

The Tennessee Titans reportedly received interest in running back Tony Pollard at the trade deadline, but will keep the veteran around, according to ESPN.

With just minutes left before the deadline, it looks like it will be a weak deadline for running backs. Pollard isn't the only significant name staying put, as the Indianapolis Colts are reportedly holding on to Jonathan Taylor as well.

The Kansas City Chiefs have reportedly traded for wide receiver Josh Downs in a deal with the Indianapolis Colts, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported.

Per NFL insider Tom Pelissero, the Chiefs sent 2027 fourth- and fifth-round picks to the Colts in exchange for Downs and a 2027 seventh-rounder.

The Chiefs were reportedly in the market for a wide receiver for quarterback Patrick Mahomes, particularly after Xavier Worthy suffered a hamstring in Week 9. Now, they get a high-upside option in Downs, whose rookie contract will expire at the end of this season.

Downs amassed 2,494 receiving yards and 12 touchdowns over four seasons with the Colts, but his tenure there has largely been marked by turnover at the quarterback position.

In his career, he's caught passes from current QB Daniel Jones, Gardner Minshew II, Joe Flacco, Anthony Richardson Sr., Philip Rivers and Riley Leonard. Mahomes is no doubt an upgrade as Downs looks to prove himself in a contract year.

The Chargers are trading linebacker Daiyan Henley to the Broncos, per ESPN's Adam Schefter. L.A. gets back a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

Henley, 26, leads the Chargers with 51 tackles. He also has 2.5 tackles for loss, including 0.5 sacks. The linebacker has one interception, a forced fumble, and two fumble recoveries.

Henley is in the final year of his rookie deal.

With their sights set on a Super Bowl title, the Buffalo Bills are adding a dynamic defensive playmaker in trading for Tennessee Titans safety Amani Hooker, NFL insider Tom Pelissero reports.

Per ESPN's Adam Schefter, the Bills are sending the Titans a second-round draft pick and will also receive a fifth-round selection in return.

The move bolsters a defense that ranks 29th in the NFL in passing yards allowed per game. Hooker joins the Bills tied for the league lead with five interceptions alongside six passes defended.

The Bills made the move after reportedly inquiring about eight-time Pro Bowl Cardinals safety Budda Baker.

After watching Carson Wentz crash and burn during a 23-6 loss to the Buffalo Bills on "Monday Night Football" in Week 9, the Minnesota Vikings came into Tuesday with one extremely obvious goal: Get another quarterback.

The team followed through on that promise, acquiring Kirk Cousins in a trade with the Las Vegas Raiders, according to NFL insider Tom Pelissero.

The Raiders will reportedly receive a 2027 third-round pick in exchange for the veteran quarterback.

The Raiders also inquired about Justin Herbert, Daniel Jones and Geno Smith, NFL insider Jordan Schultz reports.

Cousins was the most obvious fit. Once it became clear the Vikings would be in the market for a quarterback at the trade deadline, both Cousins and Indianapolis Colts starter Daniel Jones came up as popular options, as both players spent time with the team in recent seasons.

Of that pair, Cousins has actually performed well this season. Thanks to his resurgence, the Raiders rank a surprising eighth in points per game in 2026. The team ranked dead last in that category last season.

Cousins has played a significant role in the team's offensive turnaround. The 38-year-old has thrown 20 touchdowns against eight interceptions in nine games. Only Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow has more passing touchdowns this season, and he's getting MVP buzz.

Keenan Allen is reportedly headed back to California, this time to play for the San Francisco 49ers.

Allen, best known for his time with the Chargers, is being traded midway through his first season with the Indianapolis Colts, according to Tom Pelissero of The Ringer and Netflix, who reported ahead of Tuesday's 4 p.m. ET deadline that the Colts are receiving a 2027 sixth-round pick from the Niners in exchange.
