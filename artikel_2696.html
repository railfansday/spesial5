MMA community celebrates Thanksgiving 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It's been as chaotic and unpredictable year as any in the MMA world, but at least, with Thanksgiving, we can all take one day to pause and be grateful for what we have. Oh, and to stuff our fat little faces.

If there are any athletes deserving of thanks, it's certainly the men and women who throw down for our viewing pleasure, whether it's for UFC, PFL, RIZN, ONE, RAF, BKFC, or any other fight promotion big and small. Let's hope every one of these brave scrappers gets some time to spend quality time with friends and family and indulge themselves at the dinner—and if you know anyone that is stressing about making weight for an upcoming fight, please make sure to save them plenty of leftovers!

So check in on social media with Roy "Big Country" Nelson, Donald "Cowboy" Cerrone, and more as the MMA community celebrates Thanksgiving.
