Winners and losers from Georgia football's 42-7 win at Ole Miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Georgia Bulldogs faced the Mississippi Rebels on the road in another highly anticipated SEC contest on Saturday. The Rebels had a 16-game home win streak, but the Bulldogs halted it, winning 42-7 to raise their record to 8-1 this season, while Mississippi is 6-3. Here are the winners and losers from the game.

Georgia's offense was electric during the game, scoring 42 total points. Quarterback Gunner Stockton went 9 for 15 for 186 yards and four touchdown passes. Wide receiver Talyn Taylor had a great performance, catching four passes for 140 yards and three touchdowns. Running back Nate Frazier looked great, leading the team in rushing with 107 yards on 16 carries.

Mississippi wide receiver Horatio Fields was one of the only bright spots for them on offense, catching eight passes for 96 yards. Fields led the team in receiving and was a reliable target for Trinidad Chambliss.

Georgia's defense looked fantastic against Ole Miss. The Bulldogs dominated the Ole Miss offensive front and shut down the Rebels until garbage time in the fourth quarter. Georgia sacked Ole Miss five times and forced a pair of Ole Miss turnovers.

Georgia kicker Peyton Woodring missed his lone field goal in the matchup. He missed a 49-yard field goal before halftime. This is the fourth consecutive game where he's missed. But he did make all of his point after attempts. Woodring has made nine of 13 kicks this season.

Mississippi's offense did not have a good game, scoring only seven points. Trinidad Chambliss was sacked five times. They weren't able to get much going. Chambliss was forced out of the pocket multiple times and threw an interception to Georgia safety K.J. Bolden. The Rebels' run game was nonexistent, rushing for 66 yards. The Ole Miss offensive line has a very poor day and could not defend Georgia's blitz.

Georgia will take on Missouri on Saturday, November 14th.
