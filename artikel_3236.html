Black Friday Sales Have Already Started for Peloton Bikes and Equipment

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Black Friday is Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday is Nov. 30, but sales have already started on Peloton equipment, including discounts on the company's new folding treadmill. Here are the deals you can find right now on bikes, treadmills, and more. Remember that all of the items below require a $49.99 All-Access subscription to be able to use the class content and other features.

Peloton's Bikes are their signature offering, and the Cross-Training series (new as of 2025) includes swiveling screens and upgraded hardware compared to the original Bikes. Those originals are still sometimes available refurbished or as rentals: $995 for a refurbished Bike+ right now. (The more basic Bike is unavailable, according to the Peloton website.)

Peloton Cross-Training Bike, $1,295 (down from $1,695, so $400 off)

Peloton Cross-Training Bike+, $1,795 (down from $2,695, so $900 off)

And if you're looking for a rower, Peloton's Cross-Training Row+ is $2,845 (down from $3,495, so $650 off)

Treadmills, which Peloton calls Treads, are also available in a newer Cross-Training version. Here are the deals I'm seeing:

Peloton Tread Flex, which folds up to save space: $1,995 (was $2,195, so $200 off)

Peloton Tread Vision, with a camera for form analysis: $2,795 (was $3,495, so $700 off)

Peloton Tread+ Vision, with a cushioned slat belt and sled push mode, $5,395 (was $6,695, so $1,300 off)

Yes, Amazon has Black Friday sales, but when it comes to Peloton, not all the equipment is available on Amazon. Check the Peloton website or other retailers for all your options. (I've linked each one above to a place where you can buy it—some on Amazon and some from Peloton directly.) In general, it's good practice to use a price tracker to make sure you’re getting the best deal, or refer to guides like our live blog that use price trackers for you. And if you have an Amazon Prime membership, make the most of it.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, November 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday,  November 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early, and we expect sales to wind down by December 2, 2026.
