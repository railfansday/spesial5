Crimea bridge has been attacked again, says Russian-installed governor

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Assessment of damage under way, days after two attacks that destroyed road section of key bridge across Kerch strait linking annexed peninsula to Russia

The remaining Kerch rail bridge connecting Crimea to Russia was attacked overnight, leading to a halt in train services, two days after a dramatic Ukrainian air raid had knocked out the road bridge alongside.

Sergei Aksyonov, the Kremlin-appointed governor of Crimea, said on Wednesday night that there had been another attack on the bridge, then added on Thursday that trains would start crossing from “approximately 4pm”.

Emergency repair workers had made “heroic efforts” to restore the track, the governor said. Nobody was reported wounded as a result of the incident, Russia’s transport ministry added.

The latest air raid took place hours before the unprecedented three-way summit in Shenzhen, China, between Donald Trump, the US president, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping, his Russian and Chinese counterparts.

It was the third time that the $3.6bn bridge, built by Russia after its occupation of Crimea, has been struck in a week. On Tuesday, a 227-metre section of the road spanning the Kerch strait was destroyed after a complex missile and drone attack that had been prepared and planned over several weeks.

Ukrainian military sources had indicated the timing of the attack on the road bridge was deliberate, ahead of the summit, suggesting that the follow-up raid on the rail bridge overnight may have been intended to bring it down as well.

One source in Ukraine’s armed forces said this week’s attacks were designed to embarrass Putin on the world stage. “The bridge is a symbol of Putin’s rule. Destroying it sends a message to the other two great leaders that Putin is not as strong as he pretends,” the person said.

For Ukraine, the bridge across the Kerch strait is a hated symbol of Russian arrogance and colonial thinking. Ukraine has previously declared that the bridge, built eight years ago, is illegal. Kyiv had tried twice to blow it up – with a truck bomb in 2022, and a sea drone the following year.

Earlier, Ukraine critically damaged the road section of the bridge in what President Volodymyr Zelenskyy hailed as an “unprecedented” military operation. A span of the bridge’s road section collapsed, but the railway line was still standing.

Zelesnkyy described the earlier attack as the culmination of a long and complex campaign to demolish the bridge, largely involving domestically produced drones and ballistic missiles.

Flamingo FP-5 cruise missiles and Palianytsia drones struck the central road section of the bridge, bringing down its supporting arch. A range of weapons, including Ukraine’s Neputune cruise missile and the Ruta, made by Dutch company Destinus, targeted the adjacent rail section, Zelenskyy said.
