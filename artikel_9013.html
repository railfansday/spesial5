Text description provided by the architects. The Buriti apartment is located in Brasília, Brazil and has 65 m². The renovation of the apartment was planned to take advantage of and enhance the client's existing furniture and art pieces. Inspired by the buriti palm, a native tree of the brazilian cerrado, the concept permeated the entire project, influencing mainly the choices of colors and textures.
