The Best Jewelry Gifts of 2026, Curated by Vogue’s Jewelry Team

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

There’s something inherently special about gifting jewelry. It’s personal, meaningful, and, when chosen thoughtfully, stands the test of time. But with so many options on the market, how does one select a piece worthy of such investment? No need to fret—we’ve curated a selection of the best jewelry gifts at each price point to serve as your blueprint this holiday season.

Take our word for it: There’s no shortage of treasures worth wrapping up this season. From timeless classics and everyday staples to statement-making creations and future heirlooms, there’s something here for every jewelry lover to admire.

A thoughtful gift needn’t break the bank. Swarovski’s Hyperbola earrings are unique but timeless; alternatively, choose a statement piece, like Hunting Season’s Tagua pendant, or an oversized Pandora charm.

For a gift that feels a bit more indulgent, this price range offers plenty to get excited about. Gift a beautiful pair of onyx beaded earrings from Sophie Buhai, or a Charlotte Chesnais cord necklace to add depth to her everyday stack.

Here’s where you have a lot of room to play. Fine stones and exceptional craftsmanship await in this price range. Sophie Bille Brahe’s exquisite pearl drop earrings would bring any jewelry lover joy for the holidays. If you’re looking for something they never have to take off, this gold Foundrae pendant is it.

On the hunt for something particularly extravagant? If budget’s no matter, go all out with a fabulous diamond tennis bracelet from Blue Nile. Gifting a watch collector? Cartier’s Mini Tank surely belongs in her collection.
