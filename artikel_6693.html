Katy Perry Shares Personal Photos of Her and Justin Trudeau’s Europe Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Katy Perry was all smiles in the snapshots from her European getaway with Justin Trudeau. The singer shared a new series of photos and videos from their travels, offering glimpses of the couple’s time together.

“The bob’s euro debut,” Perry captioned the Instagram post on Wednesday, November 25, referring to her shorter hairstyle. The singer included photos of the couple enjoying dinner, playing cards, and taking in the sights together. She also shared a video of herself relaxing in a bathtub aboard the train, with the European scenery visible through the window.

Perry and Trudeau reportedly traveled from Paris to Venice aboard the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express, spending 24 hours in L’Observatoire, an exclusive private carriage.

According to The Times, a one-way journey in the carriage costs £80,000, or approximately $105,000.

The private carriage includes a bedroom, dining area, library, personal butler, skylights, and a bathroom with a soaking tub.

The train journey was part of a wider European trip that included stops in Barcelona and Milan before the couple made their way to Paris and Venice. While in the French capital, they also met President Emmanuel Macron and posed for a selfie that Perry included in her social media update.

The couple was first romantically linked in July 2025. In a September 2026 interview with People, Perry described Trudeau as the “love of my life.”
