High School Teacher Arrested for Allegedly Planning to Have Sex With Girl, 15

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A high school teacher in Pennsylvania was arrested after he allegedly made arrangements online to have sex with a 15-year-old girl, who was actually an undercover officer.

Fayette County District Attorney Michael Aubele said that Lester Eugene Everly II allegedly arranged to meet up and pay for sex during an online conversation with an officer, whom he believed was an adult offering him the opportunity to have sex with a 15-year-old girl they knew, according to WTAE.

Aubele went on to explain how they caught Everly, 47, in the act.

“Our police officer was posing as a person who had a girlfriend who had a 15-year-old child that he could make available for $170,” Aubele said in a statement. “So the defendant traveled to this location, South Union Township, brought other things that he was asked to bring with him, that he would need for sexual activity. We were able to confirm that this was the individual that we believed we were speaking to online.”

Everly was arrested on Saturday, November 14, as part of the undercover operation that the Pennsylvania State Police Uniontown Criminal Investigation Unit and the PSP Bureau of Criminal Investigation Computer Crime Unit ran together.

After Everly was taken into custody, police said he confessed to bringing a condom and $180 to the meetup location he previously arranged with the officer.

Everly is currently in custody at Fayette County Prison. His preliminary hearing is scheduled to take place on November 23.

In light of the scandal, Everly has been identified as a teacher at Albert Gallatin High School in Fayette County, Pennsylvania.

Following Everly’s arrest, Albert Gallatin Interim Superintendent Dr. Jason Hutchinson issued a statement regarding the citation.

“Albert Gallatin has been informed of the arrest of a staff member last evening. The safety and security of our students is always the District’s primary concern, and will be the District’s focus as this matter proceeds,” Hutchinson said, per the outlet. “The District is cooperating with law enforcement and is unable to comment further.”

It’s not currently clear whether Everly has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Pennsylvania State Police did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment when contacted about updates in the case.
