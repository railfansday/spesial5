Kim Zolciak Breaks Silence on Daughter Brielle's Wedding After Son KJ's Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kim Zolciak isn’t letting her family’s legal issues distract from daughter Brielle Biermann’s romantic Florida wedding.

“It was perfect,” Zolciak, 48, told paparazzi of her daughter’s recent nuptials on Sunday, November 22, while walking through the Southwest Florida International Airport in Fort Myers, Florida, according to footage published by Page Six.

The Real Housewives of Atlanta alum described her daughter’s special day as “beautiful,” confirming “of course” she gave a toast and it was “great.”

“It was all a dream,” Zolciak gushed as she traveled through the airport alongside boyfriend Kyle Mowitz and son Kash Biermann following the Saturday, November 21, ceremony.

When asked whether anything was “missing” amid her estranged husband Kroy Biermann’s absence at the celebratory event, Zolciak quickly responded, “No.”

Zolciak and Kroy, 41, are currently tied up in a messy divorce after splitting in 2023. The exes share four children and Kroy is the adoptive stepfather to her two eldest daughters, Brielle, 29, and Ariana Biermann.

Zolciak appeared calm on Sunday as she headed toward security, wearing a yellow shirt and matching yellow sweatpants and sneakers.

She didn’t, however, address her and Kroy’s son KJ Biermann’s absence from the wedding amid his sexual assault and sodomy arrest.

KJ, 15, was arrested in August for alleged aggravated sodomy and aggravated sexual assault, per court records obtained by Us.

Both Zolciak and KJ’s attorney have denied the allegations, claiming they are “false.” He is currently fighting the seven charges outlined in his indictment.

Despite the shadow KJ’s arrest has cast on Zolciak and her loved ones, the family was in town for Zolciak’s eldest child’s wedding on Saturday when Brielle married baseball player Billy Seidl in Naples, Florida.

The bride wore a traditional white, flowy wedding dress with a large black bow on the left side, while Seidl, 27, chose a tuxedo and black bowtie for the occasion.

Zolciak brought Mowitz as her plus one to the event, where she celebrated Brielle alongside most of her children. Daughter Ariana, 25, served as Brielle’s maid of honor, while Kash, 14, was spotted at the airport with his mom.

Zolciak and Kroy’s youngest children, twins Kaia and Kane, 12, were not seen in video footage at the airport, but Kaia did attend her big sister’s bridal shower earlier this year.
