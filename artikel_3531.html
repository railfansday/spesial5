The GTA 6 Map Is Truly, Truly Massive (Of Course)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally arrived--at least in New Zealand and Australia. Lots of people are playing the game already and have made a number of discoveries, including just how massive the map is.

Even though we knew the GTA 6 map would be big--it has more playable space than GT4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined--seeing the map for yourself shows off its truly gargantuan scale.

The streamer N3on showed off the entire GTA 6 map, and it's ... large. As confirmed previously, GTA 6 takes place in the state of Leonida, which is a stand-in for Florida. The central hub is Vice City itself (GTA's version of Miami), but the game takes players well beyond that to numerous surrounding counties.

GTA 6 also carries the franchise's "fog of war" tradition forward, so that's why you don't see specific details on the map in N3on's video. As players find new places, they will show up more clearly on the map.

But what's obvious at this point is that the GTA 6 map is gigantic. We can only wonder how long it may take to drive from one side to the other, or get all the way down to GTA 6's version of the Southernmost point in the continental US, Key West. GTA 6's version of the Florida Keys is called Leonida Keys.

GameSpot's GTA 6 review scored the game a rare 10/10. “GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7,” reviewer Tamoor Hussain said.

GTA 6 has now unseated the Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 to become the highest-rated game of 2026 on GameSpot sister site, Metacritic, and is tied with the original Ocarina of Time for the highest aggregate review score ever.

After GTA 6 reviews came online, shares of Rockstar owner Take-Two ripped higher, but the company is still down massively this year and over the past 12 months.
