One of the biggest sci-fi disaster films remains a streaming hit. Starring Jake Gyllenhaal and Dennis Quaid, the film was one of the highest-grossing movies of its time and explored one of the biggest issues facing our planet. This issue remains relevant today, so it's no surprise audiences still tune in to watch the film.

The film in question is The Day After Tomorrow, a natural disaster movie directed by Roland Emmerich, who has also worked on other notable apocalyptic sci-fi projects. Gyllenhaal plays Sam Hall, who becomes stranded in New York City with his classmates after a massive storm caused by climate change triggers a new ice age. Since its release, the film has grossed over $552.6 million worldwide, making it the sixth-highest-grossing film of 2004.

Recently, The Day After Tomorrow became available on Netflix, and nearly a week after its arrival, it continues to attract viewers. The film still sits at No. 1 on Netflix's Top 10 Movies chart in the United States, with Kidnapped in Her Own Home: The Martha Carelli Story sitting below it. Despite its continued success on the streaming charts, The Day After Tomorrow received mixed-to-negative reviews, earning a 44% critics' score and a 50% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.

If you enjoyed The Day After Tomorrow, several other movies share a similar premise. One of the easiest recommendations is to check out some of Emmerich's other projects, such as Independence Day, Godzilla, and White House Down, but the one you should definitely stream is 2012. This 2009 apocalyptic film was inspired by the 2012 phenomenon, which involved predictions that the world would end that year. The film grossed $791.2 million worldwide, making it the fifth-highest-grossing film of 2009. It stars John Cusack (Sixteen Candles), Chiwetel Ejiofor (Backrooms), Amanda Peet (The Good Wife), Oliver Platt (The Bear), and Tom McCarthy (13 Reasons Why).

Another movie to consider watching is San Andreas, a disaster film starring Dwayne Johnson. Directed by Brad Peyton, who also worked on Rampage, the film centers on a massive earthquake along the San Andreas Fault that devastates the U.S. West Coast. San Andreas also stars Alexandra Daddario (2010's Percy Jackson & the Olympians: The Lightning Thief), Carla Gugino (2026's Carrie), and Ioan Gruffudd (2005's Fantastic Four) and grossed over $475 million worldwide.
