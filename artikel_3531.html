Glen Powell's Ex Gigi Paris Is Engaged to Former NFL Player Cooper Helfet

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Model Gigi Paris has announced her engagement to former Seattle Seahawks tight end Cooper Helfet.

“[I] said yes to the man of my dreams,” Paris, 34, announced via Instagram on Thursday, November 26.

The couple got engaged during a Thanksgiving week trip to Carcassonne, France, a historic European city known for its medieval architecture.

“[We got engaged] on a mountaintop overlooking my family’s history, with a lifetime of our own ahead of us,” she revealed of his romantic proposal. “Feeling like the luckiest girl in the world. Je t’aime toujours @coophelfet.”

The certified yoga instructor posed in front of a medieval citadel with her beau, writing, “My fiancé!”

Paris and Helfet, 37, also shared multiple Instagram Stories showing them announcing their big news to family and friends. The pair were congratulated on their engagement by former Love Island host Sarah Hyland, Southern Charm‘s Naomie Olindo and former Vampire Diaries star Claire Holt, among others.

Olindo, 34, wrote to the bride-to-be, “GIGI!!!! CONGRATS!!! Love you so so much!!!!”

Paris first stepped out with Helfet in September 2025 when they attended the 27th Annual Angel Ball in New York City. She later reposted a red carpet photo of the two via Instagram, calling her new boyfriend “papiiii.”

Before dating Helfet, Paris was in a three-year relationship with Glen Powell, which ended in April 2023. Paris shared a cryptic message about knowing “your worth” and moving “onto the next” after unfollowing Powell, 38, via Instagram.

During a June 2025 appearance on the “Too Much” podcast, Paris seemingly alluded to romance rumors between Powell and his Anyone But You costar Sydney Sweeney playing a part in her own breakup with the actor. (She did not directly name either Powell or Sweeney, 29.)

“I had two options. I could either pretend like I was going along with everything and have everyone wonder, like, ‘Are they hooking up? Are they not hooking up? Is she OK with this? What the f***?'” Paris claimed. “Or stand up for myself and say, ‘No, I’m actually not OK with this, and I’m walking away.’ So that’s what I decided to do.”

She remembered feeling “shattered” by the very public discourse about her breakup with Powell at the time.

“I just wanted respect, especially if it’s gonna be public,” she declared. “Like, don’t make an ass out of me. Like, just don’t make a fool out of someone you’ve been with for over three years talking about forever with. Just have some decency, you know?”

Powell subsequently told GQ that same month that he will “always have nothing but love for her and respect” for Paris though he didn’t necessarily agree with the way she publicly framed their breakup.

“Everybody’s always going to have their own narrative on things and all that,” he added. “And she’s welcome to it. … Relationships are really hard. And when two people break up, they each go to their own brunch, and they will each tell their own narrative.”

Two months later, news broke that Powell was dating Michelle Randolph after they were spotted dancing in his hometown of Austin, Texas. Powell told TMZ in September that he was “very serious” about Randolph, 29, while giving a rare update on their relationship.
