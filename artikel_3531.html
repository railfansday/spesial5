Florence Pugh Shuts Down Rumors She Had Plastic Surgery to Change Her Face

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Florence Pugh is speaking out to shut down the idea that she had plastic surgery to change her face.

“Guys. I turned 30. I’m a woman. Body’s [sic] and faces change,” Pugh, 30, commented on Evie Magazine’s Tuesday, November 17, Instagram video. “It’s not that deep, the fact that people can’t even tell between what’s aging and what’s surgery is disturbing.”

She continued, “Sadly puppy fluff doesn’t last forever ❤️.”

Her comment came in response to the outlet’s video, where a commentator showed a throwback photo of the Don’t Worry Darling actress alongside a more recent image of Pugh and went on to discuss beauty trends in Hollywood.

“Is this a safe space? Because people are saying that Florence Pugh has fallen victim to Hollywood’s war on round faces,” the commentator said in the clip. “Don’t get me wrong, Florence Pugh still looks beautiful, but I think that we can all collectively agree that Hollywood seems to have this thing against softer features. The desirable thing in the entertainment industry and the modeling industry right now is this sculpted, snatched look.”

The commentator added, “A lot of people are pointing out how Florence used to have full, beautiful cheeks that made her face super recognizable. Now that her jaw is more defined, her chin is more pointier, her jaws are more sharp, it makes sense why people are wondering if she had work done.”

The commentator pointed to beauty trends seemingly wanting to steer away from “soft features,” before wondering if there’s a “subconscious push, this silent push, to make women look sharper, more sculpted, thinner.”

“Look at Florence. I just think she is naturally beautiful. Now, I don’t think that we should be demonizing celebrities for potentially getting plastic surgery. I don’t think we should be sending them hate,” the commentator said.

In 2024, Pugh spoke about the beauty expectations of women in Hollywood during an interview with The Sunday Times, calling it “exhausting.”

“The only thing people want to talk about is some useless crap about how they look. And so I didn’t care to abide by those rules,” she added.

Pugh has continued to put the focus on her career, with new movies coming out this holiday season as she prepares for the same-day release of Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three on December 18.
