Sparks rumors: Pau Gasol, Vanessa Bryant interested in purchasing team from Mark Walter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sparks rumors: Pau Gasol, Vanessa Bryant interested in purchasing team from Mark Walter appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

In the aftermath of Mark Walter selling the Los Angeles Lakers to Bob Iger and Josh Kushner only a little over a year after purchasing majority ownership, it was confirmed that Walter was also looking to unload the Los Angeles Sparks, a team his group has owned since 2014. While there hadn't been much news as far as potential bidders, the duo of Pau Gasol and Vanessa Bryant are reportedly pursuing purchasing the Sparks, as per Tom Friend of The Sports Business Journal.

The current estimated asking price for a potential Sparks sale is thought to be around $700 million, and will also involve the team's new state-of-the-art practice facility set to open for the 2027 season.

Although rumors regarding potential buyers for the Sparks have been light, Friend shed a little more light on a couple of groups, including the one spearheaded by Pau Gasol and Vanessa Bryant, that are vying for one of the original WNBA franchises.

In addition to the late Kobe Bryant's wife and former teammate and friend, a group only known as Swish Partners, has thrown their hat in the ring, with the desire to eventually move the Sparks to Intuit Dome, the home of the LA Clippers. The third group identified as having interest in a Sparks sale is Jose E. Feliciano, the owner of the San Diego Padres.

Both Gasol and Bryant have been heavily involved with the WNBA and women's basketball, and both have appeared at Sparks home games in recent years.

The Sparks are coming off a disappointing season in which they finished 16-28 and missed out on the playoffs for the sixth consecutive year. The team does possess two lottery picks in the 2027 WNBA Draft at No. 6 and No. 7.

Following the end of the 2026 season, the Sparks opted to part ways with head coach Lynne Roberts. New general manager Ariana Andonian is now tasked with bringing the franchise back to its former glory days when the team was a perennial contender in the WNBA.
