Pedro Caixinha back in Europe after joining 13th club of managerial career

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Former Rangers manager Pedro Caixinha has been appointed head coach of Cercle Brugge.

The Portuguese has signed a contract until June 2027, with the option of a further season, after the Belgian club parted company with Lars Friis.

Cercle Brugge beëindigt de samenwerking met Lars Friis. De Portugees Pedro Caixinha (55) volgt hem op als hoofdcoach van Groen-Zwart. De Vereniging bedankt Lars voor zijn inzet de voorbije maanden en wenst hem het allerbeste voor de toekomst. Met Pedro Caixinha haalt Cercle een coach met ruime internationale ervaring in huis. Cercle made the change after collecting six points from their opening 10 league matches, leaving them 17th in the table.

Caixinha, 55, spent seven months in charge of Rangers in 2017 during a managerial career that has taken him across Europe, South America, Mexico and the Middle East.

His most recent position was with Mexican club FC Juarez, while he has also managed Santos Laguna, Cruz Azul, Talleres, Red Bull Bragantino and Santos.

Caixinha won the league title, domestic cup and Supercopa MX with Santos Laguna and later lifted the Copa MX and Supercopa MX with Cruz Azul.

Cercle technical director Luciano Murchio said: “In Pedro, we welcome an experienced head coach who has demonstrated his qualities and achieved success at the highest level across different leagues and circumstances.

“We are convinced that he can help us achieve our objectives while respecting our playing principles and identity.

“We expect Pedro to have a positive impact on the team in the short term and help us turn around the current sporting situation.”

Caixinha will be assisted by Pedro Malta and Guilherme Gomes and will take charge of his first training session on Tuesday.
