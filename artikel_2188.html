Norwich City receive major new 2027 boost that Philippe Clement will love

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

After a torrid start to the 2026-27 Championship season, Norwich City may very well have turned a corner.

Many expected the Canaries to have a wave of momentum heading into the current campaign, after they narrowly missed out on the play-offs last season - but it hasn't worked out that way at all.

City lost eight out of their first 11 matches, and only sat outside of the relegation zone a few weeks ago due to a handful of other clubs doing even worse than them, but back-to-back wins over Charlton Athletic and Watford have pushed Philippe Clement's side up the table a little bit.

They're doing it without the sharp-shooting Mohamed Toure too, who has certainly made quite an impression since his January arrival from Randers.

When Josh Sargent had one foot out of Carrow Road at the start of 2026, Norwich needed a plan to replace the American striker, despite already having Mathias Kvistgaarden and the in-form Jovon Makama on the books.

They turned to the Danish league to acquire Australian speedster Toure, paying around £2.6 million for his services, and he would soon repay the Norfolk outfit with goals in abundance.

Finding the back of the net 10 times in just 12 appearances in the league and cup in his first half-season in England, which included two hat-tricks, Toure made a name for himself quickly, and he'd scored six goals in eight-and-a-bit matches in 2026-27 before damaging his shoulder early on against Bolton Wanderers, not long after opening the scoring.

The 22-year-old had to undergo surgery following that, and last week he revealed that a return to action was being targeted for the AFC Asian Cup with Australia, which was set to start on January 7, 2027.

It was set to be a blow for Norwich that Toure was going to be away for more than a month of action on international duty, with the Socceroos poised to get the first taste of the fit-again striker at the start of next year.

However, a curveball has been delivered in an unexpected manner, as the Asian Cup has now been postponed for nearly a year.

Due to the rising tensions between several countries in the Middle East, which has led to safety concerns and travel disruption, the call has officially been made to move the tournament, which was set to take place in Saudi Arabia, to start in December 2027 instead.

That means Norwich will soon welcome Toure back to action in the Championship when his shoulder has healed, and they will not need to worry about him travelling halfway around the world to play internationally before he's pulled on the yellow shirt of the Canaries first.

Norwich aren't the only club that have been boosted by the delayed tournament. Both Middlesbrough and Southampton were also set to lose at least one first-teamer each, with Riley McGree and Kuryu Matsuki expected to be called up to Australia and Japan respectively.

However, they will now be remaining at their respective clubs for the busy Christmas and New Year period, which will be a welcome relief for Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert, and now it's good news for Clement too.

Of course, this only kicks the can down the road until next year - the tournament is now projected to start just a couple of days before Christmas next year, and you'd imagine that not only will international squads meet up at least a week or two beforehand, but getting to the final stages of the competition will take it until the end of January, which ends up being a large chunk of fixtures missed.

For the here and now though for Norwich in particular, having Toure ready for the second half of the season exclusively for club football will be massive, as he's clearly a massive goalscoring threat at Championship level when he gets the service.
