'My understanding is' - Chris Davies confirms what's happening with August Priske at Birmingham City

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Chris Davies has issued a fresh update on August Priske after Birmingham City's in-form striker missed Tuesday night's 3-0 victory over Millwall at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

Blues and their supporters are in a buoyant mood, having won four of their last five Championship matches, with the midweek success over the Lions taking them up to second ahead of Wednesday night's fixtures.

Davies has seen goal contributions come from all angles this season, and that was certainly the case against Millwall as Alex Cochrane, Max Bird and Jay Stansfield found target.

Many of his squad are now fully aclimatised to the division after finishing 10th last season, and that includes Priske, who signed from Djurgardens IF in January.

The 22-year-old has since shown signs of being one of the division's most feared strikers, meaning Blues fans will have been relieved when learning that his omission from the matchday squad is expected to be a one-off.

Priske followed up his final-day goal against Portsmouth in May with an impressive first pre-season in the Second City, and such form has continued in recent times.

A brace in the 3-1 win at Lincoln City on Saturday took his goal tally to six goals in 11 games, although there was concern when team news dropped on Tuesday, with the Danish forward not in the squad, whilst Bird took his place in the starting lineup.

The summer recruit was key to Blues' fourth win in five Championship games

The full extent of the knock sustained at the ProAmpac Stadium hasn't been revealed, but Davies is hopeful Priske will be back involved against Swansea City on Saturday, in what is a crucial match before the international break.

“August just took a knock in the game which obviously wasn’t enough to bring him off at the time, but it’s just something that’s lingered on after the game,” said the Blues boss.

“We tried our hardest and so did he to get him available. He’s had a couple of knocks here and there and it wasn’t to be.

“We’ll be day by day gauging it now to see whether he can be available for Swansea. My understanding is that it’s not a long-term thing, it’s just about seeing how he is tomorrow and the next day.”

Priske's presence in Birmingham's attack has given those around him a new lease of life this season, as well as being full of confidence from an individual perspective.

The striker has already scored a nice variety of goals, and will hope that, if available, he can cause further issues for Vitor Matos' backline this weekend.

The Swans have shipped half of their 16 goals in their past two away games against Queens Park Rangers and Portsmouth, whilst Priske was responsible for their EFL Cup first round exit in August as he flicked home Carlos Vicente's 44th-minute cross.

It remains to be seen if Priske will be deemed fit for Saturday's encounter, and if not, it will be expected that he will be present post-international break when Birmingham travel to Stoke City.

However, Davies faces a dilemma when it comes to team selection, given how influential Bird was in an advanced midfield role and the fact Stansfield netted his second goal of the season as the leading striker.
