Swansea City in talks with 42-year-old over coaching return

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Swansea City are working on a deal to bring their former defender back to South Wales. The Swans are set to lose head coach Vitor Matos to Ligue 1 side Strasbourg and they have a big decision on the direction they want to take next.

Williams, 42, spent nine years as a player with the Welsh outfit from 2007 to 2016 and helped them rise from League One to the Premier League, making over 350 appearances in all competitions. He is currently part of Craig Bellamy’s coaching staff of the Wales national team along with Alan Sheehan.

In this latest update regarding the managerial situation at the Swansea.com Stadium, reporter O’Rourke has claimed on X that he is in discussions over a potential return. He has been targeted as a candidate to work alongside Leon Britton and Kristian O’Leary as part of a caretaker trio whilst the club search for Matos’ permanent replacement.

Swansea City are positioned in 6th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures. They are in the play-offs along with Stoke City, Wolves, West Brom, Southampton and QPR.

Swansea City could see Williams as someone to help steady the ship alongside Britton and O’Leary. He knows the club inside out and has just started his coaching journey with Wales.

The Midlands-born man was a hit during his playing days with the Swans and was popular among their fans. He also had spells at Stockport County, Everton and Bristol City before he hung up his boots in 2020.

Williams, who made 86 caps for Wales during his playing days, played with Britton so they know each other well already. Prior to linking up with Bellamy’s backroom staff team, he did some punditry work.

Swansea City will be weighing up their options as they prepare for Matos’ departure. They will be considering candidates and won’t want to rush into a decision.

In the short-term, Williams could join up with Britton and O’Leary to lead Swansea City into their upcoming matches. They face West Brom at home this weekend before an away trip to Middlesbrough next Tuesday.

In terms of who the Swans turn to next, it seems likely they will go down the abroad route and identify managers via their Jamestown Analytics model. There are plenty of available managers with EFL experience right now including Steve Cooper, Carlos Corberan, Rob Edwards, Sean Dyche and Steven Gerrard.
