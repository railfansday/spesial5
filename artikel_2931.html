Huang Is Worth Up to $10 Trillion to the Market, Ives Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dan Ives, senior managing director at Yorkville Ives & Co., discusses Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia Corp. Chief Executive Officer and his value to the AI growth cycle and overall market. "We continue to be bullish, not just on Nvidia, but the overall tech trade," Ives tells Bloomberg Television. "AI is not stopping here despite maybe scarcity of capital," he adds.
