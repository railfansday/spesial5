EZCORP: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Margins at 37% show the pricing discipline behind EZCORP's gains is paying off, but the article leaves open how long that can hold.

On its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 12, 2026, EZCORP (EZPW +1.85%) announced record full-year revenue of $1.7 billion. Organic loan growth and store additions expand the company's core earning assets. Three takeaways from the call highlight how management is building long-term shareholder value.

Consumer financial pressure continues to boost short-term borrowing, helping pawn loan outstanding--the balance of active collateralized loans generating fee income--grow 13% on a same-store basis in the U.S. for three consecutive quarters. That steady borrowing activity expands the company's recurring revenue base without relying on volatile gold prices.

Management is deploying artificial intelligence (AI), software algorithms that optimize loan values, alongside store training to price items accurately and sell inventory faster. That pricing discipline expanded merchandise sales gross margin to 37%, showing that operational efficiency drives profit gains.

The store network expanded by a net 230 locations in fiscal 2026, driven by 48 de novos -- newly constructed store locations -- and strategic acquisitions in Mexico and Central America. Opening newly built stores while integrating acquired operators extends store density across high-growth regional markets.

Elevated living expenses drive sustained demand for short-term credit across core markets. Proprietary point-of-sale software, digital loan valuation tools, and disciplined inventory control provide operational advantages as management monitors store-level execution.

Expanding regional store density while sharpening pricing discipline positions the business to compound returns.
