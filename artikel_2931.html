Preview: Wizards play Bulls on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Washington Wizards play the Chicago Bulls tomorrow night, the last weekend before Thanksgiving. Let's get to it.

How to watch: Monumental Sports Network, League Pass

Wizards: Jamir Watkins, Tristan Vukcevic, Reece Beekman and Justin Champagnie are out.

Bulls: Tobe Awaka and Caleb Wilson are out. Noa Essengue is day-to-day.

After an eight game losing streak, the Wizards finally got back in the win column yesterday against the Toronto Raptors. The Raptors were 5-9 before playing Washington and with a revamped Wizards roster, I'd like to say that the Wizards needed to win that game.

Washington is still in "this-is-a-must-win-game" mode. They are 5-11, which isn't close to where they would have hoped. The Bulls are coming off a 137-107 win over the Brooklyn Nets last night and hope a win over Washington will right their ship, too. In addition, this is the second matchup between the Wizards and Bulls. On Oct., 24, Washington won 124-120.

Expect a hard-fought battle by both teams because they're in similar situations based on the standings. I hope it's the Wizards who end up ahead on this one.
