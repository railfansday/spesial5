Get featured in Dezeen's digital guide to Miami art week 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If you’re hosting an event during Miami art week, you could be featured in Dezeen Events Guide’s digital guide to the festival.

The guide highlights the key events taking place in Miami from 30 November to 6 December 2026, including art and design fairs, exhibitions, talks, open showrooms, tours, launches, pop-up shops and parties.

Events take place across the city, with central hubs including Miami Beach and the Miami Design District.

Two of the largest events taking place during the week are Design Miami, an annual fair taking place from 1 to 6 December, and Art Basel Miami Beach, running from 4 to 6 December at the Miami Beach Convention Center.

Other festival highlights include Scope Art Show, Satellite Art Fair, Spectrum Miami and Context Art Miami.

Alongside the guide, Dezeen Events Guide is also launching an interactive map for the festival, helping you navigate the hundreds of events taking place during Miami art week.

Dezeen Events Guide offers a range of listings. Get in touch with the team at eventsguide@dezeen.com to book your listing or to discuss a wider partnership with Dezeen.

Dezeen Events Guide is our guide to the best architecture and design events taking place across the world each year.

The guide is updated weekly and includes virtual events, conferences, trade fairs, major exhibitions and design weeks.

For more details on inclusion in Dezeen Events Guide, including in our guide to Miami art week, email eventsguide@dezeen.com.
