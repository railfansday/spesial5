BYD Commercial Vehicle Sales Rise 68% in October!

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Following up our usual monthly report on BYD’s passenger vehicle sales, let’s have a look at the companies commercial vehicle sales — all electric, of course.

The company’s bus sales were up 62.1% year over year in October, rising from 367 in October 2025 to 600 in October 2026. Month over month, compared to September 2026, sales were actually down 24.1% (from 791 in September), but sales vary quite a lot from month to month in this segment, and 600 is still one of the company’s best months ever for electric bus sales.

In terms of non-bus commercial vehicle sales, they did even better. They rose 69.1% year over year, from 4,483 in October 2025 to 6,443 in October 2026, and they rose 6.4% month over month, from 6,057 in September 2026.

Overall, BYD’s commercial vehicle sales rose 68.3%, from 4,850 in October 2025 to 7,043 in October 2026. They rose 2.8% month over month, from 6,848 in September. BYD’s commercial vehicle sales total 55,489 units so far in 2026.
