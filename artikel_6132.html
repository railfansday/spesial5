New Webinar: Turning Travel Disruptions Into Guest Loyalty

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Travel is sold in pieces. Guests experience it as one trip. When a flight delay knocks out the connection, the first hotel night, and the plans after it, guests expect someone to help put it back together. That’s travel’s accountability gap.

Disruption has become the real test of loyalty, and a chance for hotels to build it beyond points. Matt Miller of Marriott International and Lindsay Tuhey of Allianz Partners join Skift to break down how hotels can support guests across the whole trip and keep ownership of the relationship.
