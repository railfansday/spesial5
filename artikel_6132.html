United Airlines Pilots Refuse ATC Direction To Fly Through “The Worst Part” Of A Severe Thunderstorm

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

United Airlines pilots scheduled to operate a nonstop flight from San Antonio International Airport to Washington Dulles International reportedly refused to accept the air traffic control's proposed routing to the US capital, after they identified that the route would take them directly through a severe thunderstorm.
