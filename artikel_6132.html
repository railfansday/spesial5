Traveler Says American Pilots Should Have Just “Had A Cup Of Coffee” Instead Of Following Federal Law

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There’s no denying that traveling by air can be stressful, and that it’s extremely frustrating when things go wrong. Furthermore, not everyone is a frequent traveler, and understands the ins and outs of how the airline industry functions.

So that brings us to a complaint from last night from an American Airlines passenger. The complaint is so incredibly off base in the first place. But what’s even worse is that when this person is confronted with reality, he just doubles down and keeps going. This person deserves to be roasted, and if you ask me, should be banned from the airline.

Late last night, a social media user posted a picture of two airline pilots on X, with the following caption:

I won’t be seeing my family this Thanksgiving because a couple pilots couldn’t drink a cup of coffee and get us home. They clocked out in two hours, but we had 3 hr flight. Thanks @AmericanAir

I won't be seeing my family this Thanksgiving because a couple pilots couldn't drink a cup of coffee and get us home. They clocked out in two hours, but we had 3 hr flight. Thanks @AmericanAir pic.twitter.com/q1I8pjsM3w

I can totally appreciate how frustrating it is when a flight is canceled, but this is actually very simple — federal regulations dictate the maximum number of hours that pilots can fly, and that’s for good reason. The airline industry operates with the goal of putting safety above all else, and pilot fatigue has been a contributing factor to many accidents over the years.

I totally get it, you want to be home for Thanksgiving, and it sucks things went wrong, but those are the facts. This is federal law, and on top of that, we should want pilots to be well rested, rather than just saying “keep working, just have another cup of coffee.”

I’d like to point out what makes this even worse — this was a flight out of Miami last night, and the airport had shut down due to a ground stop, because a FedEx MD-11 ran off the runway. That was an absolute pain for all passengers out of the airport, and I can assure you that American also wasn’t happy about that happening, and the impact it had on its operations.

All would be forgiven if this traveler either just didn’t say anything further, or acknowledged “ah, I wasn’t aware of that.” Instead, he has had dozens and dozens of posts, just going on and on and on.

He posted about how American claimed the cancelation was the airport’s fault, but insisted “it’s actually these pilots fault,” and suggested “pilots will lie to save money for their company.”

@AmericanAir will cancel your flight the day before Thanksgiving and say it's the airports fault. It's actually these pilots fault. pic.twitter.com/v4t8KPSojR

@AmericanAir pilots will lie to save money for their company @AmericanAir pic.twitter.com/7VmOKILwCL

He then attacked American’s rebooking lines at Miami Airport. Look, I’ll be the first to call out American when they fall short (and they often do!), but if you really can’t figure out why rebooking lines “wrap around the corner” on one of the busiest travel days of the year when there was an accident that shut down an airport, then you really aren’t trying very hard to understand things.

@AmericanAir why do your rebooking lines always wrap around the corner? How bad are your operations? pic.twitter.com/Id8AaTiom1

He also has an issue with bathrooms at Miami Airport, which I get, but goodness, he’s repetitive.

Why do you have the most disgusting bathrooms @iflymia

Why are your bathroom never clean@iflymia

Why are your bathrooms such a health hazard @iflymia

You should hire people to clean your bathrooms@iflymia

He then suggested that he’ll fly Delta or United next time, because they can get you where you’re supposed to go. Yeah, sure bro, they’ll get you out of Miami Airport when it’s closed due to an accident.

Next time I'll fly @united or @Delta because they can actually get you where you are supposed to be when you are supposed to be there…and paid for…unlike the incompetent airline @AmericanAir. I bet your life is full of incompetence

He’s also asking Secretary Duffy to investigate American, because “they are the most unethical and corrupt company in America.” We know the Trump administration loves to investigate corruption, I’m sure they’ll get right on that!

Can you investigate @AmericanAir they are the most unethical and corrupt company in America

Honestly, I think American is perfectly justified in banning this guy. He’s violating the company’s policy on photographing employees, and this kind of behavior doesn’t need to be tolerated.

It sucks when things go wrong while flying, especially for travel around the holidays. But there’s a fine line between a reasonable complaint, and one that’s completely uncalled for. Posting pictures of random pilots and suggesting they’re the reason you can’t see your family for Thanksgiving is not acceptable.

What’s even worse is when people explain that crew rest requirements are federally mandated, but you just remain in denial over that, and double down. American, please do everyone else a favor and ban this guy.
