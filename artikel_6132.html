Citi AAdvantage Business Card Bonus: 75K Miles Welcome Offer (Double Dip Loyalty Points!)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Citi® / AAdvantage Business™ World Elite Mastercard® is American’s primary co-branded business credit card. We’re currently seeing an excellent limited time welcome offer, which is in line with the highest ever offer that we’ve seen on the card.

That makes this the ideal time to apply for the Citi AAdvantage Business Card. Not only that, but this is a card that many people will want, given that it’s my favorite American card for earning AAdvantage elite status, since you can basically double dip Loyalty Points.

Link: Learn more about the Citi® / AAdvantage Business™ World Elite Mastercard®

The Citi AAdvantage Business Card currently has a limited time welcome offer of 75,000 AAdvantage bonus miles after spending $5,000 within the first five months. This is a highest-ever offer, in line with the best bonuses I recall seeing on the card (I covered the card’s offer history below). We don’t know when this offer will be pulled.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I value AAdvantage miles at 1.5 cents each, so to me the bonus is worth $1,125. There are some incredible uses of American AAdvantage miles, including for travel on some world class partner airlines.

Just to give a few example of AAdvantage mileage redemptions, you could redeem 57,500 AAdvantage miles for a one-way Finnair business class award from Dallas (DFW) to Helsinki (HEL)…

…or you could redeem 60,000 AAdvantage miles for a one-way Japan Airlines business class award from Dallas (DFW) to Tokyo (HND)…

…or you could redeem 40,000 AAdvantage miles for a one-way Etihad business class award (or 50,000 miles for an Etihad first class award) from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Tokyo (NRT).

In each case, these tickets would typically cost many thousands of dollars, so you’re getting incredible value. When there is award availability, AAdvantage really is a program that’s tough to beat.

Also keep in mind that credit card spending will qualify for Loyalty Points (helping you earn elite status, including Gold, Platinum, Platinum Pro, or Executive Platinum), though the welcome bonus as such won’t. Upon completing minimum spending, you’d earn a minimum of 5,000 Loyalty Points. As I’ll explain below, you can even double dip the Loyalty Points you earn.

The Citi AAdvantage Business Card 75,000-point offer is the best publicly available sign-up bonus that we’ve seen on the card. How do the previous Citi AAdvantage Business Card bonuses compare?

As you can see, the Citi AAdvantage Business Card publicly available welcome offer has most commonly varied from 65,000 to 75,000 miles, so you might as well apply when the offer is the best.

If you’re interested in applying for the Citi AAdvantage Business Card, the typical Citi credit card application restrictions apply, including that you can be approved for at most one Citi card every eight days, and at most two Citi cards every 65 days.

On top of that, the bonus isn’t available to those who have received a new account bonus on this exact card in the past 48 months. Eligibility is unrelated to having any other American Airlines credit cards.

There’s not a published credit score you need to get approved for the Citi AAdvantage Business Card. Ideally I’d hope to have a credit score of 700+, but people with lower scores have been approved, and people with higher scores have been denied.

If you’re concerned about Chase’s 5/24 rule, note that applying for a Citi business card generally won’t count toward that limit. I should also mention that at least anecdotally, this card seems to be quite easy to get approved for.

The Citi AAdvantage Business Card has an annual fee that’s waived for the first year, so you can “try” the card before you have to pay a fee. While the fee after that is technically still $99, it’s increasing to $150 as of January 24, 2027, so that’s what you’d pay on account renewal. There’s no cost to add employee cards for authorized users.

Above I mentioned how the Citi AAdvantage Business Card is the best card for earning AAdvantage elite status. The reason is because through December 31, 2028, the card essentially lets you double dip Loyalty Points. If you add an authorized user, their spending will earn 1x Loyalty Points per dollar spent for both their own account, and for the primary cardmember’s account. So this is a way to essentially get two-for-one elite status qualification, which is awesome.

The Citi AAdvantage Business Card is offering a limited time welcome bonus of 75,000 AAdvantage bonus miles after spending $5,000 within the first five months. This is a great offer on a valuable card that even has its annual fee waived for the first year.

This is also my favorite card for earning AAdvantage elite status, since you can essentially earn Loyalty Points for two members at once. Authorized user spending earns Loyalty Points for the authorized user’s AAdvantage account, and also for the primary cardmember’s AAdvantage account.
