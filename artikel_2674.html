Can Arch Manning Finally Silence the Doubters?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Arch Manning entered the 2026 college football season with enormous expectations, but the Texas quarterback has experienced plenty of criticism throughout the year. After being viewed as one of the top Heisman Trophy contenders entering the season, Manning has faced questions about his consistency, decision-making and ability to lead Texas to a national championship.

Now, after putting together his best performance of the season, Manning has an opportunity to change the conversation.

The Texas quarterback has had an up-and-down season, including difficult performances in losses to Oklahoma and Florida. Those struggles led to questions about whether head coach Steve Sarkisian should consider making a quarterback change.

However, Sarkisian continued to show confidence in his starting quarterback, and Manning responded with two consecutive victories.

His latest performance against Mississippi State was especially encouraging. Manning completed 16 of 20 passes for 220 yards and three touchdowns without throwing an interception in Texas' 31-3 victory.

It was arguably his most complete performance of the season and showed what the Texas offense can look like when Manning plays confidently.

There is no denying that Manning has faced a different level of pressure because of his last name. Coming from the Manning family, expectations have followed him throughout his college career.

However, talent has never been the biggest question surrounding Manning. His arm strength, athleticism and ability to make plays outside the pocket have always made him one of the most exciting quarterbacks in college football.

The challenge now is putting everything together consistently against the toughest competition.

Texas still has important games remaining, and Manning will have opportunities to prove that his recent improvement is here to stay.

If he continues making smart decisions, protecting the football and creating explosive plays, the doubters could quickly become believers.

Manning has already shown flashes of greatness. Now he needs to show that he can deliver consistently.

The talent is there, but the next few weeks could determine whether Arch Manning finally silences the criticism and becomes the quarterback Texas expected him to be.
