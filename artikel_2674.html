Andy Reid on Emmett Johnson: My hat goes off to him

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When running back Kenneth Walker suffered a groin injury late in the practice week, it thrust rookie Emmett Johnson into a starting role for the Week 10 matchup with the Falcons.

Johnson made the most of that opportunity.

The rookie finished the contest with 101 rushing yards on 22 carries and caught six passes for 70 yards with two touchdowns, helping power the 35-13 victory.

Head coach Andy Reid complimented Johnson on his performance in his Monday news conference.

“My hat goes off to him — I mean, he had, what, 171 yards or whatever it was of total offense,” Reid said, via transcript from the team. “That’s a lot of contribution to the group there. So, we felt comfortable with that, that he could do that. It’s another thing of actually putting the product out there and doing it, but he’s very capable. He’s got the right attitude. He’s picked things up well. He’s a smart kid.

“So, we tried to give him that 100 [rushing] yards at the end, which we normally wouldn’t do, but he was so close that I thought it was kind of a neat deal for him.”

Johnson took 22 carries on Sunday after entering the contest with 26 on the season — illustrating how he can handle an expanded workload.

That may be important moving forward, as Walker has been one of the top utilized backs in the league so far in 2026.
