Colts sign QB Shane Buechele off Bills practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Colts have added a third quarterback to their active roster.

The team announced on Tuesday that they have signed Shane Buechele off of the Bills’ practice squad. He will slot in as the third quarterback in Indianapolis behind Anthony Richardson and Riley Leonard.

Buechele has been with the Bills for most of the last four seasons, but he did have a brief stint on the Chiefs’ roster after Patrick Mahomes was injured last year. He made his regular season debut for Kansas City during that stint and went 7-of-14 for 88 yards in Week 18.

The Colts also announced that they have signed veteran defensive end Carl Lawson off of their practice squad. They placed defensive end Caden Curry on injured reserve and waived offensive tackle Charlie Heck in corresponding moves.
