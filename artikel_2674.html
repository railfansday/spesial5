Steelers activate Michael Pittman Jr., sign Max Hurleman to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Mason Rudolph will have wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. available as a target in his first start of the 2026 season.

The Steelers activated Pittman from injured reserve on Thursday, which sets him up to face the Broncos on Friday afternoon. Pittman had seven catches for 89 yards in the first three games of the season.

Rudolph will also be able to throw to Max Hurleman. Hurleman was a topic of conversation at Aaron Rodgers’s final press conference as a member of the Steelers because Rodgers questioned why the front office did not have him on the active roster.

The team remedied that on Thursday, but Rodgers will not be able to benefit since he was released after far more critical comments about the organization in the wake of his benching on Tuesday night.

Wide receiver Ben Skowronek was placed on injured reserve and the Steelers also elevated offensive lineman Doug Nester and linebacker Jacoby Windmon from the practice squad.
