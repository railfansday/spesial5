Was Rams win outshined by crazy Thanksgiving games?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Los Angeles Rams won an exciting game over the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday, and that might feel like a week ago to some because the Thanksgiving games that followed were absolutely legendary. The Chicago Bears downed the Detroit Lions in the last seconds, the Philadelphia Eagles downed the Dallas Cowboys in the final moments, and the Buffalo Bills found a way to beat the Kansas City Chiefs as time expired. Each game on Thursday came down to the wire, and each game was a blast to watch.

Factor in the Washington Commanders beating the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday and this has been an even wilder week of Thanksgiving and football.

The Rams win was big, but unless you are a Rams fan, you've probably moved on because Thursday's games were outrageous, and now we get a Friday game with the Pittsburgh Steels vs. the Denver Broncos. A normal Black Friday Game. Nothing to see here, folks, outside of just regular old football featuring two classic franchises.

"Thursday's games featured 174 combined points but were decided by just eight, and each one came down to a game-winning play in the last three seconds or less.

Bears beat Lions, 39-35, on a 6-yard Rome Odunze touchdown reception with three seconds left.

Eagles upset Cowboys, 39-38, on a 59-yard Jake Elliott field goal with two seconds remaining.

Bills defeated Chiefs, 13-10, on a 57-yard Tyler Bass field goal with no time left on the clock.

How rare was Thursday's three-peat of fantastic finishes?

Per NFL Research, this was just the second Thanksgiving slate in NFL history that featured three games decided by fewer than five points, with the other coming in 1926. Who could forget the dueling 0-0 draws between the Canton Bulldogs and Akron Indians, and Chicago Cardinals and Chicago Bears, plus the Los Angeles Buccaneers' thrilling 9-6 win over the Detroit Panthers?

Even better, this was the first Thanksgiving to feature three games decided in the final seconds, per NFL Research."

"Can Steelers bounce back after chaotic turn of events? Pittsburgh was sitting pretty at 6-2 heading into its Week 9 bye. The season has taken a dramatic turn since then, though, with a two-game losing streak preceding Tuesday's stunning late night announcement that Aaron Rodgers has been benched in favor of Mason Rudolph, one day after Mike McCarthy told reporters he was sticking with Rodgers at quarterback. Things spiraled further on Wednesday, leading the team to waive Rodgers. He re-signed with Pittsburgh in the offseason to reunite with McCarthy for one last ride in what the QB had declared would be his final NFL season. Clearly, things didn't go according to plan, with the soon-to-be 43-year-old ranking 28th in EPA per dropback (-0.17) among the 31 qualified passers in 2026, per Next Gen Stats. Now we're left to wonder if he's played his final snap, and if the career backup Rudolph can keep the Steelers' season from going off the rails."

"Bazooka Joe Milton III leads stunning Eagles comeback vs. Cowboys. The Eagles looked dead in the water, missing starters all over the field, playing with a backup QB, and were down 31-10 deep in the third quarter. Then the largest comeback in Thanksgiving history began with Joe Milton's big arm. The QB splashed fearless bombs over and over again to puncture a limp Cowboys defense. With Saquon Barkley out early with an injury, Milton diced up the Dallas defense, completing 21 of 31 passes for 331 yards, three TDs and an INT. Several of his heaves to DeVonta Smith (11 catches, 200 receiving yards, two TDs) were perfectly dropped in the bucket. Milton might not yet have consistent touch underneath or on intermediate throws, vacillating from laser to a ball four feet off the mark (see: fourth quarter INT). However, he throws a beautiful deep ball, particularly on the move. Case in point: 188 yards and all three TDs came on passes of 20-plus air yards (56.8%). Milton's rushing ability also provides pizzazz to an offense that couldn't get off the ground until Week 12. The Eagles switched to Bazooka Joe to inject explosives into a bland turkey of an offense. Boy, did that come to fruition on Thursday. He brings a cannon arm and athletic ability to overcome blocking deficiencies. He still has strides to make, and he will have to do it against a defense better than Dallas' to prove he can be a legit starter in 2027. But he jolted a sleepy game and turned it on its head. Milton makes the four-win Eagles immensely more watchable the rest of the way."

"The Cowboys owner said he did not anticipate making any coaching changes ahead of Dallas' next game, a Week 13 game at the Seattle Seahawks 11 days away, but that didn't mean Schottenheimer was free from criticism.

"These are setbacks and they're damning setbacks, there's no question about it," Jones said, via ESPN. "And especially when we feel like that we can look at parts of the game where it was ours to take and we didn't. So when you look at every bit of it, then you have to question everything."

Jones called Thursday's loss to the Philadelphia Eagles — a 39-38 defeat in which Dallas led by 21 points deep into the third quarter — "numbing." Will that feeling preclude Jones from making the rare call of dismissing his underperforming coach midseason?

Schottenheimer was not worried about his job status after the loss.

"I worry about the guys in that locker room, and it doesn't affect me," he said."

What's harder than replacing one premier receiver? How about replacing two.

Puka Nacua, who led the NFL in receptions and yards last season, is on injured reserve following knee surgery. Davante Adams, who led the NFL in touchdown receptions last season, is on injured reserve with a hamstring injury. He might not be an elite receiver with his 34th birthday approaching but had two huge games this season."

Adding two catches (on three targets) for 21 yards, the 2024 third-round pick totaled 205 yards on 34 touches.

"It meant a lot," Corum said, via the Los Angeles Times, of the birthday performance, "but it's just another game."

Only it wasn't. Corum's humble postgame response was just the opposite of his superior showing. With the third-year back turning in career highs in carries and yards, Corum also tallied the second-most rushing yards by a player on his birthday in NFL history, behind only Bo Jackson's 221 with the Los Angeles Raiders in 1987."

The Steelers (6-4) released Rodgers on Wednesday after the 42-year-old quarterback blasted McCarthy during an astonishing appearance on McAfee. In a scathing rebuke of his longtime head coach, Rodgers — who was benched for Mason Rudolph on Tuesday night — alleged that McCarthy has fallen asleep during meetings and has skipped other meetings and team events.

Rodgers said on McAfee that he had discussed McCarthy's inattentiveness with the coach and had talked to him about ceding play-calling duties."

""If you try and bench me or don't change the play-calling, then you should just release me, because I feel like, at this point, I'm doing what's right for the organization," Rodgers said.

McCarthy held a news conference on Wednesday hours after Rodgers' appearance on McAfee and told reporters, without getting into specifics, that Rodgers had made an "ultimatum" that was "outside of his job description" and that it was his decision to bench the QB.

Steelers president Art Rooney II backed McCarthy in a Thursday interview with The Pittsburgh Post-Gazette and criticized Rodgers for "insubordination" and for not being a "good teammate."

Rodgers signed a one-year contract this past spring that included $22 million guaranteed to stay with Pittsburgh for a second season, a move that he said was made in part due to the organization's decision to hire McCarthy after Mike Tomlin's 19-year tenure ended. Rodgers and McCarthy previously had spent 13 years together in Green Bay, where they won Super Bowl XLV over Rooney's Steelers."
