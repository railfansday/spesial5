Broncos face quick pivot to preparing for Mason Rudolph on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Black Friday game between the Broncos and Steelers was preceded by the Black Tuesday benching of quarterback Aaron Rodgers. Now, the Broncos will have to quickly pivot in their preparations.

As one source put it, the Broncos aren’t sure whether the unexpected move is a good thing or a bad thing.

Obviously, Rodgers has slipped. But the Steelers were 6-4. They had gone all in with the first-ballot Hall of Famer, for a second straight year. The notion that they’d pull the rug out from under Rodgers and ride with Rudolph is jarring.

Quarterbacks on winning teams don’t get benched very often. In 2004, the Giants sat Hall of Famer Kurt Warner despite a 5-4 record. But the goal then was to get rookie Eli Manning on the field. For the Steelers, they’ve made the decision that Rudolph, a veteran backup 19 regular-season starts in nine years, gives them a better chance to win.

The element of surprise could be a factor in the timing, too. It’s possible that the Steelers considered not saying anything at all.

That would have been hard to pull off. Unless coach Mike McCarthy had been able to get everyone (including Rodgers) to clam up, word would have gotten out that Rudolph was getting the first-team reps at practice.

This story is just getting started. Rodgers will surely have a reaction. And it surely won’t be a shrug of the shoulders and an acceptance that he won’t even be in uniform for the game.

Our guess? He’ll never be in a Steelers uniform again.
