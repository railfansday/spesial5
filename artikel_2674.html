Nick Bosa concedes he’s been struggling to execute his calling card

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Throughout his career, Nick Bosa has enjoyed huge success rushing the passer in a very obvious and relatively simplistic fashion.

But, after going another game without a sack in the 49ers' shootout win over the Dallas Cowboys last Sunday, Bosa conceded he has been struggling to execute parts of his game that have been his calling cards in previous years.

Bosa has long since been known as one of the game's premier power rushers, with the former Defensive Player of the Year winning primarily by translating his speed off the ball into sheer brute force.

But the devastation he can often cause has been largely conspicuous by its absence since he returned from a calf injury in Week 9, with Bosa still without sack in four games this season.

And, speaking to the media on Wednesday, Bosa acknowledged that issues with his get-off have played a part in his failure to bring down opposing quarterbacks so far this season.

Bosa said of his adjustment to Raheem Morris' defense:

"I think there's a learning curve and … a lot of guys haven't had many reps in the scheme so far. I'm trying to break it down to the basics and just kind of get back. I missed a year and then missed camp. I kind of took for granted some of the things that I did automatically, and I thought I could just kind of coast and continue to do those things automatically and focus on other things. But I need to, especially with a newer defense, really hone in on the things that made me the player that I am."

Asked what he took for granted, Bosa replied: "Just getting down, getting my feet set, and getting off the ball.

"It takes time. I'm not going to make excuses because nobody really cares. And but I'm going to get better. And I'm pretty confident in that physically and how I'm playing."

To his credit, Bosa delivered a reminder of what can happen when he gets it right as a pass rusher against Dallas when a superb bull rush against Terence Steele forced a rushed throw from Dak Prescott that should have been intercepted by Dre Greenlaw.

The 49ers need those kind of impactful rushes on a more consistent basis from their best pass rusher, though the level of attention he draws in terms of double teams and chips has played a role in negating his influence.

Bosa, though, is used to that kind of treatment and will need to find a way to overcome it if the 49er pass rush is to improve down the stretch. There may be external concern surrounding Bosa's lack of production, but nobody can doubt he is in the right mindset as he looks to reprise the form that made him one of the NFL's most feared pass rushers.
