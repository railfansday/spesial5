CFP rankings 2026: LSU ranks No. 21 in second playoff rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The second week of College Football Playoff rankings have just been released, and the LSU Tigers have fallen from No. 14 to No. 21.

LSU's CFP hopes are fading following an embarrassing 31-13 home loss to Alabama on Saturday. The Tigers now sit at 6-3 overall, leaving little room for error as the regular season winds down. While their postseason chances are slim, they are not mathematically eliminated yet. The Tigers will need to win all three games to finish 9-3 but unfortunately, they face a particularly difficult stretch against Texas and Tennessee, two top 10 opponents, before closing the regular season against Arkansas.

So, while LSU's path to the playoff remains possible, another loss would likely eliminate the Tigers from contention.
