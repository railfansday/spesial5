Raiders vs. Seahawks odds: Opening lines for Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Las Vegas Raiders had an embarrassing loss on Sunday. They couldn't hang with the San Francisco 49ers and lost in ugly fashion, 38-17 on the road.

Now the Raiders face another NFC West opponent. The Seattle Seahawks are coming to Las Vegas. The Seahawks are beat up at the quarterback position and hoping to have Sam Darnold back. It will be another tough matchup for the Silver and Black.

Fanduel Sportsbook posted odds for all the games of the 1oth week of the season. The Raiders are 3-point underdogs against the San Francisco 49ers, with an over/under (O/U) of 43.5.

With teams on a bye in Week 10, we won't see much NFL action. FanDuel lists the Jacksonville Jaguars as the biggest favorites in the upcoming games, at -9.5 over the Tennessee Titans.
