Dillon Thieneman roasts Dennis Allen for Stanky Leg attempt after beating Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dillon Thieneman roasts Dennis Allen for Stanky Leg attempt after beating Saints appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Chicago Bears are surging. On Sunday, they defeated the New Orleans Saints 21-13 at home. As usual, when they win, head coach Ben Johnson usually breaks out the celebratory "Stanky Leg."

However, that honor went to defensive coordinator Dennis Allen.

While the team cheered him on, defensive back Dillon Thieneman said that Allen shouldn't do it again, per CHGO Bears.

"We probably shouldn't have him do it again", Thieneman said. He has 2 interceptions and 30 tackles in his rookie season.

But Allen deserves credit for helping the Bears win. Altogether, Chicago's defense recorded 6 sacks, two takeaways, and a safety. Additionally, the defense also recorded 24 total pressures and held the Saints to 307 yards.

Thieneman finished the game with an interception and two tackles.

After the game, the game ball was given to defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, defensive end Montez Sweat, and cornerback Clark Phillip.

Allen has been the defensive coordinator since 2025, when Ben Johnson was hired as head coach. He was head coach of the Oakland Raiders from 2012 to 2014. Then, Allen became the Saints' head coach from 2022 to 2024, after serving as defensive coordinator from 2015 to 2021. He also served as defensive line coach from 2006 to 2007 and secondary coach from 2008 to 2010.

Before then, Allen was an assistant defensive coach with the Atlanta Falcons and the Denver Broncos from 2002 to 2011.

The Bears are now 8-2 and tied with the Detroit Lions for the top spot in the NFC North. They are also on a four-game winning streak as they prepare to play the Lions on Thanksgiving.
