Falkirk produced one of the results of the season with a stunning victory at Tynecastle, while Hibs followed their derby semi-final success with another impressive performance to heap further pressure on Aberdeen manager Stephen Robinson. Stevie Miller is joined by Ewan Murray and Cillian Sheridan to analyse Hearts' defensive collapse, assess John McGlynn's outstanding work at Falkirk and ask whether Hibs have finally turned a corner under Marink Reedijk. The panel also discusses Aberdeen's alarming slide towards the bottom of the table, the uncertainty surrounding Robinson's future and whether a managerial change could be imminent.
