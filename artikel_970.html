Man Utd expected to back Poch in January transfer window

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino is expected to be backed in the January transfer window as the club aim to address their disappointing start to the season.

A left-back, centre-back and forward are areas the club are looking to potentially strengthen in January with Pochettino expected to have input on targets they will pursue.

Despite the club's hierarchy feeling the team should be further up the table, there is also an acknowledgement that they could have further strengthened the squad in the summer in areas where they lacked depth.

Sky Sports News understands Manchester United already had plans to move in the upcoming window under former head coach Michael Carrick, before he was sacked last month following United's worst start to a league season for 40 years.

However, Pochettino is now expected to be consulted in the coming weeks and will have his say on potential recruits in January.

Fulham's Calvin Bassey and Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite are two players United are monitoring as potential options to reinforce their defence in January.

Despite Fulham's struggles this season, Bassey is a player who has impressed and has just six months left on his current deal, though his club have the option to extend that by a further year.

Fulham head coach Alvaro Arbeloa has described the 26-year-old as one of the best defenders in the world and said the club's aim is to extend Bassey's contract.

Meanwhile, Branthwaite has long been admired by United. In 2024, United had two bids rejected for the centre-back, their second being worth around £50m.

Everton are in a strong negotiating position over the 24-year-old, who has a contract until the summer of 2030.

United are looking at left-footed centre-back options as they consider potential replacements for Lisandro Martinez. His future at Old Trafford looks in doubt come next summer with his current contract expiring.

Despite United having an option for a further 12 months, he is unlikely to be offered fresh terms as Sky Sports News reported last month.

The club decided not to pursue a left-back in the summer as they focused on their midfield rebuild with the belief their long-term targets would be more attainable next year.

It remains to be seen whether that will be true come the end of the season and where that leaves players such as Newcastle United's Lewis Hall, but with five different players being used in that position with limited success, the club will look at the possibility of cover at left-back in January.

Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson continues to be monitored, as first reported by Sky Sports News in March. Fulham are aware of widespread interest in Robinson but no club has so far made a move for the 29-year-old, who worked under Pochettino with the USA.

Barcelona's Alejandro Balde was a player linked with United in the summer but was never at a stage when the move seemed likely. United were aware of his potential availability and it has not been ruled out that they revisit a deal in January if it works for all parties.

Manchester City's Rayan Ait-Nouri was an option United tentatively explore on Deadline Day but they decided against pursuing him due to City's insistence on an obligation to make the move permanent. Sky Sports News understands he is not considered a target at this stage as United look at other options.

With United's main No 9 Benjamin Sesko ruled out until at least January, the club will explore potential reinforcements in the upcoming window to re-balance the club's attack.

Although United scored seven goals in their last two games before the break, the club have missed a physical presence in Sesko's absence to lead the line.

Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin was a player of interest but his future looks likely to remain at Elland Road and United are not expected to explore a move for the forward.

Any outgoings in January could have an impact on how much the club can reinforce Pochettino's squad but numerous first-team departures are not likely with the size of the squad and long-term injuries as well as Pochettino needing time to assess his squad all set to play a factor.

Joshua Zirkzee continues to be a player who attracts interest from Italy and United could entertain a move for the Dutch forward if they receive a suitable offer but would need cover in that position.

Martinez's future will under the spotlight in January. Sky Sports News reported last month United are unlikely to offer him a new contract. His current deal expires next summer although the club hold an option for a further 12 months.

Pochettino will also have to manage other expiring contracts in his squad with Harry Maguire, Luke Shaw and captain Bruno Fernandes also in the last six months of their current contracts, although it is worth noting the club have a 12-month option on Fernandes and Maguire.
