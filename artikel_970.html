Eubank Jr and Brook in talks over blockbuster British battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Chris Eubank Jr is considering options for a return to the ring , including a potential showdown with Kell Brook; talks are ongoing for Eubank Jr vs Brook but terms are yet to be agreed; former IBF welterweight champion Brook has not fought since he defeated Amir Khan in February 2022;

Chris Eubank Jr has been holding talks with Kell Brook for a blockbuster British battle, but a deal is yet to be finalised.

Eubank Jr is considering options for a return to the ring and a showdown with Brook, the former IBF world welterweight champion, has emerged as a potential fight.

But Elliott Amoakoh, executive director of Eubank Jr's management team, Nova Sport LLC, has told Sky Sports that terms have not yet been agreed.

"Talks are ongoing but the date, purse and location are yet to be confirmed," Amoakoh told Sky Sports.

Brook has been absent from the ring since he settled his long-running rivalry with Amir Khan in February 2022, winning the grudge fight with a sixth-round stoppage.

In the aftermath of that win, Brook was linked with a fight against Eubank, although nothing materialised and the Sheffield man entered retirement.

Eubank Jr switched his attention to domestic clashes with Liam Smith, defeating the Liverpudlian emphatically in a rematch.

He beat Conor Benn on points in April 2025, then lost the return bout on the scorecards last November in his most recent fight.

Brook had been due to have a comeback fight in Dubai in March, but the event did not go ahead as planned and he is now in negotiations over resuming his boxing career against Eubank Jr.
