Scholes ruled out for season with injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Vitality Roses head coach Anna Stembridge has been hit by multiple injuries ahead of her side's upcoming Vitality Netball Horizon Series against Jamaica; Alicia Scholes will miss the remainder of the season, while Olivia Tchine and Sasha Glasgow will not make the series

Alicia Scholes has been ruled out of the remainder of the 2026 season after she suffered a ruptured ACL during a training camp with England's Vitality Roses.

England Netball say Scholes, who is the daughter of Manchester United great Paul Scholes, will commence her rehabilitation immediately and will be supported by both the governing body and London Pulse's medical and performance teams.

"We're all gutted for Alicia," Roses head coach Anna Stembridge said, as she unveiled her squads for her side's upcoming Vitality Netball Horizon Series on Tuesday.

"She's been working incredibly hard in camp, and it's been great to see her developing combinations and making her mark within the group.

"To have her season end this way is hugely disappointing, but she has the full support of both England Netball and London Pulse as she begins her rehabilitation."

Stembridge also announced Olivia Tchine and Sasha Glasgow had been omitted from both home and away squads for her side's upcoming Vitality Netball Horizon Series due to injury.

The series will see the Roses face Jamaica on November 21 and 22 in a two-match series at London's Copper Box Arena, before travelling to face the same opponent in the away leg in Kingston on December 5 and 6.

Nat Metcalf, Ella Clark and Berri Neal have all been drafted into the squad. Metcalf returns to the court after sustaining an injury at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.

Neal will act as a travelling reserve for both home and away legs, while Clark, who made her Commonwealth Games debut in the summer, will be included in the full squad for the opening match, before also moving to become a second travelling reserve for the second match.

Izzi Phillips, who had previously been a travelling reserve for the away leg, has been elevated to the squad for the full series.

Vice-captain Tchine suffered an injury in October and will miss the series as she completes her return-to-play protocols.

Glasgow had picked up her injury in training before coming into camp, and will miss the home leg of the series, before joining the squad in Jamaica for the final two matches.

Stembridge said: "It's never easy to lose players through injury, particularly at this stage of our preparations.

"Liv [Olivia Tchine] and Sasha [Glasgow] both need a little more time in their return-to-play journeys, and we'll continue to support them as they work towards getting back on court."

Reflecting on her injury, Tchine said: "I'm absolutely gutted to not be playing in the Vitality Netball Horizon Series this year.

"Just like every other England game, I was looking forward to playing against some of the world's best players. Injuries are annoying and frustrating but I'm working so hard to make sure that I'm back on court for the next series.

"In the meantime, I'll be the Vitality Roses' biggest and LOUDEST cheerleader, supporting them all the way!"
