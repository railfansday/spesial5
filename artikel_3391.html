Hands-on: Anker’s 10K MagGo 2 Pro offers fast 25W MagSafe charging with a kickstand

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Anker’s new MagGo Pro 2 power bank offers active cooling, 25W MagSafe fast charging, 10,000mAh of capacity, and a smart display – all in one package. Anker touts it as the fastest and coolest wireless power bank on the market.

Thanks to the active cooling within this power bank, you’ll notice it doesn’t get as hot as other power banks on the market. This also helps it charge faster, since it doesn’t have to throttle as quickly to keep the temperature manageable.

As a result, you can charge an iPhone 17 Pro up to 50% in just 25 minutes with this battery bank. It also supports up to 45W of output via USB-C.

On the side of the power bank, you’ll notice a smart display. This display can show you the battery level, how quickly its charging an attached device (either wirelessly or with USB-C), the internal temperature of the battery bank, as well as the battery health. You can rotate between all of these options by pressing the power button.

This power bank also offers a kickstand, which I’ve found to be one of my favorite features on recent MagSafe battery banks. It makes it easy to just prop up your phone and watch a video, no matter the environment.

If you have an iPhone Duo, this power bank will extend past the bottom of the phone. If you rotate it and use the kickstand, there’s no issue propping up your phone and charging it.

Lastly, the MagGo Pro 2 comes in a bunch of fun colors, including a burgundy to match the new iPhone 18 Pro. It’s also available in silver, blue, and gray.

If the Anker MagGo Pro 2 Power Bank interests you, you can pick one up for $109.99 on Amazon.
