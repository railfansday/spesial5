Cross Season 3's Return Timeline On Prime Video Gets Huge New Update From Series Creator

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: A major new Cross season 3 production update has been revealed that gives insight into when the crime thriller might return to Prime Video.

An adaptation of James Patterson's Alex Cross books, the TV show debuted in 2024, with Aldis Hodge playing the titular detective who investigates horrific and fatal crimes. He's also dealt with losing his wife and doing whatever it takes to discover the truth behind her murder. After two seasons, production on Cross season 3 began back in April.

In an interview with ScreenRant's Brandon Zachary for The Greatest, Cross creator, showrunner and executive producer Ben Watkins revealed that filming has now wrapped on the eight-episode third season.

"We're in post-production," he revealed, before adding that the cast and crew had to come back for two additional days of filming "because we always film in DC." Around that same time, he was "writing page revisions" before cameras rolled again.

Watkins has also watched "some rough cuts of the scenes they did" and is "so happy" with what he's seen so far. "We just finished filming that," he added. "We're just finishing the editing of the episodes. We've even started scoring the episodes already."

The past several weeks have been "crazy" for Watkins, who explained that, aside from Cross, he's been conducting research for The Greatest, his new Muhammad Ali biopic series, so that he'll be ready to go if Prime Video orders a second season. Speaking of the famous boxer, Watkins was also in Louisville, Kentucky, to attend The Muhammad Ali Humanitarian Awards.

Ben Watkins: "This is how crazy life is right now. We're in post-production on season 3 of Cross, but we had two more days of filming because we always film in DC. I was in Louisville at the humanitarian awards for the Ali Center on Saturday and Sunday, and then I'm doing research for if we get season 2 [of The Greatest], so we brought writers with us while we're doing that. Then I'm on the flight in. I'm writing page revisions for the last two days of filming of Cross season 3, and Aldis Hodge and Isaiah Mustafa are coming in there, Coby Bell is in there, and it was really surreal because I'm in this moment where The Greatest has just dropped. I'm so blessed by that, and then Cross is so near and dear to my heart, and I'm on this text chain with my Cross team, getting everything going in DC. I've just seen some rough cuts of the scenes they did. I'm so happy. We just finished filming that. We're just finishing the editing of the episodes. We've even started scoring the episodes already."

Even though Cross season 3 is now in post-production, a release window has not been revealed by Watkins or Prime Video yet. However, the timelines for the previous installments could shed light on a possible premiere date.

The first season of Cross was in production from January to May 2023 before being released on Prime Video a year-and-a-half later in November 2024. Then the cast and crew returned to film season 2 from April to September 2024 before it premiered in February 2026, which meant there was a post-production gap of one year and five months.

Based on those trajectories, Cross season 3 could premiere on Prime Video in spring 2028. That is a long wait, of course, but it would align with the schedules of the first two seasons if that's when the show does end up returning.

Hodge's Cross co-stars in the most recent season were Matthew Lillard, Jeanine Mason, Wes Chatham, Alona Tal, Isaiah Mustafa, Juanita Jennings, Samantha Walkes, Melody Hurd, Caleb Elijah and Johnny Ray Gill. Ethan Embry, Ron Perlman and Robin Givens have joined the cast for the upcoming third season.

Cross, which has received largely positive reviews from critics throughout its run so far, introduced a billionaire named Lance Durand (Lillard) earlier this year, as Alex and Kayla Craig worked together to protect him from a killer, who was later revealed to be Luz.

The Cross season 2 finale showed Luz escaping after jumping from a bridge and Alex quitting after finding out that the FBI had been covering up Durand's potential crimes. This sets up a major shakeup and new conflicts for season 3, which is now in post-production.
