These Noise-Canceling Skullcandy Headphones Are 50% Off Ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Black Friday is an excellent time to score a new pair of headphones that would otherwise be a splurge, with retailers like Amazon and Walmart already offering massive discounts on tech essentials from top brands a week ahead of the holiday. One of the early Black Friday deals that should be on your radar is the Skullcandy Crusher ANC 2 headphones, a mid-range noise-canceling option that’s perfect for bass lovers and is currently 50% off, dropping to $119.99 (originally $239.99).

PCMag says they deliver “brain-rattling levels of bass” and have a comfortable fit, with generous memory foam cushioning and minimal clamping pressure that lends itself well to long listening sessions. The aesthetic is nothing to scoff at either, with a refined and modern style. It’s worth noting they also come in a hardshell zip-up case with a fabric-lined exterior.

Powered by 40mm dynamic drivers, they offer reliable noise cancellation with the ANC slider set to max on the companion app, which lets users adjust a range of controls and listening modes, including the intensity of bass. The bass boost feature is ideal for those seeking bass-heavy headphones, but may be overwhelming for those who prefer a more balanced sound.

Battery life lasts around 50 to 60 hours per charge, with Skullcandy claiming that you get four hours of listening time from around 10 minutes of charging. While brands like Bose will outperform in ANC quality, if you’re looking for noise-canceling audio gear that checks most boxes at a reasonable price point, the Skullcandy Crusher ANC 2 headphones are a smart choice, especially at over $100 off.
