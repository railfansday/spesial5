Anitta Shows Off Brazilian Roots and Powerhouse Moves at Latin Grammys

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Anitta brought her roots to the Latin Grammys stage with a performance filled with Brazilian folk and culture. The triple-threat performed a medley of songs from her critically acclaimed album Equilibrium.

Anitta opened her spectacular performance in a heavenly white get-up. With a team of dancers, she stormed the stage and even danced into the crowd. She seamlessly stitched together songs from Equilibrium, including “Choka Choka,” “Desgraça,” “Meia Noite,” and “Ouro.”

Anitta is up for both Album of the Year and Best Portuguese Language Contemporary Pop Album at this year’s ceremony for Equilibrium. While the Brazilian pop star is a 12-time nominee, who has performed at the ceremony over the years, she has yet to take home her first Latin Grammy award.

Known as the “Queen of Brazilian Pop,” Anitta continues to expand her reach. “I’ll be at the Latin Grammys this year, singing and performing,” the Brazilian star said in Spanish earlier this week. “And I’m nominated, so I’m very happy, too. Don’t miss it, it’s going to be an incredible night.”

Anitta wasn’t lying. This year’s exciting slate of performers include Rosalía, Karol G, Daddy Yankee, Feid, Silvana Estrada, Jorge Drexler, Mon Laferte, Anitta, Carín León, Rawayana, and Elena Rose, and more. Maluma and Roselyn Sánchez are hosting the ceremony once again.
