Poker Face Fans Get Devastating Official Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It seems we’ve seen the last of Poker Face. In an interview with Variety, series creator Rian Johnson (Knives Out, Star Wars: The Last Jedi) said the reimagined version of the TV show is no longer in development. Last year, Peacock announced that the detective series was canceled after two seasons, but Johnson kept hopes alive when he revealed he was shopping a new version of the series to interested parties. This reimagined show would no longer center on Natasha Lyonne as Charlie Cale and would instead have Peter Dinklage as the new protagonist.

When asked if he was still shopping this new version of the show, Johnson said, “No, at the moment it’s in its resting place. I got very excited about the notion of going out there with Dinklage, but then the more we kind of explored it, the more it kind of didn’t really make sense. But hopefully I can do something else with him someday. He’s the best.” Johnson’s comments make it clear that the series isn’t coming back any time soon, if ever.

Poker Face ran on Peacock from Jan. 26, 2023, to July 10, 2025, with 22 episodes across two seasons. The story centers on Charlie Cale, a woman with the uncanny ability to detect when someone is lying. As Cale travels the U.S., she stumbles into case-of-the-week mysteries that usually involve murder. The series uses theinverted detective story format popularized by Columbo, where episodes reveal the killer and victim to the audience before the protagonist.Like Columbo, the show featured famous actors as guest characters, often as the killer or victim, including Cynthia Erivo, Katie Holmes, Kumail Nanjiani, Adrien Brody, and Giancarlo Esposito.

Poker Face was very well-received by critics; the series has a 97% Tomatometer score, and Lyonne won an Emmy for her portrayal of Charlie. With the show dead, we’ll have to wait and see what’s next for director Rian Johnson. After the third Knives Out entry, Wake Up Dead Man, came out in 2025, Johnson said that he and Daniel Craig were “starting to formulate ideas” for a fourth film.
