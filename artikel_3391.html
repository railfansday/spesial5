NCIS: New York Makes A Massive Breakthrough As Series Reaches Major Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NCIS universe is showing no signs of slowing down anytime soon.

The NCIS franchise continues to expand its global presence, with its newest spinoff, NCIS: New York, hitting a major milestone after its debut. According to an exclusive report from Variety, the crime drama, starring LL Cool J, has secured distribution agreements in more than 135 international markets. The deals range from traditional TV networks to streaming services across six continents.

The show's international agreements include several major entertainment companies. Paramount+ has acquired the series for multiple territories, including Australia, Austria, Brazil, Germany, Ireland, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Latin America. Other confirmed distribution partners include Global and STACKTV (Canada), M-Net (Africa), Sky (New Zealand), and Amazon (Southeast Asia).

Audiences in Japan will have access through J:COM and WOWOW, while AXN has acquired the series for Latin America. Additionally, Disney (the Balkans), STAR Channel (Spain), Viaplay (Sweden, Denmark, and Norway), and Coupang Play (South Korea) also have deals with the series. The show has also been acquired by platforms in Belgium, Greece, Iceland, Israel, and the Netherlands.

The news comes after NCIS: New York premiered on CBS on October 6, 2026. The series brings Cool J back to the franchise as Sam Hanna, the character he portrayed for 14 seasons on NCIS: Los Angeles. That latter ended its run in May 2023. However, Hanna's story is not over as the new spinoff places him in a brand-new city with a different team of agents.

The newest chapter follows Hanna as he returns to New York City, where he grew up, for an assignment. At first, it seems like a pretty straightforward mission that was supposed to be temporary. Eventually, he realizes that there is far more than first meets the eye, forcing him to play a bigger role.

Hanna joins forces with Special Agent Nick Schaeffer, who is portrayed by Scott Caan. Together, the two characters help lead the team that is responsible for investigating cases involving national security and military personnel. NCIS: New York also stars Jennifer Beals (Robyn Wells), Jacqueline Byers (Addison Ross), Shane Harper (Wyatt Sellers), and Devin Druid (Sean Sullivan).

NCIS: New York airs new episodes on Tuesdays at 9:00 PM ET and 6:00 PM PT on CBS. The series will be taking a break during the week of Thanksgiving.
