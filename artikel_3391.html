Jujutsu Kaisen Drops New Designs For Yuji, Megumi, And Yuta Ahead Of Big Season 4 Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Jujutsu Kaisen is giving fans another reason to look toward Jump Festa 2027, unveiling fresh character illustrations that put recognizable sorcerers in a coordinated cheer squad theme. The colorful artwork features Yuji Itadori, Megumi Fushiguro, and Yuta Okkotsu, alongside two important Culling Game characters.

Rather than teasing an episode or revealing a major plot development, the illustrations from @animeupdates on X offer a playful promotional look at the cast ahead of the franchise’s next event. Their arrival is timely, as the anime’s fourth season is in production and Jump Festa is scheduled to deliver information about its continuation.

The new artwork was announced by the official Jujutsu Kaisen anime account and Anime Updates for Jump Festa 2027. Its cheer squad theme features five full-length portraits in separate colored frames. Kinji Hakari appears against yellow, Kirara Hoshi gets pink, Yuji is framed in orange, Megumi in teal, and Yuta in blue.

All five characters wear dark school-style outfits, but their individual poses and accessories give the portraits distinct personalities. Hakari stands with his arms folded and a confident grin, while Kirara winks beneath her dark bangs and pink accents. Yuji looks especially upbeat, smiling broadly with both arms raised behind his head.

Megumi has a restrained expression and holds a long weapon diagonally, while Yuta wears a blue headband with one gloved hand visible. The color panels make the group feel coordinated without making the characters interchangeable. It is a lighter visual concept for a series whose Culling Game storyline is anything but cheerful.

The choice of characters is also notable. Yuji and Megumi have been main characters in the anime since its early episodes, while Yuta’s role has grown increasingly important following Jujutsu Kaisen 0 and the later story. Hakari and Kirara, meanwhile, are closely tied to the Culling Game material that the next season will continue adapting.

The illustrations are tied to Jump Festa 2027 booth, where the company plans to sell Jujutsu Kaisen merchandise using the new artwork. The booth is among the event’s manufacturer exhibits in Hall 9, and the announcement confirms related products are coming. Specific formats, prices, and sales details have not yet been disclosed.

Despite its 2027 name, Jump Festa will take place December 19 and 20, 2026, at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. The convention spans exhibition halls 1 through 9 and 11, with general hours from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. both days. Admission is free, but visitors need advance approval or an invitation.

Jujutsu Kaisen also has a Jump Super Stage on Saturday, December 19, from 3:25 p.m. to 3:55 p.m. Japan time. Guests include Junya Enoki, Mikako Komatsu, Kazuya Nakai, and Daisuke Namikawa, Japanese voice actors for Yuji, Maki, Hakari, and Choso. The program will revisit Season 3 before sharing the latest Jujutsu Kaisen Season 4 news.

The event matters because the anime’s fourth season, officially titled The Culling Game Part 2, is in production at MAPPA. Season 3 adapted the first portion of the Culling Game, leaving the larger conflict unresolved. The next installment will move the story into its next phase, with surviving players and shifting alliances facing greater danger.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 4 was announced after Season 3’s finale in March 2026, and MAPPA released a teaser trailer in June confirming production. A super teaser visual followed on August 29, placing Ryomen Sukuna at the center of an ominous image. However, the studio and production committee have not announced a premiere date.
