Coca-Cola pivots from divisive AI-generated ads for holiday campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

“Enjoy Joy” spots are part of a seasonal push that includes digital and social content, experiential activations and packaging.

Coca-Cola is going back to basics with the recently launched “Enjoy Joy” spots, showcasing moments of connection and togetherness in a decidedly traditional way. The creative direction comes after the brand ran ads during the last two years made with generative AI that — despite exceeding internal benchmarks — proved divisive with consumers. The new ads were directed by Paul Dektor and produced by Mayan Productions and arrive as holiday marketers double down on human-made creative.

The “Enjoy Joy” spots, including versions tailored to the Hispanic market, are joined by a handful of extensions across channels. Coca-Cola will run creator-led content across Meta, TikTok, Pinterest and YouTube, along with a “Grandma's Holiday Table” social series that focuses on the “abuelas, nonnas, nanas, aunties and tías” who bring holiday traditions to life. The campaign will also span streaming, audio, cinema and out-of-home channels and includes an Amazon media partnership featuring creator and commerce integrations.

“There's something special about the role Coca‑Cola plays during the holidays. People look forward to seeing how we'll show up each year, whether it's through the traditions they know and love or something entirely unexpected,” said Stacy Jackson, vice president for the Coca‑Cola Trademark, in a statement. “It's about finding new ways to make Coca‑Cola part of the season while staying true to what people have always loved about the brand.”

The brand’s long-running holiday caravan tour kicked off Nov. 5 in Nashville and will make more than 270 stops nationwide, including New York City's Times Square on Nov. 24. Each stop includes activities like photos with Santa Claus and Coke’s polar bear mascot, a gifting cabin and a beverage station. The caravan also ties into the brand’s partnership with the USO, giving consumers an opportunity to share messages with those who serve in the military.

During the holiday season, Coca‑Cola, Coca‑Cola Zero Sugar and Diet Coke will offer limited-edition packaging featuring Santa Claus, who also makes a brief appearance in the ad creative. Coca‑Cola Holiday Creamy Vanilla and its zero-sugar variant will also return to shelves for a limited time.

Coca-Cola grew net revenues by 7% to $13.4 billion in Q3 2026, beating Wall Street estimates.. The earnings were released days after the company announced the completion of its global marketing operating system review, selecting Omnicom as its media partner in North America. WPP Open X will continue to serve as Coca-Cola’s global creative partner, while Omnicom will unite media, data, CRM, loyalty and creators in a connected ecosystem in North America. The new structure will be implemented in phases beginning Dec. 1.
