Why Running Slower Can Make You a Faster Runner

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Runners love to push themselves. But holding back can help create a stronger base, especially when you’re training for a big race.

Slowing down can actually help you get faster.

On this week’s episode of The Amazing Runner’s World Show, Aly and Kristine discuss why slow runs deserve a crucial spot in your training plan.

In the previous episode, we talked about how to pace the five boroughs of the NYC Marathon.
