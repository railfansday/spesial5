Why you should upgrade to an OLED for GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

With GTA 6 bringing players back to Vice City, many are already thinking about how to get the most out of Rockstar’s next open-world game when it launches.

With neon-lit streets, crowded nightlife, tropical beaches and surrounding landscapes of Leonida, GTA 6 is Rockstar’s most visually ambitious release yet.

While much of the conversation is focused on how consoles will handle it, the display you use is likely to make a more noticeable difference.

OLED technology has become increasingly popular thanks to its deep blacks, strong contrast, and vibrant colors. For a game featuring the diverse environments shown in GTA 6’s trailers, and especially paired with HDR, those advantages make upgrading your display the obvious choice.

Unlike traditional LCD monitors, OLED displays can turn individual pixels off completely, producing those luxuriously deep blacks. That will make GTA 6’s neon-lit streets, nighttime environments, and colorful landscapes look far more impressive.

For GTA 6, those improvements will be particularly noticeable in several environments players have already seen in Rockstar’s trailers:

Modern OLED gaming monitors have also introduced technologies designed to improve brightness and visibility across different environments.

GIGABYTE’s AORUS FO32U24G features a 32-inch 4K display built around a fourth-generation Tandem WOLED panel. Its RealBlack Glossy screen treatment is designed to preserve deep blacks and perceived contrast while reducing reflections.

The monitor also features HyperNits, a technology that adjusts HDR brightness to help prevent images from appearing overly dark. According to GIGABYTE, HyperNits can increase overall brightness by up to 30% while preserving highlight details.

That will be particularly relevant to GTA 6’s varied environments, where players will frequently transition between bright sunlight, darker interiors, and nighttime scenes.

Although high-end gaming monitors are often associated with PC gaming, OLED’s picture quality advantages aren’t limited to players with expensive gaming computers.

GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S.

Both the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S support modern display features, including HDR and up to 120Hz output in compatible games.

For console players, there are three main display features worth considering:

GIGABYTE’s FO32U24G and FO27Q28G both feature HDMI 2.1 connectivity, making them options for players who use their monitors with consoles as well as PCs.

And while consoles won’t maximise these monitors’ refresh rates of 240Hz and 280Hz, OLED’s visual improvements will be just as glorious.

Even at lower frame rates, OLED’s contrast, black levels, and color reproduction will improve how supported games look. Of course, GTA 6’s final rendering resolution and performance modes remain dependent on Rockstar’s implementation.

Despite the advantages of OLED, burn-in remains one of the most common concerns surrounding the technology.

Burn-in occurs when uneven pixel wear leaves persistent image retention on a display. This can become a consideration when static elements remain visible for extended periods, including health bars, mini-maps, and other interface elements commonly found in video games.

For a game like GTA 6, where players could spend hundreds of hours exploring the open world, long-term display protection is worth considering.

GIGABYTE addresses this with AI OLED Care Pro, available on the AORUS FO32U24G and FO27Q28G. The system automatically manages brightness, performs pixel cleaning during idle periods, and powers down the display when users step away.

It also includes Cooling Force technology, which uses integrated heat pipes to help manage panel temperatures during extended use.

While OLED technology offers several advantages, choosing the right display involves more than simply deciding between OLED and LCD.

Screen size, resolution, panel technology, and room lighting can all influence which monitor makes the most sense.

For players prioritizing GTA 6’s visual presentation, GIGABYTE’s FO32U24G offers a 32-inch 4K Tandem WOLED panel, while the FO27Q28G provides a smaller 27-inch QHD alternative with a 280Hz refresh rate.

GIGABYTE’s M series also includes several options for players with different preferences:

The MO27Q28G and MO27Q28GR are particularly relevant for players deciding between matte-style anti-reflection and glossy OLED displays. Both feature fourth-generation Tandem WOLED panels, but their different screen treatments may appeal to different gaming environments.

Meanwhile, the MO27Q2A provides an alternative OLED panel technology, featuring QD-OLED panel with 1.5M:1 contrast ratio for deeper black and richer details.

Ultimately, choosing a monitor for GTA 6 depends on the display someone already owns and what they want to improve, but OLED’s contrast, black levels, and color reproduction will provide a noticeable visual upgrade.
