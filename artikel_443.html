MacBook Neo 2 with new colors expected by March 2027, report states

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Apple's most affordable and most vibrant laptop has only been out since this past March, but it sounds like the company will offer a spiffy new upgrade sooner rather than later. That's hardly a surprise, given the success of the product so far.

That's what Mark Gurman of Bloomberg is reporting, anyway. In his latest Power On newsletter, Gurman predicted that Apple will sell a second iteration of the MacBook Neo most likely by the end of March 2027. That timing is meant to line up with the iOS/macOS 27.4 upgrade cycle, at which point Gurman says we could also see the iPhone 18 and 18e models, a new OLED iPad Air, and a new budget iPad.

Apple upgrading a laptop just a year after release is not unheard of. In recent years, the company has been releasing annual upgrades of its MacBook Air and Pro lines, outfitting the devices with the latest Apple Silicon, sometimes with few other changes.

As for what to expect from the new MacBook Neo, it doesn't sound like a hugely substantial upgrade at this point in time. From a usability standpoint, the most important thing Gurman mentioned is that the A18 Pro chip powering the original Neo will be upgraded to an A19 chip, with RAM bumped from 8GB to 12GB as well.

It remains to be seen if the current $699 starting price for the MacBook Neo will go up, but it's definitely a possibility. We do live in a RAMageddon world, after all, and Apple has already raised the price of the Neo once. It's also worth pointing out that the A19 chip powering the Neo 2 would still be less capable than the M-series chips powering more expensive Mac computers. Even the new iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max have an A20 Pro chip.

Beyond the chip improvement, Apple is also looking to add new MacBook Neo colors "frequently," per Gurman. However, Gurman didn't say for certain whether or not that will start with the second iteration of the budget laptop. The first version came in silver, indigo, blush, and citrus, so Apple definitely has room to add even more hues for people to enjoy.
