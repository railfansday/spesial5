‘Good fit’ – Michael Owen backs Mauricio Pochettino to ‘reignite’ Man Utd star

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Michael Owen believes that Mauricio Pochettino has the managerial nous to get Marcus Rashford back to his best in a Manchester United shirt.

Rashford failed to make an impact under Michael Carrick after returning to Old Trafford following his loan spell at Barcelona last season.

The 29-year-old is yet to score in the Premier League this season and missed the last five matches of Carrick’s tenure due to a hip injury.

Rashford now has a fresh opportunity to revive his United career under Pochettino after the Argentine signed a two-and-a-half-year deal to replace Carrick.

And having previously tried to sign Rashford at Paris Saint-Germain, Owen believes that Pochettino could be the perfect manager to get the best out of the English forward.

‘Quite a few have tried,’ Owen told Metro. ‘It’s very hard to say. It’s the million-dollar question.

‘Everybody has their different views, but Poch has a reputation of bringing players through young players and reigniting players. He’s a top manager.

‘He’s been there, seen it, and done it at various different levels. So, he’d have as good a chance as anybody to reignite certain players.

‘It’s not like Rashford has been awful since he’s been at Manchester United, he’s done pretty well, but I’m sure there’s more in there and I’m sure Pochettino could be a good fit for him.’

Elsewhere, Owen urged Pochettino to resist calls to cash in on United captain Bruno Fernandes after he turned down the club’s latest offer of a new deal.

‘Manchester United aren’t in the luxurious position of having 20 world-class players,’ he added. ‘They’ve probably got a small handful and he is one of them.

‘How weak do you want to make the team? You need to build on the good players you’ve got.

‘I know he’s opened his mouth a little bit in recent times. He’s probably not the flavour of the month, but he’s probably been the only shining light in that Manchester United team for the last eight years or something. So, no, I would definitely not be selling him.’
