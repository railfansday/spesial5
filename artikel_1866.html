Mauricio Pochettino ‘to raid’ US Soccer as Manchester United backroom staff takes shape

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mauricio Pochettino is set to raid US Soccer as he continues to build his backroom staff at Manchester United.

The Argentine is set to take over the reins from Michael Carrick at Old Trafford on Wednesday, having overseen the United States’ Concacaf Nations League quarter-final first and second legs against Haiti.

His side won 6-0 in their first match, and he will take charge of his final game on Tuesday before flying over to Manchester on Wednesday morning.

The Athletic reports that Pochettino’s assistants have already made their way over to England to begin work, with Toni Jimenez, Jesus Perez and his son Sebastiano Pochettino all beginning their new jobs at Carrington early.

Now, it looks as though the 54-year-old is not done finalising his support crew, with it believed that analyst Alec Scott will also join him in the north west.

Scott was already in-position with US Soccer before Pochettino arrived in 2024, but he has had such an impact on the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss that he wants to bring him to the Premier League with him.

Miguel D’Agostino, Pochettino’s long-serving assistant manager, has remained with the USMNT head coach for the second leg against Haiti in Cincinnati, but he is also expected to join his colleague at United later this week.

Pochettino’s first game in charge will come this Sunday, when he oversees his new side against Liverpool at Anfield.

United sit in the relegation zone in the English top flight after a run of one win in 11 matches.
