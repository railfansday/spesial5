‘Arsenal should have been awarded a penalty’ in Hull win as Premier League panel confirm incorrect offside

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

The Premier League’s Key Match Incidents Panel has voted unanimously that Arsenal should have been awarded a penalty against Hull.

The Gunners beat Hull 1-0 in the final match before the international break to go five points clear at the top of the Premier League.

However, it could have been a much more comfortable afternoon had Arsenal been given a penalty in the first half, when it was still goalless.

Kai Havertz went down in the box as he made a run in behind, having been pulled back by Nobel Mendy.

It was not initially seen by the officials but VAR advised the referee to stop play and consult the pitchside monitor.

Farai Hallam deemed that while Havertz had been fouled, he was in an offside position and therefore a free-kick was given to Hull.

The KMI panel have now confirmed that was the incorrect decision due to the foul taking place before Havertz became active.

All five members of the group voted that both the on-field decision and the outcome of the VAR intervention was wrong.

“Although Havertz is clearly in an offside position, there is a clear and impactful holding action by Mendy before any potential offside offence by Havertz,” the panel explained.

“Given the chronology of events, a penalty should have been awarded. The panel considered the VAR intervention was correct; however, the outcome after the on-field review was considered to be incorrect as a penalty should have been awarded.”

The panel added: “Being in an offside position, in itself, is not an offence.

“IFAB Law 11 says that a player in an offside position at the moment the ball is played or touched by a team-mate is only penalised on becoming involved in active play.
