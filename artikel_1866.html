Ashley Young names Premier League star Manchester United should sign to replace Bruno Fernandes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ashley Young reckons it is time for Manchester United to move on from Bruno Fernandes and feels there is a Premier League star who should be signed to replace him.

Fernandes has consistently been the Red Devils’ best player since joining from Sporting in January 2020.

However, at 32 years old and with new manager Mauricio Pochettino arriving this week, doubts over whether Fernandes will remain the focal point of the team are being raised.

The United captain is into the final few months of his contract and has reportedly rejected two offers of a new deal.

The club has the option to extend his current contract by another month, which would prevent Fernandes leaving on a free transfer in the summer, but he could still be sold in 2027 if that option is taken up.

Former Red Devils man Young reckons it is time to move on from Fernandes and thinks the man to replace him is Nottingham Forest and England midfielder Morgan Gibbs-White.

The former Wolves star turns 27 in January and Young feels now is the time for Manchester United to make him their main man, replacing Fernandes.

‘He’s been unbelievable for the club, the standout performer,’ Young said of Fernandes on Overlap Daily. ‘But there’s been so much that’s come about. He’s turned down another contract, we’re hearing.

‘You want to build around a player. He’s 33 next year. Age is nothing but a number, but he’s not in his peak. I wouldn’t say past it, but he’s getting older now.

‘For me, I would go and sign Morgan Gibbs-White. I’m just throwing that name out there. But you’re thinking a number 10 and you want to build a team around a player.

‘He’s young, he’s hungry, he’s just got in the England squad. I think he’s got the capabilities to go and play for – no disrespect to Nottingham Forest – a bigger club. And I think he’s someone that Manchester United need, in his peak years.

‘I think he’s shown more than enough at Nottingham Forest to be able to go and say I can play for a bigger club like Manchester United and take the weight of that.’

As much as signing new players will be on the agenda for the new Manchester United manager, getting rid of unwanted squad members may be the bigger test.

Gary Neville feels there needs to be a significant cull over the next few transfer windows, with some big names ideally being axed at the end of the season.

‘If you look through it now I’d say Martinez would go, [Matthijs] De Ligt would go, [Noussair] Mazraoui would go, [Mason] Mount would go, first wave next summer. [Marcus] Rashford if you can, and [Joshua] Zirkzee,’ the former full-back said on Stick to United.

‘And then the year after you’d be looking at [Luke] Shaw, [Harry] Maguire, [Diogo] Dalot, Bruno [Fernandes], that’s your second wave and maybe then you’ve made your mind up on [Carlos] Baleba and [Andrey] Santos.

‘But the earliest Mauricio Pochettino can unravel this squad is two seasons if he does well. It could take three to get this squad unravelled.’
