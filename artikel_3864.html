Feature: Seven Improvements We'd Still Love To See In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There's no two ways about it: GTA VI is a blummin' brilliant game, well worthy of its universally acclaimed 99 Metacritic score. But just because we gave it a 10/10 here at Pure Xbox doesn't mean there isn't still some wiggle room for improvement.

Both Fraser and I have put many hours into this enormous RPG already, and have come away floored by its grandeur. However, we've also noticed a few things we'd like to see fixed or added to GTA VI to make it even better. Think of this article like adding marshmallows to a hot chocolate: we're looking to take something great and make it even sweeter.

We've whipped up a few of our own suggestions and mixed in some from across the world wide webbies to bring you our top improvements we'd like to see in GTA VI. Let's get into it:

On this page: Seven Improvements We'd Still Love To See In GTA 6

You've all noticed the darkness that appears from time to time around the screen, right? That's a vignette and GTA VI is a big fan of them. It's most noticeable when you're booking it down the North 97 highway or, in the worst-case scenario, flying over the Keys as shown in the above screenshot.

GTA VI uses vignettes to cleverly draw your focus to the most important parts of the screen. I honestly think it's a great technique for making things feel more cinematic. Although, it is quite pants that there's no option to reduce its intensity or turn it off altogether. If Rockstar could give us the ability to toggle the vignette on and off, that would be great, because it also seems to be one of the most requested changes amongst GTA VI players.

Something I've grown increasingly aware of is the inability to wait or sleep to pass time across Leonida. I was particularly irked after an 18-hole crazy golf competition against Jason saw the sun set on us, and I didn't fancy driving all the way back to my lodging in Vice City. I wanted to pass time in my car or a nearby eatery, but my only option appeared to be the 4-5 minute drive home - made worse knowing that once morning hit, I'd just want to do the drive back and play golf in the daylight.

It's probably the fault of Red Dead Redemption 2 and its extremely handy wilderness camps. They were great in a pinch to let you pass time quickly. What would be great is if GTA VI gave players the option to rough it in their car for the night or find a nearby hotel room or something.

I was on a mission to pick up some prescription drugs when my car was escorted slowly into an enemy compound and the camera suddenly switched from third-person to first-person. Seeing Lucia's impeccable nails gripped around the steering wheel made me realise there's no legitimate way to drive from a behind-the-wheel perspective.

Sure, there's that glitch that lets you play GTA VI in first-person, but it would be better if Rockstar implemented an additional first-person POV for driving.

It seems like a bit of a no-brainer, but we'd love to see AMD FSR 4.1 implementation on Xbox Series X. GTA VI is already up-and-running with FSR 4.1 on Xbox Series S which brings internal resolutions up from 720p to 1080p output. It's good going on XSS, but we'd like to see what it can do with XSX.

If a game launches with a good Photo Mode, then you may as well tack an extra 10 hours onto my playthrough. But GTA VI? Maybe add another 20-30 hours. It's gorgeous!

The Photo Mode in this game is already solid. You can set up light sources, adjust focus and aperture, and as a graduate photographer, I think it's all rather intuitive. There's even a selfie mode where players can pose Jason and Lucia together, adjust their facial expressions and change their poses.

What I'd like to see is that same flexibility expand beyond selfie mode. I'd like to be able to adjust the lovers to better match the photos I'm trying to create. It was during a tense getaway, four-stars and neck-deep in trouble, that I thought I'd take an action shot, only to find Jason had blinked and Lucia looked bored as hell. It would be great to have the option to adjust expressions, head directions, poses and all that sort of stuff.

Please fix the sliders when handling amounts of money, Rockstar. Please. It's not the biggest fix, nor the most elaborate, so I'll keep it brief.

Whenever I open my phone to buck myself some cash, I often struggle to select the right amount quickly. It takes too long for the slider to speed up as I increase the amount, but then it very quickly gets out of control, and I end up overshooting my intended figure. Plus, the sound it makes is quite horrid.

What would help is having set increments to scroll through, say, $1, $10, $50, $100 and so on. It might just smooth out the experience a smidge.

I'd like to wrap up with a quickfire segment of some of our own suggestions on how Rockstar could improve GTA VI, but also some of yours. So, let's get through it.

I'd love to be able to call NPC buddies like Cal for a round of crazy golf. At the moment, playing with a partner is limited to Jason and Lucia. I'm all for love, but sometimes you need some friendly bonding too.

Another one I'd personally like to see is custom music stations on in-game radios. Now, I know this isn't something likely to happen, but it would be cool to link my Spotify and Rockstar accounts to play my own curated playlists.

Oh, and for the love of all things matrimony, please let me make a coffee for Jason/Lucia and actually give it to them. I've experienced Jason asking for a cuppa joe, so let me do the do, y'know?

Something Fraser mentioned he'd like to see is additional minigames like bowling or darts added to the game. The developers said they ran out of time for things like this, so maybe they could add them as a post-launch update.

And to wrap up, I've spotted folks online requesting the ability to dial any phone number like you could in previous games, as well as blind firing around cover during firefights, buying housing property, and having save states pick up directly where you left off.

So, there you have it. Some of our suggestions and some we've found out in the wild, but we'd love to hear your ideas on what might make GTA VI a better game. Be sure to chat with us in the comments!
