How to cook seafood meals in Disney Dreamlight Valley

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

There are many different quests you’ll encounter in Disney Dreamlight Valley that task you with cooking up various meals. Sometimes, these missions are rather specific, making them a bit tricky to solve, as is the case with the cook seafood meals task.

With hundreds of unique recipes to choose from, narrowing it down to only the ones that work for this quest can be a tough endeavor. If you’re struggling to figure it out, here’s how to cook seafood meals in Disney Dreamlight Valley.

Seafood meals are any dishes that use at least one seafood ingredient in DDV. This includes recipes that call for specific seafood ingredients and any dishes that call for “any seafood” item, which allow you to freely toss in any type of seafood you want. Recipes included in the base game and DLC worlds all count as long as they have at least one ingredient that qualifies as seafood.

To complete the cook 10 seafood meals duty for the Seafaring Star Path event, you need to cook a total of 10 seafood-based meals. You can cook all 10 of the same dish or mix and match between a few recipes, as long as you end up cooking a total of 10.

Completing this task gets you 10 Tokens to spend in the event. It’s a decent prize, but you’ll likely need more to purchase most rewards, so consider tackling some additional tasks next, like giving a boy who never grows up his favorite gift, taking a selfie with the youngest of seven sisters from the sea, crafting a Helm Bookshelf for your nautical charts, and giving a seafaring prince his favorite gift.
