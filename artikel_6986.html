WorkMotion: Senior Product Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Salary: €38,000 gross annual salary (fixed + bonus)

Great talent doesn't stop at borders, and neither do the companies we work with. At WorkMotion, we're building the future of work: helping ambitious companies hire, onboard and manage the best people anywhere in the world through smart, flexible workforce solutions.

Remote-first, fast-paced, people-driven and globally connected, we empower businesses to build teams beyond borders. And we're looking for people who want to shape that future with us.

We don't just need a Senior Product Manager. We need a builder, a strategic thinker and a product leader who knows how to turn complex enterprise challenges into simple, scalable product experiences.

As our Senior Product Manager, Enterprise, you'll own the product strategy and roadmap for our Enterprise segment, shaping solutions that serve large, international businesses with sophisticated needs. From EOR and Direct Hiring to Contractor solutions, you'll make sure our products are ready to support enterprise customers as they grow across borders.

This is a role for someone who thrives at the intersection of customer needs, technology and business impact. You'll take ownership from discovery to launch and beyond, transforming customer insights into meaningful product improvements. You'll also explore how AI can unlock smarter workflows, reduce operational complexity and create real value for our customers.

You won't be working in a silo. You'll collaborate closely with Engineering, Design, Sales, Customer Success, Operations, leadership, enterprise clients and integration partners. Your mission? Bring everyone together around a clear product vision and deliver experiences that are high-quality, customer-centric, data-driven and built to scale.

Where Responsibility Meets Real Opportunity:

You'll join a talented and ambitious Product team based in Egypt, Germany and beyond. We're a fully distributed group of product minds who believe that great collaboration isn't about sharing an office, but about sharing ideas, challenging assumptions and building something meaningful together.

We don't just work together; we create, challenge and grow together. We value curiosity, ownership and open communication. Everyone has a voice, ideas are welcomed, and cross-functional collaboration is at the heart of how we operate.

If you're looking for an environment where you can influence product strategy, challenge the status quo and turn bold ideas into real business impact, you'll feel right at home.
