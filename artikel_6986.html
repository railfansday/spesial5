The Best Holiday Palettes for Every Vibe (Including Your Own)

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Minimalist, maximalist, or somewhere in between? Find your holiday makeup match at Ulta Beauty.

Are you a five-minute-face person, or does your beauty routine involve three eyeshadow brushes, a playlist, and absolutely no rushing? Whatever your holiday makeup personality, Ulta Beauty has a palette to match your energy. Think multitasking kits that streamline your routine, complexion powders that make your skin look lit from within, and eyeshadow collections for every party invitation. Whether you're loyal to your everyday aesthetic or never met a sparkly shade you didn't want to swatch, party season is the perfect excuse to switch things up.

Ahead, discover the best holiday makeup palettes and gift sets at Ulta Beauty, whether you're treating yourself or shopping for someone on your nice list.

A minimalist approach to holiday makeup requires multitasking formulas that cover multiple steps without a dozen different products. Look for all-in-one face and eye palettes packed with complementary colors, so you can get party ready without overthinking what goes with what.

Meet your new getting-ready shortcut: Morphe Sky's the Limit Face & Eye Palette. With eyeshadows, blush, cheek balms, and setting powder in coordinating rose and mauve tones, this all-in-one compact takes care of most of the color decisions for you. Sweep the pale pink matte shadow all over your lids, blend a little of the deeper brown into your eyelid crease for definition, and tap the champagne shade onto the center of the lid for a hint of sparkle. Finish with a swipe of rosy blush and a little cheek balm to add dewiness. Pair the palette with the Morphe Riches Come True 5-Piece Brush & Tool Set with four makeup brushes and a mini sponge, all tucked into a metallic pouch that's easy to throw into your overnight bag.

Full-glam beauty invites you to play with color, whether you want a sultry smoky eye or something softer. The Charlotte Tilbury Hollywood Instant Look in a Palette offers two distinct takes on holiday glam, depending on how much drama you're going for.

Feeling a little mysterious? The Dreamy, Bronzed Beauty version features darker eyeshadows, including an especially on-trend matte burgundy, paired with a warm, sun-kissed bronzer for a moodier take on party makeup. Prefer something more delicate? Pretty, Glowing Beauty leans into pale pink tones for an ethereal, barely-there effect. Whichever you choose, blend the deeper shadow into your crease and along your lash line for definition, then tap a touch of the sparkly option onto the center of your lids to catch the light. Complete the look with the included bronzer and blush (matte or shimmery, whatever your mood), plus finishing powder to help even out your complexion. Both palettes come in glittery packaging that makes them tempting to give as gifts. (Or, you know, not.)

And because nobody wants their smoky eye turning into a smudgy mess before midnight (or after!), finish with a spritz of L'Oréal Paris Infallible 3-Second Setting Mist Spray to help keep your handiwork in place while you dance the night away.

The secret to a glowing complexion is combining finishing powders, blushes, and highlighters that deliver a healthy sheen without piling on heavy layers. If your beauty goal is less disco ball, more just-got-back-from-a-tropical-vacation, the Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit Unlocked Palette in Wish is right up your alley.

The limited-edition face palette contains six powders that work together to blur the appearance of imperfections and give skin a flattering, diffused finish. Unlike chunky glitter, the finely milled formulas reflect light evenly to make your complexion look naturally radiant. For a glowy face, the versatile finishing powder doubles as powder foundation. Sweep it across your face to start, then add blush for a pop of color, and concentrate highlighter on the high points of your cheeks for a little extra definition.

If you happen to be more of a "there's no such thing as too much sparkle" person, top the look off with Fenty Beauty Limited-Edition Midnight Diamonds Diamond Bomb All-Over Diamond Veil, which leaves a fairy-dusted effect on cheekbones, collarbones, and anywhere else you want to sparkle.

The ultimate gift for the beauty chameleon includes a mix of eyeshadows, mascaras, and lip colors to create entirely different moods. For the friend who changes her makeup as often as her outfit, Tarte You Know You Love Me Collector's Set has plenty to keep her entertained.

The collection includes two mini Amazonian Clay eyeshadow palettes—one with warm golden shades and the other with cool rosy neutrals—so she can go from bronzed and sultry to pretty in pink without buying another palette. She’ll also get two mascaras (Tartelette Tubing for length and Maneater for volume) plus two Maracuja Juicy lip products to give lips a glossy, plumped-up appearance.

Each eyeshadow palette comes with its own reusable wristlet clutch, giving you options when it comes to gifting. Split the set into two presents for your beauty-obsessed besties, or keep the whole collection for yourself. We won't tell.
