Dragon Quest RPG The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle announced for mobile

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Of all the JRPGs, I think you could argue that the title of most famous is split evenly between Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest. And neither have been short on entries on mobile, but Dragon Quest is the latest series to get the nod, with the announcement of Dragon Quest RPG The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle!

Dragon Quest RPG The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle is a full-fledged mobile spinoff of the series. The announcement trailer shows familiar gameplay mechanics, including exploration of an isometric overworld, turn-based battles, and oddities like an upgradable home hub with different shops to unlock.

And, at its core, I think that's what will make Dragon Quest RPG attractive to many of you. Aside from some concessions to mobile, such as the slowly expanding hub town, this is a game which is leaning much more heavily into classic JRPG gameplay.

With applications for its upcoming closed beta test now open, if you're at all curious about what Dragon Quest RPG has to offer, then now's the time to sign up! And for the rest of you, keep your eyes peeled in 2027, because the Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle may turn out to be a sleeper hit.
