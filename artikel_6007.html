Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jim Geraghty’s instrumental breakdown of CNN’s blockbuster report warning that Iran is “developing plans to potentially detonate a dirty bomb in Washington” is crucial reading.

CNN’s sources revealed late Monday that “Iranian chatter” indicates that “it has stashed bomb-making materials inside the United States.” It’s “likely” that “Iran has already pre-positioned nuclear materials or chemical agents that could be laced onto a conventional weapon.” Another “stream of intelligence” suggests Iran may attempt an attack on U.S. troops in the Middle East with chemical agents. Jim establishes not only the nature of the threat but also its feasibility and Iran’s potential objectives if it carried out such a plot.
