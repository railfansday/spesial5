The Best Black Friday Handbag Deals Have Already Arrived

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

We found the best styles on sale, from labels like Toteme and Marc Jacobs

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Black Friday is coming up soon, but the best handbag sales are already rolling out. Retailers including Nordstrom, Amazon, and Net-a-Porter are currently offering dozens of early markdowns on sought-after styles from Staud, Polo Ralph Lauren, and more. Though the shopping holiday event doesn’t officially begin until November 27, you can save hundreds on your next handbag purchase if you start shopping now.

Below, find all the best early Black Friday handbag deals from Bazaar-loved labels. Whether you’re looking to pick up a sleek new shoulder bag from celebrity-favorite Freja or invest in a new office tote from Toteme, there’s no need to wait.

Nordstrom and Amazon both have massive pre–Black Friday sales, making them our top destinations for Tory Burch bags. Staples like the Romy hobo and Perry tote fit all your work essentials if you’re looking for a new commuter-friendly bag, while the Kira and Perra shoulder bags can be worn with all your holiday looks (and make excellent gifts for a loved one too).

Tory Burch Kira Turnlock Convertible Shoulder Bag

Staud fans have plenty of markdowns to choose from at Nordstrom and Fwrd, including signature silhouettes like the Maude and Harlow.

Bazaar shoppers can enjoy early access to DeMellier’s sitewide Black Friday sale. Use the code BAZAAR20 at checkout for 20 percent off sitewide, starting now. Celebrity-worn styles like Anne Hathaway’s New York shoulder bag and Kate Middleton’s Nano Montreal bag will go fast once the British label opens its sale to the public on November 20.

Net-a-Porter is the best place to shop for early Black Friday deals on Toteme bags—but at 40 percent off, stock will go fast. Signature favorites like the belted bucket bag and oversize totes are part of the offerings. And those needing an elegant party bag should pick up the red velvet cocktail shoulder bag hiding in the Mytheresa sale section.

Polo Ralph Lauren bags are always timeless. Ahead of Black Friday, shop Neiman Marcus’s selection of markdowns on fall-ready suede styles like the popular Polo ID or Polo Play bags.

Looking for a new handbag label to obsess over? Verafied’s current markdowns will quickly convert you to a fan. Shop the editor-loved Club Bag in suede for under $300.

Freja’s sleek handbags have been worn by Hailey Bieber, Katie Holmes, and Jennifer Lawrence, making them the ultimate fashion gift for the celebrity-obsessed. The New York–based brand is currently offering holiday shoppers 25 percent off sitewide.

Marc Jacobs is another smart choice for gift shoppers. The beloved New York designer is offering steep markdowns at Nordstrom on totes and shoulder bags that make a great first designer bag for a stylish loved one.
