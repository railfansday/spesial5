Attention: Peacock Just Dropped a First-Look Teaser for ‘The Traitors’ Season 5 Ahead of Its January Premiere

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Fans may be watching civilians compete for the prize pot on The Traitors: New Blood right now, but I’m thrilled to report that I’m already seated for The Traitors season 5...especially since Peacock just dropped the first look featuring the new batch of celebs.

Previous seasons saw icons from Lisa Rinna and Bob the Drag Queen to Rob Rausch and Maura Higgins, and this time around is no different. Spoiler alert: a ton of Cosmo faves have made the cast list, and I, for one, already have thoughts. For instance: I need to see The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Whitney Leavitt and drag legend Bianca Del Rio in the turret, stat!

Those are only two of the upcoming season’s cast members, but I can already confirm we’re in for yet another deliciously chaotic ride in Alan Cummings’ Scottish castle. Ahead of the January 14 premiere, here’s The Traitors season 5 cast, from Bravolebrities and Broadway icons to heartthrobs and TikTokers. But first: a sneak peek at the upcoming season.

Where you’ve seen her: Bianca is one of the most iconic drag performers, period. She won RuPaul’s Drag Race season 6 before appearing in movies like Hurricane Bianca and Everybody’s Talking About Jamie.

Where you’ve seen him: Boman gained a following on TikTok thanks to his parodies of shows like Keeping Up With the Kardashians. He also tried his hand at different TV genres on his show, Made for TV.

Where you’ve seen her: Bronwyn is known for her maximalist fashion sense and, of course, being one of The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City.

Where you’ve seen her: Casey is a writer and comedian who was on Saturday Night Live from 2008 to 2009. She also has writing and acting credits on projects like Bride Wars and Ass Backwards, as well as a starring role on Happy Endings.

Where you’ve seen her: Chloe is a two-time Olympic gold medalist. She became the youngest woman to win an Olympic snowboarding gold medal when she was 17 years old at the women’s snowboard halfpipe at the 2018 Winter Olympics.

Where you’ve seen him: Cody has been a singer-songwriter since he was a teen and even trained as a competitive swimmer in hopes of qualifying for the 2024 Paris Olympics. Oh, and he’s dated a bunch of celebs, from Kylie Jenner to Miley Cyrus.

Where you’ve seen him: Craig stars in Bravo’s Southern Charm, but he’s also appeared on Winter House, where he met his ex, Paige Desorbo. He also runs his own lifestyle and homegoods company, Sewing Down South.

Where you’ve seen her: On top of being a cast member on The Real Housewives of Atlanta, Cynthia is a model, podcaster, and actor.

Where you’ve seen him: Dom is a British actor who’s been on Lost and Lord of the Rings.

Where you’ve seen her: Emira made history by becoming Victoria’s Secret’s first Black transgender model in 2022. She was also the former beauty director at Paper magazine before she blew up on TikTok and joined the cast of Bravo’s Next Gen NYC.

Where you’ve seen her: Gizelle is one of The Real Housewives of Potomac and founder of skincare line, EveryHue Beauty.

Where you’ve seen heard him: Okay, so you might not know Iain by face…but you’d definitely recognize his voice, because he’s a comedian and the narrator of Love Island and Love Island USA.

Where you’ve seen him: Jag won season 25 of Big Brother, becoming the first Sikh-Punjabi to do so.

Where you’ve seen her: JaNa is a fan-favorite member of PPG with her besties Serena Page and Leah Kateb from Love Island USA season 6.

Where you’ve seen him: Joey was the runner-up on season 20 of The Bachelorette before he became The Bachelor in season 28 and won season 33 of Dancing With the Stars.

Where you’ve seen him: Johnny is an actor who you may recognize from Hacks, Pose, and Queer as Folk.

Where you’ve seen her: Lowkey, everywhere. But Kristin first gained notoriety for her roles on Broadway, including Wicked, You’re a Good Man, Charlie Brown, and Queen of Versailles.

Where you’ve seen her: Michele won Survivor: Kaôh Rōng in 2018 and later competed on MTV’s The Challenge.

Where you’ve seen him: Q competed on Survivor 46 and Survivor 50: In the Hands of the Fans.

Where you’ce seen her: Rachel has competed on 9 (!!!) different seasons of The Challenge.
