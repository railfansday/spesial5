Google to keep Gemini chats for 2 months, up from 72 hours, even with history off

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Google is extending how long it keeps temporary Gemini chats and conversations made with chat history turned off to around two months, saying the change aims to improve safety and security as AI systems become more capable.

OpenAI, by comparison, says it may keep temporary chats in ChatGPT for up to 30 days, whereas Anthropic retains incognito chats in Claude for 30 days by default. However, companies may keep some data longer for legal or safety reasons, or to investigate policy violations.

The longer retention period comes as Google introduces separate settings for Gemini’s chat history and AI training. Until now, both were tied to a single “Keep Activity” setting. Users will now be able to keep their conversations in their chat history without using them to train Google’s generative AI models.

Google says both new settings will be turned off by default for users who previously disabled Keep Activity. For those who had Keep Activity turned on, their chat history will remain enabled. They can, though, decide separately whether Google can use their conversations to train AI models.
