On the East Coast, skiing is about more than great lines and great snow. It’s SUVs trudging through storms with their ski racks, salt stains, and “SKI THE EAST” bumper stickers. The snow is firmer, the peaks are lower, and terrain is more dense, but no less breathtaking. Mostly, though, it’s about skiing not as a hobby but as an identity—something you do because you’ve always done it, and always done it here.

Maybe you’ve heard that the East Coast has a reputation for its “ice.” For much of the region, that’s an outdated stereotype. Sure, these spots might not get the massive powder days that some of the Western resorts are known for, but with modern innovations in snowmaking (and a few good breaks from Mother Nature), the East can more than hold its own. After all, anyone in their right mind would have chosen and East Coast winter over the West’s sad season last year.

If you’re looking for the East Coast resorts that typically get the best snow, the 10 resorts listed below are a fine place to begin. These choices, which come from SKI’s Annual Reader Resort Survey, are known for serving up the most powder days and the best snow conditions all season long.
