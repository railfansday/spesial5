OpenAI didn't acquire Odessia for its revenue. It bought a team that built a working AI travel agent in months, plus a founder who ran a lodging company, which included hotels and short-term rentals, for a decade. It's a sign ChatGPT wants to do more in travel than send users to Booking.com and Expedia.

OpenAI has acquired Odessia, the AI travel agent startup that Sonder co-founder Francis Davidson launched just five months ago. Davidson announced the deal Tuesday and said he will join OpenAI “to focus on the future of travel.” Terms weren’t disclosed.

“We’ll have more to share on the shape of the future soon,” Davidson wrote. He didn’t say whether Odessia will keep operating as a standalone company.

Davidson unveiled Odessia in June with a team of eight in San Francisco and about $6 million in seed funding led by Sequoia Capital. It plans and books flights, hotels and itineraries through a single conversation, and it opened to the public on Oct. 14 as a free concierge that users reach by text.

OpenAI has acquired Odessia, the AI travel agent startup founded by Sonder co-founder Francis Davidson, less than a year after its launch. Davidson announced the deal Tuesday and said he will join OpenAI "to focus on the future of travel." Terms weren't disclosed, and Davidson did not say whether Odessia will keep operating as a standalone company. Until now, OpenAI's travel strategy has leaned on partners: Booking.com and Expedia were among the first apps inside ChatGPT when OpenAI opened its app platform in October 2025, and OpenAI has been building travel advertising and booking into ChatGPT so users can buy without going to a merchant's site. Odessia brings what those partnerships don't — a team that has built a booking agent end to end, IATA accreditation, hotel content, and a founder who knows the economics of lodging from both the consumer and operator's side. Odessia launched in June with a team of eight in San Francisco and about $6 million in seed funding led by Sequoia Capital, and opened to the public on October 14 as a free concierge reached by text.
