Trump’s People May Have to Answer for Jensen Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Why, exactly, are we allowing Nvidia to sell advanced AI chips to China?

Jensen Huang stands beside Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick during a group photo at the G20 Innovation Ministerial Summit in Chapel Hill, N.C., September 2, 2026.

The sexiest part of the Nvidia scandal is the sex: Former (as of Thursday) CEO Jensen Huang is up to three alleged mistresses, including one potential Chinese spy who works for an Asian data-center firm. With these affairs, Huang has wrecked his personal reputation and probably his marriage.

In the public realm, however, more scrutiny should be cast on the president and his subordinates, with whom Huang had worked closely to expand Nvidia’s business to America’s chief geopolitical adversary. Huang was born in Taiwan; he should have no illusions about Beijing’s intentions for the world.

John R. Puri is the Thomas L. Rhodes Fellow at National Review.
