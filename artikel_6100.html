Capital One Venture Card Bonus: 75K Miles + $300 Credit Welcome Offer (Last Chance)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At the moment, the $95 annual fee Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card is offering a phenomenal limited time welcome bonus, making it the ideal time to apply. I’d like to provide an update that this is the last chance to pick up the card with this offer, as it’s expected to be pulled soon.

Not only is this among the best offers we’ve seen on the card, but this is also a reasonably priced travel rewards card that offers long term value, especially for everyday spending.

Link: Learn more about the Capital One Venture Card

The Capital One Venture Card is currently offering a limited time, lucrative, two-part welcome offer, whereby you can earn 75,000 Capital One bonus miles, plus a $300 Capital One Travel stays credit, after spending $4,000 within the first three months.

For context, Capital One miles can be redeemed for a minimum of one cent each, meaning the 75,000 miles will get you at least $750 in value. However, I value the miles at 1.7 cents each based on my latest analysis (see my methodology here), thanks to the value you can get by transferring the miles to travel partners. That means that to me, the 75,000 miles are worth $1,275.

There are lots of great ways to redeem Capital One miles, as they can be transferred to Capital One’s roughly 20 airline and hotel partners. That’s my preferred use of them, as you can get some amazing travel experiences this way.

Partners include programs like Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, Qatar Airways Privilege Club, Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club, and more. These are all programs that allow you to really maximize your points and get outsized value. Just to give a couple of examples of Capital One mileage redemption opportunities:

That doesn’t include the value of the $300 Capital One Travel credit, which posts upon account approval (rather than upon completing minimum spending). The idea is that this can be used for a hotel or vacation rental booking through Capital One Travel in your first cardmember year (in the first 12 months), and it’s valid across one or multiple stays. In other words, you could use it to pay virtually nothing for a $300 hotel stay. All-in-all, I’d say the Capital One Venture bonus is worth up to $1,575.

By my valuation, the current Capital One Venture Card bonus is the best publicly available offer that we’ve seen in over five years, so that makes this the ideal time to apply, and I absolutely wouldn’t wait for a better offer. Quite to the contrary, when this limited time offer gets pulled, it’ll almost certainly be worse.

For context, below is a history of the publicly available bonuses that we’ve seen on the Capital One Venture in recent years.

As you can see, we’ve never seen a publicly available welcome offer of over 75,000 miles, in terms of the mileage component. We have in the past seen $250 travel credits offered along with the miles, and this time around the travel credit is bigger. So offers really don’t get better than this on the card.

When you look at the bonus terms on the Capital One Venture Card, you’ll find the following language:

You are not eligible for this product if you have received a new cardmember bonus for the Capital One Venture card or the Capital One Venture X card in the past 48 months.

As you can see, the card follows a 48-month rule, so you’re not eligible for the welcome bonus on this card if you’ve received a new cardmember bonus on this exact card or on the Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card (learn more) in the past 48 months.

Other than that, eligibility is unrelated to having any other credit card, and Capital One doesn’t have many consistent restrictions when it comes to getting approved for cards.
