The Airport Scorecard: What Airlines and Airports Must Get Right

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

John Ackerman, CEO, Los Angeles World Airports

Régis Lacôte, Executive Vice President of Groupe ADP, Head of Operations and Innovation, and Managing Director, Paris-Charles de Gaulle Airport

Moderated by Sarah Kopit, Editor-in-Chief, Skift

Airports can use major-event deadlines to accelerate investment, but the lasting benefits depend on what changes after the crowds leave. Paris-Charles de Gaulle’s Régis Lacôte described permanent operational improvements from the Olympics, while Los Angeles World Airports’ John Ackerman said the success of LAX’s transformation will ultimately be judged over decades.
