JSX Adds Two Nonstop Routes From New City

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

JSX announced Tuesday that it will begin flying to New England for the first time in February 2027.

The carrier will operate from Jet Aviation’s terminal at Hanscom Field in Bedford, Massachusetts, northwest of Boston. Flights to Teterboro Airport in New Jersey will begin Feb. 8, with four flights on weekdays, two on Saturdays, and three on Sundays.

JSX markets the route as New York City service and requires passengers to sign up for its free Club JSX membership to book.

Service to Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport will follow on Feb. 11, with one flight each day on Mondays and Thursdays through Sundays. JSX will use 30-seat aircraft on both routes.

“We’re excited JSX has chosen Hanscom to operate these flights to the New York and Florida markets,” Hanscom Field Director Chris Willenborg said in a statement.

JSX outlined its plans for Hanscom Field at an advisory commission meeting in September. At the time, the carrier was targeting a January launch and had also proposed flights to Naples, Florida. Naples was absent from Tuesday’s announcement.
