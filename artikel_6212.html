Amex Business Gold Card Bonus: As High As 200K Points Welcome Offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The American Express® Business Gold Card is one of the most rewarding business cards if you’re looking to earn Amex Membership Rewards points.

We’re currently seeing an excellent Amex Business Gold Card welcome offer, which is the biggest bonus we’ve ever seen on the card, making it the ideal time to apply. If you’re eligible for this card and the highest possible bonus, it’s absolutely worth taking advantage of, in my opinion.

The Amex Business Gold Card currently has a massive welcome offer that’s as high as 200,000 Membership Rewards points after spending $15,000 on eligible purchases within the first three months. Welcome offers vary and you may not be eligible for an offer. Find out your offer.

Amex uses a variety of factors when it comes to deciding what offer people are eligible for, including history of opening and closing cards with Amex, spending history with Amex, how many welcome offers people have earned, etc. So, how does this Amex Business Gold Card bonus actually work?

You should find the highest possible offer through the above link. However, if you don’t, it can always be worth checking refer-a-friend links, opting in to targeted emails from Amex, or even using the CardMatch tool to see what you’re eligible for there. That being said, don’t expect to get a higher offer through any of those sources, since offers are typically targeted based on the person, rather than the application method.

It goes without saying that the Amex Business Gold bonus spending requirement is potentially significant, as it averages out to $5,000 per month. However, there are lots of ways to be creative with reaching minimum spending, and to make a plan. You could time your application around some large upcoming business expenses (whether that’s an ad campaign or buying inventory), prepay for some items, and maybe even put some expenses on credit cards that you might otherwise pay for with cash. Of course make sure you pay off everything in full by the payment due date, so that you don’t get stuck paying high interest charges.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Membership Rewards points to be worth 1.7 cents each. That means to me, 200,000 points are worth $3,400, which is an incredible offer, assuming you’re eligible. This is the biggest publicly available bonus that we’ve seen on this card, and in the past, we’ve often only seen offers of 70,000 to 100,000 points, so this is much better. I’d consider the Amex Business Gold Card bonus to be among the best welcome offers out there.

There are lots of great uses for Amex points, as they can be transferred to all kinds of useful airline and hotel partners, ranging from Air France-KLM Flying Blue, to Emirates Skywards, to Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club. I’d say the single biggest reason to get the Amex Business Gold is because of the rewards structure. Each billing cycle, the card earns 4x points in the two categories in which your business spends the most. There are a total of six eligible categories to choose from. Note that you earn 4x points on the first $150,000 in combined purchases from these categories each calendar year, and then 1x points thereafter.
