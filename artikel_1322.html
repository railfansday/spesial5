Save Up To 50 Per Cent On Everton Black Friday Deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Everton’s Black Friday deals are here, with up to 50-per-cent off selected lines online, at Everton Two (E2) and at the stadium store.

Whether you’re picking up this season’s shirt or looking for a Christmas gift for the Evertonian in your life, there are savings across kits, training wear, retro, fashion and gifts.

You can save up to 50 per cent on selected home kit lines, up to 40 per cent on selected third kit lines and up to 30 per cent on selected away kit lines. It’s a chance to get the shirt you’ve had your eye on or pick up a different kit to wear on matchday.

There’s more to explore beyond this season’s kits. Browse the training range, revisit classic Everton looks in the retro collection or pick up something from the fashion range to wear between fixtures. If you’re getting a head start on Christmas shopping, you’ll also find savings on selected Everton gifts.

The Black Friday offer runs until 11.59pm on Monday 30 November. Shop online or visit Everton Two or the stadium store to explore the deals.
