Man United mock Roma with brutal tweet after comeback victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Manchester United enjoyed their comeback win against Roma on and off the field, mocking the Giallorossi with a brutal tweet at full-time.

Roma wasted a two-goal lead at Old Trafford last night: they scored twice in the opening 17 minutes but conceded three times in the second half, falling to a 3-2 loss.

Manchester United hadn’t won a game since September, while Roma had lost only once across all competitions before last night’s meeting.

Ironically, Roma had lost to Real Madrid in a similar fashion in October, falling 4-3 at the Stadio Olimpico and conceding three goals in the dying minutes.

Manchester United kept celebrating on and off the pitch, taking to X, formerly Twitter, to share a series of cheeky posts after their victory.

One post featured the stadium screens displaying the results of previous Champions League encounters with Roma, alongside last night’s triumph. The scoreboard highlighted their emphatic 7-1 victory in April 2007 and the 6-2 win in 2021.

Manchester United captioned the image with a simple but fitting message: “Absolute Cinema.”
