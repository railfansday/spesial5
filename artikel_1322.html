‘Tactical genius’ Emery outsmarts Maresca as City crash to first league defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Enzo Maresca’s flawless start to the Premier League with Manchester City is over after another masterclass from Unai Emery.

Matches at Villa Park were a notoriously chastening experience for Pep Guardiola and it was the same old story for Maresca as City have now lost on their last four visits here.

On his fourth anniversary in charge of Villa, there was more tactical brilliance from Emery, who also did the double over Guardiola last season. It was Emery’s £65m record signing Nicolas Jackson who again underlined how some managers can get the best out of certain players.

Jackson had been a target for Emery ever since the Basque’s appointment and now has seven goals in claret and blue. It will have been an especially sweet moment for the Senegal forward, for it was Maresca who allowed him to join Bayern Munich on loan while he was in charge at Chelsea.

City’s run of seven league wins in a row always looked in danger here against a vibrant and energetic Villa. Maresca will also reflect on two poor goals conceded after errors from Marc Guéhi and goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma.

Guéhi endured a particularly difficult afternoon and was at fault for Jackson’s stunning first goal. The centre-back has struggled in recent weeks with a bad mistake in England’s defeat by Spain and he was exposed for both Ipswich goals last week. He was replaced at half-time after a booking for a foul on the excellent Jackson.

Maresca said: “I’m not worried about Marc at all, he is playing every game since the season started for us and the national team so it’s not easy. He is ok, it [taking him off] was just a technical decision and like in all games managers make changes. He was on a yellow card and sometimes one yellow card can be dangerous.”

When asked if the goals were avoidable, Maresca said: “Absolutely, both. Sometimes you have to be a bit more lucky because we only conceded those two chances. We will try again to win at Villa next season. I don’t think we deserved to lose but we learn from that and move on. You can always learn something. For sure there are things we can do better today.”

While much of the focus will be on City’s 100 per cent start to the league coming to an end, Emery got everything right here. From his team selection to his set-up and substitutions, he outsmarted Maresca on many levels. After the game, Villa defender Matty Cash described his manager as “a tactical genius”.

It was four years ago to the day that Emery grabbed this famous club by the shirt and collar and gave them an almighty shake. After a summer of much instability including many player sales, he appears to have already struck a formula with his newly assembled team.

There were a number of fine individual performances from many of the summer arrivals, such as Jackson, goalkeeper Zion Suzuki and Johan Manzambi.

Emery said: “We were very motivated and Manchester City is the best team in the world so this is the level. This year we started so poorly and we needed time to understand how we improve. The players showed the capacity to retrieve the level we’ve had over the last few years.

“Nicolas is more mature and very demanding of himself to improve. Earlier this season he was shooting over the crossbar but he is now finding the corners. There is still work to do and 11 points is not enough but we are feeling better.”

Villa were fierce from the start and ahead after six minutes. Guéhi’s mistake was punished by John McGinn, with the ball dropping to Jackson who crashed a shot into the roof of the net.

Ndiaye also struck the outside of the post and City were level seven minutes into the second half with Erling Haaland nodding in Ndiaye’s cross at the far post. Welcome to Telegraph Sport’s live coverage of Aston Villa vs Manchester City.

Premier League leaders City will attempt to maintain their flawless start to the season when they visit Villa Park for Saturday’s lunchtime kick-off.

The match marks the first meeting between the sides since the final weekend of last season, when Villa secured a 2-1 victory at the Etihad in Pep Guardiola’s final game in charge at City.

Enzo Maresca became the first manager in City’s Premier League history to win his opening seven fixtures, lifting his side three points clear of title holders Arsenal. Only Chelsea in 2005-06 and Liverpool in 2019-20 have managed a longer winning start to a top-flight campaign. Following a 3-2 victory against Ipswich Town last weekend, City rebounded from a European setback against Paris Saint-Germain by delivering a commanding 5-1 Champions League win over AEK Athens on Wednesday.

However, Unai Emery’s side will present a formidable challenge. Aston Villa enter the contest on a seven-match unbeaten streak across all competitions, headlined by a perfect record in the Champions League following midweek success against Viking FK.

Villa’s domestic form has also improved, taking seven points from their last three league outings, including a 1-0 away victory at Newcastle United in Emery’s 100th Premier League match as manager. Sitting 12th in the table, Villa remain on the hunt for their first home league win of the season, though their impressive European performances at Villa Park provide significant momentum.

Emery’s men completed a league double over City last term, including a 1-0 win on home turf. Should Villa claim victory again on Saturday, it would mark the first time in the Premier League era that they have recorded three consecutive victories over the Manchester club.

Of course, City’s campaign remains overshadowed by the recent guilty verdict from the Premier League over financial regulations.
