Juventus receive double defensive boost ahead of Venezia

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Friday’s reports confirm that both Andrea Cambiaso and Zeki Celik have returned to training with Juventus and that they should both be available for selection for Sunday’s Serie A match against Venezia.

Cambiaso missed Juve’s final match before the international break, a 2-2 draw away against Fiorentina, with a muscular issue, which ruled him out of selection for Roberto Mancini’s latest Italy national team squad for the November break.

Celik, meanwhile, had been named in the Turkey national team starting XI to face France in their final Nations League group stage fixture on Sunday evening, but limped out of the warm-up with a thigh issue.

According to updates from Gianluca Di Marzio on Friday, both players were involved in Luciano Spalletti’s training session this morning and both are expected to be available for selection when Juventus host Venezia in Serie A on Sunday (11.30 GMT).

Juventus find themselves in seventh place in the Serie A table ahead of kick-off against Venezia. They have won five, drawn three and lost three for a total of 18 points in the league thus far, 11 less than the current table-toppers, Lazio.

A match at home against the 19th-placed Venezia, on six points after 11 matches, is an opportunity for the Old Lady to return to winning ways when league action resumes this weekend.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
