Galliani would ‘absolutely’ back Mancini as Italy coach if FIGC President

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Adriano Galliani reveals who convinced him to run for FIGC President and assures he will ‘absolutely’ stick with Italy coach Roberto Mancini if replacing Giovanni Malagò.

The Italian Football Federation was thrown into chaos last month when it was ruled that Malagò did not have the requisite paperwork when he first ran for President, so his June landslide election was null and void.

Faced with the need to put the FIGC under a Special Commissioner until the next election on December 14, the CONI (Italian Olympic Committee) decided to give the role to Malagò.

At the time, it was assumed this would be just a stopgap solution until he could be elected properly, but former Milan and Monza CEO Galliani threw his hat into the ring.

“There are two candidates, the vote is in four weeks, so we’ll see what happens,” Galliani told Sky Sport Italia.

“I was convinced by some phone calls from clubs, but also from the Players’ Association and Coaches’ Association. I had said no in the past, but (Coaches’ Association President) Renzo Ulivieri told me that the rapport with the Lega Calcio was at its best when I was President. Umberto Calcagno told me the same thing.

“I have a very long and detailed proposal for my tenure, which I have already presented and deposited.”

There was the risk of further chaos when Galliani’s lawyer had presented an appeal against Malagò running for FIGC President again, because there was confusion over who had the right to propose him from Genoa.

However, that was dropped just before the deadline, so the election will go ahead on December 14.

“I told my lawyer to let it go, Italian football already has so many problems,” explained Galliani.

“I spent 50 years in Italian football, there are too many things to sort out, and I wrote them all in my plan.”

The major decision made by Malagò as FIGC President was to appoint Mancini as the Italy coach, so is that something Galliani would confirm if he won the election?
