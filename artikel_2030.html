Scott Parker already 'at risk' at West Ham as 'big problems' emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham United could find themselves in a difficult position over Scott Parker if their results do not improve quickly.

Parker took charge of his first game as the new manager at the weekend, but saw his side fall to a 2-1 defeat against Blackburn at Ewood Park, leading to concerns.

While West Ham are convinced their new manager is the right man to lead them forward, and the Blackburn defeat is not on him, they are expecting a rapid turnaround.

Former West Ham scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that the board have placed themselves in a difficult position following their decision to sack Nuno Espirito Santo.

The Hammers have now made it clear that they expect to get promoted this season, and anything less will be deemed a failure, ramping up the pressure at the London Stadium.

Parker must ensure he meets those expectations, or he will also face pressure from the board and the supporters, who expressed their frustrations at Ewood Park.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that West Ham are convinced that Parker is the right man to lead them back into the Premier Leaugue come the end of the season.

The 46-year-old has previously led Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley to promotion, and there is optimism that he will do the same with the Hammers come next summer.

However, there are concerns that the conditions at West Ham may not be ideal for the new manager, who is likely to come under pressure if their struggles continue.

"I did think it was a strange decision to go for Scott Parker, but I have heard good things around him," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's got a good record in the Championship, so that's a positive, but there are bigger problems at West Ham than who the manager is.

"I felt this way during my time there, and certainly after I left, but I have no faith in the West Ham board to make the right decisions and set the team up to be successful.

"Regardless of whether it was the right decision to sack Nuno and appoint Parker, that's not going to change the makeup of this squad.

"On paper, they should be more than good enough to win promotion to the Premier League, but recent results have proved it's not always as easy as maybe it should be.

"The problem now for Parker is, he has to turn their results around and get a positive feeling back, because otherwise he will come under pressure as well.

"West Ham have set a precedent, if they don't feel results have been good enough, they will make changes. So if Parker isn't getting results, will he be at risk as well? I wouldn't rule it out."

West Ham have been clear about what they expect for the coming season, with pressure on to earn promotion back to the Premier League at the first time of asking.

Failure to do so would be calamitous for the London Stadium club, and their decision to part ways with Nuno was based on fears they may not be able to achieve that goal.

Brown warned Football Insider prior to Nuno's dismissal that West Ham have made their demands clear and will not accept anything less than positive results.

Ultimately, defeats at home to Southampton and West Brom sparked major concerns amongst the board, and they decided to pull the trigger on the manager.

That could now be the same for Parker, and if he proves unable to turn West Ham's form around and lead them back to the top, questions will also be asked of him.
