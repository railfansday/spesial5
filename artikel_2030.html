EFL club hit with unprecedented FA punishment – it's never happened before

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Millwall have been handed a partial-stadium closure for their upcoming clash with Sheffield United in the EFL Championship, with the FA handing them an unprecedented punishment after ableist and homophobic chanting during their game with Charlton Athletic.

The chanting took place in the clash between Millwall and Charlton at the Den in January 2026 - a game that finished 4-0 to the Lions thanks to a late blitz of goals.

Official statements confirm that Millwall were in breach of FA Rule E21, which relates to a football club's responsibility to ensure its spectators and supporters (home and away) behave in an orderly fashion at matches.

Millwall have released a statement following the FA's punishment, where they have listed the sanctions they have faced for ableist and homophobic chanting towards Charlton.

Within that is confirmation that they have been handed a one-match partial stadium closure - the first time that an EFL club has been handed such a punishment from the FA.

The Athletic go on to confirm that it will be the Dockers Stand of the Den that will be impacted, with blocks 19, 20, 47 and 48 shut for the upcoming game against Sheffield United on December 8th.

That's going to impact a reported 1,274 fans who have tickets in that area, although they are invited to select an alternative seat in the stadium.

The additional one-match partial stadium closure that will be suspended until the end of next season will be activated if Millwall fans engage in more discriminatory chanting.

The statement from Millwall lays out what was chanted and why it was deemed unacceptable.

'South London S—' was the ableist chant, with 'You take it up the a—' the homophobic charge on the sanction.

Millwall's statement read: "Both chants are discriminatory and offensive, and the club condemns them in the strongest possible terms.

"Ableist and homophobic chanting, as well as any other form of discriminatory chanting, has no place in football or society. Any individual identified as engaging in such abuse will face the consequences."

Millwall's next home game comes on November 28th when they host Burnley in the EFL Championship.
