Sergej Jakirovic issues major Hull City injury update ahead of Brighton with Mohamed Belloumi latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sergej Jakirovic’s Hull City squad received an injury boost ahead of their Premier League clash with high-flying Brighton and Hove Albion.

Jakirovic is boosted by the return of Algerian winger Mohamed Belloumi for Sunday's top flight meeting with Fabian Hurzeler’s second placed Brighton at the MKM Stadium

Belloumi was sidelined for the1-0 loss at Arsenal just prior to the international break due to pain in his glutes and hamstring. Belloumi last featured in Hull’s draw with Ipswich Town but then started to struggle and missed training.

The 24-year-old is now in contention for a starting role against the Seagulls where he could be up against his fellow Algerian, Jaouen Hadjam, who has impressed at Brighton following his summer arrival from Swiss club Young Boys.

Meanwhile, Jakirovic is “very aware” that 13th placed Hull need to claim three points soon after recording a high number of draws.

Though still unbeaten at home in league action this season, the Tigers last won when defeating Coventry on August 29 and have had to settle for a point on five occasions since.

Brighton are faring incredibly well this term with 22 points to Hull’s 11, and Jakirovic is under no illusions as to the size of the task facing his squad and also the increasingly pressing need for a long-awaited victory.

“We have to be very aggressive because they’ll come at us, they pay very intensive football,” he said.

“A lot of high press chasing around the pitch. First balls, second balls, duels, it will be a demanding game and I hope we can respond.

“When I look back to Everton, Ipswich, Brentford, maybe we should have won two or three of those games but also we were down at Everton so at least we avoided defeat.

“That is always positive but we are very aware we lack some points and you cannot play all the time to just draw, draw, draw.

“It’s not enough, we need to take more risks on the pitch to try to win those games.”

Albion boss Hurzeler is keen to continue their impressive start to the season but expects a tough contest at Hull.

“First of all, I think they do an impressive job, “ said the German. “They had a brilliant start into the Premier League.

“They are very structured, also against Arsenal I think Arsenal suffered. They didn't create a lot of big chances, and therefore we need to be patient because we know also that Hull is really good in transition.

“They also have individual quality in their team. Very well-structured, very well-organised, good in transition, good in set pieces, and I think it's not an easy place to go to.”
