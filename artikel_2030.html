Fabian Schar has been talking to Swiss media about all things Newcastle United.

The defender now in his in his ninth season at St James’ Park.

Fabian Schar signed a one year contract extension that takes him up to 30 June 2027.

Set to turn 35 next month, this could well be the final Newcastle United season you feel for the classy Switzerland international.

Fabian Schar asked by SRF Sport what the expectations within the club were ahead of this 2026-27 season after so many changes in personnel across summer 2026: “We are not satisfied with how things have gone so far. But it was clear from the start that this wouldn’t be a season where we could compete for Champions League qualification as we have in the past. We have to accept reality and become more consistent as quickly as possible.”

In January the central defender suffered a severe foot injury that kept him out for over eight months.

Fabian Schar asked how it felt when he eventually returned to first team action when coming off the bench during the 2-1 home victory over Hull City in September: “It was more special than other times. Knowing everything I had to overcome to get back on the pitch changed me. You try to appreciate the situation even more and not take it for granted. The “complications” weren’t exactly minor. During that time, I doubted that things would ever work out the way they had before. But I didn’t give up and tried to keep the positives in mind. Newcastle United extending my contract early gave me the peace of mind I needed…For the club, it was a matter of weighing things up initially. I’m not getting any younger. Especially in the Premier League, it’s not easy to get a new contract at my age.”

Fabian Schar asked what Newcastle United means to him: “A great deal. When you have been with a club for so long, not only does the club grow on you, but the city does too. The people here live for Newcastle United. I feel extremely comfortable here; it’s become a second home for me.”

The Swiss defender asked what the future holds for him beyond this season: “As things stand, I’m not giving it too much thought. I still really enjoy playing football and would like to continue for a while. Where and how, we’ll see.”
