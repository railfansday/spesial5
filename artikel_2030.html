Arsenal fans have had plenty to say about Newcastle United and Bruno Guimaraes.

This comes ahead of Saturday’s match on Tyneside.

Arsenal fans had seen their team lose at St James’ Park three times in a row and not even score a single goal.

September 2025 looked like more of the same after Nick Woltemade put United ahead and Newcastle United defending well.

At 1-0, a cowardly challenge left Tino Livramento in agony and stretchered off, Arsenal taking advantage with two late goals, the winner in the very final seconds of added time.

Bruno Guimaraes now returning to St James’ Park for a first time, to give the match extra edge, as though it needed it…

Arsenal fans commenting via their Arsenal Mania message board:

‘Let’s win this before we have a nice run of home games. I see a 1-2 result but I’d like to smash this lot in the first half. A win, no injuries and a quick exit out of Mordor.’

‘We don’t ’Smash’ anyone anymore . We are 1-0 to The Mighty Arsenal though. In Mikel we trust.’

‘We will eventually , our forwards can’t keep continuing to reek. At some point they have to have a good day.

Anyhoo, I watched this lot vs Everton the other week on MNF and they were TERRIBLE. Everton could have hit them for 2/3 goals. That being said, they will 100% raise their game for us. Expecting a tight game but we’ll be too good.

‘Wonder if it’s going to be a slightly easier game now they don’t play Howeball anymore.’

‘Tough ground and right after the interlols is never ideal. These lot will have a go at us, like they always do. It will be a game full of more yellow cards than shots on goal from both teams combined. 1-0 to the Arsenal of course but a draw wouldn’t be catastrophic.’

‘But you can’t compare them this year to the last years. They lost some big players the last 2 years with Isak, Tonali, Guimares or Gordon and I think Joelinton is out injured. So from our last minute win last year I think only Thiaw and Burn will be playing this.

So I really hope we can for once win with a bit more margin. But I always take a one nill win.’

‘I agree. But. Squad selection doesn’t matter a lot when facing certain clubs in the Premier League. Away games against Newcastle is the same like playing at Old Trafford facing a rubbish Man U squad on paper but they always give us hard time. Newcastle will go hard against us and look like a top 4 Premier League team and then go to lose the next game against Ipswich at home.’

‘Don’t have a good feeling about this one, tough away game after international break where lots of our players were called up. I think 0-0 but hopefully we can scrape a 1-0 with a set piece after a boring game.’

‘I can see this being a good win and performance.

Given it’s away from home, it won’t be by a big scoreline, but I think 2-0 is very realistic.

Newcastle are in a bit of a muddle at the moment, and they’ll have had 2 weeks to prepare and it will be a frenetic start and atmosphere, but I predict us to weather the storm and then overcome that

‘Bruno Guimaraes can barely move, the guy is an absolute liability off the ball.’

‘Bruno a very good player. Got another level in him as well. We have selfish depth.’

‘We unlock Bruno if we put a runner or two (with quality to finish) ahead of him.

You can tell at the moment he’s looking up and everyone is coming to feet.’

‘Was thinking this too about Bruno, we aren’t really utilising his best attributes.

Hopefully we plan to bring in some forwards who suit his style of play.’

‘The dynamic I want to see as much as possible, Bruno deeper and playing forward with pace and precision to get us against unsettled defences. Ødegaard up the pitch.’

‘Bruno Guimaraes underwhelming in an Arsenal shirt so far. Rice/MLS should be our starting midfield in every big game this season.

It’s not going to look great that we lack quality in attack while we’ve got around £150m worth of midfield talent on the bench in Bruno and Zubi, but that’s down to questionable recruitment.’

‘The way I see it, if the game requires penetration or boldness, Bruno is your man. If the game needs control, then MLS should start.

Bruno is a creative interior in a 433. What we saw at Lyon is the truest version of him.’
