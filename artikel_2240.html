23-year-old helping West Brom forget about missing out on £5m summer target: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

West Brom needed to strengthen their right-back position in the summer after Alfie Gilchrist’s struggles, and with George Campbell better suited centrally. WBA pursued a deal for loanee Danny Imray, who had impressed during the second half of the 2025/26 term. However, it was Wrexham who sealed his signature for £5m.

The Baggies ended up recruiting Nolan Galves and Brayann Pereira to strengthen the position ahead of the 2026/27 campaign. Despite a promising start to life at The Hawthorns, Galves sustained an ACL injury that has ruled him out for the remainder of the season.

Pereira stepped in for the injured Frenchman, but his first start couldn’t have gone any worse, with a red card against Derby County. However, since his poor first impression, the 23-year-old has become one of West Brom’s best players. Now, the failure to sign Imray doesn’t look so bad.

Pereira made it just 34 minutes into his first start for West Brom before being sent off. With Galves’ injury, it left James Morrison with a potential dilemma as to whether the Baggies would have an adequate right-back for the season.

However, since returning to the starting XI, the defender has been a mainstay in WBA’s team. His latest game against Millwall was his best display yet, and it’s a positive sign of things to come from the 23-year-old.

Pereira had the most touches in the match with 92 and completed the joint-most dribbles. He also had a pass accuracy of 92% and won all four of his aerial duels. Morrison will be delighted by his turnaround in form, and it is his position to lose now for the rest of the campaign.

Imray was a tough player for West Brom to replace in the summer after his positive loan spell at The Hawthorns. 55.2% of his dribbles were successful last term, and he helped keep seven clean sheets as the West Midlands outfit avoided relegation.

WBA recruited two right-backs for under £2m. This was less than half the amount the former Crystal Palace man was sold for, and Morrison’s side can be pleased with their recruitment. While Galves’ injury is a huge loss to the Baggies, Pereira has helped soften the blow.

The summer arrival has six more successful dribbles than the former loanee and has a better pass rate by 15%. Imray has also found game time limited over the last few weeks at Wrexham. With West Brom 5th in the league and 10 points ahead of the Red Dragons, the 23-year-old could be rethinking his decision.
