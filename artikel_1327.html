Argentina call up seven Serie A players from Roma, Como, Fiorentina and Parma

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lionel Scaloni called up seven Serie A players for the Argentina squad to face Uruguay and Costa Rica, representing Como, Roma, Fiorentina and Parma.

The World Cup Finalists are due to play a friendly match against Uruguay on November 13 and then Costa Rica on November 17.

Nahuel Molina is still not available due to a ban received for the events after that World Cup Final, so he will remain to work with Roma along with injured teammate Paulo Dybala.

However, the Giallorossi are sending Leonardo Balerdi, Matias Soulé and Santiago Castro to Scaloni’s camp.

Como players Nico Paz and Maxi Perrone are also called up, along with Fiorentina talent Franco Mastantuono.

Parma defender Mariano Troilo completes the Serie A representation for Argentina.

Nico Gonzalez would’ve been part of the side, but he is one of the many Juventus players currently out injured, along with Inter captain Lautaro Martinez.

Goalkeepers: Emiliano Martinez (Chelsea), Geronimo Rulli (Manchester City), Juan Musso (Atletico Madrid)

Defenders: Santiago Beltran (River Plate), Gonzalo Montiel (River Plate), Agustin Giay (Palmeiras), Aaron Anselmino (Chelsea), Mariano Troilo (Parma), Leonardo Balerdi (Roma), Lisandro Martinez (Manchester United), Facundo Medina (Bayer Leverkusen), Nicolas Tagliafico (Lyon), Roman Vega (Zenit)

Midfielders: Ezequiel Fernandez (Bayer Leverkusen), Alexis Mac Allister (Liverpool), Nico Paz (Como), Maximo Perrone (Como), Valentin Barco (Chelsea)

Strikers: Matias Soulé (Roma), Franco Mastantuono (Fiorentina), Giuliano Simeone (Atletico Madrid), Gianluca Prestianni (Benfica), Thiago Almada (River Plate), Jose Manuel Lopez (Palmeiras), Santiago Castro (Roma), Julian Alvarez (Atletico Madrid)
