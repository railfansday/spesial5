JJ Gabriel Confirms Man Utd Move to Real Madrid With Farewell Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

JJ Gabriel has officially announced that he is set to leave Manchester United, sharing a heartfelt message on social media regarding his imminent exit from the north-west club.

The 16-year-old, who has made waves in United's academy since joining the Red Devils in 2020, is expected to sign for Real Madrid. After reports emerged suggesting that Gabriel and his representatives were assessing other opportunities, Los Blancos swept in and fought off competition from several other top clubs to land the starlet.

United reportedly fought tooth and nail to keep hold of their prodigy talent, but were ultimately unable to do so.

The Premier League giants will be due compensation, however, after developing the youngster over the last six years.

Rarely has a teenager who hasn't featured in senior football ever garnered more attention than Gabriel, whose development in the social media limelight has been heavily scrutinised by those who feel his growth should've been kept under wraps.

Only turning 16 back in October, Gabriel has remarkably already been at the centre of a transfer saga. The schoolboy's camp announced his intention to leave United in September after he wasn't included in Michael Carrick's Champions League matchday squad against Sabah FK.

Immediately, the likes of Real Madrid, Barcelona and Bayern Munich were linked with a move for Gabriel. Los Blancos eventually won the race for the wonderkid after weeks of speculation around who the youngster would opt to continue his journey with.

Gabriel has now confirmed his exit from United, sharing a short video message to his 800,000 Instagram followers on Tuesday evening.

"Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams," he said.

"Here, I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award. You gave me moments I will always treasure. To every coach, every member of staff, my teammates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow. As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything."

The Sun's Samuel Luckhurst slammed the announcement, writing: "Lots of things about academy football make one despair these days and teenagers who’ve never kicked a ball for the first team penning ‘open letters’ and farewells is one. JJ O’Cearuill/Gabriel and co. taking #mufc for a ride and then offering a ‘thank you’ takes the biscuit."

Elsewhere, Journalist Nathan Salt revealed that Gabriel's family have yet to make contact with United since their initial termination request following the Sabah squad selection debacle.

"Senior United staff learned about the JJ Gabriel statement on Instagram. Still no contact from the family since initial termination request post-Sabah," Salt wrote on X.

Unsurprisingly, social media's natural cynicism was reflected in the reaction to Gabriel's goodbye post. Many mocked the fact that the 16-year-old, who never played a minute for the United first team, felt the need to post such an extensive message and video.

"Kids doing a goodbye video. Tells you all you need to know about him and his dad," one fan wrote on X.

"Making a farewell video as if he’s a club legend who’s won multiple Premier League titles and a Champions League trophy. Will not be missed," another added.

"Lads, we’ve lost [Cristiano] Ronaldo, [Wayne] Rooney and Sir Alex [Ferguson], now we’re getting thank-you tributes from people who have never played for us," another supporter wrote.

"You would think he’s played 100 games for the club. Good riddance." - Man Utd fan on X.

"Bro thinks he’s a club legend," added another.

Meanwhile, others compared the young forward to Alejandro Garnacho, who was touted as a rising star at United before he eventually departed for Chelsea, following a breakdown in relations with the Red Devils.

"Garnacho the 2nd," wrote one fan, while another added: "Garnacho 2.0 loading lmao."

According to the latest reports, the Man United youngster is demanding significant money to remain at Old Trafford, as an exit looks likely.

Gabriel is expected to start in Real Madrid's reserve team, Real Madrid Castilla, who operate in the third tier of Spanish football.
