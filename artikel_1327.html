Malen matches Roma record with 26 goals in 2026, outscoring Kane and Mbappé

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Donyell Malen has equalled a 66-year-old Roma record after his four goals in the 5-1 win at Parma took him to 26 Serie A goals in 2026.

According to Opta, the only other Giallorossi player to score more than 25 league goals in a calendar year is Pedro Manfredini, who also scored 26 in 1960. With six weeks of the year still to go, Malen needs one more goal to become the first Roma player to reach 27.

Malen’s tally also puts him ahead of some of Europe’s top strikers this year. TuttoMercatoWeb point out that Bayern Munich’s Harry Kane, the reigning Ballon d’Or winner, and Barcelona’s Raphinha have 21 league goals each in 2026. Real Madrid’s Kylian Mbappé has 19 in La Liga. The comparison covers league goals only, so cup and international matches aren’t included.

The Dutchman scored 14 of his 26 in the second half of last season after joining from Aston Villa. He has 12 in 11 games this season, which makes him Serie A’s top scorer, three ahead of Monza’s Gustavo Varela.

He’ll turn 28 in January and has found consistency as a central striker that he never quite had in England. Roma are now working on a new contract for him, although his current deal already runs until 2030.
