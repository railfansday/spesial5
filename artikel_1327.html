England fans have been reacting on social media after watching the Three Lions in action against Croatia.

On Thursday night, Thomas Tuchel’s men were back in action as domestic football is left on the back burner while international football takes centre stage.

Under the lights at Wembley, England welcomed Croatia, who had a torrid time when they last played Tuchel’s team, losing 7-0 on home soil.

This time around, Slaven Bilic’s team produced a much-improved display but did fall behind in the 24th minute, with Morgan Rogers giving England a 1-0 half-time lead.

In the second half, England were sloppy, and Croatia got back into the match through Petar Sucic's goal in the 59th minute. The Three Lions were then awarded a penalty after Jarell Quansah was fouled inside the area, with Harry Kane making it 2-1.

Eventually, the Three Lions held on to win 2-1, putting themselves nine points clear of Croatia in their Nations League group.

With first-choice pairing Ezri Konsa and Marc Guehi unavailable, Tuchel started with Quansah and Dan Burn at centre back. Taking to social media, England fans were unimpressed by Quansah’s overall performance, after his mistake led to Croatia’s equaliser, although he did win a penalty minutes later.

One fan said: “Jarell Quansah has absolutely and utterly no business being anywhere near this England squad.”

Another tweeted: “Just playing for top clubs doesn’t make you a great player. Quansah is a liability. Always has been.”

A third fan added: “Is Quansah England's worst ever defender. I am 45 and can’t think of anyone as bad for England at the back. He is absolutely diabolical.”

Someone else wrote: “Got his favourites already I don’t know what he sees in Quansah either.”

A fifth commented: “Sick and tired of watching a boring slow bang average England with Kane doing what he likes. It’s embarrassing and Tuchel is a joke for allowing it. Quansah and Burn at the back are atrocious.”

Another fan said: “Quansah is having a mare, looks out of his depth against an average Croatia side.” One England fan added: “How does Quansah get anywhere near the England squad? Honking.”

Quansah impressed Jurgen Klopp during his time at Liverpool, earning regular game time alongside captain Virgil van Dijk.

The striker has been in brilliant goalscoring form after leaving Tottenham for Middlesbrough

However, under Arne Slot, the 23-year-old struggled and was eventually sold to German Bundesliga side Bayer Leverkusen, where he has rediscovered his form, earning him a place in Tuchel’s 2026 World Cup squad.

Looking ahead, England will be back in action on Sunday night when they take on world champions Spain at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid.
