Today’s Papers – Tresoldi top of Milan’s list, Roma in pole for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Good morning! Here are all today’s biggest stories from the Italian sports papers.

Milan go for it: Tresoldi now Gerry Cardinale is pushing to sign Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi in January, and Ruben Amorim is considering a two-man attack with Tresoldi alongside Gonçalo Ramos. The 22-year-old is also wanted by Roberto Mancini for Italy.

Endrick, a gift for Gasp Roma are also looking for more goals, and Endrick could be the answer for Gian Piero Gasperini. “I want to play,” the Brazilian says, as Real Madrid consider a loan.

Benches worth a 10 On the eve of Italy vs. France, Gazzetta compares the two coaches, Roberto Mancini and Zinedine Zidane: the strength of leaders, and of ideas.

Inter: Stones fit but under assessment, Thuram stops Inter have problems at the back and up front. John Stones has recovered from injury but is still being monitored, while Marcus Thuram has been forced to stop.

Endrick, not Milan Roma are in pole position for Endrick, and the Real Madrid forward is open to joining Gasperini. Sporting director Tony D’Amico is working to get a yes in January, and Dan Friedkin is pushing for a loan with an option to buy. Endrick, a member of the evangelical movement Athletes of Christ, has ruled out Milan, the “Devil”. He has played only 141 minutes this season under José Mourinho, with one goal and one assist. “I need to grow, I’m weighing up my future,” he says.

Ludi: ‘Como, Paz, Endrick and the bridge with Real’ Como sporting director Carlalberto Ludi discusses Nico Paz, Endrick and the club’s ties with Real Madrid.

Juve torn between Sørloth and Balogun Mateo Retegui will be hard to sign, so Juventus are choosing between Alexander Sørloth and Folarin Balogun for their new striker.

Inter, Chivu loses three It isn’t just Ange-Yoan Bonny who is out: Marcus Thuram and Aleksandar Stanković are also injured.

Napoli move for Cacciamani Napoli have contacted Torino about the winger.

Pio breaks records: it’s a challenge to Mbappé Francesco Pio Esposito already has seven goals in 13 games for Italy, and he faces Kylian Mbappé at San Siro tomorrow. His brother Sebastiano is favourite to start on the right.

Kolo on the wing brings you a No 9 Randal Kolo Muani has impressed Luciano Spalletti as a wide player, so Juventus could sell Francisco Conceição or Edon Zhegrova to make room for a new centre-forward. As well as Sørloth and Balogun, Gianluca Scamacca is now on the list, after falling behind Nikola Krstović at Atalanta. Spalletti is also working with Under-21 coach Silvio Baldini to revive Jeff Ekhator, and Juve are speeding up the permanent deal for Guglielmo Vicario.

Torino stadium: the proposal is in Torino have presented their long-promised plan for the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino to Turin’s city council. It includes a 99-year surface-rights deal, which could help attract investors and even lead to a change of ownership at the club. Mayor Stefano Lo Russo called it a partnership project for both the stadium and the Robaldo training centre: “Finally!”

Inter fall apart again: Bonny and Thuram out Inter’s attacking crisis continues. Bonny is out for a month, Thuram has left the France squad, and Stanković is also injured. Chivu is hoping for good news on Lautaro Martínez.

Tresoldi, the fan, wants Milan The Club Brugge striker, who Italy and Germany are both trying to recruit, wouldn’t hesitate to join Milan. He is top of Cardinale’s list, and Milan will make an attempt in January.

Twenty-two clubs for one national team Mancini’s squad is drawn from 22 different clubs. Nicolò Zaniolo has gone home: “Personal reasons, it isn’t easy.” Alphadjo Cissè is emotional about his call-up: “Balotelli is my role model.”
