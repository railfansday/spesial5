Elden Ring turns Malenia and Radahn into football jerseys with $400 collector’s set

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Elden Ring fans can now represent their favorite bosses with an officially licensed football jersey collection featuring Malenia, Radahn, Godfrey, and Maliketh.

Not to be outdone by the Bloodborne fashion collection, which famously allowed fans to dress up as the Hunter, gaming apparel brand Dyenamik has created four Elden Ring boss-themed jerseys.

After all, wearing shirts inspired by the game’s most iconic characters is certainly one way to flex your victory over them. This is especially true for those who spent many an hour stuck on Malenia, watching the “You Died” screen for the hundredth time (it couldn’t be me).

Whether you’re venturing back into the Lands Between in preparation for Duskbloods or just fancy showing off your love for Malenia, Radahn, Godfrey, and Maliketh, these new jerseys could be worth adding to your wardrobe.

Kicking things off is Radahn’s jersey, which features the Starscourge Heirloom as its club badge. The shirt also features a Great Rune on the back and a gravity crest across the shoulders, inspired by engravings on the Starscourge Greatswords.

Malenia’s design uses the Winged Sword Insignia for its badge, while the back features a gold vinyl emblem taken from her cape. Of course, many Elden Ring players will instantly be familiar with this design, given that the playerbase spent considerable time dueling with this sword-wielding menace.

Meanwhile, Godfrey’s royal blue shirt features patterns from his armor and uses a lion badge representing Serosh, the beast regent carried on his back. The back of the jersey features the Erdtree and the Elden Ring artifact itself.

Maliketh rounds out the boss lineup with armor-inspired patterns and an ancient sigil from his arena in Crumbling Farum Azula across the rear.

For those who would rather represent the Lands Between as a whole, there are three jerseys that feature the game’s iconic rune symbol and Erdtree sigil.

The complete six-shirt set will set you back $400 (around £299) and comes in a branded collector’s box with a matte finish, gold foiling, and a magnetic clasp. If you’re only a fan of one or two bosses, then you can also pick up individual jerseys for $93 (around £70).

Dyenamik offers international shipping, including to the UK, US, Europe, Australia, and Japan. You can check out the full Elden Ring jersey collection via the official website here.
