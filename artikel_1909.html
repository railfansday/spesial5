QN: Milan ‘swooping’ for McTominay after renewal talks ‘stall sharply’ – the latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan are eyeing up a move for Napoli star Scott McTominay, according to reports this morning. It’s another major move that could happen.

It feels like Milan have taken a new step this season. In the summer, their recruitment was superb and they took a step away from signing gambles for the future. This doesn’t seem to be a one-window thing either, with another target for next year emerging.

According to Quotidiano Nazionale, the Rossoneri are beginning their attack on the mercato and the next target in their sights is Scott McTominay, as Calciomercato.com relays. The Scotsman is out of contract in the summer of 2028 and he’s a potential departure in 2027.

Contract talks have ‘stalled sharply’ in recent weeks, and this makes him a major target for next year. With a salary of just €3 million per year, he’d likely expect a deserved raise if he moved to San Siro and Gerry Cardinale wouldn’t be afraid to work with him on that matter.

It’s a bold target and whether it’d be related to a move for Bruno Fernandes will need to be determined. Nevertheless, it’s a promising sign that Milan are ready to swoop for one of Serie A’s best players.
