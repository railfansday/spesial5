Five alive: Bexhill move on up with win over Midhurst

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Since Bexhill secured a win in the reverse fixture in September, Midhurst have struggled to recover from internal strife that saw their manager and a number of players depart, with a run of poor results that has plunged them into the relegation mire.

Meanwhile, Bexhill have managed to strike a rich vein of form with a squad that blends youth with experience and a strong sense of togetherness and solidarity.

Missing Luke Donaldson with a three-match ban, Pirates gaffer Jay Skinner-Swain kept Ash Latuske and Ryan Moir in their attacking positions on the flanks with Charlie Scarles slotting into an advanced role in midfield.

Bexhill couldn’t have hoped for a better start. In the first minute Theo Coombs had a chance well saved but from the resulting corner Charlie Playford ghosted into the six yard box to beat all-comers with a bullet header from point blank range.

Three minutes later Latuske beat two men with a powerful run down the right to set up Shonk and the Bexhill record goalscorer made no mistake in adding another to his tally.

It looked like Bexhill would run amok but it’s all credit to the young Midhurst lads that they didn’t just fold. They put in some decent enough approach moves but the Pirates defence had the measure of them.

At the other end, another goal looked almost inevitable. On 25 minutes Shonk had an effort cleared off the line from a Moir corner. Shortly after, Scarles had an effort well saved by the young Midhurst keeper who was keeping his side in with a sniff.

Just before the break there was nothing he could do though as Latuske lashed in a drive that had the keeper beaten all ends up but the ball somehow bounced back out into play off the inside of the post.

Bexhill went in two goals to the good at half-time but on the balance of play and chances it should have been more.

Almost inevitably Midhurst delivered a scare early in the second half. Just five minutes in, a runner skipped past the Bexhill defensive lines and delivered a ball which was swept into the Pirates net. It was followed minutes later by a good effort that required a fine save from Finn Holter.

But the moment soon passed for Midhurst and Bexhill got back in amongst it. The killer goal came on 55 and it was that man Coombs again, benefitting from some more great play from Latuske and swivelling to tuck the chance away.

Now it really was all Bexhill as Midhurst wilted under the pressure. On 65 minutes Latuske was at it again, this time setting up Shonk, whose shot curled just wide of the post. It was the briefest of respites for Midhurst as Moir played in Coombs on the left and this time he turned provider, chipping in a perfect cross for Shonk to nod home for 4-1.

Bexhill were rampant now and on 76 minutes substitute Jack Samways wrapped it up at 5-1 to the Pirates with the pick of the afternoon’s goals. Winning the ball just inside the box he crashed a perfect half volley into the Brockley Road End net.

It was no less than Bexhill deserved for a thoroughly professional demolition job on their hapless visitors.

The Pirates move up sixth in the league and have a chance to keep their current run going with the long trip over to Pagham on Saturday afternoon.
