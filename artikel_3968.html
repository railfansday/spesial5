Two More Games Confirmed For Xbox Game Pass In Early 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Who's up for some new release dates for Xbox Game Pass titles? Well, we've got two for you today, both of which are scheduled to join the service in early 2027.

First up is Nubby's Number Factory, a funky-looking plinko-style roguelike which has you working in a numbers factory and trying to stop the sun from exploding. Makes sense, right? Well, it's on the way to XGP on January 14, 2027.

Alongside that comes Mr Magpie's Harmless Card Game. The spooky deckbuilder had already been announced during October's ID@Xbox showcase, but now we have an official release date of February 18, 2027.

These games will be joining an already impressive batch of early 2027 Xbox Game Pass releases, battling for attention amongst the likes of Persona 4 Revival, Stranger Than Heaven, Tropico 7, and Fable.

So, here's a closer look at the two games confirmed for Xbox Game Pass in Early 2027:

A plinko-style roguelike where you work at a number factory. Launch your spherical friend, Nubby, down a pegboard to make bigger and bigger numbers! But beware, if you fail to meet your number quota, the sun will explode.

A surreal corporate horror deckbuilder where you gamble your life for cash, dodge murderous JERRY cards, and “maximise synergies” to climb the company ladder in Mr Magpie’s office nightmare.
