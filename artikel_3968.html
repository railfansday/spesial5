My Hero Ultra Rumble Mobile teaser trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Bandai Namco has released a teaser trailer for My Hero Ultra Rumble Mobile, its newly announced mobile version of the free-to-play battle royale coming. Pre-registration is available now for iOS via App Store and Android via Google Play.

As previously detailed, the mobile version will operate as its own game, and will not support cross-play between the existing console and PC versions of My Hero Ultra Rumble. Cross-play between iOS and Android is supported.

Here is an overview of the game, via its store pages:

My Hero Ultra Rumble Mobile is finally here!

Become your favorite My Hero Academia characters and fight to survive in an epic battle royale featuring eight teams of three players! Use your Quirks to team up with players online and fight your way to become the last team standing!

An online multiplayer action game where you battle as My Hero Academia characters in an epic 24-player battle royale!

Take on a vast battlefield and unleash each character’s unique Quirk! Team up with players online and fight your way to becoming the last team standing!

Every new season will add content, including characters and costumes! Dive into a battle where the tide can turn in an instant!
