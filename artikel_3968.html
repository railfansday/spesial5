Feature: What Sony Live Service PS5 Games Are Still Alive?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

All the cancelled projects and those that remain

Video games are cancelled internally all the time, but never in PlayStation's personal history have those cancellations had to happen in such a public manner. The current PS5 generation has seen the platform holder try to win the live service space, and a few years later, it's having to cancel most of the projects it revealed in attempting to achieve that.

To try and get a piece of the Fortnite pie, Sony made a hard and deliberate push into the Games as a Service space at the start of the PS5 generation. It acquired Destiny 2 studio Bungie to have it help the manufacturer do so, and at the height of the initiative, PlayStation planned to launch 12 live service games in just a few years.

The latest victim is Fairgames from Haven Studio, a developer set up to create the heist co-op title. It was announced in 2023, and three and a half years later, it's now been cancelled after failing to impress in a new round of private tests.

Times have changed, and company executives are finally learning you can't set out with the intention of making the next Genshin Impact or Apex Legends; it has to happen more naturally. Sony has learnt that the hard way, with many graves dug for its failed projects.

But what's still left of the push and remains in active development at PlayStation?

Despite the consistent failure in the space, with only one big win to its name in the form of Helldivers 2, Sony has stated it's going to persist with live service titles. Back in June, boss Hideaki Nishino said the game model "attracts users on a global level".

He continued: "The genre itself is relatively new, and I think many people are trying various things, so we also want to continue to take on challenges within that context."

From Sony's perspective, those live service games aren't going anywhere despite the track record and industry shifts.
