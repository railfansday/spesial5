This week in PC games: solve the disappearance of 700 people, build a night market for spirits, and play chess on a board that disobeys physics

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

I don't know what Edwin's been feeding the Maw in my off week, but when I entered its chambers this morning it had left game releases everywhere. Ceiling, walls, floor, they all glisten with games of every size and genre. Even after I hosed away the ones largely made with the assistance of generative AI, the sifting sieves fitted across the drains held a bounty of interesting releases.

Let's get to it, then, before the Maw wakes up and realises I've nabbed all its spoils.

Well, that was potentially too many games in all, even after striking out a fair few that were on my list when I started writing this round up.

It's a busy week for us in the warehouse. Edwin's putting the finishing touches on a series of features he's been working on for months, I'm getting the RPS 100 ready to go live next week, James is wrangling the advent calendar, Mark is working on a write up of a tasty expansion pack, and the guides team are getting everything in order head of the release of GTA 6 next week. One day we will rest, and it will be lovely. But not this week.
