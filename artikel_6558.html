Kit Keenan &amp; Gigi Powers’s Mom’s Feta Artichoke Dip

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In our Cooking With Friends series, friends of Delish visit the Test Kitchen to share their favorite recipes with you. Happy cooking!

Alongside her many talents, one quality I particularly applaud my mom for is her expertise in hosting. She is specifically known for what she dubbed “the emergency dinner party.” This is the kind of soirée that, despite being quickly organized—perhaps after a long day at work or a busy weekend afternoon—never fails to keep guests entertained and impressed. When it comes to appetizers at an emergency dinner party, the objective is to use limited ingredients, ensure ease of assembly, and deliver bangin’ flavor. For my mom’s famous feta artichoke dip, only three ingredients are absolutely crucial: feta, artichoke hearts, and olive oil. The brine from the canned artichoke hearts allows the feta to melt down in the oven, which in turn coats the artichoke hearts, creating a velvety dip. The best part about this recipe is that it’s completely customizable. My favorite variation includes adding honey, thyme, and an olive topper, which elevates the dip to a whole new level. Serve it with crunchy crostini or pita chips at your next dinner party, and your guests will go crazy.
