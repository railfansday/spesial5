Thanksgiving Potato Skins

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Making Thanksgiving potato skins is by far the tastiest way to use up all those leftovers. But these are so good that you might want to jump the gun and make them specifically for your holiday spread. Add your leftovers (or some extra Thanksgiving fixings) to a few pantry staples for the best potato skins this holiday season. The hollowed-out potatoes are an excellent vessel for extra-creamy mashed potatoes, stuffing, turkey, and dollops of cranberry sauce.

Make ahead:To make these ahead, follow the instructions through step 3. Freeze the hollowed-out potatoes in a single row on a tray and transfer to a freezer bag. Thaw overnight and proceed with steps 4 to 7 when ready to serve.
