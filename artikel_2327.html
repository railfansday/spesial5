Musiala and Diaz on the mend, Bayern expect Saibari back in early 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With their teammates away on international duty, Jamal Musiala and Bara Sapoko Ndiaye trained with the second team today.

It marks another stage of progress for both of Bayern Munich's midfielders as they near even closer to being declared full fit and available.

Luis Díaz, as pictured by BILD, continued his rehab training on the pitch today alongside rehab manager Benjamin Sommer.

The Colombian is also close to making his comeback which is expected to come against Lille (November 25) or HSV (November 28).

Unfortunately, the same cannot be said for Ismael Saibari who was at Säbener Straße today, on crutches and in a protective boot.

Saibari has been receiving treatment from doctors and physiotherapists daily since his surgery, with a 3-month absence forecasted.
