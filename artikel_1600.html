Hutchinson says position tweak helped inspire comeback: “I have to make a difference”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Omari Hutchinson was the big hero for AC Milan in their comeback win against Olympiacos, so naturally, he was very pleased in the post-match interview.

Milan thought they were heading for yet another defeat in the Europa League. Then, the Nottingham Forest loanee Hutchinson took matters into his own hands, bringing some life into the game with a great solo goal to make it 2-1.

He followed that up with two assists for the second and third goals, before ending with a hockey assist. Needless to say, he was the MOTM in our player ratings and he has certainly strengthened his position ahead of the upcoming games.

Speaking to Sky Italia after the game, as transcribed by MilanNews, Hutchinson said that he’s feeling very happy at Milan. He also discussed why he was able to change things all of a sudden, naming the position as a factor.

“It was a little different tonight. I played as a 10. Then, when I moved to the right, I had a lot more space. It depends, though. Sometimes as a 10 you have more space, and sometimes you don’t.”

You were more comfortable on the flank. How are you feeling at Milan?

“I feel very comfortable. There are a lot of players who are playing really well. When I come on, I have to make a difference.

“It doesn’t matter where I play, even though I might have more space on the right. The coach and the team are helping me. I’m still adjusting, but I’m having a lot of fun here.”
