“If there can be a way out” – Moretto states Milan have no intention of offering Tomori a renewal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It seems we are heading into the final year of Fikayo Tomori’s playing relationship with AC Milan, with talk of a renewal silent.

Next year, at the end of June, Tomori’s deal expires and if nothing is done before then, his six-year spell with the club will come to an end.

It’s been an up-and-down season for the Englishman, especially after he was included in the ‘bomb squad’ through the mercato. However, he was needed back in the squad and reintegrated.

Despite showing this fight, though, it seems unlikely that he’ll be rewarded with a new deal. There are plans to invest heavily in the club next year and the defender isn’t seen as a pillar of the team, let alone the defence. So a renewal isn’t being focused on.

As Matteo Moretto told viewers on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, Tomori previously had a chance at a renewal. However, that now seems to have gone, as MilanNews relays.

“There’s a before and after regarding Tomori. In my opinion, if Massimiliano Allegri had remained on the Milan bench, perhaps in that case there could have been a chance of a renewal for Tomori.

“Then there was the slightly peculiar way Milan handled the Tomori situation. Tomori is a player who has always been absolutely professional and deeply loves the Milan shirt; he has always shown all his commitment.

“It’s true, he was sidelined; Milan tried to find solutions on the way out right in the last days of the transfer window. His contract expires in 2027.

“There were discussions underway for the renewal when Allegri was on the bench; then, with time and with the succession of certain events, this type of renewal was no longer discussed.

“To date, the negotiations are absolutely at a standstill. There is no dialogue between the parties and I repeat, Tomori’s contract expires in 2027.

“We’ll see if in January there can be a way out, perhaps with the payment of compensation to Milan, or if it will reach zero in June, but as of today there is no trace of a possible renewal.”
