Opinion: Liverpool given green light to sign £46m sensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool have been linked with a lot of players over the last few years.

Over £600 million has been spent on them and, more change is still needed.

Obviously, this isn't the outcome that the Anfield outfit will have wanted but, it's the hand that they've seemingly been dealt.

All of their rivals are constantly looking for ways to upgrade their team and, the more that they manage to do this, the better they become and the more difficult they are to beat.

Fans expected Liverpool to be competing for silverware this term but, now that feels like it all rests on their transfer plans with the club now being handed a green light to sign one of their targets.

Barcelona want to sign Ayden Heaven which could give Liverpool the green light to sign Ronald Araujo

One of the main positions that Liverpool needed to upgrade in the 2026-27 transfer window was their defence.

Andy Robertson and Ibrahima Konate both exited Anfield for free and, the performances of Jeremie Frimpong and Conor Bradley were shoddy.

Throw in the fact that Bradley managed to suffer yet another long-term injury and, Liverpool were left with a bit-part defence.

That's why Jeremy Jacquet and Ronald Araujo were brought in.

Nobody knew how each defender would perform due to their lack of Premier League experience but, to the surprise of many, they've been two of the Reds' best players.

But, because Araujo is only on a season-long loan, his future is still undecided or, it was until this fresh update from BBC Sport.

"Fulham and Barcelona are among the clubs to have expressed an interest in Heaven, with both having tried to sign him when he decided to leave Arsenal," they wrote.

"The England U20s defender's preference is believed to be a loan move, though he would also entertain permanent offers.

"At United he has largely been used as a back-up option and, after enjoying a run in the team under former manager Ruben Amorim, he lost his place under Michael Carrick. He returned to the side only after an injury crisis hit the club last season.

"Heaven is also capable of playing at left-back and in midfield, but does not expect to be trusted as a regular starter amid the instability at Old Trafford, with the club 19th in the league.

"He is under contract until 2029 in a deal that includes an option for an extra year."

Ayden Heaven joining Barcelona could force Barcelona to sell Ronald Araujo

Araujo's versatility is one of the best things about him.

The defender can be comfortably used as a centre-back or a right-back with Iraola using him in both positions this term.

Indeed, the Uruguayan's injury history is a bit of a worry but, touch wood, he's managed to keep fit while playing for Liverpool so far.

As mentioned, Araujo's future isn't set in stone yet but, if Barca manage to sign Heaven from Manchester United, it feels like Araujo would fall further down Hansi Flick's plans.

This could mean that the La Liga giants are willing to negotiate a cheaper deal for the centre-back which, judging by the recent developments from Liverpool, is exactly what they want.

Here's hoping that a deal can be reached soon and that Barcelona find a successor for their captain soon.
