Report: Liverpool ready to sign one of the fastest players in Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool are making moves, and this one is exciting.

Ronald Araujo has already been such a stalwart presence in the right-back role for the Reds. But the Uruguayan is merely a temporary solution.

Liverpool may exercise the option to buy him on a permanent transfer from Barcelona. However, long-term Araujo cannot be considered as the club's first choice right-back.

He is a useful option to have. Someone who can really offer cover in multiple areas. But Liverpool need a proper right-back. A player capable of being a threat on the ball and go forward to create chances for his teammates.

Because the truth is Liverpool still haven't really replaced him. Jeremie Frimpong was never a serious consideration but more of a back-up option as well.

When is Conor Bradley coming back for Liverpool?The plan was to make the Northern Irishman the first-choice option for the Reds. Bradley was already competing with Alexander-Arnold for the right-back role, and he looked like he was ready to come in and be the first choice. But amid Trent's departure, Bradley had a tremendous amount of bad luck with injuries. He's now missed close to a year of football. Thankfully, he is on his way back. Bradley is training with the team and is making encouraging progress with his recovery. The Reds' medical staff are in no rush to bring him back straight away. They need to get him used to playing with the ball, physical contact and the rapid bursts of direction that football at such an elite level requires. It will still be a while until we see Bradley back looking like his old self.
