Popular Thanksgiving Product Recalled From Costco Shelves Over Concerning Contamination Risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Here's what shoppers need to know ahead of the holiday.

Costco members, listen up. A popular Thanksgiving topping is being recalled over concerns about possible cleaning chemical contamination. Here's what to watch for.

Costco is recalling its Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream ahead of the upcoming Thanksgiving holiday. The recall covers 15-ounce cans sold between October 11 and October 31, 2026, including those purchased in three-packs.

To identify the affected product, check for item number 1777000, item code AC50, and a best-if-used-by date of April 2, 2027. The cans must also have a printed timestamp between 00:35 and 02:45.

The whipped cream was sold at Costco warehouses in 12 states: Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Wisconsin.

Producer Alamance Foods voluntarily recalled the product after learning that one batch may contain a small amount of a cleaning chemical. According to a November 7 letter sent to Costco members, some shoppers reported a chemical smell and taste.

The company said no serious adverse health effects requiring medical care had been reported. The notice does not identify the chemical or say how it may have gotten into the whipped cream.

If your can matches the affected lot and timestamp, stop using it and return it to your local Costco for a full refund. The company's notice also includes photographs of the can and its printed code to help shoppers identify the product.

For questions or concerns, contact Alamance Foods at customer.care@alamancefood.com.
