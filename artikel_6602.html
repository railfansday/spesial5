Butterball Made a Tiny Turkey That Assigns Thanksgiving Chores—And It’s Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Turkey, stuffing and a little accountability are on the menu this year.

There’s always that one Thanksgiving guest who says, “Just tell me what to do!” while somehow managing to avoid doing anything. Well, Butterball has a tiny turkey for that.

On Monday, Nov. 2, the turkey brand took to Instagram to introduce the Butterball Buddy, a tiny electronic taskmaster that hands out Thanksgiving chores so the host doesn’t have to. It looks like something straight out of the ’90s, and the best part? It won’t cost you a dime.

As silly as a chore-assigning turkey sounds, the company might be onto something. Its 2026 Togetherness Report found that 85% of Thanksgiving hosts would prefer guests to offer to help, but only 38% ask. Nearly half say the final cleanup is where they could use an extra hand.

Once everyone arrives, guests pass the Buddy around like a hot potato. When it chimes and vibrates, whoever’s holding it gets a task, whether that’s setting the table before dinner or tackling the dishes afterward. Finish the job, pass it along, and it’s the next lucky helper’s turn. Think Tamagotchi, except instead of keeping a digital pet alive, you’re keeping the kitchen from turning into a disaster area.

Set an alarm. The first batch dropped Nov. 2, and two more are coming at 1 p.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 9, and Monday, Nov. 16, at ButterballBuddy.com. Quantities are limited, so the early birds get the turkey. The giveaway is open to legal U.S. residents ages 18 and older, and no purchase is necessary.

The Buddies may be in short supply, but Butterball’s cooking advice isn’t. Its Turkey Talk-Line is celebrating its 45th anniversary this year, with more than 50 experts answering questions about everything from thawing to checking for doneness through Dec. 24. You can call 1-800-BUTTERBALL, text 844-877-3456 or chat live at Butterball.com. The hotline’s contact information is printed on the back of every Buddy, too.
