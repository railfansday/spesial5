LUX Infusion: Director of Referral Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

At LUX Infusion, we’re reimagining infusion care to be more human, supportive, and connected. Inspired by lux—meaning light - we guide patients, providers, and partners through complex therapies with clarity, compassion, and confidence.

As a clinician-led, U.S.-based organization, we put people first - helping patients feel seen and supported through faster therapy starts, dedicated care coordination, and meaningful collaboration. Our commitment to inclusion, diversity, equity, and advancement (IDEA) is central to our culture, ensuring every team member feels valued and empowered to make a difference.

We’re looking for an Associate Director of Referral Services who wants their work to mean something—someone who brings care, accountability, and purpose to how referrals become treatments and how systems become services.

The Associate Director of Referral Services plays a critical role in delivering a patient‑first infusion experience by ensuring referrals move efficiently, accurately, and compassionately from intake to care delivery.

The Associate Director of Referral Services will be responsible for the oversight and management of the front end referral process including, Benefit Verification and PA processes for LUX Infusion Neurology, Immunology, Transplant and Specialty Infusion areas. The Associate Director will understand the specific BV and PA roles and support the day-to-day department activities. This position will work closely and collaboratively with other internal department leads, including Intake Coordination CRT, Nursing Services and Pharmacy Services.

In this role, you’ll contribute to work that:

Every referral represents a real person navigating a complex healthcare moment—and your leadership helps that journey begin with confidence and trust.
