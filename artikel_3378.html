DC Officially Releases First Image Of Batman's New Child

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

DC has revealed the first official image of Batman's new child, following the reveal that Catwoman is pregnant. The image has sparked debate among fans, raising more questions than answers.

The recent Absolute Batman #25 - from Scott Snyder, Nick Dragotta, Frank Martin and Tom Napolitano - ended with a pregnant Selina Kyle walking back into the Dark Knight's life, simply saying, "Bruce..."

Now, DC has revealed the cover for Absolute Batman #27, showing Bruce and Selina cooing over what appears to be their kid. The image has fans asking which character (if any) the Absolute Universe intends to adapt as Bruce's new child.

The cover - by Nick Dragotta and Frank Martin - was shared on social media, with the caption "Who says the Absolute Universe can't be cute?" The image shows a baby playing with Batman's cowl and cape while Bruce and Selina stand reflected in Catwoman's mirrored helmet.

Behind the baby is a repeated 'HAHA' motif suggesting the Joker/Jack Grimm's involvement, with the arc title 'THE DEMON CHILD' appearing alongside.

In Absolute canon, Bruce and Selina are childhood friends who grew up together, with a young Bruce Wayne working for gang lord Falcone at one point due to his threats on Selina's life. As adults, Bruce became a vigilante and Selina became a professional thief. The two recently met up, apparently conceiving a child during the period when Batman was fighting Bane.

Underneath DC Comic's Instagram post, fans have been debating the identity of the child. While some think it's Damian Wayne (Bruce's mainstream son with Talia al Ghul) others argue it's Helena Wayne, Bruce's alt-universe daughter with Catwoman, who grows up to become the vigilante Huntress.

The title 'The Demon Child' suggests it's Damian, with the 'al Ghul' family name literally meaning 'the demon.' However, Snyder previously said he wouldn't be adapting Damian, and the Absolute Universe - especially Absolute Batman - is built on wrongfooting fans. This is a world where Bruce is close friends with the Penguin and Two-Face and is hunted through the streets by his various Robins, so anything is possible.

The laughter on the cover image is a good reason for fans to be fearful. In this world, Joker is an immortal billionaire who carries out wide-ranging superhuman experiments, creating monsters including Mr. Freeze, Poison Ivy, Man-Bat and more. There are a LOT of ways Joker could be messing with Bruce. For instance, Absolute Clayface is a shapeshifter who has copied his genetic code - this may not even be Selina.

It's also worth noting that Absolute Scarecrow's introduction introduced a byzantine maze of bluffs and plans manipulating both Bruce and his family, including faking the survival of his father Thomas Wayne. Scarecrow would and could fake a Wayne baby, and while Selina doesn't have any direct ties to Joker, she's a professional criminal in a world where he wields immense influence.

Fans will find out the truth as Absolute Batman's 'The Demon Child' unfolds, but given everything Bruce has been through in the series so far, it's likely to be a bumpy, twisted ride.

Absolute Batman #25 is available now from DC Comics.
