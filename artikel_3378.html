Guy Ritchie’s $50 Million James Bond Replacement Officially Begins Streaming Conquest

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Guy Ritchie has been booked and busy in 2026 with several new releases, most of which have been hits among both critics and audiences. Much earlier in the year, Ritchie returned to Prime Video for the Sherlock Holmes prequel series, Young Sherlock, which was renewed for Season 2 after spending months in the global top 10.

It wasn’t until the theatrical launch of In The Grey (co-starring Henry Cavill and Jake Gyllenhaal) that Ritchie stumbled this year, but despite a woeful performance at the box office, the film has gone on to become one of the most-watched titles of the entire year on Starz. Later in the year, Ritchie also found success with gritty crime thriller streaming shows like The Gentlemen (starring Theo James) and MobLand (starring Tom Hardy), both of which have been renewed for new seasons.

Guy Ritchie is one of the most famous action directors in the world, and along with that prowess comes the ability to work with some of the most famous movie stars. Over the years, one of Ritchie’s most frequent collaborators has been Jason Statham — the two have worked together on five movies to date, and they’re set to reunite for a few projects coming to the big screen and streaming in the next few years.

Most recently, they teamed up for Operation Fortune: Ruse de Guerre, the James Bond-style spy thriller starring Statham as Orson Fortune, a high-level operative who recruits a movie star to help him stop the sale of dangerous weapons technology. Operation Fortune was recently removed from streaming in America, but the film is charting a comeback on VOD, where fans are renting it on platforms like Prime Video and Apple TV.

Guy Ritchie’s next movie, Wife and Dog, is coming to theaters on February 19, 2027. The film features a star-studded ensemble led by James Norton, Paddy Considine, Rosamund Pike, Anthony Hopkins, and Benedict Cumberbatch. Ritchie is also reuniting with Jason Statham for a new action thriller Viva La Madness, which is set for release in theaters less than one year later, on January 21, 2028. He and Statham are also teaming up for another action thriller, Dogfight, which will be released on Netflix at a currently unspecified date.

Check out Operation Fortune: Ruse de Guerre on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Guy Ritchie’s future projects.
