Elon Musk Owes South Africa Nothing

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

South African President Cyril Ramaphosa (left) and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk

Last week, South African President Cyril Ramaphosa arrived in Silicon Valley seeking investment. He also had a message for Elon Musk: Remember your South African roots and “give back.”

It is an extraordinary appeal from the leader of a country crippled by unemployment, corruption, and economic stagnation. His African National Congress bears most of the responsibility. Yet Ramaphosa came to America, begging bowl in hand, asking successful entrepreneurs to help repair the damage. His government, meanwhile, obstructs one of the most obvious ways Musk could help — allowing Starlink to operate in South Africa.
