Former judge accused of shooting gun toward unwanted trick-or-treaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Oklahoma Judge Jerry Herberger was arrested recently after allegedly shooting a gun toward unwanted trick-or-treaters. (Photo from the Stephens County Sheriff’s Office)

A former Oklahoma judge was arrested recently after allegedly shooting a gun toward unwanted trick-or-treaters who came near his home on Halloween.

Former Judge Jerry Herberger, 73, of Duncan, Oklahoma, was booked in the Stephens County Jail in Oklahoma for assault and battery with a deadly weapon, attempted murder and possession of a firearm while intoxicated, the Lawton Constitution reports.

Police were called Saturday night after witnesses said an intoxicated Herberger had threatened trick-or-treaters if they came to his home. With a gun in his hand, he allegedly fired off a round in the children’s direction, according to the Lawton Constitution.

As officers prepared to make contact at the home, Herberger allegedly pointed a gun at one of the officers, said Loisdawn Jones, the director of communications for the city of Duncan, Oklahoma. He then complied with officers’ commands and exited the residence, where he was taken into custody and booked into the Stephens County Jail.
