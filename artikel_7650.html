Miramar-Based Government Contractor Charged in Aviation Parts Fraud Scheme Involving Forged FAA Certificates

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A federal grand jury in the Southern District of Florida has returned an indictment charging a Miramar man with operating a multi‑year aviation‑parts fraud and investor scheme involving forged Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) certifications, fabricated military purchase orders, and more than $1 million in victim losses.
