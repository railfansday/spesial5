Crazy Maple Studio: AI-Assisted Story Writer (1099 Contract)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Crazy Maple Studio is a fast-growing company based in the Bay Area and Los Angeles, focusing on mobile gaming, reading, and streaming. We are proud to have a global team that drives our innovation and success across diverse markets.

Our flagship platform, ReelShort, is the #1 vertical streaming app in the U.S., with over 8 million DAU, delivering bite-sized episodic content that keeps viewers hooked. Other standout products include Chapters, where users shape immersive stories across drama, fantasy, and romance, and My Fiction, a platform for consuming and creating serialized romance.

We’re proud to be recognized as one of the Global Most Loved Workplaces 2025, reflecting our commitment to innovation, creativity, and an employee-first culture.Position Overview:

As an AI-Assisted Story Writer, you will create short, high-hook, pay-per-chapter stories for mobile readers, working hand in hand with external AI and our in-house AI story system. Our system guides every story through a proven pipeline; your job is to steer that process with strong editorial judgment, push every story past "good enough," and turn out titles that readers pay to finish.

This is an ROI-driven role. Every story you deliver goes into live testing, and its results (ad performance, reader conversion past the paywall, and return on ad spend) are the main measure of your success.

The ideal candidate is a commercial storyteller first: someone who instinctively understands hooks, cliffhangers, revenge payoffs, romance tension, and why a reader taps "unlock" on the next chapter. You are comfortable using AI as a creative partner, you know when to trust it and when to override it, and you are energized rather than intimidated by data.

Compensation: $3,000. This is an independent contractor (1099) position with an initial one-month term.
