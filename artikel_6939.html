The Horse Girl Trend Is Here for Your Jewelry Box

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Horseshoes and tail motifs are dominating charms and rings.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Saddles, stirrups, and horseshoes, oh my: We’ve been firmly in the year of the Fire Horse since February 2026, and the jewelry scene is officially in its horse girl era. Aside from the look being a clear classic in the world of jewelry (horseshoes were popular in Victorian-era jewels), today’s examples vary from sparkly creations to more minimal, toned-down pieces with everyday appeal. No matter the aesthetic, the jewelry of the Year of the Fire Horse is strong: This era only comes around every 60 years in the Chinese zodiac and is said to represent strength, independence, and fearlessness.

Celebrities are clearly fans: Taylor Swift just wore two double-layered horseshoe pendants to announce her Grammy nomination. Horse-ified jewelry also spanned the runway. For spring/summer 2027, both Gucci and Celine revisited the archival horsebit hardware detail and reworked it in a contemporary way to highlight the beautiful lines and sculptural symmetry of the timeless motif. Elsewhere, Chloé’s statement belt covered in huge running horses has been a fashion girl favorite and is the equivalent of jewelry for your waist.

There is perhaps nothing more classic than a chunky horseshoe ring or a horse-themed charm. Verragio and Oscar Heyman each reworked the symbol in gemstone-studded bracelets and double-motif rings. Elsewhere, Lionheart makes a slew of gloriously stunning oversize horse-head pendants and brooches brimming with diamonds and, sometimes, accessorized with mother-of-pearl saddles. The brand also partners directly with 13 Hands Equine Rescue, a nonprofit sanctuary located in Clinton Corners, New York.

But this year, equestrian jewelry is not horsing around when it comes to experimenting. One such example is Hermès’s latest collection, which has an obvious horse connection: saddles, diamond-decked horse heads, and golden saddles. Titled “Into the Horsescape,” the 90-piece lineup is quite literally the horse girl’s dream: lassos in the form of massive baguette-cut diamonds and glimmering oversize horsebit bracelets replete with pavé brown and white diamonds. Pomellato also showcased a carved horse cameo surrounded by brown diamonds and suspended from a delicate rose-gold Dressage-link chain.

If you want to lean into your inner horse girl in a more subtle, everyday way, Gucci’s iconic horsebit rings, refined drop earrings, and sleek bangles offer a sophisticated and versatile place to start your collection. Going vintage is another option: Equestrian themes have been reinterpreted in the jewelry space for hundreds of years now, so there’s ample opportunity. One thing is clear: Horse girl jewelry is galloping from the archives to the runway and into everyday style.
