Knack: Cloud Engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Location: US Based (Full-Time, Remote) or International Contractor (Full-Time, Remote)

If US-Based:This will be a remote position based in the United States. Knack offers a complete benefits package to those working in the United States. Employees must be available during standard US business hours and maintain their primary work location within the US. Any extended time working outside the United States requires prior written approval.

International Contractor:This is a full-time, remote position, but candidates must have four hours overlap with Eastern timezone.

Knack runs thousands of customer applications across multiple global regions, including dedicated private environments. As a Cloud Engineer, you’ll own and evolve that infrastructure, harden it to meet SOC 2, HIPAA, and customer security requirements, and build the infrastructure behind our growing AI capabilities.

We’re an AI-forward team. You don’t need to type every Terraform block, but you do need to understand what’s happening, direct AI tools well, verify their work, and own the outcome. This role includes a weekly 24/7 on-call rotation.

Knack is an AI-powered no-code software development platform enabling organizations to manage, analyze, and share business data. The no-code market is growing at over 30% annually and projected to be almost $200B by 2030. Founded in 2010, Knack has grown into a leading player in the no-code movement, enabling businesses, teams, and individuals to build custom applications without writing a single line of code.

Serving a wide range of industries, from healthcare and education to finance and nonprofits, and organizations from small businesses to Fortune 500s, Knack enables users to boost data visibility and streamline operations.

To date, the platform has powered 400,000 apps and stored more than 5 billion data records for over 15,000 customers. Its enterprise-grade functionality includes an AI-powered visual builder, robust data structures, user access controls, and automation capabilities, all delivered by a fully remote team committed to trust, continuous improvement, and measurable impact.

Run AWS infrastructure as code, with AI in the loop

Implement security controls with our security lead

Lead OS upgrades, runtime and end-of-life migrations, dependency updates, and certificate renewals.

Operate and scale databases and customer environments

Resolve escalations and automate repeat work

Participate in our weekly 24/7 on-call rotation, responding to production alerts and following up with fixes or runbook updates so the same issue does not repeat.

Daily, practical use of AI tools like Claude, with the judgment to critically review AI-generated scripts, Terraform, and IAM policies, and accountability for everything you ship.

Interviews will be recorded and transcribed by an AI notetaking tool so our team can focus on the conversation and review it accurately later. Notes are used only for evaluating your application. You can opt out at any time by letting your recruiter know.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
