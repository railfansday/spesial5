NEAR Token Recovers to $3.26 as Governance Finalizes 1.6% Inflation Cut

House of Stake ratified HSP-029 on Nov. 16, and the proposal lowers the maximum annual issuance of NEAR Protocol from 2.5% to 1.6%. On Tuesday, the price of NEAR dipped to $3.12 in the morning and then went back up to $3.26.

HSP-029 was written by Sal Ternullo, CEO of SVRN, and opened for forum discussion on Oct. 7. The vote closed on Nov. 2, and 99.96% of the veNEAR cast was in favor of the proposal; no veNEAR was cast against it. The Security Council's 14-day window to veto the proposal closed on Nov. 16 with no veto, according to House of Stake.

This is the second inflation cut for NEAR in just over a year. In October 2025, the maximum issuance was halved from the original 5% to 2.5%, and in September 2026, the realized issuance stood at 2.45%, according to HSP-029.

At 1.6%, NEAR will have "one of the lowest annual issuance rates among major blockchains," House of Stake said.

According to House of Stake, the reduction will start one quarter after ratification, and validators still have to adopt the protocol upgrade. The issuance cap will reach 1.6% after 24 months.

Ternullo estimated in HSP-029 that NEAR will avoid issuing 11.8 million tokens over two years. At the current price, these tokens would be worth around $38.5 million.

The proposal also calculated that the staking yield of NEAR will go down from around 5.27% to around 3.37% if the staking ratio stays the same. More than half of all NEAR is not staked, according to House of Stake.

BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes recently named NEAR among four tokens that he expects to rise before the end of the AI bubble. Hayes set targets of $6 for NEAR, $2,800 for ETH, $1,800 for ZEC, and $40 for VVV.

"The size is small to medium as conviction is meh, but if the music is playing, you gotta dance," Hayes wrote on X.

The Four Horsemen shall pump and then usher in the end of the AI bubble: $ETH = $2800 $ZEC = $1800 $NEAR = $6.00 $VVV = $40 The size is small to medium as conviction is meh, but if the music playing you gotta dance.

Total volume through NEAR Intents, the cross-chain liquidity layer of the protocol, has reached $39 billion, and the service was recently integrated into Talos OEMS, an institutional trading platform.
