Ethereum Bulls on Edge as Peter Brandt Warns of Sub-$500 Crash If ETH Loses This Crucial Support

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Ethereum bulls face a frightening downside scenario after legendary trader and classical chart analyst Peter Brandt issued a fresh warning that the second-largest cryptocurrency could collapse below $500. His bearish outlook has raised fresh concerns about ETH’s ability to withstand mounting selling pressure, particularly as market uncertainty continues to weigh on investor sentiment.

In a November 24 post on X, Peter Brandt pointed to Ethereum’s deteriorating weekly chart structure, suggesting that ETH is approaching a critical point within a multi-year consolidation pattern.

The weekly chart above shows a large symmetrical triangle that has formed over several market cycles. Its upper boundary is defined by a descending resistance line stretching from Ethereum’s November 2021 peak near $4,800, while the lower boundary consists of an ascending support line originating around the 2022 bear-market bottom.

Together, these converging trendlines have gradually narrowed Ethereum’s trading range, setting the stage for a potentially decisive breakout or breakdown.

ETH briefly broke above the triangle’s upper resistance during its 2025 rally, eventually reaching a new all-time high near $4,954 in August. However, the bullish breakout failed to produce a sustained upward move, with the cryptocurrency subsequently reversing direction.

The resulting decline has produced a series of lower highs and renewed selling pressure throughout 2026, bringing ETH increasingly close to the triangle’s lower boundary.

Brandt’s bearish outlook hinges on Ethereum losing its long-standing ascending support trendline, a development that could undermine a chart structure that has supported several significant recovery attempts since 2022.

Using $1,648 as the current reference price, ETH would need to shed approximately 16.1% to revisit the critical $1,383 support level. However, the classical chartist’s more alarming outlook points to a potential plunge below $500 if the cryptocurrency fails to defend this structural floor.

A drop from $1,648 to $500 would translate into a staggering 69.7% decline. Even if ETH first falls to $1,383, it would still face another 63.8% downside before reaching $500.

This distinction is important because the sub-$500 projection represents a much deeper bearish scenario, not an automatic consequence of losing the immediate support level.

Such a collapse would also push Ethereum below its 2022 bear-market low, potentially marking a dramatic breakdown in its longer-term price structure.

For Ethereum to begin reversing its bearish momentum, buyers must first push the altcoin king back above $1,715 and sustain a recovery beyond that threshold. Reclaiming this level would signal improving demand and offer an early sign that selling pressure is easing.

The next major hurdle sits around $1,840, which coincides with ETH’s November 23 rebound high. A decisive move above this resistance would help invalidate the recent pattern of lower highs and strengthen the case for a broader recovery.

On the downside, a confirmed weekly close below $1,383—particularly if followed by an unsuccessful attempt to reclaim that level—would signal a potentially damaging breakdown of Ethereum’s multi-year ascending support structure. If that scenario unfolds, ETH could face renewed selling pressure toward the psychologically important $1,000 mark. A deeper deterioration in market conditions could then bring Brandt’s sub-$500 projection into sharper focus.

However, the bearish forecast remains conditional rather than inevitable. Ethereum has yet to lose the critical $1,383 support, leaving its ability to defend this level central to determining whether Brandt’s worst-case scenario becomes a reality.
