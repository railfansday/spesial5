Pendle has gone from seventh place to the largest DeFi venue for tokenized stocks in roughly six weeks. Its tokenized stocks TVL stands at about $53.5 million, representing approximately 18.5% of the $289.1 million market. Impressive growth, certainly. But the PENDLE price chart is telling a less comfortable story.

The platform’s rapid rise comes as tokenized stocks expand across DeFi, Pendle ranked first in tokenized stocks TVL growth last month, while the wider market grew from roughly $13 million to $305 million over a year.

Pendle’s model splits yield bearing assets into principal tokens and yield tokens. That gives users another way to trade or fix yield exposure rather than simply holding the underlying asset.

Several developments are driving activity. Yield markets for Coinbase tokenized GOOGLc and NVDAc launched on Base, while PendleLoop introduced one click leveraged longs on seven tokenized stocks through Aave markets.

STRCx on Pendle has also passed $50 million. The platform added an RWA interface with fund level disclosures, expanding its offering beyond basic token exposure.

More than three million PENDLE tokens have reportedly been bought back since sPENDLE launched. Buybacks are running at six times V2 emissions, with purchased tokens going to ‘sPENDLE’ stakers.

The daily chart shows lower highs and lower lows, signalling weakening price structure. The $1.40 area is approaching as a potential reversal point, but support alone won’t confirm a turnaround.

Pendle’s business momentum is strong, yet the PENDLE price still needs to prove it can reverse its decline. Tokenized stocks are growing, but traders shouldn’t confuse platform adoption with guaranteed token appreciation.
