Lots of Competition to Catholicism in Peru as Pope Returns for Visit

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Members of Agua Viva, a Pentecostal megachurch in Lima, Peru, at a prayer concert on Sunday.Credit...

Strobe lights scanned a crowd of outstretched arms as a rock band took the stage, smoke billowing out and applause echoing through the coliseum. The pastor bounced out to the beat of a drum roll, a microphone curved around his face.

This Sunday’s sermon, Pastor Sergio Hornung announced, would be about “the territory still to conquer.”

“There are many places where they talk to you about God,” said Scarlet Reyes, 21, a former Catholic who started coming to this Pentecostal church, Agua Viva, two years ago. “But here, they teach you how to know God.”

Pope Leo arrives on Wednesday in Peru, where he served as a priest for two decades, rose to bishop and became a Peruvian citizen. He returns at a time when the church faces two major challenges in Latin America: growing secularization and competition from other Christian denominations.

While Catholicism remains the dominant religious force in the country and the region, evangelical churches have made enormous gains, with congregants saying these churches offer more direct relationships with God and a more expressive, modern style of worship.
