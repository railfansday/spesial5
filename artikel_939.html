Mauricio Pochettino admits Man United’s ‘situation is fragile’ but insists he has a ‘strong team’

Mauricio Pochettino has insisted he has taken over a "strong team" at Manchester United, despite acknowledging that their current predicament is a precarious one.

The Argentine's tenure as head coach began disastrously on Sunday with a heavy 4-0 defeat against rivals Liverpool, leaving them second bottom of the Premier League after 11 fixtures, ahead of only Crystal Palace.

Ahead of Wednesday's Champions League meeting in Lisbon, Sporting boss Paulo Fonseca dismissed the notion that United possess a fragility, and Pochettino pointed to their high-flying finish under Michael Carrick last term as evidence they can reverse their current slump.

"The situation is fragile," Pochettino said. "We are not fragile. We are a strong team, they showed that last season when they finished third and now we are playing in the Champions League.

"Sometimes things happen in football for different reasons. We need to identify why and already we are working on it. I think we are going to turn round the situation.

"We need to go game-by-game. In the end, being at Manchester United is about winning every single game, one is not more important than another.

"We are Manchester United and we need to believe we have the capacity to turn that situation. We are building our identity and that is the most important thing."

United captain Bruno Fernandes agreed that getting back to winning ways is his main focus and only then will the Portuguese midfielder restart talks over signing a new deal.

Fernandes, who joined United from Sporting in January 2020, is into the final year of his contract, which includes an option for another 12 months, although he has turned down two offers of an extension.

Asked what conditions have to be in place to sign a new long-term contract, Fernandes replied: “For us to start winning games and then we can start speaking about contracts. It’s not important now, it’s irrelevant.

“I know it’s my future and I shouldn’t look at it that way because I haven’t got much left in my contract but I’m more worried about getting results, trying to get the club into a position we can be proud of.

“We’ll have time to speak about that. We had conversations, the conversations stopped when that was not as important because the results and performances weren’t at the level they should have been.”

United have won two and lost two in this season’s Champions League, most recently coming from 2-0 behind at Old Trafford to claim a 3-2 win over Roma earlier this month.

Fernandes has played just one knockout tie – against Atletico Madrid in 2022 – during his stint at United.

He added: “When I started my career, I never had the opportunity in the first few years to play Champions League, so that was a faraway dream for me.

“I’m very proud of what I’ve achieved, even if I have only played one time, I still have got many years left in my career, I hope, so I’m going to have many opportunities to do that and why not this season?”
