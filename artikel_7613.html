It’s OK to wear Milwaukee Brewers attire in court, judge says

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A Wisconsin county judge is allowing people to wear Milwaukee Brewers attire in county courtrooms to celebrate the team’s recent World Series victory.

Following the Milwaukee Brewers’ historic World Series victory Wednesday, a Wisconsin county judge issued an order allowing people to wear Milwaukee Brewers attire in county courtrooms through the end of the official victory parade.

Waukesha County Presiding Judge Michael P. Maxwell, who consulted first with the chief judge and the clerk of court, issued the order after the Brewers captured their first-ever World Series title in franchise history, defeating the Tampa Bay Rays on Wednesday, Fox6 News Milwaukee reports.

“While every day our courts deal with very serious issues, like child custody disputes and holding criminals accountable, the last few weeks have seen Brewer fever in many off-the-record conversations between lawyers, parties and court staff,” Maxwell wrote in a statement.

“We thought that this could be one small way we could recognize this historic achievement by the Milwaukee Brewers organization and let folks in the court system participate in celebrating with all Wisconsin,” he added.
