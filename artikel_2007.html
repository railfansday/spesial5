Mile Oak have afternoon to forget at Hailsham

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Oak had the best of the early exchanges and were assisted by an early visit to the sin bin for Toby Brett but failed to provide any serious threat to the Stringers goalkeeper.

With Harry Robins suspended it was hoped new signing Sam Geard would provide some creativity but he was forced off through injury before a quarter of the game had passed.

The only threat to the Oak defence was the height of the Hailsham players and just before half-time they came unstuck when a long throw was launched into the box which enabled Michael Lloyd to force the ball in.

The second half saw little improvement in what was becoming a bad tempered game with players swarming around young referee Elliott Brown as a number of fouls were committed by both sides.

Oak did manage to manufacture one chance when Liam Smart headed in but was ruled offside. As the half wore on the Stringers looked the most likely to score as Mile Oak faded away like the autumn leaves.

A disappointed Sam Cornish said: “Unfortunately, it was a really frustrating afternoon. For a neutral it must have been a terrible watch on a lovely 3G surface – neither side looked like they wanted to get the ball down and play.

"We started with a different shape, which was impacted early due to the loss of Geardy, and we never really got any momentum from there on.

"The effort and work-rate was still there from our lads, but the game as a whole lacked any real quality throughout. Having said that, we also didn’t get anything go our way all afternoon & scored a perfectly good goal that was chalked off.

"It’s important we don’t dwell on the what ifs dust ourselves off and go again. Its points dropped, another defeat on the road and we can’t hide from the fact that ultimately our away form is not good enough and is something we must look to rectify quickly.”

Spirited Yapton were generally second-best, but had their moments, especially in the first half.

They trailed in the third minute when a Bosham corner to the far post was neatly headed home. Then Yapton's best efforts were shots from Aaron Tague and Josh Dean that were respectively well saved and scrambled off the line.

Early in the second period Brandon Terry equalised with a superb cross-shot but from that point Bosham took over with some impressive approach work and wrapped it up with goals in the 70th and 85th minutes.
