Aston Villa's Ibrahim Mbaye is miles from being the finished article, Unai Emery should be worried

Ibrahim Mbaye has shown some promising signs since Aston Villa signed him on a £47million deal from PSG in the summer.

The 18-year-old winger already has four assists in 12 games, two of which have come in the Champions League against Fenerbahce and Viking.

Supporters, however, are still waiting for him to properly explode at Villa Park, having to come off the bench for the last three matches.

Unai Emery has preferred to trust Alysson, 20, in Mbaye's position, who had one of his best games so far in the 4-4 draw against Manchester United.

Players are now away on international duty, and Mbaye has been given plenty of minutes in Senegal's AFCON qualification games against Sudan.

He had found the back of the net on Thursday, 12 November, but his most recent performance was far more disappointing on Monday, 16 November.

Senegal claimed a 4-2 win against Sudan, with his teammate Nicolas Jackson opening the scoring after just four minutes.

Mbaye played the entire game, completing 83 per cent of his passes, creating one chance, and he had the most touches in the opposition box [FotMob].

In such an end-to-end game, though, Mbaye would have expected to have a bigger impact. Instead, he really struggled in both halves of the pitch.

He failed to complete any of his five dribbles, won just 29 per cent of his ground duels, as well as losing possession 15 times [Sofascore].

Coming up against the 118th-ranked team in the world, Mbaye should be disappointed by how he performed ahead of returning to Villa Park.

Mbaye has only started four of Villa's first 12 games of the 2026-27 campaign, being forced to prove himself off the bench for the most part.

Back in September, the deal with PSG was already being questioned by Bryan King, who wondering why the Ligue 1 giants were open to letting him go in the first place.

Last season, he had five goal contributions in 29 games for PSG, and while he looks set to beat those numbers, he still has a while to go.

Emery clearly isn't convinced by him yet, and Mbaye won't be thrown into the lineup anytime soon if the manager was watching his most recent game.
