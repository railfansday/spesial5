Sources: Chelsea in dressing room mutiny as players rail against Xabi Alonso methods at training ground

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso is facing a mutiny in his squad after his team's heavy 4-1 defeat at home to Leeds United on Saturday, sources have told Football Insider.

The Blues' players are understood to be responding poorly to his methods at their Cobham training ground amid growing discontent behind the scenes.

Sources close to Chelsea players have outlined that there is a “terrible atmosphere in the dressing room,” while the coaching staff feel that the players “packed it in” during the defeat to Leeds.

Alonso has only been in the job for a matter of months, but is already understood to be facing significant pressure on his position.

The club hierarchy are growing increasingly frustrated over the lack of consistency from Alonso's team, as they now sit eighth in the Premier League table.

Saturday's defeat to Leeds means Chelsea are now on a run of just one win in their last five games in all competitions amid new scrutiny on the manager.

Chelsea took the lead through Emanuel Emegha against Leeds, but things quickly unravelled for them in the second half.

Goals from Dominic Calvert-Lewin, Noah Okafor and Brenden Aaronson added to Okafor's first-half strike to inflict a damaging home defeat on the Blues.

Alonso's squad became the first Chelsea side to lose at home to Leeds since 1999, with the pair major rivals due to historical events.

The discontent around the club also spread into the fans on Saturday, with a section of supporters, with a section of fans near the dugout making their feelings known over the boss' decisions.

Fans chanted "You don't know what you're doing" at the Spanish chief, while his team were also loudly booed at the full-time whistle.

The Chelsea fanbase have already turned on Alonso, and the board may soon follow suit, despite handing him a four-year deal in the summer.

Football Insider revealed on Sunday that Chelsea are currently leading the race to potentially appoint Como boss Cesc Fabregas as their next manager.

The 39-year-old led the Serie A side into the Champions League for the first time in their history last term, and already has them fighting towards the top of the table again this season.

Sources say that the likes of Arsenal and Barcelona are also tracking the Spaniard closely, but it is Chelsea who are at the forefront right now.

With Alonso's future looking bleaker by the day, it may only be a matter of time before the Chelsea hierarchy turn their attention to Fabregas.
