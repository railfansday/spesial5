Wael Mohya has quickly become one of the most exciting young talents in world football.

The teenager recently made his debut for Jurgen Klopp's German national team and has become more influential at Borussia Monchengladbach as the season has gone on.

Liverpool have been repeatedly linked with the teenager in recent weeks, and now Brighton are reportedly ready to challenge the Reds for the 17-year-old's signature.

That's according to a report from TEAMtalk, which states that both Iraola's side and the Seagulls are closely monitoring the attackers progress despite Monchengladbach's insistence that a mid-season sale will not be sanctioned.

The report claims that the transfer fee required to sign the new German international could rise to as high as €45 million (~£38m), a fee which either side would likely be happy to pay for such a talented prospect.

Brighton sit one point ahead of Liverpool in second place in the Premier League this season, and Champions League qualification is a real possibility for Fabian Hurzeler's side.

Both clubs have excellent track records of introducing young talent to their first-team playing squads, and it will likely be a close fought battle between the two clubs for Mohya's signature.

A summer deal seems the most likely for the 17-year-old, but Liverpool should start laying the groundwork now to get ahead of Brighton.
