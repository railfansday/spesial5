Honduran Illegal Alien Charged With Illegal Re-Entry of Removed Alien

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA, WILMER ALEXANDER DELCID-BAIRES, a/k/a “Wilmer Delcid,” a/k/a “Wilmer Rivera Medina,” a/k/a “Wilmer Alexander Delcid Baires,” a/k/a “Wilmer Alexander Delcidbaires,” a/k/a “Wilmer Alexander Delsid,” a/k/a “Wilmer Riveramedina,” a/k/a Wilmer Alexander Delsidbalres,” a/k/a “Wilmer Baires,” age 43, a native of Honduras, was charged on November 13, 2026, in a one-count indictment with illegal re-entry of a removed alien, in violation of Title 8, United States Code, Section 1326(a), announced U.S. Attorney David I. Courcelle.
