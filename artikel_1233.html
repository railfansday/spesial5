Edvin Austbo is Quickly Becoming Another West Ham Flop Like Taty Castellanos

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

West Ham United's frustrating season continued as they were held to a 1-1 draw by Preston North End at the London Stadium on Saturday.

Having won just six of their opening 15 league matches this term prior to the clash against the Lilywhites, West Ham and new manager Scott Parker knew that even three points wouldn't be enough to take them into the playoff positions.

However, even though they were playing the bottom club at home, they had to settle for a point despite dominating proceedings, leaving them in 10th position and three points adrift of the top eight.

The Irons face two in-form teams in Portsmouth and Stoke City next, as Parker looks to pick up his first win since replacing Nuno Espirito Santo in the dugout, having so far taken two points from nine available.

There is no doubt that West Ham have the squad to run away with the Championship title, but it simply hasn't happened for them yet.

The east London outfit can't be criticised too much for their final third exploits given they have scored 38 goals in their 16 Championship fixtures this term, but their record of 24 goals conceded is more than anyone else in the top 10, and higher than Portsmouth and Wrexham below them.

Teams West Ham Have Conceded First to This Season

West Ham are continuously giving themselves a lot to do to win games by conceding the first goal, and it is a habit they need to break if they are to move back up to the table.

Despite having experienced players like Konstantinos Mavropanos, Max Kilman and Kyle Walker-Peters at the back, the Irons are struggling from a defensive point of view, and it is seeing fans turn on them.

While West Ham have been largely prolific going the other way, there have been some disappointing players in attack too.

While Jarrod Bowen continues to be a talisman for West Ham in England's second tier, the same cannot be said for others like Taty Castellanos and Edvin Austbo.

West Ham raised eyebrows when they paid more than £25m to sign the former in the January transfer window, with the Argentine saying he would give everything in a claret and blue shirt.

While the 28-year-old was unable to keep the Irons in the Premier League, despite scoring six goals in 18 appearances, he did impress the London Stadium faithful.

Having remained at the club over the summer, the expectation was that he'd be a big hit in the Championship, but instead he has become a big transfer flop.

Castellanos has one goal in 15 league appearances, with that coming in the 6-0 thrashing of Wrexham back in September.

The South American striker reportedly wants to leave the club, which suggests he feels he is too good for the Championship, even though he is currently stinking the place out, and fans are beginning to turn on him.

Expectations were also high that summer signing Austbo could prove to be an exciting addition in the second tier this term.

West Ham agreed a fee of around €13m, which is a big fee for the second tier, to sign the 21-year-old, who helped Viking FK win their first Norwegian league title in 34 years in 2025.

Austbo is known for his pace and intelligence on the pitch, but his impact has been lacking so far in the claret and blue.

He has two goals and two assists for the Irons so far this term, but you would probably expect his goal contribution numbers to be higher in a team which has scored 38 league goals.

Austbo has started Parker's previous two matches in charge, having come off the bench in his first game against Cardiff, but he has been taken off before the hour-mark in both.

The Norwegian attacker was guilty of losing the ball in a key area which led to Preston's opener on Saturday and, after failing to create a single chance, was handed a 3/10 rating for his display.

As things stand, Austbo is quickly becoming a big-money transfer flop, and he needs to improve quickly to start repaying his fee.
