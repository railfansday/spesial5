The Best Stocking Stuffers Are Small but Special—Here Are 34 Ideas We Love

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

When we’re shopping for the holidays, we start by thinking about what’s under the tree. But sometimes the coolest gifts are the ones that end up in your stocking. Just because they’re small doesn’t mean they’re not special. That’s why our shopping team set out to find the best stocking stuffer ideas, from the pretty to the practical (and everything in between).

Some families may choose to drop in small gifts every day leading up to Christmas, and some may just hang up stockings as part of their holiday decor—but not fill them up. Regardless of your approach, everyone knows the best stocking stuffers should be small, thoughtful, useful, and not overly expensive. Think of a candle snuffer, a refreshing room mist, or a bottle stopper that keeps their favorite bottle of wine fresh.

We always recommend personalizing each stocking with thoughtful touches tailored to the recipient. Consider what they love to collect, how they spend their free time, or something they’ve mentioned wanting in recent conversations. For homebodies, think cozy socks or candle holders. The BookTok obsessive in your life will love a set of bookmarks with iconic Frank Lloyd Wright designs. If they’re foodies, surprise them with a sardine-themed mouse pad or eye mask set. When it comes to stocking stuffers, the options are endless—and we’ve rounded up over 30 brilliant ideas to help you get started.

The bookworm in your life will be so thankful for this gift. The set of magnetic bookmarks keeps their spot after late-night reading, without risk of falling out. And if they have a design eye, they’ll love the geometric motifs from Frank Lloyd Wright.

Nothing’s quite as awkward as forgetting an anniversary or birthday. This mini journal will save you from that cringe. It goes day-by-day throughout the year, so you can note all of the meaningful dates in your calendar.

A good candle snuffer is not a want, but a need. It’s so much neater than dipping the wick or just blowing it out. And this adorable one is the (literal) cherry on top. We recommend pairing it with a gourmand-heavy candle.

Don’t worry, these eye masks from the tinned fish brand Fishwife don’t smell like sardines. Instead, they’re just shaped like adorable fish. They’re reusable, so you can make your everyday eye serum feel like a hands-free spa treatment.

Anyone who needs a boost of assurance and support this holiday season will appreciate this mirror decal. It’s designed to guide you through anxious moments, and it’s repositionable. So you can see it on your bathroom mirror before bed, or carry the reminder with you on your water bottle.

We know a high-quality olive oil can make a great hostess gift, but what if it ended up in your stocking, too? This 1.7-ounce bottle of extra virgin olive oil was harvested from Flamingo Estate’s unique olive trees, and it’s just tiny enough for easy travel.

This is way more chic than the matchbooks they pick up at restaurants. You can choose between three engraving/color combos, and it’s the ideal coffee table accessory. This metallic matchbox also comes with a tassel for extra elegance.

Can one ever have too many trinket dishes? We don’t think so. This one is heartfelt enough for a partner or loved one you want to treasure, and its design is reflective of the English transferware every antique lover is hunting for.

Level up your next bottle of Cabernet with this aerator and pourer insert. Simply insert this into the bottle opening, and it’ll unlock the fullness of the wine, exposing your giftee to new flavors and ensuring a neat pour.

Or, if you prefer your drinks bubbly, this glass bottle stopper will keep your prosecco crisp until you’re ready to polish off the bottle. It comes in burnt orange ombré that you’ll reach for again and again.

The New Savant is a favorite candle brand of Associate Shopping Editor Janae McKenzie, and their team just launched a line of room mists. “Architect’s Daughter smells like the most refreshing library—grapefruit, wood, and eraser shavings that never fail to put me in a creative mood,” McKenzie says.

A striking votive holder can make even the most basic tea light candles feel extra special. This mini one from Jonathan Adler is covered in his sculptural Muse D’Or faces, with a gold interior that’ll catch the flame beautifully.
