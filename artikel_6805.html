Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce Re-Created One of Their First Public Moments After the Chiefs’ Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce made a rare move following the Kansas City Chiefs’ win over the New York Jets yesterday. The newlyweds left Arrowhead Stadium together, re-creating one of their first public moments as a couple by driving off in a vintage Cadillac Coupe DeVille. Onlookers filmed the happy couple waving to the crowd as they departed.

When Swift attended her first-ever Chiefs game in September 2023, the pair did the same thing, leaving in Kelce’s 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle 408 Stroker convertible. They seem to have a thing for the classic cars, and even raffled off a 1970 Chevelle at their wedding, an apparent homage to the 2023 moment.

The singer attended Kelce’s game against the Jets with her parents and her friend Karlie Kloss. It marked Swift’s first appearance in years with Kloss, who also attended the singer’s wedding in July.

Swift usually leaves Arrowhead Stadium undetected. Back in October 2025, she told BBC Radio 2’s Greg James that her greatest skill as a spy would be “entering and exiting the building without being seen. Just put me in a garbage can, roll me, I don’t care. Honestly, I can fit in like, a purse. Sometimes I just can’t deal with it [the paparazzi and attention] and in those times, I won’t deal with it. I’m just digging tunnels under every building I go into, airlifting in through the skylight.”

In January 2025, Parade spoke with Ty Richmond, president of event services at Allied Universal, who detailed how intricate plans can be to get VIPs in and out of football games. “When you have a very high-profile individual [coming to an event], the logistics of that person getting into the venue and getting into the specific location where they need to sit is a job and a process in itself,” he said, adding that for someone at Swift’s level of fame, thousands of security professionals could be involved in days of preparation.

“If, for instance, there are fans that are known to be targeting [a star] and/or have been a threat in the past because they’re fixated on a talent or a personality, those are all very specialized, very confidential issues that have to be understood and mitigated properly,” he added.
