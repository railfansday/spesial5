Texas Teacher Aide Arrested for Allegedly Sexually Assaulting Student

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A charter school teacher aide in San Antonio, Texas, was arrested after she was accused of sexually assaulting a student.

Charlyann Silva Guerra was arrested on Wednesday, November 18, charged with one count of sexual assault of child and one count of improper relationship between educator and student, according to Bexar County jail record viewed by Us. Silva Guerra, 26, is currently held on a combined $150,000 bond.

The San Antonio Police Department learned of the allegations against Silva Guerra on Tuesday, November 17, when the school contacted authorities regarding an allegedly improper relationship between Silva Guerra and a female student, according to an arrest affidavit obtained by KSAT.

The affidavit confirmed that Silva Guerra worked at a school, though it did not list the school’s name. However, a School of Science and Technology (SST) Hill Country spokesperson has since confirmed to the outlet that Silva Guerra had been employed with the school since August.

Officers arrived at the school after they received the report, and the student’s mother told them that she found alleged Snapchat messages between her daughter and the teacher aide that were “sexual in nature.”

Silva Guerra also worked as the girl’s basketball coach, according to the affidavit.

The victim’s mother told officers that she also found her daughter and Silva Guerra talking over FaceTime in the middle of the night, which made her suspicious of their relationship.

Officers later interviewed the teen, who allegedly admitted that she and Silva Guerra had engaged in intimate acts just five days before the teacher’s aide was arrested.

Once officers gathered sufficient evidence, Silva Guerra was arrested on the two charges.

Anna Ruiz, marketing and communications coordinator for the school, told KSAT that the district immediately took action once they learned of the allegations.

“The district terminated the employee’s contract and promptly contacted law enforcement officials to ensure the matter was properly addressed and investigated,” Ruiz said. “The safety, well-being, and protection of our students remain our highest priorities. We take all reports of potential misconduct seriously and are committed to cooperating with the appropriate authorities.”

It’s not currently clear if Silva Guerra has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following her arrest. The San Antonio Police Department had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.
