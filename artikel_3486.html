Mormon Wives: OC's Bobbi Althoff Defends Breast-Feeding Her Kids Until Age 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Bobbi Althoff has a message for mom-shamers judging her decision to breast-feed her children past toddlerhood.

“I breastfed each of my kids until they were 4,” Bobbi, 29, shared during her November 17 appearance on the “Off the Vine” podcast. “It was a lot and my boobs suffered.”

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star defended her choice, telling any haters to back off because nursing is completely natural.

“If you’re a person out there who thinks it’s weird to breast-feed a child after they have teeth or they can talk, shut the f*** up,” Bobbi declared. “Because I better not ever see you drink cow’s milk, motherf***er.”

She called it “so stupid” that critics would shame women for feeding their babies, while drinking cow’s milk as adults.

“Are you for real right now? You think it’s normal to milk a f***ing baby cow but it’s not normal for a child to have milk that’s made for them?” Bobbi asked. “Go f*** yourself. Go get f***ed.”

The reality star admitted, “I’m so passionate about [it]” before reiterating that “breast-feeding is the most natural thing.”

Bobbi also took a shot at trolls making breast-feeding into something dirty, saying, “If you want to sexualize it, you’re a freak. You’re the f***ing freak. Don’t sexualize something that is not sexual.”

Bobbi — who shares two daughters with ex-husband Cory Althoff — reassured listeners that her kids “are very healthy” and she cares “a lot” about their health and nutrition.

“People are like, ‘Oh, my God, four?’ It’s, like, yes,” she quipped. “Go look around the f***ing world and get outside your privileged little bubble of America and see that’s just what people do.”

The podcaster noted that she would never “shame” someone if they struggle to breast-feed their baby, but for her, it’s been an “amazing” experience.

“Breastmilk is amazing. I think it’s so beautiful,” Bobbi explained. “If you can, I think it is something that I’m so grateful [for].”

She confessed, “Was it easy? No. Did my nipples bleed and hurt and everytime a child latched at first, I cried in pain all night, every night? Yes. But that goes away and your body knows what to do.”

Bobbi concluded that she’s just “here to educate” those who are unaware of the benefits of breast-feeding — and how it’s OK to do it longer than some women do.

“It’s such a beautiful thing,” she added.

Bobbi made a name for herself hosting “The Really Good Podcast” before making a splash on season 1 of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, which premiered in November.

Prior to the show, Bobbi was married to Cory, 37, from 2020 to 2024 and they welcomed two daughters. Bobbi began dating basketball player Tyler Hawkins in 2024, but he confirmed in November that they called it quits two months prior.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County is now streaming on Hulu.
