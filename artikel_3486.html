Montel Williams Opens Up About Daughter's Addiction Battle, Blames Himself

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Montel Williams is sharing a rare glimpse into his daughter’s addiction that he believes stemmed from medications she was prescribed as a child to treat her epilepsy.

“As a parent, you know, I of course didn’t understand and didn’t do the right things, I think,” Williams, 70, shared in Us’ exclusive sneak peek of the Tuesday, November 24, episode of the “We’re Out of Time” podcast. “Not even realizing that I’m the one, in some ways, by allowing the doctors to prescribe medication that she was prescribed when she was young, I’m the one that turned her into a drug addict.”

Podcast host Richard Taite, who serves as the chairman of Carrara Treatment and 1 Method Centers, quickly stopped his guest from putting the blame on himself.

“You did the best you could at the time. You’re in love with your daughter,” Taite explained in the sneak peek. “You’re in love with your family. You’re one of the most decent people I’ve ever met. I think you’re going to leave that with me.”

Williams, however, still vividly remembers the years when doctors were “chasing different medications” for his daughter.

Now, the former talk show host is grateful to see his daughter in a much healthier space and “100 percent sober.”

“She was an alcoholic for a while. She’s a 100 percent sober alcoholic,” he revealed. “She doesn’t drink. She doesn’t do any drugs anymore. She is a great mother. I’ve been with her, watched her. I’m just so proud of her. It’s crazy.”

In his latest podcast interview, Williams also shares a glimpse into his love story with Tara Fowler. Married since 2007, the couple share two children. (Williams also had two children with ex-wife Rochele See.)

Reflecting on his marriage, Williams said. “We don’t have to do a lot of stuff. We don’t have to go a lot of places. We love just spending time seated together.”

“She literally changed me,” he continued, “and made me realize how much life is not meant for self alone.”

New episodes of the “We’re Out of Time” podcast are released Tuesdays at 12 p.m. PT on YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts.
