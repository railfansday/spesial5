10 Fun 2026 Games That Would Make for Great Holiday Gifts

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eric Warner is a Journalist and Multimedia Producer based in New England with over 7 years of experience producing stories for multiple publications

For many cultures and countries across the globe, the holiday season is in full swing, with many households preparing to attend family gatherings, celebrate events, and, of course, starting to purchase holiday gifts for children and other loved ones.

Several storefronts and digital retailers have already begun issuing early Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals, with many products, especially physical video games, going on sale for 25-50% off their original sale price.

With 2026 being a particularly great year for new video game releases, here are 10 fun new video games that were released this year that would make for great holiday gifts for loved ones of all kinds ranging from titles intended for children to those aimed at more mature gamers.

Mojang’s Minecraft is considered by gamers of all ages to be one of the greatest games of all time, and over the years it’s been expanded upon through numerous updates. If fans want to get an early look at the game’s first new dimension in 15 years, then they’ll have to check out Minecraft Dungeons II.

Like its 2020 predecessor, Minecraft Dungeons II is a third-person hack and slash dungeon crawler. It follows a hero as they set out to stop illagers from threatening the world with power from the new Warden-based Sift dimension, fighting a variety of new exclusive mobs along the way.

While Minecraft Dungeons II has only been out for less than two months so far and isn’t currently on sale, it would surely make a great holiday gift for any fan of Minecraft, especially kids, as it does feature local four-player co-op allowing siblings, parents, and friends to easily play together.

Avengers: Doomsday is expected to be the biggest movie release of the decade. While Marvel fans still have to wait till late December to see the film, they can quench their thirst for action-packed Marvel content by purchasing Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls.

Set in the Japanese anime-inspired Earth-358, Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls is a 2D 4v4 tag team fighting game centered around the Fighting Avengers, the Unbreakable X-Men, the Amazing Guardians, the Samurai Outriders, and the Knights of Doom fighting each other to ultimately defeat the Champion of the Universe.

Marvel Tokon is easily the best Marvel video game released this year. Not only does it feature over 20 playable heroes and villains, but it also includes five distinct campaigns following each team attempting to defeat the Champion, with the best arguably following Doctor Doom and his cadre of iconic villains.

2026 has been great for horror titles, as fantastic games like The Sinking City 2 and Resident Evil Requiem debuted earlier this year. However, I would argue that the best horror game to gift horror fans this holiday season is Tarsier Studios’ Reanimal.

Similar in gameplay and visual design to the Little Nightmares series, Reanimal is a co-op platforming survival horror game following a young brother and sister as they travel to their home island to find three friends. Tragically, they find it in ruins and filled with gigantic humanoid and animalistic monsters.

Some horror stories are best experienced alone, but Reanimal is one that family and friends alike will certainly enjoy playing through together as they tackle puzzles and try to solve the mystery surrounding the island and its inhabitants.

This fall will be a lot of fun for couch and online co-op enthusiasts.

Millions of fans around the world love Star Wars. While Disney is releasing a ton of different SW products this holiday season to tantalize shoppers, people should consider gifting their Star Wars and video game fan loved ones with the surprise hit, Star Wars: Galactic Racer.

A roguelite racing game set shortly after the Battle of Jakku, Star Wars: Galactic Racer is centered around the rise of unsanctioned races via the new Galactic Racing League, with the protagonist Shade attempting to become the new Galactic League champion.

Galactic Racer’s colorful cast of characters and intense races utilizing podracers, land speeders, speeder bikes, or skim speeders have quickly made it become one of the most critically acclaimed SW titles in years. Fans will surely fall in love with the game for the hilarious character of Biddy Blas alone.

LEGO products have become a signature gift for decades now. While many parents will undoubtedly buy their kids a few LEGO sets this holiday season, they should also consider buying them the LEGO video game, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight.

Unlike past TT LEGO titles, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight isn’t based on any particular Batman movie but is rather a conglomeration of all things Batman, utilizing a gameplay structure similar to Batman: Arkham Knight while using elements from several Batman films, comics, and shows.

Legacy may not feature as many playable characters as past TT games, but it more than makes up for it by featuring dozens of alternate costumes for each character, alternate driveable variations of the Batmobile, and a customizable Batcave on top of returning two-player co-op.

Some gamers enjoy first-person shooters while others enjoy simulators, but some players want more open-world, narrative-driven experiences such as those commonly found in role-playing games. Arguably the best RPG of 2026 is Rebel Wolves’ debut title, The Blood of Dawnwalker.

Made by the creators of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, The Blood of Dawnwalker is a medieval fantasy RPG set in the 14th century kingdom of Vale Sangora, following a half-man half-vampire named Coen as he sets out to save his family and his country from the clutches of the Ancient Roman vampire Brencis.

The story, characters, and world-building of Dawnwalker are absolutely incredible, but it also has a ton of replay value as players can invest in witchcraft, sword skills, or vampire abilities and even accidentally drain the blood of NPCs if they don’t maintain their vampiric thirst.

As we approach the end of 2026, our lives are going to get even busier as the days pass, so why not add a hundred hour adventure or two to the mix?

Many older gamers are more action-oriented, and while 2026 has seen many great action games come out over the past few months, like Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, I would argue that best action game to gift gamers this holiday season is Gears of War: E-Day.

The sixth mainline entry in the third-person Gears of War series and prequel to the first Gears game, Gears of War: E-Day follows COG soldiers Marcus Fenix, Dominic Santiago, Mags Carter, and Lucas Reyes as they struggle to fight a horrifying invasion from the subterranean Locust Horde on Emergence Day.

E-Day may only be available on PC and Xbox Series X|S consoles, but it's by far the best Xbox exclusive released in years thanks to its gruesome, action-packed, yet emotional co-op campaign, thrilling PvP multiplayer, and addictive PvE Horde Siege mode.

With Legendary Pictures and Toho releasing several great Godzilla movies in recent years, many kids are growing up as huge Godzilla fans. A perfect gift to get them this holiday season is Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered.

A remaster of 2002’s Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee, Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered is a four-player 3D fighting game with players being able to control some of Toho's most famous kaiju in city-destroying battles. Such behemoths as Godzilla, Rodan, King Ghidorah, Mechagodzilla, and more are playable.

What makes Godzilla: DAMMR an especially great gift this holiday season is its affordable price and small file size, with the game currently being sold on PS5 for $29.99 and featuring a file size of about 5 GB, making it a quick game to download and start playing.

Similar to LEGO, Pokemon has increasingly become a prevalent presence in households during the holiday season. While there are several Pokemon products to gift people this season, I would recommend people gift Pokemon Pokopia to fans.

Unlike the main turn-based RPG games, Pokemon Pokopia is social simulation game akin to Animal Crossing, with players embodying a human-imitating Ditto waking up in a post-apocalyptic version of the Kanto region and setting out to restore the area using its transformative powers.

What follows is an adorably charming experience as Ditto rebuilds habitats and befriends Pokemon from every generation, with people even able to create their own structures and minigames similar to Minecraft to play with friends.

From unforgettable adventures to clever dungeons, these games capture some of the magic that makes Zelda so special.

One of the best games to gift anyone this holiday season is undoubtedly the 2026 masterpiece that is The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for the Nintendo Switch 2.

Released to celebrate The Legend of Zelda’s 40th anniversary, 2026’s The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is a faithful modern remake of the classic Nintendo 64 action-adventure game, with the game once again following a young Link as he set out to save the kingdom of Hyrule from the evil Ganondorf.

Being a first-party Nintendo game that’s only a few weeks old, it’s unlikely that the Ocarina of Time remake will go on sale anytime soon, but it’s well worth purchasing at full price. Especially for parents who want to introduce their kids to Link’s most famous adventure for the first time.
