Blue Origin seems to be getting there with the BE-7 engine they need, but still has to demonstrate a really big rocket to Earth orbit, and then an uncrewed flight to the moon, landing, and return to lunar orbit. SpaceX has got Starship to orbit, but needs probably 20 launches just to get enough fuel up there for the lunar landing mission, and has yet to do much with the planning in-orbit fuel transfer system which is no small thing by itself. For that reason BO seems closer now.

They are going to forego a crewed circumlunar mission and just land the first one, after a robotic demonstrator mission.
