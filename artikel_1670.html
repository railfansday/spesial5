Among the young players who Iraola will be keeping a close eye on is Lamore Forrester.

Last season, he was a stand-out talent in the club's U18 side and caught the eye with his rapid speed that caused an abundance of problems for U18 teams across the country.

Forrester is probably the fastest player at the club since Raheem Sterling at academy level. He's got pace to burn and he's got the composure and technical ability to make a difference in the final third.

Unfortunately, for most of this season, he has been ruled out due to an injury despite being in great goal-scoring form over the summer in pre-season.

However, Forrester is now back. He returned to Liverpool and featured for the U18s in a friendly last week, scoring on his return in a 6-1 win over Salford City's U18 side.
