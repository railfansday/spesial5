Chivu relaxed over Inter form after late collapse against Atalanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Cristian Chivu believes "Inter can't be judged on one spell of poor play" after their Serie A draw with Atalanta on Sunday.

Despite leading 4-2 in the 79th minute, Inter drew 4-4 with Atalanta at New Balance Arena, leaving them third in the early standings.

Inter failed to win an away Serie A match despite scoring four goals for only the third time in their history, after a 5-5 draw against Torino in January 1941 and a 4-5 defeat against Fiorentina in April 2017.

They have also conceded more than 16 goals in their first 12 Serie A matches of a season for the fifth time in the three-points-per-win era, after 1998-99 (19), 2004-05 (20), 2011-12 (18) and 2022-23 (17).

Nevertheless, Chivu has switched his focus to Inter's Champions League match with Stuttgart on Wednesday, insisting he knows what went wrong against Atalanta.

"It's another Champions League match, and that's where we start, because that's the most important thing," said Chivu.

"We know where we stand in the table right now, and we're aware of our ambitions. We also know about Stuttgart, who are an aggressive, intense and strong team. We have to be ready to give our best.

"Inter can't be judged on one spell of poor play. They've done well in recent years, and the numbers show it.

"We know what didn't work for 10 minutes in Bergamo, but we need to take a clear-eyed look at things, without losing sight of the good things we did in that match and in the two before the break.

"We need to be a little more balanced in our analysis, especially us. We've also drawn our conclusions."

Inter and Stuttgart will face off for the first time. The Nerazzurri are unbeaten in their last three games against German sides in the Champions League (W2 D1), only having a longer run through their first-ever four matches against Bundesliga sides in this competition (W3 D1 – 2002-2004).

Chivu's side have won their last two Champions League games without conceding a goal (1-0 v Shakhtar Donetsk and 3-0 v Feyenoord), more wins and more clean sheets than they had in their previous eight games in the competition combined (one of each).
