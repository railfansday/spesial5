Chelsea XI vs Nottingham Forest: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso will be desperate to get his selection decisions right as Chelsea look to put damaging defeats behind them on Friday night at Nottingham Forest.

Back-to-back losses to Sunderland and Leeds has put pressure on Alonso and his players as they head to struggling Forest.

Alonso will provide an update on the fitness of key midfielder Moises Caicedo and striker Joao Pedro when he speaks at his press conference on Thursday afternoon.

Joao Pedro has been sidelined for a number of weeks, while Caicedo started against Leeds but was forced off in the first half. Alonso confirmed after the game that the Ecuadorian would require assessment ahead of the trip to the City Ground.

Jordan Henderson will likely partner Romeo Lavia if Caicedo remains sidelined.

A decision must be made over whether to stick with the 4-2-3-1 system or revert to the 3-4-2-1 formation Chelsea were deploying earlier in the season.

Should Joao Pedro remain out, Emmanuel Emegha will hope his first goal for the club, coming last week against Leeds, will be enough to see him retain his place this weekend.
