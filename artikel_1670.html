Gallery: ‘A show of strength’, ‘Milan are in the mix’ – Today’s headlines in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Serie A action returned to San Siro on Sunday night, and AC Milan treated their fans to a five-star showing against Frosinone.

It looked like a tricky game against a side who would have gone back into the European places with a draw, but Milan put on a ruthless counter-attacking performance to claim the points.

An own-goal got the scoring going, while Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic all netted. Owner Gerry Cardinale left the stadium feeling content, even speaking of a Scudetto challenge.

It was important to win after Lazio, Roma, Como and Juventus all picked up maximum points earlier in the round, and Inter’s late implosion against Atalanta means points gained on the city rivals too. Below is how the papers look this morning.

La Gazzetta dello Sport: “Milan: a show of strength. A superb Rabiot helps secure a 5-1 win over Frosinone. Ramos gets off the mark, and the team move into fourth place. The Diavolo, the Old Lady and a high-scoring pursuit.”

Corriere dello Sport: “Milan are in the mix. They beat Frosinone 5-1, moving to within five points of Gattuso’s side. A five-goal haul and 4th place. An own goal by Calvani. Ramos scores but then has to come off. Jashari, Cisse, and Pulisic also get on the scoresheet.”

QS: “Milan hit five and take flight. Frosinone swept aside.”
