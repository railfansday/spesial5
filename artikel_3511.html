An Absurd Little Immersion-Breaking Hiccup That Reminded Me GTA 6 Isn’t Perfect

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

I’ve been amazed at almost every turn by just how detailed and responsive Grand Theft Auto 6‘s open world is. It feels lived-in and organic in ways few video games have ever come close to. So I was actually surprised when GTA 6 wasn’t able to roll with one minor curve ball I threw it early on. Throughout most of my time with the game, it’s been teaching me not to underestimate its simulation, which makes the seams, when you do happen upon them, feel all the more surprising.

It happened early in the game’s story during an optional raid on a Kingfisher stash house. I had circled around to the back of the hideout on a stolen airboat and disembarked near the building that had the drugs and money inside. I tried to sneak around and take people out stealthily, but a minute in things had already gone sideways. I started quickly looting everything I could find in the house before making my way outside to the rest of the camp.

My mind switched out of “role-playing as the stoic ex-military boyfriend who’s lying low until his girlfriend gets out of prison” mode and into Far Cry “must clear all enemies from the map icon” mode. I wanted to get top dollar for my carnage, so I continued looting everything in sight in-between firing into random brush, parked vehicles, and wherever else enemy bullets seemed to be zipping out of.

At some point I ended up with a six-pack of beers in the hand that wasn’t holding my handgun. I’m not sure how I picked them up. I didn’t remember picking them up. I tried to put them down. He drank one instead. I decided to just make for my airboat and get out of Dodge. I eventually sailed back to the dock outside of Jason’s house where Cal was waiting for me to play video games in the “Feng Shui” mission.

I got off the boat, six-pack still in hand, and made my way inside. Cal told me to go to the fridge to get us some beers, but I already had beers in my hand. I’d accidentally showed up to the bros-playing-games session I didn’t even know was about to happen with the six-pack in tow (technically a five-pack now). But try as I might, I couldn’t get the game to clock that I already had beers and let me offer them to Cal.Â

It’s very possible there wasÂ some way to make that happen. GTA 6 has certainly taught me to assume that. But after fumbling around in circles for a minute putting the six-pack down on various surfaces, I gave up and went to the fridge. Opening it initiated the scripted sequence the game was waiting for and things moved along, but I was still mourning the extremely minor missed opportunity for GTA 6 to surprise me even more in response to my unscripted chaos. It’s not the perfect open-world game after all, though I’m glad Rockstar released it now instead of potentially waiting even longer to try to make it into one.
