MGM’s 113-Minute Sci-Fi Thriller Officially Lands on Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Ridley Scott's Alien introduced audiences to one of the most recognizable monsters in movie history, inspiring decades of filmmakers to come up with their own horrifying extraterrestrial creations. That led to an army of copycat movies, all of which tried to varying levels of success to replicate the thrills, the spills, the scares and the... sex? Wait, what? Back in the 1990s, MGM decided to take the idea of an alien invasion in a rather different direction, combining science fiction, body horror, and an extremely dangerous leading lady into a movie that became a surprisingly enormous box office hit.

The 1995 sci-fi horror thriller Species has officially arrived on Prime Video in the United States, joining the streaming service on November 1, 31 years after its theatrical debut. Directed by Roger Donaldson (The Recruit), the movie earned more than $113 million worldwide against a reported $35 million production budget, eventually spawning several sequels, as well as inspiring an army of teenage boys to rent the movie over and over again from their local Blockbuster in the mid 1990s.

When a beautiful human-alien hybrid escapes from observation, scientist Xavier Fitch dispatches a crew of experts to find her before she is able to fulfill her horrific purpose: to mate with unsuspecting men and produce offspring that could destroy mankind. As her deadly biological clock ticks rapidly, Fitch and his team are hurled into a desperate battle in which the fate of humanity itself hangs in the balance. The movie is one of those kitschy, guilty pleasures from the 1990s that, while also science fiction, would also probably classify as soft-core. And that's why it did so well, presumably!

The cast includes Natasha Henstridge (Ghosts of Mars) as Sil, Ben Kingsley (Gandhi) as Xavier Fitch, Michael Madsen (Reservoir Dogs) as Preston Lennox, Forest Whitaker (The Last King of Scotland) as Dan Smithson, Marg Helgenberger (CSI: Crime Scene Investigation) as Laura Baker, Alfred Molina (Spider-Man 2) as Dr. Stephen Arden, Michelle Williams (The Fabelmans) as young Sil, and Whip Hubley (Top Gun) as John Carey. Species earned approximately $113.4 million worldwide, including $60.1 million domestically. The movie holds a 42% critics' score and 31% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.
