Amazon's early Black Friday 2026 sale is already bringing substantial discounts to smart TVs, with savings ranging from $200 to $1,350 across models from Samsung, LG, Hisense, Sony, TCL, and other major brands. Several of the TVs included are from this year's lineups, so you don't necessarily have to settle for an older model to get a good price. There's also a decent variety to choose from, whether you're looking for an OLED TV with excellent contrast for movie nights, a bright mini-LED display for a sunlit living room, or simply a bigger screen without spending a fortune.

We've rounded up some of the more interesting offers below, and you can check out the rest of our Black Friday coverage for more editor-vetted tech deals as the sale continues.

Samsung’s 65-inch S90H is down to $1,397.99 from $1,897.99, saving you $500 on the company’s mid-range OLED TV for 2026. It’s a solid option for movie enthusiasts and gamers alike, thanks to its self-lit pixels that produce deep blacks and excellent contrast, particularly in darker scenes where conventional LED TVs can struggle with a grayish glow. You also get a high-refresh-rate display for smoother gaming.

If you have room for a bigger screen, Samsung’s 77-inch S85H is also worth a look, especially now that it’s down to $1,797.99 from $2,197.99, saving you $400. Although it sits below the S90H in Samsung’s 2026 OLED lineup, it still delivers the deep blacks, excellent contrast, and wide viewing angles that make OLED TVs appealing. The larger screen also makes it a good fit for family movie nights.

One of the biggest discounts in this roundup, the 2026 75-inch Hisense U7 is now $997.99, down from $1,999.99. Its mini-LED backlighting and local dimming are designed to deliver brighter highlights and better contrast, making it an appealing option for rooms with plenty of natural light. Gaming-focused features also make it worth a look if you plan to connect a current-generation console or gaming PC.

For a more affordable 75-inch option, Hisense's 2026 U6 Pro is down to $699.99 from $1,399.99, saving you $700. Like the U7, it uses mini-LED backlighting to improve contrast and bring out more detail in HDR movies and shows, although it sits a tier below the U7 in terms of overall picture performance. It also comes with Fire TV and Alexa+ built in, making it a convenient choice for households already using Amazon's smart home ecosystem.

The C6 is LG’s mid-range OLED evo model for 2026, and right now its 65-inch model is $1,399.99, down from its $2,699.99 launch price. It combines excellent pixel-level contrast with gaming features designed for fast-paced titles, making it a versatile choice for both movies and consoles. LG also supports Dolby Vision, which gives it an advantage over Samsung's OLEDs for viewers who regularly stream compatible HDR content.

Sony’s 65-inch Bravia 6, the entry-level OLED in its 2026 TV lineup, is now $1,198, down from $1,999.99, saving you $802. It delivers the deep blacks and excellent contrast you'd expect from the display technology, thanks to its self-lit pixels. Sony's picture processing also helps bring out fine details and natural-looking colors in movies and TV shows, while Dolby Vision support makes the most of compatible HDR content.

For the same $1,198, you can get a considerably larger screen with the 85-inch Sony Bravia 3 II, which is down from $1,599.99, saving you $402. That said, this 2026 model uses a conventional LED panel, so it won't quite match the Bravia 6's deep blacks and contrast, but the extra 20 inches make it an appealing option for sports and family movie nights. It also has a native 120Hz refresh rate with VRR and ALLM for smoother console gaming, along with Dolby Vision support and Google TV with Gemini built in.

Toshiba’s 75-inch Z670R is down to $699.99 from $1,299.99, saving you $600 on a 2026 mini-LED TV. It combines full-array local dimming with QLED color technology for improved contrast and brighter highlights, while Toshiba’s REGZA Engine ZRi processor helps sharpen lower-resolution content. The native 144Hz refresh rate also makes it a good option for console and PC gaming, and you get Fire TV built in for streaming, along with Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos support.

Designed to compete with Samsung's The Frame, Toshiba's 65-inch G700 ArtVision is currently $899.99 at Amazon, down from $1,297.99, saving you $398. It's a 4K QLED TV, with a native 165Hz refresh rate, built-in Fire TV, and the same REGZA Engine ZRi processor found in the Z670R. But there's more to it than watching movies and shows, as its Ambient Mode can display over 3,000 curated artworks without a subscription, while the matte anti-glare screen and included white oak frame help it pass for a framed print on the wall.

TCL’s 98-inch F8D is down to $1,444.99 from $1,699.99, saving you $255 on a massive 2026 QLED Fire TV. It uses a high-brightness LED backlight, so you won't get the same level of contrast control as TCL's more expensive mini-LED QM models, but it still offers a native 144Hz refresh rate for smoother gaming, along with Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos support. And at nearly 100 inches, there's plenty of screen space to make movies, sports, and games feel more immersive.

Roku’s 65-inch Plus Series is the most affordable TV in this roundup at $449.99, down from $649.99, saving you $200. This 2025 model combines a QLED panel with mini-LED backlighting and Dolby Vision support for improved contrast and richer colors when watching HDR content. It also runs the same Roku OS found on the company’s streaming sticks and comes with an Enhanced Voice Remote, so navigating apps and finding something to watch should feel familiar to anyone who already uses Roku.

LG’s 65-inch C6 OLED has the largest dollar discount in this sale at $1,350 off, which brings it from $2,699.99 down to $1,349.99.
