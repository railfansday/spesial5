Birmingham City man unlikely to join Premier League side, reporter believes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Birmingham City’s centre-back is reportedly on the radar of the Suffolk outfit along with fellow Premier League sides Hull City and Coventry City this winter. The BCFC man has also been linked with Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina.

Neumann, 29, is under contract with the Blues until June 2028 meaning he still has just under two years left to run down on his deal in the Midlands. His current club are sat top of the Championship table along with Middlesbrough and Stoke City after 15 fixtures.

Journalist Jones doesn’t believe a switch to Portman Road is on the cards and has delivered his verdict on the rumour, as per a report by the East Anglian Daily Times: “If Ipswich do go and sign a centre-back in January, I’d hope that they sign a high-profile option with top-level experience.

“Neumann isn’t that. Financial restrictions might limit what they can do in the market, but in my mind, it’s better to do nothing at all rather than sign the wrong player. There are question marks over the link and the source reliability as it is. I can’t see Town making this move in January.”

Birmingham City won’t want to sell Neumann, especially half-way through the season. He is a key player for BCFC and has been in fine form over recent times.

The ex-Germany youth international joined the Blues back in 2025 to bolster their defensive department. He has since enjoyed plenty of game time under the guidance of head coach Chris Davies.

Neumann has formed a solid partnership in the heart of defence with Christoph Klarer. Birmingham City have the best defensive record in the division at the moment just ahead of Southampton and West Brom.

Prior to his switch to England, he spent three years at Hannover 96. He has also had spells in the past in his native country with the likes of Schalke, Ingolstadt and Holstein Kiel.

Birmingham City don’t need to offload Neumann anytime soon. BCFC would need to find a replacement if he left.

He is likely to stick around until next summer at least as the Blues eye promotion to the top flight. Jones has seemingly poured cold water on speculation linking him with an exit to Ipswich Town.

The Tractor Boys are well stocked up with options in Neumann’s position, as are Hull City, Coventry City and Nottingham Forest. In addition, his linked clubs would also need to fork out a fee if they were to lure him away from St Andrews.

Birmingham City won 2-1 at home against Swansea City last time out with Jay Stansfield scoring twice. They are back in action after the international break with an away trip to Stoke City.
