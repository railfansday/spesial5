Swansea City vs West Brom predictions as life after Vitor Matos begins

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Swansea City welcome West Brom to the Swansea.com Stadium this weekend sitting 6th in the Championship with 26 points from 15 matches. However, four defeats in their last five games, including a 2-1 loss at Birmingham, have seen them concede 11 goals.

Their home form remains encouraging, with four wins from their last six league matches, including a 4-0 thrashing of Blackburn. The Swans will also be without head coach Vitor Matos after his departure to Ligue 1 side Strasbourg was confirmed.

West Brom, meanwhile, sit 5th with 28 points from 15 games. WBA are unbeaten in their last five, winning three and drawing two, including impressive victories over Millwall and West Ham.

James Morrison’s side have been very impressive to start the season after a poor campaign last year. However, they head into this one without key player Ousmane Diakite who has picked up an injury on international duty.

“West Brom’s consistency gives them an advantage, but Swansea’s home record could help secure a draw.

“Isaac Price has been instrumental for the Baggies, scoring six goals and providing three assists in 15 league appearances. According to FotMob, he has an xG of 5.14 from 43 shots, created 28 chances and eight big chances.

“His ability to arrive late in the box and threaten from distance makes him a major danger. Swansea’s midfield must be disciplined to restrict his influence, although Adam Idah offers the hosts their own attacking threat with four goals from 730 minutes this season.

“Matos’ departure creates uncertainty, but Swansea’s home form should make this a competitive encounter. WBA look the more consistent side, although I expect the points to be shared.”

“All the talk coming into this game is about Vitor Matos and his exit from Swansea City. There’s no hiding that this disrupts preparations, although the players will be determined to prove that they’re capable of continuing their promotion push without the boss.

“The tricky thing is that after such an unsettling week, the Swans face an excellently coached and well-drilled side in West Brom. James Morrison’s men have been excellent, and they’re on a great six-game unbeaten run in the Championship.

“One worry for WBA is injuries. They’ve got a desperately small squad, and have just seen star man Ousmane Diakite cast to the sidelines.

“Morrison will have faith they can maintain form given how well set up they are, almost regardless of personnel. Given the disrupted period in South Wales, I do lean towards an Albion triumph.”
