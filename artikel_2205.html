3 players who Middlesbrough should offload in January, including Micah Hamilton

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Middlesbrough sit 2nd in the Championship table with the latest international break upon us. It has been a fine campaign for Boro to date, but they won’t be in any mood to rest on their laurels on the pitch or in the transfer market.

Steve Gibson backed Kim Hellberg heavily in the summer with pricey deals for Will Lankshear, Radek Vitek and more. However, there will be more opportunities in the January transfer window, and incomings and outgoings are expected at the Riverside Stadium.

To streamline Hellberg’s squad and make way for new signings, we put forward three players who Middlesbrough should consider offloading in the winter…

Experienced centre-back George Edmundson was lined up for a move away from Middlesbrough late in the summer transfer window, but a deal never materialised. He has not been named in a Championship matchday squad since the opening day win over Lincoln City and is firmly down the pecking order under Hellberg.

With his contract expiring at the end of the season. the 29-year-old seems bound to leave Boro at some point in 2027. It would be beneficial for him to move on sooner rather than later though, and a winter transfer will allow him to get back to playing.

The powerful centre-back could be a shrewd signing for a team towards the bottom end of the Championship, or in the top six of League One.

Capped by England at U18 and U20 levels, McCabe is among Middlesbrough’s brightest young prospects. However, his progression has stalled somewhat in 2026 off the back of a tough loan at Plymouth Argyle.

The 20-year-old has been back in the U21s setup this season with not one senior appearance made. McCabe turns 21 in January though, so he is at the age where he needs to get more first-team football under his belt.

Given his promise, it seems unlikely that he leaves Teesside on a permanent basis. However, the club must strongly consider sending the young midfielder out on loan to a team where he is a strong tactical fit and will get plenty of opportunities to start.

Last but not least is Micah Hamilton, who is sure to be angling for a chance to kick his career back into life. His two years at Middlesbrough have not brought the opportunities he would have hoped for, but he still has exciting potential to tap into after his emergence at hometown club Manchester City.

Aged 22, his contract at the Riverside runs until 2028. But, with the likes of Rokas Pukstas, Myles Peart-Harris, Jeremy Sarmiento and Amario Cozier-Duberry arriving in the attacking midfield and wing positions during the summer, it seems unlikely that Hamilton forces his way into Hellberg’s plans.

Perhaps an impressive loan would change that, but it may be best for all parties if a permanent exit deal is struck in January. Given his high ceiling, there will be clubs further down in the EFL ready to take a chance on Hamilton if the price is right.
