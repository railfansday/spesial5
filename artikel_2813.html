Tee Higgins thinks Joe Burrow has "another step in him"

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Bengals extended their winning streak to four games against the Falcons in Madrid on Sunday and they continued to use their aerial attack to fuel their success.

Joe Burrow was 24-of-29 for 271 yards and three touchdowns in the 34-17 win and he has thrown for 1,215 yards and 16 touchdowns over the last four games. Wide receivers Ja’Marr Chase and Tee Higgins have been the main targets for Burrow and Higgins was asked after Sunday’s win if this is the best he’s ever seen Burrow play.

“No. I know he got another step in him, for sure,” Higgins said, via a transcript from the team.

Higgins said the same for the offense as a whole because there are things they could improve to find their “best form.” That prospect should be a chilling one for opposing defensive players and coaches as they try to draw up ways to slow down a red-hot Bengals offense.
