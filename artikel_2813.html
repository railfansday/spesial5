LeBron triple-double, Maxey 30-piece lead Sixers over Heat

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Philadelphia 76ers got back in the win column on Wednesday evening, taking down the visiting Miami Heat 111-104 in NBA Cup group play.

Joel Embiid was ruled out for this one after having been doubtful on the injury report since yesterday because of illness. This was the second game in a row Embiid missed due to being sick.

The Sixers were led by tremendous nights from LeBron James and Tyrese Maxey. James, who notched his first triple-double as a Sixer, put up 25 points on 9-for-15 field goal and 4-for-6 three-point shooting with 12 assists, 10 rebounds and a steal. Maxey added 30 points shooting 12-for-21 from the floor. VJ Edgecombe came alive in the second half to chip in 18 points.

The bad news is that Jaylen Brown exited the game just before halftime after seemingly favoring and grabbing at his hamstring. He was quickly ruled out for the rest of the contest with hamstring tightness. Something to be aware of going forward, for sure.

The good news is that the Sixers are now 15-4 and have off tomorrow. They're back in action Friday.

It didn't take long to see how Miami benefits from being third in the association in pace. In the first three minutes, the Heat got out ahead in transition three separate times for easy buckets before the Sixers defense even made it back up the other end. The Sixers aren't that slow, but the Heat were running circles around them early.

Jabari Walker got his third start tonight in Joel Embiid's absence, and it went right to work doing the little things right. He buried a second-chance triple off a LeBron James offensive rebound then got down the defensive end to draw an offensive foul out of Giannis. Jabari had seven points on 3-for-3 field goal shooting in his first six minutes on the floor.

If you read any of the Bell Ringers I usually write, you know I cannot stop talking about LeBron's passing. Well, I'm here to talk about it again. It's not just one pass. It's every single pass he makes. He waits until the exact millisecond to release perfectly targeted passes that set up the Sixers for sometimes wide open buckets.

Tyrese Maxey led the way for the Sixers' scoring in the first quarter with 13 points on 5-for-8 field goal, 1-for-2 three-point shooting. The consistent contribution of points helped steady the Sixers' offense after starting off the game a bit disjointed. Philly trailed Miami by three points after one.

LeBron has this tendency to just absolutely lock in offensively in insane bursts. He goes from the main facilitator to a mode of "hold on y'all, I got this myself" and, in year 24, he's absolutely still got it. James had the ball in his hands for three straight Sixers' possessions and turned each into three points — one and-1, and two threes. He wasn't done. A minute later, he pulled up again from not far over midcourt for the ultimate heat check. Three more points. It ended up being a 14-3 Sixers run that gave them the lead, courtesy of LeBron James, single-handed.

It's been a very quiet night so far from Jaylen Brown. With just four minutes left in the half, Brown has two points on 1-for-6 field goal shooting with not much else to show for himself on the stat sheet. He hasn't made a ton of mistakes, necessarily, but more so has been relatively inconsequential so far and can't seem to get a shot to fall. A far cry from last game, his return from a hamstring strain, where he looked as solid as ever.

Just a quick note (and, yes, I admit in saying this I am incredibly biased)… Giannis gets a VERY friendly whistle. I get it, he's a superstar. But compare the amount of contact he takes that is getting called a foul versus the amount of contact Tyrese Maxey takes with no whistles.

The Sixers — especially considering the absence of Joel Embiid — are doing a great job so far containing Bam Adebayo. At halftime, Adebayo had just five points on 2-for-8 field goal shooting

The Sixers ended the half on a 7-0 run to help them to a 62-53 lead going into the break. That's the good news. That bad news is that Brown left the half a few seconds early after seemingly favoring his left leg and grabbing at his hamstring. He went straight back to the locker room.

The team was quick to rule out Brown for the rest of the game due to hamstring tightness. Dean Wade started the second half in his spot.

Philadelphia has had a tendency to come out of halftime looking a bit sluggish or out of sorts… not tonight. The Sixers ripped off a quick 7-0 run just a minute or so into the second half to push their lead to double-digits. It was the usual suspects, James and Maxey, chipping in with the addition of VJ Edgecombe getting involved.

Edgecombe had a relatively quiet first half, but he seemed energized to come out for the second. The sophomore assisted the Sixers' first two buckets of the third frame and then scored the next two himself. He added a triple and a steal a few minutes later. That's one thing that Edgecombe seems to possess beyond his years: patience. It doesn't matter how few opportunities he gets early on in a game, he never gets discouraged and is always ready whenever he is called upon.

It's a different game entirely when the Sixers are actually hitting their three-pointers. With just a few minutes left in the third, six Sixers have sank at least one three-pointer and three players have multiple. They're shooting 45.8% from long range and it certainly makes holding on to a narrow lead just a bit easier.

It's been very nice to have Dominick Barlow back. In just his third game of the season after making his debut this past Saturday, Barlow continued putting in some really serviceable minutes at the backup center position featuring solid rim protection, grabbing offensive boards, putting back buckets and setting some big screens. He and Walker have been a decent one-two punch at the center position without Embiid available tonight.

The Sixers brought a nine-point lead into the final frame. Their lead was quickly extended back into the double-digits with two back-to-back triples from Anfernee Simons — his first two field goals of the night, making him the seventh Sixer to knock down a three-pointer in this one.

Apologies for the redundancy but the Lebron passes just get more and more ridiculous. It is just beyond anything I've ever seen done so well night in and night out, every single game. Here's an absurdly accurate lob to Edgecombe from half court to extend the Sixers lead to 14.

Edgecombe has absolutely come alive since halftime. From all the statistical contributions to just the extra effort plays that won't show on the sheet, he has his handprints all over this second half and was a big factor in the Sixers holding on to this lead.

The Heat aren't just going away quietly tonight. They've chipped the Sixers lead back down to just single digits with five minutes to play. This Philly team just always has to keep games interesting…

What a night from LeBron James! With his 10th rebound with under two minutes to play, James notched his first triple-double as a Philadelphia 76er.

The Sixers hold on and get back in the win column. They move to 15-4 on the season and will be back in action on Friday night.
