Missouri Pulls Off Impressive Ranked Win in Columbia

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Missouri Tigers delivered an important statement victory on Saturday night, defeating No. 16 Kentucky 28-14 at Memorial Stadium. Behind an outstanding performance from quarterback Austin Simmons and a defense that forced multiple turnovers, Missouri secured its second ranked victory of the season.

The Tigers entered the matchup looking to bounce back after consecutive losses and responded with one of their strongest performances of the year. Kentucky, meanwhile, hoped to continue building momentum and strengthen its College Football Playoff hopes.

Missouri improved to 7-4 overall and 4-4 in SEC play, while Kentucky dropped to 7-4 and 5-4 in conference action.

Simmons was the biggest difference-maker for Missouri, putting together an impressive performance through the air.

The quarterback completed 17 of 22 passes for 314 yards and four touchdowns, consistently finding opportunities against Kentucky's secondary.

His connection with Donovan Olugbode proved especially important. Olugbode finished with five receptions for 105 yards and two touchdowns, providing Missouri with a reliable target throughout the night.

Simmons also connected with Jude James and Naeshaun Montgomery for touchdown passes as the Tigers continued finding ways to reach the end zone.

Missouri established control early and carried a 14-3 advantage into halftime.

The Tigers continued adding to their lead during the second half, eventually building a 28-6 advantage heading into the fourth quarter.

While Simmons and the passing attack received plenty of attention, Missouri's defense deserves significant credit for Saturday's victory.

The Tigers forced three turnovers and consistently made things difficult for Kentucky quarterback Kenny Minchey.

Santana Banner and Sione Laulea each recorded interceptions, while Daeden Hopkins forced a fumble.

Those takeaways prevented Kentucky from establishing offensive consistency and helped Missouri maintain control.

Kentucky eventually found some success during the fourth quarter, cutting the deficit to 28-14. However, Missouri's defense made the necessary plays to prevent the Wildcats from completing a comeback.

The Tigers also held Kentucky to just 14 points despite the Wildcats reaching the red zone seven times.

Saturday's victory gives Missouri an important boost after dropping consecutive games.

Simmons' four-touchdown performance demonstrated the potential of the Tigers' passing offense, while the defense showed it can create game-changing opportunities against quality SEC competition.

Missouri will now turn its attention toward its regular-season finale against Oklahoma.

With another ranked opponent coming to Columbia, the Tigers will have an opportunity to finish the regular season with another statement victory.

For Kentucky, the loss represents a major setback after entering the matchup ranked No. 16.

Missouri took advantage of its opportunities, delivered a strong performance on both sides of the football and walked away with a 28-14 victory.
