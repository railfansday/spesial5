Vikings bring Kirk Cousins back to Minnesota

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Guess who’s back? Back again? Kirk-o’s back. Tell a friend.

The Vikings have struck a deal with the Raiders to bring quarterback Kirk Cousins back to Minnesota, according to ESPNFL.

Cousins joined the Vikings in 2018 as a free agent. He spent six years with the team before leaving as a free agent in 2024 for the Falcons.

The Raiders will receive Minnesota’s third-round pick in 2027, per the report.

It’s a small price to pay in an effort to salvage the season. Cousins has been solid for the Raiders, leading the team to a 4-5 record and throwing 20 touchdown passes and eight interceptions.

It’s the second time the Vikings have brought back a former starting quarterback. In 1967, they traded Fran Tarkenton to the Giants. In 1972, the Giants traded Tarkenton back to the Vikings.
