Chargers-Patriots Game Preview: 5 Questions with the Enemy

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This week, we were joined by Taylor Kyles of our Patriots sister site, Pats Pulpit, to help us preview this week's game against New England.

It's officially a battle of backup quarterbacks, so I hope everyone is excited for the absolutely wildcard that this game could be!

1.) Drake Maye will not be playing this game after suffering a severe lower leg injury and Tommy DeVito looks to be the guy getting the start. What can you tell us about DeVito and what are your expectations for him in this game?

Tommy DeVito was extended by the Patriots this offseason after being the third quarterback behind Drake Maye and Joshua in 2025. Unfortunately for New England, DeVito didn't play up to the new deal against the Lions. The veteran threw a bad interception during a two-minute drill before halftime, failed to target open receivers, and delivered two hospital balls on downfield throws. In fairness, DeVito was under consistent pressure and hadn't seen significant action in years, but the Patriots will need a much better effort from him this weekend.

While DeVito's vision and anticipation are questionable, he has enough arm talent to hit the crossers and go routes that made up much of his preseason and training camp menu. He also has solid pocket presence and is athletic enough to make plays with his legs when pressure closes in or plays break down. The biggest thing for DeVito will be taking what the defense gives him and minimizing turnover-worthy throws to keep the offense on schedule.

2.) Obviously this season has not lived up to the preseason expectations that followed this team after a Super Bowl appearance. Where did it all go wrong? Are there any signs that this team can rebound in 2027?

Offensively, the Patriots have simply been hammered by injuries, as Jared Wilson is the only starter who hasn't missed time due to injury. That injury misfortune was compounded by Drake Maye putting the ball in harm's way far too often, as well as a fairly mundane and at-times nonexistent rushing attack. The offense has some help coming with LG Alijah Vera-Tucker expected to return from injured reserve. However, how he looks after missing over a month is anyone's guess, though he's been impressive when on the field.

Defensively, the Patriots simply haven't played up to their talent. Carlton Davis missing multiple games with a neck injury hasn't helped, but the defense has been torched in consecutive weeks against NFC North opponents, including one game where Davis played nearly every snap. The biggest issue has been poor fundamentals, including shaky tackling, a lack of players swarming to the football, inconsistent defensive line play, and miscommunications that have led to coverage busts and big plays. Part of the blame also falls on Zak Kuhr's scheme, which seems more concerned with deploying disguised zone coverages than letting highly paid CB1 Christian Gonzalez do what he does best: play press man coverage against the opponent's top receiver as often as possible. There are also wrinkles like safeties defending wide receivers to maintain zone looks, a general abundance of off coverage, and a wide menu of coverages that I'm personally not a fan of and think works to the defense's detriment at times.

Frankly, this feels like a lost season for the Patriots, especially if the defense continues to struggle against talented, well-coached offenses like the Jaguars, Bears, Packers, and Lions, all of whom had standout performances against New England. For them to rebound in 2027, they'll need injury luck closer to what they enjoyed in 2025, but also an influx of young talent and a reset in veteran leadership, particularly on defense.

3.) If you were Chargers offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel, how would you go about attacking this Patriots defense? Where are the weak spots? Which players should the Chargers try to avoid? What about ones to exploit?

First and foremost, I'd challenge the interior defensive line and Christian Gonzalez in the run game. Jahmyr Gibbs had a lot of success cutting into the teeth of the Patriots' defense on outside zone, and Gonzalez has been shaky in run support when directly targeted. I'd also target backup outside cornerback Charles Woods, who's struggled against double moves and crafty route runners, and Christian Elliss, whose zone depth has been spotty at times.

I would avoid running at rookie Gabe Jacas, who has a run stop in every career game and plays with a tremendous motor. In the pass game, while Gonzalez hasn't played up to his usual standard this season, he's still one of the league's best corners, especially when playing press. Marcus Jones also remains one of the most competitive slot corners in the league, even if his turnover numbers are below expectation.

4.) If you were Chargers defensive coordinator Chris O'Leary, how would you try and stop this current Patriots offense? What will the team's plan be without Drake Maye? How will it differ from their normal style of offense?

I would commit all of my resources to stopping the run and forcing Tommy DeVito to win the game. The Patriots' run game hasn't been anything to write home about, but this seems like a reasonable key for both teams in a battle of backup quarterbacks. In obvious passing situations, I'd discourage DeVito from throwing outside and guide him toward the middle of the field. I'd also avoid man coverage and force him to be disciplined with his eyes and anticipate windows. The Patriots' right guard situation is still unsettled, but the right side has been susceptible to stunts and blitzes, so I'd challenge their communication as often as possible.

5.) Let's go ahead and top this off with a few lines on how you see this matchup shaking out. Who do you see coming out on top and what will the final score be?

I think coming off a bye and the embarrassment of back-to-back blowouts will propel the Patriots to a win against a vulnerable opponent, but that's far from a guarantee. If DeVito avoids egregious mistakes and makes 2-3 plays, and the defense plays up to its usual standard, this should be a fairly comfortable win for New England.
