49ers injury updates: Nate Hobbs returns to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The San Francisco 49ers injury report is out for Wednesday. The following players won't practice:

WR Mike Evans (Achilles)CB Renardo Green (back)CB Deommodore Lenoir (foot)

WR Deebo Samuel (hamstring)CB Siran Neal (knee/foot)CB Upton Stout (shoulder/neck)DT CJ West (limited)

The team officially opened the practice window for West to return from the injured reserve, which is why he's on the injury report.

Cornerback Nate Hobbs is a full go. With both starting cornerbacks out, and the only other option being rookie Ephesians Prysock, the Niners may give the veteran an opportunity. Lenoir and Green not practicing also explains why the 49ers added Jack Jones to the practice squad.

Last week, defensive coordinator Raheem Morris said Hobbs has the versatility to play inside or outside. Shanahan said, "Nate's a real good NFL corner. Seen him at a number of places. We've scrimmaged against him. I've played against him in games. He didn't have a long camp, but he had long enough with us that you could see him effective inside, effective outside. His versatility is great. Where he's needed."

There are no updates for Evans. He remains week-to-week. Shanahan said even if Evans were available for the final eight games of the season, the 49ers would have still looked to add another wide receiver.

Despite being listed as limited, Deebo Samuel said he feels ready to play this week.
