Rams expect All-Pro WR Puka Nacua to land on injured reserve with multiple knee sprains and turf toe

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Los Angeles Rams expect to place All-Pro receiver Puka Nacua on injured reserve with MCL and PCL sprains to his right knee and turf toe.

Head coach Sean McVay made the announcement to reporters on Monday. Nacua suffered the injuries during Sunday's 20-13 win over the Washington Commanders. It's not clear how long Nacua will be sidelined, but a stint on injured reserve means he'll miss a minimum of four games.

The news of Nacua's expected IR placement is another significant blow to an offense that's already playing without starting receiver and former Pro Bowler Davante Adams. Now the Rams are facing an extended period without their top two weapons in the passing game.

A two-time All-Pro who led the NFL in receptions (129) and yards per game (107.2) in 2025, Nacua was in the midst of another standout campaign despite having missed two games early in the season.

In seven games this season, Nacua has 70 catches for 715 yards and 4 touchdowns. He had his most productive game of the season against Washington prior to his late-game injury with 14 catches for 150 yards and a touchdown. He also posted two carries for 20 yards.

Nacua appeared to sustain his injury on one of those carries on the Rams' final offensive play of the game. He took contact to his knee on a tackle and was able to quickly get up. ESPN's Adam Schefter reported after the game that Nacua sustained an injury and would undergo an MRI on Monday.

Adams, meanwhile is sidelined with a ruptured hamstring he sustained in a Week 6 loss to the Arizona Cardinals.

McVay announced on Oct. 19 that Adams was expected to miss six-to-10 weeks with his injury. He's missed three games since. The earliest Adams would return under that timeline would be a Week 12 game against the Green Bay Packers on Dec. 3, while a 10-week absence would keep him out through Week 17 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

The earliest Nacua would be permitted to return from injured reserve would be a Week 15 game against the Dallas Cowboys on Dec. 20.

With both of them sidelined, Konata Mumpfield, Tutu Atwell and Jordan Whittington are the next men up on the depth chart. Mumpfield is the leading receiver among the three with 11 catches for 162 yards and a touchdown.

The Rams, meanwhile, are fighting to stay afloat in the NFC playoff picture after entering the season as the favorite to win the Super Bowl. Sunday's win over the Commanders was their third straight and improved their record to 5-4.

That leaves them on the outside looking in at the playoff picture behind the NFC West-leading San Francisco 49ers (7-1) and the 6-3 Packers, who would be the last NFC wild-card team if the playoffs started today.
