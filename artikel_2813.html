Jaguars create more salary cap space; restructure Walker Little's deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After reworking Foye Oluokun's contract, the Jacksonville Jaguars have now done the same to Walker Little's deal, freeing up additional 2026 salary cap space.

According to Spotrac, the Jaguars converted $4.89 million of Little's 2026 salary into a signing bonus, which created $3.66 million in available cap space.

In short, this is the salary cap version of kicking the can down the road. The Jaguars took a cap charge from the current year and pushed it to future seasons. The benefit in the present is that cap space is created, while Little's cap hit in future seasons will now increase.

The 2027 season is the final year of Little's deal. Per Spotrac, Little's $13 million salary next year is "unprotected."

Void years are effectively a bookkeeping measure, adding additional years to the contract on paper -- they are not an extension. Rather, they create a longer runway for cap charges to be pushed to, thus helping to keep the cap hits lower during the actual contract years.

Little has taken over as the Jaguars' starting left tackle this season with Anton Harrison out for the year.
