Lakers avoid another embarrassment to Jazz by turning 4th quarter tables

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lakers' offensive malaise, if it had to happen, came at an ideal time.

Because if the quality of competition were higher, the Lakers probably would've had a worse homestand over the last week.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers handles the ball against the Utah Jazz during a game on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

But with their 109-94 victory over the Jazz on Monday night at Crypto.com Arena in front of the soon-to-be franchise owners Josh Kushner and Bob Iger, the Lakers avoided another disaster of a loss after blowing a 16-point lead.

The Lakers improved to 15-3 with the victory, going 3-1 on the homestand that included wins over the Hornets, Kings and a loss to the Jazz last week.

Cameron Carr #43 of the Los Angeles Lakers drives to the basket against the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

Despite struggling with their 3-point shooting for the third consecutive game, the Lakers took a 53-42 lead going into halftime after being up 51-36 earlier in the second quarter thanks to their defense.

They held the Jazz to 36.7% shooting (18 for 49) in the first two quarters, and scored 11 points off Utah's 10 turnovers.

But the Jazz chipped away at their deficit quickly, turning what was a Lakers' double-digit lead into a 57-56 lead for Utah with just over eight minutes left in the third.

The Lakers' response was what they needed it to be.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots against the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

They outscored the Jazz 53-37 in the final 20 minutes, leaning on their defense to win in ways they haven't needed to for most of the season. That is a stark departure from how the team has played the final frame nearly all season.

The Lakers held the Jazz to 17 points on 31.6% shooting (6 for 19) in the fourth.

Collin Sexton (10) of the Los Angeles Lakers handles the ball against the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

Luka Dončić led the Lakers with 28 points, eight assists and four rebounds, but once again struggled with his shot (8 for 24, 3 for 14 on 3s).

Jake LaRavia (15 points, 10 rebounds), Matisse Thybulle (14 points), Collin Sexton (12 points) and Walker Kessler (10 points, 12 rebounds) all scored in double figures.
