Trevor Lawrence: Trades show the confidence in our team, what we have a chance to do

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Jaguars signaled they’re all-in on the 2026 season by making several trades to fortify their roster before the deadline.

Quarterback Trevor Lawrence told reporters in his Wednesday press conference that the moves signal a positive trend for the team.

“I think you try not to look too much into it,” Lawrence said, via transcript from the team. “We have the same job to do, and I think we have a really good team that has good chemistry. Has gelled together. We like playing together, we have great camaraderie. All those things that are just hard to put your finger on sometimes. I think we have all that. I think we’ve been playing well, and so we still have a job to do, and nothing changes from that perspective.

“But I do think, obviously with the moves made, I think it shows the confidence in our team, and what we have the chance to do. I think that we’re a really good team, and now we get even better with these guys. I think that’s all it is, is just making our team even better, and we know that we’re going to have a shot. We just got to keep playing well and we got to keep winning. I think you can’t look too much into it because we still have the same job right in front of us, but I think these guys are going to help us for sure.”

Moreover, it helps that the players acquired have significant postseason experience.

“I think to get winners, to get guys that have been there before, that experience is important,” Lawrence said. “I think that is, why would you not want that if you can? Obviously, you got to look at the players you’re getting, and you want great players, but if they’ve also won Super Bowls and been to Super Bowls, I think that’s icing on the cake or whatever. That’s a plus. So, we have some guys obviously in that group that have done that. Just excited to get that experience. I think they’ve played meaningful football. They know what it looks like and going to add that value to our team.”

We’ll see how the additions start to fit in when the Jaguars play the Titans on Sunday.
