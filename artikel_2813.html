49ers WR Mike Evans is "set for his return" on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After more than a month, 49ers receiver Mike Evans is on track to return to action.

Via Mike Garafolo of NFL Network, Evans is set for his return on Sunday night, when the 49ers face the Vikings in Mexico City.

Evans has been dealing with an Achilles injury. He left the Week 6 Monday night game against the Commanders, and he has not played since.

He did not practice on Wednesday, and he was limited in practice on Thursday and Friday.

On Friday, coach Kyle Shanahan was asked about the boost Evans’s return will provide to the team.

“I think a big one,” Shanahan said. “Having Mike out there, everyone knows how good Mike is. I think the guys have done a good job holding it down with him not out there, but Mike’s our guy and a very unique player and been doing it at a high level for a very long time. Played really unbelievable for the games he played in this year and it’d be great to get him back.”

Evans joined the 49ers in free agency, after 12 seasons with the Buccaneers. In six games with the 49ers, Evans has 27 catches for 420 yards and three touchdowns.
