The Wildcats last played the Red Wolves in 2022, when they prevailed 41-10 in a Region 21 semifinal at Twinsburg Tiger Stadium.

The Wildcats have gone on a number of deep postseason runs of late, including earning regional runners-up honors a year ago (as well as in 2021, 2022 and 2023). Mogadore's lone losses this season were to Canton Central Catholic (which lost in the Region 17 quarterfinals to powerhouse Perry) and Linsly (a private school in West Virginia). Maddox Smith is a strong dual-threat quarterback for the Wildcats, passing for 988 yards and 11 touchdowns and adding another 11 scores on the ground. Jake Hopkins (13 carries, 99 yards, TD last week against St. John) and Kade Thomas (16 carries, 97 yards, two TDs) are also coming off strong performances.

The Red Wolves have won 10 consecutive games since a loss to Conneaut to open the season. They've been stingy defensively of late, yielding just 14 points combined over their last four games. They've held seven of 11 opponents overall to single digits. The defense was especially strong last week, forcing four Dalton turnovers in a 23-8 regional quarterfinal win. Zach Shafer is a strong quarterback for Cuyahoga Heights, and scored two touchdowns (one through the air and one on the ground) against the Bulldogs.
