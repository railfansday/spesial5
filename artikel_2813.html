Kings trade Nique Clifford to Thunder for two future second-round draft picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Kings trade Nique Clifford to Thunder for two future second-round draft picks originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

The Kings are parting ways with one of their young players.

Sacramento is trading second-year guard Nique Clifford to the Oklahoma City Thunder for two second-round NBA draft picks, the team announced Sunday.

The Kings are acquiring unprotected 2028 second-round draft pick via the Utah Jazz and an unprotected 2030 second-round draft pick via the Minnesota Timberwolves.

ESPN's Shams Charania first reported news of the trade.

Clifford originally was selected by Oklahoma City with the No. 24 pick in the 2025 NBA Draft before his draft rights were traded to Sacramento for a protected 2027 first-round pick.

In two seasons with the Kings, the 24-year-old Clifford averaged 7.9 points, 3.5 rebounds and 2.3 assists per game on 41.6-percent shooting from the field and 32.6 percent from 3-point range in 83 total games (28 starts).

He now will provide guard depth for a Thunder team devastated by injuries early on this season.
