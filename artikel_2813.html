Pelicans evaluating possibilities after trade request from Dejounte Murray, who recently rebutted criticism on social media

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The New Orleans Pelicans are evaluating potential trade opportunities for Dejounte Murray after a trade request from the All-Star guard, the team announced Friday.

The news comes the same week the 30-year-old Murray rebutted criticism on social media with since-deleted posts, one of which reportedly featured profanity. The Pelicans are 4-8 this season, including 1-8 in the games Murray has appeared in.

Murray won't play in Friday night's NBA Cup group-play game between New Orleans and the Memphis Grizzlies, the Pelicans announced, citing his left ankle sprain. His agent Rich Paul and Pelicans officials were reported by ESPN's Shams Charania to be working on a resolution earlier in the day.

The Phoenix Suns have expressed interest in the 2022 All-Star, according to NBA insider Chris Haynes.

The 2026-27 campaign is his third with the Pelicans, with whom he's currently averaging 13.4 points, 4.6 rebounds, 4.4 assists and 1.6 steals per game.

That said, Murray is shooting just 29.6% from 3, and he's committed four turnovers twice so far.

In the fallout of a 145-111 home loss to the 3-8 Brooklyn Nets, the X account for Pro Pels Talk, a Pelicans podcast, posted an array of game clips, breaking down the game film without mincing words. The commentary that accompanied some of those clips questioned Murray's on-court effort.

As reported by The Athletic, Murray responded to one of those posts, which showcased footage of him apparently misplacing a pass intended for forward Saddiq Bey. The ball ended up low and away, resulting in a turnover, whereas Bey seemed to be asking for it near chest level.

"Ohhhhh let's talk about it," Murray wrote back on X in a since-deleted post, per The Athletic. "When you run post plays, are they suppose to catch on the block or the 3pt line? Let's hear it Mr social media coach, Gm and President? You have no f***ing clue what we dealing with.

"Maybe you should ask the whole team individually how they feel on a serious note and then … rethink alot of s***."

Before that, Murray engaged with an NBACentral X post that noted how the Pelicans are 3-0 without him this season.

In a quote post, Murray used a slew of crying laughing emojis and then said this, as captured in a screenshot by a social media user:

"From what's going on INSIDE This sounds like someone hit you to say this!! THE 'SABOTAGE' IS REAL!!!!!!"

On Friday, he apparently reposted Charania's report about him wanting out of New Orleans. That repost no longer appears on his X feed, however.

Charania went on "NBA Today" Friday after breaking the news to flesh it out more:

"In the last several days, I'm told, Murray has met with the organization, has made it clear to them that he wants to go somewhere else and play a meaningful, key role on another team," Charania said on the program.

"And we've seen Dejounte Murray, especially on social media, express his frustration with how things are going there. Clearly, it is not working out there. He is not going to be playing basketball in New Orleans for the time moving forward here."

Charania noted that in Murray's absence, the Pelicans will turn over the lead guard position to 2025 No. 7 overall pick Jeremiah Fears. The 20-year-old Oklahoma product has averaged 18 points and nine assists in the three games Murray has missed this season.

Murray's been available for only 54 Pelicans games since he came aboard ahead of the 2024-25 campaign. He suffered a torn Achilles that season, and that injury kept him out of a good portion of last season, too.

Murray's best NBA season occurred in 2021-22, when he earned his All-Star recognition and led the league in steals during his final go-around with the San Antonio Spurs. He also spent two seasons with the Atlanta Hawks. They ultimately traded him to the Pelicans for two first-round picks, Dyson Daniels, Larry Nance Jr., E.J. Liddell and Cody Zeller.
