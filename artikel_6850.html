I Gift This Mature Skin-Friendly Brand to My Mom and Aunts Every Holiday Season

A box of Jones Road will instantly make you the favorite.

I am the ultimate beauty Santa amongst my friends and family. Beauty gifts can be notoriously difficult to give—I mean, there are always questions of shade, hair type, skin type, and scent preferences. And for some, it might feel awkward to give someone, say, a wrinkle cream or foundation, no matter how fancy and expensive it is. But as a beauty editor, it’s basically my duty to give away the best beauty gifts every year. I’ve gifted LED masks, hair tools, high-end skincare, and even the odd designer nail polish or bodycare. Among it all, though, the most excited I’ve ever seen my family get is opening a box filled with Jones Road Beauty.

Created by makeup industry legend Bobbi Brown, Jones Road is makeup, but better. All of the brand’s products perform easily, with minimal fuss, making it a go-to for anyone who isn’t a whiz with a brush but wants to look like one. And it’s particularly fantastic for mature skin, thanks to creamy, moisturizing formulas that don’t cling to texture or settle into fine lines.

While the brand runs its annual Black Friday deal, I’m sharing a few of my favorite products below, with quick reviews. Until November 30, Jones Road is offering 15% off sitewide, and if you spend more than $100, your deal stacks up to 20% off. Is this the best deal I’ve ever seen? No, I’ll be honest. But! I can also say that Jones Road rarely goes on sale, so any discount is a good one. And if you were looking for an excuse to get your mom, aunts, cousins, siblings, or even yourself, I wouldn’t wait, as there won’t be deals like this again until after the holidays.
