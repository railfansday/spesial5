Hunter Schafer’s “Angel Food Cake” Glam Is So Dreamy

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The star paired dewy, fresh-faced makeup with a surprise hair detail at the Blade Runner 2099 premiere in London

Along with serving major fashion inspiration last night at Blade Runner 2099’s London premiere with a custom two-piece ivory Celine set, actress Hunter Schafer’s ethereal and pared-back beauty look also held Bazaar editors’ full attention. Schafer’s lighter-than-air, tousled waves, created by her longtime stylist Rena Calhoun, and delicate, strawberry-toned makeup look, courtesy of artist Sandy Ganzer, channeled “Angel Food Cake glam” for the event’s red carpet.

Schafer’s angelic makeup look was timeless and modern all at once, featuring an elevated take on the clean girl aesthetic. Dewy skin, rosy cheeks, and just-bitten glossy lips laid the foundation for the actress’s latest red carpet glow, as well as barely-there eyeliner and frosted, spun-sugar-inspired eyeshadow.

And in keeping with her effortless beauty theme, the star’s buoyant, windblown waves were anchored by an easy middle part and one tiny detail you might not have noticed: a loosely knotted low ponytail. The playful style is this season’s equivalent of business in the front, party in the back, shaped with a variety of shoulder- and mid-back layers for extra dimension.
