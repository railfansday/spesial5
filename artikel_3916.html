Grand Theft Auto VI tops $3.79 billion in day one retail sales after 32.5 million pre-orders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto VI achieved $3.79 billion in day one retail sales after selling 32.5 million units pre-release, setting a new record for entertainment and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history, Rockstar Games announced.

The long-awaited latest entry in the open-world action adventure game series launched for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19. It currently boasts a 99 out of 100 on review aggregators Metacritic and OpenCritic, making it the highest-rated game of all time on both platforms.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Rockstar Games president and founder Sam Houser in a press release. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
