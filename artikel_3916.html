This week in PC games: go helldiving in Gravhounds, wreck a hoverbike in Airframe Ultra, or feed humans to a cutaway spaceship in Hull Rupture

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Recently, I managed to dupe that preposterous hack Julian into signing a form committing him to alternate weeks of Maw-feeding duty. How he whined last Monday, as we strapped him into his neoprene bodysuit. How he begged for mercy, as we handed him his 12-foot Gallium toasting fork and elbowed him out the airlock.

Still, he's had his revenge. I don't know what Julian fed the Maw last week but he seems to have given it hiccups. I can only apologise to the population of Michigan. I'm sure all the zinc will come back eventually. Anyway, enough of Michigan: let us sit upon the ground and tell sad stories of new PC games. Here are several that are releasing this week.

Things being done in the Treehouse this week: much yelling about end-of-year feature coverage. We have all made our lists and now the lists are fighting, like rival nests of wasps, scorpions, ants and puppies. Yes puppies can form nests, or so Callum maintains. Also happening this week: strategy game coverage, editing of some really sharp freelance features, and hopefully not all that much else, because I kind of just want to play Airframe Ultra. How about you? Please, share the great games I have missed, so that I can spend the rest of today feeling ignorant and ashamed.
