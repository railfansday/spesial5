The World’s Favorite Fanfiction Website Has Finally Returned After A Hack Took It Down For Weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Readers rejoice, Archive of Our Own has returned. The Organization for Transformative Works (OTW) has announced that the popular fanfiction website has been restored after a security incident late last month required it be taken down for emergency maintenance.

[update] Identified: AO3 is now up! Some features have been temporarily disabled to allow us to bring the site back earlier than the four-week mark. At this time, icons across the site (user icons, collection icons, etc.) are disabled, external work importing is unavaila…

— AO3 Status (@status.archiveofourown.org) 2026-11-15T08:28:56.090Z

Instead of the four weeks that were projected, the outage lasted roughly two, with the site coming back in time for folks preparing for the Thanksgiving holiday break, but possibly not soon enough for authors hoping to bank some work. In the site’s absence, several AI-slop-powered imitators popped up, composed of fics scraped from the site before its shutdown. One such site offered to “pay” authors royalties from purchases of AI-generated audiobooks of their fanfics. After a massive outcry that included alerting OTW of possible copyright infringement, the site was shut down.

The shutdown galvanized online fan communities, many of which resorted to sharing databases of downloaded fics to tide readers over until AO3 could come back. But such emergency fandom measures were themselves the source of discussion and debate concerning the ethics of redistributing fandom works outside of protected spaces like AO3, potentially without authorial consent and in defiance of the etiquette that generally exists between people who write fic and those who read it.

Those discussions aren’t going anywhere, but they may take a backseat now that AO3, like Aragorn busting through the gates of Helm’s Deep, has returned. Some features, including the ability to download fics in specific e-reader formats, are still disabled for now and OTW reports that the site may respond more slowly than normal due to everybody and their mom rushing back to get to their Heated Rivalry fics.
