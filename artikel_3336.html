Under Armour, Inc. (UAA -5.21%) reported a 7% decline in total revenue for the second quarter as traffic challenges in North America and a promotional global environment weighed on results. Management indicated that the company is currently executing a multiyear transformation focused on reducing product complexity and improving operational speed through calendar compression. The company revised its full-year gross margin expansion target downward to reflect increased supply chain costs and inventory markdowns. Despite these headwinds, management maintained its adjusted operating income guidance for the fiscal year, supported by cost discipline and anticipated performance improvements in the fourth quarter.
