PlayStation Plus December 2026 Free Games Officially Unveiled

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As usual, the announcement comes via the official PlayStation Blog. While the reveal itself occurs on the last Wednesday of the month, which is today, these new Essential games won't go live until the following Tuesday, which is December 1, 2026. The Essential games for PlayStation Plus in December 2026 are:

Both Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4 (Cross-Gen Edition) and Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 are available to play on PS4 or PS5, but Amnesia: The Bunker is only on PS4. Amnesia: The Bunker is a first-person horror game where you're given a revolver gun and a flashlight as you explore and try to scavenge and craft supplies. Encounters use randomization and have unpredictable behaviors, so players will never know what to expect. It was one of the best horror games of 2023, so it's definitely worth checking out if you haven't played it already.

I reviewed Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4 at launch for ScreenRant and thought the game was pretty amazing. The arcade skater remakes both Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 and Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 4, offering some quality-of-life updates and new content while staying true to the original games. Most of the game is playing through 2-minute levels, racking up scores, and finding collecitbles, but you can also take your skating online and create your own skateparks.

Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 lets you create your own character as you explore Conton City and team up with iconic characters from the beloved manga and anime series. The game also includes online multiplayer modes, where you can battle against others or take part in Raid Battles to face off against gigantic opponents with other players.

In the meantime, if you haven't added November's Essential games to your library, be sure to do so before the rollover next week. Even if you don't plan on playing these anytime soon, you'll still have access to them later on as long as you keep an active PlayStation Plus membership. For those looking forward to December's free Essential games, those will go live on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, and will be available through Monday, January 4, 2027.
