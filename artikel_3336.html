Despite having a one-inch layer of micro coils, the Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper is far plusher than you’d think. I would describe the surface as medium-soft with just the right amount of sinkage for a comfortable night's sleep on your side and back. Special attention has been made to those with back pain, with zoned lumbar support cradling the spine and relieving pressure on sore joints. Alongside some of the best mattresses from Saatva, the Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper is the third mattress topper I've recently tested from this luxury sleep brand — so I thought I knew what to expect.I expected a micro-coil topper to be comfortable but firm like the best hybrid mattresses I've tested. After all, Saatva advertises it as a pressure-relieving topper with “firmer support.”

Imagine my surprise when I realized that the Micro-Coil Mattress Topper is the softest of the three toppers I've tested from Saatva.

The Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper has a soft, contouring surface with an enough give around the shoulders and hips to make side sleeping the most comfortable position, with back sleeping a close second. Firmness is subjective, but I personally wouldn't recommend the Micro-Coil to stomach sleepers, especially those who weigh more than me (180 lbs). For a firmer Saatva topper, I'd go for the Saatva Graphite Mattress Topper.

As a back and stomach sleeper, the topper is almost too soft for my liking, but not enough to prevent a deep night’s sleep. It's a great option for anyone looking to soften up a firm mattress without compromising back support (zoned support from the coils and foam maintain healthy spinal alignment, which is important for back pain).Temperature-wise, I did feel some stuffiness from the foam layer (more so than I did sleeping on the naturally cooling Saatva Natural Latex topper). But as long as I slept in lightweight pyjamas and breathable bedding, I was able to comfortably sleep through the night. If I wore anything heavier, I woke up to some warmth.

Ultimately, the Saatva Micro-Coil Topper is a great choice for anyone looking to soften up a too-firm mattress or prolong the life of a bed that's losing its support. Don't let the micro-coil layer fool you, it's softer than advertised. But if you're a side or back sleeper looking to sample the luxury of the best Saatva mattresses as a fraction of the cost, I highly recommend it.

Micro coils are the newest innovation for toppers so they’re often priced higher than expected. Considering the pricing of Saatva’s other mattress toppers, $315 for a queen Micro-Coil Mattress Topper seems about right for the brand. The topper is shipped for free and comes with a six-month sleep trial and one-year warrantym which is considered pretty a short warranty period for the luxury topper market.

The Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper has three different layers. The top layer is a cotton cover with Guardin botanical antimicrobial treatment. It prevents the growth of bacteria, mildew, and mold, which is helpful considering the cover is not removeable (and thus not washable).

In the centre of the mattress topper is a one-inch layer of recycled steel micro-coils. They’ve been reinforced along the edges of the topper and in the center third to provide support for your hips and lower back. On either side of the micro-coil layer is gel-infused comfort foam with CertiPUR-US certification for enhanced temperature control.

Four straps are placed on the corners to keep the topper in place. Though I ordered a king topper, it did not fully cover my king-sized mattress, exposing about an inch or two on either side of the topper. (This has been the case for all of the Saatva toppers I've tested, which makes me wonder if my actual mattress is larger than I thought).

My biggest concern about this mattress’ design might be the durability. Foam breaks down faster than natural latex and there is only an inch between your body and the micro coil layer. While I didn’t notice any durability issues during testing, the plusher, soft surface of the topper indicates a softer foam that may not last as long as a firmer foam, especially if you sleep in the same spot night after night.

After a week of testing the Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper, here’s what I can share about the ease of set up, comfort, and temperature regulation.

The Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper arrived vacuum sealed and tightly rolled into a ball. Because of the micro-coils, I had to unfurl the topper by hand and place it in a flat position on my mattress. After putting the straps into place so the topper wouldn’t move, I noticed that it didn’t completely cover my king-size mattress. There was about an inch or two of the mattress visible on either side of the topper.

The topper had a lot of creases and bunching because it appears to have unnecessary, extra fabric. By the time I slept on the topper at night, I could not feel the fabric through my sheets.

The off-gassing of the topper’s foams were noticeable as soon as I removed the packaging, and a week later, I can still smell it.

For a mattress topper with a layer of micro coils, I expected it to be firm and supportive. That’s how Saatva advertises it, but that’s not exactly what I experienced.

The Micro-Coil Mattress Topper has a plush, soft surface — I rate it as 5/10 on the mattress firmness scale, placing it as medium-plush. My shoulders and hips sunk in enough to allow for deep pressure relief when on my side and felt comfortably supportive when on my back. As a back and stomach sleeper who prefers a firmer mattress feel, the topper was slightly too soft for my preferences. However, the cushioning and cradling nature of the surface would appeal to most sleepers.

Stomach sleepers will need something firmer, like the Saatva Graphite Mattress Topper, especially those weighing over 180lbs. Comfort-wise, I’d recommend the Micro-Coil Mattress Topper to those who need to soften a firm mattress.

Thanks largely to its recycled steel coils, the Saatva Micro-Coil Mattress Topper does a decent job with temperature regulation. However, I wouldn’t suggest it for very hot sleepers.

I tested this topper during a particularly cold week in Texas in November when the temperature inside my home was between 64-69 degrees at night. Because it was so cold in the evenings, I’d sometimes fall asleep with a sweatshirt and sweatpants. When that happened, I would wake up slightly warm — but not sweating and not hot. I’d then take off my socks or change into lighter pyjamas and be fine the rest of the night.

The best mattress toppers will provide a comfortable sleep feel, regardless of the temperature of your bedroom. If you like the sound of the Micro-coil, I’d suggest light sleepwear and using breathable sheets.

Whether you overheat easily or not, I’d suggest ordering this topper in the summer and utilize the 180-night sleep trial to make sure it’s a good fit for you.

You can search the topper reviews by keyword and star rating. I typed in “micro coil” and only six reviews popped up. There could be more by other buyers who didn’t mention the term “micro-coil” in their reviews.

Five of the six reviews praised the topper for its soft, contouring feel and how it improved their sleep by making their mattress more comfortable and/or its ability to relieve their pain. One reviewer mentioned returning the micro-coil mattress because it made them very hot at night.
