VMI Launches AFM Sales On Manila-Set Action Pic ‘Big Tiger’ With Luke Goss & Katherine McNamara

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

EXCLUSIVE: VMI is launching AFM sales on Manila-set action movie Big Tiger, starring Luke Goss, Katherine McNamara, Byron Mann, and Alden Richards.

The film from action director Keoni Waxman (A Dangerous Man) is produced by Lux Angeles Studios, Birns & Sawyer, and Myriad Entertainment.

The synopsis reads: “A feared enforcer in the Philippine underworld, Chance Ryan (Goss) has spent eight months imprisoned inside the notorious Big Tiger prison facility, where survival is earned through brutal combat. When a deadly conspiracy forces him to escape, Chance returns to the streets of Angeles City, determined to reunite with May (McNamara), the woman he left behind. But May is preparing to marry John Lynch (Richards), the ambitious crime boss who betrayed Chance and seized control of his father’s criminal empire. Big Tiger is produced by Lux Angeles Studios’ Matt Macur, Binh Dang, Biajani Marrero, Mari Acevedo, Damien Douglas, Erica Steele, and Thomas Walton. Rebecca O’Hara, Steven Fabos, Robert Smith, Alden Richards, Lenny Vitulli, Jon Votta, and Vince Votta serve as executive producers. Watch on Deadline

The deal was negotiated by Matt Macur and Binh Dang, producers at Lux Angeles Studios and Jessica Russo, COO Productions + Acquisitions of VMI Worldwide.

“We’re incredibly excited to be partnering with Lux Angeles Studios on Big Tiger. The film delivers nonstop action, and we’re thrilled to be bringing it to AFM and introducing it to buyers around the world. Lux Angeles Studios has an impressive commitment to producing high-quality, commercial productions, and Big Tiger marks the beginning of what we believe will be a strong and lasting partnership. We look forward to this being the first of many projects together,” said Andre Relis, CEO of VMI Worldwide.

“By shooting in Southeast Asia and leveraging Lux Angeles Studios’ integrated production pipeline, we were able to put more action on screen while preserving Keoni Waxman’s creative vision. This is why we are excited to offer our new production and post facility in Thailand to partners like VMI and are honored to have them representing Big Tiger. We look forward to building a strong partnership together,” added Matt Macur, Co-CEO of Lux Angeles Studios.
