U2 Perform Dolly Parton’s Final Recording During NFL Halftime Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

On the eve of Thanksgiving at the NFL halftime show, U2 performed the Dolly Parton and Bono duet, “Torn,” as the country great’s image was projected on screens surrounding the stage.

It was a poignant moment during the show, as the song was the final vocal recording of Parton’s seven-decade career. The track appears on U2’s recently debuted album, Carnaval de Luz. While Parton, who shares writing credits for the song with the band and Snow Patrol’s Johnny McDaid, originally wrote “Torn” around a story of a woman trying to choose between two men, Bono and McDaid rewrote the lyrics around a mother watching her family divided. “You name the location,” Bono told Rolling Stone for U2’s recent cover story, “but everyone knows what it is.… People say in the U.S. now, people are scared to go into Thanksgiving.”

Parton, who died in August at age 80, recorded her part on the album’s closing track in June. “When she got ill,” Bono said, “she got out of her bed to sing this song. And you can hear that. U2’s halftime show during the NFL’s first Thanksgiving Eve game between the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams aired on Netflix. The streamer has been organizing special halftime performances since it began airing select NFL games a few years ago. Beyoncé performed at Netflix’s first NFL game on Christmas Day 2024, while Snoop Dogg took the stage last year.

During U2’s performance on during Wednesday’s show at Sofi Stadium, the band was joined by a massive marching choir and the Womack Sisters, who were recently nominated for Best New Artist and Best Traditional R&B Performance (“If I Let You”) at the 69th Grammys.
