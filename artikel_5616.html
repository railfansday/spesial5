Amazon’s ‘Fallout’ Filmed Inside This Rare Norman Jaffe Estate—and It Was Just Listed for $4.25 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A stunning five-bedroom Long Island estate, designed by Norman Jaffe in 1977 and featured in the popular post-apocalyptic Amazon Prime series “Fallout,” has just hit the market for $4.25 million.

Located in Old Westbury, the 5,291-square-foot home has a well-preserved midcentury modern interior, including a foyer with a 30-foot ceiling and an open floor plan with oversized windows, glass walls, stone and hardwood floors, pine ceilings, and three fireplaces. The sculptural structure has climbed its way to the top of the week’s most popular homes list.

The inviting living room has a dual-sided fireplace and glass doors that open to the rear deck and pool grounds. The industrial-style kitchen has a center island, counter seating, and an eat-in area overlooking the pristine grounds. There is also a home theater for entertaining and relaxing.

The 2.02-acre, resort-inspired lot is surrounded by mature landscaping and has expansive decks and patios with serene views. An in-ground pool with a spa and a two-car garage are also on the property.

Other properties grabbing attention this week include Red Hot Chili Peppers founding member Flea’s California compound, which is returning to the market with a price chop; a Tudor-style estate in Ohio; and two lighthouses in Michigan offered for one low price.

Why it’s here: This affordably priced, 1,800-square-foot “shouse” (shop/house) is designed for a collector, hobbyist, or outdoor enthusiast.

The front of the engineered wood-frame building houses a one-bedroom residence, and the back has a two-car garage. Built in 2012, the building, known as The Roost, even has a cold equipment storage area. The home includes a living room, a kitchen with oak cabinetry, and a bathroom with a walk-in tub/shower. A walk-up loft over the living quarters could be used for extra storage or turned into an entertainment room.

A covered patio overlooks the 3.2-acre lot and its wide open views.

Wes Well of Re/Max Advantage is the listing agent.

The property also has fenced and cross-fenced pastures, newly constructed gates, a tack room/shed, and a garage with a partly finished loft that could become future living or recreation space.

Jimmy Galusky of Railey WV Properties is the listing agent.

Skillagalee Lighthouse sits in the northeast corner of Lake Michigan, between Beaver Island and the mainland. This historic light is also fully operational and has been carefully maintained and updated. The sellers are looking for a buyer who will keep caring for it so future generations can enjoy it.
