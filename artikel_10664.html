Cyberpunk 2 director jokes game is “cancelled” after seeing GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Cyberpunk 2’s creative director has joked that the sequel is “cancelled” after seeing what Rockstar has achieved with GTA 6.

GTA 6 has been making quite a splash since its long-awaited release on November 19, with Rockstar’s open-world game becoming the joint best-reviewed game of all time.

It’s so popular that teachers have had to send round a minibus to pick up absent students, while retirement home residents as old as 86 have also been playing.

The hype for GTA 6 has been so enormous that Rockstar reported $3.79 billion in estimated retail sales by the end of its first day, making it the biggest entertainment launch in history.

As you can imagine, this makes GTA 6 a particularly daunting game for other developers looking to follow in its footsteps. This is especially true for Cyberpunk 2 creative director Igor Sarzynski, who joked that it has given him “GTAVI-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression.”

In a November 21 Bluesky post, Sarzynski praised GTA 6’s scale and attention to detail before joking that he was ready to give up game development and rake leaves instead.

“GTAVI-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real. Probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, I mean it. Scale, density, coherence, thoughtfulness of it all, just a technological and creative miracle. Cyberpunk 2 is cancelled, imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now,” Sarzynski joked.

Despite the daunting task of following up GTA 6 with his own open-world game, Sarzynski remains positive that Cyberpunk 2 will offer fans something different.

“Seriously though, I think we’re doing something of our own, with a different unique flavor. GTA is impressive in a way all great art is, it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed,” he said.

Cyberpunk 2 currently doesn’t have a release date, but we do know that it’s being made with Unreal Engine 5 and entered pre-production in May 2025.
