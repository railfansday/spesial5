Atmos Rewards is the loyalty program for Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, allowing members to earn and redeem points across both carriers. Atmos Rewards points can be used for flights on Alaska and Hawaiian, as well as a range of partner airlines, making it easier to turn everyday spending and travel into award trips around the world.

The program offers three cobranded credit cards: one business and two personal cards.

With strong welcome offers currently available on all three cards, now is a good time to consider applying if you regularly fly Alaska or Hawaiian.

Let's dig deeper into the bonuses available right now and whether they're worth applying for.

The features a valuable welcome bonus, useful earning rates and a solid suite of benefits.

Welcome offer: Earn 80,000 bonus points and a 25,000-point Global Companion Award after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first 90 days from account opening.

Based on TPG's November 2026 valuations, this bonus is worth up to $1,680 when you include the value of the 25,000-point Global Companion Award. While not the absolute top offer we've seen in the card's history, the current lower minimum spend requirement can still make it a great time to apply.

Why we like it: This welcome offer includes a Global Companion Award, which knocks up to 25,000 points off a companion’s award ticket when you book two award seats on Alaska, Hawaiian or a partner airline (including Oneworld members)in any class. The card also offers an ongoing 25,000-point Global Companion Award after each card anniversary.

Cardholders can also earn a 100,000-point Global Companion Award after spending $60,000 or more in a card anniversary year.

For frequent international travelers, earning 3 points per dollar spent on foreign transaction purchases is a major advantage and can help quickly accrue Atmos Rewards points. Matt Moffitt, TPG's contributing editor for credit cards, is based in Spain and uses this card for most of his everyday spending there since those purchases are charged in euros.

To learn more, read our full review of the Atmos Rewards Summit.

Semifrequent Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines flyers who want an affordable annual fee are the target audience for the  a mid-tier option with its own useful perks.

Welcome offer: Earn 70,000 bonus points and a $99 Companion Fare (plus taxes and fees from $23) after spending $2,500 on purchases in the first 90 days from account opening.

TPG's valuations place this offer at $1,120, plus the value of the Companion Fare. Like the Summit, this is a strong offer compared to the card's history considering its relatively easy spending requirements, but not the highest we've ever seen.

Why we like it: The Ascent offers a lower-cost way to earn Atmos Rewards points while receiving practical airline benefits. For travelers who fly Alaska or Hawaiian a few times each year, the free checked-bag benefit and preferred boarding could help justify the $95 annual fee.

The welcome offer also includes a Companion Fare, which can provide additional value for travelers who regularly fly with someone else.

To learn more, read our full review of the Atmos Rewards Ascent.

Small-business owners who fly Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines regularly the is worth considering. It underwent a refresh in Nov. 2026 and now offers new earning opportunities, additional travel perks and a flat annual fee that covers employee cards at no additional cost.

Welcome offer: Earn 90,000 bonus points after spending $6,000 on purchases in the first 90 days from account opening.

The points in this bonus are worth $1,440, per TPG's valuations. Unlike the personal cards, this represents the highest points offer we've ever seen on the card.

Annual fee: $195 per company (with free employee cards)

The refreshed card no longer includes the annual $99 Companion Fare or the $100 Alaska Lounge+ membership discount. Existing cardholders will retain their current benefits and won't automatically transition to the new version.

Why we like it: The 10-point earning rate is a standout benefit for businesses that frequently fly Alaska or Hawaiian Airlines, representing a 16% return on eligible flights booked through Atmos for Business based on TPG's November 2026 valuations.

Free employee cards extend perks like checked bags and preferred boarding to more travelers, while four annual lounge passes and waived same-day change fees add value for the primary cardholder. Together, these benefits make the card particularly appealing to businesses that frequently fly Alaska or Hawaiian.
