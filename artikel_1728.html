Amorim calls Inacio ‘a great player’ but looks closer to home: “Need to look ahead”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Goncalo Inacio has been heavily linked to AC Milan this year, and Ruben Amorim has offered a few words about him as a player.

In the past six months, Inacio’s name has become glued to Milan. The Portuguese defender has been a top-tier target for the club thanks to his ability and the fact that he’s worked with Amorim before and it hasn’t slowed down.

A move was attempted at the end of the summer mercato, but it was too close to the deadline. So, the Rossoneri have always been expected to go back in and a move seems very likely, especially with Sporting open to a sale in January. It’s simply a case of waiting now.

Speaking to Portuguese broadcaster SportTV, Amorim offered some praise for Inacio – ahead of the Rossoneri’s potential move for him in January – but kept his cards close to his chest, as MilanPress relayed.

“Inacio? Gonçalo is a great player; it was a pleasure working with him in the past. In defence, we have Pavlovic and Diawara. Diawara, obviously, is young, coming from the second division; he cost us €3 million, but he’s responding well. So we need to look ahead and consider other players in other positions.”
