Gross a Brighton ‘legend’, says team-mate Chema

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Brighton's Chema Andres has hailed Pascal Gross as a club legend ahead of the playmaker's potential milestone appearance.

Gross' next game will be his 300th appearance for Brighton, who he originally joined in 2017.

The 35-year-old left Brighton for Borussia Dortmund in 2024, but returned to the Seagulls in January and has been a standout performer this season.

Fabian Hurzeler's team sit in second in the Premier League, and Gross has been crucial.

Gross has created 29 chances in the top-flight this term, more than any other player in the competition. He has been directly involved in eight goals, which is a tally bettered by only Erling Haaland and Evanilson (both nine).

And fellow midfielder Chema sees the German as the ideal role model.

"Since day one [at Brighton], I've always watched him train every day," Andres told Brighton's media channels.

"What are his habits, what is he doing, what is he not doing? That is also really important. But the way he treats people, especially young people like me, I think is the thing that surprised me when I came here.

"How he tries to help us, how he tries to give us advice and everything else, because I think he has a lot of experience. He's a legend here in the club, and, of course, I try to listen and learn a lot from him."

Brighton will be out to continue their fantastic form when they face Hull City on Sunday.

It marks the first ever top-flight meeting between Hull and Brighton, and the first in any competition since a goalless draw in the Championship in February 2016.

Only Elliot Anderson has won more duels (74) than McBurnie in the Premier League this season (71).

McBurnie's 41 aerial duels won are third among strikers, behind Thierno Barry (45) and Dominic Calvert-Lewin (43).

Gross' goal against Brentford for Brighton last time out was his 87th Premier League goal involvement (35 goals, 52 assists), the joint-most by a German in the competition (level with Mesut Ozil).

The Brighton man leads the 2026-27 Premier League for chances created overall (29) and in open play (23).

Opta's supercomputer has the Seagulls as the favourites, but Brighton are winless in all six of their visits to MKM Stadium in all competitions (D2 L4). Their last away win against Hull came at Boothferry Park in the fourth tier in October 2000.

Hull won their first two Premier League games this season but are winless in all eight since then (D5 L3). It is the fourth time a side has won their first two games, then none of their next eight in the competition, along with Wolves in 2011-12, Hull in 2016-17, and West Brom in 2017-18, all of whom were relegated.

Their eight-game winless run is the longest in the Premier League this term, along with Manchester United.

Brighton have been leading for 54.3% of their Premier League games this season, the highest ratio of any side.

They have also scored a league-high 15 goals while already leading in the competition this term, and are one of just three sides yet to concede a goal while ahead in 2026-27 (also Arsenal and Sunderland).

Hull rank bottom of the Premier League this season for shots (103), touches in the opponent’s box (182), and expected goals (9.5), while Brighton are second for shots (169) and xG (19.4).
