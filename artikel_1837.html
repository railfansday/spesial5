Spain will hope to maintain their 100 winning per cent record in the 2026/27 Nations League when they face the Czech Republic this evening.

Spain sit top of Group A3 courtesy of their 3-2 win against England at Wembley at the start of their Nations League campaign, enough to place La Roja three points above the Three Lions with two games remaining.

If Spain and England record expected wins against the Czech Republic and Croatia, respectively, then Sunday’s clash between the two teams will decide who enjoys seeded status in the draw for the quarter finals.

The hosts are led by Spaniard Santi Denia, who became the first foreigner to manage the Czech Republic after his appointment in the summer.

Denia’s side were unable to put up much of a fight in the reverse fixture, falling to a 3-1 defeat in Oviedo and seeing Ladislav Krejci dismissed five minutes from time.

Without a win in their four games in the Nations League so far this campaign, the Czechs need to find points from somewhere to stave off relegation to the competition’s second tier.
