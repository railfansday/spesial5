Ramos upset by substitution: The clarification and why Cisse came on over Camarda

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Goncalo Ramos got his name on the scoresheet in AC Milan’s rout of Frosinone, but he was forced off in the second half.

As Sky (via Calciomercato.com) report, everything seemed great when Milan were dominating Frosinone at San Siro and scoring nearly every time they went forward. However, during the second half, a minor alarm sounded.

Ruben Amorim was forced to replace his Ramos in the 50th minute. The Portuguese striker – who had scored in the first half – was hastily removed from the pitch by the head coach shortly after Ardon Jashari’s goal to make it 3-1.

The reason? The change was made due to a minor muscle problem that the player reported to the bench, even though Ramos himself did not seem happy about the substitution at the time of the change.

The former PSG man informed the bench that he had a small calf problem and – to avoid worsening the issue – Amorim immediately opted for a substitution. Alphadjo Cisse was sent on in his place, and he scored the fourth goal just a few minutes later.

When he was taken off, Ramos showed a grimace of disapproval towards his bench, miming the ‘wait’ gesture. He then complained that he would have liked to try to stay on the pitch, but some post-match comments cleared things up.

When asked if his calf is ok, he told DAZN: “Yes, yes, everything’s fine, everything’s fine.” Rabiot later revealed the background to the substitution to Sky, admitting that he played a hand in convincing the coach.

“Gonçalo is the first player to do a lot. Today I saw he had a bit of pain in his thigh. I know he’s a very important player for us, and we have a lot of matches. I even asked the coach to let him rest a bit,” the Frenchman said.

Speaking to DAZN after the final whistle, Amorim was asked why he felt it was right for Cisse to come on rather than Francesco Camarda, the only other natural centre-forward in the squad

“We didn’t spend many minutes near the penalty area; he was behind. Today we were 40 metres from the goal, and Cisse was perfect for that. That’s why I didn’t bring on Camarda for Ramos: I needed someone who could run in behind.”
