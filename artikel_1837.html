‘This is what we dreamed of’ – Pochettino thrilled with USA farewell

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mauricio Pochettino reflected on a dream ending to his United States tenure as he prepares to take over at Manchester United.

Pochettino was appointed as Man Utd's new head coach last week, but he stayed on to oversee the USA's CONCACAF Nations League two-legged quarter-final tie with Haiti.

The USA romped to a 6-0 win on Saturday, and then followed it up with a routine 2-0 success in Cincinnati on Tuesday.

Pochettino, who took over in 2024, bows out having overseen a six-game unbeaten run since the end of the USA's World Cup campaign.

The USA had 14 shots worth a combined 1.43 expected goals, and enjoyed 74.2% possession. Christian Pulisic scored a stunning overhead kick, while Julian Hall netted his fifth goal in the space of six caps; at 18 years and 238 days old, he is the youngest player ever to net five goals for the USMNT.

"The way we played, that is what we dreamed of when we arrived," Pochettino told reporters.

"When you talk about these games, it looks like it was easy. But Haiti is a World Cup team. The players make the job easier.

"We knew that we have talented players, and in the last six games, being unbeaten and playing against a tough opponent, I see the team really enjoy playing football, playing soccer."

And Pochettino has no doubt that the future is bright for the USA, even if he will not be the coach at the helm.

"I am sure that this group of players, they will achieve big things in the future. I have no doubt," he said.

"[Experienced] players with a mix of younger, with the talent that is coming, I think it's a perfect combination.

"The way that they are leading all the things now, I think it's about being proud of the players, the whole squad, the whole pool of players.

"Of course, it's tough because it's emotional, because I really love these guys and I love the players, I love the staff, I love you, the fans and everything.

"And even if sometimes we have not agreed in our way to see things, that is life, to accept the critics, to accept that someone can disagree with you, with your ideas and see things in a different way. But that is life and you grow through there."

Steve Cherundolo will take charge of the USA's final game of 2026 in December, when they take on Jamaica in a friendly, with a new permanent coach expected to be in place for the CONCACAF Nations League semi-final against Mexico in March.
