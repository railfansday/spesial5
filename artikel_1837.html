Man City ‘doing everything we can to fix’ defensive record, says Anderson

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Manchester City goalscorer Elliot Anderson says the club are "doing everything we can to fix" their defensive issues after they came from behind to draw with Fulham.

City had lost each of their last three games heading into the clash with Fulham and went behind in the 16th minute through Rodrigo Muniz's curler.

The hosts then had an Erling Haaland goal disallowed, but Anderson's first Premier League goal for City just before the hour secured all three points.

City are winless in four Premier League games (D1 L3) for the first time since January (D3 L1), while they have trailed at half-time in four consecutive matches in the competition for only the third time, after October 1995 and April 2006.

They have also conceded in nine consecutive games in all competitions for the first time since December 2019 (10), while they have conceded the opening goal in four straight Premier League outings for the first time since April 2012 (five).

Anderson acknowledged that City need to improve defensively, but he believes they are moving in the right direction.

"It's very frustrating," Anderson told BBC Sport. "Going a goal down wasn't ideal, but it seems to be happening.

"The second half was better, more intensity; we pushed higher. We just struggled to get the second goal. We're working on that [falling behind so much]. We are doing everything we can to fix it.

"I was just playing a little bit higher up – focusing on doing the best job for the team. Fighting on the ball and off the ball.

"That's my job on the edge of the box on corners from that side. Try to get a clean connection on target. Trying to stay calm in those situations and make good contact.

"Every game, we are learning things. We'll train harder. We'll be ready for the next one."

City almost got a quickfire equaliser, with Haaland hitting the back of the net just eight minutes after Muniz's opener.

However, Haaland's initial attempt was saved by Leno, who was then deemed to have the ball under control before the Norwegian followed in to bundle it over the line, and while Enzo Maresca did not say much when asked about the decision, he was not happy about it.

"I think it's quite clear," he told BBC Sport. "But they decided something different."

Fulham, meanwhile, are now unbeaten in their last five league games, drawing each of the last three by 1-1 scorelines.

They have now dropped 10 points from winning positions in the top-flight this season; only Brentford (11) have dropped more, but it was an impressive point for Fulham, who ended a 20-game losing streak against City in all competitions.

They defended resolutely against City's pressure, limiting them to just three shots on target out of 15 overall attempts, and Alvaro Arbeloa was impressed with the performance.

"I am happy with a point but would prefer three," he told BBC Sport. "I'm so happy with our performance – how we played and pressed them.

"We came here thinking we can beat them and not just defending. We could have scored one or two more. I'm so proud of the players."

"It wasn't easy [to persuade his players they could win against City]. After the international break, it's never easy, especially coming here.

"I told them we lost the 20 games before; we're not playing those 20 games. If we believe we can beat them. If we fight for every ball and play with character and personality, we'll have the chance to beat them. They believed and played so well."
