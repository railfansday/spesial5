Boars finish short in clash with leaders: Roffey 0 – 2 Haywards Heath

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Roffey faced clear table toppers Haywards Heath, at the Fusion Aviation Stadium on Saturday, determined to gain revenge for the narrow, and perhaps undeserved, loss at Hanbury Park earlier in the season. However, in the end, despite having 14 attempts on goal with 6 on target, it was the visitors who took the points with two clinical finishes from their four on target efforts and two off target.

In the second minute Jordan Mase went down the left then cut in and switched the ball across the box to Luke Harrison whose shot took a slight deflection, taking the pace off it and Ethan McGrath in the Blues goal saved. In the fourth minute Mase again advanced before squaring it, this time to Stan Berry who hit a fierce drive just past the right-hand post, with McGrath probably covering it. Two minutes later Tom Caplin fed Moyo on the right and after his first cross was blocked at the near post he found Caplin 12 yards out but his shot although right in the corner, lacked the pace to beat McGrath.

The Boars continued to dominate possession and after a quarter of an hour, Mase set the ball back for Charlie Gibson to fire over from twenty yards. Next a Gibson corner was deflected up in the air by a defender and Pat Gibs headed it back into the danger area where Berry controlled the ball in the air before hitting a spectacular bicycle shot a foot over the bar. Gibson hit his hot wide at the end of quick break and Berry hit the side netting after good work from Harrison and Callum Kilkenny.

Heath had their first attempt on goal after twenty-two minutes when Sonny Causton was played through, but with defenders converging, hit his shot from the edge of the box straight at Charlie Burton. Back came Roffey and Teddy Wood put a twenty yarder narrowly past the right-hand post. Heath responded with Mark Goldson getting behind the defence, but Pat Gibbs held him up and the Boars got men back with his attempt ending tamely in Burton’s arms. Goldson had another chance when the ball was switched from the right, but he sliced his shot wide.

The breakthrough came on thirty-three minutes when a Roffey attack broke down, and the ball was quickly transferred to Josh Wright who had kept himself onside. Burton advanced to push him wide but Callum Kilkenny who had sprinted back went over the top of the outstretched keeper and Wright managed to squeeze the ball in at the far post despite the desperate efforts of Jack Meeres on the line. Wood replied with a shot that was saved comfortably by McGrath. After all that action the half ended quietly with the visitors having the advantage.

Roffey had the first attempt on goal after the break with Moyo’s shot being taken above his head by McGrath. The game became much more contained with Heath happy to close Roffey down and maintain their lead. The next significant action didn’t come until thirteen minutes before time when Roffey lost possession in their own area but forced Harry Shooman to shoot over. Roffey were still very much in the game, but this changed on eighty minutes as they threw men forward, the hosts were caught on the break and Wright held off Meeres and rolled the ball past Burton to double the lead. Roffey wouldn’t give up but Heath were now able to sit back and soak up the pressure. Gibsons free kick hit the wall and deflected for a corner which was cleared. As stoppage time approached the Boars had a great chance to stay in the game when Caplin spread the play out to Harrison whose cross picked out the same player, unmarked ten yards out, but he scuffed his shot and McGrath saved. There was still time for Moyo to force a save out of McGrath at the near post and the same player hit a shot wide but unfortunately time had run out for the Boars. The table toppers had shown a clinical edge and managed their way to the three points.

Roffey are still very much in the fight for a top five place and it looks like that could be between eight or nine teams and the Boars next match is away to one of them, Hassocks who sit two places above the Boars, on December 1st.

Roffey: Burton, Harrison, Gibson, Napper, Gibbs(Moyo), Meeres, Wood, Mase, Berry(Allen), Caplin, Kilkenny(Hoarty).  Subs: Pearse, Skerry
