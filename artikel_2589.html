Brittany Mahomes has touching moment with Trinity Rodman after KC Current win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Brittany Mahomes has touching moment with Trinity Rodman after KC Current win originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Brittany Mahomes showed great sportsmanship towards Washington Spirit's Trinity Rodman following Kansas City Current's NWSL championship win.

The KC Current defeated the Spirit 2-1 on Saturday, Nov. 21. As players and coaches joined in the Current's celebration of the team's first-ever NWSL championship win, Mahomes took the time to comfort Rodman. In a video posted online, Mahomes, who is a co-founder and minority stake owner in the KC Current, was seen kneeling down to give Rodman a hug, who was overcome with emotion after the loss. Saturday's loss marked the third consecutive Spirit defeat at the NWSL championship final.

"Obviously, this one hurts way worse than any of the other ones," Rodman told ESPN after the game.

"I don't even think you have words in this moment. It's too fresh, but I mean, we're all proud of each other. We're all going to be together no matter what," she continued.

Rodman added, "At the end of the day, I think there's so much to celebrate on the season that we had. It obviously sucks to lose, but you've got to give credit to Kansas [City]. They were very dominant. They were very together and they finished their chances."

While the Washington Spirit were navigating their loss in the final, Mahomes took to Instagram to celebrate

"City of CHAMPIONS!! Could not be more proud of this team and this entire organization! #KCBABY," she wrote in the caption with red and blue heart emojis.

Mahomes co-founded the KC Current alongside Wall Street power couple, Angie and Chris Long, who hold the majority stake in the KC Current.

Vanessa DiBernardo, who was named the game's MVP, reacted to the historic win.

"Yeah, this team was really special," said DiBernardo."I think there's been just a big journey that we went on through the entire year, and I think it's just even more special to end this way. I'm super grateful to just be a part of this group of girls and staff. It just felt like such a family. So yeah, grateful."

Next up for the KC Current is their championship parade and march on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 11 a.m.
