Texans OC Nick Caley weighs in on 'conservative play-calling' vs Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In Week 9 against the Los Angeles Chargers, the Houston Texans' offense was on fire with 44 total points. It looked like they were on their way to another blowout victory against the Cleveland Browns after jumping out to an early 17-0 lead.

However, the Texans' offense slowed down after that, giving up 20 straight points to Cleveland and failing to score again on offense until the fourth quarter. Houston ultimately lost 27-24, making it its fourth-largest blown lead in franchise history. After the loss, there were some criticisms that Texans offensive coordinator Nick Caley started to be conservative after getting the lead. However, he does not view it that way.

"I don't think overall, we just didn't get into a good rhythm. I thought the drive start, we didn't get really off to good drive starts," Caley said Monday. "Couple times we shot ourselves in the foot. Anytime you're going against good defense, an aggressive defense, on the road, I think it makes it harder. None of us did a good enough job starting first and foremost with me and clean it up and we're going to work hard to get that right."

After the loss, the Texans move to 3-6 and desperately need a win when they face the Indianapolis Colts on Thursday Night Football. Indianapolis is also sitting with just three wins, although they are coming off a victory against the Miami Dolphins on Sunday.

Kickoff on Thursday is scheduled for 7:30 p.m.
