The robot boom: Are businesses ready for an AI-powered workforce?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

As artificial intelligence makes machines more capable and autonomous, businesses are racing to embrace robotics. But while many executives expect to deploy robot fleets in the coming years, far fewer have a clear strategy for doing so. FRANCE 24's Charles Pellegrin speaks to John Healy, Intel's Vice President of Robotics and Industrial Solutions, about the opportunities, risks and workforce challenges behind the growing robot revolution.
