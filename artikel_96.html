Israel's opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot has six weeks to form a government

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Israeli President Isaac Herzog formally gave opposition politician Gadi Eisenkot the first chance to form the next government Sunday evening, leaving Eisenkot six weeks to try to put together a coalition to oust Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. FRANCE 24's correspondent in Jerusalem Noga Tarnopolsky has more.
