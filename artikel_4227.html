Old School RuneScape lets you test your mettle with the introduction of Hardcore Worlds

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Old School RuneScape and the whole RuneScape-verse has been going for nigh-on 20 years now, and for those looking for a challenge, there are plenty of bosses and dungeons to face. But now, even the most experienced of players can face a true trial, as today, Hardcore Worlds arrive in Old School RuneScape on all platforms, mobile included.

Hardcore Worlds function exactly as you'd expect them to, but if you're new to the concept, here's a brief explainer: In Hardcore Worlds, death is final. As in no retries, no restarts. Whether it's slow or sudden, if your character perishes, you can either discard them or transfer them to a regular server, though without the rewards or goodies available exclusively on Hardcore Worlds.

And if you're curious about stepping in and giving Hardcore Worlds a go, now's a good time! Because from today, Reaper's Raffle and World First rewards will allow you to win real-world prizes by completing tasks for the Reaper. Accomplishing tasks enters you into a raffle, with plenty of exciting goodies on offer for those who step into the Hardcore worlds.

Off the back of the Road to Restoration, it seemed as if Jagex faced a stumbling block with the community rejection of RuneScape: Reignited. But if the introduction of Hardcore Worlds is any indication, they still have plenty of crowd-pleasers up their sleeve.
