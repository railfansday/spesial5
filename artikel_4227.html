AI simulates electron hops over far longer times than conventional quantum calculations

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

To predict how a material will behave, we need to know which atoms are located where. This allows us, for example, to explain whether a material is suitable as a catalyst for certain chemical reactions. But often, that is not enough: even if all the atoms stay exactly where they are, the distribution of electric charge within the material can change dramatically. Electrons can jump from one atom to another, changing the forces between atoms as well as the material's chemical reactivity.
