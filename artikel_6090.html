Thank You, Specialist Thomas Iglesias

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier didn’t hesitate to shout his admonition to a group of highfalutin visitors, a sterling example of duty and honor.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth awards Army Specialist Thomas Iglesias the Joint Service Commendation Medal at the Pentagon, November 12, 2026.

The secretary of defense visited the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier on Veterans Day, and yet the true voice of military authority on this occasion was an Army specialist.

When the group with Pete Hegseth laughed and began to chatter after taking a photo, the voice of Specialist Thomas Iglesias rang out, “All visitors will maintain an atmosphere of silence and respect at all times!”
