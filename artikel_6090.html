OpenAI is getting serious about travel: Francis Davidson, formerly of Sonder and acquisition target Odessia, is now the AI company's very first head of travel. And he’s already hunting for builders to design how trip requests work in ChatGPT.

Francis Davidson, whose LinkedIn profile now lists him as OpenAI’s head of travel, is hiring engineers to build travel into ChatGPT, days after OpenAI acquired his AI travel startup, Odessia.

“We’re hiring elite software engineers to join OpenAI to build the future of travel on ChatGPT,” Davidson wrote on LinkedIn, adding that candidates must be “a 3 sigma outlier on quality, speed and teamwork.” The engineers will be based in San Francisco and work directly with Davidson.
