Luxury US Startup Bets Big On 10 Airbus A220s With A Private Suite And Just 54 Seats

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Airbus has confirmed Florida-based luxury airline startup Magnifica Air as the mystery buyer of ten ACJ220-300s, the corporate/private jet version of the A220-300. The carrier plans to equip each jet with just 54 seats, including a private suite. This is FAR less than the roughly 150 seats in a typical airline configuration.

These types of operations have seldom lasted long. Remember MGM Grand Air, Regent Air, MAXjet, Eos Airlines and Silverjet?

Not a bad choice. Wonder If Airbus will ever create an LR version to better support Transatlantic flights.
