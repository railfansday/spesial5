Black Friday might be best known for discounts on tech, clothing, toys, and other stuff you’d find at the mall, but some of the best Black Friday deals are on streaming services.

At a Glance: Best Black Friday Streaming Deals

For the last few years, streaming services from HBO Max to Peacock have been offering steep discounts on subscriptions and bundles. These Black Friday streaming deals are usually a discount on your first several months — and they’re well timed to winter binging sessions while the weather keeps us inside. It’s ideal if you’ve been thinking of signing up for a new streaming service, but you can also re-sign up for services you already have with a new email to unlock the discounts.

Below, we’ve rounded up some of the best Black Friday streaming deals that are currently live. These deals include sign-up discounts and free trial offers. All are live through Black Friday, and most will be available through Cyber Monday, but we recommend taking advantage as soon as you can since they won’t last.

HBO Max has always been one of the pricier streaming services, but it can pull it off with the deepest catalog of high-quality exclusive series. Right now, however, you can get HBO Max Basic With Ads for just $5.49 a month for your first 12 months, which is 50% off the normal monthly rate. The Standard and Premium tier subscriptions are not discounted, however.

The Disney+ and Hulu bundle is always one of the best deals in streaming, but Disney just slashed the price of the bundle’s ad-supported and ad-free subscriptions for Black Friday. Ad-supported plans are just $4.99/month for the first 12 months (62% off), and ad-free plans are $14.99/month for the first 12 months (32% off).

One of the steepest Black Friday streaming discounts is from Paramount+, which is offering either of its plans for just $2.99 a month for two months. That’s 79% off the Premium plan and 66% off the Essential plan.

Peacock is also offering a discount on both of its plans. The ad-supported Select plan is $2.99 a month for two months (67% off the regular price), and the ad-free Premium plan is $8.99 a month for two months (30% off).

If you’re looking for a comprehensive live TV and on-demand streaming service, check out DirecTV. The service is currently offering a five-day free trial and introductory discounts on all of its packages.

STARZ is heavily discounted for Black Friday. The service, which is normally $11.99/month or $69.99/year, is running a deal that gets you a year-long subscription for the price of a month ($12).

A good VPN is essential if you’re looking to stream content that’s not available in your physical location. One of our favorites is Proton VPN, which is offering up to 70% off for its one-year plan right now.
