Prediction: Manchester City vs Fulham

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Desperately seeking a domestic response this weekend, Manchester City will welcome Fulham to the Etihad on Saturday lunchtime.

With the rest of England’s elite making their respective returns from November’s international break, there are plenty of attractive Premier League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Saturday’s showdown from the Etihad, you can find all of the key Manchester City vs Fulham H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Manchester City might have enjoyed a sensational start to life under Enzo Maresca, the Sky Blues had certainly come crashing back down to earth prior to November’s international break. Last seen putting in another concerning display as they slumped to a deserved 3-1 defeat away at Nottingham Forest, the former Premier League champions now find themselves sat five points adrift of Arsenal and suddenly have an almighty uphill battle on their hands. In fact, watching a record-breaking Brighton leapfrog them into silver medal position, Man City have astonishingly lost each of their previous three consecutive Premier League appearances. Likewise, despite getting November underway with a rollercoaster 3-2 Champions League victory away at RB Leipzig, Maresca’s men are desperately seeking some kind of defensive grit. In fact, Saturday’s hosts have failed to muster a single clean sheet from any of their previous eight straight matchups across all competitions.

Sustaining a knee injury last month, Manchester City will once again be without former Bournemouth star Antoine Semenyo. However, aside from the Ghanian international, Maresca’s camp have reported a clean bill of health ahead of Saturday’s return.

Bagging nine goals in his 10 Premier League appearances so far this season, Norwegian icon Erling Haaland will spearhead Man City’s attack at the Etihad. Both completing their own respective record-breaking moves to Manchester over the summer, Enzo Fernandez should be partnered alongside Elliot Anderson in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, while Alvaro Arbeloa might still find some pressure on his shoulders in west London, Fulham certainly showed some real signs of life prior to November’s international break. Playing out consecutive 1-1 stalemates against Aston Villa and Newcastle prior to the fortnight interval, the former Real Madrid boss has seen his resurgent squad drag themselves out of the drop zone. In fact, Saturday’s guests are unbeaten in each of their previous four straight Premier League appearances and are enjoying what has become by far their most fruitful spell since Arbeloa’s arrival at Craven Cottage. However, more than aware that they face a monumental challenge this weekend, Fulham hold a nightmare record against the Sky Blues. Including a routine 3-0 defeat when they last travelled to the blue half of Manchester back in February, they have remarkably lost each of their previous 20 straight meetings against the former Premier League champions across all competitions.

While club captain Tom Cairney might have missed out against Newcastle prior to November’s international break after feeling some discomfort in his knee, the 35-year-old could return to Fulham’s squad here. Coming off the bench and bagging a maiden strike of the season last time out, Brazilian striker Rodrigo Muniz could be handed a full recall.

Despite recent speculation over a potential January move away from west London, Nigerian international Calvin Bassey will start at the heart of Fulham’s defence. Completing a big-money move to Craven Cottage over the summer, ex-Southampton youngster Shea Charles will start in the middle of the park.

Remarkably losing each of their final three straight Premier League appearances prior to November’s fortnight interval, Manchester City suddenly have a huge amount of pressure on their shoulders here. Sat five points adrift of defending champions Arsenal, Maresca’s men can’t afford another public slip-up on Saturday lunchtime. However, cruising their way to a stunning 3-0 victory when they last welcomed Fulham to the Etihad back in February, they have remarkably won all of their previous 20 straight showdowns against the Cottagers across all competitions.
