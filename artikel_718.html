Man Utd activate Fernandes contract extension clause

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bruno Fernandes' contract was expiring at the end of this season but it will now run until the summer of 2028; the Man Utd captain and the club are in agreement that it makes sense to return to negotiations in the future, with full focus on improving the club's current position

Sky Sports News Reporter @DanyalHKhan

Manchester United have triggered their 12-month option to extend captain Bruno Fernandes' contract.

His deal was previously expiring at the end of this season but will now run until summer 2028.

United have been in talks with Fernandes over extending his contract since the summer.

Sky Sports News understands both parties are in agreement that it makes sense to return to negotiations in the future - with full focus on improving the club's current position.

Triggering the club option now is logical from United's perspective, as clubs could have approached Fernandes over a pre-contract agreement in January. This contract clause activation prevents that.

Meanwhile, Fernandes was absent from United training on Friday - ahead of their game against Brentford on Saturday - due to illness.

Fernandes is understood to be under the weather and is being kept away from squad ahead of the Brentford game.

On whether he'll make the game, Mauricio Pochettino said: "We don't know. He got a virus in Portugal. He finished the game with a problem and today he cannot be part of the training session.

"We'll see. We are going to se during the day, and [Saturday], how he wakes up. We are not going to take any risks."

Last week, Fernandes exclusively told Sky Sports that United have offered him a new contract - but he has put discussions on hold as he insists the club's priority must be returning to winning ways.

Fernandes has rejected two contract offers from United. The club's initial proposal was made in the summer when discussions first began over a new deal, but was turned down by the Portuguese, who signed his last contract in August 2024.

The United captain said: "I've seen people saying that he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here.

"I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

"Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I've always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it's time for me to move on, I will move on.

"I'm not going to be the player that I've seen many in this time at Manchester United. They're going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, 'Bruno, it's time to part ways', I'm going to be the first one walking out the door.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And there are no negotiations going on. We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract.

"But about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.

"My aim will always be to win the Premier League and the Champions League within the club I am."

Man Utd chief executive Omar Berrada also spoke to Sky Sports last week about Fernandes' contract talks and said: "Listen, I've said before, we have a tremendous amount of respect for Bruno. We're very fortunate to have him as a captain.
