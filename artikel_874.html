Bachelor's Peter Weber Reveals He Married Pregnant Jadeyn Carr

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bachelor Nation alum Peter Weber and Jadeyn Carr privately tied the knot Nov. 11 while expecting their first child after hard-launching their relationship in August.

Peter Weber got his wish of finding a co-pilot for life.

The Bachelor star shared that he and Jadeyn Carr said "I do" in a private ceremony on Nov. 11, following the couple announcing their first pregnancy in September.

As the commercial flyer, nicknamed Pilot Pete, told fans aboard the Bachelor Nation cruise Nov. 17, "I can officially say that on 11/11, I married the love of my life."

The special gathering was made even more magical thanks to its unique wedding date, known for being a prime day to manifest big dreams coming true.

That said, keeping the conclusion of his journey to finding his final rose—which began on Hannah Brown’s season of The Bachelorette in 2019—under wraps was ultimately too exciting to withhold from his loyal fans any longer, as he revealed during a panel on the cruise.

"I was debating of revealing this here, but you guys have all been here, following my journey for a very long time," the Traitors alum explained. "I appreciate that, all the support."

While the 35-year-old, who hard-launched his relationship with the model in August, hasn't yet shared any further details about their nuptials, he did gush about the impending marriage in a cryptic post on their wedding day, when he shared an all-smiles selfie featuring Carr and her engagement ring with the caption, "Marry Me."

Following the affair, Golden Bachelor alum Renee Halverson-Wright gave some insight into the sweet occasion.

"It was the most beautiful, touching wedding ceremony," the 70-year-old replied to Bachelor Nation's Instagram, adding the celebration was "filled with so much love and exactly the way they both wanted it!"

And while the former reality star—who led season 24 of the ABC series in 2020—has kept much of their relationship private, his Aug. 13 carousel debuting their romance showed that his loved ones were already fans of his future bride. Case in point? Weber’s brother Jack Weber commented on his post, writing, "Love this," while his mom Barbara Weber wrote, "Happiness is…" alongside red heart emojis.

Following his time on The Bachelor, Weber dated Flanagan, who appeared on his season of the dating reality series. The two reunited in April 2020, but called it quits that following January. While he and Flanagan rekindled their romance in September 2022, he revealed they were no longer together in May 2023.

Lest the couple forget where their journey began, they need only walk past the master bedroom in their Dallas-area pad. That's where the graphic designer has framed the final final rose that season 17's Bachelor presented her back in that Thai forest in late 2012, right after he promised to tell her he loved her every day of the rest of their lives.

"It made sense to dry this one and make it permanent because it was accompanied by a ring and a proposal, so this is a really special thing," Catherine explained to Entertainment Tonight in 2017. Because that last rose ceremony, complete with an elephant ride and a $75,000 Neil Lane diamond, is as much part of their story as the the sweet, suburban life that followed with kids Samuel, 5, Isaiah, 3, and Mia, 2. "We don't want to shy away from the fact that we got engaged and fell in love on The Bachelor," she said, "so that is the only red rose I will allow in my house!"

The OG Bachelorette, Trista was the first to prove that a stint in the mansion could lead to love, marriage and even Bachelor babies. "Nowadays they shoot the show for, I believe, three months. So it's like 12 weeks," the author shared with Us Weekly of her 2003 season. "My show was shot in six weeks. So Ryan and I met, got engaged, got married a year later, after knowing each other for six weeks. So that's how it used to be."Their decade years holding it down as the show's success story have taught the Colorado-based pair they can handle pretty much anything. Including months sheltering in place with their kids, son Maxwell, 14, and daughter Blakesley, 13. "It's really hard for us to get a date night. I think when the kids are working, we try to take advantage of that time as much as possible," she said of scheduling around the firefighter's early bird tendencies. "But yeah, we're good. We're 16 years and this too shall pass, as they say."

Who even remembers that the sweet divorced dad originally proposed to Melissa Rycroft? Okay, probably a lot of people, as his 2009 After the Final Rose switcheroo still ranks as one of the series' most shocking moments. But if all's well that end's well, the only thing that matters is that the Seattle-based parents to daughter Riley marked 10 years of marriage this past February. "Let me apologize publicly again for putting you through the Bachelor mess that I created," Jason, also dad to teen Ty, wrote in an anniversary tribute. "With that being said... it built an incredible, unique, foundation for us to build our family—showing that we can make it through anything."

In true Bachelor fashion, native Tennesseean Peyton simply knew when she laid eyes on her future husband. Though it wasn't season 10 lead Andy Baldwin. Nearly four years after the naval officer sent her home, the Bachelor Pad alum turned up at another series-related event in 2010 and spied Chris, fresh off his showing as Ali Fedotowsky's runner-up. "It's one of those things where I've had a ton of serious relationships," she later explained to People, "and I met this kid and he was genuine, outgoing and hilarious and we just click."By the following June, the landscaper was proposing at his home in Cape Cod. And more than a decade, two kids (daughter Lyla, 5, and son Hayes, 3) and a few HGTV and DIY Network shows later, they credit the series with laying their foundation. As Peyton put it in an August 2020 Instagram post, "Without the show I'm not sure our paths would have ever crossed."

For as much as the wedding dress designer seemed to key in on Brooks Forester during her 2013 season, it was sweet mortgage lender Chris and his thoughtful prose that proved to be the perfect fit. More than seven years after their L.A. vows (during which the Desiree Hartsock Bridal designer wore two of her own original creations) they're settled in Portland, Oregon, with sons Asher, 5, and Zander, 3. "If our life were a storybook I would be the damsel in distress," the host of podcast Heart of Purpose surmised in a February 2020 post. "Chris would be the funny yet charming knight who saves the day, Asher would be the pragmatic problem solver while his little brother would be the carefree sidekick trying to kiss all the ladies!" And they would all enjoy a happy ending. As Desiree put it in a post acknowledging the choice of Clare Crawley as Bachelorette, "Reality TV worked for me!"
