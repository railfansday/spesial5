MVG still a force to be reckoned with ahead of Grand Slam semi-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Debutant Kevin Doets faces three-time champion Gerwyn Price in the first semi-final before Michael van Gerwen and Gian van Veen renew their rivalry on the big stage; watch the climax of the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports

Michael van Gerwen has shown he is still a force to be reckoned with as he prepares to face fellow Dutchman Gian van Veen in his first major semi-final in over a year.

With Luke Littler and Luke Humphries already at home, Van Gerwen will be looking to get his hands on some TV silverware for the first time since the 2025 World Series of Darts Finals.

Van Gerwen, who threw perfection in the opening leg against Tom Sykes to record his first televised nine-darter on Sky Sports in 14 years, takes on Van Veen on Sunday, with debutant Kevin Doets going head-to-head with three-time champion Gerwyn Price in the first semi-final.

"There has been a lot of criticism about me in the last couple of years, and rightly so, because I wasn't performing. Then you need to make sure you pull your finger out," said Van Gerwen, who traded nine-darters with Sykes on a historic night in Wolverhampton.

"To reach the semi-final is nice, but then to also do it the way I did it today.

"This means the world to me. It has been a battle; I have to work hard, I know that, but then to also show it, it's magnificent.

"I have been feeling good, and to also produce what I am doing, I hope I can do even more later in the tournament. I am feeling good, and when I feel good, I can do anything; I know that.

"I showed some real class and some real ability, and moving on that gives me goosebumps, that gives me the energy, and I hope I can show more tomorrow."

Van Veen defeated 2022 runner-up Nathan Aspinall in a bruising battle to set up a meeting against his Dutch compatriot.

"As a Dutchman, a Grand Slam semi-final against Michael is always a dream coming true, but as you said, the way he played today, it was phenomenal," admitted the former European champion.

"If he does that on Sunday, I'm going to be in a whole lot of trouble, but hopefully he doesn't, and hopefully I'll step up my game and then it's going to be a totally different game and very close.

"Michael played phenomenal. Fair play to him, but as I said, it's going to be a totally different game on Sunday and hopefully I'll be ready.

"I will put 100 per cent focus in that semi-final and then as soon as I hopefully get that job done, then we will worry about the final.

"If you worry already about the final before the semi, you might lose quite convincingly."

Three-time world champion John Part speaking on Sky Sports:

"Michael has handled Gian well this year. He hasn't dropped a match to him. There's been nothing really hugely consequential about any of the matches they played.

"Michael's going to be feeling really good and that's the problem for Gian.

"He's going to have to come out, grind into the match, but he'll hopefully want to spark into the flowing, really better average stuff, the A-game type stuff.

"That was pretty good quality overall for the length of format tonight, but he'll think, ' oh, I can do so much better'. And he'll want to. And against Michael, who better to do it against."

Price will take on Doets with the Welshman into an eighth successive televised semi-final at the PDC this year, although he has yet to break his duck and is without a major victory since 2022.

'The Iceman' is chasing a fourth Grand Slam title and first in five years, but Dutchman Doets is enjoying a brilliant breakout season, claiming his first senior PDC title at Players Championship 13 in May before reaching his maiden European Tour final at the Austrian Darts Open.

He powered past Jermaine Wattimena to reach his first televised PDC semi-final, with his red-hot form impressing Wayne Mardle.

"Kevin Doets won 11 of the last 13 legs against an opponent averaging a ton. That's how good he is," said Mardle.

"I feel really strongly about Kevin Doets' future.

"It wouldn't surprise me if he deals with Gerwyn Price and goes on and wins it.

"He's playing so well so often, and you've got to play good enough often enough, and he does.
