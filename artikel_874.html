James Gunn and Peter Safran Leaving as DC Studios’ Co-CEOS

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Four years after taking up the mantle as the head of DC Studios, James Gunn and Peter Safran announced they are stepping down at the end of November.

It’s a bird! It’s a plane! It’s yet another shakeup at DC Studios!

Four years after James Gunn and Peter Safran were announced as the new heads of DC Studios—overseeing the line up of DC films, TV and animation—the dynamic duo announced they are stepping down at the end of November following the purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery by Skydance. They will be replaced by former Marvel Studios executive Jeremy Latcham.

“Oh, boy. What a four years,” Gunn posted to Instagram Nov. 9. “Thanks to all of you who have supported me and Peter in our time as the heads of DC Studios.”

The Superman director shared it was “a dream come true” to oversee DC’s super-powered slate of characters like Superman, Batman and Supergirl, saying it was truly a “unique opportunity” he couldn’t say no to.

Of course, with the “highest of the highs”—like directing the upcoming Man of Tomorrow and working alongside Safran—the 60-year-old also revealed that only those closest to him know that the past four years have also “been trying, to say the least.”

“I dislike the at-times corporate nature of the job (thanks to Peter for taking the brunt of that),” he added. “I’m always a very hard worker, but as a filmmaker I would at least get to take short breaks to unwind, and l've stolen even that time from both myself and my loved ones over the past four years.”

Gunn also had a special note for the wonder woman in his life, wife Jennifer Holland, writing, “Jenn, I love you and I'm sorry and you don't need to feel guilty for being the happiest of all people that I'm no longer doing this job.”

And while he will “miss the people around” him and the heroes he got to be “co-caretaker” of, he is ready to release a new issue of his career and “get back to pure filmmaking.”

Still, saying goodbye to DC Studios does not mean ending the run of his upcoming projects, as he clarified that he is still working on a secret DC project in addition to Man of Tomorrow and producing Batman: Part II and the TV series The People v. Gorilla Grodd.

As for Latcham, who is taking up the mantle from Gunn and Safran—who themselves took over for previous DC head Walter Hamada—he has a history in the comics world, serving as producer on Iron-Man, The Avengers and Guardians of the Galaxy, which Gunn directed before being fired by Marvel over past offensive tweets.

“The chance to steward the incredible cast of DC characters to the screen in a way that excites lifelong fans while introducing their timeless stories to the next generation is a massive responsibility,” Latcham shared in a Nov. 9 press release, “that I do not take lightly.”

And Gunn and Safran were thrilled Latcham answered the Bat-signal, adding in the release, “We look forward to working with Jeremy to build the next generation of DC storytelling and to doubling down on our commitment to fans.”
