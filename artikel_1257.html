Roy Keane Named England Icon as His Toughest Opponent Alongside Zidane

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Roy Keane is one of the greatest midfielders in Premier League history, but even the fierce Manchester United icon found certain players tough to deal with.

The Irishman played a pivotal role during his 12-year spell at Old Trafford, contributing towards seven Premier League titles, one Champions League success and a host of domestic honours.

Many midfielders from his era of football are quick to cite Keane as the toughest player they ever came up against, with his hardman reputation and underrated ability making him a real handful.

But who did Keane find difficult to deal with? Well, in an interview with Gary Neville on The Overlap's YouTube channel, the 55-year-old named two players as his toughest opponents.

First, Real Madrid and France icon Zinedine Zidane was among the toughest customers Keane had to deal with. The 1998 Ballon d'Or winner was as physically imposing as he was elegant and skilful.

France legend Zinedine Zidane is regarded as one of the greatest players of all time.

He's widely regarded as one of the greatest midfield players of all time, if not the very best. Keane and Zidane famously faced each other in Manchester United's semi-final win against Juventus on the way to the 1998-99 Champions League.

Alongside Zidane, Keane picked legendary Englishman Paul Gascoigne as his other toughest opponent. Keane was just a youngster when he faced Gazza as a Nottingham Forest player, but the meeting was significant enough to still stand out.

Steven Gerrard, Frank Lampard, David Beckham and Wayne Rooney all feature as the controversial top 25 is revealed.

"When I was younger at [Nottingham] Forest, obviously I played against Gazza [Gascoigne],” he told Neville when asked his toughest opponent. "Gazza was brilliant. But overall, I suppose Zidane [would be my pick]."

While he gave the slight edge to Zidane, it's exceptional company for Gascoigne to be in. He represented the likes of Newcastle United, Tottenham and Rangers during his long and storied career. His ability with the ball at his feet was flawless, as Gazza is undoubtedly one of the most technically gifted English players ever.

Gazza also remembers his clash with Keane, but for very different reasons. He recalled telling the young midfielder that he was his 'worst nightmare'. "I remember Roy Keane once; I just used to talk to him all the time," he said.

"I remember going down the tunnel and I said: ‘Roy, you reckon you’re the new kid on the block? Welcome to your worst nightmare.’

"We won one-nil but he said afterwards in the papers: ‘Thank God that game is over with, I’ve never been talked to so much in my life.’"
