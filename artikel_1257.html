Galliani warns Malagò: ‘Juventus, Inter and Milan have changed their stance on FIGC president’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Adriano Galliani has hit back at Giovanni Malagò, claiming Juventus, Inter and Milan are among the Serie A clubs who have switched sides in the race for the FIGC presidency.

With quotes via La Gazzetta dello Sport, the two candidates traded light-hearted jabs on Thursday, with the election now just over two weeks away.

Speaking on the sidelines of a CONI council meeting, Malagò suggested he wouldn’t have run in Galliani’s position. He pointed to the U-turn made by the “technical components”, meaning the players’ union (AIC) and the coaches’ association (AIAC).

“What would I have done in Galliani’s place? Honestly, I wouldn’t have stood, given the U-turn by the technical components,” Malagò said. “But Adriano is a friend, and I’ll never say anything that isn’t affectionate. So I say it with a smile, even if I think this view is widely shared.”

Asked whether Galliani was mounting a late comeback, Malagò added: “I’ve never been chasing in this game. It’s an electoral contest like many I’ve fought, even if there have been some strange positions.”

Galliani, the former Milan and Monza executive, had his reply ready when he was approached leaving the PalaEur. He had been there for Italy’s basketball game against Turkey, which Malagò also attended.

“To my friend Giovanni, I’d say I stood because, as well as the technical components, a U-turn was also made by eight or nine Serie A clubs, including big ones like Juventus, Inter and Milan,” he said with a smile.
