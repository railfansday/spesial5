Real Madrid forward Yan Diomande has issued a statement in response to allegations made against him.

Diomande, 20, has enjoyed a rapid rise to stardom after impressing at RB Leipzig, which earned him a move to La Liga giants Madrid during the summer transfer window.

Before committing to Los Blancos, Diomande was linked with the likes of Paris Saint Germain and Liverpool, but ultimately decided to move to Spain in a deal worth a reported fee of €125 million.

Since joining Madrid, Diomande has recorded four goals and four assists in 18 matches across all competitions, with Jose Mourinho’s men currently second in the La Liga table and first in the Champions League standings.

While things seem to be going well on the pitch, Diomande has been forced to address off-the-pitch allegations that were made against him in recent days.

Taking to social media, Diomande wrote: “The internet allows you to say anything you want from behind the screen to people you would never have had the courage to say anything to face-to-face.

“But don't forget that words have consequences, and the virtual world does not protect you from responsibilities.

“This December, many people will realise that everything doesn't go unpunished and that everyone will have to take responsibility for their actions. The masks will fall.”

While he did not directly address any particular issues, the statement from Diomande seemed to stem from social media users claiming the Ivorian had been lying about his age.

Born in November 2006 in Abidjan, Ivory Coast, Diomande played for the DME Academy from 2022 to 2024 before having trials with Rangers, Chelsea, Crystal Palace and Bournemouth.

Eventually, Diomande joined Leganes in 2025. After scoring twice in 10 matches, German side Leipzig triggered a €20 million release clause in the forward’s contract to bring him to the Bundesliga.

During what was an impressive stint in Germany, Diomande racked up 13 goals in 36 matches, which caught the eyes of many big clubs around the world. Eventually, Madrid snapped up the 20-year-old winger.

On the international stage, Diomande has played 16 times for the Ivory Coast, scoring three goals, and was involved in the 2026 FIFA World Cup in the United States of America, where he produced a number of decent performances.

Although he is facing tough competition at Madrid, Mourinho stated that Diomande should not see his teammates as rivals.

He explained: “It's not about competition, we're all together and we all want the same thing. We have the same objective, there's no room for doubt. It's something that has taken us a lot of effort to build and it's one of the least valued things… but it's one of the most important.

“That the team functions as a group. We're achieving stability and it seems your objective is to create this doubt. Nobody on the team wants that.”
