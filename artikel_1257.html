Tottenham Offered Chance To Sign Ivan Toney, De Zerbi Needs Him

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After signing 10 new players in the summer across all areas of the pitch, you would think that Tottenham Hotspur's squad is in a strong state.

It would be fair to say that there is good depth across the defence and midfield in particular, with Marcos Senesi and Luca Williams-Barnett coming into the starting XI against Ipswich Town on Saturday and producing performances that make them undroppable against Sunderland next time out.

Tottenham have now won three successive Premier League matches for the first time in nearly two years, and they are now in the top half of the table having been rock-bottom earlier in the season.

Spurs are certainly improving but there is still a way to go to get to where they want to be under Roberto De Zerbi, who signed a long-term contract to build a project at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

While the north London outfit have looked strong defensively in recent matches, conceding 11 in 11 top flight matches this term, their goalscoring is more of a concern.

Saturday's double against the Tractor Boys took their tally to 12 in 11, but they need to be better than that if they want to really push on and be in contention for a European spot this term.

De Zerbi has had a striker conundrum all season, having begun the campaign with Richarlison in the centre-forward position, even though the Brazilian had informed during the summer that he wanted to leave the club.

After failing to secure a move away from Spurs, the former Everton attacker remains exiled and on the sidelines, while Dominic Solanke continues to fail to show that he is the consistent goalscorer needed.

De Zerbi has used Omar Marmoush in the striker role too, following his summer move from Manchester City, but the Egyptian international has also failed to fire there, and it's difficult to see exactly where he fits in.

It's becoming apparent that the Italian boss needs extra competition for Solanke, and TEAMtalk report that they have been contacted about the potential availability of Al-Ahli striker Ivan Toney by intermediaries.

If De Zerbi wants a striker who has proven he can put the ball in the back of the net regularly in January, there might not be many better options than the England international.

The report claims that the 30-year-old is weighing up a return to England in the New Year, with intermediaries proposing a loan move featuring an option to buy, if Al-Ahli would be willing to agree to that.

A number of Premier League clubs are said to have been contacted by those intermediaries, including Tottenham, Arsenal, Chelsea and Manchester United.

New Crystal Palace manager Thomas Frank is also keen to be reunited with Toney having worked with him at Brentford previously, and that would probably be the landing spot where he'd be guaranteed the most minutes.

Even though Toney has moved to Saudi Arabia, he has continued to be prolific and he has 16 goals in 17 appearances for Al-Ahli this term.

Such has been his form in Saudi that former Al-Ahli sporting director Rui Pedro labelled him 'one of the best strikers in the world', while his England teammate Declan Rice has also been full of praise for him in the past.

Toney's England call-ups followed on from his performances and numbers for Brentford, and his goalscoring form has continued ever since.

He's kind of like Harry Kane in a way where he's not only an out-and-out striker, he's a playmaker as well - his left and right footed passing, the way he picks out his teammates is a special technique to have.

Toney has proven that he can score regularly in the Premier League, and it will be interesting to see who wins the race to bring him back to the Premier League if it's a move that does become possible in the winter market.
