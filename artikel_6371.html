The Parking Spot Promo Code: Save 10%

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As a New Yorker without a car, I don’t often put much thought into airport parking. However, a few years ago, my mom texted me raving about The Parking Spot, a service available near Connecticut's Bradley International Airport that allows you to park your car near (but not directly at) the airport in a secure lot, then shuttle over to the terminal. She effusively recommended the process for its ease and convenience, and has continued to rave about it since. Its reach extends far beyond Bradley—with more than 70 locations at 45+ airports, it’s a service that’s available across the US for fliers looking to reduce some of the hassle that comes with travel.

Here’s how it works: You can reserve a spot ahead of time on the company’s website or app, securing your place to park and sorting out the cost up front. Once you arrive at the airport and park your car (avoiding the dreaded terminal traffic), you’ll hop in the bright yellow, polka dot-covered shuttle (whimsical!) and head over. While you’re traveling, you can get your car washed or detailed if you so wish, a nice added value. Then, when you return, you can track the shuttle in real-time using the app to get back to your car quickly.

If all of that sounds appealing—or you’re a regular to the service and are just looking for the latest promo codes—read on for ways to save on your next booking.

Fall is a great time to squeeze in a long weekend getaway—or even a full week to yourself, if you've got the PTO days to spare—ahead of the holiday busyness. If you're headed off on a trip, know that for reservations that begin by November 15, you can save 10% or more on your airport parking with promo code FALL10.

An easy way to score The Parking Spot discounts: Sign up for the company's mailing list. New users will automatically receive 10% off their first reservation. You’ll also gain access to 10–20% off promo codes on parking reservations for select cities—right now, that includes Austin, Denver, Columbus, Houston, and Salt Lake City.

Like many brands, The Parking Spot offers a military discount for active duty military and veterans. A less common, though fitting, discount is available for airline crew members. Both groups can get 25% off a reservation with a Spot Club account. As for the fine print, discounts don’t apply to Basic parking reservations and can’t be combined with other offers. Airline crew will need to submit a photo of their work badge; similarly, proof of military affiliation must be submitted to receive the discount.

If a Parking Spot promo code isn’t working at checkout, there are a few things you can do to troubleshoot. First double-check the basics—typos, extra spaces, or incorrect capitalization can all prevent a code from working correctly. You'll also want to confirm that the code is still valid, as expiration dates and location exclusions may apply.
