Gilat Satellite Networks Ltd. (GILT -20.45%) is undergoing a strategic shift toward defense-oriented revenue streams as traditional commercial markets face structural headwinds, launch delays, and customer delivery changes. Management reported that significant growth in the defense segment and improved profitability in Peru partially offset revenue declines in commercial satellite communications and terrestrial infrastructure projects. The company is currently seeking regulatory approval for the acquisition of Comtech Satellite and Space, an initiative intended to expand its United States market presence and increase defense-sector participation.
