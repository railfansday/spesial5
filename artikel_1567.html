Report: Surprise Liverpool interest set to force quick contract agreement

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Surprise interest from Liverpool is set to force a quick contract agreement. The Reds are busy looking for the right player for them.

Liverpool are in the process of weighing up midfield targets as it's increasingly clear that they require a rethink in the position. The plan of simply building around Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai looks to be out of the window already.

And that clearly was the plan. Liverpool handed a bumper new contract to each of them this year only for Andoni Iraola to opt for a midfield pairing of Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister.

It feels like the current situation isn't really tenable - especially after selling Curtis Jones in the summer and not replacing him. It all leaves Liverpool short on numbers and in need of a new direction and we fully expect it'll force them back into the transfer market.

Possibly even in the winter, in fact. Extra midfield balance would significantly increase the chances of Liverpool earning silverware this season.

And that's something which has certain teams scared.

Liverpool look at Ao TanakaTeamTalk claims that Liverpool are one to watch in the running to sign Leeds United's Ao Tanaka. Tanaka's contract expires in 2028 and that has Leeds worried, especially as interest in him grows. Interest that, apparently, does include Liverpool. They're named alongside the likes of Chelsea as wanting Tanaka and potentially making a move in 2027. Leeds, after all, would be forced to sell if they can't agree a new deal. There's no real chance of them letting him go for nothing down the line.

TeamTalk claims that Liverpool are one to watch in the running to sign Leeds United's Ao Tanaka. Tanaka's contract expires in 2028 and that has Leeds worried, especially as interest in him grows.

Interest that, apparently, does include Liverpool. They're named alongside the likes of Chelsea as wanting Tanaka and potentially making a move in 2027.

Leeds, after all, would be forced to sell if they can't agree a new deal. There's no real chance of them letting him go for nothing down the line.

Though, we'd be genuinely shocked if Liverpool was his ultimate destination. The Red part of it, anyway. Tanaka is now 28 and not really of the kind of quality that the Reds would ignore his age to get a deal done.

There's no long-term investment here, in other words, and the short-term wouldn't be good enough to justify a fee. Really the only players in that age bracket that Liverpool have signed within the past 10 years are Wataru Endo and Thiago.

Both were special cases. Endo was needed because the squad lacked a specialist defensive midfielder at a time when Liverpool really needed one. And Thiago was needed because he was Thiago.

Tanaka ticks neither box. If he were completely free, things would change but there's no chance Liverpool are considering potential free transfers in 2028. Instead, we imagine this is actually agents driving up interest to get Leeds moving on a contract.
