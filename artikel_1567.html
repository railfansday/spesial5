“He is aware of having lost his starting place at Real Madrid”: downgrade confirmed for Vinícius Jr!

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Vinícius Júnior’s status has evolved considerably at Real Madrid. Long considered an indisputable starter and one of the main faces of the Madrid project, the Brazilian winger must now deal with increased competition. According to information from José Félix Díazjournalist for A.S.the player is also perfectly aware of having lost his place in the starting lineup. An unprecedented situation for someone who had accustomed the Santiago Bernabéu to its individual differences and its decisive performances. His latest performances, less convincing than those to which he had accustomed the supporters, ultimately contributed to reshuffling the cards in the offensive hierarchy.

However, this loss of status absolutely does not mean that Vinicius was abandoned by his trainer. Quite the contrary. “Vinicius knows and is fully aware that he has lost his starting place. He knows he is no longer untouchable, but if there is one thing he has felt in recent days, it is the absolute respect and support from José Mourinho and the club.specifies, in this respect, A.S.. A particularly revealing passage of the relationship between the Portuguese technician and his player. If Mourinho does not seek to protect Vinicius from the competition nor to guarantee him a place in the eleven, the Portuguese wishes, on the other hand, to allow him to regain his best level.

This approach contrasts in particular with the relational difficulties and misunderstandings which had marked Vinicius’ previous period under Xabi Alonso. According to the Spanish daily, communication between the Brazilian and Mourinho is now excellent. The exchanges are frank, both in private and in public speaking by the Portuguese. The Madrid staff is fully mobilized to find the explosive and decisive player who had made him one of the world references in his position. For his part, Vinicius is working intensely to reverse the trend. The objective is to regain this ability to eliminate, to change matches and to constantly weigh on opposing defenses.

Real Madrid nevertheless has a certain peace of mind in this matter. Extended until June 30, 2032 last summer, Vinicius benefits from a contract which demonstrates the club’s long-term confidence. But his current figures are far from his usual standards: 12 matches played, only one goal scored and three assists. An insufficient record for a player of his caliber, even if his suspension in the championship also weighed on his season. In Madrid, no one seems to want to rush things. The club knows what the Brazilian can bring when he is playing at his best, but now expects him to demonstrate it again. You will have understood, with the recent releases of Auriverde and the rise to power of Yan Diomandé, tenure is no longer a given, it has once again become an objective.
