Keira Walsh adds another Chelsea weapon as former Arsenal target scores THIRD goal of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Graduate Sports Journalist with a love for football.

Keira Walsh is giving Chelsea opponents another decision to make: protect the penalty area or step out to stop her shooting.

Her goal in Sunday’s 3-0 victory at West Ham took her to three for the season across all competitions. Each has come from outside the box.

For a player whose passing helps organise Chelsea’s attacks, that threat adds another way to influence matches. Recent performances show her contributing both to the construction of chances and their finish.

Against Sociedad, Walsh struck from distance after a corner, helping Chelsea complete their passage into the Champions League’s league phase.

The club’s account of that qualifying victory describes a tight encounter in which clear opportunities were limited.

At Manchester United, Lucy Bronze found her near the edge of the box and Walsh drove her finish into the bottom corner.

She also supplied Lauren James’ goal that afternoon, as Chelsea’s match report records. It was a useful illustration of the choices she offers: attempt the finish herself or release somebody closer to goal.

West Ham provided another example of that balance.

Walsh had already struck the post before Alyssa Thompson carried the ball in from the left and found Wieke Kaptein. Kaptein moved it on to Walsh, whose finish gave Chelsea breathing space.

The official report also records an earlier Walsh run down the channel and delivery towards Sjoeke Nüsken.

Her involvement therefore extended beyond waiting for opportunities to shoot. She helped Chelsea approach the penalty area from different positions.

There was a similar reward against Arsenal a week earlier. Walsh’s saved effort fell to Thompson for the only goal, as covered in our report of Chelsea’s derby victory.

That sequence illustrates why a dangerous shot can matter even when the goalkeeper stops it. Chelsea had a runner ready to attack the rebound.

Our assessment is that Walsh’s shooting gives defenders an additional problem. Closing her down can open a passing route; allowing her time leaves the goalkeeper exposed to a player already finding her range.

Walsh has also explained why she feels comfortable alongside Potter.

Speaking after the Arsenal victory in an interview published by Chelsea, she said:

“Lexi is really intelligent. She makes me feel really comfortable when I’m playing next to her.”

Her wider assessment emphasised collective responsibility, with team-mates covering for each other and working to make Chelsea harder to beat.

That matters when assessing midfield influence. Walsh’s freedom to help an attack depends partly on the positioning and decisions of those around her.

Potter subsequently started alongside her in Chelsea’s confirmed team against Lyon, giving their partnership another demanding European assignment.

The encouraging feature is the trust Walsh describes. Bompastor has an experienced organiser working with a younger midfielder whose reading of the game she clearly values.

BBC Sport reported that Barcelona rejected Arsenal’s bid for Walsh in September 2024.

Chelsea completed her signing in January 2025 on a four-and-a-half-year contract. Her official club profile outlines the technical quality and ability to dictate tempo that underpinned that recruitment.

Those qualities remain central to her value. Three early goals strengthen the attacking contribution around them, without establishing that she has changed position or should abandon her deeper responsibilities.

For Bompastor, the opportunity is to preserve that balance: Walsh helping Chelsea progress through midfield, supporting those around her and recognising when the space opens for a finish.
