Birmingham City confirm major boost ahead of Swansea City clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Birmingham City's preparations for this weekend's mouth-watering clash with Swansea City at St. Andrew's have been boosted by Paik Seung-ho's return to training at EPIC.

Blues have won four of their last five Championship matches and sit third in the table ahead of the final round of fixtures before the two-week international break, with Swansea only two places below on goal difference.

Davies isn't exactly short of options at this moment in time, although James Beadle and August Priske were omitted from the matchday squad against Millwall through injury.

Paik had been a mainstay in Davies' side for much of his tenure, having arrived just months before the 41-year-old was appointed two years ago, signing from Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors in January 2024.

2026 has been a tale of frustration for the South Korean though. Having missed a portion of last season with injury misfortune, he hasn't been able to aid Birmingham's impressive start this time around for similar reasons.

The 30-time South Korean international has featured 10 times in all competitions this season, albeit only four of his eight Championship outings have come from the starting lineup.

The last of those came through a seven-minute cameo in the 2-2 draw with Middlesbrough before the extended first international break of the season.

Paik has missed the past seven Championship games after the South Korean Football Association released a statement on the decision to return him to the West Midlands club for "protection."

Earlier this week, Davies revealed the midfielder could be involved in some capacity, and images have now surfaced of the 29-year-old partaking in full training with his teammates ahead of the pivotal weekend showdown.

The former Barcelona academy graduate has also become a mainstay for his national side, featuring three times at the FIFA World Cup in the United States, Canada and Mexico, which landed Birmingham a cash windfall.

However, the Second City outfit will be somewhat relieved to have learned of another scenario involving Paik which emerged this week, even if it isn't the best news for his nation.

South Korea were one of the nations set to be involved in the Asia Cup in early 2027, but the ongoing Middle East conflict has seen the tournament postponed until 13 months' time.

This means Davies will not be losing one of his most experienced figures during the winter months, in which Championship action comes thick and fast and squad depth becomes a major requirement due to the likelihood of further knocks.

The summer recruit was key to Blues' fourth win in five Championship games

Despite his reliability in recent seasons, Paik is no longer a guaranteed starter for Birmingham given the performances put in by his midfield counterparts.

Jhon Solis has impressed significantly since agreeing a permanent move from Girona, and whilst exit talk continues to surround him, Tomoki Iwata has also earned praise.

Max Bird was moved into the '10' against Millwall, a decision which paid off with a goal and assist for the summer recruit, but he could return to a deeper role if Priske is deemed fit due to Jay Stansfield's recent form in the attacking-midfield role.

Paik could still come off the bench and play a key role though, particularly if Blues need to see the game out against Matos' side.
