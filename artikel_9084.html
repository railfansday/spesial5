Art Advisor Sophia Cohen on How the Next Generation of Collectors Is Approaching the Art Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

During during Art Week NYC, a citywide festival organized by ARTnews, Artforum, and Art in America, I was joined by art advisor Sophia Cohen to explore one of the hottest topics in the art market today: the next generation of collectors.Cohen is the founder of Siren, an art advisory and curatorial consultancy. She started her career working for dealer Gavin Brown, who, Cohen explains, taught her about the importance of being with artists of your own generation and how an art dealer can function as an important steward for the artists they represent. When Brown’s gallery closed in 2020, Cohen pivoted to Gagosian (“the exact opposite,” in her words), where she spent six years, eventually becoming an associate director. Cohen is also one of four co-chairs leading the Metropolitan Museum of Art’s Vanguard Council, an invite-only group of next-generation patrons.At our talk on Friday, November 13, at the PMC Studio in Midtown Manhattan, Cohen spoke about the advice she gives to young collectors about when and how to buy, and why it’s sometimes important to make mistakes. She also gave insight into her own collecting, and discussed how today’s patrons are structuring their giving. Watch the video above to see the whole conversation.
