Every Picture Tells a Story

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Hand drawing is arguably architecture's oldest "technology Even as we plunge headlong into the age of artificial intelligence, drawing remains a timeless, intuitive, and powerful medium for exploring. explaining, and understanding both built and imagined environments. A timeless tool for design thinking, hand drawing resonates on a deeply human level. This richly illustrated monograph features over 250 works from the 40-year career of architect Wesley L. Page, FAIA, ASAI, one of the nation's top traditional media architectural illustrators. Rather than a "how-to" book of drawing tricks and techniques, it is a visual journal organized around a purpose-based approach to drawing architecture and how understanding the overlap of drawing, design, and storytelling can profoundly impact the power and effectiveness of architectural Illustration. The drawings collectively represent a strategic approach to both conceiving of and executing drawings-not just drawing for illustration but drawing to achieve a more intentionally targeted outcome driven by story, purpose, and craft. Every Picture Tells a Story is a virtual encyclopedia that presents compelling, beautiful evidence of the lasting relevance, appeal, and storytelling capacity of hand drawing in the digital age.
