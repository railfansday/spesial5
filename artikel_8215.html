Most skiers don’t spend much time thinking about ski lifts until they’re on one. But lifts make a big difference. Faster lifts mean more runs, while older lifts have that vintage charm. Some resorts, like Mad River Glen, still operate single chairs, a relic of a bygone era. As resorts upgrade, we’re seeing more and more lifts with bells and whistles like weather-protecting bubbles and heated seats.

Below are the Top 10 East Coast resorts for lifts, as voted by SKI readers. Factors to consider included lift speed, placement (whether lifts are spread across the mountain or clustered in one area), and functionality. Keep reading to see if your favorite lifts made the list.
