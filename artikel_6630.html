Jensen Huang Traded His Leather Jacket for a Humble Cotton Zip-Up During His Nvidia Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jensen Huang is back in. The leather jackets? They’re out. The disgraced former Nvidia CEO reclaimed the top job at the American tech giant yesterday, returning to a big welcome from employees and even the president himself. (Trump told CNBC that he “made the deal” to get Huang back in as CEO.) But as Huang returned to the office where he became a billionaire, something was noticeably different. His gray hair remained neatly side-parted as ever, but his usual standout outerwear was swapped for something a whole lot humbler: a navy cotton jacket.

Or, sort of humbler, anyway. Subtle as it may be, Huang’s zip-up appears to nonetheless hail from luxury designer Dior. (The label did not immediately respond to a request for confirmation or comment.) The $3,300 number is the brand’s zippered shirt, one that he’s actually worn in the past, notably when he landed at Gimpo International Airport in Seoul in June of this year. (That was the same trip during which he got memed for wearing a leather jacket on one of the city’s sweatiest summer days.)

The 63-year-old, who cofounded Nvidia in 1993, wore high-end black leather jackets as his daily uniform for over a decade. In September, he told CBS News he owned between 30 to 40, from brands like Tom Ford, Burberry, Dior, and Dunhill. At a Stanford economic summit in 2024, Huang offered up a very practical, Silicon Valley-esque reason for his leather preference: They don’t make him itch. But they also felt like an intentional effort to use fashion to break beyond the tech industry’s traditionally geeky image, à la Mark Zuckerberg sitting front row at Prada or Adam Mosseri showing up at the Met Gala in Kartik Research.

Following the revelation of his extramarital affairs, however—most notably with an alleged Chinese spy, as well as with at least two Nvidia employees—the garments took on a new meaning. Rather than evoking the likes of James Dean or Marlon Brando, the lush outerwear instead began to suggest a man in the throes of a midlife crisis.

Huang’s return as CEO isn’t unprecedented. For example, OpenAI’s Sam Altman was reinstated after employees revolted against the board’s initial firing of the CEO. The move does, however, raise concerns about national security and Nvidia’s company culture.

The dismissal of the leather jacket marks a new chapter in Huang’s tenure at the helm of Nvidia. It had always been a talking point, and after becoming a symbol of his fall from grace, wearing it again would risk turning it into his own scarlet letter. By swapping out stiff leather for easy, approachable cotton, Huang seems to be signaling the desire for a reset. As he told his staff during his return: “I’m going to be a better CEO the second time around.”
