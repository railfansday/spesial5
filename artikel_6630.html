A Kitchen in Bloom: Inside Miele’s Lush Botanical Oasis Where Art Meets Design

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Step inside Art Basel as visionaries Tiffany Howell and Asher Young merge cutting-edge technology with untamed botanical romance, staging an immersive sensory landscape you don’t just see, but feel.

Miele crafts appliances for enduring performance, but they also curate experiences that engage all the senses. Imagine the intuitive precision of a sleek induction cooktop that subtly adjusts temperature on its own; the soft, inviting glow of a French door refrigerator that reveals a curated bounty within ample space; the alluring aroma of a built-in coffee system greeting you each morning; and the calming, almost meditative hum of a steam at work. Each of these choreographed moments elevate Miele appliances beyond a functional tool into something you fundamentally feel. By blending groundbreaking innovation and sustainable features with timeless, minimalist design, Miele frees you to embrace the pure joy of cooking and to savor the intimate, everyday moments that make a house a true home.

For this year’s Art Basel, the ultimate intersection of culture and creativity, Miele brings together two visionaries to redefine the culinary space. Tiffany Howell of Night Palm Design is passionate about creating cinematic homes that tell a deeply personal story. Asher Young is a multidisciplinary artist known for merging art with technology to create immersive spectacle. Together, they have imagined a sensory kitchen built entirely within a lush botanical garden setting. Here, the wildness of the natural world frames the precise German engineering. It is a space where the smooth, handleless silhouettes and cool touch of Miele's Obsidian Black glass stand in striking relief against their organic surroundings, harnessing technology for a design that is both tactile and state-of-the-art. “We used the sleek, matte black Miele appliances as the driving force to bring this to life,” says Howell of the striking visual contrast between nature and cutting-edge machines. “They’re like the perfect little black dress—timeless, elegant, and grounding the entire room.” A deep respect for the environment is also reflected in Miele engineering, which uses innovative technology that preserves ingredients longer—ensuring that the natural world isn't just an aesthetic inspiration, but a core priority for the brand.

Howell’s signature nostalgic romance blends with Asher’s analytical thinking and knack for creating physical, interactive experiences to make the space come alive. Young's touch ensures the installation feels genuinely dynamic, translating the unseen power of Miele’s technology into a captivating physical experience. “How can we create a space that rebalances and also is a place that you can actually engage with? So you can smell the flowers while you're washing your hand. You can feel the cool breeze as you open the fridge,” explains Young. “All these elements is not just for the sake of a visual presentation, but actually to experience and be inside.” The result is an experiential reminder that kitchens should always reflect your personality and passions. “We both said let’s bring our shared language together,” he recalls. The finished design is the undeniable heartbeat of the garden, a space with one foot rooted elegantly in the past and the other stepping boldly into the future.

“A kitchen expresses how you live, what you love and desire, and it’s also where your most cherished memories are made,” says Howell. As the Miami sun sets, the botanical space seamlessly transitions into an elegant, moody enclave for evening entertaining, inviting you to linger just a little bit longer. And in a Miele kitchen, those memories continue into the next day, beginning the moment the fragrant aroma of freshly brewed coffee announces the limitless possibilities of a brand-new morning.
