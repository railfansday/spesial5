Who could blame investors for wanting to take all of Thanksgiving week off after the tech carnage we just witnessed?

The S&P 500 (SP500) fell 8.6%, its biggest weekly drop since the April 2025 tariff crash, and it lost ground on all five trading days.

The Dow (DJI) lost 8%, its worst week since the COVID selloff of March 2020.

And the Nasdaq Composite fell 10%, also its worst week since March 2020. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) fell about 17%, its worst week since the one following the September 11 attacks.

The Nasdaq is in the red for the year, while the S&P and Dow are now in correction territory for the first time since Liberation Day.
