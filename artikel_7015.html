Jobgether: Customer Service Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Customer Service Lead based in Nevada, United States.

The Customer Service Lead will support the daily operations and performance of a high-volume customer service team within a fast-paced e-commerce environment. This role combines team leadership, employee development, and hands-on customer support to ensure consistently high service standards. You will coach and motivate representatives, monitor customer interactions, and handle complex or escalated inquiries across multiple communication channels. Working closely with management and other departments, you will help improve operational efficiency, strengthen customer satisfaction, and support customer retention. The ideal candidate is adaptable, proactive, and comfortable balancing team oversight with direct customer engagement.

As Customer Service Lead, you will help managers oversee daily service operations while ensuring representatives have the guidance and support needed to deliver excellent customer experiences.

Lead, train, coach, and develop customer service representatives, promoting strong performance, accountability, and a collaborative team environment.

Monitor customer interactions across phone, email, and live chat to ensure compliance with policies, procedures, and service standards.

Handle escalated customer inquiries, complaints, and complex issues, using sound judgment to identify appropriate resolutions.

Respond to customer requests promptly and professionally across telephone, email, and live chat.

Oversee inbound call handling to maintain efficient response times, service quality, and a positive customer experience.

Research and resolve customer complaints, ensuring appropriate follow-up and supporting customer satisfaction and retention.

Escalate unresolved or particularly complex matters to the Customer Service Manager on duty when necessary.

Collaborate with other departments and customer service colleagues to coordinate solutions and improve the overall customer experience.

Navigate multiple computer systems and applications to research inquiries, manage interactions, and resolve issues.

Stay informed about policy and procedural updates, communicating relevant changes to representatives through ongoing coaching and training.

Monitor team performance and identify opportunities to improve productivity, efficiency, and service levels.

The successful candidate will combine strong communication and problem-solving skills with the ability to guide a team in a demanding, customer-focused environment. They should be comfortable working independently, adapting to changing priorities, and maintaining service quality during periods of high activity.

Experience using Microsoft Word and Excel.

Typing speed of at least 50 words per minute.

Ability to establish clear expectations, promote accountability, provide constructive feedback, and address performance concerns.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-volume inbound call center environment while maintaining quality standards.

Ability to work with minimal supervision and manage multiple systems and applications.

Flexibility to accommodate schedule changes and work overtime when business needs require it.

At least 2 years of customer service, help desk, or call center experience in a retail environment is preferred.

At least 1 year of team leadership experience is preferred.
