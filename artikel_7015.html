Wow, Trump Actually Comes Within Shouting Distance of Empathy During His <i><em>60 Minutes </em></i>Interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Then, of course, the president does something to remind us who he really is.

The president did an interview with 60 Minutes correspondent Ross Douthat. (And may I say on behalf of American journalism all the way back to Zenger, “Oy.”) The president’s mind seemed to be functioning much like a Tilt-a-Whirl at a county fair. At one point, Douthat called him out on his stock-jobbing in office, and the president stayed very much in character, replying, quite douche-edly, “Then you should have owned stock.” Thanks, pal.

In addition, the bulk of the interview danced to the tune of how the Republicans failed him, Donald Trump, ender of all wars, savior of Israel, and god-emperor of the Middle East. This kind of thing grew tiresome at half past 2016 and wasn’t worth watching Douthat trying to keep things on track. But then this happened:

It is for moments like this that the phrase “Wait, what?” was first spoken

This is almost within shouting distance of both introspection and empathy. He’s really feeling for the deported landscapers who should have been allowed to stay so he could hire them and then not pay them. It also proves that he’s been listening to those optimistic voices—which for the moment include Bernie Sanders, of all people—who believe that the president might approve some Democratic priorities as long as he could slap his name on them.
