The Ford Bronco Frostwarden Makes 1200 HP, Thanks to Its Megazilla 2.0 Supercharged V-8

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

This search-and-rescue machine, revealed at SEMA, is what happens when Ford throws everything it has at a Bronco.

The folks at Ford brought one of the wildest Broncos we’ve ever seen to the 2026 SEMA Show this year. It’s called “Frostwarden” ... and it has 1200 horsepower.

How’d Ford extract that much horsepower from a Bronco? Well, it sure doesn’t use any version of the production engines. Instead, Ford put the also-new Megazilla 2.0 crate motor under the hood. This 7.3-liter V-8 is paired here with a 3.0-liter Gen 6 Whipple supercharger; it also has a stronger block, supercharger-specific camshaft, upgraded pistons, larger fuel injectors, and a different belt drive and damper. As a package, Ford is claiming 1200 hp and 1000 pound-feet of torque when run with race fuel. (No, it didn’t quote a pump gas number.)

The idea behind the rest of the rig was to put together a package that could be used as an arctic search-and-rescue machine. To that end, it has a custom Bilstein suspension, 74Weld portal gear lifts, upgraded Dana driveline, and 18-inch Weld bead-lock wheels wrapped in 42-inch BFGoodrich tires. It’s absolutely enormous, and gives off the impression that one could drive it anywhere, which is largely the point.

This Bronco is festooned with accessories front to back. Rigid lights should help show the way through snowstorms. A spare wheels/tire and pair of toolboxes sit atop the roof. There’s a shovel, recovery rope, fuel jugs, and more. Plus, Ford outfitted the interior with color-matching seats and accents. There’s even a “Megazilla” emblem in the steering wheel rim to make sure you don’t forget this beast has 1200 horsepower ready to unleash.

Unfortunately, Ford explicitly says these vehicle “is not available for purchase,” and we suspect something like it never will be from your local Ford dealer. However, it serves as proof that you can shoehorn a 7.3-liter supercharged V-8 under the (admittedly bulged) hood of a Bronco, and what’s not to love about that?
