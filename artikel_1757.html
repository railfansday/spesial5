FA Cup second round draw in full: Leicester City, Luton Town, Wimborne Town learn opponents

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

By Darren Plant | 09 Nov 2026 19:00, Last updated: 09 Nov 2026 19:05

Leicester City have been handed an away tie at National League South team Dover Athletic in the FA Cup second round.

On Friday night, the Foxes came through a testing encounter at National League North outfit Oxford City with a 4-2 victory, and Russell Martin's side will again be on their travels to face a non-league club in the next round of the competition.

Meanwhile, Luton Town will make the trip to the North-West to face National League team AFC Fylde.

Wimborne Town, the Southern Premier South side who stunned Crawley Town over the weekend, have been handed the reward of a home showdown with League One giants Bradford City.

Northern Premier League club Guiseley will host Notts County, while Kidderminster Harriers will make the long trip to Barrow.

The winner of the all seventh-tier tie between Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted - which will be played on Monday night - will face League Two side Barnet.

These ties will take place between December 4 and December 7.
