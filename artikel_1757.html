Hinshelwood targets Brighton bounce-back in Conference League

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jack Hinshelwood says Brighton must fine-tune some of the aspects of their defeat to Hull City in the Premier League when they return to Conference League action.

Brighton welcome Universitatea Craiova to the AMEX Stadium on Thursday fresh from losing 2-0 against Sergej Jakirovic's side late on thanks to Oli McBurnie's double.

The result saw the Seagulls miss the chance to close the gap to Premier League leaders Arsenal, with the gap now six points between the top two teams in England.

However, Brighton are now turning their attention to Europe and securing a spot in the top eight of the Conference League table against their Romanian opponents.

Brighton have won their last four major European games at home without conceding and could become just the third English side this century to win 5+ home European games in a row without conceding after Tottenham (W5 in 2020-2021) and Chelsea (W5 in 2021-2022).

The Seagulls will face a Romanian side for the first time, and Hinshelwood wants to see a response in Brighton's next two games, with Newcastle United up next on Sunday.

"We are second in the Premier League and undefeated in the Conference League. We are a difficult side to play against, and to beat," Hinshelwood told reporters.

"The Conference League is different from the Premier League; teams tend to sit deep against us, making it harder to break them down, which is why we drew 0-0 with Jablonec and had a tough time securing a win against Getafe.

"The performance [in Sunday's defeat against Hull] wasn't terrible – there are definitely parts we need to fine-tune and hold ourselves accountable for – but it's important that we show a good reaction this Thursday and Sunday with two big games at home."

Brighton have kept a clean sheet in 73% of their major European games (8/11), including all three matches in the Conference League this season – it is the highest such rate among sides to play 5+ games on the European stage.

However, they come up against a Craiova side that have made a solid start to their campaign, sitting level on points with Brighton but three places below them on goal difference.

Craiova have won their last two games in the Conference League and will be looking to win three in a row in major European competitions for the first time since a four-game streak in the UEFA Cup in 1984.

And Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler warned his players against complacency, saying: "[Universitatea Craiova] are a good side – they have seven points, just like us – and they play good football.

"The way Craiova plays is impressive; we shouldn't underestimate them. They play with a back three and two defensive midfielders.

"Their wingers are very good, and their striker is excellent. They are strong both with and without the ball."
