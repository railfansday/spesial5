Watch: Antonio Nusa scores in Norway win as Arsenal fans ‘agree’ on £66m January transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Antonio Nusa has been the talk of the town among many Arsenal supporters after scoring for Norway on Saturday.

The RB Leipzig winger is reportedly on Arsenal’s radar ahead of the January transfer window, with Mikel Arteta keen to bolster his attacking options after being left short following the departures of Gabriel Jesus, Gabriel Martinelli, Reiss Nelson and Leandro Trossard during the summer.

Predominantly a left-sided attacker, Nusa can also operate on the right flank and he continued his impressive start to the campaign for club and country at the weekend.

Named in the Norway starting lineup for their Nations League clash against Wales, the 21-year-old opened the scoring just before the hour mark before further goals from Andreas Schjelderup and Erling Haaland secured a 3-1 victory.

Nusa was also on target for Leipzig in their 3-2 loss to Manchester City in the Champions League earlier in the month, and caught the eye in general with his performance.

It is now seven goals and nine assists in 20 appearances across all competitions for both club and country for Nusa so far this season, the latest of which has prompted Arsenal fans on social media to demand a January transfer.

On X, formerly Twitter, one user wrote: “Bring Antonio Nusa to Arsenal”, while another Gunners supporter went one further to suggest a stunning double deal.

They wrote: “Nusa + Kroupi. £170M combined. “Two elite young attacking talents who can transform our frontline and secure the future. Berta, get it done. We are building a dynasty.”

Another user posted: “This guy needs to come to the Premier League to continue his excellent form. Martin Odegaard, I'm leaving this to you as agent. Talk to him, explain the role he will play in Arsenal squad. Antonio Nusa is an aggressive attacker that will cause nightmare to defenders.”

These Arsenal fans might get their wish as it has been reported by TEAMtalk that the club are accelerating their work on a potential left-sided signing ahead of the January transfer window, with Leipzig said to value Nusa at around €80million (£66million).
