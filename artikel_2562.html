David Bailey proving Jets right as he closes in on rookie sack record

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As David Bailey builds his case for Defensive Rookie of the Year, Jets coach Aaron Glenn reflected on the team's decision to select Bailey with the No. 2 overall pick.

The pick was highly debated at the time, mainly coming down to Bailey and Arvell Reese, who went to the Giants at No. 5. Both players were outstanding prospects, but Glenn said the Jets saw Bailey's ability to rush the passer as a separator.

"I'm a huge believer in, you build a team from the inside out and when you have someone of the magnitude that you know has a chance to affect the game by affecting the quarterback," Glenn said, "which at the end, if you can affect the quarterback, man, that makes a huge difference in winning and losing. So, that was a huge part of the decision-making of getting a guy that can affect the quarterback and he is a front-line guy. That actually just went into our philosophy in general and I love the pick. I think he's doing a great job. A lot of things that he can improve on, but that's the reason why."

David Bailey looks on before the Jets' loss to the Bills on Nov. 15, 2026 at MetLife Stadium. Getty Images

Bailey has played very well for the Jets and has a team-best 9 ½ sacks. He is on the verge of setting the franchise record for sacks by a rookie. Hugh Douglas owns the mark with 10 in 1995. He also has a shot at the NFL rookie record of 14 ½ by Jevon Kearse with the 1999 Titans.

Bailey stands out for his play and his style. He does not wear gloves or wristbands and does not celebrate sacks.

"I mean, it's kind of weird when you see it on tape, especially coming out of college because everyone has five towels, everyone has six, seven wristbands on, but I get it," Glenn said. "Some people say you look good, play good, but I guess that's what he looks good doing because he damn sure plays good for us. But he's a throwback and the thing that he wants to, man, just put the ball down and let me line up and let me go play, and I do like that about him."

WR Garrett Wilson was asked about former teammate Aaron Rodgers getting benched in Pittsburgh.

"Yeah, I saw," Wilson said. "No [reaction], I just worry about what's going on over here."
