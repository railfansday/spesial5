Mavericks star Cooper Flagg opens up on why tandem with PJ Washington works

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Dallas Mavericks finally ended their three-game losing streak on Wednesday with a 116-94 win over the Golden State Warriors. Once again, the Cooper Flagg and PJ Washington duo stepped up to lead Dallas to victory despite some early shooting struggles. Flagg acknowledged that his partnership with Washington has continued to work effectively and also explained what makes their on-court combination successful.

During the postgame press conference, Flagg said that consistency in their approach is what makes their partnership effective.

"I just think it needs to look the same every game," Flagg said. "When we're both in that right headspace, right mentality. It needs to be a mentality, make or miss; we have to do all the little things that we did tonight, no matter the outcome of the shots."

Washington also explained his approach to building an effective partnership with Flagg and pointed to Flagg's defensive intensity as one of the biggest strengths of their partnership.

"Just applying pressure," Washington said. "When he's applying pressure on the defensive end, it feels like he gets a steal every other play or a blocked shot. My thing to him is just do everything. Myself, I just try to be as good as I can playing off him and Kyrie. Whether that's rebounding, passing, making shots, just doing all the little things."

Flagg was undoubtedly the standout performer for Dallas against Golden State. He finished with 33 points on 11-of-15 shooting from the field. He also recorded five rebounds, nine assists, and four steals while converting all eight of his free-throw attempts. The 19-year-old has now recorded four 30-point performances this season.

Washington also played a crucial role in the Mavericks' victory, contributing 21 points, five rebounds, and three blocks while knocking down five three-pointers.

Meanwhile, Golden State struggled to generate consistent offensive production throughout the night as Stephen Curry's absence continued to affect the team's scoring capabilities.

Dallas capitalized on the Warriors' offensive struggles to secure an important win and improve to 8-7 on the season. Meanwhile, Golden State also fell to 8-7 after suffering its fourth consecutive loss.
