Confirmed 2. Bundesliga lineups: Dynamo Dresden vs. Greuther Fürth

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Thomas Stamm makes four changes from the 2-1 defeat away to Hannover last time out. Ahmet Muhamedbegović, Simon Straudi, Ben Bobzien and Vincent Vermeij come in for Friedrich Müller, Alexander Rossipal, Jakob Lemmer and Christoph Daferner.

Heiko Vogel names an unchanged side after last week’s late win over Holstein Kiel. League joint top scorer Felix Klaus starts whilst last week’s match winner Aaron Keller remains on the bench.

Dynamo Dresden (4-2-3-1): Grill; Sterner, Rapp, Muhamedbegović, Straudi; Wagner, Raschl; Cović, Hauptmann, Bobzien; Vermeij

Greuther Fürth (4-2-3-1): Hellstern; Dehm, Heuer, Blank, Itter; Will, Bansé; Klaus, Appelkamp, Pohlmann, Sillah
