Lights briefly go out to delay Commanders-Bengals ‘MNF’ game as Roger Goodell looks on

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A brief power outage at Northwest Stadium paused Monday night's game between the Bengals and Commanders.

The outage only lasted a few minutes before the lights returned and allowed the game to continue.

Stadium lights turn off during the third quarter between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders at Northwest Stadium. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

But the "Monday Night Football" showdown was cast into darkness as the Commanders were starting to line up for third-and-14 in the third quarter.

The lights across the stadium went out, causing the refs to halt play.

Commissioner Roger Goodell was in attendance and was shown on the broadcast in a suite after the lights went out.

NFL commissioner Roger Goodell looks on as the lights go out during Commanders-Bengals. ESPN/X

Players look on after the lights go out during the third quarter of the game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders at Northwest Stadium. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"Somebody turned out the lights," ESPN broadcaster Joe Buck said on air.

After the lights returned, the Commanders eventually were forced to settle for a field goal to tie the game at 10.
