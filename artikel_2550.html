As LSU rosters RJ Luis, Georgia AG encourages SEC to punish university - severely

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The attorney general of Georgia called out LSU in a letter to SEC commissioner Greg Sankey on Wednesday, Nov. 18, regarding "an issue of grave concern" after the Tigers added former NBA guard RJ Luis to their roster on Nov. 17.

In the letter, according to multiple reports, Christopher Carr writes that LSU has a "pattern of destructive behavior" with breaking the conference's rules. It's the second time he has written a complaint to the SEC.

Luis, who played in the preseason with the Boston Celtics but never played in a regular season NBA game, went undrafted in the 2025 NBA Draft after winning Big East Player of the Year honors with St. John's. Luis was granted a preliminary injunction on Nov. 13 by a local judge, which prevented him or LSU from being punished for him being on the roster.

The SEC confirmed it would honor the preliminary injunction.

"If LSU does in fact roster one or more pro athletes or demonstrates concrete plans to do so, we would strongly encourage the SEC to take all measures available to address this conduct, up to and including suspending and/or removing LSU from the conference," Carr wrote.

The Luis saga is the not the first regarding an LSU athletics program. The SEC filed a federal lawsuit against LSU in September after the Tigers were set to add NFL signees Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris to their roster. The former Ole Miss standouts were set to play for LSU before the Tigers ultimately decided not to roster them ahead the season opener against Clemson.

While the SEC is adhering to the terms of the preliminary injunction, the conference isn't supportive of the move.

"This is a deeply concerning and disappointing development," the conference wrote in a statement on Nov. 17. "The conference will continue to uphold and defend the standards and policies established by its member universities."

As it stands, Luis is a huge addition for an LSU program that's already having success this season. The Tigers are coming off an 81-80 road win over Gonzaga, led by Donovan Dent, who scored 23 points with 10 assists in the game after receiving a fifth season of eligibility due to court order.

Carr is the latest to express concern with LSU and its recent roster choices.

"Put simply," Carr wrote, "LSU is again attempting to benefit from the legal chaos it helped to coordinate and encourage."
