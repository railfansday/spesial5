NWSL Playoffs: Tactical breakdowns and predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The National Women’s Soccer League playoffs are upon us, with the San Diego Wave and Utah Royals kicking things off this Saturday.

Only three times in the 12 previous full NWSL campaigns has the regular season Shield winner gone on to win the Championship, so the saying that “anything can happen” holds true here and makes predictions tricky.

Nonetheless! In this article, we at The Equalizer will focus on some tactical details and take a stab at how each of the four games could play out.

Utah are a solid defensive outfit, and will likely need to be at their absolute best without the ball to stand any chance of winning this game. They could well match up with San Diego in their basic formation, and may attempt to mark player for player in central midfield, but that aggressive strategy is extremely difficult to execute against the Wave.
