Brent Venables discusses the impact of Kip Lewis, Owen Heinecke at OU

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When the Oklahoma Sooners take the field on Saturday against the Texas A&M Aggies, it will be the last time several key players play in front of fans in Norman. Two of those players are linebackers, Kip Lewis and Owen Heinecke.

They lead the Sooners in tackles this year. Heinecke leads the team with 83 tackles and tackles for loss at 9.5, whereas Lewis is second with 50 tackles and three sacks. Both arrived in Norman in 2022 and have played a big part in turning the defense around. The Sooners were known as one of the worst defensive programs in college football over the past decade. They finished No. 122 in total defense in Brent Venables' first year as head coach. But due to the help of Lewis, Heinecke and several other key players, Oklahoma has become one of the best defensive programs in college football over the last three seasons.

Venables talked about the impact Lewis and Heinecke have had in their time at Oklahoma.

"We're in a much different place than we were four and a half years ago when they got here," Venables said. "We wouldn't be here if it weren't for guys like that and several other players prior to them. I think they recognize the responsibility that they have to kind of be every bit of good of leaders and ambassadors that other players have as well."

Lewis and Heinecke have been part of Oklahoma's defensive transformation, helping establish the physicality and consistency that have become defining traits of the unit under Venables. Their production this season reflects their importance, but their impact extends beyond the stat sheet. Both have helped set the standard for younger players and played a role in building the culture that has made the Sooners' defense one of the program's biggest strengths.
