Umamusume: Pretty Derby to bring its hit concert series to the UK with new event in London

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

With Umamusume: Pretty Derby's planned concert in Los Angeles sadly cut short earlier this year due to visa issues for performers, many of you may have resigned yourselves to one less date on the highly anticipated tour. But, for the first time ever, Umamusume: Pretty Derby's live concert is coming to Europe from January 19th to the 20th!

The 7th Event World Tour: The Stage concert, taking place at the Eventim Apollo, will open ticket sales on November 17th, and features the voice talents of the Umamusume cast, performing in-character with hit songs from the popular multiplatform mobile game. Names such as Chisa Kimura (Daiwa Scarlet), Sumire Uesaka (Agnes Tachyon) and Ayaka Ohashi (Vodka).

But, bottom line, the United States' loss is the UK's gain. And for many European fans, this presents a pretty exciting opportunity to attend the concert, as London is much more accessible than Los Angeles.

With Umamusume: Pretty Derby's live concert experience set to arrive at the Hammersmith Apollo, a venue that's hosted acts such as Buddy Holly and The Beatles, I think it's safe to assume that January will be an exciting time for you to be a fan!
