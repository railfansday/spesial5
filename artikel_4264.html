Genesis Wants to Build and Sell the 1100-HP X Skorpio Desert Runner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

And despite previous plans to limit the vehicle to wealthy customers in the Middle East, the luxury trophy truck could make its way Stateside.

Now that GTA VI has launched, something seems to have gotten into the minds of the executives over at Genesis. The company is actively working to bring the 1100-horsepower X Skorpio concept to production by 2030, Hyundai CEO Jose Muñoz said, according to an Automotive News report.

If you haven’t seen the X Skorpio before now, we’d forgive you for thinking it came straight out of the newest title from Rockstar Games. The batmobile-esque desert runner is built on a tubular frame with an integrated roll cage, and its bodywork is made of carbon fiber, Kevlar, and fiberglass. Oh, and it’s packing a dedicated race engine that produces 1100 horsepower, 850 pound-feet of torque, and a sound that will send all the dogs in your neighborhood scurrying for cover.

Limited production of the X Skorpio was always all but guaranteed. Genesis told Car and Driver that the first orders for the X Skorpio arrived within minutes of the concept’s reveal.

That said, Muñoz’s latest comments directly contradict the brand’s messaging when it first showed the X Skorpio concept earlier this year. When we spoke with Luc Donckerwolke, the brand’s chief creative officer, at the reveal of the X Skorpio in January, he told us outright that there were no plans to bring it to the United States and that it would instead remain a toy for wealthy customers in the Middle East. Perhaps enough American customers with the money to purchase a trophy truck as a hobby have lobbied Genesis to bring the wild machine Stateside.

Those customers will need to be willing to put up with a few drawbacks, though, because even if a production version of the X Skorpio does make its way to Genesis showrooms in the U.S., its design will keep it from earning street-legal status.
