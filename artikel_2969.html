Cowboys injuries: Multiple starters ruled out vs. Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Dallas Cowboys and the Tennessee Titans are heading into the weekend going into two different directions when it comes to injuries. The Titans have gotten healthier as the week has progressed, whereas the Cowboys haven't seen many significant changes in health since the week started.

The Cowboys are about to play two games in a span of five days, so it's going to be a brutal stretch to keep enough able bodies available.

Here's what final injury report of the week says ahead of gameday.

DaRon Bland (Ankle), Tyler Booker (Ankle), Jake Ferguson (Knee/Ankle), P.J. Locke (Concussion), and Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) have been ruled out. Since all have them have not practiced this week, their absence Sunday comes as no surprise.

Luke Schoonmaker (Illness) and Shavon Revel Jr. (Illness) were sidelined yesterday with illnesses. Schoonmaker did not practice today, he is questionable. However, Revel did practice and carries no injury designation into Sunday. Jaishawn Barham (Hip/Groin) was added to the injury report today and he is questionable. Jalen Thompson (Hanstring) is questionable to finally make his return from injured reserve.

Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle) will play Sunday. Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle/Foot), Malik Hooker (Groin), and Juanyeh Thomas (Hamstring) will also play this week.
