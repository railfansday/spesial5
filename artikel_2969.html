LSU president orders RJ Luis benched amid SEC expulsion threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The RJ Luis Jr. drama came to an inevitable and ignominious end Thursday, Nov. 19, as the LSU guard is to be benched by Will Wade at the behest of LSU President Wade Rousseau after playing in one game on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Luis, who played under the protection of a court order, scored 11 points for the Tigers in his season debut against Louisiana-Monroe, which ended in a 79-67 LSU win. Luis was added to the roster Tuesday, Nov. 17, with Rousseau saying in a statement:

"LSU is a member institution that will act in accordance with that order. The conference has confirmed it will do the same and has stated in writing that it 'will abide by the terms of the preliminary injunction while it remains in place and will not impose penalties or fines on LSU if (it) places Mr. Luis on its men's basketball roster."

In a course reversal Thursday, Rousseau said: "In my role as President of the LSU System, after further review, I have instructed LSU Men's Basketball Coach Will Wade to not play RJ Luis."

In a statement, Luis' lawyer Elliot Abrams said there were threats of expelling LSU from the SEC if Luis continued to play.

"The SEC's decision to threaten a member with expulsion if it plays an eligible student-athlete is reprehensible and seriously harms a young person who was caught in the middle of a power struggle having nothing to do with him," Abrams wrote in a statement to USA TODAY Sports.

District Attorney Tony Clayton, another of Luis' attorney's, told The Daily Advertiser, part of the USA TODAY Network, that Luis told him to give up the fight.

Luis declared for the 2025 NBA Draft and was in the Jazz and Celtics organizations, but joined a lawsuit to get another year of eligibility after being waived before the start of the 2025 season.

Wade didn't elaborate on his decision to play Luis after Wednesday's game.

"I'm going to refer to our university's statement from yesterday," he said. "I'll be happy to answer any questions about how he played, but going to refer in terms of anything about his eligibility and rostering him to the university's statement yesterday.

"Everybody who played tonight is eligible."

LSU went through this same situation with football, when Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris were cleared to play by the courts, added to the roster, and ultimately later removed. Expulsion talks arose during that time as well.
