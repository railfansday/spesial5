d-Matrix Named a Top 5 Vendor to Watch in 2026 HPCwire Editors' Choice Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

d-Matrix earns a spot on the 23rd Annual HPCwire Awards list for its breakthrough memory-centric compute platform for AI inference

CHICAGO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- d-Matrix has been named one of the Top 5 Vendors to Watch in the 23rd edition of the HPCwire Readers' and Editors' Choice Awards, presented at the 2026 International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage, and Analysis (SC26). d-Matrix was named to this list because of its innovation in purpose-built inference silicon, which tightly integrates compute and memory to efficiently accelerate AI inference.

"As AI infrastructure investment accelerates, we're honored to be recognized as a vendor to watch by HPCwire," said Sid Sheth, founder and CEO of d-Matrix. "Inference is fast becoming the defining AI workload, and we built d-Matrix on the principle that tightly integrating compute and memory delivers the ultra-low-latency and energy efficient inference needed for AI to thrive. This recognition comes as the industry sees the need for specialized compute that can deliver efficient and fast inference at scale."

The coveted annual HPCwire Readers' Choice Awards are determined through a nomination and voting process with the global HPCwire community, as well as selections from the HPCwire editors. The awards are an annual feature of the publication and constitute prestigious recognition from the HPC community. They are revealed each year to kick off the annual supercomputing conference, which showcases innovation in high-performance computing, networking, storage, and data analysis.

"As HPCwire celebrates 38 years of covering high-performance computing, the combined power of HPC and AI is accelerating discovery and reshaping what science and industry can achieve. Our 2026 Readers' and Editors' Choice Awards celebrate the people, organizations, and innovations driving that progress," said Tom Tabor, CEO of TCI Media, publishers of HPCwire. "Throughout the world, our community is tackling grand challenges whose solutions will improve lives for generations to come. Yet too often, these accomplishments remain out of the spotlight, and the people behind them receive even less recognition for their contributions to society. These awards help bring their achievements to light. Drawing on the expertise of our worldwide readership and the perspective of our editorial team, they recognize the depth, ingenuity, and impact of HPC innovation across the globe. To all of this year's winners, our sincere gratitude and heartfelt congratulations."

More information about the awards and a full list of winners is available here: https://www.hpcwire.com/2026-hpcwire-awards-readers-editors-choice
