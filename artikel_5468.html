EXCLUSIVE: Kylie Jenner’s $38 Million Concrete Mansion Is Back on the Market With a Glamorous New Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kylie Jenner has relisted her concrete fortress in Los Angeles for $38 million after giving the property a complete overhaul.

The reality TV personality, 29, initially put the seven-bedroom Holmby Hills property on the market on Dec. 22, according to property records, just over five years after she snapped it up for $36.5 million in 2020.

The extraordinary dwelling then lingered on the market until June 24, when Realtor.com® exclusively revealed that the property had vanished from the selling block.

At the time, a source close to Jenner suggested to Realtor.com that the property would be returning to the market. In June, the listing came back with a very steep—and enticing—price cut as well as a sparkling new set of images and a fresh property description. Then, just last month, the home was once again removed from the market.

Now, the property has reappeared on the selling block with a fresh set of listing images—some of which have been polished by AI to show potential buyers what the property could look like.

The home, which is listed with Joshua Altman of Douglas Elliman of California, is described in its listing as "one of Los Angeles' most prestigious enclaves."

The living area comes with a cozy fireplace and an expansive view of the backyard, which features a resort-style pool.

The chef's kitchen is equipped with a marble island and seating area, plenty of cabinet space, and state-of-the-art appliances.

The spacious dining area is surrounded by all-white walls and topped by an elegant chandelier.

One of the many entertaining areas features a second bar and a pool table, making it the perfect place to welcome guests.

"Featuring 7 bedrooms, 14 bathrooms, a dedicated guard house, 4-car garage, and 5 additional covered parking spaces, this is the quintessential Holmby Hills trophy estate. A rare offering where grand-scale living, world-class amenities, and absolute discretion converge at one of Los Angeles' most coveted addresses," the listing reads.

Jenner may be trying to replicate the luck she had with her Hidden Hills mansion, which was recently sold for $15.3 million.

Jenner, who is currently in a high-profile relationship with actor Timothee Chalamet, purchased the sprawling 13,200-square-foot Hidden Hills dwelling for $12.05 million in late 2016, when she was just 19. It served as her primary residence for many years.

After listing the home for $20.25 million, Jenner sold it for $15.3 million, Realtor.com exclusively revealed.

Jenner will not be quitting the Hidden Hills neighborhood—where the majority of her family members also reside—however. She purchased a huge plot of land in the area for $15 million in 2020, where she spent more than five years building her dream estate.

Among the features of her newly designed dwelling were a 12-car garage, a guesthouse, a sport court, and a security office, as well as an enormous swimming pool.

As of June 2025, images revealed that work on the project was nearly completed, suggesting that Jenner may have been preparing to move into her new abode full time.

In January, Jenner took her fans on a tour of the nearly completed property, showing off the different spaces and gushing about some of the design details that had been added, including the stone exteriors and the reclaimed wood floors.
