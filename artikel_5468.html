MyRegistry.com Expands Beyond Gifting with New Private Shopping List and Price-Drop Alerts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The new private shopping list lets users save products from any stores, get alerts when prices drop, and keep personal lists separate from gift lists and registries.

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MyRegistry.com, the leading universal registry and gift list platform, announces the launch of Shopping List, a new tool that makes everyday shopping smarter, more organized and more personal.

The new Universal Shopping List gives users a dedicated, private space to add items from any store in the world, revisit them anytime, and get notified when prices drop. Registries and gift lists are built to be shared with friends and family. Shopping List is built for the user alone: a single place to keep everything they want, so it no longer gets lost across browser tabs, screenshots and abandoned carts. The list stays private and separate from users' registries and gift lists, so personal shopping plans stay personal.

"Finding something you love doesn't always mean you're ready to buy it or you want someone to buy it for you," said Nancy Lee, President of MyRegistry.com. "With Shopping List, we're giving our users a smarter, more personal way to keep track of the things they want, without putting them on a public gift list. Whether they're waiting for the right moment or the right price, their next great find is always within reach."

Beyond Gifting: A Smarter Way to Shop for Yourself

The launch marks a natural evolution for MyRegistry.com, extending the convenience of its universal gift registry platform into everyday personal shopping.

As part of the rollout, items previously saved in users' Private category will automatically move to Shopping List, creating a dedicated home for personal shopping finds without requiring users to start over.

With Shopping List, MyRegistry.com is making it easier to go from "I want that" to "Now's the time to buy."

Shopping List is available now to all MyRegistry.com users at no cost on web, iOS and Android apps.
