As 2026 winds down, there's one more thing worth checking off your to-do list: using any remaining credit card statement credits before they expire.

Many premium rewards cards offer statement credits that can help offset their annual fees, but some of these benefits come with a catch: They're use-it-or-lose-it, meaning you won't get another chance to use them once they expire.

If your card offers separate credits for the first and second halves of the calendar year, you generally have until Dec. 31 to use your remaining second-half credit before it resets on Jan. 1, 2027.

Other benefits reset monthly, quarterly or annually (either by calendar year or cardmember anniversary), so it's worth keeping track of when each of your credits expires.
