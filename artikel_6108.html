Singapore Airlines Cuts Elite Upgrade Rewards For 2027—And Halves The Time To Use Several Smaller Perks

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Singapore Airlines is cutting the upgrade certificates its biggest spenders earn from three to one for every 100,000 PPS Value, starting January 1, 2027. Regular KrisFlyer members lose both of their milestone upgrades. And several smaller rewards will expire in six months instead of a year. They’re replacing most of the upgrades with mileage discounts. Those will be more useful to members who couldn’t use their certificates, and there’s an improvement for unused PPS upgrades. But the package reduces the upside of being especially loyal to Singapore Airlines. The airline’s announcement also includes a doubled service fee, ScootPlus awards, and a replacement for the KrisShopper shopping program. KrisFlyer Loses Both Milestone Upgrades KrisFlyer Milestone Rewards are earned by regular, Silver and Gold members based on eligible Elite miles accumulated during the calendar year. Singapore Airlines and Scoot flights count. Partner airline flights don’t, even when they count towards elite status. Eligible spending through Kris+, KrisShop and Pelago can contribute, but only up to 5,000 Elite miles for regular and Silver members, or 10,000 for Gold. Elite Miles   2026 Reward   2027 Reward 1,000   1,000 bonus miles on next Scoot flight + 40% Pelago discount   40% Pelago discount 2,500   20% Scoot discount   1,000 bonus miles on next Scoot flight 5,000   1,000 KrisPay miles   S$20 KrisShop discount 7,500   —   20% Scoot discount 10,000   S$20 KrisShop discount   2,000 KrisPay miles 15,000   —   1,000 bonus miles after qualifying ground purchase 20,000   25% bonus miles on one flight segment   Unchanged 30,000   50% bonus miles on one flight segment   Unchanged 40,000   5,000-mile redemption discount   Unchanged 60,000   Double miles on one flight segment   Unchanged 75,000   Short haul advance upgrade   10,000-mile redemption discount 100,000   Economy to premium economy advance upgrade   20,000-mile redemption discount Rewards through the 15,000 Elite mile level will have six month validity. The existing Scoot rewards and KrisShop coupon currently last a year. Pelago improves from three months to six. Rewards at 20,000 Elite miles and above keep their twelve month validity. The 40% Pelago discount is capped at S$20. The S$20 KrisShop coupon requires a S$50 purchase. The Scoot discount applies to the base fare, rather than the entire ticket price. The 2,000 KrisPay miles are app spending credit, not 2,000 airline miles you can move into KrisFlyer. You have six months to claim them, then six months to spend them. The new 1,000-mile bonus requires at least S$10 in a qualifying transaction. For a Pelago experience, the activity must also be completed within the reward’s validity. The Scoot discount now takes three times as many Elite miles to earn, and you get half as long to use it. Moving it to 7,500 also puts it beyond the 5,000 miles that regular and Silver members can count from ground spending alone. The old certificates required an eligible paid fare and available upgrade space. The 75,000-mile reward was restricted to specified short routes, and the 100,000-mile reward only moved you from coach to premium economy. Solitaire PPS Members Give Up Two Of Three Upgrades PPS Club is Singapore’s separate program for premium cabin customers. PPS Value generally tracks Singapore dollar spending on business class, first class and Suites, excluding airport taxes. Limited ground spending also counts. The 30,000 and 40,000 PPS Value rewards stay the same. The changes above those levels affect Solitaire PPS Club members. PPS Value   2026 Reward   2027 Reward 30,000   Two double miles vouchers   Unchanged 40,000   50,000-mile redemption discount   Unchanged 60,000   One advance upgrade   No reward 70,000   —   Two 50,000-mile redemption discounts 75,000   One advance upgrade   No reward 100,000   One advance upgrade   One advance upgrade, convertible to two 50,000-mile discounts These rewards remain valid for twelve months. The schedule repeats with each 100,000 PPS Value, up to 500,000 per membership year. That cuts the maximum number of upgrade certificates from fifteen to five. An established Solitaire member earning 60,000 to 69,999 PPS Value in a membership year loses the upgrade at 60,000 and gets no replacement. Someone who reaches 100,000 gets three separate 50,000-mile discounts plus one upgrade, or five discounts if they convert that upgrade. Those successful at turning eligible paid business class fares into first class or Suites lose valuable opportunities. Unused PPS Upgrades Get A Second Life From January 1, a still-valid, unused PPS advance upgrade can be converted into two separate 50,000-mile redemption discounts. If you leave it unused, conversion happens automatically at expiry. The replacement discounts get a fresh twelve months. That includes eligible certificates earned in 2026 at 60,000, 75,000 and 100,000 PPS Value. This doesn’t apply to the short haul and premium economy upgrades earned through ordinary KrisFlyer Milestone Rewards. Those still need to be used before expiry. Higher Fees, ScootPlus Awards And A Shopping Program Replacement Starting December 1, 2026, the offline service charge doubles from US$25 or 2,500 miles to US$50 or 5,000 miles. It applies to transactions handled by phone or an airline office. PPS Club members and supplementary cardholders are exempt, as are transactions that can’t be performed online. This is an additional service charge, separate from applicable change or redeposit fees. ScootPlus awards are coming in early 2027, bookable through Scoot’s website or app. Prices haven’t been announced. Meanwhile, KrisShopper will close in January, with members automatically enrolled in a new program covering KrisShop, Kris+ and Pelago. Existing vouchers remain valid until their expiration dates, and KrisFlyer miles and status credits already earned remain in members’ accounts.
