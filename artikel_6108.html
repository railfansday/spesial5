How Proactive AI Is Changing the Airline Customer Experience

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Erik Zahnlecker, Industry Lead, Travel, Sierra

Moderated by Alison McCarthy, Content Director, Skift Studio

Passengers remember a well-handled disruption far more than an on-time flight, Sierra’s Erik Zahnlecker said, and AI agents can now give every traveler an immediate response. The same agents can use what they learn from passenger interactions to build relationships between trips and drive bookings, upgrades, and ancillary revenue that traditional marketing can’t match.
