Mexico’s President Spent 21 Hours In Coach Getting To China—And Sat In A Middle Seat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mexico’s President Claudia Sheinbaum spent about 21 hours getting to China in economy on China Southern Airlines. She reportedly sat in 33B. A middle seat, on a trip across the Pacific, to represent her country at a summit of world leaders. That’s quite a contrast with Donald Trump’s enthusiasm for accepting a Boeing 747 in royal configuration from Qatar. But while Sheinbaum is winning praise for saving taxpayers money, she should have flown business class. The 64 year old president traveled from Mexico City through Tijuana to Shenzhen for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit. Here’s her departure update: Claudia Sheinbaum’s update on her trip to Shenzhen, November 15, 2026 21 Hours Includes The Stop In Tijuana She left Mexico City early Sunday, November 15, on China Southern flight CZ8032 and arrived in Shenzhen on Monday. The Airbus A350-900 made its scheduled technical stop in Tijuana along the way. The first flight took 3 hours 12 minutes and the transpacific segment clocked in at 14 hours 56 minutes, with about two and a half hours on the ground between them. China Southern operates the route nonstop from Shenzhen to Mexico City, with the Tijuana stop in the other direction. Several stories report that seat 33B is in the back of the aircraft. In fact, it’s the front of standard economy. On arrival she posted video of her welcome in Shenzhen. An airport bus brought her to the red carpet, where an honor guard and performers waited. She received a presidential welcome. Which reminds me a little bit of when I’ve booked first class awards on Lufthansa, but the intra-European segment was only available in coach. Nobody ever could quite figure out how a coach passenger had a vehicle waiting for them on the tarmac.
