Virginia child dies in state’s first measles-associated death of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Death adds to national measles outbreak that recently cost the US its measles elimination status after 26 years

Virginia reported its first measles-associated death of 2026 ⁠on Friday, involving a child who tested positive for the infectious disease, the state’s health department said.

The death adds to a worsening national measles outbreak that recently cost the US its measles elimination status after 26 years.

Pennsylvania, the hardest-hit US state, reported an eighth measles-related death and confirmed 1,631 measles cases as of Friday.

Wisconsin has ⁠also reported two deaths, while New York state recorded its first measles-associated ​death, involving a ​child.

The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has confirmed six ​measles-associated deaths so far in 2026, though it does not ​specify the states ‌where the deaths were ​recorded.

A discrepancy between ​state and federal death tallies arose after the CDC, under the Trump administration’s health secretary, Robert F Kennedy Jr, began using National Center for Health Statistics mortality data instead of state figures.

Virginia has reported 220 measles ⁠cases so far in 2026, including 40 among children four years old or younger and 75 among those age five to 12, state ⁠health department data showed as of Thursday.

Of those cases, 179 involved unvaccinated individuals, 39 were associated with people ‌whose vaccination status was ‌unknown and two occurred with vaccinated individuals.

“Virginia has not had a measles-related death ‌since 1978,” said Frederic Bertley, immunologist and chief executive officer of the Center of Science and Industry. Bertley added that the measles virus infection is both alarming and evidence that measles is gaining a foothold and becoming a growing problem for the US.

Virginia health officials have reported increased measles activity driven largely by outbreaks ‌in the state’s central region. Buckingham county has reported 127 cases, the most in the state, while neighboring Charlotte county has recorded 34 ​cases.
