Taylor Swift Sits With Karlie Kloss at Kansas City Chiefs Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Taylor Swift appeared at a Kansas City Chiefs game for the first time in nearly a month as the team attempted to right the ship following a series of losses — and she brought a friend.

Swift, 36, took to her usual perch at Arrowhead Stadium to watch the Chiefs take on the New York Jets on Sunday, November 8, this time joined by longtime BFF (and Missouri native) Karlie Kloss, per social media images. The pop star last attended a game on October 18, when the Chiefs defeated the Los Angeles Chargers —  their last victory.

Since then, Kansas City has dropped road games to the Seattle Seahawks and Denver Broncos. Swift did not attend either loss, continuing her trend of not traveling with her husband, Travis Kelce, and the team on road trips.

Swift, who has been tight with Kloss for years and even invited the supermodel to her wedding, has attended all three Kansas City home games this season, all Chiefs wins.

Kelce, 37, addressed the Chiefs’ losing streak after the team’s loss to the Broncos on November 1.

“You know, obviously, in our hearts, we should have come out with a victory, but at the same time, you know, that’s not the reality of things,” Kelce told reporters. “The reality of things is we gotta fix a lot, and the little things — mistakes — it starts with the person that you’re looking [at] in the mirror, and I’m one of them.”

Kelce had three catches for 71 yards in the Broncos loss. He has not caught a touchdown pass since Week 3 against the Miami Dolphins on September 27.

The tight end added, “I’ve been frustrated with myself on how long I’ve been in the league and the procedural stuff that’s happened for the past three games really, since the bye [week]. It’s frustrating. You just can’t f***ing do it, and excuse my language. I gotta be better for my teammates, and that sets the tone for a lot of people in this room, and everybody’s just got to up the ante from that.”

Up next, the Chiefs hit the road to battle the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday, November 15.
