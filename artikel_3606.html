GTA 6 Is Crashing On Some PS5s With CE-108255-1 Error Code

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After years of waiting, GTA fans can finally play GTA 6, but some players are encountering a dreaded error code that makes the game crash or otherwise fail.

Users are reporting receiving the CE-108255-1 error code, which players might see when a game crashes mid-session or fails to load entirely. Crashing mid-game in GTA 6 would be particularly frustrating given how saves work.

Sony's official word is that players can try updating their game to fix the issue. If it's really that simple, that would be a welcome result. But people are saying that doesn't always work and are asking Rockstar to put out an official patch.

Reddit user Acrobatic-Promise458 encountered this issue and documented their experience. The person said the game was working normally until they reached a certain point in the story, at which point the game crashes continually. They said Rockstar support directed them to Sony's own support team, but it was no use.

People online (via Gizmodo) are theorizing that the crashing issues with GTA 6 on PS5 might be due to the game's intense demands on the hardware, and it could be specifically tied to older PS5s.

Rockstar has yet to officially respond to these reports. Rockstar's official GTA 6 error troubleshooting page isn't much help, as it only links to the support sites for PlayStation and Xbox and offers no specific help. Players can submit a support ticket to Rockstar to get more help with this issue or others.

These issues do not appear to be very widespread, but if you're experiencing the CE-108255-1 error on PS5, that's no consolation to you.

GTA 6 has reportedly sold 25 million copies and earned more than $2 billion in revenue already, and those numbers are only expected to grow higher.
