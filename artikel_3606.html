Chatting With People In GTA Online About Why They’re Not Playing GTA 6: ‘$80 Is Too Much For Me, Bro’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grand Theft Auto 6 is the biggest video game on console right now. Everyone in my life, even people who don’t normally play these kinds of games, is booting up GTA 6 and spending hours exploring Vice City. But what about the people still playing GTA Online? What do they think of the newest entry which, for the time being, remains single-player? I dropped back into Rockstar’s existing multiplayer juggernaut a few days after GTA 6‘s launch to check out the vibes and ask people how they were feeling. The answers varied, but a surprisingly common response was that they were still playing GTA Online because they just couldn’t afford to buy GTA 6 right now.

GTA 5‘s live-service platform is still be very popular in 2026, but when I returned recently following the launch of GTA 6, it was definitely emptier than usual. I hopped into different sessions looking for players who were actually chatting on the mic, and found most lobbies to not be anywhere near close to full. In fact, many had only a few players hanging out in lobbies that support up to 30. I also ended up back in the same sessions again and again, which is not something that usually happens in GTA Online. And the sessions I landed in were filled with mostly low-level players and kids. A lot of the high-level kings and queens of GTA Online were missing. I assume they were chilling in Vice City.

One of the first people I spoke to was one of these GTA Online kids. They sounded no older than 12, and when I asked if they had played GTA 6, they told me no. I asked why and was told: “It sucks, bro.” I asked for more details, pretending I had yet to play it and was curious about it.

At one point, the younger kid almost sounded like they were crying. It was awkward. Later, I ended up back in that session, and the kid who may have been crying was still very mad. Nobody else was talking in that session, so I hopped to a new one.

GTA Online is a community of players who will kill you in a heartbeat with a giant tank or flying car, but won’t ever say a single word to you before, during, or after. Shy little murderers. It’s been like this for years. So as I hopped from session to session, I also texted players via the in-game phone. It’s clunky, but I did get a few replies from people more willing to chat via text.

One person I texted, a level 7 player, confirmed that they just started playing GTA Online that very night. When I asked about GTA 6, they said they wanted to play, but it costs too much. So instead, the game’s historic launch inspired them to go back and finally give GTA Online a try. They had owned GTA 5 for a bit now and decided to kill time waiting for GTA 6 by making money in GTA Online. Another person I texted in-game simply told me GTA 6 looked boring and dumb. They apparently had no interest in playing it. Another player simply texted back, “$80 is too much for me, bro.”

In a different session, I found a gamer who claimed that everything they’d seen about GTA 6 made the game look “bad.”

Another person joked that he must want to play GTA 6 instead. And then he added: “My parents won’t buy GTA 6 for me because it’s, like, filled with boobs and shit.”

After playing GTA Online again and chatting with more people over voice and text, what I learned is that while some players are staying away from GTA 6 because of silly complaints about it being woke, the vast majority of players either don’t care about that or have no idea some online are complaining about that crap. Instead, the real reason most of them aren’t playing GTA 6 is simply that they don’t have the money to buy it right now.
