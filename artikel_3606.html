Fable Is Looking A Lot Darker Than We Expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Amid a modest 25th-anniversary celebration, Xbox dropped a new preview of the Fable reboot. This snippet — which runs for under four minutes — offers plenty of gameplay and a closer look at some of the ghoulish enemies you'll face, set in a slice of Albion that looks surprisingly grim compared to the lighthearted Fable teasers that came before it.

"The Hero of Briar Hill has entered the depths of Deepwood alongside Arden, a local Hero, to investigate why strange magical happenings have been troubling the people of Oakshire," the video synopsis reads. "Here, they cross a threshold where the natural magic of Deepwood appears corrupted by an outside force, and they discover hints as to who or what could be responsible."

One thing to note about this snippet of gameplay is that it's pulled from the PC build of Fable, with the settings cranked up to Ultra. It's a pretty good-looking glimpse of what you can expect from Fable next year, even if it feels different from its cheekier predecessors and more like a feudal action slice reminiscent of Control Resonant toward the end, with its red-hued aesthetic.

Of course, four minutes of Fable doesn't tell the whole story, and with the main campaign alone estimated to take 20 hours to finish, that doom-and-gloom feeling probably won't be present throughout the entire game.

Fable launches February 23, 2027, for Xbox Series X|S, PS5, and PC, with Playground Games primarily handling development. Turn 10 Studios also assisted with development, and following recent changes at Xbox, it was merged into Playground Games.

In addition to Fable, Xbox also revealed that new Wolfenstein and Doom games are in development, and Xbox CEO Asha Sharma’s open letter to the Xbox community suggested that future game development might be more reliant on AI.
