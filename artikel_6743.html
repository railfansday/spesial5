Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando Give a Peek Inside Their Wedding Rehearsal Dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are getting ready to say “I do.” The couple celebrated their upcoming wedding with a rehearsal dinner on Friday, November 20, surrounded by family and friends.

Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, were photographed arriving at the dinner hand in hand at a restaurant in Southern California. For the occasion, the bride-to-be opted for an understated all-black look with flats, while her fiancé coordinated in a black suit and white button-up. Cyrus’s mother, Tish Cyrus, and her sister, Noah Cyrus, were also in attendance.

Some of the evening’s details were also shared by Cyrus’s stepsister, Lily-Rose Purcell, on Instagram Stories. The photos showed a candlelit table decorated with dainty floral arrangements in autumn hues, alongside her espresso martinis and a series of small cakes.

Another photo revealed the dinner menu, which featured the couple’s initials, “M+M,” at the top. Guests were reportedly treated to a meal that included a salad, filet and lobster, followed by chocolate caramel layer cake, vanilla crème brûlée, and raspberry sorbet.

The rehearsal dinner comes ahead of the couple’s wedding this weekend. TMZ has reported that the ceremony is expected to take place in Southern California, though the couple has not publicly confirmed the location or date.
