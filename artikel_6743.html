Lululemon’s Black Friday Sale Is Here—Shop These Fan Favorites Before They Sell Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The beloved activewear brand rarely offers deals like this.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Lululemon loyalists know the beloved activewear label rarely hosts sales, so its Black Friday event is essential for fans of the brand (and anyone gift-shopping for them). While Alo, Gymshark, and other major workout brands are offering sitewide discounts, Lululemon only marked down select styles—thankfully, this year’s sale assortment includes fan favorites like Belt Bags, Define Jackets, and Energy Bras.

A limited number of discounts means items will sell out fast, especially in classic colorways like black and gray. To help you capitalize on this rare opportunity, we’ve broken down what’s worth shopping while Lululemon’s Black Friday deals are live.

Yoga pants, sports bras, running shorts, and other Lululemon workout clothes are currently up to 40 percent off—and the Align collection and other popular programs like Nulu and Scuba will sell out fast. When it comes to sticking to your New Year’s fitness resolutions, a cute new Define Jacket will provide the extra motivation you need to hit the gym.

Athleisure staples like Scuba hoodies and BeCalm sweatsuits are also included in Lululemon’s Black Friday sale. Be forewarned that many cozy pieces will sell out before Cyber Monday, so don’t leave items lingering in your cart for more than a few hours. If the items you were eyeing are already out of stock, peek at the men’s department for additional deals on sweatshirts, lounge pants, and light jackets.

Lululemon Scuba Oversized Half-Zip Hoodie

The brand’s popular Dual Pouch Wristlet, currently half off, is the kind of item that can spark fights at white elephant parties. Longtime Lululemon fans would be thrilled to find velvety Flat Mate Slippers under the tree or Find Your Balance Grip Socks in their stocking.

Lululemon’s discounted puffers and rain jackets are the perfect outerwear for casual winter outings. The Rain Rebel Waterproof Jacket and Daydrift Jacket, both under $150, will keep you protected from the elements as you head to a workout studio or the grocery store. And at 40 percent off, the travel-friendly Pack It Down Jacket is almost guaranteed to sell out before Cyber Monday.
