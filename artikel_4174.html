Pokémon Go offers you the chance to encounter Shiny Gigantamax Inteleon in upcoming event

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With the nights closing in and the weather getting cold, you may find it a bit difficult to get in the mood for going out. But Pokémon Go has you covered, with a major new event taking place next month, as Gigantamax Inteleon Max Battle Day kicks off this December!

For the first time in Pokémon Go, you'll have a chance to encounter the Shiny Gigantamax Inteleon on December 5th, between 1400-1700 local time. Fortunately, you won't have much trouble finding Max Battles and trainers to help you out, as Pokémon Go is also debuting new improvements to the map to let you find battles more easily.

And, of course, for $4.99 you can also grab yourself the event-exclusive Timed Research, featuring additional XP and Particles, as well as double XP for the duration.

All in all, it's looking to be a pretty solid event. And don't worry if it's a bit chilly where you are and you can't get out of the house. Because Battle Day will also see a marked increase in the number of Remote Raids you can access, so everyone can get in on the fun!
