Microsoft Announces Last-Minute Restock Of The Xbox Series X25 Console

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Available at launch through the Microsoft Store and participating retailers

As we mentioned in our original version of this article, Xbox's tweet made it unclear whether the drop would happen on November 11th going into November 12th, or November 12th going into November 13th.

A bunch of people set their watches for "midnight local time" last night, and nothing ended up happening. Therefore, the new belief is that the drop will happen at midnight on the evening of November 12th.

This also appears to have been confirmed on the official Xbox website.

If you're keen for one of these, best of luck! Hopefully you manage to grab one.

Original Story: Ever since the Xbox Series X25 console first became available for pre-order in August, it's become hard to get your hands on one. The "Limited Edition" system quickly sold out, and became a target for scalpers within 24 hours as well.

If you were hoping to add the Series X25 to your collection (or the accompanying controller, for that matter), you might still have a chance - Microsoft says that a "limited" restock will be available on November 12th to mark the launch.

This apparently goes live at "midnight local time", which we assume means November 11th going into November 12th (although the tweet doesn't make this fully clear):

As you can see, the restock will be available on the official Microsoft Store / Xbox Store, but also at other participating retailers that have yet to be named. We assume most of them will be the ones that carried the original pre-order.

You can feel free to go back to our pre-order guide for the Xbox Series X25 console if you want easy to access to various links and pricing info, but just be aware that things might be a bit different for this restock, so it's worth searching around at midnight local time if the Microsoft Store version sells out quickly.
