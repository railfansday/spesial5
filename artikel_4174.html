New Rabbids Project Teased By Voice Actor

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The current voice actor for the Rabbids has teased a secretive new project to do with the characters, and it likely isn’t related to Legends Retold.

2026 has been a big year for any platforming fans with good taste, as it has marked the start of Rayman’s big return after more than a decade out of the limelight. Not only did his first game get the remaster treatment earlier in the year, but the series' most recent and critically acclaimed outing, Legends, is getting a remake that makes a lot more sense than you might think.

If all goes well, Rayman might finally be back in action and where he belongs after far, far too long. The situation is reminiscent of the first time that the limbless wonder came back after being sidelined by the Rabbids, who are now ironically in a similar position without much more going on than a cameo in Legends Retold. That could be changing in the near future, though.

As pointed out by Twitter user Dycamax, the current voice of the Rabbids (as seen in the TV show, Rabbids Invasion), Damien Laquet recently shared a video on his TikTok account to celebrate his time with the series. Via Google Translate, Laquet said that he’s “reuniting” with the Rabbids in a “fantastic Parisian studio”.

We spoke to legendary composer Grant Kirkhope about Rayman Legends Retold, Assassin's Creed, and Donkey Kong.

The voice actor then went on to say that he can’t tell us any more, but that it’s a “fantastic project” that he’s “absolutely delighted about”. While there’s obviously not much information about what's being worked on, it’s clear that one of the Ubisoft teams is working on something to do with the Rabbids.

Since we know that the Rabbids are making an appearance in Rayman Legends Retold, Laquet voicing some lines for them is a very easy leap to make. However, with that game being so close to completion and it seeming like the Rabbids are just background cameos, I think this is something else.

Then again, we did see evidence of the Rabbids appearing throughout Retold, so maybe there’s more to it than that.

As Invasion made clear, the Rabbids have a lot of love for the world of TV and movies, so it’s possible that Laquet is hinting at another outing like that. My personal preference is obviously a new game starring the Rabbids that doesn't rely on Rayman’s name, but we’ll have to wait and see what comes of it. Rabbids Go Home 2 would be incredible, though.
