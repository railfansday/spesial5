Roma ponder new contract for Malen to ward off interest from abroad

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In just a few months, Donyell Malen has become an absolute focal point for Roma fans and Gian Piero Gasperini’s team.

The Dutch striker knows only one verb: to score. And he does so with a frequency that has surprised even the most optimistic: 27 goals in 34 games for the Giallorossi since he set foot in the capital.

An extraordinary performance that has inevitably attracted the attention of major European teams, with the spotlight shifting from the Premier League big names to the Bundesliga.

Several clubs are monitoring Donyell Malen ahead of the summer transfer window, but Roma doesn’t want to be caught off guard.

Roma wants to secure Donyell Malen. As Corriere dello Sport reports, the Giallorossi club, given the striker’s exceptional performances and the resulting interest from major European clubs, is considering a contract renewal with a salary adjustment that recognizes the player’s value.

Bobby had accepted a salary of approximately €3.7 million plus bonuses until 2030, less than the €4.6 million plus €700,000 he earned at Aston Villa.

The agreement was accompanied by a handshake, with the possibility of renegotiating the deal if his performances met expectations.

Malen has kept all his promises, which is why Roma is ready to talk to his entourage about treating him as a top-level center forward.

The Roman club will offer the born-1999 player a new salary that will exceed €4 million net, exceeding the current threshold represented by Mile Svilar, who is currently the highest-paid player in the Giallorossi squad. Roma is preparing to secure its striker.
