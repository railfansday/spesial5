It goes without saying (yet here I am saying it) that Grand Theft Auto 6 is going to be huge. We don't really have a sense as to just how huge at this point: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has boasted that GTA 6 preorder numbers are "unprecedented," but he's refused to provide specifics because "the raw numbers could be potentially misleading." But huge, for sure.

Now both Sony and Xbox have joined in the 'gonna be huge' chorus, each of them announcing that preorders for GTA 6 are, definitionally, unprecedented.

"Grand Theft Auto 6 is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store," Sony proclaimed on the PlayStation Blog. "Given this unprecedented demand, we’ve joined with Rockstar Games to allow players to start pre-loading the game nine days early, so they’re ready to play on launch day. We’ve also adopted a rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as respective countries hit midnight on November 19."

Xbox made a similar announcement on its own website, calling GTA 6 "the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store" and encouraging users to begin preloading as soon as possible because of "the unprecedented demand" for the game.

Alongside Xbox Series X/S consoles, Xbox said GTA 6 will also be playable on Xbox One consoles through Xbox Cloud Gaming. That service is also available on PC, but Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Bell confirmed in October that GTA 6 will not be streamable on our platform.

Today marks the first step in our new 10-year strategic partnership with Take-Two. GTA VI, already the most pre-ordered game ever on Microsoft Store, will be playable on XBOX One through cloud gaming when it launches on November 19.November 10, 2026

But while nobody is serving up specifics on the number of GTA 6 copies sold via preorder, we do have a rough indicator of how much money Take-Two expects to make when it comes out of the gate. The publisher is projecting net bookings of $3.35 to $3.5 billion for its current fiscal quarter, "driven by the groundbreaking release of Grand Theft Auto 6" on November 19. That's roughly double what Take-Two took in during the same quarter in its previous year, when it reported net bookings of $1.76 billion.

"The launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company," Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said during yesterday's quarterly financial report.
