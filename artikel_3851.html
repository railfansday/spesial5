Last week saw empty carts in the hunt for Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks, but with Black Friday looming at the end of the week we're expecting more units in the next few days. Nintendo itself has confirmed that its own next bout of restocks will land in January 2027, but with movement at stores in both the US and UK this week, it's still worth keeping an eye on the wider market. Retailers have been incredibly quiet for some time now, but this week's holiday sales could change all that.

Stores often stockpile hard-to-find gadgets ready for Black Friday week, using drops as an opportunity to get more eyes on their sites and lift visibility on their deals. It's happened with the PS5, Xbox Series X, PlayStation Portal, and Sony's 30th Anniversary kit over the last six years, and I'm pretty sure it's happening with Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks in 2026.

So where do we start? I'd recommend keeping the retailers listed below close to your chest. These stores have all dropped Zelda Switch 2 stock in the past but have been quiet over the last two weeks. Make sure you're signed in with payment and delivery information saved, and then check back regularly. We'll be right here, rounding up all the latest updates live as they happen.

I've been tracking hard-to-find stock for over six years now, starting with the incredibly limited releases of the PS5 and Xbox Series X. I've been reporting on how retailers operate with high-demand products, from anniversary releases to brand new consoles, ever since then - including the Nintendo Switch 2 itself.

Amazon was dropping new Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks every few days at the start of the handheld's life, though only in very limited numbers. It's been particularly quiet for the last week, though.

Best Buy hasn't dropped any new Legend of Zelda Switch 2 handhelds since it ran its own midnight launch event for Ocarina of Time (with consoles up for grabs). Online, we're still 'sold out' here, though more inventory is on the cards.

Nintendo itself has announced that it won't be running Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks until January 2027, so it's likely out of the race this Black Friday.

GameStop is only running Legend of Zelda Nintendo Switch 2 restocks in stores, so you won't find any online stock here. The store looks like it's about to start running Pro Controller and carry case restocks online, though.

Amazon UK has been a pretty reliable source of Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks in the last few weeks, even if things have slowed down in recent days. EE has had two Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks since launch day, but the boost here comes from those bundles. The site has previously been offering discounts on Ocarina of Time remake and Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream when bought with the device.

Smyths Toys has returned its Legend of Zelda Switch 2 listing this week, after removing the page entirely when it ran out of stock a while back. That's a fantastic sign for more drops to come over Black Friday.

Argos was previously offering Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock ahead of launch, but things look to have run dry now. The store organises its inventory by postcode, though, so it's always worth checking your local area's delivery and collection options.

Currys previously had a listing page up for the Legend of Zelda Switch 2, but it seems to have disappeared now, instead sending us to a generic consoles search page.

Nintendo has also stated more stock is on the way, so you'd expect its own store to be one of the first with fresh handhelds up for grabs.

Land on GameStop's Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock page and your heart, like mine, might skip a beat. The retailer is advertising its focus on in-store restocks with a giant red button where the 'add to cart' option would normally sit. Like many hunting Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock I saw those handhelds and then got straight to the Pro Controller. Unfortunately, this is a console-only drop from Argos, with no delivery options for the gamepad itself.

Still, the 40th Anniversary handheld itself remains up for grabs, nearly an hour after it first dropped.

Walmart in the US and Smyths Toys in the UK. Sure, other stores are likely going to also be running their own Black Friday Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks this week, but with such early movement from these retailers it's well worth keeping them a little closer over the next couple of days.
