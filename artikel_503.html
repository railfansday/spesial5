The best early Black Friday PlayStation deals: PS5 games, cheap controllers, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

I’m not a "gamer" per se, but I grew up in a PlayStation household, so I’m very familiar with Lara Croft, Crash Bandicoot, and Sora. My sister is much more committed — she's even taken time off work to play GTA 6. I love playing video games during my downtime, but she’s on another level.

Between the two of us and the rest of my family, there’s usually a game, controller, or accessory on someone’s wish list. With Black Friday coming up, I’m looking through offers at Amazon, the PlayStation Store, and other retailers to see whether it makes sense to buy now or later.

If you’re shopping for a console, you might not find it on sale until Black Friday proper (though there are no guarantees). We haven't seen any deals worth mentioning just yet, but we're keeping an eye out.

Below, I’ve rounded up the early PlayStation deals worth checking, whether you’re buying a gift or just want something new to play.

Whether you’re checking off your gift list or shopping for yourself, buying another controller is almost always a good idea. If the battery dies mid-game, you can swap controllers and charge the other. Plus, my nephews love getting all the different colors — they have a pretty big collection now.

Right now, the PlayStation DualSense Wireless Controller in Chroma Pearl is $59.99 at Amazon, down from $79.99. That’s $20 off, or 25% in savings. It has haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, a built-in microphone, and a headset jack. This price is for Chroma Pearl, so check the price again if you switch colors before adding it to your cart.

PlayStation DualSense Edge Wireless Controller (Midnight Black) — $169 $199.99 (save $30.99)

If Marvel’s Spider-Man 2 has been on your list, the PS5 Standard Edition is $29.99 at Amazon, down from $69.99. That’s $40 off, or about 57% in savings. This time, you can play as both Peter Parker and Miles Morales as they take on Venom, Kraven, and other familiar villains around New York. It’s a physical copy, though, so check which PS5 you’re buying it for. If it doesn’t have a compatible disc drive, this version won’t work.

Marvel's Wolverine (PS5) — $49.97 $69.99 (save $20.02)

Ghost of Yōtei (PS5, Complete Edition) — $49.99 $69.99 (save $20)

I just bought my dad a PlayStation Portal, and he loves it because he can play PlayStation in bed on Sundays. Honestly, that’s a pretty good way to spend a Sunday. If someone on your gift list already has a PS5 but doesn’t always want to sit in front of the TV, this is def a deal worth considering.

The PlayStation Portal bundle is $299.98 at Amazon and includes the remote player, a PowerA protection case, and a PowerA charging station. The Portal has an 8-inch 1080p screen and streams compatible games installed on your PS5 over WiFi. It doesn’t download and run games locally, so you’ll need a suitable connection and a paired PS5 for Remote Play.
