Balerdi to miss PSG clash due to ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Leonardo Balerdi was playing a perfect match in Parma until the 54th minute, when Elphege beat him to the score to make it 1-1, one-on-one with Svilar.

The Argentine defender had been struggling, and Gasperini was ready to replace him, but he didn’t have time to prevent the equalizer.

It will be difficult to see the former Marseille player in Paris. Balerdi will certainly sit out the start against Luis Enrique’s PSG, scheduled for Wednesday at 9:00 PM, if he bites the bullet.

As Corriere dello Sport reports, the Argentine defender has a sore right ankle, and it’s not solely due to a knock he received in Parma.

The Giallorossi center-back has been struggling with minor issues for a few days and had asked for a substitution from Gasperini at the Tardini, shortly before being beaten by Elphege, who scored the goal that temporarily tied the game at 1-1. In place of Balerdi, as in Parma, Evan Ndicka will be in Paris.
