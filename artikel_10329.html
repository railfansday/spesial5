Google Fi brings back network switching with ‘secondary’ partner, expands W+ beyond Pixel

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Google Fi Wireless is rolling out a big update today with the return of network switching thanks to a “secondary” partner.

Google Fi is now bringing back a second partner as part of its new “super network.” Most users will be on the “Primary network,” but Google Fi will automatically switch users to the “Secondary network” if it meaningfully improves performance with so-called “smart thresholds” for doing so. At launch, there is no manual switching.

Google is not naming the second network today, but did share that Ultra Wideband is supported. Officially, you are switching between “two of America’s fastest 5G networks.”

Network switching will first be supported on Pixel 9 and newer devices that have a Google Fi eSIM (and no other SIM active). It makes use of Android’s Multiple Enabled Profiles (MEP) eSIM technology that allows for two carrier profiles on a single chip.

It might be important to note that T-Mobile actually bought out Sprint, and added their towers to their network so its safe to say that Google Fi still works with the same coverage as before the merger.

The Google Fi app gains a “Your super network” card in the Home tab that shows whether you’re connected to the Primary or Secondary network. “See activity” lets you view switching history and when it last occurred. Google will also note if you’re in range of W+ networks.

Google today is also expanding its Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ network beyond Pixel phones to select Samsung Galaxy (S23, Fold/Flip 5, and newer) and modern Motorola phones. These premium Wi-Fi networks are available in millions of airports, malls, stores, and restaurants. The connection happens automatically, with the Google Fi Virtual Carrier Network encrypting data.

The final update today sees the companion app gain “Ask Fi.” This AI-powered chat is meant to quickly provide answers and guided troubleshooting for common issues. You can request human support with seamless handoff that keeps the chat context.
