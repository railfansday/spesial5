Second Circuit denies Kalshi and Coinbase interim relief against New York and Connecticut

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Second Circuit has refused to shield Kalshi and Coinbase from New York and Connecticut while their appeals proceed. The ruling lets both states press state-court cases against the companies' sports event contracts and deepens a circuit split headed for the Supreme Court.
