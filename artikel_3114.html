MINISO: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Its international push has shifted from rapid store openings to unit-level efficiency, a slower path that tests whether the model can replicate abroad.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:32PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 17, 2026, MINISO (MNSO +5.98%) announced a 14.3% year-over-year revenue increase to CNY 6.63 billion. The company is shifting its retail network toward larger-format stores while relying on proprietary intellectual property (IP) products to drive repeat customer purchases. Three key takeaways from management explain how this strategic transition impacts long-term profitability.

MINISO is shifting its revenue mix toward in-house character designs to expand gross margins. The company saw rapid adoption of its flagship YOYO character line, which generated over CNY 1 billion in gross merchandise value (GMV) during the first three quarters.

Management is closing inefficient smaller locations to focus resources on larger store formats that attract higher foot traffic. These specialized venues contribute a disproportionate share of sales while generating better single-store productivity.

Rising overhead costs across direct-operated international markets prompted management to slow store openings to prioritize unit-level profitability. Focusing on operational execution allows regional distribution networks to absorb existing inventory before expanding further.

Revenue momentum is holding steady across core retail channels, and executive leadership is confident in the full-year trajectory. Expanding large-format store coverage, deepening membership loyalty, and controlling direct-sourcing costs remain central drivers of long-term value creation.

Unifying supply chain strengths with a globalized store expansion strategy establishes a sustainable foundation against macroeconomic headwinds.
