Reinsurance Group (RGA) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Investment gains and pension risk deals drove a sharp earnings jump.

Reinsurance Group of America, Incorporated (RGA -0.02%) reported a significant increase in adjusted operating income driven by robust investment performance and favorable biometric claims across global segments. Management highlighted the full deployment of the Ruby Re third-party capital vehicle and strong activity in the U.S. pension risk transfer market, completing four transactions totaling $800 million. The company announced a strategic plan to simplify reporting into four segments starting in the fourth quarter to better reflect internal management and the convergence of Traditional and Financial Solutions business. Management confirmed the company is maintaining its intermediate-term financial targets, supported by a healthy pipeline and a strong capital position with $1.8 billion in excess capital.
