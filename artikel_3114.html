Hesai: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Pricing in Hesai's core lidar market is settling into rational competition, a shift that matters for whether margins hold as newer businesses scale.

On its third quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Hesai (HSAI +0.63%) reported that non-automotive robotics businesses reached over 50% of total revenue. This expansion transforms the company from an automotive component supplier into a broader physical artificial intelligence infrastructure platform. Three key takeaways from management explain where the business is heading next.

Fast-growing robotics applications and software tools are diversifying revenue away from legacy vehicle lines. Hesai's strategic growth initiatives, or SGI, generated 135 million Chinese yuan in nine months, exceeding its original full-year target.

Humanoid robots require dozens of specialized motion modules, creating a high-volume hardware market beyond optical sensors. Hesai designs core components in-house to capture market share as customer deployments expand.

Price competition in main light detection and ranging, or lidar, sensors is stabilizing following severe industry discounting. Multi-sensor configurations per vehicle help protect overall margin structures.

Robotics demand is expanding faster than legacy automotive supply chains, supporting near-term gross margins close to 40%. Hesai retains an edge through in-house component integration, proprietary chips, and global manufacturing standards while tracking pricing discipline across emerging segments.

Long-term execution relies on shifting customer vehicle architectures toward multi-lidar arrays and establishing hardware standards across humanoid platforms.
