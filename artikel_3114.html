PRA Group: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Management expects legal collection costs to peak in 2026 and fall in 2027, a shift that could free up cash for investors weighing PRA's long-term payoff.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, PRA Group (PRAA -1.63%) announced that disciplined cost management helped drive trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA, a measure of cash-oriented operating profit, up 9% year over year. Debt buyers rely on tight expense controls and selective portfolio purchasing to maintain returns across shifting credit cycles. Three key takeaways from management explain how these operational moves position the business for the long term.

Management is prioritizing net returns by redirecting capital away from a competitive U.S. market and toward European debt portfolios. European cash collections exceeded internal expectations by 3% during the quarter, supporting the strategic pivot.

Operational restructuring has eliminated more than 215 corporate roles since October 2025, trimming overhead staffing by over 25%. These workforce reductions cut third-quarter compensation and benefits expenses by 11% year over year, establishing a lower cost base for future operations.

Upfront investments in court-based debt recovery expanded third-quarter U.S. legal cash collections by 19% year over year to $148 million. As teams clear the remaining backlog of accounts, management projects annual legal collection costs will crest in 2026 and decline in 2027, freeing up future cash flow.

Multimarket flexibility allows the business to maintain buying discipline while expanding digital debt recovery capabilities. Deleveraging, or reducing debt relative to earnings, brought net leverage down to 2.6 times earnings for an eighth consecutive quarter.

Cloud infrastructure upgrades and automated workflow tools continue to enhance agent efficiency. Management expects these structural operational changes to compound long-term returns.
