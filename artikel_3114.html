Nabors Industries: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Redeploying idle rigs into high-margin international contracts may allow the company to grow without loading up its balance sheet.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 20, 2026 at 4:45PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Nabors Industries (NBR -6.47%) reported third-quarter adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) of $246 million. Higher fleet utilization and disciplined debt reduction give the company clear financial flexibility to expand internationally without overextending its balance sheet.

Transferring unassigned drilling equipment from North America to expanding international fields lets the company secure long-term contracts without buying new hardware. This rotation strategy increases earnings on existing equipment without demanding capital expenditure.

High rig utilization across major energy markets limits the supply of available drilling hardware. As active capacity shrinks in key basins, energy producers pay higher day rates to secure reliable high-specification equipment.

Repaying high-cost debt lowers fixed financial costs and expands net free cash flow. Lower interest bills ensure that operating cash flow goes straight toward balance sheet improvement and selective capital deployment.

Operational gains and higher technology adoption are boosting margins despite operational frictions in the Middle East. High fleet utilization, deep client relationships, and automated technology give the company clear competitive scale. Management focuses on pricing discipline and contract coverage.

Falling interest costs and disciplined capital allocation position the company for sustainable growth.
