Knicks' Jalen Brunson Defends Taylor Swift: ‘Can’t Believe I Have to Say This’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Five months after Taylor Swift caused a stir by sitting courtside at the NBA Finals, New York Knicks superstar Jalen Brunson is hoping to put an end to the drama once and for all.

Brunson, 30, exclusively told Us that Swift, 36, is “obviously” welcome at Knicks’ games weeks after the point guard’s teammate, Tyler Kolek, said Swift’s support of the team was “performative” during an NBA 2K livestream on November 5.

“I can’t believe I have to say this,” Brunson said via his partnership with Lowe’s. “But I didn’t say what was said. Being one of the leaders of the team — the captain of the team — I guess I now have to help somebody out. Yes, she’s a member of the Knicks fandom. Yes, she is.”

Swift attended Game 4 of the 2026 NBA Finals in June, sitting courtside with friends Mariska Hargitay and Alana and Este Haim.

Knicks broadcaster Monica McNutt went viral for a hot mic moment as Swift made her way to her seat at Madison Square Garden. “She’s not a Knicks fan. Get out of here, girl,” McNutt, 37 said at the time.

After winning the NBA title, Brunson issued a message of support for McNutt.

“I just want to say something to the Swifties: She’s a really good one,” he said at the time. “Cut her some slack. It’s all good, I promise.”

With the holidays approaching, Jalen explained how he and his wife, Ali, have formulated traditions with their daughter Jordyn, 2, in a multi-religion household.

“We’re still learning, but we’re growing,” Jalen told Us. “She was raised Jewish, I was raised Christian. For us, it’s kind of in between. Her side of the family has really demonstrated looking at both. Her mom’s side is Christian, I think her mom converted for [Ali’s] father. So they celebrate both.”

He added, “We’re going to let Jordyn eventually decide what she wants to do as she gets older, but she’s going to understand both.”

This holiday season, Jalen has partnered with Lowe’s to bring added excitement to Black Friday shopping.

“We’re just looking to make Black Friday a little bit more fun this year,” Jalen explained. “It’s exciting, I’m honored. It’s going to be a lot of fun.”

On November 27, the first 150 MyLowe’s Rewards members at participating Lowe’s stores will receive exclusive giveaways and more than $15 million in prizes will be available for four lucky MyLowe’s Rewards members at every store.

As for his own holiday shopping routine, Jalen admitted that becoming the unofficial King of New York after the Knicks’ title win this summer has made things a little bit more difficult.

“I try to do in-store shopping as much as I can,” he said. “Sometimes it doesn’t happen because over the past year it’s gotten kind of hectic. It’s been a lot. But I love being able to give gifts. I feel like it’s more rewarding than receiving gifts.”
