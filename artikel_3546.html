Harry Shum Jr. Honors Late 'Glee' Costar Naya Rivera on 'DWTS'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harry Shum Jr. looked back on his experience filming Glee during the latest episode of Dancing With the Stars.

“I feel like Dancing With the Stars is synonymous with glee … and to be able to dance to Naya’s version, it means so much to so many people, including myself. We miss you, baby girl,” Shum, 44, said during the semifinals on Tuesday, November 17, after his second dance of the night.

After starting the show with a paso doble to “We Found Love” by Rihanna featuring Calvin Harris, Shum and Jenna Johnson set their quickstep to Mark Ronson’s “Valerie” featuring Amy Winehouse, a song performed by the New Directions during season 2 of Glee. The late Naya Rivera sang lead for the fan-favorite performance.

Ahead of Tuesday’s semifinals, Shum confirmed he and Johnson were using the Glee arrangement for inspiration. “It’ll be sung by the band, the wonderful Dancing With the Stars band. And just that energy, feeling that for a quickstep … it’s gonna be beautiful. Can’t wait for y’all to see it,” he noted in an Instagram Story.

In rehearsals, Shum spoke about how “meaningful” the song was to Glee fans. “Glee definitely changed my life,” he added. “It gave me the opportunity to not only dance, but act. And I never thought I would be able to dance to that song again.”

Both of Shum and Johnson’s dances on Tuesday night earned perfect scores from the judges.

Shum began his career as a backup dancer for pop icons including Jennifer Lopez and Mariah Carey before starring on Glee from 2009 to 2015. The cast weathered a handful of tragedies through the years, including the deaths of Cory Monteith and Mark Salling in 2013 and 2018, respectively. Rivera, meanwhile, died in 2020 after an accidental drowning in California.

“I’m failing miserably to process this news. I always imagined old future senior moments where we would hear your infectious laughter down the hall knowing that our funny bone was in for a treat,” Shum wrote in a tribute to Rivera at the time. “To many people, myself included, you were the life of the party. Not only able to rock when fun was to be had after a long day but that shining friend that was always willing to listen, offer sympathy, perspective and at times, give much needed levity to any situation.”

Shum referred to Rivera as “a beast,” praising her dedication and performance chops.

“You deserved more. … You gave life your all and I hope all the good that you have given to the world will be returned in abundance when you reunite with our brother in the heavenly skies,” he continued. “I’m so grateful for our memories. We will make sure to keep your legacy and spirit alive so Josey will grow up to know the incredible woman you were. Love you, Naya. You are already missed. Eternally.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
