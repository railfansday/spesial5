Grand Theft Auto 6 hasn't even been out for a week yet, and someone has already done the math on how many buildings in the whole of Leonida you can actually walk into. For those of you who are curious: it's 1,563 buildings.

That is frankly ridiculous. I don't think I've ever been inside 1,563 different buildings in my entire life, although I am a creature of habit, so that could explain things. The player who shared these stats, Aileos, does note that there are 5,588 buildings in total, so the enterable buildings only account for a fraction of that, just over 25%.

There are some who will look at this with a glass-half-empty perspective, and I get it. This world is so cool, it's easy to want more of it, but as another player points out, "This would've sent us into psychosis a few years back". Sometimes it's better to focus on what we have rather than what we don't have. After all, how many buildings will you realistically enter in your GTA 6 playthrough? Probably not 1,563.

We've spent the past week here in the PC Gamer Slack channel marvelling at just how many random encounters you can stumble across in GTA 6. Some of us have found sentient NPCs. One of us was actually recognised by a convenience store clerk who then asked to get robbed, and I think we've all seen videos of the furry rescue mission.

But just because this world already has loads of awesome activities, it doesn't mean we aren't hoping for more. A massive casino and go-kart track have been spotted by players, both of which have huge "Coming Soon" signs draped outside of them.

These buildings could be lying in wait for GTA 6 Online, or they could be added to singleplayer sometime in the future. Regardless, those are two more buildings that will potentially welcome players soon.
