Man Told Neighbor He 'Killed His Girl' After Allegedly Strangling 91-Year-Old

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A Florida man knocked on a neighbor’s door and revealed he killed his longtime friend immediately after he allegedly strangled the woman for approximately 10 minutes and became shocked when she was no longer breathing, police said.

Victor Dorantes-Velazquez was arrested Sunday, November 15, on a first-degree murder charge in connection with the woman’s death, according to arrest paperwork filed in court.

Officers called to a “suspicious incident” at the suspect’s Hallandale Beach apartment on Saturday, November 14, found Dorantes-Velazquez, 77, sitting in a chair in his living room, next to a bed where a 91-year-old woman was in the fetal position, unresponsive, per a probable cause affidavit obtained by Us.

The woman, who Dorantes-Velazquez described as his friend of several years, was declared dead, the affidavit states.

According to Dorantes-Velazquez’s sworn statement, he confronted and strangled the woman on Saturday after accusing her of stealing items he discovered were missing, including his car keys, phone and a brown wallet with more than $1,000 in cash.

The woman, who had been living with Dorantes-Velazquez for a few months, denied the theft and kept laughing at him as he confronted her about his missing items throughout the day, Dorantes-Velazquez told police.

Dorantes-Velazquez “advised at one point, as he continued asking for his property back, the victim began yelling at him” and also “tried to scratch him,” a detective wrote in the affidavit.

He allegedly held her down onto the bed by her shoulders before she tried to grab pill bottles to throw at him, according to Dorantes-Velazquez’s sworn statement.

Dorantes-Velazquez said he “lost his temper” and began to strangle his friend, per the affidavit.

“[She] told [Dorantes-Velazquez] to let her go and she would give him his belongings so [Dorantes-Velazquez] released his grip at which time [she] began laughing at [him],” an officer rewrote in the affidavit, adding that this caused Dorantes-Velazquez to “lose control.”

The woman tried to fight him off as he allegedly strangled her for about 10 minutes until she appeared motionless and didn’t seem to be breathing, according to the affidavit.

Dorantes-Velazquez told police he was “shocked” and took an elevator to the apartment building’s fourth floor, where he knocked on a neighbor’s unit to seek help.

The neighbor said in his statement to police that Dorantes-Velazquez “asked him to call 911 because he had killed his girl,” the affidavit states.

Afterward, the neighbor and another resident followed Dorantes-Velazquez to the elevator.

According to the affidavit, while inside the elevator, Dorantes-Velazquez allegedly confessed, “I grabbed her by her neck and killed her because she took my wallet and money.”

After the elevator doors opened, Dorantes-Velazquez went to another woman and asked her to call 911, the affidavit states.

Dorantes-Velazquez is detained at the North Broward Bureau detention facility in Broward County, records show.

Information on whether he retained legal counsel wasn’t immediately available.
