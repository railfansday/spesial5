2028 Honda CR-V Will Go Hybrid-Only, Debut a New Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The new “Honda Hybrid” system is more efficient, increases power, and comes paired with an electric AWD setup.

We’re not done with 2026 quite yet, but the 2028 Honda CR-V is already coming in hot. On Thursday, the brand offered up its first official details about the next-gen version of the company’s highly important crossover—and big changes are in stow

First off, the 2028 model will be a complete redesign: underpinned by a new platform, boasting a fresh design inside and out, and powered by a new “Honda Hybrid” system. Not only is the hybrid new, but it will be the only powertrain choice available for the seventh-gen CR-V. This hybrid-only approach follows in the successful footsteps of the current Toyota RAV4, Honda’s biggest rival in the compact SUV segment.

When it comes to details, Honda is being purposely vague, but still giving us a few items to chew on. The new platform this CR-V will ride on increases body rigidity and reduces weight compared to the current car. It also has greater modularity built in, which Honda says will allow for greater parts commonality between models.

The Honda Hybrid powertrain itself will build on the two-motor hybrid system we’ve seen for years. Honda says it’s aiming for a 10-percent improvement to fuel efficiency gained by expanding the range where the engine operates most efficiently and an increase in the efficiency of the hybrid unit. More power is promised from the four-cylinder Atkinson cycle engine, and we’ll also see a new electric AWD system make its debut on this CR-V. Per usual, Honda is also claiming the CR-V will bring further enhancements to the “joy of driving,” which is always heartening to hear.

A single image of the new CR-V logo on a dark metallic blue paint was provided as a teaser. It’s rather edgy in appearance, far more so than the current logo’s font. We suspect this new sharper logo design will translate into the rest of the car, but Honda is holding back from showing us the SUV’s design for now.

This next-gen CR-V will continue to be built in North America with production spread out across three plants, two in the U.S. and one in Canada.
