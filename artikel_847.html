Anderson steps up, Berge impresses - player ratings for Man City v Fulham

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Elliot Anderson scored his second goal of the season for Manchester City

Elliot Anderson's stunning second-half volley rescued a point against Fulham, but Manchester City extend their winless run to four games in the Premier League.

Here's how I rated the players, and how you rated the players.

Gianluigi Donnarumma: Has been in poor form recently but could do nothing about the opener. Made a very important save to deny Rodrigo Muniz. 6

Abdukodir Khusanov: Not a natural right-back and noticeable at times, cutting back instead of marauding forward with ball at his feet. 6

Ruben Dias: Another game where captain Dias and his side have failed to keep a clean sheet. 6

Marc Guehi: Missed England's recent matches through injury but did not look in any trouble in this game. 7

Nico O'Reilly: Almost scored at one end and could have given one away at the other when he tried to play for offside. 6

Ayyoub Bouaddi: Given a start in place of Enzo Fernandez and grew in the second half but was he able to fully grasp his opportunity? 7

Elliot Anderson: The pick of the home players when his side needed him the most. Covered every blade of grass and equalised with a sensational volley. 9

Iliman Ndiaye: Needs to start stepping up. Created very little and hasn't really shone in the absence of Antoine Semenyo. 6

Jeremy Doku: Another who has to start performing. Did very little of note here and was penalised for a foul throw when chasing a winner. Unforgivable. 6

Phil Foden: Showed flashes of his talent but not enough to influence the game. 6

Erling Haaland: Had a goal in the first half disallowed and blazed another decent opportunity over. 6

Subs not used: Rulli, Kovacic, Reis, Samba, Lewis.

Bernd Leno: Made some routine saves but Anderson's strike was past him in a flash. 6

Kenny Tete: Solid at right-back, keeping Doku in check. 7

Calvin Bassey: The captain has been linked with a move to Manchester United and was a solid presence in the heart of the backline. 6

David Affengruber: The Austrian made a couple of crucial blocks to deny certain goals. 7

Ryan Sessegnon: Decent performance against the tricky Ndiaye. 6

Sander Berge: Shielded his back four superbly for much of the game in an impressive showing. 7

Alex Iwobi: Booked in the opening five minutes but recovered well. 6

Oscar Bobb: Returned to City and provided the assist for the first goal. 7

Shea Charles: Steady but unspectacular showing for another player against his former club. 6

Josh King: Earned a call-up to England recently and was lively in the final third. 7

Rodrigo Muniz: Marvellous curled effort to open the scoring. 8

Subs not used: Kaiser, Cuenca, Angel, Castagne.

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.
