The Challenge Alum Tyler Duckworth’s Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Three months after MTV star Tyler Duckworth died at the age of 44 in his North Dakota apartment, his cause of death has been confirmed.

More information has come to light about Tyler Duckworth’s passing.

Three months after The Challenge winner died at the age of 44, his cause of death was confirmed as heart disease.

According to TMZ, citing the North Dakota State Forensic Examiner’s Office, Tyler died of hypertensive cardiovascular disease, which is when the heart and blood vessels become damaged due to long-term high blood pressure. His death, per the outlet, was confirmed to be of natural causes.

Tyler’s mother Joni Duckworth shared the news of her son’s passing in an Aug. 13 Facebook post, noting she’d share service details “as soon as his dad and I figure it out.”

At the time, the Grand Forks Police Department told E! News that Tyler was found in a bathroom in his North Dakota apartment. Though they were still waiting on toxicology results at the time, police noted there were no signs of foul play, nor did Tyler’s death appear suspicious.

Before his success on The Challenge, which he won twice, Tyler made his reality TV debut on Real World: Key West in 2006. Following his passing, many of Tyler’s former reality TV costars from both shows paid him tribute, including Johnny “Bananas” Devenanzio.

“You were more than a friend," Johnny shared in a video posted to Instagram Aug. 15. "More than a roommate. You were the brother I never had. Rest easy, Tyler. You’ll always be family.”

In fact, though they clashed on Real World, Tyler and Johnny’s bond was so close he also spoke at Tyler’s funeral.

“Lights will guide you home,” Johnny captioned a post from the ceremony. “And I hope wherever they lead you, you know how deeply you were loved, and how much of you we’ll carry with us forever. Rest easy, brother.”

Tyler’s funeral was also attended by Real World’s Jose Tapia, who honored his former costar with a moving tribute on social media.

“Tyler wasn’t just my castmate. He was my roommate and really my first friend in that house,” Jose wrote on Aug. 16. “Tyler was brilliant, incredibly witty, strong as a bull, and had an energy that could fill a room. If you knew him, you know.”

And after detailing some of the memories they shared, Jose concluded, “Rest easy, roommate. Love you, big bro.”
