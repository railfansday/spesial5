The 42 Best Gifts for All Types of Travelers, From Luxe Flight Essentials to Road Trip Gadgets

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Shower your favorite five-star vagabonds with these treats for their next staycation or whirlwind adventure.

By Danielle Directo-Meston, Erin Lassner November 7, 2026 12:27pm

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

For some travelers, there’s nothing like rolling up to the gate with a luxe piece of luggage and well-prepared flight kit, or setting out on a road trip with all of the gear needed for a smooth ride. Some of the best gifts for travelers who love a luxurious experience don’t need to cost an arm and a leg — plenty of essentials can turn any trip into a five-star one.

For instance, compact toiletry bags with the perfect number compartments can keep them organized like a jet-setting pro, while comfy airport shoes make security checks feel like a breeze. Glampers will love a chic camera for capturing their memories. And for those bitten by the travel bug (and itching for their next adventure), a subscription to coffee or snacks from around the world will transport them back to their favorite places. Check out our top picks below, from softside luggage by Hollywood-loved brand Away (Meghan Markle and Jessica Alba have been spotted with its gear) to red-eye-ready essentials from Tumi (F1 superstar Lando Norris and singer Reneé Rapp have starred in its campaigns) and more. Whether you’re shopping for holiday gifts, or you’re looking for presents for the frequent flyers and road-trippers in your life, we’ve rounded some of the best travel gifts for every type of adventurer.

Got a homesick transplant or someone who just wants to rep their home state? Celebrity-loved designer Maya Brenner’s pendant necklaces are available in a range of countries (including France, England, Italy, China, Japan, Mexico, Germany and more) and all 50 U.S. states. Each charm comes on a 16-inch cable chain in sterling silver or 14-karat gold. Meghan Markle, Abigail Spencer, Rihanna and Yvonne Strahovski and are just a few stars who’ve worn her pieces.

For only $130, Quince’s roomy polycarbonate shell carry-on has 360-degree spinner wheels, a sturdy aluminum telescopic trolley handle with soft rubber for comfort, interior compression panels, TSA-approved locks and a removable laundry bag. The capacity offers enough space for six to nine outfits, and there’s a 18-by-12.5-inch interior pocket for storage. Available in a range of colorways — both core and seasonal — Quince’s signature suitcase is also offered in a smaller carry-on size and multiple checked-in varieties.

Hoka’s Clifton 11 is the latest iteration of the brand’s versatile Clifton line. These everyday sneakers are one of our favorite shoes for traveling, thanks to their heel tab that makes pulling them on a breeze after TSA checks. The sturdy silhouette also makes it easy to run through airports and cities alike.

The Empire Carryall Bag 48 is genuinely our dream weekender, especially in the newer Loved Leather finish which only gets better with age. Bonus: Its intentional lived-in look means you don’t have to be too precious with it — this stunner is suited for even the craziest of travel days.

For a long day of travel, there’s no better companion than an insanely quenching mask, and 111Skin’s Celestial Black Diamond Eye Mask is just that. Specifically created to target tired-looking eyes, the cult-favorite skin product is a Hollywood magnet, with the brand’s famous fans including Anne Hathaway, Kim Kardashian, Jennifer Anniston and Bella Hadid.

At first glance, this duo looks like an ultra chic toiletry bag set. And yes, it is. But it’s also so much more. Lined with Pristinium, OTM’s signature temperature-protective material, each case is not just created to carry your products, but built to protect them — ensuring all your formulas work just as intended.

Perfect as a stocking stuffer gift for travelers who don’t want to leave their beauty and grooming routines at home, this 11-piece travel set comes with reusable bottles and containers in stylish black and white prints. It includes three flat squeeze pouches, a mini spray bottle, two pump bottles, two mini jars with resealable lids and a storage case, plus a funnel, spatula and pipette for easily refilling. Everything is TSA approved and BPA-free, too.

For the traveler who loves to keep their baggage ultra-light, Samsonite’s Proxis global carry-on spinner weighs just 4.7 pounds. The expandable suitcase is made of the company’s durable new Roxkin outer shell that’s designed to bounce back, while the built-in USB port lets jet-setters keep their tech powered. Other features include TSA-friendly locks, rubber spinner wheels and interior organizers and zippered pockets.

If there’s one thing that many travelers learned in the past few years, it’s that lost luggage can put a tailspin in your plans. A smart tracker — like Apple’s AirTag — can help travelers keep tabs on their belongings.

Maison Wheat’s super-soft basics are our go-to uniforms for flights and long car rides, thanks to their eco-friendly blend of Tencel modal and spandex that make for easy layering on the plane. Our favorite pairing? We love the women’s Knoxville ribbed tank with an elevated silhouette and the Los Angeles joggers with a comfy, elastic-free waistband and tapered look. For jet-setting men, we recommend the Oxford quarter zip or the San Diego sweatpant. (The brand was co-founded by actor Huw Collins and his wife, Molly Shaheen.)

When they can’t decamp to far-flung destinations or don’t want to leave their favorite fragrances at home, this trio of luxe perfumes by cult-cool luxury brand Byredo will keep recipients smelling chic during their travels. (Anne Hathaway, Rosie Huntington-Whiteley and stylist Courtney Mays have said they’re fans.)

The three-piece set includes the best-selling scents Bal d’Afrique (a warm fragrance with notes of bergamot, lemon, neroli, African marigold, violet, musk, Moroccan cedarwood and more), the light and floral Blanche (notes of white rose, pink pepper, peony and sandalwood) and the woody Gypsy Water (bergamot, lemon, juniper berries, pepper, pine needles and vanilla).

Minimalist nature lovers won’t mind forgoing the fuss that comes with massive vacation homes with gift card for Postcard Cabins. Formerly Getaway, the tiny cabin rental company’s dog-friendly Outposts are located just outside of Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Los Angeles, Nashville, New Orleans, New York, Portland and other major cities, and stays range from $99 to $319 and up per night.

If those airline-provided pillows aren’t supportive enough, Purple’s Harmony Anywhere travel bundle may be a great gift for frequent flyers who want a more comfortable option. It features a pillow with a ventilated Talalay latex core and GelFlex grid.

For the frequent traveler, peace of mind is one of the best gifts. Get them safely from the tarmac to their dreamy destination (or home after a long day in the air) with a pre-booked private car from Blacklane, which has a global carbon offset program. The company’s iOS and Android app makes it easy to hail a ride or schedule one in advance, and its professional chauffeurs will treat your giftee to a five-star experience with door-to-door pick-up and drop-off in cities such as New York, Los Angeles, London, Dubai, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs, Miami and beyond. You can also hire chauffeurs by the hour and book seamless airport transfers (drivers will track your flight and wait up to one hour for free). The best part? Pricing is all-inclusive, so no need for your recipient to bring cash for tips.

Not every hotel room or charming Airbnb is designed with modern technology in mind — and for frequent travelers who also tote around cameras, smartwatches, tablets and other gear, this international power adapter ensures that their gadgets are juiced up no matter the locale.

Inspire your favorite traveler to relive their adventures with a gift card to Artifact Uprising, which lets recipients create beautiful hardcover photo books, calendars, framed prints, wood display boxes and other memorabilia.

Whether we’re flying or sitting in the passenger, Cabeau’s neck pillow is our go-to for snoozing comfortably while traveling. (Kristen Bell, Lizzo and Heather McMahan have also flown with it.) The adjustable strap and raised sides keep your head from rolling forward (or onto your seat neighbor) and straining your neck, and the design helps to support spine alignment. We like that it folds away into a compact case that can be attached to your backpack to save space.

Foster a love for languages and gift them a lifetime membership to Rosetta Stone. Plans include access to over 25 languages (including French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Swedish, Tagalog, Greek and much more) through expert-designed Dynamic Immersion courses and TruAccent speech recognition for perfecting accents.

Photography enthusiasts won’t mind making space in their luggage for Polaroid’s travel-friendly Go instant camera, which clocks in at just 4.1 inches long by 3.3 inches wide by 2.4 inches tall. The palm-sized device produces approximately 2.6-by-2-inch photos, and shutterbugs can play with double exposure, flash or no flash and a self-timer.

A luxe cashmere scarf is a vacation-ready staple that your giftee might not splurge on for themselves, making it the perfect travel gift. Jet-setting stars like Angelina Jolie, Eva Longoria, and Jennifer Garner are among those who have worn White + Warren’s best-selling oversized wrap, which can transform into a warm blanket on chilly days and cold flights. This unisex accessory is made of seven-gauge, two-ply cashmere and measures 81 inches by 36 inches. It’s available in an array of colors and in a smaller size.

For travelers with a sensitive stomach, LifeStraw’s handy reusable stainless steel straws are a discreet way to drink tap water while filtering out dirt, bacteria and microplastics. The Climate Neutral Certified company is known for its humanitarian efforts to make clean water accessible in underserved communites, refugee camps and elsewhere through its Safe Water Fund, while its give-back program helps to provide a year of safe water to a child in need with every straw purchase.

Label lovers can arrive at their destination in style with Gucci’s signature GG duffle made with the fashion house’s iconic monogram design.

If your adventure-loving giftee is furry and four-pawed, this hiking backpack by Ruffwear ensures they (and their biped parent) have the proper carryall for stowing a day’s worth of essentials. It has two saddlebags designed to stabilize the weight, reflective trim for the evening and other low-visibility settings, and reinforced leap attachment points.

For the active adventurer (or fitness lover) who lives in their leggings (and hates VPL), comedy writer-founded brand Skimpies makes one of the best gifts for women who want to travel light. The organic cotton liner is designed to replace underwear — recipients simply stick it directly into their leggings, shapewear or other tight-fitting bottoms to enjoy wedgie-free adventures.

For the build-their-own-adventure type who also doesn’t mind letting a pro handle the planning, a gift card to 57 Hours will let them book outdoor excursions guided by an expert. From rock climbing in Joshua Tree and backpacking in Alaska, to hiking Iceland’s volcanic trails and a 15-day trek on the Annapurna circuit in Nepal, there’s truly something for every type of expeditioner.

For travel-obsessed families, No Reception Club’s Hideaway Carry-On Duffel is a versatile piece of luggage for parents who don’t want to sacrifice style and organization when adventuring with kids. The removable Cubby packing organizer has customizable sections for flexibility, and the bundle includes a dopp kit, a pair of hanging straps, detachable backpack straps, a sternum strap and laundry sack.

If their perfect vacation involves cozying up in a cabin or decorating their own abode like a home away from home, author Laura May Todd’s How to Get Away is filled with inspiring imagery and expertise from architects, designers and homeowners. From modern forest respites and contemporary cottages to coastal retreats and more, the 256-page coffee table book explores seven types of retreats that will spark even more wanderlust.

For when they can’t hop on a plane and sip on an espresso or latte at a coffee bar across the world, Atlas Coffee Club’s monthly subscription will deliver global cups of joe to them instead along with a postcard from where the beans were grown. Choose from half, single or double bag subscriptions every two or four weeks; as well their roast preference (light to dark or a variety) and grind type (whole bean or ground).

This set of seven hexagonal capsules by Cadence will help your giftee save space while keeping their skincare, jewelry, medication and more all in one place. The tops, sides and labels are magnetic, so the pieces snap together into a honeycomb design, and the wide mouth makes it easy to add your favorite apothecary products (while ditching the travel-sized bottles). The labels are removable and customizable, and the containers are made from a blend of recycled plastic pulled from beaches and reused scrap plastic.

The outdoorsy folks on your list will appreciate Cotopaxi’s newly-designed Allpa 35L backpack that now boasts 100 percent recycled fabrics, mesh shoulder straps for comfort, an exterior stretch water bottle pocket and a luggage pass-through strap. It’s got plenty of zippered organizers and pockets inside to keep you organized for a week, too (which is why it’s our favorite compact carry-on backpack).

Overpacking can be totally avoidable when you give your favorite traveler this portable luggage scale, which can weigh baggage up to 110 pounds. The pocket-sized device conveniently turns off automatically to save the battery, and it also senses your luggage’s temperature.

Whether they’re a touring musician or a jet-setting festgoer, these reusable noise-canceling earplugs by Vibes protect your giftee’s hearing. Fitted with silicon tips (which come in different sizes for a snug fit), the earbuds filter out damaging decibel levels without silencing out all sound, so wearers can still enjoy concerts and other environments where they want to be tuned into their surroundings.

When they don’t know what weather they’ll be landing in, Quince’s water-repellent puffer jacket (also available in women’s sizes) makes it easy for giftees to pack and unpack for unpredictable temperatures.

Away’s expandable softside carry-on gives travelers the peace of mind that their belongings will stay dry and protected on almost any trip.

Puffer Hug’s signature travel blanket has a fleece-lined kangaroo pocket and a cozy foot pocket, and folds into a compact pillow — making it a great gift for road trippers and jetsetters alike.

Available in chic colorways, celeb-loved travel brand Tumi’s compact crossbody has zippered pockets on the inside and outside, plus a handy key leash — making it versatile for flights, errands and every activity in between.

For the vagabonds who want to be ready to hang out at a moment’s notice, Wise Owl Outfitters’ portable hammock lets them post up at camp (or at airports, if necessary). It’s made of 210D ripstop nylon and takes about a minute to set up; a kids version is also available.

If they frequently explore far-flung locales that are outside of cell coverage areas, gift them the peace of mind of reliable communication with Spot X’s satellite messaging and GPS tracking device with Bluetooth. Users can connect to their smartphone to access their contacts and easily send messages and S.O.S. alerts, track their routes, quickly check in with family or friends and more. A Spot X contract plan is required; prices range from $12 to $40 per month depending on coverage needs.

For the wanderlust who prefers experiences over tchotchkes, contribute to their next vacation fund with an Airbnb gift card. Available in custom amounts, this is one of the best travel gifts for frequent adventurers looking for their next getaway, as Airbnb has thousands of hosts located across the globe.

For the cool creative that’s always in transit, treat them to this essential travel set from Bellroy, which uses sustainably sourced leather and recycled materials for all of its products. The wide-opening toiletry kit plus is ideal for stowing away (and easily accessing) skin care, grooming products, medications and more, while the tech kit zips open flat for easy access to cables and other accessories.

Whether they’re a backpacker, a preparedness enthusiast or someone who just prefers peace of mind when they’re sipping water in the wild, your loved one will appreciate this filtering 24-ounce water bottle from Grayl. The purifier cartridge removes viruses, bacteria and protozoa and filters out particulates (such as dirt and microplastics), chemicals and heavy metals from a total of 65 gallons of water before it needs to be replaced.
