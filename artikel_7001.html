Kylie Jenner’s Deer-Print Ashi Studio Gown Is an Unexpected (and Welcome) Surprise

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

From leopard-print coats to zebra bikinis, Kylie Jenner has never shied away from an animal print. But at the Los Angeles County Museum (LACMA) Art+Film Gala, she made the case for a something a little unexpected.

Joined by the likes of Alex Consani, Paris Hilton, and Nicole Kidman, Jenner arrived at the museum and stopped for photos in front of Chris Burden’s famed public art installation, Urban Light, which doubled as a red carpet for the evening. There, among the oversize street lights, she was seen wearing a low-back Ashi Studio gown in a brown-and-silver deer print.

The piece, pulled from the house’s Fall/Winter 2026 couture collection and styled by Alexandra Rose and Mackenzie Grandquist, was stitched from precisely-cut panels of fabric to fit snug around the body. The skintight number featured a halter neckline and an open back, and from the knees, it relaxed into a long, floor-sweeping skirt. A dramatic tassel detail finished things off.

The evening marked Jenner’s first time attending the LACMA Art+Film Gala, which held its inaugural edition back in 2011. The event was founded to support the museum’s ongoing efforts to expand its programming, making film more central to its exhibitions and education. Like always, this year’s version welcomed everyone from models to actors to philanthropists to musicians to athletes, bringing together the biggest names in the worlds of art, film, fashion, and entertainment. Once again, Gucci signed on as the sponsor, and Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow served as co-chairs.
