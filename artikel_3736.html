All Cloudreach Falls Aniimo locations

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Whisperwake Isles region has opened up in Aniimo, and by exploring this vast land, you can find and collect all kinds of new creatures. One of the areas you must traverse is Cloudreach Falls, which has quite a collection of Aniimo for you to find.

In each unique area you can explore, tracking down all of the Aniimo who call the region home can be tough, as they’ve all got only a few specific spawn locations, and many of them can only be found under special conditions. If you’re struggling to find any of these creatures, here are the locations of all Aniimo found around the Cloudfall Reach region in Aniimo.

In total, there are 14 unique Aniimo you can encounter around Cloudreach Falls. Some of them are exclusive to this region, while others can be found around a few different areas in Aniimo.

Each of the 14 Aniimo wandering around Cloudreach Falls has a few different habitat locations. Here’s exactly where to find all Cloudreach Falls Aniimo and a breakdown of any special conditions that must be met to get them.

Jeglid spawns at quite a few spots around Cloudreach Falls, including the upper middle region and the lower middle area, close to the teleport points in both areas. You can also find them around the lower eastern edge of this region.

You can find Jeglid (Tempest form) near the middle, upper right edge of Cloudreach Falls. This Aniimo only spawns during stormy weather, so you’ll have to wait and watch for this condition, or work to change the weather for it to spawn. It only spawns around the two spots marked below, making it fairly easy to spot once the conditions are right.

You can find Jeburst close to the water near the middle of Cloudreach Falls, east of the teleport point in this area. Jeburst also spawns a bit further south, near some water in this region.

Jeburst (Tempest form) can be found in the water pool at the bottom of the waterfall near the northeast end of this area. This Aniimo only spawns in stormy weather, though, so you must wait for these conditions to find and catch one.

Dewlop (Waterfall form) is pretty prevalent, with lots of different spawn regions around this area. This Aniimo is so common that you’re likely to run into it naturally while simply exploring. If not, though, you can visit any of the areas marked below to encounter them. They’re particularly abundant up north.

The Dewple (Waterfall form) is a bit of a special one, as it can only be found at one specific spot in the Cloudreach Falls area. To locate it, head to the spot marked below, find the large rock in the pool of water at this location, and use an Aniimo ability to destroy the rock. Jump into the water geyser that’s released upon breaking the rock, and capture the Dewple (Waterfall form) Aniimo once it appears in this stream.

You can find Saws swimming around a few different areas, including near the middle teleport point, close to the south end of the area, and on the small island a bit offshore. This Aniimo sticks to the water, so when searching for them, be sure to look in the water to find this species.

Whirlbite spawns around most of the same areas as Saws, and they also stick close to the water. You can go searching for them in the water pools up north, near the south end of Cloudreach Falls, and over on the small island off the coast.

To get Whirlbite (Prismana form), head to the Bloom marked on the map below. Nurture this Bloom frequently and catch all Aniimo that spawn around it. Eventually, you’ll increase the Prismana Flow state enough for Whirlbite (Prismana form) to spawn. When they do, you’ll get a special pop-up letting you know, and their location will be marked on the map.

You can find Eko in abundance around the northern end of Cloudreach Falls. Look all around the teleport point situated in the middle of this area to spot lots of them wandering around.

There are only two habitat spawn spots for Eklue. One is southwest of the middle teleport point and the other is northeast of it, close to the edge of the island. This Aniimo is fairly rare, so scan carefully to ensure you don’t miss it.

Ekrim can be found at three locations around Cloudreach Falls. One is located a bit east of the middle teleport point, and the other two are slightly south of it. This Aniimo is also quite rare, so there’s only one to be found at each spot.

You can find loads of Puffby roaming around the tall cliffs situated near the northwest end of Cloudreach Falls. They’re fairly common as long as you’re hunting for them in this area.

Hypnowls are by far the most abundant Aniimo in the Cloudreach Falls region. Of all the creatures you can find here, this is the one you’re likely going to run into the most, especially when exploring the lower southern part of this area.
