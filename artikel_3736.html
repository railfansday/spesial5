Funghi Days announced for console

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SUCCESS Corporation has announced Funghi Days, a new console game in celebration of the Nameko / Funghi series’ 15th anniversary. Platforms and a release date were not announced.

The Funghi series began with the mobile game Mushroom Garden, which launched in June 2011. Funghi was originally a character in the Touch Detective series before branching out into its own franchise.
