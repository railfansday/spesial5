The headline news has spread around the world: Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Now, director Denis Villeneuve and producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman have responded to their pick for 007.

As per Amazon News, Villeneuve thanked all those who took part in the audition process for James Bond, describing them as "extraordinary talent", noting there can "only be one 007."

Villeneuve added, "I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin."

Pascal and Heyman, the new stewards of the James Bond franchise after Amazon MGM's acquisition, said, "James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times."

As for the man himself, Pattinson has described landing the role of James Bond as the "honor of a lifetime."

In a statement, Pattinson said, "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I'm deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, [executive producer] Tanya [Lapointe], and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."

After months of speculation and build-up, the new Bond shortlist was reportedly whittled down to Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and two 007-come-latelys: Nicholas Hoult and Robert Pattinson.

Pattinson, though, landed the role – with the first look at the actor in a tuxedo being revealed by the 007 social media team.
