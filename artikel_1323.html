Gary Neville Names 3 Man Utd Stars Mauricio Pochettino Should Sell

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Gary Neville has urged new Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino to sell three players before thinking about cashing in on full-back Diogo Dalot.

United have endured a dismal start to the new campaign, winning just one of their opening ten Premier League matches, which leaves them stranded in the relegation zone. Several players are subsequently receiving pelters for their performances, while the club's hierarchy have made a change in the dugout.

Pochettino has replaced Michael Carrick, who was relieved of his duties last Wednesday after weeks of speculation suggesting that he was walking on a tightrope.

It's widely accepted that United need to enact a significant squad overhaul in upcoming transfer windows, but not everyone agrees as to who should make way and who shouldn't.

Diogo Dalot has faced the brunt of frustrations from United supporters this season, with many of a Red Devil persuasion advocating for the Portuguese to be axed from the team on a weekly basis.

Against Aston Villa at Old Trafford prior to the international break, Dalot was arguably culpable for Villa's first goal, while he later conceded a penalty which led to the away side's third strike. For many United fans, this specific performance was the tipping point, although for many more, that point of no return came some time ago.

Due to Luke Shaw's lack of fitness this term, Dalot has been forced to play out of position at left-back. This may be what has ultimately earned the 27-year-old sympathy from Neville, who defended the player on Stick to United on Tuesday.

"I think if he was in a decent back four he'd be alright," the pundit said.

"But he's got a shocking back four alongside him. Positionally, he's not great. He could be better on the ball. He could be better at everything he does. Of course he could. I just think he's such an easy target, Dalot. I honestly do."

Instead of selling Dalot immediately, Neville called on Pochettino to part ways with Noussair Mazraoui, Matthijs de Ligt and Lisandro Martinez instead.

"I'd be getting (Noussair) Mazraoui well gone before him. I'd be getting (Lisandro) Martinez gone well before him. I'll be getting (Matthijs) De Ligt gone well before him.

"(De Ligt has) got one big problem. He doesn’t play football. If a player is injury-prone and doesn't play football, just because he hasn't played doesn't mean to say that he should go above a player who's played every single week and he's throwing his hand up. You want someone who's actually reliable and plays. De Ligt has to go. De Ligt, Martinez, Mazraoui."

Mauricio Pochettino is a leading contender to take over as Man Utd manager, and he already has his own Dele Alli at Old Trafford.

De Ligt hasn't played a minute of Premier League football for almost a year, with his last outing for United in the English top flight coming away at Crystal Palace on November 30th 2025. Meanwhile, Martinez's recent interview chastising United's board, as well his red card against Chelsea, seem to have placed him into the 'point of no return' category in the north-west side's squad.

As for Mazraoui, the Moroccan wasn't a favourite of Carrick's, but certainly has a chance of revitalising his United career under Pochettino.
