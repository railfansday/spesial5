Fibocom Showcases U6G-Capable FWA and PCBA-Level Solutions at MWC Doha 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

DOHA, Qatar, Nov. 8, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- At MWC Doha 2026, Fibocom is showcasing its 5G and 5G-Advanced broadband portfolio, featuring FG390, FG370 and FG670 modules alongside PCBA-level solutions for indoor units (IDUs), outdoor units (ODUs), MiFi devices and 5G dongles. The portfolio addresses diverse network and device requirements across global markets, as commercial Upper 6 GHz (U6G) deployment in the Middle East opens new opportunities for next-generation FWA.

In the UAE, e& UAE has launched a commercial U6G network, while du has announced end-to-end U6G connectivity using commercially available CPE equipment. These developments highlight the growing demand for FWA devices capable of taking advantage of new spectrum.

Responding to this market development, Fibocom is showcasing the U6G-enabled version of FG390, supporting NR n104 and cross-band carrier aggregation with Sub-6 GHz. It provides a connectivity option for OEMs developing next-generation CPEs, ODUs and enterprise gateways.

For existing Sub-6 GHz deployments, FG370 and FG670 offer additional choices to meet different FWA performance, hardware configuration and cost requirements.

From Wireless Modules to PCBA-Level Solutions

Beyond wireless modules, Fibocom provides PCBA-level solutions for IDUs, ODUs, MiFi devices and 5G dongles, helping OEMs simplify board-level design and integration and accelerate product development.

For portable connectivity with moderate data-rate requirements, 5G RedCap MiFi solutions based on RU311 and FG132 balance bandwidth, power consumption and cost, while also supporting 4G LTE connectivity.

Fibocom's engineering capabilities are backed by commercial deployment experience. In a previously announced Nordic operator project, a customer won an operator tender with its FG370-based Wi-Fi 7 CPE and subsequently delivered the devices in volume. Fibocom contributed to the 18-antenna design and system integration, helping address complex hardware design and integration challenges.

By combining wireless modules, PCBA-level solutions and proven engineering experience, Fibocom supports OEMs in developing broadband devices for current network deployments while preparing for the next phase of FWA evolution.

Fibocom, founded in 1999, is China's first wireless communication module company listed on both the A-share and H-share markets. Fibocom leverages wireless communication and artificial intelligence as its core technologies to provide integrated hardware and software solutions that empower industry applications. These solutions accelerate the transformation from "Connect Everything" to "Intelligent Connectivity" across diverse industries.

Fibocom's one-stop solutions encompass cellular communication, AI, automotive, and GNSS modules, as well as AI toolchains, supporting industry-side and mainstream large model integration, and providing AI Agent, global connectivity, and cloud services, driving the digital intelligence upgrades in industries such as robotics, consumer electronics, low-altitude economy, intelligent transportation, smart retail, and smart energy.
