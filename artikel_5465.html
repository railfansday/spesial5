Weichert Real Estate Affiliates Named Nine-Time Top Franchise for Veterans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Weichert Real Estate Affiliates, Inc. (WREA) has been recognized as one of the nation’s Top Franchises for Veterans by Franchise Business Review for the ninth time.

According to a release, the rankings were developed using benchmark questions collected directly from over 2,000 franchise owners across hundreds of franchise brands. Participants evaluated their franchise organizations in areas such as leadership, training and support, operations, franchisee relations and overall satisfaction. All 50 brands selected for this year’s list received some of the strongest ratings from veterans regarding their franchise ownership experience.

For veterans, franchise ownership offers an opportunity to build upon the leadership, discipline and commitment developed during military service. Through its comprehensive training programs, business development resources, marketing support, technology platforms and collaborative network, Weichert provides a pathway for veterans seeking to grow successful real estate businesses in their local markets, the release noted.

“Being recognized as a Top Franchise for Veterans for the ninth time is a tremendous honor and a reflection of the culture of support we strive to provide throughout our network,” said Bill Scavone, president of WREA. “Veterans bring exceptional leadership, discipline and dedication to their businesses, and we are proud to help them achieve their goals by providing the training, technology, resources and ongoing support needed to succeed.”

The company says the recognition adds to a growing list of honors earned by WREA and reinforces the company’s reputation as a franchise organization dedicated to helping entrepreneurs achieve their goals while maintaining the independence of local ownership.

For more information, visit weichertfranchise.com.
