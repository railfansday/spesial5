Schumer calls Trump's inaction over Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's affair a 'dereliction of duty'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Schumer urged Republicans to back bills limiting China's access to U.S. technology as lawmakers sought to question Huang

Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer accused President Donald Trump of a "dereliction of duty" on Tuesday over reports that Trump was told over a year earlier that Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang was involved in an affair with someone suspected of spying for China, yet took no action. Schumer, a New York Democrat, made the remarks on the Senate floor.

"One of our most important companies that is involved in national security, and the CEO might be having an affair with a Chinese spy, and Trump did nothing about it," Schumer said. "What a dereliction of duty. He's supposed to protect America, not China, not his friends."

Schumer said Trump "has been busy trying to defend Jensen Huang when he ought to be defending our national security." He said Trump invited Huang to join a visit to Beijing, took Huang's advice on granting China access to some of the most advanced U.S. AI chips, and accepted a $25 million donation from Nvidia for his ballroom.

Schumer urged Senate Republicans to side with Democrats on the AI OVERWATCH Act, the MATCH Act and the Chip Security Act, bills meant to sever China's access to leading-edge American technology. He also asked the Trump administration to brief the Senate and House intelligence committees on China's efforts to spy on Nvidia and other U.S. AI companies.

He said Congress must find out how Nvidia's lobbying push shaped national security decisions, among them the easing of important export restrictions.

Trump said Monday that Huang told him he did not know Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, who is not related to the Nvidia executive, was an alleged spy for China. "I really believe him," Trump said. He said he learned of the allegations "not too long ago" and called Huang an "honorable man."

Nvidia's board began an independent inquiry on Sunday, and Huang said he had been "unfaithful to my wife." The Justice Department is conducting a criminal probe of Megaspeed International, a Singapore-based firm suspected of illegally funneling Nvidia AI chips to China.

House Foreign Affairs Committee Chairman Brian Mast, Select Committee on China Chairman John Moolenaar, and House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence Chairman Rick Crawford sent a letter to Huang on Nov. 5. It asks him to appear for a joint, closed-door briefing on Nov. 18 about his interactions with Alice Huang and Nvidia's security protocols against foreign espionage. The chairmen asked him to accept that date or propose another by Nov. 11.

Mast told reporters at the Capitol on Tuesday that he had not heard whether Huang responded, according to CNBC. Moolenaar told CNBC's "Squawk Box" on Tuesday that Congress needs to "get to the bottom" of Huang's relationship with Alice Huang.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries said Monday that "we're going to need a full briefing specifically as it relates to the Nvidia issue."
