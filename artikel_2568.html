Extra time PK advances Kansas City Current to semifinals over Angel City

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Michelle Cooper converted the game’s lone goal after Kayla Sharples drew the penalty kick

The No. 4 seed Kansas City Current came out on top 1-0 after extra time against No. 5 seed Angel City in the second quarterfinal of the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League Playoffs early Saturday. After falling in their home quarterfinal last season, the Current redeemed themselves thanks to an early extra-time penalty kick converted by Michelle Cooper.

If any of the four quarterfinals were expected to be full of goals, it was this matchup between the two highest-scoring NWSL teams this regular season, each with 54 total. Kansas City entered on a five-game winning streak and with the best home field advantage in the NWSL — undefeated in their last 32 regular season home matches but with a home postseason loss in the middle. Meanwhile, Angel City were arguably in their best form of the year, riding a 10-match unbeaten streak.

Despite those expectations, it was a largely defensive game between the scoring titans. The most dangerous goalscoring chance of the first half came 19 minutes in when Haley Hopkins sent a ball into the middle of the box where both Cooper and Temwa Chawinga had beaten their defenders. Roughly 12 yards from goal and with only the goalkeeper to beat, Chawinga headed the bouncing ball directly into the waiting hands of Angelina Anderson.

With neither team wanting to make the mistake that gave their opponent an opening, the game continued like a cautious stalemate.

Angel City did well to slow the game down and limit the Current’s signature high pressure, but the home team grew into the second half, and they were able to control the game more and more. Without a shot on target in the latter 45 minutes, the visitors defended with everything they had as Kansas City piled on the pressure.

Anderson was forced into her best save of the night two minutes into stoppage time as she tipped Lo’eau LaBonta’s long-range rocket over the crossbar. Five minutes later, Maiara Niehues had Angel City’s best chance to that point, with time and space in the box, but put it just inches wide of the far post.

Defender Savy King came off the field limping, in tears, in the 10th minute of stoppage time, after going down grabbing at the back of her leg. She went directly back to the locker room and was replaced by forward Riley Tiernan.

After nine minutes of stoppage time turned into 15, the referee finally blew the whistle to send the game to extra time.

Kansas City quickly picked up where they left off in the second half, peppering the goal with chance after chance blocked by Angel City’s desperate numbers in the box. But three minutes in, that became a corner kick where the Current were awarded the perfect chance to go ahead — a penalty kick.

Anderson was penalized for shoving Kayla Sharples to the ground in the back, not allowing her to make a play on the near-Olimpico that Riley Tiernan then headed off the line. The foul was called on the field; VAR did not see any clear and obvious error in the call to trigger a review. Michelle Cooper stepped up to the spot and coolly and powerfully dispatched the shot to break the deadlock.

Angel City had been putting all their energy into defending up until that point, so conceding the opener meant they had to take more risks to find an equalizer. That left previously largely unavailable space in behind for Chawinga to threaten.

Sveindís Jónsdóttir had a great chance in behind the Current defense 30 seconds into the second half of extra time, but after rounding the goalkeeper, she sent her shot into the side netting. Angel City made a Hail Mary substitution in the final minute — bringing in veteran Claire Emslie and record-setting 17-year-old goalscorer Maddie DiMaria for her NWSL debut — but it was too little too late.

The Kansas City Current will face the winner of the No. 1/No. 8 Sunday matchup between NJ/NY Gotham FC and Seattle Reign in the semifinals.
