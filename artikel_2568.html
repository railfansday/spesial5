Falcons vs. Bengals prediction: Stars on display in Madrid as Cincinnati seeks first international win

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Following a week without an international game, the NFL heads back overseas in Week 9 when it sends the Atlanta Falcons and Cincinnati Bengals to Spain. The Bernabéu will play host to American football for the second time in as many years as two divisional contenders descend upon Madrid.

The Bengals are in search of their first win away from American soil in franchise history after going 0-1-1 in their previous two opportunities (the most recent contest dating back to 2019), while the Falcons seek to improve to .500 in games abroad. Atlanta is 2-3 away from the United States and last season lost in Berlin.

This contest pits one of the league's hottest teams against a squad that aims to reestablish positive momentum. Cincinnati rides a three-game win streak into the Madrid Game and scored no fewer than 31 points in any of those triumphs, albeit with two of the matchups coming against bottom-tier competition. Atlanta, on the other hand, lost in two of its last three outings and went scoreless in its most recent showing.

Both sides are alive and well in their respective divisions, but the context between the two differs greatly. The Falcons are just a half game back of first place in the NFC South, which may be the weakest division in football with no teams at or above .500 through the season's first two months. The Bengals' position at half a game out of the AFC North lead is the superior standing considering three teams own winning records.

Fans of American football in Spain are in for a treat, because this game has all the makings of a shootout. There is not an offense in the NFL that is clicking quite like the Bengals over the last three weeks, and between Burrow, Chase, Higgins, London and Robinson, five of the biggest names in football will be in action in a star-studded battle. Falcons quarterback Michael Penix Jr. should bounce back from last week's dreadful 8-for-24 showing in the rain, so Atlanta ought to have enough juice to keep pace with a team that is averaging 36.3 points per game since Oct. 11.

In a matchup where points will be aplenty, the team with the better defense holds the edge. It is not as though Atlanta is vastly superior on that side of the ball, but it is much more reliable and is about to insert one of its projected top producers into the lineup for the first time this season. There is not a more opportune time for a disruptive pass rusher like Pearce to make his first appearance than against arguably the best quarterback in the NFL. That alone could be the difference in a high-variance matchup like this one. Pick: Falcons 34, Bengals 30
