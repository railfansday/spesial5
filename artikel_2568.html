Why Anthony Volpe could be Yankees' starting 2B in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Why Anthony Volpe could be Yankees' starting 2B in 2027 originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The New York Yankees have a few positional needs this offseason, including third base, second base, and catcher.

Jazz Chisholm Jr. is a free agent, and the Yankees have no obvious replacement at second base.

Chisholm hit 19 home runs and stole 41 bases this past season, but his overall performance felt underwhelming. A reunion with the two-time All-Star does not seem probable.

New York's internal options at second base include Anthony Volpe, Jose Caballero, and Luis Garcia Jr. However, the latter was a poor defender at the middle infield spot with the Washington Nationals before moving to first base.

Volpe seems to be the top candidate for the gig, The Athletic's Brendan Kuty wrote.

"Some Yankees coaches believe Volpe could quickly become one of the best defensive second basemen in the game, thanks to his smarts, motor, and quick turns at second base," Kuty wrote.

"With the position presumably less of a physical burden on Volpe than shortstop, there's an argument he could see an uptick in his offense, which has disappointed (career 84 wRC+)."

With George Lombard Jr. at shortstop, Volpe's future with the Yankees might be at second base.

According to Kuty, Yankees coaches are optimistic he can be a very strong defensive second baseman.

Brandon Lowe and Luis Arraez are two of the top free agents available at the position this offseason, but don't be surprised if Volpe enters spring training as the leading man for the job.
