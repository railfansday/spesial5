Sights and Sounds from San Antonio’s Gold Cup early round win at Sacramento

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Spurs put together two of their finest wins during SEGABAGA road victories over Sacramento and Portland. When the NBA schedule came out, my sister and I reviewed which game that we'd want to go to, saw the airfare prices to and from Austin and San Antonio…. and concluded Sacramento made the most sense for both of us.

Some observations from an excellent night in the Golden Center:

The best comp that I could come up with was slightly slower and less athletic Allen Iverson with Jamal Murray's shooting range on offense. However, he and the rest of his teammates seemed lost on most defensive possessions, which resulted in quite a few paint points for San Antonio.

Not only were there wide swaths of the lower bowl that had empty seats, but there was entire rows in the second level that lacked humans, too. Though the Kings did themselves no favors by falling and staying behind by nearly 20 points most of the game, the ambience itself lacked the fervor of past seasons.

Things I saw in-person that I haven't noticed on the good ole television:

Luke Kornet seems 15-20 pounds heavier than last season.

Domantas Sabonis tried a lot of his bull-in-a-china-shop physicality on Wembanyama in the paint – something that worked well in earlier seasons – but found it no longer works for him beyond occasionally.

Dylan Harper (see highlights below) looked like the best guard out there.

Wembanyama suffered his infamous face-plant during game 2 of the first-round series and turned his ankle rather gruesomely on Sabonis' closeout tonight – so maybe I shouldn't be going to these games in-person anymore.

I'm sad I didn't get video evidence of Jordan McLaughlin's dunk attempt in the second half. It was impressive!

Chik-Fil-A rewarded its fans with a free appetizer if a Spur missed two free throws in a row, and this might have been the biggest win tonight in Sacramento…. with the exception that fans had to claim their victory a) in the app and b) before 10 AM the next morning. I'll take breakfast tacos anytime.

What started as a bad omen but ultimately was a big nothing-burger: the Spurs' rim was being repaired for over 20 minutes right after Wembanyama finished his pre-game prep, affecting the tip-off by roughly 15 minutes or so. On a personal note, this was the first chance to watch the Spurs in-person with my sister and her two kids since my dad passed. During the last Spurs game here in Sacramento on March 17, my dad suffered his last major fall hurriedly trying to get into his TV room to watch the game. My Kings rep Gavin was kind enough to gift us post-game free throws, and Shirley credited my dad for willing her free throw into the net.
