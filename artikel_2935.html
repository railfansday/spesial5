Nick Herbig misses another practice ahead of Steelers-Eagles matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Steelers outside linebacker Nick Herbig was a non-participant for the second straight practice per Pittsburgh's Thursday injury report as he deals with a hip/ribs injury, the team announced.

However, both safety DeShon Elliott (knee) and wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. (foot) improved from limited participants Wednesday to full participants on Thursday. Defensive back Jalen Ramsey, who missed Wednesday's practice for non-injury/personal reasons, also returned in full on Thursday.

Linebacker Patrick Queen, who is dealing with a broken thumb, was limited for the second straight practice. Wide receiver Roman Wilson and guard Gennings Dunker logged full practices again as they, along with Pittman, remain on injured reserve but in their 21-day return windows.

Darnell Washington, TJ Watt, and Cam Heyward were full participants on Thursday after rest days on Wednesday.

The Eagles, the Steelers' Week 11 opponent, have yet to release their Thursday injury report at the time of this writing. On Wednesday, Philadelphia linebacker Zach Baun (back) was the team's lone non-participant. Key players such as DeVonta Smith, Nolan Smith, and Dontayvion Wicks were limited. Quarterback Jalen Hurts remains on injured reserve.
