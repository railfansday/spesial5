If I Could Buy Just 1 Dividend King With $1,000 This Month, It Would Be This One

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One of this year's surprising winners can keep the upticks coming.

By Rick Munarriz – Nov 24, 2026 at 11:00AM EST

Target (TGT +4.15%) shares hit another 52-week high on Tuesday. Bucking the market volatility isn't easy, but the discount retailer has turned heads with its 78% gain in 2026.

New CEO Michael Fiddelke is showing early signs of success in turning the "cheap chic" chain around. With stock gains on top of a 56-year streak of rising quarterly distributions, this is the Dividend King that I would buy with $1,000 this month.

Target stock is on the rise, but it wasn't always that way. The shares are still 35% below the all-time highs reached five years ago. It struggled in recent years, losing market share to online and offline rivals. It's clawing back now.

Fiddelke was promoted from within the company to lead the way in February. Internal CEOs often play it safe, but that wasn't going to shake the mass-market retailer from its recent bout of stagnancy. Fiddelke came in with a bold four-pillar plan to turn its fortunes around, and it's showing early signs of success.

The fiscal third-quarter results it announced this month were the latest in a series of bar-raising reports. Store-level comps rose 4.6%. A 9.8% jump in digital comp sales lifted overall comps 5.6% higher. Shoppers are returning to Target, with store traffic up 5% for the quarter.

Dividend Kings -- stocks that have come through with at least 50 consecutive years of payout hikes -- don't usually double the way that Target has over the past year. Stocks that have more than doubled over the past year aren't usually cheap or have high yields, but Target checks both boxes, trading for 16 times trailing earnings and a respectable yield of 2.8%. A $1,000 investment will generate $28 in annual dividend income, with hikes likely to continue next year and beyond.

It bears pointing out that tariff refunds helped boost its recent profitability, but momentum is clearly on Target's side now. Like one of its shopping carts with greased wheels, Target is rolling now. It's a stock I wouldn't mind buying, even after it hit a fresh 52-week high this week.
