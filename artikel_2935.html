Falcons snap counts from another dreary Sunday against the Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It's snap counts time, whether you like it or not.

Beaux Collins getting 25 snaps was not something I expected, but then, the Falcons were down by a lot late. Collins got open enough twice in the end zone for Penix to look his way, but didn't come down with either target; the Falcons clearly like what he brings to the table enough to get him more involved at the likely expense of Zaccheaus and Blair going forward. This team is still searching for a reliable third receiver and Zachariah Branch is on injured reserve, so I'd still stump for seeing more of the reserve tight ends.

Otherwise, nothing here is really a surprise. These are familiar faces who just need to play better.

I thought we'd see more Brown and perhaps Bowman at safety, but Hellams did indeed step into Jessie Bates' spot and play nearly every snap. What's more, he was good, leading the team in tackles with no misses and acquitting himself in coverage on a day where most of the secondary got torched. I've long been a Hellams fan but thought he had essentially fallen out of this team's plans on defense; Hellams can play his way into a more significant role in 2027 if he keeps this up.

Pearce is still getting up to speed, and the team's edge rushers are providing sporadic pressure at this point; I fully recognize the job is tougher against Mahomes in the first place. Getting Pearce ramped up and Abdullah back in the rotation should help with that somewhat, but when Snowden is your most productive guy off the edge, you might be in a little bit of trouble. Zero offense intended!

Hughes has been effectively parked after struggling throughout the season, but this is now back-to-back weeks where Terrell and Henderson have been in hell, Terrell in particular. It's not completely out of the question for Hughes to find his way back into playing time if that continues, but the grimness of possibly benching one of your starters for a starter who was previously benched is not doing wonders for my mood right now.

A pretty successful day on special teams as the rest of the team fell apart, with Abdullah getting a massive workload. Moody delivered an onside kick and made all his tries, Bailey had a fine day punting, the coverage teams didn't allow any significant returns, and Blair had the play of the day grabbing the onside kick. The last couple of weeks have given me hope that this group is settling in, and the kicker change gives me more hope. I just hope "special teams were good" isn't the nicest thing I can say about this team the rest of the way.

The only other note is that the Falcons keep talking about numbers as a reason for parking player like Bowman and Avieon Terrell, the latter of whom was inactive this week; I'm not sure getting a handful of snaps out of TJ Quinn on special teams is worth sitting an effective defender.
