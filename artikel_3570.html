Lindsey Vonn Posts Gruesome Photos and Videos After Her 9th Leg Surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lindsey Vonn provided another graphic — and gory — look into the aftermath of her latest leg surgery as she continues her arduous recovery process.

Vonn, 42, shared a series of photos and videos via social media on Saturday, November 14, including some of her bloody left leg shortly after last week’s operation.

“First few days have gone as well as could be expected,” she wrote via Instagram. “Got a little bit of fresh air in between rehab sessions. That little bit [of] normalcy helps get me through the day. One step at a time…”

Vonn underwent surgery on her ACL on Wednesday, November 11, the ninth procedure she’s had done on her left leg after multiple crashes earlier this year, including one at the 2026 Winter Olympics.

“It took over 5 hours to remove all of the plates and screws from my leg and replace my ACL,” she shared on Thursday, November 12. “I am home from the hospital now and thankful to be on the final road to a full recovery.”

Vonn showed off a plastic bag filled with the metal plates and screws that were taken out during surgery.

Before undergoing the latest surgery, Vonn admitted the date had been “looming on my calendar for a while now… and the time has finally come.”

“I’ve been nervous and anxious about it…but at the same time excited to hopefully put this behind me,” she wrote via Instagram. “This will be my 9th surgery and I’m praying my last. There are still things that could change in surgery so we will have to wait and see how it goes but I will keep you posted on my progress when I am out of surgery and recovering.”

Vonn added, “I am in the best hands though and thankful for the help of my doctors, physical therapist, nurses and medical staff 🙏🏻. Thank you again for all of the support over these last 9 months. It has truly meant the world to me to receive so much love and encouragement. 🙏🏻❤️ As my Mother said, ‘Today is another great day!’… I hope she’s right 🙏🏻🤞🏻.”

The skier completely ruptured her ACL during a crash in the final race of the World Cup season in Crans-Montana, Switzerland, on January 30.

Despite the injury, Vonn still competed at the Winter Olympics days later. She suffered a compound fracture and tibial plateau fracture in her left leg during a crash in the women’s downhill final on February 8.
