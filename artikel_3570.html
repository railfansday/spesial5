Donald Trump Jr. Fumes After Netflix Posts Steamy Promo on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Donald Trump Jr. is not a fan of Netflix’s latest teaser for its hit show The Hunting Wives.

The streaming giant posted a steamy clip via X on Thursday, November 26, to promote the popular series. The footage featured characters Sophie (Brittany Snow) and Margo (Malin Åkerman) passionately kissing in bed.

Netflix captioned the post, “This Thanksgiving plate.”

Trump, 48, evidently took umbrage at the post. In response, he fumed via his own X account, “What is wrong with you??? It’s Thanksgiving.”

The Hunting Wives has been a breakout success for Netflix. The series, which premiered in July 2025, was originally filmed for Starz before Lionsgate Television acquired it (and Netflix licensed U.S. rights to the series for a year).

After moving to Netflix, there was an increase in sizzling scenes including between love interests Sophie and Margo as their romance heated up.

Based on May Cobb’s book of the same name, The Hunting Wives features Sophie, who moved from Boston to Texas and found herself drawn to prominent socialite Margo amid the politics, murder and chaos occurring in their small town.

Netflix has used trailers that showcased the characters’ sizzling sexual chemistry previously, including a recent teaser in October ahead of season 2 kicking off.

“Let’s wash off all of last night’s sins,” Margo told Sophie as they took a bath together in the promotional clip, to which Sophie replied, “I don’t think we could wash off what we did last night.”

Speaking exclusively to Us when the show debuted, Åkerman, 48, explained the role sexuality played in the show.

“[My character] gains power through many different ways. Part of her manipulation is sex, it is sexual and it’s how a lot of people operate in this world and in order to gain control. So when you have that power — and she’s very aware of it — she knows how to use it and she does,” Åkerman told Us.

She continued, “So I like that about her. That’s what becomes controversial and fun and salacious and saucy, and that’s why we want to watch her.”

For her part, Snow, 40, explained during an October 2025 appearance on the “Not Skinny, Not Fat” podcast why Åkerman was the perfect person to work with for such NSFW scenes.

“Something really great about Malin, too, is she’s really, really attuned to what I was thinking or what others were thinking in terms of comfortability,” she shared. “So she was always very aware — [since] she’s Swedish — that she’s more open with nudity. She is still a woman and wants to look good and isn’t just walking around naked. But I was more conscientious about it.”

Snow continued: “She would go like, ‘Do you want me to put my body here so you don’t have to show this part of yourself?’ I was like, ‘This is so selfless and nice.’”
