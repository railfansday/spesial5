Finding the right sports bra is crucial. That’s because a well-fitted one keeps your girls locked in and comfortable during jogs, stretches, and squats, so you don’t have to worry about spillage or bounce.

To find the best of the best sports bras, our fitness and style editors tested more than 50 options—from high-impact sports bras designed to minimize bounce to softer options from our fave activewear brands that prioritize comfort. We evaluated each pick for support, fit, comfort, and performance across a range of workouts and body types.

The Performance Flex Bra offers medium support, but we’ve found it minimizes bounce without restricting movement, thanks to a higher neckline and light built-in padding that helps me feel secure while I train. The first time I pulled on the Forme Power+ Bra, I instinctively rolled my shoulders forward like I usually do at my desk—except this time, they didn’t stay there. Within seconds, the bra gently guided them back, opening up my chest and lifting my upper spine so I stood noticeably taller without consciously trying. It’s a subtle shift, but one you feel immediately: less hunching, more length through your torso, and a sense that your upper body is quietly being coached into better alignment.

The Power+ is made from a compressive blend of polyester, nylon, and spandex and features multiple tension fabrics with strategically placed, double-layered support panels throughout the back and shoulders. Together, they create a gentle pull that encourages your shoulders to stay back and your chest open. Despite its posture-focused design, the Power+ also functions as a solid sports bra. The compression keeps everything comfortably locked in during strength sessions, cardio workouts, and high-impact movement, while the breathable fabric prevents that heavy, trapped-in-sweat feeling some compressive bras create. Wide straps, generous arm openings, and a full-coverage back also help smooth things out and give the bra a flattering, secure fit.

Another unexpected perk: It’s just as helpful during everyday wear as it is in the gym. When looking for a sports bra, you should know the difference between compression and encapsulation in your bra. Compression, which can be found in the Nike or Lululemon sports bras, offers the feeling of your boobs being so supported that they’re a bit squished and pushed down. Encapsulation lifts, cradles, and separates your girls while still eliminating boob bounce. It can be found in the Beyond Yoga bra.
