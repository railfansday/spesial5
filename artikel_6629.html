Will <i><em>Blade Runner 2099</em></i> Get a Season 2? What We Know So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Blade Runner 2099 serves as the third official entry into the Blade Runner universe, with Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer starring as the leads in Prime Video’s latest installment in the cyberpunk franchise. In the series, the women team up to form a Replicant duo on a quest to track down a fellow Blade Runner who’s gone rogue—except Schafer’s character, Cora, keeps her human identity a secret from Olwen (Yeoh). The stakes are high and the fight scenes are bloody, but the actresses’ chemistry with each other leads to several standout performances on-screen.

The show received just eight episodes, but fans are already wondering if they’ll see more of Schafer and Yeoh together on-screen for a second installment. Here’s what we know about the possibility of Blade Runner 2099 season 2.

No, Blade Runner 2099 has not been renewed for a second season, and it likely won’t be—the show was released as an eight-episode limited series for Prime Video. The show’s creator and showrunner, Silka Luisa, revealed during a July 2026 event at San Diego Comic-Con that the arc of the show was always meant to be complete with just one season.

“It was always considered a limited series,” she said in an interview with Collider. “Part of making the transition from film to TV is a really big jump for something that’s as cinematic as Blade Runner, and so, in that sense, it was really important to us that we told a story that, in a way, is like a movie in the sense that there’s a beginning, there’s a middle, and there’s an end.”

There’s a chance that Blade Runner could receive another series in the future, but executive producer Broderick Johnson explained in an interview with Polygon that he doesn’t want to expand upon the franchise too soon.

“We’ve been very judicious not to overexpose the IP or flood the marketplace, but strategically we’re always thinking about the next chapters in the franchise,” he said. “This was a long, challenging, but very fulfilling journey that we hope audiences enjoy, and it really fills out a lot more of the mythology because it’s all in canon. We’re definitely thinking about new things to do with the next iteration.”
