Vogue’s Best Dressed People of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Our goal was 50—just 50!—of the best-dressed people of the year, a list to commemorate the unforgettable fashion moments of a very topsy-turvy 2026. Sounds easy…but, well, list-making never is. There were athletes who turned game days into runways, actors who owned the red carpet and the sidewalk, and method-dressing movie stars who artfully ignored the meaning of the word cut.

We wanted them all, plus some highly fashionable characters from unexpected fields and walks of life, and we wanted couples, which we insisted should be counted as singletons (a technicality!). We struggled and horse-traded and voted and arrived finally at…55! (The same number as last year.) Here is the fruit of our labor: an unranked, diligently categorized, highly subjective list of Vogue’s best dressed of 2026. And it all begins with our cover star, a selection that was the result of a rather heated debate. Enjoy!

Written by: Christian Allaire, Margaux Anbouba, Taylor Antrim, Max Berlinger, Laird Borrelli-Persson, Anna Cafolla, Liam Hess, Alexandra Hildreth, Hannah Jackson, Joan Kennedy, Irene Kim, Anna Grace Lee, Nicole Phelps, Madeleine Schulz, and Emma Specter.

STORRIE TIME Conner Storrie brings the Ken-ergy in a miniaturized version of his Gucci Best Dressed cover look. Coach watch pendant.

Why 2026 was his year: Playing brooding Russian hockey player Ilya Rozanov in the gay romance Heated Rivalry—in various states of deshabille—launched this 26-year-old Hollywood hopeful waiting tables in Los Angeles to cultural supernova. His last 12 months have seen a kind of fashion miracle: Storrie is now better known for what he wears than what he doesn’t. Click here for his cover-story conversation with Chloe Malle—which ranged from Storrie’s punk roots to his adoration of Parker Posey, his side hustle in “provocateur” photography, and more.
