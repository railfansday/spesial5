How to Make the Most of Amazon’s Early Black Friday Deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Here’s what’s worth shopping early versus what can wait.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Amazon’s Black Friday sale is one ELLE editors never skip. The retailer just announced its official lead-up sale starts November 19, but you don’t need to wait until then to save big. Hundreds of worthwhile markdowns on fashion, beauty, tech, and home products are already live, including sought-after items like Dyson vacuums, Apple AirPods, Shark hair tools, Puma Speedcat sneakers, and Tory Burch handbags during the retailer’s countdown to Black Friday.

Here, we’ll highlight the best markdowns to shop early and offer insights on what you can wait to buy. Shop early, and you can spend the holiday weekend enjoying time with your loved ones instead of scrolling online. Whether you’re finalizing your fall wardrobe, upgrading your tech, or getting ahead on gift shopping, Amazon has plenty of active deals to get the job done.

Amazon’s Black Friday sale is famous for its discounts on tech items like headphones, TVs, and laptops. In years past, we’ve seen Apple products sell out before the holiday, so it’s worth taking advantage of the brand’s existing markdowns to avoid missing out. Popular art TVs from Samsung and Hisense are also included in the retailer’s lead-up deals, but Kindles and Oura rings are still full price.

Amazon sales are one of our secret sources for great handbags. Versatile everyday shoulder bags, totes, and party-ready mini-bags from Kate Spade, Vince, and other popular labels make for easy gifts—but at up to 50 percent off, they’re worth keeping for yourself.

Amazon’s lead-up deals are especially strong in hair care. CHI’s classic flat iron is under $40, and Shark slashed prices on its viral multi-stylers. Those who can’t wait until Cyber Monday for a restock can grab discounted shampoos, hair masks, hairspray, and other essential products from Lolavie, Shu Uemura, and other premium brands.
