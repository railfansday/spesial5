Songs of Freedom Benefit Show For Women & Children of Ukraine to Feature Paul McCartney, Barbra Streisand, Sean Penn & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The show will take place at Co-Op Lie in Manchester, U.K. on Feb. 3, 2027.

A huge benefit show aimed at raising money for the women and children of Ukraine nearly five years into the country’s war against aggressor Russia will take place at Co-Op Live in Manchester, U.K. on Feb. 3, 2027. According to the list of the first wave of announced performers, Songs of Freedom will feature sets from Bob Geldof, The Doobie Brothers, the Pretenders’ singer Chrissie Hynde, as well as the Lathums, Nick Lowe, Inspiral Carpets, Lottery Winners, Brooke Combe, Chris Difford, Midge Ure, Alabama 3, Graham Parker and Johnny & the Hardiwckes. While a headliner act has not yet been revealed, the show is also slated to feature video appearances from Sir Paul McCartney, Barbra Streisand, Sean Penn, Ukrainian rockers Okean Elzy, Huey Lewis & the News, Ron Sexsmith, and Flyte.

“I am really pleased to have the opportunity to play my part in helping the Children and Communities in Ukraine,” said Live Aid producer Harvey Goldsmith in a statement. “This war has gone on far too long, and more and more children are suffering as a result. The concert at Co-Op in Manchester will highlight their plight. We must stand together and play a role in pressuring the need to stop this war.”

Proceeds from tickets sales will go to support a variety of organizations, including UNITED24, Voices of Children, People of the Future and the Masha Foundation.

Other acts slated to participate in the show include: The Molotovs, Yee Loi, The Blockheads, Joe King Carraskco, Springbok Nude Girls, Within Temptation, Davina Michelle, Boombox, Schmalgauzen, Songs for Ukraine Choir and the London Metropolitan Orchestra.
