Serena Williams winds down her VC firm, launches new fund, seeks $200M

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Serena Williams and longtime business partner Beth Ferreira announced Monday the launch of an early-stage firm called Starfire Ventures.

The firm is seeking to raise $200 million in capital to back AI-native companies that are “solving critical, underserved problems for individuals across the experience economy,” including in areas such as fintech and health and wellness, the fund said in a press release announcing its launch.

This fund is separate from Serena Ventures, which Williams founded in 2022. A rep for Starfire confirmed that Serena Ventures has recently cut its last check, though a team remains to support the existing portfolio. Serena Ventures backed more than 100 companies and raised over $100 million in capital. Ferreira worked as an advisor and general partner at Serena Ventures and has co-invested with Williams since 2015 when they backed MasterClass’ seed round.

This new firm, Starfire, is a continuation of that business partnership, with Williams saying in the press release that it was “designed to live beyond” them and that the goal remains to broaden “access to capital” and create “opportunities for more people to participate in the ownership and growth of transformative businesses.”
