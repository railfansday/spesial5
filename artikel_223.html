Nvidia’s long-time boss Jensen Huang has stepped down over the scandal, and the company has appointed finance chief Colette Kress as interim chief executive.

double quotation markThe Board of Directors announced today that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately.

double quotation markNvidia was already facing an unusually demanding expectations bar. The risk is not that Nvidia suddenly loses its technology roadmap, that roadmap is largely visible for the next several years. The risk is execution.
