Senegal-Morocco, a new standoff looms after the CAS verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Senegal can breathe. After more than ten months of controversy surrounding the CAN 2025 final, the verdict is in. Winner on the field (1-0) against Morocco, Senegal was then deprived of its title by the CAF appeal jury, which awarded the victory on the green carpet to the host country following the incidents that occurred during the match. But the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) finally returned the trophy to Sadio Mané’s teammates.

The day after this decision, the Confederation of African Football revealed the three finalists for the 2026 African Ballon d’Or. Unsurprisingly, Senegal and Morocco are largely represented. CAF selected two Senegalese internationals, Sadio Mané and Pape Gueye, as well as three Moroccans: Achraf Hakimi, Brahim Diaz and Ismaël Saibari.

An absentee, however, causes a lot of talk. This is Ismaïla Sarr, the Crystal Palace winger, who is not among the finalists. The Senegalese nevertheless had a remarkable season, winning the Conference League with his club. Top scorer in the competition with nine goals, he was also named best player of the event.

The duel between Senegal and Morocco also continues in the other categories. For the trophy for the best young African player of the year, the Moroccan Ayyoub Bouaddi and the Senegalese Ibrahim Mbaye will compete with the Ivorian Yan Diomandé. Finally, Cape Verde joins the two CAN 2025 finalists among the contenders for the title of best African selection of the year. The winners will be announced on December 2.
