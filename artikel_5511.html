Mountain Modern Home With Views of Two Ski Resorts Lists for $15.3 Million in Park City

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

At the end of a private drive in Park City, UT, a mountain modern home with views of not one but two ski resorts has come on the market for $15,250,000.

Designed by Aspen-based CCY Architects, the home at 15 Eagle Pointe Court balances modernist sophistication with mountain coziness, featuring Telluride Gold stonework, an oxidized Corten steel roof and Western red cedar siding.

"Modern lines of steel and glass are paired with warm wood finishes and natural stone, providing seamless indoor-outdoor living within a private mountain landscape," says listing agent Michael LaPay of Summit Sotheby's International Realty Park City.

LaPay knows the house well. He represented the original owners, who built it in 2005, and has handled its last two sales as well.

The four-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom residence spans 7,053 square feet on 2.69 acres in Park Meadows.

"This property's size and location at the end of a private drive is a rarity in the coveted Park Meadows neighborhood, positioned on a height of land that takes in panoramic ski run views," LaPay says.

Inside, the floors are French Vanilla slate from India. The great room opens to a south-facing deck that looks out at the runs of Park City Mountain Resort and Deer Valley Resort, each 10 to 15 minutes away.

Recent renovations reworked the kitchen and bathrooms. The kitchen now has gourmet appliances, custom cabinetry and an oversized island topped in marble, and the primary bathroom comes with an expansive steam shower. Other updates include a custom bunkroom, a gym and outdoor turf areas for recreation.

Outside, heated patios are linked by sculptural staircases, and the driveway is heated, too. A three-car garage completes the property.

"There is a wonderful walking path up to a summit that is perfect for a 'sundowner' gathering to enjoy sunsets," LaPay says.

The property borders Round Valley, a preserved open space with trails for hiking, biking and cross-country skiing that are maintained year-round.

Besides being close to the area's top ski resorts, the estate is less than 20 minutes from the historic Main Street and its shops, galleries and restaurants, and a five-minute drive from the Park Meadows Country Club.

Salt Lake City International Airport and private terminals in Salt Lake City and Heber City are all about 30 minutes away, close enough for a weekend of skiing.
