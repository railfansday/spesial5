webAI Launches webAI App for On-Device AI With No Per-Token Charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The webAI app lets anyone build teams of AI experts that work across Slack, DocuSign, Hugging Face, Apple apps and more, while their files stay on their Mac.

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- webAI today released the webAI app for Mac to the public for the first time. The app runs webAI's new small reasoning models on the user's own computer, so people can use AI on private work without sending files to the cloud or paying per token.

Most AI runs in the cloud, where usage is metered by tokens or capped by plan, and every document goes to someone else's servers. webAI runs the model on the Mac itself. A user can work through a sensitive client proposal or an unfinished business plan, revise it as many times as they want, and never hit a token limit.

"With the webAI app, we're making cloud AI optional," said David Stout, CEO and co-founder of webAI. "Why should you have to send a private document to an online service just to get help with it? Your Mac can do the work. You keep control of what you share, and you can ask as many follow-up questions as you need without watching a token meter."

In the app, users create Personas: AI experts with a role, knowledge and tools the user chooses. Several Personas can work together on the same task. A consultant could have one pull client requirements from project notes, a second draft the proposal and a third check the draft for anything missed.

Connectors work with the apps you already use, including Slack, DocuSign, Hugging Face and Apple apps. You control what each Persona can access and what actions it can take. A Persona can pull a client's latest decisions from Slack, see which contracts are still unsigned in DocuSign, and draft follow-ups for your approval. You spend less time hunting for context and copying details between apps.
