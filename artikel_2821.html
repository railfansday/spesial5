Lakers coach unveils eyebrow-raising reason for Luka Doncic’s viral clipboard moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

PHOENIX — Luka Dončić went viral for grabbing the team's clipboard from the coaching staff and bringing it into the players' huddle late during the Lakers' win over the Suns.

And the reasoning wasn't as entertaining as the video made it out to seem.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers talks to the media after a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

While assistant coaches Scott Brooks and Lindsey Harding were using the clipboard in the coaches' huddle during a timeout with 3:04 left in the game with the Lakers leading the Suns 122-114, Dončić grabbed the clipboard out of Harding's hands because he had a message to deliver to his teammates.

The message was about something he saw defensively a minute before the timeout.

"We had switched [to] zone and Collin Gillespie missed a [3-pointer], we were soft with our pickup point," coach JJ Redick said. "He's a laser, he's a four-point line pickup guy. He took a 3 and missed it, but ended up getting an offensive rebound. Luka was just saying just be up versus their lasers."

Luka Doncic #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers celebrates during a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Luka Doncic #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers attempts a shot against Jordan Goodwin #23 of the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Arizona, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Dončić wasn't as forthcoming with what he communicated with his teammates.

"That stays in the team, man," he said. "That will stay in the team. They know what I was communicating."

Dončić's message seemed to have gotten through, with the Lakers holding on for their eighth consecutive victory. That includes winning all four games on Los Angeles' road trip.

They now head home to face the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday, the first of a back-to-back at The Crypt.
