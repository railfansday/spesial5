Colts are expected to take best offer for Jonathan Taylor at deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The trade chatter regarding Colts running back Jonathan Taylor has lasted for two weeks. It will last at least two more days.

The current belief in league circles is that the Colts will take the best offer that's on the table as the deadline approaches at 4:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday.

Sure, the Colts would like to get as much as they can, ideally landing the same package the Panthers got from the 49ers for running back Christian McCaffrey. But the Colts are likely to take what they can get, with the market for Taylor set by the competition among the teams that make an offer.

Regardless, the 2-7 Colts are ready to sell. At this point, they should be ready to move anyone — and everyone. They're hoping to stockpile draft picks for the next General Manager. The more picks that compile, the more attractive the job becomes.

So whether it's the teams that have been linked to Taylor (Giants, Vikings, Seahawks, Broncos, Packers) or someone unexpected (like the 49ers), a trade is expected to happen before the clock strikes 4:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10.
