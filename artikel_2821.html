The Chiefs took care of business in soft three-game stretch

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's time for the Kansas City Chiefs to turn the page to a perennially important matchup with the AFC's No. 1 seed, the Buffalo Bills, on Thanksgiving night. It's the first of three road games against teams with winning records (the Los Angeles Rams on the following Thursday before squaring off with the Cincinnati Bengals).

The anticipation for this game has built since the Chiefs' last-second loss to the Denver Broncos in Week 8, which sent Kansas City to a 5-2 record. Since then, the softest three-game stretch of the team's schedule provided a chance to ramp up before squaring off with Buffalo.

In that time, the Chiefs seldom played with their food, keeping their foot on the gas pedal against the New York Jets, Atlanta Falcons, and Arizona Cardinals for a combined 104-32 score. Backup quarterback Justin Fields took the final snaps for the offense in all three games, including almost the entirety of the fourth quarter against the Cardinals.

It's exactly what the team needed ahead of the rest of the schedule, seemingly filled with playoff contenders.

The Chief took care of business in style, too. The team has averaged 422 total yards over the last four weeks, including the loss to Denver, a rate that would lead the NFL for the season by more than 20 yards.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes has also been dominant in this stretch, rarely losing rhythm with the Chiefs' offense since the debacle against the Seattle Seahawks. He has not thrown an interception since that game nearly a month ago.

There was only so much Kansas City could prove about its odds of winning the Super Bowl this year in the last three wins, but the team did seemingly all anyone in Chiefs Kingdom could ask for: dominate bad teams thoroughly.

Now, the Chiefs need to take the next step and find a way to win crucial road games, especially against conference foes vying for the No. 1 seed like Buffalo and Cincinnati.
