Eagles QB unexpectedly ruled questionable vs. Giants: Trade could be next

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Eagles backup quarterback Tanner McKee was ruled questionable for Sunday's game against the New York Giants with a rib injury.

This decision comes four days after the Eagles benched McKee for Joe Milton. If McKee is not cleared to play against the Giants, fifth-round rookie quarterback Cole Payton will be the top backup.

McKee was a limited participant during Friday's practice.

After Jalen Hurts was placed on injured reserve, McKee became the starter in Week 6. He completed 72 of 114 passes for 638 yards and two touchdowns in three starts. He also threw two interceptions.

"Obviously, that's always a difficult thing to think through because it isn't ever just on one person," Eagles coach Nick Sirianni said on Friday about benching McKee. "Matter of fact, I think Tanner played solid, but this is just what I felt like we needed to do right at this particular moment to give ourselves the best chance to win this weekend."

On Friday, Sports Illustrated reported that NFL teams believe the Eagles could attempt to trade McKee before Tuesday's NFL trade deadline. That would clear the way for Payton to be the backup for the remainder of the season.

McKee is likely playing his final season with the Eagles. He is not expected to re-sign with the team as a free agent this upcoming offseason.

Milton or Payton could become the long-term backup quarterback for the Eagles. Milton joined the Eagles last month from the Dallas Cowboys' practice squad.
