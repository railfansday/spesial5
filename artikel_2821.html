Iowa basketball announces Brava Roof Tile as jersey patch partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Iowa Athletics and the Hawkeyes' men's basketball program have announced Brava Roof Tile as its first official jersey patch partner.

Brava's logo will appear on the team's jerseys above the Big Ten conference emblem. Iowa debuted the patch for the first time during its Nov. 10 matchup against Virginia Tech at the Tyson Events Center in Sioux City, Iowa.

In addition to jersey patch placement, the partnership will include student-athlete influencer marketing opportunities featuring Iowa basketball players, allowing Brava to extend its reach through authentic, player-driven content.

"Becoming the first jersey patch partner for Iowa men's basketball is a proud milestone for our entire team and a reflection of what we've built together at Brava," said Adam Brantman, CEO of Brava. "This partnership brings our brand to a national stage alongside the Hawkeyes while celebrating our Iowa roots. We're excited to support the student-athletes and share this milestone with our team and Hawkeye fans everywhere."

Founded in 2008 in Washington, Iowa, Brava has grown into one of the nation's leading manufacturers of premium synthetic roofing products. From its headquarters and manufacturing operations in eastern Iowa, the company produces high-performance roofing solutions that authentically replicate the appearance of natural cedar shake, slate, and Spanish barrel tile while delivering superior durability, weather resistance, and long-term value.

Brava's products are installed on residential, commercial, and historic properties across all 50 states and throughout North America, making the company a trusted choice for architects, contractors, and property owners seeking the beauty of traditional roofing materials without the maintenance challenges. With more than 600 employees, Brava is a significant Iowa-based employer and a strong  example of the state's growing advanced manufacturing sector.

The partnership was secured by Learfield's Hawkeye Sports Properties, the exclusive revenue generation partner of Iowa Athletics responsible for managing the department's corporate sponsorship agreements.
