Everton XI vs Brentford – Predicted lineup and team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Everton travel to Brentford on Monday night looking to extend a run of three straight wins in all competitions, in the final Premier League match of gameweek 11.

David Moyes's side beat Coventry City 3-1 before the international break, Thierno Barry scoring twice and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall once, and have won three in a row in all tournaments after beating Newcastle United in the EFL Cup and the league. Everton lost back-to-back league games to Chelsea and Arsenal after an unbeaten start of six games. They have won three, drawn one and lost one at the Hill Dickinson Stadium, but only one of five away, the 1-0 win at Newcastle. Everton are unbeaten in their last four Premier League matches at Brentford, drawing three and winning one, and the sides drew 2-2 there on 11 April.

Everton have a full squad available, with Moyes confirming the Toffees are injury-free heading into gameweek 11. Jack Grealish is restored to the squad following his breach of club discipline a few weeks ago. The Manchester City loanee played in a friendly during the international break.

A place on the bench is more likely than a start for Grealish, with Tyler Dibling and Tyrique George expected to keep the wide roles. They will support Thierno Barry and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall, who both scored in the win over Coventry. Brentford will be without the suspended Igor Thiago, who misses three matches after his red card at Brighton. Callum Wilson is expected to replace him, and Fabio Carvalho could return for the hosts. Brentford have conceded just three league goals at home this season, and all three of their home wins have been to nil.

Everton possible starting XI: Pickford; Rohl, Tarkowski, Branthwaite, Mykolenko; Garner, Armstrong; Dibling, Dewsbury-Hall, George; Barry

The match kicks off at 20:00 GMT on Monday, 23 November 2026.

The match will be broadcast live on Sky Sports.
