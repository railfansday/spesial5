Michigan State Men’s and Women’s Soccer Teams Selected For NCAA Tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NCAA held its Selection Shows this afternoon for the men's and women's tournaments. For both Spartan squads, it was not a matter of if they would make it in, but rather how good of a seed would they earn.

The Men's Team finished their season with a 10-5-3 record after a first-round loss in the Big Ten Tournament. Despite the early exit in that tournament, the Spartans still picked up a 4-seed for the NCAA Tournament and thus have a first-round bye in the six-round, 48-team event.

MSU will get things going this coming Sunday, November 22 at 2 PM ET, with a home game against the winner of Marshall and Bowling Green. There is no television information at this point. That first-round contest is this Thursday at Marshall. If MSU advances past that, then they would go on to the third round to take on, presumably, the 1-seed in our region, Akron.

The tournament's first four rounds are all on campus sites at the highest seeded team. If Akron gets upset in their second-round game, we will then get to host another game.

If the Spartans can get on a run, this year's College Cup (Final Four) is being held in Cary, NC, and will be played on December 11 and 14.

On the women's side, our Spartans got an even better placement. Unlike the men, the women play a full 64-team field so there are no byes. That said, the MSU Women's Team, fresh off a 15-4-2 campaign and a berth in the Big Ten Tournament final, earned one of the four 1-seeds and are rewarded with playing a 16-seed to get things going.

This will be the Lady Spartans 5th straight appearance in the NCAA Tournament under Coach Jeff Hosler. We have won at least one game in each of the previous four tournaments and have made it to the round of 16 the last three seasons. This year, we open with Central Connecticut State. The game is this coming Saturday at 3:30 PM ET and will stream on ESPN+.

As a 1-seed, MSU would play at home as long as they advance up until the College Cup. The women's tournament is also ending in Cary, NC.

Since we know our football team will not be appearing in any postseason this year, let's all give some support to what the rest of the world calls football and catch some soccer action this coming weekend as both our teams chase tournament glory.
