Kentucky vs. Wichita State game discussion and pregame reading

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Kentucky Wildcats are back in action tonight as they welcome the Wichita State Shockers to town in what has become a must-win game for the Cats entering into the seventh game of the season. Game time is set for 9 PM ET on ESPN2.

The biggest storyline in this game will be the battle of the frontcourt. The Shockers pose a big threat with William Berg in the paint, while the Cats have struggled in the post quite a bit to start the season. They will also be shorthanded as Malachi Moreno is expected to be out in tonight's game as he is in concussion protocol.

That turns this game into one where the backcourt of Zoom Diallo, Alex Wilkins, and Milan Momcilovic have to really play well and lead this game. Not just in scoring, but also in dictating the pace. Can the Cats make the Shockers play the style of game they want to? If so, that will really help give the Cats a chance.

It is no secret of the struggles this team has had so far this year. A win tonight will help calm those down. Lose this game, though, and the critique will be even louder. An important one in Rupp Arena tonight.
