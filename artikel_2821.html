Carson Wentz has foot stepped on, but stays in game

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Vikings quarterback Carson Wentz had his foot stepped on by offensive lineman Joe Huber with 7 seconds remaining in the third quarter.

The Vikings were forced to call a timeout after the play clock was running down, and then Wentz again threw incomplete before heading into the sideline medical tent.

The Vikings had 34 yards in the third quarter, with Wentz going 0-for-4 with two interceptions. He should have had a third on the play his foot was stepped on, hitting linebacker Terrel Bernard in the numbers. Bernard dropped it.

Max Brosmer was warming up while Wentz was in the tent.

Wentz is only the fourth quarterback in the past 14 seasons with multiple interceptions and no completions in a quarter, via Josh Dubow of the Associated Press. Wentz joins Matthew Stafford against the Vikings in 2021; Zach Wilson against the Patriots in 2021; and Marcus Mariota against the Dolphins in 2018.

The Bills lead the Vikings 20-6 with 6:17 left in the fourth quarter.
