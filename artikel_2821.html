Chelsea Starting XI vs Nottingham Forest: Confirmed Team News and Predicted Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chelsea head to the City Ground on Friday night needing a response. Two straight defeats, 2-1 against Sunderland and 1-0 against Leeds, have sharpened the focus on Xabi Alonso and his squad, and there is no hiding from it. Results drive the conversation, and Chelsea are giving themselves problems.

The one clear positive is Moises Caicedo. The midfielder has been passed fit after going off in the first half against Leeds, which matters because Chelsea have looked short of control without him. Alonso needs stability in the middle of the pitch and Caicedo gives him that.

There is less good news elsewhere. Joao Pedro remains unavailable with the thigh injury that has already cost him several weeks, while Maxence Lacroix is also out. Jorrel Hato is still missing too, and Reece James remains a long-term absentee as he continues his recovery from knee surgery. His expected return is still mid-January.

This is where Chelsea's recent slide starts to make more sense. Joao Pedro's absence has hurt them badly. His movement, link play and finishing have been missing, and the attack has lacked fluency without him. Emmanuel Emegha has stepped in and finally got his first goal for the club against Leeds, which at least gives Alonso something to think about.

Emegha should keep his place. A striker scores, you do not overcomplicate it. Chelsea need presence up front and they need a penalty-box reference point. He offers both, even if the overall performance level around him still needs work.

The bigger question is shape. Chelsea can stay with the 4-2-3-1, or go back to the 3-4-2-1 used earlier in the season. Right now, the simpler option looks the smarter one. Keep Caicedo and Romeo Lavia together, let Cole Palmer operate centrally, and avoid turning this into a tactical experiment.

Forest have had their own problems, but Chelsea are in no position to treat this lightly. They need order, aggression and fewer excuses.

Chelsea predicted XI vs Nottingham Forest: Martinez, Acheampong, Fofana, Colwill, Chavarria, Caicedo, Lavia, Rogers, Palmer, Neto, Emegha.

Kick-off is 8pm on Friday November 27, 2026, at the City Ground in Nottingham. For Chelsea, the equation is straightforward. Win, and the noise dies down a little. Lose, and it gets louder.
