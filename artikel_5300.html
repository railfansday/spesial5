4 things to know about your Hulu account as the app folds into Disney+

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Disney is folding Hulu into Disney+ in December 2026. See what happens to your plan, profiles, watch history and live TV.

Disney is making this move to strengthen its own flagship app, not to improve Hulu. Chief Executive Officer Josh D'Amaro and Chief Financial Officer Hugh Johnston said that "our ambition is for Disney+ to become the digital centerpiece of The Walt Disney Company." The company expects the combined app to bring more engagement and less churn, according to TechRadar. In plain terms, Disney wants people to watch more and cancel less, so it has every reason to make the move easy and little reason to make anyone start over.

The sensible step is to stay put and keep an eye on the bill, because one app is the only clear gain so far.
