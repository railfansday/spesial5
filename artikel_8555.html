Open Pibank Savings if you want one of the highest savings rates on any balance and rarely withdraw. It charges no fees, even for wires, but a wire is the only way to withdraw, and your bank may charge to receive it. If you move money often, choose an account with free transfers to and from your bank.

Pibank Savings, from the Miami bank Pibank, N.A., is managed in an app and suits cash you move out of a low-paying account and plan to leave alone. On $10,000, each 0.10 percentage point Pibank pays above another account adds $10 a year, less than a $15 fee your bank might charge to receive one wire.

Pibank Savings is worth it for savers who withdraw a few times a year or whose bank charges nothing to receive a wire, since Pibank charges nothing to send one. Savers who move money in and out every month should pick an account with free transfers to and from their bank instead.
