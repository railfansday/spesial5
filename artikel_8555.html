Join Survey Junkie if you want small rewards for spare minutes and fit the audiences brands look for, since you can cash out from $5 and PayPal usually pays within hours. Skip it if you need real income, because its own figures work out to under $2 an hour.

Survey Junkie pays adults points, 100 to the dollar, for answering brands’ market research surveys, though a survey can screen you out partway if you don’t fit the audience a brand wants. Its help center says three surveys a day earns $40 a month, and if each takes its 15-minute average, a 30-day month of that pays about $1.78 an hour by our math, before counting screen-outs.

Survey Junkie is worth joining for adults who’d turn spare minutes into PayPal money or gift cards and belong to the groups brands want to survey, since $5 is enough to cash out. If you want more for your time, join the waitlist at Prolific, where researchers must pay study participants at least $8 an hour.
