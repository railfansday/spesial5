Hollyland Named Official Sponsor of APEC 2026 Shenzhen Meetings

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting will take place in Shenzhen on November 18 and 19, following the APEC CEO Summit and concluding a series of APEC ministerial and senior officials' meetings. The meetings will mark China's third time hosting APEC and Shenzhen's debut as an APEC host city.

Hollyland, a Shenzhen-founded provider of wireless audio and video transmission solutions, has been named an official sponsor of APEC 2026. The company will support media operations throughout the meetings with professional audio, intercom, and video transmission technologies, helping facilitate communication and coverage at one of the region's most important international gatherings.

"Shenzhen's manufacturing depth and engineering talent have played an important role in shaping Hollyland," said Lima Wen, CEO of Hollyland. "We are honored to support APEC Shenzhen 2026. As Shenzhen is the city where Hollyland was founded, witnessing it host the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting for the first time makes this occasion especially meaningful."

Hollyland will provide LARK M2S wireless microphones at a designated service desk in the APEC Media Center, where participating media can access the equipment and technical support for their reporting needs. The ultra-mini system is designed to stay unobtrusive on camera while capturing clear speech in crowded, fast-moving reporting conditions, from corridor scrums to stand-ups on the exhibition floor.

Hollyland will also equip the APEC Media Center's on-site studio with its Solidcom series wireless intercom systems, enabling seamless communication among production personnel throughout live operations. Complementing this setup, the company's Pyro series solutions will power wireless camera signal transmission, reception and real-time monitoring, delivering stable video feeds to support the production team's workflow and on-site decision-making.

The deployment builds on Hollyland's extensive experience supporting high-pressure production environments. Its solutions are widely used across film and television productions, major sporting events and live stage productions, where seamless coordination between camera, audio, lighting and directing teams relies on stable and reliable wireless communication.

Hollyland is a leading provider of wireless products, specializing in wireless intercom systems, video transmission systems, monitors, wireless microphones, and live streaming cameras. Since 2013, Hollyland has been serving millions of users around the world in various sectors, including filmmaking, telecasting, video production, live events, exhibitions, theaters, houses of worship, and individual content creators. It has built a sales network covering approximately 160 countries and regions, supported by regional teams and offices worldwide.
