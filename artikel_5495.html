Candy Crush Saga Kicks Off Game On Season, Bringing XBOX's 25th Anniversary to Millions of Candy Crush Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Candy Crush gives iconic XBOX game worlds a playful new twist in Game On Season

The four-week celebration marks 25 years of XBOX with collectible pins and six familiar game themes re-recorded with an orchestra, choir and accordion.

NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Candy Crush SagaÂ® is marking 25 years of XBOXÂ® by giving eight iconic game worlds a playful Candy twist. Over four weeks, millions of mobile players can collect pins inspired by those worlds, earn exclusive rewards to celebrate XBOX's 25th celebration and compete in an arcade-themed Leagues event. Together, the experiences give players a playful new way to reconnect with some of gaming's most recognizable characters and worlds.

At the heart of Game On Season is an XBOX 25 pin collection featuring characters and moments from all eight franchises like the Master Chief's helmet, Cacodemon, Dr. Neo Cortex and more. Players earn pins as they crush through levels and build their collection across the season.

The collection doesn't stay on screen. We've turned some of the virtual pins into physical pins and VIP players in the US can enter a contest at candycrush.com for a chance to win one of 1,000 limited-edition pin packs.

On the audio front, once again Candy Crush Saga takes players on an audio journey. Six franchises from across the XBOX family of studios from Halo, Fallout, Sea of Thieves, Forza, DOOM and Banjo-Kazooie have been "Candyfied" and woven into Candy Crush Saga gameplay. Players will revel as they recognize familiar themes getting delicious audio twists while they match their way through the Game On Season. What's even more fun is if you miss a track or want to hear it again, they are also available now across streaming platforms and participating studio channels including Spotify.

There is a long and proud history of making the audio from Candy Crush Saga feel bigger than the device it is played on and this behind-the-scenes video shows how the music came together with Dominique Devoucoux, Head of Audio at King, and Sebastian Aav, Audio Lead at King, sharing their vision for the project. Also in the video, composer Federico Truzzi walks through how themes were "Candyfied" and recorded by the German Film Orchestra Babelsberg, the Bulgarische Orthodoxe Chor Berlin e.V. (BoChorBerlin) choir and, of course, an accordion player.

"Over 25 years XBOX has given players some of the most iconic franchises in entertainment," said Paula Ingvar, General Manager of Candy Crush. "Game On Season lets players celebrate those memories inside the game they love playing every day. Hearing the Halo theme played by an orchestra, a choir and an accordion in Candy Crush makes our team smile."
