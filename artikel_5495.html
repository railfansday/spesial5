TEN Holdings Announces Closing of $2.0 Million Registered Direct Offering

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

NEW YORK, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- TEN Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: XHLD) (the "Company" or "TEN Holdings"), the technology company behind TEN, a Digital Experience Platform that powers the next generation of enterprise digital engagement, today announced the closing of its registered direct offering of an aggregate of 3,389,831 shares of the Company's common stock at a purchase price of $0.59 per share.

The gross proceeds from the offering were approximately $2.0 million, before deducting offering expenses payable by the Company.

The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the offering for working capital and general corporate purposes, supporting its ongoing operations and strategic initiatives as it continues its transition toward a technology-driven, recurring-revenue business model.

"This financing represents another important step in strengthening our financial position as we continue executing our long-term growth strategy," said Virgilio Torres, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of TEN Holdings. "Over the past several months, we have made meaningful progress in reducing our operating cost structure, expanding our technology capabilities, and transitioning toward a more scalable SaaS business model. This additional capital will provide us with greater financial flexibility to support our operations and continue building on that momentum."

Mr. Torres added, "With our new TEN Digital Experience Platform launched and our focus increasingly shifting toward recurring software revenue, we believe we are entering an important phase in the Company's development. We remain committed to disciplined capital allocation, improving operational efficiency, and positioning TEN Holdings for sustainable long-term growth and shareholder value creation."

The shares of common stock were offered pursuant to an effective shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-299485), which was declared effective by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") on October 26, 2026. The offering was made only by means of a prospectus supplement and accompanying base prospectus. A prospectus supplement relating to the offering has been filed with the SEC and is available on the SEC's website at www.sec.gov.

TEN Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: XHLD) is a New York-based technology company focused on developing enterprise software solutions that enable organizations to create, manage, deliver, and measure digital experiences. Through its flagship Digital Experience Platform, TEN, the Company provides technology solutions for enterprise communications, virtual events, webinars, and continuing education, supporting digital experiences with up to 50,000 concurrent attendees.

Building on more than a decade of experience serving Fortune 500 companies, TEN Holdings is evolving its platform into a broader, AI-enabled enterprise technology ecosystem. Its product roadmap includes marketing automation, CRM integrations, advanced engagement analytics, AI-powered capabilities, and content management solutions designed to help organizations streamline workflows, strengthen customer engagement, and generate actionable business insights.

The Company's strategy is centered on expanding its SaaS offerings, increasing recurring revenue opportunities, and delivering scalable technology solutions to enterprise customers. Planned capabilities remain subject to ongoing development, and there can be no assurance regarding their timing or availability.
