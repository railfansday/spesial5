Comcast is cutting more than 1,000 jobs in its cable and broadband divisions

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Comcast has filed layoff notices in four states and expects a charge of more than $1 billion tied to the overhaul

Comcast $CMCSA is preparing to eliminate over 1,000 positions in its cable and broadband units as part of a restructuring ahead of its NBCUniversal spinoff, according to Bloomberg.

Notices filed by Comcast list 634 job cuts in Pennsylvania, 119 in New Jersey, 159 in Washington state and 82 in California, according to Bloomberg.

A Comcast spokesperson told Bloomberg that some further layoffs fell below the threshold for filing notices, meaning the true scale of the cuts exceeds what the company has disclosed. Comcast is withholding an overall tally of the cuts.

Comcast told the mayor of Philadelphia in a letter that it intends to post over 1,100 new roles across the company, with more than 500 of them at its headquarters campus in Philadelphia, according to Bloomberg.

Comcast Co-CEO Mike Cavanagh said on Oct. 22 that the company has begun "the most significant operating and cost transformation in our company's history." Chief Financial Officer Jason Armstrong said the overhaul would force Comcast to record a charge above $1 billion during the fourth quarter.

Comcast's connectivity business has shrunk. In the third quarter, the company lost 144,000 domestic broadband residential customers and 274,000 domestic video customers. Connectivity & Platforms revenue fell 2.7% to $19.7 billion. Content & Experiences revenue rose 6.5% to $10.5 billion.

Comcast announced in June that it would spin off NBCUniversal and Sky into a separate publicly traded company. Cavanagh and Co-CEO Brian Roberts said the separation "remains on track." The company also suspended its share repurchase program beginning in the third quarter and expects the pause to last through the proposed separation.

Cable channels such as CNBC and MS Now left Comcast in January through a spinoff that created Versant Media Group, according to Bloomberg.
