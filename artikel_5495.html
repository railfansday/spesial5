Forget Nvidia: Stocks are set for their best week since early August

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Nasdaq has gained 3.2% so far this week, with the S&P 500 on course to finish up 2.9%. Nvidia stock was recovering

The Dow Jones Industrial Average, the S&P 500, and the Nasdaq $NDAQ are all tracking toward their strongest weekly showings since the start of August, even after all three fell Thursday when Nvidia $NVDA founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO.

The Nasdaq has gained 3.2% so far this week, with the S&P 500 on course to finish up 2.9%. The Dow is on track for a gain of nearly 2%. Heading into Friday's session, Dow futures were up 78 points, a 0.2% gain, while S&P 500 contracts were 0.2% higher and Nasdaq-100 contracts edged up 0.1%.

Nvidia stock fell as much as 14% on Thursday before closing down 4.3% after Huang stepped down amid revelations of multiple extramarital affairs, including with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. Nvidia stock was up about 2% in premarket trading Friday.

Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will lead the company as interim CEO. The board has opened a search for a permanent successor that will weigh candidates from within Nvidia as well as outsiders, according to CNBC.

Nvidia is slated to release its fiscal third-quarter earnings on Tuesday. Kress is expected to face questions then about financing deals Huang struck. Anthropic shares are expected to begin trading on Thursday.

Oil prices jumped Thursday following a Truth Social post in which President Donald Trump wrote, "We don't need a deal with Iran." The comment stoked fears of a prolonged U.S.-Iran war. Oil prices barely moved Friday, as West Texas Intermediate crude futures held above $88 a barrel and Brent crude futures stayed above $96.

The October producer price index, which gauges inflation at the wholesale level, is due Friday. Economists forecast that the headline figure will increase 0.4% month over month and 6.6% year over year, according to CNBC.

Microsoft $MSFT stock was down in premarket trading. The company is trying to fix a broad outage that has disrupted both Teams and Outlook.
