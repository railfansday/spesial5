PEGATRON Advances Agentic AI and HPC Infrastructure with High-Density Computing Solutions at SC26

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CHICAGO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PEGATRON, a globally recognized provider of comprehensive AI infrastructure and server solutions, will showcase its latest AI and high-performance computing (HPC) platforms at SC26, demonstrating how high-density CPU infrastructure, GPU-accelerated systems, and high-performance storage can address the evolving demands of agentic AI and HPC. Its portfolio spans CPU-intensive execution and data processing to accelerated computing, providing flexible and scalable infrastructure to support increasingly complex AI workloads.

As AI moves beyond training and inference toward autonomous agents that execute complex tasks, infrastructure requirements are evolving. Agentic AI relies on CPUs to manage concurrent agent environments, tool executions, sandboxed code, reinforcement learning (RL) evaluations, and data processing. Meeting these demands requires high-density CPU infrastructure that operates alongside GPU-accelerated systems at AI factory scale.

NVIDIA Vera CPU Rack: Scaling Agentic AI Infrastructure

Based on the NVIDIA MGX modular reference architecture, the NVIDIA Vera CPU Rack integrates 256 liquid-cooled NVIDIA Vera CPUs, delivering more than 22,500 cores, more than 45,000 threads, and up to 300 TB/s of aggregate LPDDR5X memory bandwidth. Supporting 22,500 sandboxes and delivering 80% faster agentic CPU performance, it helps AI factories scale agent execution, reinforcement learning (RL) evaluations, and data-processing pipelines. NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet and NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand networking enable AI factories to scale CPU-intensive workloads alongside accelerated compute.

NVIDIA Vera spans rack-scale CPU infrastructure, accelerated systems, and standalone servers, and Pegatron's portfolio reflects that range. The RA4803-72N3 is a fully liquid-cooled NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 rack integrating 72 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and 36 NVIDIA Vera CPUs for frontier-scale AI training, RL, and large-scale agentic inference.
