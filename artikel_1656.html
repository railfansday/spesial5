Opinion: Julian Ward finds perfect solution to major Liverpool problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Andoni Iraola has a lot of fires that he still needs to put out at Liverpool.

The Spaniard took over from Arne Slot and, even though he inherited an incredible squad, they needed training so that they could carry out the tasks that he needed them to.

One of the main things about Iraola's teams is that they never stop running.

Whether they're on the attack or defending, constant pressing is a key part of Iraola's tactics.

This means that he needs players with a lot of stamina and ones who can make a difference whenever they're used.

So, the fact that a fair few members of their squad are underperforming is a major concern which is why Julian Ward finding a solution to one of their problems is hugely helpful.

Liverpool are eyeing a move for Josko Gvardiol

Liverpool's defence may have been upgraded in the summer but, it still isn't strong enough.

Right now, three of their back four have managed to cement themselves in their positions.

Ronald Araujo, Jeremy Jacquet and Virgil van Dijk have all been in fine form this term with only a couple of mistakes emerging from the players.

However, left-back has been a completely different story.

Kostas Tsimikas and Milos Kerkez have been poor, there's no two ways about it.

The duo have been interchanged all season and when one plays, fans always end up wanting the other to play instead.

Neither of them have met expectations which has caused several links with left-backs to emerge.

And now, thanks to an update from El Nacional CAT, the perfection transfer for Liverpool to explore has reared its head.

"Barcelona are calmly considering the possibility of trying to sign Gvardiol, although this would involve a significant investment," they wrote.

"The Sky Blues won't let go of a player for whom they paid over €90 million, making him one of the most expensive defenders of all time. Nor will they allow him to leave mid-season, so they would have to wait until the summer transfer window.

"Furthermore, there is another obstacle that makes it extremely difficult to see the former Dinamo Zagreb and RB Leipzig player at the Spotify Camp Nou, and for him to be available to Hans-Dieter Flick.

"There will be a lot of competition for Gvardiol The situation surrounding Gvardiol and City has attracted the attention of many clubs , who are willing to try their luck and fight for his signing.

"For example, he has been linked with Paris Saint-Germain, Liverpool, Bayern Munich, Chelsea, and Al-Nassr. In other words, Barça will face several rivals in the fight to secure his services."

Right now, it's unclear exactly what Liverpool are going to do in the upcoming transfer windows.

They have been linked with many different players and, since they need to bolster several positions, they're going to have to choose their transfers wisely.

If they don't do this and go out and spend another £100 million + on someone, they'll be digging themselves into an even deeper hole than they're already in.

Gvardiol is a perfect transfer for Liverpool as, not only does he already have plenty of Premier League experience under his belt but, he can also cover two positions with one of them being left-back.

And, since the futures of a lot of Iraola's centre-backs are unclear, he could help them cover that area of the pitch too.

Obviously, nothing is guaranteed yet but, of all the players who have been linked with the Reds recently, Gvardiol feels like one of the most exciting names to have been mentioned.
