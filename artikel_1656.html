MN: The new Beckham – Adidas want McTominay at Milan and could aid the move

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AC Milan have reportedly become a player in the race to sign Scott McTominay and there are more reasons than his quality alone.

Signing someone like McTominay would be a statement from Milan. They’ve already returned to signing quality, rather than complete gambles, and the potential arrival of the Scot(t) would only further that idea.

Renewal talks with Napoli have stalled and Gerry Cardinale is prepared to offer him a way to stay in Italy, reports have suggested today.

According to MilanNews, the reason behind the new interest in McTominay could be commercial. Of course, he’s an excellent player, but with Adidas’ return to the Rossoneri shirt, they would like to partner it with a ‘high-profile spokesperson’.

There are already several representatives of the German brand at Milanello – like Mike Maignan, Diego Moreira and Francesco Camarda –  and they are hoping someone like McTominay could be one of the faces of a new era.

With this, there would be the possibility that Adidas ‘contributed’ to the deal. Through commerical agreements, image rights or even initiatives for the player, the Diavolo would not be alone if they attempted to bring him to San Siro.

Adidas were involved in David Beckham’s move to Real Madrid and with McTominay, it could be repeated.
