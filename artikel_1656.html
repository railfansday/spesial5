Chances to put things right in Europe: The five key battles in Olympiacos vs. Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan will return to a city where they have a glorious history, but this time in dire need of a win to get their European campaign going.

Tonight’s Europa League game against Olympiacos feels a long way away from those Champions League final triumphs in Athens against Barcelona (1994) and Liverpool (2007). However, Ruben Amorim’s side still face a must-win test.

Having lost three of four in the UEL thus far, Milan are currently outside even the top 24 places, so they must secure the majority of the remaining 12 points on offer to climb. Their hosts tonight have started much better and are inside the top eight, with 10 points on the board.

Amorim will go with a strong side, or the best possible at least with the injury situation. Pietro Terracciano, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Luka Modric, Samuel Chukwueze, Strahinja Pavlovic, Davide Bartesaghi, Matteo Gabbia, Alexis Saelemaekers and Francesco Camarda are all out.

We start with a duel on Milan’s right flank between Diego Moreira and Bruno Onyemaechi. For the Nigerian defender, it will be just his seventh start of the season (out of 15 games played) and this might be arguably his toughest test yet.

Moreira meanwhile has been outstanding in Serie A, with four goals and four assists to his name already. Yet, he hasn’t really got going in Europe yet just like the whole team, given he doesn’t have a goal contribution outside of domestic action. Tonight is a chance to get the ball rolling.

The main threat for Olympiacos is without doubt the attacking midfielder Jota Silva. The former Besiktas man is already on six goals from his 14 appearances (plus one assist) and has really made the No.10 role his. That said, he too is without a goal or assist in Europe so far.

Meanwhile, this is a huge opportunity for Ardon Jashari, as he admitted in his pre-match press conference. The Swiss did very well off the bench against Frosinone and Modric’s injury has opened up a spot for him to take the reins this evening, though he has not always delivered so far when given that task.

Moving back to Milan’s attack, a big night awaits for Goncalo Ramos. He scored in the 4-0 rout of RB Salzburg but is dry in Europe aside from that. The Portuguese ended a one-month goal drought on Sunday by scoring against Frosinone in a move he started and finished, which offers encouragement after a difficult start in red and black.

His opponent will be someone who knows Milan very well, but from the other side of the city. Lorenzo Pirola spent several years in Inter’s academy and has found himself in Athens as one of the leaders of the Olympiacos defence. Tonight will carry a bit of extra meaning, especially amid talk of a potential call-up from Roberto Mancini.

Armando Gonzalez is the one who will lead the line for the home side at the Karaiskakis Stadium. The 23-year-old Mexican has scored once so far in the Europa League, but he has three goals overall in four starts and is vying for that starting No.9 position.

Koni De Winter was solid in Sunday’s win meanwhile, and yet he is in need of a performance having really struggled in his battle with Lenny Joseph in the defeat to Ferencvaros last month. The Haitian international – not even their normal starting striker – constantly got the better of the Belgian, who has been up and down in recent weeks.

Finally, we end with the contest between two midfield veterans: Remo Freuler and Adrien Rabiot. The latter has become the anchor of the Erythrolefkoi midfield, and he knows the Rossoneri very well having played for many years in Serie A. The 34-year-old Swiss international also has four assists from a deep-lying playmaker role.

Meanwhile, it has been a tale of two competitions for Rabiot, just like Moreira. The Frenchman has tallied 5 goals and 6 assists in Serie A, but only one assist in European play, and he actually drew criticism after his performances against Bournemouth and Ferencvaros. It is time to bring that league form onto the continent.
