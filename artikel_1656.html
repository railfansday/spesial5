Opinion: Liverpool wonderkid is set to be promoted, he's blown everyone away

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

An exciting wonderkid is set to be promoted, and he's blown everyone away.

During his roller-coaster of a spell at the club as emotions ebbed and flowed Arne Slot made a plethora of mistakes. However, among his gravest errors was his inability to fathom the importance of player development at Anfield.

Liverpool operate with an emphasis on bringing through young talent and integrating them into the first-team.

It's a policy that has not only created a lot of important senior players, and has helped to bulk out the squad but it has also allowed the club to operate successfully when it comes to fiscal matters.

Bringing players through and developing them from the academy has enabled Liverpool to make pure profit and increase their transfer budget pretty much every summer.

Not wanting to adhere to that policy is what ultimately was Slot's biggest mistake.

How Iraola is doing things differently at LiverpoolThe change has been apparent from the moment Iraola took over. He immediately joined the club earlier and started working with the Reds' U21 team before the first-team had even arrived. He took a 15-year-old in Joshua Abe, who then turned 16-years-old, on the pre-season tour to the United States. And now he's integrated Lewis Koumas into the team as a back-up attacker giving him tons of confidence and showing how he can make an impact. Trey Nyoni has been sensational under Iraola as well and continues to get better and better and more tailor-made for the double pivot role. Iraola has a genuine interest in the club's young players. He interacts with youth coaches and keeps a close tab on the emerging youngsters from the academy.
