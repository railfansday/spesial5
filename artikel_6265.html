How To Combine World Of Hyatt Points Between Accounts For Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The major hotel loyalty programs all have different policies when it comes to combining points between accounts belonging to different members. I’ve already covered the options and policies for Hilton Honors and Marriott Bonvoy, and in this post, I’d like to take a look at the policy of World of Hyatt.

The good news is that World of Hyatt recently introduced a new way to transfer points to others for free, and I’d argue the program now has among the most generous policies in the industry. Long story short, you can transfer or receive up to 525,000 points per year, and this can all be done online, and at no cost. In this post, I’d like to look more closely at the mechanics of Hyatt points transfers.

World of Hyatt offers a generous feature to allow members to transfer points to others at no cost. Let’s first cover the basic rules for Hyatt points transfers:

It’s worth noting that this system is much better than it used to be. Back in the day combining Hyatt points required completing a form and took days, and you could only combine points in order to have sufficient points for a specific redemption. That policy no longer applies.

The process of transferring World of Hyatt points between members online is straightforward. To start, you can either follow this link, or go to your account overview page, and go to the section that reads “Gift your points more seamlessly.”

On the next page, you’ll want to go to the “Gift points or eligible awards” section, and then click “Gift your points.”

There you’ll first want to select how many points you want to transfer, using the slider.

Then on the next page, you’ll need to enter the account information for the account you want to move points to.

Then you’ll just need to confirm the details, and that’s it! Regarding transfer times, reports suggest transfers take anywhere from a few minutes to several days. It does seem like the more points you transfer, the more likely that it takes days for the transfer to go through. So if you are moving a large chunk of points, don’t be surprised if it takes some time.

What are the benefits to being able to combine points between World of Hyatt accounts? On the most basic level, it allows you to combine points so you have enough for an award stay. In other words, if you have two accounts with 15,000 points each, you can combine the points so you have enough for a one night stay at a hotel costing 30,000 points.

Furthermore, if you’re traveling as multiple people and one person has elite status, there’s value to booking the stay out of their account, so that you could take advantage of World of Hyatt Globalist or World of Hyatt Explorist benefits, for example.

However, thanks to the Guest of Honor program, it’s actually pretty easy to give elite perks to friends and family nowadays. For that matter, even Suite Upgrade Awards and Club Access Awards are transferable nowadays.

Lastly, combining points is also considered “activity” for the purposes of keeping your account alive, so it would reset the expiration of points.

World of Hyatt has a great system for letting members combine and share points. World of Hyatt members can transfer up to 525,000 points per year to other members, with limited restrictions. This is possible online, and typically points transfers happen pretty quickly.

There’s lots of value to this, and I’d say Hyatt’s policy is comparable to Hilton Honors for being the best in the industry.
