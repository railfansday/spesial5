Trying to Build Muscle and Lose Fat? Don’t Overlook This Part of Body Recomp

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Crushing your workouts is only half the equation.

When it comes to body recomposition, showing up and crushing your workouts is only half the equation. You also need to pay attention to your nutrition to build muscle and lose fat.

And that part of the job isn’t always as straightforward as exercise. In the gym, you know you need to regularly lift enough to challenge your muscles and get your cardio in. In the kitchen, body recomposition typically involves two key things: getting enough protein and managing your calorie intake. As simple as that sounds, it isn’t always easy.

There are a few ways to approach these goals, but one key to making them more sustainable and manageable (and therefore easier to stick to!) is to track your macronutrients, or macros. The three macronutrients are carbohydrates, fats, and protein, and each plays a unique role in your body recomposition journey.

Carbs get a lot of unnecessary hate, but they’re your body’s primary energy source, says Dana White, RD. Your body relies on glucose, a sugar that is broken down from carbs. Without enough of it, your energy, concentration, and mood can all be affected.

Fats are vital to your body’s overall functioning. They support the health of your cells, help your body absorb vitamins like A, D, E, and K, and play a big role in satiety, White says. Protein, perhaps the most obvious of the three, plays a major role in muscle repair and development—important at any time, but especially on a body recomp journey.

Counting your macros can help you stay on track with your body recomposition journey. To find out how much of each macro you need and how to fill your plate, try our Eating for Body Recomp Guide.
