Paid Ads Academy: Paid Ads Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

At Paid Ads Academy, you'll join one of the fastest-growing woman-owned startups in advertising education, where alignment with our vision, mission, and values is non-negotiable.

Vision: To be the go-to advertising training partner for entrepreneurs and their teams.

Mission: Empower entrepreneurs and their teams to become findable® for what they do by mastering paid ads in-house.

Paid Ads Academy is a fast-growing, woman-owned company and equal opportunity employer. We hire for excellence, character, and drive.

The Paid Ads Specialist owns the day-to-day execution of Paid Ads Academy's own paid media, the campaigns that drive traffic and leads into our challenges, masterclasses, and evergreen funnels. You take the targeting, budget, and testing plan the Senior Paid Ads Coach sets and run it: building campaigns, watching spend daily, and reporting on what is working across multiple platforms: Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Tiktok, etc.

The Senior Paid Ads Coach owns strategy, budget authority, and the performance thresholds. You own execution: the builds, the daily optimizations inside those thresholds, the tracking hygiene, and the reporting that tells everyone the truth about what happened. A strategy is only as good as the account it runs in.

What makes this different from an agency seat is whose money it is. This is our budget, driving leads into the programs that fund the company. When cost per lead drifts, it is not a client conversation, it is our margin. We also teach paid ads for a living, which means our own accounts should be the strongest example of the work we put in front of students. You will be one of the people who makes that true.

This is a full-time role, and we're looking for people who are all in. That means this is the work, not one of a few things you're juggling.

To protect focus and prevent burnout, we don't permit outside work or side gigs for full-time team members. If you currently run a business or have a commitment you're not looking to step away from, this likely isn't the right fit, and that's okay. We'd rather be upfront so we're aligned from the start.

We've found the people who thrive here are the ones whose energy and vision are fully on this business. That focus is what helps the whole team win.

Please note: applicants must reside within the US to be considered.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
