A Fixer-Upper Could Be Your Path To Homeownership

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

When the homes you love keep landing outside your budget, it may be time to look at the ones other buyers skip.

A fixer-upper won’t be the right call for everyone. Plenty of buyers want a home that’s ready on move-in day. But homes that need some updating could get you in the door for a lot less.

More buyers are starting to realize it too. And Danielle Hale, Chief Economist at Realtor.com, explains why:

“Fixer-uppers give buyers a way to break into the housing market at a time when affordability is still stretched thin. For those with the vision. . . fixer-uppers provide both a starting point in the market and the chance to create a home that’s truly their own.”

If that sounds like you, here’s why a fixer-upper could be worth a closer look.

Because these homes need work, they tend to have a lower asking price up front. According to AmeriSave, fixer-uppers usually sell for 20% to 30% less than what they’re worth on the market.

That’s a big difference up front and it could be what gets you into a home today. And odds are you don’t have to tackle all the repairs at once – you can space them out over time as it works for you.

Just remember repair costs can add up quickly and eat into those savings if you don’t plan ahead. So, before you make an offer, get estimates from a contractor and set a repair budget you’re comfortable with. There are even some loan types designed to help pay for the work.

With a renovation loan, you can roll the cost of the work into your mortgage, so you don’t have to cover it all out of pocket once you move in. Here are a few options from FHA, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, and the VA (see visual below):

Each program has its own rules, and not every lender offers all of them. So, talk to a trusted lender to see which options are available to you and which make sense for how you plan to tackle updates and repairs over time.

Some cities even offer tax breaks to buyers who renovate a fixer-upper and raise its value. These programs vary by location, so check with your city or a tax professional to see what’s available where you want to live.

If you want to check for more information yourself, go to your local county or city official dot-gov website. Then, use the search bar and look for: [City or County Name] AND “home rehabilitation” OR “property tax incentive” OR “housing repair”

A local agent can also help you find fixer-uppers in the neighborhoods you have your heart set on.
