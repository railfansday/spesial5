Joint Letter on Low-Carbon Steel Credits in the Review of EU CO₂ Emission Standards for Cars

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Creating predictable demand for genuinely low-carbon steel is urgent. If properly designed, low-carbon steel credits can create a lead market for green steel and secure a future for a green steel industry in Europe.

Many green steel projects have been announced across Europe in recent years. However, some are facing setbacks due to a lack of offtake for low-carbon steel products. Without strong and timely lead market policies, Europe risks losing industrial capacity, jobs and technological leadership.

Creating predictable demand for genuinely low-carbon steel is therefore urgent. If properly designed and unencumbered by additional flexibility or extensions of scope of the car CO₂ emission standards Regulation, low-carbon steel credits can create a lead market for green steel and secure a future for a green steel industry in Europe.

Transport & Environment’s (T&E) vision is a zero-emission mobility system that is affordable and has minimal impacts on our health, climate and environment. Created over 30 years ago, we have shaped some of Europe’s most important environmental laws.
