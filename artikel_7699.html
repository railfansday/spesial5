Executives of Healthcare Provider Charged by Indictment in Fraud that Caused Millions in Losses to D.C. Medicaid

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A 10-count Indictment, unsealed today in U.S. District Court, charges two executives of a District-based healthcare provider and one of their Community Support Workers in connection with a scheme to bill D.C. Medicaid for mental health services for juveniles that were never provided or were invoiced at grossly inflated rates, causing a loss to the District of Columbia’s Medicaid program that exceeded millions of dollars, announced U.S. Attorney Jeanine Ferris Pirro.
