The nominees for this year’s Game Awards have been announced. GTA VI has (rightfully) racked up a bunch of noms, as have Control Resonant and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. But there’s a lot of head-scratching going on at the games nominated for Best Fighting Game, shining an uncomfortable light on how anemic the genre has gotten outside of the big twoâTekken and Street Fighter.

Woof. Let’s break down these nominees. 2XKO is dead, with active development ending on the game at the end of December, a few short weeks after the Game Awards take place. Invincible Vs has had its development team busted down to a “skeleton crew.” The Avatar Legends team features a developer accused of sexual harassment and is a part of the currently shrinking Paramount Games thanks to the Skydance merger. I’m not sure that many people even knew about All’s Justice, which feels like it was thrown in there to pad out the list.

It’s hard to make a fighting game viable. NetherRealm’s Mortal Kombat 1 earned favorable player sentiment when it was released in 2023, but the game fell off so hard in the years since that EVO 2026 marked the first time a NetherRealm game hadn’t been featured in the main event lineup since 2011. It seems like nearly every new fighting game fails either before or right after it launches. (Paging Multiversus.)
