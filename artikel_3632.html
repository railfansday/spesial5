WoW: Forever Wants To Get Rid Of The Idea Of “BiS” Gear

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

World of Warcraft: Forever is approaching itemization and "best-in-slot" (BiS) gear differently, with developer Blizzard instead wanting players to have more boss- or biome-specific choices.

In the latest episode of the WoW: Forever Podcast, Blizzard dove deep on the MMORPG's itemization changes, how it approached designing Forever's new items, class changes, and more.

On the subject of raid gear and chase items, Forever senior game designer Aidan Moon said something that could be seen as controversial in certain circles of the game's community.

"I essentially want to get rid of BiS," Moon said. "I think that BiS is not a good thing for a video game that's supposed to be about [the] role-playing aspects of it."

BiS is WoW shorthand for essentially the best item that could possibly be in a given gear slot for a specific class. Look up any WoW guide for WoW Classic or modern WoW, and you'll find class BiS gear lists telling players exactly what they should be wearing in order for their character to be as powerful as possible. Endgame in many ways revolves around acquiring your BiS gear, first your "pre-raid" BiS from gear available in max-level dungeons, and then BiS gear, like tier sets, from raids.

It's for that exact reason Forever introduced new items that grant additional stats or effects against certain enemy types or even in certain locations. Moon gave an example of specific items that deal more damage against Murloc enemies, and that players might want to have more gear tailored to fighting against a Murloc boss in a raid encounter. 

Forever's raids will feature multiple distinct biomes within them, Moon revealed, giving players more factors to consider when choosing what gear to use for specific parts of each raid. One wing of a raid may be snowy, while another may be cavernous, changing what gear could be more optimal for those encounters.

"I think your BiS should be different [for] every single boss if you're actually making a theoretical BiS list," Moon said. "It should be based on the creature and the biome and a mixture of those two. You might have an item that's really good against snowy, and then another item in that same slot that's really good against Murlocs. Then there's a Murloc boss in a snowy area and you're just like 'Which one do I wear?' And then eventually you get to a point where you're swapping those things out or it's too much cognitive load and you don't want to do that. It's for those players that really want to optimize." 

Trying to get rid of BiS lists is a big change for WoW, which has revolved around the idea for over two decades. Players can already encounter some of the itemization ideas Moon discussed while leveling to 60, with low-level gear from dungeons and quests dealing more damage against specified enemy types or even granting additional movement speed in specific zones.

"I don't want you to have to ever think about BiS, I just want you to think about the fight right in front of you and how you're going to fight it," Moon said.

Of course, having more biome- or enemy-type-specific items means hoarding more pieces of gear as opposed to selling old ones when an upgrade comes around. That will no doubt quickly fill up the inventories and banks of players looking to min-max for every possible scenario. Classic WoW is already notorious for having limited bag space, and players running around with multiple sets of hyper-specific gear will only make that pain point worse. 

Blizzard sounds like it's aware of that problem. Principal game designer Kris Zierhut said bigger bags are on that way, but that it's not needed right now immediately post-launch. However, Blizzard knows players will eventually need more bag space as well as better ways to organize and search for owned items to make putting together encounter-specific sets of gear easier.

It's a massive change, but the strategy Blizzard is employing seems to be working. Forever's launch broke WoW concurrent and weekly player records, and more than 15 million characters have been created in the MMO so far. Blizzard recently lifted the veil on how Forever's new PvP system will work, revealing how classic WoW's infamous rank grind will now be a thing of the past.
