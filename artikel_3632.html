Rockstar Founder Issues Rare Statement As GTA 6 Launches To Superb Reviews And Huge Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We might not hear from him much, but Rockstar co-founder Sam Houser remains highly involved with the studio and its games, and that includes GTA 6, which is available now in New Zealand and everywhere else on November 19.

To mark the game's launch, Houser released a statement in which he said Rockstar has once again tried to create a game in GTA 6 that "exceeds your expectations" and shows "what this medium is capable of, and why we believe games are so special."

He told GamesRadar: "Rockstar Games was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling, become essential to modern culture, and to be thought of as stories and worlds that live on with us."

The executive went on to say that he hopes GTA 6 feels "both familiar and entirely new," and above all else, something that people find to be fun.

"This is a narrative set in a world of possibilities--and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels, and concludes," he added.

Finally, Houser reminded people that GTA 6 might be the pinnacle of the franchise so far, but Rockstar isn't closing up shop anytime soon.

"We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you," he said.

It's possible it won't be another 13 years for the next GTA game, as Rockstar's owner, Take-Two, has said the "nature and velocity" of GTA releases will change.

Sam Houser rarely releases statements, gives interviews, or appears in Rockstar's promotional efforts, so even this celebratory statement is notable. GQ writer Sam White recently posted a series of tweets (and then deleted them) saying an interview he had planned with Sam Houser fell apart at the last minute.

On GTA 6, Sam Houser was involved in every aspect of the game's creative process.

Sam Houser's brother, Dan Houser, left Rockstar in 2020 after the successful launch of Red Dead Redemption II. He started a new company, Absurd Ventures, which is working on an open-world comedy game that will be revealed soon.

GTA 6 arrives for everyone on November 19, and it's expected to be a massive success, the likes of which the industry hasn't seen since ... 2013's GTA 5. In GameSpot's GTA 6 review, Tamoor Hussain said there might not be another game like it until the inevitable GTA 7.
