You Can Buy a Fujifilm GFX Eterna 55 for $6,500 Less Than Before

Filmmakers will soon be able to get their hands on the large-format Fujifilm GFX Eterna 55 cinema camera for considerably less money than before.

As NewsShooter reports, a “body-only” version of the GFX Eterna 55 has appeared for preorder at some retailers, including B&H. It costs $9,999, compared to $16,499 for the standard GFX Eterna 55 package.

Saving $6,500 is significant, so what does that extra money actually get video creators?

The standard GFX Eterna 55 includes a custom 5-inch external touchscreen monitor with a mount and hood, a dedicated top handle with customizable controls, and AC power cables. Whether that bundle is worth $6,500 will depend on the individual user, of course.

PetaPixel YouTube host Dmitry Koshutin has used the GFX Eterna 55 extensively and believes the top handle is arguably the camera’s best feature.

“The top handle is probably the best feature of the Eterna,” Koshutin says. “It’s very sturdy, and the controls are very handy. I would still want that, knowing how well it’s built.”

As of yet, the GFX Eterna 55’s dedicated top handle is not listed as an optional accessory or available for separate purchase. At least for now, the only way for filmmakers to get it is by buying the standard GFX Eterna 55 kit for $16,499.

Koshutin is also a fan of the Eterna’s included external monitor, especially for filming fast-paced action, noting that its proprietary connection works very well. However, he adds that third-party monitors using SDI or HDMI can also be strong alternatives, offering larger or brighter displays.

“Having said that, $10,000 is quite a deal,” Koshutin concludes. “For filmmakers who already own top handle accessories and a monitor, shaving that extra $6,500 off makes a lot of sense without losing much functionality.”

The Fujifilm GFX Eterna 55 (Body Only) is available for preorder now at $9,999. It is also listed on Fujifilm’s own store, though it is marked “out of stock” and not yet available for preorder there. B&H currently lists the body-only GFX Eterna 55 as “coming soon.”

The standard GFX Eterna 55 kit with all the accessories and add-ons remains available for $16,499 and is currently in stock.

Additionally, the dedicated external electronic viewfinder for the GFX Eterna 55 that Fujifilm unveiled in September is expected to ship by the end of December, per B&H. Priced at $1,649.95, it offers GFX Eterna 55 users an alternative way to frame their shots without an external monitor.
