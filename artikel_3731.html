Surviving Mars: Relaunched DLC ‘Machine Utopia’ announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Publisher Paradox Interactive and developer Haemimont Games have announced colony management game Surviving Mars: Relaunched downloadable content “Machine Utopia.” It will launch on December 9.

Here is an overview of the downloadable content, via Paradox Interactive:

“Machine Utopia” is an industry and automation-focused expansion that includes new buildings, new game features, and new leaders, themed around letting players add robot workers, heavy mining operations, and building better trains for their colonies, alongside other new content. “Machine Utopia” will be available either individually or as part of the Surviving Mars: Relaunched Ultimate Edition and the Prime Mission expansion pass on the same date.

“Machine Utopia” brings automation to Surviving Mars: Relaunched in all-new ways, allowing players to build a new Martian colony where machines do the heavy lifting. Staff workplaces with robots and allow your automatic residents to handle the hazardous work, or try running a colony with no humans at all. Extract vast resources with blast mining, and keep the freight moving with an expanded train network.

Surviving Mars: Relaunched is available now out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store.
