Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Original Story: Sorry to keep bombarding you with GTA 6 hype, but we're literally one week away from the biggest video game launch of all time, and even 20 seconds' worth of gameplay is getting millions of views ahead of November 19th's launch.

This latest footage comes from a weird source - an Instagram account called "Only in Dade". The gameplay shows someone cruising down the road and looking at supercars out the window, while a mass of NPCs are also shown either admiring the vehicles or just making their way down the street.

Here's the footage (we can't embed the original, so you'll find the Reddit repost below):

This is clearly a marketing stunt and not necessarily representative of what you'll be seeing in the game, but it does appear to be real GTA 6 footage - Rockstar Games was spotted sharing and liking the video shortly after it went live.

It also appears to be a take on a similar real-life video from Miami that was taken last month:

Our best look at gameplay for GTA 6 to date has still been the Extended Preview from back in August, which certainly gives us a better idea of what to expect than 20 seconds' worth of gameplay.

We'll take anything we can get at this point though, and it's making the wait even harder for November 19th!
