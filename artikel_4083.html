Where To Play Every Game Of The Year Nominee For 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The votes have been cast and the nominees for 2026's Game of the Year have been revealed. Six of the past year's biggest and best games, and as always, the games that have and haven't made the cut are being hotly debated. There's also a remake in the mix this year along with a game that hadn't been released when the nominations were announced, so a lot for those who think the awards should have clearer criteria to discuss.

Whether you agree with all six nominees or not, it's hard to argue that the games in the running this year aren't six of the six biggest games released this calendar year. The games that, if you haven't played anything else this year, or if there are some you missed, you should probably try your best to play before we get to 2027 and another year of new games begins. Whether you're wondering whether you have the right hardware before buying the nominees, want to know if they have a physical release, or you just want to know how much they cost, you'll find everything you need to know to fill in your GOTY gaps below.

GTA 6 is finally here, and its Game of the Year nomination has caused a stir. The game will be here by the time the awards are handed out, but it was nominated two days before release. That doesn't matter to you right now, though. The launch of GTA 6 is mere hours away as I type this, and if you're not one of the 15 million people who have placed a pre-order, here's what you need to know.

As long as you ignore the state of rent, wages, and world economics, GTA 6's price tag isn't that bad, you guys!

GTA 6 will only be available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S when released. Its PC launch will follow at a later, still unrevealed, time. There are two editions to choose from - standard and ultimate. The standard edition will provide tens, if not hundreds, of hours of entertainment, but there will be various stores, barbers, and tattoo parlors that are off-limits if you don't own the ultimate edition.

The standard edition of GTA 6 is $79.99 on both PS5 and Series X|S, while the ultimate edition is $99.99 on both platforms.

GTA 6 isn't the only game that has caused a stir by picking up a GOTY nomination this year. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has poked the beehive that is remakes being eligible to win the prestigious award. If there was ever a remake that was going to qualify, it's a universally loved one like this that somehow reimagines one of the greatest games of all time without upsetting any of the original's fans.

There is only one place you can play the Ocarina of Time remake, and that's on Switch 2. There are no different editions to choose from, but there is a physical release, and since this is a Switch 2 exclusive, there's a price difference between its digital and physical versions. A digital copy of Ocarina of Time costs $59.99, but if you want a physical copy, it'll cost you $69.99.

Robert Pattinson might be the next James Bond on the big screen, but Patrick Gibson became the video game Bond this year in the critically acclaimed 007 First Light. A performance so good that not only were fans suggesting Gibson could potentially make the jump to Bond movies, but the odds of that happening spiked significantly after the game was released.

First Light is the first nominee on the list that's available to play on Steam. It's also on PS5 and Series X|S, and while a Switch 2 version was promised, it has been delayed until March 18, 2027. I have played a little bit of the game on Switch 2, and for what it's worth, I didn't encounter any issues and was a little surprised that its release was moved into 2027.

It's amazing to see just how far this series has come since the low point that was Resident Evil 6. The trilogy that has followed, alongside the remakes of games from its heyday, has put Resident Evil firmly back on top of the video game mountain. That's why Resident Evil Requiem, a game many would describe as the best new Resi game in decades, is in the running to be crowned Game of the Year.

If you're trying to play all of these games before a winner is crowned, or you want to get one under your belt quickly, Requiem is the place to start. By far the shortest nominee on this year's GOTY list, with a story that takes around 11 hours to play through according to How Long To Beat. It's also the only one of all six nominees that's available on all major platforms, with those who played on Switch 2 awarding it, and Capcom's other day-one Switch 2 releases this year, high praise.

Phantom Blade Zero is a game people have been waiting a long time for. Shown off for the first time well over three years ago, S-Game's action-packed epic finally arrived in October, and it did not disappoint. Rave reviews and a player base that wholeheartedly agrees with what the critics had to say about it have landed it among 2026's Game of the Year nominees.

Your platform options are limited for this one - just PS5 for console players, but it is on PC too if you prefer to play there. There's no physical release either, which is unfortunate, although a physical edition of the PS5 version has been promised for 2027. Hopefully there aren't any issues getting that out on time, with PlayStation pushing forward with its promise to stop putting games on discs in 2028.

Control Resonant might be the most surprising inclusion among this year's Game of the Year nominees. Not because it doesn't deserve to be on this list, but because it's the only game of the six where the combined reaction to its release wasn't overwhelmingly positive, unlike the other five. Not that many people had anything negative to say about it, just that it didn't seem to blow everyone away quite as much as its GOTY competitors.

Now that said, if you want to play the best games 2026 has to offer, Control Resonant should unquestionably be on your list, particularly if you liked the first game and you're an Alan Wake fan. It's available on PS5, Xbox, and PC, there's a physical version if you're into that kind of thing, and it's slightly more expensive and includes an extra outfit, resources, and a digital artbook and soundtrack.

Steam has incredible sales all year round, but nothing quite compares to the deals you can get during the holiday season!
