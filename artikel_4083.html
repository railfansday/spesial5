Lara Croft's PS1 to PS5 Glow-Up to Be Revealed in Tomb Raider Cover Story

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A comprehensive deep dive into the 30-year history of Lara Croft and the Tomb Raider series shall be the focus of next month's Game Informer magazine.

Across 18 pages of the upcoming issue, it'll dedicate 12 of them to next year's Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis remake. Then, in another six pages, it's got a retrospective covering the classic franchise.

You can see the cover art for the physical Game Informer issue below, which incorporates every version of Croft across her eras. It's been done by franchise art director Brenoch Adams:

Having visited Crystal Dynamics and talked to the Flying Wild Hog team, the site returns with details and quotes on "why it’s tackling Lara Croft’s seminal adventure once again (having already remade the game once in Tomb Raider: Anniversary), how this version of the game differs from previous incarnations and why, discuss the teams' controversial use of AI development tools, and how Legacy of Atlantis plants seeds for the upcoming Tomb Raider: Catalyst."
