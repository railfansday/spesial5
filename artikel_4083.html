Talking Point: How Often Do You Buy The Games Leaving Xbox Game Pass?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Once a game is marked for removal from Xbox Game Pass, how often do you buy it? The thought crossed my mind whilst I was putting together our recent roundup of November leavers, and it made me curious to hear the Pure Xbox community's thoughts.

On November 15th, 1000xRESIST will vanish from all tiers of Xbox Game Pass. That means if I want to play it - and I believe I do - then I'll have to buy it. As is customary with games marked for removal, Xbox usually slaps on a discount until the deed is done and the game is gone. 20% unless it's been featured in a sale elsewhere.

That makes 1000xRESIST a £13.39 / $15.99 game, knocking a whopping £3.35 / $4.00 off its full price. It's a fairly cheap game, but that's not much of a discount in the grand scheme of things. Considering I'm short on time to play it before it leaves Xbox Game Pass, and I'm still unsure whether it'll be my kind of game, I'd like to see a more convincing discount before pulling the trigger.

Xbox isn't shy of a good sale, either. I should know: I spend a fair amount of time rummaging through them, hoping to find you the best deals. In my experience, 20% is about 20% off the sweet spot for a sale price. It's a tough sell for games that have been lingering in your backlog for the year-or-so they've spent on Xbox Game Pass for free (assuming we're counting XGP as girl math).

Thinking about it, I've maybe bought two or three games between the moment they're nominated as a leaver and the day they actually leave Xbox Game Pass. What typically happens is that I gamble and wait for the game to go on sale good and proper. That, or I cave and fork out more money than its leaving discount would have cost me.

So, I'd like to put the question to you: how often do you buy the games leaving Xbox Game Pass? If it's something you do regularly, then great! Tell us which games you've bought and why. If not, how come? I'd also love a chat about what you do once a game is announced as a gonner. Do you rush to play it? Can you start a game knowing you don't have enough time to finish it? I want to know!
