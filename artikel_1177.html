Spalletti angered by reporters: ‘How many times have you asked me about Vlahovic?’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Luciano Spaletti grew frustrated at reporters during a press conference on Wednesday afternoon, growing tired of questions about Juventus’s need for a new centre-forward during the January transfer window: ‘How many times have you asked me about Vlahovic?’.

Spalletti spoke to the media at a pre-match press conference on Wednesday ahead of Juventus’s Europa League match against Omonoia on Thursday night. You can read Spalletti’s press conference in full here.

Spalletti had also spoken to the media after Juve’s 4-0 win over Venezia in Serie A on Sunday, making it clear that “we need a physical striker who knows how to play with his back to goal.”

There has been speculation over Juventus’s striker situation ever since Spalletti took over just over a year ago. Dusan Vlahovic ultimately ended up leaving the club on a free transfer at the end of last season after more than a year of contract ‘will he, won’t he’. ‘

Juventus’s pursuit of Randal Kolo Muani lasted most of the summer, after a full transfer window of unsuccessful negotiations the year prior, and a long list of other names, including the likes of Alexander Sorloth, Gianluca Scamacca, Gustavo Varela and various others have been linked with the Bianconeri since then.

Spalletti was asked again about his need for a new striker on Wednesday.

“Calling it a ‘desperate cry’ were your words, not mine,” Spalletti argued. “How many times have you asked me about Vlahovic? Even after he left. I spoke about the need for certain characteristics, not a certain player.

“I want to try Kolo in a different position. Woltemade has done very well since coming in, but can’t play all the time. I thought I would put Kolo Muani back there [at centre-forward] for the Venezia game. That was not a desperate cry. I never said that I don’t like Woltemade.

“We also have Ekhator now and we have to see how he’s doing. He’s a promising young player, we’ll let him play a few times and see if we already have those qualities in-house. When Nico returns, he also has the qualities of a no.9.”
