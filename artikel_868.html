Next James Bond Revealed After Years-Long Search: See His First Teaser

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

More than five years after Daniel Craig's martini was shaken, not stirred, Robert Pattinson will officially take on the role of James Bond in the upcoming film.

The latest James Bond update is shocking. Positively shocking.

After years of speculation as to who would be holstering the Walther PPK and asking for his martini shaken, not stirred, Robert Pattinson is grabbing the keys to the Aston Martin as the titular secret agent.

Several leading men have vied for the role, with Daniel Craig last pouring himself a glass of Macallan in 2021's No Time to Die. In fact, Jack Lowden—whose Slow Horses costar Gary Oldman publicly threw his support behind—Callum Turner, Jacob Elordi, Henry Cavill and Damson Idris were just a few names that sparked casting rumors

Although the actors' involvement was simply speculation over the years, former Bond Pierce Brosnan—who headlined GoldenEye, Tomorrow Never Dies, The World Is Not Enough and Die Another Day between 1995 and 2002—believes Aaron Taylor-Johnson could keep the British end up.

"I think he would be very good," he reflected in an April 2025 interview with Yahoo! Entertainment, adding that they costarred in a 2009 film that he also executive produced. "I cast him in one of the movies I made a long time ago called The Greatest, actually, and he played the Greatest."

"This young man came on the set," Brosnan recalled of the then-18-year-old actor, "and took the space, filled the space with passion and energy. So yeah, I think if he wished it, wanted it and got it, he would be great."

For Taylor-Johnson's part, however, he doesn't have an inflated opinion of himself when it comes to the possibility of the prestigious honor.

"It's not really for me to say anything," the now-36-year-old told Esquire in August 2023. "I just focus on the things I can have my hands in right now. What's in front of me right now."

Although several celebs have been fancast as the 007 agent, the opportunity is quite daunting. For instance, Theo James isn't sure if he's up for the pressure.

"Everyone's interested in that because it's a big part of British cultural identity, but that probably wouldn't be me," he admitted to The Guardian in March 2024. "I do think there are better people for that job. And, honestly, it would be terrifying: if you do that, there's no going back."

Meanwhile, Glen Powell was adamant that anyone but him should take on the role since he's more suited for a cowboy hat than a tux.

"I'm a Texan," he told The Hollywood Reporter in August. "My family and I joke around, I can play Jimmy Bond, but I should not be playing James Bond. Get an authentic Brit for that job. That's who belongs in that tuxedo."
