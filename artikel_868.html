Did Nicolas Cage Really Gain 60 Pounds to Play John Madden? He Says...

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nicolas Cage's transformation for Madden—the biopic in which he plays the legendary NFL coach while costarring with Christian Bale—included putting on 25 pounds plus prosthetics.

Nicolas Cage refuses to fumble a role by not looking the part.

After all, the National Treasure alum put on weight in addition to wearing a heavy bodysuit while portraying famed Oakland Raiders coach John Madden instead of relying on "a frozen mask of prosthetics."

"Sort of a hybrid of gaining weight—I gained about 25 pounds and added some prosthetics to achieve a 60-pound look," Cage exclusively told E! News' Will Marfuggi at the Madden premiere. "Enough of myself where I could still be expressive."

And though the dramatic body transformation brought the Longlegs actor closer to walking in on the late 85-year-old's cleats, that doesn't mean Cage didn't put his own artistic spin on the character.

"I'm not an impressionist. I lean more towards being a surrealist," the Oscar winner explained. "I'm trying to find the energy, the imagination, the heart of the man and dig deep within and try to inform it with something that is emotionally accurate."

Still, the 62-year-old—who attended the film's debut ahead of its release on Prime Video and in select theaters Nov. 18 with wife Riko Shibata, 31—studied Madden's "cadence and some of his gestures" to bring authenticity to his interpretation of the NFL legend.

In his words, Cage's game tape ahead of shooting entailed "a lot of reviewing interviews that he gave, and watching his announcing, and watching some old footage of him on the football field."

And while the Leaving Las Vegas star took hold of the character with both hands, he still managed to leave his work on the field while filming, so that he could focus on being dad at home to their 4-year-old August, who is the youngest of Cage's brood that also includes sons Weston, 35, and Kal-El, 21, from prior relationships.

"I think I left it on the set," he said, referencing one of his previous projects as he added, "I would come home and put the other hat on and be The Family Man."

Aside from appreciating his wife's respect for his creative process, Cage praised his onscreen best friend Christian Bale—also lauded for his shocking physical metamorphoses in films like The Dark Knight, Fighter and Vice throughout his career—for being both an actor and an artist.

"That was really the heart of the movie," the Ghost Rider alum confessed of their chemistry. "It was the second time I'd worked with Christian. We did a picture a million years ago called Captain Corelli's Mandolin. So it was nice to have some more scenes to do with him."

As for whether Cage—a comic book lover who co-created his own series—nerded out in the presence of Batman, Bale's most powerful role?

"Not at all," Cage quipped. "Didn't come up in a single bit."
