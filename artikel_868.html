Jeremy Doku has revealed he did not think about Manchester City’s Premier League charges when he signed a new five-year contract this summer.

The winger put pen to paper on a five-year deal in August and, the following month, City were found guilty of 114 charges by an independent panel.

While City are appealing against the verdict and are adamant they are innocent, possible punishments could include big points deductions that result in relegation or ensure they miss out on Champions League football.

But Doku, a 2023 signing from Rennes, said it was a simple decision for him to agree to stay at the Etihad Stadium until 2031.

"I don't think it is a difficult thing for me to sign a new contract,” he explained. “When you look at this club, the trophies they won, it was a easy choice for me. I am happy here, my wife and son are here. It was an easy choice for me. I didn't think a lot, things needed to be right, as soon as everything was from both sides, it was an easy choice.”

He said the charges did not come into consideration, adding: "I signed because I was happy to sign, I didn't think about anything else."

Maresca has referred to City’s off-field troubles as “noise” and Doku is trying to ignore it.

"I know this is all juicy for you guys but we have a big week, Napoli [on Tuesday] and Arsenal,” he said. “We have enough to speak about. I am not the right person to speak about the charges, I don't really want to get on that topic. I can speak about football."
