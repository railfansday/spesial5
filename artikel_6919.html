Head of Product - AdTech at Yodo1

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

You will: Identify the next big opportunities in AdTech and build them from zero to one

Must have: 5+ years of experience building and scaling AdTech products, with deep expertise in mobile advertising and a proven track record of turning complex technology into products customers love.

At Yodo1, we don't just build and grow games, we turn them into global successes that become household names.

With 14+ years in the games space, we've reached 3.5 billion+ players and supported 6,000+ developers worldwide. From games like Transformers: Earth Wars, Rodeo Stampede and Ski Safari, to bringing iconic IPs from Hasbro, AMC, Netflix, Paramount and Sony Pictures to leading studios like Rivergame, Candivore, 37Games and Tencent Studios, we connect creativity with global expertise.

Our work spans game development, publishing, IP licensing and ad monetization across mobile, PC and console. We build games, grow franchises and help developers reach players worldwide. We love games. We play games. And together, we grow games that last.

We believe in the power of games and that everyone deserves the opportunity to build a life they love, from anywhere in the world.

We're fully remote by design. We embrace Work From Anywhere, with no fixed hours or limits on time off. We trust our people to own their work and deliver results.

We're driven by performance, curiosity, and continuous improvement. We experiment often, challenge assumptions and value impact over titles.

We are looking for an entrepreneurial Head of Product, AdTech to help shape the future of mobile game growth.

We're looking for someone who sees beyond today's products. Someone who understands the entire advertising ecosystem, can identify opportunities others overlook, and has the vision and technical judgment to turn ambitious ideas into successful products.

You'll be a strategic partner to our leadership, helping define where we go next, what we build, and how we win.

Entrepreneurial. You think like a founder, see opportunities where others see problems, and love building products from zero to one.

Visionary but practical. You can see where an industry is heading while knowing exactly what needs to be built today.

Customer-obsessed. You understand customers' businesses, not just their feature requests, and solve problems that create measurable value.

Technically curious. You understand how advertising systems work and are excited about what AI and automation make possible.

A builder and a coach. You bring people together, raise the bar for product thinking, and help teams turn ideas into exceptional products.

Performance-driven and growth-oriented. You have an intrinsic will to succeed while unafraid to try, fail, and learn by experimenting with new approaches and testing ideas.

SMART. You work and communicate in ways that are Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Own the long-term product vision, strategy, and roadmap across our AdTech business

Identify the next big opportunities in mobile advertising by deeply understanding customers, market shifts, emerging technologies, and unmet needs.

Shape how AI and automation transform mobile monetization and user acquisition, making sophisticated advertising capabilities accessible to developers of every size.

Connect monetization, user acquisition, and data into an intelligent growth ecosystem that helps studios make better decisions and grow profitably.

Take products from concept to launch and scale, making clear prioritization decisions and holding yourself accountable for measurable customer and commercial results.

Extensive hands-on experience building and scaling products in AdTech, mobile advertising, or adjacent performance marketing technology.

A strong understanding of the mobile advertising ecosystem, including ad monetization, mediation, user acquisition, DSPs, SSPs, bidding, attribution, and advertising economics.

A proven track record of taking complex technical products from idea to launch and delivering measurable commercial impact

Experience working with data-intensive platforms, optimization systems, machine learning, or AI-powered products.

Strong commercial judgment and the ability to connect product decisions to customer outcomes, revenue, and business growth.

Compensation. We pay based on qualifications, experience, and fit for the role. We provide an allowance to purchase your own device or equipment and offer variable compensation through our annual bonus program.

Growth. We offer an environment where you will have the trust and freedom to try, fail, learn fast, and make a real impact while accelerating your career, and where anyone with a great idea can pitch it directly to our management team.

WFA. Work from anywhere in the world, travel when and where you like, get paid in your currency of choice, and enjoy flexible hours and unlimited paid time off.

Community. Work with an energetic, fast-learning, supportive, and diverse team of Yodies across six continents and 30+ countries who share and support new ideas and get things done.
