Principal burnout looks different in the leaders who stayed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The exodus headlines have quieted. A March 2026 RAND report drawn from the American School District Panel estimates that principal turnover fell to about 8 percent in 2024-2025, down from a pandemic high of 16 percent, though still above prepandemic levels.
