Here’s What Jan Ravnik Said That Got Censored During the ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik had a lot to celebrate after the Dancing With the Stars finale thanks to getting some of their highest scores of the season and doing an absolutely incredible freestyle—and Jan was so pumped that he went ahead and swore on live TV, causing ABC to panic-bleep him.

While chatting with Julianne Hough about Tatyana in the skybox, Jan gushed, “I’m just extremely proud of her,” and then added, “She fucking killed it” (which producers speedily censored).

Tatyana reacted to Jan swearing in a post-episode interview with Extra, saying, “I wasn’t that shocked…he was just being real, it’s live TV though.” Jan also chimed in with, “It was just a perfect way to describe the last dance.”

Jan and Tatyana had one of the sweetest partnerships of the season, and Jan gushed about her in a BTS video for ABC, saying, “Thank you. You are an incredible human being; I think you are the nicest human being that I ever met. You are so talented. You’re an incredible dancer.” Oh, and he added, “You are definitely going to be invited to my wedding,” if you want something to read into!
