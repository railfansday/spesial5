Cosmo Reads Got Readers Ready for the Holidays With a Book-ish Manicure Party

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Majesty’s Pleasure opened its doors and came up with the cutest nail designs for the big event!

We get it, the holidays get so hectic! And with so many books out there to read—including some new ones from Cosmo Reads—sometimes you forget to schedule that mani right before seeing the family again or getting ready for the many Friendsgiving parties on the calendar. Luckily, Cosmo Reads came to the rescue with manis and pedis for some of the imprint’s biggest readers in honor of the imprint’s 2027 slate that will launch in January!

Majesty’s Pleasure in NYC was the place to be as attendees matched their manis, pedis, and drinks to some of Cosmo Reads’s upcoming books: Oath of the Chosen by Nicole Hollerman, The Sex Detective by Anna Natasha, A Killer’s Guide to Falling by Kristin Wollett, and Let Me Get Back to You by Liana Haley.

Nicole herself also joined in on the fun, getting a matching set that fits perfectly with Oath of the Chosen and quickly became a favorite amongst attendees thanks to its perfect fall burgundy color!

Guests like @its.amandas.bookshelf, @hopelessbooklover_, @tattletav, @deirdrerosemorgan, @bookbaristas, @whimsy_bookowl, @la_joie_de_livre, @readswithelise, @booksandlooksss dropped by and were also able to pick up copies of upcoming Cosmo Reads books as well as some recent favorites like Bethan Croome’s Reign and Iman Hariri-Kia’s Once Upon a Timeline.

The biggest get of the night? The brand-new Cosmo Reads tote that was not only ready to carry all the new reads home but also quickly became the ultimate must-have for the night!

Of course, it wouldn’t be a book party without celebrating our trio of Cosmopolitan covers that feature Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, and Maya Hawke, who currently star in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. It was the ultimate bookish paradise and the best way to kick off cozy reading season!
