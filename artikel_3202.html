This Kindle Scribe Is 30% Ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

With early Black Friday deals in full force, there’s never been a better time to stock up on tech essentials, including highly giftable tablets and e-readers. Purchasing a refurbished model is a great way to maximize your value, which is why this like-new Amazon Kindle Scribe (64GB) is on our radar at 30% off, bringing its price down to $282.99 (originally $404.99). Shoppers can also save an extra 20% and get an Amazon gift card if they trade in an old, eligible Kindle.

This model of the Kindle Scribe, released in 2024, comes in Metallic Jade with a 10.2-inch glare-free e-Ink black-and-white screen, 300 ppi, 16-level grayscale, and an adjustable built-in front light. It includes a Premium Pen that attaches magnetically and doesn’t require charging, allowing you to easily mark up documents, add notes to books you’re reading, or write in notebooks. Plus, its paper-like feel offers an organic transition for notebook users.

This model also includes AI features, from summarizing notes to turning messy handwriting into readable font. It lasts for up to 12 weeks of reading or three weeks of writing with around 30 minutes of daily use, and charges in around 2.5 hours. For those who want a large-screen Kindle for reading and also expect to take notes or annotate books and documents, this like-new Amazon Kindle Scribe (64GB) is a functional multitasker for everyday use. At $282.99, it’s about $122 off, making it a strong deal on a 64GB model with the Premium Pen included.
