GTA 6 is launching with a full Saturday morning cartoon series players can watch in-game

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

GTA 6 is getting its own animated TV show that players will be able to watch inside the game, and it now has a teaser trailer.

The show is called Macca & Friends, and the trailer says it’s “coming November 19 on Weazel,” the fictional TV network from the GTA series.

The cartoon follows Macca the Gator, a pink alligator in a palm tree shirt who fans will recognize from the figure in GTA 6’s $400 Collector’s Box.

The teaser was first shared by DiscussingFilm on November 13 under the title “Macca & Friends: Exclusive Teaser Trailer,” and plays out like an ad for a real Saturday morning cartoon, with Macca joined by a group of other talking animals and a muscle-bound cop.

Rockstar has been hinting at the show for over a month. Its official store describes Macca as the star of a hit television show popular across Leonida, and the same collection includes a shot glass featuring another character, Chunkee the Manatee.

A Macca & Friends mural also appeared in October at the real-life Burger Shot stand inside Miami’s Kaseya Center, carrying the same “Coming November 19 on Weazel” tagline, in photos taken by nvtnp_ and shared by GTA 6 Countdown.

Rockstar is now marketing GTA 6 with a real-life Burger Shot food stand in Kaseya Center, Miami.They continue preparing for the major Miami Heat event on November 18.(via @nvtnp_) pic.twitter.com/hbAlRKP4A4

Rockstar is yet to explain how the show fits into the game, so it’s not clear how many episodes there will be or exactly where players can watch it.

In-game TV has been part of the series for years, with GTA 4 and GTA 5 both letting players sit down and watch full shows from their safehouses, so Macca & Friends is likely to air the same way.
