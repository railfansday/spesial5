Tony Hawk kicked out of iconic Pro Skater 3 park during anniversary video

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tony Hawk has revealed that he was kicked out of a park featured in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 while filming a video to celebrate the game’s 25th anniversary.

Hawk visited Pershing Square in Los Angeles on October 30 – one of the real-world locations recreated in the game – to mark the 25th anniversary of Pro Skater 3’s release.

Hawk shared footage of his visit on Instagram, showing him skating through Pershing Square and replicating clips from the 2001 game.

However, filming seemingly didn’t go entirely to plan. “Happy 25th THPS3!” Hawk wrote in the video’s caption, adding, “fun fact: we got kicked out of Pershing Square while shooting this today.”

Hawk later clarified that a security guard had approached the group and asked them to leave.

“She just shoo’d us away,” he explained. “It was very anticlimactic.” Hawk didn’t reveal why they were told to leave.

Pershing Square is one of several real-world Los Angeles locations recreated in Pro Skater 3. The game’s Los Angeles level also features landmarks inspired by the nearby Downtown Car Wash, which Hawk revisited for the video.

Originally released on October 30, 2001, Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 was the first entry in the series to launch on PlayStation 2.

The game was also brought back in 2025 as part of Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 + 4, a remake collection developed by Iron Galaxy.
