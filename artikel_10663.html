Apple Music 7.0 for Android rolls out with Liquid Glass redesign

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Following a month of beta testing, Apple Music 7.0 is rolling out with a Liquid Glass redesign for Android, as well as the new icon for the streaming service.

This update starts with a tweaked music note icon in black or white, depending on your device theme, replacing the previous red background. Similarly, the accent color throughout Apple Music is now blue.

The app now has a floating bottom bar that lets you see content above, below, and to the sides. There is a small amount of translucency that primarily allows you to make out solid colors underneath the navigation element. The mini-player gets the same treatment and is no longer docked to the bottom bar. However, compared to iOS, scrolling does not collapse the bar into a more compact version.

Version 7.0 introduces a very modern, edge-to-edge design for Android’s system status and navigation bars.

Dear Apple, use Material 3 Expressive for your apps. Google has honored your design language by building their apps with Liquid Glass, now you need to return the favor and build with Google's design language

Top bars across Apple Music have been updated. The page title and overflow menu are now on the same line, while the three-dot icons are no longer themed red and have been placed in circular containers. The Library tab sees a big update with “Edit Pins” and “Edit Categories” now part of the three-dot menu.

Artist pages have been thoroughly redesigned with the background now themed based on the cover image. Matching the iOS design, there’s a prominent play button and info page, while share has been placed in the top-right corner.

The Settings page has also been modernized with better grouping. There are no major changes to Now Playing, which did not see any big updates on iOS.

With a recent release, Apple Music for Android switched to a new versioning system that aligns with iOS. Today’s version 7.0 matches iOS 27 and is now widely rolled out via the Play Store.
