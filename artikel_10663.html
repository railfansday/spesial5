Elegoo's top-tier resin 3D printer with a staggeringly detailed 16K print resolution for amazing, detailed prints. Comes complete with auto-bed leveling and tilt-release for easy print removal. There's even a camera for watching your prints take shape, with time-lapse stills.

The Saturn 4 Ultra 16K is a resin 3D printer with a 4-inch capacitive touchscreen at the front. This means you can access several of its built-in smart features directly without needing to connect remotely. It has a build volume of 211.68 x 118.37 x 220mm, which is slightly smaller than the original 4 Ultra, but only just.

There's also a 10-inch, 16K LCD screen that comes with this printer. That isn't a display, in case you're a resin printer newbie, but a mask that you'll use to block or allow UV light to pass through and cure each individual layer as the print continues. You're getting a resolution of 11,520 x 5,120 with this printer, which matches up to an accuracy of 14 microns. That might not mean much, but a human hair has 70 microns of thickness, to give you a point of comparison. It's also an improvement over the original Saturn 4 Ultra's 12K screen.

You're also getting a much improved vat heater with this model. The Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 16K's integrated vat heater means you're able to maintain temperatures as you print, making the print process more reliable, even if the ambient temperatures around the printer are much colder, giving you a clear 86°F (30°C). There are mechanical sensors to help prevent the plate from crushing the screen if a print fails, along with a tilt-release feature that will move the tank away to peel resin layers one by one, with an overall 150mm/hr print speed. You've got power-loss recovery that will help to protect your print progress, even if there's an outage, and you've also got a camera built-in so you can create time-lapse videos of your progress, but it'll also let you monitor it as you go.
