Dead hippo to be honoured on football club tifo

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fiona first rose to fame when she was born premature at Cincinnati Zoo in 2017

Major League Soccer side FC Cincinnati will honour Fiona, a hippo that recently died at the city's zoo, with a tifo display at their match with Nashville SC on Saturday.

Fiona, a social media star since her premature birth at the zoo in 2017, died on Thursday after swallowing a woollen hat that had slipped into her enclosure.

The Ohio side will now raise a banner depicting the nine-year-old semi-aquatic mammal in their post-season MLS Cup encounter with Nashville, who won the Supporters' Shield for being the highest-placed team in the league campaign.

Cincinnati said Fiona was a "beloved hippo who became a symbol of Cincinnati and captured hearts around the world".

The tifo will be produced by fan group The Forge Cincy, who painted the display on Sunday in a garage next to Cincinnati's TQL Stadium.

The club will also make a donation in Fiona's memory for every fan in attendance to the Cincinnati Zoo and Botanical Garden.

Fan group The Forge Cincy had previously spent two weeks creating a tifo for their "Hell is Real" October derby match against Ohio rivals Columbus

Cincinnati's Nick Hagglund, who hails from the city and is in his eighth season with the team, previously paid tribute to Fiona after scoring in his side's 3-1 victory over Orlando on the day of her death.

The defender yelled "that's for Fiona", mimicking the ears on top of her head, in a celebration video, external on the club's Instagram.

Later, on his Instagram account, external, Hagglund posted, "This one was for her @fionahippo @cincinnatizoo.

"Cincinnati needed a win on the day after the passing of our beloved hippo, a symbol of hope and resilience for our city, and the lads answered the call."

Cincinnati's tifo is one of a number of tributes paid to the hippo, with Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Tee Higgins wearing a Fiona sweater, external prior to his American football franchise's defeat by the Washington Commanders on Monday.

Meanwhile handlers of the blue hippo balloon at New York's annual Macy's Thanksgiving Parade will wear a purple patch in her honour.

At birth, Fiona weighed 13kg (29lb), which the zoo said was about half the previous lowest recorded birth weight for her species, and she previously inspired an ice cream flavour and a character in Cincinnati's ballet.

Fiona swallowed the woollen hat around 10 days prior to her death, with the item getting into her enclosure by accident after a visitor tossed it in the air and it caught on a ceiling fan that flung it through a small opening.

"We probably couldn't make [it] happen again if we tried," said zoo spokesperson Michelle Curley.

Hippos normally live up to 50 years in human care, according to the zoo.

Nashville finished top of the MLS Eastern Conference, 31 points ahead of ninth-placed Cincinnati.

However, having beaten Orlando in a one-off wildcard round, Cincinnati defeated Nashville 1-0 in the opening match of their best-of-three round one series.

Should Cincinnati win on Saturday they will progress to a one-off conference semi-final with either Chicago Fire or Charlotte FC, with a conference final prior to the season-ending MLS Cup final.
