Signing off September’s extended international break with a gritty 1-0 victory against Wales in Cardiff, Denmark have won three of their previous four Nations League appearances. However, getting October underway with a rollercoaster 4-2 defeat against Portugal, Tuesday’s hosts are searching for some essential defensive stability. In fact, Riemer’s men have failed to register consecutive clean sheets across all competitions over the past 13 months.
