SenseNet lands $43M financing deal for early wildfire detection and intelligence

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SenseNet, a startup providing wildfire detection and intelligence, on Tuesday announced a $43 million financing package. The Vancouver, B.C.-based company says the funding will support its expansion across the U.S. and internationally.

The startup uses a suite of technologies for the rapid detection of wildfires, including environmental sensors, AI-enabled cameras, satellite data, drone technology and predictive analytics. Its monitoring footprint covers 450 million acres in a dozen countries.

SenseNet says its technology has provided early alerts for more than 10,000 fires, in some cases hours before the first 911 calls.

“Wildfire protection is no longer just about detecting a fire as quickly as possible. It’s about understanding what’s happening, what could happen next, and giving the right people the information they need to act,” Hamed Noori, co-founder and CEO of SenseNet, said in a statement.

Noori launched SenseNet in 2019 with Chief Technology Officer Shahab Bahrami. Both hold doctorates in electrical and computer engineering from the University of British Columbia.

The Series B round was led by Idealist Capital, with participation from Fusion Fund, Stormbreaker Ventures, Thin Line Capital, National Bank of Canada and The Sitka Foundation.

The financing includes purchases of newly issued shares, which put money into SenseNet; secondary share sales, which pay existing shareholders; and debt. SenseNet did not disclose how much of the $43 million falls into each category.

The startup previously raised $12.78 million and has 30 employees, according to PitchBook.

SenseNet competes with companies including OroraTech, Dryad and Pano AI, whose cameras, software and alerting services are used by Washington’s Department of Natural Resources to detect wildfires. The companies are developing their technology as warming temperatures and drier conditions increase wildfire risks.
