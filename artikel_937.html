Legendary football commentator John Motson‘s voice is set to return to EA FC 27 through the use of AI - three years after his death.

Motson, who died aged 77 in 2023, worked on the BBC’s flagship Match of the Day programme since 1971 and covered more than 2,000 televised games, including 29 FA Cup finals and 10 World Cups.

He retired from live commentary in 2008 but continued to work on Match of the Day programme until he hung up the microphone in 2018.

EA Sports said they worked with John Motson’s family to bring back his iconic voice in a “digital tribute” through the use of new AI-tools, “bringing a mix of both his original audio and an AI-recreation trained by recordings of the man himself”.

The commentator featured in EA Sports’ annual FIFA game series from 1996 to 2006.

A studio spokesperson said EA Sports have “worked with extensive care and respect to create a digital tribute to one of the game’s greatest voices, inspiring a whole new generation of football fans.”

“My father adored being the voice of the game, and we are so grateful to the team at EA SPORTS FC for this touching tribute and preserving his memory in-game,” said Fred Motson, son of John Motson.

“His voice inspired millions of players across generations every time they picked up a controller, and now a whole new set of fans will get to experience his legacy and commentary.”

After starting as a newspaper reporter in Barnet and at the Sheffield Morning Telegraph, Motson joined the BBC in 1968 as a sports presenter on Radio 2.

His long career also took in two Olympic Games and Wimbledon’s memorable 1988 FA Cup final triumph against Liverpool at Wembley, as the Crazy Gang beat the Culture Club.

Awarded the OBE in 2001 for services to broadcasting, Motson hung up his microphone for the BBC at the end of the 2017-18 Premier League season.

EA Sports football game series began in 1994 with the release of FIFA International Soccer, with the FIFA name continuing until 2023, when the termination of EA and FIFA’s licensing agreement led to the 2024 game being called FC.

John Motson’s voice will be added to EA FC 27 in Title Update v1.3.0 on 26 November as an optional commentary selection.
