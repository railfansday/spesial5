Arsenal close to new deals for Raya, Timber and Hincapie

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

David Raya and Jurrien Timber were about to enter the last 18 months of their Arsenal contracts; both players have rubberstamped their statuses as the best players in their positions in Europe; Piero Hincapie only joined Arsenal on a permanent basis last summer

Arsenal are close to finalising new deals with David Raya, Jurrien Timber and Piero Hincapie.

Sky Sports News reported on Monday that midfielder Declan Rice has verbally agreed a new contract and champions Arsenal are set to also tie down Raya, Timber and Hincapie.

Raya's current deal expires in 2028 but he is on the verge of signing an extension after establishing himself as one of the world's best goalkeepers since joining Arsenal, initially on loan from Brentford, in 2023.

Defender Timber joined Arsenal from Ajax in the same summer as Raya and his current contract is due to expire in 2028.

Speaking about reports he had agreed a new Arsenal deal, following the Netherlands 5-0 Nations League defeat against Germany on Monday, Timber told ESPN Netherlands: "Those are good reports. They are definitely on the right track! It will all work out, yes.

"If I extend, hopefully a few years will be added. You know better than I do. I feel at home here, I'm enjoying myself. Quinten [Timber - his twin brother at Crystal Palace] is in London now too, so we are doing well.

"I think we will definitely still be competing for trophies in the coming years. The club is happy with me as well, so that is good too. It all looks good."

Defender Hincapie - an initial loan signing from Bayer Leverkusen in 2025 - is also in talks over an extension with Arsenal confident he too will commit his long-term future to the club.

Arsenal are going through a major contract renewal surge at the moment. Mikel Arteta has one of the best squads in Europe and - after the Arsenal manager committed to new terms earlier this year - he wants to keep it that way.

Other Arsenal players to commit to new contracts in the last 18 months include Bukayo Saka, Gabriel and William Saliba - while the long-term futures of high-profile youngsters such as Ethan Nwaneri, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Max Dowman have been secured.

In Timber, Raya and Declan Rice, Arsenal have ended any speculation about players moving towards the final year of their contract. The days of the early 2010s where Arsenal were sweating over the long-term futures of their major stars is clearly over.

But there are a still a handful of players who still have their futures hanging in the balance. The most pressing is captain Martin Odegaard, who has become a key player again after an injury-hit campaign last term.

The Norwegian midfielder, whose contract expires in 2028, has publicly revealed his desire to stay, but will a deal be struck before more questions and doubts start coming?

Another player entering the last 18 months of his contract is Kai Havertz - who again has had doubters and injury issues in recent years. With Arsenal's attack a clear area that needs to be improved, does one of Arteta's highest-paid players get another bump?

Ben White is another player whose contract expires in 2028, though the club have the option of an extra year extension on his deal. With Jurrien Timber, Ezri Konsa and Cristhian Mosquera also available for Arsenal in his position - a decision on his long-term future will also need to come soon.

The same contract expiry date also applies for Mikel Merino, who is struggling for regular minutes in a stacked Arsenal midfield that includes Lewis-Skelly, Bruno Guimaraes and Martin Zubimendi, who looks set to stay and fight for his place.

When Arsenal don't see a long-term vision for a player, they make it fairly clear when they let them go into the final year of their contracts. Gabriel Martinelli, Leandro Trossard and Gabriel Jesus found that out, despite all three playing plenty of minutes across the season.

So what do Arsenal do with Odegaard, Havertz, White and Merino?
