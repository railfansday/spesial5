12 Best Video Game Stocking Stuffers Under $30 That People Actually Want

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ugh. It’s my least-favorite time of year: Black Friday season. I hate this overwhelming capitalist-ass shopping holiday for many reasons, but one of my more petty pet peeves is the big push for the blandest, most cringey “gamer”-themed gifts ever at mainstream shopping destinations. I’m talking like, this “Smells like video game rage” candle, neon lights and blankets that say stuff like, “Can’t hear you, I’m gaming,” or the classic “I paused my game to be here” T-shirt. Crap that really leans into the awful “gamer” stereotype and that, for many of us, is embarrassing to receive from a relative who knows, like, one fact about us, which is that we are into video games.

If you’ve arrived at this article and have been guilty of giving such gifts before, please absolve yourself. The internet does not make this easy. Gaming gift guides are full of this junk, and if you’re not into games yourself, finding decent stuff for someone who likes them can be really annoying and challenging, especially on a budget. Most gamers probably have a lot of the tech and games they want already, and knowing what people have and what they might like can be extremely challenging if you don’t have an easy way to look at their Steam libraries.

So I’m here to give you a hand with 12 ideas for stocking stuffer gifts (read: will reasonably fit in a normal-sized Christmas stocking) under $30 that will probably be relevant to folks who like playing video games on the regular. These are all things that most people will be at least mildly pleased to receive and will probably use as long as you put in the bare minimum amount of thought to make sure you’re not getting Switch accessories for someone who doesn’t have a Switch or something.

Good controllers are so dang expensive and cheap ones are usually garbage, but the 8BitDo controllers are both of solid quality and inexpensive. These pleasant wireless controllers come in several cute pastel colors, are usable for PC gaming (or Android, I guess), and are quite inexpensive for as good as they are. Normally $29.99 on Amazon, as I write this they’re on sale for $22.99.

It’s normally $49.99, but Amazon has a Black Friday deal going that brings it all the way down to $18.99, so you may want to jump on it soon.

The Nintendo Switch 2 came out a year and a half ago, and with it came what’s effectively webcam support. However, my experience is that not a lot of people are using this feature, not because it’s a bad idea necessarily, but because they don’t want to buy the expensive bespoke webcam Nintendo is selling and don’t want to move the normal one they have on their PC over to their Switch every time. So right now, for $24.98 on Amazon ($34.99 normally) you can grab a Hori USB camera that’s licensed by Nintendo and works with the Switch 2. Actually having this set up with the Switch 2 will probably prompt more people to actually bust it out (it would certainly work on me).

If your gamer likes to sit at a desk, grab ’em a mug warmer like this Sweetfull option that’s currently $16.99, down from $19.99. It’s exactly what it sounds like, keeping the liquid warm. Great for tea, coffee, and hot cocoa drinkers who have told their families to stop buying them tea/coffee/cocoa every year because they now have too much.
