Love Is Blind’s Sarah Reveals Where She Stands With Ex John Amid Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Love Is Blind season 11 star Sarah Williams holds no grudges against her estranged husband, John Shannon, amid their ongoing divorce.

“I think I would say that we’re friends, but at a distance,” Sarah, 34, said on the Wednesday, November 18, episode of the “What’s the Reality” podcast. “I’m putting myself first.”

Sarah and John, 33, got engaged sight unseen on the latest season of Love Is Blind, which debuted on Netflix last month after filming in 2025. While the twosome were the only season 11 couple to get married, Sarah filed for divorce in June. John claimed during the tell-all reunion that their marriage ended after he made a “mistake” by exchanging explicit texts with a stripper. Sarah, however, later revealed that there were other reasons for her divorce petition, including losing self-confidence after John’s alleged mistreatment and name-calling.

John has since issued a public apology to Sarah, who has tried to maintain certain boundaries with her ex.

“The distance has been great, I will say,” she noted on Wednesday’s episode. “Now, when we do talk, I’m like, ‘No, no, we’re not talking about that [and] I’m not doing that.’ It gets easier and easier with time.”

Sarah continued, claiming, “He also hasn’t signed the [divorce] papers.”

When pressed by podcast host AD Smith about whether John is likely trying to “hold on to what little he has left” or “doesn’t want [the marriage] to be over,” Sarah speculated that may be the case.

“I think it’s a little bit of both,” she added. “I think he will now. … [The experiment is serious and] that’s the one thing I wish viewers would know is that, like, when you’re in it, it’s so serious.”

Sarah is now looking forward to her post-divorce chapter, though diving back into the dating scene is not on her agenda just yet.

“I’m going to take a little break from dating. I think I just need to get a clear head, remind myself of who I am,” she said. “And then, I don’t know. I don’t have any expectations. I’m like, ‘I just need a break.’”

Even so, Sarah remains hopeful to walk down the aisle again.

“Give me some time, [but] I think marriage is so great,” she noted. “You just have to find it, [but] it’s like, ‘Where?’”

Sarah further revealed that she’ll take the lessons from her failed marriage into future relationships.

“This was obviously, like, a crazy life learning experience for me. I don’t know how it would have been if I hadn’t said yes [at the altar],” she mused. “It was really hard. I’m grateful for the learning experience, but I wish we had just said, ‘Let’s try to date,’ and it would have gone differently. I would have left — and John knows this — I would have left fast.”

Love Is Blind is currently airing on Netflix.
