Brooks Laich and Wife Katrin Are Expecting Baby No. 2: 'Little Blessing'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brooks Laich and his wife, Katrín Tanja Davíõsdóttir, are growing their brood.

“Baby #2. Another little blessing joining our family in May and we couldn’t be more excited!!!” the former NHL star, 43, and the former CrossFit athlete, 33, wrote in a joint Instagram post on Sunday, November 22.

The couple shared the news alongside a video of them on a white-sand beach with their daughter, Emberly, 13 months, as Laich and the little one kissed and played with Davíõsdóttir’s growing bump.

Laich and Davíõsdóttir, who tied the knot in August at their Idaho home, then shared a sweet kiss on the lips for the camera before they raced up to the camera with Emberly to switch the recording off.

The news drew a string of congratulatory messages from fans – and Laich’s ex-wife, Julianne Hough. “Yayyyyy sibling on the way … congratulations,” Hough, 38, commented, adding a red heart emoji. The words from Hough, who was married to Laich for nearly three years before the pair announced their separation in May 2020, sparked a response from Laich. “@juleshough Emberly is very excited to be a big sister,” Laich wrote.

A fan weighed in on Hough’s support of her ex, throwing in, “Just love to see how you’re so incredibly happy for them! Love truly comes in so many forms.”

Laich and Davíõsdóttir first sparked romance rumors by “liking” a few of her Instagram posts several months after his split from Hough. Hough filed for divorce from Laich in November that year.

In the summer of 2021, Laich took his relationship with Davíõsdóttir public by commenting on one of her July Instagram posts, “Day 1 of the @nobull @crossfitgames is complete. Proud of how this girl competed today!” The new couple were also seen publicly kissing during a livestream from the 2021 NOBULL CrossFit Games later that month.

The pair announced their engagement three years later, and within six months, they revealed that Davíõsdóttir was pregnant with Emberly. “Our greatest blessing 🤍🫶🏼✨ Baby Laich coming this fall. Mom & Dad can’t wait to meet you, our little one!!!!!!” read the caption of their joint May 2025 post.

Hough, meanwhile, has kept her romantic life private in the wake of her split from Laich. While she was linked to various reality TV stars, no relationships have been confirmed since.

Most recently, Bachelor Nation’s Tanner Courtad was the subject of online dating rumors before he addressed their brief tryst on iHeartRadio’s “Bachelor Happy Hour” podcast earlier this month. “That was short-lived, but it was fun,” Courtad, 33, said on the November 3 episode. “I was going to Dancing With the Stars to support Joey [Graziadei], and I was sitting in the front row, and Ezra [Sosa] came over to me, and he goes, ‘Hey, our host thinks you’re cute. Can I give you her number?’”

He claimed that he and Hough met “in the parking lot” after the show before going on a sushi date. “It was very short-lived,” he said at the time. “Julianne’s great, but it was never anything more than just, like, a single date.”
