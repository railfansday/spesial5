AudienceView: Digital Marketing Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Expected compensation: Base salary starting at $85,000 CAD per annum

Vacancy status: This posting is for an existing vacancy.

AudienceView is passionate about helping live event organizations bring people together through unforgettable experiences. Our unified event commerce platform helps organizations better understand, engage, and grow their audiences while creating stronger relationships and more connected experiences across the entire event journey.

We support organizations across performing arts, music, athletics, higher education, fairs, festivals, attractions, and more. By combining technology, audience intelligence, and deep industry expertise, AudienceView helps organizations make informed decisions, simplify operations, and build sustainable growth.

Four core values set the tempo for how we work:

AudienceView is made up of people who believe wholeheartedly in the power and magic of live events. Come transform live entertainment with us.

We are looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to own and scale our B2B demand generation engine. In this role, you will drive pipeline growth and lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) by orchestrating high-impact paid campaigns, targeted ABM programs, and sophisticated marketing automation workflows. If you are a data-driven marketer who loves connecting the dots between paid media, email nurtures, and sales execution, we want to talk to you.

Few people tick every box on a list like this. If most of it sounds like you and the rest sounds like something you'd love to learn, we still want to hear from you.

No black holes and no endless rounds. Here's the run of show, so you know exactly what's coming and when:

Every interview is held face to face over Microsoft Teams with a member of our team on camera. If we interview you, we will tell you whether a hiring decision has been made for this role within 45 days of your last interview.

How we use artificial intelligence in hiring

We use AI in one place only. Our hiring team may use Microsoft Copilot to transcribe interviews and draft interview notes, so that interviewers can concentrate on the conversation instead of typing. You will always know before any recording or AI note-taking begins, and you can ask us to take notes manually instead — it will not affect your application.

We do not use AI to screen, assess, rank or select applicants, and no automated system makes a hiring decision at any stage. Every shortlisting, interview and offer decision is made by people on our hiring team.

Equal opportunity, inclusion and accessibility

Diversity and inclusion have always been at the core of our values at AudienceView. A diverse workforce with wide perspectives and creative ideas benefits our clients, the communities where we operate, and all of us as colleagues. We welcome applications from qualified people of every background.

We are committed to an accessible hiring process and provide accommodations and adjustments to applicants with disabilities on request, at every stage of recruitment and employment.

AudienceView does not offer employment to anyone without first ensuring that qualified candidates speak directly with a member of the hiring team. All qualifying conversations take place face to face over Microsoft Teams. We do not make offers of employment without meeting candidates, we never make an offer by text message, and we never ask candidates to pay a fee. If you are asked for personal or financial information by text, or before you have met a member of our hiring team, please disregard it.

If this role sounds like your kind of show and your experience lines up with what we’re looking for, we’d love to hear from you. We’re grateful for every application we receive; however, only qualified candidates selected to move forward in our hiring process will be contacted.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
