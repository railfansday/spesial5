Nobody needs 87 nearly identical photos of their dog, but who has the heart to delete them? If you’re the sentimental type when it comes to your digital files, Scramble Cloud is offering 20TB of lifetime cloud storage for $314.96 (reg. $1,440) this Black Friday with the code CLOUD.

Developed in Germany, Scramble Cloud uses end-to-end encryption to protect files before they even leave your device, along with a zero-knowledge architecture designed to prevent the provider from accessing your stored content. Two-factor authentication adds another layer of account protection.

Scramble Cloud isn’t just a digital dumping ground, either. You can organize documents using a familiar folder structure, preview compatible files in your browser, and stream MP4 videos directly from your account. And if you’d like to subject your family to all those vacation photos, private sharing links, encrypted groups, and customizable file permissions let you decide who gets access to what.
