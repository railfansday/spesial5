Meta now offers more than 150 3D movies that you can buy or rent to watch in VR on its Quest 3 and 3S headsets, the company announced today. The movies are available in Meta’s TV app and include titles like Avatar, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Jurassic World Rebirth, Wicked: For Good, and a Billie Eilish concert film co-directed by Eilish and James Cameron.

The company suggests watching movies in a Theater view that’s rolling out with a new Meta Quest update. Theater view “provides a big screen and dims your surroundings to create a cozy, personal cinema experience,” Meta says.
