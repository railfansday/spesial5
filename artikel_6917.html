Lyra Health: Pediatric Clinical Quality Consultant

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Lyra Clinical Associates partners with Lyra Health, a leading provider of evidence-based mental health care, serving more than 20 million people globally in partnership with employers and more than 100 million through health plan and partner relationships. The company has delivered more than 15 million sessions of mental health care, published more than 35 peer-reviewed studies, and delivered unmatched outcomes in terms of access, clinical effectiveness, and cost efficiency. Lyra members have access to care in one day, and nine out of ten improve with the support of providers like you. Lyra is transforming access to life-changing mental health care through Lyra Empower, the only fully integrated, AI-powered platform combining the highest-quality care and technology solutions. You will be employed by Lyra Clinical Associates.

Reporting to the Clinical Quality Manager of Pediatric Therapy and Health Plans, the Clinical Quality Consultant (CQC) is a key clinical leadership position within the Pediatric Therapy Department, responsible for clinical quality governance, contractor engagement, contractual alignment, and operational excellence across the pediatric therapy independent contractor (1099) network, as well as clinical quality oversight of pediatric coaching services.

As the primary point of contact and clinical subject matter expert for pediatric therapy independent contractors, you will lead clinical quality assurance and continuous improvement initiatives, provide clinical consultation, and partner with cross-functional teams to develop and facilitate contractor onboarding, training and educational resources while respecting contracted providers' independent business status, professional autonomy, and independent clinical judgment.

This is an advanced clinical individual contributor role without direct personnel management responsibilities. You will also maintain an active pediatric therapy caseload, with up to 40% of working hours allocated to direct, face-to-face clinical care delivered virtually. Responsibilities may evolve based on organizational and program needs.

Contractor Engagement, Onboarding & Training

Serve as the primary point of contact and clinical liaison for pediatric therapy independent contractors, supporting contractor engagement, recruitment, clinical qualification review, and onboarding.

Partner with the Training and Onboarding Operations team to develop, facilitate and continuously improve contractor onboarding orientation, training and educational resources alignment with applicable clinical quality standards, contractual requirements, regulatory compliance and member safety.

Foster collaborative professional relationships, gather contractor feedback, and facilitate access to clinical consultation, optional ongoing education, and provider resources.

Clinical Quality Assurance & Contractual Alignment

Conduct clinical quality assurance activities, including authorized clinical documentation and service delivery reviews, to evaluate contracted services against applicable clinical, contractual, ethical, and regulatory standards.

Analyze applicable contractual key performance indications (KPIs), service-level agreements (SLAs), clinical outcomes, and quality metrics to identify trends, risks, and opportunities for improvement.

Provide clinical consultation and recommendations regarding complex clinical presentations, evidence-based interventions, risk assessment, safety planning, and level-of-care considerations.

Communicate clinical quality findings, escalate clinical, safety, regulatory, and contractual concerns, and collaborate with designated stakeholders regarding contractual remediation and follow-up.

Clinical Quality Oversight of Pediatric Coaching

Provide licensed clinical expertise, quality oversight, and consultation regarding pediatric coaching services, including clinical case reviews, risk identification, escalation, and level-of-care considerations within applicable scope-of-service parameters.

Collaborate with the Pediatric Coach Manager and relevant stakeholders to promote clinical quality, member safety, and continuous quality improvement.

Operational Excellence & Continuous Improvement

Develop and refine scalable internal processes, standard operating procedures (SOPs), clinical quality assurance tools, reporting mechanisms, and contractor-facing resources.

Lead designated quality improvement initiatives and partner with Clinical Quality, Legal, Compliance, Operations, and other stakeholders to advance contractual alignment, regulatory compliance, and pediatric program development.

Maintain appropriate distinctions between clinical quality oversight, contractual accountability, and employment-based personnel management.

Maintain an active pediatric therapy caseload, providing evidence-based individual and family therapy, clinical assessments, individualized treatment planning, caregiver engagement and interdisciplinary care coordination.

Ability to work full-time, approximately 40 hours per week, remotely, with afternoon and evening coverage aligned with Pacific Time (PT). Preferred working hours are approximately 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM PT, with flexibility based on organizational needs.

Ability to maintain a private, professional workspace and reliable internet connectivity appropriate for confidential telehealth services.

Must reside in and be authorized to work in the United States and meet applicable licensing, credentialing, payer enrollment and pre-employment requirements.
