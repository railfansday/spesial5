‘I’m a Celebrity’ Tensions Between AJ Odudu and Mariella Frostrup Prompt ITV to Issue Statement: ‘We Take the Welfare of the Celebrities Very Seriously’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

ITV has responded to backlash over a heated exchange between AJ Odudu and Mariella Frostrup on “I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here!” which sparked accusations of racial microaggressions on social media.

In a statement, an ITV spokesperson said “The production monitors everyone in camp 24/7 and if at any time we feel someone has overstepped the line by using inappropriate language or displaying unacceptable behaviour, we would take steps to intervene and speak to everyone involved.” Related Stories Pink Announces 2027 Sphere Residency in Las Vegas

“The duty of care for our contestants is of the utmost importance to us. We take the welfare of the celebrities very seriously and have measures in place to ensure they are supported at every stage of the process: before, during and after filming,” the statement continued.

The incident, which aired on Sunday evening, saw Odudu, the host of “Big Brother,” clashing with fellow contestant Frostrup over cooking duties in the camp.

During the clash, Frostrup accused Odudu of being “aggressive” and creating a “drama out of a crisis.” Odudu pushed back, telling Frostrup ”dramatic’ and ‘aggressive’ are very harsh words,” she said.

Although Frostrup later apologized, the feud sparked criticism on social media, with several British television personalities accusing Frostrup of using language associated with racial microaggressions.

The show faced similar controversy in 2023 when Black British presenter Nella Rose clashed with fellow contestant Fred Sirieix after he joked that he was old enough to be her father. Rose, whose father had died, took offense at the remark. The clash prompted 861 complaints to U.K. broadcasting regulator Ofcom.
