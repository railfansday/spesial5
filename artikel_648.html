The National Dog Show happens on Thursday (Nov. 26) at Greater Philadelphia Expo Center & Fairgrounds in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, with a start time of 12 p.m. ET/9 a.m. PT.

The National Dog Show broadcasts on NBC, while it’s available to stream on Peacock.

Singer Kendal Conrad is set to perform the American national anthem, “The Star-Spangled Banner.”

For cord-cutters, there are a few ways to watch The National Dog Show if you don’t have cable, especially if you want to watch for free. DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services, such as Fubo and Hulu + Live TV, offer free trials, so you can watch for free.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including NBC, at $49.99 per month for the first two months of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day trial to watch for free, if you sign up now.

Sling Blue comes with 40+ broadcast and cable channels.

Sling TV offers the Blue plan, which comes with NBC. With more than 40 channels, the streaming plan goes for $54.99 per month. All plans come with a free cloud DVR and an extra 600 or so free channels, too. Please note: Pricing and channel availability vary from market-to-market.

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 230 channels, including local and cable networks, like NBC, that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and on web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free if you act fast and sign up now.

Comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select.

Hulu + Live TV might be best for those who want all of these streaming services together in one package.

There are two different plans offered on Peacock: The Premium Plan is on sale for $8.99 per month for the first three months of service (was $12.99 per month), or the Premium Plus Plan for $19.99 per month. If you’re looking to save even more, the annual plan for $89.99 per year (was $129.99 per year), or $199.99 per year, respectively. With the Premium Plan, it’s ad-supported and you’ll receive access to a wide range of movies, TV shows, originals, live sports from NBC Sports and live news from NBC News.

Peacock’s Premium Plus plan comes with everything in the Premium plan, no ads, your local NBC channel live and the ability to download and stream offline.
