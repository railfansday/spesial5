Sixers Bell Ringer: Philly continues crunch-time magic with win over Orlando

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5VJ Edgecombe – 3Joel Embiid – 2LeBron James – 1Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

Playing without Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Dean Wade, the Sixers looked to be on their way in the early going to one of those losses you just chalk up to being shorthanded on the road, trailing Orlando, 40-27, after one quarter. However, they kept chipping away and, at long last, retook the lead midway through the fourth quarter at 107-106. Then, as this Philadelphia 76ers team has done every single time thus far this season in clutch time, they handled their business to pull out a close 121-116 win. The Sixers are now 13-3 on the year with the last-place Bucks coming for a visit to Philadelphia on Saturday. Let's talk Bell Ringer.

VJ Edgecombe: 24 points, 8 rebounds, 6 assists, 2 steals, 4 turnovers

While the "Edgecombe's development is being hurt" narrative is nonsense, I do understand the desire to play out an alternate timeline where VJ gets his usage ramped up in his sophomore season. We caught a glimpse of it Wednesday night with Maxey and Brown out and it sure was fun to watch. Edgecombe had the ball in his hands a lot more often and did a group job creating shots for both himself and his teammates. VJ nailed a number of mid-range jumpers out of the pick-and-roll and had a nice night from behind the arc, shooting 4-of-9 from three. He made some huge plays in the fourth quarter to get the Sixers over the top. It was Edgecombe driving to draw the defense and kick out for a Jabari Walker three that finally put Philly back ahead. Then, with the Sixers trailing again inside of two minutes and the shot clock about to expire, it was VJ who hit a contested three to put his team back in front for good.

LeBron James: 22 points, 3 rebounds, 6 assists, 1 turnover

It's nice when your top two leading scorers are out to be able to turn the man who has scored more points than anyone in the history of the sport and ask him to do a little more bit in that department tonight. LeBron tied his Sixers season-high with 22 points, turning up the gas in the second quarter to get the Sixers back into the game with nine points and two assists in the frame. Later with the game on the line in the final minute, Philadelphia handed the keys of the offense over to LeBron, and King James responded by hitting an open jumper to make it a two-possession game, and then drawing multiple defenders to find a wide-open Anfernee Simons for the dagger three to close it out.

Joel Embiid: 26 points, 6 rebounds, 7 assists, 2 steals, 3 blocks, 3 turnovers

It was a slow start for Joel on the offensive end of the court, but he stuck with it and eventually wore the Magic down on the interior. The Sixers played a little more inside-out than usual with Maxey and Brown out, and Joel splayed it out to his teammates for some nice set-ups. He earned plenty of trips to the foul line, going 12-of-14 from the charity stripe, and worked some productive two-man game as a screener with Edgecombe. Embiid was also excellent on the defensive end, looking spry and mobile in navigating screens and defending shots in the paint.

Tacko Fall: 2 points, 3 rebounds, 3 blocks, 1 turnover

A number of other players played pivotal roles tonight, including Simons and Labaron Philon, but I have to give a quick shout out to Tacko in his first real minutes as a Sixer. Nick Nurse finally gave the 7-foot-6 big man a shot and Fall made a legitimately positive impact on the game. Whenever he entered the game, the Sixers dropped back into a 2-3 zone with Tacko manning the center of the back line and the Magic didn't know what to do. Tacko collected three blocks and deterred a handful of other would-be scorers who entered the lane. It was a very effective change-up strategy. The Sixers' backup center was a plus-seven, while Embiid was a minus-one in a win. What a world.
