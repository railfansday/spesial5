Saints rookie WR Jordyn Tyson plans to make debut vs. Browns after missing first 7 games with hamstring injury: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The delayed debut of New Orleans Saints rookie wide receiver Jordyn Tyson is reportedly right around the corner.

Tyson, this year's No. 8 overall pick who missed the Saints' first seven games with a right hamstring injury, plans to play Sunday against the Cleveland Browns, according to NFL insider Jordan Schultz.

The former Arizona State standout "should be good to go," Schultz reported Saturday.

Tyson's latest hamstring setback occurred in August during a training camp practice. He left that joint session with the Jacksonville Jaguars early once his run after the catch ended with an awkward fall near the sideline.

Saints head coach Kellen Moore told reporters that Tyson's foot slipped out from under him as he was hit from behind. Moore clarified that the issue wasn't related to the receiver's injury track record.

"I think my understanding of this one, any player that was put in his situation where your foot slips and you get shoved from behind, this has nothing to do with Jordyn's history," Moore said at the time, per ESPN.

"Any player in that situation probably has the same diagnosis coming out of this."

Notably, Tyson missed four games last season at Arizona State with multiple hamstring problems, and his injury obstacles affected his pre-draft process.

Still, the Saints selected him in the top 10.

Tyson began his college career at Colorado, where he teased his tantalizing potential by averaging 21.4 yards per reception and housing a punt return as a freshman in 2022.

Funny enough, that special teams score came against his future school, Arizona State, on a day when he also reeled in five passes for 115 yards and another TD.

In 2024, Tyson broke out with the Sun Devils, helping them reach the College Football Playoff and finished with 75 catches, 1,101 receiving yards and 10 touchdowns. Even during a shortened 2025 campaign, he totaled nine TDs, with one of them arriving on the ground.

Staying on the field has been a challenge for the 6-foot-2, 203-pound Tyson. Down the stretch of his lone go-around at Colorado, he suffered a torn ACL, MCL and PCL. He missed the end of a memorable 2024 season with the Sun Devils due to a broken collarbone that sidelined him for the Big 12 title game and the CFP.

But he's thought to have WR1 upside, given his ball skills — including his knack for making contested catches — and his ability to play every receiver spot on the field with impressive quickness.

At the moment, the 2-5 Saints are tied for third in the league with 39 pass attempts per game. Without a reliable run game, QB Tyler Shough is having to chuck the ball around the yard.

Having Tyson at his disposal should help.
