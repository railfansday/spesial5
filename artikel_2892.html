Myles Garrett, Terrance Ferguson to practice Wednesday, while Rams CB Jaylen Watson will have season-ending surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay provided injury updates for his team on Wednesday.

The updates were highlighted by both defensive end Myles Garrett and tight end Terrance Ferguson practicing in a limited capacity. McVay also told reporters that cornerback Jaylen Watson will have season-ending shoulder surgery.

Garrett has been sidelined since Week 2 with a knee injury that required arthroscopic surgery and landed him on injured reserve. The defensive end had to sit out a minimum of four games on IR, but he has missed the past seven games.

The injury's timing hurt even more for fans excited to see Garrett paired with the recently unretired Aaron Donald. As fate would have it, Donald wasn't ready to make his season debut until Week 2, so after Garrett was added to IR, Donald returned from 2.5 years of retirement.

Garrett and Donald could finally debut together this week against the Washington Commanders, depending on how Garrett's first week back at practice goes.

Ferguson has also been on injured reserve because of an ankle injury. He hasn't played since Week 3 and is one of several offensive skill-position injuries Los Angeles has been dealing with.

The Rams have a deep tight end room, but Ferguson caught seven passes for 63 yards and a touchdown before getting hurt.

While McVay announced two players are close to returning to game action, he also said the Rams will be without one player for the remainder of the season.

Watson will have season-ending shoulder surgery after getting multiple medical opinions. The cornerback has played in seven games this season and has 24 total tackles. He is one of only four Los Angeles players with an interception this season, logging his lone pick against the Buffalo Bills.

Los Angeles heads into this weekend's road game at Washington with a 4-4 record. The Rams entered the season as this year's Super Bowl favorite, but currently sit third in the NFC West.
