NWSL legend Jess Fishlock calls it a career after 24 years in emotional farewell

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Wales icon Jess Fishlock has brought the curtain down on her remarkable playing career after 24 extraordinary years.

The 39-year-old played her final match for Seattle Reign over the weekend, a 1-0 loss to Gotham FC in the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL) play-off quarter-finals.

The midfielder was visibly moved as she walked off the field after being substituted on in the 74th minute, with her teammates equally struggling to contain their emotions when she delivered a farewell speech following the game.

Jess Fishlock's team-mates were in tears as she said goodbye

"No tears. I've had a great career," she told them via Wales Online. "I've loved it."

Fishlock will now join Rhian Wilkinson's coaching staff for the upcoming FIFA Women's World Cup play-off against Norway in December, having previously retired from international football in October 2025.

She did so having netted 48 goals in 166 appearances, both of which stand as records for any player in either the men's or women's game.

Fishlock began her career with Cardiff City back in 2002 and went on to compete in eight different countries. She also collected UEFA Women's Champions League winners' medals with FFC Frankfurt and Olympique Lyonnais.

Having additionally won three NWSL Shields throughout a 14-season tenure with Seattle, she announced back in April that this season would mark her last.

"I've loved it," an emotional Fishlock told the BBC, as she wiped away tears after losing to Gotham on Sunday. "And the truth is I've loved that my last year was with this group, because they have just given me so much, and not just on the field.

"I look back and there's just so much to think about, not only with Reign but with Wales," she added.

"It would have to be one of those panorama snapshots because it's too difficult.

"All I know is what I'm most proud of deeply in my soul is that... I've stayed with Wales - that was difficult for 20 years, wouldn't do it any other way - stayed with the Reign for 14 years, wouldn't do it any other way.

"Because for me, when you win with your people - with the people that you've struggled with, the people that you've lost with, the people that you've failed with - when you win with your people, there is no better feeling in the world."
