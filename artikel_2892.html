What the numbers say about Cowboys vs Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Every week, we'll examine the numbers behind the next opponent on the schedule. Looking at the recent numbers and trends for each team, we'll add it all up to see which squad may have the edge. This week, the 4-6 Dallas Cowboys will look to gain ground in the NFC East, going up against the struggling 1-8 Tennessee Titans at home.

Dak Prescott threw for 343 yards and an absurd five touchdowns in last week's loss against the 49ers. That's not a stat you'll see too often in the NFL. This tied Prescott's career high for touchdowns in a single game.

George Pickens blew up after missing some time with injury, recording 145 yards through the air and three touchdown receptions. CeeDee Lamb and Ryan Flournoy posted similar stat lines to one another, each over 70 receiving yards and a touchdown.

The defense had a terrible day, yet still managed to sack Brock Purdy five times. The 49ers quarterback had only been sacked eight times on the year before that game.

As a team, Dallas finished with 439 total yards, 27 first downs and an 80% fourth-down conversion rate on five attempts. Despite these fantastic numbers, Dallas fell to the 49ers 46-40.

Cam Ward threw for 231 yards and two touchdowns in the Titans 40-23 loss to the Jaguars.

Tony Pollard continues to be a dual-threat at running back, racking up eight receptions for 66 yards and one touchdown while also finishing with 33 rushing yards on nine carries.

The Titans defense struggled on the day against Trevor Lawrence, allowing 352 passing yards and three touchdowns from the Jaguars quarterback. The Jaguars would finish with 459 total yards on the day.

The Titans have one primary weapon. He used to wear the Cowboys star.

Tony Pollard is the only threat that the Cowboys opponent has on offense. Stop him and you should easily take this win home. Allow him to beat you in the flats on short passes or to gut you for big gains up the middle on the ground and this will be another embarrassing performance by the Cowboys.

If the defense was ever going to make a stand and say enough is enough, this is the week. The Titans lack talent everywhere on both sides of the ball. There is no reason that the defense shouldn't be able to tear the Titans up unless they make mental mistakes, refuse to tackle, and forget the foundations of football.

Unfortunately we've seen each of those items week after week. Will the be the game that turns things around for the defense, especially with the new play caller, Mike Pettine? If so, they'll need to prevent Tony Pollard from wrecking the game.

Is Bill Murray here? Because this feels like Groundhog Day. Another week against a bad team where Dallas heavily outmatches them on paper. Yet we've seen the Cowboys take a lighter to the paper and burn it to a crisp this season, performing to the level of their competition, or worse, time after time.

This team will go toe-to-toe with one of the best teams in the league in the 49ers, putting up 40 points in a slugfest. But also they'll completely shut down and score just 13 points against a bad Packers defense. Or they'll allow 33 points to a terrible Cardinals offense.

Perhaps switching defensive play-callers will play in the Cowboys favor this week in a matchup against a young, inexperienced quarterback in Cam Ward. Over the last five weeks, Dallas ranks second in the NFL in sacks. They'll need to continue that trend and pressure the Titans quarterback throughout the game, forcing him to make errors. Disguises are the name of the game on defense when going up against young passers. It's not different this week.

Dallas should be able to win this game against the Titans without too much trouble. Unfortunately for us, "should" and "reality" have lived in vastly different universes this year. If there is one last hope for a stress free Sunday for Cowboys fans this season, this would be it. Let's hope Dallas makes it so.
