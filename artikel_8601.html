Jimmy, in theatres now. Biopic of Jimmy Stewart, concentrating on his WW2 service as a pilot and ending with his role in "It's a Wonderful Life." Stewart was profoundly affected by his experience, and it took him a while to get back on his feet. He even had a bit of PTSD while filming the movie. The actor KJ Apa nails Stewart's unique way of speaking and his gangly appearance.

There's a fun cameo in the movie by 86-year-old Karolyn Grimes. Who, you ask? She was six years old when she played little ZuZu Bailey in "It's a Wonderful Life."
