Anyone buying a home should have a real estate attorney. The P&S should have a clause that it will be reviewed by said attorney within X days and that it can be cancelled within that period for any reason.

Myself, I would never waive inspections. Never. You can waive the seller making adjustments, but never waive the inspections.

A relative bid on a house recently, well failed inspection, contaminated. They walked.
In central NJ (in the RE market environment that exists now and has been the case for the last several years) it is customary to waive inspections - other than structural and environmental. It would be highly unusual to waive those as you cannot assess those risks without a thorough inspection. I would go one step further and say because of the hot market, anyone not waiving waiving inspections (other than structural and environmental) AND appraisal contingencies will have their offer tossed and won't be considered by the seller. Oh, and you better be bidding considerably over asking or you are out too. Yeah, the market is that nuts. I know these things as my daughter and SIL put bids in on over two dozen homes over a two year period and not one time did they make it past the initial bidding. In every case they were told "thanks but not thanks" - even with offers over asking. That was until we backstopped them with an all cash offer. That worked. Offer was made waiving inspections other than structural and environmental.
