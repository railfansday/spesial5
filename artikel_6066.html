The Selective Persecution of Nathan Cofnas

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

What happened to free expression in academia?

A Ku Klux Klan hood placed by activist group Led By Donkeys covers the Cecil Rhodes statue at Oriel College in Oxford, Britain, November 12, 2026, ahead of a scheduled talk by U.S. academic Nathan Cofnas (inset).

Toby Shepheard/Reuters, The Winston Marshall Show/YouTube

T he organization Led By Donkeys pulled off a stunt at Oxford University: Two activists dressed as construction workers used a cherry-picker truck to place a Ku Klux Klan hood over the head of the Cecil Rhodes statue at Oriel College around 5 a.m. on Thursday, and they placed a sign saying “RACISTS WELCOME HERE” in its hands. Why? “Next week, this college will host a talk by the white supremacist Nathan Cofnas, who stated that in a fair meritocratic system, black people like me would ‘disappear from almost all high-profile positions outside of sport and entertainment,’” Kehinde Andrews said . Andrews is the first professor of black studies in the United Kingdom and author of, among other books, The Psychosis of Whiteness.

Cofnas is no stranger to controversy. He’s currently under fire for previous writings about IQ differences across racial groups and for exposing the extensive academic misconduct committed by Cambridge professor Jason Arday, who was found dead amid the ensuing controversy. “It was a media lynching,” Andrews claimed. The organization repeatedly suggested on social media that, by hosting Cofnas, the college is “staying true to form” because “it continues to display this statue of Cecil Rhodes, the notorious imperialist who created brutally racist colonies in southern Africa.”

Abigail Anthony is a staff writer at National Review.
