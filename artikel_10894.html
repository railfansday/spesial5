Free Christian GTA 6 parody game released where the devil hunts you instead of cops

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A free Christian parody of Grand Theft Auto is launching alongside GTA 6, and getting five stars will bring Satan after you instead of the cops.

With players finally jumping into Vice City, satire site The Babylon Bee is opening up a city of its own. The game is called Grand Faith Auto, and it takes place in New Mercy, a 3D open world you can explore right in your browser.

The setup will look familiar to anyone who’s played GTA, as there are cars to take, storefronts and billboards dotted around the city, and a radar to help you find your way.

But while the real thing often throws you into the criminal underworld, Grand Faith Auto is all about being an upstanding citizen.

Instead of the traditional Wanted level, doing good deeds earns you stars of divine approval. Get all five, and the devil himself comes looking for you instead of the cops.

One of the deeds you can do around New Mercy is pull a driver out of their car and preach to them before driving off in it yourself. You can also offer a “Lady of the Night” a lift to church, or try to feed the hungry in a scenario involving a closed chicken restaurant, a deli under attack, and a box of pocket Bibles.

There’s even a stealth mission where you sneak into a cartel warehouse and swap its drugs for little figurines. You’ll have to stay out of sight, because getting spotted by a guard is an instant fail.

You’re not completely unarmed, though, as you can wield a holy shotgun to fend off the imps tracking you down. A shot to the head stuns them, leaving you free to kick them back to hell. After that, the instructions tell you to go to church.
