We checked each vendor's public pricing and product pages in October 2026. Pin is covered first because it spans the widest scope; the others follow in no ranked order.

Pin (pin.com) is an AI recruiting platform that handles candidate sourcing, outreach and interview scheduling in one product. For teams replacing LinkedIn Recruiter, Pin is the most direct one-tool replacement.

Best for: teams and agencies replacing LinkedIn Recruiter that want broader search, outreach and scheduling in one seat-priced tool with published prices. See also AI recruiting tools for agencies.

SeekOut is a sourcing platform for finding and engaging candidates across 1B+ profiles and your ATS. Its pricing page lists Sourcing Core at $179 a month, or $149 billed annually, with three seats, AI multi-step campaigns and a 14-day free trial; higher tiers are custom. The page lists email outreach and no interview scheduling.

Good for: small sourcing teams that want deep search, though outreach is email only, scheduling isn't listed and higher tiers are custom.

hireEZ is a talent acquisition platform that adds sourcing, CRM, outreach, screening and scheduling on top of your ATS. It lists 1B+ profiles across 45+ platforms, email, SMS and InMail engagement, and an AI Scheduler. Team pricing isn't published, and solo recruiters start at $494 a month.

Good for: mid-size and enterprise teams consolidating tools around an existing ATS, though team pricing is unpublished and solo starts at $494 a month.

Gem is a recruiting platform that layers a CRM, AI sourcing, outreach, scheduling and analytics on top of your ATS. It searches 800M+ profiles and runs multi-channel sequences, though its pricing page doesn't name the channels. Scheduling is an add-on to Gem + your ATS. Pricing is custom by headcount; the only published number is $130 a month for startups with 1 to 10 employees on yearly billing.

Good for: growth-stage and larger teams that want CRM and scheduling in one stack, though pricing is sales-led and scheduling is an add-on.

Findem is an AI talent-intelligence platform. Its site lists Multichannel Sourcing, a Talent CRM and a Scheduling Agent, and says it maps 1B+ career paths. Pricing isn't published; you request a demo.

Good for: larger talent teams that want attribute-rich search, though buying is demo-only and no price is published.

Fetcher is a sourcing platform that pairs a 500M+ candidate database with optional done-for-you sourcing. Its pricing page lists a custom Enterprise tier, Amplify at $649 a month on annual billing ($849 monthly), Growth at $379 ($499 monthly) and Self-serve at $115. It lists email outreach and a LinkedIn sourcing plugin, and no scheduling.

Good for: small teams that want extra sourcing capacity without building a process first, though scheduling isn't listed and the $115 entry tier jumps to $379.

"Not listed" means absent from the vendor's pricing page, not necessarily from the product.
