Episode 8: Best Ground Covers for Pollinators

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On this episode of Super Cool Plants, we’re getting down and dirty to explore ground covers that do more than fill in the gaps. It’s true that the right low-growing plants can add texture and beauty to the bottom layer of your garden, but did you know that many creepers also provide valuable food and habitat for pollinators?

In fact, some of the earliest blooming plants for pollinators are those spring ground covers that many of us push on past at the nursery. From flowering carpets buzzing with bees to tough, adaptable plants that thrive where turfgrass struggles, we’ll dig into some super cool options for creating a living ground cover that supports pollinators and brings your garden to life.
