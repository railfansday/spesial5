Real REMAX Group confirms additional layoffs after merger

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Real REMAX Group confirmed additional organizational changes after its REMAX acquisition. Executives previously disclosed a 14% headcount reduction and more than $40 million in annualized gross cost savings tied to integration efforts.  AI Summary

After a merger or acquisition closes, companies almost always look for cost synergies. These efforts frequently result in layoffs, which they did at the recently formed Real REMAX Group, a RealTrends Verified firm.

During the company’s third-quarter 2026 earnings call with investors and analysts, which occurred a little over two months after The Real Brokerage closed its acquisition of REMAX, executives said the company reduced its combined headcount by more than 14%, eliminating 150-plus positions across the combined organization.

This came as the company said it found more than $40 million in annualized gross cost savings and expects to realize more than $30 million in adjusted EBITDA savings in 2027, which could increase to more than $40 million in 2028.

At the time of the earnings call, executives said the initial phase of headcount restructuring was largely complete but that they were still looking for ways to simplify operations and improve efficiencies.

But based on a recent slew of posts on LinkedIn, it appears that headcount reductions are not over just yet. Over the past week, several individuals who claim to be former REMAX employees shared posts stating that they’d been impacted by a recent round of layoffs at the company.

A spokesperson for Real REMAX Group told HousingWire via email that following the acquisition, “Real REMAX Group is continuing to evaluate opportunities that streamline operations, create efficiencies and better integrate the two organizations.”

“As part of this ongoing process, we can confirm that additional organizational changes have taken place at the company,” the spokesperson wrote. “We will continue to assess our business needs to ensure we have the right structure and resources in place to support our long-term strategic priorities and better serve real estate professionals.”

The spokesperson declined to share details regarding the size of the layoffs, which departments were impacted or if the layoffs were performance related.

Real REMAX Group is not alone in laying off employees as part of an attempt to rightsize after a merger.

Earlier this year, Compass International Holdings announced it was laying off 110 employees after it closed its acquisition of Anywhere Real Estate. Compass has estimated $500 million in total net cost synergies over the next three years.
