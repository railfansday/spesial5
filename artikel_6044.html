There's no doubt about it: The airport lounge wars are heating up with credit card issuers like American Express, Capital One and Chase launching new outposts at a blistering pace.

American Express maintains the largest footprint with its Global Lounge Collection, while Capital One has been expanding its map of Capital One airport lounges.

However, it's Chase in particular that has turned up the heat, expanding its U.S. lounge collection since 2023, with more on the way, including a location at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

Here’s everything you need to know about the family of Chase Sapphire Lounges by The Club, their amenities and how to access them.

Chase’s lounge collection includes nine Sapphire Lounge locations. The JFK location is temporarily closed.

Additional lounges are planned at the following airports:

Chase has partnered with Airport Dimensions to build out its lounges. Airport Dimensions currently operates dozens of lounges worldwide, most of which are branded as “The Club” or “Club Aspire” and are largely accessible via Priority Pass membership.

This location was the first branded Chase lounge to open in the U.S.

It's located in the Terminal B-to-C connector near gates B39 and B40 and is accessible post-security from Terminals B, C and E. Passengers departing from Terminal A will need to clear security again when returning to their departure terminal.

The lounge has an expansive footprint, at over 11,000 square feet with seating for over 200 guests, though lines to get in can certainly form at busy times, with waits topping an hour. It is open from 5 a.m. until 11 p.m. daily.

In addition to a variety of restaurant- and living room-style seating areas, there is a self-serve buffet at one end. Guests can also order a la carte dishes created by Boston-area chef Douglass Williams as well as specialty cocktails and Massachusetts-brewed beers from table-mounted QR codes.

The lounge also has wellness rooms with massage chairs and comfortable loungers for relaxation and products from Brooklyn-based fragrance brand Apotheke.

The newest Chase Sapphire Lounge opened at Dallas Fort Worth International Airport in July 2026. Located post-security near gate D25 in Terminal D, the 18,000-square-foot lounge is one of the largest in the Sapphire network. It is open from 5 a.m. until 10 p.m. daily.

In addition to Chase's signature made-to-order dining (with dishes like blue corn butterscotch pancakes from favorite local restaurant Encina), an all-day buffet and full-service bar, the DFW location introduces several features unique to the network. Travelers can visit the first dedicated Sapphire whiskey lounge featuring guided tastings of Texas whiskeys (and a specialty honey pecan Old Fashioned cocktail), book a complimentary 30-minute Face Haus treatment, freshen up in a shower suite or relax in dedicated rest areas.

Families will also appreciate the children's play area and private nursing room, while travelers in a hurry can take advantage of a grab-and-go food station — a feature Chase is currently testing at this location.

Chase and Airport Dimensions revamped The Etihad Lounge at Dulles, located in Concourse A near gate A14.

The space is small, at just over 5,000 square feet, but features two levels with various seating areas, including a library-themed space, gender-neutral restrooms, shower suites, a reflection room for quiet meditation and a family room.

As with the other lounge locations, this one features locally-inspired food and cocktails plus locally-brewed java from Commonwealth Joe Coffee Roasters.

The lounge is currently open from 6 a.m. until 10 p.m. daily.

Chase's lounge at LAS is located past security in Terminal 1, Concourse C, between gates 23 and 24. It opened in December 2025, spans two stories, covers 4,590 square feet and has seating for up to 87 people. It is open from 4:30 a.m. until 11 p.m. daily.

Though compact compared to the other Chase Sapphire lounges, this one packs a punch in terms of ambience. The space features colorful artwork and a lively atmosphere, not to mention a rather glamorous "Champagne parlor" plus a menu of cocktails inspired by Sin City and a wine list curated by New York retailer Parcelle.

Food options include bites like pork buns and a spicy cucumber salad from Momofuku.

There are no showers, spa services or meditation rooms here, though.

The issuer is bringing a lounge to LAX on an unconfirmed date. It will be adjacent to gate 148 near the connector to the west gates at Tom Bradley International Terminal.

Now that all terminals at LAX are connected airside (post-security), any traveler with access could theoretically visit this lounge, although it is a bit of a hike from other terminals.

The new lounge will measure around 9,000 square feet, which is on the smaller side — especially for an airport the size of LAX — so expect some long wait times once it opens, much as you'll find at the nearby Centurion Lounge.

The Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club at JFK is located in Terminal 4 near gate A2, not far from the security checkpoint and up one level.

Like the location at IAD, this space is a redesigned Etihad Lounge rather than one Chase created from scratch, and it's rather limited in size at just 7,600 square feet.

It is usually open from 4:30 a.m. until 9 p.m. daily, but at the time of publication, it is temporarily closed due to plumbing issues. There are grab-and-go food options, though.

Typically, you can find a buffet of small plates, plus seasonal items (but always the famous burger) available for order via QR code. This lounge also has three shower suites.

The LaGuardia Sapphire Lounge is the largest of the collection, at 21,000 square feet spanning two floors. It is located in Terminal B and is open from 4:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. daily.

The lounge houses plenty of spacious seating areas, including a restaurant-style section and more casual vignettes. There are also family-oriented areas, including a children's playroom and arcade.

The lounge offers a buffet and a seasonal menu featuring select dishes from Sailor, a restaurant in Fort Greene, Brooklyn, plus locally inspired cocktails by Apotheke and wines chosen by Parcelle.

There's also a wellness area where guests can enjoy Face Haus facial treatments, guided meditations from podcaster Devi Brown and private rest pods.

Uniquely, the lounge features Reserve Suites, Chase’s lounge-within-a-lounge concept, which you can reserve for $2,200-$3,000 for a three-hour private retreat.

This 20,000-square-foot Chase lounge opened in February 2025 in the connector between Terminal D (home of Air Canada, Delta Air Lines, Spirit Airlines, Alaska Airlines and United Airlines) and Terminal E (where gates for Frontier Airlines, JetBlue, Alaska Airlines, Southwest Airlines and Spirit Airlines can be found).

If you go through the Terminal D Transportation Security Administration checkpoint, it is upstairs and is open from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

It offers a similar layout to other Chase lounges, but with some unique features including a wellness center with two treatment rooms for facials, two shower suites, a game room, Elixr Coffee drinks and a bar with Philadelphia Eagles-themed cocktails, plus a beer garden and local dishes courtesy of Fishtown eatery Middle Child Clubhouse.

The Sapphire Lounge at PHX opened in November 2024 and is located at the new eight-gate facility in Terminal 4 near gate D11.

This lounge began accepting reservations in June 2025. Sapphire Reserve cardmembers departing from Phoenix can reserve their visit from 14 days up to 24 hours before arriving at the lounge, so you don't have to be physically present to make your reservation. That's because this is the smallest lounge, at just 3,500 square feet.

The lounge is open from 4:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. daily, though its site notes that hours may vary.

There are a few different seating areas, with the tables around the central cocktail bar being in high demand. There's also an indoor terrace separated from the terminal, where a retrofitted Airstream food truck serves specialties from Gallo Blanco. Guests will also find Arizona-based Press Coffee drinks on hand, craft cocktails and a Parcelle-selected list of wines.

Located in San Diego's Terminal 2, this 11,000-square-foot Chase lounge opened in December 2024 with plenty of surfer-themed art and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the tarmac.

The lounge is open from 5 a.m. until 10 p.m. daily and has a swanky wraparound cocktail bar, a small buffet area (plus à la carte dishes by Oscars Mexican Seafood on offer via QR code) and plenty of distinct seating areas.

There's also a wellness area with four relaxation pods and Face Haus treatments, a family room, showers and a nursing room.

Chase is planning a new 16,000-square-foot Sapphire Lounge in Concourse A at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport. The Port of Seattle approved the project in October 2026, with a planned $70 million investment covering the initial build-out and a future refurbishment. An opening date has not yet been announced.

Select Etihad travelers (the airline’s Gold and Platinum elite members and those flying in business or first class on Etihad-operated flights) can visit the IAD and JFK locations before their flights.

For years, Amex's network of Centurion Lounges was the major player in the card-run lounge space, but in recent years, Chase has quickly caught up, opening a series of lounges around the country in record time, with more on the way. With the airport lounge competition intensifying among major credit card issuers, the real winners are cardholders, though.

That said, these lounges feel like they're getting more crowded by the day, so when you get to an airport with one, make a beeline to the entrance and put your name on the list for access just in case there's a wait. After all, you wouldn't want to miss out on their many amenities.
