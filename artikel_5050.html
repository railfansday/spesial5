Bitcoin Price Hit $55,130 Two Days After Anthropic’s IPO: Is The Bottom In?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bitcoin news: BTC hit $55,130 two days after Anthropic’s IPO. After SpaceX, two daily closes confirmed the bottom. Here’s what to watch.

In Bitcoin news this weekend, BTC fell to $55,130 early on Saturday, November 21, two days after Anthropic’s Nasdaq listing. That was its lowest price since September 2024, and it came as US spot Bitcoin ETFs, funds that hold actual bitcoin, posted a fourth straight day of outflows.

The drop did not last. Bitcoin closed Saturday back above $60,000 and trades near $61,466 on Monday, 11% above the low.

We flagged this setup before the listing. After SpaceX went public in June, Bitcoin kept falling for 19 days before it found a bottom, and we expected the Anthropic listing to follow a similar path. It did, only faster: the low came on day two, and it was deeper.

So is $55,130 the low, and which daily close would confirm that Bitcoin has turned?

The selling in this week’s Bitcoin news started with the funds. Figures from SoSoValue’s ETF dashboard show US spot Bitcoin ETFs losing $669 million on Friday, November 20. BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) accounted for $545 million of it.

That followed a $582 million outflow on Thursday, when IBIT lost a record $541 million. Over the five trading days from November 16 to 20, the funds lost about $1.79 billion by our count of the daily figures.

Morgan Stanley’s MSBT is the exception. It took in $49.6 million on Thursday and $2.3 million on Friday. The fund launched in April with a 0.14% fee, the lowest of any Bitcoin fund at the time.

The low itself came on a Saturday, when ETFs do not trade. Bitcoin fell from about $58,950 to $55,130 in the early hours, then closed the day higher than it opened.

To see what a confirmed bottom looked like last time, we lined up Bitcoin’s daily candles from each listing day on Binance.

After SpaceX’s June 12 listing, Bitcoin bottomed at $57,800 on July 1, 9.1% below its listing-day close. This time it fell 11.1% below the listing-day close of $61,987, and it got there in two days instead of 19.
