Sheffield United need to play 22-year-old next season when he returns to Bramall Lane: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sheffield United let the attacker head out the exit door on a temporary basis again in the summer to help him get some more experience under his belt. He has since flourished away from SUFC and they will be pleased with his development in League Two.

Marsh, 22, has enjoyed plenty of game time with the Spirerites under the guidance of their head coach Paul Cook and he has become one of their key players over recent times. He has made 16 appearances in all competitions in this campaign, 12 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with an eye-catching eight goals and three assists to date.

He is due to return to the Blades next year and he remains under contract with the South Yorkshire outfit until June 2028 so still just under two years left to run down. Chris Wilder should have him in his long-term plans after his impressive campaign to date.

Sheffield United are aiming to get into the Championship play-offs this term. They are sat in 10th place and are two points off the top eight behind both QPR and Southampton after 15 fixtures.

Sheffield United will see Marsh as one for the future. He is versatile and can play as a centre-forward or as an attacking midfielder which means he will be a useful weapon for SUFC down the line.

At the age of 22, he has bags of potential and the capacity to improve over the coming years. The Blades will be keeping tabs on his development at Chesterfield and will be satisfied with how he is doing.

The ex-England youth international, who played for the Three Lions’ U20s side back in 2024, has risen up through the academy at Bramall Lane. He was a regular at various different youth levels.

Marsh has played five times for Sheffield United’s senior team to date. Prior to his switch to Chesterfield, he was loaned out to Doncaster Rovers, Fleetwood Town and Falkirk to get some exposure to men’s football.

Sheffield United have an option to extend Patrick Bamford‘s stay until 2028. However, there are question marks over the long-term futures of Tyrese Campbell, Tom Cannon and Jordan Ayes, whilst Romelle Donovan and Ben Brereton Diaz will return to Brentford and Southampton respectively next year.

Therefore, the Blades will be short of attacking options by next term. That means that Marsh has an ideal opportunity to stake a claim when he is back from Chesterfield.
