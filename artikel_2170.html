Highly-rated Reading youngster agrees out-of-window loan move

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Reading youngster Tyler Sackey has featured only in cup matches for the club over the past three seasons. His three senior appearances this term have come in the EFL Trophy, and the 20-year-old has impressed in his outings.

Aside from his EFL Trophy appearances, Sackey has played for the U21s in Premier League 2, operating from central midfield. He impressed last term, and now looks set for a chance to prove his abilities on the senior stage away from Leam Richardson‘s squad.

Pete O’Rourke states that Reading have agreed to send Sackney on loan to National League side Aldershot Town. It will give him a chance to play frequent first-team football for the first time in his promising career.

Sackey signed a new contract at the Berkshire outfit in the summer, keeping him tied down until 2029.

On Sackey’s senior debut, he scored in a 3-1 EFL Trophy win against West Ham United U21s. He also scored against Walsall in the same competition in the 2024/25 season.

The Reading prospect has recent experience against National League sides, including scoring against Woking in a pre-season friendly with a composed left-footed finish this summer.

Sackey offers a creative presence in central midfield and further forward as a No. 10. He has made 158 successful passes in the Premier League 2 this term while winning 59.5% of his duels and having 270 touches. Combined with 27 defensive contributions, it shows the Royals youngster’s well-rounded game and his work rate out of possession in winning the ball back and showing to receive the ball.

This will be Sackey’s first chance at regular senior minutes, so he needs to be given time to adjust to the physical demands of the game and settle into his role in the team and dressing room at Aldershot.

Reading aren’t afraid to trust youthful creative midfielders, so Sackey has the potential to pave his way into the first team.

Charlie Savage is a prime example of this, following a youth career with Manchester United and a brief loan with Forest Green Rovers. He quickly became a key influence, and Bristol City loanee Josh Stokes has been entrusted with a similar role, having provided three assists so far this season.

Sackey will hope to impress with Aldershot Town to soon replicate those performances for his parent club.
