This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who has been a software engineer, has shipped real agent systems in production, and has successfully built and managed engineering teams at a growing start-up or scale-up. The right person is energized by technical depth, operational excellence, and turning ambitious agent ideas into reliable, customer-facing systems.

Team leadership: Build, lead, and grow a high-performing team of engineers focused on AI agent systems. Hire, coach, and develop talent while establishing strong technical and cultural standards. Create an environment where strong engineers want to stay and grow.

Production agent systems: Drive the design, development, and continuous improvement of production agent capabilities including orchestration, tool-calling (including MCP and related protocols), evaluation frameworks, reliability, observability, and safe failure modes. Technical quality and judgment: Raise the bar on architecture, design quality, reliability, and service ownership for agent systems that operate in high-stakes, regulated environments.

Cross-functional impact: Partner with Forward-Deployed Engineers and customer teams to ship agents into customer environments and turn field feedback into platform improvements.

Operational excellence: Establish clear planning, execution, and incident processes that support predictable delivery without unnecessary overhead. Own reliability and post-incident learning for agent systems.

Talent and leadership development: Recruit senior individual contributors and emerging leaders. Create an environment where strong engineers want to stay and grow.

Scale and reliability: Ensure AI agent systems meet the performance, security, auditability, and reliability standards required by financial institutions that rely on our platform to process billions of risk decisions.
