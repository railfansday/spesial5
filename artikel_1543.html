Rabiot reveals belief Milan are ‘growing’ and commends Ramos: “Not everyone understands”

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Adrien Rabiot heaped praise on Goncalo Ramos for what he does for the team, but he also explained what AC Milan’s ‘problem’ is.

Milan put on a show for their fans at San Siro on Sunday evening as they beat Frosinone 5-1. Gabriele Calvani’s own goal put the home side ahead, before Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic all netted to make it a rout.

It is a third consecutive league victory after the wins over Inter and Genoa and it moves the Rossoneri on to 27 points, back inside the Champions League places. Up next: a trip to Athens to face Olympiacos on Thursday in the Europa League, and a chance to demonstrate consistency.

After the full-time whistle, Rabiot spoke alongside Ramos to both Sky and DAZN, offering his thoughts on what was a very successful evening in front of all the fans at San Siro. His words are via MilanNews.

Are you starting to build a good identity?

“Our problem is that we have to be focused for 90 minutes. We did well today, even though we conceded a bit at the end, but that’s normal. As I said, we’re growing as a team: the quality of our play, the team spirit, the sacrifice for 90 minutes, and everyone.

“Gonçalo is the first to do a lot. Today I saw he had a bit of pain in his thigh. I know he’s a very important player for us, and we have a lot of games: I even asked the coach to let him rest a bit. But everyone’s attitude was great today, and we have to keep it up.

“In these last few games, when we play like this, when we’re so focused and manage to do what we work towards in training, I think we’re all enjoying ourselves, even tonight. We created a lot, we scored a lot of goals. What we have to focus on is enjoying playing together.”

Why didn’t you shoot before the first goal?

“I wanted Pulisic to score, but the defender came back quickly. It’s true that I could have shot, but I get more pleasure from providing an assist than from scoring. But I did the assist afterwards.”

“He does a great job. Not everyone understands it, but he does an incredible job. When we get in and score, it’s also credit to us. Then we feel happy when he scores, also because it gives him confidence. Good for us and for him, for everyone.”
