Moretto: Endrick remains Roma’s top target for January

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Roma’s priority for the winter transfer window is Endrick.

The Brazilian has been a name that’s been circulating around the Giallorossi since last summer.

Transfer market expert Matteo Moretto took stock of the situation in an interview with the radio station.

Moretto reiterated that Endrick is Roma’s priority: the Giallorossi have confirmed to the player that, should he wish to leave Madrid, he is their top priority for January.

Real Madrid wants to loan him out, a deal that would benefit Roma, who would have no difficulty completing the deal.
