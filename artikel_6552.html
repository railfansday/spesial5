Pommes Duchesse

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This classic French side dish hides two potatoes in one: a crisp, elegantly swirled exterior enclosing a fluffy, nutmeg-spiced interior. Tender baked russets are mashed with butter and egg yolks and piped into mounds, then brushed with an egg wash and baked for color and crunch.
