Cacio E Pepe Mac &amp; Cheese

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Stovetop mac and cheese is the equivalent of childhood heaven. While I’ll never turn my head at the classic version, this cacio e pepe mac and cheese delivers the same comforting nostalgia, but with a grown-up upgrade. The best part? You most likely already have all of the ingredients on hand. Trust me—once you try this simple, elevated version, you’ll be putting your Kraft days behind you.

The components:All of the ingredients are relatively budget-friendly; the only item you’ll be spending a bit more on is good Pecorino Romano. To make it, you’ll need to toast the black pepper in some butter, then add the condensed milk and whole milk. Once the liquid comes to a simmer, add the American cheese and stir constantly until melted. Off the heat, add the Pecorino and shells and continue to cook for about a minute, just until the sauce tightens around the pasta. Once it coats every noodle perfectly, you’re ready to dig in.

Storage:It’s hard to believe there will be leftovers, but if you’re meal prepping for lunch, store portions in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in the microwave or on the stove with a little extra milk to loosen.
