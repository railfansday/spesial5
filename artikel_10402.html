Google Pixel held its ground in the US despite a middling Pixel 11 launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The latest quarterly data for the US smartphone market reveals that Google Pixel is holding its ground despite the overall market faltering, and the other two major Android brands struggling.

The top smartphone brands in the United States include Apple, Samsung, Motorola, and Google Pixel in that order. Apple continues to absolutely dominate at a whopping 57% market share in Q3 2026, up from 50% during the same period last year.

Samsung came in at 25%, up 1% from last year (likely driven by a very successful Fold 8 launch during Q3), while Motorola shrank from 12% to 8%.

Google Pixel made up 6%, down 1% from Q3 2025, Counterpoint Research shows. Q3 is important for Google as it includes the company’s new August release window for new flagships. It’s not surprising that Google dropped a percentage point year-over-year, as the company’s Pixel 11 launch was not particularly exciting, especially as prices rose over the previous generation. If anything, only dropping 1% must be pretty encouraging given the overall market conditions and RAMageddon of it all.

Something to note – Google Pixel is technically growing this year compared to last. Just out of view on this latest update, Q2 2026 saw Google’s share up 1% year-over-year, and Q1 was also up compared to the same period in 2025. Counterpoint previously mentioned that the Pixel brand “remains aggressive in postpaid” carriers, but notes that Pixel 11 shipments were “weaker YoY.”
