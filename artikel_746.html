P!nk Adds 2028 Dates to Sphere Residency Due to Huge Demand: ‘This Is Crazy’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In a video promoting the six-pack of fresh dates, the "Trustfall" singer said there were three million people queued up for tix.

P!nk added another six-pack of dates to her residency run at Sphere in Las Vegas on Wednesday (Nov. 18). In an Instagram post announcing the 2028 shows, the “Trustfall” singer said she was blown away by the intense thirst for tickets to the first headlining shows by a solo female pop artist at the high-tech, multi-sensory venue. “OK, this is crazy,” says P!nk in an accompanying video in which she hides her mouth under a blanket while chilling on a couch. “I’m nauseous! There’s three million people in line, on line … in line? On line? for the Sphere. I don’t know what to say. I think that’s the most people in the whole world and I’m gonna throw up … And … AHHHHHH!!!! And so that’s what she did, tacking on a handful of 2028 shows on Jan. 19, 21, 22, 26, 28 and 29. The fresh run will also be accompanied by a trio of newly announced gigs on Nov. 3, 5 and 6, 2027.

P!nk, who is no stranger to high-wire acts, posted a video earlier this week of her trip to the top of the Sphere to shoot some promo shots in not ideal circumstances. “I’m not a weatherman, but I was right about the wind,” the singer said in the caption to a post featuring footage of the early morning shoot buffeted by 44 m.p.h. winds and sub 50-degree temperatures. “Coldest, scariest, most fun morning of my life. I LOVED IT!!!” the thrill-seeking pop star enthused.

It will be P!nk’s first residency and she will make history as the first solo female pop star to join the long list of headliners Sphere has hosted to date, including: U2, Phish, Dead & Company, Anyma, Kenny Chesney, Chase & Status, the Zac Brown Band, Illenium, Metallica, Eagles, Backstreet Boys and Carín León. So far, No Doubt’s 18-date summer 2026 run at the venue is the only one to feature a female-fronted act.

P!nk’s 2027 Sphere residency dates: Sept. 29, Oct. 1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 27, 29 and 30, Nov. 3, 5, 6 and Jan. 18, 21, 22, 26, 28, 29, 2028.

The presale for the new shows starts on Thursday (Nov. 19) at 10 a.m. PT
