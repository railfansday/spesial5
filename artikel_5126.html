A ransomware group called The Gentlemen has claimed it breached Nvidia, adding the chipmaker to its dark web leak site with a “SOON” label and a countdown of about 10 days, according to cybersecurity news account International Cyber Digest. The listing does not yet include any stolen files, and there is no public evidence so far that Nvidia’s data has been taken. Nvidia has not confirmed any breach. The Gentlemen operates a ransomware-as-a-service model, renting its tools to other hackers, and says it has hit more than 1,000 victims across many countries and industries. Ransomware groups often use countdown timers to pressure companies into paying before data is released, and some claims later prove exaggerated or false. The report comes a day after Nvidia beat earnings forecasts in its first results since Jensen Huang’s resignation, and follows a week of security incidents involving TSMC employee data and a reported attack through web platform Optimizely.
