Nvidia interim CEO Colette Kress urged employees to look ahead at the company’s first all-staff meeting since Jensen Huang’s resignation, CNBC reported, citing an attendee. Kress told staff, “Let’s move forward,” and also brought up pay raises during the hour-long session, held at Nvidia’s Santa Clara headquarters and streamed on Microsoft Teams. She called the third quarter a great one, after Nvidia beat forecasts on Tuesday. Board chair Harvey Jones spoke about the search for a permanent CEO, alongside senior leaders including Ian Buck, who heads the hyperscale and high-performance computing business and is the top pick on Kalshi to become the next CEO. Co-founder Chris Malachowsky and engineering chief Jonah Alben also addressed staff. The meeting comes as some employees push for Huang’s return, with one senior employee reportedly warning the board it could lose irreplaceable talent. Others welcomed the greater visibility into leadership. The board has said it unanimously accepted Huang’s resignation and will not reinstate him.
