Mutsamudu, Comoros, November 27, 2026 – MEXC, a pioneer in 0-fee digital asset trading, announced the launch of the Golden Gratitude Gala. In appreciation of users’ support over the past year, the event features MEXC’s largest prize pool of the year, with up to 10 million USDT in prizes, including a 40-ounce gold bar and BTC.

The Golden Gratitude Gala is a flagship event of MEXC’s inaugural Global Blue Friday Initiative, an annual user appreciation day that MEXC plans to hold on the Friday after Thanksgiving. The initiative is an opportunity to thank users for their support, feedback, and engagement over the past year. The Golden Gratitude Gala brings this message to life through a substantial rewards program and exclusive benefits for VVIP users, making MEXC’s gratitude tangible.

Registration for the Golden Gratitude Gala is now open, with the Early Bird period running through November 30, 15:55 UTC. The main event runs from 16:00 UTC on November 30 to 08:00 UTC on December 21, when official registration also closes. The lucky draw period ends at 08:00 UTC on December 22.
