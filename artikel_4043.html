Guide: GTA 6 Release Times: All The Key Details Xbox Players Need To Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It's finally here! We've officially reached the launch week of GTA 6 on Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, PS5 and even the Xbox One (kind of), 13 long years since the most recent entry in the series back in 2013.

With that in mind, we thought we'd put up a quick release times guide so you know exactly when you can start playing the game, and we've also thrown in a few extra details about what we're expecting for Xbox One players at launch.

So, here are all the deets you need to know for Thursday!

On this page: GTA 6 Release Times: All The Key Details Xbox Players Need To Know

The official GTA 6 release date is Thursday, November 19th. You may still see people playing it on Wednesday though, as depending on where you live, certain players will be getting access to it earlier than others.

GTA 6 releases at midnight local time around the world. This means you'll be able to start playing it on Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S and PS5 as soon as November 18th turns into November 19th in your region.

Here's how it all works out based on a Universal Coordinated Time (UTC) time zone, for example:

Technically, yes. Due to the staggered rollout, players in New Zealand will be getting first access to GTA 6 at midnight in their time zone, which equates to 11am on November 18th here in the UK, and even earlier in the United States.

We urge caution around this, as Xbox's terms of service specifically state that you shouldn't switch regions unless you're actually located in the country you're switching to, but we'll leave the decision up to you.

News Here's What Xbox Owners Need To Know About 'Moving To New Zealand' For GTA 6

You may have noticed that we didn't mention Xbox One in the release times section up above, and that's because we don't quite know what's happening with it. In a nutshell, Xbox One players will be forced to use Xbox Cloud Gaming in order to get access to GTA 6, which in theory should require buying the game before access is granted.

There are too many unknowns ahead of launch about how this works though. Will cloud access be made available immediately? Will you be able to use the free-with-ads version of the cloud to play GTA 6, or will it require paying for the service? Is it just Xbox One that you can play it on, or will mobile and PC devices be supported too?

That's where we stand ahead of launch, so while GTA 6 definitely will be playable through the cloud on Xbox One in some form, it's probably best to wait until it's been released before handing over any money as things stand.
