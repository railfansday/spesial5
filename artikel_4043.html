'It's Unbelievably Hard to Achieve': Cyberpunk 2077 Boss Pours Praise on 'Masterpiece' GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

From Night City to Vice City, the boss of CDPR has nothing but nice things to say about GTA 6.

With the PS5 release of Cyberpunk 2077, the Polish studio established itself as an open world big boy, having previously released one of the greatest RPGs ever in The Witcher 3.

And so it’s only fitting for co-CEO Michal Nowakowski to pour praise on Rockstar’s latest release, now the highest rated game of all time.

“Rockstar Games created a masterpiece that I am planning to explore in the coming weeks and months. Massive congratulations to the whole team who worked on the game. It is unbelievably hard to achieve what you just did. Hats off to all of you.”

I personally believe it’s going to take years, if not decades, to surpass GTA 6.

The sheer density of the open world, from its dizzying array of art assets to the complexity of its sandbox simulation, is just in another universe to everything else.

Even if you’re not enjoying the game, it’s hard to argue against the standards being set here.

Of course, a high bar will raise the levels of absolutely everyone. I personally can’t wait to see what CDPR delivers with The Witcher 4 and its next Cyberpunk game.
