Lucky Luke Retro Collection Brings Several Games to the US for the Very First Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Atari has announced another retro games collection, and this one marks the United States debut of multiple old school classics.

This is Lucky Luke: The Lonesome Cowboy Collection, a compilation of retro titles developed by Graphite Lab and published by Infogrames. It's scheduled for launch on PS5 and PS4 on 13th January, 2027.

For those that don't know, Lucky Luke is a character that's been around since the 1940s. Originally a French-Belgian comic book character, he predates Asterix and The Smurfs, and has been adapted across various media throughout the decades.

That includes video games, of course, and six of his previous interactive excursions are included here:

"Every game features quality-of-life improvements, including improved graphics, save states, achievements, visual filters, custom screen bezels, and rebindable controls," reads the press release. "'How to Play' guides onboard unfamiliar players and help them solve difficult challenges."

Interestingly, several of these games never made it outside of Europe; in the above list, I've put an asterisk next to the games that will be brought to the USA for the first time in this collection.

As well as just compiling classic titles, the Lonesome Cowboy Collection includes a bonus called the Frontier Experience, which is a brand new, first-person feature that acts as a museum mode and shooting gallery.

You can use this mode to learn more about each game, hunt down collectibles, play minigames, and view old artwork.

I remember playing a demo of Lucky Luke on the PS1 back in the day, and can still recall the music from that one stage I went through over and over again.
