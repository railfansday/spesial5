AI maps overlooked housing to reveal gaps between Chicago's buildings and zoning rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Duplexes, townhouses and small apartment buildings, often called mid-density housing, could help cities ease shortages and improve affordability. However, there is little data on how much of this housing exists. A new study by Yale School of the Environment scientists that focuses on Chicago found that it is widespread across the city, yet much of it sits in neighborhoods where current zoning doesn't allow that housing type.
