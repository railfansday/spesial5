Mint Mobile, a prepaid carrier T-Mobile owns, sells T-Mobile service to people willing to pay for 3, 6, or 12 months at once. After the first term, a year of Unlimited costs $360 plus taxes and up to $33 in fees, against $540 plus taxes and fees at T-Mobile Prepaid with AutoPay, whose first 50GB a month gets priority on busy networks.

Mint fits budget-minded users with strong T-Mobile coverage who can prepay a year, since a new customer’s first year costs $180 and smaller plans renew at $15 to $25 a month on 12-month terms.
