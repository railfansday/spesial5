Rangers and Portsmouth swerved transfer bullet as Frank Lampard hit the buffers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Portsmouth head into the international break on an excellent run of form, as John Mousinho's side dare to dream of a place in the Championship eight come May.

Portsmouth, after a difficult start to the campaign where they won just two of their opening eight league games, have bounced back superbly, and instead of looking like relegation candidates, look like an outside shout for a play-off place.

Head coach Mousinho has done a superb job of taking Pompey from the depths of League One and cementing their status in the Championship, as now, the 40-year-old is aiming to take them a level higher.

Recruitment, on the whole, has been exceptional at Fratton Park over recent years despite a lack of funds, as Portsmouth may well have dodged an expensive bullet courtesy of their lack of financial muscle.

Under the ownership of Eisner's, Portsmouth have never spent beyond their means; instead, they have recruited smartly by uncovering undervalued talent across the globe.

Nicolas Schmid joined from Blau Weiss Linz in Austria, Adrian Segecic arrived from Sydney FC in Australia, Rocco Shein from Fredrikstad in Norway, and Marko Milovanovic headed to Fratton Park from Spanish outfit Almeria.

Whilst this summer has seen Pompey splash the cash a bit more than what they have been used to, just six months ago was not a similar story, having lost out on the capture of Surinamese winger Jahnoah Markelo from Swiss outfit FC Zurich to Coventry City.

Mousinho revealed he was interested in signing the winger during the January window in April 2026, but cited his reasoning for not completing the deal as the hefty fee, as Markelo headed to then-division rivals Coventry for around £4.3 million instead.

Following his move to the CBS Arena, Markelo found himself competing for minutes alongside the likes of Romain Esse, Min-hyeok Yang, Tatsuhiro Sakamoto, and Ephron Mason-Clark, with Brandon Thomas-Asante also offering an option out wide.

Markelo came through the ranks of FC Twente before spending time with Go Ahead Eagles, NK Kustosija Zagreb, NK Celje, and FC Zurich before his move to Coventry, but what looked to be an exciting signing at the time quickly proved to be a confusing one.

The 23-year-old was only included in eight of 17 Championship matchday squads he was available for, playing just a total of 44 minutes across five substitute appearances for the Sky Blues, who would eventually earn promotion to the Premier League via the title, to little impact from Markelo.

Following Coventry's promotion, it was already apparent that Markelo did not have a big role to play under Lampard, and heading up to a division likely meant even fewer opportunities would be afforded to him, and so that proved to be.

Just six months after signing for Coventry for around £4.3 million, a permanent exit was on the cards for the winger, as Scottish giants Rangers reportedly opened talks with the Sky Blues over a deal for Markelo.

However, the Surinamese instead opted to head to the Middle East with United Arab Emirates-based outfit Shabab Al-Ahli, where he has further failed to set the world alight.

Markelo has made just eight appearances for the club to date across all competitions, failing to register a single goal contribution, as right now, it looks like Portsmouth and Rangers have dodged a bullet with this deal.

With Markelo's lack of impact at either Coventry or Shabab Al-Ahli in the past 11 months or so, it's looking increasingly likely that both Portsmouth and Rangers have gotten away with one here.

Had Pompey had the funds to rival the Sky Blues for Markelo's signature back in January 2026, he may well have headed to Fratton Park instead, though whether he would have had better fortunes under Mousinho remains to be seen.

As it stands, the Surinamese has failed to justify his £4.3 million transfer fee, as Portsmouth and Rangers must both be relieved.
