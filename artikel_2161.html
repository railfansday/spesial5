GdS: Two games, one shot and an angry team-mate – ‘nightmare’ for Ramos extends to Portugal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan welcomed back a rejuvenated Goncalo Ramos after the last international break, but they will not do so this time.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport write, Ramos is finding himself ‘trapped in a nightmare season’, which is now also taking its toll on him for the national team too. As a reminder, he scored three goals in three starts (against Norway, Denmark and Norway again) during the September-October break.

This allowed him to put his struggles behind him for a couple of weeks, but things are now getting tough with the Seleção as well. Last Saturday – in a 4-1 defeat against Denmark – he finished the match without even touching the ball in the penalty box.

It was frighteningly similar to the isolation seen at Milan, and the problem is that his numbers didn’t improve even in Wales. Portugal won 3-2 but not only did the former PSG man not get his name on the scoresheet, he also didn’t participate in any of the goals directly.

His numbers in the 59 minutes he was on the pitch: 1 shot attempted (a header from Neto’s pass from the left in the 47th minute), 4/7 successful passes, 11 touches, and 1 unsuccessful dribble. The story of a striker who is not involved.

That’s not all, either. Gonçalo also angered Bruno Fernandes. It happened during a counter-attack, with the Portuguese captain breaking down the left and crossing low, expecting Ramos to be there. Gonçalo had stopped though, and Fernandes’s reprimand was inevitable.

The two would later have another exchange at the end of the match, with Bruno reminding his team-mate – who was disappointed to be brought off after less than an hour – of the responsibility of replacing a giant like Cristian Ronaldo. Oh, and Bruno Fernandes has been linked today, by the way.

In short, Goncalo Ramos is ‘twisted in a spiral he can’t seem to escape’. After the game in Cardiff last night, he has reached 661 minutes without a goal for club and country. The last one was exactly a month ago, on October 18th against Atalanta.
