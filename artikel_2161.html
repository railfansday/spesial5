Sheffield United face Sydie Peck fear as Newcastle & Sunderland update emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sheffield United midfield ace, Sydie Peck, has continued to generate transfer interest through his strong performances in the Championship attracting a plethora of potential Premier League suitors.

According to TEAMtalk, North East rivals Newcastle United and Sunderland are among a cluster of seven top-flight sides closely monitoring the 22-year-old's progress at Bramall Lane as the January transfer window edges ever closer.

Peck has been one of the standout performers in this part of the Steel City for some time now, even though United are currently in the thick of a third successive campaign in the second tier.

Despite well-documented disputes at boardroom level somewhat hampering Chris Wilder's summer transfer plans, the Blades are currently 10th in the Championship, having taken 22 points from their opening 15 matches.

Developments in S2 will also be on hold when it comes to Peck's current focus after the midfielder was called up to Lee Carsley's England Under-21s squad for a second successive international window for the Young Lions' upcoming friendlies with Portugal and France.

Peck has been an ever-present for United this season, with his constant availability also recently seeing him rack up over 100 appearances in all competitions for the South Yorkshire side.

Last season was a standout campaign for the London-born midfielder as he was nominated for the Championship's Young Player of the Season award, eventually losing out to current Wolves loanee, Jordan James, and he has gone from strength-to-strength so far this term.

Peck has scored four times and posted two assists in the season's early weeks, with such numbers significantly boosted after a recent first-half hat-trick in the 3-2 win over Preston North End at Deepdale.

The midfielder was the subject of reported interest from Brentford, Nottingham Forest and West Ham United in the summer, but they could end up behind Newcastle and Sunderland in the race for his services.

However, the likes of AFC Bournemouth, Brighton and Crystal Palace have also been credited with interest, with the former and latter of said trio both involved in this season's UEFA Europa League.

As previously mentioned, speculation was rife over Peck's immediate future in the summer after a disappointing 2025/26 season as a collective for the Blades.

It was recently claimed that a formal bid was turned down by the club in the summer, and that there remains a hope that the midfield ace will become a modern-day cult hero at Bramall Lane despite the potential for him to further his career at the highest level.

Peck is currently under contract with United until June 2028, and it has been reported that he is one player the club are, understandably, looking to secure fresh terms with in the coming weeks and months.

Even so, the current deal which the youth international is currently on means the Championship side still hold plenty of bargaining power, and the sheer number of suitors could also help ramp up Peck's valuation.

It was recently claimed by Football League World's Sheffield United fan pundit, Jimmy from Blades Ramble, that Peck is currently worth £15m, but that the club should be aiming for, at a bare minimum, £20m, for top-flight clubs to prize away the Blades' prized asset.
