All Bundled Up: Winter Picks for Photographers

When it comes to creating content during the winter, the last thing you want to worry about is your gear. You want to focus on capturing your images and videos. To accomplish that, you need gear that becomes an extension of your own artistic process. Before you set out on that cold weather shoot, stock up on these ProMaster products and you’ll be able to stay warm, protect your equipment, and keep creating all season long.

The pocket on the back of the hand gives you the option to store an extra memory card close at hand or slip in a handwarmer to keep your hands toasty. You’ll still be able to dial in your settings and finetune your setup with fingertips that fold back and stay back with magnets.

When you’re in cold, wet weather, you need to keep your gear safe and dry. That’s where the insulated, all-weather Camera Parka from ProMaster comes in. Crafted from a PU-coated nylon outer shell that’s water and wind resistant and cotton batting insulation that provides warmth.

It features two built-in clear windows, so you can see both the back and top of your camera and can operate the controls. Three lens ports are included with this kit to fit a wide variety of lenses. Open the bottom of the parka for easy attachment to a tripod. And, if you’re not wearing gloves, the fleece-lined interior is soft and comfortable against your hands.

When you’re out shooting in extreme temperatures, the last thing you want to worry about is if your memory card can keep up. With a unique molded design, they’re up to three times stronger than standard SD cards.

These cards don’t have common failure points like the lock switch and thin plastic ribs, which improves their durability. The bright red color makes these cards easy to spot if they end up buried in your bag or dropped in the snow. Rugged SD Cards are made for creators who push their gear to the limit. Available in UHS-I V30, UHS-II V60, and UHS-II V90 options, with capacities ranging from 32GB to 512GB.

Creating photography and video content in bright white wintery conditions can prove especially challenging. HGX Prime CPL filters take it to the next level with a Repellemax II Anti-static barrier that resists dust, dirt, oil, water and fingerprints, while still transmitting brilliant light to achieve the clearest possible image. Cutting-edge multi-coatings and black rimmed glass pass the clearest light to your sensor.
