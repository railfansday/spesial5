Broncos vs. Steelers practice participation report: Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Denver Broncos are getting healthier at the perfect time heading into the home stretch of the regular season. The last remaining guy in the secondary to have been missing time, safety Brandon Jones, is looking like he is all the way back for this Black Friday game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Nearly everyone was a full participant outside of safety Taylor Rapp who is dealing with a quadricep issue and defensive end Sai'vion Jones who injured his hamstring. The team has been trying out interior defensive lineman this week, so that hamstring injury doesn't sound like a minor one for Jones. Something to keep an eye one. Of course, we all have heard by now that running back J.K. Dobbins has been suspended for conduct detrimental to the team. You have to wonder if we've seen his last snap in Denver now.
