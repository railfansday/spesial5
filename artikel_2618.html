Joe Burrow makes MVP statement and Bengals destroy Steelers in blowout win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Joe Burrow is looking more and more like the NFL's MVP this season. And on Sunday night, the Cincinnati Bengals looked like a real threat to win a Super Bowl.

The Bengals' absolute dismantling of the Pittsburgh Steelers seemed like a possible dawning of something special for Cincinnati. They were that good in a 38-18 win against the Steelers, who were 6-2 coming in. The Bengals are 7-2 and now have the lead spot in the AFC North race.

Burrow was nearly perfect, the Bengals' offensive line stymied the Steelers' pass rush and Cincinnati's defense absolutely shut down Pittsburgh. Burrow had 409 yards and four touchdowns, and could have had much more if the Steelers kept the game competitive. Ja'Marr Chase also lit it up with 11 catches for 231 yards. Burrow had at least four total touchdowns for the fifth straight game, tying an NFL record set by Peyton Manning in 2004 according to NBC. Burrow is on pace for more than 5,000 yards and 50 touchdowns this season. The Bengals have accepted a pass heavy approach and Burrow is executing it at a very high level.

It was a statement in the AFC North race. But after the way the Bengals played on Sunday night in all phases, winning a division should be just the first part of Cincinnati's vision board. The team that a big national television audience watched on Sunday night is capable of making a deep playoff run and maybe even finally winning it all.

The Bengals' mauling of the Steelers was unbelievable. It was the kind of domination you see in college, not the NFL.

When the Bengals took a 17-0 lead with 5:28 left in the second quarter, some of the stats were hard to believe. Burrow was 21 of 26 for 245 at that point, and Aaron Rodgers was 0 of 7. It was the longest streak without a completion to start a game in Rodgers' 22-year career. He'd be 0 of 8 before completing his first pass. Chase had 135 yards at that point. The Steelers as a team had 14. The Bengals had 18 first downs while the Steelers had one.

At halftime, Burrow had 302 yards and three touchdowns. He hit Dohnte Meyers for a 15-yard touchdown with nine seconds left in the half to give the Bengals a 24-3 lead. The Steelers were out-gained 337-40 in the first half.

It is very hard to beat a professional team that thoroughly. Sometimes it can happen against a completely overmatched team or one that is heavily injured at the end of a lost season. This was a Steelers team that was tied for first place in the division coming in and was reasonably healthy.

It was arguably the best half of football any NFL team has played all season.

The Bengals' offense is very good and has been for some time. The defense is still working to get respect, and that might have been the most promising part of a fantastic win against the Steelers.

The Bengals made Rodgers look like a quarterback who will turn 43 years old next month. He missed on several throws and was harassed by the Bengals' pass rush most of the night. The Steelers couldn't even run the ball against a Cincinnati defense that had been vulnerable against good rushing attacks. Nothing within Pittsburgh's offense worked. Through three quarters the Steelers were averaging a minuscule 1.9 yards per play. That's probably a bad sign for Pittsburgh going forward, but also improvement from Cincinnati after it gave up 411 yards and 30 points to the Steelers in a Week 2 loss.

Since the Bengals came up just short of beating the Rams in Super Bowl LVI, the franchise seemed to be taking steps back. They missed the playoffs altogether each of the past three seasons. The offensive line has regularly struggled to protect Burrow and the defense sunk near the bottom of the NFL. Those areas have been cleaned up this season, through investments on the offensive line in the draft and free agency and a big addition on defense with an aggressive offseason trade to get defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence II.

The top of the AFC is tough this season. But an MVP-level quarterback with two superstar receivers, a solid offensive line and a much improved defense gives the Bengals a shot at doing something special. If they play in January like they did on Sunday night, they'll be tough to beat.
