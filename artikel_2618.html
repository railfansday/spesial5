NFL flexes Raiders-Browns to Sunday night in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Reigning No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza will make his primetime debut sooner than originally scheduled.

The NFL announced on Tuesday that it has flexed the Week 12 matchup between the Raiders and Browns in Cleveland to Sunday Night Football.

The two teams were originally scheduled to kick off at 1 p.m. with Patriots-Chargers scheduled for Sunday night.

New England and Los Angeles will now kick off at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS. The matchup between the Titans and Jaguars in Jacksonville has also been moved to 1 p.m. on Fox.

Currently 4-6, the Raiders will have a chance to be 5-6 when they come to Cleveland as they’ll take on the Broncos in Denver this weekend. It will be Mendoza’s second career start, with the matchup against the AFC North foe in Week 12 his third.

The surprising 6-4 Browns got back in the win column with a 27-24 victory over the Texans on Sunday. All six of Cleveland’s victories this season have come via a fourth-quarter comeback.

Cleveland and Las Vegas faced one another last season, with the Browns coming away with a 24-10 victory in Week 12. It was the first start of Shedeur Sanders’ career, though he is now serving as the backup to Deshaun Watson.

This will be the first Sunday night game in Cleveland since 2019, when the Browns fell to the Rams in Week 3.
