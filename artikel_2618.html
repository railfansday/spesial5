NBA Cup 2026 Update: Standings, clinching scenarios, with critical Cup games on NBC and Peacock Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

This is the deciding week for the NBA Cup group stage.

Games on Tuesday (on NBC and Peacock), Wednesday (on ESPN), and Friday (on Prime) will determine the six group-stage winners and two wild cards that will advance to the knockout rounds. The two Tuesday night games on NBC and Peacock — Boston at Washington at 8 p.m. ET, then Dallas at Denver at 10 p.m. — are both critical games in their groups.

Let's break down the group standings, with a reminder that the winner of each group advances, plus one wildcard from each conference, and after head-to-head, the next tiebreaker is point differential — it can be good to run up the score.

1. Dallas (2-0, +17 point differential) 2. Denver (2-0, +16) 3. Phoenix (1-1, +3) 4. Houston (1-2, -7) 5. Utah (0-3, -29)

Analysis: Denver hosts Dallas Tuesday night in a game that will essentially decide this group, and you can watch it at 10 p.m. ET on NBC and stream it on Peacock. If Denver wins, they are in as the Group A winners. If Dallas picks up the victory, it still needs Phoenix to lose to Houston Wednesday night, or Dallas can beat Phoenix on Friday, but either way Dallas very likely would advance.

1. Minnesota (2-0, +32) 2. Oklahoma City (2-1, +36) 3. Memphis (1-1, 0) 4. LA Clippers (1-1, -25) 5. New Orleans (0-3, -43)

Analysis: Minnesota travels to Oklahoma City on Wednesday night in the key game for this group. If Minnesota wins, it is in the driver's seat but still needs a Memphis loss in its last two games to clinch the group (and the Grizzlies are now without Cameron Boozer for a month). The Thunder lost to the Clippers in group play, so OKC still needs LA to lose another game to secure the division even if they beat Minnesota.

1. San Antonio (2-0, +53) 2. Los Angeles Lakers (2-1, -9) 3. Portland (1-1, +7) 4. Golden State (1-1, -21) 5. Sacramento (0-3, -30)

Analysis: Los Angeles at San Antonio Friday night is an epic early-season clash between the two top teams in Group C and the top two teams in this week's NBC Sports NBA Power Rankings. San Antonio also plays Portland on Wednesday in a game with key implications for the group.

1. Orlando (3-0, +17) 2. Toronto (2-1, +10) 3. Detroit (1-1, +13) 4. Brooklyn (0-2, -18) 5. Milwaukee (0-2, -22)

Analysis: Orlando hosts Detroit on Tuesday night and if the Magic win then they win the group. If the Pistons win on Tuesday, the door is thrown wide open, and things get complicated between Detroit, Toronto and Orlando (the Nets and Bucks are not going to advance).

1. Miami (2-0, +32) 2. Cleveland (2-1, +21) 3. Philadelphia (1-1, -19) 4. Indiana (1-2, -27) 5. New York (0-2, -7)

Analysis: We will not have a repeat NBA Cup champion as the Knicks are all but officially eliminated. For Miami, it's simple: Beat Philadelphia Wednesday and they win the group. If the 76ers win, they would take the group with a win over struggling Indiana on Friday.

1. Boston (2-0, +53) 2. Atlanta (2-1, +21) 3. Washington (2-1, +13) 4. Chicago (1-2, -18) 5. Charlotte (0-3, -69)

Analysis: If Boston can beat Washington Tuesday night in a game you can watch on NBC and Peacock (8 p.m. ET), they will be in the driver's seat heading into Friday's game against Atlanta. If the Wizards win we get chaos, because Washington lost to Atlanta, giving the Hawks the head-to-head tiebreaker.

One thing that has remained steady over the four years of the NBA Cup is the format. The only real change this season is that the NBA Cup semifinals will take place on a team's home court (the higher East and West seed remaining), and the NBA Cup Final will be played at the Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, home to the Butler Bulldogs — and the place where the real-life game the movie "Hoosiers" was based on was played.

All 30 teams were drawn into one of six groups (five teams in each group, three East groups and three West groups). Each team plays the other teams in its group once — four games total — and those games count double as both regular season games and NBA Cup games.

The six group winners plus a wild card from each conference — determined by record and point differential — go into an eight-team knockout round bracket (East vs. East, West vs. West). The knockout round games are single-elimination.

The Final two teams, one East and one West, will head to Indianapolis for the Dec. 11 championship game.

NBA Cup games are played on specially designed courts with the home team wearing their Statement Edition uniforms.

Money. And make no mistake, that is the biggest motivation. Players talk openly about it. How much money? Here's the breakdown:

Player on championship team: $604,985 Player on runner-up: $241,994 Player on semifinals losing team: $120,997 Player on quarterfinal losing team: $60,499

While even the championship money is comparable to another game check for a star like Giannis Antetokounmpo or Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, it can be a meaningful bump for players on minimum salaries at the end of the bench. The stars realize who they are playing for when things get serious. The winning team gets a trophy, and teams will hang a banner. Even the Knicks. Eventually.

The New York Knicks, behind stellar play from NBA Cup MVP Jalen Brunson, knocked off Victor Wembanyama and the San Antonio Spurs — a Cup Final loss that would be echoed come June and the NBA Finals.

In 2024, Milwaukee bounced back from an ugly 2-8 start to the season, found its footing in NBA Cup games, and went on a run behind Giannis Antetokounmpo, reaching the Cup Finals against Oklahoma City. Once there, Antetokounmpo had a 26-point triple-double, completely controlling the game on both sides of the ball. LeBron James and the Los Angeles Lakers won the inaugural 2023 NBA In-Season Tournament (before it was officially branded as the NBA Cup).

The NBA is moving the NBA Cup Final to Indianapolis's iconic Hinkle Fieldhouse, the home of Butler University.

Famously, Hinkle Fieldhouse hosted the 1954 Indiana high school state championship game that inspired the 1986 movie "Hoosiers," and climactic scenes from the film were shot there.
