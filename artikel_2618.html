Vikings-Falcons Second Injury Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Minnesota Vikings and Atlanta Falcons released their first injury reports in advance of their matchup on Sunday at U.S. Bank Stadium. Here they are:

PUP = Physically unable to participate list.

IR = Injured reserve. Note: only starters on IR are listed.

Probable = the league defines a Probable injury designation as a 75% or greater chance of playing. Historically, 95% of players designated probable are active and played on game day.

Questionable = the league defines a Questionable injury designation as being 'uncertain' of whether the player will play or not. This replaces the former definition of having a 50% chance of playing. Historically, 70-75% of players with a Questionable injury designation were active and played on game day.

Doubtful = The league defines a Doubtful injury designation to mean the player is unlikely to participate in the game. In practical terms, teams use the designation when a player has less than a 50% chance to suit up, and active game appearances from this designation remain rare. Historically only around 6% of players with a Doubtful designation are active and played on game day.

Out = will not play. Players with this designation are not active on game day and league rules prohibit them from playing.

For the Vikings, a little more clarity in Thursday's injury report. Christian Darrisaw didn't practice again so he's doubtful at this point for Sunday. Addison won't play either, as expected. And it looks like Isaiah Rodgers is more doubtful to play as well. The rest look more on track to play on Sunday.

For the Falcons, no upgrades among the DNPs yesterday, meaning all of them are more doubtful to play on Sunday, including starting guard Matthew Bergeron and starting wide receiver Jahan Dotson. Moreover, backup defensive end Za'Darius Smith didn't practice again today, but the reason went from a rest day to a knee injury which makes it less likely he'll play on Sunday. And two additional starters showed up on Thursday's injury as limited, which creates some uncertainty on their status for Sunday's game as well.

We'll have an update tomorrow with injury designations.
