Use HeyPiggy for a few dollars from spare minutes, since it shows each survey’s pay before you start and lets you cash out from $5. Don’t count on it for steady income. Its advertised sample surveys work out to roughly $7 to $16 an hour, before time lost to surveys that disqualify you.

We worked out that range from the three sample surveys on HeyPiggy’s homepage, which pay $0.70 for 6 minutes, $1.89 for 11, and $3.29 for 12. Berlin-based Make Opinion GmbH runs the site and its iPhone and Android apps for survey takers in most countries, and a survey from one of its research partners pays up to $10.

HeyPiggy suits people who fill spare minutes with short surveys and cash out each time they reach $5. Anyone who needs steady income should look for a part-time job, which pays for every hour you work.
