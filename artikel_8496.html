Use Karma if you shop online for specific items and can wait for a sale, since its free extension and app alert you when a saved item drops in price or comes back in stock. Don’t count on its cash back, which you can’t withdraw for 90 days after you buy, or before you have $30.

Karma keeps the items you save from any online store in one list and also warns you when one is about to sell out. Its browser extension and app apply coupon codes at checkout, too, and earn you cash back at partner stores such as Levi’s, Chanel, and Adidas.

Shoppers who save what they want and buy when an alert says the price fell get the most from Karma, and pay nothing for it. Those who already earn cash back through a portal such as Rakuten and trust their code finder can skip it and set price-drop alerts with a free price history tracker.
